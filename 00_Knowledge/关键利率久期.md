# 关键利率久期是在指定节点冲击与插值规则下衡量价格的局部曲线敏感度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Key-rate duration measures local curve sensitivity under specified node shocks and interpolation rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键利率久期（key-rate duration，KRD；也称局部久期或关键期限久期）衡量：某个关键期限的利率按约定方式变动一点时，价格相对改变多少。“只动一个节点”并不意味着只改变一个孤立日期；相邻期限如何跟着变化，由冲击形状与插值规则决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Key-rate duration (KRD), also called partial duration, measures the relative price response to a small, prescribed change at one key maturity. Moving one node need not alter only one isolated date: the shock shape and interpolation rule determine how neighbouring maturities move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定曲线报价、复利坐标和重估模型。以 $b_i(t)$ 表示第 $i$ 个节点的单位冲击形状，峰值节点取 1，令曲线从 $r_0(t)$ 变为 $r_0(t)+x_i b_i(t)$。若重估全价为 $P_i(x_i)$，$P_i(0)=P_0>0$，则在可微处：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the curve quotation, compounding coordinate, and repricing model. Let $b_i(t)$ describe a unit shock at node $i$, with value 1 at that node. The shocked curve is $r_0(t)+x_i b_i(t)$. If its repriced dirty price is $P_i(x_i)$ and $P_i(0)=P_0>0$, then, at a differentiable point:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
KRD_i=-\frac{P_i'(0)}{P_0},\qquad
\widehat{KRD}_i(h)=\frac{P_i(-h)-P_i(h)}{2hP_0}.
$$

例如在 2、5、10 年节点上采用分段线性零息率冲击，5 年节点上移 1bp、相邻两节点不动。于是 3.5 年上移 0.5bp，7.5 年也上移 0.5bp；这是一座三角形冲击，不是把“5 年债券”单独改价。若改为冲击平价报价后重新构建零息曲线，得到的是另一种敏感度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With piecewise-linear zero-rate shocks at 2, 5, and 10 years, raise the 5-year node by 1bp while holding its neighbours fixed. The 3.5-year rate then rises 0.5bp, as does the 7.5-year rate. This is a triangular curve shock, not a separate repricing of only a “five-year bond.” Bumping par quotes and rebuilding the zero curve defines a different sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若组合的小幅曲线变化可写为 $\Delta r(t)=\sum_i\Delta x_i b_i(t)$，同一模型的一阶估计为 $\Delta P/P_0\approx-\sum_i KRD_i\Delta x_i$。例如市值 500 万美元、某节点 $KRD_i=2$，该节点上移 10bp 而其余节点固定，约损失 1 万美元。各节点可以异号，所以总和很小不代表每段暴露都小；符合 [[关键久期加总条件]] 可保证总和在导数意义下等于同口径平行曲线久期，但不符合这些条件时，具体头寸的数字仍可能偶然相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For small curve changes of the form $\Delta r(t)=\sum_i\Delta x_i b_i(t)$, the same model gives $\Delta P/P_0\approx-\sum_i KRD_i\Delta x_i$. A USD 5 million position with node duration 2 loses approximately USD 10,000 when that node rises 10bp and the others stay fixed. Node sensitivities can have opposite signs, so a small total need not mean small local exposures. The [[关键久期加总条件|aggregation conditions]] are sufficient to guarantee that the derivative-based total equals parallel curve duration under the same convention; without them, the numbers can still coincide accidentally for a particular position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结果必须附节点集合、最短和最长期限外的处理、插值或重建规则、固定利差及步长。换节点或换规则会改变数字；它不是脱离模型而天然唯一的“某期限风险”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report the node set, treatment beyond its endpoints, interpolation or rebuilding rules, frozen spreads, and bump size. Changing these choices changes the numbers: “risk at a maturity” is not uniquely defined independently of the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
