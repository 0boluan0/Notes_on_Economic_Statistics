# 有偏自我归因是不对称地把成功归于自身而把失败归于外部因素
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Biased self-attribution asymmetrically credits oneself for success and external factors for failure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有偏自我归因是解释自身结果时采用偏向维护能力评价的不对称标准：成功更容易被归因于技能或判断，失败更容易被归因于运气、环境或他人。这种归因模式可使确认原判断的结果更明显地提高自信，而相反结果不能相称地下调自信；[[DHS信心反馈]]把这种差异纳入动态金融模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Biased self-attribution uses asymmetric standards that protect one's assessment of ability: success is more readily attributed to skill or judgment, failure to luck, circumstances, or others. Confirming outcomes may then increase confidence more than contradictory outcomes reduce it; [[DHS信心反馈|DHS confidence feedback]] incorporates this asymmetry into a dynamic financial model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个最小识别场景是事前采用相同方法的两次判断：命中时解释为“方法准确”，未命中时却在没有新增证据的情况下仅解释为“偶然干扰”。需要检查的是两种解释采用的证据标准，而不是简单要求成功与失败后的数值更新必须完全对称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal diagnostic setting compares two judgments made using the same method: a hit is credited to the method, whereas a miss is attributed solely to chance without additional evidence. The issue is the evidential standard used for the explanations, not a blanket requirement for numerically symmetric updates after success and failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果成功与失败对能力确实有不同的信息含量，不对称更新可以合理。判定“有偏”必须排除这种差异；它也不自动意味着当事人始终[[过度自信]]，因为归因机制与自信水平不是同一对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asymmetric updating may be justified when success and failure genuinely differ in their information about ability. Calling it biased requires addressing that possibility. The mechanism also does not imply persistent [[过度自信|overconfidence]]: an attribution process and a confidence level are different objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The diagnostic setting is independently constructed; the information-content qualification prevents equating every asymmetric update with bias.
