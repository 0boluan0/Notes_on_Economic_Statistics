# 社会必要劳动时间规定商品价值量而必要劳动时间划分工人的工作日
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Socially necessary labour time determines commodity value, while necessary labour time divides the worker's day*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[社会必要劳动时间]] 回答的是：一种商品在社会正常生产条件和平均劳动熟练程度、强度下，需要多少劳动时间。工作日分析中的 [[必要劳动|必要劳动时间]] 回答的是：一个工人需要在日内劳动多久，才能创造其劳动力价值的等价物。判断术语时，先确定正在解释哪一种商品的价值，还是正在划分工人的工作日。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[社会必要劳动时间|Socially necessary labour time]] asks how much labour a commodity requires under normal social conditions of production and average skill and intensity. [[必要劳动|Necessary labour time]] in the analysis of the working day asks how long a worker must labour to create an equivalent of the value of their labour-power. To interpret the term, first identify whether the argument concerns a commodity's value or the division of the worker's day.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两者通过 [[劳动力价值]] 相接：社会生产条件决定再生产劳动力所需生活资料的价值；在劳动力按价值出售的条件下，工人需要用工作日的一部分创造该价值的等价物。工人日内生产的可以是纱，而他的生活资料可以是食物、衣物等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two are connected through the [[劳动力价值|value of labour-power]]. Social conditions of production determine the value of the means of subsistence needed to reproduce labour-power. When labour-power sells at its value, the worker must create its equivalent during part of the working day. The worker may produce yarn while consuming food, clothing and other means of subsistence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一件纱在社会正常条件下总共需要1小时劳动，某工人的日劳动力价值则相当于4小时平均社会劳动。前一个数字规定一件纱的价值量，后一个数字在相同劳动折算和按价值支付的条件下，规定该工人一天的必要劳动时间；它们可以同时成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose a unit of yarn requires one hour of labour in total under normal social conditions, while a worker's daily labour-power is worth four hours of average social labour. The first figure determines the value of a unit of yarn. With consistent labour conversion and payment at value, the second determines the worker's daily necessary labour time. Both can hold simultaneously.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第9章第1节及注5](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm)：第1节用生活资料的价值等价物界定必要劳动；注5明确说明“necessary labour-time”先用于一般商品、此后也用于劳动力商品的生产，并承认术语复用的困难。结合 [第1章第1节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch01.htm#S1)的社会必要劳动时间定义核验两者的对象。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。1小时与4小时的并列为教学构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 9, Section 1 defines necessary labour through the equivalent of subsistence value. Note 5 explicitly identifies the term's use for commodities generally and subsequently for the particular commodity labour-power, acknowledging the difficulty of reusing it. Chapter 1, Section 1 supplies the definition of socially necessary labour time. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The one-hour and four-hour comparison is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
