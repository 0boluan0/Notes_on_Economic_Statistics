# 人口转型是一个人口由高生育和高死亡水平转向低生育和低死亡水平的长期过程

人口转型是从高生育、高死亡水平转向低生育、低死亡水平的长期过程。常见路径是死亡水平先下降而生育下降较迟，于是自然增长一度加快；随后生育下降，人口增长逐渐放缓，年龄结构也随出生队列的更替而改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Demographic transition is the long-run movement from high fertility and mortality to low fertility and mortality. A common path has mortality declining before fertility, initially accelerating natural increase. Fertility decline then slows population growth, while successive birth cohorts reshape the age distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“先后”是常见的经验路径，不是每个国家都必须依次经历、长度相同的固定阶段。战争、疾病冲击、出生波动和迁移可以改变观察到的路径；低生育与低死亡也不要求人口立即静止，[[人口惯性]]和净迁入仍然重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This ordering is a common empirical path, not a compulsory sequence of stages of equal duration for every country. Mortality crises, fertility fluctuations and migration can alter the observed path. Low fertility and mortality do not require immediate population stationarity; [[人口惯性|population momentum]] and net immigration still matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阅读图时，[[粗出生率]]与[[粗死亡率]]的差反映同一暴露口径下的自然增长频率；比较女性生育强度使用[[总和生育率]]。死亡的年龄构成效应也须单独检查。年龄结构出现有利窗口后能否形成实际收益，接着看[[人口红利]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a rate diagram, the difference between the [[粗出生率|crude birth rate]] and [[粗死亡率|crude death rate]] measures natural increase relative to the same exposure. Use the [[总和生育率|total fertility rate]] for fertility intensity, and check mortality’s age-composition effect separately. Whether a favourable age window produces an economic gain is addressed by the [[人口红利|demographic dividend]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
