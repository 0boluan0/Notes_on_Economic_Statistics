# Stirling’s formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

斯特林公式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A factorial counts arrangements: for a positive integer $n$, $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$ is the number of ways to put $n$ distinct objects in order, as in [[排列|permutations]]. The exact product quickly becomes large. Stirling’s formula replaces it with an expression built from a power, an exponential, and a square root, while specifying what “close” means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阶乘用于计数排列：对正整数 $n$，$n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$ 是把 $n$ 个不同对象排成一列的方法数，见[[排列]]。这个精确乘积很快变大。斯特林公式用幂、指数和平方根组成的表达式近似它，并说明这里的“接近”是什么意思。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $A_n=\sqrt{2\pi n}(n/e)^n$, where $e$ is the base of the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] and $\pi$ is the circle constant. The symbol $\sim$ means [[渐近等价|asymptotic equivalence]]: the ratio tends to $1$ as the positive integer $n$ tends to infinity. The convention $0!=1$ is separate; this displayed approximation is used for $n\ge1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $A_n=\sqrt{2\pi n}(n/e)^n$，其中 $e$ 是[[自然对数]]的底，$\pi$ 是圆周率。符号 $\sim$ 表示[[渐近等价]]：当正整数 $n$ 趋于无穷时，比值趋于 $1$。约定 $0!=1$ 是另一回事；这里的近似式用于 $n\ge1$。

> [!summary] The asymptotic formula · 渐近公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The factorial divided by $A_n$ tends to $1$. This controls relative error in the limit; it is not an equality at a fixed finite $n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> \[
> n!\sim\sqrt{2\pi n}\left(\frac ne\right)^n
> \]
> 的含义是
> \[
> \frac{n!}{\sqrt{2\pi n}(n/e)^n}\longrightarrow1.
> \]
> 这是渐近等价，不是有限 \(n\) 的恒等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $10!=3\,628\,800$, whereas $A_{10}\approx3\,598\,695.62$. The difference is about $30\,104.38$, but its size relative to the true factorial is only about $0.8296\%$. A large absolute difference can coexist with a small relative error. The theorem alone does not give a numerical error bound for an assigned $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$10!=3\,628\,800$，而 $A_{10}\approx3\,598\,695.62$。两者相差约 $30\,104.38$，但相对于真实阶乘的误差只有约 $0.8296\%$。绝对差较大与相对误差较小可以同时发生。定理本身没有给指定 $n$ 的数值误差界。

## Logarithms and growth
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对数与增长速度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking natural logarithms makes the main structure easier to see.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取自然对数后，主结构更容易看见：

\[
\ln(n!)=n\ln n-n+\tfrac12\ln(2\pi n)+o(1).
\]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $o(1)$ denotes a sequence tending to zero: it is exactly $\ln(n!/A_n)$. Thus the error in the logarithm tends to zero. A weaker statement such as $\ln(n!)\sim n\ln n$ cannot by itself recover the precise multiplicative factor $\sqrt{2\pi n}$. In the following growth comparison, the base $c$ is fixed and positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $o(1)$ 表示趋于零的数列，它恰好是 $\ln(n!/A_n)$。因此这里要求对数的误差趋于零。较弱的 $\ln(n!)\sim n\ln n$ 本身不能恢复精确的乘法因子 $\sqrt{2\pi n}$。下面的增长比较限定底数 $c$ 固定且为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every fixed $c>0$, $n!/c^n\to\infty$, while $n!/n^n\to0$. For the first ratio its logarithm is $n\ln n-n-n\ln c+\tfrac12\ln(2\pi n)+o(1)$, which tends to infinity; for the second it is $-n+\tfrac12\ln(2\pi n)+o(1)$, which tends to minus infinity. This converts factorials in permutation counts, binomial coefficients, and probabilities into sums that are easier to compare.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此阶乘比任何固定底数的指数 \(c^n\) 增长更快，但远小于 \(n^n\)。它可把排列数、二项式系数和概率中的阶乘表达式变成便于比较的加法尺度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These comparisons concern limits of ratios, not inequalities asserted for every small $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确地说，前一比值 $n!/c^n$ 的对数是 $n\ln n-n-n\ln c+\tfrac12\ln(2\pi n)+o(1)$，趋于正无穷；后一比值 $n!/n^n$ 的对数是 $-n+\tfrac12\ln(2\pi n)+o(1)$，趋于负无穷。因此两比值分别趋于无穷和零。这些比较描述比值的极限，并非断言每个较小 $n$ 都满足某个大小关系。

## Why the constant is exactly √(2π)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么常数恰好是 √(2π)

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof has two tasks: show that $n!/[\sqrt n(n/e)^n]$ approaches some positive constant, then determine that constant. This proves the asymptotic formula without assuming the sharper finite bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明分成两件事：先说明 $n!/[\sqrt n(n/e)^n]$ 趋于某个正常数，再确定这个常数。这个路线不预先假设更强的有限规模界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### A positive limiting constant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个正的极限常数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $a_n=n!/[\sqrt n(n/e)^n]$. Direct cancellation of consecutive factorials gives the logarithmic decrease $d_n$. Put $t=1/(2n+1)$, so $0<t\le1/3$. Subtract the two absolutely convergent [[对数函数幂级数|logarithm series]] at $t$ and $-t$ to obtain the series below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $a_n=n!/[\sqrt n(n/e)^n]$。约去相邻阶乘的公共因子，得到对数下降量 $d_n$。再令 $t=1/(2n+1)$，于是 $0<t\le1/3$。将 $t$ 与 $-t$ 处绝对收敛的[[对数函数幂级数]]相减，就得到下面的展开。

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_n:=\ln\frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}}
&=\left(n+\tfrac12\right)\ln\left(1+\frac1n\right)-1,\\
\ln\frac{1+t}{1-t}&=2\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{2j+1}}{2j+1},\\
d_n&=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\frac{t^{2j}}{2j+1}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every term is positive. Replacing each denominator by $3$ makes the sum strictly larger, because the terms with $j\ge2$ have larger denominators. Summing the resulting [[几何级数|geometric series]] gives a bound that cancels when added over consecutive $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一项都为正。把所有分母换成 $3$ 会使和严格增大，因为 $j\ge2$ 的项原分母更大。将所得[[几何级数]]求和，得到一个相邻相加时会抵消的上界。

$$
0<d_n<\frac{t^2}{3(1-t^2)}
=\frac1{12n(n+1)}
=\frac1{12}\left(\frac1n-\frac1{n+1}\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, $\ln a_{N+1}=\ln a_1-\sum_{n=1}^{N}d_n$ decreases but stays above $\ln a_1-1/12$. A decreasing real sequence bounded below has a finite limit. Exponentiating shows that $a_n\to C$ for some $C>0$; positivity follows from the finite logarithmic limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $\ln a_{N+1}=\ln a_1-\sum_{n=1}^{N}d_n$ 递减，却始终大于 $\ln a_1-1/12$。有下界的递减实数列存在有限极限。再取指数，得到 $a_n\to C$，其中 $C>0$；正性来自对数极限有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### Determining the constant by an integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用积分确定常数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonnegative integer $m$, define $I_m=\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^m x\,dx$. Then $I_0=\pi/2$ and $I_1=1$. For $m\ge2$, [[分部积分|integration by parts]] with $u=\sin^{m-1}x$ and $dv=\sin x\,dx$ has a zero boundary term and gives $I_m=(m-1)(I_{m-2}-I_m)$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非负整数 $m$，定义 $I_m=\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^m x\,dx$。有 $I_0=\pi/2$、$I_1=1$。当 $m\ge2$ 时，在[[分部积分]]中取 $u=\sin^{m-1}x$、$dv=\sin x\,dx$，边界项为零，得到 $I_m=(m-1)(I_{m-2}-I_m)$。于是

$$
I_m=\frac{m-1}{m}I_{m-2},\qquad
I_{2n}=\frac\pi2\prod_{k=1}^{n}\frac{2k-1}{2k},\qquad
I_{2n+1}=\prod_{k=1}^{n}\frac{2k}{2k+1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $0<\sin x<1$ in the interior of the integration interval, $I_{2n+1}<I_{2n}<I_{2n-1}$. Let $W_n$ be the product below. The first inequality, together with $I_{2n+1}/I_{2n}=(2/\pi)W_n$, gives its lower-side comparison with $\pi/2$. The second uses $I_{2n-1}=(2n+1)I_{2n+1}/(2n)$ and gives the upper-side comparison. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于积分区间内部有 $0<\sin x<1$，所以 $I_{2n+1}<I_{2n}<I_{2n-1}$。记下面的乘积为 $W_n$。第一个不等式配合 $I_{2n+1}/I_{2n}=(2/\pi)W_n$，给出 $W_n<\pi/2$；第二个不等式配合 $I_{2n-1}=(2n+1)I_{2n+1}/(2n)$，给出另一侧比较。因此

$$
W_n:=\prod_{k=1}^{n}\frac{(2k)^2}{(2k-1)(2k+1)},\qquad
W_n<\frac\pi2<\frac{2n+1}{2n}W_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These inequalities squeeze $W_n$ to $\pi/2$. Now express the same product in factorials and substitute the definition of $a_n$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些不等式将 $W_n$ 夹向 $\pi/2$。再把同一个乘积改写成阶乘，并代入 $a_n$ 的定义：

$$
W_n=\frac{2^{4n}(n!)^4}{((2n)!)^2(2n+1)}
=\frac{a_n^4}{a_{2n}^2}\frac{n}{2(2n+1)}
\longrightarrow\frac{C^2}{4}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore $C^2/4=\pi/2$. Since $C>0$, $C=\sqrt{2\pi}$, completing the proof of $n!\sim A_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $C^2/4=\pi/2$。因为 $C>0$，得到 $C=\sqrt{2\pi}$，从而证明 $n!\sim A_n$。

## A limit versus a finite error bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限与有限误差界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strict bound at a particular size requires more than the symbol $\sim$. [[Robbins阶乘界|Robbins’ factorial bounds]] provide explicit logarithmic error bounds for every positive integer $n$; the present theorem establishes the main asymptotic formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若题目要求有限样本的严格上下界，不能只引用符号 \(\sim\)。可以改用 [[Robbins阶乘界]]；它给出每个正整数 \(n\) 都成立的显式余项范围，而本卡只承担主渐近式。

> [!question]- Check the meaning · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $a_n\sim b_n$, may we replace one by the other as an exact equality at a fixed finite $n$?
>
> **Answer:** No. The ratio tends to $1$ as $n\to\infty$; a finite-size error needs a separate estimate or an explicit bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 已知 \(a_n\sim b_n\)，能否在某个有限 \(n\) 上把两者直接写成相等？
>
> **答案：** 不能。它只保证比值在 \(n\to\infty\) 时趋于 1；有限 \(n\) 的误差需要另行估计或使用显式界。
