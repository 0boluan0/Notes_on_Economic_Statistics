# Pigeonhole principle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*鸽巢原理／抽屉原理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose every sock is red, green or blue. Three socks could have three different colours. With four socks, however, at least two must share a colour: if each of the three colours appeared at most once, there could be at most three socks.

The **pigeonhole principle** makes this capacity argument precise. The objects are the “pigeons,” the categories are the “holes,” and every object must be assigned to exactly one category. To apply it, first specify the [[计数对象|objects being counted]] and the rule assigning them to categories. The categories need not be equally likely or equally populated; no randomness is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设每只袜子都是红、绿、蓝三种颜色之一。三只袜子可能各不相同；但四只袜子里至少有两只同色，因为如果每种颜色最多只出现一只，总共最多就只有三只。

**鸽巢原理**把这种“总容量不够”的论证写清楚。对象是“鸽子”，类别是“巢”，每个对象必须恰好分到一个类别。使用时先确定[[计数对象]]，再说明把对象分到类别的规则。类别不必等可能，也不必装得一样多；整个结论不需要随机性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How many must share a category?

Put $m$ objects into $n$ boxes, where $m\ge0$ and $n\ge1$ are integers. At least one box contains at least $\lceil m/n\rceil$ objects. The ceiling notation $\lceil x\rceil$ means the smallest integer greater than or equal to $x$; for instance, $\lceil7/3\rceil=3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*至少有几个对象同类？*

把 $m$ 个对象分进 $n$ 个盒子，其中 $m\ge0$、$n\ge1$ 都是整数，至少有一个盒子包含 $\lceil m/n\rceil$ 个对象。向上取整符号 $\lceil x\rceil$ 表示不小于 $x$ 的最小整数，例如 $\lceil7/3\rceil=3$。

> [!important] The strict threshold · 保证成立的严格阈值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a positive integer $q$, to guarantee at least $q$ objects in some box, it is enough that
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对正整数 $q$，要保证某个盒子至少有 $q$ 个对象，只要
>
> $$
> m>(q-1)n.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If every box held at most $q-1$ objects, the total would be at most $(q-1)n$, contradicting the inequality. For unrestricted assignments this threshold is sharp: at $m=(q-1)n$, putting exactly $q-1$ objects in every box avoids having $q$ in any box.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果每个盒子最多装 $q-1$ 个，总量就最多是 $(q-1)n$，与不等式矛盾。没有额外分配限制时，这个阈值不能再降低：在 $m=(q-1)n$ 时，每盒恰放 $q-1$ 个，就没有任何盒子达到 $q$ 个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For seven objects and three boxes, $7>2\times3$ guarantees a box with at least three objects. A distribution of $3,2,2$ shows that “at least four” is not guaranteed. More generally, $m=qn$ guarantees at least $q$ in some box, not $q+1$; the equally filled distribution is the counterexample. If $m=0$, the ceiling bound is zero and says only that a box contains at least zero objects. The condition $n\ge1$ avoids division by zero and ensures there is a box to talk about.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

七个对象放进三个盒子，因为 $7>2\times3$，必有一盒至少三个；分布 $3,2,2$ 则说明不能保证至少四个。一般地，$m=qn$ 只能保证某盒至少 $q$ 个，不能保证 $q+1$ 个，均匀分配就是反例。若 $m=0$，取整下界也是零，只说某盒至少有零个对象。要求 $n\ge1$ 既避免除以零，也保证确实有盒子可讨论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The function formulation

Let $A$ be a finite set of objects, $B$ a nonempty finite set of categories, and $f:A\to B$ a function assigning every object to one category. For $b\in B$, the set $f^{-1}(b)=\{a\in A:f(a)=b\}$ contains the objects assigned to $b$. With $k$ a nonnegative integer, the generalised principle is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用函数表示分配*

设 $A$ 是有限对象集合，$B$ 是非空的有限类别集合，函数 $f:A\to B$ 把每个对象分到一个类别。对 $b\in B$，集合 $f^{-1}(b)=\{a\in A:f(a)=b\}$ 包含所有被分到 $b$ 的对象。对非负整数 $k$，广义原理写成

$$
|A|>k|B|\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{there exists }b\in B\text{ with }|f^{-1}(b)|\ge k+1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The groups $f^{-1}(b)$ are disjoint and cover $A$. If each group had at most $k$ objects, the [[加法法则|sum rule]] would give $|A|\le k|B|$, a contradiction. This proof also shows why the assignment must cover every object: leaving some objects unassigned would invalidate the capacity comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各组 $f^{-1}(b)$ 互不重叠，并且覆盖 $A$。如果每组最多 $k$ 个，由[[加法法则]]就会得到 $|A|\le k|B|$，与前提矛盾。这也说明为什么每个对象都必须被分配：若把部分对象留在盒子之外，总容量比较就不成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the existence guarantee tells us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它证明存在，不证明位置

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Among thirteen people, at least two share a birth month: assign each person to one of the twelve months, and $13>12$ forces a collision. The conclusion does not identify which month or which people, and it does not say that a specified month has two people. It says nothing about birthdays falling on the same day.

Counts alone give an existence guarantee, not the complete distribution or a particular matching pair. If the actual assignments are available, one can inspect them to find a crowded category; the pigeonhole argument itself does not supply that information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

十三个人中至少有两人出生月份相同：把每个人分到十二个月之一，$13>12$ 就保证出现同类对象。结论没有指出哪一个月、哪两个人，也没有保证某个指定月份有两个人；它更没有说生日是同一天。

只凭总数量能得到存在保证，得不到完整分布或指定配对。如果已有实际分配记录，可以逐项检查找出拥挤类别；鸽巢论证本身没有提供这些具体信息。

> [!example] One hundred integers · 一百个整数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each of one hundred integer entries has exactly one remainder in $0,1,\ldots,9$ when divided by $10$. These are ten categories, so at least ten entries have the same remainder: $\lceil100/10\rceil=10$. This is the meaning of being [[同余|congruent modulo ten]]. Entries are the counted objects; repeated integer values still occupy separate positions in the list. Ten entries in every remainder class show why eleven are not guaranteed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一百个整数条目除以 $10$，都恰有一个余数落在 $0,1,\ldots,9$ 中。只有十类余数，所以至少十个条目余数相同：$\lceil100/10\rceil=10$，即它们[[同余|模十同余]]。这里数的是条目，即使数值重复，列表中的位置仍是不同对象。每种余数各有十个条目，就说明不能保证十一项同余。
