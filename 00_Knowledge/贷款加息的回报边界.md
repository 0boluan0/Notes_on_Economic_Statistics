# 提高合同利率不保证提高每笔贷款的预期净回报

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A higher contractual loan rate need not raise expected net return per loan*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[债务合约]]约定收到多少钱，与银行实际预期收回多少钱是两件事。在无抵押、有限责任且无破产处理成本的简化贷款中，贷款额为 $B$、利率为 $i$，承诺到期支付 $D=B(1+i)$；若项目收入为 $Y$，银行实际回收为 $\min(Y,D)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repayment promised by a [[债务合约|debt contract]] differs from expected actual recovery. In a simplified loan with no collateral, limited liability, and no bankruptcy-processing costs, a loan of $B$ at rate $i$ promises $D=B(1+i)$. If project revenue is $Y$, the bank actually recovers $\min(Y,D)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A(i)=\mathbb E_i[\min(Y,B(1+i))],\qquad
\Pi(i)=A(i)-B(1+f)-c.
$$

$A(i)$ 是每笔贷款的预期回收额；若每笔贷款的融资成本率 $f$ 和其他成本 $c$ 固定，$\Pi(i)$ 才是这里的预期净利润。仅减去本金 $B$ 不等于已经扣除了全部融资与经营成本。下标 $i$ 提醒我们：利率可能同时改变获贷池的类型权重和借款人的行动分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$A(i)$ is expected recovery per loan. With fixed funding-cost rate $f$ and other per-loan costs $c$, $\Pi(i)$ is expected net profit in this model. Subtracting principal alone does not deduct every funding and operating cost. The subscript $i$ emphasises that rates may also change type weights in the financed pool and the distribution of borrower actions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若固定同一个项目收入分布，$\min(Y,D)$ 随 $D$ 不减，所以承诺额提高的直接回收效应不为负。回报下降必须来自申请／获贷构成、行动或成本等其他变化；不能把它归因于“较高承诺额本身使固定现金流更少”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed revenue distribution, $\min(Y,D)$ is nondecreasing in $D$, so the direct recovery effect of a higher promised payment is nonnegative. A decline must come from selection, actions, costs, or another change, not from a larger promise somehow reducing an unchanged cash flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用[[贷款利率筛选效应|两类型例子]]，若潜在人群中安全型占 $80\%$、风险型占 $20\%$，银行在申请人中不另作类型选择：$D=110$ 时两型都申请，预期回收为 $0.8\times110+0.2\times0.8\times110=105.6$；$D=115$ 时只剩风险型，回收为 $0.8\times115=92$。在相同融资与其他成本下，净回报也下降。这个比较没有说明 110 是最优承诺额，均衡还须另查[[均衡信贷配给条件]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reuse the [[贷款利率筛选效应|two-type example]], with $80\%$ safe and $20\%$ risky potential borrowers and no additional type selection by the bank. At $D=110$, both apply and expected recovery is $0.8\times110+0.2\times0.8\times110=105.6$. At $D=115$, only risky types remain and recovery is $0.8\times115=92$. Net return also falls if funding and other costs are unchanged. This does not establish 110 as the optimal repayment; see [[均衡信贷配给条件|equilibrium-rationing conditions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
