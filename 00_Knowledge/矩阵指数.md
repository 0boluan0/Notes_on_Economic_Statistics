# Matrix exponential
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*矩阵指数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **matrix exponential** extends the ordinary exponential function from numbers to square matrices. It combines the identity, the matrix itself, and all its higher powers with progressively smaller factorial weights. The output is another matrix of the same size. Multiplying a vector by it produces a new vector; the resulting transformation is central to [[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|solving constant-coefficient differential equations]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**矩阵指数**把普通指数函数从数推广到方阵：它把单位矩阵、矩阵本身及更高次幂按阶乘权重加在一起，得到另一个同尺寸矩阵。这个新矩阵乘以向量，会产生新的向量；这种变换是[[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|求解常系数微分方程]]的关键。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with the scalar series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从普通指数的级数出发*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[指数函数幂级数|scalar exponential series]] writes $e^x=1+x+x^2/2!+x^3/3!+\cdots$. Here $k!=1\cdot2\cdots k$ and $0!=1$. For a square matrix, use [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] to form $A^2=AA$, $A^3=AAA$, and so on. Replace the scalar constant $1$ by the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I$, which leaves vectors unchanged. Every summand then has the same dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[指数函数幂级数]]把普通指数写成 $e^x=1+x+x^2/2!+x^3/3!+\cdots$，其中 $k!=1\cdot2\cdots k$，$0!=1$。对方阵，用[[矩阵乘法]]构成 $A^2=AA$、$A^3=AAA$ 等；把常数1替换成保持向量不变的[[单位矩阵]] $I$。这样，各个加数都有相同尺寸。

> [!important] Definition · 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ real or complex matrix, with integer $n\ge1$, and let $t$ be a real or complex scalar. Define
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A$ 是 $n\times n$ 实矩阵或复矩阵，整数 $n\ge1$；$t$ 是实数或复数标量。定义
>
> $$e^{tA}=\exp(tA)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^kA^k}{k!}=I+tA+\frac{t^2A^2}{2!}+\cdots,\qquad A^0=I.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[无穷级数|infinite sum]] means the limit of its finite partial sums: first stop at $k=m$, then let $m$ increase. The matrix limit is obtained by taking the limit of each entry. This series converges absolutely for every such $A$ and $t$; a reason is given below. Writing $e^A$ simply sets $t=1$. In particular, $e^{0A}=I$ and $e^{t0}=I$ for the zero matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无穷级数|无限求和]]指有限部分和的极限：先在 $k=m$ 处停下，再让 $m$ 不断增大。矩阵极限就是每个元素的极限。对上述每个 $A$ 和 $t$，这个级数都绝对收敛，下文给出理由。写 $e^A$ 只是取 $t=1$。特别地，$e^{0A}=I$；对零矩阵也有 $e^{t0}=I$。

> [!example] An infinite definition can give a finite answer · 无限定义也可以得到有限结果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. Multiplication gives $N^2=0$, so all terms from the second power onward vanish exactly. Thus $e^{tN}=I+tN$. Applied to the column $\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}$, this sends it to $\begin{pmatrix}a+tb\\b\end{pmatrix}$: the second coordinate stays fixed while contributing to the first.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$，相乘得到 $N^2=0$，所以从二次幂起的各项精确为零。因此 $e^{tN}=I+tN$。它把列向量 $\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}$ 变成 $\begin{pmatrix}a+tb\\b\end{pmatrix}$：第二个坐标保持不变，同时影响第一个坐标。
>
> $$e^{tN}=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This $N$ is not [[对角化|diagonalizable]]: its only eigenvalue is zero and its [[特征对|eigenvectors]], the nonzero vectors that $N$ merely scales, lie on a single line. Nevertheless its exponential is well-defined. The [[Jordan块的指数|exponential formula for a larger Jordan block]] gives a related finite-polynomial calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个 $N$ 不能[[对角化]]：它只有特征值零，[[特征对|特征向量]]（被 $N$ 仅作倍数伸缩的非零向量）只在一条直线上。但它的矩阵指数仍然有定义。更大的 Jordan 块也有相关的[[Jordan块的指数|有限多项式指数公式]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the infinite sum converges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么这个无限和收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[谱范数|spectral norm]] $\|A\|_2$, the largest factor by which $A$ can stretch vector length. Applying $A$ repeatedly stretches length by at most $\|A\|_2^k$, so $\|A^k\|_2\le\|A\|_2^k$. Consequently
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用[[谱范数]] $\|A\|_2$，即 $A$ 对向量长度的最大伸缩倍数。反复作用 $k$ 次，伸缩倍数至多为 $\|A\|_2^k$，因此 $\|A^k\|_2\le\|A\|_2^k$。于是

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left\|\frac{t^kA^k}{k!}\right\|_2\le\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(|t|\|A\|_2)^k}{k!}=e^{|t|\|A\|_2}<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix terms are bounded by a convergent ordinary exponential series. Every entry is bounded in magnitude by the spectral norm, so every entry series converges absolutely. This also gives convergence in any fixed norm on matrices of this size: write a matrix as the finite sum of its entries times the coordinate matrices and apply the triangle inequality. No eigenvector basis is needed for this argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵各项的大小被一个收敛的普通指数级数控制。每个元素的模都不超过谱范数，因此每个元素对应的级数都绝对收敛。对这个固定尺寸矩阵空间上的任意范数，也能得到收敛：把矩阵写成有限个“元素乘坐标矩阵”之和，再用三角不等式即可。整个论证不需要特征向量基。

> [!warning] Not entrywise exponentiation · 不是逐元素取指数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $t=0$, the matrix exponential is $I$, with zero off-diagonal entries. Exponentiating each entry of the zero matrix separately would give ones everywhere, which is a different operation. Even for a diagonal matrix, $e^{tA}$ keeps its off-diagonal zeros and exponentiates only the diagonal values.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $t=0$ 时，矩阵指数为 $I$，非对角位置是零。若把零矩阵的每个元素分别取指数，则会得到处处为1的矩阵，那是另一种运算。即使 $A$ 是对角矩阵，$e^{tA}$ 也保留非对角位置的零，只把对角值变成普通指数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,-1)$, then $e^{tA}=\operatorname{diag}(e^{2t},e^{-t})$. If $A$ is similar to a diagonal matrix, [[对角化计算矩阵指数|diagonalization]] can simplify the calculation. The [[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|differential-equation theorem]] separately explains why this series produces the required trajectory and why that trajectory is unique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,-1)$ 时，$e^{tA}=\operatorname{diag}(e^{2t},e^{-t})$。如果 $A$ 与对角矩阵相似，可以借助[[对角化计算矩阵指数|对角化]]简化计算。[[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解]]则另行解释：为什么这个级数给出的轨迹满足所需方程，以及为什么轨迹唯一。
