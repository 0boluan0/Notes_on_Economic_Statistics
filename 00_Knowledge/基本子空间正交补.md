# Orthogonal complements of the fundamental subspaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*基本子空间正交补*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix separates input directions into those it can detect and those it sends to zero. On the output side, the vectors it can produce are perpendicular to the constraints that every output must satisfy. The theorem below identifies these two pairs completely: each space contains **all** directions perpendicular to its partner, so no direction is missing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵把输入方向分成它能辨别的部分与被它送到零的部分。在输出侧，矩阵能够产生的向量，与每个输出都必须满足的约束方向垂直。下面的定理完整确定这两对空间：每一边都包含与另一边垂直的**全部**方向，没有遗漏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A matrix that adds two inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看一个把两个输入相加的矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real matrix below. It takes a three-coordinate column vector to a two-coordinate column vector; the superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的实矩阵。它把三坐标列向量变成二坐标列向量；上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A(a,b,c)^T=(a+b,c)^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[行空间|row space]], written as column vectors, consists of $(s,s,t)^T$ with arbitrary real $s,t$. The [[零空间|nullspace]] consists of inputs producing zero, so it contains exactly $(u,-u,0)^T$. Their [[向量点积|dot product]], the sum of coordinatewise products, is $su-su=0$. More than that, every input splits as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把[[行空间]]的向量写成列向量，得到全部 $(s,s,t)^T$，其中 $s,t$ 为任意实数。[[零空间]]包含输出为零的输入，所以恰为全部 $(u,-u,0)^T$。两者的[[向量点积|点积]]把对应坐标乘积相加，得到 $su-su=0$。而且，每个输入都能拆成

$$(a,b,c)^T=
\left(\frac{a+b}{2},\frac{a+b}{2},c\right)^T+
\left(\frac{a-b}{2},\frac{b-a}{2},0\right)^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first vector lies in the row space and keeps the same output as the original input; the second lies in the nullspace and changes no output. Thus these perpendicular spaces cover the entire input space $\mathbb R^3$. On the output side, every $(p,q)^T$ is reachable by taking $(p,0,q)^T$. Therefore the [[列空间|column space]] is all of $\mathbb R^2$, and the [[左零空间|left nullspace]] is just $\{0\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项位于行空间，保留原输入的输出；第二项位于零空间，不改变任何输出。因此，这两个垂直空间一起覆盖整个输入空间 $\mathbb R^3$。输出侧的每个 $(p,q)^T$ 都能由 $(p,0,q)^T$ 产生，所以[[列空间]]是整个 $\mathbb R^2$，[[左零空间]]则只有 $\{0\}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two pairs, in two ambient spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两对空间，分别位于输入侧与输出侧*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, where $m,n$ are positive integers. Use the standard [[内积|inner product]] $u^Tv$. Write $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$ for the column space and $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$ for the nullspace. The [[四个基本子空间|four fundamental subspaces]] also include $C(A^T)$, the row space represented by columns, and $N(A^T)$, the left nullspace. For a subspace $S$, its [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] $S^\perp$ consists of every vector in the specified ambient space perpendicular to every vector of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，其中 $m,n$ 为正整数，使用标准[[内积]] $u^Tv$。记列空间为 $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$，零空间为 $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$。[[四个基本子空间]]还包括用列向量表示的行空间 $C(A^T)$，以及左零空间 $N(A^T)$。子空间 $S$ 的[[正交补]] $S^\perp$ 收集指定外部空间中，与 $S$ 的每个向量都垂直的全部向量。

> [!important] Complete pairs · 完整的配对
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the input space $\mathbb R^n$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在输入空间 $\mathbb R^n$ 中：
>
> $$N(A)=C(A^T)^\perp,\qquad C(A^T)=N(A)^\perp.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the output space $\mathbb R^m$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在输出空间 $\mathbb R^m$ 中：
>
> $$N(A^T)=C(A)^\perp,\qquad C(A)=N(A^T)^\perp.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every perpendicular direction is included
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么不会漏掉垂直方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation $Ax=0$ says that the dot product of $x$ with **each row** is zero. This is equivalent to being perpendicular to every linear combination of the rows: one direction follows by distributing the dot product over a combination, and the other follows because each row is itself such a combination. Consequently $N(A)=C(A^T)^\perp$ is an equality, not just a containment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方程 $Ax=0$ 表示 $x$ 与**每一行**的点积为零。这等价于与行的每个线性组合都垂直：一个方向由点积对线性组合展开得到，反方向则因为每一行本身也是这样的组合。因此得到的是相等 $N(A)=C(A^T)^\perp$，不只是包含关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To reverse the pair, use the finite-dimensional [[正交分解定理|orthogonal decomposition theorem]]. Put $S=C(A^T)$. Every input $z$ has a unique split $z=s+w$ with $s\in S$ and $w\in S^\perp=N(A)$. If $z$ is perpendicular to $N(A)$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把配对反过来，使用有限维的[[正交分解定理]]。令 $S=C(A^T)$。每个输入 $z$ 都能唯一拆成 $z=s+w$，其中 $s\in S$、$w\in S^\perp=N(A)$。若 $z$ 与 $N(A)$ 垂直，则

$$0=z^Tw=(s+w)^Tw=w^Tw.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sum of real squares is zero only when $w=0$, so $z=s\in S$. Conversely every member of $S$ is already perpendicular to $N(A)$. This proves $C(A^T)=N(A)^\perp$. Applying the same argument to $A^T$ gives the output pair. Equivalently, this last step uses the finite-dimensional [[双重正交补|double-complement identity]] $(S^\perp)^\perp=S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数平方和为零迫使 $w=0$，所以 $z=s\in S$。反过来，$S$ 的每个向量本来就与 $N(A)$ 垂直。这证明了 $C(A^T)=N(A)^\perp$。再对 $A^T$ 使用相同论证，就得到输出侧的配对。等价地，反向配对也可直接使用有限维的[[双重正交补]]恒等式 $(S^\perp)^\perp=S$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dimension check agrees with this proof. If $r$ is the [[矩阵秩|rank]] of $A$, [[行秩等于列秩|row-rank equality]] and [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity]] give dimensions $r,n-r$ on the input side and $r,m-r$ on the output side. Each pair adds to its ambient dimension. Thus one could also prove containment first and then use equal dimensions to get equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维数核对也与证明一致。若 $r$ 是 $A$ 的[[矩阵秩|秩]]，由[[行秩等于列秩]]与[[秩零度定理]]，输入侧维数为 $r,n-r$，输出侧为 $r,m-r$，各自相加得到所在空间的维数。因此，也可以先证明包含关系，再用维数相等把包含升级为相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Orthogonality alone does not fill a space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## “正交”与“正交补”不同

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb R^3$, the $x$-axis and $y$-axis are perpendicular, but together they miss the $z$-direction. They are not orthogonal complements in $\mathbb R^3$. The completeness step above is essential. It also gives the unique [[直和|direct-sum decompositions]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中，$x$ 轴与 $y$ 轴垂直，但两者一起仍漏掉 $z$ 方向，所以它们不是 $\mathbb R^3$ 中的正交补。上面证明完整性的步骤不可省略。它也给出两个分量唯一的[[直和|直和分解]]：

$$\mathbb R^n=C(A^T)\oplus N(A),\qquad
\mathbb R^m=C(A)\oplus N(A^T).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\oplus$ means every vector is a unique sum of one vector from each space; it is not a set union. No full-rank assumption is needed. For the zero matrix, the row and column spaces are zero, while the corresponding nullspaces fill their ambient spaces. These statements use the standard inner product; changing the inner product can change which spaces are orthogonal complements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\oplus$ 表示每个向量都能唯一写成两边各一个向量之和，并不是集合并集。定理不要求满秩。对零矩阵，行空间和列空间均为零，而对应的零空间充满各自的外部空间。这些公式使用标准内积；改变内积，哪些空间互为正交补也可能改变。

> [!question]- Why not pair the row space with the left nullspace? · 为什么不把行空间与左零空间配对？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> They generally live in different spaces, $\mathbb R^n$ and $\mathbb R^m$. Even if $m=n$, they need not be complements. For $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, the row space is the line through $(1,1)^T$, but the left nullspace is the line through $(0,1)^T$; their dot product is $1$. Special matrices may make additional pairs coincide, but the theorem does not guarantee that.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它们一般分别位于 $\mathbb R^n$ 与 $\mathbb R^m$，连所在空间都不同。即使 $m=n$，也未必互为正交补。例如 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ 的行空间由 $(1,1)^T$ 张成，左零空间由 $(0,1)^T$ 张成，两向量点积为 $1$。特殊矩阵可能让别的配对碰巧成立，但定理不保证这一点。

## Complex coordinates require conjugate transpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复坐标必须使用共轭转置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$, use the standard Hermitian inner product $u^*v=\sum_j\overline{u_j}v_j$. Here the bar conjugates a complex number, and $A^*=\overline A^T$ is its [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]. The correct geometric pairs are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$，使用标准 Hermitian 内积 $u^*v=\sum_j\overline{u_j}v_j$。横线表示复共轭，$A^*=\overline A^T$ 是[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]。正确的几何配对为

$$N(A)=C(A^*)^\perp,\quad C(A^*)=N(A)^\perp;
\qquad N(A^*)=C(A)^\perp,\quad C(A)=N(A^*)^\perp.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed $(A^*y)^*x=y^*Ax$. This vanishes for every $y\in\mathbb C^m$ exactly when $Ax=0$: for the reverse implication choose $y=Ax$, so $(Ax)^*(Ax)=\sum_j|(Ax)_j|^2=0$. The same orthogonal-decomposition argument then proves the reverse pair, using squared absolute values. Apply it to $A^*$ for the other pair. The algebraic row space represented by $C(A^T)$ still has its meaning, but its vectors are conjugated to obtain $C(A^*)$; the spaces need not coincide. The [[行空间|row-space definition]] gives an explicit complex example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是因为 $(A^*y)^*x=y^*Ax$。它对每个 $y\in\mathbb C^m$ 都为零，当且仅当 $Ax=0$：反向取 $y=Ax$，便有 $(Ax)^*(Ax)=\sum_j|(Ax)_j|^2=0$。再用相同的正交分解论证，以绝对值平方和证明反向配对；对 $A^*$ 重复即可得到另一对。用 $C(A^T)$ 表示的代数行空间仍有其意义，但需要把其中向量取共轭才得到 $C(A^*)$，两空间未必相同。[[行空间]]的定义卡给出了明确的复数例子。
