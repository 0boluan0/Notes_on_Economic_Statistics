# 在线性需求和可比广义成本下半数法将原有出行的矩形收益与新增出行的三角形收益相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With linear demand and comparable generalised costs, the rule of a half adds the existing-trip rectangle and induced-trip triangle.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交通改善降低每次出行的广义成本时，原来就出行的人获得全部成本节约；因此新增的出行，其收益应按需求曲线衡量。若相关区间的需求为线性，新增出行的平均净收益恰好是单位成本降幅的一半，这就是半数法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reduction in generalised travel cost fully benefits existing trips. Induced trips are valued along demand. With linear demand over the relevant interval, their average net gain is half the per-trip cost reduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设同一时期的出行量为 $Q_0\to Q_1$，广义成本为 $c_0\to c_1$，其中 $c_1<c_0$。广义成本把所感知的时间成本、票价或收费、运行费用放到一致的货币单位。保持这条需求关系及其他相关条件不变，[[消费者剩余]] 的变化为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let trips change from $Q_0$ to $Q_1$ and generalised cost from $c_0$ to $c_1<c_0$. Generalised cost consistently monetises perceived time, charges and operating costs. Holding the relevant demand relation and other conditions fixed, the change in [[消费者剩余|consumer surplus]] is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta CS
=Q_0(c_0-c_1)+\frac12(Q_1-Q_0)(c_0-c_1)
=\frac{Q_0+Q_1}{2}(c_0-c_1).
$$

例如 $c:10\to8$、$Q:100\to120$：原有出行收益为 $100\times2=200$，新增出行收益为 $\frac12\times20\times2=20$，合计 **220**。把改善后的全部 120 次出行都乘以 2 会算成 240，多算了 20。新增出行刚好在新成本处愿意发生时，边际那一次的净剩余接近零，而非完整的 2。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $c:10\to8$ and $Q:100\to120$, existing trips gain 200 and induced trips gain 20, totalling 220. Multiplying all 120 trips by two gives 240 and overstates the benefit by 20. The marginal trip at the new cost earns approximately zero surplus, not the full saving of two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它计算的是用户剩余，不是整个项目净社会收益。收费下降若导致运营方收入减少，须另按 [[转移支付核算]] 对齐；建设成本、未包含的外部影响也须另查。普通需求面积的福利解释遵守 [[剩余与补偿度量]] 的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is user surplus, not total project net benefit. Match reduced charges with operators' lost receipts through [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]], and check construction costs and omitted external impacts. The welfare interpretation follows [[剩余与补偿度量|the conditions for surplus and compensation measures]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非线性需求下，梯形只是近似；大幅成本变化应积分或细分区间。若某种交通方式原本不存在，无法定义可比的 $c_0$，也不能随手令其为零套公式。网络中路线和方式相互替代时，则需一致的需求模型，而不是把各条线路独立相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonlinear demand the trapezoid is approximate; large changes require integration or intermediate points. An absent travel mode does not imply a baseline cost of zero. Network substitutions require a consistent demand model rather than independent sums by route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
