# Utility functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*效用函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A utility function assigns a number to each possible bundle of goods so that comparing the numbers reproduces a person's preferences. A preferred bundle gets a larger number; two bundles the person regards as equally good get the same number. The numbers make an ordering easier to calculate with. In the ordinary consumer-choice model considered here, they do not measure how many units of happiness someone feels. The alternatives here are specified bundles received with certainty.

A **bundle** is simply a list of quantities. With two goods, $x=(x_1,x_2)$ records how much of good 1 and good 2 is present. A consumption set $X$ specifies the allowed bundles. A utility function $u:X\to\mathbb R$ takes a whole bundle as input and gives one real score as output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效用函数给每种商品组合一个数值，使比较这些数值能够重现一个人的偏好：更喜欢的组合分数更高，认为一样好的组合分数相同。数值把排序变成了便于计算的对象。在本卡讨论的普通消费者选择模型中，它不测量一个人感到了多少“单位的快乐”。这里比较的是确定获得的商品束。

**商品束**就是一串商品数量。两种商品时，$x=(x_1,x_2)$ 记录商品1和商品2各有多少；消费集合 $X$ 规定允许哪些组合。效用函数 $u:X\to\mathbb R$ 把整个商品束作为输入，输出一个实数分数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading a utility score
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样读效用分数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!example] A tie between different bundles
> Suppose the model uses $u(x_1,x_2)=x_1+2x_2$ for nonnegative quantities. For $A=(2,1)$, $B=(0,2)$ and $C=(1,2)$, the scores are $u(A)=4$, $u(B)=4$ and $u(C)=5$. The model represents indifference between $A$ and $B$, and a strict preference for $C$ over either. The formula is a chosen model of this person's ranking, not a claim about everybody's tastes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 不同组合也可以一样好
> 设模型在非负数量上使用 $u(x_1,x_2)=x_1+2x_2$。对 $A=(2,1)$、$B=(0,2)$、$C=(1,2)$，分数分别是 $4$、$4$、$5$。模型表示这个人对 $A$、$B$ 无差异，并且严格更喜欢 $C$。这是对该人排序的一种建模选择，不是在宣称所有人都有这种偏好。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $x\succeq y$ to mean “the person considers $x$ at least as good as $y$.” A function **represents** this preference relation when, for every $x,y\in X$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x\succeq y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u(x)\ge u(y).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $x\succ y$ means strict preference and corresponds to $u(x)>u(y)$; $x\sim y$ means indifference and corresponds to $u(x)=u(y)$. The comparison runs in both directions: a proposed formula must match all the intended comparisons. An arbitrary assignment of scores need not represent a previously specified preference. For example, the cycle $A\succ B$, $B\succ C$, $C\succ A$ would require $u(A)>u(B)>u(C)>u(A)$, which real numbers cannot satisfy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x\succeq y$ 表示“这个人认为 $x$ 至少和 $y$ 一样好”；表示偏好要求上述双向对应对所有 $x,y\in X$ 成立。严格偏好 $x\succ y$ 对应 $u(x)>u(y)$，无差异 $x\sim y$ 对应 $u(x)=u(y)$。任意写下一个公式，未必就表示了事先给定的偏好。例如 $A\succ B$、$B\succ C$、$C\succ A$ 的循环会要求 $u(A)>u(B)>u(C)>u(A)$，实数排序不可能满足它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The scale can change while the ranking stays fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*刻度可以变，排序仍然不变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word **ordinal** means that the ordering is what the scores encode. In the example, replacing $u$ by $v=3u+7$ gives scores $19,19,22$ for $A,B,C$. All comparisons remain the same, although the gap between $C$ and $A$ changes from $1$ to $3$. Replacing $u$ by $u-100$ gives negative scores while preserving the same ordering. Neither the zero point nor the numerical gap is fixed by these preferences alone.

More generally, let $g$ be strictly increasing on the values taken by $u$: whenever $a<b$, we have $g(a)<g(b)$. Set $v(x)=g(u(x))$. Strict increase preserves and reflects the order, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u(x)\ge u(y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad g(u(x))\ge g(u(y)).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore $v$ represents exactly the same preferences as $u$. A merely nondecreasing rescaling may merge distinct scores: the constant map $g(s)=0$ would make all three example bundles tied. It would no longer represent their original strict comparison. Full preference preservation is a stronger request than [[单调变换保持拟凹性|preservation of quasiconcavity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**序数**表示分数编码的是顺序。例中改用 $v=3u+7$，$A,B,C$ 的分数变成 $19,19,22$，排序完全一样，但 $C$ 与 $A$ 的分差从 $1$ 变成了 $3$。改成 $u-100$ 后分数为负，排序仍然不变；偏好本身没有固定零点或分差。

一般地，若 $g$ 在 $u$ 的取值上严格递增，就保留且能够反推出大小关系，因此 $v=g\circ u$ 表示同一偏好。仅非减则可能把不同值压成相同值：常数变换 $g(s)=0$ 会把例中的三个商品束都变成无差异，丢掉原来的严格比较。完整保留偏好排序，比[[单调变换保持拟凹性|只保留拟凹性]]要求更强。

## Using the function to study choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样用它研究选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bundles with the same score form an [[无差异曲线|indifference set]], a way to draw the ranking in input space. To model an actual choice, also specify a feasible set $F\subseteq X$, such as the bundles affordable under a budget. If a best bundle is attained, maximizing $u$ over $F$ chooses a bundle at least as preferred as every other feasible one. Utility alone does not specify that budget or guarantee that a maximizer exists.

> [!warning] Scores belong to a representation of one person's ranking
> A score of $10$ for one person and $5$ for another does not by itself show that the first is twice as well off. Even within one person's ranking, a score of $10$ need not mean twice the satisfaction of a score of $5$. Interpersonal comparisons and aggregation need additional assumptions about scales; see [[社会福利的效用尺度|utility scales in social welfare]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同分商品束组成[[无差异曲线|无差异集合]]，把排序画在输入空间里。研究实际选择还要给出可行集合 $F\subseteq X$，例如预算允许购买的组合；如果最优解取得，在 $F$ 上最大化 $u$ 就选出了至少不差于其他可行组合的商品束。效用函数本身不提供预算，也不保证最优解一定存在。

> [!warning] 分数属于对某个人排序的一种表示
> 一个人得 $10$、另一个人得 $5$，不能仅凭这两个数断定前者过得好一倍；即使是同一个人的 $10$ 和 $5$，也不自动表示满足程度翻倍。跨人比较和加总还需要额外的尺度假设，见[[社会福利的效用尺度]]。
