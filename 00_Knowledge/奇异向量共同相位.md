# Paired signs and phases of singular vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*奇异向量的共同符号与相位*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two calculations can report opposite arrows for a singular direction and still describe exactly the same matrix. A [[奇异向量|singular-vector pair]] consists of a unit input direction $v$ and its unit output direction $u$, linked by a positive stretch $\sigma$. Reversing both arrows preserves that link. In complex coordinates the same freedom is multiplication by a common number of absolute value one, called a **phase**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两次计算可能把奇异方向的箭头画成相反方向，却仍描述同一个矩阵。[[奇异向量|奇异向量对]]包含单位输入方向 $v$ 和单位输出方向 $u$，两者通过正伸缩量 $\sigma$ 相连。同时反转两个箭头，联系不变。复数坐标中，这种自由推广为同时乘一个绝对值为1的数，称为**相位**。

> [!example] The same axis, opposite arrows / 同一条轴，两个相反箭头
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=\operatorname{diag}(3,1)$, the first coordinate vector $e_1=(1,0)^T$ satisfies $Ae_1=3e_1$. Both $(u,v)=(e_1,e_1)$ and $(-e_1,-e_1)$ are valid pairs for $\sigma=3$. Their matrix contribution is identical: $3e_1e_1^T=3(-e_1)(-e_1)^T$. Flipping only the output vector gives $-3e_1e_1^T$ instead. Here $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and $\operatorname{diag}$ constructs a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $A=\operatorname{diag}(3,1)$，第一坐标向量 $e_1=(1,0)^T$ 满足 $Ae_1=3e_1$。$(e_1,e_1)$ 与 $(-e_1,-e_1)$ 都是 $\sigma=3$ 的合法配对，产生相同矩阵分量；只反转输出向量则把该分量变成负数。$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，$\operatorname{diag}$ 表示构造[[对角矩阵]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When phase is the only freedom
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*何时仅有共同相位自由*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real or complex $m\times n$ matrix, $m,n\ge1$. A positive [[奇异值|singular value]] $\sigma$ is **simple** if it occurs only once in the singular-value list. Equivalently, the [[特征空间|eigenspace]] of $A^*A$ for $\sigma^2$ is one-dimensional. For normalized singular vectors, the paired equations are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$m,n\ge1$。正[[奇异值]] $\sigma$ 若只在奇异值列表中出现一次，就称为**简单奇异值**；等价地，$A^*A$ 对应 $\sigma^2$ 的[[特征空间]]是一维的。单位奇异向量满足配对方程

$$
Av=\sigma u,\qquad A^*u=\sigma v,\qquad \|u\|_2=\|v\|_2=1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The star is [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]; $\|\cdot\|_2$ is [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. Every other normalized pair $(\widetilde u,\widetilde v)$ for this same simple positive value has the form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

星号是[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]，$\|\cdot\|_2$ 是[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]。同一个简单正奇异值的任何另一组单位配对 $(\widetilde u,\widetilde v)$ 都形如

$$
\widetilde u=\alpha u,\qquad \widetilde v=\alpha v,\qquad |\alpha|=1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, the paired equations give $A^*Av=\sigma^2v$. Since that eigenspace is one-dimensional, $\widetilde v=\alpha v$; unit length forces $|\alpha|=1$. Positivity lets us divide by $\sigma$, so $\widetilde u=A\widetilde v/\sigma=\alpha u$. This explains why the same multiplier is required on both sides, rather than two independently chosen phases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

配对方程给出 $A^*Av=\sigma^2v$。由于该特征空间只有一维，必有 $\widetilde v=\alpha v$；单位长度迫使 $|\alpha|=1$。$\sigma>0$ 允许相除，因此 $\widetilde u=A\widetilde v/\sigma=\alpha u$，解释了为什么两侧必须用同一个乘数，而不能独立选择相位。

## What stays unchanged
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保持不变的对象*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[SVD秩一展开|rank-one matrix contribution]] uses the outer product $uv^*$, which takes an input $x$ to $u(v^*x)$. Conjugation cancels the common phase:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[SVD秩一展开|秩一矩阵分量]]使用外积 $uv^*$，把输入 $x$ 映为 $u(v^*x)$。共轭会消去共同相位：

$$
(\alpha u)(\alpha v)^*=\alpha\overline{\alpha}\,uv^*=uv^*.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\overline{\alpha}$ is the complex conjugate and $\alpha\overline{\alpha}=|\alpha|^2$. In real SVDs the only possibilities are $\alpha=1$ and $-1$. Over the complex numbers, $\alpha=i$ is another example, with $i^2=-1$: $(iu)(iv)^*=i(-i)uv^*=uv^*$. The input coordinate changes to $\widetilde v^*x=\overline{\alpha}v^*x$, but multiplying by $\widetilde u$ cancels that change. The reconstructed output therefore stays the same.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\overline{\alpha}$ 表示复共轭，$\alpha\overline{\alpha}=|\alpha|^2$。实数 SVD 中只允许 $\alpha=1$ 或 $-1$；复数域还可取 $\alpha=i$、$i^2=-1$，此时 $(iu)(iv)^*=i(-i)uv^*=uv^*$。输入坐标变成 $\widetilde v^*x=\overline{\alpha}v^*x$，再乘 $\widetilde u$ 就消去变化，所以重建输出保持不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When comparing two results, match the same singular value and compare its one-dimensional subspace or its whole matrix contribution before treating a sign change as an error. A rule such as making a chosen nonzero entry positive only fixes a reporting convention; it adds no information about $A$. Apply the adjustment to the paired column too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较两次结果时，先对齐同一个奇异值，再比较它的一维子空间或整个矩阵分量，不能把变号本身当作错误。要求某个选定非零元素为正，只是固定一种报告约定，并没有给 $A$ 增添信息；调整时必须同时处理配对的另一列。

> [!warning] The assumptions matter / 条件不可省略
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a [[重奇异值下方向不唯一|repeated positive value]], different basis vectors can mix within the entire equal-value block, so phase is not the only freedom. For [[SVD零块补基不唯一|zero directions]], the input and output completions are independent. Distinct but very close positive values still have only phase freedom in exact arithmetic; their sensitivity to perturbations is a separate numerical issue.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[重奇异值下方向不唯一|重复正奇异值]]允许整个等值块内混合不同基向量，不再只差相位。[[SVD零块补基不唯一|零方向]]则允许输入与输出分别补基。不同但很接近的正值在精确数学中仍只有相位自由；对扰动敏感是另一个数值问题。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 7.65 and 7.70–7.74, PDF pp.285, 287–288](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=285): multiplicity through Gram eigenspaces and paired SVD construction. The one-dimensional phase proof and examples above follow directly from those identities.
- [MathWorks, svds, output arguments U and V](https://www.mathworks.com/help/matlab/ref/svds.html): paired sign differences can occur across valid numerical outputs. [LAPACK, SVD error analysis](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node97.html): vector sensitivity depends on separation from other singular values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 第7.65及7.70–7.74条核对 Gram 特征空间重数与 SVD 配对构造；一维相位证明及例子由这些恒等式直接推导。
- MathWorks 的 U、V 输出说明核对合法计算结果可能成对变号；LAPACK 的 SVD 误差分析核对方向敏感性与奇异值间隔的关系。
