# Population quantile
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*总体分位数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **population quantile** turns a desired cumulative probability into a value threshold. A [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]] goes in the other direction: $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$ gives the probability at or below a value $x$. A quantile asks where that accumulated probability first reaches a chosen level $p$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**总体分位数**把指定的累计概率转成数值门槛。[[累积分布函数]]走的是反方向：$F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$ 告诉我们，取值不超过 $x$ 的概率是多少；分位数则问，这个累计概率最早在哪里达到指定水平 $p$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The probability $p$ has no measurement units, while its quantile has the same units as $X$. For example, a $0.9$ quantile of a waiting-time distribution is a time threshold, not a probability of $0.9$ seconds. The median corresponds to the level $p=0.5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概率 $p$ 没有计量单位，分位数则与 $X$ 使用相同单位。例如，等待时间分布的 $0.9$ 分位数是一个时间门槛，不能把它读成“0.9 秒的概率”。中位数对应的水平是 $p=0.5$。

> [!abstract] Read a CDF backwards with a generalised inverse · 用广义逆反查 CDF
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $0<p<1$, define
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $0<p<1$，定义
>
> $$q_p=\inf\{x\in\mathbb R:F_X(x)\ge p\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The braces collect all real thresholds whose cumulative probability is at least $p$. The symbol $\inf$ means infimum, the greatest lower bound: here it selects the leftmost threshold in that set. A CDF's right-continuity and its limits zero and one ensure that this threshold is finite and is actually included for every $0<p<1$. The function $p\mapsto q_p$ is called the generalised inverse CDF.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 花括号收集所有让累计概率至少达到 $p$ 的实数门槛。$\inf$ 表示下确界，也就是最大的下界；在这里，它选出这组门槛中最左边的那个。CDF 的右连续性及两端分别趋于零和一，保证对每个 $0<p<1$，这个门槛都是有限数，而且确实属于这组集合。把 $p$ 映射到 $q_p$ 的函数称为 CDF 的广义逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Jumps can pass over the requested probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*跳跃可以直接越过指定概率*

> [!example] Two possible values · 只有两个可能取值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $P(X=0)=0.3$ and $P(X=2)=0.7$. Before zero the cumulative probability is zero; from zero up to but excluding two it is $0.3$; at two it jumps to one:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $P(X=0)=0.3$、$P(X=2)=0.7$。在零之前，累计概率为零；从零到二之前，累计概率为 $0.3$；到二时跳到一：
>
> $$F_X(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x<0,\\0.3,&0\le x<2,\\1,&x\ge2.\end{cases}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The $0.3$ quantile is zero, but the $0.5$ and $0.9$ quantiles are both two. To reach a cumulative probability of at least $0.5$, we must include the entire jump at two. Thus $q_{0.5}=2$ even though $F_X(2)=1$, not $0.5$. Solving $F_X(x)=0.5$ would find no solution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $0.3$ 分位数是零，$0.5$ 与 $0.9$ 分位数则都是二。要让累计概率至少达到 $0.5$，必须把二处的整段跳跃计入。因此 $q_{0.5}=2$，尽管 $F_X(2)=1$ 而不是 $0.5$；直接求解 $F_X(x)=0.5$ 会发现无解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general guarantee is $P(X<q_p)\le p\le P(X\le q_p)$. The difference between the strict and non-strict inequalities is the possible probability mass at the threshold. Therefore a quantile does not necessarily put exactly $p$ of the probability below it or at it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般能保证的是 $P(X<q_p)\le p\le P(X\le q_p)$。严格与非严格不等号之间的差别，正是门槛处可能存在的点概率。因此，分位数不一定让它以下、或不超过它的概率恰好等于 $p$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Ordinary inversion is a special case
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*普通反函数是特殊情形*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the CDF is continuous, it cannot jump over $p$, so $F_X(q_p)=p$. If it also increases strictly through that level, the solution is unique and ordinary inversion works. For $F_X(x)=x^3$ on $[0,1]$ (zero below the interval and one above it), the quantile is $q_p=p^{1/3}$. The median is $2^{-1/3}\approx0.79370$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 CDF 连续，就不能跳过 $p$，因此有 $F_X(q_p)=p$。若它在经过这一水平时还严格递增，解就唯一，可以使用普通反函数。对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $F_X(x)=x^3$（区间左侧为零、右侧为一），分位数是 $q_p=p^{1/3}$，中位数为 $2^{-1/3}\approx0.79370$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A flat part can make a median nonunique under the usual two-sided inequalities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若存在平坦段，通常的双侧中位数条件可能允许不止一个值：

$$P(X\le m)\ge\tfrac12,\qquad P(X\ge m)\ge\tfrac12.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For equal probability masses at zero and two, every $m\in[0,2]$ satisfies these inequalities. The generalised-inverse convention nevertheless gives one selected value, $q_{0.5}=0$: the leftmost admissible median. Different definitions of “a median” and “the selected median quantile” should not be silently interchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若零和二各有一半概率，每个 $m\in[0,2]$ 都满足这两条不等式。不过，广义逆约定仍给出一个确定选择：$q_{0.5}=0$，也就是最左边的中位数。要区分“一个满足条件的中位数”与“按照约定选出的中位分位数”。

> [!warning] Keep the probability level inside the stated range · 留意概率水平的范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The definition above uses $0<p<1$. Substituting $p=0$ into the same formula over all real $x$ gives $-\infty$, since every CDF value is at least zero. At $p=1$, the qualifying set may be empty if the CDF never reaches one at a finite value; with the convention $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$, the result is then infinite. Texts and software may define endpoint quantiles separately, so state the convention rather than assuming both endpoints are finite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上面的定义限定为 $0<p<1$。如果在全体实数上直接把同一公式中的 $p$ 换成零，因为所有 CDF 值都不小于零，结果会是 $-\infty$。在 $p=1$ 时，如果 CDF 在任何有限位置都达不到一，合格集合就为空；采用 $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$ 的约定时，结果为正无穷。教材和软件也可能另行定义端点分位数，因此应说明约定，不能默认两个端点都是有限数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card concerns a distribution in a probability model. A [[样本分位数|sample quantile]] is calculated from finitely many observations and may use interpolation or another convention. That estimation choice is separate from the population definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里讨论的是概率模型中整张分布的分位数。[[样本分位数]]由有限观测计算，还可能使用插值或其他约定；这些估计选择应与总体定义分开。
