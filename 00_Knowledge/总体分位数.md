# 总体的 p 分位数是累计概率首次达到 p 的最左阈值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A population p-quantile is the leftmost threshold where the CDF reaches p*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $0<p<1$，定义广义分位数
$$q_p=\inf\{x:F_X(x)\ge p\}.$$
连续严格递增时可解 $F_X(q_p)=p$；离散或有平坦段时使用下确界定义，不能强求等号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a probability strictly between zero and one, use the infimum of thresholds whose CDF reaches that level. Ordinary inversion works when the CDF is continuous and strictly increasing; the general definition also handles jumps and flat parts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若 $P(X=0)=0.3,P(X=2)=0.7$，则 $q_{0.5}=2$，而 $F_X(2)=1$。中位数条件为 $P(X\le m)\ge1/2$ 且 $P(X\ge m)\ge1/2$；所有满足该条件的 $m$ 可能不唯一，广义分位数规定了其中的最左选择。[[样本分位数]]另受有限数据和插值规则影响。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With masses 0.3 and 0.7 at zero and two, the median quantile is two even though its CDF value is one. Median inequalities may admit several values; the quantile convention selects the leftmost. [[样本分位数|Sample quantiles]] additionally depend on finite-data interpolation rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
