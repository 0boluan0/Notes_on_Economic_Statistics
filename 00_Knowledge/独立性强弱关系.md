# 有限二阶矩下独立推出均值独立，均值独立推出零协方差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With finite second moments, independence implies mean independence, which implies zero covariance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X,Y$ 二阶矩有限，则
$$X\perp Y\;\Longrightarrow\;E[Y\mid X]=E[Y]\;\Longrightarrow\;\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0.$$
第一步因为整张条件分布不变；第二步使用
$$E[XY]=E[X E[Y\mid X]]=E[X]E[Y].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With finite second moments, an unchanged full conditional law fixes its mean, and total expectation turns that fixed mean into a factorised product expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两个反向一般都不成立，分别见 [[均值独立不推独立]] 与 [[零协方差不推均值独立]]。均值独立有方向，独立和协方差则对称。不要把“仅一个条件矩不变”升级成“整张条件分布不变”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both reverse implications fail in general; separate [[均值独立不推独立|mean-independence]] and [[零协方差不推均值独立|zero-covariance]] counterexamples show why. Independence and covariance are symmetric, whereas mean independence is directional.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
