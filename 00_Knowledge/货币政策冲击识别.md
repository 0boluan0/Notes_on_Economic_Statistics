# 识别货币政策的因果效应须分离政策对经济信息的内生反应与外生政策变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identifying monetary policy's causal effects requires separating endogenous responses to economic information from exogenous policy variation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中央银行通常因通胀、需求和预测变化而调整政策。因此，“加息后经济仍增长”或“降息后失业仍上升”不能单独说明政策的因果方向：没有该次政策变化时，经济也可能因同一冲击走向另一条路径。先界定干预是什么、比较哪个期限和哪一个反事实，再选择识别来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Central banks respond to inflation, demand and forecasts. Growth after a hike or unemployment after a cut does not identify policy's causal sign: the common underlying shock could have produced a different path without the intervention. Define the policy intervention, horizon and counterfactual before selecting identifying variation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个静态反例：令 $u$ 为需求冲击，政策按 $i=u$ 反应，产出变化由 $\Delta y=u-0.5i$ 给出。观测数据中 $\Delta y=0.5i$，利率与产出正相关；但保持 $u$ 不变，外生把 $i$ 提高一单位的效果是产出减少 0.5。这只是展示内生反应的教学模型，不是估计真实政策系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a static counterexample, let a demand shock $u$ induce policy $i=u$, with $\Delta y=u-0.5i$. Observed output satisfies $\Delta y=0.5i$, a positive association. Holding $u$ fixed, an exogenous one-unit increase in $i$ instead lowers output by 0.5. This illustrates endogeneity rather than estimates an actual policy coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种研究路径使用公告窄窗口中的利率意外，减少其他消息混入。仍须说明测量的是当次目标利率、未来路径还是多种资产变动；[[意外信息定价]]负责事前预期基准。公告前可预测成分、提前泄漏和同时发布的信息，仍可能破坏将意外当外生冲击的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One approach uses narrow-window rate surprises to limit concurrent news. Specify whether they measure the current target, a future path or multiple market-price movements; [[意外信息定价|surprise measurement]] supplies the pre-event benchmark. Predictable components, leakage and co-released information can still undermine exogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把意外对公告前信息作回归并取残差，是一种清理可预测成分的方法，但残差与所选控制变量不相关，不等于与所有遗漏冲击独立。[[货币政策公告的信息效应]]进一步区分政策消息与央行透露的经济判断。最后，金融价格的即时反应与数季后的产出、价格反应需要不同估计步骤；不能把一个窗口内价格变化直接当总需求乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Residualising surprises against pre-announcement information can remove predictable components, but orthogonality to selected controls is not independence from every omitted shock. [[货币政策公告的信息效应|Central-bank information effects]] distinguish policy news from economic assessments. Immediate asset-price reactions and later macroeconomic responses also require different estimation steps; a window return is not a demand multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Jarociński and Karadi, Deconstructing monetary policy surprises](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp2133.en.pdf)，2018 年 6 月修订稿，印刷第 4–5 页／PDF 第 5–6 页：支持政策内生反应、公告识别与同窗信息问题。静态方程是独立反例。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Printed pages 4–5 support policy endogeneity, announcement identification and simultaneous information. The static equations are a constructed counterexample.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco, Monetary Policy Surprises](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/monetary-policy-surprises/)，正文关于原始意外、正交化与冲击代理的段落：支持短窗利率路径意外、公告前信息正交化与将意外用作冲击代理的目的，不把代理的构造当外生性的无条件证明。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The methodological paragraphs support narrow-window path surprises, orthogonalisation and their use as shock proxies, not unconditional proof of exogeneity.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
