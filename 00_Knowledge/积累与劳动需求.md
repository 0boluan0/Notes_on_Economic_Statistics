# 积累对劳动需求的影响取决于可变资本规模及其对应的用工条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Accumulation affects labour demand through variable capital and the employment conditions it represents.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Marx资本积累|积累]]增加总资本，增加多少劳动需求还要看其中用于购买劳动力的[[可变资本]]。在每名劳动者同期间的劳动力价格、工时、强度及资本周转口径不变时，可变资本增加才对应更多同类劳动者；若只是每人价格上涨，同样的可变资本增加并未多雇人。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[Marx资本积累|Accumulation]] expands total capital, but additional labour demand depends on [[可变资本|variable capital]] used to purchase labour power. With unchanged labour-power prices per worker over the same period, hours, intensity and turnover basis, more variable capital corresponds to more comparable workers. A rise caused solely by a higher price per worker does not imply additional employment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若[[资本价值构成]]不变，总资本扩张会同比扩大可变部分；这种情况下，劳动需求甚至可能超过现有供给并推高工资。若生产方法改变，使可变部分占比下降，则要另算它的绝对规模。原典例中，总资本从6000增至18000，可变资本占比从50%降到20%，可变资本却从3000增至3600。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With unchanged [[资本价值构成|value composition]], total capital growth proportionately expands its variable part; demand may even exceed available labour and raise wages. If changed production methods reduce the variable share, its absolute amount must be recalculated. In Marx's example, total capital rises from 6,000 to 18,000 and the variable share falls from 50% to 20%, yet variable capital rises from 3,000 to 3,600.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给这个例子补上可比用工条件：每人同期间劳动力价格为30、预付可变资本都只周转一次，便对应100人变为120人。用工占资本的比例降低，可以与雇佣人数增加同时发生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add comparable employment conditions to the example: a labour-power price of 30 per worker over the same period and one turnover of advanced variable capital in both cases. Employment then rises from 100 to 120. Labour demand relative to capital can fall while the number employed rises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总量之外还要看变动落在哪里。更新旧设备可能排出原有工人，新增资本又可能在同一部门或其他部门吸收劳动，两种过程在时期、技能和地点上未必接得上。这种吸收和排斥形成的相对过剩人口，进入[[产业后备军]]的分析；不能只从总资本增加读出每位原有劳动者的就业结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also examine where change occurs. Renewing old equipment may displace workers while additional capital recruits labour in the same or other sectors. Timing, skills and locations need not match. The relative surplus population formed through attraction and displacement enters the analysis of the [[产业后备军|industrial reserve army]]. Total capital growth alone does not establish each incumbent worker's employment outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
