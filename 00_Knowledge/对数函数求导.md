# Derivatives of logarithmic functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对数函数求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] turns a positive quantity into the exponent that produces it. Its [[导数|derivative]] tells us how much that log scale changes when the original quantity changes a little. The rate is $1/x$: near $x=1$ the slope is one, while near $x=100$ it is only $0.01$. A fixed absolute change therefore matters less on the log scale when the starting quantity is larger.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自然对数]]把一个正数变成产生它的指数。它的[[导数]]说明原数量变化一点时，对数尺度会变化多少。这个速率是 $1/x$：在 $x=1$ 附近斜率为一，在 $x=100$ 附近却只有 $0.01$。所以同样的绝对变化，起始数量越大，在对数尺度上的影响就越小。

> [!example] A small relative change · 小的相对变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Going from $100$ to $101$ changes the logarithm by $\ln101-\ln100=\ln1.01\approx0.00995033$. The derivative at $100$ predicts approximately $(1/100)\cdot1=0.01$. In general, for small $h$ with $x+h>0$, $\ln(x+h)-\ln x\approx h/x$. The right side is the original relative change; the approximation becomes accurate as $h\to0$ for a fixed $x>0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $100$ 变成 $101$，对数的精确变化是 $\ln101-\ln100=\ln1.01\approx0.00995033$。在 $100$ 处用导数预测，约为 $(1/100)\cdot1=0.01$。一般地，当 $h$ 很小且 $x+h>0$ 时，$\ln(x+h)-\ln x\approx h/x$。右侧正是原数量的相对变化；固定 $x>0$ 后让 $h\to0$，近似就越来越准确。

## The inverse relationship gives the slope
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*由反函数关系找到斜率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x>0$, let $y=\ln x$. Then $e^y=x$. The [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] has positive derivative $e^y$, so the [[反函数导数|inverse-derivative rule]] applies: the inverse slope is the reciprocal of the original slope at the corresponding point. Equivalently, the [[链式法则|chain rule]] applied to $e^{y(x)}=x$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x>0$，令 $y=\ln x$，就有 $e^y=x$。[[自然指数函数]]的导数 $e^y$ 为正，因此可以使用[[反函数导数|反函数求导规则]]：反函数的斜率，是对应点处原函数斜率的倒数。等价地，对 $e^{y(x)}=x$ 使用[[链式法则]]，得到

$$e^y y'=1,\qquad\frac{d}{dx}\ln x=\frac{1}{e^y}=\frac1x,\qquad x>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This reasoning uses the already-established exponential derivative; it does not define $e$ by assuming the logarithm derivative being proved. For a general fixed base $a>0$, $a\ne1$, change of base gives $\log_a x=\ln x/\ln a$. Since $\ln a$ is a nonzero constant,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个推导使用已经建立的指数函数导数，没有反过来用待证的对数导数定义 $e$。对一般固定底数 $a>0$、$a\ne1$，换底式为 $\log_a x=\ln x/\ln a$。由于 $\ln a$ 是非零常数，

$$\frac{d}{dx}\log_a x=\frac{1}{x\ln a},\qquad x>0,\ a>0,\ a\ne1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $(\log_2 x)'=1/(x\ln2)$, whereas $\log_{1/2}x$ has a negative derivative because $\ln(1/2)<0$. Base one is excluded before differentiation: $\log_1 x$ is not an inverse function, and the displayed denominator would be zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $(\log_2 x)'=1/(x\ln2)$；$\log_{1/2}x$ 的导数则为负，因为 $\ln(1/2)<0$。底数一在求导前就必须排除：$\log_1 x$ 不是一个反函数，上式的分母也会为零。

## Compositions and absolute values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复合函数与绝对值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $u$ is differentiable and positive on the interval being considered, the outer derivative is $1/u(x)$ and the inner derivative is $u'(x)$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $u$ 在所考虑的区间上可导且为正，外层导数为 $1/u(x)$，内层导数为 $u'(x)$，所以

$$\frac{d}{dx}\ln u(x)=\frac{u'(x)}{u(x)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\ln(3x-2)$, first require $x>2/3$; then its derivative is $3/(3x-2)$. The rational derivative expression also exists at $x<2/3$, but this does not extend the original logarithmic function to those inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\ln(3x-2)$，先要求 $x>2/3$，再得到导数 $3/(3x-2)$。这个有理式在 $x<2/3$ 也能计算，却不能据此把原来的对数函数延伸到那些输入。

> [!warning] A different function has a different domain · 换了函数，也换了定义域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a differentiable $u$ with $u(x)\ne0$, the function $\ln|u(x)|$ has derivative $u'(x)/u(x)$. Locally $u$ has a fixed sign. If it is positive, use the usual rule; if it is negative, $\ln|u|=\ln(-u)$ and the two minus signs cancel. Therefore $\ln|3x-2|$ has derivative $3/(3x-2)$ on each of the intervals $x<2/3$ and $x>2/3$, but is undefined at $2/3$. The absolute value does not allow differentiation across the missing point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对可导的 $u$，只要 $u(x)\ne0$，$\ln|u(x)|$ 的导数就是 $u'(x)/u(x)$。在该点附近，$u$ 的符号保持不变：正值时用普通规则；负值时写成 $\ln(-u)$，两个负号相消。因此 $\ln|3x-2|$ 在 $x<2/3$、$x>2/3$ 两个区间上都有导数 $3/(3x-2)$，但在 $2/3$ 本身无定义。加绝对值并不允许跨过这个缺失点求导。
