# 无穷级数与幂级数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Infinite series and power series*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 判断无限次相加或无界区间上的累积是否仍落在有限值上，并控制近似误差。
> **具体锚点：** $1+1/2+1/4+\cdots=2$，但调和级数 $1+1/2+1/3+\cdots$ 发散；项趋于零不足以保证总和存在。
> **核心难点：** 收敛是部分和或截断积分的极限性质；不同判别法有各自适用结构。
> **为什么重要：** 数值近似、概率尾部、Taylor 展开和长期动态都依赖收敛。
> **继续：** 先做必要条件和结构识别，再选判别法；幂级数近似回到 [[导数的应用#线性与二次近似|局部近似]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** A series asks whether infinitely many terms have a finite limiting sum and, when used for approximation, how large the omitted tail can be.
> **Concrete anchor:** $1+1/2+1/4+\cdots=2$, whereas $1+1/2+1/3+\cdots$ diverges. Terms tending to zero are necessary but not sufficient.
> **Central difficulty:** Convergence concerns partial sums, and each test recognises a different structure. A test that is inconclusive is not evidence of divergence.
> **Why it matters:** Numerical approximation, probability tails, Taylor expansions, and long-run dynamics depend on convergence and error bounds.
> **Continue with:** Check the term condition and standard structures first; for integral analogues see [[反常积分|improper integrals]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数项级数与必要条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical series and the necessary term condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\sum a_n$ 收敛指部分和 $S_N$ 有有限极限。于是 $a_n\to0$ 是必要条件但非充分条件。几何级数、望远镜级数先求显式部分和；正项级数可用比较、极限比较、积分、比值或根值判别。比值极限等于 1 时没有结论，应换方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\sum a_n$ converges when its partial sums $S_N$ approach a finite limit. Therefore $a_n\to0$ is necessary but not sufficient. For geometric and telescoping series, compute explicit partial sums first. Positive-term series may suit comparison, limit comparison, integral, ratio, or root tests. A ratio-test limit of one is inconclusive and calls for another method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 绝对、条件与交错收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Absolute, conditional, and alternating convergence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sum|a_n|$ 收敛，则原级数绝对收敛并可安全重排。交错级数可在项绝对值单调趋零时条件收敛；此时截断误差不超过下一项，但任意重排可能改变和。必须区分“绝对值级数是否收敛”和“符号抵消后是否收敛”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\sum|a_n|$ converges, then the original series converges absolutely and may be rearranged safely. An alternating series converges when term magnitudes decrease monotonically to zero; its truncation error is at most the next magnitude, but arbitrary rearrangement of a conditionally convergent series can change its sum. Separate convergence of the absolute-value series from convergence caused by sign cancellation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 幂级数与 Taylor 级数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Power series and Taylor series*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

幂级数在收敛半径内绝对收敛，端点要逐个检查。Taylor 级数由各阶导数决定，但函数拥有所有阶导数不保证等于其 Taylor 级数；需要余项趋零。实际近似应同时给展开中心、有效范围和余项界，而非只写若干项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A power series converges absolutely inside its radius of convergence, while each endpoint must be checked separately. A Taylor series is determined by derivatives at its centre, but possessing derivatives of every order does not ensure equality with that series; the remainder must tend to zero. A useful approximation states its centre, range of validity, and error bound rather than listing terms alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判别与误差控制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Testing and error control*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判别顺序应是：项是否趋零、是否标准结构、正项还是交错、能否与已知基准比较。求“和”与判“收敛”是不同任务，多数判别法只回答后者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful order is: do the terms tend to zero, is there a standard structure, are terms positive or alternating, and can they be compared with a benchmark? Finding a sum and proving convergence are different tasks; most convergence tests answer only the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：Taylor 近似与余项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: Taylor approximation with a remainder*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 0 附近，$e^x=1+x+x^2/2+R_2(x)$。Lagrange 余项为 $R_2(x)=e^\xi x^3/6$，其中 $\xi$ 介于 0 与 $x$ 之间。当 $|x|\le0.1$ 时，$|R_2(x)|\le e^{0.1}(0.1)^3/6<1.85\times10^{-4}$。因此“用 $1+x+x^2/2$ 近似”只有和展开中心、目标范围与误差界一起报告才完整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Near zero, $e^x=1+x+x^2/2+R_2(x)$. The Lagrange remainder is $R_2(x)=e^\xi x^3/6$ for some $\xi$ between zero and $x$. If $|x|\le0.1$, then $|R_2(x)|\le e^{0.1}(0.1)^3/6<1.85\times10^{-4}$. Thus the polynomial becomes a meaningful approximation only when its centre, target range, and error bound are also reported.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*

### 为什么 $a_n\to0$ 不能证明 $\sum a_n$ 收敛？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does $a_n\to0$ not prove convergence of $\sum a_n$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它只说明单项变小，没有控制累积速度；调和级数的项趋零但部分和仍无界。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It controls individual terms but not their cumulative rate. Harmonic-series terms tend to zero while its partial sums remain unbounded.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么时候可以把 Taylor 多项式当作可靠近似？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When may a Taylor polynomial be treated as a reliable approximation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 已说明展开中心和邻域，并能用 Taylor 余项或其他误差界证明遗漏项在目标范围内足够小。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> State the expansion centre and neighbourhood, and use a Taylor remainder or another bound to show that omitted terms are sufficiently small on the target range.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 比值判别的极限等于 1 意味着什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does a ratio-test limit of one mean?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 判别无结论，不是级数发散；调和级数与 $\sum1/n^2$ 的比值极限都是 1，但一个发散、一个收敛。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The test is inconclusive, not evidence of divergence. Both the harmonic series and $\sum1/n^2$ have ratio-test limit one, yet one diverges and the other converges.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分绝对收敛与条件收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish absolute and conditional convergence in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 绝对收敛表示即使去掉符号也有有限和，因而不依赖抵消；条件收敛依赖正负抵消，绝对值级数发散。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Absolute convergence survives removal of all signs and therefore does not depend on cancellation. Conditional convergence relies on sign cancellation while the absolute-value series diverges.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
