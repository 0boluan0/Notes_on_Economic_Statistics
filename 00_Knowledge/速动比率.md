# 速动比率是按明确范围划定的速动资产与流动负债之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quick ratio divides quick assets, under an explicit asset definition, by current liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

速动比率 $QR=QA/CL$，通常以 $CL>0$ 解读短期债务相对于较易变现资产的账面覆盖。CPA口径将货币资金、交易性金融资产及各类应收款项等列为速动资产，排除存货、预付款等非速动项目；不同资料的范围应先核对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quick ratio $QR=QA/CL$ usually measures book coverage by relatively liquid assets when $CL>0$. CPA includes monetary funds, trading financial assets, and receivables, while excluding inventory, prepayments, and other non-quick items. Check the asset definition before comparing sources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ABC的速动资产为 $44+20+398+12=474$，流动负债300，故比率1.58。应收票据与其他应收也在内，所以不能从速动资产只减“应收账款”一个科目便得到[[现金比率]]的分子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC has quick assets of $44+20+398+12=474$ and current liabilities of 300, giving 1.58. Notes and other receivables are included, so subtracting only trade accounts receivable does not produce the numerator of the [[现金比率|cash ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“速动”是分析分类，不保证按账面值立即变现；还要看坏账、冻结、交易价格与回收日期。现销比例高的企业可能无需大量应收，不能以统一大于1的标准机械排名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Quick” is an analytical classification, not a guarantee of immediate conversion at book value. Credit losses, restrictions, market prices, and collection dates matter. Cash-sales businesses may need few receivables, so a universal threshold above one is inappropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
