# 市场失灵指市场配置未达到所采用的效率基准
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market failure is a failure of a market allocation to meet the adopted efficiency benchmark*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场失灵是指，给定资源、技术与相关约束，市场形成的配置没有达到用来评价它的效率基准。公共经济学常以[[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto 效率]]为基准；局部均衡模型也会在明确条件下考察[[总剩余]]。分析时必须说清是哪种效率、在哪个模型中未实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market failure occurs when the market allocation, given resources, technology, and relevant constraints, falls short of the efficiency benchmark used to evaluate it. Public economics commonly uses [[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto efficiency]]; partial-equilibrium models may examine [[总剩余|total surplus]] under stated conditions. An analysis must identify both the efficiency criterion and the model in which it fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，某项生产给旁人造成未计入交易价格的损害，私人决策面对的成本就可能低于社会成本，交易数量因而偏离效率基准。关键是找出这样的激励或信息缺口，而不是仅列出“市场力量、信息、公共品、外部性”等名称；具体机制分别见[[外部性数量偏离]]与[[公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件.canvas|公共品主题图]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if production harms others and the damage is not reflected in the transaction price, private decision-makers may face costs below social costs and choose an inefficient quantity. The task is to identify such an incentive or information gap, not merely name categories. See [[外部性数量偏离|the externality quantity wedge]] and the [[公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件.canvas|public-goods map]] for the mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格很高、某人买不起商品或分配不合意，本身都还没有完成市场失灵的证明。[[政府干预的效率与分配理由|分配理由]]可以独立提出；而效率缺口是否能由一项可实施政策改善，要另作[[福利定理边界|制度与约束比较]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A high price, someone's inability to afford a good, or an undesirable distribution does not by itself establish market failure. A [[政府干预的效率与分配理由|distributional rationale]] can be raised separately. Whether a feasible policy can improve an efficiency shortfall requires a further [[福利定理边界|comparison of institutions and constraints]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
