# 市场异象候选要成为可实施证据必须通过风险成本外样本数据挖掘与容量检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An anomaly candidate must survive risk, cost, out-of-sample, data-mining, and capacity checks to become implementable evidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子复核规则
> 历史平均超额收益只是异象候选。要成为对市场有效性的可实施挑战，模式至少应在合理风险模型下存在，扣除适用的点差、冲击、借券、融资和税费后仍有经济意义，在冻结规则的新样本中复制，并对搜索过的信号、参数和子样本做多重检验校正。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Historical average excess return is only an anomaly candidate. To become an implementable challenge to market efficiency, the pattern should survive reasonable risk models, remain economically meaningful after applicable spreads, impact, borrowing, funding, and taxes, replicate under a frozen rule in new data, and adjust for the signals, parameters, and subsamples that were searched.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

容量是成本检验的一部分但不能被一个固定手续费替代。策略规模增加会耗尽可交易量、扩大冲击或使借券困难；小资金可复制的毛收益不保证大资金还能成交。应报告信号覆盖的市值与流动性、换手、头寸集中、可借券范围和不同规模下的净收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capacity is part of implementation-cost analysis but cannot be represented by one fixed commission. Scaling a strategy can consume available volume, enlarge price impact, or make borrowing difficult. A gross return reproducible with small capital need not be tradable at scale. Report the market capitalisation and liquidity of the signal universe, turnover, position concentration, borrow availability, and net returns at different strategy sizes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使模式全部通过，也仍需区分三类解释：遗漏风险补偿、行为误价与套利限制、或制度/微观结构造成的测量规律。正确结论是限定条件下的稳健证据，而不是从一个因子或日历效应宣布整个市场无效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even after these checks, distinguish omitted risk compensation, behavioural mispricing with limits to arbitrage, and measurement regularities created by institutions or market microstructure. The proper conclusion is robust evidence under stated conditions, not a declaration that an entire market is inefficient because of one factor or seasonal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个策略样本内 $t=2.2$ 且毛收益很高，是否已可实施？
>
> **答案：** 不能。要知道共搜索了多少策略，做真正外样本复核，并检验风险、点差、冲击、借券、换手和规模容量；否则显著性和净收益都可能虚高。

## 来源与核验

- [Fama (1991), “Efficient Capital Markets: II”](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1991.tb04636.x)，pp. 1585–1589：核对数据挖掘、样本外复制、交易成本和风险模型解释异象的边界。
- [Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016), “…and the Cross-Section of Expected Returns”](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhv059)：核对大规模因子搜索中的多重检验问题。
- [Patton & Weller (2020), “What You See Is Not What You Get”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.02.012)：核对纸面异象收益与真实实施成本、价格冲击及容量之间的差距。
- [[训练验证测试划分]] 与 [[滚动起点评估]]：复用规则选择与时序外样本评价的分工；[[证券交易成本]] 与 [[Alpha基准依赖性]]：分别复用实施摩擦和模型相对绩效边界。
