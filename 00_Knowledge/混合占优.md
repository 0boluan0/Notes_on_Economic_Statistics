# 一项纯策略可能不被任何单个纯策略占优，却被其他纯策略的某个概率组合在所有对手情形下严格占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A pure strategy may escape dominance by every single pure alternative yet still be strictly dominated in every opponent contingency by a probability mixture of other pure strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查严格劣势时，逐行或逐列比较只能发现**纯策略对纯策略**的占优。完整检验还要问：能否把其他策略按某组固定概率混合，使该混合在对手每一种可能纯策略下都比目标策略收益更高？若能，目标策略同样不可能成为任何信念下的[[最佳反应]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise row or column comparisons detect only pure-against-pure dominance. A complete test also asks whether one fixed mixture of the other strategies yields a strictly higher payoff against every pure profile of the opponents. If so, the target can never be a [[最佳反应|best response]] to any belief.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判定条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Criterion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标纯策略 $s_i^0$ 被混合策略 $\sigma_i$ 严格占优，当且仅当

$$
u_i(\sigma_i,s_{-i})
=
\sum_{s_i\in S_i}\sigma_i(s_i)u_i(s_i,s_{-i})
>
u_i(s_i^0,s_{-i})
\quad\text{for every }s_{-i}\in S_{-i}.
$$

只检查对手的纯策略组合已经足够：若每个纯情形中的差额都为正，它们按任意对手混合策略加权后仍为正。反过来，只要某个纯情形失败，就不能声称“对所有对手选择”严格占优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed inequality is the complete criterion. It is enough to test the opponents' pure profiles because any mixed-opponent payoff difference is a weighted average of those pure-profile differences. A single failing pure contingency defeats the universal claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个纯比较看不出的例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An Example Missed by Pairwise Pure Comparisons*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只看列玩家收益：

|  | $L$ | $M$ | $R$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $T$ | 10 | 0 | 3 |
| $B$ | 0 | 10 | 3 |

$L$ 与 $M$ 都没有单独严格占优 $R$：一个在 $T$ 下好、另一个在 $B$ 下好。但 $\frac12L+\frac12M$ 对 $T$ 与 $B$ 都给出 5，而 $R$ 始终给 3，所以该混合策略严格占优 $R$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither $L$ nor $M$ alone dominates $R$: each performs badly against one row. Their equal mixture, however, pays 5 against either row, while $R$ always pays 3. The mixture therefore strictly dominates $R$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 多个策略时可写成可行性问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Feasibility Formulation for Larger Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当候选组合不明显时，可寻找概率 $\lambda_t$ 与统一改进幅度 $\varepsilon>0$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{t\ne s_i^0}\lambda_tu_i(t,s_{-i})
&\ge u_i(s_i^0,s_{-i})+\varepsilon
&&\forall s_{-i},\\
\sum_{t\ne s_i^0}\lambda_t&=1,
&\lambda_t&\ge0.
\end{aligned}
$$

若存在 $\varepsilon>0$ 的可行解，就找到了严格占优混合。这个线性形式只负责验证一组已经明确的收益数据，不替代对玩家、策略与收益的建模判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a larger finite game, the displayed linear feasibility system searches for mixture weights and a common positive margin. A feasible solution with $\varepsilon>0$ certifies strict mixed dominance. The calculation verifies a specified payoff table; it does not replace the modelling judgement that produced that table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个方向不要写反
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Do Not Reverse the Comparison*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要删除 $s_i^0$，必须证明**某个策略组合占优目标 $s_i^0$**。证明“$s_i^0$ 占优某个混合策略”只说明那个特定混合不吸引人，不能据此删除其支持集里的任何纯策略。占优关系比较的是完整对象，不能把混合的平均收益结论拆回每个组成策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Eliminating $s_i^0$ requires a mixture that dominates that target. Showing instead that $s_i^0$ dominates one particular mixture says nothing by itself about whether any pure strategy in that mixture's support may be deleted. An average-payoff comparison cannot be decomposed into componentwise dominance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $\frac12L+\frac12M$ 在两个对手纯策略下都严格胜过 $R$，就足以说明它也胜过对手的任意混合？
>
> **答案：** 对手混合后的收益差是两个纯情形收益差的概率加权平均。每一项都严格为正，任何非负且和为 1 的权重都仍给出严格正的平均差。
