# Integrating powers of tangent and secant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正切正割幂积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For tangent and secant powers, a useful [[换元积分|substitution]] starts by setting aside a complete derivative factor. For example, in $\int\sec^4x\,dx$, two of the four secant factors form $\sec^2x$, the derivative of $\tan x$. The remaining two can also be expressed in tangent. This turns the integral into a polynomial calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正切与正割的幂积分，可以先把一组完整的导数因子留出来，再作[[换元积分]]。例如在 $\int\sec^4x\,dx$ 中，四个正割因子中的两个组成 $\sec^2x$，恰好是 $\tan x$ 的导数；余下两个也能改写成正切。这样就把积分变成多项式计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The functions are $\tan x=\sin x/\cos x$ and $\sec x=1/\cos x$, where $x$ is real and measured in radians. Work on an open interval where $\cos x\ne0$, so both functions are defined. Dividing $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$ by $\cos^2x$ gives the identity $1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x$. From the [[正弦余弦求导|sine and cosine derivatives]] and the [[商法则|quotient rule]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两个函数分别为 $\tan x=\sin x/\cos x$、$\sec x=1/\cos x$，其中 $x$ 为以弧度计量的实数。先选一个 $\cos x\ne0$ 的开区间，保证两者都有定义。把 $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$ 除以 $\cos^2x$，得到恒等式 $1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x$。由[[正弦余弦求导]]与[[商法则]]，

$$(\tan x)'=\frac{\cos^2x+\sin^2x}{\cos^2x}=\sec^2x,
\qquad
(\sec x)'=\frac{\sin x}{\cos^2x}=\sec x\tan x.$$

> [!example] Reserve the derivative of tangent · 留出正切的导数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Split $\sec^4x$ into two squared factors. Rewrite one using $\sec^2x=1+\tan^2x$, and keep the other with $dx$. With $u=\tan x$ and $du=\sec^2x\,dx$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $\sec^4x$ 拆成两个平方因子。一个用 $\sec^2x=1+\tan^2x$ 改写，另一个与 $dx$ 留在一起。取 $u=\tan x$、$du=\sec^2x\,dx$，得到
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \int\sec^4x\,dx
> &=\int(1+\tan^2x)\sec^2x\,dx\\
> &=\int(1+u^2)\,du
> =\tan x+\frac{\tan^3x}{3}+C.
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $C$ is an arbitrary real constant on the chosen interval. Differentiating gives $(1+\tan^2x)\sec^2x=\sec^4x$, confirming that the result is an [[原函数|antiderivative]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $C$ 是所选区间上的任意实常数。求导得到 $(1+\tan^2x)\sec^2x=\sec^4x$，确认答案确实是一个[[原函数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two direct polynomial routes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两条主路由

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $\int\tan^m x\sec^n x\,dx$ for nonnegative integers $m,n$. A zero exponent means the corresponding factor is absent. If both are zero, the problem is simply $\int1\,dx=x+C$ and no trigonometric domain restriction is needed. Otherwise keep the interval condition above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $\int\tan^m x\sec^n x\,dx$，其中 $m,n$ 为非负整数。指数为零表示该因子不出现；如果两个指数都为零，题目只是 $\int1\,dx=x+C$，不需要三角函数的定义域限制。否则保留上面的区间条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If **$n$ is positive and even**, then $n\ge2$, so there really are two secant factors available. Reserve $\sec^2x\,dx$, use $u=\tan x$, and express the remaining secant pairs in $u$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 **$n$ 是正偶数**，就有 $n\ge2$，原式确实含有两个可取出的正割因子。留下 $\sec^2x\,dx$，令 $u=\tan x$，把剩下的成对正割也改成 $u$：

$$\int\tan^m x\sec^n x\,dx
=\int u^m(1+u^2)^{(n-2)/2}\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If **$m$ is positive and odd and $n\ge1$**, reserve $\sec x\tan x\,dx$ instead. The remaining tangent power is even, so $\tan^2x=\sec^2x-1$ removes it. With $u=\sec x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 **$m$ 是正奇数且 $n\ge1$**，可以改为留下 $\sec x\tan x\,dx$。剩下的正切次数为偶数，因此能用 $\tan^2x=\sec^2x-1$ 消去。取 $u=\sec x$，得到

$$\int\tan^m x\sec^n x\,dx
=\int(u^2-1)^{(m-1)/2}u^{n-1}\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both transformed integrands are polynomials. For example, $\int\tan^3x\sec x\,dx$ uses the second route and becomes $\int(u^2-1)\,du=\sec^3x/3-\sec x+C$. The condition $n\ge1$ matters: without a secant factor, we cannot simply reserve one while leaving everything else unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种新被积函数都是多项式。例如，$\int\tan^3x\sec x\,dx$ 使用第二条路线，变为 $\int(u^2-1)\,du=\sec^3x/3-\sec x+C$。条件 $n\ge1$ 很重要：如果原式没有正割因子，不能在其他部分不变的情况下凭空取出一个。

> [!question]- When both routes work, which is shorter? · 两条都可用时，哪条更短？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\int\tan^3x\sec^2x\,dx$, $u=\tan x$ immediately gives $\int u^3du=\tan^4x/4+C$. Choosing $u=\sec x$ is also valid, but first requires replacing the remaining $\tan^2x$ by $u^2-1$. Compare the resulting polynomial before committing to a route.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $\int\tan^3x\sec^2x\,dx$，取 $u=\tan x$，立即得到 $\int u^3du=\tan^4x/4+C$。取 $u=\sec x$ 也合法，但需要先把剩下的 $\tan^2x$ 换成 $u^2-1$。选路前可以先比较所得多项式的复杂程度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When neither direct route applies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两条直接路线都不适用时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a pure tangent power such as $\tan^3x$, the secant exponent is zero. First create useful terms by writing $\tan^2x=\sec^2x-1$, then integrate the resulting difference:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\tan^3x$ 这样的纯正切幂，正割指数为零。可以先用 $\tan^2x=\sec^2x-1$ 产生便于处理的项，再对得到的差式积分：

$$\begin{aligned}
\int\tan^3x\,dx
&=\int\tan x\sec^2x\,dx-\int\tan x\,dx\\
&=\frac12\tan^2x+\ln|\cos x|+C.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term uses $u=\tan x$. For the second, $\tan x=\sin x/\cos x$ with $u=\cos x$ gives $-\int du/u=-\ln|u|$. The [[对数函数求导|logarithm derivative]] verifies this on either sign of the nonzero cosine. Hence subtracting $\int\tan x\,dx$ produces the plus sign in front of $\ln|\cos x|$ above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项用 $u=\tan x$。第二项把 $\tan x$ 写为 $\sin x/\cos x$，取 $u=\cos x$，得到 $-\int du/u=-\ln|u|$；[[对数函数求导]]保证余弦为正或为负时，只要不为零都成立。因此，上式减去 $\int\tan x\,dx$ 后，对数前面是加号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $m$ is even and $n$ is odd, rewriting tangent pairs as $\sec^2x-1$ leads to odd secant powers. Basic antiderivatives or [[分部积分|integration by parts]] can then help; for instance $\int\sec^3x\,dx$ is not covered by either direct polynomial route. These two tests are useful choices, not an exhaustive two-case classification of all exponent pairs. Negative or fractional powers require separate domain and sign analysis as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $m$ 为偶数、$n$ 为奇数，把成对的正切换成 $\sec^2x-1$ 后，会得到奇次正割幂，可以进一步使用基本原函数或[[分部积分]]；例如 $\int\sec^3x\,dx$ 就不属于上面两条直接多项式路线。两个检查条件是有用的选择，不是对所有指数组合的穷尽二分。负数或分数幂也需要另外分析定义域和符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For cotangent and cosecant, use $\cot x=\cos x/\sin x$ and $\csc x=1/\sin x$ on intervals where $\sin x\ne0$. The analogous identity is $1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x$, but both derivative pairs carry a minus sign:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对余切和余割，在 $\sin x\ne0$ 的区间使用 $\cot x=\cos x/\sin x$、$\csc x=1/\sin x$。对应恒等式为 $1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x$，但两组导数因子都带负号：

$$d(\cot x)=-\csc^2x\,dx,\qquad
 d(\csc x)=-\csc x\cot x\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All these antiderivative identities are understood on intervals where their functions are defined. They do not bridge a zero of the original denominator. Different domain intervals may have [[原函数相差常数|different additive constants]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些原函数恒等式都在相应函数有定义的区间内理解，不能跨过原分母的零点。定义域分成不同区间时，各段可以有[[原函数相差常数|不同的加法常数]]。
