# 训练—评测污染是评测题、答案、模板或近似版本进入模型训练数据，使分数不再纯粹衡量未见样本泛化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Training–evaluation contamination occurs when evaluation questions, answers, templates, or close variants enter model training data, so the score no longer purely measures generalization to unseen examples*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 当评测内容在预训练或后训练期间成为训练暴露，模型的得分可能同时反映泛化和先前见过该内容的收益。污染的核心不是“模型答对了”，而是评测原本要求独立的数据边界被破坏。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When evaluation content becomes training exposure during pretraining or post-training, a score may reflect both generalization and benefits from prior exposure. Contamination is not defined by the model answering correctly; it is the breach of the data boundary on which the evaluation relies.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 哪些重叠可能构成污染
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What overlap can constitute contamination*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

污染可以是完整题目与答案，也可以是能实质透露任务或解法的近似版本、翻译、模板和标注解释。是否构成有效暴露取决于评测声明测什么：只出现通用短语通常证据很弱，题目和答案逐字进入训练则证据很强。因此审计必须报告匹配对象、匹配规则、阈值和可检索训练语料的覆盖范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contamination can involve exact questions and answers or close variants, translations, templates, and rationales that materially reveal the task or solution. Whether overlap counts depends on the evaluation claim: a generic phrase is weak evidence, whereas verbatim question–answer exposure is strong. An audit must therefore report the matched object, rule, threshold, and coverage of the searchable training corpus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它改变什么解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What interpretation changes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

污染不会自动告诉我们分数被抬高多少。它首先使“这是对未见样本的表现”这一解释失去干净依据；污染 detector 的标记率与实际得分增益是两个量，见 [[污染标记率与得分增益]]。训练前移除匹配样本与训练后检查既有暴露也不是同一操作，见 [[训练后重叠审计不等于去污染]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contamination does not by itself quantify score inflation. It first weakens the claim that the result measures performance on unseen examples. A detector's flagged rate and the resulting score gain are different quantities; see [[污染标记率与得分增益]]. Removing matches before training and inspecting prior exposure after training are also distinct operations; see [[训练后重叠审计不等于去污染|A post-training overlap audit is not decontamination]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反复查看同一测试集并据此挑选 prompt、解码参数或模型，即使测试文本从未进入训练语料，也会让测试集参与开发。这是另一种威胁，单列为 [[测试集自适应复用]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeatedly inspecting one test set to select prompts, decoding settings, or models makes the test set part of development even when its text never enters training data. That separate threat is [[测试集自适应复用]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 最小例子
> 一道考试题及标准答案在后训练数据中出现，随后模型在同一道题上得分。这个分数仍是真实观测，但不能再被单独解释成“模型把能力泛化到了未见题”。若只匹配到题干中的一句通用定义，则需要更细的证据，不能直接判定同等污染。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An exam question and its reference answer appear in post-training data, after which the model is scored on that same item. The score is still an observation, but it no longer by itself demonstrates generalization to an unseen question. A match on one generic phrase is much weaker and cannot be treated as equivalent contamination without further evidence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> detector 报告某 benchmark 有 40% 样本匹配训练语料。能否由此断定模型分数高估了 40 个百分点？
>
> **答案：** 不能。40% 是特定匹配规则下的标记比例；分数增益还取决于重叠内容、样本难度、训练利用程度和反事实 clean 模型表现。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Brown et al. (2020), *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165)：第 2.2 节与第 4 节记录 benchmark overlap 过滤及过滤 bug 后的事后分析。
- [Grattafiori et al. (2024), *The Llama 3 Herd of Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783)：第 5.1.4 节明确讨论 detector 的误报、漏报以及不同 benchmark 的污染影响。
- [Singh et al. (2024), *Evaluation data contamination in LLMs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03923)：系统比较污染匹配方法与阈值，并检验被标记样本是否带来表现收益。
