# 回归模型设定应从目标量、时间顺序与用途开始而不是从可用列开始
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression specification should begin with the target, temporal order, and intended use rather than the available columns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子程序
> 先写清总体、结果变量、目标量、焦点比较、时间顺序和模型用途，再把问题写成可解释的条件均值或预测规则。数据表里有某一列，不等于它应进入模型；统计显著也不能替代这一决定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First state the population, outcome, target quantity, focal comparison, temporal order, and intended use, and only then express the problem as an interpretable conditional mean or prediction rule. The availability or statistical significance of a column does not by itself justify including it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么顺序不能反过来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the order cannot be reversed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一组数据可以服务不同目标。预测关心新样本损失；描述关心当前总体中的条件关系；因果估计还要说明反事实比较与识别假设。三种目标可能选择不同变量、损失函数和验证证据。若先让逐步回归、p 值或信息准则决定变量，再补写研究问题，模型可能已经改变了要估计的对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same data can serve different goals. Prediction concerns loss on new observations; description concerns conditional relationships in the stated population; causal estimation additionally requires a counterfactual comparison and an identification argument. These goals can imply different variables, losses, and validation evidence. Letting stepwise selection, p-values, or information criteria choose variables before the question is fixed can silently change the target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个通用起点是
$$
E(Y\mid X)=m(X;\beta),
$$
但这只是均值表示。哪些变量属于 $X$、哪些变换有实质含义，以及 $\beta$ 能否作因果解释，仍由目标、时间顺序、机制和研究设计决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A general starting point is $E(Y\mid X)=m(X;\beta)$, but this is only a mean representation. The target, temporal order, mechanism, and research design still determine which variables belong in $X$, which transformations are meaningful, and whether $\beta$ has a causal interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么不能把“所有可用列先放进去，再按显著性删除”当作通用设定方法？
>
> **答案：** 因为变量是否应进入取决于目标量、时间顺序和机制；显著性只是在已经给定模型后描述样本证据，不能决定目标或识别结构。
