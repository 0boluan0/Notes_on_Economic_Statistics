<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Starting a regression specification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*回归设定的起点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specifying a regression means deciding what relationship the model should represent before estimating its coefficients. Start with a question precise enough to judge an answer: whose outcome, measured when, compared with what, and for what use? The same spreadsheet can support several questions, with different appropriate models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归设定是在估计系数之前，决定模型应该表示什么关系。起点是一个足够具体、能据此判断答案的问题：研究谁的什么结果，在哪个时点测量，与什么比较，用来做什么？同一张数据表可以服务多个问题，而适合的模型可能不同。

> [!example] One student dataset, three questions / 同一份学生数据，三个问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose records contain entrance scores, tutoring participation, term attendance and final exam scores.
>
> **Description:** how do average final scores differ across recorded entrance scores and tutoring groups? This describes a conditional association in the stated population.
>
> **Prediction:** at the start of term, which students are likely to need help? A model may use entrance scores and information already recorded then; end-of-term attendance is not yet available.
>
> **Causal effect:** how would final scores change if eligible students were offered tutoring rather than not offered it? This requires a credible comparison of interventions. A tutoring coefficient in an observational regression does not establish that comparison by itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 假设记录包含入学成绩、是否参加辅导、学期出勤和期末成绩。
>
> **描述：** 不同入学成绩、辅导组别的学生，期末平均成绩有什么差别？这是所指总体中的条件关联。
>
> **预测：** 学期开始时，哪些学生可能需要帮助？可使用入学成绩和当时已记录的信息；期末才知道的出勤情况还不能使用。
>
> **因果效应：** 对符合条件的学生提供辅导，而非不提供，会怎样改变期末成绩？这需要可信的干预比较，仅有观察数据回归中的辅导系数并不能建立该比较。

## Why the question comes before the columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么顺序不能反过来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These uses require different evidence. A descriptive coefficient depends on the population and variables conditioned on. Prediction must be assessed on observations representing the intended use, with the [[预测时点与信息集|information available at the prediction time]]. A causal question requires a research design and assumptions linking observed comparisons to interventions; see [[预测与因果目标|prediction and causal targets]]. Good prediction, a large coefficient and a small p-value are not substitutes for that link.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同用途需要不同证据。描述系数取决于总体和条件变量集合。预测要使用[[预测时点与信息集|预测时点实际可用的信息]]，在能代表预定使用场景的观测上评价。因果问题则需要研究设计和假设，把观察到的比较连接到干预，见[[预测与因果目标]]。预测准确、系数很大或 p 值很小，都不能替代这层联系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write down the observational unit and population, the outcome and its measurement time, the comparison or forecast required, and when each candidate variable becomes available. Then choose how to represent the relationship and how to assess it. For the prediction example, “final exam score for incoming students, forecast before term using entrance information” is already more useful than “regress score on all available columns.” It identifies a possible information leak before any fitting takes place.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写清观察单位与总体、结果变量及其测量时点、所需比较或预测，以及各候选变量何时可得，再选择关系的表达方式和评价证据。对上例来说，“根据入学信息，在开学前预测新生期末成绩”，已经比“用所有可用列回归成绩”更有用：它让我们在拟合之前发现可能的信息泄漏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the target is an average outcome given specified information, the [[条件期望|conditional mean]] is a natural object. Let $Y$ denote the outcome, $X$ the chosen input variables, $m$ a proposed mean function and $\beta$ its coefficients. The statement below asserts that the chosen function represents the conditional mean; it is a modelling assumption, not something the notation proves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目标是给定特定信息后的平均结果，[[条件期望|条件均值]]就是自然的研究对象。记 $Y$ 为结果、$X$ 为所选输入、$m$ 为提出的均值函数、$\beta$ 为其中的系数。下面的式子声称所选函数表示条件均值；这是模型假设，不是符号本身证明的事实。

$$
E(Y\mid X)=m(X;\beta).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, $m(X;\beta)=\beta_0+\beta_1 X_1+\beta_2 X_2$ proposes an additive straight-plane mean. A curved or interaction term changes that representation. A [[条件均值与线性投影|best linear approximation]] can still be defined when the complete mean is not linear, but its coefficients answer a different question. For prediction, the output and [[点预测损失|loss function]] also matter: targeting an average, a quantile or a probability is not the same task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$m(X;\beta)=\beta_0+\beta_1 X_1+\beta_2 X_2$ 提出一个可加的平面均值；曲线项或交互项会改变这一表达。完整均值并非线性时，仍可定义[[条件均值与线性投影|最佳线性近似]]，但系数回答的问题有所不同。对预测，还要说明输出和[[点预测损失|损失函数]]：预测均值、分位数和概率不是同一个任务。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only after this target is explicit should candidate specifications be compared. Variable availability, statistical significance and fit scores supply information within a modelling problem; they do not define its purpose. Adding controls can change the [[多元回归系数|partial comparison represented by a coefficient]]. Record such a change rather than silently treating the new coefficient as an estimate of the old target. Revise the question when evidence warrants it, and make that revision explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

明确目标后，才有依据比较候选设定。变量可得性、统计显著性和拟合评分提供的是某个建模问题内部的信息，并不能定义问题的用途。增加控制变量可能改变[[多元回归系数|系数表示的控制后比较]]。应记录这种变化，而不是默认为新系数仍在估计原来的目标。证据足够时可以修订问题，但要明确写出修订。

## 继续
