# 逐年约束未经周期调整的预算余额可能在衰退中要求财政紧缩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An annual constraint on the unadjusted budget balance can require fiscal tightening during a downturn.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当衰退使税收自动下降或福利支出增加，而一条逐年的[[预算余额规则]]已经接近约束界限时，维持未经周期调整的余额可能要求增税或削支。这会抵销[[财政自动稳定器]]，并可能放大需求低迷；是否发生取决于规则口径、初始余量和政策传导条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a downturn automatically reduces revenue or raises benefits while an annual [[预算余额规则|balance rule]] is near its limit, preserving the unadjusted balance may require tax increases or spending cuts. This offsets [[财政自动稳定器|automatic stabilisers]] and can amplify weak demand, conditional on headroom, rule design and transmission.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设单独的首轮预算算例：税收为产出的 40%，支出 42，余额下限 −2。产出为 100 时税收 40、余额 −2；产出降到 95 时税收 38、余额 −4。暂不考虑政策对产出的反馈，要恢复 −2 就需削支 2 或取得等额净收入增量。这是约束机制的演示，不是均衡乘数计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a separate first-round illustration, revenue is 40% of output, spending is 42 and the balance floor is −2. At output 100, revenue is 40 and the balance −2; at output 95, revenue is 38 and the balance −4. Ignoring feedback to output, restoration requires spending cuts of 2 or an equivalent net revenue increase. This is not an equilibrium multiplier calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定[[周期调整预算余额]]的界限，可以容许已估计的周期性收支反应，但不自动容许额外的主动刺激：若 CAB 已触及下限，新增赤字措施仍可能违反规则。合理的余量、跨期安排与[[财政逃逸条款]]可提供不同形式的弹性，不能把“结构性口径”本身当成逆周期政策保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed [[周期调整预算余额|CAB]] limit can accommodate estimated cyclical responses, but not arbitrary discretionary stimulus. If CAB is already at its floor, new deficit-increasing measures can still breach the rule. Headroom, intertemporal arrangements and [[财政逃逸条款|escape clauses]] provide different forms of flexibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
