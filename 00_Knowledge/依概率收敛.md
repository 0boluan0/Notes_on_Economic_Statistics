# Convergence in probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*依概率收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An approximation can still be wrong after many steps, yet become increasingly unlikely to be wrong by an appreciable amount. **Convergence in probability** makes this precise: choose any positive error tolerance, keep it fixed, and ask whether the probability of exceeding it tends to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个近似在做了很多步之后仍可能出错，但出现明显误差的可能性可以越来越小。**依概率收敛**表达的就是这种保证：任选一个正的误差容忍值，固定它，再看超出它的概率是否趋于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $X_n$ is the approximation at index $n=1,2,\ldots$, and $X$ is the target, which may itself be random. A [[随机变量|random variable]] assigns a number to each outcome. Put the approximation and target on the same [[概率空间|probability space]] so that $|X_n-X|$ measures their error on the **same outcome**. No independence assumption is part of this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $X_n$ 是第 $n=1,2,\ldots$ 个近似，$X$ 是目标，而目标本身也可以随机。[[随机变量]]为每个结果指定一个数值。把近似与目标放在同一[[概率空间]]上，$|X_n-X|$ 才能比较**同一个结果**下两者的误差。定义不要求它们独立。

> [!important] Every fixed tolerance · 每个固定容忍值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> We write $X_n\xrightarrow{p}X$ when, for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)\longrightarrow0\qquad(n\to\infty).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The expression inside $P$ describes an event: the error is larger than $\varepsilon$. Its probability is an ordinary number, and those numbers must approach zero. This must work for $0.1$, $0.001$, and every other positive tolerance; checking just one is insufficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 满足这个条件时记作 $X_n\xrightarrow pX$。$P$ 内描述的是“误差超过 $\varepsilon$”这个事件；它的概率是一个普通数值，这些概率必须趋于零。$0.1$、$0.001$ 以及其他每个正容忍值都要满足，只检查一个不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A shrinking chance of a unit error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Draw one number $U$ [[连续均匀分布|uniformly from $[0,1]$]], so an interval's probability equals its length. At step $n$, report one if $U\le1/n$ and zero otherwise. Using an [[事件指示变量|indicator]] to encode this yes/no rule gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只抽一次 $U$，它[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布在 $[0,1]$]] 上，所以区间的概率就是其长度。第 $n$ 步在 $U\le1/n$ 时报告 1，否则报告 0。用[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]把这条判断写成

$$X_n=\mathbf1\{U\le1/n\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the target to be zero. For $0<\varepsilon<1$, an excessive error occurs exactly when $X_n=1$, giving probability $1/n$. For $\varepsilon\ge1$, the error never exceeds the tolerance, giving probability zero. Thus **every** positive tolerance is covered, and $X_n\xrightarrow p0$. At $n=100$, a unit error still has probability $0.01$; convergence does not promise perfection at a finite index.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取目标为 0。当 $0<\varepsilon<1$ 时，超差事件恰好是 $X_n=1$，概率为 $1/n$；当 $\varepsilon\ge1$ 时，误差不会超过容忍值，概率为零。因此**所有**正容忍值都满足定义，$X_n\xrightarrow p0$。在 $n=100$ 时，大小为 1 的误差仍有 $0.01$ 的概率；收敛不承诺有限步时完全正确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nor must exact equality become likely. The deterministic approximation $Y_n=1/n$ converges in probability to zero, because for each fixed $\varepsilon$ its error eventually falls below $\varepsilon$. Yet $P(Y_n=0)=0$ for every $n$. The definition also gives no guarantee for a shrinking tolerance chosen as a function of $n$: with $\varepsilon_n=1/(2n)$, $P(|Y_n|>\varepsilon_n)=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确相等的概率也不必趋于 1。确定性的近似 $Y_n=1/n$ 依概率收敛到 0，因为对每个固定 $\varepsilon$，误差最终都会小于它；但每个 $n$ 都有 $P(Y_n=0)=0$。定义也不保证随 $n$ 缩小的容忍值仍然适用：若取 $\varepsilon_n=1/(2n)$，则 $P(|Y_n|>\varepsilon_n)=1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This controls the error probability **at each index**, rather than the whole future of a realised sequence; the distinction is explained in [[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|almost-sure versus probability convergence]]. It does imply [[概率收敛推出分布收敛|convergence in distribution]], whose converse generally fails except for a [[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|constant limit]]. When a statistical estimator approaches its target parameter this way, the property is called [[估计量一致性|consistency]]; [[弱大数定律|weak laws of large numbers]] provide important examples for sample averages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它控制的是**每个索引处**的误差概率，而不是一条已实现序列的整个未来；具体差别见[[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|几乎必然与依概率收敛的关系]]。它能够推出[[概率收敛推出分布收敛|依分布收敛]]，反向一般不成立，但[[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|常数极限]]是例外。统计估计量以这种方式趋近目标参数时，称为[[估计量一致性|一致性]]；[[弱大数定律]]提供了样本均值的重要例子。

> [!question]- Is one tolerance enough? · 一档误差够吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $P(|X_n-X|>0.1)=1/n$. Must $X_n\xrightarrow pX$? No. For a concrete counterexample take $X=0$ and $X_n=1$ when the same uniform $U\le1/n$, and $X_n=0.05$ otherwise. The displayed probability is $1/n$, but $P(|X_n|>0.01)=1$ forever.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $P(|X_n-X|>0.1)=1/n$，能断言依概率收敛吗？不能。例如令 $X=0$，在同一个均匀变量 $U\le1/n$ 时取 $X_n=1$，否则取 $0.05$。超过 $0.1$ 的概率确实是 $1/n$，但超过 $0.01$ 的概率始终为 1。
