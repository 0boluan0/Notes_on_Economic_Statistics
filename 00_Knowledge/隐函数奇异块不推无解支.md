# 待解变量 Jacobian 块奇异只使标准隐函数定理失效，不推出局部解支不存在
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A singular Jacobian block for the solved-for variables only invalidates the standard implicit function theorem; it does not prove that no local branch exists*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 逻辑边界
> 对关系 $F(u,v)=0$，$D_vF(u_0,v_0)$ 可逆是标准 [[隐函数定理]] 保证局部唯一 $C^1$ 解支 $v=g(u)$ 的充分条件。若这个块奇异，正确结论只有：该版本的定理不再提供保证。不能由“条件失败”推出“解支不存在”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a relation $F(u,v)=0$, invertibility of $D_vF(u_0,v_0)$ is a sufficient condition under the standard [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] for a locally unique $C^1$ branch $v=g(u)$. If this block is singular, the correct conclusion is only that this theorem no longer supplies a guarantee. Failure of the condition does not imply absence of a branch.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 奇异但仍有唯一光滑解支
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A singular block with a unique smooth branch*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令
$$
F(x,y)=y^3-x^3.
$$
在原点，$F_y(0,0)=0$，所以不能直接套用以 $y$ 为待解变量的标准定理。但实数范围内方程等价于 $y=x$，仍有唯一且光滑的局部解支。这个例子已经否定了“Jacobian 块奇异 $\Rightarrow$ 无隐函数”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $F(x,y)=y^3-x^3$, one has $F_y(0,0)=0$, so the standard theorem cannot be applied with $y$ as the solved-for variable. Over the reals, however, the equation is equivalent to $y=x$, which is a unique smooth local branch. This example disproves the implication from a singular block to the absence of an implicit function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同样的奇异性也可能伴随多支
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The same singularity can also accompany branching*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令
$$
F(x,y)=y^2-x^2.
$$
原点仍有 $F_y=0$，但零集由 $y=x$ 与 $y=-x$ 两支组成，不能在原点附近唯一写成一条 $y=g(x)$。因此，奇异块本身既不能证明有解支，也不能证明无解支；它只告诉我们必须改用直接因式分解、更高阶展开或其他局部分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $F(x,y)=y^2-x^2$, one again has $F_y=0$ at the origin, but the zero set contains the two branches $y=x$ and $y=-x$ and cannot be represented there by one locally unique function $y=g(x)$. A singular block therefore proves neither the existence nor the absence of a branch; it signals the need for factorization, higher-order expansion, or another local analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与导数公式的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relation to the derivative formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $D_vF$ 奇异时，[[隐函数导数公式]] 中的 $(D_vF)^{-1}$ 不存在，所以那条公式不能使用。即使某条光滑解支碰巧存在，它的导数也要从该解支本身或另一种可用表示中求出，不能把一个不存在的矩阵逆硬写进答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $D_vF$ is singular, the inverse in the [[隐函数导数公式|implicit-derivative formula]] does not exist, so that formula is unavailable. Even if a smooth branch happens to exist, its derivative must be obtained from the branch itself or another valid representation; a nonexistent matrix inverse cannot be inserted into the calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 看到 $D_vF$ 奇异时，能够立即下的唯一结论是什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What is the only immediate conclusion justified when $D_vF$ is singular?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 标准隐函数定理及其逆矩阵导数公式不能直接使用；局部解支是否存在、是否唯一，仍需另行分析。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The standard implicit function theorem and its inverse-matrix derivative formula cannot be applied directly. Existence and uniqueness of a local branch still require separate analysis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [MIT 18.022, Implicit Function Theorem handout](https://math.mit.edu/~larsh/teaching/F2007/handouts/implicit.pdf)：核验可逆 Jacobian 块是该定理给出局部唯一解支的假设，因此块奇异时只能判定定理不可用。
- LSE EC400，*SOFP Slides Lecture 3*（课程幻灯片） slides 14–17：核对课程中的应用语境；这里把其中过强的必要性措辞收回到标准充分条件。
- 两个多项式例子由直接因式分解核验：$y^3-x^3=(y-x)(y^2+xy+x^2)$ 在实数原点附近只给出 $y=x$，而 $y^2-x^2=(y-x)(y+x)$ 给出两支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The MIT 18.022 handout verifies that an invertible Jacobian block is an assumption under which the theorem supplies a locally unique branch; singularity therefore establishes only that the theorem is unavailable.
- EC400 SOFP Slides Lecture 3, slides 14–17, supplies the course application context; the statement here replaces its overstrong necessity wording with the standard sufficient condition.
- The two polynomial examples are checked by direct factorization: over the reals near the origin, $y^3-x^3=(y-x)(y^2+xy+x^2)$ yields only $y=x$, whereas $y^2-x^2=(y-x)(y+x)$ yields two branches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
