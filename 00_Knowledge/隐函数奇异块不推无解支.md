<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Singular blocks and implicit branches
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*隐函数的奇异块与解支*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A failed derivative test leaves a question open. The equation $y^3-x^3=0$ gives the simple real solution $y=x$, even though its derivative with respect to $y$ vanishes at the origin. Thus a singular block in the **implicit function theorem** does not itself prove that a local solution is absent. Existence, uniqueness and differentiability must then be checked from the equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数检验失败，会把问题留下来。方程 $y^3-x^3=0$ 的实数解就是简单的 $y=x$，尽管它关于 $y$ 的导数在原点为零。因此，**隐函数定理**中的块奇异，本身不能证明局部解不存在。此时要从方程另行判断存在性、唯一性和可微性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a $C^1$ system $F(u,v)=0$ near a zero $(u_0,v_0)$, $u\in\mathbb R^p$ denotes the inputs and $v\in\mathbb R^q$ the unknowns, with positive integers $p,q$ and $q$ equations. Here $C^1$ means continuous first [[偏导数|partial derivatives]] nearby. The $q\times q$ [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian block]] $D_vF$ contains the derivatives of those equations with respect to the unknowns. **Singular** means that this square block has no [[逆矩阵|inverse]]. The [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] assumes the block is invertible and then guarantees a unique $C^1$ local graph of all nearby solutions. Failure of this sufficient hypothesis does not negate that conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零点 $(u_0,v_0)$ 附近考察 $C^1$ 方程组 $F(u,v)=0$，其中 $u\in\mathbb R^p$ 表示输入，$v\in\mathbb R^q$ 表示待解变量，$p,q$ 为正整数，共有 $q$ 个方程。$C^1$ 表示附近的一阶[[偏导数]]连续。$q\times q$ 的[[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian 块]] $D_vF$ 收集各方程对待解变量的偏导。**奇异**表示这个方块没有[[逆矩阵]]。[[隐函数定理]]假设它可逆，从而保证全部附近解构成唯一的 $C^1$ 局部图像；这个充分假设失败，不等于结论的否定成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A singular block with a unique smooth branch
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 奇异但仍有唯一光滑解支

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take real $x,y$ and $F(x,y)=y^3-x^3$. Its partial derivative is $F_y(x,y)=3y^2$, so the one-entry block at $(0,0)$ is zero. Yet factorization gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取实数 $x,y$，令 $F(x,y)=y^3-x^3$。偏导为 $F_y(x,y)=3y^2$，所以在 $(0,0)$ 处，只有一个元素的方块为零。但因式分解给出

$$y^3-x^3=(y-x)(y^2+xy+x^2),\qquad
y^2+xy+x^2=\left(y+\frac{x}{2}\right)^2+\frac{3x^2}{4}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second factor is positive except when $x=y=0$. Therefore every real zero satisfies $y=x$, including the origin. There is exactly one branch $g(x)=x$, and it has derivatives of every order. For any $\varepsilon>0$, its graph describes all solutions in $(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\times(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二个因子除 $x=y=0$ 外都为正。因此，每个实数零点都满足 $y=x$，原点也一样。唯一解支是 $g(x)=x$，它具有任意阶导数。对任意 $\varepsilon>0$，它的图像都给出 $(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\times(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$ 内的全部解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same zero set can also be written as $\widetilde F(x,y)=y-x=0$, whose $y$ derivative is $1$. The derivative test depends on the chosen defining equations. The cubic representation has lost first-order information at the origin without changing its solution set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个零集还可写为 $\widetilde F(x,y)=y-x=0$，其关于 $y$ 的导数为 $1$。导数检验依赖所选的定义方程：立方表示在原点丢掉了一阶信息，却没有改变解集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Singularity may accompany several branches
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同样的奇异性也可能伴随多支

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $F(x,y)=y^2-x^2$, the block at the origin is again $F_y(0,0)=0$, but
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $F(x,y)=y^2-x^2$，原点仍有 $F_y(0,0)=0$，然而

$$F(x,y)=(y-x)(y+x)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y=x\ \text{or}\ y=-x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both lines are smooth branches through the origin. In every open product neighbourhood of the origin, sufficiently small nonzero $x$ has two distinct nearby solutions, $x$ and $-x$. Selecting $g(x)=x$ is possible, but no single graph describes **all** nearby zeros with a unique $y$ for each $x$. Distinguish “a branch exists” from the local uniqueness asserted by the theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条直线都是经过原点的光滑解支。在原点的每个开乘积邻域内，足够小的非零 $x$ 都有两个不同的附近解 $x$ 与 $-x$。可以选取 $g(x)=x$，但不能用单一图像描述**全部**附近零点并让每个 $x$ 只有一个 $y$。要区分“存在一条解支”与定理所说的局部唯一性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The other possibilities are also different. For $F(x,y)=y^3-x$, every real $x$ has the unique real solution $g(x)=\sqrt[3]{x}$, but the quotient at zero is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他情形也需要区分。对 $F(x,y)=y^3-x$，每个实数 $x$ 都有唯一实数解 $g(x)=\sqrt[3]{x}$，但原点处的差商为

$$\frac{g(h)-g(0)}{h}=\frac{1}{|h|^{2/3}}\longrightarrow+\infty\qquad(h\to0,\ h\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So this continuous branch has no finite derivative at zero. By contrast, $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ has only the real zero $(0,0)$: there is no solution branch defined for every $x$ in an open interval around zero. Both equations still have $F_y(0,0)=0$. These direct examples show why the singular block alone determines none of the three properties: existence over an input neighbourhood, uniqueness, or differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，这条连续解支在零点没有有限导数。相反，$F(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ 只有一个实数零点 $(0,0)$，不存在定义在零点开区间内、对每个 $x$ 都给出解的函数。这两个方程在原点也都有 $F_y(0,0)=0$。这些直接可算的例子说明：输入邻域上的存在性、唯一性与可微性，三者都不能只由奇异块判定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What happens to the derivative formula?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与导数公式的关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the theorem's hypotheses, the [[隐函数导数公式|implicit derivative formula]] uses $(D_vF)^{-1}$. At a singular block that inverse does not exist, so the formula cannot be evaluated there. If a differentiable branch has been justified independently, the [[多元链式法则|chain rule]] still gives the identity $D_uF+D_vF\,Dg=0$ along that branch, but it may no longer determine $Dg$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在定理的假设下，[[隐函数导数公式]]使用 $(D_vF)^{-1}$。块奇异时，这个逆不存在，所以该处不能计算那条公式。若已通过其他方法证明某条解支可微，[[多元链式法则]]仍沿解支给出恒等式 $D_uF+D_vF\,Dg=0$，但它可能不再确定 $Dg$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y^3-x^3=0$ at the origin, this identity reduces to $0+0\,g'(0)=0$: it says nothing about the slope. The explicit branch $g(x)=x$ supplies the missing answer $g'(0)=1$. For $y^3-x=0$, assuming a finite derivative would instead yield $-1+0\,g'(0)=0$, a contradiction consistent with the divergent quotient above. The equation, not the vanishing denominator alone, decides what follows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对原点处的 $y^3-x^3=0$，恒等式退化为 $0+0\,g'(0)=0$，没有告诉我们斜率。显式解支 $g(x)=x$ 补上答案 $g'(0)=1$。对 $y^3-x=0$，若假设存在有限导数，则会得到 $-1+0\,g'(0)=0$ 的矛盾，与上面差商发散一致。结论由方程进一步分析决定，不能仅看分母为零。

> [!question]- What remains to be checked? · 还要检查什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When the selected block is singular, the standard theorem and its inverse-matrix formula are unavailable at that point. Check the actual local zero set: can each nearby input be assigned a solution, are there other nearby solutions, and is the selected branch differentiable? Direct factorization, another defining equation, or higher-order analysis may answer these questions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所选块奇异时，该点不能直接使用标准定理及其逆矩阵公式。应检查实际局部零集：每个附近输入是否有解、附近是否还有其他解、所选分支是否可微？直接因式分解、另一种定义方程或高阶分析可以分别回答这些问题。
