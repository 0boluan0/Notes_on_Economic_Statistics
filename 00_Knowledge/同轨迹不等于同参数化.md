# Same trace, different parametrizations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同轨迹不等于同参数化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two journeys can visit exactly the same places while taking different routes through time. For a [[参数曲线|parametric curve]], its **trace** is the set of positions visited; its **parametrization** also records the input interval and the position at each input. A picture of the trace alone cannot tell whether the point moved clockwise or counterclockwise, moved quickly or slowly, or covered an arc more than once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两次行进可以经过完全相同的地点，却以不同的时间顺序经过。对[[参数曲线]]，**轨迹点集**只记录经过哪些位置，**参数化**还记录输入区间以及每个输入对应的位置。单凭轨迹图，无法知道点是顺时针还是逆时针、快还是慢、是否把某段弧走了不止一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three journeys around one circle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同一个圆上的三次行进*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using angles in radians, on the same interval $0\le t\le2\pi$ consider
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

角度以弧度计，在相同区间 $0\le t\le2\pi$ 上考虑

$$
\gamma_1(t)=(\cos t,\sin t),\qquad
\gamma_2(t)=(\cos2t,\sin2t),\qquad
\gamma_3(t)=(\cos t,-\sin t).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three images are the unit circle. The first makes one counterclockwise turn; the second makes two counterclockwise turns because its angle runs from $0$ to $4\pi$; the third makes one clockwise turn. At $t=\pi/2$, for example, their positions are respectively $(0,1),(-1,0),(0,-1)$. Equal images do not mean equal positions at each parameter value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三者的像集都是单位圆。第一条逆时针走一圈；第二条的角度从 $0$ 增至 $4\pi$，因此逆时针走两圈；第三条顺时针走一圈。例如在 $t=\pi/2$，位置分别是 $(0,1),(-1,0),(0,-1)$。像集相同，并不意味着各参数值对应的位置相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the [[正弦余弦求导|sine and cosine derivative rules]] and the inner factor $2$ from the [[链式法则|chain rule]], their coordinate derivatives are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用[[正弦余弦求导]]，并由[[链式法则]]计入内层因子 $2$，逐坐标求导得到

$$
\gamma_1'(t)=(-\sin t,\cos t),\qquad
\gamma_2'(t)=(-2\sin2t,2\cos2t),\qquad
\gamma_3'(t)=(-\sin t,-\cos t).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Speed is the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] of the derivative, namely $\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2}$. The three speeds are $1,2,1$. Thus the second curve moves twice as fast per unit of parameter and covers twice as much arc over the same interval. If instead its interval were $[0,\pi]$, it would complete just one turn. The interval and the map jointly determine coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

速率是导数向量的[[欧氏范数]]，即 $\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2}$，三条曲线的速率分别为 $1,2,1$。因此第二条每单位参数移动得快一倍，在同一区间走过的弧长也多一倍。但若把它的区间改成 $[0,\pi]$，就只走一圈。覆盖情况由区间和映射共同决定。

## Changing the parameter without adding a traversal
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*改变参数而不额外描画一遍*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful change of parameter is $t=\phi(s)$, where $\phi:J\to I$ is a continuous strictly monotone bijection: each old parameter value is used exactly once. The new map is $\eta(s)=\gamma(\phi(s))$. Increasing $\phi$ preserves the order of the journey; decreasing $\phi$ reverses it. Where the relevant derivatives exist, the [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种有用的换参数方式是 $t=\phi(s)$，其中 $\phi:J\to I$ 是连续、严格单调的双射，即每个旧参数值恰好使用一次。新映射为 $\eta(s)=\gamma(\phi(s))$。$\phi$ 递增时保留行进顺序，递减时反转顺序。在相关导数存在的地方，[[链式法则]]给出

$$\eta'(s)=\gamma'(\phi(s))\phi'(s),\qquad
\|\eta'(s)\|_2=\|\gamma'(\phi(s))\|_2\,|\phi'(s)|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $\phi(s)=2s$ maps $[0,\pi]$ onto $[0,2\pi]$ and completes the same one-turn circle faster. The decreasing map $\phi(s)=2\pi-s$ on $[0,2\pi]$ reverses that turn. When $\phi$ is $C^1$, meaning it has a continuous derivative, and $\phi'$ never vanishes in the interior, it is a regular change of parameter there: nonzero velocities stay nonzero, and the [[参数曲线切线的速度条件|tangent line]] stays the same, even if its direction arrow reverses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$\phi(s)=2s$ 把 $[0,\pi]$ 映满 $[0,2\pi]$，用更短参数区间完成同一圈；在 $[0,2\pi]$ 上取递减映射 $\phi(s)=2\pi-s$，则反向走完这一圈。若 $\phi$ 为 $C^1$，即有连续导数，并且内部各点的 $\phi'$ 都非零，就在内部构成正则换参数：非零速度仍非零，[[参数曲线切线的速度条件|切线]]保持不变，虽然方向箭头可能反转。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nonzero-derivative condition is stronger than preserving the trace and order. On $[-1,1]$, $(s,0)$ and $(s^3,0)$ traverse the same segment once in the same direction. The second has zero velocity at $s=0$, so this change is not regular there. Conversely, two turns around a circle cannot be obtained from the once-traced interval by a monotone bijection: that would use each old stage only once, not run through all stages twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数非零比“保留轨迹与顺序”要求更强。在 $[-1,1]$ 上，$(s,0)$ 与 $(s^3,0)$ 都沿同一方向把同一线段走一次；但第二条在 $s=0$ 速度为零，因此这个换参数在那里不正则。反过来，绕圆两圈也不可能由单圈区间上的单调双射得到：双射只把每个旧阶段使用一次，不会把全部阶段再运行一遍。

## What elimination and length can tell us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*消参与长度各保留什么信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Eliminating $t$ in the circle examples gives $x^2+y^2=1$, with no record of direction, speed or number of turns. In general the equation alone can even include positions outside the actual trace: $(\cos t,\sin t)$ for $0\le t\le\pi$ gives only the upper semicircle. One must keep range restrictions before concluding that two images are equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

圆例消去 $t$ 后都得到 $x^2+y^2=1$，没有方向、速率或圈数记录。一般情况下，这个方程甚至可能包含实际轨迹之外的位置：$0\le t\le\pi$ 上的 $(\cos t,\sin t)$ 只经过上半圆。因此，先保留范围限制，才能判断两个像集是否相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the conditions of [[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|the arc-length theorem]], the integral of speed counts distance actually travelled. The three full-circle parametrizations above have lengths $2\pi,4\pi,2\pi$. Reversing direction does not cancel length. Returning once to the starting point does not add a second arc; tracing a whole arc again does. For [[旋转曲面面积与重复覆盖|surface area of revolution]], coverage of the generated surface must also be checked: even distinct parts of a once-traced plane curve can generate the same surface patch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|弧长定理]]的条件下，速率积分累计实际走过的距离。上述三条整圆参数化的长度为 $2\pi,4\pi,2\pi$。反向不会抵消长度；仅在终点返回起点，不会额外增加一段弧，而把整段弧再走一遍才会重复计长。对于[[旋转曲面面积与重复覆盖]]，还须核对生成表面的覆盖：即使平面曲线只描一次，它的不同部分也可能生成同一块表面。

> [!question]- Does one Cartesian equation prove one traversal? · 同一消参方程能证明同一次描画吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. First verify the actual images using the parameter intervals. Then check order and coverage. If an arc-length integral is involved, check its regularity assumptions and integrate speed along the given journey; equal traces alone do not imply equal travelled lengths.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不能。先结合参数区间核对实际像集，再查顺序与覆盖。若要使用弧长积分，还须核对其正则条件，并沿给定行进过程积分速率；仅有相同轨迹点集，不能推出相同路程。
