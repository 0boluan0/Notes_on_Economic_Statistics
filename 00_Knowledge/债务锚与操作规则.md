# 债务锚提供中长期财政参照而操作规则约束可用于引导年度决策的财政变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A debt anchor provides a medium- to long-term fiscal reference, while operational rules constrain variables used to guide annual decisions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债务锚通常以债务水平或债务率表达中长期参照，为财政路径提供方向；操作规则把这个方向连接到年度余额或支出等变量。债务也受增长、利息和估值变化影响，因此单独公布一个债务界限，并不能直接告诉部门今年可以安排多少支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A debt anchor provides a medium- to long-term reference for debt or its ratio to GDP. Operational balance or expenditure rules translate that direction into annual constraints. Growth, interest and valuation changes also affect debt, so a debt limit alone does not determine departments' annual spending allocations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种连接需要使用[[债务率动态]]及情景分析：不同增长、利率和冲击下，相同的年度余额未必通向同一债务率。[[债务稳定初级余额]]给出特定假设下稳定债务率所需的初级余额，选择政策路径时还须考虑[[债务可持续性]]及安全余量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The connection requires [[债务率动态|debt dynamics]] and scenarios. The same annual balance can imply different debt paths under different growth, interest and shocks. The [[债务稳定初级余额|debt-stabilising primary balance]] is conditional on its assumptions; policy design also considers [[债务可持续性|sustainability]] and buffers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

锚和操作规则应相容，但不必每年都等号成立：债务上限不是要求每年借到上限，最低余额要求也不是禁止做得更好。增加更多规则可能造成重复或冲突，关键是能否用少量可解释的约束连接长期目标与年度决策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Anchors and operational rules should be consistent, but limits need not bind every year. A debt ceiling does not instruct government to borrow up to it. Additional rules can become redundant or inconsistent; the task is a clear connection between long-term objectives and annual choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
