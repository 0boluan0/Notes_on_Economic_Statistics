# Locality of improper-integral convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反常积分敛散的局部性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When deciding whether an [[反常积分|improper integral]] has a finite total, a piece whose integral is already known to be finite cannot decide the result for an unresolved tail or singularity. Adding that piece changes the total by one finite number. **Locality** means that we can isolate each problematic approach and study it where the problem occurs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断一个[[反常积分]]的总量是否有限时，已经确认积分有限的一段，不能决定尚未处理的尾部或奇点是否收敛。把这段加回来，只是增加一个有限数。**敛散的局部性**就是：把各个需要取极限的方向隔离出来，在问题实际发生的地方研究它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, removing the stretch from $1$ to $100$ does not make the divergent tail of $1/x$ converge. That stretch contributes $\ln100$, a large but finite amount. The indefinitely extending part remains. Here $\ln$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], and $(\ln x)'=1/x$ for $x>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，把 $1/x$ 从 $1$ 到 $100$ 的一段去掉，并不会让发散的尾部收敛。这一段贡献 $\ln100$，虽然不小，却仍然有限；无限延伸的部分还在。这里 $\ln$ 是[[自然对数]]，在 $x>0$ 时有 $(\ln x)'=1/x$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a finite change of starting point is harmless
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么可以改变尾部的有限起点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<c$ be finite real numbers. Assume the real-valued function $f$ is Riemann integrable on every finite interval $[a,T]$ with $T>a$. This means each ordinary [[定积分|definite integral]] exists before we take a limit; continuity on those intervals is sufficient. For a finite cutoff $T>c$, [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<c$ 是有限实数，实值函数 $f$ 在每个有限区间 $[a,T]$（$T>a$）上都黎曼可积。这要求取极限之前，每个普通[[定积分]]已经存在；在这些区间上连续是一个充分条件。对有限截断点 $T>c$，[[定积分区间可加性]]给出

$$\int_a^T f(x)\,dx=C+\int_c^T f(x)\,dx,
\qquad C=\int_a^c f(x)\,dx\in\mathbb R.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $C$ is fixed while $T$ moves. If the tail on the right tends to a finite number $L$, the left side tends to $C+L$. Conversely, if the left side tends to a finite number $K$, subtracting $C$ shows that the tail tends to $K-C$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$T$ 在变化时，$C$ 是固定的。若右边尾部趋于有限数 $L$，左边就趋于 $C+L$；反过来，若左边趋于有限数 $K$，减去 $C$ 后，尾部就趋于 $K-C$。因此

> [!important] Same convergence, possibly different values · 敛散相同，数值可以不同
>
> $$\int_a^\infty f\text{ converges}
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \int_c^\infty f\text{ converges}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This argument does not require $f\ge0$. Adding a finite constant cannot create a finite limit from an oscillating or unbounded accumulation. When both integrals converge, their values differ by $C$; the values need not be equal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个论证不要求 $f\ge0$。给振荡或无界的累积加一个有限常数，不能使它突然具有有限极限。两边都收敛时，它们的值相差 $C$，不必相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same reasoning applies near a finite singular endpoint. If $f$ is integrable on every $[a+\varepsilon,b]$, where $a<c<b$ and $0<\varepsilon<c-a$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限奇端点，也可以作同样处理。若 $a<c<b$，且 $f$ 在每个 $[a+\varepsilon,b]$ 上可积，其中 $0<\varepsilon<c-a$，则

$$\int_{a+\varepsilon}^b f=\int_{a+\varepsilon}^c f+\int_c^b f.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As the positive cutoff $\varepsilon$ tends to zero, the last term stays finite and fixed. Thus convergence at $a$ can be tested using only the part from $a$ to $c$. For finitely many singularities or infinite ends, [[反常积分逐坏点拆分|split each problematic approach]] and check its own limit. Locality does not permit cancellation between divergent pieces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正截断量 $\varepsilon$ 趋于零时，最后一项保持有限且固定。因此，只研究 $a$ 到 $c$ 的部分，就能判断在 $a$ 附近是否收敛。若奇点或无穷端有限多个，应[[反常积分逐坏点拆分|逐个拆开有问题的趋近方向]]，检查各自的极限。局部性不允许发散部分互相抵消。

## Keep the comparison focused on the same endpoint
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 不要把比较函数自身的无关奇点带进来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $f(x)=1/\sqrt{x^2+10}$ on $[0,\infty)$. On $[0,1]$ it is continuous, so that contribution is finite. On the tail $x\ge1$, compare it with $g(x)=1/x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $[0,\infty)$ 上的 $f(x)=1/\sqrt{x^2+10}$。它在 $[0,1]$ 上连续，因此这段的贡献有限。对 $x\ge1$ 的尾部，将它与 $g(x)=1/x$ 比较：

$$\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+10}}
=\frac1{\sqrt{1+10/x^2}}\longrightarrow1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both functions are positive there. The [[反常积分极限比较判别|limit comparison test]] says their tails have the same convergence behaviour because the ratio tends to a positive finite constant. The [[无穷远 p 积分判别|power-integral test]] with $p=1$ gives divergence of $\int_1^\infty dx/x$, so the original integral also diverges. Starting the comparison at $100$ would give the same verdict.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两函数在那里都为正。[[反常积分极限比较判别]]说明：比值趋于有限正常数时，两条尾部同敛散。取 $p=1$ 的[[无穷远 p 积分判别]]，可知 $\int_1^\infty dx/x$ 发散，所以原积分也发散。把比较起点改为 $100$，结论不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting $\int g$ at zero would introduce a second, unrelated divergence: $g$ is singular there, whereas $f$ is not. A comparison for large $x$ establishes a conclusion about the tail, not about a new singularity at the comparator's other endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把 $g$ 的积分从零开始，就会引入另一个无关的发散原因：$g$ 在零处有奇点，$f$ 却没有。对充分大的 $x$ 所作的比较，只判断尾部，不是在研究比较函数另一端新出现的奇点。

## What may be removed or replaced
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bounded interval is not automatically a finite contribution. For example, take $f(x)=1/x$ for $0<x\le1$ and $f(x)=1/x^2$ for $x>1$. The tail after $1$ converges to $1$, but the part near zero diverges, so the integral from zero to infinity diverges. The omitted interval was finite in length, not finite in accumulated area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有界区间并不自动意味着贡献有限。例如，令 $0<x\le1$ 时 $f(x)=1/x$，$x>1$ 时 $f(x)=1/x^2$。从 $1$ 开始的尾部收敛为 $1$，但零附近的部分发散，所以从零到无穷的积分发散。被略去的区间只是长度有限，累积面积并不有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proper integrability is a convenient sufficient condition for removing a finite piece, but is not necessary. A piece may itself be an improper integral whose separate endpoint limits have already been proved to converge. For example, $\int_0^1x^{-1/2}\,dx=2$ by the [[零附近 p 积分判别|power test near zero]]; it can be added to any separately convergent tail. What is needed in the argument is a well-defined finite number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正常可积是移除有限区间时方便使用的充分条件，却不是必要条件。那一段本身也可以是反常积分，只要各个端点极限已分别证实收敛。例如，由[[零附近 p 积分判别]]得到 $\int_0^1x^{-1/2}\,dx=2$，它可以与另外已证收敛的尾部相加。论证真正需要的是一个定义明确的有限数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, if two functions agree beyond a fixed cutoff, and both initial pieces have finite integrals, their convergence is identical. When they converge, their full integrals differ only by those finite initial contributions. Changing a function on a bounded interval without checking its integrability does not meet this condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同理，若两个函数在某个固定截断点之后完全相同，且各自初始段的积分都有限，那么它们的敛散一致；当它们收敛时，完整积分只相差初始段的有限贡献。只说“在有界区间上改了函数”，却不检查可积性，并不满足这个条件。

> [!question]- A tail starting at $100$ · 从 $100$ 开始的尾部
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\int_{100}^{\infty}f$ converges, when does $\int_0^\infty f$ converge? Check that the omitted part from $0$ to $100$ has a finite integral: it may be proper, or improper with every required limit separately convergent. The tail alone does not settle that check.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已知 $\int_{100}^{\infty}f$ 收敛，何时能推出 $\int_0^\infty f$ 收敛？还需确认被略去的 $0$ 到 $100$ 部分具有有限积分：可以正常可积，也可以是所有所需极限均独立收敛的反常积分。只知道尾部收敛，还没有完成这一检查。
