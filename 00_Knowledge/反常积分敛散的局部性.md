# 反常积分的敛散由各问题点附近行为决定而有限正常区间只改变数值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Improper-integral convergence is determined near each problematic point, while a regular finite interval changes only the value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子规则
> 若 $f$ 在每个避开奇点的有限闭区间上正常可积，那么改变无穷尾部的有限起点、或在远离奇点的有限区间把 $f$ 改成另一个正常可积函数，不会改变反常积分的敛散性。它会改变积分的数值，却不会让一个收敛尾部变发散，或反过来。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If $f$ is properly integrable on every finite closed interval away from its singularities, changing a finite starting point of an infinite tail, or replacing $f$ by another properly integrable function on a finite interval away from a singularity, does not change convergence. It may change the integral's value, but it cannot turn a convergent tail into a divergent one or vice versa.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意有限 $A<B$，
$$\int_A^b f=\int_A^B f+\int_B^b f.$$
第一项是固定有限数，因此当 $b\to\infty$ 时，左侧有有限极限当且仅当右侧尾部有有限极限。这就是比较判别只需“对充分大的 $x$”成立的原因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite $A<B$, $\int_A^b f=\int_A^B f+\int_B^b f$. The first term is one fixed finite number, so as $b\to\infty$ the left side has a finite limit exactly when the remaining tail does. This is why comparison inequalities need hold only for sufficiently large $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不要把比较函数自身的无关奇点带进来

判断 $\int_0^\infty dx/\sqrt{x^2+10}$ 的无穷尾部时，可从 $1$ 开始与 $1/x$ 比较。$1/x$ 在 $0$ 的奇点与原函数在无穷远的尾部不是同一个问题；从 $0$ 比较会混入无关发散。先把有限区间 $[0,1]$ 单独确认正常可积，再比较尾部，逻辑才干净。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To test the tail of $\int_0^\infty dx/\sqrt{x^2+10}$, begin at $1$ when comparing with $1/x$. The singularity of $1/x$ at zero is unrelated to the original function's tail at infinity. Starting the comparison at zero mixes in an irrelevant divergence. Verify the finite interval $[0,1]$ separately, then compare the tail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

“有限区间不影响敛散”以该段确实正常可积为前提。若有限区间内藏着未识别的奇点，它本身就是新的问题点，必须拆开，不能作为“有限修补”略过。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule assumes that the finite interval is properly integrable. If it contains an unrecognised singularity, that point is a new improper endpoint and must be split rather than dismissed as a finite modification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知 $\int_{100}^{\infty}f(x)dx$ 收敛，什么时候能推出 $\int_0^\infty f(x)dx$ 收敛？
>
> **答案：** 还要确认 $f$ 在 $[0,100]$ 上正常可积、没有未处理奇点；此时只增加一个有限数。
