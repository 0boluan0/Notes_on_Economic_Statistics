# 实二次型只由矩阵的对称部分决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real quadratic form depends only on the symmetric part of its matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 对称化恒等式
> 对任意 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 和 $x\in\mathbb R^n$，二次型 $q_A(x)=x^TAx$ 满足
> $$
> x^TAx=x^T\left(\frac{A+A^T}{2}\right)x.
> $$
> 因而每个实二次型都有唯一的对称代表矩阵 $S=(A+A^T)/2$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every real square matrix $A$, the quadratic form $q_A(x)=x^TAx$ equals $x^T((A+A^T)/2)x$. Hence every real quadratic form has the unique symmetric representative $(A+A^T)/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $K=(A-A^T)/2$，则 $K^T=-K$。标量 $x^TKx$ 等于自己的转置 $x^TK^Tx=-x^TKx$，所以必为零。交叉项也因此成对合并：在二维中，对称矩阵
$$
\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{bmatrix}
$$
对应 $ax_1^2+2bx_1x_2+cx_2^2$，中间系数是 $2b$ 而不是 $b$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Writing $A=S+K$ with $K^T=-K$, the scalar $x^TKx$ equals its own negative and is therefore zero. Off-diagonal terms consequently combine in pairs: a symmetric $2\times2$ matrix with off-diagonal entry $b$ contributes $2bx_1x_2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条恒等式不允许把非对称矩阵直接塞进“对称矩阵的特征值判据”。应先换成 $S$。例如 $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&4\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$ 的特征值都为 $1$，但其二次型由 $S=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{bmatrix}$ 决定，存在负方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity does not license applying symmetric eigenvalue tests directly to a nonsymmetric matrix. First replace it by $S$. The displayed triangular matrix has eigenvalues $1,1$, yet its symmetric part has eigenvalues $3,-1$, so its quadratic form takes negative values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数域不能原样把转置改成共轭转置：一般的 $x^*Ax$ 只有实部由 Hermitian 部分决定，详见 [[复二次型实部]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over the complex field, plain transposition cannot simply be replaced by conjugate transposition: only the real part of a general $x^*Ax$ is determined by the Hermitian part; see [[复二次型实部|the Hermitian-part identity for a complex quadratic form]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么任意实斜对称矩阵 $K$ 都满足 $x^TKx=0$？
>
> **答案：** 该量是标量，转置后既不变，又因 $K^T=-K$ 变成其相反数。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** Why does every real skew-symmetric matrix $K$ satisfy $x^TKx=0$?
>
> **Answer:** The expression is a scalar, so transposing it leaves it unchanged; but $K^T=-K$ makes the transpose its negative. It must therefore be zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
