# Finite-sample bias of self-normalised importance sampling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*自归一抽样的有限样本偏差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Correct weights do not make every weighted procedure unbiased. [[自归一化重要性抽样|Self-normalised importance sampling]] divides one random sum by another, using the same observations in both. The average of these realised ratios generally differs from the ratio of their averages. Its [[估计量偏差|bias]] is this repeated-sampling mean minus the target, whenever that mean exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重正确，不代表所有加权程序都无偏。[[自归一化重要性抽样]]把两个来自同一批观测的随机和相除；这些实际比率的平均，一般不等于两个平均值之比。它的[[估计量偏差|偏差]]，是在这个重复抽样均值存在时，用该均值减去目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One sample exposes the difference
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个样本就能看出区别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the target [[概率质量函数|probabilities]] be $p(1)=3/4$, $p(0)=1/4$, while the proposal is $q(1)=q(0)=1/2$. Record $h(x)=x$, so the target mean is $\mu=3/4$. The proposal covers both target outcomes, and the exact weights $w=p/q$ are $3/2$ at $1$ and $1/2$ at $0$: both are strictly positive. With $N=1$, the weight cancels completely:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设目标[[概率质量函数|概率]]为 $p(1)=3/4$、$p(0)=1/4$，提议概率为 $q(1)=q(0)=1/2$，记录 $h(x)=x$，因此目标均值 $\mu=3/4$。提议覆盖两个目标结果，精确权重 $w=p/q$ 在 $1$ 处为 $3/2$、在 $0$ 处为 $1/2$，都严格为正。当 $N=1$，权重完全相消：

$$
\widehat\mu_{SNIS}=\frac{h(X_1)w(X_1)}{w(X_1)}=X_1,
\qquad E_q[\widehat\mu_{SNIS}]=\frac12,
\qquad \operatorname{Bias}=-\frac14.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The draw still has proposal law $q$. Calculating its weight more accurately cannot change this cancellation. In contrast, the [[重要性抽样|ordinary IS]] output is $h(X_1)w(X_1)$, equal to $3/2$ or $0$ with equal probability; its expectation is $3/4$. These are two different estimators of the same target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观测仍按提议分布 $q$ 生成；把权重算得更精确，无法改变这个相消。相比之下，[[重要性抽样|普通 IS]] 输出是 $h(X_1)w(X_1)$，等概率取 $3/2$ 或 $0$，其期望为 $3/4$。两者针对同一目标，却是不同估计量。

> [!example] Two draws still need a repeated-sampling calculation · 两次抽样仍须对所有样本平均
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For two iid proposal draws, $(0,0)$, $(0,1)$, $(1,0)$ and $(1,1)$ each have probability $1/4$. Their SNIS estimates are $0,3/4,3/4,1$. The mean is $5/8$, so the bias is $5/8-3/4=-1/8$. Bias is smaller here than at $N=1$, but this one example is not a universal monotonicity or convergence-rate theorem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个 iid 提议观测产生的 $(0,0)$、$(0,1)$、$(1,0)$、$(1,1)$ 各有概率 $1/4$，对应 SNIS 为 $0,3/4,3/4,1$。平均值为 $5/8$，偏差为 $5/8-3/4=-1/8$。这里的偏差比 $N=1$ 小，但这个例子不是普遍的单调性或收敛速度定理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the unbiasedness argument stops
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无偏论证在哪一步停下来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed positive integer $N$, write $\widetilde w=c\,p/q$ with one constant $0<c<\infty$. Assume full target coverage and $E_p|h|<\infty$. Set $A_N=N^{-1}\sum_i h(X_i)\widetilde w(X_i)$ and $B_N=N^{-1}\sum_i\widetilde w(X_i)$. For draws with marginal law $q$, [[期望线性性|linearity]] gives $E[A_N]=c\mu$ and $E[B_N]=c$. Where $B_N>0$, SNIS is $A_N/B_N$. There is no expectation rule that turns $E[A_N/B_N]$ into $E[A_N]/E[B_N]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定正整数 $N$，写 $\widetilde w=c\,p/q$，共同常数满足 $0<c<\infty$。假定覆盖完整目标且 $E_p|h|<\infty$，记 $A_N=N^{-1}\sum_i h(X_i)\widetilde w(X_i)$、$B_N=N^{-1}\sum_i\widetilde w(X_i)$。观测边际分布为 $q$ 时，由[[期望线性性]]有 $E[A_N]=c\mu$、$E[B_N]=c$。在 $B_N>0$ 处，SNIS 等于 $A_N/B_N$；不存在把 $E[A_N/B_N]$ 自动改成 $E[A_N]/E[B_N]$ 的期望法则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numerator and denominator share the draws and usually move together, but dependence alone is not the whole explanation: taking a reciprocal is nonlinear even for an independent denominator. The safe calculation averages the ratio itself. The linked [[期望与非线性变换不可交换|nonlinear-expectation distinction]] explains the general obstacle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分子与分母共享观测，通常会一起变动，但不能把原因仅归为依赖：即使分母独立，取倒数也是非线性运算。可靠的做法是对比率本身求平均；[[期望与非线性变换不可交换]]解释了这一普遍障碍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Bias requires a defined, integrable estimator
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*谈偏差前，估计量须有定义且可积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the weight sum can be zero, the bare ratio is not a real-valued estimator on every sample. Specify a failure or fallback rule before claiming an unconditional expectation; conditioning on positive total weight asks a different question. If $h$ is bounded and a finite constant is used on zero-weight samples, the completed estimator is bounded and its bias exists. For unbounded $h$, target integrability alone does not automatically give finite-sample integrability of the ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果权重和可能为零，裸比率便不是每个样本上都有定义的实值估计量。声称无条件期望前，须先说明失败或备用规则；只在权重和为正的条件下求期望，是另一个问题。若 $h$ 有界，且零权重样本填入固定有限数，补全后的估计量有界，偏差存在。若 $h$ 无界，只有目标可积，并不会自动保证有限样本比率可积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, on $(0,1)$ let $p(x)=2x$, $q(x)=1$ and $h(x)=1/x$. Then $E_p|h|=2$ and all sampled weights are positive almost surely. Yet for $N=1$, SNIS equals $1/X_1$ with $X_1$ uniform, whose expectation is infinite. A finite ordinary bias is not available in that case. The bounded two-point example above avoids both zero denominators and this integrability issue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在 $(0,1)$ 上取 $p(x)=2x$、$q(x)=1$、$h(x)=1/x$。此时 $E_p|h|=2$，抽到的权重几乎必然为正；但当 $N=1$，SNIS 等于均匀观测的 $1/X_1$，期望为无穷，此时没有通常的有限偏差。前面的有界二点例子避开了零分母与可积性问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Generally biased” does not mean biased for every problem. A constant $h$ is exact whenever the denominator is positive; if $q=p$, positive weights are constant almost surely and SNIS is the usual unbiased sample mean for integrable $h$. Other cancellations can occur. Conversely, [[自归一抽样强一致性|strong consistency]] does not establish finite-sample unbiasedness, a universal bias rate, or convergence of expectations without additional conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“通常有偏”不是“所有问题都有偏”。只要分母为正，常数 $h$ 就会被精确再现；若 $q=p$，权重几乎必然是同一个正常数，SNIS 就是可积 $h$ 的通常无偏样本均值，也可能存在其他抵消。反过来，[[自归一抽样强一致性|强一致性]]不会自动给出有限样本无偏性、普遍偏差速度，或不加其他条件的期望收敛。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Owen, [Chapter 2, §2.7, PDF pp. 30–31](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-intro.pdf#page=30): ratios of means and their generally nonzero finite-sample bias. Its delta-method approximations are not used as unconditional rate guarantees here.
- Owen, [Chapter 9, §9.2, PDF pp. 8–9](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-is.pdf#page=8): SNIS versus ordinary IS, and the exact constant-function case.
- Cardoso et al., [BR-SNIS, NeurIPS 2022, PDF pp. 1–2](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/04bd683d5428d91c5fbb5a7d2c27064d-Paper-Conference.pdf#page=1): the self-normalised ratio and bias under specified conditions. The finite-state and unbounded-integrand examples here are direct calculations, not examples taken from that paper.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Owen 第 2 章 §2.7，PDF pp. 30–31：核对均值之比通常具有有限样本偏差；不把其中 Delta 方法近似当作无条件速度保证。
- Owen 第 9 章 §9.2，PDF pp. 8–9：核对 SNIS 与普通 IS 的区别及常数函数的精确性。
- Cardoso 等，NeurIPS 2022《BR-SNIS》，PDF pp. 1–2：交叉核对自归一比率及特定条件下的偏差。本卡有限状态与无界被积函数例子直接计算，并非引自该论文的算例。
