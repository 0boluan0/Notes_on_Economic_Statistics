# Choosing a root-finding method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求根方法的选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing a root-finding method means deciding which information to use to locate a solution of $f(x)=0$, and what evidence will justify the returned answer. A root is an input $r$ for which the function output is zero. Computing a plausible decimal is only one part of the job: we also need to know which root is being sought, how close the answer must be, and what to report if the calculation fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择求根方法，就是决定用什么信息寻找方程 $f(x)=0$ 的解，以及用什么证据说明返回的答案可靠。根是使函数输出为零的输入 $r$。算出一个看似合理的小数只是其中一步：还要说明想找哪个根、答案需要多准确，以及计算失败时报告什么。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three ideas organize this choice. A **bracket** is an interval whose endpoints have opposite function signs; with continuity, the [[介值定理|Intermediate Value Theorem]] certifies that it contains at least one root. A [[导数|derivative]] describes local slope and can suggest a faster next guess. A [[求根残差|residual]] is the remaining value $f(x)$ at a guess; it measures how well the equation is satisfied, which is different from knowing the distance to a root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以用三个概念组织选择。**括区间**的两个端点函数值异号；再有连续性，[[介值定理]]就保证其中至少有一个根。[[导数]]描述局部斜率，可以帮助生成更快的下一次猜测。[[求根残差]]是在候选点还剩下的函数值 $f(x)$，衡量方程满足得怎样，与知道候选点离根多远是两回事。

> [!tip] Start with the information you have · 从已有信息出发
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Which method is fastest?” has no context-free answer. First ask what structure is available: finite candidates, ordered directional feedback, a continuous sign-changing bracket, a reliable derivative, usable interpolation, or only repeated function evaluations. Then ask which error certificate is required and how costly failure would be. The solver must follow these premises rather than forcing the problem into a familiar code template.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “哪个方法最快”不是脱离问题就能回答的。先问手里有什么结构：有限候选、有序方向反馈、连续异号区间、可靠导数、可用插值，还是只剩一次次函数评价；再问需要哪种误差证书、一次失败有多贵。方法应服从这些前提，而不是反过来把问题硬塞进熟悉的代码模板。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Choose a root solver by the available certificates, local information, error target, and cost of failure: preserve a sign-changing bracket when available, then accelerate safely with reliable derivatives or interpolation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Route the problem by its certificate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先按证书分流

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Available structure | Starting point | Evidence retained | Main cost or boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finite candidates and a correct test | [[枚举验证\|Exhaustive verification]] | Exhausting the set can establish no solution within that set | Many candidates can be expensive |
| A fixed-step grid in a real interval | [[固定步长求根\|Fixed-step root search]] | Only the chosen grid is covered | The grid [[固定步长漏根\|may miss the entire acceptance region]] |
| Continuity and opposite endpoint signs | [[二分求根\|Bisection]] | At least one root stays bracketed | Linear bracket contraction; about one binary digit per update |
| Proximity to a simple root and a reliable derivative | [[Newton迭代\|Newton iteration]] / [[Newton局部收敛\|local convergence]] | A squared-error bound under the local hypotheses | No general bracketing certificate; an arbitrary start need not enter the local neighborhood |
| Both a bracket and fast candidate steps | [[混合求根\|A safeguarded hybrid]] | Retain the bracket and accelerate when safe | The acceptance and progress rules must be explicit |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 可用结构 | 合理入口 | 能直接保留的证据 | 主要代价或边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 有限候选与正确谓词 | [[枚举验证]] | 穷尽后可证明该集合内无解 | 候选多时成本高 |
| 实区间内的固定步长离散网格 | [[固定步长求根]] | 只覆盖所选网格 | [[固定步长漏根\|可能跨过整个接受区域]] |
| 连续函数与端点异号 | [[二分求根]] | 至少一个根始终被括住 | 线性收敛；每轮约增加 1 个二进制有效位 |
| 靠近简单根且导数可靠 | [[Newton迭代]] / [[Newton局部收敛]] | 局部条件成立时有误差平方界 | 无全局括区间证书；任意初值不保证进入该邻域 |
| 同时有括区间和快速局部步 | [[混合求根]] | 保留括区间，安全时加速 | 接受规则必须明确 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[二分查找|Binary search]] searches ordered discrete candidates; it is not a continuous root solver. The methods share a halving intuition but maintain different invariants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二分查找]]属于有序离散候选的搜索，不是连续求根器；它进入本图是为了说明“折半”共享直觉，却依赖不同不变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bracketed problem, **preserving a root and making progress are separate obligations**. Retaining opposite signs only keeps a root inside; an acceptance rule must also force the uncertainty interval to shrink. The simple rule in [[混合求根|safeguarded hybrid root-finding]] accepts a candidate only in the central part of the bracket and otherwise uses the midpoint. Brent-type interpolation methods use their own safeguards; they do not require a derivative and should not be described as Newton methods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对已经括住根的问题，**保留根与取得进展是两项不同要求**。维持异号只说明根还在里面；接受规则还必须促使不确定区间缩小。[[混合求根]]给出一个简单规则：只接受落在括区间中央部分的候选，否则改用中点。Brent 类插值方法使用自己的保护规则，不需要导数，不能把它们统称为 Newton 方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Let the requested error determine the evidence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 误差目标会改变选择

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the deliverable requires a certified positional error, the [[二分误差界|bracket width]] is more informative than an apparently stable sequence. If only the [[求根残差|residual]] $|f(x)|$ is observed, it needs [[残差控制根误差|conversion conditions such as a derivative lower bound]] before it becomes a positional bound. When function or derivative evaluations are expensive, compare their cost rather than iteration counts alone. When failure is costly, preserving a bracket and an explicit status can matter more than saving one or two evaluations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若必须交付可证的位置误差，[[二分误差界|括区间宽度]]比“看起来稳定”更直接。若只观察 [[求根残差|残差]] $|f(x)|$，还要经 [[残差控制根误差|导数下界等转换条件]] 才能把它翻译成位置误差。导数或函数评价很贵时，应按调用成本比较，而不是只数迭代轮数；失败会造成重大损失时，保留括区间和显式状态通常比追求少一两次评价更重要。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even a mathematically suitable algorithm needs tolerances, units, iteration limits, NaN handling, and overflow handling; these belong to [[数值迭代停止条件|the stopping and failure policy]]. Returning only a floating-point number is an incomplete interface. At minimum, return a termination status and enough evidence to explain the claimed error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即便算法数学上合适，阈值、单位、最大迭代、NaN 和 overflow 仍由 [[数值迭代停止条件]] 统一规定。求根器返回一个浮点数不是完整接口；至少还要返回收敛状态和足以解释误差的证据。

> [!example] One equation, two useful routes · 同一个方程，两种可用路线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Find the positive root of $f(x)=x^2-2$ with positional error at most $10^{-6}$. On $[1,2]$, $f$ is continuous, $f(1)=-1$, and $f(2)=2$. In exact arithmetic, after $n$ completed bisection updates, the new bracket midpoint has error at most $2^{-(n+1)}$. Thus $n=19$ updates suffice, since $2^{-20}\approx9.54\times10^{-7}$. This counts bracket updates, not all evaluations or the initial midpoint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 求 $f(x)=x^2-2$ 的正根，要求位置误差不超过 $10^{-6}$。在 $[1,2]$ 上，$f$ 连续，且 $f(1)=-1$、$f(2)=2$。精确算术中，完成 $n$ 次二分区间更新后，新区间中点的误差至多为 $2^{-(n+1)}$。因此 $n=19$ 次更新就足够，因为 $2^{-20}\approx9.54\times10^{-7}$。这里数的是区间更新次数，并非全部函数评价，也没有把初始中点算作一次更新。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a reliable derivative is available, starting Newton at $x_0=1.5$ gives $x_1=17/12\approx1.41667$ and $x_2=577/408\approx1.41422$. These are promising candidates, but speed alone is not a certificate. On $[1,2]$, $f\prime(x)=2x\ge2$, so a candidate in this interval with a reliably evaluated residual of magnitude at most $2\times10^{-6}$ has positional error at most $10^{-6}$. Alternatively, keep a safeguarded bracket and use its width. The solver may generate candidates one way and justify its answer another way.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若能可靠计算导数，从 $x_0=1.5$ 启动 Newton 会得到 $x_1=17/12\approx1.41667$、$x_2=577/408\approx1.41422$。这些候选很有希望，但速度本身不是误差证书。在 $[1,2]$ 上，$f\prime(x)=2x\ge2$，因此区间内的候选若经可靠评价得到残差绝对值不超过 $2\times10^{-6}$，位置误差就不超过 $10^{-6}$。也可以维持受保护的括区间，直接使用区间宽度。生成候选与证明答案可靠可以采用不同信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual bound above is a mathematical statement about the true function value. If the evaluation is itself approximate, include a bound on its evaluation error before claiming a certified root error. Similarly, rounded midpoints may eventually equal an endpoint, so the exact-arithmetic update count does not remove the need to detect floating-point stagnation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的残差界是关于真实函数值的数学结论。如果函数评价本身存在近似误差，声明严格根误差证书前还要计入该评价误差。同样，舍入后的中点最终可能等于端点，因此精确算术的更新次数并不能代替浮点停滞检测。

> [!warning] A missing sign change is not proof of no root · 没有异号不等于没有根
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $(x-1)^2$ has a root at one but the same positive sign at zero and two. A bracketing solver cannot use these endpoints as a strict sign-change certificate; that says nothing about the root\'s nonexistence. Conversely, $1/x$ has opposite signs at $-1$ and $1$, but is not continuous on the interval and has no zero there. Check the model and domain before choosing the algorithm. A Newton start may still be useful without a bracket, but its local conditions and failure modes then matter explicitly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 函数 $(x-1)^2$ 在1处有根，但在0和2处同为正。括区间求根器不能把这两个端点当作严格异号证书，这不表示根不存在。反过来，$1/x$ 在 $-1$ 和1处异号，却不在整个区间连续，也没有零点。选算法前应先检查模型和定义域。没有括区间时仍可能使用 Newton 初值，但必须明确其局部条件与失败边界。

> [!question] Apply the decision · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> You have a sign-changing interval for a continuous function and can also evaluate its derivative reliably. Why need you not choose between pure bisection and pure Newton?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已找到连续函数的异号区间，同时也能可靠计算导数。为什么不必在“纯二分”与“纯 Newton”之间二选一？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Use the interval to retain existence and location evidence and Newton to propose faster steps; fall back to bisection when a proposal leaves the allowed region or gives insufficient progress. Keep the bracket, and require a progress safeguard as well.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 两类信息可以分工：异号区间保留根存在和位置范围，Newton 候选负责加速；候选越界或缺乏进展时退回二分法。关键是始终维护括区间，而不是无条件接受快速步。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The progress rule is essential: bracket preservation alone does not prove shrinking width or termination.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中进展规则不可省略：仅仅保留括区间，不能证明区间宽度趋零或程序终止。
