# 期初年金比普通年金整体提前一期因而价值乘一加利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An annuity due is one period earlier than an ordinary annuity, so its value is multiplied by one plus the rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子关系
> 期初年金在每期期初付款，普通年金在每期期末付款；同额、同期数且每期有效率同为 $r$ 时，期初年金的每笔现金流都早一期。因此
> $$PV_{due}=PV_{ordinary}(1+r),\qquad FV_{due}=FV_{ordinary}(1+r).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> An annuity due pays at each period-beginning, while an ordinary annuity pays at each period-end. With equal payments, the same number of periods, and effective rate $r$, every due payment occurs one period earlier. Hence
> $$PV_{due}=PV_{ordinary}(1+r),\qquad FV_{due}=FV_{ordinary}(1+r).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘 $(1+r)$ 不是额外奖励，而是整条时间线平移一期的结果。若只提前部分付款、第一笔延后或评价时点改变，就不能对整个普通年金因子统一乘一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying by $(1+r)$ is not a bonus; it follows from shifting the whole timeline one period earlier. If only some payments move, the first payment is deferred, or the valuation date changes, one common multiplier no longer applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么正利率下期初年金的现值与终值都高于同额普通年金？
>
> **答案：** 每笔付款都早一期：折到今天时少折现一期，移到终点时多复利一期，所以两种价值都乘 $(1+r)>1$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why are both PV and FV of an annuity due higher than those of an otherwise identical ordinary annuity when the rate is positive?
>
> **Answer:** Every payment occurs one period earlier: it is discounted one period less to the present and compounded one period more to the endpoint, so both values multiply by $(1+r)>1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
