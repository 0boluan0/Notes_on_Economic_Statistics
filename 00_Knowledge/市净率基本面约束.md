# 在清洁盈余与稳定增长条件下市净率由股权回报率、股权成本和增长率共同决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under clean-surplus and stable-growth assumptions, justified P/B depends jointly on return on equity, the cost of equity and growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $B_0>0$ 是估值日、下一期的期初普通股每股账面权益；下一期EPS为 $E_1=ROE\,B_0$。这里 $ROE$ 特指下一期盈利除以期初权益，不直接套用任意历史或平均权益口径的[[权益净利率]]。股数不变，没有增发、回购或直接计入权益而绕过盈利的项目，故清洁盈余关系为 $B_1=B_0+E_1-D_1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let positive $B_0$ be ordinary book equity per share at valuation, before next-period earnings $E_1=ROE\,B_0$. This ROE uses expected earnings over opening equity, not an arbitrary historical or average-equity [[权益净利率|ROE measure]]. With fixed shares, no issuance or repurchases, and no equity changes bypassing earnings, clean surplus gives $B_1=B_0+E_1-D_1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若[[利润留存率]] $0\le b<1$、正的ROE及股权成本 $k_e$ 恒定，股利为 $D_1=(1-b)E_1$；保留利润使账面权益、盈利和股利同按 $g=bROE$ 增长，且 $k_e>g$。由[[戈登增长模型]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With constant [[利润留存率|retention]] $0\le b<1$, positive ROE and cost of equity, dividends are $(1-b)E_1$. Retention produces common book-equity, earnings and dividend growth $g=bROE$, with $k_e>g$. The [[戈登增长模型|Gordon model]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{P_0}{B_0}
=\frac{(1-b)ROE}{k_e-g}
=\frac{ROE-g}{k_e-g}
=1+\frac{ROE-k_e}{k_e-g}.
$$

例如 $B_0=10$、$ROE=15\%$、$b=40\%$、$k_e=10\%$：$E_1=1.5$，$D_1=0.9$，$B_1=10.6$，$g=6\%$；于是 $P_0=0.9/(10\%-6\%)=22.5$，模型[[市净率]]为2.25倍，而不是把当前账面权益再乘一个多余的 $1+g$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $B_0=10$, ROE 15%, retention 40% and cost of equity 10%, next earnings are 1.5, dividends 0.9 and closing book equity 10.6, implying 6% growth. Value is 22.5 and justified [[市净率|P/B]] is 2.25. No additional $1+g$ belongs here because ROE already uses next-period earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这些条件内，$ROE>k_e$ 对应高于账面的模型价值，$ROE=k_e$ 对应1倍；条件不成立时，不能据历史ROE或一次性盈利机械作此判断。式子没有证明账面权益是清算保证，也没有把所有公司的市场PB变成会计恒等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within these assumptions, ROE above the cost of equity produces a premium to book, and equality gives P/B of one. Historical ROE or one-off earnings cannot justify that conclusion outside the model. Book equity is not a liquidation guarantee, and market P/B is not an accounting identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
