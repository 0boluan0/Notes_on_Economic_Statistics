# 训练后的重叠审计只能发现并评估暴露影响，不能把已训练模型变回未暴露状态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A post-training overlap audit can detect and assess exposure but cannot return a trained model to an unexposed state*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 时间点改变了方法能做什么
> [[基准去污染]]在训练前修改模型将会看到的数据；训练后重叠审计面对的则是已经发生的暴露。审计可以找出可疑重叠、分开报告 clean 与 flagged 样本、下调结论强度或撤回不可信结果，却不能撤销已经写入参数更新的训练经历。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[基准去污染|Benchmark decontamination]] changes what a model will see before training. A post-training overlap audit examines exposure that has already occurred. It can identify suspicious overlap, report clean and flagged subsets separately, weaken claims, or withdraw unreliable results, but it cannot undo training updates caused by prior exposure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同一个 detector，两个不同动作
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The same detector can support two different actions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 时间点 | detector 命中后可以做什么 | 模型的暴露状态 |
|---|---|---|
| 训练前 | 从候选训练数据中删除或隔离命中内容，再开始训练 | 命中内容可以不进入训练 |
| 训练后 | 标记评测样本、比较 clean/flagged 结果、解释或停用受影响分数 | 已发生的暴露保持不变 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before training, detector matches can be removed or quarantined from candidate training data, preventing that exposure. After training, the same kind of detector can label evaluation items and support stratified reporting, reinterpretation, or result withdrawal, but the model's exposure history remains unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

删掉训练语料中的原文件也不能倒放优化过程。一次暴露怎样影响参数，取决于它出现时的模型状态、batch、学习率和随后的更新；事后没有一个局部“删除键”能恢复未暴露的反事实模型。如果目标是得到真正未见过这些内容的模型，通常需要从暴露前的状态用修正后的数据重训，而不是把一次审计称作追溯性去污染。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deleting the source file after training does not reverse optimization. The parameter effect of an exposure depends on the model state, batch, learning rate, and later updates, so there is no local deletion operation that recovers the counterfactual unexposed model. Producing such a model generally requires retraining from a pre-exposure state on corrected data rather than relabelling an audit as retroactive decontamination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 训练后审计仍然有明确用途
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A post-training audit still has a clear use*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次可信的审计至少应报告 detector、阈值、匹配对象、可检索训练语料的覆盖范围、clean/flagged 样本量及各自结果，并说明改写、翻译、答案单独暴露等漏检风险。它回答“现有分数还能怎样解释”，而不是假装暴露没有发生。检测到多少重叠也不能直接换算为得分被抬高多少；这条测量边界见 [[污染标记率与得分增益]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credible post-training audit should report the detector, threshold, matched objects, searchable-corpus coverage, clean and flagged sample counts and results, and blind spots such as paraphrase, translation, or answer-only exposure. It asks how the existing score can still be interpreted rather than pretending exposure never occurred. The amount of flagged overlap also cannot be converted directly into score inflation; see [[污染标记率与得分增益]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] Brown et al. 的过滤 bug
> GPT-3 的训练前 benchmark 过滤因 bug 漏掉部分重叠，而重新训练成本过高。Brown et al. 随后构造只保留未标记样本的 clean evaluation subsets，比较原始分数与 clean-subset 分数，并对严重受影响的结果加注或不报告。这是对既有模型的事后审计与结果处置，不是让 GPT-3 重新成为未暴露模型。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A bug in GPT-3's pre-training benchmark filter left some overlap, and retraining was infeasible. Brown et al. then built clean evaluation subsets from unflagged items, compared their scores with full-set scores, and marked or omitted severely affected results. That was post-hoc auditing and result handling for an existing model, not restoration of an unexposed GPT-3.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 模型发布后，团队发现 20% 的评测题与可检索训练语料匹配，并只在剩余 80% 上重算分数。这个模型现在是否已经“去污染”？
>
> **答案：** 没有。新的分数可以帮助评估影响，但模型仍保留原来的训练暴露；若需要未暴露模型，必须用修正后的数据重新训练相应阶段。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Brown et al. (2020), *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165)：第 4 节与附录 C 记录训练前过滤 bug、无法重训后的 clean-subset 分析，以及对严重重叠结果加注或不报告的处置。
- [Singh et al. (2024), *Evaluation data contamination in LLMs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03923)：系统比较训练后污染检测方法和阈值，并把检测结果与被标记样本的表现影响分开评估。
