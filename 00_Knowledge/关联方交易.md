# 关联方交易是报告主体与其关联方之间转移资源、服务或义务的交易
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A related-party transaction transfers resources, services, or obligations between a reporting entity and a related party.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关联方交易的关键是交易双方具有适用规则认定的关联关系，而不是有没有收钱。以 IAS 24 为本页财报语境，控制、共同控制、重大影响及关键管理人员等关系可能使个人或实体成为关联方；同一集团的母子公司和姐妹公司是典型例子。具体关联范围应按所用准则识别，不能仅凭“客户很重要”判定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The defining feature is a related-party relationship under the applicable rules, not whether payment occurs. In this page's IAS 24 reporting context, control, joint control, significant influence, and key-management relationships can establish relatedness; parents, subsidiaries, and fellow subsidiaries are typical examples. Apply the relevant standard rather than treating every important customer as related.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如母公司免费替子公司提供行政服务，虽未收费，仍有服务转移，因而属于关联方交易；若母公司只持有子公司而本期未发生资源、服务或义务转移，则有关系但不据此虚构一笔交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A parent providing administrative services to a subsidiary for free still transfers services and therefore undertakes a related-party transaction. Ownership without a transfer of resources, services, or obligations establishes a relationship, but does not invent a transaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别后还要核对金额、未结余额、条款和担保等；该定义不等于交易不合法或利润已被操纵，见[[关联交易不等于利润操纵]]。分析母公司单独报表和集团合并报表时也应区分：集团内部交易通常在合并时抵销，不能把内部销售重复算成集团对外收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identification is followed by examination of amounts, balances, terms, and guarantees. It does not establish illegality or manipulation; see [[关联交易不等于利润操纵|the inference boundary]]. Distinguish separate from consolidated statements: intragroup transactions are generally eliminated on consolidation, rather than counted again as external group sales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
