# 数据可视化与不确定性表达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Data Visualization and Uncertainty Communication*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 让图形准确回答一个数据问题，并把样本量、分布与不确定性呈现出来。
> **具体锚点：** 两组平均值相同，原始点和区间却可能显示完全不同的离散程度与重叠。
> **核心难点：** 图形编码、坐标范围和聚合会改变读者感知；漂亮不等于忠实。
> **为什么重要：** 图既是探索工具，也是论证的一部分，错误视觉语义会制造不存在的模式。
> **继续：** 先写图要回答的问题，再选择位置、长度或颜色等编码并显示必要的不确定性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Make a figure answer one data question accurately while exposing sample size, distribution, and uncertainty.
> **Concrete anchor:** Two groups can have the same mean while raw observations and intervals reveal very different spread and overlap.
> **Central difficulty:** Visual encodings, axis ranges, and aggregation alter perception; attractiveness is not fidelity.
> **Why it matters:** A plot is both an exploratory tool and part of an argument, so incorrect visual semantics can create a pattern that is not present.
> **Continue with:** State the question first, then choose encodings such as position, length, or color and display the necessary uncertainty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 图形语义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Visual Semantics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间用线、类别比较用点/条、分布用 histogram/ECDF/box/violin、两个连续变量用散点。坐标轴、单位、基线、聚合和样本量必须清楚；双轴和截断轴需谨慎。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use lines for time, points or bars for categories, histograms, ECDFs, boxes, or violins for distributions, and scatterplots for two continuous variables. Axes, units, baselines, aggregation, and sample size must be clear; dual axes and truncated axes require caution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不确定性表达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Communicating Uncertainty*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

显示原始点、区间或分布，不用过度平滑隐藏异质性。置信区间、预测区间和数据范围意义不同，标签要准确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Show raw observations, intervals, or distributions rather than hiding heterogeneity with excessive smoothing. Confidence intervals, prediction intervals, and data ranges mean different things and require accurate labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 解读边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limits of Interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图发现模式不自动给因果。颜色和面积会影响知觉，优先可访问配色、直接标签和最少装饰。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A visual pattern does not automatically establish causality. Color and area affect perception; prefer accessible palettes, direct labels, and minimal decoration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：均值柱状图为何不够
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Why a Bar Chart of Means Is Insufficient*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较两种教学法成绩时，只有均值柱会隐藏样本量、离群点和组内分布。用 jittered raw points 加均值与置信区间，可同时看到中心、不确定性和异质性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When comparing scores under two teaching methods, bars of means hide sample size, outliers, and within-group distributions. Jittered raw points with means and confidence intervals reveal center, uncertainty, and heterogeneity together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目的是预测新学生，还需要 prediction interval；均值的 confidence interval 不能冒充个体结果范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the goal is to predict a new student's score, a prediction interval is needed; a confidence interval for the mean cannot stand in for the range of individual outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 趋势由少数点驱动：显示原始点并做稳健/分层检查，而不是只画拟合线。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A trend is driven by a few points: show raw observations and perform robust or stratified checks instead of displaying only a fitted line.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 图看似差异巨大：检查截断轴、面积编码、不同样本量和聚合粒度。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A figure makes a difference look enormous: inspect truncated axes, area encoding, unequal sample sizes, and aggregation grain.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 区间含义说不清：回到估计对象与抽样/预测问题，重新选择并准确命名区间。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  An interval cannot be explained: return to the estimand and sampling or prediction question, then choose and label the interval accurately.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-Check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么均值柱状图可能掩盖关键信息？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a bar chart of means hide important information?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它不显示样本量、分布形状、离群点和组内异质性，不同数据可产生同一均值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It hides sample size, distribution shape, outliers, and within-group heterogeneity; very different data can have the same mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### confidence interval 与 prediction interval 回答什么不同问题？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How do a confidence interval and a prediction interval answer different questions?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 前者表达总体参数估计不确定性，后者表达新个体结果范围，通常更宽。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The former expresses uncertainty about an estimated population parameter; the latter describes a new individual outcome and is normally wider.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 图中出现相关模式后为何不能直接写因果？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a causal claim not follow directly from a visual association?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 图没有自动排除混杂、反向因果、选择和测量偏差；识别需额外设计与假设。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A plot does not automatically rule out confounding, reverse causality, selection, or measurement bias; identification requires additional design and assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
