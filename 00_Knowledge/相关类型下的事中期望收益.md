# 相关类型下，事中期望收益必须使用给定自身类型的联合条件分布；只有相应独立性成立时才可退化为无条件边际
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With correlated types, interim expected payoff must use the joint distribution conditional on one's own type; it can be replaced by an unconditional marginal only under the corresponding independence condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 决策规则
> 玩家 $i$ 已观察到 $t_i$，所以比较行动时必须使用[[事中信念]] $p_i(d\theta,dt_{-i}\mid t_i)$。若自身类型会改变对 $\theta$ 或 $t_{-i}$ 的判断，对所有 $t_i$ 重复使用同一无条件边际会丢失决策时已经掌握的信息。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Player $i$ has already observed $t_i$, so action comparisons must use the [[事中信念|interim belief]] $p_i(d\theta,dt_{-i}\mid t_i)$. If the own type changes beliefs about $\theta$ or $t_{-i}$, reusing one unconditional marginal for every $t_i$ discards information already available at the time of choice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有限或可数情形
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Finite or Countable Case*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定纯策略组合 $s$，玩家 $i$ 在类型 $t_i$ 下的事中期望收益为

$$
U_i(s\mid t_i)
=
\sum_{(\theta,t_{-i})}
u_i\!\left(\theta,s_i(t_i),s_{-i}(t_{-i})\right)
p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i).
$$

权重必须随 $t_i$ 使用相应的条件分布。[[边际分布不定联合分布|边际概率相同并不足以证明可以忽略条件]]，因为不同联合分布可以具有相同边际。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weights must use the conditional distribution associated with the observed $t_i$. Equal marginals do not justify dropping the conditioning, because different joint distributions can share the same marginals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一般可测空间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*General Measurable Spaces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相应积分形式为

$$
U_i(s\mid t_i)
=
\int_{\Theta\times T_{-i}}
u_i\!\left(\theta,s_i(t_i),s_{-i}(t_{-i})\right)
p_i(d\theta,dt_{-i}\mid t_i),
$$

并要求被积收益对该条件分布可积。若采用随机化策略，还要对行动随机化再取期望，但类型与状态的权重仍来自同一个条件 belief。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integrand must be integrable under this conditional distribution. With randomized strategies, expectation must also cover action randomization, while the weights on types and payoff states still come from the same conditional belief.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 何时可以使用无条件边际
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When an Unconditional Marginal Is Valid*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有在玩家 $i$ 的 belief 下，未知对象 $(\theta,t_{-i})$ 与自身类型 $t_i$ 满足相应独立性时，才有

$$
p_i(d\theta,dt_{-i}\mid t_i)
=
p_i(d\theta,dt_{-i})
$$

并可在事中期望中使用无条件边际。仅有 $t_{-i}$ 与 $t_i$ 独立还不够：若 $\theta$ 仍与 $t_i$ 相关，收益相关状态的条件权重仍会变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An unconditional marginal is valid only when $(\theta,t_{-i})$ is independent of $t_i$ under player $i$'s belief. Independence of $t_{-i}$ and $t_i$ alone is insufficient if the payoff state $\theta$ remains correlated with $t_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一替换还要求模型给出能够定义 $p_i(d\theta,dt)$ 的事前分布。若模型只直接给出各类型的 $p_i(d\theta,dt_{-i}\mid t_i)$，无条件边际并不是可凭这些条件 beliefs 自动恢复的对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This replacement also requires an ex-ante distribution that defines $p_i(d\theta,dt)$. If the model specifies only the type-by-type beliefs $p_i(d\theta,dt_{-i}\mid t_i)$, an unconditional marginal cannot be recovered automatically from those conditional beliefs alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
