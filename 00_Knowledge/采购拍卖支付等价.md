# 在风险中性、独立同分布私人成本、最低成本中标且最高成本类型获得同一期望租金时，一价与二价采购拍卖具有相同的事前期望支付
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With risk-neutral bidders, independent identically distributed private costs, efficient lowest-cost allocation, and the same expected rent for the highest-cost type, first- and second-price procurement auctions have the same ex-ante expected payment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采购拍卖中的支付等价，是标准 revenue-equivalence 逻辑把“最高估值获胜”反向写成“最低成本中标”。它比较的是采购方的**事前期望支付**，不是两种机制中的报价函数、每次实现的付款或中标者利润逐状态相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Payment equivalence in procurement is the standard revenue-equivalence logic with “highest value wins” reversed to “lowest cost wins.” It equates the buyer's **ex-ante expected payment**, not bidding functions, realized payments in every auction, or state-by-state winner profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两种机制为何仍能等价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the Two Formats Can Still Be Equivalent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一价采购拍卖由最低报价者中标，并按自己的报价获得支付；二价采购拍卖仍由最低报价者中标，但按第二低报价获得支付。在对称、单调均衡中，两者都把项目交给最低成本企业。若投标者风险中性、私人成本独立同分布，而且最高成本边界类型在两种机制中获得相同的期望租金，包络逻辑使每个成本类型的期望支付由同一分配概率确定，进而得到相同的事前平均支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In first-price procurement, the lowest bidder wins and is paid its own bid; in second-price procurement, the lowest bidder wins and is paid the second-lowest bid. In symmetric monotone equilibrium, both allocate the project to the lowest-cost firm. With risk-neutral bidders, i.i.d. private costs, and the same expected rent for the highest-cost boundary type, the envelope argument makes each type's expected payment depend on the same allocation probability, yielding the same ex-ante average payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 课程语境中的关键边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Critical Boundary in the Course Setting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/03_game theory/05_贝叶斯博弈#5.2. 政府招标例子|课程的政府招标例子]]先描述“我的成本估计也透露对手可能看到什么”，这会引入[[相关类型|相关信号]]，甚至共同成本成分。这样的信息结构不自动满足独立私人成本条件，所以不能仅凭一价／二价标签推出支付等价；必须另行验证类型独立、私人价值（私人成本）、对称性与风险中性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's government-procurement example first says that a firm's own cost estimate is informative about its rival's estimate, introducing correlated signals and possibly a common-cost component. That information structure does not automatically satisfy independent private costs. Payment equivalence therefore cannot be inferred from the first- versus second-price labels alone; independence, private costs, symmetry, and risk neutrality must be checked separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 常见失效方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Common Failure Directions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险厌恶、相关类型或共同价值、非对称成本分布、保留价、参与约束差异，以及机制不再把项目交给同一成本类型，都可能破坏上述等价。失效后，哪种机制更便宜没有统一方向，需要重新求解相应的[[贝叶斯纳什均衡|BNE]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk aversion, correlated types or common values, asymmetric cost distributions, reserve rules, different participation constraints, or mechanisms that no longer allocate to the same cost type can all break the equivalence. Once it fails, there is no universal ranking of procurement cost; the relevant [[贝叶斯纳什均衡|BNE]] must be solved again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
