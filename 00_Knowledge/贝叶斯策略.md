# 贝叶斯博弈中的纯策略是从自身类型到行动的可测映射，完整计划须覆盖所有可能类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A pure strategy in a Bayesian game is a measurable mapping from the player's own type to an action, and a complete plan covers every possible type*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

玩家行动前会观察自己的类型，因此**贝叶斯纯策略**必须预先回答每个可能的 $t_i$ 到来时采取什么行动：$s_i:T_i\to A_i$。一次实际行动 $s_i(t_i)$ 只是完整策略在已实现类型上的取值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because a player observes their own type before acting, a **pure Bayesian strategy** must prescribe an action for every possible $t_i$: $s_i:T_i\to A_i$. The realized action $s_i(t_i)$ is only one value of the complete strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 纯策略覆盖整个类型空间

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Pure Strategy Covers the Entire Type Space*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对玩家 $i$，纯策略为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For player $i$, a pure strategy is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s_i:T_i\longrightarrow A_i,
\qquad t_i\longmapsto s_i(t_i).
$$

若 $T_i=\{L,H\}$、$A_i=\{A,B\}$，完整纯策略共有四个：

$$
(A,A),\ (A,B),\ (B,A),\ (B,B),
$$

其中两个位置分别规定 $L$ 与 $H$ 类型的行动。只说“玩家选 $A$”没有说明是哪个类型，也没有覆盖另一类型，因此不是完整策略。

当 $T_i$ 不可数时，通常还要求 $s_i$ 可测，使 $s_i(t_i)$ 与收益的期望真正可定义。可测性不是装饰性术语；任意不可测的“映射”可能根本无法赋予行动事件概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $T_i=\{L,H\}$ and $A_i=\{A,B\}$, the four pure strategies are $(A,A)$, $(A,B)$, $(B,A)$, and $(B,B)$, with one component for each type. Saying only that “the player chooses $A$” neither identifies the relevant type nor covers the other type, so it is not a complete strategy. For uncountable type spaces, the map is normally required to be measurable so that induced action events and expected payoffs are well defined. Measurability is not decorative terminology: an arbitrary nonmeasurable “mapping” may not assign probabilities to action events at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它与普通完整计划是一回事

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*It Is an Ordinary Complete Contingent Plan*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[行动与策略]]中的原则没有改变：行动是一个决策点上的选择，策略是覆盖所有自身可能决策情形的计划。贝叶斯博弈只是把“自身类型”加入情形索引。给定所有人的完整策略后，玩家 $i$ 在类型 $t_i$ 下仍须对未知 $(\theta,t_{-i})$ 取期望，才能进入[[贝叶斯纳什均衡]]的最佳反应检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordinary distinction between action and strategy is unchanged: an action is a choice at one decision point, whereas a strategy is a plan for every contingency the player may face. A Bayesian game adds the player's own type to that contingency index. Once every player's complete strategy is fixed, player $i$ at type $t_i$ must still take expectations over the unknown $(\theta,t_{-i})$ before applying the [[贝叶斯纳什均衡|Bayesian Nash equilibrium]] best-response test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 玩家有高、低两种类型，每种类型都可选进入或退出。候选描述只写“低类型进入”。它为什么还不是完整的纯策略？
>
> **答案：** 它漏掉了高类型的行动规定。完整纯策略必须同时说明低类型与高类型分别做什么。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** A player has high and low types, each of which can choose to enter or stay out. A candidate description says only “the low type enters.” Why is this not yet a complete pure strategy?
>
> **Answer:** It omits the prescribed action for the high type. A complete pure strategy must specify what both the low type and the high type do.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
