# 规模经济是给定技术和投入价格下长期平均成本随产量增加而下降的现象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Economies of scale occur when long-run average cost falls with output at given technology and input prices.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规模经济指在某一产量区间内，企业能调整全部投入并选择成本较低的生产方式时，单位产出的成本随产量扩大而下降。若长期成本为 $C(q)$，$q>0$，就看 $C(q)/q$ 在所讨论区间是否下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Economies of scale occur over an output range when all inputs can be adjusted and cost-minimizing production choices lead unit cost to fall as output rises. For long-run cost $C(q)$, $q>0$, examine whether $C(q)/q$ falls over the stated range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，给定技术、投入价格与产品质量，最小成本在产量 100 时为 1000、产量 200 时为 1600，则平均成本由 10 降至 8，说明这两个规模之间存在平均成本下降。若只是同一固定厂房短期多开工、原料降价或技术变好，就不能不加区分地把观察到的成本下降都归为长期规模经济。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At fixed technology, input prices, and quality, minimum costs of 1,000 for 100 units and 1,600 for 200 units imply average costs of 10 and 8. This establishes a cost reduction between those scales. Greater short-run utilization of a fixed plant, cheaper inputs, or improved technology should not automatically be attributed to long-run scale economies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规模经济可能形成[[进入壁垒]]，但不必无限持续；规模扩大也可能引起协调成本。重复经验带来的变化见[[学习效应]]，金融领域具体机制见[[金融交易成本的规模经济]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Scale economies may create [[进入壁垒|entry barriers]], but need not continue indefinitely; expansion can also raise coordination costs. See [[学习效应|learning effects]] for accumulated experience and [[金融交易成本的规模经济|financial transaction-cost scale economies]] for a specific application.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
