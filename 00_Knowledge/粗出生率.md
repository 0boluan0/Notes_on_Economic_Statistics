# 粗出生率是一定时期的活产数除以同期总人口人年并按每千人年表示的出生频率

粗出生率以全体人口而非仅育龄女性作为暴露分母，衡量一个人口中活产事件的总体频率。“粗”表示尚未剔除年龄、性别构成的影响；它不能直接解释成每位女性平均生育多少孩子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The crude birth rate measures the overall frequency of live births using exposure for the entire population, not just women of reproductive age. “Crude” means that age and sex composition have not been standardized. It is not the average number of children per woman.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若期间活产数为 $B$，同期全体人口暴露为 $PY$ 人年，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $B$ live births and total population exposure of $PY$ person-years during the interval,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
CBR=1000\frac{B}{PY}.
$$

例如一年内 20 个活产、总暴露 1,000 人年，粗出生率是每千人年 20 个活产。年度数据有时用年中人口近似该年人年；时间跨度不是一年时，不能省略暴露时间而直接拿某时点人数作分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Twenty live births over 1,000 person-years give 20 live births per 1,000 person-years. Annual statistics may approximate person-years by the mid-year population. For a different interval length, exposure time cannot be discarded by substituting a population snapshot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在年龄别生育率相同时，育龄女性在人口中的比重不同也会改变粗出生率。比较生育强度应进一步看[[总和生育率]]及其年龄范围；用出生数解释人口增减则回到[[人口变动恒等式]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even with identical age-specific fertility rates, different shares of women of reproductive age can change the crude birth rate. To compare fertility intensity, inspect the [[总和生育率|total fertility rate]] and its age coverage. To explain population change using birth counts, return to the [[人口变动恒等式|population balancing equation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The WPP2024 glossary defines live births per population exposure and age-specific fertility. The composition effect, conditions for the annual approximation and example follow from these denominator definitions. WPP population exposure should not be replaced directly by the mid-year population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
