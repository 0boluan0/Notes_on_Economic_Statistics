# 给未来社会效益贴现需要说明跨代消费权重而不能只诉诸融资利率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discounting future social benefits requires explicit intergenerational consumption weights, not just a borrowing rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代际评价使用[[社会贴现率]]时，必须说明为什么未来的消费或损害被赋予该权重。[[社会时间偏好率]]中的纯时间权重，与未来消费增长带来的边际效用变化不是同一回事：正贴现率不自动意味着未来的人本身具有更低道德地位，政府融资利率也不能代替这种伦理与经济判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intergenerational appraisal must explain the weights attached to future consumption or harm when using a [[社会贴现率|social discount rate]]. Pure time weighting in [[社会时间偏好率|social time preference]] differs from the marginal-utility effect of consumption growth. A positive rate does not automatically assign lower moral status to future people, and a borrowing rate cannot replace these ethical and economic judgements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期结果对选率很敏感。仅为比较同一笔实际货币等价值，100年后的100元在恒定3%下现值约5.20元，在恒定1%下约36.97元。算术没有决定哪个率应采用；应把消费增长、纯时间权重与风险假设分开展示，沿用[[敏感性、情景与模拟]]进行透明比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-horizon results are rate-sensitive. For the same real monetary equivalent of 100 in 100 years, present value is about 5.20 at a constant 3% and 36.97 at 1%. Arithmetic does not choose the appropriate rate. Display consumption-growth, pure-time-weight and risk assumptions separately, reusing [[敏感性、情景与模拟|sensitivity, scenario and simulation analysis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不可逆环境变化或难以替代的生命健康影响，还要求检查货币等价值是否适切；不能先假设任何未来损害都能用更多普通消费完全抵消，再把这一假设藏进一个贴现率。英国2026长期指南要求特定长期评价额外比较剔除纯时间偏好的结果，这是明确揭示规范假设的政策做法，不是所有社会评价的一条无条件选率定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Irreversible environmental changes or poorly substitutable life and health effects also require checking whether the monetary equivalent is appropriate. Do not assume every future harm is fully offset by more ordinary consumption and conceal that assumption inside a rate. The UK's 2026 long-horizon guidance additionally compares results excluding pure time preference in specified appraisals: a policy practice exposing normative assumptions, not an unconditional universal rate-selection theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
