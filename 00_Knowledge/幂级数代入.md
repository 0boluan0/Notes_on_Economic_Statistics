# Substitution in power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*幂级数代入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution reuses a known series by giving it a new input. If a formula expands a function of $u$, replace **every occurrence of $u$** by the same expression in $x$. The permitted inputs must change with it: the substituted value must land where the original series represents the original function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代入是把已知级数接到一个新输入上。如果公式展开的是关于 $u$ 的函数，就把其中**每一个 $u$** 换成同一个关于 $x$ 的表达式；允许的输入也随之变化，因为代入值必须落在原级数确实表示原函数的地方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Change both the expression and its domain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*表达式和有效范围一起改变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the [[几何级数|geometric-series identity]] $1/(1-u)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^n$ for $|u|<1$. To expand $1/(1-4x^2)$, choose $u=4x^2$. The first terms and the validity condition become
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从[[几何级数]]恒等式 $1/(1-u)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^n$、$|u|<1$ 出发。要展开 $1/(1-4x^2)$，取 $u=4x^2$，得到

$$
\frac1{1-4x^2}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(4x^2)^n
=1+4x^2+16x^4+64x^6+\cdots,
\qquad |4x^2|<1\iff |x|<\frac12.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=1/4$, the ratio is $u=1/4$ and the sum is $4/3$. At $x=\pm1/2$, every series term equals one, so the series diverges. At $x=1$, the rational expression still has the finite value $-1/3$, but the terms $4^n$ do not tend to zero. A function being defined at an input does not make this particular series expansion valid there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x=1/4$ 处，公比为 $u=1/4$，和是 $4/3$；在 $x=\pm1/2$ 处，每项都是一，级数发散。在 $x=1$ 处，有理式仍有有限值 $-1/3$，但级数通项 $4^n$ 不趋零。函数在某点有定义，不代表这一份级数展开也在那里有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why substitution itself is legitimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*代入本身为什么成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $F(u)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_nu^n$ is a known [[幂级数|power-series representation]], with finite real coefficients and [[幂级数收敛半径|radius]] $0<R\le\infty$. Let $g(x)$ be a finite real-valued expression wherever it is defined. For each fixed allowed $x$, set $u_0=g(x)$. If $|u_0|<R$, the original identity already says that the finite partial sums $\sum_{n=0}^N c_nu_0^n$ tend to $F(u_0)$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $F(u)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_nu^n$ 是已知的[[幂级数]]表示，系数为有限实数，[[幂级数收敛半径|半径]]为 $0<R\le\infty$。设 $g(x)$ 在其定义域内取有限实数值。固定一个允许的 $x$，记 $u_0=g(x)$。若 $|u_0|<R$，原恒等式已经说明有限部分和 $\sum_{n=0}^N c_nu_0^n$ 趋于 $F(u_0)$，因此

$$F(g(x))=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n[g(x)]^n\qquad\text{when }|g(x)|<R.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ is the nonnegative integer summation index; $x$ is held fixed while $N$ grows. No terms have been reordered, and no derivative or integral has been exchanged with a limit. If equality with $F$ is independently known at an original boundary value, substitution is also valid at inputs mapped to that value. The interior formula alone grants no such boundary identity; use [[幂级数端点判别|endpoint analysis]] when needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $n$ 是非负整数求和索引；令 $N$ 增大时，$x$ 始终固定。这个操作没有改变项的顺序，也没有把求导或积分与极限交换。若另已证明原级数在某个边界值处也等于 $F$，映到该值的输入也可代入；仅凭内部公式不能自动获得这种边界恒等式，需要时查[[幂级数端点判别]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the result is another power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*何时结果仍是幂级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $g(x)=\lambda(x-b)^m$, with nonzero real $\lambda$, real centre $b$, and positive integer $m$, substitution gives integer powers of $x-b$ directly:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $g(x)=\lambda(x-b)^m$，其中实数 $\lambda\ne0$、中心 $b$ 为实数、$m$ 为正整数，代入后直接得到 $x-b$ 的整数次幂：

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n\lambda^n(x-b)^{mn},\qquad
|\lambda|\,|x-b|^m<R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient at degree $mn$ is $c_n\lambda^n$; all degrees not divisible by $m$ have coefficient zero. Thus the new radius is $r=(R/|\lambda|)^{1/m}$ when $R$ is finite and positive, and $r=\infty$ when $R=\infty$. To see that this is the exact radius, points inside give original inputs with $|u|<R$, while points outside give $|u|>R$, where the original series diverges. Inserting the zero coefficients does not change convergence: the partial sum through degree $K$ is the original substituted partial sum through index $\lfloor K/m\rfloor$, the greatest integer at most $K/m$. Boundary inputs still need separate examination. If $\lambda=0$, the expression is simply the constant $F(0)=c_0$, so no division by $|\lambda|$ is appropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

次数 $mn$ 的系数是 $c_n\lambda^n$，不能被 $m$ 整除的次数系数都是零。因此，有限正半径变为 $r=(R/|\lambda|)^{1/m}$；若 $R=\infty$，则 $r=\infty$。这是精确半径：内部映到 $|u|<R$，外部映到 $|u|>R$，而原级数在后者发散。补入零系数不会改变收敛性，因为截止次数 $K$ 的部分和，就是原代入级数截止索引 $\lfloor K/m\rfloor$ 的部分和；该符号表示不超过 $K/m$ 的最大整数。边界输入仍需单独检查。若 $\lambda=0$，结果只是常数 $F(0)=c_0$，不应再除以 $|\lambda|$。

> [!warning] A substituted series need not yet be a power series / 代入式未必已是幂级数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a general $g$, the terms $c_n[g(x)]^n$ need not be constant coefficients times integer powers of a single $x-b$. For example, $\sum(\sin x)^n=1/(1-\sin x)$ when $|\sin x|<1$ is a valid substituted series, but is not written as a power series in $x$. Expanding each inner expression and collecting infinitely many contributions is an additional operation that needs its own justification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一般的 $g$ 未必使 $c_n[g(x)]^n$ 成为固定系数乘上同一个 $x-b$ 的整数次幂。例如，当 $|\sin x|<1$ 时，$\sum(\sin x)^n=1/(1-\sin x)$ 是有效代入式，却还没有写成关于 $x$ 的幂级数。继续展开内部表达式、收集无穷多项，是另外一个需要依据的操作。

## An exponential becomes a Gaussian
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从指数函数得到高斯函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[指数函数幂级数|exponential series]] represents $e^u$ for every real $u$, with coefficient $1/n!$; $n!$ is the factorial and $0!=1$. Substituting $u=-t^2$ transforms the entire power, so $(-t^2)^n=(-1)^nt^{2n}$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[指数函数幂级数]]用系数 $1/n!$ 在所有实数 $u$ 上表示 $e^u$；$n!$ 是阶乘，约定 $0!=1$。代入 $u=-t^2$ 时要替换整个幂，因此 $(-t^2)^n=(-1)^nt^{2n}$：

$$e^{-t^2}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nt^{2n}}{n!}
=1-t^2+\frac{t^4}{2!}-\frac{t^6}{3!}+\cdots,\qquad t\in\mathbb R.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All powers are even and the signs alternate by index $n$. The new radius remains infinite because every finite $t$ gives a finite allowed input $-t^2$. Integrating this expansion to obtain the [[误差函数幂级数|error-function series]] is a further step governed by [[幂级数逐项积分|term-by-term integration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有幂次都是偶数，正负号按索引 $n$ 交替。每个有限 $t$ 都给出允许的有限输入 $-t^2$，所以新半径仍为无穷。将它积分得到[[误差函数幂级数]]，还要使用[[幂级数逐项积分]]的依据。
