# 向已知幂级数代入新表达式时须同步代入通项并重算收敛条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Substituting into a power series requires transforming both its general term and its convergence condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $F(u)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_nu^n$ 在 $|u|<R$ 成立，代入 $u=g(x)$ 后，在 $|g(x)|<R$ 的输入上有 $F(g(x))=\sum c_n[g(x)]^n$。先代通项，再简化幂次与符号，同时把有效条件改写为关于 $x$ 的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $F(u)=\sum c_nu^n$ holds for $|u|<R$, substituting $u=g(x)$ gives $F(g(x))=\sum c_n[g(x)]^n$ where $|g(x)|<R$. Transform the general term before simplifying, and translate the validity condition into one on $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程中常用 $g(x)=\lambda(x-b)^m$，其中 $\lambda\ne0$、$m$ 为正整数。所得式已按 $x-b$ 的整数次幂排列：$\sum c_n\lambda^n(x-b)^{mn}$，半径由 $|\lambda||x-b|^m<R$ 得到。任意函数 $g$ 的代入式未必已经是关于 $x$ 的幂级数；若还要展开并合并无穷项，须另有相应收敛依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the common substitution $g(x)=\lambda(x-b)^m$ with nonzero $\lambda$ and positive integer $m$, the result is already a power series in $x-b$. Its radius follows from $|\lambda||x-b|^m<R$. Substitution of an arbitrary function need not produce a power series in $x$; further infinite expansion and regrouping need justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 [[指数函数幂级数]] 代入 $u=-t^2$，得到
$$
e^{-t^2}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nt^{2n}}{n!}
=1-t^2+\frac{t^4}{2!}-\frac{t^6}{3!}+\cdots,
\qquad R=\infty.
$$
由几何级数代入 $u=4x^2$，则得到 $1/(1-4x^2)=\sum4^nx^{2n}$，条件为 $|x|<1/2$；原半径 $1$ 不应原样保留。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substituting $u=-t^2$ into the [[指数函数幂级数|exponential series]] gives the displayed alternating even-power expansion with infinite radius. In the geometric series, substituting $u=4x^2$ instead gives $\sum4^nx^{2n}$ for $|x|<1/2$, so the original radius one must change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
