# 数字签名的来源认证依赖公钥与身份的可信绑定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Digital-signature origin authentication depends on a trusted binding between a public key and an identity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 数学验证成功首先说明签名、消息与输入的公钥相匹配。要进一步说“这是某个人或机构签的”，验证者还必须确认公钥有效，并通过可信机制把这把公钥及其私钥持有者绑定到目标身份。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Successful mathematical verification first shows that the signature, message, and supplied public key match. To go further and say that a particular person or organisation signed, the verifier must establish that the public key is valid and bind it and its private-key holder to the intended identity through a trusted mechanism.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

攻击者可以生成自己的密钥对、替换软件和签名，再把攻击者公钥冒充为发布者公钥。此时签名验证仍可能成功；失败发生在密钥分发和身份绑定，而不是签名算法。证书链、带外核对指纹、预装信任根或首次使用后固定密钥，都是不同的绑定制度，各自带有不能被“signature valid”隐藏的信任假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An attacker can generate a key pair, replace the software and signature, and present the attacker's public key as the publisher's. Signature verification may still succeed; the failure is in key distribution and identity binding, not in the signature algorithm. Certificate chains, out-of-band fingerprint checks, pre-installed trust roots, and trust-on-first-use with later pinning are different binding regimes with assumptions that “signature valid” must not hide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，验证界面最好把两个问题分开：签名是否与这把公钥匹配，以及这把公钥为什么值得作为目标身份的公钥信任。后者应能追溯到具体证书路径、指纹渠道或预配置信任锚。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A verification interface should therefore separate two questions: whether the signature matches this public key, and why this key is trusted as the key of the intended identity. The second answer should be traceable to a concrete certificate path, fingerprint channel, or preconfigured trust anchor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 验证器显示“signature valid”，为什么还要核对签名公钥的指纹或证书链？
>
> **答案：** “valid”只证明签名与输入的那把公钥匹配；指纹、证书链或另一可信渠道才把这把公钥接到预期发布者身份上。

## 来源与核验

- [NIST SP 800-89, *Recommendation for Obtaining Assurances for Digital Signature Applications*](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/89/final)：核对有效公钥、私钥持有和密钥对所有者身份是彼此不同的 assurance。
- [RFC 5280, Sections 3.1 and 6, *Internet X.509 PKI Certificate and CRL Profile*](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc5280)：核对证书把公钥值绑定到 subject，而路径验证以信任锚为基础验证名称与目标公钥的绑定。
- [MIT Missing Semester, “Key distribution”](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/2020/security/#key-distribution)：支持把公钥映射到现实身份是非对称密码系统的独立难题。
