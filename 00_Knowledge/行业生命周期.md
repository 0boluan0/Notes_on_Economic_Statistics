# 行业生命周期是以需求增长和竞争盈利变化描述行业演化阶段的框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The industry life cycle is a framework for describing industry evolution through changes in demand growth, competition, and profitability.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业生命周期用一组阶段组织行业从形成到扩张、成熟及可能衰退的变化。课程采用初创、成长、成熟、衰退四阶段；其他教材可另列洗牌期。阶段划分是分析框架，不是每个行业必经且长度固定的自然定律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The industry life cycle organizes the emergence, expansion, maturation, and possible decline of an industry. The course uses four stages: introduction, growth, maturity, and decline; other texts distinguish a shakeout stage. These are analytical categories, not inevitable stages of fixed duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，新服务从少数试用者扩展到大众采用，随后转向替换需求，可用初创—成长—成熟来描述；技术替代也可能让它跳过长期成熟期。原课生命周期图的纵轴是产量，不是股价。具体阶段证据见[[行业生命周期判断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A service may move from a few trial users to broad adoption and then replacement demand, resembling introduction, growth, and maturity. Technological substitution may instead bypass a long mature phase. The course diagram plots output, not stock prices. See [[行业生命周期判断|assessing life-cycle stages]] for the required evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
