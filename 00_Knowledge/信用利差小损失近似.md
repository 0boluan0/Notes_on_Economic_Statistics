# 到期回收模型的年化连续复利信用利差等于负对数损失因子除以期限且小损失时近似期间预期损失率除以期限

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under terminal recovery, the annual continuously compounded credit spread is the negative log loss factor divided by maturity, approximated for small losses by horizon expected loss divided by maturity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用[[单期信用债定价]]的全部条件，令 $x=q^{\mathbb Q}(1-R)=q^{\mathbb Q}LGD$ 且 $0\le x<1$，$T>0$ 以年计。把同期限无风险零息率与债券承诺收益率都写成年化连续复利率 $r,y$，则：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain all assumptions of [[单期信用债定价|one-period terminal-recovery pricing]]. Let $x=q^{\mathbb Q}(1-R)=q^{\mathbb Q}LGD$ with $0\le x<1$, and measure $T>0$ in years. Express both the matched risk-free zero rate and the bond's promised yield as annual continuously compounded rates $r,y$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=Fe^{-rT}(1-x)=Fe^{-yT}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s=y-r=-\frac{\ln(1-x)}{T}.$$

因为 $-\ln(1-x)=x+O(x^2)$，小损失因子 $x$ 下才有 $s\approx q^{\mathbb Q}LGD/T$。$q^{\mathbb Q}$ 是整个 $T$ 年的累计概率，不是年化强度。两年累计概率 2%、损失率 60% 时，$x=0.012$，精确年化连续利差约 60.363 基点，近似为 60 基点；直接写 120 基点会漏掉期限换算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $-\ln(1-x)=x+O(x^2)$, the approximation $s\approx q^{\mathbb Q}LGD/T$ applies when the loss factor $x$ is small. Here $q^{\mathbb Q}$ is cumulative over $T$ years, not an annual intensity. A two-year cumulative probability of 2% with 60% severity gives $x=0.012$: the exact annual continuous spread is about 60.363 basis points, versus the 60-basis-point approximation. Using 120 basis points would omit the maturity conversion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计息口径也不能省略。若改用整个 $T$ 年的一期间有效无风险率 $i$ 和承诺收益率 $j$，则 $P=F(1-x)/(1+i)=F/(1+j)$，因此：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compounding matters too. For effective returns $i$ and $j$ over the entire $T$-year period, $P=F(1-x)/(1+i)=F/(1+j)$, so:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$j-i=\frac{(1+i)x}{1-x}.$$

这不是年化连续利差；小 $x$ 时先近似为 $(1+i)x$，还需 $i$ 足够小才能再简化为 $x$。$q^{\mathbb Q}$、年化[[违约强度模型|违约强度]]与真实世界 PD 也不能交换符号。$x=1$ 时价格为零，有限的承诺收益率不存在，不能继续使用上述对数式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not the annual continuous spread. For small $x$ it is approximately $(1+i)x$; replacing that by $x$ also needs a sufficiently small $i$. Cumulative $q^{\mathbb Q}$, annual [[违约强度模型|default intensity]], and real-world PD are not interchangeable. When $x=1$, price is zero and no finite promised yield exists, so the logarithmic expression is outside its domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
