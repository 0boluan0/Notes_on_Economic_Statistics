# 在Romer的对称生产结构中，更多品种使相同资本总量产生更多产出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In Romer's symmetric production structure, more input varieties raise output from a fixed total quantity of capital.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Romer品种扩张模型]]用不同的专门机器承担不同生产用途。各品种相同、数量都为 $x$ 时，设计范围 $A$ 越大，同一资本总量可以分布到更多用途上。产出增加依赖模型设定：不同品种并非完全替代品，每个品种内部的投入又有递减的边际贡献。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Romer品种扩张模型|Romer expanding-variety model]] assigns distinct productive uses to specialized machines. With symmetric varieties supplied in quantity $x$, a larger design range $A$ spreads the same capital across more uses. The gain depends on the assumed technology: varieties are not perfect substitutes, and each variety has diminishing marginal contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta\in(0,1)$，其中 $\alpha,\beta>0$；$H_Y$ 为最终品人力资本，$L$ 为劳动力，$\eta>0$ 为每单位耐用品的资源成本。由 $K=\eta Ax$ 得 $x=K/(\eta A)$，代回生产函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta\in(0,1)$, with positive $\alpha,\beta$. Let $H_Y$ be production human capital, $L$ labor and $\eta$ the resource cost of each durable unit. Since $K=\eta Ax$, substitution of $x=K/(\eta A)$ gives the following aggregate expression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=H_Y^\alpha L^\beta A\left(\frac K{\eta A}\right)^\gamma
=\eta^{-\gamma}H_Y^\alpha L^\beta A^{1-\gamma}K^\gamma.
$$

固定 $K,H_Y,L$ 时，$A$ 翻倍使 $Y$ 乘以 $2^{1-\gamma}$，虽然每个品种的数量减半。增加品种分散了在原有单一用途上继续堆机器的递减损失。设计数量已经给定时，这个计算只说明怎样使用可用生产机会；它没有免除创造新增设计的 [[Romer研发人力配置|研发机会成本]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding $K,H_Y,L$ fixed, doubling $A$ multiplies output by $2^{1-\gamma}$ even though each variety's quantity halves. The additional uses reduce the diminishing returns from concentrating more machines in existing uses. This comparison takes available designs as given; creating the additional designs still incurs the [[Romer研发人力配置|opportunity cost of research]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可手算的教学例子是 $\gamma=1/2$、$H_Y^\alpha L^\beta=1$、$K/\eta=4$：只有一种机器时 $x=4$、$Y=\sqrt4=2$；有四种机器且均匀配置时，每种 $x=1$、$Y=4\sqrt1=4$。机器总单位数都是4，产出却翻倍。这里比较的是四种专门用途，不是把同一种机器改贴四个标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a constructed example, take $\gamma=1/2$, $H_Y^\alpha L^\beta=1$ and $K/\eta=4$. One variety receives four units and produces $\sqrt4=2$; four symmetric varieties receive one unit each and produce $4\sqrt1=4$. Total machine units remain four. These are distinct uses in the assumed technology, not four labels on the same machine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要区分两种比较：**固定 $K$** 时 $Y\propto A^{1-\gamma}$；[[Romer平衡增长率|平衡增长路径]]上每种数量 $x$ 不变、$A$ 与 $K$ 一起增长，才有 $Y\propto A$。而固定 $A$ 时仍有 $Y\propto K^\gamma$，物质资本的递减收益没有消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish a fixed-capital comparison, where $Y\propto A^{1-\gamma}$, from the [[Romer平衡增长率|balanced growth path]], where each variety's quantity remains constant while $A$ and $K$ grow together, giving $Y\propto A$. With $A$ fixed, output still varies as $K^\gamma$: physical capital retains diminishing returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
