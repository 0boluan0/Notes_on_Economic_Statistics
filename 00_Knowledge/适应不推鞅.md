# Adaptedness and the martingale property
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*适应性与鞅性质*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing a process's current value does not tell us whether its future is conditionally fair. A clock reading $X_n=n$ is completely known at time $n$, but the next reading is certainly $n+1$, not $n$. This simple example is [[适应过程|adapted]] to any chosen filtration and has a finite expected absolute value at every time, yet it is not a [[鞅定义|martingale]]. Adaptedness is an observability condition; the martingale property also constrains conditional averages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道一个过程现在的值，并不能说明它的未来在条件平均意义下是否公平。时钟读数 $X_n=n$ 在时刻 $n$ 完全已知，但下一次读数必然是 $n+1$，而不是 $n$。这个简单例子相对于任何选定的滤过都[[适应过程|适应]]，每个时刻的绝对值期望也有限，却不是[[鞅定义|鞅]]。适应性是可观测条件；鞅性质还要约束条件平均。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ records the information available by each time. For a real process $X=(X_n)$, adaptedness means that $X_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable: that information determines the current value. To be a martingale, every $X_n$ must also be integrable, meaning $E|X_n|<\infty$, and its next value must satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ 记录每个时刻已经掌握的信息。对于实值过程 $X=(X_n)$，适应性表示 $X_n$ 对 $\mathcal F_n$ 可测，即这些信息足以确定当前值。要成为鞅，还要求每个 $X_n$ 可积，即 $E|X_n|<\infty$，并且下一期取值满足

$$
E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n\quad\text{almost surely, for every }n\ge0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] on the left averages over what is still unknown while retaining the current information. “Almost surely” permits exceptions with probability zero. In discrete time, these one-step equalities imply the equalities for all later times by the [[塔式法则|tower property]]. Adaptedness alone supplies neither integrability nor the displayed equality; the examples here even satisfy integrability, so their failure lies in the equality itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边的[[条件期望]]保留当前已知信息，只对仍未知的部分平均。“几乎必然”允许概率为零的例外。在离散时间中，由[[塔式法则]]，这些一步等式可推出任意更晚时刻的等式。适应性本身既不保证可积，也不保证上式；这里的反例甚至都满足可积性，失败的恰好是条件平均等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reveal the next coin early
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 提前知道下一步的反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take independent fair coin results $\xi_k\in\{-1,1\}$, each sign having probability $1/2$. Let $S_0=0$ and $S_n=\xi_1+\cdots+\xi_n$ be the accumulated gain after $n$ rounds. With the [[自然滤过|natural filtration]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$, only the first $n$ results are known; $\mathcal F_0$ contains no coin results. Then $S$ is adapted and $|S_n|\le n$, so each value is integrable. Independence and the next coin's zero mean give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取相互独立的公平硬币结果 $\xi_k\in\{-1,1\}$，两种符号的概率各为 $1/2$。令 $S_0=0$、$S_n=\xi_1+\cdots+\xi_n$ 为前 $n$ 轮累计收益。使用[[自然滤过]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$ 时，只知道前 $n$ 次结果；$\mathcal F_0$ 尚不包含硬币结果。此时 $S$ 适应，且 $|S_n|\le n$，所以每期可积。利用独立性和下一枚硬币的零均值，得到

$$
E[S_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]
=S_n+E[\xi_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]
=S_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now keep exactly the same random process, but give the observer one extra result in advance. Write $\mathcal G_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_{n+1})$. This is another filtration: it also accumulates information, and $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal G_n$ at every time. Already at time $0$, $\mathcal G_0$ reveals $\xi_1$. The current total $S_n$ remains observable and integrable. However, the entire next value $S_{n+1}$ is now known, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在保留完全相同的随机过程，却让观察者提前多知道一次结果。记 $\mathcal G_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_{n+1})$。这也是滤过：信息同样逐步累积，而且每个时刻都有 $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal G_n$。时刻 $0$ 的 $\mathcal G_0$ 就已经揭示 $\xi_1$。当前累计值 $S_n$ 仍然可观测且可积，但整个下一期取值 $S_{n+1}$ 也已知，所以

$$
E[S_{n+1}\mid\mathcal G_n]=S_{n+1}=S_n+\xi_{n+1}\ne S_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inequality holds on every outcome in this coin model because $\xi_{n+1}$ is always $1$ or $-1$. Thus the same $S$ is a martingale for $(\mathcal F_n)$ and is not a martingale for $(\mathcal G_n)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这个硬币模型中，$\xi_{n+1}$ 总为 $1$ 或 $-1$，因此不等式在每个结果上都成立。同一个 $S$ 相对于 $(\mathcal F_n)$ 是鞅，相对于 $(\mathcal G_n)$ 却不是。

> [!example] The conditional average changes · 条件平均怎样改变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After a first win, $S_1=1$. Under $\mathcal F_1$, the second total is $0$ or $2$, with conditional probability $1/2$ each, so its conditional mean is $1$. Under $\mathcal G_1$, the observer also knows the second coin: the conditional mean is already $0$ on a loss or $2$ on a win. Neither equals the current total $1$. No probabilities or trajectories were changed; only the information used for conditioning changed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一轮赢了以后，$S_1=1$。在 $\mathcal F_1$ 下，第二轮累计值是 $0$ 或 $2$，条件概率各为 $1/2$，所以条件均值为 $1$。在 $\mathcal G_1$ 下，观察者还知道第二枚硬币：若输，条件均值已经是 $0$；若赢，已经是 $2$，两者都不等于当前累计值 $1$。概率和路径没有改变，改变的只是求条件平均时使用的信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mechanism does not require identical distributions or coin-valued increments. It also works for independent integrable $\xi_k$ with $E\xi_k=0$ and $P(\xi_k\ne0)>0$ for every $k$. Finite sums remain integrable since $E|S_n|\le\sum_{k=1}^nE|\xi_k|<\infty$. The same two conditional-expectation calculations hold, and the enlarged-information equality fails with positive probability. Independence is a sufficient way to construct this example, not a general requirement in the definition of a martingale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一机制不要求增量同分布，也不要求只能取硬币的两个值。只要 $\xi_k$ 相互独立、可积，并且对每个 $k$ 都有 $E\xi_k=0$、$P(\xi_k\ne0)>0$，同样成立。有限和仍可积，因为 $E|S_n|\le\sum_{k=1}^nE|\xi_k|<\infty$。两种条件期望的计算不变，而扩大信息后的鞅等式以正概率失败。独立性是构造本例的充分方法，并不是鞅定义的一般要求。

## What survives when information grows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 这个反例划出的边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal G_n$, every current value measurable under $\mathcal F_n$ is still measurable under $\mathcal G_n$. Enlarging a filtration therefore preserves adaptedness. It leaves the values' integrability unchanged as well. It can nevertheless change conditional means, and hence destroy a martingale property. In the example, the next increment is even [[离散可预测过程|predictable relative to the enlarged filtration]]: $\xi_{n+1}$ is known at time $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal G_n$，原来对 $\mathcal F_n$ 可测的当前值，对 $\mathcal G_n$ 仍可测。因此扩大滤过会保留适应性，取值本身的可积性也不变。但条件均值可能改变，鞅性质也就可能被破坏。本例中，下一步增量甚至[[离散可预测过程|相对于扩大后的滤过可预测]]：时刻 $n$ 已经知道 $\xi_{n+1}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This says “can destroy”, not that every enlargement must do so. The test is always whether the conditional equality still holds for the specified larger filtration. A claim that a process is a martingale must identify its information flow; merely checking that its current values are observable does not perform that test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里说的是“可能破坏”，不是每次扩大信息都一定破坏。判断标准始终是：相对于指定的更大滤过，条件等式是否仍然成立。声称一个过程是鞅时必须说明它的信息流；仅检查当前值可观测，并没有完成这个检验。
