# 成本发现外部性是先行者的试错信息在未获充分补偿时惠及后来者的现象

成本发现外部性（cost-discovery externality）是先行者通过试验发现某项活动在本地的成本与经营可行性，而可模仿的信息让未承担试错成本、也未充分补偿先行者的后来者受益。这里的“发现”可以是把既有产品或技术适配到当地，不要求发明世界上从未有过的产品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cost-discovery externality arises when pioneers experiment to discover the local costs and commercial viability of an activity, while imitable information benefits entrants who neither bore the experimentation costs nor fully compensate the pioneers. Discovery can mean adapting an existing product or technology to local conditions, rather than inventing something new to the world.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

问题在于试错的私人回报与信息的社会价值可能不一致：先行者承担失败损失，成功却可能很快引来模仿者，使其只能取得部分收益。因此，即使某些试验从社会角度值得进行，企业也可能不愿率先尝试。仅仅“不知道哪个产业好”并不等于已经识别出这种[[外部性]]，还必须说明信息如何扩散及为何难以获偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Private returns to experimentation can differ from the social value of information. Pioneers bear failure losses, while successful trials may attract imitation and leave them with only part of the return. Some socially worthwhile trials may therefore go unattempted. Mere uncertainty about promising industries does not establish this [[外部性|externality]]: diffusion and limited appropriability must also be explained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，第一家企业花费资源检验某种水果在当地的病害控制、冷链和合格率，成功后其他企业能够据此判断是否进入；如果后来者不承担相应试验成本，又无需为这些信息付费，先行者的回报就可能低于试验产生的总价值。这是机制例子，不表示所有跟随进入都应付费，更不表示该水果项目已经值得补贴。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A teaching example is a pioneer testing disease control, cold-chain requirements and acceptable yields for a fruit crop. Successful trials help other firms decide whether to enter. If those firms neither share the trial costs nor pay for the information, the pioneer's return may fall short of the trial's total value. This does not imply that all entry should be charged or that this project merits a subsidy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它与[[学习外部性]]的着重点不同：成本发现解释“这项活动在这里是否可行”的信息如何产生与扩散；学习外部性强调经验和知识如何改善生产能力。二者可以同时存在。政策若以发现为理由，应追踪新增试验和可传播的信息，而不是把既有生产规模或所有新设备投资当成发现；允许有信息价值的试验失败，也不等于允许失败项目无限续期，后续仍需[[产业支持的停止与延期|支持复核]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The emphasis differs from [[学习外部性|learning externalities]]: cost discovery concerns information about whether an activity is viable locally, while learning concerns improvements in productive capabilities. They can coexist. Discovery-based support should track additional trials and useful information, not equate existing output or every new machine with discovery. Allowing informative failures does not justify indefinite funding; [[产业支持的停止与延期|support still requires review]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Rodrik（2004），[Industrial Policy for the Twenty-First Century](https://downloads.unido.org/ot/47/88/4788272/20001-_23093.pdf#page=10)，为UNIDO撰写、封面注明2004年9月版本，印刷8–11页（PDF10–13）：支持本地成本的自我发现、先行者与模仿者的收益不对称及试错激励；印刷21–22页（PDF23–24）支持聚焦新活动、绩效标准和到期机制。
- 水果试验是依该机制构造的例子，不是来源案例的效果估计。私人回报偏低是有条件的机制判断，不是所有试验都具有正社会净收益的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The September 2004 UNIDO version supplies the self-discovery mechanism and the distinctions concerning new activities, performance and termination. The fruit example is constructed, and the incentive mechanism does not establish positive net benefits for every trial.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
