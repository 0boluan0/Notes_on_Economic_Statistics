# 零概率类型的条件信念不能由普通 Bayes 分式或共同先验唯一确定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A zero-probability type's conditional belief is not uniquely determined by the ordinary Bayes ratio or by a common prior alone*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对共同先验 $\pi$ 下有正边际概率的类型，条件信念可写为

$$
\pi(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)
=
\frac{\pi(\theta,t_i,t_{-i})}{\pi_i(t_i)}.
$$

若 $\pi_i(t_i)=0$，分母为零，普通 Bayes 分式没有定义。此时不能声称该类型的 belief 已由 $\pi$ 唯一推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an own type with positive marginal probability, the conditional belief is obtained by the ordinary Bayes ratio. When $\pi_i(t_i)=0$, the denominator vanishes, so the ratio is undefined and the prior does not uniquely determine that type's belief.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有限类型的处理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Treatment in finite type spaces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限模型可以只在正概率支持集上使用普通条件化；若仍要分析一个零概率类型，就必须把该类型的 interim belief 另行作为模型原语，或明确指定一份 conditional-belief version。任意指定不等于“由 Bayes 法则推出”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite model may use ordinary conditioning only on the positive-probability support. If a zero-probability type is still to be analyzed, its interim belief must be supplied as an additional primitive or through an explicitly selected conditional-belief version. Such a specification is not a conclusion of the ordinary Bayes rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连续类型的处理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Treatment in continuous type spaces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标准可测空间上可选取 regular conditional distribution 作为条件信念核，但它通常只在类型边际分布 almost everywhere 的意义下唯一。改变零测类型集合上的取值不会改变共同先验；若模型要逐点约束每个类型，就必须额外固定所用 version，或直接把每个 $p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)$ 作为原语。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On standard measurable spaces, a regular conditional distribution can be selected as a conditional-belief kernel, but it is generally unique only almost everywhere under the type marginal. Altering its values on a null set of types does not change the prior. A model that imposes pointwise conditions on every type must therefore fix a version or take each $p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)$ as a primitive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[MIT OCW 14.126, Muhamet Yildiz, *Game Theory Lecture Notes*, Chapter 3 §3.2，正文 pp.73、76](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14.126-game-theory-spring-2024/mit14_126_s24_yildiz-lecture-notes.pdf)：说明零概率类型造成 ex-ante 表示中的伪均衡，并指出 Bayes 条件化在 $p_i(t_i)=0$ 处不连续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MIT explains the equilibrium problem created by zero-probability types and the failure of the ordinary conditional-belief formula at $p_i(t_i)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[UC Berkeley Statistics, Will Fithian, “Measure Theory Basics: Conditional probability”](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~wfithian/courses/stat210a/measure-theory-basics.html)：核对 $P(B)=0$ 时普通条件概率分式失效，以及零测事件条件化的内在歧义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Berkeley measure-theory notes verify that the elementary conditional-probability ratio fails when the conditioning event has probability zero and that null-event conditioning is intrinsically ambiguous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[Matthew O. Jackson, Tomas Rodriguez-Barraquer, and Xu Tan, “Epsilon-Equilibria of Perturbed Games”，§2，正文 p.5](https://web.stanford.edu/~jacksonm/emailgame.pdf)：把条件期望写成任意 version，并把 interim 条件规定为对 $P$-almost all types 成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Jackson, Rodriguez-Barraquer, and Tan explicitly use any version of conditional expectation and formulate interim optimality for almost all types.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
