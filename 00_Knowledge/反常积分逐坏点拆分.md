# Splitting improper integrals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反常积分逐坏点拆分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[反常积分|improper integral]] may contain more than one place where an ordinary finite-interval calculation stops being enough. An unbounded tail is one such place; approaching an interior singularity from the left and from the right gives two more. **Each approach must produce its own finite limit before the contributions can be added.** A finite result from one part cannot repair divergence in another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个[[反常积分]]里，可能有不止一个位置需要超出普通有限区间积分的处理。无穷远端是一个；从左边、右边分别逼近内部奇点，又是两个。**每个方向都必须独立得到有限极限，之后才能相加。** 一部分算出有限值，不能修复另一部分的发散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here we consider a real function with finitely many possible singularities or infinite endpoints. Assume it is Riemann integrable on every closed finite interval that avoids those points; continuity there is a sufficient condition. A “bad point” in the instructions below means a location requiring a limit, not every ordinary jump discontinuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里考虑实函数，可能的奇点和无穷端只有有限多个。假设在避开这些位置的每个有限闭区间上，函数都黎曼可积；在那里连续是一个充分条件。下文的“坏点”指需要取极限的位置，并不是把普通跳跃间断都视为反常奇点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An interior singularity has two sides
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个内部奇点有两个方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $a<c<b$ are finite, $c$ is the only possible singularity, and the outer endpoints are ordinary. Choose positive cutoffs $\varepsilon<c-a$ and $\delta<b-c$. They leave a gap around $c$, so both truncated integrals are proper:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设有限数 $a<c<b$ 中，$c$ 是唯一可能的奇点，外侧两个端点没有问题。选取正截断量 $\varepsilon<c-a$、$\delta<b-c$，在 $c$ 附近留出缺口，让两个截断积分都成为普通积分：

$$L=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\int_a^{c-\varepsilon}f(x)\,dx,
\qquad
R=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\int_{c+\delta}^b f(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both $L$ and $R$ exist as finite real numbers, define $\int_a^b f=L+R$. Otherwise the ordinary improper integral diverges. The different letters emphasize independent limits. Writing a single cutoff is harmless only after independent convergence has been justified; imposing equal cutoffs to obtain cancellation computes a different object, the [[Cauchy 主值与反常积分|Cauchy principal value]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $L,R$ 都存在且为有限实数，就定义 $\int_a^b f=L+R$；否则普通反常积分发散。使用不同字母，是为了强调两个极限相互独立。只有已证明各侧独立收敛以后，才可以放心用同一截断量简化书写；靠强制相等来制造抵消，计算的是另一种对象——[[Cauchy 主值与反常积分|柯西主值]]。

> [!example] An infinite value of the function need not give an infinite integral · 函数无界，积分仍可能有限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=1/\sqrt{|x|}$ on $[-1,1]$ with $x\ne0$, each side is integrable after truncation. The [[零附近 p 积分判别|power-integral calculation near zero]], with $p=1/2$, gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[-1,1]$ 上、$x\ne0$ 时的 $f(x)=1/\sqrt{|x|}$，每一侧截断后都可积分。利用 $p=1/2$ 的[[零附近 p 积分判别|零附近幂积分计算]]，得到
>
> $$\int_{-1}^{-\varepsilon}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{|x|}}=2(1-\sqrt\varepsilon),
> \qquad \int_{\delta}^{1}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x}}=2(1-\sqrt\delta).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both limits are $2$, so the improper integral is $4$. No relationship between $\varepsilon$ and $\delta$ is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两侧极限分别为 $2$，因此反常积分为 $4$；不需要规定 $\varepsilon$ 与 $\delta$ 之间的关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Separate the tails and any singular endpoints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*无穷远端与奇端点也要分别处理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is locally integrable on the whole real line, choose any finite splitting point $d$ and examine
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在整条实线的每个有限闭区间都可积，任选一个有限分割点 $d$，分别检查

$$\lim_{A\to-\infty}\int_A^d f(x)\,dx,
\qquad
\lim_{B\to+\infty}\int_d^B f(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only when both are finite does their sum define $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f$. Changing $d$ moves the same finite piece from one side to the other; [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]] shows that the total and convergence do not change. This argument requires that the intervening finite piece is proper, or has already been separately justified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有两边都有限，它们的和才定义 $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f$。改变 $d$，相当于把同一段有限积分从一边移到另一边；[[定积分区间可加性]]保证总值与敛散性不变。这要求被移动的有限区间本身可正常积分，或其反常极限已经另行得到验证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a piece has problems at **both** ends, insert an ordinary point in between and separate the two limits again. For example, in $\int_0^\infty (x-3)^{-2}\,dx$, the problematic approaches are $3$ from the left, $3$ from the right and $+\infty$. Splitting also at $4$ isolates them into $(0,3)$, $(3,4)$ and $(4,\infty)$. On the first piece,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果某一段的**两端**都有问题，就在中间插入一个普通点，再把两个极限分开。例如 $\int_0^\infty (x-3)^{-2}\,dx$ 需要分别处理从左趋近 $3$、从右趋近 $3$，以及趋近 $+\infty$。再在 $4$ 分割，就把它们隔离到 $(0,3)$、$(3,4)$ 和 $(4,\infty)$ 三段。在第一段，

$$\int_0^{3-\varepsilon}\frac{dx}{(x-3)^2}
=\frac1\varepsilon-\frac13\longrightarrow+\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That one failed limit already proves divergence of the whole expression. The other side of $3$ also diverges, although the tail from $4$ to infinity equals $1$. These statements follow by evaluating $-1/(x-3)$ on each legal truncation, not by inserting the singular point itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一个失败的极限已经证明整个积分发散。$3$ 的另一侧同样发散，尽管从 $4$ 到无穷的尾积分等于 $1$。这些结论来自在每个合法截断区间上计算 $-1/(x-3)$ 的端点差，而不是把奇点本身代进去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Scope of the rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 范围边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With finitely many problematic approaches, the procedure is finite: locate them, isolate each side, take the separate limits, and add only finite results. Infinitely many singular points, especially with an accumulation point, require additional control of the infinite collection. Convergence of every isolated small piece alone does not ensure that all pieces have a finite total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当需要处理的方向只有有限多个时，这个流程也是有限的：定位、隔离各侧、分别取极限，最后只相加有限结果。若有无限多个奇点，尤其是存在聚点，还要额外控制这无限多段的整体累积；每个孤立小段都收敛，不保证全部相加仍有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sign-changing example $1/x$ makes the independent requirement especially visible: the left integral on $[-1,0)$ tends to $-\infty$, and the right one on $(0,1]$ tends to $+\infty$. Neither is a finite number to add. The linked [[Cauchy 主值与反常积分|principal-value comparison]] calculates exactly what a synchronized truncation changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

变号例子 $1/x$ 尤其能说明为何要求独立收敛：$[-1,0)$ 上的左侧积分趋向 $-\infty$，$(0,1]$ 上的右侧积分趋向 $+\infty$，没有两个有限数可供相加。[[Cauchy 主值与反常积分|主值区别卡]]会具体计算，同步截断究竟改变了什么。
