# 每个无穷端或奇点都必须拆成独立极限且全部收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every infinite endpoint or singularity must be split into an independent limit, and all such limits must converge*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子规则
> 若积分区间含有限多个可逐点分离的问题点，先在每一点拆开。对内部奇点 $c\in(a,b)$，
> $$\int_a^b f:=\int_a^c f+\int_c^b f$$
> 只有左右两个单侧反常积分都分别收敛时才有普通意义。任意一段发散，整个反常积分就发散。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If an interval contains finitely many isolated problematic points, split at every one. For an interior singularity $c\in(a,b)$, $\int_a^b f:=\int_a^c f+\int_c^b f$ has its ordinary meaning only when both one-sided improper integrals converge separately. If any component diverges, the whole integral diverges.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

双无穷区间也要任选有限点 $c$，定义为
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f:=\int_{-\infty}^{c}f+\int_c^{\infty}f,$$
并要求两边各自收敛。不能用同一个参数强行同步左右截断，让两个本来不存在的极限抵消。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a doubly infinite interval, choose a finite $c$ and define $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f:=\int_{-\infty}^{c}f+\int_c^{\infty}f$, requiring both parts to converge. One may not force the two truncations to move together so that two nonexistent one-sided limits cancel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\int_0^\infty (x-3)^{-2}dx$ 的问题点不仅是 $\infty$，还包括内部奇点 $3$。在 $3$ 任一侧的积分已经发散，远端即使收敛也不能挽救总积分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\int_0^\infty (x-3)^{-2}dx$, the problematic set contains both infinity and the interior singularity $3$. Divergence on either side of $3$ settles the whole integral; a convergent far tail cannot rescue it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 范围边界

若问题点有无限多个并在区间内聚集，仅仅验证每个孤立小段各自收敛还不够；还要把聚点作为新的问题端点，并控制这些分段积分形成的无穷累积。本原子处理课程中的有限坏点情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If infinitely many problematic points accumulate inside the interval, checking every isolated piece separately is not sufficient. The accumulation point becomes another problematic endpoint, and the infinite accumulation of the component integrals must also be controlled. This atom covers the course's finite-problematic-point setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $\int_{-1}^{1}1/x\,dx$ 的左右发散符号相反，为什么仍不收敛？
>
> **答案：** 普通定义要求两边各自为有限值；一个趋 $-\infty$、一个趋 $+\infty$，所以条件已经失败。
