# Why a jump cannot be repaired at one point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*跳跃间断不可补值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A jump has two incompatible nearby destinations. Inputs just to the left approach one output, while inputs just to the right approach another. Moving the single value at the dividing point cannot join these two trends. This is why a jump differs from a [[可去间断的唯一补值|removable hole]], where both sides already agree on the value that is missing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跳跃间断有两个互不相容的附近趋势：从左边靠近时，输出趋向一个数；从右边靠近时，却趋向另一个数。移动分界点上的单个值，无法把这两种趋势接起来。这与[[可去间断的唯一补值|可去的缺口]]不同：后者附近已经共同指向同一个待补值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A step with a gap of two
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the nearby rule fixed and let $c$ be any real value assigned at zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持附近的规则不变，用任意实数 $c$ 作为零处的点值：

$$g_c(x)=\begin{cases}
1,&x<0,\\
c,&x=0,\\
3,&x>0.
\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[双侧极限判定|left-hand limit]] at $0$ is $1$ and the right-hand limit is $3$. Choosing $c=1$ joins the left side but misses the right; choosing $c=3$ does the reverse. Choosing the midpoint $c=2$ misses both. More generally,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零处的[[双侧极限判定|左极限]]是 $1$，右极限是 $3$。取 $c=1$ 只能接上左边，取 $c=3$ 只能接上右边；取中点 $c=2$ 则两边都接不上。一般地，

$$2=|3-1|\le |3-c|+|c-1|,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
so at least one of the two distances from $c$ is at least $1$. For every positive input distance $\delta$, there is an input on that offending side within $\delta$ of zero. Its output remains at least $1$ away from $c$. Thus even the output tolerance $1/2$ required by [[一点连续|continuity at zero]] cannot be achieved, whatever $c$ is.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以，$c$ 到两个侧边值的距离中，至少一个不小于 $1$。无论给定多小的输入距离 $\delta>0$，都能在相应一侧、距离零不足 $\delta$ 的地方找到输入，其输出离 $c$ 仍至少为 $1$。因此，不管 $c$ 是多少，连[[一点连续|零处连续]]所要求的输出容差 $1/2$ 都无法实现。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The general obstruction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一般情况下为什么也不行*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a$ be a target point with allowed domain inputs arbitrarily close on each side. A **finite one-sided limit** describes the nearby outputs using only that side; write $L_-$ for the left value and $L_+$ for the right value. A **jump discontinuity** means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设目标点 $a$ 的左右两边都有可以任意靠近它的合法输入。**有限单侧极限**只检验对应一侧的附近输出，用 $L_-$ 表示左侧值、$L_+$ 表示右侧值。**跳跃间断**指

$$\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x)=L_-\in\mathbb R,\qquad
\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)=L_+\in\mathbb R,\qquad L_-\ne L_+.$$

> [!important] No successful real point value · 没有成功的实数点值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With the nearby domain and all nearby values fixed, no definition or redefinition of $f(a)$ makes the resulting function continuous at $a$. The size of the jump is $|L_+-L_-|>0$; the signed difference $L_+-L_-$ also records its direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定附近的合法输入及这些点的值后，无论怎样定义或重新定义 $f(a)$，所得函数都不能在 $a$ 连续。跳跃大小是 $|L_+-L_-|>0$；带符号的差 $L_+-L_-$ 还记录了跳跃方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a chosen value $c$ made the function continuous, every allowed approach to $a$ would have output limit $c$. In particular, the left restriction would give $L_-=c$ and the right restriction would give $L_+=c$, contradicting their inequality. Equivalently, the [[双侧极限判定|two-sided limit criterion]] fails before we even choose a point value, and [[点值不决定极限|changing only that value]] cannot alter either side's limit. The proof does not require the nearby branches to be constant or monotone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某个补值 $c$ 能使函数连续，那么从每种合法方向靠近 $a$，输出都必须趋于 $c$。特别地，限制在左边得到 $L_-=c$，限制在右边得到 $L_+=c$，与左右不等矛盾。也可以说，尚未选择点值时，[[双侧极限判定|双侧极限判据]]就已经失败；[[点值不决定极限|只改该点的值]]不能改变任一侧的极限。证明不要求附近两支是常数或单调函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What this conclusion does and does not test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both side limits must be finite to call the failure a jump here. Opposite infinities for $1/x$ at zero are not a jump between two real heights. If one side oscillates without a limit, that is another obstruction, explained in [[间断类型判别|the classification of discontinuities]]. These failures also cannot be repaired at one point, but should not be justified by claiming two finite side limits exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里称为跳跃时，两侧极限都必须有限。$1/x$ 在零处的左右无穷方向相反，不是两个实数高度之间的跳跃。若某一侧振荡而没有极限，那又是另一种障碍，见[[间断类型判别]]。这些情况同样无法改单点修复，但不能把理由写成“两侧有限极限都存在”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The domain is part of the question. Restrict the step example to $[0,\infty)$ and set $g(0)=3$; it is then continuous at its endpoint because only right-side inputs remain. This does not repair the original two-sided function: it changes which inputs are tested. At an isolated domain point there is no jump test at all, because neither side supplies arbitrarily close inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义域也是问题的一部分。把开头的阶跃例子限制到 $[0,\infty)$，并取 $g(0)=3$，它就在端点连续，因为只剩右侧输入。这没有修复原来的双侧函数，而是改变了被检验的输入范围。在定义域孤立点处，更不能套跳跃判别，因为左右都没有任意接近的输入。
