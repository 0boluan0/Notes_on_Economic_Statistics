# 左右极限不相等时改单点无法恢复连续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Unequal one-sided limits cannot be repaired by changing one point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 若 $a$ 处的有限左、右极限都存在但不相等，则双侧极限不存在。因此无论怎样定义或修改 $f(a)$，都不能使 $f$ 在 $a$ 连续。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the finite left- and right-hand limits at $a$ both exist but are unequal, the two-sided limit does not exist. No definition or redefinition of $f(a)$ can therefore make $f$ continuous at $a$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一命题只刻画“有限左右极限都存在但不相等”的跳跃情形。无穷间断和振荡间断也不能通过改一个点修复，但理由不是“两个有限单侧极限不相等”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proposition addresses the jump case in which two finite one-sided limits exist but differ. Infinite and oscillatory discontinuities also cannot be repaired by changing one point, but not because two finite one-sided limits are unequal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x)=1$ 而 $\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)=3$，那么把 $f(a)$ 定义为 $1$、$3$ 或其他值都无法消除跳跃。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x)=1$ while $\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)=3$, assigning $f(a)$ the value $1$, $3$, or anything else cannot remove the jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 左极限为 $1$、右极限为 $3$ 时，能否选择一个 $f(a)$ 使函数连续？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the left-hand limit is $1$ and the right-hand limit is $3$, can any choice of $f(a)$ make the function continuous?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。双侧极限已经因左右不等而不存在，单个点值无法改变这一事实。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The two-sided limit already fails because the one-sided limits differ, and a single point value cannot change that fact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
