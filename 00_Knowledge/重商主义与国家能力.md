# 重商主义政策论述常把贸易金属航运与国家能力相连
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mercantilist policy arguments often linked trade, specie, shipping, and state capacity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 历史机制
> 在一些近代欧洲政策论述中，对外销售超过对外购买可使差额以金银等国际支付手段返回；保护本国承运、扩大制造与贸易、征收贸易相关税收，则被当作同时扩大商船、海员、可课税交易和国家可动员支付能力的手段。这是一组反复出现的历史联系，不是所有“重商主义者”共有的一条定律。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In some early-modern European policy arguments, selling more abroad than was purchased could return the balance in internationally acceptable specie. Reserving carriage for domestic shipping and expanding manufactures and trade were treated as ways to enlarge merchant fleets, seafaring labour, taxable transactions, and the state's command over payments. These are recurrent historical links, not one law shared by every “mercantilist.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金银在这里不必被理解为最终消费品意义上的全部财富。它们既可用于跨境结算，也可支付军需和难以用本国商品直接取得的进口。航运限制也不只针对商品价格：英格兰的 Navigation Laws 把部分运输保留给英格兰或殖民船只，贸易、船队与海员储备因而可以同时进入政策计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gold and silver need not be treated here as all wealth in the sense of final consumption. They could settle cross-border obligations and pay for military supplies or imports not directly obtainable with domestic goods. Navigation restrictions also targeted more than commodity prices: England's Navigation Laws reserved parts of carriage for English or colonial ships, so trade, the merchant fleet, and the supply of seafarers could enter the same policy calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条机制有三重边界。第一，关税、国内消费税、公共信用和国内税基都可能支持财政军事能力，不能把国家力量归因于海关或金属一项。第二，顺差、运输保护和特许垄断的成本由谁承担必须另行分析。第三，重建历史理由不等于赞成当代关税或贸易战；现代货币、金融和供应链条件已经不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mechanism has three boundaries. First, customs, domestic excises, public credit, and the domestic tax base could all support fiscal-military capacity; state power cannot be attributed to customs or specie alone. Second, who bore the costs of surpluses, shipping protection, and chartered monopolies requires separate analysis. Third, reconstructing a historical rationale is not an endorsement of present-day tariffs or trade wars under different monetary, financial, and supply-chain conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“争取贸易顺差”在这个历史机制里不只是“多卖商品赚利润”？
>
> **答案：** 差额可能带来可跨境支付的金属，贸易与承运还会影响船队、海员、税收和国家采购能力；但这些联系的强弱必须按具体国家和制度核验。

## 来源与核验

- [Thomas Mun, *England's Treasure by Forraign Trade*（University of Michigan, EEBO 影印）](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/e/eebo/A51598.0001.001?view=toc)：核对“出口总值超过进口总值，差额以 treasure 返回”这一位作者的具体论证。
- [UK Parliament, “The Navigation Laws”](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/tradeindustry/importexport/overview/navigationlaws/)：核对 1651 以后把部分进口与殖民贸易保留给英格兰或殖民船只的实际制度，而不是把“航运保护”停留在抽象口号。
- [Dal Bó et al., “Dissecting the Sinews of Power” (2025)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-economic-history/article/dissecting-the-sinews-of-power-international-trade-and-the-rise-of-britains-fiscalmilitary-state-16891823/1CC13874CAFFCF289E9A79CD7AF43FF7)：核对贸易品相关间接税与英国财政军事国家的联系，同时保留国内消费税、公共信用和其他财政来源的边界。
- [David Hume, “Of Money”](https://www.econlib.org/library/LFBooks/Hume/hmMPL.html?chapter_num=31)：用其“人口与商品才是国家真正力量”的论证限制“金属本身等于国力”的过度概括。
