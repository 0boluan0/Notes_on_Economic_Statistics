# 一致风险度量同时满足单调性、现金平移、正齐次与次可加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A coherent risk measure satisfies monotonicity, cash translation, positive homogeneity, and subadditivity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 一致风险度量是一个把损失随机变量映射为资本或风险金额的函数 $\rho$，并同时满足四项聚合公理。它描述风险函数的结构，不判断输入分布是否估计正确。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A coherent risk measure maps a loss random variable to a capital or risk amount and satisfies four aggregation axioms. It describes the risk functional, not whether the input distribution was estimated correctly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对损失变量，

$$
L_1\le L_2\Rightarrow\rho(L_1)\le\rho(L_2),
\qquad
\rho(L+c)=\rho(L)+c,
$$

$$
\rho(\lambda L)=\lambda\rho(L)\;(\lambda\ge0),
\qquad
\rho(L_1+L_2)\le\rho(L_1)+\rho(L_2).
$$

四式依次是单调性、现金平移、正齐次与次可加。次可加给出组合风险不超过分项风险之和的聚合边界；它不保证实际依赖结构已被正确估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four expressions are monotonicity, cash translation, positive homogeneity, and subadditivity. Subadditivity bounds portfolio risk by the sum of stand-alone risks; it does not guarantee that real dependence was modelled correctly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个风险度量满足四项公理，能否推出它的明日预测会准确？
>
> **答案：** 不能。一致性是函数性质，预测准确性还取决于数据、模型、参数和实现。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** If a risk measure satisfies all four coherence axioms, does that imply that tomorrow's forecast will be accurate?
>
> **Answer:** No. Coherence is a property of the functional; predictive accuracy also depends on data, models, parameters, and implementation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 在损失为正的口径下，现金平移写成加号；收益口径的符号会不同。
- 一致性不等于保守性、稳健性、易估计或易回测。
- 公理不能代替用途、监管与治理判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- With positive loss, cash translation carries a plus sign; a profit convention changes signs.
- Coherence is not conservatism, robustness, ease of estimation, or ease of backtesting.
- Axioms do not replace use-specific regulatory and governance judgement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
