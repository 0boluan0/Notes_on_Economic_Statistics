# 图、树、匹配与着色
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graphs, Trees, Matchings, and Coloring*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 用顶点和边表示对象间关系，并利用连通、无环、匹配与着色结构解决可达性和冲突问题。
> **具体锚点：** 地铁站是顶点、线路相邻是边；BFS 按距离分层，因而在无权图中找到最短路径。
> **核心难点：** walk、path、cycle、连通与树的条件不可互换；算法正确性依赖探索边界不变量。
> **为什么重要：** 网络、依赖、调度、匹配市场和状态空间都能建成图。
> **继续：** 先声明有向/无向、是否允许重复边和目标结构，再选择遍历或结构定理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Represent relationships with vertices and edges, then exploit connectivity, acyclicity, matching, and coloring to solve reachability and conflict problems.
> **Concrete anchor:** Treat stations as vertices and direct connections as edges; BFS explores by distance layer and therefore finds shortest paths in an unweighted graph.
> **Central difficulty:** Walks, paths, cycles, connectivity, and tree conditions are not interchangeable, and traversal correctness depends on an explored-frontier invariant.
> **Why it matters:** Networks, dependencies, schedules, matching markets, and state spaces can all be modeled as graphs.
> **Continue with:** State directedness, edge conventions, and the target structure before choosing a traversal or theorem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 图、walk、path 与 cycle
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graphs, Walks, Paths, and Cycles*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图由顶点和边组成。walk 可重复顶点/边，path 通常不重复顶点，cycle 闭合。度数和等于边数的两倍；连通分量把互相可达顶点分组。有向图要区分入度、出度和强/弱连通。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A graph consists of vertices and edges. A walk may repeat vertices or edges, a path normally repeats no vertex, and a cycle is closed. In an undirected graph, the sum of degrees is twice the number of edges. Connected components group mutually reachable vertices; directed graphs distinguish in-degree, out-degree, and strong from weak connectivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 树、匹配与着色
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trees, Matchings, and Coloring*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限无向树等价刻画包括连通无环、任意两点唯一简单路径、n 个顶点有 n-1 条边。匹配要求边不共享端点，完美匹配覆盖所有顶点；着色要求相邻顶点颜色不同。不同问题需要不同结构定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalent characterizations of a finite undirected tree include connected and acyclic, a unique simple path between every pair, and n vertices with n-1 edges together with connectivity or acyclicity. A matching uses edges with no shared endpoint, a perfect matching covers every vertex, and a proper coloring gives adjacent vertices different colors. Different problems require different structural theorems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算法与证明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Algorithms and Proofs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

BFS 给无权最短路并分层，DFS 揭示连通与环。正确性常由不变量和已探索边界证明，而非只看运行结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BFS layers an unweighted graph and finds shortest paths, while DFS exposes connectivity and cycles. Correctness is normally proved from an invariant over the explored frontier rather than inferred from observed output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：课程先修关系是否成环
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Detect a Cycle in Course Prerequisites*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把课程作为顶点，先修课指向后续课。若 DFS 在当前递归路径中再次遇到灰色顶点，就得到有向环，说明先修要求无法按任何拓扑顺序完成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Represent courses as vertices and direct an edge from each prerequisite to the later course. If DFS reaches a gray vertex already on the current recursion path, it has found a directed cycle, showing that no topological completion order exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅遇到已完成的黑色顶点不表示成环，因为它不在当前路径上。这个颜色不变量区分“共享依赖”与“循环依赖”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Encountering a completed black vertex does not indicate a cycle because it is absent from the current path. The color invariant distinguishes a shared dependency from a circular dependency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 只因有 n-1 条边就称为树：还需连通或无环条件，排除一个成环分量与一个孤立分量。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A graph is called a tree solely because it has n-1 edges: add connectivity or acyclicity to exclude a cyclic component plus an isolated component.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- BFS 给出错误距离：检查是否在入队时标记 visited，以及边是否真的无权/等权。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  BFS gives a wrong distance: check whether vertices are marked visited when enqueued and whether edges are genuinely unweighted or equally weighted.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 把 walk 当 path：明确是否允许重复顶点，尤其在计数与最短路证明中。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A walk is treated as a path: state whether repeated vertices are allowed, especially in counting and shortest-path proofs.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimum Self-Check*

### 树的 n-1 条边条件为什么需与连通或无环合用？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must the n-1 edge condition for a tree be combined with connectivity or acyclicity?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 任意 n 顶点 n-1 边图未必自动是树；结合连通或无环之一才排除断开或成环。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> An arbitrary graph with n vertices and n-1 edges need not be a tree; combining the count with connectivity or acyclicity excludes disconnected or cyclic counterexamples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 BFS 在无权图中给最短路？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does BFS give shortest paths in an unweighted graph?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 队列按从源点的边数分层处理顶点，顶点第一次被发现时不可能存在更少边但尚未处理的路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The queue processes vertices in layers of edge distance from the source, so when a vertex is first discovered, no path with fewer edges remains unprocessed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 匹配与着色分别排除哪种冲突？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What kind of conflict does matching exclude, and what kind does coloring exclude?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 匹配选择不共享端点的边；着色给相邻顶点不同颜色。它们约束的对象不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A matching selects edges that share no endpoint, while coloring assigns different colors to adjacent vertices; they constrain different objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
