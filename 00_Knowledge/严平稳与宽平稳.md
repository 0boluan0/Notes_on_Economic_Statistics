# 严平稳过程有有限二阶矩时必为宽平稳
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A strictly stationary process with finite second moments is wide-sense stationary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 若 $\{X_t\}$ 严平稳且 $E|X_t|^2<\infty$，那么 $E[X_t]$ 为常数，且
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},X_t)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_h,X_0)
> $$
> 只依赖 $h$，所以过程宽平稳。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Strict stationarity plus finite second moments makes the mean constant and the covariance a function of lag alone, so the process is WSS.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明只需要严平稳定义的一维和二维部分：一维平移不变给出相同均值与方差；把 $(X_{t+h},X_t)$ 共同平移 $-t$，得到与 $(X_h,X_0)$ 相同的联合分布。有限二阶矩是使这些矩和协方差存在的必要前提，不能省略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only the one- and two-dimensional parts of strict stationarity are needed. One-dimensional invariance fixes the mean and variance; shifting $(X_{t+h},X_t)$ by $-t$ gives the law of $(X_h,X_0)$. Finite second moments are essential because otherwise these quantities may not exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “严平稳一定宽平稳”缺了什么条件？
>
> **答案：** 缺有限二阶矩。没有它，均值、方差或协方差可能根本不存在。
