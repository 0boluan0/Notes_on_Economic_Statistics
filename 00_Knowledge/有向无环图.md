# Directed acyclic graphs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有向无环图*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **directed acyclic graph**, abbreviated **DAG**, is a directed graph with no cycle that follows the arrows all the way around. It is useful for recording dependencies: some tasks must come before others, without a chain of requirements eventually demanding that a task precede itself. The restriction concerns the arrows, not whether the drawing looks circular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**有向无环图**，简称 **DAG**，是没有有向环的有向图：不存在沿箭头绕行一圈的环。它适合表示依赖关系：某些任务必须先于另一些任务，但一串先后要求不能最终要求某项任务先于它自己。限制针对的是箭头方向，而不是图形画得像不像圆圈。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Follow the arrows in a small example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*沿着小例子的箭头走*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ mean preparing data, $B$ fitting a model, and $C$ writing a report. Suppose the edges are $A\to B$, $B\to C$, and $A\to C$. As in [[图模型语义|graph modeling]], the arrow $u\to v$ means that $u$ must be completed before $v$. A route can move from $A$ toward $B$ and $C$, but there is no arrow leading back from $C$ and no way to return to the starting vertex after taking a positive number of steps. This is a DAG.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
让 $A$ 表示准备数据，$B$ 表示拟合模型，$C$ 表示撰写报告，边为 $A\to B$、$B\to C$、$A\to C$。按照[[图模型语义|图建模]]中的约定，箭头 $u\to v$ 表示必须先完成 $u$，再做 $v$。沿路线可以从 $A$ 走向 $B,C$，但 $C$ 没有返回上游的边，走过正数步后无法回到起点。这是一张 DAG。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now add $C\to A$. The route $A\to B\to C\to A$ is a directed cycle, so the new graph is not a DAG. Even without going through $B$, the two edges $A\to C$ and $C\to A$ form a directed two-cycle. Adding just one edge can therefore destroy acyclicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
现在加入 $C\to A$。路线 $A\to B\to C\to A$ 构成有向环，新图就不再是 DAG。即使不经过 $B$，$A\to C$ 与 $C\to A$ 也形成有向二环。因此，只加一条边就可能破坏无环性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The precise condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*准确条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here graphs have finitely many vertices and edges. In a [[图类型的建模边界|directed graph]] $G=(V,E)$, $V$ is the vertex set, $E$ records edges, and every step must go from an edge's tail to its head. A [[游走路径与环|directed cycle]] is a sequence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
本卡讨论顶点和边都有限的图。在[[图类型的建模边界|有向图]] $G=(V,E)$ 中，$V$ 是顶点集合，$E$ 记录边，每一步必须从边的起点走向终点。[[游走路径与环|有向环]]是一串

$$v_0\to v_1\to\cdots\to v_{k-1}\to v_0,\qquad k\ge1,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $v_0,\ldots,v_{k-1}$ are distinct and every displayed arrow is an edge of the graph. The integer $k$ counts edges. Only the final return repeats a vertex. The graph is a **DAG exactly when no such directed cycle exists**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
其中 $v_0,\ldots,v_{k-1}$ 互不相同，每个显示的箭头都是图中的边，整数 $k$ 表示边数；只有最后返回时重复起点。**不存在这样的有向环，图才是 DAG。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive-length requirement matters. Staying at $v$ and traversing no edges gives a length-zero closed walk; it does not prevent a graph from being a DAG. A self-loop $v\to v$, however, is a one-edge cycle and does prevent it. If parallel directed edges are allowed, several copies of $u\to v$ with $u\ne v$ do not by themselves provide a return route; an additional route from $v$ back to $u$ would.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
正长度要求很重要。停在 $v$ 而不走边，是零长度闭游走，不会使图失去 DAG 性质；但自环 $v\to v$ 是一条边的环，会使图不再是 DAG。若允许有向平行边，$u\ne v$ 时多条同向 $u\to v$ 本身并没有提供返回路线；若再有从 $v$ 返回 $u$ 的路线，才会形成环。

> [!warning] Acyclic after following directions · 必须沿方向判断无环
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The original example $A\to B$, $B\to C$, $A\to C$ becomes an undirected triangle when arrows are forgotten. It is nevertheless a DAG. Thus the underlying undirected graph of a DAG need not be a [[无向树|tree]], which is a connected undirected graph without cycles. A DAG also need not be connected: add a separate isolated vertex and no directed cycle is created.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 原例 $A\to B$、$B\to C$、$A\to C$ 去掉箭头后是一个无向三角形，但它仍是 DAG。因此，DAG 的底层无向图不必是[[无向树|树]]，即连通且无环的无向图。DAG 也不必连通：另加一个孤立点不会产生有向环。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What this says about a dependency model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这对依赖模型意味着什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The edge meaning must first be fixed. If $u\to v$ means “$u$ is a prerequisite of $v$,” following arrows moves toward later tasks. If it means “$u$ depends on $v$,” the scheduling interpretation goes the other way. Reversing every arrow preserves whether a directed cycle exists, but reverses which end of an edge must be performed first under these two conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
必须先固定边的含义。若 $u\to v$ 表示“$u$ 是 $v$ 的前置任务”，沿箭头是走向后续任务；若它表示“$u$ 依赖 $v$”，安排任务时就要按相反方向理解。把所有箭头反向不会改变是否存在有向环，但在这两种约定下，边的哪一端必须先完成恰好相反。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[拓扑序|topological order]] lists every vertex once with each arrow pointing forward in the list. The fact that every finite DAG admits such a list is the separate [[DAG 与拓扑序等价|DAG–ordering theorem]]. Acyclicity alone does not specify task durations, available workers, or a shortest completion time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[拓扑序]]把每个顶点恰好列一次，使每条箭头都从列表前面指向后面。每张有限 DAG 都能找到这种列表，是另一个[[DAG 与拓扑序等价|DAG 与拓扑序等价定理]]。无环性本身没有规定任务时长、可用人手或最短完成时间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An edgeless graph is acyclic, including a single isolated vertex. If the graph model permits the empty vertex set, the empty graph is also a DAG: there is no cycle to violate the condition. This is an explicit extension of the nonempty-vertex convention in the MIT MCS source.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
没有边的图是无环图，单个孤立顶点也包括在内。若图模型允许空顶点集，那么空图也是 DAG，因为不存在违背条件的环。这是对 MIT MCS 来源所用非空顶点约定的明确扩展。
