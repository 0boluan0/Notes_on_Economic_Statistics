# 公共支出与成本—收益分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public Expenditure and Cost-benefit Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原主题名称：公共支出、社会保险与公共选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Original topic name: Public Expenditure, Social Insurance, and Public Choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 判断政府为何支出、项目是否值得、风险如何在个人与社会间分担，以及政治过程如何改变资源配置。
> **具体锚点：** 一座桥的合同支出不是其全部社会成本，收费收入也不是新增社会收益；必须比较无桥反事实下的时间、土地、维护与交通变化。
> **核心难点：** 支出规模不是福利本身；转移支付、公共投入和政府购买的资源含义不同，社会保险还同时处理市场缺失与再分配。
> **为什么重要：** 教育、医疗、养老、基建和地方财政不能只按会计金额评价。
> **继续：** 先做项目逻辑与反事实，再比较成本收益、分配、保险和政治执行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Determine why government spends, whether a project is worthwhile, how risks are shared between individuals and society, and how political processes alter resource allocation.
> **Concrete anchor:** A bridge's contract expenditure is not its full social cost, and toll revenue is not a new social benefit; the analysis must compare time, land, maintenance, and traffic with the no-bridge counterfactual.
> **Central difficulty:** The amount spent is not welfare itself. Transfers, public investment, and government purchases have different resource meanings, while social insurance addresses both missing markets and redistribution.
> **Why it matters:** Education, health care, pensions, infrastructure, and local public finance cannot be evaluated from accounting totals alone.
> **Continue with:** Establish the project's logic and counterfactual first, then compare costs and benefits, distribution, insurance, and political implementation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 支出分类与经济含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Expenditure Categories and Their Economic Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府购买直接使用资源，转移支付改变购买力但不直接计入当期资源成本，投资性支出形成未来公共资本。经常/资本分类和功能分类回答不同问题；预算标签不能替代真实机会成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government purchases directly use resources, transfers change purchasing power without directly consuming current resources, and investment expenditure creates future public capital. Current/capital and functional classifications answer different questions; budget labels cannot substitute for real opportunity cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价前先把会计支出转换成增量社会资源：哪些劳动、土地和资本因项目而离开其他用途，哪些款项只是群体之间的转移，哪些税收筹资会改变行为。避免把“花了多少钱”同时当成本、产出和福利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before evaluation, translate accounting expenditure into incremental social resources: identify labor, land, and capital diverted from other uses; payments that merely transfer purchasing power across groups; and behavioral changes induced by taxation. Do not use “money spent” simultaneously as cost, output, and welfare.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 成本—收益分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cost-benefit Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确定基准反事实，列出所有受影响群体的增量成本/收益，折现到共同时间，处理外部性、风险和影子价格。净现值为正是效率证据，但估值不确定、分配和不可货币化后果需单列。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the baseline counterfactual, list incremental costs and benefits for every affected group, discount them to a common date, and account for externalities, risk, and shadow prices. A positive net present value is evidence of efficiency, but valuation uncertainty, distribution, and non-monetized consequences must be reported separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准步骤是：明确“无项目”世界；识别受影响者和时间；用机会成本而非支出价格计量资源；选择与风险和代际口径一致的社会贴现率；做敏感性与分配表；最后检查无法货币化的权利、生态和实施风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The standard sequence is to define the world without the project; identify affected parties and timing; value resources at opportunity cost rather than expenditure price; select a social discount rate consistent with risk and intergenerational treatment; report sensitivity and distributional tables; and finally assess rights, ecological effects, and implementation risks that resist monetization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 教育、医疗与基础设施
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Education, Health Care, and Infrastructure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公共介入可能来自外部性、信贷约束、信息不对称、保险市场缺失和公平目标。供给公共化、补贴需求方或规制私人供给的激励不同；拥挤、质量和地区可达性决定真实效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public intervention may respond to externalities, credit constraints, asymmetric information, missing insurance markets, or equity goals. Public production, demand-side subsidies, and regulation of private supply create different incentives. Congestion, quality, and geographic access determine actual effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一项目可能同时含多种机制，例如疫苗既有私人健康收益又有感染外部性。应逐项匹配工具：信息问题可能需要披露，信贷约束可能需要融资，地区网络垄断可能需要规制；不能用“重要行业”替代机制诊断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same project may contain several mechanisms; a vaccine, for example, generates both private health benefits and infection externalities. Match instruments to each mechanism: disclosure may address information, finance may address credit constraints, and regulation may address regional network monopoly. Calling a sector “important” is not a substitute for diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：跨河桥梁
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: A River Crossing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一座桥的成本不仅是施工合同，还包括占地、维护和施工期拥堵；收益包括节省时间、事故减少和新增可达机会，但收费收入主要是使用者向运营者的转移。若交通量来自别处道路，需扣除重复计算；若桥诱发新出行，则用需求曲线下的增量剩余而不是把所有时间节省按既有交通量外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cost of a bridge includes not only the construction contract but also land, maintenance, and construction-period congestion. Benefits include time savings, fewer accidents, and new access, while toll revenue is largely a transfer from users to the operator. Traffic diverted from other roads must not be counted twice. For newly induced trips, value incremental surplus under the demand curve rather than extrapolating time savings from existing traffic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若项目“收益”使用建成后的结果却没有无项目反事实，容易把趋势当项目效果；若同时计算工资支出和工人机会成本，可能重复计成本；若只报总净现值而不报谁得谁失，效率结论会掩盖分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using post-completion outcomes without a no-project counterfactual confuses trends with project effects. Counting both wage expenditure and workers' opportunity cost may double-count cost. Reporting only aggregate net present value conceals distribution by failing to show who gains and who loses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么转移支付 100 元不等于社会资源成本正好 100 元？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a transfer of 100 yuan not necessarily a social resource cost of exactly 100 yuan?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它主要重新分配购买力；真实资源成本包括筹资扭曲、管理和受益者行为变化，转移本身不是资源被消耗。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It mainly redistributes purchasing power. Real resource costs include financing distortions, administration, and behavioral responses; the transfer itself is not a resource being consumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 成本—收益分析为什么必须先写反事实？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must cost-benefit analysis begin with a counterfactual?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 只有项目相对无项目造成的增量变化才属于项目；否则会把本来就会发生的趋势和支出归给项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Only incremental changes caused by the project relative to no project belong to the project; otherwise pre-existing trends and expenditures are wrongly attributed to it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 净现值为正为什么还不能结束政策判断？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a positive net present value not finish the policy judgment?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 还需检查估值和贴现假设、风险、分配、不可货币化后果与执行可行性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Valuation and discounting assumptions, risk, distribution, non-monetized consequences, and implementation feasibility still need examination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
