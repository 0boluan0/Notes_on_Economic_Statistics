# 财政支出的替代效应指危机改变税负容忍和政府职责后支出可能转向新的平台
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Peacock–Wiseman displacement effect describes a possible shift to a new expenditure plateau after a crisis changes tolerated taxation and government responsibilities.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Peacock 与 Wiseman 的替代效应，是对公共支出为何可能呈阶梯状变化的历史解释。平时，公众对可接受税负有相对稳定的看法，政府希望增加的支出未必能筹到钱；战争等重大冲击可能同时提高紧急支出需求，并使原先难以接受的筹资方式获得支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Peacock and Wiseman explain why expenditure may move in steps. In ordinary times, views about acceptable taxation constrain feasible spending. A major disturbance can increase emergency needs and make previously unacceptable taxation politically feasible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

冲击结束后，应急开支可以回落，但新形成的职责、持续给付和税负观念未必恢复原状。例如，战时临时任务可能结束，战争抚恤却仍须支付。因此，危机后支出可能落在高于危机前水平的平台；“阶梯”不是说战后还保持战时最高开支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Emergency spending can recede while new responsibilities, continuing benefits and attitudes to taxation persist. Wartime tasks may end but war pensions remain. The post-crisis plateau can exceed the previous level without preserving the wartime peak.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验这个解释，不能只找一张支出上升的图。还要追问：实际支出相对哪条危机前趋势变了？税收制度或公众接受度怎样变了？哪些支出义务延续下来？若只观察 [[财政支出占比]]，也要排除 GDP 暂时下降带来的分母效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rising spending graph is insufficient. Identify the pre-crisis trend, changes in taxation or its acceptance, and obligations that persist. When using the [[财政支出占比|expenditure-to-GDP ratio]], separate changes caused by a temporary GDP decline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原作者明确没有主张替代一定向上，也没有说这一机制能独自解释公共部门演变。它与 [[瓦格纳假说]] 的侧重点不同：前者强调冲击如何改变政治与财政约束，后者强调发展过程中的长期需求和职责变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The authors explicitly reject inevitable upward displacement and a single-mechanism explanation of government growth. The emphasis differs from [[瓦格纳假说|Wagner's hypothesis]]: crisis-induced political and fiscal changes versus long-run development of functions and demand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Peacock and Wiseman, Determinants of Government Expenditure, 1961](https://www.nber.org/system/files/chapters/c2304/c2304.pdf)，正文第 26–28 页：支持可容忍税负、危机后持续职责与平台变化；第 28 页明确说明替代并非必然向上，也不是唯一解释。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Printed pp. 26–28 supports tolerated taxation, continuing obligations and displacement; p. 28 explicitly limits its direction and explanatory scope.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
