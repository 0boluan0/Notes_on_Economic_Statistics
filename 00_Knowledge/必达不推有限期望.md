# Almost-sure hitting and infinite mean waiting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*必达不推有限期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“It will happen eventually with probability one” does not put a useful upper bound on how long it takes. Rare paths can wait so long that they dominate the average. This distinction matters whenever a proof of eventual arrival is used to justify an expected waiting-time calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“它最终以概率一发生”没有为等待时间提供足够强的上界。少量路径可能等得极久，以至于主导平均数。凡是想用最终会到达的证明，来支持平均等待时间计算时，都需要分清这两件事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a target $A$ in a [[离散时间Markov链|Markov chain]], write $\tau_A=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\in A\}$, with $\infty$ for no hit, and use $\Pr_i,E_i$ for probability and [[期望|expectation]] starting at $i$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[离散时间Markov链]]中的目标 $A$，记 $\tau_A=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\in A\}$，从不命中时取 $\infty$；用 $\Pr_i,E_i$ 表示从 $i$ 出发的概率与[[期望]]。那么

$$
E_i\tau_A<\infty\ \Longrightarrow\ \Pr_i(\tau_A<\infty)=1,\qquad\text{but the converse can fail.}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The forward implication follows from $E_i\tau_A\ge N\Pr_i(\tau_A=\infty)$ for every positive integer $N$. The reverse implication is the issue: no individual path has to wait forever for the mean to be infinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正向推论来自：对任意正整数 $N$，都有 $E_i\tau_A\ge N\Pr_i(\tau_A=\infty)$。关键在反向推论：即使几乎没有路径永远等待，平均数仍可能无穷。

> [!example] A fair walk starting one step from the target · 距目标一步的公平游走
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start at $X_0=1$ on the integers. At each step add $+1$ or $-1$, independently and with probability $1/2$ each. The target is $0$, so $\tau_0=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n=0\}$. It is tempting to expect a modest average because the target is so close. In fact,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在整数线上从 $X_0=1$ 出发，每一步独立地以各 $1/2$ 的概率加一或减一。目标是 $0$，因此 $\tau_0=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n=0\}$。目标只有一步之遥，似乎平均等待应该不长；实际却有
>
> $$
> \Pr_1(\tau_0<\infty)=1,\qquad E_1\tau_0=\infty.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> We can establish both facts using finite intervals, without assuming a return-time theorem or applying an optional-stopping identity to an unbounded time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下面用有限区间证明这两点，无须先假设回返时间定理，也无须对无界停时直接套用可选停止恒等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First prove that the target is eventually reached
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先证明最终会命中*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put a temporary upper boundary at an integer $M>1$, and stop on first reaching either $0$ or $M$. From any interior state, $M$ consecutive left steps would reach $0$; their probability is $2^{-M}$. Repeating this bound in blocks of $M$ steps shows that exit occurs almost surely with a finite mean. The [[命中概率第一步方程|first-step hitting equations]] for $u_i=\Pr_i(\tau_0<\tau_M)$ are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在整数 $M>1$ 处设置临时上边界，首次到达 $0$ 或 $M$ 就停止。从任何内部状态出发，连续 $M$ 步向左就会到达 $0$，这段步长符号全向左的概率为 $2^{-M}$。按每段 $M$ 步重复这个界，可以证明一定会退出，而且平均退出时间有限。令 $u_i=\Pr_i(\tau_0<\tau_M)$，[[命中概率第一步方程]]为

$$
u_0=1,\quad u_M=0,\quad u_i=\tfrac12u_{i-1}+\tfrac12u_{i+1}\quad(1\le i<M).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interior equation says successive differences are equal, so the boundary values give $u_i=1-i/M$. In particular, $u_1=1-1/M$. Hitting $0$ before $M$ implies eventually hitting $0$, hence $\Pr_1(\tau_0<\infty)\ge1-1/M$ for every $M$. Letting $M$ increase forces this probability to be one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内部方程说明相邻差值相等，两个边界于是给出 $u_i=1-i/M$，特别地 $u_1=1-1/M$。先到 $0$ 而不是 $M$，当然意味着最终到达 $0$，所以对每个 $M$ 都有 $\Pr_1(\tau_0<\infty)\ge1-1/M$。让 $M$ 不断增大，就得到概率为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Then show why the mean cannot be finite
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*再证明均值为什么不可能有限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T_M=\min(\tau_0,\tau_M)$ be that same two-boundary exit time, and $e_i=E_iT_M$. Its finite means obey the [[期望命中时间方程|expected-time equations]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $T_M=\min(\tau_0,\tau_M)$ 是刚才到任一边界的退出时间，$e_i=E_iT_M$。这些有限均值满足[[期望命中时间方程]]

$$
e_0=e_M=0,\qquad e_i=1+\tfrac12e_{i-1}+\tfrac12e_{i+1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Direct substitution gives $e_i=i(M-i)$; the two boundary values and second-difference equation determine this finite-interval solution uniquely. Thus $E_1T_M=M-1$. On every path, reaching either boundary can only happen earlier than or at the hit of $0$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接代入可得 $e_i=i(M-i)$；两个边界和二阶差分方程唯一确定这个有限区间解。因此 $E_1T_M=M-1$。逐条路径看，到达任一边界的时间不可能晚于命中 $0$，所以

$$
\tau_0\ge T_M,\qquad E_1\tau_0\ge M-1\quad\text{for every integer }M>1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No finite number is at least $M-1$ for all $M$. For instance, the temporary barriers $M=2,10,100$ already give mean lower bounds $1,9,99$ steps. These bounds grow without changing the original target or starting point; therefore $E_1\tau_0=\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有任何有限数能对所有 $M$ 都不小于 $M-1$。例如临时边界 $M=2,10,100$ 已经给出平均等待至少为 $1,9,99$ 步的下界。这些下界不断增大，但原目标与原起点始终没有改变，因此 $E_1\tau_0=\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The infinite first-step recurrence shows the same obstruction. If $m_1=E_1\tau_0$ were finite, the nonnegative equations would force $m_2,m_3,\ldots$ to be finite successively. With $m_0=0$ they would have the form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无限状态的第一步递推也揭示同一个障碍。若 $m_1=E_1\tau_0$ 有限，非负方程会依次迫使 $m_2,m_3,\ldots$ 都有限。结合 $m_0=0$，它们必然具有形式

$$
m_i=1+\tfrac12m_{i-1}+\tfrac12m_{i+1}\quad(i\ge1),\qquad m_i=Ci-i^2\quad(i\ge0),
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $C=m_1+1$ is finite. Eventually $Ci-i^2<0$, contradicting a nonnegative waiting-time mean. This is why one cannot accept a formally solved recurrence without checking its sign on the entire state space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $C=m_1+1$ 为有限数。但 $Ci-i^2$ 最终会小于零，与等待时间均值非负矛盾。因此不能只把递推式形式上解出来，还必须检查整个状态空间上的符号。

> [!warning] What this example does and does not say · 反例的准确范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The tail identity $E_i\tau_A=\sum_{n\ge0}\Pr_i(\tau_A>n)$ explains the distinction. Almost-sure hitting says these tail probabilities tend to zero; finite mean requires their sum to be finite. The example is an infinite-state chain. On a finite time-homogeneous chain, almost-sure hitting from a given state does imply finite mean there, by the uniform block argument in [[期望命中时间方程|the mean-time card]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 尾概率恒等式 $E_i\tau_A=\sum_{n\ge0}\Pr_i(\tau_A>n)$ 解释了区别：几乎必然命中只说明尾概率趋于零，均值有限却要求它们的和有限。本例是无限状态链。对有限时间齐次链，从给定状态几乎必然命中，确实推出该起点的均值有限，证明见[[期望命中时间方程]]中的统一分段界。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Starting at $0$ would instead give $\tau_0=0$. The first **positive return** $\tau_0^+=\inf\{n\ge1:X_n=0\}$ is different: it takes one step to $1$ or $-1$ and then an excursion of the kind just analyzed. By symmetry it returns almost surely but has infinite mean, which is [[正常返与零常返|null recurrence]]. This return classification is a consequence of the example, not a substitute for its hitting-time proof.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若起点就在 $0$，则 $\tau_0=0$。首次**正时刻回返** $\tau_0^+=\inf\{n\ge1:X_n=0\}$ 是另一个量：先走一步到 $1$ 或 $-1$，再经历刚才分析的远游。由对称性，它几乎必然返回，但平均回返时间无穷，这就是[[正常返与零常返|零常返]]。回返分类是该例子的推论，不能代替对命中时间本身的证明。

The barrier comparison above supplies an explicit proof for a walk starting at one.
- [MIT 6.436J, Lecture 24, PDF pp. 2–4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-436j-fundamentals-of-probability-fall-2018/087af3cedbc9def5b156c5e1665ac79c_MIT6_436JF18_lec24.pdf#page=4): distinguishes recurrence from finite mean return and connects the symmetric integer walk with its excursion from $1$ or $-1$. The present proof separates time-zero hitting from positive return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Weber 例 3.3、3.5 与定理 4.2（PDF 第 14–16、18–19 页）支持有限区间破产概率及平均退出时间、无限公平游走的命中结论和期望方程的最小非负解。正文用临时边界比较，明确证明从一出发的结论。
- MIT 6.436J 第 24 讲（PDF 第 2–4 页）区分常返与平均回返有限，并联系对称整数游走及从 $1$ 或 $-1$ 出发的远游。本文证明明确区分包含零的命中与正时刻回返。
