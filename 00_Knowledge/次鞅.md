# Submartingale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*次鞅*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **submartingale** is a process whose conditional average future value is at least its current value. After using the information already available, the predicted change has a nonnegative average. This allows actual losses: the restriction concerns the average over possible futures compatible with what is known now.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**次鞅**是一种过程：给定当前信息后，未来值的条件平均不低于当前值。利用已有信息预测变化时，平均变化非负。它允许实际损失，因为限制的是与当前信息相容的各种未来结果的平均。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A process with favourable average changes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

> [!example] An upward conditional average can include a loss · 条件均值向上，单步仍可能亏损
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start at $X_0=0$. At each independent step, gain $1$ with probability $3/4$ and lose $1$ with probability $1/4$. After any observed history, the next increment still has mean $3/4-1/4=1/2$. If the current total is $x$, the next total has conditional mean
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $X_0=0$ 开始，每一步独立地以概率 $3/4$ 收益 $1$，以概率 $1/4$ 损失 $1$。不论此前看见哪段历史，下一步增量的均值仍为 $3/4-1/4=1/2$。若当前累计值是 $x$，下一步的条件均值就是
>
> $$\frac34(x+1)+\frac14(x-1)=x+\frac12\ge x.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Yet a loss occurs with probability $1/4$. A path that goes down on one step is fully consistent with a nonnegative conditional average change. Each fixed-time value is integrable because $|X_n|\le n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 但仍有 $1/4$ 的概率发生损失。因此，某一步实际下降完全可以与条件平均变化非负同时成立。每个固定时刻都可积，因为 $|X_n|\le n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, let $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ be independent integrable increments with a common mean $\mu\ge0$, and put $X_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\xi_k$, $X_0=0$. Their distributions need not be identical. With the [[自然滤过|natural filtration]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$, the past total is known and the next increment is independent of that history. Thus $E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n+\mu\ge X_n$. Finite sums are integrable, so this proves the submartingale property by the one-step criterion below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，令 $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ 为相互独立的可积增量，共有均值 $\mu\ge0$，设 $X_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\xi_k$、$X_0=0$；它们不必同分布。使用[[自然滤过]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$ 时，过去累计值已知，下一步增量独立于这些历史。因此 $E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n+\mu\ge X_n$。有限和可积，结合下文的一步判据便证明了次鞅性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the definition keeps fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定义必须连同信息一起读*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机过程|stochastic process]] $X=(X_t)$ records a random value at each time on one probability space. A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ records the accumulating information. Being [[适应过程|adapted]] means $X_t$ is already determined by the information available at time $t$, or is $\mathcal F_t$-measurable. The expression $E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$ is the [[条件期望|conditional average]] of a later value using the earlier information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机过程]] $X=(X_t)$ 在同一概率空间上记录各时刻的随机值，[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ 记录逐步累积的信息。[[适应过程|适应]]表示时刻 $t$ 的信息已经足以确定 $X_t$，也就是 $X_t$ 对 $\mathcal F_t$ 可测。$E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$ 是利用较早信息计算较晚取值的[[条件期望|条件平均]]。

> [!important] Submartingale relative to the given filtration · 相对于指定滤过的次鞅
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real adapted process $X$ is a submartingale with respect to $(\mathcal F_t)$ when every $E|X_t|<\infty$ and, for each fixed $s\le t$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$\boxed{E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]\ge X_s\quad\text{almost surely}.}$$
>
> 实值适应过程 $X$ 相对于 $(\mathcal F_t)$ 是次鞅，是指每个时刻都有 $E|X_t|<\infty$，且每对固定的 $s\le t$ 都满足上式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inequality compares two random variables using the **same earlier information**, not two unrelated unconditional averages. “Almost surely” means it can fail only on an event of probability zero, for the fixed pair of times being considered. Per-time integrability is enough; neither a uniform bound over time nor finite second moments are part of this definition. The values may be negative, and $\ge$ allows equality: every [[鞅定义|martingale]] is also a submartingale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不等式比较的是使用**同一份较早信息**得到的两个随机变量，而不是两个脱离信息的普通平均值。“几乎必然”允许当前固定时刻对对应的零概率例外。定义只要求逐时点可积，不要求跨时间统一有界或二阶矩有限。过程值可以为负，$\ge$ 也允许等号，因此每个[[鞅定义|鞅]]都是次鞅。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Checking just the next increment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 离散时间的一步判据

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At integer times, with adaptedness and integrability already established, the condition is equivalent to either of the following one-step inequalities, almost surely for every $n$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在整数时间，已经确认适应性与可积性之后，只需逐期验证下面任一不等式；两者等价，并且对每个 $n$ 都在几乎必然意义下成立：

$$
E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\ge X_n
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
E[X_{n+1}-X_n\mid\mathcal F_n]\ge0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why this covers more than one step, use the [[塔式法则|tower property]] and the fact that conditional expectation preserves inequalities. For two steps,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么一步足以覆盖更远未来？使用[[塔式法则]]，以及条件期望会保持不等式方向这一性质。两步时有

$$
\begin{aligned}
E[X_{n+2}\mid\mathcal F_n]
&=E\!\left[E[X_{n+2}\mid\mathcal F_{n+1}]\mid\mathcal F_n\right]\\
&\ge E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\ge X_n
\quad\text{a.s.}
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeat for any finite number of steps. Conversely, a condition for all later times includes the next time. The general definition does not require independent increments: it asks directly for their conditional mean to be nonnegative. The independent-step model above is one way to guarantee that fact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意有限步数重复即可；反过来，所有较晚时刻的条件当然包含下一时刻。一般定义不要求增量独立，而是直接检查增量条件均值非负；上面的独立步进模型只是保证它的一种方法。

> [!warning] Average direction is not a rising path · 平均方向不等于路径上升
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Taking expectations shows $E[X_t]\ge E[X_s]$. This is a consequence, not a replacement for the conditional inequality. Nor does it assert $X_t\ge X_s$ on every path. If the expectations are constant as well as the process being a submartingale, the conditional gap must vanish; [[恒均值次鞅即鞅|a constant-mean submartingale is a martingale]] explains that stronger conclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 再取期望会得到 $E[X_t]\ge E[X_s]$，但这是推论，不能替代条件不等式，更不意味着每条路径都有 $X_t\ge X_s$。如果已经是次鞅，并且普通均值还保持恒定，那么条件差距必须为零；[[恒均值次鞅即鞅]]说明了这一更强结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negating a submartingale produces a [[超鞅|supermartingale]] with the same filtration. Another important source of submartingales is [[凸变换产生次鞅|convex transformation of a martingale]], provided the transformed values are integrable; that theorem has its own proof using conditional Jensen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给次鞅取负号，会得到相对于同一滤过的[[超鞅]]。次鞅的另一重要来源是[[凸变换产生次鞅|对鞅作凸变换]]，前提是变换后的值可积；该定理另用条件 Jensen 给出证明。
