# 常微分方程是在一个自变量上把未知函数及其有限阶导数联系起来的方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An ordinary differential equation relates an unknown function and finitely many of its derivatives with respect to one independent variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 常微分方程通常写成
> $$
> F(x,y,y',\ldots,y^{(n)})=0.
> $$
> 它的阶数是实际出现的最高导数阶数。一个解不是某个数，而是定义在指定区间上的函数 $y$，其所需导数存在，并且代回方程后在区间每一点都成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An ordinary differential equation has the form $F(x,y,y',\ldots,y^{(n)})=0$ with one independent variable. Its order is the highest derivative that actually appears. A solution is a function on a stated interval whose required derivatives exist and satisfy the equation pointwise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $y'+xy=0$ 是一阶方程，$4y''-y=e^x$ 是二阶方程。验证候选解要计算方程所需的每阶导数、代回并保留定义域；只在几个点数值相等，或只满足初值，都不能证明它是解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation $y'+xy=0$ is first order, while $4y''-y=e^x$ is second order. To verify a candidate, compute every required derivative and substitute on the proposed domain. Agreement at a few points or satisfaction of an initial condition alone does not establish a solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

微分方程描述的是变化规律；初值、边界条件和模型适用区间再从解族中选择轨迹。写出方程不等于证明解存在或唯一，也不等于证明模型正确。[[分离变量]] 只解决具有特定乘积结构的一阶方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ODE describes a law of change. Initial or boundary data and the model's valid interval then select a trajectory. Writing the equation does not prove existence, uniqueness, or empirical adequacy. Separation of variables applies only to a particular first-order structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $y=1/x$ 验算某个方程时必须同时报告 $x\ne0$？
>
> **答案：** 解是“函数加区间”；公式在零点无定义，不能声称方程在穿过零的区间逐点成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** When verifying $y=1/x$ as a solution, why must you also state that $x\ne0$?
>
> **Answer:** A solution consists of a function on a specified interval. The formula is undefined at zero, so it cannot satisfy the equation pointwise on an interval crossing zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
