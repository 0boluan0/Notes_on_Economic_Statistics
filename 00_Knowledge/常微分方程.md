# Ordinary differential equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*常微分方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **ordinary differential equation**, or **ODE**, specifies a rule for an unknown function and its rates of change. In $y'=2x$, the input is $x$, the unknown output is $y(x)$, and the [[导数|derivative]] $y'$ is its slope. The equation asks for a curve whose slope at every input $x$ is $2x$. It does not directly give the height of that curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**常微分方程**，简称 **ODE**，用未知函数及其变化率规定一条规律。在 $y'=2x$ 中，$x$ 是输入，$y(x)$ 是未知的输出，[[导数]] $y'$ 表示斜率。方程要求曲线在每个输入 $x$ 处的斜率等于 $2x$，却没有直接规定曲线的高度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $y=x^2$ and $y=x^2+3$ both work because their derivatives are $2x$. By contrast, $y=2x$ does not work on an interval: its derivative is $2$, which equals $2x$ only at $x=1$. A solution must satisfy the rule throughout its interval, not merely at one tested point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$y=x^2$ 与 $y=x^2+3$ 都符合要求，因为它们的导数都是 $2x$。但 $y=2x$ 不能在一个区间上解这个方程：它的导数是 $2$，只有在 $x=1$ 才等于 $2x$。解必须在整个区间上满足规律，而不只是通过某一个测试点。

## One independent variable, possibly several derivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个自变量，可以出现多阶导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Ordinary” means that differentiation is with respect to **one independent variable**. That variable may be position $x$ or time $t$. The notation $y''$ means differentiate $y$ twice; more generally, $y^{(n)}$ is the [[高阶导数|nth derivative]], where $n$ is a positive integer. A scalar ODE can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“常”指的是只对**一个自变量**求导。这个变量可以是位置 $x$，也可以是时间 $t$。$y''$ 表示连续求导两次；一般地，$y^{(n)}$ 是[[高阶导数|第 n 阶导数]]，其中 $n$ 是正整数。一个标量常微分方程可以写成

$$F\bigl(x,y(x),y'(x),\ldots,y^{(n)}(x)\bigr)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $F$ is the given relation between the input, the function value and its derivatives; $y$ is what we seek. The **order** is the highest derivative that actually appears. Thus $y'+xy=0$ is first order, while $4y''-y=e^x$ is second order. The exponent in $y^2$ is not a derivative order: $y'=y^2$ is still first order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $F$ 是预先给定的关系，把输入、函数值及各阶导数联系起来；需要求的是 $y$。**阶数**由实际出现的最高阶导数决定。因此 $y'+xy=0$ 是一阶方程，$4y''-y=e^x$ 是二阶方程。$y^2$ 中的指数不是导数阶数：$y'=y^2$ 仍是一阶。

> [!abstract] A solution includes its interval · 解要带上区间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the real scalar equations considered here, take a nonempty open interval $I$. A **solution on $I$** is a real-valued function $y:I\to\mathbb R$ whose required derivatives exist and for which the equation is defined and holds at every $x\in I$. The same formula on different intervals can describe different solution branches.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里讨论实数标量方程。取非空开区间 $I$，**区间 I 上的解**是实值函数 $y:I\to\mathbb R$：所需导数都存在，而且对每个 $x\in I$，原方程都有定义并成立。同一个公式放在不同区间上，可以描述不同的解支。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Closed or half-open time intervals can also be used when endpoint behaviour is specified. The differential equation is then checked on the interior, with endpoint values or one-sided derivatives stated according to the chosen convention. Writing an interval explicitly prevents a formula from silently crossing a singular point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若另外规定端点行为，也可以使用闭区间或半开时间区间。此时在内部检验微分方程，并按采用的约定说明端点值或单侧导数。明确写出区间，能防止一个公式被不加说明地延伸过奇点。

## Verify by differentiating and substituting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*通过求导与代回验证*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the second-order equation $4y''-y=e^x$. The candidate $y=e^x/3$ has $y'=e^x/3$ and $y''=e^x/3$, by [[指数函数求导|exponential differentiation]]. Consequently, for every real $x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑二阶方程 $4y''-y=e^x$。候选函数 $y=e^x/3$ 按[[指数函数求导]]得到 $y'=e^x/3$、$y''=e^x/3$。因此对每个实数 $x$，

$$4y''-y=4\frac{e^x}{3}-\frac{e^x}{3}=e^x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proves it is a solution on $\mathbb R$. It does not prove it is the only solution. Verification answers whether a proposed function works; solving for all possibilities and selecting one from data are further questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这证明了它是整个 $\mathbb R$ 上的一个解，却没有证明它是唯一解。验证回答的是某个候选函数是否成立；寻找全部可能性、再根据数据选取一条，是另外的问题。

> [!example] The formula 1/x cannot cross zero · 1/x 不能穿过零点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $y=1/x$, the derivatives are $y'=-1/x^2$ and $y''=2/x^3$. They give $x^2y''+3xy'+y=2/x-3/x+1/x=0$. This proves a solution on $(0,\infty)$, and separately one on $(-\infty,0)$. It proves nothing at zero, where the proposed function is undefined. Saying only “$x\ne0$” describes two intervals; neither is a solution interval that crosses zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $y=1/x$，有 $y'=-1/x^2$、$y''=2/x^3$，从而 $x^2y''+3xy'+y=2/x-3/x+1/x=0$。这证明它在 $(0,\infty)$ 上是一个解，在 $(-\infty,0)$ 上另有一个解；零点处候选函数无定义，无法由这一步验证。只写“$x\ne0$”实际描述了两个区间，不能把它们当作穿过零点的一个解区间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[通解与初值|initial condition]] specifies a starting value, for example $y(0)=3$; boundary data may instead constrain values at different inputs. These conditions supplement the equation. They do not automatically establish existence, uniqueness, or the validity of a physical model. A [[差分方程|difference equation]] instead relates values at discrete steps, whereas an ODE uses derivatives. [[分离变量|Separation of variables]] is one solution method for a special first-order structure, not a procedure for every ODE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[通解与初值|初值条件]]规定一个起始值，例如 $y(0)=3$；边界条件则可能约束不同输入处的值。这些条件补充方程本身，却不会自动证明解存在、唯一或物理模型正确。[[差分方程]]联系离散步上的取值，常微分方程则使用导数。[[分离变量]]只处理一种特殊的一阶结构，并不是所有常微分方程的通用算法。
