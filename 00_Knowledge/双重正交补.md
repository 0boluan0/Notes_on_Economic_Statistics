<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Double orthogonal complements
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*双重正交补*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] of a subspace collects all directions perpendicular to it. Taking the complement **again** asks which vectors are perpendicular to all those perpendicular directions. For a line through the origin in ordinary three-dimensional space, the first operation gives a plane and the second returns the line. In infinite dimensions, the answer can include limit points missing from the original subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个子空间的[[正交补]]收集与它垂直的全部方向。**再取一次**正交补，就是问哪些向量与刚才那些垂直方向全都垂直。在通常三维空间中，从过原点的直线出发，第一次得到平面，第二次回到直线；在无限维中，答案却可能补入原来的子空间中缺失的极限点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A line, a plane, and the return trip
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从直线到平面，再回到直线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the standard [[内积|inner product]] on $\mathbb R^3$ and let $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$, the [[张成|span]] consisting of all multiples of $(1,1,0)^T$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], writing coordinates as a column. The first complement is $S^\perp=\{(a,-a,c)^T:a,c\in\mathbb R\}$. For $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$ to lie in the second complement, it must satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中使用标准[[内积]]，取 $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$，这个[[张成|张成空间]]包含 $(1,1,0)^T$ 的全部倍数。这里 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，将坐标写成列向量。第一次取补得到 $S^\perp=\{(a,-a,c)^T:a,c\in\mathbb R\}$。若 $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$ 要位于第二次的正交补，就必须满足

$$\langle x,(a,-a,c)^T\rangle=a(x_1-x_2)+cx_3=0
\quad\text{for every }a,c\in\mathbb R.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing $a=1,c=0$ gives $x_1=x_2$; choosing $a=0,c=1$ gives $x_3=0$. Hence $(S^\perp)^\perp=S$. We write $S^{\perp\perp}$ for the same operation. Both complements are taken in the **same ambient space and inner product**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $a=1,c=0$ 得 $x_1=x_2$；令 $a=0,c=1$ 得 $x_3=0$，因此 $(S^\perp)^\perp=S$。也用 $S^{\perp\perp}$ 表示同一操作。两次取补必须使用**同一个外部空间和内积**。

> [!important] The finite and Hilbert-space statements / 有限维与 Hilbert 空间的结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $S$ is a finite-dimensional subspace of a real or complex inner-product space $V$, then $S^{\perp\perp}=S$. More generally, if $S$ is any linear subspace of a Hilbert space $H$, then
> $$S^{\perp\perp}=\overline S.$$
> Here $\overline S$ is the closure: the set of limits of sequences from $S$. Thus $S^{\perp\perp}=S$ exactly when $S$ is closed, within the Hilbert-space setting.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果 $S$ 是实或复内积空间 $V$ 的有限维子空间，则 $S^{\perp\perp}=S$。更一般地，如果 $S$ 是 Hilbert 空间 $H$ 的任意线性子空间，则双重正交补等于闭包 $\overline S$，即 $S$ 内序列的全部极限。因此，在 Hilbert 空间这一设定中，$S^{\perp\perp}=S$ 当且仅当 $S$ 闭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Hilbert space is complete for the distance from its inner product: every Cauchy sequence, whose later terms become arbitrarily close to one another, has a limit in the space. Closedness describes the subspace rather than the ambient space. Finite-dimensional subspaces are closed and have their own finite-basis decomposition, so the first statement does not require $V$ to be complete or finite-dimensional.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hilbert 空间在内积给出的距离下完备，即每条 Cauchy 序列（后面的项彼此可以任意接近）都在空间内有极限；闭性则描述子空间是否包含其中收敛序列的极限。有限维子空间都是闭的，而且有自己的有限基分解，所以第一条结论不要求 $V$ 完备，也不要求 $V$ 有限维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why neither inclusion can fail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 两个包含关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First work in a Hilbert space. Every $s\in S$ is perpendicular to every member of $S^\perp$, so $S\subseteq S^{\perp\perp}$. An orthogonal complement is closed: if $x_n$ are perpendicular to each fixed vector $y$ and $x_n\to x$, continuity of the inner product gives $\langle x,y\rangle=\lim_n\langle x_n,y\rangle=0$. Therefore $S^{\perp\perp}$ contains all limits of sequences from $S$, proving $\overline S\subseteq S^{\perp\perp}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先在 Hilbert 空间中证明。每个 $s\in S$ 都与 $S^\perp$ 的每个成员垂直，所以 $S\subseteq S^{\perp\perp}$。正交补总是闭的：如果 $x_n$ 都与每个固定的 $y$ 垂直，而且 $x_n\to x$，内积的连续性就给出 $\langle x,y\rangle=\lim_n\langle x_n,y\rangle=0$。因此 $S^{\perp\perp}$ 包含 $S$ 内序列的全部极限，得到 $\overline S\subseteq S^{\perp\perp}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the reverse inclusion, take $x\in S^{\perp\perp}$. The closure $\overline S$ is a closed linear subspace. Apply the [[正交分解定理|closed-subspace orthogonal decomposition]] to write $x=s+e$ with $s\in\overline S$ and $e\in(\overline S)^\perp$. The same limit argument shows $(\overline S)^\perp=S^\perp$: being perpendicular to $S$ also makes a vector perpendicular to its limits. Since $x\in S^{\perp\perp}$ and $e\in S^\perp$, while $s\perp e$, we get
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向包含中，取 $x\in S^{\perp\perp}$。闭包 $\overline S$ 是闭线性子空间，可以使用[[正交分解定理|闭子空间的正交分解]]，写成 $x=s+e$，其中 $s\in\overline S$、$e\in(\overline S)^\perp$。同样的极限论证给出 $(\overline S)^\perp=S^\perp$：垂直于 $S$ 的向量，也垂直于其极限。因为 $x\in S^{\perp\perp}$、$e\in S^\perp$，且 $s\perp e$，有

$$0=\langle x,e\rangle=\langle s+e,e\rangle
=\langle s,e\rangle+\langle e,e\rangle=\|e\|^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strict positivity of the inner product forces $e=0$, so $x=s\in\overline S$. This proves equality. For finite-dimensional $S$ in an arbitrary inner-product space, apply the finite-dimensional decomposition directly to $S$ in the same argument; no closure or completeness step is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内积的严格正性迫使 $e=0$，因此 $x=s\in\overline S$，等式得证。如果 $S$ 是任意内积空间中的有限维子空间，则在同一论证中直接对 $S$ 使用有限维正交分解，不需要闭包或完备性步骤。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A dense subspace can still miss vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么闭性不能省略

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\ell^2$ be the Hilbert space of infinite sequences $x=(x_1,x_2,\ldots)$ satisfying $\sum_{j=1}^\infty|x_j|^2<\infty$, with inner product $\langle x,y\rangle=\sum_j\overline{x_j}y_j$. Let $c_{00}$ be its subspace of sequences with only finitely many nonzero entries. Truncating any $\ell^2$ sequence after $N$ entries gives a member of $c_{00}$, and the squared error is the tail $\sum_{j>N}|x_j|^2\to0$. Thus $c_{00}$ is dense: its closure is all of $\ell^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\ell^2$ 表示满足 $\sum_{j=1}^\infty|x_j|^2<\infty$ 的无限序列 $x=(x_1,x_2,\ldots)$ 所构成的 Hilbert 空间，内积为 $\langle x,y\rangle=\sum_j\overline{x_j}y_j$。令 $c_{00}$ 是其中只有有限个非零项的序列子空间。把任意 $\ell^2$ 序列截断到前 $N$ 项，便得到 $c_{00}$ 的成员，误差平方是趋于零的尾和 $\sum_{j>N}|x_j|^2$。因此 $c_{00}$ 稠密，即闭包是整个 $\ell^2$。

> [!example] Arbitrarily close without attaining the target / 可以任意接近，却取不到目标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sequence $x=(1/2,1/4,1/8,\ldots)$ lies in $\ell^2$ because its squared length is $\sum_{j=1}^\infty4^{-j}=1/3$, a convergent [[几何级数|geometric series]]. It is not in $c_{00}$, since every entry is nonzero. Its $N$-term truncation $x^{(N)}$ satisfies $\|x-x^{(N)}\|^2=4^{-N}/3\to0$. Thus $c_{00}$ is not closed, and the distance from $x$ to $c_{00}$ has infimum zero without a nearest point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 序列 $x=(1/2,1/4,1/8,\ldots)$ 属于 $\ell^2$，因为长度平方是收敛[[几何级数|等比级数]] $\sum_{j=1}^\infty4^{-j}=1/3$；但每项都非零，所以不属于 $c_{00}$。前 $N$ 项截断 $x^{(N)}$ 的误差平方为 $4^{-N}/3\to0$。因此 $c_{00}$ 不闭，$x$ 到它的距离下确界为零，却不存在最近点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every coordinate vector (one entry 1, all others zero) belongs to $c_{00}$. A vector perpendicular to all of them has every coordinate zero. Hence $c_{00}^\perp=\{0\}$ and $c_{00}^{\perp\perp}=\ell^2$, not $c_{00}$. This also shows why a zero orthogonal complement implies **density**, rather than equality, for an arbitrary subspace of a Hilbert space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个坐标向量（某一项为 1，其余为零）都属于 $c_{00}$。若与它们全都垂直，每个坐标就必须为零。因此 $c_{00}^\perp=\{0\}$，而 $c_{00}^{\perp\perp}=\ell^2$，并不是 $c_{00}$。这也说明，在 Hilbert 空间的一般子空间中，正交补为零只能推出**稠密**，不能直接推出等于整个空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## If the starting object is only a set, take its span
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*起点只是集合时，必须先考虑张成空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any subset $U$ of a Hilbert space, $U^\perp=(\operatorname{span}U)^\perp$, because testing perpendicularity against all elements also tests every finite linear combination. Applying the subspace result therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 Hilbert 空间中的任意子集 $U$，有 $U^\perp=(\operatorname{span}U)^\perp$，因为与全部成员垂直，也就与它们的每个有限线性组合垂直。把子空间结论用于张成空间，得到

$$U^{\perp\perp}=\overline{\operatorname{span}U}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In finite-dimensional ambient space the closure can be omitted, but the span cannot. For example, the singleton $U=\{(1,1,0)^T\}$ has the same first complement as the opening line, so its double complement is the whole line $S$, not the singleton. The empty-set case also fits: its span is $\{0\}$, and $\varnothing^{\perp\perp}=\{0\}$. The ambient space, scalar field and inner product remain fixed throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限维外部空间中可以省去闭包，却不能省去张成。例如单点集合 $U=\{(1,1,0)^T\}$ 与开头直线有相同的第一次正交补，故双重正交补是整条直线 $S$，不是原单点。空集也符合规则：其张成是 $\{0\}$，所以 $\varnothing^{\perp\perp}=\{0\}$。整个过程中，外部空间、标量域和内积都保持固定。
