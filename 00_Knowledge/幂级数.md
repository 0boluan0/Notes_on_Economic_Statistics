# Power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*幂级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A power series adds constant multiples of successive powers of the displacement from a fixed centre. Choosing an input turns that expression into an ordinary infinite sum. Different inputs can give very different answers: one sum may converge, while another keeps growing or oscillating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

幂级数把相对于固定中心的偏移量，依次取零次、一次、二次等幂，乘上固定系数后相加。选定一个输入，就得到一个普通的无穷和。换一个输入，结果可能完全不同：有的和收敛，有的不断增长或振荡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with $1+(x-2)+(x-2)^2+\cdots$. The centre is $2$, so it is the displacement $x-2$ that gets raised to each power. At $x=5/2$, this becomes $1+1/2+1/4+\cdots$, which sums to $2$ by the [[几何级数|geometric-series formula]]. At $x=3$, it becomes $1+1+1+\cdots$ and diverges. At the centre $x=2$, only the constant term remains, giving $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看 $1+(x-2)+(x-2)^2+\cdots$。中心是 $2$，被逐次乘方的是偏移量 $x-2$。代入 $x=5/2$，得到 $1+1/2+1/4+\cdots$，由[[几何级数]]公式知和为 $2$；代入 $x=3$，得到 $1+1+1+\cdots$，发散；在中心 $x=2$，只留下常数项，和为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What stays fixed, and what varies?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些量固定，哪些量变化？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here we use real inputs, a fixed real centre $a$, and a sequence of finite real coefficients $c_0,c_1,c_2,\ldots$. The coefficients may depend on their index $n$, but not on the input $x$. The power series about $a$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡使用实数输入、固定实数中心 $a$，以及一列有限实数系数 $c_0,c_1,c_2,\ldots$。系数可以随索引 $n$ 改变，却不随输入 $x$ 改变。以 $a$ 为中心的幂级数写为

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n
=c_0+c_1(x-a)+c_2(x-a)^2+\cdots.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ gives the nonnegative integer power. Its coefficient $c_n$ scales that power: for instance, $c_2=3$ makes the quadratic contribution $3(x-a)^2$. The zeroth-power factor is the constant function $1$, including at $x=a$; this convention makes the first term $c_0$. Every positive-power term vanishes at the centre, so the series always sums to $c_0$ there. A series beginning at $n=1$ fits the same definition by setting $c_0=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

索引 $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ 指定非负整数次幂。系数 $c_n$ 决定该次幂的倍数：例如 $c_2=3$ 时，二次项是 $3(x-a)^2$。零次幂因子按恒等于 $1$ 的函数理解，在 $x=a$ 也如此，因此首项是 $c_0$。其余正次幂项在中心全部为零，所以中心处的和一定是 $c_0$。从 $n=1$ 开始的级数，也可令 $c_0=0$ 后纳入同一定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A function value is a limit of finite polynomials
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*函数值来自有限多项式的极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[无穷级数|partial sum]] through index $N$ is $S_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{N}c_n(x-a)^n$, a polynomial with $N+1$ displayed terms, some of which may be zero. For a fixed $x$, convergence means that the numbers $S_N(x)$ approach a finite real limit as $N\to\infty$. Where this happens, define $f(x)=\lim_{N\to\infty}S_N(x)$. The set of such inputs is the series' domain of convergence. Writing infinitely many terms does not by itself define a real value at every input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加到索引 $N$ 的[[无穷级数|部分和]]是 $S_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{N}c_n(x-a)^n$，这是一个写出 $N+1$ 项的多项式，其中部分项可能为零。固定 $x$ 后，收敛要求这些数 $S_N(x)$ 在 $N\to\infty$ 时趋于有限实数。对这样的输入，定义 $f(x)=\lim_{N\to\infty}S_N(x)$；这些输入组成级数的收敛域。写下无穷多项，并不自动在每个输入上定义一个实数值。

> [!example] One expression, two domains / 同一表达式涉及两个定义域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the opening example, the geometric ratio is $r=x-2$. Thus
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 开篇例子的几何公比为 $r=x-2$，所以
>
> $$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x-2)^n=\frac1{1-(x-2)}=\frac1{3-x},\qquad |x-2|<1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The series converges exactly for $1<x<3$. At $x=1$, its partial sums alternate between $1$ and $0$; at $x=3$, they increase without bound. At $x=4$, the fraction has the finite value $-1$, but the series is $1+2+4+\cdots$ and diverges. The domain of the fraction is larger than the region where this particular series represents it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 级数恰在 $1<x<3$ 收敛。在 $x=1$，部分和在 $1$ 与 $0$ 之间交替；在 $x=3$，部分和无界增长。代入 $x=4$，分式虽然得到有限值 $-1$，级数却是发散的 $1+2+4+\cdots$。分式的定义域，比这个特定级数表示它的范围更大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[幂级数收敛半径|radius of convergence]] describes how far this convergence region extends from the centre. It may be zero, meaning that only the centre works, or infinite, meaning that every real input works. When the radius is finite and positive, [[幂级数端点判别|endpoint testing]] decides the two remaining boundary inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[幂级数收敛半径]]描述收敛范围从中心向外能延伸多远。半径可能为零，此时只有中心可用；也可能为无穷，此时所有实数输入都可用。若半径有限且为正，再由[[幂级数端点判别]]处理剩下的两个边界输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Polynomials and Taylor series fit inside this definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*多项式与 Taylor 级数的位置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A polynomial is a power series with all sufficiently late coefficients zero. For example, $3+2(x-2)-(x-2)^2$ has $c_0=3,c_1=2,c_2=-1$ and $c_n=0$ for $n\ge3$. Its partial sums stop changing once $N\ge2$, so it converges for every real input. If instead the coefficients are chosen from a function's successive derivatives at the centre, divided by the corresponding factorials, the result is its [[Taylor级数|Taylor series]]. Those derivative coefficients specify a candidate representation; proving it equals the original function is the separate question in [[Taylor级数等于函数|Taylor-series equality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多项式是后续系数最终全为零的幂级数。例如，$3+2(x-2)-(x-2)^2$ 对应 $c_0=3,c_1=2,c_2=-1$，且 $n\ge3$ 时 $c_n=0$。从 $N\ge2$ 起，部分和不再变化，所以每个实数输入都收敛。如果系数改为某函数在中心的各阶导数除以对应阶乘，就得到它的 [[Taylor级数]]。这些导数系数给出候选表示；是否等于原函数，还需处理[[Taylor级数等于函数]]中的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an [[普通生成函数|ordinary generating function]], the same notation can also be used formally, with the variable serving as a coefficient marker rather than a numerical input. This card uses the numerical interpretation: choose $x$, form partial sums, and test their limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[普通生成函数]]中，相同记号也能按形式方式使用：变量只标记系数位置，并不代入数值。本卡使用数值解释，即选定 $x$、形成部分和，再判断其极限。
