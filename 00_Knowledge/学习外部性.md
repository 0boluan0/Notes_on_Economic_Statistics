# 学习外部性是未被投入者充分取得的学习收益惠及其他生产者的现象

本卡所说的学习外部性（learning externality），是生产者通过实践、试验或培训积累的知识改善了其他生产者的能力，而这部分收益未通过价格或契约被原投入者充分取得。它是[[外部性|正外部性]]的一种，关键在“谁承担学习投入、谁取得收益、补偿是否进入投入决策”，而不只是成本随经验下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, a learning externality arises when knowledge accumulated through practice, experimentation or training improves other producers' capabilities, but the originator does not fully capture that benefit through prices or contracts. It is a [[外部性|positive externality]]: the key is who bears the investment, who benefits, and whether compensation enters the investment decision—not simply whether experience lowers costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[学习效应]]可以完全发生在一家企业内部：企业从反复生产中提高效率，并通过自己的成本节省取得回报。只有进一步指出知识如何外溢，以及哪些收益没有被内部化，才能提出学习外部性的理由。观察到低成本、大企业或高累计产量，都不能单独完成这个判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[学习效应|Learning effects]] can be wholly internal: a firm learns through repeated production and captures the return through its own cost savings. An externality claim additionally needs a transmission mechanism and uncaptured benefits. Low costs, large firms or high cumulative output alone do not establish it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，甲厂改进工艺后，自己的每件工时从10降到8，这是内部学习的教学例子。若甲厂培养的技术人员使乙厂也掌握该工艺，乙厂因此额外节约资源，且这部分知识收益没有通过有关安排补偿甲厂，才出现所讨论的外部收益。人员流动本身不是充分证据，还要核实知识确实被传递、采用并产生了增量收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a teaching example, Plant A reduces labor hours per unit from 10 to 8 after improving its process: that is internal learning. If workers trained at A transfer the process to B, creating additional resource savings for which A is not compensated, there is an external benefit. Worker mobility alone is insufficient evidence; knowledge must actually be transmitted, adopted and generate an incremental benefit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

未获补偿的外部收益可能使私人学习投入低于社会合意水平，但不直接推出某行业应获得保护或某项补贴的数额。还要检验[[外部性内部化|私人内部化安排]]、收益的额外性、政策的执行与融资成本，并区分内部生产率改善和真正的外溢；不能用“学习”一词省略这些步骤。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Uncompensated benefits can make private learning investment lower than socially desirable, but do not by themselves establish a case for protection or a subsidy amount. Assessment must consider [[外部性内部化|private internalization]], additionality, implementation and financing costs, while separating internal productivity improvements from actual spillovers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Fernandes 与 Reed（2026），[Industrial Policy for Development](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/b98ce474-f652-4b58-8c74-a65210da7d4c/content#page=157)，2026年3月31日修订版，印刷125–126页（PDF157–158）：支持私人收益与外部收益的区别、学习与知识扩散、培训后人员流动等机制，并强调外部性大小随行业和研究情境变化。
- 外部性与内部化的通用边界复用所链接共享对象。两厂工时例为教学构造；10与8不是来源中的估计，也不据此计算政策最优值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The report supports the private–external benefit distinction and knowledge-diffusion mechanisms, with context-dependent magnitudes. Existing shared objects supply the general incentive boundary. The two-plant example is constructed rather than estimated and does not identify an optimal policy.
