# 股权自由现金流是完成经营再投资和债务现金收支后可供股权投资者分配的现金流
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Free cash flow to equity is cash available to equity investors after operating reinvestment and debt cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股权自由现金流（FCFE）衡量在维持所预测经营和融资安排后，股东还可以取走多少现金。与面向全部资本提供者的[[企业自由现金流|FCFF]]不同，它已经考虑债权人的利息、还本以及新借款。这里的“可以分配”不等于公司当年实际发出的股利；FCFE 也可能为负，表示既定预测还需要股权资金支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFE measures cash available to shareholders after the forecast operating and financing needs are met. Unlike [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]], it accounts for debt interest, repayments, and new borrowing. Availability is not actual dividends; negative FCFE indicates an equity funding requirement under the forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对只有普通股和债务的简化非金融企业，使用一致的经营与非现金营运资本口径，假设利息可按税率 $T$ 当期抵税、没有其他利润或现金调整：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a simplified nonfinancial firm with only common equity and debt, use consistent operating and non-cash working-capital definitions. Assume current interest deductibility at tax rate $T$ and no other profit or cash adjustments:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$FCFE_t=FCFF_t-I_t(1-T)+NB_t,$$
$$NB_t=\text{新借款现金收入}_t-\text{偿还本金现金支出}_t.$$

由 $NI=NOPAT-I(1-T)$ 还可写成 $FCFE=NI+Dep-CapEx-\Delta NWC+NB$。例如 FCFF 为 50，利息 8，税率 25%，借入 15、偿还本金 10，则 FCFE 为 $50-6+5=49$。如果只发股利 30，余下 19 的留存和未来用途仍需在估值中交代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using $NI=NOPAT-I(1-T)$ also gives $FCFE=NI+Dep-CapEx-\Delta NWC+NB$. FCFF of 50, interest of 8, a 25% tax rate, borrowing of 15, and principal repayment of 10 produce FCFE of 49. If dividends are only 30, the retained 19 and its future use still matter for valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债务账面余额的增加不一定等于净借款现金：汇率折算、非现金租赁或债务转股等都会使两者不同。有优先股、非经营收益或现金税差异时，也须把分配范围与调整列清。FCFE 按[[贴现率口径匹配|股权资本成本]]折现；不能因为公式从 FCFF 开始，就继续使用 WACC。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changes in book debt need not equal net borrowing cash because of currency translation, non-cash leases, or debt conversion. Preferred claims, non-operating income, and cash-tax differences require explicit adjustments. Discount FCFE at the [[贴现率口径匹配|cost of equity]], not WACC.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
