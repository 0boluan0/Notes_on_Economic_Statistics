# 泊松过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Poisson process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 描述随机事件的到达时间、计数和长期发生率。
> **具体锚点：** 若电话在不相交时间段独立到达且稳定平均每小时 6 次，计数可用齐次泊松过程近似，等待时间则为指数分布。
> **核心难点：** Poisson 计数与 exponential 间隔是同一结构的两面；更新过程只保留独立同分布间隔，不再要求无记忆。
> **为什么重要：** 排队、可靠性、保险索赔和跳跃风险模型都从到达过程开始。
> **继续：** 先掌握齐次 Poisson，再看非齐次、复合与 renewal；状态依赖到达应转向 [[连续时间马尔可夫链]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It describes arrival times, counts, and long-run event rates.
> **Concrete anchor:** If telephone calls arrive independently in disjoint intervals at a stable average rate of six per hour, a homogeneous Poisson process may approximate the counts, while waiting times are exponentially distributed.
> **Central difficulty:** Poisson counts and exponential interarrival times are two views of the same structure. A renewal process retains iid interarrival times but no longer requires memorylessness.
> **Why it matters:** Queueing, reliability, insurance claims, and jump-risk models all begin with arrival processes.
> **Continue with:** First master the homogeneous Poisson process, then non-homogeneous and compound variants; for general iid interarrivals see [[更新过程|renewal processes]]. State-dependent arrivals call for [[连续时间马尔可夫链|continuous-time Markov chains]].

## 齐次泊松过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The homogeneous Poisson process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计数过程 N(t) 从 0 开始、有独立平稳增量，并满足短区间内一次到达概率约为 $\lambda h$、多次到达为 $o(h)$。于是 $N(t)\sim Poisson(\lambda t)$，均值和方差均为 $\lambda t$。这些假设都要有业务含义，不能仅凭计数直方图像 Poisson 就确认。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The counting process $N(t)$ starts at zero, has independent stationary increments, and has probability approximately $\lambda h$ of one arrival and $o(h)$ of multiple arrivals in a short interval of length $h$. Consequently, $N(t)\sim Poisson(\lambda t)$, with both mean and variance equal to $\lambda t$. Each assumption needs a substantive interpretation; a Poisson-looking count histogram is not enough to establish the process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由独立平稳增量，可将区间切成小段并用 Binomial 极限得到 Poisson 计数。这一推导解释了两个必要直觉：不同不相交窗口没有共同冲击，且发生率在时间平移后不变。若这两点不可信，参数 $\lambda$ 即使拟合得很好也缺乏过程含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independent stationary increments allow an interval to be divided into small pieces, with a Binomial limit yielding the Poisson count. This derivation exposes two essential intuitions: disjoint windows share no shocks, and the event rate is invariant under a time shift. If either is implausible, a well-fitted $\lambda$ has little process-level meaning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 到达间隔与条件分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interarrival times and conditional distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相邻到达间隔独立同分布为 $Exp(\lambda)$；指数分布的无记忆性对应“从现在起等待多久与已等多久无关”。给定 $N(t)=n$，n 个到达时刻分布等同于 n 个 $[0,t]$ 均匀变量的次序统计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Successive interarrival times are iid $Exp(\lambda)$. The memoryless property says that the remaining wait does not depend on how long one has already waited. Conditional on $N(t)=n$, the $n$ arrival times have the same distribution as the order statistics of $n$ independent Uniform$[0,t]$ variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 n 次到达时刻 $S_n$ 是 n 个指数间隔之和，故服从 Gamma/Erlang 分布。计数事件与等待事件互为补集：$\{N(t)\ge n\}=\{S_n\le t\}$。遇到“第几次到达前是否完成”时，选择更容易的一侧计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The time $S_n$ of the $n$th arrival is the sum of $n$ exponential interarrivals and therefore has a Gamma/Erlang distribution. Counting and waiting events are complementary descriptions: $\{N(t)\ge n\}=\{S_n\le t\}$. For questions about whether the $n$th arrival occurs by a deadline, compute on whichever side is simpler.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非齐次与复合泊松
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Non-homogeneous and compound Poisson processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非齐次 Poisson 用时间变化强度 $\lambda(t)$，区间计数均值为积分 $\Lambda(a,b)=\int_a^b\lambda(s)ds$，增量仍独立但不平稳。复合 Poisson 给每次到达附加独立标记/损失 $Y_i$，总量 $S(t)=\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)}Y_i$；其方差同时包含频率和严重度波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A non-homogeneous Poisson process has a time-varying intensity $\lambda(t)$, so the expected count on an interval is $\Lambda(a,b)=\int_a^b\lambda(s)ds$. Its increments remain independent but are no longer stationary. A compound Poisson process attaches an independent mark or loss $Y_i$ to each arrival, giving $S(t)=\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)}Y_i$; its variance reflects both frequency and severity uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Y_i$ 的均值为 $\mu$、方差为 $\sigma^2$，则 $E[S(t)]=\lambda t\mu$，$Var(S(t))=\lambda t(\sigma^2+\mu^2)=\lambda tE[Y^2]$。第二项不是重复计算，而是随机到达次数带来的额外方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the $Y_i$ have mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, then $E[S(t)]=\lambda t\mu$ and $Var(S(t))=\lambda t(\sigma^2+\mu^2)=\lambda tE[Y^2]$. The second component is not double-counting; it is the additional variance caused by the random number of arrivals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：呼叫中心
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: a call centre*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若呼叫率为每小时 6 次，则半小时计数 $N(0.5)\sim Poisson(3)$。半小时无呼叫概率为 $e^{-3}$；等待首个呼叫超过 30 分钟的概率也是 $P(S_1>0.5)=e^{-6(0.5)}=e^{-3}$。这不是两个巧合公式，而是计数—等待的同一事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a call rate of six per hour, the half-hour count is $N(0.5)\sim Poisson(3)$. The probability of no call in that half hour is $e^{-3}$. The probability that the first-call waiting time exceeds 30 minutes is likewise $P(S_1>0.5)=e^{-6(0.5)}=e^{-3}$. These are not two coincidentally matching formulas; they describe the same count–waiting event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 建模诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查事件定义、重复时间戳、暴露量、季节性和过度离散。若到达互相激发、被资源限制或强度受状态影响，Poisson 独立增量可能失败。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the event definition, duplicate timestamps, exposure, seasonality, and overdispersion. If arrivals excite one another, are constrained by capacity, or have state-dependent intensity, the independent-increments assumption of a Poisson process may fail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值显著小于方差可能提示聚集或未建模的强度异质性；均值大于方差可能提示容量约束或抑制。诊断应在可比暴露窗口中进行，并先处理日内/周内季节性，否则混合不同强度本身就会制造过度离散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variance substantially above the mean may indicate clustering or unmodelled intensity heterogeneity; variance below the mean may indicate capacity constraints or inhibition. Diagnostics should compare windows with equal exposure and first account for intraday or weekly seasonality, since mixing different rates itself creates overdispersion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Poisson 过程的计数率和指数等待时间如何对应？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How do Poisson counts correspond to exponential waiting times?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 强度为 λ 的 Poisson 过程相邻间隔 IID 服从 Exp(λ)；反过来 IID 指数间隔生成该 Poisson 计数过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> In a Poisson process of rate $\lambda$, successive interarrivals are iid Exp$(\lambda)$. Conversely, iid exponential interarrivals generate that Poisson counting process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 给定一小时内恰有 n 次到达，为什么到达点像均匀撒在区间？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional on exactly $n$ arrivals in an hour, why do their times look like uniform points scattered over the interval?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Poisson 过程条件于总计数后，次序到达时刻具有 n 个独立均匀点排序后的联合分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Conditional on the total count, ordered Poisson arrival times have the joint distribution of $n$ independent uniform points arranged in increasing order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 计数均值接近方差是否足以确认 Poisson 过程？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Is a count mean close to its variance sufficient to establish a Poisson process?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不足。还需检查独立增量、稳定或明确变化的强度、事件定义与暴露量；不同过程也可能偶然给出相似边际矩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. Independent increments, a stable or explicitly modelled intensity, event definitions, and exposure must also be checked. Other processes can coincidentally have similar marginal moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
