# 结构转型、生产率重配与非正规部门
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Structural Transformation, Productivity Reallocation, and Informality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释劳动力、产出和企业如何在农业、工业、现代服务与非正规活动之间重新配置，并判断这种移动是否真正提高总生产率。
> **具体锚点：** 一名劳动者从每人产出 2 的农业转到每人产出 8 的制造业会提高总体生产率；若他只转到每人产出 1.5 的拥挤非正规服务，“离开农业”反而会拖累总体。
> **核心难点：** 总生产率变化既来自每个部门内部改善，也来自就业份额在部门间移动；后者不保证为正。
> **为什么重要：** 它防止把城市化、工业份额或非农就业任一指标当成发展成功的自动证据。
> **继续：** 结构转型的经典起点见 [[Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型]]；城市失业与非正规就业见 [[Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains how labor, output, and firms are reallocated among agriculture, industry, modern services, and informal activity, and whether that movement actually raises aggregate productivity.
> **Concrete anchor:** Moving a worker from agriculture with output per worker of 2 to manufacturing with output per worker of 8 raises aggregate productivity. Moving the worker into congested informal services with output per worker of 1.5 can make “leaving agriculture” reduce it.
> **Central difficulty:** Aggregate productivity changes through improvements within sectors and shifts in employment shares across sectors. The reallocation component is not guaranteed to be positive.
> **Why it matters:** It prevents urbanization, an industrial share, or nonfarm employment from being treated as automatic proof of successful development.
> **Continue:** For the classic starting point, see [[Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型|The Lewis–Fei–Ranis Dual-Economy Model]]. For urban unemployment and informal work, see [[Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门|Todaro Migration, Urban Unemployment, and Informality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 什么在变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is changing?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发展常伴农业就业/产出份额下降、工业和现代服务上升、城市化及企业规模变化。生产率提升来自部门内改进与劳动跨部门重配；后者不保证为正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Development often accompanies falling agricultural shares in employment and output, rising industry and modern services, urbanization, and changing firm size. Productivity gains come from both within-sector improvement and labor reallocation across sectors; the latter is not guaranteed to be positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能只看产出份额。一个资本密集的资源部门可有很高产出，却吸纳很少劳动力；就业重配仍可停滞。同样，就业离开农业可能是进入高生产率工厂，也可能是进入低生产率街头服务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Output shares alone are insufficient. A capital-intensive resource sector can produce a great deal while absorbing little labor, leaving employment reallocation stagnant. Likewise, leaving agriculture can mean entering a productive factory or entering low-productivity street services.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 生产率分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Productivity decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设部门 $i$ 的劳动生产率为 $p_i=Y_i/L_i$，就业份额为 $s_i=L_i/L$，总体劳动生产率为

$$P=\frac{Y}{L}=\sum_i s_i p_i.$$

两期变化可用一种常见近似分为

$$\Delta P\approx\sum_i s_{i,0}\Delta p_i+\sum_i p_{i,0}\Delta s_i+\sum_i\Delta s_i\Delta p_i.$$

第一项是部门内改善，第二项是按初始生产率计的重配，第三项是交互项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let labor productivity in sector $i$ be $p_i=Y_i/L_i$, its employment share be $s_i=L_i/L$, and aggregate labor productivity be

$$P=\frac{Y}{L}=\sum_i s_i p_i.$$

A common two-period approximation is

$$\Delta P\approx\sum_i s_{i,0}\Delta p_i+\sum_i p_{i,0}\Delta s_i+\sum_i\Delta s_i\Delta p_i.$$

The first term is within-sector improvement, the second is reallocation valued at initial productivity, and the third is an interaction term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，农业初始占 60% 就业且 $p_A=2$，现代部门占 40% 且 $p_M=8$，则 $P_0=4.4$。若 10% 劳动力从农业转入现代部门且部门内生产率不变，$P_1=0.5\times2+0.5\times8=5$，纯重配提高 0.6。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, agriculture initially employs 60% of labor with $p_A=2$, while the modern sector employs 40% with $p_M=8$, so $P_0=4.4$. If 10% of labor moves from agriculture to the modern sector while sector productivities stay fixed, $P_1=0.5\times2+0.5\times8=5$. Pure reallocation raises productivity by 0.6.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 双重经济与非正规部门
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dual economies and the informal sector*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

高低生产率部门并存反映技能、资本、制度和进入壁垒。非正规部门既可吸纳就业也可限制扩张；政策不能只靠压缩其统计占比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coexistence of high- and low-productivity sectors reflects skills, capital, institutions, and barriers to entry. Informal activity can absorb employment but also constrain scale, so policy cannot focus only on shrinking its measured share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非正规不等于同一种“落后企业”。有的企业是生存型自雇，有的在正规和非正规之间调整规模，有的因信贷、土地、登记或执法壁垒无法扩张。诊断应看生产率、增长意愿、税费与服务交换，而不只看是否登记。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Informality does not denote one uniform class of backward firms. Some are survival self-employment, some adjust scale across formal and informal margins, and some cannot expand because of credit, land, registration, or enforcement barriers. Diagnosis should examine productivity, growth intent, and the exchange between taxes and public services, not registration alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断原则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostic principle*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先识别最紧约束及其互补条件，不把一国成功经验当普遍配方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the binding constraint and its complements before treating another country's success as a universal recipe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 看到农业就业份额下降，却不检查目的部门生产率和就业质量。
- 把总生产率增长全部归于重配，不做部门内/跨部门分解。
- 用取消登记费用作为所有非正规企业的唯一处方，忽略需求、技能、信贷与公共服务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Observing a fall in agricultural employment without checking destination-sector productivity and job quality.
- Attributing all aggregate productivity growth to reallocation without separating within-sector and cross-sector components.
- Treating lower registration fees as the single remedy for every informal firm while ignoring demand, skills, credit, and public services.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 劳动力离开农业是否一定提高总生产率？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does labor leaving agriculture necessarily raise aggregate productivity?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不一定；目的部门若同样低生产率或存在拥挤/失业，重配贡献可很小甚至为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. If the destination is equally unproductive or suffers congestion or unemployment, the reallocation contribution can be small or negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释“部门内改善”和“跨部门重配”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words within-sector improvement and cross-sector reallocation.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 前者是同一部门的每名劳动者产出上升；后者是就业份额从较低生产率部门移向较高生产率部门。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The first means output per worker rises inside a given sector. The second means employment shares move from lower- to higher-productivity sectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一项“正规化”政策应如何判断成功？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How should the success of a formalization policy be judged?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不只看登记数，还要看生存、规模、生产率、信贷、劳动者保护、税收和公共服务使用，并与政策成本比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Look beyond registrations to survival, scale, productivity, credit, worker protection, taxes, and public-service use, and compare those gains with policy costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
