# 政府失灵指政府无法或不愿主要服务公民利益

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government failure is the inability or unwillingness of government to act primarily in its citizens' interests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡采用 Saez 公共经济学课程的用法：**政府失灵是政府无法或不愿主要服务公民利益的情形**。信息和行政能力限制可以使政府无法做到，决策者的激励与公众目标不一致可以使其不愿做到。判断某个具体政策是否属于政府失灵，必须先说明“公民利益”采用什么福利、分配或权利标准，以及比较的是哪个可行反事实。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card follows Saez's public economics usage: **government failure occurs when government cannot or will not act primarily in its citizens' interests**. Information and administrative limits can constrain its ability, while incentives that diverge from public objectives can undermine its willingness. Applying the concept to a policy requires an explicit welfare, distributional, or rights criterion and a feasible counterfactual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政策没有达到某项宣布的目标、政策产生效率成本、以及政治过程中的激励问题，是需要分别核查的三件事。目标落空还要检查目标可达性和外生冲击；效率成本还要与分配或保险收益一起评价；寻租与俘获则是需要证据的机制。单凭有人受损或目标未完全实现，尚不能完成政府失灵的判定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A missed policy target, an efficiency cost, and an incentive problem in the political process require separate checks. A missed target raises questions about feasibility and external shocks; efficiency costs must be assessed alongside distributional or insurance benefits; rent seeking and capture are mechanisms that require evidence. Losses to some people or incomplete target attainment alone do not establish government failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[公共选择分析]]解释参与者与决策规则如何产生政府结果；[[福利定理边界]]说明为何现实制度的优劣需要另作比较。政府失灵这个概念既不把政府视为全知全能，也不预先判定政府一定比市场差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[公共选择分析|Public choice analysis]] explains how participants and decision rules produce government outcomes; [[福利定理边界|the boundaries of the welfare theorems]] explain why actual institutions require separate comparison. The concept of government failure neither assumes an omniscient government nor establishes that government always performs worse than markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Emmanuel Saez，UC Berkeley Econ 131，Political Economy，PDF pp.18、24、26–27](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~saez/course131/political_ch09.pdf)：p.24 支持能力／意愿与公民利益的定义；p.18 说明政治信息假设的限制；pp.26–27 比较公私供给及具体市场条件。正文对目标、福利和机制的区分是应用该定义时的判定边界。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Page 24 supplies the definition in terms of ability, willingness, and citizens' interests; page 18 discusses political information limits; pages 26–27 compare public and private provision under specific market conditions. The distinctions among targets, welfare, and mechanisms specify how the definition is to be applied.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Coase（1960），The Problem of Social Cost，印刷 pp.17–18、43–44（PDF pp.18–19、44–45）](https://sites.socsci.uci.edu/~jkbrueck/course%20readings/Econ%20272B%20readings/coase.pdf)：支持评估实际可行制度、运行与转换成本和完整后果；政府介入可能改善效率，也可能成本过高。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  These pages support comparing feasible institutions, their operating and transition costs, and their full consequences. Government intervention may improve efficiency or impose excessive costs.
