# 不同日期的现金流必须先换算到同一评价时点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cash flows at different dates must first be converted to a common valuation date*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子原则
> 不同日期的现金流不是可直接相加或比较的同一种“计价单位”。先选定评价时点，再用对应的跨期换算因子把每笔现金流移到该时点，才能比较方案。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Cash flows dated at different times are not directly comparable units. Choose a valuation date first, then move every cash flow to that date with the appropriate intertemporal conversion factor before comparing alternatives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一年期可比机会成本为 $5\%$，今天的 $100$ 与一年后的 $105$ 等值，是因为 $100(1.05)=105$，不是因为两个数字相等，也不是因为货币会物理“腐烂”。评价时点可以是今天或某个未来日期；只要换算关系一致，比较结论不应因评价时点的选择而改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the one-year comparable opportunity cost is $5\%$, 100 today is equivalent to 105 in one year because $100(1.05)=105$, not because the two numbers are equal or money physically decays. The valuation date may be today or a future date; with consistent conversion factors, the comparison should not depend on which common date is chosen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么不能直接用“今天支出 100、两年后收入 110，所以赚 10”判断项目价值？
>
> **答案：** 100 与 110 位于不同日期。必须先把两者换到同一时点，并计入与该现金流匹配的机会成本，差额才有经济含义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can you not conclude that a project creates 10 of value merely because it costs 100 today and pays 110 in two years?
>
> **Answer:** The 100 and 110 are dated differently. Their difference has economic meaning only after both are converted to one date using the opportunity cost appropriate to the cash flow.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
