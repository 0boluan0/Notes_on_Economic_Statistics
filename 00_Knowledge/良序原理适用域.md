# The well-ordering principle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*良序原理及其适用域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A collection can have a first member even when it never ends. The set $\{5,7,9,\ldots\}$ has no largest member, but it has a smallest one: $5$. The well-ordering principle guarantees this kind of starting point for every nonempty collection of nonnegative integers. This is what makes it legitimate to speak of a first failure among integer-indexed cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个集合即使没有尽头，也可能有第一个成员。$\{5,7,9,\ldots\}$ 没有最大成员，却有最小成员 $5$。良序原理保证：非负整数中任意非空的一批数，都有这样的起点。正因如此，才可以在整数编号的情形中选取第一次失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What “least” requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*“最小”究竟要求什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$; zero is included here. A set $S\subseteq\mathbb N_0$ is a collection whose every member is one of these integers. “Nonempty” means it has at least one member. A least element $m$ must satisfy **both** $m\in S$ and $m\le s$ for every $s\in S$. The symbol $\in$ means “is a member of,” and $\subseteq$ means every member of the first set belongs to the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里记 $\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$，明确包含零。集合 $S\subseteq\mathbb N_0$ 的每个成员都必须是这样的整数；“非空”表示至少有一个成员。最小元 $m$ 必须**同时**满足 $m\in S$，以及对每个 $s\in S$ 都有 $m\le s$。符号 $\in$ 表示“是其中的成员”，$\subseteq$ 表示前一集合的全部成员都属于后一集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Well Ordering Principle states that every nonempty set of nonnegative integers has a least element. Before invoking it, check all three conditions: the set is nonempty, its elements are nonnegative integers, and the argument needs a least element. Only then may one choose a “smallest bad object” or “first failure.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
良序原理说：每个非空的非负整数集合都有最小元素。应用时必须逐项确认三个条件——集合非空、元素都是非负整数、所需的是最小元——然后才能选取“最小的坏对象”或“最早失败的位置”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first two items are hypotheses of the principle; the last identifies the conclusion being used. Formally,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
前两项是原理的假设；最后一项是在辨认论证需要调用的结论。形式上，

$$
\varnothing\ne S\subseteq\mathbb N_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists m\in S\;\forall s\in S,\ m\le s.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\varnothing$ is the empty set, $\exists$ means “there exists,” and $\forall$ means “for every.” The least element is unique: if $m$ and $m^{\prime}$ both qualified, then $m\le m^{\prime}$ and $m^{\prime}\le m$, so they would be equal. The set itself need not be finite. The principle guarantees existence; it does not promise that an unspecified collection has a membership test or an efficient search algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
其中 $\varnothing$ 是空集，$\exists$ 表示“存在”，$\forall$ 表示“对每一个”。最小元是唯一的：若 $m$ 与 $m^{\prime}$ 都符合要求，则 $m\le m^{\prime}$ 且 $m^{\prime}\le m$，所以二者相等。集合本身不必有限。原理保证的是存在性，并没有保证一个尚未具体描述的集合具有成员判定方法或高效搜索算法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choosing a first counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*怎样选取第一次失败*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if a proposition about nonnegative integers has a counterexample, collect all counterexample indices in $C\subseteq\mathbb N$. Under the supposition $C\ne\varnothing$, the Well Ordering Principle supplies a least counterexample $c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，若某个关于非负整数的命题存在反例，就可以把所有反例的下标组成集合 $C\subseteq\mathbb N$。在 $C\ne\varnothing$ 的反设下，良序原理给出最小反例 $c$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In that paragraph $\mathbb N$ uses the same nonnegative-integer convention as $\mathbb N_0$. The claim $P(n)$ is a [[命题与谓词|predicate]], a statement with a truth value once $n$ is specified. The set of failures contains exactly those allowed indices where $P(n)$ is false. Choosing its least member tells us every allowed smaller index succeeds; it does **not** by itself show that a failure is impossible. The remaining contradiction is the task of the [[最小反例递降|minimal-counterexample method]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
上段中的 $\mathbb N$ 与这里的 $\mathbb N_0$ 采用相同的非负整数约定。陈述 $P(n)$ 是一个[[命题与谓词|谓词]]，指定 $n$ 后就有真值。失败集合恰好收集论域中使 $P(n)$ 为假的下标。取其最小成员，只能告诉我们所有更小的合法下标都成功，并不能自动证明失败不可能；余下的矛盾需要由[[最小反例递降|最小反例法]]完成。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An integer starting point may be shifted
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*整数起点可以平移*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative integers do not automatically prevent a least element. Suppose $S$ is a nonempty set of integers and there is an integer $L$ such that $s\ge L$ for every $s\in S$. Translate it to $T=\{s-L:s\in S\}\subseteq\mathbb N_0$. This set is nonempty, so it has a least member $t$. Because $t\in T$, $L+t$ belongs to $S$; and $t\le s-L$ gives $L+t\le s$ for every $s\in S$. Thus $L+t$ is the least member of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
含有负整数并不自动排除最小元。设 $S$ 是非空整数集合，且存在整数 $L$ 使每个 $s\in S$ 都满足 $s\ge L$。平移得到 $T=\{s-L:s\in S\}\subseteq\mathbb N_0$。这个集合非空，故有最小成员 $t$。由 $t\in T$ 可知 $L+t\in S$；再由 $t\le s-L$ 得到对每个 $s\in S$ 都有 $L+t\le s$。因此 $L+t$ 就是 $S$ 的最小成员。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $\{-3,-1,1,3,\ldots\}$ translates by $L=-3$ to $\{0,2,4,6,\ldots\}$, and its least member is $-3$. This is a consequence of the stated principle, not a claim that arbitrary integer sets have minima.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，取 $L=-3$，集合 $\{-3,-1,1,3,\ldots\}$ 平移成 $\{0,2,4,6,\ldots\}$，其最小成员是 $-3$。这是上述原理的推论，并不表示任意整数集合都有最小元。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nonemptiness is necessary; the empty set has no least element.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
非空是必要条件；空集没有最小元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conclusion cannot be transferred unchanged to arbitrary subsets of the reals. For example, $\{1/n:n\ge1\}$ is nonempty and bounded below but has no least element.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
不能把结论原样套到任意实数子集。例如 $\{1/n:n\ge1\}$ 非空且有下界，却没有最小元素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each member $1/n$, the member $1/(n+1)$ is strictly smaller. Zero lies below every member, but zero does not belong to this set, so it cannot be its least member. Likewise, the integer set $\{-1,-2,-3,\ldots\}$ has no least member because any $-n$ is followed by the smaller member $-(n+1)$. These arguments check the actual sets; being outside $\mathbb N_0$ alone would not prove failure, as the shifted example above shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对每个成员 $1/n$，另一个成员 $1/(n+1)$ 都更小。零低于所有成员，但零不属于集合，所以不能充当它的最小元。同样，整数集合 $\{-1,-2,-3,\ldots\}$ 没有最小元，因为任意 $-n$ 后面都有更小的成员 $-(n+1)$。这些论证检查了具体集合；仅仅不属于 $\mathbb N_0$ 并不能证明没有最小元，上面的平移例子已经说明这一点。

> [!question]- Check the domain · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Can the Well Ordering Principle be applied directly to $\{5,7,9,\ldots\}$? What about $\{-1,-2,-3,\ldots\}$?
>
> **Answer:** Yes for the first set: it is nonempty, consists of nonnegative integers, and has least element $5$. The second set is outside the stated domain and has no least element.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 集合 $\{5,7,9,\ldots\}$ 能否直接应用良序原理？集合 $\{-1,-2,-3,\ldots\}$ 呢？
>
> **答案：** 前者可以：它非空且由非负整数构成，最小元是 $5$。后者不满足非负整数条件，而且没有最小元。
