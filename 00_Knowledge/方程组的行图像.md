# The row picture of a linear system is the intersection of its equation solution sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性方程组的行图像把解集表示为各行方程解集的交*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, the **row picture** regards each equation $a_i^Tx=b_i$ as a geometric constraint on the input $x\in\mathbb R^n$. The complete solution set consists of points satisfying every constraint:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，**行图像**把每一行方程 $a_i^Tx=b_i$ 看成输入 $x\in\mathbb R^n$ 所受的几何约束。完整解集由同时满足全部约束的点构成：

$$
\{x:Ax=b\}=\bigcap_{i=1}^{m}\{x:a_i^Tx=b_i\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonzero row defines a line for $n=2$, a plane for $n=3$, and an affine hyperplane in general. These sets need not pass through the origin. A zero row is a separate case: $0=0$ imposes no restriction, while $0=b_i\ne0$ makes the whole system inconsistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非零系数行在 $n=2$ 时定义直线，在 $n=3$ 时定义平面，一般情形定义仿射超平面；这些集合未必经过原点。零行要另行处理：$0=0$ 不施加限制，$0=b_i\ne0$ 则使整个系统无解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $2x-y=0$ and $-x+2y=3$, the lines meet at $(1,2)$. The [[Ax的列组合解释|column picture]] describes the same solution as the weights combining the columns into $b=(0,3)^T$. The row picture is not the [[行空间|row space]], which is the span of the coefficient rows rather than the intersection of equation solution sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $2x-y=0$ 与 $-x+2y=3$，两条直线相交于 $(1,2)$。[[Ax的列组合解释|列图像]]把同一个解理解成将各列组合为 $b=(0,3)^T$ 的权重。行图像不是[[行空间]]：后者是系数行向量的张成，并非各方程解集的交。
