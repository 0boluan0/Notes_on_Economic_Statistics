# 发行注册涉及法定发行与披露要求但不构成对证券投资价值的保证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Issuance registration concerns statutory issuance and disclosure requirements, not a guarantee of investment merit.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“可以依法发行”与“值得以这个价格买入”是两个判断。以中国证监会 2023 年全面注册制说明为例，审核包括发行条件、上市条件和信息披露要求，不能简化成“交上资料、看起来真实就可以”；但监管审核也不替投资者承诺回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Eligibility to issue securities and attractiveness at the offered price are different judgments. The CSRC's 2023 explanation of registration reform includes issuance, listing and disclosure requirements, rather than mere document submission. Regulatory review does not promise an investment return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一家公司依法披露业务风险并完成发行程序，仍可能因为经营恶化而亏损。投资者仍须检查[[股票|股权现金流权利]]、发行价格和风险。具体板块的财务、市值、行业定位与程序要求，应查询适用市场、日期和版本；这张卡只保留“合规门槛不等于价值背书”的可迁移辨析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A company that satisfies issuance procedures and discloses risks can still suffer business losses. Investors must still examine [[股票|equity rights]], price and risk. Board-specific thresholds require the applicable market, date and rule version; this atom preserves the distinction between compliance and investment merit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
