# 地理既可直接影响生产与市场成本也可通过历史制度间接影响发展
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Geography can affect development directly through production and market costs and indirectly through historical institutions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 双通道
> **直接通道：** 气候与疾病生态影响健康和劳动生产率，土壤与水分影响农业适生性，距海岸、港口或市场的距离影响运输与贸易成本。**间接通道：** 这些条件还可通过定居、殖民策略、人口分布或政策选择塑造制度，再影响当代发展。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Direct channels:** climate and disease ecology can affect health and labour productivity; soil and water can affect agricultural suitability; distance from coasts, ports, or markets can affect transport and trade costs. **Indirect channels:** the same conditions can shape institutions through settlement, colonial strategies, population distribution, or policy choices, and institutions can then affect present development.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

地理收入相关性不会自动告诉我们是哪条通道在作用。例如热带疾病环境既可能直接影响当代健康，又可能历史上影响欧洲人定居与殖民制度。若要把后者用作制度的工具变量，就必须额外辩护它没有绕过制度直接影响当代收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A geography-income correlation does not reveal which channel operates. A tropical disease environment might directly affect current health and might also have shaped European settlement and colonial institutions. Using the latter variation as an instrument for institutions therefore requires a separate defence that geography has no route to current income outside the institutional channel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

地理持久不等于结果被决定。公共卫生、交通基础设施、农业技术、迁移和贸易制度都可以改变某一地理条件对生产和机会的映射。所以可以对通道做因果分析，却不能从纬度或气候直接推出一个国家的命运。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Persistent geography is not deterministic destiny. Public health, transport infrastructure, agricultural technology, migration, and trade institutions can change how a physical condition maps into production and opportunity. The channels can be studied causally without inferring a country's fate from latitude or climate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 为什么“离海岸远”和收入低的相关不等于地理决定论？
>
> **答案：** 距离可能通过运输成本作用，但基础设施、贸易规则、技术和人口迁移会改变这条机制的强度；相关也可能混合了制度与历史通道。

## 来源与核验

- [Gallup, Sachs & Mellinger, “Geography and Economic Development”](https://doi.org/10.3386/w6849)：核对运输成本、疾病负担、农业生产率等直接通道，以及地理对政策与制度的间接通道。
- [Acemoglu, Johnson & Robinson, “The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development”](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.91.5.1369)：核对疾病环境→定居可行性→殖民制度→1985–1995 年制度指标的间接历史通道。
- [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=18|本地课件第 18 页]]：核对不以单一因素或单一国别经验解释全部发展路径的课程边界。
