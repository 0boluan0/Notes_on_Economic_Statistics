# 时间线先固定现金流日期再决定贴现指数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fix cash-flow dates on a timeline before choosing discount exponents*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子步骤
> 先标出评价时点、每笔现金流的准确发生时点、正负号和计息期间，再写贴现指数。时点 $t$ 的现金流折到时点 $0$，指数应是从 $0$ 到 $t$ 实际跨过的计息期数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Mark the valuation date, exact date and sign of every cash flow, and the compounding interval before writing any exponent. A cash flow at time $t$ discounted to time 0 uses the actual number of compounding periods between 0 and $t$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“第 2 年发生”并不足以确定指数：第 2 年初等于第 1 年末，而第 2 年末才距今天两年。把年中现金流当成年末现金流会多折现半期，从而在正贴现率下低估现值；把第三年末误写成第二年末则会高估现值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Occurs in year 2” does not determine the exponent. The beginning of year 2 is the end of year 1, whereas the end of year 2 is two years from today. Treating a mid-year cash flow as year-end adds half a period of discounting and understates present value at a positive rate; moving a year-3 payment to year 2 overstates it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 今天是 $t=0$。一笔现金流在第 3 年年初发生。按年度复利折到今天时指数是多少？
>
> **答案：** 第 3 年年初就是第 2 年年末，所以跨过两期，指数为 2，而不是 3。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Today is $t=0$. A cash flow occurs at the beginning of year 3. Under annual compounding, what exponent discounts it to today?
>
> **Answer:** The beginning of year 3 is the end of year 2, so it is two periods away and the exponent is 2, not 3.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
