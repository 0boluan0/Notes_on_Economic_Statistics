# 现金流量与负债比率是经营活动现金流量净额相对于总负债的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash-flow-to-liabilities ratio compares net operating cash flow with total liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课定义为 $CFO/L_{end}$，通常写成百分比，分母用期末总负债。ABC为 $246/1040\approx23.6538\%$；它比[[现金流量比率]]的流动负债分母范围更广。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course defines the ratio as $CFO/L_{end}$, usually stated as a percentage using ending total liabilities. ABC gives about 23.6538%. Its denominator is broader than current liabilities in the [[现金流量比率|operating-cash-flow ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它比较一期经营现金生成与存量义务，不是所有债务的合同摊还率。把倒数称为“还清债务年数”，还需极强假设：未来每期CFO不变、全部可用于清债、不新增债务且忽略其他资金需要；通常不应作这种直接预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio compares one period's cash generation with outstanding obligations, not a contractual amortisation rate. Calling its reciprocal years to repay requires strong assumptions: constant future CFO, all of it available for repayment, no new debt, and no competing funding needs. It is generally not a direct repayment forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总负债须与主体和时点一致；总负债为零时不可除，CFO为负时应描述为经营净耗现。判断偿债仍需[[偿债能力的报表外核查]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liabilities must match the entity and date. Zero liabilities make division undefined; negative CFO indicates net operating cash consumption. Solvency assessment still requires [[偿债能力的报表外核查|additional checks]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
