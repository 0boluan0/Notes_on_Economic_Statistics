# 信用利差是债券收益率相对明确匹配基准收益率的差额

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credit spread is a bond's yield difference from an explicitly specified, matched benchmark yield.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信用利差（credit spread）把含信用风险债券的收益率与选定基准相比。本卡先固定常见的单券收益率差口径：同一观察时点、同一币种，匹配到期日并统一计息与报价约定，计算债券收益率减基准收益率。基准可以采用相应政府债，但政府债不因此自动满足模型中的[[无风险利率口径]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credit spread compares a credit-risky bond with a selected benchmark. This entry fixes the common yield-difference convention: use the same observation time and currency, match maturity, and align compounding and quotation conventions before subtracting the benchmark yield. A government bond can be the benchmark without automatically satisfying a model's [[无风险利率口径|risk-free-rate requirements]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$s=y_{\mathrm{bond}}-y_{\mathrm{benchmark}}.$$

若相容口径下债券收益率为 5.4%、基准为 4.0%，利差为 1.4 个百分点，即 140 个基点；这不是“高出 1.4%”的相对变化率。若债券收益率不变而基准下降 0.2 个百分点，利差也会扩大 20 个基点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compatible yields of 5.4% and 4.0% imply a spread of 1.4 percentage points, or 140 basis points, not a 1.4% relative change. If the bond yield is unchanged while the benchmark falls by 0.2 percentage points, the spread widens by 20 basis points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“信用”是报价名称，不表示差额已经剔除流动性、税收和嵌入期权。匹配期限也未必匹配全部现金流权重。使用 ASW、Z-spread 或其他模型利差时，必须另报相应定义，不能悄悄代入上式；具体口径对照复用[[债券CDS基差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The label does not mean that liquidity, taxes, and embedded options have been removed. Matching maturity need not match the full cash-flow profile. ASW, Z-spread, and other model-based spreads require their own definitions and must not silently replace this measure; see [[债券CDS基差|bond–CDS basis conventions]] for the comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
