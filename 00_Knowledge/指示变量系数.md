# Indicator-variable coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指示变量系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[事件指示变量|indicator]] records group membership: $D=1$ for the comparison group and $D=0$ for the reference group. In a regression, its coefficient often describes how far apart the two fitted group levels are. To interpret it, write the prediction once with $D=0$ and once with $D=1$, keeping the other inputs the same. The difference tells you what the coefficient measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]记录属于哪一组：比较组记为 $D=1$，基准组记为 $D=0$。在回归中，它的系数常用来表示两组拟合水平相差多少。读系数时，把其他输入保持相同，分别代入 $D=0$ 和 $D=1$；两次预测相减，就能看出系数衡量的对象。

> [!example] A wage comparison at the same education level · 教育相同时比较工资
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a model for mean hourly wage is
> $$E[wage\mid training,education]=12+1.8\,training+0.7\,education.$$
> Here $training$ is $1$ for trained workers and $0$ otherwise; $education$ counts years. At $12$ years of education, the untrained group's predicted mean is $12+0.7(12)=20.4$, and the trained group's is $22.2$. The coefficient $1.8$ is the gap $22.2-20.4$, in wage units per hour. It is neither the trained group's wage level nor a claim about each person's wage. At $16$ years, the levels become $23.2$ and $25.0$, while this model keeps the gap at $1.8$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设上式描述平均时薪，其中 training 对参加培训者取 $1$，其余取 $0$；education 是教育年数。教育同为 $12$ 年时，未培训组预测均值为 $12+0.7(12)=20.4$，培训组为 $22.2$。系数 $1.8$ 是两者之差，单位是每小时的工资单位；它既不是培训组的工资水平，也不是对每个人工资的断言。教育为 $16$ 年时，两组水平变为 $23.2$ 和 $25.0$，这个模型中的差仍为 $1.8$。

## Why the subtraction works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么代入相减能读出系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ be an integrable real outcome, so $E[|Y|]<\infty$. The [[条件期望|conditional mean]] $E[Y\mid D,Z]$ describes its average given the group and the included controls $Z=(Z_1,\ldots,Z_k)^T$. Consider the additive model
$$E[Y\mid D,Z]=\beta_0+\beta_DD+\gamma^TZ,\qquad
\gamma^TZ=\sum_{j=1}^k\gamma_jZ_j.$$
$\beta_0$ is the intercept; $\beta_D$ multiplies group membership; the coefficients $\gamma_j$ multiply the controls. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], and the displayed sum explains the compact product. The model contains no interaction involving $D$, so the control terms have the same coefficients in both groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $Y$ 是可积的实值结果，即 $E[|Y|]<\infty$。[[条件期望|条件均值]] $E[Y\mid D,Z]$ 描述给定组别和已纳入的控制变量 $Z=(Z_1,\ldots,Z_k)^T$ 后的平均结果。上式中 $\beta_0$ 是截距，$\beta_D$ 乘组别记录，$\gamma_j$ 分别乘各控制变量；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，展开的求和说明了紧凑乘积的含义。这个加性模型没有包含 $D$ 的交互项，因此两组共用相同的控制变量系数。

> [!important] A difference, measured at common inputs · 同一输入处的差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At a common control value $z$,
> $$m_0(z)=\beta_0+\gamma^Tz,\qquad
> m_1(z)=\beta_0+\beta_D+\gamma^Tz,$$
> so $m_1(z)-m_0(z)=\beta_D$. The common terms cancel. The reference mean itself is $m_0(z)$, not generally $\beta_0$: the intercept alone gives that mean at $z=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在共同控制值 $z$ 处，两组模型均值分别为上式的 $m_0(z)$ 与 $m_1(z)$。相减时共同项抵消，差就是 $\beta_D$。基准组均值本身是 $m_0(z)$，一般不只是 $\beta_0$；仅在 $z=0$ 处，截距才单独给出该组模型均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a statement about actual conditional means when the mean model is correct and both group-specific means are defined at the comparison values. For continuous controls, conditional means are versions defined almost surely; a model evaluated at a point with no support is an extrapolation. If the fitted line is only a [[条件均值与线性投影|best linear approximation]], the same subtraction describes its fitted gap, without proving that the true conditional mean has that shape. The normality or equal-variance assumptions used for some inference procedures are not needed for this algebra.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有均值模型正确、比较位置的两组条件均值都有定义时，这才是实际条件均值的等式。连续控制变量的条件均值按几乎处处的版本理解；在没有支持的位置代入模型，属于外推。若拟合式只被解释为[[条件均值与线性投影|最佳线性近似]]，相减仍给出拟合差，却不证明真实条件均值就是这个形状。某些推断程序使用的正态或同方差假设，不是这一步代数的条件。

## What OLS gives in an observed sample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*实际样本里的 OLS 系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an intercept and $D$ as the only regressor, [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares]] chooses the two group levels to minimise squared residuals. Assume both groups contain observations. Writing their candidate levels as $a$ and $a+b$, the criterion splits into
$$\sum_{i:D_i=0}(y_i-a)^2+\sum_{i:D_i=1}(y_i-a-b)^2.$$
Each group's squared deviations are smallest at its sample mean. Therefore $\hat\beta_0=\bar y_0$ and $\hat\beta_D=\bar y_1-\bar y_0$. A hat marks an estimated coefficient; $\bar y_d$ is the observed average in group $d$. For wages $(18,22)$ in group $0$ and $(21,25)$ in group $1$, these estimates are $20$ and $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有截距和 $D$ 时，[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]选择两个组水平，使残差平方和最小。假设两组都有观察，把候选水平写为 $a$ 与 $a+b$，平方和就分为上面的两组。每组的平方偏差在该组样本均值处最小，所以 $\hat\beta_0=\bar y_0$，$\hat\beta_D=\bar y_1-\bar y_0$。帽子表示估计，$\bar y_d$ 是样本中第 $d$ 组的平均。例如第 $0$ 组工资为 $(18,22)$、第 $1$ 组为 $(21,25)$，截距估计为 $20$，指示系数估计为 $3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This finite-data identity needs no random-sampling assumption. It applies to unweighted, unconstrained OLS with both groups present. After adding controls, the indicator estimate generally differs from the raw group-average difference: it is the gap between the fitted predictions at common controls. Unique coefficients additionally require the [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full-rank design condition]], meaning that no included column is an exact combination of the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一有限数据恒等式不要求随机抽样，但针对的是两组都有观察的、未加权且无约束的 OLS。加入控制后，指示系数一般不再等于原始组均值之差，而是同一控制值处的拟合预测差。系数唯一还要求[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|设计满列秩]]，即没有已纳入的列能由其他列精确组合出来。

## Interactions and causal claims change the question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*交互项与因果解释怎样改变问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a continuous control $X$ also enters through $\eta DX$, with interaction coefficient $\eta$, the [[类别连续交互|group difference becomes]] $\beta_D+\eta x$ at $X=x$. The coefficient on $D$ alone gives the gap at $x=0$; $\eta=0$ is the special case of a constant gap. Always substitute into the complete model, including every interaction. The [[交互主效应基准|reference value]] may itself have little substantive meaning or no data support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若连续控制变量 $X$ 还通过 $\eta DX$ 进入模型，其中 $\eta$ 是交互系数，则在 $X=x$ 处的[[类别连续交互|组差变为]] $\beta_D+\eta x$。单独的 $D$ 系数给出 $x=0$ 时的差，$\eta=0$ 才是差不随 $x$ 变化的特殊情形。应代入包含所有交互项的完整模型；这个[[交互主效应基准|参照值]]本身也可能没有实际意义或缺乏数据支持。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For training or policy, the wage gap is initially an association. Calling it an intervention effect needs a justified [[条件均值因果边界|causal identification argument]]. One sufficient route uses well-defined treatment and consistency, conditional exchangeability, and positive probabilities for both treatments over the target control distribution. These are one identification route, not universal necessary conditions for every causal design; coding a variable as zero or one establishes none of them. In the wage example, the statement supported by the mean model alone is “a conditional mean difference of $1.8$ at the same education”, not “training raises each wage by $1.8$”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

培训或政策例子中的工资差首先是关联。把它叫作干预效应，需要成立的[[条件均值因果边界|因果识别论证]]。一条充分路径使用定义明确的处理及一致性、条件可交换性，以及目标控制变量分布上两种处理都有正概率。这是一条识别路径，不是每种因果设计都必须满足的唯一条件；把变量编码为零或一，也不会建立这些假设。因此，工资例子仅由均值模型支持的读法是“教育相同处条件均值差为 $1.8$”，不是“培训使每个人工资增加 $1.8$”。
