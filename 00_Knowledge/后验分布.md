# 后验分布是在模型和先验下以观测数据为条件的未知量分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A posterior is the conditional distribution of an unknown quantity given the observed data, model, and prior*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 后验由[[Bayes法则]]给出：$\pi(\theta\mid x)=L(\theta;x)\pi(\theta)/m(x)$。离散参数有 $m(x)=\sum_tL(t;x)\pi(t)$；连续参数改为积分。要求归一化常数有限且正。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Bayes’ rule combines likelihood and prior, then divides by their sum or integral over all parameter values. The normalising constant must be finite and positive. This gives the conditional parameter distribution under the chosen model and prior.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

两种袋子的先验均为 $1/2$、似然为 $0.729$ 和 $0.001$ 时，$m(x)=0.365$，故后验为 $0.3645/0.365$ 与 $0.0005/0.365$。同一似然配不同先验可得到不同后验；增加数据也不在所有模型下保证先验影响消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal priors and likelihoods 0.729 and 0.001 give marginal evidence 0.365. Dividing the two joint weights by this constant gives the posterior. Different priors can give different posteriors for the same likelihood; prior influence does not vanish universally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
