# Multinomial theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*多项式定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The multinomial theorem expands a nonnegative integer power of a sum with several terms. Its coefficients count how many choices from the individual factors produce the same final product.

For example, $(x+y+z)^2$ has two factors. From each factor choose one of $x,y,z$, giving nine choices. The product $xy$ arises twice: choose $x$ then $y$, or $y$ then $x$. The product $x^2$ arises only once. After collecting equal products, we obtain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
多项式定理展开的是：多个项之和的非负整数次幂。它的系数记录，从各个因子中选项时，有多少种选择会形成同一个最终乘积。

例如，$(x+y+z)^2$ 有两个因子，每个因子都可以选 $x,y,z$ 之一，共有九种选择。乘积 $xy$ 出现两次：先选 $x$ 再选 $y$，或者先选 $y$ 再选 $x$。而 $x^2$ 只出现一次。合并相同乘积后得到

$$
(x+y+z)^2=x^2+y^2+z^2+2xy+2xz+2yz.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nine factor choices have become six distinct monomials—products specified by the exponents of the variables. Coefficients keep track of the choices merged into each monomial.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
九种因子选择合并成了六个不同的单项式，也就是由各变量指数指定的乘积。系数保留了每个单项式合并了多少种选择的信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Assigning factor positions to variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把因子位置分给不同变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $m\ge1$ be the number of terms, written $x_1,\ldots,x_m$, and let the exponent $n\ge0$ be an integer. In an expansion of $(x_1+\cdots+x_m)^n$, let $k_i$ count the factor positions from which we choose $x_i$. Thus each $k_i$ is a nonnegative integer and $k_1+\cdots+k_m=n$: every factor contributes exactly one choice.

To get $x_1^{k_1}\cdots x_m^{k_m}$, assign the $n$ distinct factor positions to $m$ named groups of these sizes. The group named $i$ contains the positions choosing $x_i$. The [[多项式系数|multinomial coefficient]] counts these assignments:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $m\ge1$ 是相加的项数，各项记为 $x_1,\ldots,x_m$，指数 $n\ge0$ 是整数。展开 $(x_1+\cdots+x_m)^n$ 时，用 $k_i$ 记录有多少个因子位置选了 $x_i$。所以各 $k_i$ 都是非负整数，且 $k_1+\cdots+k_m=n$，因为每个因子恰好贡献一次选择。

要得到 $x_1^{k_1}\cdots x_m^{k_m}$，就把 $n$ 个不同的因子位置分给 $m$ 个有名称的组，并规定这些组的大小。第 $i$ 组包含选择 $x_i$ 的位置。[[多项式系数]]数的正是这种分配：

$$
\binom{n}{k_1,\ldots,k_m}=\frac{n!}{k_1!\cdots k_m!}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbols $x_i$ can be chosen repeatedly, but factor positions are distinct and each belongs to only one group. For ordinary commuting variables, all choices with the same counts produce the same monomial. Summing over the possible count tuples gives the theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
符号 $x_i$ 可以重复被选中，但因子位置彼此不同，而且每个位置只属于一组。对通常可交换的变量，同一组次数对应的所有选择都会产生同一个单项式。把所有可能的次数元组对应的项加起来，就得到定理。

> [!important] Multinomial expansion / 多项式展开
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For integers $m\ge1$, $n\ge0$ and real or complex $x_1,\ldots,x_m$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 对整数 $m\ge1$、$n\ge0$，以及实数或复数 $x_1,\ldots,x_m$，
>
> $$
> \boxed{(x_1+\cdots+x_m)^n
> =\sum_{\substack{k_1,\ldots,k_m\ge0\\k_1+\cdots+k_m=n}}
> \binom{n}{k_1,\ldots,k_m}x_1^{k_1}\cdots x_m^{k_m}}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sum runs over integer tuples only. A zero $k_i$ means that no factor chooses $x_i$; use $0!=1$ and omit that variable's zeroth-power factor. When $n=0$, the sole tuple is $(0,\ldots,0)$ and the result is the constant polynomial $1$. When $m=1$, the sole tuple is $(n)$ and the formula reduces to $x_1^n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 求和只遍历整数元组。$k_i=0$ 表示没有任何因子选择 $x_i$，使用 $0!=1$，并省去该变量的零次幂因子。$n=0$ 时，唯一的元组是 $(0,\ldots,0)$，结果是常数多项式 $1$。$m=1$ 时，唯一的元组是 $(n)$，公式就成为 $x_1^n$。

> [!example] The coefficient of $x^2yz^2$ / 求 $x^2yz^2$ 的系数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $(x+y+z)^5$, this monomial uses $x$ twice, $y$ once, and $z$ twice. Choose the two $x$ positions from five, then the $y$ position from the remaining three; the last two positions must contribute $z$. The coefficient is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在 $(x+y+z)^5$ 中，这个单项式使用两次 $x$、一次 $y$、两次 $z$。先从五个位置中选两个给 $x$，再从剩余三个中选一个给 $y$，最后两个位置只能给 $z$。系数为
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{2,1,2}=\frac{5!}{2!1!2!}
> =\binom52\binom31=30.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A monomial such as $x^2yz$ has total exponent $4$, so its coefficient in this degree-$5$ expansion is zero. This statement concerns the formal polynomial in distinct variables, before any substitution that identifies variables or replaces them by constants.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 而 $x^2yz$ 的指数之和是 $4$，所以它在这个总次数为 $5$ 的展开式中的系数为零。这是在把各变量视为不同形式变量时的多项式结论，发生在合并变量或把变量代成常数之前。

## Counting monomials versus counting choices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*单项式个数与选择次数不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For distinct formal variables over the real or complex numbers, the possible monomials correspond to the nonnegative exponent tuples whose sum is $n$. By [[隔板法|stars and bars]], their number is $\binom{n+m-1}{m-1}$. The total number of factor choices is instead $m^n$: each of the $n$ positions chooses one of $m$ symbols. Consequently, the sum of all coefficients is $m^n$, which also follows by setting every $x_i=1$ in the theorem. In the opening square, these are six monomials and nine choices.

For $m=2$, the multinomial coefficient reduces to $\binom n{k_2}$ and the theorem becomes the [[二项式定理|binomial theorem]]. Both results use nonnegative integer powers and commutative multiplication. If products such as $xy$ and $yx$ cannot be identified, their choices cannot be combined into the same term in this way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对实数或复数上的不同形式变量，可能出现的单项式对应于指数和为 $n$ 的非负整数元组。由[[隔板法]]，单项式个数为 $\binom{n+m-1}{m-1}$。而全部因子选择的数量是 $m^n$，因为 $n$ 个位置各有 $m$ 种符号可以选。因此，全部系数之和等于 $m^n$；把定理中的所有 $x_i$ 代成 $1$，也能得到这个结论。开头的平方例子对应的就是六个单项式、九种选择。

当 $m=2$ 时，多项式系数简化成 $\binom n{k_2}$，定理就成为[[二项式定理]]。这两个结果都使用非负整数次幂和乘法交换性。若 $xy$ 与 $yx$ 不能视为相同乘积，就不能这样把它们的选择次数合并为同一项。
