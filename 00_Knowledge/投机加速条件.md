# 投机解码只有在每轮新增 token 的基线成本超过草稿、验证与协调成本时才加速

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Speculative decoding speeds up generation only when the baseline cost of a round's emitted tokens exceeds its drafting, verification, and coordination costs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[投机解码]]通过一轮交付多个 token 来摊薄目标调用的耗时，但还要支付草稿、批量验证和协调成本。在局部平稳、输出足够长的成本模型中，应把一轮的总成本除以该轮平均实际输出数，再与普通目标生成每 token 的成本比较。候选数或接受率单独都不能决定是否加速。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[投机解码|Speculative decoding]] amortizes target-call time by delivering multiple tokens per round, while also paying for drafting, batched verification, and coordination. In a locally stationary cost model with sufficiently long output, compare total round cost divided by mean actual emitted tokens with the target baseline's per-token cost. Neither candidate count nor acceptance rate alone determines whether generation speeds up.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从每轮成本得到判据

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deriving the condition from round costs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $T>0$ 为相同条件附近普通目标生成一个 token 的时间，$D$ 为一次草稿步的时间，$V_\gamma$ 为验证 $\gamma$ 个候选的一轮目标计算时间，$O_\gamma$ 为验收、同步和状态协调等额外时间，$K$ 为一轮实际提交的 token 数，$\gamma$ 是正整数。先分析草稿、验证与协调顺序执行的单请求模型：这些时间互不重叠，常量能代表研究区间，启动和末轮截断开销可忽略，则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T>0$ be the ordinary target's time to generate one token under comparable nearby conditions, $D$ one draft step's time, $V_\gamma$ the target-computation time for a round verifying $\gamma$ candidates, $O_\gamma$ additional acceptance, synchronization, and state-coordination time, and $K$ the number of tokens actually committed in a round, with positive integer $\gamma$. First consider a single-request model with sequential drafting, verification, and coordination: these intervals do not overlap, the constants represent the interval studied, and startup and final-round truncation costs are negligible. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_\gamma=\gamma D+V_\gamma+O_\gamma,\qquad
S_\gamma\approx\frac{T\,\mathbb E[K]}{C_\gamma},
$$
$$
S_\gamma>1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
T\,\mathbb E[K]>\gamma D+V_\gamma+O_\gamma.
$$

$S_\gamma$ 是普通生成耗时除以投机耗时的加速比。如果实现重叠执行部分工作，$C_\gamma$ 应改用实际经过时间，不能重复加上重叠区间。若轮次成本也随机变化，应比较长运行的总耗时除以总输出数；在相应稳定条件下用 $\mathbb E[C]/\mathbb E[K]$，不能用各轮 $C/K$ 的算术平均替代。上述式子也没有把满负载服务的吞吐和单请求生成时间当作同一个指标，实际对比须符合 [[推理性能可比性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$S_\gamma$ is baseline generation time divided by speculative generation time. If an implementation overlaps work, $C_\gamma$ must use actual elapsed time without double-counting overlapping intervals. If round costs also vary randomly, use total elapsed time divided by total emitted tokens over a long run, or $\mathbb E[C]/\mathbb E[K]$ under suitable stability conditions. The arithmetic mean of per-round $C/K$ is not a substitute. These expressions also do not identify saturated serving throughput with one request's generation time; actual comparisons must meet [[推理性能可比性|the inference-performance comparability conditions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 接受率何时能给出闭式公式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When acceptance rates yield a closed form.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先排除 EOS 和剩余额度导致的提前截断。根据 [[投机前缀验收]]，一轮连续接受 $n$ 个候选后还提交一个修正或 bonus token，故 $K=n+1$。记 $E_i$ 为“前 $i$ 个候选全部通过”的事件，不需要独立性就有

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First exclude early truncation caused by EOS or the remaining output allowance. Under [[投机前缀验收|speculative prefix acceptance]], a round with $n$ consecutively accepted candidates also commits one correction or bonus token, so $K=n+1$. Let $E_i$ be the event that the first $i$ candidates all pass. Without requiring independence,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathbb E[K]=1+\sum_{i=1}^{\gamma}\Pr(E_i).
$$

这是把 $n$ 写成“至少接受 1 个、至少接受 2 个……”的指示量之和，再取期望。若进一步假设各位置验收可以按相同且独立的接受率 $\alpha$ 处理，才有 $\Pr(E_i)=\alpha^i$，因此

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This writes $n$ as the sum of indicators for accepting at least one candidate, at least two, and so on, then takes expectations. Only with the additional assumption of equal, independent acceptance probability $\alpha$ at each position does $\Pr(E_i)=\alpha^i$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathbb E[K]=\sum_{i=0}^{\gamma}\alpha^i
=\frac{1-\alpha^{\gamma+1}}{1-\alpha}\quad(0\le\alpha<1),
\qquad
\mathbb E[K]=\gamma+1\quad(\alpha=1).
$$

论文把不同前缀的接受概率 $\beta$ 简化为独立同分布，并记 $\alpha=\mathbb E[\beta]$。实际 $\beta$ 随前缀变化；仅知道全局平均接受率，不能保证前 $i$ 个候选全通过的概率精确等于 $\alpha^i$。单个前缀上的 $\beta=\sum_v\min(p(v),q(v))$ 由 [[投机采样修正]]确定；它与跨位置独立性是两回事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper treats prefix-dependent acceptance probabilities $\beta$ as i.i.d. and writes $\alpha=\mathbb E[\beta]$. In practice, $\beta$ varies with the prefix; knowing a global mean acceptance rate alone does not establish that the first $i$ candidates all pass with probability exactly $\alpha^i$. [[投机采样修正|Speculative sampling correction]] gives $\beta=\sum_v\min(p(v),q(v))$ at one prefix; independence across positions is a separate assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 论文理想模型下的成本比

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The cost ratio in the paper's idealized model.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若计算资源足以并行验证，使整轮目标时间 $V_\gamma=T$，忽略额外协调 $O_\gamma=0$，并令 $c=D/T$，就得到论文的加速公式：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If sufficient computation resources allow parallel verification with total target time $V_\gamma=T$, additional coordination is ignored so $O_\gamma=0$, and $c=D/T$, the paper's speedup formula becomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
S_\gamma=\frac{1-\alpha^{\gamma+1}}{(1-\alpha)(1+\gamma c)}
\quad(\alpha<1).
$$

当 $\gamma=1$，$S_1=(1+\alpha)/(1+c)$，所以 $\alpha>c$ 已足以找到有收益的候选长度。反过来，若 $\alpha\le c$，则 $\sum_{i=1}^{\gamma}\alpha^i\le\gamma\alpha\le\gamma c$，没有任何正整数 $\gamma$ 能让加速比超过 1。这一“存在有益 $\gamma$ 当且仅当 $\alpha>c$”结论依赖上面的理想条件，不能直接用于 $V_\gamma>T$ 或 $O_\gamma>0$ 的实现。$\alpha=1$ 时用有限和形式得到 $S_\gamma=(\gamma+1)/(1+\gamma c)$，无需计算 $0/0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\gamma=1$, $S_1=(1+\alpha)/(1+c)$, so $\alpha>c$ is sufficient for a beneficial candidate length. Conversely, if $\alpha\le c$, then $\sum_{i=1}^{\gamma}\alpha^i\le\gamma\alpha\le\gamma c$, and no positive integer $\gamma$ yields speedup above 1. Thus a beneficial $\gamma$ exists exactly when $\alpha>c$ under these idealized conditions; this cannot be transferred directly to implementations with $V_\gamma>T$ or $O_\gamma>0$. For $\alpha=1$, the finite sum gives $S_\gamma=(\gamma+1)/(1+\gamma c)$ without evaluating $0/0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个加速与减速的成本场景

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A cost scenario showing both speedup and slowdown.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以下是人工场景，非实测：$\alpha=.7$、$\gamma=3$，普通目标步 $T=20\,\mathrm{ms}$，草稿步 $D=2\,\mathrm{ms}$，整轮验证 $V_3=24\,\mathrm{ms}$，协调 $O_3=2\,\mathrm{ms}$。采用独立接受近似，有

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a constructed scenario, not a measurement: $\alpha=.7$, $\gamma=3$, ordinary target step $T=20\,\mathrm{ms}$, draft step $D=2\,\mathrm{ms}$, verification round $V_3=24\,\mathrm{ms}$, and coordination $O_3=2\,\mathrm{ms}$. Using the independent-acceptance approximation,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathbb E[K]=1+.7+.49+.343=2.533,
$$
$$
C_3=3\times2+24+2=32\,\mathrm{ms},\qquad
S_3\approx\frac{20\times2.533}{32}=1.583.
$$

若把验证时间当作 $20\,\mathrm{ms}$ 并忽略协调，就会算出理想加速比 $2.533/(1+3\times.1)=1.948$。若保持原来的实际成本设定，只把接受率降到 $.2$，则 $\mathbb E[K]=1.248$，$S_3=.78$，反而变慢。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treating verification as $20\,\mathrm{ms}$ and ignoring coordination instead gives an ideal speedup of $2.533/(1+3\times.1)=1.948$. Keeping the original cost assumptions but lowering acceptance to $.2$ gives $\mathbb E[K]=1.248$ and $S_3=.78$, a slowdown.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增大 $\gamma$ 同时增加潜在输出与草稿、验证成本，应比较对应的 $S_\gamma$。首拒绝后的候选已经占用了计算却不能提交，所以更少的串行目标轮数也不保证更少的总算术工作。剩余输出很短或较早遇 EOS 时，还须按实际交付数重新评估收益。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Increasing $\gamma$ raises both potential output and drafting/verification cost, so compare the corresponding $S_\gamma$. Candidates after the first rejection have consumed computation without becoming committed output; fewer serial target rounds therefore do not guarantee fewer total arithmetic operations. Very short remaining output or early EOS also requires reassessing the benefit using actual emitted tokens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
