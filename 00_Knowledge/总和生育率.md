# 总和生育率是假想女性按某时期年龄别生育率度过全部生育年龄且不因死亡退出时的平均活产数

总和生育率把同一时期不同年龄女性的生育率拼成一位假想女性的一生，回答“若这些年龄别生育率持续适用，她会有多少个活产”。它是时期的合成指标，不是该年所有女性已生孩子的平均数，也不是某个真实出生队列最终生育量的预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The total fertility rate combines one period’s fertility rates at different ages into a hypothetical reproductive lifetime. It asks how many live births a woman would have if those rates applied throughout that lifetime without mortality. It is a synthetic period measure, neither the average number of children already born to women that year nor a forecast of completed fertility for a real birth cohort.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f_a$ 是年龄组 $a$ 每女性人年的活产数，组宽为 $h_a$ 年，覆盖所用资料的全部生育年龄，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f_a$ is live births per woman-year in age group $a$, of width $h_a$ years, across the source’s reproductive-age coverage,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
TFR=\sum_a h_a f_a.
$$

如果年龄别生育率按每千女性人年发布，先除以 1,000。WPP2024 按单岁估计 10–54 岁的生育率，其定义写作假想女性存活至 55 岁；常见资料只报告 15–49 岁，因此读取数据时必须核清覆盖范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rates published per 1,000 woman-years must first be divided by 1,000. WPP2024 estimates single-age rates for ages 10–54 and defines the hypothetical woman as surviving to age 55. Many data sources report only ages 15–49, so coverage must be checked rather than assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如九个五岁组从 10–14 岁至 50–54 岁，每女性人年的生育率依次为 $0,0.02,0.08,0.10,0.10,0.06,0.03,0.01,0$，合计 $0.40$，则 $TFR=5\times0.40=2.0$ 个活产／女性。这不意味着人口当期恰好不增不减；出生总数还受各年龄女性人数影响，见[[人口惯性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nine five-year groups from 10–14 through 50–54, suppose rates per woman-year are $0,0.02,0.08,0.10,0.10,0.06,0.03,0.01,0$. Their sum is $0.40$, giving $TFR=5\times0.40=2.0$ live births per woman. This does not imply zero current population growth: births also depend on the numbers of women at each age; see [[人口惯性|population momentum]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
