# 债券指数按既定样本估价权重和收益口径汇总一组债券的表现
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A bond index summarizes a set of bonds using specified constituents, valuation, weights and return conventions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券指数不是把几只债券的票面利率求平均。它先按市场、币种、信用、剩余期限或其他规则确定样本，再用指定价格与权重计算表现；指数还需处理付息、到期、发行和样本调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond index is not an average of coupon rates. It selects bonds by market, currency, credit, remaining maturity or other criteria, then applies specified prices and weights. Coupon payments, maturity, issuance and constituent changes also require rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用时特别核对：价格是成交、交易商报价还是估值；用净价还是含[[债券应计利息|应计利息]]的全价；是否包括息票及再投资；本币还是换算后的收益。固定现金流的估值关系复用[[固定现金流债券定价]]，但估值不等于当前能按同一价格卖出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check whether inputs are trades, dealer quotations or evaluated prices; whether prices include [[债券应计利息|accrued interest]]; whether coupons and reinvestment are included; and which currency is used. [[固定现金流债券定价|Bond valuation]] supplies a pricing relation, not a guarantee of execution at that value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“一律排除短期限债券和浮息债”不是债券指数的定义。例如 FTSE 的 0–1 年系列专门覆盖短端，EuroFIG 专门跟踪浮息票据。某综合指数的筛选条件不能推广到所有债券指数。两张指数是否可比较，最终仍由[[指数口径核对]]决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Excluding all short-maturity or floating-rate securities is not part of the definition. FTSE has 0–1 year series and EuroFIG tracks floating-rate notes. One aggregate index's selection rules cannot be generalized to every bond index; use [[指数口径核对|benchmark specification checks]] before comparing series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
