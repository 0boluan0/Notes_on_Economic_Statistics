# 期货合约是通过交易所交易并按结算规则逐日结算盈亏的标准化未来交易合约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A futures contract is a standardized exchange-traded future commitment with gains and losses settled daily under its settlement rules*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期货规定标的、合约单位、到期月份和交割或现金结算方式，交易者在市场上形成期货报价。多空双方都承担合约义务，并通过清算机制和保证金安排履约。标的可以是商品，也可以是汇率、利率相关工具或股票指数；指数期货通常结算指数差额，不需要真的交付“一份指数”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Futures standardize the underlying, contract unit, expiry, and delivery or cash-settlement method, leaving the quoted price to trading. Both sides have obligations supported by clearing and margin arrangements. Underlyings include commodities, currencies, interest-rate instruments, and equity indices; index futures typically settle a cash difference rather than deliver an index itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设持仓数量和乘数不变，多头一手合约乘数为 $q$，第 $i$ 次结算价为 $F_i$，则从上一结算到这次结算的盈亏是 $q(F_i-F_{i-1})$。各次现金盈亏相加，忽略利息、费用与违约，得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one unchanged long contract with multiplier $q$ and settlement price $F_i$, each settlement generates $q(F_i-F_{i-1})$. Summing the cash settlements, before interest, fees, or default, gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}q(F_i-F_{i-1})=q(F_n-F_0).$$

例如乘数为每点 10 元，报价从 100 到 95 再到 108：先支付 50 元，再收到 130 元，合计赚 80 元。最终赚钱并不抹去中途那笔现金需求。初始保证金是履约担保，不是购买标的的首付款，也不是损失上限；资金压力见[[保证金顺周期]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a multiplier of 10 currency units per point, quotes of 100, 95, and 108 generate a payment of 50 followed by a receipt of 130: a net gain of 80. That eventual gain does not remove the earlier funding need. Initial margin secures performance; it is neither a down payment on the underlying nor a loss cap. See [[保证金顺周期|margin procyclicality]] for funding stress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[远期合约]]相比，关键区别是标准化、交易清算安排和中途盈亏结算。若考虑这些现金流的融资或再投资，不能只因最终未计息加总相同便断言远期与期货价值完全相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compared with a [[远期合约|forward]], key differences are standardization, trading and clearing arrangements, and interim cash settlement. Once funding or reinvestment is included, equal undiscounted sums do not establish equal contract values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
