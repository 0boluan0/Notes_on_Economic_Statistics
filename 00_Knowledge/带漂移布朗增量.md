# Increments of Brownian motion with drift
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*带漂移布朗运动的增量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An increment measures how much the process changes over an interval, rather than where it ends up. For the [[带漂移布朗运动|Brownian model with drift]] $X_t=x+\mu t+\sigma W_t$, the starting level $x$ cancels when we subtract two positions. What remains is the drift accumulated during the interval plus a fresh Brownian displacement.

We take deterministic constants $x,\mu\in\mathbb R$ and $\sigma>0$, and [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] $W$. Let $s\ge0$ be a fixed starting time and $h>0$ the fixed interval length. Writing $t=s+h$, the increment is $X_t-X_s$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增量衡量一段时间内改变了多少，而不是最后停在哪里。对[[带漂移布朗运动|带漂移布朗模型]] $X_t=x+\mu t+\sigma W_t$，两个位置相减时起点 $x$ 消去，留下这一段内积累的漂移和新的布朗位移。

这里取确定常数 $x,\mu\in\mathbb R$、$\sigma>0$，$W$ 为[[标准布朗运动]]。令 $s\ge0$ 是固定起始时刻，$h>0$ 是固定区间长度，写 $t=s+h$，增量就是 $X_t-X_s$。

> [!important] Length controls the increment law · 区间长度决定增量律
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[正态分布|normal distribution]] notation $N(m,v)$ uses variance as its second parameter. Then
>
> $$X_{s+h}-X_s\sim N(\mu h,\sigma^2h).$$
>
> Its mean is $\mu h$, its variance is $\sigma^2h$, and its [[标准差|standard deviation]] is $\sigma\sqrt h$. These depend on $h$, not on $s$. Every finite collection of increments over deterministic nonoverlapping intervals is [[相互独立|mutually independent]]. Intervals may share an endpoint, but not a positive stretch of time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[正态分布|正态记号]] $N(m,v)$ 的第二参数是方差。因此上式给出均值 $\mu h$、方差 $\sigma^2h$ 和[[标准差]] $\sigma\sqrt h$，都依赖长度 $h$ 而不依赖起点 $s$。任意有限组预先固定且不重叠区间的增量[[相互独立]]。区间可以共用一个端点，但不能共享正长度的一段时间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Deriving the law and independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 结论怎样从定义得到

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtract the two model expressions:

$$X_{s+h}-X_s=\mu h+\sigma(W_{s+h}-W_s).$$

The Brownian increment has law $N(0,h)$. A [[Gaussian仿射闭包|fixed affine transformation of a Gaussian variable]] adds $\mu h$ to its mean and multiplies its variance by $\sigma^2$, giving the stated law. On disjoint intervals, the underlying Brownian increments are mutually independent. Applying a fixed function separately to each of these variables preserves their mutual independence; the drift and scale are not shared random shocks.

For several intervals, including overlapping ones, the increments are jointly Gaussian: write them as linear combinations of the [[布朗有限维分布|jointly Gaussian Brownian values]] at their endpoints. Joint Gaussianity does not make overlapping increments independent. Shared pieces of Brownian motion create covariance, as the [[布朗运动不平稳|moving-window example]] shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把模型在两个时刻的表达式直接相减，得到上式。布朗增量服从 $N(0,h)$；[[Gaussian仿射闭包|Gaussian 变量的固定仿射变换]]让均值加上 $\mu h$、方差乘以 $\sigma^2$，从而得到所述分布。不交区间的基础布朗增量相互独立，对每个变量分别施加固定函数仍保持相互独立；这里的漂移和尺度不是共同的随机冲击。

多个区间即使重叠，增量仍联合 Gaussian：把它们写成各端点[[布朗有限维分布|联合 Gaussian 布朗位置]]的线性组合即可。但联合 Gaussian 不会让重叠增量自动独立，共享的布朗运动段会产生协方差，具体见[[布朗运动不平稳|滑动窗口例子]]。

> [!example] One quarter of a time unit · 四分之一个时间单位
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $X_t=3-2t+4W_t$, every increment of length $h$ has law $N(-2h,16h)$. At $h=1/4$, this is $N(-1/2,4)$: mean $-1/2$, variance $4$, standard deviation $2$. The increments from $0$ to $1/4$ and from $1$ to $5/4$ have this same law and are independent. The starting level $3$ does not enter either increment law.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $X_t=3-2t+4W_t$，长度为 $h$ 的增量都服从 $N(-2h,16h)$。当 $h=1/4$ 时得到 $N(-1/2,4)$：均值 $-1/2$、方差 $4$、标准差 $2$。从 $0$ 到 $1/4$ 和从 $1$ 到 $5/4$ 的增量都服从这个分布，且彼此独立。起点 $3$ 不出现在任一增量律中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Setting $s=0$ and using $X_0=x$ gives $X_t\sim N(x+\mu t,\sigma^2t)$ for $t>0$. For deterministic $s,t\ge0$, the [[协方差|covariance]] of the levels is

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X_s,X_t)=\sigma^2\min(s,t).$$

The deterministic drift changes means but contributes no covariance. Thus levels at distinct positive times are dependent when $\sigma>0$, even though disjoint increments are independent. At $t=0$, $X_0=x$ is constant. If $h=0$ or we extend the model to $\sigma=0$, the corresponding increment is deterministic; $N(m,0)$ denotes a point mass at $m$, not an ordinary normal density. Constant increments remain independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $s=0$，利用 $X_0=x$，便得到 $t>0$ 时 $X_t\sim N(x+\mu t,\sigma^2t)$。对固定的 $s,t\ge0$，水平值的[[协方差]]为上式。确定性漂移改变均值，但不贡献协方差。因此，当 $\sigma>0$ 时，不同正时刻的位置彼此依赖，尽管不交区间的增量独立。在 $t=0$，$X_0=x$ 是常数。如果 $h=0$，或者将模型扩展到 $\sigma=0$，相应增量便是确定的；$N(m,0)$ 表示在 $m$ 处的单点质量，没有普通正态密度。常数增量仍然独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Drift and fluctuation on a short interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 短时间尺度的含义

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over length $h>0$, the magnitude of the mean displacement is $|\mu|h$, while its random standard deviation is $\sigma\sqrt h$. With fixed $\sigma>0$,

$$\frac{|\mu|h}{\sigma\sqrt h}=\frac{|\mu|}{\sigma}\sqrt h\longrightarrow0\qquad(h\downarrow0).$$

The formula also holds when $\mu=0$, with ratio zero throughout. It compares distributional scales: on a short interval the drift is small relative to the spread. It does not assert that every realized random displacement exceeds the drift. When $\sigma=0$, there is no random fluctuation and this ratio is undefined.

All times and intervals in these statements are deterministic. Choosing endpoints by inspecting a realized path is a different question; these fixed-time increment laws alone do not justify a formula for an arbitrary path-selected interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在长度 $h>0$ 上，平均位移的绝对值为 $|\mu|h$，随机位移的标准差为 $\sigma\sqrt h$。固定 $\sigma>0$ 时，两者比值如上趋于零。$\mu=0$ 时公式也成立，比值始终为零。这是分布尺度的比较：短区间内漂移相对于分散程度较小，不是断言每个实际随机位移的绝对值都超过漂移。$\sigma=0$ 时没有随机波动，该比值无定义。

这些结论中的时刻与区间都是预先确定的。观察实际路径后再挑选端点，是另一个问题；仅凭固定时刻的增量律，不能给任意按路径选择的区间套用同一公式。
