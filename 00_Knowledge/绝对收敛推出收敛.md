# 实数或复数级数绝对收敛时原级数必收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Absolute convergence of a real or complex series implies convergence of the original series*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sum|a_n|<\infty$，则 $\sum a_n$ 有有限和。实际判断带符号的级数时，这允许我们先用 [[级数直接比较判别]]、[[级数比值判别]] 或 [[级数根值判别]] 控制绝对值，再把收敛结论带回原级数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\sum|a_n|$ is finite, then $\sum a_n$ converges. One may therefore apply the [[级数直接比较判别|comparison]], [[级数比值判别|ratio]], or [[级数根值判别|root test]] to the magnitudes before concluding convergence of the signed series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是任意一段尾部都满足
$$
\left|\sum_{n=p}^{q}a_n\right|\le\sum_{n=p}^{q}|a_n|.
$$
右侧随着起点 $p$ 后移能一致变得任意小，所以原级数的部分和彼此越来越接近；实数和复数的完备性保证这样的部分和序列有极限。这里需要控制整段尾部，而不只是单个 $a_n$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every finite tail satisfies $|\sum_{n=p}^{q}a_n|\le\sum_{n=p}^{q}|a_n|$. Absolute convergence makes the right side uniformly small for all $q\ge p$ once $p$ is large. The partial sums are therefore Cauchy, and completeness of the real or complex numbers gives a limit. This controls whole tails, not just individual terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向不成立，[[条件收敛]] 正是反例所属的类别。因此绝对值级数发散时，应回头分析符号结构，不能直接宣布原级数发散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse fails for [[条件收敛|conditionally convergent series]]. Failure of absolute convergence therefore calls for examining signs, rather than declaring the original series divergent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
