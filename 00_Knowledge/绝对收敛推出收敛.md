# Absolute convergence implies convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*绝对收敛推出收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the total size of all contributions is finite. Then the original signed or complex contributions cannot keep producing large changes far out in the sum: the size of any remaining change is bounded by the remaining total magnitude. This is why proving [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]] is enough to prove convergence of the original series, even when its sum is hard to calculate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假如所有贡献的大小加起来有限，那么即使原来的贡献带有正负号或复数方向，求和进行到很后面时，也不可能继续产生很大的变化：任何剩余变化都被剩余总幅度控制。这就是为什么证明[[绝对收敛]]，便足以证明原级数收敛，即使原和不容易算出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite real or complex terms $a_n$, let $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$ be the original [[无穷级数|partial sums]], and let $A_N=\sum_{n=1}^N|a_n|$ be their magnitude totals. Here $n$ and $N$ are positive integers, and $|a_n|$ means absolute value or complex magnitude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设每项 $a_n$ 是有限实数或复数，$S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$ 是原[[无穷级数|级数的部分和]]，$A_N=\sum_{n=1}^N|a_n|$ 是累计幅度。这里 $n,N$ 为正整数，$|a_n|$ 表示绝对值或复数的模。

> [!important] The implication / 保证收敛的方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A_N\to A<\infty$, then $S_N$ converges to a finite real or complex number $S$. In particular,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果 $A_N\to A<\infty$，那么 $S_N$ 趋于某个有限实数或复数 $S$。特别地，
>
> $$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|<\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n\text{ converges}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Control every finite tail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*控制每一段有限尾和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare two late partial sums, with integers $q>p\ge1$. Their difference adds just the terms between positions $p+1$ and $q$. The triangle inequality says that the magnitude of a finite sum is at most the sum of its magnitudes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较两个较晚的部分和，设整数 $q>p\ge1$。它们的差只包含位置 $p+1$ 到 $q$ 的各项。三角不等式说明：有限和的大小，不超过各项大小之和。

$$
|S_q-S_p|
=\left|\sum_{n=p+1}^{q}a_n\right|
\le\sum_{n=p+1}^{q}|a_n|
=A_q-A_p
\le A-A_p.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last inequality holds because the nonnegative totals $A_q$ increase to $A$. Given any desired error $\varepsilon>0$, choose a cutoff $M$ such that $A-A_p<\varepsilon$ whenever $p\ge M$. The same cutoff then gives $|S_q-S_p|<\varepsilon$ for **every** $q>p\ge M$. By symmetry it controls either ordering of two late indices. The later endpoint $q$ can be arbitrarily far away.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个不等式来自非负累计值 $A_q$ 递增到 $A$。任给误差要求 $\varepsilon>0$，都能选一个门槛 $M$，使 $p\ge M$ 时 $A-A_p<\varepsilon$。于是同一个门槛对**每个** $q>p\ge M$ 都保证 $|S_q-S_p|<\varepsilon$；交换两个下标，也得到相同控制。较晚的端点 $q$ 可以离 $p$ 任意远。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sequence with this property is called **Cauchy**: all sufficiently late terms are close to one another, without first naming a candidate limit. The **completeness of the real numbers** says every real Cauchy sequence has a finite real limit; this follows from the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property]]. Thus the argument proves real-series convergence, not merely boundedness of its partial sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具有这个性质的数列称为 **Cauchy 数列**：足够晚的所有项彼此接近，不必先猜出极限是什么。**实数的完备性**保证每个实数 Cauchy 数列都有有限实数极限；这个性质可由[[实数的确界性质]]推出。因此，上面的论证证明了实数级数真正收敛，而不只是部分和有界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex partial sums, write $S_N=u_N+i v_N$, where $u_N,v_N$ are real and $i^2=-1$. Each coordinate difference is no larger than the complex magnitude: $|u_q-u_p|\le|S_q-S_p|$ and $|v_q-v_p|\le|S_q-S_p|$. Both coordinate sequences are real Cauchy sequences, so $u_N\to u$ and $v_N\to v$. Therefore $S_N\to u+i v$. This explains the needed completeness of the complex numbers using real completeness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复数部分和，写成 $S_N=u_N+i v_N$，其中 $u_N,v_N$ 是实数，$i^2=-1$。每个坐标的差都不超过复数差的模：$|u_q-u_p|\le|S_q-S_p|$、$|v_q-v_p|\le|S_q-S_p|$。所以两列坐标都是实数 Cauchy 数列，分别有极限 $u_N\to u$、$v_N\to v$，从而 $S_N\to u+i v$。这就用实数完备性解释了证明需要的复数完备性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now that $S$ exists, let $q\to\infty$ in the finite-tail inequality with $p=N$ fixed. It also gives a truncation bound:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在已经知道 $S$ 存在，在有限尾和不等式中固定 $p=N$，再令 $q\to\infty$，便得到截断误差界：

$$
|S-S_N|\le A-A_N=\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}|a_n|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An example with arbitrary signs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*符号可以自由变化的例子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose any sequence of signs $\epsilon_n$, each either $+1$ or $-1$. The signs need not alternate. Let $a_n=\epsilon_n/2^n$ for $n\ge1$. Its magnitude series is the [[几何级数|geometric series]] $\sum2^{-n}=1$, whatever the sign pattern. Thus every such original series converges. After $N$ terms, its remaining error is at most $2^{-N}$; after ten terms, the guarantee is $1/1024$. The theorem establishes a sum and a bound without needing a closed formula for that sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取符号序列 $\epsilon_n$，每项为 $+1$ 或 $-1$，不要求正负交替。对 $n\ge1$，令 $a_n=\epsilon_n/2^n$。无论符号怎样安排，幅度级数都是[[几何级数]] $\sum2^{-n}=1$，所以原级数一定收敛。加完 $N$ 项后，剩余误差至多为 $2^{-N}$；加完十项，保证界为 $1/1024$。不需要原和的闭式公式，也能证明它存在并控制误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In other problems, use [[级数直接比较判别|comparison]], the [[级数比值判别|ratio test]], or the [[级数根值判别|root test]] to establish convergence of $\sum|a_n|$. This theorem then transfers that conclusion to the original signs or complex directions. Each chosen test still needs its own hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对其他通项，可以先用[[级数直接比较判别]]、[[级数比值判别]]或[[级数根值判别]]证明 $\sum|a_n|$ 收敛，再用本定理把结论带回原来的符号或复数方向。所选判别本身的条件仍须检查。

> [!warning] Small terms are weaker than small tails / 单项小不等于整段尾和小
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the harmonic series, $a_n=1/n\to0$, but the tail from $p+1$ to $2p$ contains $p$ terms each at least $1/(2p)$, so it totals at least $1/2$. The required uniform tail control fails. Conversely, failure of absolute convergence does not imply divergence of the original series: [[条件收敛|conditional convergence]] supplies convergent counterexamples.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对调和级数，$a_n=1/n\to0$，但从 $p+1$ 到 $2p$ 的尾段有 $p$ 项，每项至少为 $1/(2p)$，故总量至少为 $1/2$。证明所需的统一尾和控制并不成立。反过来，不绝对收敛也不意味着原级数发散：[[条件收敛]]提供了原级数仍收敛的反例。
