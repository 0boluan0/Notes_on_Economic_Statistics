# Computing matrix powers by diagonalization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用对角化计算矩阵幂*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix power means applying the same linear action repeatedly. For example, $A^2=AA$ uses [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]]; it does not square each entry separately. If we can describe the action in an [[对角化|eigenvector basis]], each coordinate is simply multiplied by its own number. Repeating the action then means taking powers of those numbers and converting back.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵的幂表示反复施加同一个线性作用。例如 $A^2=AA$ 使用[[矩阵乘法]]，不是把每个元素分别平方。如果能通过[[对角化]]找到特征向量基，每个坐标就只乘自己的一个数；反复作用于是变成对这些数取幂，再换回原坐标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First see the repeated action
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看反复作用怎样变简单*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$. It sends $(a,b)^T$ to $(a+b,2b)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] to write a column. The vectors $x_1=(1,0)^T$ and $x_2=(1,1)^T$ satisfy $Ax_1=x_1$ and $Ax_2=2x_2$. They are [[特征对|eigenvectors]] with respective eigenvalues $1$ and $2$, and form a [[基|basis]]: every input has a unique expression as their combination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$，它把 $(a,b)^T$ 送到 $(a+b,2b)^T$；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，用于写列向量。向量 $x_1=(1,0)^T$、$x_2=(1,1)^T$ 满足 $Ax_1=x_1$、$Ax_2=2x_2$，是分别对应特征值1、2的[[特征对|特征向量]]。它们还是一组[[基]]：每个输入都能唯一写成二者的组合。

$$S=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
S^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad A=S\Lambda S^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $S$ stores the basis columns, $S^{-1}$ is its [[逆矩阵|inverse]], and $\Lambda$ stores the matching eigenvalues. For an input $(a,b)^T$, conversion gives coordinates $(a-b,b)^T$. After $k$ applications, these become $(a-b,2^k b)^T$. Converting back gives $(a+(2^k-1)b,2^k b)^T$, so for every integer $k\ge0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $S$ 的列放基向量，$S^{-1}$ 是它的[[逆矩阵]]，$\Lambda$ 放按相同顺序配对的特征值。输入 $(a,b)^T$ 换成新坐标后为 $(a-b,b)^T$；作用 $k$ 次后变成 $(a-b,2^k b)^T$，再换回得到 $(a+(2^k-1)b,2^k b)^T$。因此对每个整数 $k\ge0$，

$$A^k=\begin{pmatrix}1&2^k-1\\0&2^k\end{pmatrix}.$$

> [!example] Check zero, one and two steps / 检查零步、一步、两步
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $k=0$, this gives the [[单位矩阵|identity]] $I_2$, meaning no action. At $k=1$, it gives the original $A$. At $k=2$, it gives $\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\0&4\end{pmatrix}$, agreeing with direct multiplication. Squaring the entries separately would incorrectly leave the upper-right entry equal to $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $k=0$ 得到[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$，表示还没有施加作用；$k=1$ 恢复原矩阵；$k=2$ 得到 $\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\0&4\end{pmatrix}$，与直接相乘相同。若逐元素平方，右上角会错误地留在1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the middle coordinate changes disappear
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么中间的换坐标会消去*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work over a fixed field $\mathbb F$, either $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Suppose $A$ is $n\times n$, $n\ge1$, and has a diagonalization $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$ over that field. The columns of the invertible $S$ must be an eigenvector basis, with the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ in the same order. The notation $\operatorname{diag}$ places these values on the main diagonal and zeros everywhere else. Whether such a basis exists is the separate [[可对角化判据|diagonalizability criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定数域 $\mathbb F$ 为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。设 $A$ 为 $n\times n$ 矩阵、$n\ge1$，并在该域上有分解 $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$。可逆矩阵 $S$ 的列必须构成特征向量基，$\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ 是按相同顺序配对的[[对角矩阵]]；$\operatorname{diag}$ 把所列值放在主对角线上，其余位置为零。是否存在这样的基，由另一张[[可对角化判据]]判断。

$$A^2=S\Lambda\underbrace{S^{-1}S}_{I_n}\Lambda S^{-1}
=S\Lambda^2S^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every additional multiplication cancels another adjacent $S^{-1}S$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每多乘一次，就再消去一对相邻的 $S^{-1}S$，因此

$$\boxed{A^k=S\Lambda^kS^{-1}\quad(k=0,1,2,\ldots).}$$
$$\Lambda^k=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1^k,\ldots,\lambda_n^k)\quad(k\ge1),\qquad A^0=\Lambda^0=I_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero eigenvalues are allowed. At exponent zero, use the identity directly, including when $A$ is the zero matrix. For a negative integer $k=-m$ with $m\ge1$, define $A^{-m}=(A^{-1})^m$ only when $A$ is invertible. In this diagonalization that means every $\lambda_i\ne0$, and the same formula uses $\lambda_i^{-m}$. The opening example gives $A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1/2\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$. For a quick even-power check, $\operatorname{diag}(2,-1)^4=\operatorname{diag}(16,1)$: the negative eigenvalue becomes positive at this even exponent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许出现零特征值。指数为零时直接使用单位矩阵，即使 $A$ 是零矩阵也一样。对负整数 $k=-m$、$m\ge1$，只有 $A$ 可逆时才定义 $A^{-m}=(A^{-1})^m$；在这组对角化中，就是每个 $\lambda_i\ne0$。此时同一公式使用 $\lambda_i^{-m}$。开头例子的逆为 $A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1/2\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$。再检查一个偶次幂：$\operatorname{diag}(2,-1)^4=\operatorname{diag}(16,1)$，负特征值在这个偶数指数下变为正数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read an evolving vector mode by mode
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逐个模式读取向量怎样变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $u_0$ be an initial vector and let $u_{k+1}=Au_k$ describe one multiplication per step. Write $c=S^{-1}u_0$, whose entries $c_i$ are the initial coefficients in the eigenvector basis $x_1,\ldots,x_n$. Then, for $k\ge0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $u_0$ 为初始向量，递推 $u_{k+1}=Au_k$ 表示每步乘一次。令 $c=S^{-1}u_0$，其元素 $c_i$ 是初值在特征向量基 $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ 下的系数。对 $k\ge0$，有

$$u_k=A^ku_0=S\Lambda^kc=\sum_{i=1}^n c_i\lambda_i^k x_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $k=0$, each scalar factor in this sum is the empty product $1$, even for a zero eigenvalue. For later steps, a mode with $|\lambda_i|<1$ decays, one with $|\lambda_i|>1$ grows if its initial coefficient is nonzero, and a negative real eigenvalue alternates sign. Nonreal conjugate pairs in a real system describe rotation and oscillation. The largest eigenvalue modulus is the [[谱半径|spectral radius]], but an individual initial vector need not contain that mode.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$k=0$ 时，和式里的每个标量因子都按空乘积取1，包括零特征值。之后，$|\lambda_i|<1$ 的模式衰减；$|\lambda_i|>1$ 的模式在初始系数非零时增长；负实特征值使符号交替。实系统中的非实共轭对描述旋转和振荡。最大的特征值模是[[谱半径]]，但一个具体初值未必含有对应模式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, with $A=\operatorname{diag}(0.8,1.1)$ and $u_0=(2,1)^T$, the state is $u_k=(2\cdot0.8^k,1.1^k)^T$: its first component disappears and its second grows. Starting instead at $(2,0)^T$ gives a decaying trajectory for the same matrix. The [[矩阵幂趋零判据|criterion for vanishing matrix powers]] asks for decay for every initial vector, which is a stronger question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=\operatorname{diag}(0.8,1.1)$、$u_0=(2,1)^T$ 时，$u_k=(2\cdot0.8^k,1.1^k)^T$，第一个分量消失，第二个增长。若改从 $(2,0)^T$ 出发，同一个矩阵却给出衰减轨迹。[[矩阵幂趋零判据]]要求对每个初值都衰减，是更强的问题。

> [!warning] An exact formula has prerequisites / 精确公式仍有前提
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the eigenvectors do not form a basis, powers still exist, but this diagonal formula is unavailable; use [[Jordan块的幂|Jordan-block powers]] or direct multiplication. A real matrix may admit the formula over $\mathbb C$ even when it has no real eigenbasis; its integer powers remain real. Also, converting coordinates through $S$ can amplify small input errors when that conversion is sensitive. [[条件性与算法稳定性|Problem conditioning and algorithmic stability]] explain why algebraic diagonalization alone does not guarantee a reliable floating-point calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 特征向量凑不成基时，矩阵幂仍存在，只是不能使用这个对角公式；可以改用[[Jordan块的幂]]或直接相乘。实矩阵即使没有实特征基，也可能在复数域上使用公式，其整数幂仍为实矩阵。另外，通过 $S$ 换坐标时，若坐标转换对输入很敏感，就可能放大小扰动。[[条件性与算法稳定性]]解释了为什么代数上能对角化并不自动保证浮点计算可靠。
