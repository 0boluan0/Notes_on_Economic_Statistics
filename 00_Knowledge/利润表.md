# 利润表是列示报告主体在一定期间确认的损益项目及净利润或净亏损的报表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An income statement reports recognised profit-or-loss items and the resulting net profit or loss for a period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润表（income statement）按会计确认规则汇总期间内进入损益的收入、费用及相关利得损失，解释净利润或净亏损如何形成。它是期间成果表，不是客户收款单；报告期可以是一年，也可以是明确的一段中期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An income statement summarises the income, expenses, and related gains and losses recognised in profit or loss, explaining the period's net result. It reports performance rather than customer cash receipts, over a year or a specified interim period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以忽略税费、无其他损益的赊销为例：本期交付商品并满足收入确认条件，确认收入100，同时转出账面成本60，利润增加40；客户尚未付款，所以不能据此说本期已经收到40现金。相应应收款与存货变化落在[[资产负债表]]，现金收支另看[[现金流量表]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a credit sale with no taxes, fees, or other profit-or-loss items. Delivery satisfies revenue-recognition requirements, producing revenue of 100 and an expense for carrying cost of 60. Profit rises by 40 even though the customer has not paid. Receivables and inventory appear in the [[资产负债表|balance sheet]], while the [[现金流量表|cash-flow statement]] separately reports cash movements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净利润也不等于全部权益变动：所有者投入和分配不是经营损益，其他综合收益则按适用准则列在净利润之外。在采用利润与其他综合收益合并列报的格式时，仍要辨认净利润小计与综合收益总额；完整权益桥见[[所有者权益变动表]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net profit does not capture every equity movement. Owner contributions and distributions are not operating results, and other comprehensive income is reported outside profit or loss under the applicable framework. A combined presentation still distinguishes net profit from total comprehensive income; the full equity reconciliation belongs in the [[所有者权益变动表|statement of changes in equity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [SEC，Beginners' Guide，Income Statements及Cash Flow Statements小节](https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/begfinstmtguide.htm)：支持期间收入费用与净损益、利润和现金的区别。100与60为明确简化的自拟例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The SEC sections support period income, expenses, net results, and their distinction from cash. The 100/60 credit-sale example is original.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [AASB 101，第7、10A、27—28、88—89、106段；PDF第8—9、13、23、26页](https://www.aasb.gov.au/admin/file/content105/c9/AASB101_07-15_COMPdec22_01-24.pdf#page=8)：核对权责发生基础、损益与其他综合收益、所有者交易的分类边界；本卡不把某版本的营业利润小计定义推广到所有准则和期间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
AASB 101 verifies accrual reporting and the distinction among profit or loss, other comprehensive income, and owner transactions. No version-specific operating-profit subtotal is imposed on every accounting framework or period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
