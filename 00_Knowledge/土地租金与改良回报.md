# 农场支付给地主的总租金可以同时包含地租与改良资本回报

一笔写在租约上的农场租金，未必全是 Ricardo 所说的地租。土地的天然生产条件可以带来收益，地主修建的仓库、排水系统和围栏也可以带来收益；把两者都付给同一个人，不意味着它们来自同一种经济机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A farm's contractual rental payment need not consist entirely of rent in Ricardo's sense. Natural conditions can generate a land return, while a landlord's storage buildings, drainage and fences can generate a return on invested capital. Payment to the same recipient does not make these the same economic mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Ricardo 在第二章开头把狭义地租限定为使用土地原有生产能力的报酬，并用两块面积和天然肥力相同的农场作比较：一块经过改良，另一块没有。前者租得更贵，额外支付的一部分是在使用已经投入的资本，而不是它原本就更肥沃。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the start of Chapter II, Ricardo restricts rent to payment for the land's original productive powers. He compares farms of equal size and natural fertility, only one of which has been improved. Part of its higher rental payment remunerates the use of invested capital, not greater original fertility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以，分析 [[级差地租]] 时，要先问观察到的“租金”究竟包括什么。如果一块农场每年付 1,200，其中经明确估值有 300 对应建筑和排水资本的使用，便不能把全部 1,200 都作为原有土地能力的报酬。这里的 300 是教学例子中给定的分解，不是从总租金本身就能识别出的数额；实证上还要处理折旧、维修责任与合同期限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before using an observed rental payment to study [[级差地租|differential rent]], establish what it covers. If a farm pays 1,200 annually and an explicit valuation assigns 300 to buildings and drainage, the whole 1,200 cannot be attributed to original land quality. The 300 is an assumption in this teaching example, not something identifiable from the total alone. Empirical work must also address depreciation, maintenance obligations and the contract term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原书用“原有且不可毁坏的能力”说明这一分析边界。这是其历史定义，不是在证明土壤永不退化。实际土地质量能够因侵蚀、施肥和灌溉而变化；做应用时须说明哪些条件被视为土地禀赋，哪些属于需要补偿的投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo's phrase about original and indestructible powers defines his historical analytical category; it does not establish that soil never degrades. Erosion, fertilization and irrigation can change land quality. An application must specify what is treated as an endowment and what as remunerated investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》II.2](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=3#2.2)：支持狭义地租与包含资本利息、利润的合同支付之间的区分，以及同面积、同天然肥力农场的比较。
- 1,200／300 的货币例为独立构造，只说明分类不能从收款人身份推出；不是历史农场租金数据，也不是土地估值方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter II, paragraph 2, supplies the distinction and matched-farm comparison. The monetary example is constructed to illustrate classification, not to estimate historical rents or prescribe a valuation method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
