# 经营营运资本是经营性流动资产减经营性流动负债
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating working capital is current operating assets less current operating liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经营营运资本（ONWC）是企业在短期经营循环中的净占用：先划定经营性流动资产 $OCA$ 和经营性流动负债 $OCL$，再计算 $ONWC=OCA-OCL$。应收和存货通常占用资金，应付供应商等经营负债则提供一部分支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ONWC is net investment in the short-term operating cycle. After identifying current operating assets $OCA$ and liabilities $OCL$, calculate $ONWC=OCA-OCL$. Receivables and inventory usually tie up funds, while trade payables and other operating liabilities provide some support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它不同于全部流动资产减全部流动负债的[[营运资本]]。例如 CPA 把 ABC 的 44 货币资金视为经营所需，经营性流动资产为 700，经营性流动负债为 228，故 ONWC 为 472；普通营运资本却为 $700-300=400$，差额来自流动负债的功能分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It differs from [[营运资本|net working capital]] calculated using all current assets and liabilities. CPA treats ABC's cash of 44 as required for operations, giving current operating assets of 700 and liabilities of 228, so ONWC is 472. Ordinary working capital is $700-300=400$; the difference comes from the functional classification of current liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估值模型也常用“非现金经营营运资本”，把现金全部留到现金与金融资产的调整中。两种约定可以服务不同模型，但不能拿一种的利润和资本投入配另一种的现金变化。用于[[企业自由现金流]]时，应明确现金是否包含，并按同一口径计算期初、期末及其差额；ABC 剔除现金后为什么得到不同现金流，见[[自由现金流的净经营资产表示]]中的 19 单位现金口径桥，分类依据见[[管理用报表分类]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Valuation models also commonly use non-cash operating working capital, handling all cash separately. Different conventions can serve different models, but their profit, investment, and cash movements must not be mixed. In [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]], specify whether cash is included and measure both endpoints consistently. The 19-unit cash bridge in [[自由现金流的净经营资产表示|the NOA representation]] explains ABC's different cash-flow results; see also [[管理用报表分类|the classification method]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
