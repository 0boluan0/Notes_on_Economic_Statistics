# 营业利润率是按明确报表口径计算的营业利润占相应营业收入的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating profit margin is operating profit divided by corresponding revenue under a specified reporting basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

营业利润率回答在所采用的经营利润层次上，每单位收入留下多少利润。若营业利润为 $OP$、同期间匹配收入为 $S>0$，则 $m_{op}=OP/S$。先声明报表采用哪些经营项目，才能跨公司比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating margin measures profit remaining per unit of revenue at the chosen operating-profit level. For operating profit $OP$ and matched period revenue $S>0$, $m_{op}=OP/S$. Cross-company comparisons require a stated classification of operating items.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一份简化非金融损益表中，收入 100、销售成本 60、销售与管理费用 20，没有其他经营项目，则 $OP=20$，营业利润率为 20%。它比 40% 的[[毛利率]]多扣了期间经营费用；若还有利息和所得税，则与[[营业净利率]]也不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified non-financial income statement with revenue of 100, cost of sales of 60, selling and administrative expenses of 20, and no other operating items, operating profit is 20 and operating margin is 20%. It deducts more expenses than the 40% [[毛利率|gross margin]], and differs from [[营业净利率|net margin]] when interest and income tax remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要把任何名为“营业利润”的小计都无条件翻成 EBIT。不同准则及版本对投资收益、利息、汇兑等归类不同；中国报表营业利润还可能包含财务费用等。若本题采用 EBIT/S，必须明确是息税前利润率，而非仅凭中文科目名称认定二者相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not automatically equate every reported operating-profit subtotal with EBIT. Standards and versions classify investment income, interest, and exchange effects differently; Chinese operating profit may include finance expenses. If an exercise uses EBIT/S, identify it explicitly as an EBIT margin rather than inferring equivalence from a line-item label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
