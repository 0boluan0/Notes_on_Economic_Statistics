# 财务报表附注是解释编制基础、重要会计信息及报表项目的组成部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Notes are the financial-statement component explaining the preparation basis, material accounting information, and reported items.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务报表附注（notes）用叙述、明细和调节信息解释报表数字，并提供按准则需要披露、却未直接列成表内金额的信息。附注属于[[财务报表]]，不是可有可无的阅读提示，也不是与报表分离的管理层宣传材料。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Notes explain reported figures through narrative, breakdowns, and reconciliations, including required information not recognised as a line-item amount. They form part of the [[财务报表|financial statements]], rather than optional reading tips or separate management promotional material.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，资产负债表只显示应收款账面额45；附注明示原额50减损失准备5，并解释重要估计，才使读者看清“45”如何得到。这个拆分不自动证明预计收款正确，但提供了核查的对象。会计政策、关键判断、估计不确定性、担保或承诺等披露要按相关事项和适用准则判断，不能只记一张固定清单。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a balance sheet may show receivables of 45 while a note explains a gross amount of 50 less a loss allowance of 5 and the important estimates involved. The breakdown does not prove that expected collections are correct, but makes the measurement reviewable. Policies, judgements, estimation uncertainty, guarantees, and commitments require disclosures appropriate to the issue and framework, not merely a memorised checklist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读附注应沿具体项目的交叉索引查明范围和计量，再回到报表比较。例如同样的资产金额，成本计量与公允价值计量未必可直接比较。披露也不能自动补救错误确认或计量：正文数字错了，不能仅靠一句说明使其正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow the cross-reference for a particular item, establish its scope and measurement, and then return to the comparison. Equal amounts measured at cost and at fair value need not be directly comparable. Disclosure does not itself repair incorrect recognition or measurement; an explanatory sentence cannot make an incorrect number correct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [AASB 101，第7、18、33、112—125段；PDF第7、10、13、27—28页](https://www.aasb.gov.au/admin/file/content105/c9/AASB101_07-15_COMPdec22_01-24.pdf#page=27)：支持附注作为报表组成、系统交叉参照、重要政策与估计、披露不能补救不当政策；50−5=45为自拟计量拆分，不是损失率建议。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
AASB 101 supports the role of notes, cross-references, material policies and estimates, and the inability of disclosure to cure inappropriate accounting. The 50−5=45 breakdown is original and is not a recommended loss rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [SEC，Beginners' Guide，Read the Footnotes小节](https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/begfinstmtguide.htm)：核对政策、所得税、养老金等明细对解释表内数字的作用；只取附注阅读职责，不把列举当作所有企业的穷尽要求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The SEC section illustrates how policies, tax, and pension details inform interpretation. Its examples are not treated as an exhaustive requirement for every entity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
