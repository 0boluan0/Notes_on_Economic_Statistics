# 融资账户追保应先固定权益比例口径再计算价格阈值和具体补足操作
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fix the equity-ratio convention before calculating a margin threshold and the operation that restores compliance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先限定模型：账户只有 $N>0$ 股融资买入证券，每股价 $P>0$，证券市值 $V=NP$，债务 $D>0$ 在当前计算时点已包括应计利息；没有其他资产负债。要求按证券市值的固定比例 $0<m<1$ 保有权益。此时 $E=NP-D$，低于该维持要求的条件为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the model first: the account holds only $N>0$ financed shares at $P>0$, so $V=NP$. Debt $D>0$ includes accrued interest at the calculation time; there are no other assets or liabilities. Required equity is a fixed fraction $0<m<1$ of securities value. With $E=NP-D$, a maintenance breach occurs when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{NP-D}{NP}<m
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
P< P^*=\frac{D}{N(1-m)}.
$$

等于阈值时恰好满足该比例，不代表有额外缓冲。此式不是实际自动平仓价：券商要求改变、报价跳跃、费用或操作延迟都会改变实际结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equality meets the specified ratio exactly, without a buffer. This is not an automatic execution price: changed requirements, price jumps, costs, and operational delays can alter actual outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主操作设为投资者投入现金 $C$，立即用于偿还债务，不买卖证券。于是 $V$ 不变、$D'=D-C$、$E'=E+C$。恢复最低比例所需现金为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the main operation, the investor contributes cash $C$ and immediately repays debt, without trading securities. Then $V$ is unchanged, $D'=D-C$, and $E'=E+C$. The minimum contribution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_{\min}=[mV-E]^+=[D-(1-m)V]^+.
$$

例如 $N=100,D=6,000,m=0.4$，阈值为每股 100 元。现价 90 元时 $V=9,000,E=3,000$，要求 3,600 元；投入 600 元偿债后，债务 5,400 元、权益 3,600 元，比例恰为 40%。不是投入 $40\%\times6,000$，因为要求的分母是证券市值，不是借款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $N=100,D=6,000,m=0.4$, the threshold is 100 per share. At 90, $V=9,000,E=3,000$, against required equity of 3,600. A 600 contribution repays debt to 5,400 and raises equity to 3,600, exactly 40%. The amount is not $40\%\times6,000$: the denominator is securities value, not debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若现金留在账户而不偿债，必须重读比例定义。若规则仍以证券市值 $V$ 为分母、现金全额计入权益，缺额仍是 $[mV-E]^+$。若另行约定按全部资产 $V+C$ 计算权益比例，才有 $(E+C)/(V+C)\ge m$，从而
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If cash stays in the account instead of repaying debt, inspect the ratio definition. If securities value $V$ remains the denominator and cash fully counts toward equity, the shortfall remains $[mV-E]^+$. Only under a separately specified total-asset denominator $V+C$ does $(E+C)/(V+C)\ge m$ imply
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_{\min}^{\text{all-assets}}=\frac{[mV-E]^+}{1-m}.
$$

后一假设在同例给出 1,000 元：$(3,000+1,000)/(9,000+1,000)=40\%$。它是不同计量模型，不是把证券账户监管公式改写后的同一个答案。现金是否自动抵债、能否留存、分母及抵押折算率均须明确；提交证券、卖券偿债或存在多空组合时，必须重新列资产负债，不能套用这两个补现金式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The latter assumption requires 1,000 in the example: $(3,000+1,000)/(9,000+1,000)=40\%$. It is a different measurement model, not an alternative answer under the original securities-value rule. Specify cash netting, retention, the denominator, and collateral credit. Securities deposits, asset sales to repay debt, or long–short portfolios require a new balance sheet rather than either cash formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
