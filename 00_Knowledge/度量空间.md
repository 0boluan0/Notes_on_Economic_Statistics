# Metric spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*度量空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a number line, the distance from $2$ to $5$ is $|5-2|=3$. A **metric space** keeps the idea of distance while allowing the objects to be something other than numbers or points in a drawing. It consists of a collection of objects together with a rule that assigns a distance to every pair. Once the rule is fixed, “within one unit” and “getting closer” have precise meanings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在数轴上，$2$ 到 $5$ 的距离为 $|5-2|=3$。**度量空间**保留距离的思想，但对象可以不是数字，也不必是图上的点。它由一组对象以及给每一对对象指定距离的规则组成。规则确定后，“相距不到一单位”和“越来越近”才有精确含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What a distance rule must satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*距离规则必须满足什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $X$ for the set of objects and $x,y,z$ for its elements. The notation $X\times X$ means all ordered pairs $(x,y)$ of elements of $X$. A function $d:X\times X\to[0,\infty)$ assigns each pair a **finite nonnegative real number** $d(x,y)$. The infinity symbol in $[0,\infty)$ does not allow infinite distances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $X$ 表示对象集合，$x,y,z$ 表示其中的元素。$X\times X$ 表示所有有序对 $(x,y)$ 的集合。函数 $d:X\times X\to[0,\infty)$ 给每一对指定一个**有限非负实数** $d(x,y)$。区间 $[0,\infty)$ 中的无穷符号并不允许距离取无穷。

> [!important] The metric conditions · 度量条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every $x,y,z\in X$, require
> $$d(x,y)=0\iff x=y,\qquad d(x,y)=d(y,x),$$
> $$d(x,z)\le d(x,y)+d(y,z).$$
> Together with nonnegativity, these conditions say that only identical objects have zero distance, distance is symmetric, and going directly is no longer than going via an intermediate object. The pair $(X,d)$ is the metric space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意 $x,y,z\in X$，要求上面的等式与不等式成立。连同非负性，它们分别表示：只有同一个对象与自身距离为零；交换两端不改变距离；直接到达不会比经过一个中间对象更远。配对 $(X,d)$ 就是度量空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real coordinate points, the familiar rule is $d(x,y)=\|x-y\|_2$. Here the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] measures the length of the difference vector. For example, the distance from $(1,2)$ to $(4,6)$ is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. A general metric space needs no vector addition, origin or coordinate system: the distance rule itself is the structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实坐标点之间的常见规则是 $d(x,y)=\|x-y\|_2$，其中[[欧氏范数]]测量差向量的长度。例如 $(1,2)$ 到 $(4,6)$ 的距离为 $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$。一般度量空间不要求向量加法、原点或坐标系，距离规则本身就是所需结构。

> [!warning] A nonnegative score need not be a metric · 非负分数不一定是度量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The squared-distance rule $s(x,y)=(x-y)^2$ on the real line is nonnegative, symmetric and zero only at identical points, but it fails the triangle inequality: $s(0,2)=4>1+1=s(0,1)+s(1,2)$. All conditions must hold; checking only the first few is insufficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实数上的平方距离 $s(x,y)=(x-y)^2$ 非负、对称，并且只在两点相同时为零，却不满足三角不等式：$s(0,2)=4>1+1=s(0,1)+s(1,2)$。必须同时检查全部条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Open balls describe a permitted distance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*开球表示允许的距离范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a centre $x\in X$ and a radius $r>0$. The **open ball** is
$$B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}.$$
The braces collect exactly those points whose distance from $x$ is strictly less than $r$. Thus on the usual real line $B_2(2)=(0,4)$: the endpoints are excluded because their distance is exactly $2$. A ball need not look round; its membership is determined by $d$ and by the ambient set $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定中心 $x\in X$ 和半径 $r>0$，**开球**定义为 $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$。大括号收集距中心严格小于 $r$ 的全部点。因此通常实数距离下，$B_2(2)=(0,4)$；端点距离恰好为 $2$，所以不包括。球不一定画成圆，其成员由 $d$ 和环境集合 $X$ 决定。

> [!example] Distance without coordinates · 不需要坐标的距离
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On any set, define the discrete metric by $d(x,y)=0$ if $x=y$ and $d(x,y)=1$ otherwise. If $x\ne z$, at least one of $x\ne y$ and $y\ne z$ holds, so the triangle inequality follows. For $0<r\le1$, $B_r(x)=\{x\}$; for $r>1$, $B_r(x)=X$. A set of labels such as $\{a,b,c\}$ therefore becomes a metric space without assigning coordinates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在任意集合上，令相同元素的距离为 $0$，不同元素的距离为 $1$，就得到离散度量。若 $x\ne z$，则 $x\ne y$、$y\ne z$ 至少有一个成立，因此三角不等式成立。半径满足 $0<r\le1$ 时球只有中心；$r>1$ 时球为整个 $X$。于是标签集合 $\{a,b,c\}$ 也可以成为度量空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the triangle inequality gives local room
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*三角不等式为什么提供局部余量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $y$ lies in $B_r(x)$, it has positive room $\delta=r-d(x,y)>0$ before reaching the distance threshold. Whenever $d(y,z)<\delta$, the triangle inequality gives
$$d(x,z)\le d(x,y)+d(y,z)<d(x,y)+\delta=r.$$
Therefore $B_\delta(y)\subseteq B_r(x)$: around every point of a ball there is a smaller ball staying inside it. This is the local condition used to define an [[开集|open set]]. The same balls provide the language of [[序列收敛|metric convergence]] and [[连续性|continuity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $y\in B_r(x)$，到距离门槛还剩正余量 $\delta=r-d(x,y)>0$。只要 $d(y,z)<\delta$，三角不等式就给出 $d(x,z)<r$，因此 $B_\delta(y)\subseteq B_r(x)$。这说明球中每一点周围都有一个仍留在球内的小球，正是[[开集]]定义使用的局部条件。这些球也为[[序列收敛]]和[[连续性]]提供语言。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing $d$ can change which sets are open. Conversely, different numerical distance rules can generate the same open sets; that distinction is developed in [[拓扑等价度量|topologically equivalent metrics]]. Always specify both $X$ and $d$ before using a metric claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改变 $d$ 可能改变哪些集合是开的；反过来，不同的数值距离也可能生成相同开集，见[[拓扑等价度量]]。使用度量结论之前，要明确 $X$ 和 $d$。
