# 按期望净收益排序需要对所比较的风险保持风险中性等条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ranking by expected net benefits requires conditions such as risk neutrality over the risks being compared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期望净收益准则按各可能结果的概率加权货币净收益排序。要把它作为一般福利排序规则，需要额外条件。一套明确的充分条件是：结果已在同一评价时点、计价单位及背景下可比，而且对所比较的货币风险采用风险中性的评价；分配与未货币化影响也不能被未经说明地排除。某一组项目的均值排序与福利排序偶然一致，并不证明评价者风险中性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expected net-benefit criterion ranks probability-weighted monetary net outcomes. Using it as a general welfare-ranking rule requires additional conditions. One explicit sufficient set is comparable dates, units and background conditions and risk-neutral evaluation of the monetary risks in question. Distributional and unmonetised impacts cannot be silently excluded. Agreement between mean and welfare rankings for one set of projects does not prove risk neutrality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在期望效用模型中，固定背景财富 $w$，净收益为随机变量 $Y$，真实情景概率 $p_s\ge0$、总和为1，且期望存在。若评价效用为递增仿射形式 $u(x)=a+bx$、$b>0$，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an expected-utility model, fix background wealth $w$ and let $Y$ be random net benefits, with actual scenario probabilities summing to one and finite expectations. For increasing affine utility $u(x)=a+bx$, $b>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E[Y]=\sum_s p_sY_s,\qquad E[u(w+Y)]=a+bw+bE[Y].$$

因此均值越大，期望效用越大。若效用非线性，均值就可能不够：终期财富确定为250，与各半概率取100或400，均值同为250；但 $u(x)=\sqrt{x}$ 时，前者效用约15.81，后者期望效用为15，决策者偏好确定方案。相应货币换算见[[风险的确定性等价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a larger mean raises expected utility. With nonlinear utility, the mean may be insufficient: certain terminal wealth of 250 and equally likely wealth of 100 or 400 both average 250, but square-root utility yields about 15.81 versus 15. The decision-maker prefers certainty; see [[风险的确定性等价|certainty equivalents]] for the corresponding monetary comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算期望只是描述概率分布的一个量，不自动完成风险补偿。这里使用的实际概率与[[风险中性定价]]的定价权重不是同一概念；政府项目规模大或存在多种风险，也不自动证明全部风险可忽略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Computing an expectation summarises a distribution; it does not automatically account for risk compensation. Actual probabilities here differ conceptually from the pricing weights in [[风险中性定价|risk-neutral valuation]]. A large government project or many risks does not by itself prove that all risk is negligible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
