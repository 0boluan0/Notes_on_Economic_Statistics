# 同一顶点出发的 n 条边向量所张成单纯形的体积等于其行列式绝对值除以 n 阶乘
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The simplex spanned by $n$ edge vectors from one vertex has volume equal to the absolute determinant divided by $n!$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心公式
> 若 $v_1,\ldots,v_n\in\mathbb R^n$ 是从同一顶点出发的边向量，则它们张成的 $n$ 维单纯形体积是
> $$
> \operatorname{Vol}_{\mathrm{simplex}}
> =\frac{|\det[v_1\ \cdots\ v_n]|}{n!}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The simplex generated by edge vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ has volume $|\det[v_1\ \cdots\ v_n]|/n!$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准单纯形
$$
\Delta_n=\left\{t\in\mathbb R^n:t_i\ge0,\ \sum_{i=1}^nt_i\le1\right\}
$$
的体积是 $1/n!$。令 $V=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_n]$；线性映射 $t\mapsto Vt$ 恰好把 $\Delta_n$ 送到顶点为 $0,v_1,\ldots,v_n$ 的单纯形，并把 $n$ 维体积缩放 $|\det V|$ 倍，因此得到上面的公式。

二维时，三角形面积为相应平行四边形面积的一半：
$$
\operatorname{Area}_{\triangle}=\frac{|\det[v_1\ v_2]|}{2}.
$$
三维时，四面体体积为相应平行六面体体积的六分之一：
$$
\operatorname{Vol}_{\mathrm{tetrahedron}}=\frac{|\det[v_1\ v_2\ v_3]|}{6}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The standard simplex $\Delta_n=\{t_i\ge0:\sum_i t_i\le1\}$ has volume $1/n!$. The linear map with columns $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ sends it to the target simplex and scales volume by the absolute determinant. Thus a triangle has half the area of its parallelogram, and a tetrahedron has one sixth of the volume of its parallelepiped.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 三条边向量组成的列矩阵行列式绝对值为 $12$，对应四面体的体积是多少？
>
> **答案：** $12/3!=2$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The absolute determinant of the column matrix formed by three edge vectors is $12$. What is the volume of the corresponding tetrahedron?
>
> **Answer:** $12/3!=2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
