# Volume of a simplex
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单纯形的行列式体积公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A triangle occupies half of the parallelogram made from two of its edges at one vertex. A tetrahedron occupies one sixth of the corresponding parallelepiped. The general rule divides the edge determinant's absolute value by $n!$, but the edges must be **differences from the same vertex**, and the volume dimension must be specified.

A [[单纯形|simplex]] includes the filled region between its vertices: points are weighted averages with nonnegative weights summing to one. Let $n\ge1$ be an integer and choose vertices $p_0,\ldots,p_n\in\mathbb R^n$. Set $v_i=p_i-p_0$ and arrange these edge vectors as columns:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三角形的面积是同一顶点两条边围成的平行四边形面积的一半；四面体的体积是对应平行六面体体积的六分之一。一般公式把边向量行列式的绝对值除以 $n!$，但这些边必须是**从同一个顶点出发的差向量**，还必须说明测量哪一维体积。

[[单纯形]]包括顶点之间填满的区域，其中各点都是非负且总和为一的权重所给出的顶点平均。设整数 $n\ge1$，顶点 $p_0,\ldots,p_n\in\mathbb R^n$。令 $v_i=p_i-p_0$，把这些边向量排成列：

$$V=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_n],\qquad
S=\left\{\sum_{i=0}^n\theta_i p_i:\theta_i\ge0,\ \sum_{i=0}^n\theta_i=1\right\}.$$

> [!important] Ambient $n$-dimensional volume · 环境中的 $n$ 维体积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$, the volume is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 记 $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$，则体积为
> $$\operatorname{Vol}_n(S)=\frac{|\det V|}{n!}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the $v_i$ are [[线性无关|independent]], $S$ is a full-dimensional $n$-simplex. If they are dependent, the same convex-hull set has zero ambient $n$-volume; it is not a nondegenerate $n$-simplex under the definition linked above.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 若 $v_i$ [[线性无关]]，$S$ 就是满维的 $n$ 维单纯形。若它们相关，同样定义的凸包集合之环境 $n$ 维体积为零；按照上面的定义，它不是非退化的 $n$ 维单纯形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Subtract a common vertex before taking the determinant

For $p_0=(1,1)$, $p_1=(3,2)$, $p_2=(2,3)$, the edges are $(2,1)$ and $(1,2)$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先减去同一个顶点，再取行列式*

取 $p_0=(1,1)$、$p_1=(3,2)$、$p_2=(2,3)$，边向量为 $(2,1)$ 与 $(1,2)$，因此

$$V=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\quad
\det V=4-1=3,\quad \operatorname{Area}(S)=\frac32.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using $p_1,p_2$ directly as the columns would instead describe edges from the origin, producing a different triangle. Changing the base vertex is allowed if **all** edge differences are recomputed: from $p_1$ the edges are $(-2,-1)$ and $(-1,1)$, whose determinant is $-3$ and whose absolute value is still three. Ordinary area does not depend on the ordering of the vertices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若直接把 $p_1,p_2$ 排成列，描述的却是从原点出发的边，会得到另一个三角形。可以更换基准顶点，但必须重新计算**所有**边差：以 $p_1$ 为基准，边为 $(-2,-1)$ 和 $(-1,1)$，行列式为 $-3$，绝对值仍是三。普通面积不依赖顶点的排列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the factor is $1/n!$

The standard simplex is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么是 $1/n!$？*

标准单纯形为

$$\Delta_n=\left\{t\in\mathbb R^n:t_i\ge0,\ \sum_{i=1}^n t_i\le1\right\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $d_n=\operatorname{Vol}_n(\Delta_n)$. For $n=1$, the set is $[0,1]$, so $d_1=1$. For $n\ge2$, fix the last coordinate at $t_n=s\in[0,1]$. The remaining coordinates form $(1-s)\Delta_{n-1}$, whose $(n-1)$-dimensional volume is $(1-s)^{n-1}d_{n-1}$: each of its $n-1$ directions is scaled by $1-s$. Summing these slice volumes through the last coordinate gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $d_n=\operatorname{Vol}_n(\Delta_n)$。$n=1$ 时集合就是 $[0,1]$，故 $d_1=1$。$n\ge2$ 时，把最后一个坐标固定为 $t_n=s\in[0,1]$，剩余坐标构成 $(1-s)\Delta_{n-1}$，其 $n-1$ 维体积为 $(1-s)^{n-1}d_{n-1}$：因为其中的 $n-1$ 个方向都缩放了 $1-s$ 倍。沿最后一个坐标累加切片体积，得到

$$d_n=\int_0^1(1-s)^{n-1}d_{n-1}\,ds
=\frac{d_{n-1}}{n}
=\frac1{n!}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the same principle as [[切片体积|integrating cross-sectional volume]], applied recursively in the dimension. The endpoint slice at $s=1$ has zero $(n-1)$-volume. Finally, $t\mapsto p_0+Vt$ takes $\Delta_n$ exactly onto $S$: the omitted vertex weight is $\theta_0=1-\sum_i t_i$. Translation by $p_0$ does not change volume, and the [[行列式体积与取向|linear determinant-volume rule]] contributes $|\det V|$. Multiplying this by $d_n$ proves the formula. If $V$ is singular, the affine image $p_0+V\Delta_n$ lies in a translated lower-dimensional subspace, and both sides are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与[[切片体积|累加截面体积]]是同一原理，只是对维数递推使用；$s=1$ 的端点切片之 $n-1$ 维体积为零。最后，映射 $t\mapsto p_0+Vt$ 恰把 $\Delta_n$ 送到 $S$，其中省去的顶点权重是 $\theta_0=1-\sum_i t_i$。平移 $p_0$ 不改变体积，[[行列式体积与取向|线性的行列式体积规则]]贡献 $|\det V|$，乘以 $d_n$ 就证明了公式。$V$ 奇异时，仿射像 $p_0+V\Delta_n$ 落在平移后的低维子空间中，等式两边都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，二维与三维分别为

$$\operatorname{Area}_{\triangle}=\frac{|\det[v_1\ v_2]|}{2},\qquad
\operatorname{Vol}_{\mathrm{tetrahedron}}=\frac{|\det[v_1\ v_2\ v_3]|}{6}.$$

> [!example] The tetrahedron volume · 四面体体积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For common-vertex edges $(2,1,-1)^T$, $(2,3,1)^T$, and $(-1,0,4)^T$, the column determinant is $24-8-4=12$. The ordinary parallelepiped volume is $12$ and the tetrahedron volume is $12/3!=2$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so the displayed triples are columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 同顶点边向量为 $(2,1,-1)^T$、$(2,3,1)^T$、$(-1,0,4)^T$ 时，列矩阵的行列式为 $24-8-4=12$。普通平行六面体体积为 $12$，四面体体积为 $12/3!=2$。其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]，这些三元组排成列。

## The dimension belongs to the measurement

A triangle in $\mathbb R^3$ has two edge vectors and can have positive area, but its ordinary three-dimensional volume is zero. A $3\times2$ edge matrix has no ordinary determinant. To compute its area, choose orthonormal coordinates in its plane, or use the [[矩形Jacobian体积因子|Gram factor]] for its two-dimensional parallelogram and then divide by $2!$. The square formula above concerns $n$ edges in $\mathbb R^n$; it does not turn a lower-dimensional area into ambient volume.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先确定测量的维数*

$\mathbb R^3$ 中的三角形有两个边向量，可以有正面积，但普通三维体积为零。$3\times2$ 边矩阵没有普通行列式。计算面积时，可以在其平面内选标准正交坐标，或者用[[矩形Jacobian体积因子|Gram 因子]]计算二维平行四边形面积，再除以 $2!$。上面的方阵公式讨论的是 $\mathbb R^n$ 中的 $n$ 条边，不会把低维面积变成环境体积。
