# Rank of a sample covariance matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本协方差的秩*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sample covariance matrix can describe fewer independent directions than there are measured variables. This always happens when there are too few observations, and it can also happen when variables repeat the same information. The rank bound tells us when an ordinary matrix inverse is impossible before attempting to compute it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

样本协方差能描述的独立方向，可能少于测量变量的个数。观测太少时一定如此；变量重复记录同一信息时也可能如此。秩上界能让我们在实际求逆之前，就判断普通矩阵逆何时必然不存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a complete real [[多元数据矩阵|data matrix]] $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$, with $n\ge2$ observations and $p\ge1$ variables. Write $X_c=HX$ for the centered data, where the [[中心化矩阵|centering matrix]] is $H=I_n-\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$. The observed [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance]] is $s=X_c^TX_c/(n-1)$. Its [[矩阵秩|rank]] counts independent linear directions. The following statement is about each fixed data table, so it needs no probability model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取完整实数[[多元数据矩阵]] $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$，含 $n\ge2$ 次观测、$p\ge1$ 个变量。令 $X_c=HX$ 为中心化数据，[[中心化矩阵]] $H=I_n-\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$。观测的[[样本协方差矩阵]]为 $s=X_c^TX_c/(n-1)$，其[[矩阵秩|秩]]计算独立线性方向的个数。以下结论针对每张固定数据表，无需概率模型。

> [!important] A deterministic bound · 确定性的上界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every such table,
> $$\operatorname{rank}(s)=\operatorname{rank}(X_c)\le\min(p,n-1).$$
> Moreover, $s$ is invertible exactly when $X_c$ has $p$ independent columns. Thus $p\ge n$ guarantees singularity; $p<n$ only leaves room for invertibility.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每张这样的表都满足上述秩上界。$s$ 可逆当且仅当 $X_c$ 有 $p$ 个线性无关的列。因此 $p\ge n$ 时必奇异，$p<n$ 只是允许可逆，并不保证可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Centering and the Gram matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 上界来自中心化和 Gram 矩阵

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each column of $X_c$ has sum zero. Its last entry is determined by the preceding $n-1$ entries, so the column lies in the $(n-1)$-dimensional subspace $\{v\in\mathbb R^n:\mathbf1_n^Tv=0\}$. Every vector in this subspace is unchanged by $H$, while $H\mathbf1_n=0$. Thus $H$ has rank $n-1$, and $X_c=HX$ can have at most $n-1$ independent columns; it also has only $p$ columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X_c$ 的每列元素和为零，最后一个元素由前 $n-1$ 个决定，因此该列落在 $(n-1)$ 维子空间 $\{v\in\mathbb R^n:\mathbf1_n^Tv=0\}$ 中。这个子空间内每个向量经 $H$ 不变，而 $H\mathbf1_n=0$，所以 $H$ 的秩为 $n-1$。$X_c=HX$ 至多有 $n-1$ 个独立列，同时它总共也只有 $p$ 列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does multiplying by $X_c^T$ preserve this rank? For any coefficient vector $a\in\mathbb R^p$,
$$a^TX_c^TX_ca=\|X_ca\|_2^2.$$
Hence $X_c^TX_ca=0$ implies $X_ca=0$, and the reverse implication is immediate. The two matrices have the same [[零空间|zero-producing coefficient vectors]], so the [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]] gives equal ranks. Multiplying by the nonzero number $1/(n-1)$ changes neither rank nor invertibility. This is also the [[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram criterion]] for positive definiteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么左乘 $X_c^T$ 后秩不变？对任意系数向量 $a$，上式把二次型写成长度平方。因此 $X_c^TX_ca=0$ 蕴含 $X_ca=0$，反方向直接成立。两矩阵的[[零空间|产生零的系数向量]]完全相同，由[[秩零度定理]]得到同秩。乘以非零数 $1/(n-1)$ 不改变秩或可逆性；这也是[[Gram矩阵正定判据]]的应用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Certain singularity versus possible singularity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 必奇异与可能奇异要分开

> [!example] Enough rows can still leave a line · 行数够多也可能只落在一条线上
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $n=3,p=2$, observations $(1,2),(2,4),(3,6)$ satisfy $x_{i2}=2x_{i1}$. Their sample covariance is
> $$s=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> s\begin{bmatrix}2\\-1\end{bmatrix}=0.$$
> Although $p<n$, the rank is only one. By contrast, $(1,2),(2,4),(3,3)$ gives $s=\begin{bmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{bmatrix}$ with determinant $3/4>0$, so it is invertible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $n=3,p=2$ 时，$(1,2),(2,4),(3,6)$ 的第二变量恒为第一变量的两倍，样本协方差如上，并把非零向量 $(2,-1)^T$ 映成零。虽然 $p<n$，秩仍只有一。换成 $(1,2),(2,4),(3,3)$ 则得到所列的另一矩阵，行列式为 $3/4>0$，这时才可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, $X_ca=0$ for a nonzero $a$ means that $a^Tx_i=a^T\bar x$ for every observed row: a fixed nonzero linear combination is constant across the data. Constant columns, duplicate columns and exact affine relationships are examples. Conversely, no such relation means full column rank. This explains why the relevant condition is that the observations span all $p$ affine directions, not merely that their count exceeds $p$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，某个非零 $a$ 满足 $X_ca=0$，等价于所有观测行都有 $a^Tx_i=a^T\bar x$：一个固定非零线性组合在数据中恒定。常数列、重复列和精确仿射关系都是例子。不存在这种关系才意味着满列秩。因此真正需要的是观测张成全部 $p$ 个仿射方向，而不仅是行数大于 $p$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n=30,p=40$, the rank is at most $29<40$, so the covariance is certainly singular. For $n=40,p=30$, the bound permits rank $30$ but does not guarantee it. Singular sample covariance has zero [[广义方差|determinant-based generalized variance]] and prevents direct use of $s^{-1}$. The [[Wishart秩与可逆性|almost-sure rank under a Gaussian sampling model]] is a separate probabilistic result; it must not be substituted for checking a realized or rounded data table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$n=30,p=40$ 时秩至多 $29<40$，必奇异；$n=40,p=30$ 时上界允许秩为 $30$，却不保证达到。奇异样本协方差的[[广义方差|行列式型广义方差]]为零，不能直接使用 $s^{-1}$。[[Wishart秩与可逆性|Gaussian 抽样模型下几乎必然达到的秩]]是另一个概率结论，不能代替对已观测或舍入数据的检查。

> [!warning] Near-singularity is a numerical question · 近奇异是数值问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This theorem concerns exact algebraic rank. A full-rank matrix may still have a very small minimum eigenvalue and an unstable inverse. Conversely, sample singularity by itself does not prove that population covariance is singular. Inspect the data geometry and the model separately; a successful software inverse is not a conditioning guarantee.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本定理讨论精确代数秩。满秩矩阵仍可能最小特征值很小，求逆很不稳定；反过来，样本奇异本身也不证明总体协方差奇异。数据几何与总体模型须分别判断，软件成功返回逆矩阵并不是条件良好的保证。
