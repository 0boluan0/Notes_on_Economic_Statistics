# 投资时钟阶段识别须先固定增长通胀指标和实时分类规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investment-clock phase identification requires explicit growth and inflation measures and real-time classification rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用[[美林投资时钟]]时，先规定国家、目标资产和决策日期，再选择增长与通胀代理、频率、平滑窗口以及“改善/下行”的阈值。统一[[经济数据的实时口径|发布版本]]后分别判断两轴，最后给出阶段及分类不确定性；不能看到 GDP 增长为正便直接写“复苏”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To apply the [[美林投资时钟|investment clock]], specify the country, assets, and decision date, then choose proxies, frequency, smoothing, and directional thresholds. Use [[经济数据的实时口径|available vintages]], assess both axes, and report the phase with classification uncertainty. Positive GDP growth alone does not establish recovery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如自定教学规则：比较同频、已发布序列的最近三个月均值与此前三个月均值。若增长代理从 −1 改善到 0.5，而通胀代理从 2% 降到 1.5%，该规则给出“复苏”象限。这只是明确规则下的判断，不是对 RLAM 专有信号的复制，也不证明价格已经触底。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As a constructed rule, compare the latest three-month average with the preceding three-month average using available, equally frequent series. A growth proxy improving from −1 to 0.5 with inflation falling from 2% to 1.5% gives recovery under that rule. This does not replicate RLAM's proprietary signals or prove that asset prices have bottomed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两轴代理彼此冲突、接近阈值或受到一次性基数影响，应保留多个候选阶段并检查敏感性。[[产出缺口]]、实际增长率及其变化不是同一变量，更不能把图的波形机械解释为 GDP 水平二阶导数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conflicting proxies, threshold proximity, or temporary base effects justify alternative phases and sensitivity checks. The [[产出缺口|output gap]], real growth, and changes in growth are different quantities; a stylized waveform is not automatically the second derivative of GDP levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
