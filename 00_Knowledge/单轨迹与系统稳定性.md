# One trajectory and stability of the whole system
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单轨迹与系统稳定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Watching one state return to zero tells us what happened from that initial state. It does not generally tell us what happens after starting in another direction. A growing direction can be absent from the chosen initial state, or present but invisible to the measured output. This distinction matters when using a simulation or observed path to judge a linear system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看见一个状态回到零，只说明从那个初值出发发生了什么，通常不能据此判断换一个方向出发会怎样。某个增长方向可能根本不在选定初值中，也可能已经存在，却没有被测量输出显示出来。用一条模拟或观测轨迹判断线性系统时，需要分清这件事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A decaying axis can sit inside an unstable system
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一条衰减坐标轴可以处在不稳定系统中*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$, where $x_k$ is a real two-coordinate state at integer step $k\ge0$. The constant matrix $A$ gives the same update at every step, with no external input. The [[对角化计算矩阵幂|matrix-power solution]] is $x_k=A^kx_0$. For
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$，$x_k$ 是整数步 $k\ge0$ 时的实二维状态。固定矩阵 $A$ 在每步施加相同更新，没有外部输入。[[对角化计算矩阵幂|矩阵幂解]]为 $x_k=A^kx_0$。取

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}0.8&0\\0&1.1\end{pmatrix},\qquad x_0=\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad x_k=\begin{pmatrix}2\cdot0.8^k\\0\end{pmatrix}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first coordinate shrinks and the second stays exactly zero. The horizontal axis $W=\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$ is an [[不变子空间|invariant subspace]]: starting there keeps the state there. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] to write a column. Every trajectory confined to $W$ decays, but $W$ contains only one of the two independent coordinate directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一个分量缩小，第二个始终恰好为零。水平轴 $W=\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$ 是[[不变子空间]]：从里面出发，就一直留在里面。这里 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，用于写列向量。限制在 $W$ 内的每条轨迹都衰减，但 $W$ 只包含二维空间中一个坐标方向。

> [!example] An arbitrarily small missing component eventually dominates / 任意小的遗漏分量最终也会增长
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replace the initial state by $(2,\varepsilon)^T$, with any real $\varepsilon\ne0$. Then $x_k=(2\cdot0.8^k,\varepsilon\,1.1^k)^T$. The second coordinate eventually grows without bound, however small $|\varepsilon|$ was. For any chosen finite horizon $K$, however, choosing $|\varepsilon|<\eta/1.1^K$ keeps its magnitude below a chosen detection scale $\eta>0$ for every $0\le k\le K$. A finite window can therefore miss growth that occurs later.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把初值改成 $(2,\varepsilon)^T$，其中实数 $\varepsilon\ne0$ 可以任意小，则 $x_k=(2\cdot0.8^k,\varepsilon\,1.1^k)^T$。无论 $|\varepsilon|$ 多小，第二个分量最终都无界增长。不过，对任意选定的有限观察步数 $K$，只要取 $|\varepsilon|<\eta/1.1^K$，这个分量在全部 $0\le k\le K$ 都小于检测尺度 $\eta>0$。有限窗口因而可能漏掉后来才显现的增长。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second coordinate is an eigenvector mode with eigenvalue $1.1$, in the language of [[特征对|eigenpairs]]. Its coefficient in the original initial state was zero. Moreover, starting at $(0,\varepsilon)^T$ gives arbitrarily small initial departures from zero that eventually become large. Thus the origin is unstable in the full two-dimensional system, even though the dynamics restricted to $W$ are asymptotically stable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按[[特征对]]的语言，第二个坐标方向是特征值为 $1.1$ 的模式，原先初值中它的系数为零。另外，从 $(0,\varepsilon)^T$ 出发，可以得到任意接近零、以后却变大的轨迹。因此整个二维系统的原点不稳定，尽管限制在 $W$ 上的动力学是渐近稳定的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same hidden direction appears in continuous time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续时间也会隐藏同样的方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use $y'(t)=By(t)$ at real time $t\ge0$. The prime denotes a coordinatewise time [[导数|derivative]]. The [[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|constant-matrix solution]] is $y(t)=e^{tB}y(0)$. With
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改用实时间 $t\ge0$ 下的 $y'(t)=By(t)$，撇号表示逐坐标对时间求[[导数]]。[[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解]]给出 $y(t)=e^{tB}y(0)$。取

$$B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&0.2\end{pmatrix},\qquad y(0)=\begin{pmatrix}1\\\varepsilon\end{pmatrix},\qquad y(t)=\begin{pmatrix}e^{-t}\\\varepsilon e^{0.2t}\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\varepsilon=0$, the whole trajectory decays. If $\varepsilon\ne0$, the second coordinate grows without bound. The [[自然指数函数|exponential factors]] explain the continuous-time rule: negative real parts of eigenvalues produce decay, while positive real parts produce growth. Do not transfer the discrete rule $|\lambda|<1$ to this differential equation; for example, the positive number $0.2$ produces growth here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\varepsilon=0$，整条轨迹衰减；若 $\varepsilon\ne0$，第二个分量无界增长。[[自然指数函数|指数因子]]解释了连续时间规则：特征值实部为负对应衰减，实部为正对应增长。不能把离散规则 $|\lambda|<1$ 搬到这个微分方程中；例如正数 $0.2$ 在这里产生增长。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What would justify a statement about the system?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么证据才能支持关于整个系统的结论？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[离散系统谱稳定性|discrete]] and [[连续系统谱稳定性|continuous]] criteria concern asymptotic stability of zero: sufficiently small initial departures stay small for all future time and eventually approach zero. For these fixed finite-dimensional homogeneous linear systems, this is equivalent to decay from **every** initial state. It is an all-directions statement, not a statement about the particular state selected for a plot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[离散系统谱稳定性|离散]]和[[连续系统谱稳定性|连续]]判据讨论零点渐近稳定：足够小的初始偏离，在以后所有时间都保持小，并最终趋零。对本卡固定、有限维、齐次的线性系统，这等价于**所有**初值的轨迹都衰减；它是关于全部方向的结论，不是只关于画图时选中的那个状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A full matrix analysis can establish this: require all complex eigenvalues to have modulus below $1$ in discrete time, or negative real part in continuous time. Another exact route is to prove decay for each vector in a [[基|basis]] of the state space. Linearity then gives decay for every fixed linear combination. Merely simulating those basis trajectories for a finite number of steps does not prove their limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以通过完整矩阵分析建立这个结论：离散时间要求全部复特征值的模小于1，连续时间要求全部实部为负。另一个精确途径，是证明状态空间的一组[[基]]中，每个基向量出发的轨迹都趋零；线性性随后保证任意固定线性组合也趋零。但仅把这些基轨迹模拟有限步，并没有证明它们的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One path can become conclusive when additional structure rules out hidden directions. For example, in the known scalar system $x_{k+1}=ax_k$, observing the exact limit $x_k\to0$ from a nonzero $x_0$ implies $a^k=x_k/x_0\to0$, hence $|a|<1$. The two-dimensional counterexample shows why that inference is not valid for an arbitrary vector system without such extra information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果额外结构已经排除了隐藏方向，一条轨迹也可能足以定论。例如在已知标量系统 $x_{k+1}=ax_k$ 中，若从非零 $x_0$ 出发的**精确极限**是 $x_k\to0$，就有 $a^k=x_k/x_0\to0$，因而 $|a|<1$。前面的二维反例说明，缺少这类额外信息时，不能对任意向量系统照搬这个推断。

> [!warning] A measured output can hide even an excited mode / 模式已经激活，测量仍可能看不到
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a sensor reports only the first coordinate, $z_k=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix}x_k$, using [[矩阵乘法|a row times the state]]. In the discrete example with $\varepsilon\ne0$, the report is still $z_k=2\cdot0.8^k\to0$, although the unmeasured second coordinate grows. A zero initial state is an even simpler limitation: every homogeneous linear system keeps it at zero, including unstable systems.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若传感器只报告第一个分量，即 $z_k=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix}x_k$，这是[[矩阵乘法|行向量乘状态]]。在 $\varepsilon\ne0$ 的离散例子中，报告仍是 $z_k=2\cdot0.8^k\to0$，但未被测量的第二个分量正在增长。零初值是更简单的边界：任何齐次线性系统都会让它始终为零，包括不稳定系统。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The opposite shortcut also fails: early growth alone does not establish asymptotic instability, because [[非正规矩阵瞬态|nonnormal transient growth]] can precede decay. Before interpreting a graph, identify whether it shows the full state or an output, which initial directions it tests, and whether the evidence concerns a finite window or a proved long-run limit. Those distinctions determine what the graph can support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反方向的捷径也不成立：仅有早期增长，不能认定系统渐近不稳定，因为[[非正规矩阵瞬态]]可以先增长、后衰减。解释一张图之前，要明确它显示完整状态还是某个输出、测试了哪些初始方向，以及证据是有限窗口还是已经证明的长期极限。这些区别决定了图形能支持什么结论。
