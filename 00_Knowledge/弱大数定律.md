# Weak law of large numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*弱大数定律*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Toss a coin repeatedly and record heads as $1$, tails as $0$. The average of these records is the proportion of heads. A **weak law of large numbers** explains when an average like this becomes unlikely to be far from its target as we collect more observations. It concerns the reliability of an average, not whether each individual observation becomes less random.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反复投掷硬币，把正面记为 $1$、反面记为 $0$，这些记录的平均值就是正面比例。**弱大数定律**解释这样的平均值在什么条件下会随着观测增多，越来越不容易明显偏离目标。它讨论平均值的可靠程度，而不是单个观测是否变得不再随机。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_i$ be the $i$th [[随机变量|random observation]], $S_n$ the sum of the first $n$ observations, and $\bar X_n$ their sample mean. The symbol $\mu$ will denote a finite, nonrandom target, usually the common [[期望|expectation]] of the observations: their average across possible outcomes, weighted by probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X_i$ 表示第 $i$ 个[[随机变量|随机观测]]，$S_n$ 表示前 $n$ 个观测的总和，$\bar X_n$ 表示它们的样本均值。$\mu$ 表示有限、非随机的目标，通常是各观测共同的[[期望]]，也就是按概率加权的总体平均。

$$
S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i,\qquad \bar X_n=\frac{S_n}{n}.
$$

> [!important] What the weak conclusion says · 弱大数定律的结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A weak law has a conclusion of the form $\bar X_n\xrightarrow p\mu$. This means [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]]: for every fixed tolerance $\varepsilon>0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 弱大数定律的一类典型结论是 $\bar X_n\xrightarrow p\mu$，其中的[[依概率收敛]]表示：对每个固定的容忍值 $\varepsilon>0$，
>
> $$
> P(|\bar X_n-\mu|>\varepsilon)\longrightarrow0.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To read the quantifiers: choose an error tolerance $\varepsilon$ and an allowed failure probability $\delta>0$. There is then an index $N(\varepsilon,\delta)$ such that every $n\ge N$ has error probability below $\delta$. The index may depend on both choices; neither tolerance is silently allowed to shrink with $n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 量词的顺序是：先选误差容忍值 $\varepsilon$ 与允许的失败概率 $\delta>0$，然后存在 $N(\varepsilon,\delta)$，使每个 $n\ge N$ 的超差概率都小于 $\delta$。这个起点可以依赖两种要求；不能悄悄把容忍值改成随 $n$ 缩小的数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A usable theorem, and why its assumptions matter
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个可直接使用的版本

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classical integrable version says: if the observations are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed (iid)]] and $E|X_1|<\infty$, then $\bar X_n\xrightarrow p\mu=E[X_1]$. Identical distributions give a common target; independence controls how the observations vary together. Absolute integrability means that the average magnitude is finite, so the signed expectation is well defined and finite. **Finite variance is not required by this version.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典可积版本是：若观测[[独立同分布]]，且 $E|X_1|<\infty$，则 $\bar X_n\xrightarrow p\mu=E[X_1]$。同分布提供共同目标；独立性控制观测如何共同波动。绝对可积表示平均绝对大小有限，因此带正负号的期望有定义且有限。**这个版本不要求有限方差。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are other sufficient assumptions. For example, [[有界方差弱大数定律|uncorrelated observations with a common mean and uniformly bounded variances]] obey a direct Chebyshev bound; identical distributions are unnecessary there. The name “weak law” describes the probability-convergence conclusion, rather than prescribing one universal set of assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他条件也可以充分保证这一结论。例如，[[有界方差弱大数定律|两两不相关、具有共同均值且方差一致有界]]时，可以直接用 Chebyshev 不等式给出上界；那个版本不要求同分布。“弱大数定律”说明结论采用依概率收敛，并不规定所有版本必须共享同一套假设。

> [!example] A sample proportion · 样本比例
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For iid fair-coin records, $E[X_i]=1/2$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)=1/4$. The bounded-variance theorem gives $P(|\bar X_n-1/2|\ge0.05)\le100/n$. At $n=10\,000$, the probability of an error of at least five percentage points is at most $0.01$. This is an upper bound, not the exact probability, and it does not say that the next toss is nearly determined.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对于独立的公平硬币记录，$E[X_i]=1/2$，$\operatorname{Var}(X_i)=1/4$。有界方差版本给出 $P(|\bar X_n-1/2|\ge0.05)\le100/n$。在 $n=10\,000$ 时，误差至少为五个百分点的概率至多 $0.01$。这是上界，不是精确概率，更不意味着下一次投掷几乎已确定。

## Why a finite mean can be enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 没有有限方差时为什么仍可能成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key is to separate ordinary observations from an increasingly remote tail. For a cutoff $K>0$, define $Y_i^{(K)}=X_i\mathbf1\{|X_i|\le K\}$ and $R_i^{(K)}=X_i-Y_i^{(K)}$. The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] keeps $X_i$ inside the cutoff and replaces it by zero outside. For fixed $K$, the $Y_i^{(K)}$ are iid and bounded, so their average approaches $E[Y_1^{(K)}]$ by the bounded-variance theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是把通常大小的观测和越来越远的尾部分开。取截断值 $K>0$，定义 $Y_i^{(K)}=X_i\mathbf1\{|X_i|\le K\}$，以及 $R_i^{(K)}=X_i-Y_i^{(K)}$。[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]保留截断范围内的 $X_i$，把范围外的取值替换为零。固定 $K$ 后，$Y_i^{(K)}$ 仍独立同分布且有界，所以其均值由有界方差版本趋近 $E[Y_1^{(K)}]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remaining average is controlled by [[Markov不等式|Markov’s inequality]] applied to its absolute value. Write $r_K=E[|X_1|\mathbf1\{|X_1|>K\}]$. Absolute integrability and the [[控制收敛定理|dominated convergence theorem]] give $r_K\to0$, while
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

剩下部分的平均可以对其绝对值使用 [[Markov不等式]]控制。记 $r_K=E[|X_1|\mathbf1\{|X_1|>K\}]$。绝对可积与[[控制收敛定理]]给出 $r_K\to0$，而且

$$
P\!\left(\left|\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nR_i^{(K)}\right|>\frac{\varepsilon}{3}\right)\le\frac{3r_K}{\varepsilon},\qquad |E[Y_1^{(K)}]-\mu|\le r_K.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $K$ first so that both tail effects are small, then choose $n$ large for that fixed cutoff. The triangle inequality splits $|\bar X_n-\mu|$ into the bounded average’s error, the tail average, and the difference of their target means. This proves the iid integrable weak law without demanding a finite second moment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先选 $K$ 让两个尾部影响都足够小，再对这个固定截断值选大的 $n$。三角不等式把 $|\bar X_n-\mu|$ 分成截断平均的误差、尾部平均，以及两个目标均值的差。这就证明了独立同分布且绝对可积的弱大数定律，无须有限二阶矩。

> [!warning] A limit is not a guarantee for every future sample · 极限不保证此后每次都正确
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The weak conclusion controls an error probability at each sample size. It alone does not say that one realised infinite path eventually stays close forever; that is the stronger [[强大数定律|strong-law conclusion]]. Neither law repairs a biased sampling scheme: an iid sample from the wrong population approaches the mean of that sampling distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 弱结论逐个样本量控制超差概率，本身不保证同一条已实现的无限路径最终永久保持接近；那是[[强大数定律]]的更强结论。两种定律都不能修复有偏抽样：若独立同分布地从错误总体抽样，平均值仍然趋向那个抽样分布的均值。
