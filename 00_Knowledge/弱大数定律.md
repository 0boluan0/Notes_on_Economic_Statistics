# 弱大数定律是一类说明样本均值依概率收敛到目标均值的极限定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A weak law of large numbers is a limit theorem asserting that a sample average converges in probability to its target mean*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对随机变量序列 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$，令
> $$\bar X_n=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nX_i.$$
> 弱大数定律指一类形如
> $$\bar X_n\xrightarrow p\mu$$
> 的结论：样本量增加时，样本均值明显偏离目标均值 $\mu$ 的概率趋于零。具体需要哪些独立性、同分布、矩或依赖条件，取决于采用的定理版本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A weak law states that a sample average $\bar X_n$ converges in probability to a target mean $\mu$. The exact assumptions vary across versions and may concern independence, identical distributions, moments, or dependence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“弱”描述的是收敛方式：结论是 [[依概率收敛]]，不是说结果不重要，也不是说假设一定比所有强大数定律更弱。[[强大数定律]]则给出几乎必然收敛，需要按其自身版本核验条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weak refers to the mode of convergence. It does not mean the result is unimportant, nor does it impose one universal assumption set. A strong law instead concludes almost-sure convergence under its own stated conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个可直接使用的版本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One directly usable version*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X_i$ 两两独立、具有共同均值，而且方差一致有界，Chebyshev 不等式可以直接证明弱大数定律。完整条件、证明和边界见 [[有界方差弱大数定律]]。这一版本方便计算，但不能反过来当作所有弱大数定律的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise independence, a common mean, and uniformly bounded variances give one convenient Chebyshev-based version. Those assumptions define that theorem, not the whole concept of a weak law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 最小解释
> 若 $\bar X_n\xrightarrow p\mu$，则对任意固定 $\varepsilon>0$，
> $$P(|\bar X_n-\mu|>\varepsilon)\to0.$$
> 它允许每个有限样本量仍出现明显误差，也不保证同一条无限样本路径从某一刻起永久贴近 $\mu$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The conclusion allows visible errors at every finite sample size. It does not by itself say that each realised infinite path eventually stays close to the target forever.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一条弱大数定律的结论是什么？“两两独立且方差一致有界”是否属于这个概念本身的固定定义？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What is the conclusion of a weak law, and are pairwise independence plus uniformly bounded variances part of the concept's fixed definition?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 结论是样本均值依概率收敛到目标均值。两两独立和方差一致有界只是一种具体版本的充分条件，不是所有弱大数定律共同的定义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The conclusion is convergence in probability of the sample average to its target mean. Those assumptions belong to one sufficient theorem, not to the concept's universal definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
