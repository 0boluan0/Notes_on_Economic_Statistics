# 控制碰撞点或其后代会解除该处的路径阻断；若其余部分未被阻断，非因果路径随之打开并可能制造选择关联
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditioning on a collider or its descendant removes the block at that point; if the rest of the path is unblocked, a noncausal path opens and may create selection-induced association*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 共同结果与共同原因的调整方向相反
> 在路径
> $$
> A\rightarrow C\leftarrow U\rightarrow Y
> $$
> 中，两支箭头的头部在 $C$ 相遇，所以 $C$ 是这条路径上的碰撞点。未条件化时，路径在 $C$ 处封闭；控制 $C$、按 $C$ 分层、只保留某个 $C$ 值，或条件化 $C$ 的后代，会在图上打开这条路径，使 $A$ 与 $U$ 在分析样本中可能相关。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $A\rightarrow C\leftarrow U\rightarrow Y$, arrowheads meet at $C$, making it a collider on this path. Without conditioning, the path is blocked at $C$. Adjusting for $C$, stratifying or selecting on it, or conditioning on its descendant opens the path in the graph and can associate $A$ with $U$ in the analysed data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同原因 $A\leftarrow U\rightarrow Y$ 会产生混杂，通常要阻断；共同结果 $A\rightarrow C\leftarrow U$ 原本已经阻断，反而不应因为 $C$ 能提高拟合就打开。碰撞点是变量相对于某条路径的角色：同一个变量在另一条路径上可能不是碰撞点，所以调整集必须按完整因果结构判断，不能凭相关矩阵或“显著预测 Y”决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common cause $A\leftarrow U\rightarrow Y$ creates confounding and generally needs blocking. A common effect $A\rightarrow C\leftarrow U$ already blocks its path and should not be opened merely because $C$ predicts $Y$. Collider status is path-specific, so adjustment must follow the full causal structure rather than a correlation matrix or predictive significance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个线性例子能看见偏误。令 $A,U,\varepsilon_C,\varepsilon_Y$ 相互独立、均值为零，$\operatorname{Var}(A)=\operatorname{Var}(U)=1$，并设
$$
C=A+U+\varepsilon_C,
\qquad
Y=\tau A+\gamma U+\varepsilon_Y,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_C)=\sigma_C^2.
$$
不控制 $C$ 时，$Y$ 对随机 $A$ 的总体回归斜率是 $\tau$；同时控制 $C$ 后，$A$ 的总体偏回归系数为
$$
\beta_{A\mid C}=\tau-\frac{\gamma}{1+\sigma_C^2}.
$$
因此即使 $A$ 与 $U$ 原本独立，控制共同结果仍可削弱、夸大或逆转估计效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the displayed linear system, the unadjusted population slope on random $A$ is $\tau$, whereas adjustment for $C$ gives $\beta_{A\mid C}=\tau-\gamma/(1+\sigma_C^2)$. Thus conditioning on a common effect can attenuate, magnify, or reverse an estimated effect even when $A$ and $U$ were initially independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件化也可能在样本形成时发生。入院、被检测、回复问卷、成为平台用户或进入完整案例样本，若相应的入选指标同时受暴露和某个结果原因影响，它就在这条路径上构成碰撞点。数据只保留入选者时，碰撞点已经被固定；之后不把它写进回归并不能恢复原总体。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditioning can occur during sample formation. If an inclusion indicator—such as hospitalisation, testing, survey response, platform entry, or complete-case inclusion—is caused jointly by the exposure and another cause of the outcome, it is a collider on the corresponding path. Once the dataset contains only selected units, omitting the selection variable from the final regression does not restore the original population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图上开路表示非因果关联有可能沿该路径传递，但不保证每个参数化模型中的偏差都非零或方向固定。关系很弱、参数恰好抵消，或某些特殊选择机制，都可能使一次具体计算中的偏差为零。这不把碰撞点变成好控制，而是提醒我们区分结构风险与某一次数值实现。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An open path permits noncausal association; it does not guarantee nonzero bias of a fixed sign in every parameterisation. Weak links, parameter cancellation, or particular selection mechanisms may produce zero bias in a specific calculation. They do not turn a collider into a generally valid control, but distinguish structural risk from one numerical realisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 治疗原本随机分配，但研究者只分析后来住院的人。住院同时受治疗和潜在虚弱程度影响，虚弱又影响死亡。在住院者样本内，治疗分配还与潜在虚弱程度独立吗？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Treatment was randomised, but researchers analyse only later hospitalised patients. Hospitalisation depends on treatment and latent frailty, which also affects mortality. Within the hospitalised sample, is treatment assignment still independent of latent frailty?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不独立。限制到住院者等于条件化共同结果，打开“治疗 $\to$ 住院 $\leftarrow$ 虚弱 $\to$ 死亡”路径；原来的随机分配机制并未消失，但随机化带来的边际可比性没有在这个筛选后的样本中保留下来。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. Restricting to hospitalised patients conditions on a common effect and opens treatment $\to$ hospitalisation $\leftarrow$ frailty $\to$ mortality. The original assignment mechanism has not vanished, but its marginal comparability is not preserved in the selected sample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
