# The Brownian reflection principle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*布朗反射原理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A path can reach a high level and later fall back below it. Checking only its final position misses those earlier visits. The **reflection principle** gives an exact way to count them for standard Brownian motion: the probability of reaching a positive level by a fixed time is twice the probability of ending above that level. The factor two comes from pairing two kinds of paths by reflection after their first visit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条路径可以先达到高处，后来又跌回去。只检查最终位置，会漏掉先前已经到过高处的路径。对标准布朗运动，**反射原理**给出精确的计数办法：在固定时刻之前达到某个正水平的概率，等于最终位于该水平上方概率的两倍。这个“两倍”来自首次到达后反射所建立的两类路径配对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $W_s$ be the position of a [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] at time $s$: it starts at zero, has continuous paths, and disjoint increments are independent with zero-mean Gaussian laws whose variances are their time lengths. Fix a finite observation time $t>0$ and a level $a>0$ above the starting point. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $W_s$ 表示[[标准布朗运动]]在时刻 $s$ 的位置：从零出发、路径连续，不交增量独立，且服从均值零、方差等于时长的 Gaussian 分布。固定有限观察时刻 $t>0$，并取高于起点的水平 $a>0$。定义

$$M_t=\sup_{0\le s\le t}W_s,\qquad
\tau_a=\inf\{u\ge0:W_u=a\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$M_t$ is the highest position seen by time $t$; continuity on this closed finite interval means the supremum is attained. The symbol $\inf$ selects the earliest hit; if the set is empty, put $\tau_a=\infty$. Since a continuous path starting below $a$ cannot jump over it, $\{M_t\ge a\}=\{\tau_a\le t\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$M_t$ 是截至 $t$ 看到的最高位置；闭的有限区间上路径连续，所以这个上确界确实会取到。$\inf$ 选出最早到达时刻；若根本没有到达，就约定 $\tau_a=\infty$。连续路径从 $a$ 下方出发，不能直接跳过它，因此 $\{M_t\ge a\}=\{\tau_a\le t\}$。

> [!important] A path event becomes a Gaussian tail · 路径事件化成 Gaussian 尾概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For standard Brownian motion, finite $t>0$ and $a>0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对标准布朗运动、有限 $t>0$ 及 $a>0$，
>
> $$P(M_t\ge a)=P(\tau_a\le t)
> =2P(W_t\ge a)
> =2\left[1-\Phi\!\left(\frac a{\sqrt t}\right)\right].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$ for $Z\sim N(0,1)$ is the [[正态分布|standard normal CDF]]. Since $W_t\sim N(0,t)$, division by its standard deviation $\sqrt t$ gives the threshold $a/\sqrt t$. The formula concerns at least one hit before the deadline, not a count of crossings.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$，其中 $Z\sim N(0,1)$，是[[正态分布|标准正态累积分布函数]]。由于 $W_t\sim N(0,t)$，用标准差 $\sqrt t$ 除阈值便得到 $a/\sqrt t$。公式计算的是截止时刻前至少到达一次的概率，不是穿越次数。

> [!example] The path that comes back down still counts · 已经上去又跌回来的路径仍要计入
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $t=1,a=1$, the endpoint tail is $P(W_1\ge1)\approx0.1587$, but $P(M_1\ge1)\approx0.3173$. Paths that touched $1$ and finish below $1$ contribute the other $0.1587$. In the reflection pairing, an endpoint $0.4$ on such a path becomes $2(1)-0.4=1.6$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $t=1,a=1$ 时，终点尾概率 $P(W_1\ge1)\approx0.1587$，但 $P(M_1\ge1)\approx0.3173$。曾触及 $1$、终点却低于 $1$ 的路径贡献另外的 $0.1587$。在反射配对中，这类路径若终点为 $0.4$，镜像终点就是 $2(1)-0.4=1.6$。

## How reflection pairs the paths
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 反射怎样建立两类路径的对应

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The event $\{M_t\ge a\}$ consists of paths that end at or above $a$, together with paths that hit $a$ and end below it. Keep each path unchanged until its first hit. After the hit, reflect every vertical displacement about the horizontal line at height $a$: a value $x$ becomes $2a-x$. Reflecting twice restores the original path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件 $\{M_t\ge a\}$ 包含两类路径：终点在 $a$ 或更高处的路径，以及曾触及 $a$、终点却低于它的路径。首次触及以前保持原路不变；触及以后，把各个位置关于高度为 $a$ 的水平线反射，即把 $x$ 换成 $2a-x$。连续反射两次会恢复原来的路径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A geometric pairing alone does not prove equal probabilities. We must also know that this operation preserves the Brownian law. The first hit is a [[停时定义|stopping time]]: by any time $u$, the observed past tells us whether the level has already been reached. No future observation is needed. The [[布朗强Markov性|Brownian strong Markov property]] says that after a finite stopping time, displacements from the current position form a fresh standard Brownian motion independent of the information already observed. This extends the fixed-time independent-increment rule to a permissible random time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几何上能配对，还不足以证明概率相等；还要知道反射保持布朗运动的分布。首次到达是一个[[停时定义|停时]]：到任意时刻 $u$，只看已经观察到的历史，就知道是否已经到过该水平，无须查看未来。[[布朗强Markov性]]说明，在有限停时之后，相对于当时位置的位移构成一条新的标准布朗运动，并独立于已经观察到的信息。它把确定时刻的独立增量规则延伸到符合停时条件的随机时刻。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To avoid assuming in advance that $\tau_a$ is finite, use the bounded stopping time $T=\min(\tau_a,t)$. Define an entire reflected process by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了不预先假设 $\tau_a$ 有限，使用有界停时 $T=\min(\tau_a,t)$。定义整条反射过程

$$R_u=\begin{cases}
W_u,&0\le u\le T,\\
2W_T-W_u,&u>T.
\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The post-$T$ displacement $W_{T+v}-W_T$ can be replaced by its negative without changing its law: centered Gaussian increments are symmetric. Independence from the [[停时信息集|information at $T$]] means this also preserves its joint law with the preceding path. Thus $R$ has the same process law as $W$. If no hit has occurred by $t$, then $T=t$ and the path up to the deadline is unchanged. If a hit has occurred, $W_T=a$ and the stated mirror about $a$ is exactly what happens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把停时后位移 $W_{T+v}-W_T$ 换成它的负数，不会改变其分布，因为中心 Gaussian 增量具有对称性。它又独立于[[停时信息集|截至 $T$ 的信息]]，因此与之前路径的联合分布也保持。于是 $R$ 与 $W$ 具有相同的过程律。如果到 $t$ 还没有触及，就有 $T=t$，截止以前的路径不变；如果已经触及，则 $W_T=a$，反射恰好就是上面所说的关于 $a$ 的镜像。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pairing gives the event identity $\{M_t\ge a,W_t<a\}=\{R_t>a\}$. Conversely, any continuous path ending above $a$ has already hit it, so reflection brings its endpoint below $a$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种配对给出事件恒等式 $\{M_t\ge a,W_t<a\}=\{R_t>a\}$。反方向也成立：任何终点高于 $a$ 的连续路径都已触及它，反射会把终点送到 $a$ 下方。因此

$$P(M_t\ge a,W_t<a)=P(R_t>a)=P(W_t>a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gaussian endpoint has no atom: $P(W_t=a)=0$. Adding the two disjoint kinds of paths now yields $P(M_t\ge a)=2P(W_t\ge a)$. The equality of strict and weak inequalities here comes from the endpoint's continuous distribution, not from ignoring paths which touch the barrier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Gaussian 终点没有点质量，即 $P(W_t=a)=0$。把两类不相交路径相加，就得到 $P(M_t\ge a)=2P(W_t\ge a)$。这里终点的严格与非严格不等号概率相同，是因为终点分布连续，不是把触及边界的路径忽略掉。

## Scope and boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 适用边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simple factor two relies on zero drift. For [[带漂移布朗运动|Brownian motion with drift]], reflecting the future reverses its drift as well, so it does not preserve the original law. A different crossing formula is needed. The result here is for a one-sided positive barrier and a fixed deadline; a two-sided exit event or a path-dependent deadline needs its own argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

简单的两倍关系依赖零漂移。对[[带漂移布朗运动]]，反射未来也会把漂移方向反过来，因此不再保持原来的分布，越界公式需要另算。这里讨论的是单侧正水平和固定截止时刻；双侧离开事件或依路径而变的截止时刻，都需要自己的论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The boundary $a=0,t>0$ gives probability one because the process starts at zero, and the displayed tail formula also equals one. For $a<0$, the event $M_t\ge a$ is already certain at time zero, whereas twice the endpoint tail would exceed one, so that formula must not be extended there. At $t=0,a>0$, the hitting probability is zero; the expression containing $1/\sqrt t$ is not evaluated at zero. Finally, $t=1$ means one model time unit, not automatically one year.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在边界 $a=0,t>0$ 时，由于从零开始，事件概率为一，尾概率公式也等于一。若 $a<0$，事件 $M_t\ge a$ 在零时刻就已经发生，但两倍终点尾概率会大于一，所以不能把公式延伸到这里。当 $t=0,a>0$ 时，到达概率为零；含 $1/\sqrt t$ 的表达式不能直接代入零。最后，$t=1$ 表示一个模型时间单位，并不自动代表一年。

> [!question]- Why is the endpoint law alone insufficient? · 为什么只知道终点分布还不够？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $W_t\sim N(0,t)$ describes one observation. It does not record whether the path was previously above $a$ and returned. Reflection uses continuity, symmetry and the stopping-time structure of the whole Brownian process to recover the missing path probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $W_t\sim N(0,t)$ 只描述一次观察，不能记录此前是否到过 $a$ 上方又跌回来。反射利用整条布朗过程的连续性、对称性和停时结构，把缺少的路径概率恢复出来。
