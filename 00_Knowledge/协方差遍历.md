# 相关遍历要求固定滞后乘积的时间平均恢复总体相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Correlation ergodicity requires the time average of fixed-lag products to recover ensemble correlation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 对均值为 $\mu$ 的实值过程和固定滞后 $h\ge0$，本原子采用去均值的协方差遍历口径，要求
> $$
> \frac1{n-h}\sum_{t=1}^{n-h}(X_{t+h}-\mu)(X_t-\mu)
> \longrightarrow\gamma(h)
> $$
> 按指定方式收敛。它是关于乘积过程 $Z_t^{(h)}=(X_{t+h}-\mu)(X_t-\mu)$ 的均值遍历命题，不由 $X_t$ 的均值遍历自动推出。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At a fixed nonnegative lag, covariance ergodicity asks the $(n-h)^{-1}$ time average of centred products to converge to $\gamma(h)$. This is a mean-ergodicity statement about the product process $Z_t^{(h)}$, and it does not follow automatically from mean ergodicity of $X_t$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若课程使用 raw correlation，则去掉中心化并把目标改为 $R(h)=E[X_{t+h}X_t]$；两种口径不应混写。复值过程还要在第二个因子上取共轭。检验乘积平均的方差需要四阶矩或等价的四阶依赖信息。只有在 Gaussian 等附加结构下，四阶矩才可化成二阶协方差；一般过程不能仅凭一条 $\int|\gamma(\tau)|^2d\tau$ 公式断言相关遍历。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the raw-correlation convention the products are not centred and the target is $R(h)$; for complex processes the second factor is conjugated. The variance of a product average involves fourth moments or equivalent fourth-order dependence. Gaussian assumptions can reduce these terms to covariances, but a general process cannot be declared correlation-ergodic from a second-order formula alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $X_t$ 已经对均值遍历，是否自动保证样本协方差在每个固定滞后都收敛？
>
> **答案：** 不保证。后者是乘积过程的均值遍历问题，其方差涉及四阶结构；$X_t$ 本身的均值遍历只解决一阶平均。
