# 流动比率是同一时点流动资产与流动负债之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The current ratio is current assets divided by current liabilities at the same date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动比率 $CR=CA/CL$，通常在 $CL>0$ 时解释为一元流动负债对应多少流动资产。$CA=700,CL=300$ 时，$CR=2.3333$；这不是现金足够支付债务2.3333次的保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The current ratio $CR=CA/CL$ usually describes current assets per unit of current liabilities when $CL>0$. Values of 700 and 300 give 2.3333, not a guarantee that available cash can pay debt 2.3333 times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $CA>0$，以[[营运资本]]占流动资产的比例 $w=(CA-CL)/CA$ 表示，则 $CR=1/(1-w)$。因此营运资本的绝对额增加，也可能伴随流动比率下降：610/220≈2.7727，而700/300≈2.3333。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $CA>0$, define [[营运资本|working capital]] as a share of current assets by $w=(CA-CL)/CA$; then $CR=1/(1-w)$. Absolute working capital can increase while the current ratio declines: 610/220 is about 2.7727, versus 700/300 at about 2.3333.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存货可能难卖，应收可能逾期，经营也需保留必要资产；流动负债的具体到期日并不相同。不存在跨行业统一的“必须大于2”规则，应结合[[速动比率]]和[[偿债能力的报表外核查]]；流动负债为零时不把未定义比率强写成普通数值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inventory may be illiquid, receivables overdue, and some assets essential to operations; liabilities also mature at different dates. There is no universal requirement to exceed two. Use the [[速动比率|quick ratio]] and [[偿债能力的报表外核查|additional solvency checks]]. Zero current liabilities make the ratio undefined rather than an ordinary numerical score.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
