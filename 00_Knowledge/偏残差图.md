# Partial residual plots
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*偏残差图*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A partial residual plot helps you inspect the **shape assigned to one predictor in a multiple regression**. Suppose a model predicts a test score using study time and previous attainment. The raw score-versus-time plot mixes both predictors' associations with the score. A partial residual plot removes the fitted contribution of the other predictors, while keeping study time itself on the horizontal axis.

The construction starts with a fitted model. A [[回归残差|residual]] is the observed outcome minus its fitted value. Add the chosen predictor's fitted contribution back to that residual. The resulting vertical coordinate contains the chosen contribution **plus what the complete fitted model still missed**. It is therefore useful for checking whether a straight-line contribution looks plausible; it is not a directly observed causal response curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

偏残差图帮助你检查**多元回归给某个解释变量安排的关系形状**。例如，用学习时间和既往成绩预测考试分数，原始“分数—时间”图会混合两个变量与结果的关联。偏残差图扣除其他变量已经拟合出的贡献，同时把原来的学习时间保留在横轴上。

它从一个已经拟合的模型出发。[[回归残差|残差]]是观测结果减去拟合值；把所关注变量的拟合贡献加回残差，就得到新的纵坐标：**这个变量的拟合贡献，加上完整模型仍未解释的部分**。它适合帮助检查直线形状是否合理，却不是直接观测到的因果响应曲线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Add one contribution back
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把一项贡献加回去*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an unweighted [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least-squares fit]], which minimises squared residuals, on the same $n$ observations. Let $y_i$ be the outcome, $x_{ij}$ the chosen predictor, and $x_{i\ell}$ the other predictor columns. There are $k$ predictors plus an intercept. Assume the design has [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]], so their coefficients are uniquely determined. Write the fitted values and residuals as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同样的 $n$ 条观测做不加权的[[普通最小二乘]]，即最小化残差平方和。$y_i$ 是结果，$x_{ij}$ 是所关注的变量，$x_{i\ell}$ 是其他解释变量列；模型共含 $k$ 个解释变量及截距。假设设计[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]，使这些系数能够唯一确定。拟合值与残差为

$$
\hat y_i=\hat\beta_0+\sum_{\ell=1}^{k}\hat\beta_\ell x_{i\ell},
\qquad e_i=y_i-\hat y_i.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The hat marks a fitted coefficient; $j$ picks the column under inspection. Its **partial residual** is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数上的帽子表示拟合得到的数值，$j$ 指定正在检查的那一列。相应的**偏残差**是

$$
r_i^{(j)}=e_i+\hat\beta_jx_{ij}
=y_i-\hat\beta_0-\sum_{\ell\ne j}\hat\beta_\ell x_{i\ell}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Plot $(x_{ij},r_i^{(j)})$ and add the fitted contribution line $\hat\beta_jx_{ij}$. A smooth curve through the points can reveal departures from that line. The vertical difference (point minus contribution line) is exactly $e_i$. The intercept has been subtracted; adding it back shifts every point and the reference line by the same constant, without changing their shape. Software that adds the component line to the partial-residual plot may call this a **component-plus-residual** or **CCPR** display.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

画出 $(x_{ij},r_i^{(j)})$，并加上拟合贡献直线 $\hat\beta_jx_{ij}$。穿过散点的平滑曲线可以提示它与这条直线的偏离；每个点到贡献线的竖直差恰好就是 $e_i$。这里已扣除截距，若把截距加回所有点及参考线，只会整体平移而不改变形状。软件把贡献直线叠加在偏残差图上时，也可能称其为 **component-plus-residual** 或 **CCPR** 图。

## A bend that a straight coefficient cannot represent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个直线系数无法表达的弯曲*

> [!example] Six constructed observations / 六条构造的观测
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At each $x\in\{-1,0,1\}$, observe one case with $z=-1$ and one with $z=1$. Set $y=10+3z+2x+x^2$. Fit only an intercept, $x$, and $z$. Least squares gives $\hat y=10+2/3+2x+3z$, so $e=x^2-2/3$. The table groups the two cases at each $x$; after removing the fitted $z$ contribution, they coincide vertically.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在每个 $x\in\{-1,0,1\}$ 处，各观察一条 $z=-1$ 和一条 $z=1$ 的数据，令 $y=10+3z+2x+x^2$。只用截距、$x$ 和 $z$ 拟合，最小二乘得到 $\hat y=10+2/3+2x+3z$，所以 $e=x^2-2/3$。下表把同一 $x$ 的两条观测归在一行；扣除拟合的 $z$ 贡献后，两点的纵坐标重合。
>
> | $x$ | $y$ for $z=-1,1$ | $e$ | $r^{(x)}=e+2x$ | Line / 直线 $2x$ |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | $-1$ | $6,12$ | $1/3$ | $-5/3$ | $-2$ |
> | $0$ | $7,13$ | $-2/3$ | $-2/3$ | $0$ |
> | $1$ | $10,16$ | $1/3$ | $7/3$ | $2$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The endpoints lie above the contribution line while the middle lies below it. That suggests checking a quadratic term. The construction makes the reason transparent: the omitted $x^2$ is exactly the curvature left in the partial residual. With real noisy data, a similar-looking bend is a clue to investigate, not proof that a quadratic model is correct.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两端都高于贡献直线，中间却低于直线，因此值得检查二次项。在这个构造中，原因很明确：遗漏的 $x^2$ 正是留在偏残差里的曲率。真实含噪数据中类似的弯曲只是待调查的线索，不能证明二次模型正确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[OLS正规方程|OLS normal equations]] force $\sum_i e_i=0$ and $\sum_i x_{ij}e_i=0$. Consequently, fitting a straight line to this plot reproduces slope $\hat\beta_j$ and intercept zero. Recovering that slope is built into the construction. The diagnostic information lies in departures around the line, not in rediscovering its fitted slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[OLS正规方程]]强制 $\sum_i e_i=0$ 和 $\sum_i x_{ij}e_i=0$，因此对这张图再拟合直线，会得到原来的斜率 $\hat\beta_j$ 和零截距。重现这个斜率是构造本身保证的；真正需要查看的是直线周围还剩什么形状。

## What the adjustment can leave behind
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这种扣除还可能留下什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The other contributions are **estimated from the specified model**. If the model gives another predictor the wrong shape, some of its unmodelled pattern can remain in these partial residuals. When predictors are strongly related, a plot can make the available information look more convincing than it is: the horizontal axis still spans the original $x_j$, even if little variation remains after accounting for the other predictors. NIST specifically cautions that the plotted variation can understate uncertainty in this situation. Read the plot alongside the design and [[附加变量图|the added-variable plot]], whose horizontal axis does remove the other predictors' linear explanation of $x_j$.

A column-specific plot also needs care when the same underlying variable enters through $x$, $x^2$, or interactions such as a [[类别连续交互|categorical–continuous interaction]]. Adding back only $\hat\beta_x x$ does not recover the model's entire contribution of $x$. Specify which terms are being examined and, for interactions, at which values of the other variable. A binary predictor gives only two horizontal locations, so it cannot reveal detailed curvature between them. With a saturated full-rank fit, every residual is zero and the plot simply lies on the fitted contribution line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他贡献是**按已指定模型估计出来的**。若模型给另一个变量安排了错误的形状，它尚未解释的结构可能留在这些偏残差中。解释变量关联很强时，图形也可能让信息显得过于充分：原始 $x_j$ 横轴仍有很宽的范围，但扣除其他变量以后，真正可区分的变动可能很少。NIST 特别提醒，这种情况下图中表现的变动可能低估不确定性。要结合设计和[[附加变量图]]一起看；后者的横轴会扣除其他变量对 $x_j$ 的线性解释部分。

若同一原变量同时以 $x$、$x^2$ 或交互项（例如[[类别连续交互|类别与连续变量交互]]）进入模型，单列图更需要谨慎：只加回 $\hat\beta_xx$，并没有恢复模型中 $x$ 的全部贡献。应说明检查的是哪些项；有交互时，还要说明另一个变量取什么值。二元变量只有两个横轴位置，无法显示两者之间的细致曲率。满秩的饱和拟合中，每个残差都为零，图形只会落在拟合贡献直线上。

> [!note] A view of a fitted model / 拟合模型的一种视图
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These coordinates are sample algebra: drawing them needs no normal-error assumption. They provide neither an automatic significance test nor a causal identification argument. Follow a plausible shape through [[设定修复循环|a documented model revision]], then check the revised fit and its [[残差图边界|remaining diagnostic limits]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 坐标来自样本代数，画图不要求误差正态；它也不会自动提供显著性检验或因果识别。把合理的形状线索带入[[设定修复循环]]，再检查修改后的拟合及其[[残差图边界|诊断边界]]。
