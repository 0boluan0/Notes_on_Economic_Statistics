# 护城河证据应联结优势机制、可观察结果与竞争侵蚀条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Evidence for a durable advantage should connect its mechanism, observable outcomes, and conditions for competitive erosion.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评估所谓“护城河”，要把“企业有持久竞争优势”这一主张转成可以检查的因果链：什么使客户愿意多付或使同等交付更省成本；为什么竞争者难以复制、替代或绕过；这种优势预期能维持多久。单期高利润或市场份额大是待解释的结果，不是自动充分的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To assess a claimed moat, turn “durable competitive advantage” into a testable chain: what supports customer willingness to pay or lower comparable delivery costs, why rivals cannot readily copy, substitute for, or bypass it, and how long that protection may last. High profit in one period or a large market share is an outcome to explain, not sufficient proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可按三步记录：先指明[[价值链]]中的优势机制，再寻找相匹配的价格、成本、客户留存或交付记录，最后写明会削弱该机制的证据。比较应控制产品组合、景气和会计口径；用[[投入资本回报率]]辅助评估时还须核对投入资本与必要回报率，不能把高毛利直接等同于创造价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record the mechanism in the [[价值链|value chain]], seek matching price, cost, retention, or delivery evidence, and specify observations that would weaken the explanation. Comparisons should account for product mix, the business cycle, and accounting definitions. [[投入资本回报率|ROIC]] evidence also needs consistent invested capital and required returns; a high gross margin alone does not establish value creation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，虚构供应商声称客户更换系统需要重新培训和迁移历史数据。可检查实际迁移成本、提价后的留存及竞争产品的迁移工具；若一键迁移使流失明显上升，原先的持久性判断就应下调。留存本身仍可能来自合同期限或暂时补贴，不能直接证明所声称的机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a fictional supplier claims customers must retrain staff and migrate historical data to switch systems. Examine actual migration costs, retention after price increases, and rival migration tools. If one-click migration materially raises churn, revise the durability assessment. Retention alone may instead reflect contract terms or temporary subsidies, so it does not uniquely identify the claimed mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一种证据组织方法，不是给所有企业设同一个数值门槛，也不保证未来竞争结果。用户受益的[[网络效应]]、企业能保留利润和当前股票是否便宜是不同问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a way to organize evidence, not a universal numerical threshold or a guarantee about future competition. [[网络效应|Network benefits]] for users, profit capture by the firm, and whether its stock is inexpensive are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
