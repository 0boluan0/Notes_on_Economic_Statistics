# 外键约束参照存在性而不保证关系基数或业务唯一

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A foreign key enforces reference existence, not relationship cardinality or business uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 外键要求引用值能在被引用的唯一键中找到匹配，从而防止悬空引用。它本身不限制多少行可以引用同一目标，也不保证引用列在业务上唯一。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A foreign key requires each non-null reference to match a referenced unique key, preventing dangling references. By itself, it neither limits how many rows may reference the same target nor makes the referencing column unique in business terms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 `orders(customer_id REFERENCES customers)` 中，外键保证每个非空 `customer_id` 对应一个已存在客户；它不会阻止同一客户拥有一百个订单。这正是一对多关系的常见实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In `orders(customer_id REFERENCES customers)`, the foreign key ensures that every non-null `customer_id` names an existing customer. It does not stop one customer from having a hundred orders; that is the usual representation of a one-to-many relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若业务规则是“每位客户最多一份当前档案”，还需要在档案表的 `customer_id` 上加唯一约束。若关系是多对多，则通常用连接表保存两端外键，并用二者组合或更细的键标识连接事实。外键解决“指向的对象存在吗”，基数与唯一性还要由其他约束表达。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the rule is “at most one current profile per customer,” the profile's `customer_id` also needs a unique constraint. A many-to-many relationship normally uses a junction table with foreign keys to both sides and a composite or finer key for each relationship fact. A foreign key answers “does the referenced object exist?”; cardinality and uniqueness require other constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可空外键还允许“目前没有引用对象”。对复合外键，PostgreSQL 默认的 `MATCH SIMPLE` 只要任一引用列为 `NULL` 就不要求匹配；`MATCH FULL` 则只允许整组都为空或整组都找到匹配。因此，是否必须参与关系还要由列的 `NOT NULL` 与复合外键的匹配规则共同表达。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nullable foreign key also permits “no referenced object at present.” For a composite foreign key, PostgreSQL's default `MATCH SIMPLE` waives matching when any referencing column is null, whereas `MATCH FULL` permits either an all-null reference or a complete match. Participation therefore depends on both nullability and the composite key's matching rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> `orders.customer_id` 有外键后，能否据此断言每位客户只有一个订单？
>
> **答案：** 不能。外键只检查订单所指客户存在；多个订单可以引用同一客户。限制一对一还需要引用列上的唯一约束。
