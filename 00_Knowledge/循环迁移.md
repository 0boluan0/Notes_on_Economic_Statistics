# 循环迁移是同一人在不同地区或国家之间反复迁移的过程

循环迁移（circular migration）强调一个人的迁移历程具有重复性，而不是只记录某次迁出或迁入。这里采用 FAO 的概念口径，既可以是国内地区之间，也可以是国家之间，单次停留可以较短或较长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Circular migration describes repeated movements in a person's migration history, rather than a single departure or arrival. Under the FAO's conceptual usage adopted here, it can occur between domestic areas or countries, with shorter or longer individual stays.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，同一劳动者经历“农村A → 城市B → 农村A → 城市B → 农村A”，可以用来说明循环；只看到他某年在B工作，还不能知道是否存在这样的历程。**季节性**强调迁移跟着某个季节安排，**循环性**强调反复发生：每年农闲外出务工可以兼具两者，不按固定季节重复迁移则未必是季节性迁移。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An illustrative history is rural A → city B → rural A → city B → rural A. Observing one year of employment in B does not establish that history. Seasonality concerns timing; circularity concerns repetition. Annual off-season work can be both, while repeated moves need not follow a seasonal schedule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用于统计时，还必须明确什么移动算迁移、观察多长时间、至少发生几次、每次停留多久。上面的路径只是概念例子，不是替所有调查规定统一门槛。识别了循环形态，也不能由形态本身判断收入、风险或生活状况是否改善；[[迁移的家庭风险分担|家庭收入安排]]只是可能需要另行检验的一条机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measurement must specify qualifying moves, the observation window, repetition thresholds and durations of stay. The path above is conceptual, not a universal survey rule. Nor does circularity alone establish improved income, risk or living conditions; [[迁移的家庭风险分担|household risk sharing]] is one mechanism requiring separate evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
