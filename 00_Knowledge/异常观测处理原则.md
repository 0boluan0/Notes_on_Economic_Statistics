# Handling flagged observations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*被标记观测的处理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When an observation is flagged, the immediate question is why the analysis reacts to it. [[删点影响定义|Deletion influence]] measures a change in a specified fitted result; it does not reveal whether the measurement is false. [[影响诊断阈值|A screening cutoff]] helps prioritize inspection. The decision to correct, exclude, change the model or retain the case needs evidence about the data and the research target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观测被标记后，首先要弄清分析为什么对它敏感。[[删点影响定义|删点影响]]衡量指定拟合结果的变化，不会告诉我们测量是否虚假。[[影响诊断阈值|筛查阈值]]帮助安排核查顺序；更正、排除、改模型还是保留，需要关于数据与研究目标的证据。

> [!example] The same numerical signal can have different explanations / 同一个数值信号可能有不同原因
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit an intercept and slope by [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares]] to $(x,y)=(0,1),(1,3),(2,5),(3,70)$. The full-sample slope is $20.9$; omitting the last case gives $2$. This is clear sensitivity, but does not establish that $70$ is wrong.
>
> If the source record says $7$, correcting a transcription error gives the line $y=1+2x$. If the source really says $70$, changing it to $7$ merely because $7$ fits the line would invent a measurement. A valid extreme outcome may instead expose a limited model or a population difference requiring investigation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 对 $(x,y)=(0,1),(1,3),(2,5),(3,70)$ 用[[普通最小二乘]]拟合截距与斜率，全样本斜率是 $20.9$，去掉末点后是 $2$。结果显然敏感，却没有证明 $70$ 错了。
>
> 若原始凭据写的是 $7$，更正抄录错误后得到 $y=1+2x$。若原始凭据确实是 $70$，只因 $7$ 更贴合直线就改成 $7$，是在编造测量。真实极端结果也可能暴露模型局限或需要调查的总体差异。

## Trace the decision from evidence to action
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 可审计的处理顺序

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start at the original record. Check units, decimal places, category coding, joins, duplicated records, missing-value handling and the measurement procedure. A verified transcription error supports a documented correction; an invalid measurement may require exclusion or a missing-data treatment consistent with the study. Keep the original value and the reason in an audit trail. A surprising but possible value is not a confirmed error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先回到原始记录，核对单位、小数点、类别编码、表连接、重复记录、缺失处理与测量程序。确认抄录错误后，可以留下依据并更正；无效测量则可能需要按研究方案排除或作缺失处理。审计记录保留原值和原因。数值意外但可能发生，不等于错误已被确认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next, state whom the analysis concerns. [[总体与样本|The target population]] specifies the units, period and measurements to which the claim is meant to apply. A rare valid member still belongs to that target. Apply eligibility rules grounded in the study, rather than choosing a rule because it produces a desired sign or significance level. If a defensible change narrows the target, say so explicitly: evidence about the narrower group does not automatically answer the original question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着明确分析针对谁。[[总体与样本|目标总体]]界定结论适用的单位、时段和测量。罕见但有效的成员仍属于该目标。纳入条件应由研究依据支持，而不是因为某条规则能得到想要的系数方向或显著性才选择它。若有依据的调整缩小了目标范围，应明确报告；关于较窄群体的证据不会自动回答原问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then investigate what the fitted model may be missing. Curvature, an interaction, a different measurement scale or a group-specific mechanism may explain a pattern. A [[设定修复循环|model revision]] should follow a reasoned candidate mechanism and be checked again; removing inconvenient cases cannot by itself establish that the remaining model is correct. When several rows belong together, choose the [[删点单位边界|deletion unit]] before interpreting a sensitivity comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再检查模型可能漏掉什么。曲率、交互、不同测量尺度或分组机制都可能解释图形。[[设定修复循环|修改模型]]要有候选机制的依据，并重新核查；删掉难拟合的观测，本身不能证明剩余模型正确。若若干行属于同一单元，先明确[[删点单位边界|删除单位]]，再解释敏感性比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally compare the quantities that matter: a chosen coefficient, uncertainty interval, prediction at a stated location, or covariance estimate. Record the full-sample result and the justified alternative, with the same quantity clearly defined in both. In the example, report the slope change from $20.9$ to $2$ and what was learned from the source record; a rise in $R^2$ alone would not explain the decision. If evidence remains inconclusive, retain the valid observation in the main analysis appropriate to the stated target and report the sensitivity, rather than silently presenting only the preferred fit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后比较真正关心的量：某个系数、不确定性区间、指定位置的预测，或协方差估计。记录全样本结果与有依据的替代分析，并确保两边比较的是说清楚的同一个量。例子中应报告斜率从 $20.9$ 到 $2$ 的变化，以及原始凭据提供了什么信息；只报告 $R^2$ 上升，解释不了处理决定。若证据仍不足，就在符合既定目标的主分析中保留有效观测、报告敏感性，而非只悄悄展示更喜欢的一次拟合。

## What robust regression can and cannot address
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 稳健回归不是通用修复

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some robust regressions, including common residual-based M-estimators, repeatedly downweight large residuals; this is a common use of iteratively reweighted least squares (IRLS), an algorithm for computing a chosen estimator. A high-leverage case can pull the fitted surface toward itself and leave a small residual, so a residual-based weighting rule does not automatically resist unusual predictor configurations. A group can also support its own fitted pattern. Specify which contamination mechanism the estimator addresses, what quantity it estimates, and how dependence and leverage are handled; the word “robust” alone supplies none of these guarantees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些稳健回归（包括常见的基于残差的 M 估计）反复减小大残差点的权重，这是迭代重加权最小二乘（IRLS）计算某种估计量的常见用途。高杠杆点可能把拟合面拉向自己而留下小残差，因此基于残差的权重规则并不自动抵抗异常的解释变量组合。一组点也可能相互支持自己的拟合形状。需要说明估计量针对哪类污染、估计什么量，以及如何处理依赖与杠杆；“稳健”这个词本身不提供这些保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Automatically removing every flagged case changes the analysed sample and may create outcome-dependent selection. It does not rewrite how the original sample was collected. Likewise, a coefficient becoming significant after deletion establishes sensitivity, not a reason for exclusion. Report the flag, the evidence checked, the action and its reason, and the conclusions that change or survive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自动移除所有被标记点，会改变实际分析样本，并可能造成依赖结果的选择；这不等于改写了原始样本已经发生的收集过程。同样，删点后系数变得显著，说明结果敏感，并不构成排除理由。报告中交代标记依据、核查证据、处理动作与原因，以及哪些结论改变、哪些仍成立。
