# Weighted average
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*加权平均*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **weighted average** gives each value a share of influence determined by how much it represents. If two equally sized groups have values $10$ and $20$, their average is $15$. If the first group has three members and the second has one, the average over people is $(3\cdot10+1\cdot20)/4=12.5$. The weights answer the question: what are we averaging over?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**加权平均**按照每个值所代表的份量来决定它的影响。若两个等大的组分别取值 $10$ 和 $20$，平均值是 $15$；若第一组有三人，第二组只有一人，按人平均就是 $(3\cdot10+1\cdot20)/4=12.5$。权重回答的问题是：我们按什么份量来平均？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite list of real values $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ and nonnegative weights $w_1,\ldots,w_n$ with positive sum $W$, define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限个实数值 $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ 及非负权重 $w_1,\ldots,w_n$，要求总权重 $W$ 为正，定义

$$W=\sum_{i=1}^nw_i>0,\qquad\bar f_w=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^nw_if_i}{W}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normalised weights $p_i=w_i/W$ sum to one. Thus $\bar f_w=\sum_i p_if_i$ gives a share to each value. A zero weight contributes nothing, while multiplying every weight by the same positive constant leaves the average unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

归一化后的权重 $p_i=w_i/W$ 之和为一，于是 $\bar f_w=\sum_i p_if_i$ 就是给每个值分配一份占比。零权重没有贡献；所有权重同乘一个正数，平均值保持不变。

## From amounts to a weight density
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从离散份量到权重密度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuum of locations $x$ on a finite interval $[a,b]$, $a<b$, let $f$ be a real-valued function on this interval and let $w(x)\ge0$ describe **weight per unit of $x$**. On a narrow piece of width $\Delta x$, its amount is approximately $w(x)\Delta x$. The value $f(x)$ on that piece therefore contributes approximately $f(x)w(x)\Delta x$. A [[定积分|definite integral]] adds these contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限区间 $[a,b]$ 上连续分布的位置 $x$，其中 $a<b$，设 $f$ 为该区间上的实值函数，令 $w(x)\ge0$ 表示**每单位 $x$ 的份量**。宽为 $\Delta x$ 的窄片所占份量近似为 $w(x)\Delta x$，它的值 $f(x)$ 对加权总值的贡献近似为 $f(x)w(x)\Delta x$。[[定积分]]把这些贡献累积起来。

> [!important] Normalise by the total weight · 除以总权重
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Assume that $w$ and the product $fw$ are Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$, and that $0<W=\int_a^bw(x)\,dx<\infty$. Then the weighted average is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $w$ 与乘积 $fw$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上黎曼可积，并且 $0<W=\int_a^bw(x)\,dx<\infty$。加权平均为
>
> $$\bar f_w=\frac{\int_a^bf(x)w(x)\,dx}{\int_a^bw(x)\,dx}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking $w=1$ recovers the [[积分平均值|integral average]], which gives equal-length pieces equal influence. A constant $f=c$ has numerator $cW$, hence average $c$. If $m\le f(x)\le M$ everywhere, multiplying by $w\ge0$, using [[积分保序性|order preservation]], and dividing by $W>0$ shows $m\le\bar f_w\le M$. The denominator removes the units of weight, leaving the units of $f$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $w=1$ 就恢复[[积分平均值]]：等长小段具有相同影响。若 $f=c$ 为常数，分子就是 $cW$，平均值仍为 $c$。若处处 $m\le f(x)\le M$，先乘以 $w\ge0$，用[[积分保序性]]，再除以 $W>0$，就得到 $m\le\bar f_w\le M$。分母约去权重的单位，结果保留 $f$ 的单位。

## Average temperature over a volume
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*按体积平均温度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use height $y$ measured in metres on $0\le y\le1$. Suppose each horizontal layer of water has temperature $T(y)=100-30y$ degrees Celsius and cross-sectional area $A(y)=\pi y$ square metres. Here the numerical formulas use metre-based coordinates, so their coefficients carry the required units. A thin layer has volume approximately $A(y)\Delta y$; its area, rather than a constant, is the weight density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用米作为高度 $y$ 的单位，取 $0\le y\le1$。设每个水平水层内温度相同，温度为 $T(y)=100-30y$ 摄氏度，截面积为 $A(y)=\pi y$ 平方米。这些数值公式采用米制坐标，系数带有相应单位。薄层体积近似为 $A(y)\Delta y$，所以权重密度是截面积，而不是一个常数。

$$W=\int_0^1\pi y\,dy=\frac\pi2,\qquad\int_0^1(100-30y)\pi y\,dy=\pi(50-10)=40\pi.$$

> [!example] Equal volumes receive equal influence · 等体积享有同等份量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The volume-weighted temperature is $40\pi/(\pi/2)=80$ degrees Celsius. Uniform averaging by height gives $\int_0^1(100-30y)\,dy=85$. The upper layers are colder and contain more water, so their greater volume lowers the volume-weighted result. If density is a positive constant, it cancels from numerator and denominator, so the mass-weighted average is also $80$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 按体积平均的温度是 $40\pi/(\pi/2)=80$ 摄氏度。按高度均匀平均则得 $\int_0^1(100-30y)\,dy=85$。上层更冷，而且水更多，因此更大的体积份量使结果降低。若水的密度处处为同一正数，它会在分子、分母中约去，所以按质量平均也为 $80$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If density varies with height, a mass average instead uses $w(y)=\rho(y)A(y)$, where $\rho(y)$ is mass per unit volume. The temperature and geometry alone then do not determine a mass-weighted answer. Selecting the weight is part of modelling, before any integral is calculated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若密度随高度变化，按质量平均应使用 $w(y)=\rho(y)A(y)$，其中 $\rho(y)$ 是单位体积的质量。此时仅知道温度和形状，还不能决定按质量平均的答案。选择权重是建模的一步，发生在计算积分之前。

## Which weights define an average?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么样的权重能定义平均值？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The total weight must be positive. Nonnegative $w$ that is nonzero at only one point can still have integral zero; pointwise nonzero somewhere is not enough. If $W=0$, or the required integrals do not exist, the displayed ratio does not define this finite average. Negative weights also change the interpretation: for values $10,20$ and weights $2,-1$, the ratio is $0$, outside the range of the two values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总权重必须为正。即使非负函数 $w$ 在某一个点非零，其积分仍可能为零；“某处不为零”并不够。若 $W=0$ 或所需积分不存在，上面的比值就不能定义这种有限平均。负权重也会改变解释：对值 $10,20$ 使用权重 $2,-1$，比值为 $0$，已经落在两值的范围以外。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are useful connections once the weight is specified. A [[质心|centre of mass]] averages position using mass as the weight. In the common case of continuous $f$, normalising $w$ gives a [[概率密度函数|probability density]] $p(x)=w(x)/W$ on $[a,b]$, zero outside; the same average is the [[期望|expectation]] $\mathbb E[f(X)]=\int_a^bf(x)p(x)\,dx$. These are different applications of the same normalised accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确定权重后，还能连接其他概念：[[质心]]以质量为权重平均位置。在常见的 $f$ 连续情形，归一化 $w$ 得到 $[a,b]$ 上的[[概率密度函数]] $p(x)=w(x)/W$，区间外取零；同一个平均值就是[[期望]] $\mathbb E[f(X)]=\int_a^bf(x)p(x)\,dx$。它们是同一种归一化累积在不同问题中的应用。
