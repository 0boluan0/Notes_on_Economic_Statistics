# 加权平均是以非负权重累计的函数值除以严格为正的总权重
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A weighted average divides the nonnegatively weighted total of function values by a strictly positive total weight*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[a,b]$、$a<b$ 上，设权重密度 $w(x)\ge0$ 可积、总权重 $W=\int_a^bw(x)\,dx$ 满足 $0<W<\infty$，并且 $fw$ 可积。$f$ 相对于权重 $w$ 的加权平均定义为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $[a,b]$, with $a<b$, suppose the weight density $w(x)\ge0$ is integrable, its total weight satisfies $0<W=\int_a^bw(x)\,dx<\infty$, and $fw$ is integrable. The weighted average of $f$ is defined by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar f_w=\frac{\int_a^bf(x)w(x)\,dx}{\int_a^bw(x)\,dx}.
$$

$w(x)dx$ 表示小段拥有多少份量，例如人数、长度、体积或质量；$f(x)w(x)dx$ 才是该小段对累计值的贡献。离散版本为 $\sum_iw_if_i/\sum_iw_i$，同样要求非负权重且总权重严格为正。取 $w=1$ 就恢复 [[积分平均值]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$w(x)dx$ represents the amount assigned to a small interval, such as people, length, volume, or mass. Its contribution to the total is $f(x)w(x)dx$. The discrete version is $\sum_iw_if_i/\sum_iw_i$, also requiring nonnegative weights and a positive total. Setting $w=1$ recovers the [[积分平均值|integral average]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母是归一化所必需的：$f=c$ 时，分子为 $cW$，所以平均值仍是 $c$。$W=0$ 时没有可平均的总份量；若 $w$ 或 $fw$ 不可积，则本卡定义的有限加权平均不能直接使用。将 $w/W$ 视为概率密度时，同一表达式是该分布下 $f(X)$ 的 [[期望]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator normalises the weights: a constant $f=c$ gives numerator $cW$ and average $c$. If $W=0$, there is no total amount to average. If $w$ or $fw$ is not integrable, this finite-average definition does not apply directly. Interpreting $w/W$ as a probability density makes the same expression the [[期望|expectation]] of $f(X)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

锅中水在高度 $0\le y\le1$ 的温度为 $T(y)=100-30y$，截面积为 $w(y)=\pi y$，且密度均匀。按水的体积平均温度为 $40\pi/(\pi/2)=80$；直接按高度平均得到 $85$，因为上方较冷处实际容纳更多水。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose water at height $0\le y\le1$ has temperature $T(y)=100-30y$, cross-sectional area $w(y)=\pi y$, and uniform density. The volume-weighted temperature is $40\pi/(\pi/2)=80$. Averaging uniformly by height gives $85$, because that weighting overlooks the greater volume of cooler water near the top.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
