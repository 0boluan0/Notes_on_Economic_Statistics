# Screening thresholds for regression diagnostics
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*影响诊断的筛查阈值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A diagnostic cutoff draws attention to observations worth investigating. It does not decide whether a record is wrong or whether it belongs in the analysis. Different measures also ask different questions: [[Hat杠杆值|leverage]] describes a predictor configuration, [[学生化残差|studentized residuals]] compare discrepancies on an adjusted scale, and [[删点影响定义|deletion influence]] asks how a specified fitted result changes when a case is omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断阈值用来找出值得进一步核查的观测，并不决定记录是否错误、是否应留在分析中。不同统计量也在问不同问题：[[Hat杠杆值|杠杆值]]描述解释变量的位置，[[学生化残差]]把偏差放到经过调整的尺度上比较，[[删点影响定义|删点影响]]则问去掉一个观测后，某个指定拟合结果改变多少。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose a full-rank OLS model has $n=100$ observations and $p=4$ estimated coefficients, including the intercept. Its average leverage is $p/n=0.04$. An observation with $h_{ii}=0.10$ exceeds a twice-average line, $0.08$, but not a three-times-average line, $0.12$. The data and fit have not changed when the flag changes: only the screening convention has.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一个满列秩 OLS 模型用了 $n=100$ 个观测、估计 $p=4$ 个系数（包括截距），平均杠杆值是 $p/n=0.04$。若某点 $h_{ii}=0.10$，它超过“平均值两倍”的 $0.08$，却没超过“三倍”的 $0.12$。标记结果变了，数据与拟合都没变，变的只是筛查约定。

## Common reference lines, with their meanings
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有明确来源的常见筛查线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following rules refer to ordinary unweighted, full-column-rank linear regression. Here $n$ is the actual fitted sample size and $p$ counts independent estimated coefficients, including an intercept when present. The statistic itself must be defined: case deletion must preserve the required rank, and denominators and residual degrees of freedom must be valid. Do not turn an undefined diagnostic into zero or apply these rules unchanged to a different model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下表针对普通无权重、满列秩线性回归。$n$ 是实际拟合的样本量，$p$ 数独立估计的系数，有截距就把它计入。还要先确认统计量本身有定义：删点后仍满足所需秩条件，分母与残差自由度有效。不能把未定义量当成零，也不能把这些经验线原样搬到另一类模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Measure | Common screening convention | What it draws attention to |
|---|---|---|
| Leverage $h_{ii}$ | NIST: $h_{ii}>2p/n$; PSU: $h_{ii}>3p/n$ | A relatively unusual predictor configuration; no response value is used. |
| Internally / externally studentized residual $r_i,t_i$ | Absolute values around $2$ invite inspection; values above $3$ stand out more. | An adjusted residual discrepancy. The two versions use different variance estimates. |
| [[Cook距离\|Cook's distance]] $D_i$ | PSU: investigate $D_i>0.5$; $D_i>1$ is a stronger flag. | Joint change in the fitted mean vector, scaled by the full-fit residual variance. |
| [[DFFITS]] | NIST: magnitude above $1$ for small/moderate samples, or $2\sqrt{p/n}$ for large samples; PSU: $2\sqrt{(p+1)/(n-p-1)}$ when $n>p+1$. | Change in the fitted value at the deleted case's predictor location, on its specified scale. |
| [[DFBETAS]] | A common line is $\lvert DFBETAS_{ij}\rvert>2/\sqrt n$. | Change in coefficient $j$ on the standardized scale; not a cutoff for raw DFBETA. |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 统计量 | 常见筛查约定 | 提醒检查什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 杠杆值 $h_{ii}$ | NIST：$h_{ii}>2p/n$；PSU：$h_{ii}>3p/n$ | 相对特殊的解释变量组合；计算未使用结果值。 |
| 内部/外部学生化残差 $r_i,t_i$ | 绝对值约 $2$ 值得查看，超过 $3$ 更突出 | 经尺度调整的残差偏差；两种版本采用不同的方差估计。 |
| [[Cook距离]] $D_i$ | PSU：$D_i>0.5$ 进一步检查，$D_i>1$ 是更强的标记 | 整个拟合均值向量的变化，以全样本残差方差缩放。 |
| [[DFFITS]] | NIST：小中样本看绝对值 $1$，大样本看 $2\sqrt{p/n}$；PSU：$2\sqrt{(p+1)/(n-p-1)}$，要求 $n>p+1$ | 被删点解释变量位置上的拟合值变化，使用该量规定的尺度。 |
| [[DFBETAS]] | 常见线为 $\lvert DFBETAS_{ij}\rvert>2/\sqrt n$ | 系数 $j$ 的标准化变化；不适用于原始 DFBETA。 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the $n=100,p=4$ example, the two sample-size-dependent DFFITS lines are $0.400$ and approximately $0.459$. A magnitude of $0.43$ lies between them. The DFBETAS line is $0.20$; a value of $-0.30$ crosses it in magnitude, while its negative sign still tells us the direction of the coefficient change under the stated subtraction convention. Screening by magnitude does not make the sign uninformative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $n=100,p=4$ 的例子里，两条随样本量变化的 DFFITS 线分别为 $0.400$ 和约 $0.459$；绝对值 $0.43$ 落在两者之间。DFBETAS 的线是 $0.20$，因此 $-0.30$ 的绝对值越线；负号仍说明按所用相减顺序定义的系数变化方向。用绝对值筛查，不等于符号没有信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Another textbook convention compares Cook's distance with percentiles of an [[F分布|$F_{p,n-p}$ reference distribution]]; PSU and NIST discuss a value near or above its median as a substantial-influence signal. This is a reference scale, not a claim that an individual $D_i$ has that exact null distribution, and it does not automatically supply a valid test p-value. Always identify which convention you used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种教材约定把 Cook 距离与 [[F分布|$F_{p,n-p}$ 参考分布]]的百分位比较；PSU 与 NIST 将接近或超过其中位数作为影响较明显的信号。这是在选参照尺度，不是在宣称单个 $D_i$ 的精确零假设分布就是它，也不自动产生有效检验的 p 值。使用时应说明采用哪套约定。

## What a flag cannot establish
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不允许从阈值直接推出的结论

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed line is not a universal error probability. Even the exact [[Student t分布|Student-$t$]] calibration of an externally studentized residual requires a correct fixed-design linear mean, independent Gaussian errors with common positive variance, valid deletion rank and positive residual degrees of freedom. Internally studentized residuals do not have that same exact $t$ law. Looking across many cases introduces a further issue: a per-case error rate is not the probability of any false flag across the whole search. The [[并事件上界|union bound]] explains why several opportunities must be considered together; regression residuals need not be independent for that bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定经验线不对应普适的误报概率。即使要给外部学生化残差使用精确 [[Student t分布|Student-$t$]] 校准，也需要正确的固定设计线性均值、独立同方差且方差为正的正态误差、有效的删点秩与正的残差自由度。内部学生化残差没有同一个精确 $t$ 分布。若同时检查许多点，还多出一个问题：逐点误报率不等于整轮搜索中至少一次误报的概率。[[并事件上界]]解释了为什么多次机会要一起考虑；应用这个界不要求回归残差彼此独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare the entire pattern as well as a chosen line. A case below it may still dominate the rest; many small crossings do not establish many recording errors. When $3p/n\ge1$, no leverage can exceed that line because $h_{ii}\le1$: an empty flag list then says little about sensitivity. Nor does a lack of single-case flags rule out [[删点单位边界|joint influence masked by other cases]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

除了选定的线，还要比较整组数值。没越线的点也可能远高于其余点；很多点略微越线，并不证明很多记录都有错。当 $3p/n\ge1$ 时，由于 $h_{ii}\le1$，任何杠杆值都不可能越过这条线；此时空标记列表几乎没说明敏感性。逐点没有标记也不能排除[[删点单位边界|被其他观测掩蔽的成组影响]]。

> [!important] A flag starts an investigation / 标记是核查的起点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A flag does not prove a recording error, membership outside the target population, or the superiority of the deleted fit. Use [[异常观测处理原则|the flagged-observation procedure]] to connect the numerical signal to evidence and a specific analysis target.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 标记不证明记录错误、不属于目标总体，也不证明删点拟合更正确。沿[[异常观测处理原则]]把数值信号接到实际证据和明确的分析目标。
