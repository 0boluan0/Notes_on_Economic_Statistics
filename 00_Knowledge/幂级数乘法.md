# 两幂级数在共同绝对收敛区间内可按总次数卷积相乘且乘积半径至少为原半径较小者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two power series multiply by coefficient convolution in their common interior, with product radius at least the smaller input radius*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设同中心展开 $f(x)=\sum a_n(x-c)^n$、$g(x)=\sum b_n(x-c)^n$ 的半径分别为 $R_f,R_g>0$。在 $|x-c|<\min(R_f,R_g)$ 内，
$$
f(x)g(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}d_n(x-c)^n,
\qquad d_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_kb_{n-k}.
$$
第 $n$ 个系数收集的是所有总次数为 $n$ 的配对，而不是只乘 $a_n b_n$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For expansions about the same centre with positive radii, the displayed convolution gives their product in the common interior. The coefficient of degree $n$ collects every pair of degrees adding to $n$, not just $a_n b_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此处两级数均绝对收敛，所有乘积项的绝对值总和有限，所以可以按总次数重新分组。例如 $(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n)^2$ 的第 $n$ 个系数有 $n+1$ 对贡献，得到 $\sum(n+1)x^n=(1-x)^{-2}$（$|x|<1$）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute convergence makes the total absolute sum of product terms finite, permitting regrouping by total degree. Squaring $\sum x^n$ gives $n+1$ contributing pairs at degree $n$, hence $\sum(n+1)x^n=(1-x)^{-2}$ for $|x|<1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积级数的半径只保证至少为 $\min(R_f,R_g)$，抵消可能扩大它：$(1-x)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ 在 $|x|<1$ 等于 $1$，卷积后的所有高次系数为零，所得常数级数的半径为 $\infty$。共同区间以外，不能继续把原来发散的级数当作数值因子相乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product radius is at least the smaller radius and can increase through cancellation. Multiplying $(1-x)$ by $\sum x^n$ cancels every positive-degree coefficient, leaving the constant series with infinite radius. Outside the common interval, the original divergent series cannot be used as numerical factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
