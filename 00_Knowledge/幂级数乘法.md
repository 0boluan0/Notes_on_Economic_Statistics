# Multiplication of power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*幂级数乘法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying two power series follows the same degree bookkeeping as multiplying two polynomials: every term in one factor multiplies every term in the other. Contributions with the same total exponent are added together. For infinite series, this calculation also needs convergence control so that the collected coefficients actually represent the product of the two sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个幂级数相乘时，次数整理与两个多项式相乘相同：一个因子的每项乘另一个因子的每项，再把总指数相同的贡献相加。由于这里有无穷多项，还需要收敛控制，保证整理出来的系数确实表示两个和的乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Collect pairs whose degrees add to the target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*收齐次数之和等于目标的配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $y=x-c$, the displacement from a common centre $c$. For [[幂级数|power series]] $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_ny^n$ and $g(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}b_ny^n$, the first coefficients of the candidate product are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $y=x-c$，即输入相对于共同中心 $c$ 的位移。对[[幂级数]] $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_ny^n$ 与 $g(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}b_ny^n$，候选乘积的前几个系数为

$$d_0=a_0b_0,\qquad d_1=a_0b_1+a_1b_0,\qquad d_2=a_0b_2+a_1b_1+a_2b_0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pair of degrees $k$ and $n-k$ contributes $a_kb_{n-k}y^n$. Adding the $n+1$ possible pairs gives $d_n=\sum_{k=0}^n a_kb_{n-k}$. This finite sum is called coefficient convolution; the resulting infinite series is the Cauchy product. Multiplying just $a_nb_n$ would miss the other pairs and would even pair two terms of total degree $2n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

次数 $k$ 与 $n-k$ 的一对贡献为 $a_kb_{n-k}y^n$。把这 $n+1$ 对相加，得到 $d_n=\sum_{k=0}^n a_kb_{n-k}$。这个有限求和称为系数卷积，得到的无穷级数称为 Cauchy 乘积。只乘 $a_nb_n$ 会漏掉其他配对，而且这两个原项相乘的总次数实际上是 $2n$。

> [!important] Product in the common interior / 在共同内部区间相乘
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the coefficients be finite real numbers and let the [[幂级数收敛半径|radii of convergence]] be $R_f,R_g>0$, allowing infinity. At every real input with $|x-c|<\min(R_f,R_g)$, both factors converge [[绝对收敛|absolutely]], and
> $$f(x)g(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}d_n(x-c)^n,\qquad d_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_kb_{n-k}.$$
> The product series also converges absolutely there. Each coefficient is a finite sum; the common centre is essential to this degree formula.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设系数为有限实数，[[幂级数收敛半径]]分别为 $R_f,R_g>0$，允许为无穷。当实数输入满足 $|x-c|<\min(R_f,R_g)$ 时，两个因子均[[绝对收敛]]，并且上式成立，乘积级数也绝对收敛。每个系数都只含有限次相加；这个次数公式要求两份展开使用同一个中心。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the infinite collection is allowed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么可以整理无穷多个配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix one input in the common interior. Set $u_i=a_iy^i$, $v_j=b_jy^j$, and $U=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}|u_i|$, $V=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}|v_j|$. These two magnitude totals are finite. The product partial sum through degree $N$ is the finite triangular collection $T_N=\sum_{i+j\le N}u_iv_j$, where both indices start at zero. Its magnitude series satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定共同内部的一个输入。令 $u_i=a_iy^i$、$v_j=b_jy^j$，并记 $U=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}|u_i|$、$V=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}|v_j|$。这两个幅度总和都有限。乘积截止次数 $N$ 的部分和，是三角形索引范围内的有限和 $T_N=\sum_{i+j\le N}u_iv_j$，两个索引均从零起。其幅度级数满足

$$\sum_{n=0}^{N}|d_ny^n|\le\sum_{i+j\le N}|u_i||v_j|\le UV.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nonnegative partial sums on the left are increasing and bounded, so the collected series is absolutely convergent. To identify its sum, compare $T_N$ with the finite square product $P_M=(\sum_{i=0}^M u_i)(\sum_{j=0}^M v_j)$. For $N\ge2M$, every pair in that square is in the triangle. Each additional pair has $i>M$ or $j>M$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边的非负部分和递增且有界，故整理后的级数绝对收敛。为确定其和，把 $T_N$ 与有限正方形乘积 $P_M=(\sum_{i=0}^M u_i)(\sum_{j=0}^M v_j)$ 比较。当 $N\ge2M$ 时，正方形内的每对索引都已进入三角形；每一对额外配对至少满足 $i>M$ 或 $j>M$，所以

$$|T_N-P_M|\le V\sum_{i>M}|u_i|+U\sum_{j>M}|v_j|\qquad(N\ge2M).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both tails on the right tend to zero as $M$ grows. Also $P_M\to f(x)g(x)$ by the ordinary limit rule for a product of two convergent sequences. Given any error tolerance, first choose $M$ making these two tail contributions and $|P_M-f(x)g(x)|$ small. The same choice controls **every** $N\ge2M$, proving $T_N\to f(x)g(x)$. Thus the proof checks the entire sequence of collected partial sums, not just selected grouping endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $M$ 增大，右边两段幅度尾和都趋零；由两个收敛数列的乘积极限规则，另有 $P_M\to f(x)g(x)$。任给精度要求，先选 $M$，使这两个尾部贡献以及 $|P_M-f(x)g(x)|$ 都足够小；同一个选择控制**每个** $N\ge2M$，从而 $T_N\to f(x)g(x)$。这个证明检查了整理后全部部分和，而不只是某些方便的分组终点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Square a geometric series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把几何级数平方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n)^2$, every original coefficient is one. Degree $n$ receives one from each of the pairs $(0,n),(1,n-1),\ldots,(n,0)$, so $d_n=n+1$. The [[几何级数|geometric sum]] then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n)^2$，原系数全是一。次数 $n$ 从 $(0,n),(1,n-1),\ldots,(n,0)$ 这 $n+1$ 对中各收到一个一，因此 $d_n=n+1$。由[[几何级数]]的和得到

$$\frac1{(1-x)^2}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(n+1)x^n=1+2x+3x^2+4x^3+\cdots,\qquad |x|<1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=1/2$, the sum is $4$. This is a convolution identity, even though the two factors happen to be the same series. At $x=\pm1$, the product-series terms fail to tend to zero, so these endpoints do not belong to its convergence interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x=1/2$ 处，和为 $4$。虽然两个因子恰好相同，这仍然是在计算卷积。在 $x=\pm1$ 处，乘积级数通项不趋零，所以两个端点都不属于收敛区间。

> [!warning] The radius can increase through cancellation / 抵消可以扩大半径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The product radius is **at least** $\min(R_f,R_g)$; equality is not guaranteed. For $(1-x)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$, the convolution gives $d_0=1$ and $d_n=1-1=0$ for every $n\ge1$. The resulting constant series has infinite radius, although the geometric factor has radius one. At $x=2$, the constant series still sums to one, but the original geometric series diverges: one cannot evaluate the original product of series there.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 乘积半径**至少**是 $\min(R_f,R_g)$，并不保证取等。例如 $(1-x)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ 的卷积系数满足 $d_0=1$，每个 $n\ge1$ 都有 $d_n=1-1=0$。所得常数级数的半径为无穷，而几何因子的半径只有一。在 $x=2$ 处，常数级数仍然等于一，原几何级数却发散，因而不能在那里把原来的两个级数和相乘。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem is sufficient, not a claim that every other product fails. At a boundary input, the same absolute-tail proof works if both numerical factor series converge absolutely there. Conditional convergence alone does not provide that control, so a shared endpoint is not automatically included; check it separately with [[幂级数端点判别|endpoint analysis]]. If the two centres differ, first obtain justified expansions in a common variable before using this coefficient formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本定理给出充分保证，并不是说其他乘法全都失败。若边界输入处两个数项级数也都绝对收敛，上面的绝对尾和证明同样适用；只有条件收敛则不提供这种控制，所以不能自动纳入共同端点，应另作[[幂级数端点判别|端点检查]]。若两份展开的中心不同，需要先得到以同一个变量展开的有效表示，再使用此系数公式。
