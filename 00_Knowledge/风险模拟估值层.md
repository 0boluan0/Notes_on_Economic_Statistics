# 风险模拟估值层把每个模拟状态或路径映射为同一口径下的组合价值与损失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The valuation layer in risk simulation maps every simulated state or path into portfolio value and loss under one convention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 风险模拟估值层是在风险因子情景已经生成之后，按照预先固定的头寸、现金流、估值时点和定价规则，计算每个情景下组合价值并形成情景损失的步骤。它回答“这个情景对当前组合值多少钱”，不负责决定情景以多大概率出现。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After risk-factor scenarios have been generated, the valuation layer applies fixed positions, cash-flow boundaries, valuation times, and pricing rules to compute portfolio value and scenario loss. It answers “what is today's portfolio worth in this scenario?”, not how likely the scenario is.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对第 $i$ 个终点状态或有序路径 $X^{(i)}$，估值器先得到持有期末价值与期间现金流，再按 [[风险度量口径]] 形成损失：

$$
V_H^{(i)}=\mathcal V\!\left(X^{(i)};\text{positions, contracts, valuation rules}\right),
$$

$$
L_i=V_0-\left(V_H^{(i)}+\text{interim cash flows under the chosen convention}\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each terminal state or ordered path, the valuation engine computes horizon value and interim cash flows, then converts them into loss under the fixed [[风险度量口径|risk-measure convention]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估值层可以采用逐情景全重估、闭式或数值定价、内层模拟，或经验证的敏感度与代理近似。若持有期末还有未到期衍生品，外层真实世界情景与情景内条件定价的角色见 [[风险模拟P-Q分工]]；若中间触碰或现金流顺序会改变价值，输入必须是 [[风险模拟路径依赖|完整有序路径]]，而不只是终点状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The layer may use full revaluation, analytic or numerical pricing, inner simulation, or a validated sensitivity or surrogate approximation. If derivatives remain alive at the horizon, [[风险模拟P-Q分工|outer-P and inner-Q roles]] must be separated. If intermediate touches or cash-flow order affect value, the input must preserve the [[风险模拟路径依赖|ordered path]], not only the terminal state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两条情景都把股票价格带到 100，但只有一条曾触及障碍 120。对普通欧式头寸，终点状态可能足够；对障碍期权，估值层必须读取触碰历史，才能给出正确损失。

> [!question]- 自检
> 已经生成了一百万条风险因子路径，是否就已经得到一百万个组合损失？
>
> **答案：** 没有。路径还必须进入与头寸、现金流和估值口径一致的估值层，才能转换为组合价值与损失。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** Do one million simulated risk-factor paths already constitute one million portfolio losses?
>
> **Answer:** No. Each path must pass through a valuation layer consistent with positions, cash flows, and the valuation convention before it becomes a portfolio loss.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 全重估、闭式定价与近似代理是估值实现选择，不是不同的风险因子概率模型。
- 估值器本身正确，不证明外层分布、依赖或时间动态正确。
- 采用近似时，平均误差小仍不足以保证尾部风险量准确，见 [[估值近似验证]]。

## 来源与核验

- Federal Reserve, [*Nested Simulation in Portfolio Risk Measurement*](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/nested-simulation-in-portfolio-risk-measurement.htm)：核对外层生成持有期风险因子状态、情景内重估各头寸并形成组合损失分布的估值层结构。
- [Basel Framework, MAR30.17–30.18](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/mar/30/inforce/2023-01-01/published/2024-07-05)：核对定价模型、估值公式与风险转换计算必须作为模型验证的独立对象。
