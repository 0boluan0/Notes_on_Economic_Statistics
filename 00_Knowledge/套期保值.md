# 套期保值是用新增头寸抵销既有或预期经济活动的特定风险敞口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Hedging offsets a specified risk exposure in an existing position or expected economic activity with an additional position*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

套期保值先问“原来怕什么”，再决定对冲方向。未来要出售大豆的农场怕价格下跌，可卖出相关期货；未来需要买入大豆的加工厂怕价格上涨，可买入相关期货。两者现在都未必持有现货，关键是未来收入或支出怎样随价格变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the original exposure. A farmer expecting to sell soybeans fears a price fall and may sell related futures. A processor expecting to buy them fears a price rise and may buy futures. Neither needs to hold inventory today; the issue is how future receipts or costs respond to price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

忽略融资、费用与违约，农场将在同一时点出售 $Q$ 吨，卖出同等数量期货，初始期货价 $F_0$，平仓价 $F_T$，实际售粮价格 $S_T$。现货收入加期货盈亏为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ignoring funding, costs, and default, a farmer selling $Q$ tonnes at $S_T$ and closing an equal short futures position entered at $F_0$ and closed at $F_T$ receives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$QS_T+Q(F_0-F_T)=Q(F_0+b_T),\qquad b_T=S_T-F_T.$$

若 $Q=100$、$F_0=2200$，最后 $S_T=2000$、$F_T=1980$，收入合计为 222,000；若 $F_T=2020$，合计为 218,000。两种情况下现货价相同，最终收入仍因基差不同而不同。在上述固定数量模型中，若到期基差确定，就锁定了每吨收入；匹配期限、数量和交割品有助于控制差异，但实际还须检查歉收、品级差、提前平仓和保证金需求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $Q=100$ and $F_0=2200$, terminal prices $S_T=2000$, $F_T=1980$ produce receipts of 222,000; if $F_T=2020$, receipts are 218,000. The same spot price does not guarantee the same hedged receipts. In this fixed-quantity model, a known terminal basis locks receipts per tonne. Matching dates, quantities, and deliverables helps control differences; output shortfalls, grade differences, early closure, and margin funding remain relevant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，套保效果看**原头寸加对冲头寸**，不看对冲交易单独是否赚钱。若风险用敏感度描述，可进一步用[[Delta对冲]]或[[DV01对冲]]求头寸，而不是一律按名义金额一比一对冲。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assess the combined original and hedge positions, not whether the hedge alone makes money. Where exposure is measured by sensitivity, use [[Delta对冲|Delta hedging]] or [[DV01对冲|DV01 hedging]] instead of automatically matching notional amounts one for one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
