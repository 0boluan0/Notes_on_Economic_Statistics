# 风险 Monte Carlo 是从联合因子模型生成当前组合损失分布的模拟方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk Monte Carlo is a simulation method that generates a current-portfolio loss distribution from a joint factor model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 风险 Monte Carlo 先生成未来联合风险因子情景，再把每个情景施加到当前固定组合并估值，得到模型隐含的损失样本。VaR 与 ES 是这组损失的尾部统计量，而不是随机数本身。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Risk Monte Carlo first generates future joint risk-factor scenarios, then applies each scenario to today's fixed portfolio and values it, producing model-implied loss samples. VaR and ES are tail statistics of those losses, not properties of the random numbers alone.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设估值时点为 $t_0$、持有期为 $H$。对 $i=1,\ldots,N$，

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At valuation time $t_0$ and horizon $H$, for $i=1,\ldots,N$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x^{(i)}_{t_0:t_0+H}\sim P_{\widehat\theta},
\qquad
L_i=-\left[V^{(i)}_{t_0+H}-V_{t_0}\right],
$$

其中路径或终点情景、价值计算和损益边界必须与 [[风险度量口径]] 一致。只有终点状态重要时可以直接模拟终点；路径依赖产品需要完整有序路径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paths or terminal scenarios, valuation, and the P&L boundary must follow the [[风险度量口径|risk-measure convention]]. Terminal simulation is enough only when value depends on the terminal state; path-dependent products require an ordered path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险 Monte Carlo 需要分别决定外层 $P$ 与条件定价 $Q$ 的 [[风险模拟P-Q分工]]、[[风险因子联合生成]]、[[风险因子时间动态]]、[[风险模拟路径依赖]]，再由 [[风险模拟估值层]] 把情景转换为组合损失；若使用代理近似，还要单独做 [[估值近似验证]]。增加模拟次数只能减小抽样误差，[[路径数不修模型|不能修复模型误设]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk Monte Carlo separately requires a choice of [[风险模拟P-Q分工|outer-$P$/inner-$Q$ roles]], [[风险因子联合生成|joint factor generation]], [[风险因子时间动态|time-series dynamics]], and [[风险模拟路径依赖|path treatment]], followed by a [[风险模拟估值层|valuation layer]] that converts scenarios into portfolio losses. A proxy also requires separate [[估值近似验证|approximation validation]]. More paths reduce sampling error but [[路径数不修模型|cannot repair model misspecification]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个程序生成了一百万个随机数，是否已经完成风险 Monte Carlo？
>
> **答案：** 没有。还必须把随机数映射为经校准的联合风险因子情景，对当前组合按既定口径估值，并从损失样本计算目标风险量。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** Has a program completed risk Monte Carlo merely by generating one million random numbers?
>
> **Answer:** No. Random draws must be mapped into calibrated joint factor scenarios, applied to the current portfolio under a fixed valuation convention, and converted into the target loss statistic.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 模拟可以生成历史之外的情景，但只能生成模型允许的情景。
- 复杂模拟不自动优于简单方法；适用性取决于组合与验证证据。
- 数值收敛、实现正确和模型适用是三种不同状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Simulation can generate states outside history only if the model permits them.
- Complexity does not automatically improve suitability; the portfolio and validation evidence decide.
- Numerical convergence, correct implementation, and model adequacy are distinct states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Basel Committee, *An Internal Model-Based Approach to Market Risk Capital Requirements*](https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbsc224.pdf)：核对随机风险因子情景重估当前组合的基本流程。
- [RiskMetrics Group, *Return to RiskMetrics*](https://www.msci.com/documents/10199/dbb975aa-5dc2-4441-aa2d-ae34ab5f0945)：核对风险因子—组合价值映射与模拟风险度量流程。
- Glasserman, [*Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-21617-1)：核对金融路径生成、估值与数值误差的分层。
- Art B. Owen, [*Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples*, Chapter 2](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-intro.pdf)：核对有限样本的 Monte Carlo 抽样误差与标准误。
