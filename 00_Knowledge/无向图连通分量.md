# Connected components
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无向图连通分量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **connected component** is one whole piece of an undirected graph: inside the piece every vertex can be reached from every other, and no vertex outside it can be reached. Components let us separate a network into independent reachability regions. A component need not be densely connected; a long chain is one piece, even though most pairs have no direct edge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**连通分量**是无向图中完整的一块：块内任意两点互相可达，而块外没有点能够从块内到达。分量把网络分成彼此独立的可达区域。分量不要求连接稠密；一条长链就是一块，即使大多数点对之间没有直接边。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three pieces in one graph
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一张图里的三块*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $V=\{a,b,c,d,e,f\}$ with exactly the undirected edges $\{a,b\},\{b,c\},\{d,e\}$. The components have vertex sets $\{a,b,c\}$, $\{d,e\}$ and $\{f\}$. The first includes the two edges through $b$; the second includes its single edge. The isolated vertex $f$ is also a component because its length-zero path reaches itself. The set $\{a,b\}$ is connected, but is not a whole component: it can still be enlarged by adding $c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $V=\{a,b,c,d,e,f\}$，恰好有无向边 $\{a,b\},\{b,c\},\{d,e\}$。三个分量的顶点集分别为 $\{a,b,c\}$、$\{d,e\}$ 和 $\{f\}$。第一块包含经过 $b$ 的两条边，第二块包含它的一条边。孤立顶点 $f$ 也算分量，因为零长度路径使它从自身可达。$\{a,b\}$ 虽然连通，却不是完整分量，因为还能把 $c$ 加进去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The full reachable set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*完整的可达集合*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a finite [[图类型的建模边界|undirected graph]] $G=(V,E)$ with at least one vertex. The set $V$ contains the vertices and $E$ the edges. A [[游走路径与环|path]] follows edges without repeating vertices, and [[图距离与可达性|reachability]] permits a length-zero path. Write $u\sim v$ when a path connects $u$ and $v$. For any $v\in V$, put
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑至少有一个顶点的有限[[图类型的建模边界|无向图]] $G=(V,E)$，其中 $V$ 为顶点集、$E$ 为边集。[[游走路径与环|路径]]沿边行进且不重复顶点；[[图距离与可达性|可达性]]允许长度为零的路径。若路径连接 $u,v$，记为 $u\sim v$。对任意 $v\in V$，令

$$C(v)=\{u\in V:u\sim v\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The component of $v$ is the subgraph consisting of all vertices in $C(v)$ and **all original edges whose endpoints lie there**. “Subgraph” means that these vertices and edges are taken from $G$; we do not invent a direct edge between every reachable pair. This component is maximal by inclusion among connected subgraphs: it cannot be enlarged inside $G$ and remain connected. Maximal does not mean that it has the largest number of vertices among all components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$v$ 所在的分量由 $C(v)$ 中的全部顶点，以及**原图中两端都在该集合内的全部边**组成。“子图”表示顶点和边都从 $G$ 中取出，并不是给每对可达点新加一条直接边。这个连通子图按包含关系是极大的：无法在 $G$ 内继续扩大而保持连通。“极大”并不表示它比所有其他分量的顶点都多。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why these sets form a partition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么真的是等价关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relation $\sim$ has three properties. It is **reflexive**: $v\sim v$ by the zero-length path. It is **symmetric**: reversing an undirected path gives a path back. It is **transitive**: if $u\sim v$ and $v\sim w$, join the two paths into a walk and use [[游走去环得到路径|detour deletion]] to obtain a path from $u$ to $w$. A relation with these three properties is called an **[[等价关系|equivalence relation]]**. Its equivalence class $C(v)$ collects exactly the vertices related to $v$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关系 $\sim$ 有三个性质。它**自反**：零长度路径给出 $v\sim v$。它**对称**：无向路径可以反向读取。它**传递**：若 $u\sim v$、$v\sim w$，把两条路径拼成游走，再用[[游走去环得到路径|删除绕行段的方法]]得到 $u$ 到 $w$ 的路径。同时具备这三个性质的关系叫作**[[等价关系]]**，其等价类 $C(v)$ 收集与 $v$ 有关系的全部顶点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every vertex lies in its own class, so the classes cover $V$. If $C(u)$ and $C(v)$ share a vertex $x$, paths through $x$ show that $u\sim v$. Any vertex reachable from $u$ is then reachable from $v$, and conversely; hence $C(u)=C(v)$. Distinct classes are therefore disjoint. This is a **partition**: each vertex belongs to exactly one component, independent of which starting vertex we used to find it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个顶点都在自己的类中，所以这些类覆盖 $V$。若 $C(u)$ 与 $C(v)$ 共有顶点 $x$，经过 $x$ 的路径说明 $u\sim v$；从 $u$ 可达的点也就从 $v$ 可达，反之亦然，因此 $C(u)=C(v)$。所以不同类彼此不交。这就是一个**划分**：每个顶点恰好属于一个分量，不取决于从哪个起点开始寻找。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No edge can join distinct components: that one edge would already give a path between its endpoints and merge their classes. Likewise, there can be no longer path between different components. Within $C(v)$, any two vertices are connected through $v$; deleting repetitions leaves a path entirely in that same class. These observations establish both connectedness and maximality of the component subgraph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同分量之间不可能有边，否则这一条边就会使两端可达、归入同一类；也不可能有更长的跨分量路径。在 $C(v)$ 内，任意两点都能经过 $v$ 连起来，删掉重复段后得到的路径也完全留在同一类内。这就同时说明了分量子图的连通性和极大性。

## Connected graphs and boundary cases
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连通图与边界情形*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonempty graph is **connected** when every pair of its vertices is joined by a path. Equivalently, it has exactly one component. If it has $n\ge1$ vertices and $c$ components, then $1\le c\le n$. One vertex with no edges is connected. An edgeless graph on $n$ vertices has $n$ singleton components. If the empty vertex set is allowed, it has zero components; whether to call that graph connected is a separate convention, so the one-component equivalence above was stated for nonempty graphs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空图称为**连通的**，是指任意两个顶点间都有路径；等价地，它恰好只有一个分量。若有 $n\ge1$ 个顶点、$c$ 个分量，则 $1\le c\le n$。只有一个顶点而没有边的图是连通的；有 $n$ 个顶点而没有边的图有 $n$ 个单点分量。若允许顶点集为空，则它有零个分量；是否把这种图称为连通，另需约定，因此上面的“一个分量”等价表述限定于非空图。

> [!example] Positive degree does not join separate pieces · 度数为正也可能分成几块
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take four vertices and only two disjoint edges, $\{a,b\}$ and $\{c,d\}$. Every vertex has [[度数、入度与出度|degree]] $1$, but there are two components. Positive degree rules out isolated vertices in this loopless example; it does not ensure routes between different pieces.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 四个顶点只有两条互不相交的边 $\{a,b\}$、$\{c,d\}$ 时，每个顶点的[[度数、入度与出度|度数]]都是 $1$，却有两个分量。在这个无自环例子中，度数为正排除了孤立顶点，却没有保证不同块之间存在路线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parallel copies of an existing edge do not change the reachable vertex sets, and self-loops do not connect different vertices. Numerical edge weights also do not change these components as long as the same edges remain available. If an application keeps only positive-weight edges, as in a [[加权图 Laplacian|weighted Laplacian]], first form that support graph and then compute its components; a zero-weight edge in the original graph need not survive that step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已有边的平行副本不改变可达顶点集合，自环也不能连接不同顶点。只要同样的边仍然允许经过，边上的数值权重本身也不改变这些分量。若应用只保留正权边，例如[[加权图 Laplacian|加权拉普拉斯矩阵]]所用的支撑图，就应先形成那张新图，再求它的分量；原图中的零权边不一定保留下来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a directed graph, one-way reachability need not be symmetric and does not in general give this partition. Requiring travel in both directions gives [[强连通与弱连通|strong components]], while discarding directions gives weak connectivity. For the present undirected graph, following all available routes from one starting vertex finds exactly its component; repeating from an unvisited vertex finds another. The algorithmic conditions are explained in [[全图搜索与强连通边界|full graph search]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有向图中的单向可达未必对称，不能直接保证得到这里的划分。要求两个方向都可达，得到[[强连通与弱连通|强连通分量]]；丢掉方向后再看连通性，则是弱连通。对本卡的无向图，从一个起点沿全部可走路线访问，恰好找到它所在的分量；再从未访问点开始，就能找到另一个分量。具体算法条件见[[全图搜索与强连通边界|全图搜索]]。
