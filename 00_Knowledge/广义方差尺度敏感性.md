# Generalized variance under changes of units
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*广义方差的单位与坐标变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[广义方差|Generalized variance]] changes when the numerical units of measurements change. Converting a length from meters to centimeters multiplies its numerical value by $100$, its variance by $100^2$, and—if other coordinates stay unchanged—the covariance determinant by $100^2$. The underlying observations have not become more variable physically; their numerical description has changed.

This matters when comparing datasets or models. A covariance determinant carries the **product of squared coordinate units**. It is meaningful to compare its magnitude only after specifying the variables, dimension, units and covariance convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[广义方差]]会随测量单位的数值换算改变。长度从米换成厘米，数值乘以 $100$，方差乘以 $100^2$；若其他坐标不变，协方差行列式也乘以 $100^2$。观测本身并没有在物理上变得更分散，改变的是数值描述。

比较数据集或模型时，这一点很重要。协方差行列式携带**各坐标单位平方的乘积**；比较大小之前，必须说明变量、维度、单位和协方差定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How coordinate scaling enters the product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 单位换算会被平方后进入乘积

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the real $p$-dimensional random vector $X$ have finite second moments and covariance $\Sigma$. A fixed affine transformation $Y=AX+b$ first forms linear combinations using the real $p\times p$ matrix $A$, then adds a fixed vector $b$. The [[协方差仿射变换|covariance transformation rule]] gives $\operatorname{Cov}(Y)=A\Sigma A^T$. Taking determinants and using [[行列式乘法性|multiplicativity]] and [[行列式转置不变性|transpose invariance]] yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实 $p$ 维随机向量 $X$ 的二阶矩有限，协方差为 $\Sigma$。固定仿射变换 $Y=AX+b$ 用实 $p\times p$ 矩阵 $A$ 形成线性组合，再加固定向量 $b$。由[[协方差仿射变换|协方差变换公式]]，$\operatorname{Cov}(Y)=A\Sigma A^T$。取行列式，使用[[行列式乘法性]]及[[行列式转置不变性]]，得到

$$
\det\operatorname{Cov}(Y)
=\det(A)\det(\Sigma)\det(A^T)
=\det(A)^2\det(\Sigma).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a reversible change of coordinates, $A$ is invertible. The identity itself also holds for singular square $A$: then the output covariance determinant is zero because a direction has been lost. If $A$ is rectangular, the covariance transformation still holds, but $\det(A)$ is not defined and this squared-determinant formula cannot be used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可逆坐标变换要求 $A$ 可逆。等式本身对奇异方阵 $A$ 也成立：这时某个方向丢失，输出协方差行列式为零。如果 $A$ 是长方形矩阵，协方差变换仍成立，但 $\det(A)$ 没有定义，不能使用这个行列式平方公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For separate unit conversions, $A=\operatorname{diag}(a_1,\ldots,a_p)$, so the factor is $(a_1\cdots a_p)^2$. Multiplying one coordinate's numerical values by $10$ multiplies generalized variance by $100$. Translating the origin with $b$ has no effect because subtracting the new mean cancels the same translation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐坐标换单位时，$A=\operatorname{diag}(a_1,\ldots,a_p)$，因子就是 $(a_1\cdots a_p)^2$。只把一个坐标的数值乘以 $10$，广义方差便乘以 $100$。用 $b$ 平移原点没有影响，因为减去新均值时同一平移会相消。

> [!example] A complete meters-to-centimeters calculation · 米换厘米的完整计算
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(4,9)$, whose determinant is $36$. Convert only the first coordinate by $A=\operatorname{diag}(100,1)$. Then
> $$A\Sigma A^T=\operatorname{diag}(40000,9),\qquad
> \det(A\Sigma A^T)=360000=10000\times36.$$
> The factor is $10000$, not $100$, because covariance uses squared lengths.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取行列式为 $36$ 的 $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(4,9)$，只对第一坐标乘 $100$。新协方差为 $\operatorname{diag}(40000,9)$，行列式为 $360000=10000\times36$。因子为 $10000$ 而不是 $100$，原因是协方差使用长度的平方。

## Preserving volume does not preserve shape
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保持体积尺度，不等于保持形状*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rotation or reflection represented by an [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrix]] satisfies $A^TA=I$ and $|\det(A)|=1$, so it preserves generalized variance. But orthogonality is not necessary: every invertible square map with $|\det(A)|=1$ preserves it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正交矩阵]]表示的旋转或反射满足 $A^TA=I$、$|\det(A)|=1$，所以保持广义方差。但正交性不是必要条件：每个满足 $|\det(A)|=1$ 的可逆方阵变换都保持它。

> [!example] A shear changes covariance but preserves its determinant · 剪切改变协方差，却保留行列式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\Sigma=I_2$ and $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$,
> $$A\Sigma A^T=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> \det(A\Sigma A^T)=2-1=1=\det\Sigma.$$
> The first output is $X_1+X_2$, so its variance and its covariance with the second output have changed. Nevertheless, this shear has determinant one. It changes shape while preserving the [[行列式体积与取向|volume factor]]. Similarly, stretching one axis by $2$ and another by $1/2$ preserves the product.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $\Sigma=I_2$，用上述剪切矩阵变换，得到协方差 $\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}$，行列式仍为 $1$。第一输出是 $X_1+X_2$，其方差和它与第二输出的协方差都改变了；但剪切行列式为一，改变形状的同时保持[[行列式体积与取向|体积因子]]。类似地，一轴拉长为两倍、另一轴缩为一半，也保持乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With positive-definite $\Sigma$, generalized variance is unchanged exactly when $|\det(A)|=1$. If $\Sigma$ is already singular, both determinants remain zero for every square $A$, so equality of those zeros cannot diagnose volume preservation by the map. The exact transformation formula, not the label “scaling” or “shear,” decides the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $\Sigma$ 正定时，广义方差不变当且仅当 $|\det(A)|=1$。若 $\Sigma$ 已奇异，则每个方阵 $A$ 下两边都仍为零；这些零相等，不能用于判断变换是否保持体积。结论应由变换公式决定，不能只看“缩放”或“剪切”的名字。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If all marginal variances are positive, the [[相关矩阵|correlation matrix]] $R$ instead describes coordinates standardized to variance one. Writing $D=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_p)$ gives $\Sigma=DRD$ and
$$\det\Sigma=\left(\prod_{j=1}^p\sigma_j^2\right)\det R.$$
Thus $\det R$ removes positive separate unit conversions, but summarizes a different, standardized object. It is not invariant under arbitrary variable mixing, and standardization does not remove exact linear dependence. Neither determinant alone determines whether a chosen measurement scale is appropriate for an analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若各边际方差都为正，[[相关矩阵]] $R$ 描述的是标准化到方差一后的坐标。记 $D=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_p)$，则 $\Sigma=DRD$，并有
$$\det\Sigma=\left(\prod_{j=1}^p\sigma_j^2\right)\det R.$$
因此 $\det R$ 消除了正向逐坐标单位换算的影响，但概括的是另一个经过标准化的对象。它并不对任意变量混合都不变，标准化也不能消除精确线性依赖。两个行列式都不能单独决定某种测量尺度是否适合当前分析。
