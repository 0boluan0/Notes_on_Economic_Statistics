# 协变量匹配用可观测特征相近的单位构造处理比较

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Covariate matching constructs treatment comparisons from units with similar observed characteristics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 协变量匹配根据处理前的可观测特征，为处理单位寻找可比较的未处理单位。其因果解释需要相关的条件交换性、重叠和潜在结果一致性；特征接近本身不能消除未观测混淆。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Covariate matching compares treated units with untreated units having similar observed pretreatment characteristics. Causal interpretation needs suitable conditional exchangeability, overlap, and consistency; observed similarity alone does not remove unmeasured confounding.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

匹配回答“怎样构造比较组”，不是自动识别定理。所保留的人群和加权方式也会影响估计目标，见[[估计对象三分]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matching constructs a comparison group rather than supplying identification automatically. Retained units and weights can change the [[估计对象三分|estimand]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
