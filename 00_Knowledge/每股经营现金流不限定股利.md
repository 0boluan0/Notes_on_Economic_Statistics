# 每股经营现金流既不是可分配利润也不单独限定每股股利的上限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating cash flow per share is neither distributable profit nor a standalone upper bound on dividends per share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[每股经营现金流]]只缩放本期经营现金净流入。股利还取决于现金余额、投资与融资、到期义务、留存资金需求以及适用的利润分配和偿付限制；“超过本期 CFO 就只能借款”并不成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[每股经营现金流|Operating cash flow per share]] scales only current operating cash flow. Dividends also depend on cash balances, investing and financing, obligations, retention needs, and applicable distribution and solvency constraints. Exceeding current CFO does not necessarily require borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个仅检验现金来源的简例：期初可动用现金 100，本期 CFO 为 20，无其他投资、融资或汇率变动；若合同与法律均允许、也无需保留其他资金，派现 50 后仍有现金 70。50 大于本期 CFO，但没有新增借款。反过来，即使 CFO 为 100，若有必需的资本支出与到期债务，也不能据此断言这 100 都可派发。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a cash-source counterexample, take unrestricted opening cash of 100 and CFO of 20, with no other investing, financing, or exchange-rate movements. If contracts and law permit distribution and no other funds must be retained, a dividend of 50 leaves 70 without new borrowing. Conversely, CFO of 100 does not establish that all 100 is available for dividends when capital spending and debt obligations remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个反例不声称“有现金就合法可分”：现金与会计可分配利润是不同对象。评估经营活动留给全部资金提供者的现金时，应使用条件清楚的[[企业自由现金流]]；讨论利润分配比例则使用[[股利支付率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The counterexample does not equate available cash with legal distributability. Cash and accounting distributable profit are different objects. Use properly specified [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] for cash available from operations to all capital providers, and the [[股利支付率|payout ratio]] for the share of earnings distributed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
