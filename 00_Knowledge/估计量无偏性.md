# Unbiasedness of an estimator
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*估计量的无偏性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **unbiased estimator** is an estimation rule whose average result, over possible samples from the same model, equals the quantity it is trying to estimate. Individual estimates can be too high or too low. Unbiasedness says that these signed errors balance in expectation, at every parameter value covered by the claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**无偏估计量**是一条估计规则：按同一个模型考虑各种可能样本，它所得结果的平均，等于想估计的目标。某次估计可以偏高，也可以偏低；无偏性说的是这些带正负号的误差在期望中抵消，而且所讨论的每个参数值都要满足。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An average of possible answers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把可能的答案取平均*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a [[总体与样本|population]] with values $0,2,4,6$, each selected with probability $1/4$. Its mean is $3$. Draw twice independently with replacement, and estimate the population mean by the sample average $T_2=(X_1+X_2)/2$. The [[估计对象三分|estimator]] is this rule; an observed pair $(0,2)$ produces the estimate $1$, with error $1-3=-2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一个[[总体与样本|总体]]的数值为 $0,2,4,6$，每次各以 $1/4$ 的概率选到，总体均值为 $3$。独立、有放回地抽两次，用样本平均 $T_2=(X_1+X_2)/2$ 估计总体均值。[[估计对象三分|估计量]]是这条规则；若观察到 $(0,2)$，本次估计值为 $1$，误差为 $1-3=-2$。

> [!example] Centred correctly, without always being correct · 中心正确，不等于次次正确
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sixteen ordered pairs are equally likely. Their averages are $0,1,2,3,4,5,6$, occurring respectively $1,2,3,4,3,2,1$ times. Thus the [[期望|probability-weighted mean]] of the answers is
> $$E[T_2]=\frac{0\cdot1+1\cdot2+2\cdot3+3\cdot4+4\cdot3+5\cdot2+6\cdot1}{16}=3.$$
> This is the centre of the [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]], not a promise that the next sample average will be $3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 十六个有序样本对等可能。样本平均 $0,1,2,3,4,5,6$ 分别出现 $1,2,3,4,3,2,1$ 次，所以答案的[[期望|概率加权平均]]为 $3$。这是[[抽样分布]]的中心，不是对下一次样本平均必为 $3$ 的保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This calculation checks one population. To establish a claim about a whole model of unknown means, the same rule must have the right expectation for every population allowed by that model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个计算检查了一个总体。若声称某条规则在整个未知均值模型中无偏，就必须检查模型允许的每个总体，而不只是这个例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The model-wide definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对整个模型的定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\theta$ index the possible data-generating laws, and let $g(\theta)$ be the finite real quantity we want to estimate. It may be $\theta$ itself, or one function of a larger parameter. The [[统计量|statistic]] $T_n$ uses $n$ observations. The subscript in $E_\theta$ means “average using the sampling law at this fixed $\theta$”; it does not average over possible values of $\theta$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\theta$ 标记可能的数据生成规律，$g(\theta)$ 是要估计的有限实数目标：可以是 $\theta$ 本身，也可以是较大参数中的某个函数。[[统计量]] $T_n$ 使用 $n$ 个观察。$E_\theta$ 的下标表示“按这个固定 $\theta$ 对应的抽样规律取平均”，不是把各种 $\theta$ 再平均一次。

> [!definition] Unbiased for the stated target · 对指定目标无偏
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the sample size $n$ under discussion, $T_n$ is unbiased for $g(\theta)$ throughout the model when, for **every** allowed $\theta$,
> $$E_\theta[|T_n|]<\infty,\qquad E_\theta[T_n]=g(\theta).$$
> Equivalently, its [[估计量偏差|bias]] $E_\theta[T_n]-g(\theta)$ is zero everywhere in the model. For a sequence of estimators, state the sample sizes for which unbiasedness is claimed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对所讨论的样本量 $n$，若**每个**允许的 $\theta$ 都满足上式，则 $T_n$ 在整个模型中是 $g(\theta)$ 的无偏估计量。等价地，[[估计量偏差|偏差]] $E_\theta[T_n]-g(\theta)$ 在整个模型上为零。若讨论估计量序列，还须明确哪些样本量满足无偏性。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite-expectation condition matters: a finite number obtained from one sample does not imply that the rule has a finite sampling expectation. Without that expectation, the ordinary equality defining unbiasedness is unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限期望条件很重要：一次样本算出了有限数，并不说明这条规则的抽样期望有限。若该期望不存在，就不能使用通常定义无偏性的期望等式。

## Why the sample mean is unbiased
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么样本均值无偏*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose each $X_i$ has finite absolute expectation and the same mean $\mu$. By linearity of expectation,
$$E[\bar X_n]=E\!\left[\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right]=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nE[X_i]=\frac{n\mu}{n}=\mu.$$
Here $\bar X_n$ denotes the sample mean, and $1/n$ gives each observation equal weight. This proof works for every allowed $\mu$ and every positive integer $n$. It does not require independence or finite variance: those assumptions enter other questions, such as the spread or consistency of the average. The [[样本均值无偏性|sample-mean result]] also gives the coordinate-by-coordinate version for vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设每个 $X_i$ 的绝对期望有限，且均值都为 $\mu$。由期望线性性得到上式。$\bar X_n$ 表示样本均值，系数 $1/n$ 给每个观察相同权重。证明对每个允许的 $\mu$ 和每个正整数 $n$ 都成立，不需要独立性，也不需要有限方差；这些条件会出现在平均数的波动或一致性等其他问题中。[[样本均值无偏性]]还给出向量按坐标计算的版本。

> [!warning] One lucky parameter value is insufficient · 只在一个参数值碰巧正确还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The constant rule $T_n=7$ has expectation $7$ regardless of the data. It is correct in expectation when the target equals $7$, but it is not unbiased throughout a model whose unknown mean can also be $3$ or $10$. The target and the set of allowed models are part of the claim.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 常数规则 $T_n=7$ 无论数据怎样，期望都是 $7$。目标恰好为 $7$ 时它在期望上正确，但若模型的未知均值也允许是 $3$ 或 $10$，它就不是对整个模型无偏。目标和允许的模型范围，都是这项判断的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unbiasedness describes the centre, while the [[标准误含义|standard error]] describes spread when the variance is finite. An unbiased rule can still fluctuate widely. Nor does unbiasedness guarantee [[估计量一致性|consistency as more data become available]]: the separate [[无偏不推出一致|counterexample]] uses an unbiased rule that keeps only the first observation and never gains precision from the rest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无偏性描述中心；方差有限时，[[标准误含义|标准误]]描述分散程度。无偏规则仍可以波动很大。无偏也不保证[[估计量一致性|数据增多时的一致性]]：[[无偏不推出一致|独立反例卡]]中的规则虽然无偏，却一直只使用第一个观察，其余数据再多也不能让它更精确。
