# 无偏估计量在模型的每个参数值处都有等于目标的抽样期望

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An unbiased estimator has sampling expectation equal to its target at every parameter value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 对参数空间中的每个 $\theta$，若 $E_\theta[T_n]=\theta$，则 $T_n$ 是 $\theta$ 的无偏估计量。对一般目标 $g(\theta)$，右侧改为 $g(\theta)$。通常还要求这一性质在所讨论的各样本量成立。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An estimator is unbiased when its sampling expectation equals the target at every parameter value in the model. For a target function, use that function on the right. State the sample sizes for which the claim holds.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

[[样本均值无偏性]]来自期望线性性，不要求每次样本均值恰好等于总体均值。常数猜测 $T_n=7$ 仅在目标恰好为 7 时碰巧命中，不是整个未知均值模型中的无偏估计量。无偏不保证误差小，也[[无偏不推出一致|不保证一致]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[样本均值无偏性|unbiased sample mean]] need not equal its target in any particular sample. A constant guess of seven is not unbiased across a model of unknown means. Unbiasedness neither guarantees small error nor [[无偏不推出一致|implies consistency]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
