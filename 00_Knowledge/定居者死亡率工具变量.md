# 定居者死亡率工具变量只有在相关性外生性与排除限制成立时才识别制度效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The settler-mortality instrument identifies an institutional effect only if relevance, exogeneity, and exclusion hold*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 设计与条件
> Acemoglu、Johnson 与 Robinson（2001）在前欧洲殖民地样本中，用历史上欧洲人潜在定居者死亡率的对数 $Z$，作为 1985–1995 年平均征收风险保护指标 $I$ 的工具，估计 $I$ 对 1995 年 PPP 人均收入对数 $Y$ 的效应。这里的“当代”是相对于论文的历史样本而言，不是今天的制度或收入。识别链是：
> $$Z\rightarrow\text{定居可行性}\rightarrow\text{殖民策略}\rightarrow\text{历史制度}\rightarrow I\rightarrow Y.$$
> 这条链只有在 $Z$ 确实预测 $I$，$Z$ 与未观测收入决定因素外生，且 $Z$ 没有绕过 $I$ 影响 $Y$ 的其他通道时才成立。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In former European colonies, Acemoglu, Johnson, and Robinson (2001) use the log of potential European settler mortality $Z$ as an instrument for average protection against expropriation in 1985–1995, $I$, when estimating its effect on log PPP income per capita in 1995, $Y$. “Current” in this design is relative to the historical sample, not to today's institutions or income. The chain runs from mortality through settlement feasibility, colonial strategy, historical institutions, and the measured institution to income. Identification requires relevance, exogeneity to unobserved determinants of income, and no route from $Z$ to $Y$ outside $I$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一阶段显著只说明样本中有相关证据；单看 $p$ 值不能证明工具足够强，更不能证明排除限制。历史疾病环境若还通过 1995 年前后的疾病负担、人口结构、人力资本或其他殖民遗产影响收入，就可能破坏排除限制。这些通道不能仅靠第一阶段检验消除，需要历史论证、替代结果与稳健性检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A statistically significant first stage supplies evidence of relevance, but its $p$ value alone does not establish adequate strength and never establishes exclusion. If historical disease ecology affects 1995 income through contemporaneous disease burden, population composition, human capital, or other colonial legacies, exclusion may fail. These channels cannot be eliminated by a first-stage test alone and require historical argument, alternative outcomes, and robustness analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数据本身也是识别的一部分。Albouy（2012）质疑了死亡率来源、跨人群可比性和第一阶段稳健性；Acemoglu、Johnson 与 Robinson（2012）回应说，修正样本处理和异常值后结果仍稳健。这组争论意味着应报告数据构造、样本选择和弱识别敏感性，而不是仅引用原始点估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Data construction is part of identification. Albouy (2012) challenged the mortality sources, comparability across populations, and first-stage robustness; Acemoglu, Johnson, and Robinson (2012) replied that corrected sample treatment and outlier handling preserve the relationship. The dispute calls for transparent data construction, sample choices, and weak-identification sensitivity rather than reliance on the original point estimate alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外推边界同样重要：样本是前欧洲殖民地，处理变量是特定时期的征收风险保护指标，工具提供的是一类历史变异。若制度效应随国家或制度水平而异，2SLS 系数还取决于这类历史变异实际推动了哪些制度变化，以及估计采用的函数形式。即使设计内部有效，也不会自动给出“所有国家、所有制度、任何改革”的总体平均效应、通用系数或具体政策方案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
External validity remains limited: the sample consists of former European colonies, the treatment is a period-specific protection-against-expropriation measure, and the instrument supplies one kind of historical variation. With heterogeneous institutional effects, the 2SLS coefficient also depends on which institutional changes that variation induces and on the functional form imposed. Even an internally valid design does not automatically yield a population-average effect, a universal coefficient for every country, institution, or reform, or a ready-made policy prescription.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 定居者死亡率对 1985–1995 年制度指标的第一阶段很强，为什么还不足以证明工具有效？
>
> **答案：** 强第一阶段只支持相关性；还需辩护死亡率变异的外生性和“只经制度影响 1995 年收入”的排除限制，并检查数据和样本稳健性。

## 来源与核验

- [Acemoglu, Johnson & Robinson (2001), “The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development”](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.91.5.1369)：核对原始样本、变量年份、死亡率→定居→殖民制度→1985–1995 年制度指标的识别链和工具变量主张。
- [Albouy (2012), “The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development: An Empirical Investigation: Comment”](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.102.6.3059)：核对死亡率赋值、可比性、样本与弱识别的经验质疑。
- [Acemoglu, Johnson & Robinson (2012), “Reply”](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.102.6.3077)：核对原作者对数据删除、campaign 编码和异常值稳健性的回应；本卡不把争论任一方当成无条件定论。
