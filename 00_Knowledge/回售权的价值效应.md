# 其余合同与风险相同时持有人回售权使债券价值不低于无回售权债券

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With other contractual and risk conditions unchanged, a holder put makes a bond worth at least as much as its nonputtable counterpart.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加入[[可回售债券|回售权]]但保留其余债务权利时，持有人至少可以选择不行权，照旧持有；有利时再按约定回售。因此在一致的无套利模型中，回售选择价值 $U\ge0$，有：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a [[可回售债券|put right]] is added while other debt rights remain unchanged, the holder can always decline to exercise and retain the original position, exercising only when useful. In a consistent arbitrage-free model, the put value $U\ge0$ gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_{\mathrm{puttable}}=P_{\mathrm{straight}}+U\ge P_{\mathrm{straight}}.$$

设只有一个回售决策点，息票刚付完，发行人确定能即时按 100 支付，且无交易摩擦。继续价值为 92 时回售，价值为 108 时继续持有，所以该点价值为 $\max(V_{\mathrm{continue}},100)$。这说明利率上升压低继续价值时，回售权可以提供保护。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At one put decision immediately after a coupon, suppose payment of 100 is certain and immediate, with no trading frictions. The holder puts at continuation value 92 and keeps the bond at 108, so decision-point value is $\max(V_{\mathrm{continue}},100)$. The option can thus protect against declining continuation value when rates rise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

100 的下限依赖本例的当场行权与确定支付。尚未到回售日、合同条件未满足或发行人可能违约时，不能把未来回售承诺视作当前无风险现金下限；同样，比较两只债券必须固定息票、信用、期限等条件，不能只比较产品标签。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The floor of 100 depends on immediate exercise and certain payment in this example. Before the put date, when conditions are unmet, or when the issuer may default, a future repurchase promise is not a current risk-free cash floor. Comparing actual bonds also requires matched coupons, credit, maturity, and other terms, not just product labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
