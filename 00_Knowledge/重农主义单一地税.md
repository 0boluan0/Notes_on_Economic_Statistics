# 重农主义单一地税是以土地净产品为税基并取代多重间接税的直接税主张
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The physiocratic single land tax is a direct levy on net land product intended to replace multiple indirect taxes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> **impôt unique（单一税）** 是重农主义主张用一项直接落在土地净收入上的税，取代加在工资、商品流通和消费上的多重间接税。它的税基不是农业销售收入的全部，而是耕作者补回年度预付与原始预付的年度更新后，才交给所有者阶级的 [[重农主义净产品]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The **impôt unique** is the physiocratic proposal to replace multiple indirect taxes on wages, circulation, and consumption with one direct levy on net land revenue. Its base is not total agricultural sales revenue, but the [[重农主义净产品|net product]] paid to the proprietor class after cultivators have restored annual advances and the annual renewal of original advances.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 税的对象与税基
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Taxpayer and tax base*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这套理论中，耕作者的预付是恢复下一轮生产的条件，不应当作已经形成的可支配剩余。单一地税因而针对归所有者阶级的净土地收入，而不是直接抽走耕作者恢复生产所需的预付。为什么重农主义者认为这样的税制更少损害再生产，由 [[单一地税的重农推导]] 单独论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cultivators' advances are conditions for restoring the next production cycle, not already-formed disposable surplus. The single land tax therefore targets net land revenue accruing to the proprietor class rather than the advances cultivators need to reproduce output. The separate [[单一地税的重农推导|derivation note]] explains why physiocrats believed this design would do less damage to reproduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不是现代土地税的通用结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*It is not a universal conclusion about modern land taxes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个概念属于“只有农业产生净产品”的特定剩余理论。它不等于今天所有土地税、地价税或房产税，也不能不经新的税基、归宿和行为反应分析，就直接当成现代税收建议。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The concept belongs to a specific surplus theory in which agriculture alone generates net product. It is not identical to every modern land, land-value, or property tax, and it cannot become a current tax recommendation without new analysis of the base, incidence, and behavioural responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一笔农业销售收入中，哪一部分才是重农主义单一地税的税基？
>
> **答案：** 先补回年度预付和原始预付的年度更新；只有剩下、归所有者阶级的土地净收入才是这项税的税基。
