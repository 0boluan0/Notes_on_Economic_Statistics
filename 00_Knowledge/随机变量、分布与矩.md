# 随机变量、分布与矩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Random variables, distributions, and moments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把随机结果映射成数值，并用分布、期望、方差和协方差概括其可能取值与不确定性。
> **具体锚点：** 一次投硬币的结果是正面或反面；令正面为 1、反面为 0 后，Bernoulli 随机变量既保留概率，也能计算均值和方差。
> **核心难点：** pmf、density 与 probability 不是同一个对象；期望不必是可取值，协方差只描述线性共同变化。
> **为什么重要：** 统计量、回归误差、随机过程状态和风险损失都首先是随机变量。
> **继续：** 获得信息后的平均见 [[条件期望与独立性]]；大量独立重复的极限见 [[大数定律与中心极限定理]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It maps random outcomes to numbers and uses distributions, expectations, variances, and covariances to summarise possible values and uncertainty.
> **Concrete anchor:** A coin flip produces heads or tails. Coding heads as 1 and tails as 0 creates a Bernoulli random variable whose probability, mean, and variance can all be calculated.
> **Central difficulty:** A pmf, a density, and a probability are not the same object. An expectation need not be a possible outcome, and covariance captures only linear co-movement.
> **Why it matters:** Statistics, regression errors, states of stochastic processes, and financial losses are all modelled first as random variables.
> **Continue with:** For averages conditional on information, see [[条件期望与独立性|conditional expectation and independence]]. For limits of many independent repetitions, see [[大数定律与中心极限定理|laws of large numbers and central limit theorems]].

## 随机变量与分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Random variables and distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机变量是从样本空间到数值的函数。离散变量用 pmf，连续变量用 density，二者都由 CDF 统一。连续分布在单点概率为 0，密度值本身不是概率；区间概率是密度积分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A random variable is a function from the sample space to numerical values. Discrete variables are described by a pmf and continuous variables by a density; both are unified by the CDF. A continuous distribution assigns probability zero to any single point, and a density value is not itself a probability; interval probabilities are integrals of the density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CDF $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$ 必须单调不减、右连续，并在两端趋向 0 与 1。离散情形中跳跃大小就是点概率；绝对连续情形中，若可微则 $f_X(x)=F_X'(x)$。从 CDF 出发可避免把离散和连续公式混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The CDF $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$ must be non-decreasing and right-continuous, with limits zero and one at the two ends. In a discrete distribution, each jump equals a point probability. In an absolutely continuous distribution, where differentiable, $f_X(x)=F_X'(x)$. Starting from the CDF helps prevent formulas for discrete and continuous cases from being mixed incorrectly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 期望、方差与协方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Expectation, variance, and covariance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E[X]$ 是长期平均或分布中心，线性性不要求独立。$\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[(X-EX)^2]$ 衡量平方偏离。协方差衡量线性共同变化；$\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)$ 只有在协方差为 0 时才等于方差和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$E[X]$ is a long-run average or a centre of the distribution, and linearity of expectation does not require independence. $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[(X-EX)^2]$ measures squared deviation. Covariance measures linear co-movement; $\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)$ equals the sum of the variances only when their covariance is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算时优先使用 $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-E[X]^2$，但解释仍应回到围绕均值的平方偏离。线性变换满足 $E[aX+b]=aE[X]+b$ 与 $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(X)$；常数平移不增加不确定性，尺度放大会平方地放大方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For computation, $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-E[X]^2$ is often convenient, but interpretation should return to squared deviations around the mean. Linear transformations satisfy $E[aX+b]=aE[X]+b$ and $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(X)$. Adding a constant creates no uncertainty, whereas rescaling magnifies variance quadratically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 变量变换与分布传播
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Transformations and propagation of distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Y=g(X)$，先问 $g$ 是否一一对应和单调。离散情形可把所有映到同一 y 的点概率相加；连续且单调时使用 $f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))\left|d g^{-1}(y)/dy\right|$。绝对值 Jacobian 是局部长度缩放，漏掉它会使密度积分不再为 1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $Y=g(X)$, first ask whether $g$ is one-to-one and monotone. In a discrete problem, add the probabilities of all $x$ values mapped to the same $y$. In a continuous monotone case, use $f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))\left|d g^{-1}(y)/dy\right|$. The absolute Jacobian is the local change of scale; omitting it prevents the transformed density from integrating to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：Bernoulli 与 Binomial
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: Bernoulli and Binomial variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X\sim Bernoulli(p)$，则 $P(X=1)=p$、$P(X=0)=1-p$，所以 $E[X]=p$、$\operatorname{Var}(X)=p(1-p)$。若 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ 独立同分布且 $S_n=\sum_iX_i$，则 $S_n\sim Binomial(n,p)$，$E[S_n]=np$、$\operatorname{Var}(S_n)=np(1-p)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X\sim Bernoulli(p)$, then $P(X=1)=p$ and $P(X=0)=1-p$, so $E[X]=p$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=p(1-p)$. If $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are independent and identically distributed and $S_n=\sum_iX_i$, then $S_n\sim Binomial(n,p)$, with $E[S_n]=np$ and $\operatorname{Var}(S_n)=np(1-p)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里期望相加不需要独立；方差化为方差和却使用了独立带来的零协方差。若是不放回抽样，指标变量仍可给期望，但协方差通常为负，Binomial 分布也不再适用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding expectations here does not require independence. Reducing the variance to a sum of variances does use the zero covariances implied by independence. Under sampling without replacement, indicator variables still give the expectation, but their covariances are generally negative and the Binomial distribution no longer applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 误区与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Misconceptions and failure diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最常见的错误包括：把 density 高度当区间概率；把期望当最可能结果；看到协方差为零就断言独立；以及在矩不存在时仍机械计算方差。诊断顺序是先确认支持集，再检查总概率，随后检查所需矩是否有限，最后才使用近似或极限定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common errors include treating density height as an interval probability, treating an expectation as the most likely outcome, inferring independence from zero covariance, and mechanically calculating variance when the required moments do not exist. A reliable diagnostic order is to identify the support, verify total probability, check that the required moments are finite, and only then invoke approximations or limit theorems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 连续随机变量在一点的 density 很高，是否表示该点概率很大？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If a continuous random variable has a high density at one point, does that point have high probability?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不表示。单点概率仍为 0；density 只有与一个小区间的宽度结合并积分后才形成概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. The probability of a single point is still zero. A density becomes a probability only after being integrated over an interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)$ 中没有 b？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does $b$ not appear in $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 加上常数只平移所有结果，不改变它们围绕各自均值的距离；乘以 a 才会把偏离放大为 a 倍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Adding a constant shifts every outcome equally and does not change distances from the new mean. Multiplication by $a$ scales every deviation by $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 不相关为什么通常不能推出独立？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does lack of correlation generally not imply independence?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 协方差只检查线性共同变化，变量仍可能有确定的非线性关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Covariance detects only linear co-movement; the variables may still have a deterministic nonlinear relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
