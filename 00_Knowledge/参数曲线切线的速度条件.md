# Tangents from a velocity vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*参数曲线切线的速度条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A moving point's instantaneous direction can determine a tangent without first eliminating the parameter. For the [[参数曲线|parametric curve]] $\gamma(t)=(t,t^2)$ for $t\in\mathbb R$, at $t=1$ the position is $(1,1)$ and the velocity is $(1,2)$. A line through that position in that direction is $(1,1)+s(1,2)$, where $s$ is any real number. In Cartesian form it is $y-1=2(x-1)$, the familiar tangent to the parabola.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不必先消去参数，运动点的瞬时方向就能决定切线。对[[参数曲线]] $\gamma(t)=(t,t^2)$（$t\in\mathbb R$），$t=1$ 时位置是 $(1,1)$，速度是 $(1,2)$。经过该点、沿这个方向的直线是 $(1,1)+s(1,2)$，其中 $s$ 为任意实数。写成直角坐标方程就是 $y-1=2(x-1)$，即熟悉的抛物线切线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A nonzero velocity supplies a direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*非零速度向量提供方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $t_0$ be an interior point of the parameter interval. Assume $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$ is **$C^1$ on an open interval around $t_0$**: both coordinate [[导数|derivatives]] exist and are continuous there. Write $P=\gamma(t_0)$ for the position and $w=\gamma'(t_0)=(x'(t_0),y'(t_0))$ for the velocity. A parameter value with $w\ne(0,0)$ is called regular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $t_0$ 是参数区间内点。假设 $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$ 在 $t_0$ 附近的某个开区间上为 **$C^1$**，即两个坐标的[[导数]]都存在且连续。记位置为 $P=\gamma(t_0)$，速度为 $w=\gamma'(t_0)=(x'(t_0),y'(t_0))$。若 $w\ne(0,0)$，称该参数值为正则的。

> [!important] Tangent line and slope · 切线与斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these assumptions, the tangent to this local branch is the line $\{P+sw:s\in\mathbb R\}$. If $x'(t_0)\ne0$, its slope is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述条件下，这一局部分支的切线是 $\{P+sw:s\in\mathbb R\}$。若 $x'(t_0)\ne0$，斜率为
>
> $$\frac{dy}{dx}\bigg|_{t=t_0}=\frac{y'(t_0)}{x'(t_0)}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $x'(t_0)=0$ and $y'(t_0)\ne0$, the line is vertical: $x=x(t_0)$. A finite slope is then unavailable, but the tangent exists. If $y'(t_0)=0$ and $x'(t_0)\ne0$, it is horizontal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $x'(t_0)=0$、$y'(t_0)\ne0$，切线竖直，方程为 $x=x(t_0)$；此时没有有限斜率，但切线存在。若 $y'(t_0)=0$、$x'(t_0)\ne0$，切线水平。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the geometric reason, let $h\ne0$ be a small parameter increment. The derivative definition gives the displacement quotient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几何理由来自导数定义。令 $h\ne0$ 是一个很小的参数增量，则位移差商满足

$$\frac{\gamma(t_0+h)-P}{h}\longrightarrow w.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $w$ is nonzero, this quotient is nonzero for all sufficiently small $h$. Its direction approaches that of $w$. The chord from $P$ to $\gamma(t_0+h)$ spans the same line as its quotient by $h$; a negative $h$ reverses the arrow, not the line. Thus the secant lines approach the line through $P$ in direction $w$. This argument already uses only differentiability at $t_0$ and a nonzero derivative; the stated $C^1$ assumptions also ensure the convenient local graph used next.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $w$ 非零，当 $h$ 足够小时，这个差商也非零，其方向趋近 $w$ 的方向。从 $P$ 到 $\gamma(t_0+h)$ 的弦与除以 $h$ 后的向量张成同一直线；负的 $h$ 只反转箭头，不改变直线。因此割线趋近经过 $P$、方向为 $w$ 的直线。这一步实际上只需要 $t_0$ 处可导且导数非零；本卡所述 $C^1$ 条件还保证下一步方便使用的局部函数图像。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the slope quotient is legitimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么可以使用斜率商*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x'(t_0)\ne0$, continuity of $x'$ keeps its sign fixed near $t_0$. The [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] therefore makes $x(t)$ strictly monotone on a smaller interval. It has a differentiable local inverse $t=q(x)$ by the [[反函数导数|inverse-derivative theorem]], with $q'(x(t_0))=1/x'(t_0)$. Hence this branch can be written $Y(x)=y(q(x))$, and the [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $x'(t_0)\ne0$，$x'$ 的连续性保证它在附近保持同一符号。由[[拉格朗日中值定理]]，$x(t)$ 在一个较小区间内严格单调。根据[[反函数导数]]定理，它有可导的局部反函数 $t=q(x)$，且 $q'(x(t_0))=1/x'(t_0)$。因此这一分支可以写成 $Y(x)=y(q(x))$，再用[[链式法则]]得到

$$Y'(x(t_0))=y'(t_0)q'(x(t_0))=\frac{y'(t_0)}{x'(t_0)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This verifies the existence of the local graph before dividing. For $\gamma(t)=(t^2,t)$ at $t=0$, instead use the nonzero vertical component: the velocity is $(0,1)$, so the tangent is $x=0$. The graph $x=y^2$ makes the same geometry visible without an undefined division by $x'(0)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这样先确认局部函数图像存在，再做除法。对 $\gamma(t)=(t^2,t)$ 的 $t=0$，则使用非零纵向分量：速度是 $(0,1)$，切线为 $x=0$。图像 $x=y^2$ 也直接显示这个几何事实，无须除以零的 $x'(0)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Zero velocity leaves the question open
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零速度不能直接决定有无切线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both coordinate derivatives vanish, the first-order vector supplies no direction. A tangent can still exist. For $\gamma(t)=(t^2,t^4)$ at zero, every nonzero nearby point has $y/x=t^2\to0$. The trace is $y=x^2$ with $x\ge0$, and the secant lines approach the horizontal line $y=0$, despite $\gamma'(0)=(0,0)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两个坐标导数都为零，一阶向量没有提供方向，但切线仍可能存在。对 $\gamma(t)=(t^2,t^4)$ 的零点，附近每个非零点都满足 $y/x=t^2\to0$。轨迹为 $x\ge0$ 部分的 $y=x^2$；虽然 $\gamma'(0)=(0,0)$，割线仍趋近水平线 $y=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There need not be one tangent, however. For $\eta(t)=(t^2,t|t|)$, both derivatives at zero are again zero. Its second coordinate has derivative $2|t|$, including value zero at the origin, so this curve is $C^1$. For $t>0$ the secant slope is $1$; for $t<0$ it is $-1$. These are different lines, so the two-sided branch has no single limiting tangent. Zero speed alone cannot distinguish these two examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但也可能没有同一条切线。例如 $\eta(t)=(t^2,t|t|)$ 在零点的两个导数仍为零；第二坐标的导数是 $2|t|$，在原点取零，所以曲线为 $C^1$。当 $t>0$ 时割线斜率为 $1$，当 $t<0$ 时为 $-1$；两条直线不同，因此这个双侧分支没有唯一的极限切线。仅知道零速，无法区分这两个例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an endpoint, one must explicitly use a one-sided secant/derivative or specify a differentiable extension; the interior theorem does not silently create values outside the parameter interval. At a self-intersection, apply the criterion separately at each parameter value: distinct branches through the same geometric point can have different tangents. For length rather than tangents, [[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|the arc-length formula]] permits zero velocity under its own regularity conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在端点，必须明确使用单侧割线或单侧导数，或者给出可导延伸；内点定理不会自动补出参数区间外的取值。若曲线自交，应对每一个参数值分别使用判据：经过同一几何点的不同分支可能有不同切线。若问题是求长度，[[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|弧长公式]]在它自己的正则条件下允许零速度。

> [!question]- What should be checked when x′ vanishes? · 横向速度为零时查什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First check $y'$. If it is nonzero, the tangent is vertical. If both components vanish, inspect secants, suitable higher-order leading terms or a valid one-sided reparametrization. The symbol $0/0$ gives no tangent information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先查 $y'$；若它非零，切线竖直。若两个分量都为零，就另查割线、适用的高阶首项或合法的单侧重参数化。符号 $0/0$ 不能提供切线信息。
