# 价值链功能升级是通过取得设计或营销等新职能改变链上的分工位置

价值链功能升级是企业在价值链中取得设计、营销等新职能，从而改变自身承担的活动组合和分工位置。判断对象是实际承担了什么职能，而不是出口额是否增长，或企业是否已经参与[[全球价值链]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Functional upgrading occurs when a firm acquires new functions in a value chain, such as design or marketing, changing its activities and position in the division of work. The question is which functions it actually performs, not whether exports have grown or whether it already participates in a [[全球价值链|global value chain]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造对比：同一家服装厂在原有代工环节减少次品和工时，是流程改进；转向技术更复杂的产品，是产品升级；开始负责产品设计、寻找客户和营销，则涉及功能升级。三种变化可以同时发生，但“同一件事做得更有效率”并不等于“取得新的职能”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a garment factory, reducing defects and hours within its existing assembly role is process upgrading; moving into more sophisticated products is product upgrading; taking responsibility for design, customer acquisition and marketing involves functional upgrading. These changes can coexist, but performing an existing activity more efficiently is not the same as acquiring a new function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新职能需要相应能力，也受客户关系和链上治理安排影响。WDR 2020的柬埔寨服装业案例区分本地组装、境外制造企业总部承担的采购与客户联络，以及主导品牌掌握的创意设计和营销：就业和出口扩张本身不能证明本地企业已经取得这些新职能。功能升级也不是利润、工资或社会福利必然提高的同义词；这些结果仍须单独观察。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
New functions require capabilities and are shaped by buyer relationships and chain governance. WDR 2020's Cambodian garment example distinguishes local assembly, sourcing and customer acquisition at foreign manufacturing headquarters, and creative design and marketing controlled by lead brands. Employment and export growth do not establish that local firms have acquired these new functions. Functional upgrading is also not synonymous with guaranteed increases in profits, wages or social welfare; these outcomes need separate evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
