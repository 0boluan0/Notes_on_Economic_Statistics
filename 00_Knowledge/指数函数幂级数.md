# The exponential power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指数函数幂级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponential power series computes the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] $e^x$ by adding powers of $x$ with rapidly shrinking coefficients. For instance, at $x=1$ it turns the number $e$ into the limit of the ordinary sums $1$, $1+1$, $1+1+1/2$, and so on. A finite sum gives an approximation; a bound on the omitted tail tells us how accurate it is.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数函数幂级数用 $x$ 的各次幂乘上迅速缩小的系数，再相加，计算[[自然指数函数]] $e^x$。例如取 $x=1$，数 $e$ 就是 $1$、$1+1$、$1+1+1/2$ 等普通有限和的极限。截取有限项得到近似，控制剩下的尾和则能说明近似有多准。

> [!important] One expansion for every real input / 对每个实数都成立的展开
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every real $x$, the following series converges absolutely and equals $e^x$. Its [[幂级数收敛半径|radius of convergence]] is infinite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个实数 $x$，下面的级数都绝对收敛且等于 $e^x$，[[幂级数收敛半径]]为无穷。

$$e^x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!}=1+x+\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^3}{3!}+\cdots.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integer $n$ is the power index. The factorial $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$ for $n\ge1$, with $0!=1$, gives $2!=2$, $3!=6$, and $4!=24$. Thus the coefficient of $x^n$ is $1/n!$. The zero-degree term is the constant $1$, including at $x=0$. A [[Taylor级数|Taylor series]] centred at zero is also called a Maclaurin series; the formula above is this particular function's Maclaurin expansion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整数 $n$ 表示幂次。阶乘在 $n\ge1$ 时为 $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$，并约定 $0!=1$，所以 $2!=2$、$3!=6$、$4!=24$。因此 $x^n$ 的系数是 $1/n!$。零次项是常数 $1$，在 $x=0$ 处也如此。以零为中心的 [[Taylor级数]]又叫 Maclaurin 级数，上式就是指数函数的这一展开。

## Why these coefficients give the exponential
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这些系数为什么给出指数函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By [[指数函数求导|exponential differentiation]], every derivative of $e^x$ is again $e^x$, so its value at zero is always $1$. The Taylor coefficient is the derivative value divided by its order factorial, giving $1/n!$. This determines a candidate series. Two separate checks make it a representation of the function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[指数函数求导]]，$e^x$ 的每阶导数仍为 $e^x$，在零处的值都为 $1$。Taylor 系数等于该阶导数值除以对应阶乘，于是得到 $1/n!$。这先确定了候选级数；还要分别检查它有和，以及这个和就是原函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix $x\ne0$. The ratio of successive term magnitudes is $|x|/(n+1)\to0$, so the [[级数比值判别|ratio test]] proves absolute convergence. At $x=0$, the sum is directly $1+0+0+\cdots$; no ratio $0/0$ is needed. This establishes convergence for every real input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定 $x\ne0$。相邻项绝对值之比为 $|x|/(n+1)\to0$，所以[[级数比值判别]]保证绝对收敛。在 $x=0$ 处，直接得到 $1+0+0+\cdots$，不需要计算 $0/0$。这样就证明了每个实数输入处都收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next write $P_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^N x^n/n!$. On the segment joining $0$ to the fixed $x$, every derivative of $e^t$ has magnitude at most $e^{|x|}$. The [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder bound]] therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再记 $P_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^N x^n/n!$。在连接 $0$ 与固定输入 $x$ 的线段上，$e^t$ 的各阶导数绝对值都不超过 $e^{|x|}$。因此由 [[Taylor余项]]的上界，

$$|e^x-P_N(x)|\le e^{|x|}\frac{|x|^{N+1}}{(N+1)!}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last factor tends to zero because it is a term of the convergent magnitude series just established. The factor $e^{|x|}$ is fixed while $N$ grows. This proves [[Taylor级数等于函数|equality with the function]], for negative inputs as well as positive ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后的阶乘分式趋零，因为它是刚刚已证明收敛的绝对值级数的通项；令 $N$ 增大时，$e^{|x|}$ 保持固定。于是证明了 [[Taylor级数等于函数|级数确实等于原函数]]，负输入也包括在内。

## Computing e with an explicit error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用明确误差计算 e*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=1$, retaining terms through $n=5$ gives six terms and $P_5(1)=163/60$. To bound the error without already knowing $e$, observe that the first omitted term is $1/6!=1/720$. Every subsequent magnitude is at most $1/7$ times its predecessor. Comparing the tail with a [[几何级数|geometric series]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $x=1$，保留到 $n=5$，共六项，得到 $P_5(1)=163/60$。若不想预先知道 $e$ 的大小就估计误差，可以注意首个遗漏项是 $1/6!=1/720$；后面每项至多是前一项的 $1/7$。把尾部与[[几何级数]]比较，得到

$$0<e-\frac{163}{60}\le\frac{1/720}{1-1/7}=\frac7{4320}<0.001621.$$

> [!warning] Infinite radius does not make a fixed truncation uniformly accurate / 无限半径不保证固定截断处处准确
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The statement holds for each fixed real $x$ as the number of terms grows. It does not say that six terms are accurate for every real $x$. For another base $b>0$, first use $b^x=e^{x\ln b}$, where $\ln$ denotes the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], so the terms become $(x\ln b)^n/n!$. More general replacements, such as $x=-t^2$, use [[幂级数代入|power-series substitution]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 结论是：固定任意实数 $x$，增加项数就能逼近它的函数值；并不是六项能在整个实轴上都给出准确近似。若底数为其他 $b>0$，先用 $b^x=e^{x\ln b}$，其中 $\ln$ 表示[[自然对数]]，对应项就变为 $(x\ln b)^n/n!$。例如 $x=-t^2$ 这样的替换见[[幂级数代入]]。
