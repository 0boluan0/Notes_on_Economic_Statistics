# 指数函数在零点的幂级数对所有实数收敛到函数本身
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The exponential power series at zero represents the function for every real input*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对所有 $x\in\mathbb R$，
$$
e^x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!}
=1+x+\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^3}{3!}+\cdots,
\qquad R=\infty.
$$
系数由 $(e^x)^{(n)}|_{x=0}=1$ 得到；当 $x\ne0$ 时，各项绝对值比值为 $|x|/(n+1)\to0$，保证绝对收敛。$x=0$ 时级数直接成为 $1+0+0+\cdots$，同样绝对收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every derivative of $e^x$ equals one at zero, giving the displayed coefficients. For $x\ne0$, the absolute term ratio $|x|/(n+1)$ tends to zero. At $x=0$, the series is directly $1+0+0+\cdots$. Together these prove absolute convergence everywhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还需确认和是 $e^x$：固定 $x$，连接 $0$ 与 $x$ 的区间上，各阶导数都不超过 $e^{|x|}$。由 [[Taylor余项]]，
$$
|R_{N,0}(x)|\le e^{|x|}\frac{|x|^{N+1}}{(N+1)!}\longrightarrow0.
$$
所以 [[Taylor级数等于函数|余项条件]] 成立。特别取 $x=1$，得到 $e=1+1+1/2!+1/3!+\cdots$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the segment from zero to a fixed $x$, every derivative is bounded by $e^{|x|}$. The [[Taylor余项|Taylor bound]] therefore tends to zero, which proves [[Taylor级数等于函数|equality with the function]]. At $x=1$, both the constant and linear terms contribute one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
