# 在 Smith 的分析中收入中节省并重新用于生产的部分扩大下一轮生产的预付资源

Smith 解释资本积累时，区分了取得收入和怎样使用收入：生产创造出可以节省的东西，但只有把一部分收入保留下来，并由自己或他人投入生产，才会扩大支持以后生产的资源。这里的关键是“节省后用于生产”，不只是收入比以前多。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith distinguishes earning income from using it. Production supplies what can be saved, but the resources supporting future production expand only when part of that income is retained and employed productively, either by its owner or by someone else. The key is saving followed by productive use, not simply a rise in income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也解释了他为什么说节省的部分仍然会被消费。原收入所有者不再把它用于自己的消费，而是用它支持 [[Smith生产性劳动|生产性劳动者]]，或贷给别人作同样用途。劳动者仍要吃饭、穿衣，生产仍要使用材料；不同的是，这些资源进入了能够留下可出售产品、补回预付并取得利润的过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This explains why he says that what is saved is still consumed. The original recipient forgoes personal consumption and uses the amount to support [[Smith生产性劳动|productive workers]], or lends it to another person for that purpose. Workers still eat and wear clothing, and production still uses materials. The difference is that these resources enter a process leaving a vendible product capable of replacing advances with a profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个构造例：经营者已经从本期销售收入中留足维持原有生产所需的预付，余下100个货币单位作为本期收入。如果他个人消费80，把其余20用于新增生产的材料和劳动者生活支持，那么新增预付是20。不能把全部销售收入都算成新增资本，因为其中有一部分只是补回原先投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a constructed example. After reserving enough of this period's sales to maintain the existing productive advances, a proprietor has an income of 100 monetary units. If personal consumption is 80 and the remaining 20 finances materials and support for workers in additional production, the extra advance is 20. Total sales cannot be counted as newly accumulated capital because part merely replaces earlier inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新增资源如何支持更细的工作安排，见 [[分工的预付条件]]：劳动者可以在产品完成并售出前继续专门生产，也可以配备更多材料和更合适的工具。这就把积累和 [[分工增产机制|生产率改善]] 连起来了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[分工的预付条件|Advance provision for division of labour]] explains how additional resources support specialised production before completion and sale, as well as more materials and suitable tools. This connects accumulation to [[分工增产机制|improvements in productivity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这段论证有明确前提。Smith 在关键段落中假定，节省的部分会立即由本人或他人用作资本，以取得利润。如果把钱长期闲置，或者没有发生相应的生产投入，就不能直接套用同一过程。这里讲的是 Smith 的再生产机制，并没有仅凭“储蓄增加”便证明任何经济状态下实际投资和总产出都必然增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The argument has an explicit premise. In the crucial passage, Smith treats the saved portion as immediately employed as capital for profit by its owner or someone else. Money left idle, or saving without corresponding productive use, does not follow that process. This is Smith's mechanism of reproduction, not a proof that higher saving necessarily raises real investment and aggregate output in every economic situation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Smith，[《国富论》第二篇第三章](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=18)，第13–19段，重点第15–18段：节省收入、自用或贷出、生产性劳动，以及节省部分仍被消费且立即用于资本的前提；第4–6段支持补回资本与收入用途的区分。100、80、20为构造例，新增预付按100−80计算。
- [第二篇导论](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=15)，第2–4段：预先资源支持分工与工具改进。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Book II, Chapter III, paragraphs 13–19, especially 15–18, supply saving, lending or direct employment, and the premise of immediate productive use; paragraphs 4–6 distinguish replacement from revenue and its uses. The numbers are constructed, with the additional advance calculated as 100 minus 80. Paragraphs 2–4 of the introduction to Book II connect advance provision to division of labour and equipment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
