# 效用可能性边界由可行且不存在无人受损而至少一人获益的替代效用向量组成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The utility possibility frontier contains feasible utility vectors with no alternative that benefits someone without harming anyone*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效用可能性边界（utility possibility frontier，本卡指强 Pareto 边界）是[[效用可能集]]中不被 Pareto 支配的效用向量集合。“被支配”指存在同一可行替代，使每个人至少一样好，而且至少一人严格更好。因此，这里沿用[[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto 有效]]的标准，而不是把几何外轮廓全部称为有效边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The utility possibility frontier here means the strong Pareto frontier: utility vectors in the [[效用可能集|utility possibility set]] that are not Pareto dominated. Domination requires one feasible alternative that leaves everyone at least as well off and makes someone strictly better off. This uses the existing [[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto-efficiency criterion]], not the entire geometric boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(\mathcal U)=\{v\in\mathcal U:\nexists w\in\mathcal U,
\ w_i\ge v_i\ \forall i,\quad w_j>v_j\ \text{for some }j\}.
$$

判断时先确认候选点和替代点属于同一个可行效用集，再逐人比较。例如，若 $(2,1)$ 与 $(2,2)$ 均可行，后者就支配前者，故 $(2,1)$ 不属于本卡的前沿。弱边界使用不同的不等号；完整区别及“某个人已达最高效用却仍非强有效”的例子，见[[强弱Pareto边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First establish that the candidate and alternative belong to the same feasible utility set, then compare utilities person by person. If both $(2,1)$ and $(2,2)$ are feasible, the latter dominates the former, so $(2,1)$ is not on this frontier. The weak frontier uses different inequalities; see [[强弱Pareto边界|strong versus weak Pareto frontiers]] for the distinction and an example where one person's utility is already maximal without strong efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
