# Direct comparison test for series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*级数直接比较判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose we add the positive amounts $1/(n^2+1)$ for $n=1,2,3,\ldots$. We may not know their exact total, but each amount is no larger than $1/n^2$. The [[p级数判别|p-series test]] says that the latter amounts have a finite total. Direct comparison turns this observation into a proof that the smaller amounts also have a finite total. Its other use is to prove divergence: amounts at least as large as those in a divergent nonnegative series cannot have a finite total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设我们依次加上 $n=1,2,3,\ldots$ 对应的正数 $1/(n^2+1)$。即使不知道精确总和，也能看到每一项都不超过 $1/n^2$；[[p级数判别]]告诉我们，后者的总和有限。直接比较把这一观察变成证明：较小的非负项也有有限总和。它的另一用途是证明发散：若各项至少与某个发散非负级数的项一样大，总和就不可能有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[无穷级数|infinite series]] $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ is defined through its finite partial sums $S_N=a_1+\cdots+a_N$. Convergence means that these totals approach a finite real number as the number of terms $N$ increases. We will compare such totals, not just ask whether individual terms get small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无穷级数]] $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ 通过有限部分和 $S_N=a_1+\cdots+a_N$ 定义。收敛是指随着项数 $N$ 增加，这些累计总量趋于一个有限实数。下面比较的正是这些总量，而不只是看单项是否变小。

> [!important] A convergent upper bound or a divergent lower bound · 收敛上界或发散下界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let every $a_n,b_n$ be a finite real number. Suppose some positive integer $N_0$ satisfies $0\le a_n\le b_n$ for every $n\ge N_0$. Then:
> - If $\sum b_n$ converges, $\sum a_n$ converges.
> - If $\sum a_n$ diverges, $\sum b_n$ diverges to $+\infty$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设所有 $a_n,b_n$ 都是已定义的有限实数，并且存在正整数 $N_0$，使每个 $n\ge N_0$ 都满足 $0\le a_n\le b_n$。那么：
> - 若较大的级数 $\sum b_n$ 收敛，较小的级数 $\sum a_n$ 也收敛。
> - 若较小的级数 $\sum a_n$ 发散，较大的级数 $\sum b_n$ 也趋于 $+\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Eventually” means from one fixed index $N_0$ onward, with no later exceptions. The individual terms need not decrease. A finite prefix may even contain negative terms: after $N\ge N_0$, the full partial sum is a fixed finite prefix plus the tail sum, so it converges exactly when that tail does. This does not repair an undefined term such as $1/0$; the original series must first have a valid starting index.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“最终成立”是指从某个固定索引 $N_0$ 起，之后没有例外；不要求单项本身递减。有限个前项甚至可以为负：当 $N\ge N_0$，完整部分和等于一个固定的有限前缀加上尾和，因此二者同时收敛或发散。但这不能修复 $1/0$ 这样的未定义项；原级数首先要有合法的起点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why comparing terms controls the total
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逐项比较为何能控制总量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start where the inequalities hold. For each integer $N\ge N_0$, define the finite tail sums $A_N$ and $B_N$ by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从不等式成立的位置开始。对每个整数 $N\ge N_0$，定义有限尾和 $A_N,B_N$：

$$A_N=\sum_{n=N_0}^{N}a_n,\qquad B_N=\sum_{n=N_0}^{N}b_n,\qquad 0\le A_N\le B_N.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding a nonnegative term never decreases either tail sum. If $\sum b_n$ converges, the increasing totals $B_N$ have a finite upper bound, and so do $A_N$. To see why $A_N$ then converges, let $s$ be the [[上确界|supremum]], or least upper bound, of all its values; the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property of the real numbers]] guarantees that this finite $s$ exists. For any tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, some $A_K$ exceeds $s-\varepsilon$. Every later $A_N$ lies between $A_K$ and $s$, so $0\le s-A_N<\varepsilon$. This proves convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每加一项非负数，尾和都不会减少。如果 $\sum b_n$ 收敛，递增的累计量 $B_N$ 有有限上界，$A_N$ 因而也有有限上界。为什么这能保证 $A_N$ 收敛？取所有 $A_N$ 的[[上确界]]，即最小上界 $s$；[[实数的确界性质]]保证这个有限的 $s$ 存在。任给容差 $\varepsilon>0$，总有某个 $A_K>s-\varepsilon$。之后每个 $A_N$ 都介于 $A_K$ 与 $s$ 之间，因此 $0\le s-A_N<\varepsilon$，这就证明了收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, if the nonnegative tail $\sum a_n$ diverges, its increasing partial sums cannot be bounded, by the argument just given. Once $A_N$ exceeds any chosen level, it stays above that level; $B_N\ge A_N$ therefore does too. Both tail sums tend to $+\infty$. Adding their finite prefixes cannot change these conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，如果非负尾部 $\sum a_n$ 发散，由刚才的论证，它的递增部分和就不可能有界。$A_N$ 一旦超过任意指定高度，之后就一直超过；由于 $B_N\ge A_N$，$B_N$ 也如此。因此两者的尾和都趋于 $+\infty$，加回有限前缀不会改变结论。

## Choosing the direction in an example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在例子中选择比较方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example, the larger denominator makes the positive fraction smaller:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头例子中，正分数的分母增大，数值就减小：

$$0<\frac1{n^2+1}\le\frac1{n^2}\quad(n\ge1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The upper series converges because its exponent is $p=2>1$. Thus $\sum 1/(n^2+1)$ converges, without needing its exact sum. For a divergence example, $2n+1\le3n$ for $n\ge1$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上界级数的指数 $p=2>1$，所以收敛；于是 $\sum 1/(n^2+1)$ 也收敛，无须求出其精确和。要证明发散，可以用 $n\ge1$ 时的 $2n+1\le3n$，得到

$$\frac1{2n+1}\ge\frac1{3n}=\frac13\frac1n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lower series is one third of the divergent harmonic series, the $p=1$ case of the p-series test. Multiplying every finite partial sum by $1/3$ still gives unbounded totals. Hence $\sum 1/(2n+1)$ diverges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下界级数是调和级数的三分之一，即 p 级数判别中发散的 $p=1$ 情形。把每个有限部分和乘 $1/3$，累计总量仍然无界，因此 $\sum 1/(2n+1)$ 发散。

> [!warning] The reversed comparison gives no verdict · 反向比较不能据此定性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both $1/n^2$ and $1/(2n)$ are at most $1/n$, yet the first series converges and the second diverges. Both $2/n^2$ and $1/n$ are at least $1/n^2$, again with different outcomes. A divergent upper bound or a convergent lower bound alone leaves the question open.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $1/n^2$ 与 $1/(2n)$ 都不超过 $1/n$，但前者级数收敛，后者发散。$2/n^2$ 与 $1/n$ 又都不小于 $1/n^2$，结果仍然不同。因此，仅有发散上界或收敛下界，不能作出敛散结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nonnegativity is essential to this argument. For example, $-1\le 2^{-n}$, and the positive [[几何级数|geometric series]] $\sum 2^{-n}$ converges, but $\sum(-1)$ has partial sum $-N$ and diverges. For signed terms, a useful alternative is to bound their magnitudes $|a_n|$ by a convergent nonnegative series. This proves [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]], meaning that the series of magnitudes has a finite sum. If convenient inequalities are hard to find, [[级数极限比较判别|limit comparison]] can supply them from a term ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非负性是这个论证的关键。例如，$-1\le2^{-n}$，而正项[[几何级数]] $\sum2^{-n}$ 收敛，但 $\sum(-1)$ 的部分和为 $-N$，显然发散。对有正有负的项，可改用某个收敛非负级数控制其绝对值 $|a_n|$，从而证明[[绝对收敛]]，即绝对值级数的和有限。如果不容易直接找到合适不等式，[[级数极限比较判别]]可以由通项比值产生所需的界。
