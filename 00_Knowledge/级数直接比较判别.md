# 最终非负的级数可用收敛上界证明收敛或用发散下界证明发散
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An eventually nonnegative series converges under a convergent upper bound and diverges above a divergent lower bound*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两级数的每一项都有定义，并且存在整数 $N_0$，使所有 $n\ge N_0$ 都满足 $0\le a_n\le b_n$。若 $\sum b_n$ 收敛，则 $\sum a_n$ 收敛；若 $\sum a_n$ 发散，则 $\sum b_n$ 发散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose every term is defined and $0\le a_n\le b_n$ for all $n\ge N_0$. If $\sum b_n$ converges, so does $\sum a_n$; if $\sum a_n$ diverges, so does $\sum b_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较方向决定结论：证明收敛要找到比目标更大的可求和尾部；证明发散要找到比目标更小的发散尾部。比一个发散级数更小，或比一个收敛级数更大，都不能据此定性。有限个前项只改变部分和的常数，不影响敛散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove convergence, bound the target tail above by a summable tail. To prove divergence, bound it below by a divergent tail. Being smaller than a divergent series or larger than a convergent one is inconclusive. A finite prefix changes partial sums only by a constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理由是对有限尾和逐项相加：$\sum_{n=N_0}^N a_n\le\sum_{n=N_0}^N b_n$。非负项使这些尾和单调不减；收敛上界使左边有界，发散下界使右边无界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sum the inequalities over a finite tail. Nonnegative terms make its partial sums nondecreasing; a convergent upper bound keeps the smaller sums bounded, while a divergent lower bound forces the larger sums to be unbounded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $0<1/(n^2+1)\le1/n^2$，所以由 [[p级数判别]] 得到 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty1/(n^2+1)$ 收敛。若目标项有正有负，应先考虑比较 $|a_n|$，由此检验的是 [[绝对收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $0<1/(n^2+1)\le1/n^2$, so the [[p级数判别|p-series test]] proves convergence of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty1/(n^2+1)$. For signed terms, comparison can instead be applied to $|a_n|$ to test [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
