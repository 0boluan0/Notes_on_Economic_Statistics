# Brownian quadratic variation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*布朗二次变差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Quadratic variation** measures accumulated movement by adding squared changes along a time grid, then making that grid finer. For standard Brownian motion on a fixed interval $[0,T]$, the limiting quantity is $T$. The changes keep becoming smaller, but there are more of them; their squared sum retains a nonzero limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**二次变差**先沿时间网格把各次变化平方后相加，再不断细化网格，以此衡量累计运动。对固定区间 $[0,T]$ 上的标准布朗运动，这个极限量等于 $T$。单次变化越来越小，变化的段数却越来越多，所以平方和保留了非零的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $W_t$ is the position of a [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] at time $t$. An increment is the difference between two positions. Squaring an increment removes its sign: an upward move and a downward move can both contribute positively even if their net change is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $W_t$ 是[[标准布朗运动]]在时刻 $t$ 的位置；增量是两个位置之差。把增量平方会消去符号：即使最终净变化为零，一次上升和一次下降都可能贡献正数。

> [!example] A finite grid is only an approximation · 有限网格只是近似
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose recorded positions at times $0,1/2,1$ are $0,1/2,0$. The two squared changes sum to $(1/2)^2+(-1/2)^2=1/2$, while using only the two endpoints gives zero. Neither finite calculation must equal the limit $T=1$. Refining a grid is not even guaranteed to increase the sum: splitting a change $a+b$ replaces $(a+b)^2$ by $a^2+b^2$, a difference of $-2ab$, whose sign can vary.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设时刻 $0,1/2,1$ 记录的位置依次是 $0,1/2,0$，两次变化的平方和为 $(1/2)^2+(-1/2)^2=1/2$，只看首尾则得到零。有限次计算都不必等于极限 $T=1$。细化网格甚至不保证平方和增大：把变化 $a+b$ 拆成两段，会把 $(a+b)^2$ 换成 $a^2+b^2$，差值为 $-2ab$，可正可负。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a finite $T>0$. A **partition** is a finite list of times spanning the interval. For the $n$th grid, write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定有限的 $T>0$。**分割**就是从区间起点排到终点的一组有限时刻。第 $n$ 套网格记作

$$\pi_n:\quad 0=t_{n,0}<t_{n,1}<\cdots<t_{n,k_n}=T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $n$ labels the grid, and $k_n$ counts its intervals. Write $\Delta t_{n,i}=t_{n,i}-t_{n,i-1}$ for interval $i$'s length. The **mesh** is the longest interval, $|\pi_n|=\max_i\Delta t_{n,i}$. All these times are deterministic: they are fixed without inspecting the random path. Define the squared-increment sum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$n$ 给网格编号，$k_n$ 是这套网格的区间数。第 $i$ 段长度为 $\Delta t_{n,i}=t_{n,i}-t_{n,i-1}$；**网格大小**指最长一段的长度，即 $|\pi_n|=\max_i\Delta t_{n,i}$。这些时刻都必须是确定性的，不能看过随机路径以后再挑选。定义增量平方和

$$Q_n=\sum_{i=1}^{k_n}(W_{t_{n,i}}-W_{t_{n,i-1}})^2.$$

> [!important] The limit and its meaning · 极限及其含义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $|\pi_n|\to0$, then $Q_n\to T$ in [[均方收敛|mean square]], meaning $E[(Q_n-T)^2]\to0$. Consequently it converges [[依概率收敛|in probability]] too: for each fixed tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, $P(|Q_n-T|>\varepsilon)\to0$. This is commonly written $[W]_T=T$, with the partition and convergence convention understood.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $|\pi_n|\to0$，则 $Q_n$ [[均方收敛]]到 $T$，即 $E[(Q_n-T)^2]\to0$；因而也[[依概率收敛]]：对每个固定容忍值 $\varepsilon>0$，$P(|Q_n-T|>\varepsilon)\to0$。常用记号 $[W]_T=T$ 会把所用分割与收敛方式省略掉。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the limit is the interval length
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么极限是 T

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within each fixed grid the increments are independent and have laws $N(0,\Delta t_{n,i})$, where the second parameter is variance. Their squares therefore have expectations $\Delta t_{n,i}$. Adding gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在每套固定网格内，各增量相互独立，并服从第二参数为方差的 $N(0,\Delta t_{n,i})$；其平方的期望就是 $\Delta t_{n,i}$。相加得到

$$E[Q_n]=\sum_i\Delta t_{n,i}=T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An expectation alone does not prove concentration around $T$. We also need the variance. For $Z\sim N(0,v)$, the normal fourth moment is $E[Z^4]=3v^2$, while $E[Z^2]=v$. Thus $\operatorname{Var}(Z^2)=3v^2-v^2=2v^2$. Independence within the grid makes the variances add:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有期望等于 $T$，不能证明结果集中在 $T$ 附近，还需要方差。若 $Z\sim N(0,v)$，正态分布的四阶矩为 $E[Z^4]=3v^2$，而 $E[Z^2]=v$，因此 $\operatorname{Var}(Z^2)=3v^2-v^2=2v^2$。同一网格内的独立性让各项方差可以直接相加：

$$\begin{aligned}
E[(Q_n-T)^2]
&=\operatorname{Var}(Q_n)
=2\sum_i(\Delta t_{n,i})^2\\
&\le2|\pi_n|\sum_i\Delta t_{n,i}
=2T|\pi_n|\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inequality uses $\Delta t_{n,i}\le|\pi_n|$ for every term. Each $Q_n$ has a finite second moment, so this is convergence inside $L^2$, not merely a formal error expression. The grids need not be equally spaced or nested, and sums from different grids need not be independent. For $m$ equal intervals, the formula simplifies to $E[Q]=T$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Q)=2T^2/m$. At $T=1,m=100$, the standard deviation of $Q$ is $\sqrt{0.02}\approx0.1414$; a single realised sum is still random.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不等式逐项使用了 $\Delta t_{n,i}\le|\pi_n|$。每个 $Q_n$ 都有有限二阶矩，因此确实是在 $L^2$ 中收敛，而不只是形式上的误差式。网格不必等距或嵌套，不同网格得到的平方和也不必独立。若等分成 $m$ 段，就有 $E[Q]=T$、$\operatorname{Var}(Q)=2T^2/m$。例如 $T=1,m=100$ 时，$Q$ 的标准差为 $\sqrt{0.02}\approx0.1414$，单次实现的平方和仍然是随机数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the same realised path converges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 几乎处处版本必须写出分割条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[几乎必然收敛|Almost-sure convergence]] asks more: outside one probability-zero set, the successive sums along the chosen sequence must converge for the same realised path. One convenient sufficient condition is $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|\pi_n|<\infty$. Indeed, [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev's inequality]] and the variance bound give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[几乎必然收敛]]要求更多：除去一个零概率集合，同一条已实现路径沿选定分割序列计算的平方和必须收敛。一个方便的充分条件是 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|\pi_n|<\infty$。由 [[Chebyshev不等式]]与上面的方差界，

$$P(|Q_n-T|>\varepsilon)\le\frac{2T|\pi_n|}{\varepsilon^2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed $\varepsilon$, the probability that any error after index $N$ exceeds it is at most the sum of these bounds over $n\ge N$, which tends to zero. Apply this argument to the countable tolerances $1,1/2,1/3,\ldots$: almost surely every such tolerance is eventually met, so $Q_n\to T$. This is the first Borel–Cantelli argument; independence between grids is unnecessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $\varepsilon$ 后，从第 $N$ 套网格开始还有某次误差超过它的概率，不大于对所有 $n\ge N$ 的上述概率界求和，而这个尾和趋于零。再对可数个容忍值 $1,1/2,1/3,\ldots$ 使用该论证，就得到几乎必然最终满足每个容忍值，即 $Q_n\to T$。这就是第一 Borel–Cantelli 论证，不需要不同网格之间独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, split $[0,T]$ into $2^n$ equal intervals at stage $n$. The mesh is $T2^{-n}$ and its sum is finite, so these **dyadic refinements** give almost-sure convergence. MIT's stated condition $n^2|\pi_n|\to0$ is another sufficient condition, since it makes the mesh eventually bounded by $1/n^2$. More generally, deterministic **nested** partitions with mesh tending to zero also give almost-sure convergence, as recorded by Pitman–Yor; nested means each grid retains every previous grid point. That broader result is not obtained by requiring the mesh sum to be finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如第 $n$ 次把 $[0,T]$ 等分成 $2^n$ 段，网格为 $T2^{-n}$，这些网格大小的和有限，因此这种**二进细分**给出几乎必然收敛。MIT 所列的 $n^2|\pi_n|\to0$ 也是充分条件，因为它使网格最终不超过 $1/n^2$。更一般地，确定性的**嵌套**分割只要网格趋于零，也有几乎必然收敛；Pitman–Yor 记载了这一结果。嵌套指每套网格保留前一套的所有时点，这个更一般的结论不要求网格大小之和有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the chosen scheme and the quantifiers visible. The mean-square theorem alone does not assert simultaneous pathwise convergence for every possible sequence, and it is not a theorem about grids selected after inspecting a path. Random sampling schemes require their own hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要明确所选网格方案与量词。均方收敛定理本身没有声称对所有可能序列同时逐路径收敛，也不是针对看完路径才选择的网格；随机抽样方案需要另行规定条件。

## Squared movement and total movement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*平方运动量与总运动量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For comparison, a continuous function $f$ has finite [[函数总变差|total variation]] on $[0,T]$ when all its absolute-increment sums are bounded by one finite number $V$. Continuity on this compact interval makes the largest increment tend to zero as the mesh vanishes, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作个比较：若连续函数 $f$ 在 $[0,T]$ 上的所有绝对增量和都被某个有限数 $V$ 控制，就称其[[函数总变差|总变差]]有限。闭有界区间上的连续性使网格趋零时的最大增量也趋零，因此

$$\sum_i(\Delta f_i)^2\le\max_i|\Delta f_i|\sum_i|\Delta f_i|\le\max_i|\Delta f_i|V\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Brownian motion instead has quadratic variation $T>0$. The separate result [[布朗路径无限总变差|Brownian paths have infinite total variation]] develops the consequence for whole paths and all nontrivial intervals. Finite quadratic variation and infinite total variation therefore describe different measurements of the same motion. At the degenerate endpoint $T=0$, both the squared-increment sum and its limit are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

布朗运动的二次变差却是 $T>0$；由此怎样推出整条路径在所有非退化区间上的结论，见[[布朗路径无限总变差]]。有限二次变差与无限总变差，描述的是同一种运动的不同度量。退化端点 $T=0$ 时，增量平方和及其极限都为零。
