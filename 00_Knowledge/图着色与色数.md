# 图着色与色数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graph coloring and chromatic number*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当一对相互冲突的对象不能使用同一种资源时，怎样把所有对象分组，并判断最少需要多少组或多少种资源？
> **具体锚点：** 每门考试是一个 vertex；若有学生同时选两门课，就在两门考试之间连 edge。给 vertex 的 color 是考试时段，相邻 vertices 必须异色；chromatic number 是消除全部冲突所需的最少时段数。
> **核心难点：** 找到一个 $k$-coloring 只证明 $\chi(G)\le k$；要证明色数恰为 $k$，还要用 clique、odd cycle 等结构给出 $\chi(G)\ge k$。maximum degree 给的是上界而不是下界。
> **为什么重要：** exam scheduling、frequency assignment、register allocation 和 mutually incompatible jobs 都是同一个 conflict-partition 模型；bipartite testing 则是 $2$-coloring 的精确特例。
> **继续：** 从 [[#从冲突图到 proper coloring]] 建模，用 [[#如何证明 chromatic number]] 同时建立上下界；若只关心允许边中怎样配对，而不是把冲突顶点分组，进入 [[二分图匹配与 Hall 定理]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When two conflicting objects cannot share the same resource, how can all objects be partitioned, and what is the minimum number of groups or resources required?
> **Concrete anchor:** Represent every exam by a vertex and connect two exams when some student takes both. A vertex color is an exam slot, adjacent vertices must receive different colors, and the chromatic number is the minimum number of slots that eliminates all conflicts.
> **Central difficulty:** Constructing a $k$-coloring proves only $\chi(G)\le k$. To prove that the chromatic number equals $k$, a lower bound such as a clique or odd cycle is also required. Maximum degree supplies an upper bound, not a lower bound.
> **Why it matters:** Exam scheduling, frequency assignment, register allocation, and mutually incompatible jobs share the same conflict-partition model. Bipartite testing is exactly the special case of two-colorability.
> **Continue with:** Model the problem in [[#从冲突图到 proper coloring|from conflict graphs to proper colorings]], then establish both bounds in [[#如何证明 chromatic number|proving the chromatic number]]. If the problem asks which allowed edges can be paired rather than how conflicting vertices can be grouped, continue to [[二分图匹配与 Hall 定理|bipartite matching and Hall's theorem]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从冲突图到 proper coloring
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From a conflict graph to a proper coloring*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给 simple undirected graph $G=(V,E)$，proper $k$-coloring 是函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a simple undirected graph $G=(V,E)$, a proper $k$-coloring is a function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
c:V\to\{1,\ldots,k\},\qquad uv\in E\Rightarrow c(u)\ne c(v).
$$

图是 $k$-colorable，表示存在至多使用 $k$ 种颜色的 proper coloring；chromatic number
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A graph is $k$-colorable when some proper coloring uses at most $k$ colors. Its chromatic number is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\chi(G)=\min\{k:G\text{ is }k\text{-colorable}\}.
$$

颜色只是组标签，没有红蓝绿的内在含义。每个 color class 都是 independent set，因为其中任意两点不能相邻；反过来，把 $V$ partition 成 $k$ 个 independent sets 就得到 $k$-coloring。因此 graph coloring 也可以理解为“用最少的无冲突组覆盖全部 vertices”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Colors are merely group labels and carry no intrinsic red, blue, or green meaning. Every color class is an independent set because no two of its vertices are adjacent. Conversely, partitioning $V$ into $k$ independent sets gives a $k$-coloring. Graph coloring can therefore be understood as covering all vertices with the fewest conflict-free groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

建模成败取决于 edge 的含义。若 edge 表示“不能同时发生”，color 才能代表 time slot、frequency 或 register；若 edge 反而表示“允许配对”，目标通常是 matching。方向、容量、连续时间长度或“每个时段最多容纳 $q$ 个对象”等额外约束，也可能使普通 vertex coloring 不再足够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model succeeds or fails with the meaning of an edge. A color represents a time slot, frequency, or register only when an edge means “cannot occur together.” If an edge instead means “may be paired,” the natural objective is usually a matching. Direction, capacity, continuous durations, or constraints such as at most $q$ objects per slot may also require more than ordinary vertex coloring.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 如何证明 chromatic number
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to prove the chromatic number*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要证明 $\chi(G)=k$，必须同时完成两件不同的事：构造一个 proper $k$-coloring，给上界 $\chi(G)\le k$；再证明任何 $k-1$ colors 都不够，给下界 $\chi(G)\ge k$。只画出三种颜色不能排除两种颜色，只找到 triangle 也只给 $\chi(G)\ge3$，不能说明三色一定够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove $\chi(G)=k$, two different tasks are required: construct a proper $k$-coloring to show $\chi(G)\le k$, and prove that $k-1$ colors cannot suffice to show $\chi(G)\ge k$. Drawing a three-coloring does not rule out two colors. Finding a triangle gives only $\chi(G)\ge3$ and does not show that three colors suffice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 常用下界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Common lower bounds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 若 $G$ 含 clique $K_r$，其中每两点相邻，故 $r$ 个 vertices 必须颜色各异，$\chi(G)\ge r$。
- 若 $G$ 含 odd cycle，则不能二着色，所以 $\chi(G)\ge3$。
- 若已知 maximum independent set 大小为 $\alpha(G)$，每种颜色至多容纳 $\alpha(G)$ 个 vertices，因此 $\chi(G)\ge\lceil |V|/\alpha(G)\rceil$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If $G$ contains a clique $K_r$, every pair of its vertices is adjacent, so all $r$ vertices need distinct colors and $\chi(G)\ge r$.
- If $G$ contains an odd cycle, it is not two-colorable, so $\chi(G)\ge3$.
- If the maximum independent-set size is $\alpha(G)$, each color can contain at most $\alpha(G)$ vertices, giving $\chi(G)\ge\lceil |V|/\alpha(G)\rceil$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 常用上界与 greedy coloring
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Common upper bounds and greedy coloring*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任何给定 vertex order 都可做 greedy coloring：依次给当前 vertex 使用其已着色邻居没有用过的最小颜色。若 maximum degree 是 $\Delta$，当前点最多禁止 $\Delta$ 种颜色，故总能用不超过 $\Delta+1$ colors，得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any chosen vertex order, greedy coloring assigns each vertex the smallest color not already used by its colored neighbors. If the maximum degree is $\Delta$, a current vertex can forbid at most $\Delta$ colors, so at most $\Delta+1$ colors always suffice:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\chi(G)\le \Delta(G)+1.
$$

这只是保证，不通常是 exact answer。star $K_{1,m}$ 的中心 degree 为 $m$，但中心一种颜色、所有 leaves 另一种颜色，所以 $\chi=2$。greedy 结果还依赖 vertex order：差的顺序可能使用多于 $\chi(G)$ 的颜色；因此算法产生的是可核验上界，不自动是 minimum coloring。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a guarantee, not usually the exact answer. The center of the star $K_{1,m}$ has degree $m$, yet one color for the center and another for every leaf gives $\chi=2$. Greedy coloring also depends on vertex order: a poor order may use more than $\chi(G)$ colors. The algorithm therefore provides a checkable upper bound, not automatically a minimum coloring.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Bipartite、二着色与 odd cycle 是同一件事
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bipartiteness, two-colorability, and odd cycles are the same condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 finite undirected graph，下列三件事等价：vertices 可 partition 为左右两侧且每条 edge 横跨两侧；图存在 proper 2-coloring；图不含 odd cycle。若图非空但完全没有边，则 $\chi(G)=1$；若至少有一条边且 bipartite，则 $\chi(G)=2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite undirected graph, three conditions are equivalent: the vertices can be partitioned into two sides with every edge crossing between them; the graph has a proper two-coloring; and the graph contains no odd cycle. A nonempty graph with no edges has $\chi(G)=1$. A bipartite graph with at least one edge has $\chi(G)=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“bipartite 推出二着色”直接给左右侧不同颜色。“二着色推出无 odd cycle”也直接：沿 cycle 颜色必须交替，回到起点需要偶数步。困难方向是“无 odd cycle 推出二着色”：对每个 connected component 选 root，用 BFS distance 的 parity 着色；若有 edge $uv$ 连接同 parity 的两点，root 到 $u,v$ 的两条 BFS-tree paths 去掉共同前缀，再加 $uv$ 会形成 odd cycle，矛盾。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Bipartite implies two-colorable” follows by coloring the two sides differently. “Two-colorable implies no odd cycle” is also immediate: colors must alternate around a cycle, so returning to the start takes an even number of steps. For the harder direction, choose a root in every connected component and color vertices by the parity of their BFS distance. If an edge $uv$ joined vertices of the same parity, remove the common prefix of their two BFS-tree paths from the root and add $uv$; the result would be an odd cycle, a contradiction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个算法同时给两种 certificate：成功时输出每个 component 的 two-coloring；失败时输出一条 odd cycle，说明为何不可能二着色。仅说“BFS 遇到冲突”不够，保留两条 parent chains 与冲突 edge 才能让结论被复查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The algorithm supplies either of two certificates: on success, a two-coloring for every component; on failure, an odd cycle explaining why two colors are impossible. Saying only that “BFS found a conflict” is insufficient. Retaining the two parent chains and the conflicting edge makes the conclusion independently checkable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子：考试排期为什么恰需三个时段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: why an exam schedule needs exactly three slots*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设考试 $A,B,C$ 两两有共同学生，因而形成 triangle；另有考试 $D$ 只与 $A$ 冲突，$E$ 只与 $B$ 冲突。triangle 中三个 vertices 必须颜色各异，所以 $\chi(G)\ge3$。这不是因为“看起来复杂”，而是 $K_3$ 给出不可绕过的 lower-bound certificate。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose exams $A,B,C$ pairwise share students and therefore form a triangle. Exam $D$ conflicts only with $A$, and $E$ only with $B$. The three triangle vertices require distinct colors, so $\chi(G)\ge3$. This is not because the graph “looks complicated”; the $K_3$ is a lower-bound certificate that no coloring can evade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再构造 coloring：$c(A)=1,c(B)=2,c(C)=3,c(D)=2,c(E)=1$。逐边检查，所有相邻端点颜色不同，所以 $\chi(G)\le3$。上下界合并才得到 $\chi(G)=3$，并把三个 colors 解释为三个 exam slots。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now construct a coloring: $c(A)=1,c(B)=2,c(C)=3,c(D)=2,c(E)=1$. Checking every edge shows that adjacent endpoints have different colors, so $\chi(G)\le3$. Only after combining the lower and upper bounds may we conclude $\chi(G)=3$ and interpret the three colors as three exam slots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若现实中每场考试有不同长度、教室容量有限，或同一天连续两场也算冲突，必须把这些约束编码进更丰富的 scheduling model；普通 coloring 只保证同一 color class 内没有由现有 edges 表示的 pairwise conflict。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If exams have different lengths, rooms have limited capacity, or consecutive exams on the same day also count as a conflict, those constraints require a richer scheduling model. Ordinary coloring guarantees only that a color class contains none of the pairwise conflicts already encoded as edges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与相邻概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and neighboring concepts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 常见说法 | 问题 | 修复 |
|---|---|---|
| “我找到 4-coloring，所以 $\chi=4$” | 只证明上界 | 再证明 3 colors 不可能，或把结论降为 $\chi\le4$ |
| “图有高 degree，所以色数高” | degree 不给一般下界；star 是反例 | 用 clique、odd cycle、$|V|/\alpha$ 等给下界 |
| “无 triangle，所以 bipartite” | 还可能有 $C_5,C_7,\ldots$ | 检查所有 odd cycles，或直接做 BFS two-coloring |
| “颜色编号不同就是不同结构” | 交换颜色标签不改变 partition | 比较 color classes，而不是标签名称 |
| “disconnected graph 的色数相加” | components 可复用同一组颜色 | $\chi(G)$ 是各 components 色数的最大值 |
| “matching 和 coloring 都在处理冲突，所以相同” | matching 选互不共享端点的 edges；coloring partition vertices | 先确认决策对象是 edge 还是 vertex |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Common claim | Problem | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| “I found a four-coloring, so $\chi=4$.” | This proves only an upper bound | Also prove that three colors are impossible, or state only $\chi\le4$ |
| “The graph has high degree, so its chromatic number is high.” | Degree is not a general lower bound; a star is a counterexample | Use a clique, odd cycle, or $|V|/\alpha$ lower bound |
| “The graph has no triangle, so it is bipartite.” | It may still contain $C_5,C_7,\ldots$ | Rule out every odd cycle, or run BFS two-coloring directly |
| “Changing color numbers changes the coloring structure.” | Permuting labels leaves the partition unchanged | Compare color classes rather than label names |
| “The chromatic numbers of disconnected components add.” | Components can reuse the same colors | $\chi(G)$ is the maximum chromatic number of its components |
| “Matching and coloring both handle conflict, so they are the same.” | A matching selects edges without shared endpoints; coloring partitions vertices | First determine whether the decision objects are edges or vertices |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

vertex coloring 与 edge coloring 也不同。前者使相邻 vertices 异色；后者使共享端点的 edges 异色。地图着色还需要先构造区域 adjacency graph；只在一点接触是否算相邻必须由应用定义，不能从图论公式倒推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Vertex coloring also differs from edge coloring. The former separates adjacent vertices; the latter separates edges sharing an endpoint. Map coloring first requires a region-adjacency graph, and the application must decide whether contact at a single point counts as adjacency. Graph-theoretic formulas cannot make that modeling choice retroactively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么证明 $\chi(G)=k$ 必须同时有上界和下界？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does proving $\chi(G)=k$ require both an upper and a lower bound?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 一个具体 $k$-coloring 只说明 $k$ 种颜色够用，不能排除更少颜色；一个 obstruction 只说明某些颜色数不够，也不能保证 $k$ 种一定能完成。两者夹住同一数值才是等号。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A concrete $k$-coloring shows only that $k$ colors suffice and does not rule out fewer. An obstruction shows only that some smaller number fails and does not guarantee that $k$ succeeds. Equality requires both bounds to meet.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么含 odd cycle 的图不能 two-color？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a graph containing an odd cycle not be two-colored?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 沿 cycle 每经过一条边就必须换色。奇数次换色后回到起点会要求起点同时取另一种颜色，产生矛盾。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every edge around the cycle forces a color change. After an odd number of changes, returning to the starting vertex would require it to have the opposite color, a contradiction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### maximum degree 为 20，能否推出 $\chi(G)$ 接近 21？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If the maximum degree is 20, can we infer that $\chi(G)$ is close to 21?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。$\Delta+1=21$ 是一般上界，不是下界；$K_{1,20}$ 的 maximum degree 是 20，但色数只有 2。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. $\Delta+1=21$ is a general upper bound, not a lower bound. The star $K_{1,20}$ has maximum degree 20 but chromatic number two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 三个考试形成 triangle，另有任意多只连接其中一个考试的 leaves，最少要几个时段？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three exams form a triangle, with arbitrarily many leaves each attached to only one of those exams. What is the minimum number of slots?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 三个。triangle 给下界 3；先给 triangle 三种颜色，每个 leaf 再选不同于其唯一邻居的任一已有颜色，给上界 3。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Three. The triangle gives the lower bound. Color the triangle with three colors, then assign every leaf any existing color different from its sole neighbor, giving the matching upper bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个 BFS two-coloring 失败时，怎样给出可复查的失败证书？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When BFS two-coloring fails, how can it provide a checkable certificate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 保留发生同色冲突的 edge $uv$，以及 BFS parent forest 中 root 到 $u,v$ 的路径；删去共同前缀后把两条路径与 $uv$ 合起来，得到一条 odd cycle。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Retain the conflicting same-color edge $uv$ and the two parent paths from the BFS root to $u$ and $v$. Removing their common prefix and adding $uv$ yields an odd cycle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
