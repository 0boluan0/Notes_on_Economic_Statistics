# 最终非零项的相邻绝对值比极限小于一保证绝对收敛而大于一保证发散
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For eventually nonzero terms, a limiting absolute ratio below one gives absolute convergence and a ratio above one gives divergence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实数或复数级数 $\sum a_n$ 的项最终非零，且相邻项的绝对值比有扩展实数极限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a real or complex series $\sum a_n$ has eventually nonzero terms and the absolute ratio has an extended-real limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right|\in[0,+\infty].
$$

若 $L<1$，级数绝对收敛；若 $L>1$（包括 $+\infty$），级数发散；若 $L=1$，本判别无结论。若比值极限不存在，或分母在任意远处仍为零，本卡的极限版本不能直接使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $L<1$, the series converges absolutely. If $L>1$, including $+\infty$, it diverges. If $L=1$, the test is inconclusive. This limit version cannot be applied directly when the limit does not exist or zero denominators occur arbitrarily far out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $L<1$，取 $L<q<1$，充分远处 $|a_{n+1}|\le q|a_n|$，反复应用得到几何衰减上界。若 $L>1$，充分远处绝对值持续按大于 1 的固定比例增长，因而通项不趋于零。使用绝对值使结论也适用于变号级数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $L<1$, choose $q$ between $L$ and one; eventually $|a_{n+1}|\le q|a_n|$, giving a geometric upper bound. For $L>1$, magnitudes eventually grow by a fixed factor above one, so the terms do not approach zero. Absolute values allow signed terms as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $a_n=(-1)^n3^n/n!$ 时，$|a_{n+1}/a_n|=3/(n+1)\to0$，所以绝对收敛。阶乘在相邻项相除时能大量约去，适合这种判别。边界的反例是 $\sum1/n$ 与 $\sum1/n^2$：两者都得到 $L=1$，但前者发散、后者收敛，须改用 [[p级数判别]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a_n=(-1)^n3^n/n!$, the ratio is $3/(n+1)\to0$, proving absolute convergence. Factorials often simplify under adjacent-term division. At the boundary, both $\sum1/n$ and $\sum1/n^2$ give $L=1$, despite opposite outcomes; the [[p级数判别|p-series test]] distinguishes them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
