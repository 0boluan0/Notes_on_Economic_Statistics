# Ratio test for series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*级数比值判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio test asks how the size of one term compares with the size of the preceding term. If, from some point onward, each new magnitude is at most a fixed positive factor below one times the preceding magnitude, the remaining terms fit under a convergent geometric series. The test detects this situation by taking the limit of the adjacent size ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比值判别考察后一项的大小相当于前一项的多少。如果从某处起，每一步的大小都不超过前一步乘上同一个小于一的正数，剩下的各项就能被收敛的几何级数控制。这个判别通过相邻项大小之比的极限识别这种情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A factorial makes the comparison simple
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*阶乘让相邻比较变得简单*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $a_n=(-1)^n3^n/n!$, where $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$ is the factorial of the positive integer $n$. The sign alternates, while the first four magnitudes are $3,\ 9/2,\ 9/2,\ 27/8$. They do not decrease strictly from the start. Dividing consecutive magnitudes is more informative:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $a_n=(-1)^n3^n/n!$，其中 $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$ 是正整数 $n$ 的阶乘。符号交替，前四项的绝对值则为 $3,\ 9/2,\ 9/2,\ 27/8$，并非从一开始就严格递减。比较相邻两项的大小更有用：

$$
\frac{|a_{n+1}|}{|a_n|}
=\frac{3^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}\frac{n!}{3^n}
=\frac{3}{n+1}\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every $n\ge5$, this ratio is at most $1/2$. Thus $|a_6|\le|a_5|/2$, $|a_7|\le|a_5|/2^2$, and so on. The tail is bounded term by term by a [[几何级数|geometric series]] with ratio $1/2$. The finitely many earlier terms contribute only a finite amount. Consequently $\sum |a_n|$ converges: the original series is [[绝对收敛|absolutely convergent]], so its alternating signs cannot destroy convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每当 $n\ge5$，这个比值至多为 $1/2$。因此 $|a_6|\le|a_5|/2$、$|a_7|\le|a_5|/2^2$，之后同理。尾部逐项不超过公比为 $1/2$ 的[[几何级数]]；前面有限多项只贡献一个有限数。因此 $\sum|a_n|$ 收敛，原级数[[绝对收敛]]，交替符号不会破坏其收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The limit criterion and its assumptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极限判据及其前提*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ be an [[无穷级数|infinite series]] with finite real or complex terms. For a complex number $u+iv$, its magnitude is $\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$, where $i^2=-1$; convergence of complex partial sums means convergence of both their real and imaginary parts. Assume that the terms are **eventually nonzero**: some integer $N_0$ satisfies $a_n\ne0$ for every $n\ge N_0$. The tail ratios are then defined. Suppose their limit exists, allowing $+\infty$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ 是各项为有限实数或复数的[[无穷级数]]。复数 $u+iv$ 的模为 $\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$，其中 $i^2=-1$；复数部分和收敛指其实部与虚部分别收敛。假设各项**最终非零**：存在整数 $N_0$，使每个 $n\ge N_0$ 都有 $a_n\ne0$，于是尾部的相邻比值都有定义。再假设这些比值的极限存在，允许取 $+\infty$：

$$L=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right|\in[0,+\infty].$$

> [!important] Three outcomes · 三种结果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $L<1$, the series converges absolutely. If $L>1$, including $L=+\infty$, the series diverges because its terms fail to tend to zero. If $L=1$, this test gives no decision. The limit is a limit of **term-size ratios**, not a proposed sum of the series.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $L<1$，级数绝对收敛；若 $L>1$，包括 $L=+\infty$，通项不趋于零，级数发散；若 $L=1$，本判别不能作出结论。这里求的是**相邻通项大小之比**的极限，不是在求级数的和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the threshold is one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么以一为分界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $L<1$, choose a fixed real number $q$ with $L<q<1$. By [[序列收敛|the definition of a sequence limit]], there is an integer $N\ge N_0$ such that $|a_{n+1}|/|a_n|\le q$ for every $n\ge N$. Repeated multiplication gives, for every integer $j\ge0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $L<1$ 时，选一个固定实数 $q$，满足 $L<q<1$。由[[序列收敛|数列极限的定义]]，可以找到整数 $N\ge N_0$，使每个 $n\ge N$ 都有 $|a_{n+1}|/|a_n|\le q$。反复相乘，对每个整数 $j\ge0$ 得到

$$|a_{N+j}|\le |a_N|q^j,
\qquad
\sum_{j=0}^{m}|a_{N+j}|\le |a_N|\sum_{j=0}^{m}q^j\le\frac{|a_N|}{1-q}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $m$ is any finite nonnegative integer. These nonnegative tail partial sums increase but have a common finite upper bound; equivalently, apply [[级数直接比较判别|direct comparison]] with the geometric series. This proves absolute convergence. [[绝对收敛推出收敛|Absolute convergence then implies convergence]] of the original real or complex series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $m$ 是任意有限非负整数。非负尾部部分和不断增加，却有同一个有限上界；也可以直接对几何级数使用[[级数直接比较判别]]。这证明绝对收敛，再由[[绝对收敛推出收敛]]得到原实数或复数级数收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $1<L<+\infty$, choose $1<q<L$. Eventually $|a_{n+1}|\ge q|a_n|$, so $|a_{N+j}|\ge |a_N|q^j$. The same argument works for $L=+\infty$ by choosing any fixed $q>1$. Since $a_N\ne0$, these magnitudes grow without bound. The [[级数通项判别|necessary condition that the terms tend to zero]] fails. For complex partial sums, the same necessary condition follows from $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$, where $S_n$ is the sum of the first $n$ terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $1<L<+\infty$ 时，选 $1<q<L$。最终有 $|a_{n+1}|\ge q|a_n|$，所以 $|a_{N+j}|\ge|a_N|q^j$。若 $L=+\infty$，任选固定的 $q>1$，同样推导成立。由于 $a_N\ne0$，这些大小不断增长到无穷，违反[[级数通项判别|通项必须趋于零的必要条件]]。复数部分和也有同一必要条件：记前 $n$ 项和为 $S_n$，则 $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where this version stops
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这个版本的适用边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a_n=1/n$ and for $a_n=1/n^2$, the adjacent ratios are respectively $n/(n+1)$ and $(n/(n+1))^2$, both tending to one. The first series diverges and the second converges by the [[p级数判别|p-series test]]. Thus $L=1$ does not mean divergence, and $L<1$ is not necessary for convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $a_n=1/n$ 与 $a_n=1/n^2$，相邻比值分别为 $n/(n+1)$ 与 $(n/(n+1))^2$，都趋于一。但由[[p级数判别]]，前者发散，后者收敛。因此 $L=1$ 不等于发散，$L<1$ 也不是收敛的必要条件。

> [!warning] Check the denominator before taking a limit · 先检查分母
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite number of zero terms can be left in the finite prefix. Infinitely many zero terms prevent application of this adjacent-ratio limit version: division by zero is not a numerical ratio to substitute into the rule. If all sufficiently late terms are zero, convergence is immediate. Otherwise the [[级数根值判别|root test]] can sometimes handle the zeros directly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 有限多项为零，可以把它们留在有限前缀中；若有无穷多个零项，就不能直接套用这里的相邻比值极限版本，除以零不是一个可以代入判据的数值比。若最终所有项都为零，级数当然收敛；其他含零项情形有时可直接使用[[级数根值判别]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even with no zero terms, a missing ratio limit does not imply divergence. For example, set $a_{2k-1}=a_{2k}=4^{-k}$ for integers $k\ge1$. Adjacent ratios alternate between $1$ and $1/4$, but the partial sums after $2K$ terms equal $2\sum_{k=1}^{K}4^{-k}\to2/3$; after $2K+1$ terms the extra $4^{-(K+1)}$ tends to zero. Hence the full series converges. This is a case for another argument, not a failed convergence claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使没有零项，比值极限不存在也不意味着发散。例如对整数 $k\ge1$，令 $a_{2k-1}=a_{2k}=4^{-k}$。相邻比值在 $1$ 与 $1/4$ 之间交替；但前 $2K$ 项和为 $2\sum_{k=1}^{K}4^{-k}\to2/3$，前 $2K+1$ 项和只多出一个趋于零的 $4^{-(K+1)}$，所以整个级数收敛。此时需要换一种论证，而不是断言级数不收敛。
