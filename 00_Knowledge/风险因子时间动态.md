# 多期或路径风险模拟必须规定风险因子的时间动态，终点联合分布不能替代完整路径模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multi-period or path risk simulation must specify risk-factor time dynamics; a terminal joint distribution cannot replace a full path model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 同一个终点分布可以来自不同的时间过程
> [[风险因子联合生成]] 规定目标时点的边际和横截面依赖；若损失还取决于中间状态、累计现金流或再平衡，就必须另外规定状态如何随时间递推。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[风险因子联合生成|Joint factor generation]] specifies margins and cross-sectional dependence at the target horizon. If loss also depends on intermediate states, accumulated cash flows, or rebalancing, the evolution of states through time must be specified separately.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对网格 $t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_m=t_0+H$，完整路径模型需要给出联合路径律。它可以按条件分布写成

$$
P(dx_{t_1},\ldots,dx_{t_m}\mid\mathcal F_{t_0})
=P(dx_{t_1}\mid\mathcal F_{t_0})
\prod_{j=2}^m
P(dx_{t_j}\mid x_{t_1},\ldots,x_{t_{j-1}},\mathcal F_{t_0}).
$$

只有在另有 Markov 等结构假设时，条件项才能进一步缩短为只依赖最近状态。终点分布 $P(x_{t_m})$ 是这条路径律的一个边际，不能反推出中间触碰、持续时间或冲击顺序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a grid from $t_0$ to the horizon, a full path model specifies the conditional joint law of all intermediate states. Only an additional structure such as a Markov assumption permits the conditioning history to be shortened. The terminal law is merely one marginal of this path law and cannot recover intermediate crossings, durations, or shock order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间动态至少要对实际重要的机制作出选择并接受校验，例如：均值回复、条件波动率与波动聚集、共同或个别跳跃、利率和信用利差期限结构、跨期相关，以及状态变化后参数是否更新。并非每个模型都必须包含全部机制；必须包含的是对目标头寸和期限有实质影响的机制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Time dynamics must choose and validate mechanisms material to the use case, such as mean reversion, conditional volatility and clustering, common or idiosyncratic jumps, term-structure evolution, serial dependence, and state-dependent parameters. A model need not contain every mechanism, but it must represent those material to the portfolio and horizon.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若所有头寸价值只依赖持有期末状态，直接生成经验证的终点联合分布可能已经足够；生成更细路径只会增加成本。若头寸具有路径依赖，则由 [[风险模拟路径依赖]] 进一步规定哪些时间顺序必须保留。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When every position depends only on the horizon state, a validated terminal joint generator may be sufficient and a finer path only adds cost. Path-dependent positions instead pass to [[风险模拟路径依赖|the path-dependence requirement]], which specifies which ordering information must be retained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两个模型给出相同的一年期股票价格联合分布，但一个允许年内共同跳跃，另一个只在年末一次抽取终点。它们能否因此给障碍头寸相同的风险？
>
> **答案：** 不能。相同终点边际不确定年内触碰与冲击顺序；障碍头寸还需要经验证的时间动态。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** Two models have the same one-year joint terminal distribution, but one allows common jumps during the year while the other samples only a terminal state. Must they give the same risk for a barrier position?
>
> **Answer:** No. Equal terminal margins do not determine intrayear crossings or shock order; the barrier position also requires validated time dynamics.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 横截面依赖描述同一时点怎样共同变化；时间依赖描述跨时点怎样递推，两者不能互换。
- 路径模型结构正确不等于时间离散已经足够精细；后者见 [[路径模拟离散化误差]]。
- 增加路径数只能更精确地逼近已指定的动态模型，不能补上遗漏的均值回复、跳跃或状态变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Cross-sectional dependence describes co-movement at one time; temporal dependence describes evolution across times. They are not interchangeable.
- A suitable path model does not prove that its numerical time grid is sufficiently fine; see [[路径模拟离散化误差|path-simulation discretisation error]].
- More paths only approximate the specified dynamics more precisely; they cannot add omitted mean reversion, jumps, or state variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
