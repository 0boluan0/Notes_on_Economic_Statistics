# 抽样分布是在指定数据生成与抽样机制下统计量的概率分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A sampling distribution is the probability distribution of a statistic under a specified data-generating and sampling mechanism*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 固定总体参数、抽样机制、样本量 $n$ 和统计规则 $T_n$ 后，数据的随机性诱导 $T_n$ 的概率分布，称为抽样分布。它可以离散或连续；一次样本算出的数值只是其中一次实现。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fixing the population parameter, sampling mechanism, sample size, and statistic determines its sampling distribution. This distribution can be discrete or continuous. One observed estimate is one realisation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

若 $X_i\overset{iid}{\sim}N(\mu,\sigma^2)$，则 $\bar X_n\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2/n)$。原始观察的分布与均值的分布不同。模拟可以近似展示这个分布，但并不创造它；[[样本量与模拟重复次数]]分别控制每次样本的信息量和模拟近似的精细度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For iid normal data, the sample mean is normal with variance $\sigma^2/n$. This differs from the distribution of an individual observation. Simulation approximates the sampling distribution; it does not create it. See [[样本量与模拟重复次数|sample size versus simulation repetitions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
