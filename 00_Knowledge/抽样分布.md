# Sampling distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*抽样分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a new sample, calculate its average, then imagine starting again with another sample. The average can change even though the population has not changed. A **sampling distribution** describes which values a [[统计量|statistic]]—a rule calculated from the data—can produce, and with what probabilities, under a specified way of generating and sampling the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重新抽一份样本，计算平均数，再设想从头抽另一份。总体没变，算出的平均数仍可能不同。**抽样分布**说明：在指定的数据生成与抽样方式下，一个[[统计量|由数据算出的规则]]可能取哪些值，各有多大概率。

> [!example] Two draws from four values · 从四个数抽两次
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use the [[总体与样本|population]] with values $0,2,4,6$, each selected with probability $1/4$. Draw twice **independently with replacement**: put the first selection back before drawing again, so it can occur twice. Write the two random observations as $X_1,X_2$ and their average as $\bar X_2=(X_1+X_2)/2$.
>
> There are $4\times4=16$ equally likely ordered pairs. The pairs $(0,2)$ and $(2,0)$ both give average $1$; the pairs $(0,6),(2,4),(4,2),(6,0)$ all give average $3$. Counting pairs gives the complete distribution:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 采用[[总体与样本|总体]]数值 $0,2,4,6$，每次以 $1/4$ 的概率选中各值。**放回、独立地抽两次**：第一次抽到的放回，因此可以重复抽到。两次随机观察记为 $X_1,X_2$，均值为 $\bar X_2=(X_1+X_2)/2$。共有 $16$ 个等可能有序数对；$(0,2)$ 与 $(2,0)$ 都给出均值 $1$，而 $(0,6),(2,4),(4,2),(6,0)$ 都给出均值 $3$。逐一计数得到：
>
> | $m$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ | $6$ |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | $P(\bar X_2=m)$ | $1/16$ | $2/16$ | $3/16$ | $4/16$ | $3/16$ | $2/16$ | $1/16$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the observed sample is $(0,2)$, its observed mean is $1$. That one number is a realization of this distribution. It is neither the whole distribution nor the population mean, which here is $3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果这次观察到 $(0,2)$，算出的均值是 $1$。这个数只是上表分布的一次实现，既不是整个分布，也不是本例等于 $3$ 的总体均值。

## What stays fixed when samples vary?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重新抽样时，哪些东西保持不变？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before observing the data, the sample $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ is random. Applying the same calculation rule $g$ gives the random quantity $T_n=g(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$. Its sampling distribution is determined by the joint probability model for the observations, the sample size $n$, and the rule $g$. Population parameters are held fixed in this calculation, even if their true values are unknown to us. Different possible parameter values may therefore give different sampling distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察之前，样本 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ 是随机的，同一计算规则 $g$ 产生随机量 $T_n=g(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$。观测的联合概率模型、样本量 $n$ 和规则 $g$ 共同确定它的抽样分布。计算时总体参数固定，即使我们不知道真实值；不同可能的参数值因而可能对应不同抽样分布。

> [!important] The distribution belongs to the rule under the sampling model · 分布属于抽样模型下的统计规则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A sampling distribution is the probability distribution of $T_n$, not the distribution of the raw observations and not a list of values inside one sample. Repeated sampling explains its meaning; we need not physically collect many samples for the distribution to exist. It can be discrete, continuous, or even concentrated at a single value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 抽样分布是 $T_n$ 的概率分布，不是原始观察值的分布，也不是一份样本内部的数据列表。重复抽样用来解释其含义，不需要真的采集许多份样本才有这个分布。它可以离散、连续，甚至集中于一个数。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, a single draw takes only $0,2,4,6$, but the average can also take $1,3,5$. Changing the sampling mechanism changes the answer: without replacement, $(0,0)$ cannot occur, so the probability that the two-draw mean is $0$ becomes zero. The population values alone do not determine the sampling distribution. [[独立同分布|Iid sampling]] is one possible model, not part of the definition itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例中，单次观察只取 $0,2,4,6$，均值却还能取 $1,3,5$。改变抽样方式也会改变答案：不放回时不可能抽到 $(0,0)$，两次均值等于零的概率就变为零。因此，仅列出总体数值还不够。[[独立同分布|Iid 抽样]]是一种可能模型，不是抽样分布定义本身的要求。

## An exact law and a simulated picture
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*精确分布与模拟图像*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For another example, let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be iid [[正态分布|normal]] observations with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2>0$. Their average has the exact law
$$\bar X_n\sim N\!\left(\mu,\frac{\sigma^2}{n}\right).$$
This follows from [[Gaussian仿射闭包|closure of Gaussian variables under fixed linear combinations]], together with the variance of the average. It holds for every positive integer $n$ under these assumptions; it is not a large-sample approximation. Non-normal observations do not generally give an exactly normal average at a fixed sample size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一个例子是 $n$ 个 iid [[正态分布|正态]]观察，均值为 $\mu$、方差为 $\sigma^2>0$。平均数精确服从上式，依据是[[Gaussian仿射闭包|固定线性组合仍为 Gaussian]]及平均数的方差。这里每个正整数 $n$ 都成立，不是大样本近似；非正态观察在固定样本量下一般不会给出精确正态的均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simulation can generate many samples of the chosen size and plot the resulting statistics. More simulation repetitions improve the approximation to the same law; increasing the size of each sample can change the law itself. These are the two different controls in [[样本量与模拟重复次数|sample size versus simulation repetitions]]. A smooth-looking histogram is not evidence that every sampling distribution has a density: the seven-point law above remains discrete however many times it is simulated. The [[标准误含义|standard error]] summarizes the spread of this law when its variance is finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模拟可以按指定样本量生成很多份样本，再画出各次统计量。增加模拟重复次数，是更精细地近似同一个分布；增加每份样本的大小，则可能改变分布本身，见[[样本量与模拟重复次数]]。图看起来平滑，不代表每个抽样分布都有密度：上面的七点分布无论模拟多少次都仍离散。方差有限时，[[标准误含义|标准误]]用一个数概括这个分布的分散程度。
