# 资本技术构成是生产资料实物规模与运用它们所需劳动量的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The technical composition of capital relates the physical means of production to the labour required to employ them.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本技术构成从生产过程的实物方面说明：一定数量的劳动运用多少机器、材料等生产资料。它关心劳动与生产资料怎样组合；与此相对，把投入生产资料和购买劳动力的资本价值相比，得到[[资本价值构成]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The technical composition describes the material side of production: how much machinery, material and other means of production a given amount of labour employs. It concerns their productive combination; comparing the capital values invested in means of production and in purchasing labour power instead gives the [[资本价值构成|value composition of capital]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在同样长度和强度的标准工作日中，10名纺纱工原先处理100单位棉花，改用新的设备后处理200单位。这描述了每单位劳动处理材料的实物关系变化。若要完整比较技术构成，还须记录设备和其他生产资料的变化；不同种类的机器、棉花和燃料不能把裸数量相加成一个无单位的总量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, ten spinners working days of the same length and intensity initially process 100 units of cotton and process 200 after adopting new equipment. This describes a change in the physical relation between material processed and labour. A fuller comparison must also record equipment and other inputs; counts of different machines, cotton and fuel cannot simply be added into a unitless total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一劳动运用更多生产资料，可能表现为配备更多机器，也可能表现为更快处理更多原料。它尚未给出不变资本与可变资本的价值比，因为生产资料和劳动力的单位价值还会变化。技术关系怎样进入价值关系，见[[资本有机构成]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More means of production per unit of labour may mean additional machinery or more material processed in the same time. It does not yet determine the ratio of constant to variable capital, because the unit values of productive inputs and labour power may change. See [[资本有机构成|the organic composition of capital]] for the connection between technical and value relations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
