# 每个候选管线只在对应训练数据上拟合，验证数据可以选择候选，但参与选择的验证结果不能再作为独立最终评价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Each candidate pipeline is fitted only on its training data; validation may select among candidates, but selection evidence is not an independent final evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 三种信息角色
> **拟合候选：** 每个候选的插补、中心与尺度、监督筛选、PCA 方向和模型系数，只能从对应训练数据估计。**选择候选：** validation loss 本来就可以在已冻结的候选之间选择超参数或模型。**最终评价：** 一旦 validation 参与选出了赢家，它就是选择证据，不能再把同一分数当成赢家完全独立的最终成绩。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Candidate fitting:** imputation, centring and scaling, supervised screening, PCA directions, and model coefficients for each candidate are estimated only from its training data. **Candidate selection:** validation loss may legitimately choose a hyperparameter or model among frozen candidates. **Final evaluation:** once validation selects the winner, that evidence is no longer an independent final score for the winner.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## fit 与 apply 要分开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Separate fitting from application*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个 train/validation split，应先在 training portion 得到管线参数 $\hat\eta_{train}$ 与模型 $\hat f_{train}$，再对 validation observation 计算

$$
\hat y_i
=
\hat f_{train}\!\left(T(x_i;\hat\eta_{train})\right).
$$

validation 的均值或标签不能回头参与这个候选的 $\hat\eta_{train}$；validation error 却可以用来比较候选并选出下一步使用的 $\lambda$、成分数或模型。事先由外部知识固定的常数变换不需要“重新学习”；但只要某个拟合数值来自本数据，例如全样本均值、target encoding 或“与 $y$ 最相关的变量”，就必须在相应训练折内估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each split, obtain pipeline parameters $\hat\eta_{train}$ and a model $\hat f_{train}$ from the training portion, then apply that frozen candidate to validation observations. Validation means or labels may not leak back into the candidate fit, but validation error may compare candidates and select a value of $\lambda$, a component count, or a model. A transformation fixed a priori from external knowledge need not be re-learned; any fitted value estimated from the present data—including a full-sample mean, target encoding, or variables chosen by correlation with $y$—belongs inside the corresponding training fold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两条合法的 ridge 选择路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two valid paths for selecting a ridge penalty*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若另留了独立 test，可在 development data 上用普通 K 折比较一组固定的 $\lambda$：每个 $\lambda$、每一折都只在该折训练部分拟合 scaler 与 ridge 系数，validation loss 的平均值选出 $\lambda$；随后用选定 $\lambda$ 在全部 development data 重拟合一次，test 只在最后评价一次。这条路径不要求为了普通调参再无条件套一层 CV。

若没有独立 test，或目标是估计“连同调参在内的整条选择 procedure”的外部表现，则使用 nested CV：每个 outer-training split 内再用 inner CV 选择 $\lambda$ 并重拟合，outer-validation split 只评价这次已经完成的选择。外层误差评价的是选择流程；内层误差负责选择，二者不能互换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an untouched test set, ordinary K-fold CV may compare a fixed grid of ridge penalties on the development data. For every penalty and fold, fit the scaler and ridge coefficients only on that fold's training portion; average validation loss to select $\lambda$, refit the selected pipeline on all development data, and use the test set once at the end. Ordinary tuning does not automatically require another nested layer when an independent final test is retained.

Without an independent test, or when the goal is to estimate the performance of the entire tuning procedure, use nested CV. Each outer-training split runs inner CV to select $\lambda$ and refit; the outer-validation split evaluates only the completed selection. Inner loss selects, while outer loss evaluates the selection procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

泄漏通常让验证任务比部署任务容易，因而分数偏乐观；但“每个具体样本一定提高多少”没有固定答案。它损害的是评估设计的独立性，而不是保证某次数字必然按一个方向变化。

[[PCA验证与泄漏]]把这条规则具体化为中心、尺度、载荷和成分数；[[分类验证与泄漏]]把它具体化为类别先验、协方差、特征选择和阈值。它们是领域专用应用，不应各自重复发明一般原则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Leakage usually makes validation easier than deployment and hence optimistically biased, but no fixed amount or direction is guaranteed in every realised sample. [[PCA验证与泄漏|PCA validation]] specialises the rule to centring, scaling, loadings, and component count; [[分类验证与泄漏|classification validation]] specialises it to priors, covariance, feature selection, and thresholds. They are domain applications of this shared principle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 用五折 CV 选 ridge 的 $\lambda$ 时，验证折的误差能否参与选择？这五折中最低的误差能否同时当作最终独立成绩？
>
> **答案：** 可以参与选择；这正是 validation 的职责。但同一误差已经帮助选出赢家，不能再作为完全独立的最终评价。最终评价要用未触碰的 test，或让调参发生在 nested CV 的 inner loop、由 outer fold 评价整条流程。

## 来源与核验

- scikit-learn, [Cross-validation: evaluating estimator performance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) 与 [Common pitfalls and recommended practices](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html)：核验 validation 用于模型选择、独立 test 用于最终评价，以及每个候选的标准化、插补、PCA 和特征选择只能在训练数据上 `fit`。
- Cawley and Talbot（2010），[On Over-fitting in Model Selection and Subsequent Selection Bias in Performance Evaluation](https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume11/cawley10a/cawley10a.pdf)：核验模型选择必须被视为拟合流程的一部分，并在每次评价训练中重新执行。
