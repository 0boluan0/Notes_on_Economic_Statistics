# The sum as the span of a union
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*子空间之和等于并集的张成*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two ways to combine the directions in two subspaces. Their [[子空间之和|sum]] takes one vector from each and adds them. The [[张成|span]] of their union allows every finite linear combination of vectors chosen from either side. These constructions produce exactly the same set: many terms can always be regrouped into one vector from each subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个子空间中的方向组合起来，可以有两种做法。[[子空间之和]]从两边各取一个向量相加；并集的[[张成]]允许从任意一边选取向量，作所有有限线性组合。这两种构造得到完全相同的集合：再多的项，也能重新归成每个子空间贡献一个向量。

> [!example] Many terms become two summands · 多项可以归成两项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $S$ be the horizontal axis and $T$ the vertical axis in $\mathbb R^2$. The vectors $(1,0),(2,0)$ lie in $S$, and $(0,1)$ lies in $T$. A combination from their union can look like
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 让 $S$ 为 $\mathbb R^2$ 中的横轴，$T$ 为纵轴。$(1,0),(2,0)$ 在 $S$ 中，$(0,1)$ 在 $T$ 中。选取并集中的向量，可以作这样的组合：
>
> $$2(1,0)-3(0,1)+4(2,0)=(10,-3).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The numbers $2,-3,4$ multiply the whole chosen vectors. Group the terms from $S$ together and the term from $T$ separately:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $2,-3,4$ 分别乘选中的完整向量。把来自 $S$ 的项归在一起，把来自 $T$ 的项单独留下：
>
> $$\underbrace{[2(1,0)+4(2,0)]}_{(10,0)\in S}+\underbrace{[-3(0,1)]}_{(0,-3)\in T}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The result is one vector in $S$ plus one in $T$, so it belongs to $S+T$. This regrouping works because each original set is already a [[子空间|subspace]] and therefore contains all combinations of its own vectors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 结果就是一个 $S$ 中的向量加一个 $T$ 中的向量，所以在 $S+T$ 中。之所以可以这样归并，是因为原来的两个集合已经是[[子空间]]，各自包含自身向量的全部线性组合。

> [!abstract] The identity · 两种构造得到同一个空间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For subspaces $S,T$ of the same vector space over the same scalar field,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对同一标量域上同一向量空间的子空间 $S,T$，
>
> $$S+T=\operatorname{span}(S\cup T).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The union $S\cup T$ retains vectors belonging to either side. Its span includes every **finite** linear combination of those vectors. The subspaces may be infinite-dimensional; no infinite series is being taken here.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 并集 $S\cup T$ 保留属于任意一边的向量；它的张成收集这些向量的所有**有限**线性组合。子空间可以是无限维的，这里并没有取无穷级数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why both containments hold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么左右两边互相包含*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First take a vector in $S+T$. It has the form $s+t$ with $s\in S,t\in T$. Both summands lie in the union, and $s+t=1s+1t$ is a finite linear combination of them. Thus every vector in the sum belongs to the span of the union.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先取 $S+T$ 中的向量。它形如 $s+t$，其中 $s\in S,t\in T$。两项都在并集中，而 $s+t=1s+1t$ 就是它们的有限线性组合，所以和集中的每个向量都属于并集的张成。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, take any vector in $\operatorname{span}(S\cup T)$. It is a finite sum of scalar multiples of vectors from the union. Assign each chosen vector to a subspace that contains it; if it belongs to both, put that term in either group, once. Collecting the terms gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，取 $\operatorname{span}(S\cup T)$ 中的任意向量。它是并集中向量的若干标量倍数相加得到的有限和。把每个选中的向量分到一个包含它的子空间；若它两边都属于，就任选一组放入，该项只计一次。归并后得到

$$
\underbrace{\alpha_1s_1+\cdots+\alpha_rs_r}_{s\in S}
+\underbrace{\beta_1t_1+\cdots+\beta_qt_q}_{t\in T}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r,q$ count the terms assigned to the two groups, and $\alpha_i,\beta_j$ are their scalar coefficients. Closure keeps the first group in $S$ and the second in $T$. If a group has no terms, use its zero vector. The whole result is therefore $s+t\in S+T$. We have proved both containments and hence equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r,q$ 分别是分到两组的项数，$\alpha_i,\beta_j$ 是对应的标量系数。封闭性保证第一组仍在 $S$ 中，第二组仍在 $T$ 中。若某组没有项，就取该子空间的零向量。整个结果于是写成 $s+t\in S+T$。两个方向都包含，便得到相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The smallest subspace containing both
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*“最小”说的是包含关系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity also characterizes $S+T$ without choosing any particular vectors. It is a subspace containing $S$ and $T$, and every other subspace $W$ containing both must contain $S+T$. Indeed, such a $W$ contains each $s\in S$ and $t\in T$, so its addition rule forces it to contain every $s+t$. “Smallest” here means contained in every other candidate, not a claim about counting points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个等式还给出一种不依赖具体向量选择的描述：$S+T$ 是包含 $S,T$ 的子空间，而且任何同时包含两者的其他子空间 $W$ 都必须包含 $S+T$。因为 $W$ 包含每个 $s\in S$ 和 $t\in T$，它的加法封闭性就迫使它包含每个 $s+t$。这里“最小”是指包含在每个其他候选子空间内，不是在比较点的个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the two coordinate axes, the union is only the axes, whereas its span—and hence their sum—is the whole plane. More generally, two distinct lines through zero span their plane; if the lines coincide, their sum stays that line. Whenever one subspace contains the other, the union is already the larger subspace and taking its span adds nothing. The [[子空间并集判据|union criterion]] tells exactly when this happens for two subspaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于两条坐标轴，并集只有两条轴，张成则是整个平面，所以和也等于整个平面。更一般地，两条不同的过零直线张成它们所在的平面；若直线重合，和仍是那条线。只要一个子空间包含另一个，并集本来就是较大的子空间，再取张成不会添加新向量。[[子空间并集判据]]恰好说明两个子空间何时出现这种情况。
