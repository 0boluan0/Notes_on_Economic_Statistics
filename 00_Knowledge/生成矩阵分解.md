# Generator decomposition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*生成矩阵分解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To describe a jump process, we need two answers at each state: **how long to wait**, and **where to go next**. A homogeneous [[CTMC定义|continuous-time Markov chain]] stores both answers in its [[生成矩阵约束|generator]] $Q=(q_{ij})$. Decomposing the generator separates the speed of the clock from the probabilities of destinations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

描述一个跳跃过程，需要在每个状态回答两件事：**等多久**，以及**下一步去哪里**。齐次[[CTMC定义|连续时间 Markov 链]]把两种信息都放进[[生成矩阵约束|生成矩阵]] $Q=(q_{ij})$。生成矩阵分解就是把时钟速度与去向概率分开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider states $A,B,C$, in that row and column order, with rates measured per hour:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如状态按 $A,B,C$ 的顺序排列，速率单位为每小时：

$$
Q=\begin{pmatrix}-5&2&3\\4&-4&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $A$, the rates toward $B$ and $C$ are $2$ and $3$. Their sum $5$ determines the waiting-time scale; their proportions $2/5$ and $3/5$ determine the next destination. From $B$, the only possible destination is $A$, at rate $4$. State $C$ has no outgoing rate and is absorbing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $A$ 去往 $B,C$ 的速率分别为 $2,3$，合计 $5$ 决定等待时间的尺度，比例 $2/5,3/5$ 决定下一次去向。从 $B$ 只能去 $A$，速率为 $4$。$C$ 没有任何离开速率，是吸收态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Separate one row into speed and choice

Assume a standard time-homogeneous pure-jump chain with finite or countable state space, nonnegative off-diagonal rates and finite [[出口率|exit rates]] $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}=-q_{ii}$. At an entry into a nonabsorbing state $i$, let $H$ be the time until departure and $J$ the state entered at that departure. The [[嵌入跳链|jump-chain]] probabilities $r_{ij}$ and [[CTMC指数停留时间|holding-time]] law are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把一行分成速度与选择*

假设是有限或可数状态空间上的标准时间齐次纯跳链，非对角速率非负，[[出口率]] $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}=-q_{ii}$ 有限。进入非吸收状态 $i$ 时，用 $H$ 表示到离开为止的等待，用 $J$ 表示离开后进入的状态。[[嵌入跳链]]的去向概率 $r_{ij}$ 与[[CTMC指数停留时间|停留时间]]规律为

$$
r_{ij}=\frac{q_{ij}}{q_i}\ (j\ne i),\qquad r_{ii}=0,
\qquad H\mid i\sim\operatorname{Exp}(q_i).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation “given $i$” means that the entry state has been specified. Conditional on this state, $H$ and $J$ are [[条件独立|independent]]: for every $s\ge0$ and $j\ne i$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“给定 $i$”指已经指定本次进入的状态。在这个条件下，$H$ 与 $J$ [[条件独立]]：对每个 $s\ge0$ 及 $j\ne i$，

$$
\Pr(H>s,J=j\mid i)=e^{-q_is}r_{ij}
=\Pr(H>s\mid i)\Pr(J=j\mid i).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the factorisation, first require no departure for $s$ units, which has probability $e^{-q_is}$. At that time the chain is still in $i$; the Markov and time-homogeneous restart gives the same eventual destination probabilities $r_{ij}$. At a random entry time this reasoning uses the [[CTMC强Markov性|strong Markov property]]. It holds before explosion. Mixing different entry states need not preserve independence, and the sequence of all waits need not be iid because the visited states can have different rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个分解可以这样理解：先要求 $s$ 个时间单位内不离开，概率为 $e^{-q_is}$；此时链仍在 $i$，Markov 性与时间齐次使后续重新具有相同的最终去向概率 $r_{ij}$。在随机进入时刻使用这一论证，需要[[CTMC强Markov性|强 Markov 性]]，结论用于爆炸之前。把不同进入状态混在一起，不一定保留独立性；整个等待序列也不一定独立同分布，因为访问状态的速率可能不同。

> [!example] Waiting does not select a destination / 等得久不改变本状态的去向比例
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In state $A$ above, $\mathbb E(H\mid A)=1/5$ hour. The chance of waiting longer than $1/5$ hour **and then** jumping to $B$ is $e^{-1}(2/5)\approx0.147$. Conditional on having waited that long, the next destination is still $B$ with probability $2/5$. The product describes one visit to a specified state, not unconditional independence across an entire trajectory.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上例在 $A$ 中有 $\mathbb E(H\mid A)=1/5$ 小时。等待超过 $1/5$ 小时，**随后**跳到 $B$ 的概率为 $e^{-1}(2/5)\approx0.147$。给定已经等了这么久，下一次去向为 $B$ 的概率仍是 $2/5$。这个乘积描述的是指定状态的一次访问，不是整条路径上的无条件独立性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reassemble the matrix

For a finite state space of size $m$, let $R=(r_{ij})$ be the completed jump-chain matrix. At an absorbing state, set $r_{ii}=1$ and the other entries in that row to zero; no division by $q_i=0$ is performed. Let $D=\operatorname{diag}(q_1,\ldots,q_m)$ be the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] of exit rates, with zero off-diagonal entries, and $I$ the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重新组合矩阵*

对有 $m$ 个状态的有限状态空间，用 $R=(r_{ij})$ 表示补全后的跳链矩阵。吸收态约定 $r_{ii}=1$、该行其余元素为零，不进行除以 $q_i=0$ 的运算。令 $D=\operatorname{diag}(q_1,\ldots,q_m)$ 为把出口率放在主对角、其余元素为零的[[对角矩阵]]，$I$ 为[[单位矩阵]]。于是

$$
\boxed{Q=D(R-I).}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Left multiplication by $D$ scales row $i$ by $q_i$. Its off-diagonal entries are $q_ir_{ij}=q_{ij}$. At a positive-rate state the diagonal is $q_i(0-1)=-q_i$; at a zero-rate state the entire row is zero. This verifies every entry. For a countable state space, the same rowwise identities apply; no claim about bounded infinite-dimensional operators is needed here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$D$ 在左侧相乘，使第 $i$ 行整体乘以 $q_i$。非对角元为 $q_ir_{ij}=q_{ij}$；正速率状态的对角元为 $q_i(0-1)=-q_i$，零速率状态的整行则为零，逐项都与 $Q$ 一致。可数状态空间中仍可逐行使用这些恒等式，这里不额外声称有关有界无限维算子的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the three-state example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在三状态例子中，

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}5&0&0\\0&4&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad R=\begin{pmatrix}0&2/5&3/5\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product gives the original $Q$. In particular, the completed self-loop at $C$ contributes $0(1-1)=0$, not a physical transition. If instead we multiply every exit rate by $10$, keeping $R$ and the initial law fixed, every holding-time distribution is shortened by a factor of $10$ and the state-order law is unchanged. The new process has the law of the original process run at time $10t$, before any explosion. Changing rates differently across states does not amount to one common clock rescaling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相乘就恢复原来的 $Q$。特别是 $C$ 的补全自环贡献为 $0(1-1)=0$，不代表真实转移。若保持 $R$ 和初始分布不变，把每个出口率都放大 $10$ 倍，则每段停留时间的分布都缩短为原来的十分之一，状态顺序的分布不变。爆炸之前，新过程与把原过程的时间改成 $10t$ 同分布。若对不同状态使用不同的速率倍数，就不是统一的时钟缩放。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a zero-rate state, waiting is infinite and there is no next genuine destination; the independence statement for $H,J$ above was restricted to $q_i>0$. A construction using [[竞争指数时钟|competing independent exponential clocks]] provides the same factorisation, but it is an alternative way to build the chain, not a requirement to assign a separate physical clock to every edge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零速率状态中，等待为无穷，也没有下一次真实去向；上面的 $H,J$ 独立性陈述已限定 $q_i>0$。用[[竞争指数时钟|相互独立的指数时钟竞争]]也能构造相同分解，但这只是构造链的一种方式，并不要求每条边都对应一个真实的物理时钟。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Berestycki and Sousi, Applied Probability, §1.6, pp. 12–15](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~ps422/notes-new2015.pdf#page=12): finite-rate $Q$-matrices, jump probabilities, absorption completion, independent exponential waiting construction and strong Markov property.
- [Whitt, Continuous-Time Markov Chains, §3.3, PDF pp. 12–13](https://www.columbia.edu/~ww2040/4106S11/CTMCchapter121906.pdf#page=12): the joint factorisation of waiting time and destination, with rate-normalised destination probabilities. The displayed matrix identity and numerical example are checked entry by entry from these definitions.
- [MIT 6.436J, Lecture 24, pp. 7–8](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-436j-fundamentals-of-probability-fall-2018/087af3cedbc9def5b156c5e1665ac79c_MIT6_436JF18_lec24.pdf#page=7): $q_{ij}=q_ir_{ij}$, the negative exit-rate diagonal, and the competing-clock construction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Berestycki–Sousi §1.6，第 12–15 页：有限出口率生成矩阵、跳转概率、吸收补全、独立指数等待构造及强 Markov 性。
- Whitt §3.3，PDF 第 12–13 页：等待时间与目的地的联合分解、按速率归一化的去向概率。矩阵恒等式及数值例由这些定义逐项核算。
- MIT 6.436J 第 24 讲第 7–8 页：$q_{ij}=q_ir_{ij}$、负出口率对角元及竞争时钟构造。
