# Changing the reference group
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*基准组重编码*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reference group is the starting level from which a categorical regression reports differences. Changing it rewrites the same group levels using a different starting point. The numbers in the coefficient table can change even when the predictions do not. The useful question is whether we have only changed the representation, or also changed which predictions the model is allowed to produce.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基准组是类别回归报告差异时的起点。更换基准组，是从另一个起点重新表达同一组水平。因此，系数表中的数字可以改变，而预测完全不变。关键在于区分：只是换了表达方式，还是同时改变了模型允许产生的预测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reconstruct the same three means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先还原同样的三个均值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the three group means be $\mu_A,\mu_B,\mu_C$. In [[多类别指示编码|reference-group coding]], an intercept gives the reference level and the other coefficients give differences from it. With $A$ as the reference, write the coefficients as $(\beta_0,\beta_B,\beta_C)$; with $B$ as the reference, use $(\tilde\beta_0,\tilde\beta_A,\tilde\beta_C)$. The tilde marks the new coordinates, not new data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设三组均值为 $\mu_A,\mu_B,\mu_C$。在[[多类别指示编码|基准组编码]]中，截距给基准水平，其他系数给相对它的差。以 $A$ 为基准时记系数为 $(\beta_0,\beta_B,\beta_C)$，以 $B$ 为基准时记为 $(\tilde\beta_0,\tilde\beta_A,\tilde\beta_C)$；波浪号表示新坐标，不是新数据。

> [!example] The same levels, different differences · 水平相同，差的起点不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $(\mu_A,\mu_B,\mu_C)=(20,26,35)$,
> $$\begin{aligned}
> (\beta_0,\beta_B,\beta_C)&=(20,6,15),\\
> (\tilde\beta_0,\tilde\beta_A,\tilde\beta_C)&=(26,-6,9).
> \end{aligned}$$
> The second coding reconstructs $A$ as $26-6=20$, $B$ as $26$, and $C$ as $26+9=35$. For an observed outcome $22$ in group $A$, either model predicts $20$ and leaves the same [[回归残差|residual]], $22-20=2$. A negative coefficient after recoding does not mean that the group's level changed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三组水平若为 $(20,26,35)$，两套系数如上。第二套把 $A$ 还原为 $26-6=20$，$B$ 为 $26$，$C$ 为 $26+9=35$。若 $A$ 组某个观察结果为 $22$，两种表达都预测 $20$，留下相同的[[回归残差]] $22-20=2$。换编码后系数变负，不表示该组水平发生了改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general the translation is
$$\tilde\beta_0=\beta_0+\beta_B,\qquad
\tilde\beta_A=-\beta_B,\qquad
\tilde\beta_C=\beta_C-\beta_B.$$
It is reversible: $\beta_0=\tilde\beta_0+\tilde\beta_A$, $\beta_B=-\tilde\beta_A$, and $\beta_C=\tilde\beta_C-\tilde\beta_A$. Thus neither representation loses a group level. This also explains why changing the education reference from high school to bachelor’s changes the intercept without dropping the bachelor’s observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般的翻译规则如上，而且可以逆向还原：$\beta_0=\tilde\beta_0+\tilde\beta_A$，$\beta_B=-\tilde\beta_A$，$\beta_C=\tilde\beta_C-\tilde\beta_A$。两种表达都没有丢失任何组水平。因此把学历基准从高中改为本科，会改变截距，却不会删去本科观察。

## Why ordinary least-squares fits stay unchanged
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么 OLS 拟合结果不变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A design matrix $X$ collects one row per observation and one column per included regressor. Its [[列空间|column space]] is the set of all prediction vectors $Xb$ obtainable by varying the coefficients $b$. For the same observed outcomes and unweighted, unconstrained [[普通最小二乘|OLS]], two designs with the same column space offer exactly the same candidate predictions. They therefore select the same unique closest fitted vector and the same residual vector. This remains true even if some coefficient representations are nonunique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设计矩阵 $X$ 的每行对应一个观察，每列对应一个已纳入的回归变量。它的[[列空间]]是系数 $b$ 变化时所有可能的预测向量 $Xb$。对相同结果数据使用未加权、无约束的[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]，两个设计若列空间相同，允许选择的预测就完全相同，因而选出同一个唯一的最近拟合向量，残差向量也相同。即使某些系数表示不唯一，拟合向量的结论仍成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both designs use full-column-rank bases for that same space, their coefficient coordinates are related by an invertible linear transformation. Ordinary complete reference-group recoding, with all included terms translated consistently, supplies such a change when the designs are full rank. Equality of column spaces alone should not be read as a claim that arbitrary redundant coefficient vectors are unique or have a bijective coordinate map. An empty group or another exact dependency still needs the [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|rank and estimability check]]. Here an estimable contrast means a combination whose value is determined by the model mean vector on the observed design: different coefficient representations of that vector must give the same contrast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两个设计都用满列秩的基来表示同一个空间，系数坐标之间可用可逆线性变换联系。通常的完整基准组重编码在各项一致转换、设计满秩时，正是这种换法。不能仅凭列空间相同，就把任意冗余系数向量都说成唯一，或认为它们必有一一对应的坐标变换。空组或其他精确依赖仍需检查[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|秩与可估性]]。这里的可估对比，是其数值由观测设计上的模型均值向量确定的组合：同一均值向量即使用不同系数表示，也必须给出相同的对比值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same contrast must ask the same question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*比较同一个问题，才有同一个推断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, the $C$-versus-$B$ contrast is
$$\delta=\mu_C-\mu_B=\beta_C-\beta_B=\tilde\beta_C=9.$$
The [[零假设与备择假设|null hypothesis]] $\delta=0$ asks the same question in both codings. But testing the old $C$ coefficient alone asks whether $C=A$, whereas testing the new $C$ coefficient alone asks whether $C=B$. Their estimates, standard errors and $p$-values need not agree: they are different contrasts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在例子中，$C$ 相对 $B$ 的对比是上式 $\delta=9$。[[零假设与备择假设|零假设]] $\delta=0$ 在两种编码下问的是同一个问题。但单独检验旧 $C$ 系数为零，是问 $C=A$；单独检验新 $C$ 系数为零，是问 $C=B$。这两项估计、标准误和 $p$ 值不必相同，因为对比已经换了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Uncertainty must be translated with the coefficients. Let $V$ be the finite covariance matrix of the estimated coefficient vector. In the old coding,
$$\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_C-\hat\beta_B)
=V_{CC}+V_{BB}-2V_{BC}.$$
The [[协方差|covariance]] $V_{BC}$ records how the two estimates fluctuate together. The same contrast has the same variance under the coherently transformed new matrix. If $V$ is unknown, replacing it by one complete covariance estimate produces the corresponding estimated variance, with the same transformation rule. For example, $V_{BB}=4$, $V_{CC}=9$, $V_{BC}=2$ give variance $9$ and standard error $3$. [[线性组合推断|Linear-combination inference]] supplies the general rule. Using the same covariance procedure and calibration preserves inference for the same estimable contrast; comparing differently defined coefficient rows does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数变化时，不确定性也要一起翻译。令 $V$ 是估计系数向量的有限协方差矩阵。旧编码下，差的方差如上；其中[[协方差]] $V_{BC}$ 记录两项估计怎样共同波动。新矩阵若按同一变换一致转换，同一对比的方差不变。若 $V$ 未知，用一整张协方差估计替换，就得到相应的估计方差；它遵守同样的转换规则。例如 $V_{BB}=4$、$V_{CC}=9$、$V_{BC}=2$，给出方差 $9$、标准误 $3$。[[线性组合推断]]给出一般规则；使用相同的协方差程序和校准时，同一可估对比的推断保持一致，但含义不同的系数行不能这样比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With [[类别类别交互|interactions between factors]], a main-effect coefficient describes a simple comparison within the reference level of the other factor. Changing that level changes the question asked by its coefficient test. A scientifically defined cell contrast or a joint test of the same restrictions can still be translated and kept unchanged. The label “main effect” alone does not specify that target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在[[类别类别交互|类别间交互]]时，某主效应系数描述另一因子的基准层内的简单比较。更换那一层，会改变该系数检验的问题。具有明确含义的单元格对比，或针对同一组限制的联合检验，仍可通过翻译而保持不变；单写“主效应”并没有充分说明目标。

> [!warning] Preserve the whole model · 必须保留完整模型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep the observations, outcome, fit criterion and allowed model terms the same. Recode all relevant interactions as well as their lower-order terms. Dropping a term, changing the analysed sample, or imposing a new coefficient restriction can change the prediction space. A penalty placed on particular coefficient coordinates may also change the fitted solution unless the penalty itself is transformed. Reference-group invariance is about representing the same model, not about arbitrary edits to a regression command.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要保持观察、结果、拟合准则及允许的模型项相同，并一起重编码相关交互和低阶项。删除某项、改变分析样本或施加新的系数约束，可能改变预测空间。若惩罚针对某套特定系数坐标，除非惩罚也随之转换，拟合解也可能改变。基准组不变性说的是同一模型的不同表达，不是任意改动回归命令都无影响。
