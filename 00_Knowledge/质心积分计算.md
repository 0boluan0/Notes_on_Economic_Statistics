# Calculating centres of mass by integration
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*质心积分计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[质心|centre of mass]] averages position using mass as its weight. To calculate it for a continuous object, choose small pieces whose mass and position can be described, add their contributions by integration, and divide the position-weighted total by the total mass. The main choice is **how to describe a piece**, not which integration formula to try first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[质心]]以质量为权重平均位置。计算连续物体的质心时，先选择质量和位置容易表达的小份材料，用积分累加它们的贡献，再把“位置乘质量”的总量除以总质量。关键先在于**怎样描述一小份材料**，再考虑使用哪种积分公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a rod on a finite interval $[a,b]$, $a<b$, with nonnegative [[定积分|Riemann-integrable]] linear density $\lambda(x)$, the small mass is $dm=\lambda(x)\,dx$. If its total mass is positive, the one-coordinate calculation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

细杆位于有限区间 $[a,b]$、$a<b$，线密度 $\lambda(x)$ 非负且[[定积分|黎曼可积]]，则小份质量为 $dm=\lambda(x)\,dx$。只要总质量为正，一个坐标的计算就是

$$M=\int_a^b\lambda(x)\,dx,\qquad
\bar x=\frac{\int_a^b x\lambda(x)\,dx}{M}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a planar object we need both coordinates. The first moment **about the $y$-axis** is $M_y=\int x\,dm$, because $x$ measures signed horizontal position relative to that axis. Similarly $M_x=\int y\,dm$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平面物体需要两个坐标。**关于 $y$ 轴**的一阶矩为 $M_y=\int x\,dm$，因为 $x$ 表示相对该轴的带符号水平位置；同理，$M_x=\int y\,dm$。因此

$$M=\int dm,\qquad \bar x=\frac{M_y}{M},\qquad\bar y=\frac{M_x}{M}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The axis subscript and coordinate letter cross over. These are first moments, with one power of a signed coordinate. Squaring distances would calculate a different quantity and would erase the signs needed to locate the centre.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

轴的下标与坐标字母是交叉对应的。这是一阶矩，只使用一次方的带符号坐标；改用距离平方会算出别的量，也会抹去定位质心所需要的正负号。

## A uniform sheet between two curves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两条曲线之间的均匀薄片*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the region be $a\le x\le b$ and $g(x)\le y\le f(x)$, where $a<b$ are finite, $f,g$ are continuous on $[a,b]$, and $f\ge g$. Assume its [[曲线间面积|area]] $A$ is positive and its area density is a positive constant $\rho$. A thin vertical strip at $x$ has width $dx$, height $f(x)-g(x)$ and centre height $[f(x)+g(x)]/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设区域为 $a\le x\le b$、$g(x)\le y\le f(x)$，其中 $a<b$ 有限，$f,g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续且 $f\ge g$。假设[[曲线间面积|区域面积]] $A>0$，单位面积的质量为正常数 $\rho$。在 $x$ 处取竖细条，宽为 $dx$，高为 $f(x)-g(x)$，中心高度为 $[f(x)+g(x)]/2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The strip contributes area $dA=(f-g)\,dx$ and mass $dm=\rho(f-g)\,dx$. Its horizontal coordinate is $x$; its mean vertical coordinate is the midpoint height. Multiplying these coordinates by the strip mass and adding all strips gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

细条贡献面积 $dA=(f-g)\,dx$、质量 $dm=\rho(f-g)\,dx$。它的水平坐标为 $x$，平均竖直坐标为中点高度。将这些坐标乘以细条质量，再累加所有细条，得到

$$M=\rho\int_a^b(f-g)\,dx,\qquad
M_y=\rho\int_a^b x(f-g)\,dx,$$
$$M_x=\rho\int_a^b\frac{f+g}{2}(f-g)\,dx
=\frac{\rho}{2}\int_a^b(f^2-g^2)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These differential expressions abbreviate limits of [[黎曼和|rectangle sums]] as the strips become thinner. Dividing by $M=\rho A$ cancels the constant density:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些微元表达式简写了细条越来越窄时[[黎曼和|矩形和]]的极限。再除以 $M=\rho A$，常密度就约去了：

> [!important] Uniform-lamina formulas · 均匀薄片公式
> $$A=\int_a^b(f-g)\,dx,\qquad
> \bar x=\frac{\int_a^b x(f-g)\,dx}{A},\qquad
> \bar y=\frac{\frac12\int_a^b(f^2-g^2)\,dx}{A}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use actual upper and lower boundaries and the same region in every integral. The factor $1/2$ in the vertical moment comes from the strip's average height; it is not an extra density factor.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个积分都必须使用同一区域的真实上、下边界。竖直一阶矩中的 $1/2$ 来自细条的平均高度，不是额外的密度因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula retains the correct sign below the axis. For a uniform rectangle $0\le x\le1$, $-3\le y\le-1$, its area is $2$ and its vertical area moment is $[(-1)^2-(-3)^2]/2=-4$. Hence $\bar y=-2$, exactly the midpoint height. Replacing the signed moment by an absolute value would put the answer on the wrong side of the axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区域在坐标轴下方时，公式仍保留正确符号。例如均匀矩形 $0\le x\le1$、$-3\le y\le-1$，面积为 $2$，竖直面积矩为 $[(-1)^2-(-3)^2]/2=-4$，所以 $\bar y=-2$，恰是中点高度。若擅自把一阶矩取绝对值，就会把答案放到坐标轴的另一侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose the easier slicing direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*选择更方便的切片方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Horizontal strips can be simpler. For finite $c<d$, suppose the region is $c\le y\le d$ with continuous left boundary $L(y)$ and right boundary $R(y)$ satisfying $L\le R$. Its width is $R-L$. Under the same continuity, uniform-density and positive-area assumptions,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

横切有时更简单。设 $c<d$ 有限，区域为 $c\le y\le d$，连续的左、右边界分别为 $L(y)$、$R(y)$，且 $L\le R$，则横条宽度就是 $R-L$。在同样的连续、均匀密度与正面积条件下，

$$A=\int_c^d(R-L)\,dy,\qquad
\bar y=\frac{\int_c^d y(R-L)\,dy}{A},\qquad
\bar x=\frac{\frac12\int_c^d(R^2-L^2)\,dy}{A}.$$

> [!example] A parabolic region · 抛物线围成的区域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $(x-1)^2\le y\le4$. The boundary intersection gives $-1\le x\le3$. Uniform density and reflection symmetry about $x=1$ give $\bar x=1$. To find $\bar y$, horizontal strips avoid squaring the parabola: for $0\le y\le4$, their endpoints are $1-\sqrt y$ and $1+\sqrt y$, so their width is $2\sqrt y$.
> $$A=\int_0^4 2\sqrt y\,dy=\frac{32}{3},\qquad
> \int_0^4 y\,2\sqrt y\,dy=\frac{128}{5},\qquad
> \bar y=\frac{128/5}{32/3}=\frac{12}{5}.$$
> Thus the centroid is $(1,12/5)$. The upper strips are wider, so they contribute more mass; the average height exceeds the halfway height $2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $(x-1)^2\le y\le4$。由边界相交得到 $-1\le x\le3$。密度均匀且区域关于 $x=1$ 对称，所以 $\bar x=1$。求 $\bar y$ 时，横切不用再把抛物线平方：在 $0\le y\le4$，左右端点为 $1-\sqrt y$ 与 $1+\sqrt y$，宽度是 $2\sqrt y$。面积 $A=32/3$，竖直面积矩为 $128/5$，因此 $\bar y=12/5$，形心为 $(1,12/5)$。上方细条更宽、质量更多，所以平均高度大于上下端点的中点高度 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a shape requires several disjoint pieces, add their masses and signed moments before dividing. Averaging their centre coordinates without weighting by the piece masses is generally incorrect. When upper/lower or left/right formulas change, split the corresponding integral at those changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若形状需要分成多个互不重叠的部分，应先累加各部分的质量与带符号一阶矩，再做除法。直接平均各部分的质心坐标而不按质量加权，通常不正确。上、下或左、右边界公式发生改变时，在相应位置分段积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## If density is not constant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*密度不恒定时怎样处理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If area density is $\rho(x)$, constant along each vertical strip but varying between strips, retain it in all three integrals: replace the common $\rho$ above by $\rho(x)$ inside each integrand. For example, on the unit square with $\rho(x)=2x$ in consistent units, $M=\int_0^1 2x\,dx=1$ and $M_y=\int_0^1 2x^2\,dx=2/3$, so $\bar x=2/3$ instead of the geometric midpoint $1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若面积密度为 $\rho(x)$，即每根竖条内部密度相同、不同竖条之间可以变化，就要把它保留在三个积分中：将前面的公共 $\rho$ 改成各个被积函数中的 $\rho(x)$。例如单位正方形上取相容单位下的 $\rho(x)=2x$，则 $M=\int_0^1 2x\,dx=1$、$M_y=\int_0^1 2x^2\,dx=2/3$，所以 $\bar x=2/3$，不是几何中点 $1/2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If density also varies along a strip, its mass centre need not be the geometric midpoint. Return to the actual mass element $dm$ and calculate the strip's mass and first moments before integrating across strips. Always retain nonnegative density, positive finite total mass and finite absolute first moments. Finally check units and symmetry of the mass distribution; these often expose a missing factor or wrong slice coordinate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若密度在一根细条内部也变化，细条的质量中心就未必是几何中点。此时应回到真实质量微元 $dm$，先求该条的质量和一阶矩，再跨细条积分。始终保留非负密度、正的有限总质量和有限绝对一阶矩条件。最后检查单位与质量分布的对称性，常能发现漏因子或切片坐标用错。
