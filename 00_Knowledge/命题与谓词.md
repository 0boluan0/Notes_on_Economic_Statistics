# Propositions and predicates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*命题与谓词*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“$2+3=5$” says something that is true. “$2+3=6$” says something that is false. Both are **propositions**: each makes a definite claim. By contrast, “$x+3=5$” leaves an input open. It is true for $x=2$ and false for $x=1$. We use a **predicate** to describe such a condition on inputs. Before proving a statement, determine whether its inputs and meaning have actually been fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“$2+3=5$”说了一件真事，“$2+3=6$”说了一件假事；两者都是**命题**，因为各自作出了确定的断言。“$x+3=5$”则留下一个尚未指定的输入：$x=2$ 时为真，$x=1$ 时为假。我们用**谓词**描述这种关于输入的条件。开始证明之前，先检查输入及表达式的含义是否已经确定。

> [!summary] The distinction · 核心区别
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A **proposition** is a statement with a definite truth value in its stated context. A **predicate** contains one or more unfixed variables; it becomes a proposition only after the domain is specified and the variables are substituted or bound by quantifiers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> **命题**是在给定语境中具有确定真值的陈述。**谓词**含有尚未固定的变量；只有在说明论域后代入变量，或用量词约束变量，它才形成命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A truth value is not the same as knowing it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有真值，不等于我们已经知道真值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card uses classical mathematical logic: a fully specified proposition is true or false, although we may not yet know which. A false statement is still a proposition; a question such as “What is $x$?” and an instruction such as “Choose an integer” are not assertions of truth. **Fixed context** includes the domain of objects, the meanings of the symbols, and any parameters referred to in the statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里使用经典数学逻辑：一个已经完全说明的命题有真或假的真值，但我们可能还不知道是哪一个。假陈述仍然是命题；“$x$ 是什么？”这样的问句，以及“选一个整数”这样的指令，都没有在断言某件事为真。**固定语境**包括对象的论域、符号的含义，以及陈述中涉及的参数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“The stock market will rise tomorrow” is not yet a fixed proposition when “tomorrow” and “the market” vary with context. Once the date, market, and index are specified, the statement can have a definite truth value even if we do not yet know it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
单独写“明天股市会上涨”时，“明天”和“股市”会随语境变化，尚不是固定命题；若改成指定日期、市场与指数的陈述，它可以具有确定真值，即使我们现在还不知道该真值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In mathematics, “the truth value is not known” and “the sentence has not yet been specified” are different problems. A well-defined claim may need a difficult proof. An ambiguous claim first needs its date, domain, variables, or terminology clarified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“还不知道真值”与“尚未把陈述说清楚”是两种不同的问题。一个含义确定的断言可能需要很难的证明；含糊的断言则应先说明日期、论域、变量或术语。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Evaluate inputs, or quantify over them
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*代入输入，或对输入作量化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $P(x)$ for the condition $x^2=4$, with $x$ allowed to be an integer. The letter $P$ names the condition; it is not an extra unknown number. Substituting $2$ gives the true proposition $P(2)$, while substituting $3$ gives the false proposition $P(3)$. Here $P(2)$ reports truth, whereas the numerical expression $2^2$ reports the number $4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
用 $P(x)$ 表示条件 $x^2=4$，并规定 $x$ 可以取整数。字母 $P$ 是这个条件的名字，不是另一个未知数。代入 $2$ 得到真命题 $P(2)$；代入 $3$ 得到假命题 $P(3)$。这里 $P(2)$ 表示真假，而数值表达式 $2^2$ 表示数 $4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Instead of choosing one input, a **quantifier** specifies how the condition is asserted over a domain. The symbol $\forall$ means “for every,” and $\exists$ means “there exists at least one.” The notation $x\in\mathbb Z$ says that $x$ belongs to the set of integers. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
不选定某个输入，也可以用**量词**说明在论域中怎样断言这个条件。符号 $\forall$ 表示“对每个”，$\exists$ 表示“至少存在一个”；$x\in\mathbb Z$ 表示 $x$ 属于整数集合。因此

$$
\forall x\in\mathbb Z:P(x)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\exists x\in\mathbb Z:P(x)
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
are two propositions. The first is false because $P(0)$ is false; the second is true because $P(2)$ is true. Neither statement asks us to leave $x$ as an unspecified input: its quantifier tells us how to consider all allowed values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
是两个命题。第一个为假，因为 $P(0)$ 为假；第二个为真，因为 $P(2)$ 为真。它们都没有把 $x$ 留作尚待指定的输入：量词已经规定了应怎样考虑允许的取值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $x^2=4$ is a predicate while $x$ remains unspecified. Over the integers, “$2^2=4$” is a proposition, and so is $\exists x\in\mathbb Z:x^2=4$. The domain cannot be left ambiguous: $\exists x:x^2=2$ is true over the reals but false over the rationals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，$x^2=4$ 没有指定 $x$ 时是谓词。若论域为整数，则“$2^2=4$”是命题，$\exists x\in\mathbb Z:x^2=4$ 也是命题。论域不能省得不明不白：同一句 $\exists x:x^2=2$ 在实数中为真，在有理数中为假。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb R$ is the real-number domain and $\mathbb Q$ the rational-number domain, whose elements are ratios of integers with nonzero denominator. The domain change in the last example matters: the real number $\sqrt2$ is available in $\mathbb R$, while its absence from $\mathbb Q$ is proved in the [[反证法|irrationality example]]. With several variables, the [[量词次序|order of quantifiers]] also matters; binding all inputs does not make different quantified statements equivalent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $\mathbb R$ 是实数论域，$\mathbb Q$ 是有理数论域；有理数可写成分母非零的整数比。上例中的论域改变确实重要：实数 $\sqrt2$ 在 $\mathbb R$ 中，而它不属于 $\mathbb Q$ 的证明见[[反证法|无理性例子]]。有多个变量时，[[量词次序]]也会影响含义；即使输入全部受到约束，不同的量化陈述也未必等价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check every remaining free variable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*检查每个仍然自由的变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A variable occurrence is **bound** when it is governed by the corresponding quantifier; an occurrence left outside such a binding is **free**. Specifying one variable does not automatically specify the others. Let $R(x,y)$ mean $x<y$, with both inputs in $D=\{0,1\}$. After substituting $x=0$, $R(0,y)$ still has the free input $y$. It is false at $y=0$ and true at $y=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个变量出现处如果受到相应量词的约束，就称为**约束出现**；没有受到这种约束的出现处称为**自由出现**。指定一个变量，不会自动指定其他变量。令 $R(x,y)$ 表示 $x<y$，两输入都取自 $D=\{0,1\}$。即使先代入 $x=0$，$R(0,y)$ 仍留下自由输入 $y$：$y=0$ 时为假，$y=1$ 时为真。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, $\exists x\in D:R(x,y)$ binds $x$ but leaves $y$ free. It is false at $y=0$ and true at $y=1$. Adding an outer quantifier for $y$ produces a closed statement: $\forall y\in D\,\exists x\in D:R(x,y)$ is false, whereas $\exists y\in D\,\exists x\in D:R(x,y)$ is true. To obtain a fully specified proposition from an open condition, supply values for **all** remaining free inputs or bind them all, while keeping the interpretation fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
同样，$\exists x\in D:R(x,y)$ 约束了 $x$，却仍留下自由变量 $y$；它在 $y=0$ 时为假，在 $y=1$ 时为真。再为 $y$ 加外层量词，就得到闭合的陈述：$\forall y\in D\,\exists x\in D:R(x,y)$ 为假，而 $\exists y\in D\,\exists x\in D:R(x,y)$ 为真。要从开放条件得到完全说明的命题，应给**全部**剩余自由输入赋值，或全部加以量化，同时固定解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Predicate” does not require the truth value to change between inputs. For example, $x=x$ is true for every allowed $x$, yet the displayed formula still has a free variable. Its universal closure $\forall x\in D:x=x$ explicitly binds that variable. Nor does binding numerical variables fix an undefined relation symbol: the meaning of a name such as $Likes(x,y)$ must also have been specified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“谓词”不要求换一个输入就必须改变真值。例如 $x=x$ 对每个允许的 $x$ 都成立，但这个写出的公式仍有自由变量；其全称闭包 $\forall x\in D:x=x$ 才显式约束该变量。同样，只约束数值变量，也不会自动定义关系符号：例如 $Likes(x,y)$ 这个名称究竟表示什么，仍须事先说明。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A formula may contain letters while all its variables are already bound by quantifiers. The presence of a variable symbol alone does not make it merely a predicate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个公式即使含字母，也可能已经用量词把所有变量约束起来；不能只凭“看见变量”就判定它不是命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The introductory first-order convention here uses a stated nonempty domain, as in the cited course. Truth, proof, and a writer's knowledge of the truth remain separate: the [[全称命题与反例|examples-versus-proof distinction]] explains why several successful inputs do not establish a universal claim. The present card identifies what is being asserted; it does not certify that the assertion has been proved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里沿用所引课程的入门一阶逻辑约定，使用明确的非空论域。真假、证明以及作者是否知道真值仍是不同问题；[[全称命题与反例]]说明了为什么若干成功输入不能证明全称断言。本卡辨认正在断言什么，并不因此宣布断言已经得到证明。

> [!question]- Apply the distinction · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over the integers, is $P(x): x$ is even a proposition or a predicate? What about $\forall x\,P(x)$?
>
> **Answer:** $P(x)$ is a predicate because $x$ is free. $\forall x\,P(x)$ is a proposition, although it is false.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在整数论域中，$P(x): x$ 为偶数是命题还是谓词？$\forall x\,P(x)$ 呢？
>
> **答案：** $P(x)$ 是谓词，因为 $x$ 尚未固定；$\forall x\,P(x)$ 是命题，虽然它是假命题。
