# Inner product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*内积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **inner product** compares two vectors and returns one number. When both inputs are the same vector, that number gives its squared length. When the inputs differ, the comparison helps describe alignment and perpendicularity. The familiar [[向量点积|dot product]], which multiplies matching real coordinates and adds the results, is one example. An inner product specifies the geometry of a space; choosing different weights can change that geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**内积**把两个向量变成一个数。当两个输入是同一向量时，这个数给出它的长度平方；输入不同时，比较结果帮助描述方向的对齐与垂直。熟悉的[[向量点积]]把实坐标逐项相乘再相加，是内积的一个例子。内积规定空间的几何，选取不同权重可以改变这套几何。

> [!example] One comparison, and two lengths / 一次比较与两个长度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For real coordinate vectors $x=(3,4)$ and $y=(2,-1)$, the usual dot product gives $\langle x,y\rangle=3\cdot2+4(-1)=2$. Self-comparisons give $\langle x,x\rangle=25$ and $\langle y,y\rangle=5$, so their lengths are $5$ and $\sqrt5$. The comparison value $2$ is neither a length nor a normalized similarity score.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实坐标向量 $x=(3,4)$、$y=(2,-1)$ 的通常点积为 $\langle x,y\rangle=2$。自身比较得到 $\langle x,x\rangle=25$、$\langle y,y\rangle=5$，长度分别为 $5$、$\sqrt5$。比较值 $2$ 本身既不是长度，也不是经过归一化的相似度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What makes the rule an inner product?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样的规则才叫内积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[向量空间|vector space]] allows addition and scalar multiplication. On such a space $V$ over the real numbers $\mathbb R$ or complex numbers $\mathbb C$, an inner product assigns a scalar $\langle x,y\rangle$ to every pair $x,y\in V$. The angle brackets name this operation. We use the convention that the **first argument is conjugate-linear and the second is linear**, matching the coordinate formula $x^*y$. Here a bar means complex conjugation, which changes $a+bi$ to $a-bi$ for real $a,b$ and $i^2=-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[向量空间]]允许向量相加和乘以标量。对实数域 $\mathbb R$ 或复数域 $\mathbb C$ 上的空间 $V$，内积为每对 $x,y\in V$ 指定标量 $\langle x,y\rangle$，尖括号表示这项运算。本卡采用**第一个自变量共轭线性、第二个自变量线性**的约定，与坐标公式 $x^*y$ 一致。横线表示复共轭：对实数 $a,b$，把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule must satisfy three requirements. A self-product is a nonnegative real number and is zero only for the zero vector. Swapping the arguments conjugates the answer. Finally, adding or scaling the second input has the corresponding effect on the result. For all $x,y,z\in V$ and scalars $\alpha,\beta$, these requirements are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这项规则须满足三个要求：自身内积是非负实数，而且只有零向量的自身内积为零；交换两个自变量会让结果取共轭；对第二个输入相加、缩放，会对应地改变结果。对所有 $x,y,z\in V$ 和标量 $\alpha,\beta$，要求写成

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\langle x,x\rangle\ge0,\qquad \langle x,x\rangle=0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ x=0,\\
&\langle x,y\rangle=\overline{\langle y,x\rangle},\\
&\langle x,\alpha y+\beta z\rangle
=\alpha\langle x,y\rangle+\beta\langle x,z\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conjugate symmetry and second-slot linearity imply the first-slot rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由共轭对称与第二个自变量的线性，得到第一个自变量的运算规则

$$
\langle \alpha x+\beta y,z\rangle
=\overline\alpha\langle x,z\rangle+\overline\beta\langle y,z\rangle.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over $\mathbb R$, conjugation does nothing: the operation is symmetric and linear in both arguments. Some books instead make the first argument linear. Either convention is valid, but coefficients and conjugates must follow the chosen one consistently. An **inner-product space** is the vector space together with its chosen inner product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实数域，共轭不改变数，所以运算是对称的，且对两个自变量都线性。有些教材约定第一个自变量线性；两种约定都有效，但系数及共轭位置必须始终一致。**内积空间**就是向量空间连同选定的内积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why complex coordinates need conjugation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复坐标为什么要取共轭*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x,y\in\mathbb C^n$, the standard inner product is $x^*y=\sum_{j=1}^n\overline{x_j}y_j$, where $^*$ is [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose, then conjugate every entry. Its self-product is $\sum_j|x_j|^2$, a sum of nonnegative real squared moduli. The ordinary transpose $x^T$ alone does not do this.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x,y\in\mathbb C^n$，标准内积是 $x^*y=\sum_{j=1}^n\overline{x_j}y_j$，其中 $^*$ 表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]：先转置，再对每个元素取共轭。自身内积为 $\sum_j|x_j|^2$，是非负实数的模平方和；仅用普通转置 $x^T$ 做不到这一点。

> [!example] The comparison may be complex / 不同向量的内积可以是复数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $z=(1+i,2)^T$ and $w=(i,1)^T$. Then $z^*z=|1+i|^2+4=6$, while $z^Tz=(1+i)^2+4=4+2i$, which cannot be a squared length. Also $z^*w=3+i$ and $w^*z=3-i$. Positivity is required for self-products; it does not require every comparison of different vectors to be real or positive.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $z=(1+i,2)^T$、$w=(i,1)^T$，则 $z^*z=6$，但 $z^Tz=4+2i$，不能作为长度平方。另有 $z^*w=3+i$、$w^*z=3-i$。正定性要求的是自身内积；不同向量的内积并不必须为实数或正数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Changing weights changes the geometry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*改变权重，就改变几何*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb R^2$, another valid inner product is $\langle x,y\rangle_M=2x_1y_1+x_2y_2$. The first coordinate receives twice the weight. For the opening vectors it gives $8$ instead of $2$, and the squared length of $(3,4)$ becomes $2\cdot9+16=34$. Every weight is positive, so the self-product can vanish only when both coordinates vanish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中，另一个有效内积是 $\langle x,y\rangle_M=2x_1y_1+x_2y_2$，第一坐标获得两倍权重。对开头两个向量，结果由 $2$ 变为 $8$；$(3,4)$ 的长度平方则变为 $2\cdot9+16=34$。由于每个权重为正，自身内积只有在两个坐标都为零时才消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, a real symmetric or complex Hermitian [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]] $M$ of size $n\times n$ defines $\langle x,y\rangle_M=x^*My$ on $\mathbb F^n$, where $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Hermitian means $M^*=M$; positive definite means $x^*Mx>0$ for every nonzero $x$. These supply conjugate symmetry and strict positivity, while matrix multiplication supplies linearity. The usual coordinate inner product uses $M=I_n$, the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，$n\times n$ 的实对称或复 Hermitian [[正定矩阵]] $M$ 在 $\mathbb F^n$ 上定义 $\langle x,y\rangle_M=x^*My$，其中 $\mathbb F$ 为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。Hermitian 指 $M^*=M$，正定指每个非零 $x$ 都满足 $x^*Mx>0$；前者提供共轭对称，后者提供严格正性，矩阵乘法提供线性。通常坐标内积使用 $M=I_n$，即[[单位矩阵]]。

> [!warning] Nonnegative alone is insufficient / 仅仅非负还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The rule $x_1y_1$ on $\mathbb R^2$ assigns self-product zero to the nonzero vector $(0,1)$, so it is not an inner product on that space. The rule $x_1y_1-x_2y_2$ is also invalid: the same vector has self-product $-1$. Symmetry alone does not provide a length geometry.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 上，规则 $x_1y_1$ 给非零向量 $(0,1)$ 的自身内积为零，因此不是该空间上的内积。规则 $x_1y_1-x_2y_2$ 也不合格：同一向量的自身内积为 $-1$。单有对称性不能提供长度几何。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Length and perpendicularity use this same choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*长度与垂直共用同一套选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The induced length is $\|x\|=\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$. Two vectors are [[正交|orthogonal]] when their inner product is zero. Both depend on the same chosen rule: $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ are orthogonal for the standard dot product but have weighted inner product $1$ under the [[对角矩阵|diagonal weight matrix]] $M=\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$. Fix the inner product before interpreting lengths, angles or nearest-point projections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由内积得到的长度是 $\|x\|=\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$；两个向量的内积为零时称为[[正交]]。二者都取决于同一个选定规则：$(1,1)$、$(1,-1)$ 在标准点积下正交，但在[[对角矩阵|对角权重矩阵]] $M=\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$ 下的加权内积为 $1$。解释长度、夹角或最近点投影之前，须先固定内积。
