# Euclid's lemma
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*素数整除乘积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a product is divisible by $5$, at least one of its integer factors must already be divisible by $5$. This is a special feature of a [[素数|prime]] divisor. A composite divisor can be assembled from different factors: $6$ divides $2\cdot3$ even though it divides neither $2$ nor $3$. Euclid’s lemma makes the prime case precise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一个乘积能被 $5$ 整除，那么它的整数因子中至少有一个已经能被 $5$ 整除。这是除数为[[素数]]时的特殊性质。合数除数却可能由不同因子共同凑成：$6$ 整除 $2\cdot3$，却既不整除 $2$，也不整除 $3$。Euclid 引理准确说明了素数情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that [[整除|divisibility]] $p\mid m$ means $m=pk$ for some integer $k$. The hypothesis concerns the product $ab$; the conclusion concerns the separate factors. No positivity assumption is imposed on $a$ or $b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先回忆[[整除]]：$p\mid m$ 表示存在整数 $k$ 使 $m=pk$。前提谈整个乘积 $ab$，结论谈其中的单个因子；这里不要求 $a,b$ 为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The statement and the meaning of “or”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*结论以及“或”的含义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[素数|prime]] $p$ and integers $a,b$, Euclid's lemma states:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $p$ 是[[素数]]，$a,b$ 是整数。Euclid 引理断言：

$$p\mid ab\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p\mid a\ \text{或}\ p\mid b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here “or” means **at least one**, allowing both. For example, $3$ divides both factors in $6\cdot9$. A zero factor also causes no exception: $p\mid0$ because $0=p\cdot0$. Negative factors are allowed by the integer-multiple definition; for example, $5\mid(-6)\cdot10$ and $5\mid10$, even though $5\nmid-6$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“或”表示**至少一个**，也允许两者都成立，例如 $3$ 同时整除 $6\cdot9$ 的两个因子。某个因子为零也不例外：由 $0=p\cdot0$ 可知 $p\mid0$。整数倍定义同样允许负因子，例如 $5\mid(-6)\cdot10$ 且 $5\mid10$，虽然 $5\nmid-6$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why Bézout proves the result
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么 Bézout 等式能证明它*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[最大公因数|gcd]] is the greatest positive common divisor. If $p\nmid a$, its value for $(p,a)$ must be $1$: it is a positive divisor of the prime $p$, hence either $1$ or $p$, and the latter would divide $a$. The pair is not both zero because $p>1$. [[Bézout等式|Bézout’s identity]] then guarantees integer coefficients $x,y$ expressing this gcd as $xp+ya$. These are coefficients, not new restrictions on the factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[最大公因数]]是最大的正公因数。若 $p\nmid a$，$(p,a)$ 的 gcd 必须为 $1$：它是素数 $p$ 的正因子，只能为 $1$ 或 $p$，而后一种会使 $p\mid a$。由于 $p>1$，这一对输入不全为零。[[Bézout等式]]于是保证存在整数系数 $x,y$，把 gcd 写成 $xp+ya$；它们是组合系数，不是对因子的额外限制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $p\mid a$, the conclusion already holds. Otherwise $\gcd(p,a)=1$. [[Bézout等式|Bézout's identity]] gives $xp+ya=1$; multiplying by $b$ yields $xpb+yab=b$. Both terms on the left are divisible by $p$, so $p\mid b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $p\mid a$，已经得到结论。否则 $\gcd(p,a)=1$；由[[Bézout等式]]写 $xp+ya=1$，乘以 $b$ 得 $xpb+yab=b$。左侧两项都被 $p$ 整除，所以 $p\mid b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the last divisibility step, use the original hypothesis explicitly: write $ab=pk$ with $k\in\mathbb Z$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一步可以把原前提明确代入：由 $p\mid ab$ 写成 $ab=pk$，其中 $k\in\mathbb Z$，于是

$$b=xpb+yab=xpb+ypk=p(xb+yk).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The multiplier $xb+yk$ is an integer, which is exactly the witness required for $p\mid b$. Thus both cases are proved. We never divided by $a$, $b$ or a possibly zero quantity, and we did not assume unique prime factorization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数 $xb+yk$ 是整数，恰好给出了 $p\mid b$ 所需的见证。两种情况都已证明；过程中没有除以 $a$、$b$ 或任何可能为零的量，也没有假设唯一素因数分解。

> [!example] Turn one into the required factor · 把一变成所需因子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $p=5$, $a=6$, $b=10$. Since $-1\cdot5+1\cdot6=1$, multiplication by $10$ gives $-50+60=10$. Here $ab=60=5\cdot12$, so the final integer multiplier is $(-1)10+1\cdot12=2$: $10=5\cdot2$. The same argument works for arbitrary allowed inputs; this example displays its coefficients.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $p=5$、$a=6$、$b=10$。由 $-1\cdot5+1\cdot6=1$，乘以 $10$ 得 $-50+60=10$。这里 $ab=60=5\cdot12$，最终整数系数为 $(-1)10+1\cdot12=2$，即 $10=5\cdot2$。一般证明适用于所有允许的输入，这个例子把系数具体展示出来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## More than two factors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多于两个因子时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Primality is essential: $6\mid2\cdot3$, but $6$ divides neither factor. For a prime, repeated application extends the result to any finite product. This lets [[唯一素因数分解|unique prime factorization]] match factors one at a time. Using unique factorization to prove this lemma, then the lemma to prove unique factorization, would be circular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“素数”是关键条件：$6\mid2\cdot3$，但 $6$ 既不整除 $2$，也不整除 $3$。对素数则可反复使用引理，得到“整除有限个整数的乘积，就整除其中某一个”。这使[[唯一素因数分解]]能够逐一匹配两边的素因子。证明本引理时不能反过来先假设唯一分解，否则会循环论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For completeness, let $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ be integers with $n\ge1$. When $n=1$, the conclusion is simply the hypothesis $p\mid a_1$. Suppose the result is proved for $n$ factors and $p\mid(a_1\cdots a_n)a_{n+1}$. The two-factor lemma gives either $p\mid a_{n+1}$, which finishes the proof, or $p\mid a_1\cdots a_n$, to which the induction hypothesis applies. Therefore some factor is divisible by $p$. If one uses an empty product, its value is $1$, which no prime divides, so the hypothesis would be false.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整写出推广：令 $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ 为整数，$n\ge1$。当 $n=1$ 时，结论就是前提 $p\mid a_1$。假设对 $n$ 个因子已成立，且 $p\mid(a_1\cdots a_n)a_{n+1}$。两因子引理给出两种情况：若 $p\mid a_{n+1}$，已经完成；若 $p\mid a_1\cdots a_n$，就对前 $n$ 个因子使用归纳假设。因此总有一个因子被 $p$ 整除。若采用空乘积，其值为 $1$，而任何素数都不整除 $1$，所以这时前提为假。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The claim identifies at least one divisible factor; it does not say that the entire power of a prime in the product must lie in one factor. For instance, $2^2\mid2\cdot2$, but $2^2$ divides neither factor. The dependency here is gcd and Bézout first, then Euclid’s lemma, then the uniqueness proof in [[唯一素因数分解|unique prime factorization]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结论指出至少一个因子能被该素数整除，并没有说乘积中某个素数的全部幂次必须集中在一个因子里。例如 $2^2\mid2\cdot2$，但 $2^2$ 不整除任何一个因子。这里的依赖顺序是先有 gcd 与 Bézout 等式，再证明 Euclid 引理，最后用于[[唯一素因数分解]]的唯一性证明。
