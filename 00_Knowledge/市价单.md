# 市价单是不由委托人指定成交限价而请求按可得价格执行的委托

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A market order requests execution at available prices without a limit price specified by the investor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡所说的普通市价单，是投资者指定买卖方向和数量、但不另设买入最高价或卖出最低价，请求按可得价格执行的委托。它通常着重尽快执行，而不像[[限价单]]那样由委托人约束最差成交价。具体名为“市价”的委托种类仍须按交易场所的定义识别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary market order, as used here, specifies side and quantity but no investor-set maximum purchase price or minimum sale price, requesting execution at available prices. It normally emphasises prompt execution, unlike a [[限价单|limit order]] with an investor-set worst price. A venue's specifically named market-order types must still be read under that venue's definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“不指定限价”不表示场所没有价格保护、停牌或订单接纳条件，也不保证立即、全部或必定成交。没有合格对手量、交易暂停或触及保护边界时，结果须按规则处理；最后成交价和提交时看到的最佳报价都不是成交承诺。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No investor-set limit does not mean that venue price protection, halts or acceptance conditions disappear. Immediate, complete or eventual execution is not guaranteed. Missing eligible liquidity, a pause or a protection boundary requires rule-specific handling. Neither the last trade nor the quote seen when submitting the order is an execution promise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若静态卖盘为 100.00 元 50 股、100.20 元 100 股，在允许逐档执行且没有其他约束的假设下，买入 120 股会取 50 股和 70 股，[[逐档成交均价|均价]]约为 100.1167 元，而不是把 120 股全按最佳卖价 100.00 元计算。这个推演不保证真实订单到达时的簿况仍相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a static ask book of 50 shares at 100.00 and 100 at 100.20, assuming execution across levels is permitted without other constraints, a 120-share purchase takes 50 and 70 shares. Its [[逐档成交均价|average fill price]] is about 100.1167, not 100.00 for all shares. This calculation does not guarantee the same book when a live order arrives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
