# Two-sided limits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*双侧极限判定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An input can approach a real number from smaller values or from larger values. A two-sided limit asks whether **both approaches lead to the same output**. For example, if a function stays at $1$ just to the left of $0$ and at $3$ just to the right, moving closer to $0$ does not resolve the disagreement. There is no single limiting output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入可以从较小的数靠近一个实数，也可以从较大的数靠近。双侧极限要判断：**这两种趋近是否走向同一个输出**。例如，函数在 $0$ 左边一直取 $1$，右边一直取 $3$，即使输入继续靠近 $0$，两边也不会达成一致，因此没有一个共同的极限值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that a [[函数极限|function limit]] tests every sufficiently close allowed input other than the target point itself. Write the domain as $D\subseteq\mathbb R$, the target input as $a$, and a proposed finite output limit as $L$. The symbols
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[函数极限]]检查的是：除目标点本身以外，所有足够近的合法输入。把定义域记为 $D\subseteq\mathbb R$，目标输入记为 $a$，候选的有限输出极限记为 $L$。记号

$$\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x),\qquad \lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
mean approaching through $x\in D$ with $x<a$ and with $x>a$, respectively. The minus and plus signs describe the **side of the input**, not the sign of the output. Each side must contain inputs arbitrarily close to $a$; otherwise that one-sided limit is not defined under the cluster-point convention used here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分别表示从 $D$ 中满足 $x<a$ 和 $x>a$ 的输入趋近。上标减号、加号表示**输入在哪一侧**，不是输出的正负。相应一侧必须确实有可以任意靠近 $a$ 的输入；否则，按这里采用的聚点约定，不定义该侧极限。

> [!important] Agreement of the two sides · 两侧必须一致
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f:D\to\mathbb R$ and $a$ is a cluster point from **both** sides: for every $r>0$, each of $D\cap(a-r,a)$ and $D\cap(a,a+r)$ is nonempty. For a finite real number $L$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $f:D\to\mathbb R$，并且 $a$ 是定义域的**双侧**聚点：对每个 $r>0$，$D\cap(a-r,a)$ 与 $D\cap(a,a+r)$ 都非空。对有限实数 $L$，
>
> $$\lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D}}f(x)=L
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \lim_{x\to a^-}f(x)=L\ \text{and}\ \lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)=L.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The domain need not contain an entire interval around $a$; it must supply arbitrarily close inputs on each side. Whether $a$ itself belongs to $D$ is irrelevant to this limit test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定义域不必包含 $a$ 周围的整个区间，只需在每一侧都有任意近的输入。$a$ 本身是否属于 $D$，不影响这个极限判据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why one neighbourhood can control both sides
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么能用同一个邻域控制两侧*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the full limit is $L$, its guarantee already covers all nearby domain points. Testing only the left side or only the right side therefore gives the same limit.

Conversely, request any output tolerance $\varepsilon>0$. The left limit supplies an input radius $\delta_->0$, and the right limit supplies $\delta_+>0$. Choose $\delta=\min\{\delta_-,\delta_+\}$. Every $x\in D$ with $0<|x-a|<\delta$ lies on one of the two sides and satisfies that side's guarantee, so $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$. This is exactly the full limit definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若完整极限为 $L$，它的保证已经覆盖了附近所有定义域点，因此只检查左侧或右侧，仍然得到同一个极限。

反过来，任取输出容差 $\varepsilon>0$。左极限给出输入半径 $\delta_->0$，右极限给出 $\delta_+>0$。取 $\delta=\min\{\delta_-,\delta_+\}$。每个满足 $0<|x-a|<\delta$ 的 $x\in D$，必定位于其中一侧，并符合该侧的保证，因此 $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$。这正是完整极限的定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A jump and a successful match
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=1$ when $x<0$ and $f(x)=3$ when $x>0$, the one-sided limits are $1$ and $3$. They exist separately but disagree, so the two-sided limit does not exist. Even assigning $f(0)=2$ cannot bridge the gap: [[点值不决定极限|a single point value does not change nearby limits]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $x<0$ 时 $f(x)=1$，$x>0$ 时 $f(x)=3$。左右极限分别为 $1$ 与 $3$，各自存在，却不相等，所以双侧极限不存在。即使设 $f(0)=2$ 也填不平这个差距，因为[[点值不决定极限|单点取值不改变附近的极限]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For comparison, define $g(x)=2+x$ on the left of $0$ and $g(x)=2-x$ on the right. Both sides tend to $2$, since in either case $|g(x)-2|=|x|$. Choosing $\delta=\varepsilon$ works on both sides. Thus $\lim_{x\to0}g(x)=2$, even if $g(0)$ is left undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作为对照，令 $g(x)$ 在 $0$ 左侧等于 $2+x$，右侧等于 $2-x$。两侧都趋于 $2$，因为无论哪一侧都有 $|g(x)-2|=|x|$；取 $\delta=\varepsilon$ 就同时控制了两侧。因此 $\lim_{x\to0}g(x)=2$，即使没有定义 $g(0)$ 也成立。

## Endpoints and other boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,\infty)$, approaching $0$ through the domain means approaching from the right. There are no negative inputs to test, so requiring a left limit would apply the two-sided criterion outside its assumptions. At an isolated domain point, there are no other domain inputs arbitrarily close at all; the cluster-point definition does not assign a punctured limit there. [[一点连续|Continuity at a point]] handles endpoints and isolated points directly through its own definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于定义在 $[0,\infty)$ 上的 $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$，从定义域内靠近 $0$ 就是从右侧靠近。没有负输入可供检查，强求左极限就超出了双侧判据的前提。若是定义域的孤立点，附近根本没有任意近的其他定义域点，按聚点定义就不在该处赋予去心极限。[[一点连续]]通过自身定义处理端点和孤立点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card concerns **finite real limits**. Unbounded outputs use the distinct definitions in [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|infinite limits and limits at infinity]]; the theorem above should not be read as declaring infinity a real value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡讨论的是**有限实数极限**。输出无界时使用[[无穷极限与无穷远极限]]中的另一套定义，不能把上面的定理读成“无穷也是一个实数值”。
