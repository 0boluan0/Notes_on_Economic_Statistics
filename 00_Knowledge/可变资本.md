# 可变资本是用于购买劳动力并由其使用再生产自身等价物和剩余价值的资本部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Variable capital purchases labour-power whose use produces its equivalent and surplus value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可变资本（variable capital）是资本用于购买 [[Marx劳动力|劳动力]] 的部分：生产中使用这种劳动力的活劳动，既创造相当于劳动力自身价值的等价物，也可以创造由资本占有的 [[剩余价值]]。在剩余生产的分析中，前者记作 $v$，后者记作 $s$，活劳动形成的新价值为 $v+s$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variable capital is the part of capital used to purchase [[Marx劳动力|labour-power]]. Living labour, the use of that capacity in production, creates an equivalent of the value of labour-power and can also create [[剩余价值|surplus value]] appropriated by capital. In the analysis of surplus production, the equivalent is denoted by $v$ and the surplus by $s$, so living labour creates new value of $v+s$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“可变”描述这一资本部分在增殖过程中的作用。用于购买劳动力的货币可以是一个确定数额；进入生产后发挥作用的是劳动力的使用。工资金额保持不变，也仍然属于可变资本。它与 [[不变资本]] 的区别，在于购买的要素如何参与价值形成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Variable” describes this part of capital's role in valorisation. The money spent on labour-power may be a definite sum; what operates in production is the use of labour-power. A wage payment of unchanged size still counts as variable capital. The distinction from [[不变资本|constant capital]] concerns how the purchased factors participate in forming value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作一个教学构造：劳动力一天按其400单位价值出售，工人在正常且不变的强度下劳动8小时，平均社会劳动每小时表现为100价值单位。新价值800中，400补回 $v$ 的等价物，另400为 $s$。价值单位、劳动折算和劳动力按价值支付的条件必须一致，才能这样计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a constructed example, suppose a day's labour-power sells at its value of 400 units. The worker works eight hours at normal, unchanged intensity, and each hour of average social labour is expressed in 100 value units. Of the 800 units of new value, 400 replace the equivalent of $v$ and the other 400 constitute $s$. This calculation requires consistent value units and labour conversion, with labour-power paid at its value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第8章](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch08.htm)，活劳动再生产劳动力价值等价物并产生超额的段落及章末可变资本定义；[第9章第1节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm#S1)，解释确定的£90何以称为可变资本的段落：支持购买对象、活劳动作用与名称含义。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。8小时例为按这些条件构造的教学计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 8 supplies the definition and the role of living labour in producing an equivalent plus a surplus. Chapter 9, Section 1 explains why a definite sum of £90 is called variable capital. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The eight-hour illustration is constructed using these analytical conditions.
