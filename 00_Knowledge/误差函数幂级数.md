# 误差函数的幂级数由指数级数代入负平方后逐项积分得到并在整个实轴成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The error-function series follows by substituting a negative square into the exponential series and integrating termwise*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对所有实数 $x$，
$$
\operatorname{erf}(x)
=\frac{2}{\sqrt\pi}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{n!(2n+1)}
=\frac{2}{\sqrt\pi}\left(x-\frac{x^3}{3}
+\frac{x^5}{5\cdot2!}-\frac{x^7}{7\cdot3!}+\cdots\right),
\qquad R=\infty.
$$
分母 $n!$ 来自指数函数，另一个因子 $2n+1$ 来自对 $t^{2n}$ 积分；二者不能合写成 $(2n+1)!$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed expansion holds on the whole real line. The factor $n!$ comes from the exponential series, while $2n+1$ comes from integrating $t^{2n}$. Their product is not $(2n+1)!$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推导只需两步：在 [[指数函数幂级数]] 中代入 $-t^2$，再在任意有限的 $0$ 到 $x$ 区间上逐项积分，最后乘 $2/\sqrt\pi$。代入所得级数半径为无穷，积分保持该半径，所以对每个有限 $x$ 操作成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitute $-t^2$ into the [[指数函数幂级数|exponential series]], integrate termwise from zero to any finite $x$, and multiply by $2/\sqrt\pi$. The substituted series has infinite radius, which integration preserves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $0\le x\le1$ 上，项绝对值递减；保留 $n=0,1,2$ 后，由 [[交错级数误差界]]，误差不超过 $2x^7/(\sqrt\pi\,7\cdot3!)$。例如 $x=1/2$ 时误差小于 $2.10\times10^{-4}$。对较大 $|x|$，先检查余项中的项是否已经递减，再使用下一项误差界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $0\le x\le1$, term magnitudes decrease. After retaining $n=0,1,2$, the [[交错级数误差界|alternating-series bound]] is $2x^7/(\sqrt\pi\,7\cdot3!)$, below $2.10\times10^{-4}$ at $x=1/2$. For larger inputs, verify decreasing tail magnitudes before applying a next-term bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
