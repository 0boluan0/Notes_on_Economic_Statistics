# The error-function power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*误差函数幂级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[误差函数|error function]] is defined by a Gaussian integral. Its power series gives a way to calculate it using only powers and factorials. The first approximation is $2x/\sqrt\pi$; the next corrections are cubic, then fifth degree. The central questions are why this infinite calculation represents the integral and how much error a finite cutoff leaves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[误差函数]]由高斯积分定义，它的幂级数提供了一种只使用幂和阶乘的计算方法。最初近似是 $2x/\sqrt\pi$，随后用三次项、五次项继续修正。关键在于：为什么这项无穷计算确实表示那个积分，以及有限截断还会留下多少误差。

> [!important]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every finite real $x$,
>
> $$\operatorname{erf}(x)=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}
> \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n x^{2n+1}}{n!(2n+1)}
> =\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\left(x-\frac{x^3}{3}+\frac{x^5}{5\cdot2!}-\frac{x^7}{7\cdot3!}+\cdots\right).$$
>
> The index $n$ starts at zero, with $0!=1$; for $n\ge1$, $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$. The [[幂级数收敛半径|radius of convergence]] is infinite, and the series converges absolutely at every such $x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 索引 $n$ 从零开始，约定 $0!=1$；$n\ge1$ 时，阶乘为 $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$。这个级数的[[幂级数收敛半径]]为无穷，在每个有限实数输入处都绝对收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Substitute first, then integrate on a finite segment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先代入，再在有限线段上积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the [[指数函数幂级数|exponential series]] $e^u=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^n/n!$, valid for every real $u$. The [[幂级数代入|substitution]] $u=-t^2$ changes the entire power: $(-t^2)^n=(-1)^nt^{2n}$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从对每个实数 $u$ 都成立的[[指数函数幂级数]] $e^u=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^n/n!$ 出发。[[幂级数代入|代入]] $u=-t^2$ 时，要替换整个幂：$(-t^2)^n=(-1)^nt^{2n}$。所以

$$e^{-t^2}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nt^{2n}}{n!}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a finite $x$. The segment joining $0$ and $x$ is contained in some bounded interval $[-r,r]$. The series has infinite radius, so [[幂级数逐项积分|termwise integration]] applies throughout that segment, with one tail bound valid across it. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定有限的 $x$，连接 $0,x$ 的线段包含在某个有界区间 $[-r,r]$ 中。原级数半径为无穷，所以[[幂级数逐项积分]]能在整段使用同一个尾界，从而得到

$$\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt
=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n}{n!}\int_0^x t^{2n}\,dt
=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{n!(2n+1)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $n!$ was already present in the exponential; the factor $2n+1$ comes from integrating $t^{2n}$. Their product is not $(2n+1)!$. The lower limit makes every term zero at $x=0$, matching $\operatorname{erf}(0)=0$. Negative $x$ uses the usual reversed-limit convention, so all odd powers change sign together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$n!$ 原本就在指数级数中，因子 $2n+1$ 来自对 $t^{2n}$ 积分。两者的乘积不是 $(2n+1)!$。下限固定为零，使每一项在 $x=0$ 都为零，与 $\operatorname{erf}(0)=0$ 一致。负的 $x$ 使用通常的反向积分约定，因此所有奇次幂一起改变符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonzero fixed $x$, write the unsigned terms without their common scale factor as $b_n=|x|^{2n+1}/[n!(2n+1)]$. Their ratio is

$$\frac{b_{n+1}}{b_n}=|x|^2\frac{2n+1}{(n+1)(2n+3)}\longrightarrow0.$$

The [[级数比值判别|ratio test]] therefore confirms absolute convergence at every nonzero finite input; at zero, every term is zero. Infinite radius concerns finite inputs. It does not authorise replacing the upper integration limit by infinity and integrating these polynomial terms one by one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定非零 $x$，把公共比例因子之外的项幅度记作 $b_n=|x|^{2n+1}/[n!(2n+1)]$。上面的相邻幅度比趋于零，因此[[级数比值判别]]再次确认每个非零有限输入处都绝对收敛；零点则每项都为零。无穷半径讨论的是有限输入，并不允许把积分上限换成无穷，再逐项积分这些多项式项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A truncation bound for every real input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每个实输入都可使用的截断界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the terms with indices $0$ through $N$, where $N\ge0$ is an integer, and call their sum $P_N(x)$. It contains $N+1$ nonzero-power terms and has degree $2N+1$:

$$P_N(x)=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\sum_{n=0}^{N}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{n!(2n+1)}.$$

We can bound the exact difference $\operatorname{erf}(x)-P_N(x)$ by bounding the Gaussian integrand's finite Taylor remainder first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取整数 $N\ge0$，保留索引从 $0$ 到 $N$ 的项，将其和记作 $P_N(x)$。它含有 $N+1$ 个非零幂项，次数为 $2N+1$。要估计精确差值 $\operatorname{erf}(x)-P_N(x)$，可以先控制高斯被积函数的有限 Taylor 余项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the [[Taylor余项|Lagrange remainder theorem]] to $g(u)=e^{-u}$ about $u=0$, at the nonnegative input $u=t^2$. This function has continuous derivatives of every order on the whole segment; $|g^{(N+1)}(v)|=e^{-v}\le1$ for $v\ge0$. Hence, including $t=0$ directly,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $g(u)=e^{-u}$ 在 $u=0$ 展开，并在非负输入 $u=t^2$ 处应用[[Taylor余项|Lagrange 余项定理]]。函数在整段有各阶连续导数，而且 $v\ge0$ 时 $|g^{(N+1)}(v)|=e^{-v}\le1$。因此，也把 $t=0$ 的直接等号包括在内，

$$\left|e^{-t^2}-\sum_{n=0}^{N}\frac{(-1)^nt^{2n}}{n!}\right|
\le\frac{|t|^{2N+2}}{(N+1)!}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[积分绝对值界|absolute-value integral bound]] on the finite segment of length $|x|$, and multiply by $2/\sqrt\pi$. This proves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在长度为 $|x|$ 的有限线段上使用[[积分绝对值界]]，再乘以 $2/\sqrt\pi$，便证明

$$\boxed{\left|\operatorname{erf}(x)-P_N(x)\right|
\le\frac{2|x|^{2N+3}}{\sqrt\pi\,(N+1)!(2N+3)}\quad(x\in\mathbb R,\ N\ge0).}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive $x$, the exponential remainder also has sign $(-1)^{N+1}$, so even $N$ gives an upper estimate and odd $N$ a lower estimate. Negative inputs reverse this sign by oddness. At $x=0$, the bound is identically zero. For each fixed nonzero finite $x$, the bound tends to zero as $N$ grows: its consecutive ratio is $|x|^2(2N+3)/[(N+2)(2N+5)]\to0$. The bound therefore gives a sufficient cutoff for any prescribed absolute error; it does not give one cutoff for the entire unbounded real line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x>0$ 时，指数函数余项还带有符号 $(-1)^{N+1}$，所以偶数 $N$ 给出上估计，奇数 $N$ 给出下估计；负输入由奇性反转这个方向。在 $x=0$ 处，上界恒为零。对每个固定的非零有限 $x$，随着 $N$ 增大，上界趋于零，因为连续两界之比为 $|x|^2(2N+3)/[(N+2)(2N+5)]\to0$。因此可以为指定的绝对精度找到足够的截断位置，但不是用同一个截断位置控制整条无界实数轴。

> [!example]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $x=1/2$, keep indices $0,1,2$. Then
>
> $$P_2(1/2)=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\left(\frac12-\frac1{24}+\frac1{320}\right)
> =\frac{443}{480\sqrt\pi}\approx0.520700,$$
>
> and the error is at most $1/(2688\sqrt\pi)<2.10\times10^{-4}$. Since $N=2$ is even and $x>0$, this is an upper estimate; subtracting the bound gives a certified lower estimate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=1/2$ 处保留索引 $0,1,2$，得到上式的 $P_2(1/2)\approx0.520700$。误差至多为 $1/(2688\sqrt\pi)<2.10\times10^{-4}$。由于 $N=2$ 为偶数且 $x>0$，这是上估计，减去误差界就得到有保证的下估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $0<|x|\le1$, the magnitudes decrease from the start, so the [[交错级数误差界|alternating-series bound]] gives the same first-omitted-term estimate. For larger inputs, its monotone-tail hypothesis must be checked rather than assumed. The all-real bound above instead comes from a separate finite Taylor argument and remains valid even before the magnitudes start decreasing. Large terms that later cancel can still make numerical summation sensitive to rounding; the displayed estimate controls truncation error, not rounding error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $0<|x|\le1$，幅度从开始就递减，因此[[交错级数误差界]]也给出同一个第一遗漏项估计。输入较大时，必须检查交错判别要求的单调尾部，不能直接假定。上面适用于全实数的界，则来自独立的有限 Taylor 论证，即使幅度尚未开始递减也仍然有效。不过，较大的项后来互相抵消，仍可能让数值求和对舍入敏感；显示的估计控制截断误差，不控制舍入误差。
