# 口令验证器应保存带独立 salt 与成本参数的专用 password hash
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Password verifiers should store a dedicated password hash with a per-password salt and cost parameter*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子原则
> 服务端不应保存可直接取回的明文口令，也不应只做一次快速通用 hash。它应使用专门的 password hashing scheme，以口令、每份口令记录独立的 salt 与可调成本参数生成 verifier，并同时记录方案、版本和成本以支持日后迁移。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A server should neither retain recoverable plaintext passwords nor apply one fast general-purpose hash. It should use a dedicated password-hashing scheme that derives a verifier from the password, a per-record salt, and a tunable cost parameter, while recording the scheme, version, and cost for later migration.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

salt 不需要保密。现行 NIST 指南要求每份口令记录保存至少 32 bit、以尽量减少碰撞的 salt。它让相同口令在不同记录中通常产生不同 verifier，阻断一次预计算表对整库复用，并迫使攻击者分别测试猜测。成本参数让每次离线猜测付出可控计算代价；它应在不损害验证器性能的前提下尽可能高，并随计算性能提升而提高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A salt need not be secret. Current NIST guidance requires a salt of at least 32 bits for each password record, chosen to minimise collisions. It makes equal passwords normally produce different verifiers, prevents one precomputed table from being reused over the database, and forces guesses to be tested separately. The cost factor makes each offline guess deliberately expensive; it should be as high as practical without harming verifier performance and should rise as computing performance improves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

salt 和高成本都不能把弱口令变成不可猜。取得 verifier 数据库的攻击者仍可离线枚举候选，只是每次猜测更贵；在线攻击还要靠速率限制、泄露口令阻断和更强认证器。额外的 verifier 专有秘密属于另一条隔离边界，见 [[pepper 与验证器分离]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither a salt nor a high work factor makes a weak password unguessable. An attacker who obtains the verifier database can still enumerate candidates offline, although each guess costs more. Online attacks additionally require rate limiting, compromised-password blocking, and stronger authenticators. A verifier-only secret is a separate isolation boundary; see [[pepper 与验证器分离|pepper and verifier separation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两个用户选了同一口令，为什么各自独立的 salt 应让数据库中的 verifier 不同？这能否阻止攻击者猜 `password123`？
>
> **答案：** 不同 salt 使同一候选必须分别计算，避免直接看出复用并阻断全库预计算复用；但攻击者仍能针对每个 salt 测试 `password123`，所以弱口令依然危险。

## 来源与核验

- [NIST SP 800-63B-4, “Password Verifiers”](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html#password-verifiers)：核对 salt、password hashing scheme、成本参数和方案／成本元数据的要求；这是 2025 年定稿并取代上一版的现行 NIST 指南。
- [NIST SP 800-63B-4, Appendix A](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b/passwords/)：核对离线攻击仍可枚举候选，salted costly hashing 只提高猜测成本而不替代口令强度与在线限速。
