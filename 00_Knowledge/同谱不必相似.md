# Equal spectra and similarity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同谱与相似：相同的特征值还遗漏了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Eigenvalues describe scalar multipliers, but do not describe every aspect of a matrix's action. Two matrices can have exactly the same eigenvalues, including the same repetition counts, and still represent transformations that no invertible change of coordinates can identify. This card explains what is missing from that spectral summary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
特征值记录作用中的标量倍数，却没有描述矩阵作用的全部结构。两个矩阵可以有完全相同的特征值，连重复次数都一样，仍无法通过可逆换坐标互相转换。这张卡说明这份谱摘要遗漏了什么。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For square matrices over the same field, [[相似矩阵|similarity]] means $B=M^{-1}AM$ for an invertible matrix $M$. It implies [[相似保持特征多项式|equal characteristic polynomials]]. “Spectrum” sometimes means only the set of distinct eigenvalues. We use the stronger comparison here: the [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]] is identical, so, over a field containing all its roots, the eigenvalues and their [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic multiplicities]] agree as well. Even this stronger information is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对同一数域上的方阵，[[相似矩阵|相似]]是指存在可逆矩阵 $M$，使 $B=M^{-1}AM$；它会推出[[相似保持特征多项式|特征多项式相同]]。“谱”有时只指不同特征值组成的集合。这里采用更强的比较：[[特征多项式]]完全相同，因此在包含全部根的域中，特征值和[[代数重数与几何重数|代数重数]]也完全相同。即使这些资料都给齐，仍然不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same multiplier, different freedom
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*倍数一样，自由方向却不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Let $I_2$ be the two-dimensional [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], and compare
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在实数域或复数域上，令 $I_2$ 为二阶[[单位矩阵]]，比较

$$A=2I_2=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first doubles every coordinate vector. The second also doubles each coordinate, but adds the second input coordinate into the first output:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
前者把每个坐标向量都变成自身的两倍；后者除了把各坐标翻倍，还把第二个输入坐标加进第一个输出坐标：

$$A\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}2x\\2y\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}2x+y\\2y\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both characteristic polynomials are $(t-2)^2$: the only eigenvalue is $2$, counted twice. But which vectors actually become twice themselves? For $A$, every vector does. For $B$, the equation requires $y=0$. The corresponding [[特征空间|eigenspaces]], which include zero as well as all eigenvectors for this value, are therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两者的特征多项式都是 $(t-2)^2$，唯一特征值都是 $2$，计两次。但哪些向量真的会变成自身的两倍？对 $A$，每个向量都符合；对 $B$，则必须满足 $y=0$。所以相应的[[特征空间]]——包括零向量与这个值对应的全部特征向量——分别为

$$E_2(A)=\mathbb F^2,\qquad
E_2(B)=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}x\\0\end{pmatrix}:x\in\mathbb F\right\},\qquad
\dim E_2(A)=2,\quad \dim E_2(B)=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb F$ is the chosen field, and [[维数|dimension]] counts the independent directions needed for a basis. The plane needs two; the line needs one. This is a difference in geometric multiplicity, despite matching algebraic multiplicities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $\mathbb F$ 是所选数域，[[维数]]计算一组基需要多少个独立方向。整个平面需要两个，直线只需要一个。这就是几何重数不同，尽管代数重数相同。

> [!important] Why no change of basis can fix the difference · 为什么换基无法消除差别
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Similarity [[相似保持特征空间维数|preserves every eigenspace dimension]], so dimensions $2$ and $1$ rule it out. There is also a direct check: for every invertible $M$,
> $$M^{-1}(2I_2)M=2M^{-1}M=2I_2.$$
> The scalar matrix is similar only to itself, and $B\ne2I_2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 相似变换[[相似保持特征空间维数|保留每个特征空间的维数]]，所以维数 $2$ 与 $1$ 已经排除了相似。也可以直接计算：对任意可逆 $M$，上式始终等于 $2I_2$。这个标量矩阵只与自身相似，而 $B\ne2I_2$。

## What further comparisons can and cannot establish
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*继续比较哪些量，又能判断到哪一步*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example also has the same [[迹与特征值总和|trace]] (diagonal sum $4$) and [[行列式|determinant]] ($4$). In dimension two, these two numbers actually determine the whole polynomial, $t^2-(\operatorname{tr}A)t+\det A$; nevertheless they do not determine similarity. In higher dimensions they generally record even less than the whole polynomial.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
上例的[[迹与特征值总和|迹]]也相同，即对角元之和 $4$；[[行列式]]也都是 $4$。在二维中，这两个数其实已经确定整个多项式 $t^2-(\operatorname{tr}A)t+\det A$，但仍不能确定相似性。更高维时，它们一般还只记录了特征多项式的一部分资料。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mismatch of eigenspace dimensions is a decisive obstruction. Matching these dimensions is not, in general, a complete certificate either: higher-dimensional matrices can share them but have different lengths of [[Jordan链|Jordan chains]]. When the polynomial splits over the field, the full [[Jordan形唯一性|eigenvalue-labelled Jordan block sizes]] give the classification up to similarity. That is a further test, not information already contained in the root list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
特征空间维数不一致，可以直接否定相似；但维数都相同，一般也还不是完整的确认凭证。更高维矩阵可能具有相同的这些维数，[[Jordan链]]的长度却不同。当多项式在所选域分裂时，完整的[[Jordan形唯一性|带特征值标签的 Jordan 块尺寸]]才给出相似分类。这是进一步的检验，不是根列表里已经包含的资料。

> [!tip] A useful sufficient case · 一个可以确认相似的情形
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If both matrices are [[对角化|diagonalizable over the same field]] and have the same characteristic polynomial, order their eigenvector bases so that both diagonal forms are the same $\Lambda$. Then $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$ and $B=T\Lambda T^{-1}$ imply $B=M^{-1}AM$ with $M=ST^{-1}$. The eigenvalues may repeat. Distinct eigenvalues are one way to guarantee diagonalizability, not a necessary extra condition here.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若两个矩阵都[[对角化|在同一域上可对角化]]，且特征多项式相同，可以调整特征向量基的排列，使两个对角形都等于同一个 $\Lambda$。由 $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$、$B=T\Lambda T^{-1}$，取 $M=ST^{-1}$ 即得 $B=M^{-1}AM$。允许特征值重复；互异特征值只是保证可对角化的一种方式，并不是这里还必须添加的条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The opening pair answers the practical check “Does the same polynomial for two $2\times2$ matrices prove similarity?”: no. Compute what their eigenspaces contain before interpreting equal roots as equal structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
开头的一对矩阵也回答了实际核对问题：“两个 $2\times2$ 矩阵的特征多项式相同，就能证明相似吗？”不能。应继续求出特征空间中究竟有哪些向量，而不是把根相同直接读成结构相同。
