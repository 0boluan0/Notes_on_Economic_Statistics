# The inverse of a product reverses the order of the individual inverses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵乘积的逆等于各因子的逆按相反次序相乘*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A,B$ are invertible square matrices of the same size, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A,B$ 为同尺寸可逆方阵，则

$$
(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both products give the identity, as required by the [[逆矩阵|definition of an inverse]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两侧相乘均得到单位矩阵，符合[[逆矩阵|逆矩阵定义]]：

$$
(AB)(B^{-1}A^{-1})=A(BB^{-1})A^{-1}=I,
\qquad
(B^{-1}A^{-1})(AB)=B^{-1}(A^{-1}A)B=I.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $ABx$, $B$ acts first and $A$ acts second. To undo the composition, $A^{-1}$ acts first and $B^{-1}$ second. Associativity moves parentheses; it does not permit exchanging factors. The same reasoning reverses any finite product of invertible factors, including the elementary matrices in [[消元与LU分解|LU factorization]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $ABx$ 中，先作用 $B$，再作用 $A$；撤销时先作用 $A^{-1}$，再作用 $B^{-1}$。结合律允许移动括号，不允许交换因子。任意有限个可逆因子的乘积都按同一规则反序取逆，[[消元与LU分解|LU 分解]]中的初等矩阵也如此。
