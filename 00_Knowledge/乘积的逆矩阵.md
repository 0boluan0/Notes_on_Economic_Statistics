# Inverse of a matrix product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘积的逆矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To undo two actions, undo the last action first. The **inverse of a matrix product** follows this same order. In [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]], $ABx$ means that $B$ acts on $x$ first and $A$ acts second. An [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] reverses an action, so we recover $x$ by applying $A^{-1}$ first, then $B^{-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撤销两次操作时，要先撤销最后一次。**矩阵乘积的逆**也遵循这个次序。按[[矩阵乘法]]的作用顺序，$ABx$ 先对 $x$ 做 $B$，再做 $A$。[[逆矩阵]]用来撤销操作，因此恢复 $x$ 时，先做 $A^{-1}$，再做 $B^{-1}$。

> [!example] Undo scaling before mixing · 先撤销缩放，再撤销混合
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $B$ add twice the second coordinate to the first, and let $A$ scale the two coordinates by $2$ and $3$ respectively:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $B$ 把第二个坐标的两倍加到第一个坐标上，$A$ 则把两个坐标分别乘以 $2$ 和 $3$：
>
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\quad B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad AB=\begin{pmatrix}2&4\\0&3\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Starting with $(1,2)^T$, $B$ gives $(5,2)^T$ and $A$ gives $(10,6)^T$. First divide the coordinates of this final output by $2$ and $3$; then subtract twice the second coordinate from the first. The corresponding recovery matrix is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $(1,2)^T$ 出发，$B$ 得到 $(5,2)^T$，再经 $A$ 得到 $(10,6)^T$。要恢复，先把最终输出的两个坐标除以 $2$、$3$，再从第一个坐标减去第二个坐标的两倍。对应的恢复矩阵为
>
> $$
> B^{-1}A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\\0&1/3\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&-2/3\\0&1/3\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It sends $(10,6)^T$ back to $(1,2)^T$. The matrix written on the right acts first, so “apply $A^{-1}$ first” is written $B^{-1}A^{-1}$, not $A^{-1}B^{-1}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它把 $(10,6)^T$ 送回 $(1,2)^T$。写在右边的矩阵先作用，所以“先做 $A^{-1}$”要写成 $B^{-1}A^{-1}$，而不是 $A^{-1}B^{-1}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Verify both recovery orders
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*验证两个恢复次序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A,B$ be invertible $n\times n$ matrices of the same finite size, with real or complex entries. Then $AB$ is invertible and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A,B$ 是同一有限尺寸 $n\times n$ 的可逆矩阵，元素为实数或复数。那么 $AB$ 也可逆，且

$$
(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove this for every input, check the two identities required by the definition of an inverse. Write $I_n$ for the identity matrix, which leaves every $n$-coordinate vector unchanged. Associativity allows us to regroup adjacent factors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要证明它对每个输入都有效，只需验证逆矩阵定义要求的两条恒等式。$I_n$ 表示让每个 $n$ 维向量保持不变的单位矩阵。结合律允许对相邻因子重新加括号：

$$
\begin{aligned}(AB)(B^{-1}A^{-1})&=A(BB^{-1})A^{-1}=AI_nA^{-1}=I_n,\\(B^{-1}A^{-1})(AB)&=B^{-1}(A^{-1}A)B=B^{-1}I_nB=I_n.\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matching inverse factors become adjacent and cancel; no factors have been exchanged. Thus $B^{-1}A^{-1}$ is a two-sided inverse of $AB$, and [[逆矩阵唯一性|uniqueness of the inverse]] identifies it as $(AB)^{-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应的逆因子变得相邻并消去，过程中没有交换因子。因此 $B^{-1}A^{-1}$ 是 $AB$ 的双侧逆，再由[[逆矩阵唯一性]]，它就是 $(AB)^{-1}$。

> [!warning] Keeping the original order usually fails · 保持原因子次序通常不成立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the example, $A^{-1}B^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&-1\\0&1/3\end{pmatrix}$. It sends $(10,6)^T$ to $(-1,2)^T$, so it does not undo $AB$. The formula also requires every displayed ordinary inverse to exist; it is not a rule for arbitrary rectangular matrices or singular factors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上例中，$A^{-1}B^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&-1\\0&1/3\end{pmatrix}$。它把 $(10,6)^T$ 送到 $(-1,2)^T$，没有撤销 $AB$。公式还要求所写的每个普通逆都存在，不能直接用于任意矩形矩阵或奇异因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Undo a longer chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*撤销更长的操作链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive integer $k$, repeated use of the same cancellation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $k$，反复使用同样的消去方法，得到

$$
(A_1A_2\cdots A_k)^{-1}=A_k^{-1}\cdots A_2^{-1}A_1^{-1},
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
provided all factors are invertible square matrices of the same size. This is why the elementary matrices in [[消元与LU分解|LU factorization]] are undone in reverse order. The rule concerns the order of matrix actions; it does not assume multiplication is commutative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前提是各因子都为同尺寸可逆方阵。因此，[[消元与LU分解|LU 分解]]中的初等矩阵也要按相反次序撤销。这个规律说的是矩阵作用的次序，没有假设矩阵乘法可交换。
