# Rank and nullity of left multiplication
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵左乘映射的秩与零度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a matrix $A$ and let the entire matrix $X$ vary. The operation $X\mapsto AX$ is a [[线性映射|linear map]] between [[矩阵空间|spaces of matrices]]. Its input is one whole matrix, so its input dimension counts every independently variable entry of $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定矩阵 $A$，让整个矩阵 $X$ 变化。操作 $X\mapsto AX$ 是两个[[矩阵空间]]之间的[[线性映射]]。它的一个输入是一个完整矩阵，所以输入空间的维数要数 $X$ 中全部独立变化的元素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a first example, let $A$ keep the first row and erase the second:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看一个例子：让 $A$ 保留第一行、抹去第二行。

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
X=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
AX=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input has four independent parameters $a,b,c,d$. The output can vary through two parameters $a,b$. To get zero output we must set $a=b=0$, while $c,d$ remain arbitrary. Thus the output space has dimension $2$, and the set of inputs killed by the map also has dimension $2$. The single matrix $A$ has [[矩阵秩|rank]] $1$, because it has only one independent column; acting on two input columns gives the larger map rank $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入有 $a,b,c,d$ 四个独立参数；输出由 $a,b$ 两个参数变化。若要输出为零，必须令 $a=b=0$，但 $c,d$ 仍可任取。因此输出空间是二维，被映射消去的输入集合也是二维。单个矩阵 $A$ 的[[矩阵秩|秩]]为 $1$，因为它只有一个独立列；它对两个输入列共同作用时，映射的秩则为 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $A$ is $m\times n$ with rank $r$, and $X$ has $p$ columns, hence size $n\times p$. Denote this map by $L_A$. Write $X=[x_1\ \cdots\ x_p]$. Multiplication acts separately on each column:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为秩为 $r$ 的 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$X$ 有 $p$ 列，尺寸为 $n\times p$。将这个映射记作 $L_A$，把 $X$ 写成 $[x_1\ \cdots\ x_p]$。左乘对每一列分别作用：

$$
L_A:M_{n,p}(\mathbb R)\to M_{m,p}(\mathbb R),
\qquad L_A(X)=AX=[Ax_1\ \cdots\ Ax_p].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **kernel**, written $\ker L_A$, consists of the input matrices sent to zero. Thus every column must lie in the ordinary [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)$. By the [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]] for $x\mapsto Ax$, each column has $n-r$ independent parameters, and the $p$ columns can be chosen separately, giving $p(n-r)$ dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**核**记作 $\ker L_A$，就是被映到零矩阵的全部输入。因此每一列都必须属于普通的[[零空间]] $N(A)$。由 $x\mapsto Ax$ 的[[秩零度定理]]，每列有 $n-r$ 个独立自由度，$p$ 列可以分别选择，所以总维数为 $p(n-r)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **image**, written $\operatorname{im}L_A$, consists of all achievable output matrices. Every output column lies in the [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$. Conversely, choose a preimage separately for each column in $C(A)$ and place them side by side to construct $X$. Hence the condition is sufficient too, and the image has $pr$ dimensions. The dimension of the kernel is called the map's **nullity**, while the dimension of the image is its **rank**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**像**记作 $\operatorname{im}L_A$，就是全部能够得到的输出矩阵。每个输出列都属于[[列空间]] $C(A)$；反过来，对任意落在 $C(A)$ 中的各列，分别找一个原像并排起来，就构造出了 $X$。所以该条件也充分，像空间有 $pr$ 维。核的维数叫这个映射的**零度**，像的维数叫它的**秩**。

$$
\dim\ker L_A=p(n-r),\qquad
\dim\operatorname{im}L_A=pr,\qquad
p(n-r)+pr=np.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This count can also be seen in bases: put one basis vector of $N(A)$ into one column of an otherwise zero matrix, repeating for every nullspace basis vector and every column position. These matrices form a basis of $\ker L_A$. The same construction with a basis of $C(A)$ gives a basis of the image. Different columns occupy different coordinates, so their freedoms do not overlap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以直接看基：把 $N(A)$ 的某个基向量放进某一列，其余列全为零；遍历全部零空间基向量和全部列位置，得到 $\ker L_A$ 的一组基。对 $C(A)$ 的基做同样构造，就得到像的基。不同列占据不同坐标，所以自由度不重复。

> [!example] One column versus a whole matrix · 一列与整个矩阵
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A$ is $3\times3$ of rank $2$, then $x\mapsto Ax$ has nullity $1$ and rank $2$. For $3\times3$ inputs $X$, $L_A$ instead has nullity $3$ and rank $6$. Its domain has dimension $9$. The matrix $A$ still has rank $2$; it is the map on three independently varying columns whose rank is $6$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $A$ 是秩为 $2$ 的 $3\times3$ 矩阵，$x\mapsto Ax$ 的零度、秩是 $1,2$。当输入改成 $3\times3$ 的 $X$ 时，$L_A$ 的零度、秩是 $3,6$，定义域维数为 $9$。$A$ 本身的秩仍是 $2$；秩为 $6$ 的是对三个可独立变化的列共同作用的映射。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $r=0$, then $A=0$: every input is killed, and the image is the zero space, with dimension $0$ and an empty basis. If $r=n$, the kernel is the zero space. The columnwise argument also works over $\mathbb C$, provided all dimensions are taken over $\mathbb C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $r=0$，则 $A=0$：所有输入都被消去，像是零空间，维数为 $0$、基为空。若 $r=n$，核就只有零矩阵。逐列论证也适用于 $\mathbb C$，但全部维数都要按复数域计算。
