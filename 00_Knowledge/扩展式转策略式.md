# 扩展式博弈的策略式表示以完整应对计划为纯策略，并把每个策略组合诱导的终端收益作为收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The strategic-form representation of an extensive-form game uses complete contingent plans as pure strategies and assigns each strategy profile the payoff of its induced terminal outcome*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把扩展式博弈写成策略式，不是把树上每条边都当作一项策略，而是先为每名玩家列出其[[行动与策略|完整应对计划]]。若玩家 $i$ 的信息集集合为 $\mathcal I_i$，纯策略集合是

$$
S_i=\prod_{I\in\mathcal I_i}A(I).
$$

每项 $s_i\in S_i$ 都必须在玩家可能到达的每个信息集指定行动，包括均衡路径外的信息集。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Converting an extensive-form game to strategic form does not turn every edge of the tree into a strategy. It first lists each player's [[行动与策略|complete contingent plans]]. If player $i$ has information sets $\mathcal I_i$, the pure-strategy set is the displayed product. Every $s_i\in S_i$ specifies an action at each information set that player might face, including off-path information sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从策略组合得到收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From a Strategy Profile to Payoffs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定策略组合 $s=(s_i)_{i\in N}$ 与自然节点的概率，沿树执行这些计划会诱导一个终端历史 $z(s)$，或终端历史上的概率分布。确定性情形下，策略式收益定义为

$$
v_i(s)=u_i\bigl(z(s)\bigr);
$$

有自然随机化时，则对终端收益按诱导概率取期望。所得策略式博弈为

$$
\Gamma=\bigl(N,(S_i)_{i\in N},(v_i)_{i\in N}\bigr).
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given a strategy profile and the probabilities at chance nodes, following the plans through the tree induces a terminal history or a distribution over terminal histories. In the deterministic case, strategic-form payoff is $v_i(s)=u_i(z(s))$; with chance moves, it is the corresponding expected terminal payoff. These objects define the strategic-form game $\Gamma$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三步转换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three-Step Conversion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 按玩家逐个列出其信息集，并在每个信息集选择一项行动，形成全部完整纯策略。
2. 对每个策略组合从根节点沿树走到终端；遇到非路径上的计划也不能从策略名称中删掉。
3. 把诱导的终端收益或期望终端收益填入策略式收益表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**1.** For each player, select one action at every information set and enumerate all complete pure strategies.<br>
**2.** For each strategy profile, follow the tree from the root to its induced terminal outcome; do not remove off-path prescriptions from the strategy labels.<br>
**3.** Enter the induced terminal payoff, or expected terminal payoff, into the strategic-form table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 转换保留收益比较，却丢掉序贯结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Conversion Preserves Payoff Comparisons but Hides Sequential Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

策略式保留完整策略组合及其收益，因此可以求[[纳什均衡]]；但它通常隐藏行动先后、观察结构以及某个路径外行动位于哪个续局。由同一策略式收益表不能反推出唯一的扩展式博弈，也不能只看矩阵判断一个 Nash 是否满足[[子博弈完美均衡|子博弈完美性]]。可信性与子博弈检验必须回到原树。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strategic form preserves complete strategy profiles and their payoffs, so it supports Nash-equilibrium analysis. It generally hides timing, observability, and the continuation in which an off-path action occurs. A strategic-form payoff table does not identify a unique extensive form, and the matrix alone cannot establish subgame perfection; credibility and subgame tests must return to the original tree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
