# 股票内在价值是在明确经营及风险假设下对该股份未来经济利益的现值估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A share's intrinsic value estimates the present value of its future economic benefits under explicit operating and risk assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股票内在价值回答的是：考虑这份[[股权]]未来能获得的现金、现金出现的时间和承担的风险，今天值多少？它是附带假设的经济价值估计，不是股票交易屏幕上的报价，也不是资产负债表上的每股净资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intrinsic value asks what this [[股权|equity claim]] is worth today given its future cash benefits, their timing, and their risk. It is a conditional economic estimate, distinct from a quoted market price or accounting book value per share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，假设一份股份一年后会收到股利 4，并能以 106 卖出；与这些预期收款匹配的必要回报为 10%，则本模型估值为 $(4+106)/1.10=100$。106 也需要未来盈利、分配或届时估值来支持，不能因为希望以高价转卖就随意填入。[[股利贴现模型]]把这条逻辑继续向后展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a share is expected to pay 4 in dividends and sell for 106 after one year, with a matching required return of 10%. Its conditional value is 100. The resale estimate must itself be supported by future earnings, distributions, or valuation assumptions. The [[股利贴现模型|dividend discount model]] extends this reasoning through time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若市场报价 90，只能说它低于这套假设给出的 100；还要判断自己与市场的差异来自哪项预测、风险估计或遗漏。[[反向估值]]可帮助检验价格所需的条件，不能保证价格何时收敛，也不能把模型值与市价之差当作已经赚到的收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A market price of 90 is below the model's 100, but the difference may arise from forecasts, risk estimates, or omissions. [[反向估值|Reverse valuation]] helps examine the conditions needed to support a price. The difference is neither a realised gain nor a guarantee of convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
