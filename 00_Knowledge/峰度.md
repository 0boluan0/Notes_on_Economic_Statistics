# 峰度用标准化四阶中心矩度量远离均值的观察对四阶偏离的贡献

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Kurtosis is the standardised fourth central moment and is sensitive to extreme deviations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 当四阶矩有限且 $\sigma>0$ 时，峰度为 $\gamma_2=E[(X-\mu)^4]/\sigma^4$；超额峰度为 $\gamma_2-3$，正态分布的峰度为 3。必须说明报告哪一种口径。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With a finite fourth moment and positive standard deviation, kurtosis is the standardised fourth central moment. Excess kurtosis subtracts three, the normal-distribution reference value. State the convention.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

样本常用 $m_4/m_2^2$ 或其调整版本。四次方使大偏离的贡献很大，不能把峰度简单解释成“曲线峰顶高度”；其含义与[[偏度]]、[[四分位距]]不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common sample version is $m_4/m_2^2$, sometimes adjusted. Large deviations receive large fourth-power weights, so kurtosis is not simply peak height. It differs from [[偏度|skewness]] and the [[四分位距|interquartile range]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
