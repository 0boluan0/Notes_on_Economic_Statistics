# 固定资本与流动资本是按生产资本价值周转方式划分的部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fixed and circulating capital are divisions of productive capital according to how its value turns over.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定资本是以劳动资料等形式持续参与多轮生产、其预付价值随使用逐步转入产品并逐步收回的生产资本部分。流动资本则在相应生产和流通过程中，其预付价值整体得到补偿，随后重新预付；原材料与购买劳动力的资本部分都属于这种周转方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fixed capital is the part of productive capital that functions through repeated production processes in forms such as instruments of labour, with its advanced value passing piecemeal into products and being recovered gradually. Circulating capital has its advanced value replaced as a whole through the corresponding production and circulation process and is then advanced again. Raw materials and the capital advanced for labour power share this turnover form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例中，价值100的机器可用于10轮相同生产，平均每轮转移价值10；每轮所需的原料20则全部耗用并需重新购入。机器仍有90留在原物上时，第一轮产品已带走机器价值10和原料价值20。这正是[[预付与耗用资本|全额预付与分期耗用]]的区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, a machine worth 100 serves ten identical production rounds and transfers an average value of ten each round. Materials worth 20 per round are fully consumed and must be purchased anew. While 90 remains in the machine after the first round, the product carries ten of machine value and 20 of material value. This illustrates [[预付与耗用资本|full advances and consumption over time]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分类依据是价值周转和生产中的职能。轮船能够移动，仍可作固定资本；机器在制造商那里等待出售时是商品资本，投入买方的生产后才充当固定资本。同一物品的物理属性不能单独决定它在资本运动中的身份。另一条按增殖作用分类的轴，见[[两种资本分类]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classification depends on value turnover and productive function. A mobile ship can be fixed capital; a machine awaiting sale by its manufacturer is commodity capital and becomes fixed capital when employed in the buyer's production. Physical properties alone do not determine its role in capital's movement. See [[两种资本分类|the two classifications of capital]] for the separate classification by function in producing value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
