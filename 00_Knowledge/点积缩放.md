# 在分量独立且零均值单位方差的假设下，除以键维度平方根使点积方差变为一

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With independent zero-mean unit-variance components, dividing a dot product by the square root of the key dimension gives variance one*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $q$ 和 $k$ 的全部 $2d_k$ 个分量相互独立，并且各自均值为 0、方差为 1，那么点积 $q\cdot k$ 的方差为 $d_k$。因此，[[缩放点积注意力]]中的除数 $\sqrt{d_k}$ 在这些假设下恰好把分数方差变为 1；不需要额外假设分量服从正态分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If all $2d_k$ components of $q$ and $k$ are mutually independent, each with mean zero and variance one, then the dot product $q\cdot k$ has variance $d_k$. Under these assumptions, division by $\sqrt{d_k}$ in [[缩放点积注意力|scaled dot-product attention]] makes the score variance exactly one. No normality assumption is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 方差怎样随维度累积

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How variance accumulates with dimension*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $u_r=q_rk_r$。由独立性与零均值，$\mathbb E[u_r]=0$；又因为每个分量的二阶矩为 1，得到

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $u_r=q_rk_r$. Independence and zero means give $\mathbb E[u_r]=0$. Each component has second moment one, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(u_r)
=\mathbb E[q_r^2k_r^2]-\mathbb E[q_rk_r]^2
=\mathbb E[q_r^2]\mathbb E[k_r^2]-0
=1.
$$

不同 $u_r$ 也相互独立，所以点积求和时协方差项为零。再使用 $\operatorname{Var}(aZ)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(Z)$：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $u_r$ terms are also independent, so cross-covariances vanish when the dot-product terms are summed. Applying $\operatorname{Var}(aZ)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(Z)$ then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(q\cdot k)
=\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum_{r=1}^{d_k}u_r\right)=d_k,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}\left(\frac{q\cdot k}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)
=\frac{d_k}{d_k}=1.
$$

例如 $d_k=64$ 时，以上假设给出的未缩放分数标准差为 8；除以 8 后标准差为 1。这个数值是条件推导的结果，不是对训练后所有 attention head 的实测断言。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $d_k=64$, these assumptions give unscaled scores a standard deviation of eight. Dividing by eight makes the standard deviation one. This is a conditional calculation, rather than an empirical claim about every trained attention head.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 这项缩放为何有用

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the scaling is useful*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

点积差距很大时，[[Softmax]] 可能给出接近 one-hot 的权重。设 $p=\operatorname{softmax}(s)$，其局部导数满足

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Large differences between dot-product scores can make [[Softmax]] weights approach a one-hot vector. For $p=\operatorname{softmax}(s)$, the local derivatives satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial p_i}{\partial s_j}
=p_i\left(\mathbf1_{i=j}-p_j\right).
$$

当一项权重趋近 1、其余趋近 0 时，这些导数趋近 0。控制分数尺度是避免过早进入这种饱和区域的动机；这个局部结论并不等价于任意训练损失的所有梯度都会消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As one weight approaches one and the others approach zero, these derivatives approach zero. Controlling score scale is motivated by avoiding premature saturation. This local statement does not imply that every gradient of an arbitrary training loss vanishes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用这条命题的条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditions for applying the proposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

训练得到的 Q/K 可能相关、均值不为零或方差不为一；[[自注意力]]的 Q/K 来自同一表示，也不会自动满足上述独立性。此时仍可在模型中使用 $1/\sqrt{d_k}$，但不能仅凭这项除法断言实际分数方差恰好为 1。推导说明了一个尺度选择的理由，其结论必须和假设一起使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Learned Q/K components may be correlated, have nonzero means, or have variances different from one. Their shared source in [[自注意力|self-attention]] does not automatically satisfy the independence assumptions. A model can still use $1/\sqrt{d_k}$, but the division alone does not establish that actual scores have variance one. The derivation motivates a scale choice whose precise conclusion depends on its assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
