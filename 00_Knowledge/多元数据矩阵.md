# Multivariate data matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多元数据矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **multivariate data matrix** puts measurements into a rectangular table: each row holds one observation's record, and each column holds one variable. This arrangement lets us compare complete observations or follow a single variable across them. The row must have a stated meaning: it might be a person, a household, or one visit by a person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**多元数据矩阵**把测量整理成矩形表格：每行放一份观测记录，每列放一个变量。这样既能比较完整观测，也能沿着一列查看同一个变量。必须说明一行代表什么：可能是一个人、一个家庭，也可能是某个人的一次就诊。

> [!example] Three students, two measurements each · 三名学生，每人两项测量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Record study time in hours and test score in points, in that order:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 按顺序记录学习时长（小时）与测验分数（分）：
>
> $$X_{\mathrm{data}}=\begin{bmatrix}2&61\\5&74\\7&83\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The second row is student 2's complete record, $(5,74)$. The first column is the three study times, $(2,5,7)^T$. The entry $x_{32}=83$ is student 3's score. There are $n=3$ observations and $p=2$ variables, even though there are six numerical entries.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第二行是第2名学生的完整记录 $(5,74)$；第一列是三人的学习时长 $(2,5,7)^T$；元素 $x_{32}=83$ 是第3名学生的分数。这里有 $n=3$ 份观测、$p=2$ 个变量，尽管共有六个数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rows, columns and entries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 行、列和元素

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive integers $n,p$, write the recorded value of variable $j$ in observation $i$ as $x_{ij}$, where $i=1,\ldots,n$ and $j=1,\ldots,p$. When all entries are real numerical values, the table is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $n,p$ 为正整数，$x_{ij}$ 表示第 $i$ 份观测中第 $j$ 个变量的记录，其中 $i=1,\ldots,n$、$j=1,\ldots,p$。若所有元素都是实数，表格写成

$$
X_{\mathrm{data}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
x_{11}&\cdots&x_{1p}\\
\vdots&&\vdots\\
x_{n1}&\cdots&x_{np}
\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We often express an observation as a column vector $x_i=(x_{i1},\ldots,x_{ip})^T\in\mathbb R^p$. The symbol $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so row $i$ of the matrix is $x_i^T$, not $x_i$. Thus one table can be viewed as $n$ points in the $p$-dimensional space of variables. A column, by contrast, contains $n$ values of a single variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

我们常把一份观测写成列向量 $x_i=(x_{i1},\ldots,x_{ip})^T\in\mathbb R^p$。记号 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]，所以矩阵的第 $i$ 行是 $x_i^T$，而不是 $x_i$。整张表因此可以看成变量空间中的 $n$ 个 $p$ 维点；一列则是某一个变量的 $n$ 次取值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Averaging each column gives the [[样本均值向量|sample mean vector]], still with $p$ coordinates. Subtracting those column means is [[中心化矩阵|column centering]]. Neither operation calls for adding hours to points. Entries in one column must refer to the same variable with compatible units; different columns can have different units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每列分别取平均，得到仍有 $p$ 个坐标的[[样本均值向量]]；再减去各自列均值，就是[[中心化矩阵|按列中心化]]。这两种操作都不需要把小时与分数相加。同一列必须对应同一个变量、使用一致单位，不同列则可以有不同单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some books and software store observations in columns. Transposing the table changes its dimensions from $n\times p$ to $p\times n$ without changing the underlying records. The meanings of $n$ and $p$ stay fixed, but formulas must follow the chosen orientation. Moving a whole row must also move its observation identifier; rearranging one column alone can destroy the pairing of variables within an observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些教材或软件把观测存放在列中。转置把尺寸从 $n\times p$ 改为 $p\times n$，并没有改变原始记录；$n$ 与 $p$ 的含义不变，但公式必须随存储方向调整。移动整行时还要一起移动观测标识；只单独重排一列，则可能破坏同一份观测内不同变量的配对。

> [!note] Recorded values and a probability model · 实际记录与概率模型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ is the already observed numerical table, despite the capital letter. A sampling model may instead describe row $i$ before observation by a [[随机向量|random vector]] $X_i$. The recorded row then contains one realization $x_i$. The rectangular layout [[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|does not establish independence or a common distribution]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里的 $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ 虽使用大写字母，却指已经看到的数值表。抽样模型可以把观察前的第 $i$ 行描述成[[随机向量]] $X_i$，实际记录则是它的一次实现 $x_i$。矩形排布本身[[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|不能证明独立或同分布]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $X_{\mathrm{data}}\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$ also assumes a complete numerical representation. Category codes, timestamps, missing-value markers and measurement units need their own interpretation; encoding a category as an integer does not make differences between its codes meaningful. Those checks belong to [[多元数据诊断|multivariate data diagnosis]]. As a quick index check, in a $120\times8$ table, $x_{37,5}$ is observation 37's value of variable 5.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X_{\mathrm{data}}\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$ 还假定数据已完整表示为数值。类别编码、时间戳、缺失标记和单位都需要相应解释；把类别编码成整数，不会自动让编码之差具有意义。这些检查见[[多元数据诊断]]。检验下标理解时，$120\times8$ 表中的 $x_{37,5}$ 就是第37份观测的第5个变量值。
