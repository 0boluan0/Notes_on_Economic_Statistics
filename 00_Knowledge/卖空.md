# 卖空是卖出不拥有或需借入交付的证券而建立空头头寸的交易
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Short selling establishes a short position by selling securities not owned or to be borrowed for delivery*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖空（short selling）通常指卖出自己不拥有、需借入以完成交付的证券。以借股卖空为例，卖出收到现金并不消灭借券义务：交易者须按证券借贷安排返还同种、同量证券，常通过以后买回股票来平仓。这里定义实际证券交易，不把所有负 Delta 的衍生品头寸都称为卖空证券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Short selling normally means selling securities that are not owned and must be borrowed for delivery. In a borrowed-stock short sale, the sale proceeds do not extinguish the securities loan: equivalent securities must be returned, commonly by buying shares back. This defines a securities transaction, not every derivative position with negative Delta.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，借入并卖出 100 股，每股 50 元，随后每股 40 元买回 100 股返还。开仓收 5,000 元、买回付 4,000 元，价差为 1,000 元；借券费、股息补偿及交易成本还须另计，见[[卖空损益]]。卖出所得也不自动成为可自由提取的资金，要受账户和抵押安排约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, borrow and sell 100 shares at 50, then buy back 100 shares at 40 for return. Proceeds of 5,000 less repurchase cost of 4,000 give a price gain of 1,000 before borrowing fees, distribution compensation, and trading costs; see [[卖空损益|short-sale profit and loss]]. Proceeds are not automatically freely withdrawable cash; account and collateral arrangements matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖空可以表达看跌判断，也可以用于[[套期保值|对冲其他头寸]]或提供交易流动性；它的目的不能单凭交易方向确定。什么销售须按监管口径标为 short、何时须事先借券及哪些例外适用，要按市场规则另判，不能把上述经济例子当成全球统一的法律定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A short sale may express a bearish view, [[套期保值|hedge another position]], or supply liquidity; direction alone does not establish purpose. Regulatory short-sale marking, borrowing requirements, and exceptions depend on the market. The economic example is not a universal legal classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
