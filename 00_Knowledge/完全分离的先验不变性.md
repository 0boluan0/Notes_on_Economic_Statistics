# 在标准信号博弈的 weak PBE 中，只要先验对所有类型保持 full support，完全分离候选的路径上退化 posterior 不随先验数值改变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a standard signaling game under weak PBE, the degenerate on-path posterior of a completely separating candidate is invariant to prior probabilities as long as every type retains full support*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 不变的是路径上类型识别
> 完全分离使每个路径上信号只可能由一个类型发送。只要该类型在先验中仍有正概率，Bayes 分子与分母含有同一个先验因子，条件化后 posterior 对该类型等于 1。改变各类型的正先验权重不会改变这项路径上推断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under complete separation, each on-path signal can be sent by only one type. As long as that type retains positive prior probability, the same prior factor appears in the numerator and denominator of Bayes' rule, leaving posterior probability one on that type. Changing positive prior weights does not change this on-path inference.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Bayes 消去先验数值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bayesian cancellation of prior magnitudes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设有限类型集合为 $\Theta$，先验 $\mu_0$ 满足 $\mu_0(\theta)>0$ 对所有 $\theta$ 成立。若完全分离策略的类型支持互不相交，并且信号 $s$ 只属于类型 $\theta_s$ 的支持，则

$$
\sigma_1(s\mid\theta_s)>0,
\qquad
\sigma_1(s\mid\theta)=0
\quad(\theta\ne\theta_s).
$$

[[路径上一致性]]给出

$$
p(\theta_s\mid s)
=
\frac{\mu_0(\theta_s)\sigma_1(s\mid\theta_s)}
{\sum_{\theta'}\mu_0(\theta')\sigma_1(s\mid\theta')}
=1.
$$

对其他类型 posterior 为 0。把 $\mu_0$ 换成任何仍对所有类型赋正概率的先验 $\widetilde\mu_0$，同一消去仍成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the finite prior have full support, and suppose type-specific signal supports are disjoint. If on-path signal $s$ belongs only to type $\theta_s$'s support, Bayes' rule reduces to the displayed ratio and yields posterior one on $\theta_s$. Replacing the prior by any other full-support prior leaves the cancellation unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 接收者路径上反应的相应不变性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The corresponding invariance of the on-path receiver response*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察 $s$ 后，接收者面对的是已识别类型 $\theta_s$ 与已观察信号 $s$，所以路径上最佳反应集合为

$$
\arg\max_{a\in A}u_2(\theta_s,s,a),
$$

不再含先验权重。若最优行动唯一，路径上接收者行动随 full-support 先验数值改变而保持不变；若存在并列最优，最佳反应集合保持不变，但具体混合选择仍可能不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After observing $s$, the receiver faces the identified type $\theta_s$ and the observed signal, so the on-path best-response set no longer contains prior weights. A unique best response is therefore invariant to changes among full-support priors. With ties, the best-response set remains invariant, although the selected mixture may differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 结论的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary of the result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这项不变性只陈述完全分离候选的**路径上推断**。若某类型的先验概率降为零，它原先使用的信号也会变成零概率事件，weak PBE 的路径上 Bayes 条件便不再钉住 posterior。若多个类型共享一个正概率信号，posterior 会包含各类型的先验权重，因此 pooling 或 semi-separating 推断通常随先验改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The invariance concerns only on-path inference under complete separation. If a type's prior probability falls to zero, its formerly used signal becomes a zero-probability event and weak-PBE on-path Bayes consistency no longer pins down the posterior. If several types share a positive-probability signal, posterior beliefs retain their prior weights, so pooling or semi-separating inference generally changes with the prior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

路径外 beliefs、发送者偏离收益、均衡选择规则和采用的 refinement 仍须另行检查。因此，从路径上 posterior 不变不能直接推出整个 PBE 集合、福利或比较静态都对先验不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Off-path beliefs, sender deviation payoffs, equilibrium-selection rules, and the chosen refinement still require separate checks. Invariance of on-path posteriors alone does not establish invariance of the entire PBE set, welfare, or comparative statics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两类型完全分离候选中，先验从 $(0.2,0.8)$ 改为 $(0.6,0.4)$，两类仍各有正概率且继续使用不同信号。观察高类型信号后的 posterior 是否改变？
>
> **答案：** 不改变。该信号仍只由高类型发送，Bayes 条件化后高类型 posterior 仍为 1；这只说明路径上推断不变，并未单独验证整个候选仍是 PBE。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** In a completely separating two-type candidate, the prior changes from $(0.2,0.8)$ to $(0.6,0.4)$ while both types retain positive probability and continue to use different signals. Does the posterior after the high type's signal change?
>
> **Answer:** No. Only the high type sends that signal, so its posterior remains one. This establishes invariance of on-path inference, not by itself that the whole candidate remains a PBE.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
