# 购买力平价是消除经济体间价格水平差异以换算对应支出的统计转换率

购买力平价（PPP）是用于跨经济体价格比较的转换率。它让一笔本币支出能够按可比价格表达，从而分开“花的钱更多”和“购买的商品服务量更多”。这里讨论的是统计换算，不是在预测外汇市场下一步会怎样定价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Purchasing power parity (PPP) is a conversion rate for comparing price levels across economies. Expressing local expenditure at comparable prices helps separate spending more money from obtaining more goods and services. This is a statistical conversion, not a forecast of foreign-exchange pricing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设完全相同的一篮子商品在本地卖 200 本币单位，在参照经济体卖 50 美元。这个简化篮子的 PPP 为 $200/50=4$ 本币单位／参照购买力单位。按这一转换率，800 本币对应 $800/4=200$ 个可比购买力单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an identical basket costs 200 local currency units and 50 US dollars in the reference economy. Its simplified PPP is $200/50=4$ local units per reference purchasing-power unit. At that rate, 800 local units correspond to $800/4=200$ comparable purchasing-power units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\text{PPP换算后的量}=
\frac{\text{以本币计的相应支出}}{\text{相应PPP转换率}}.
$$

实际国际比较使用许多商品和服务的价格及支出权重进行汇总，不是挑一件商品算比价。GDP、家庭消费、实际个人消费等有各自对应的 PPP；计算哪一种量，就要用对应的转换率。[[汇率与购买力比较]]说明如何按用途选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actual international comparisons aggregate prices and expenditure weights across many goods and services, rather than pricing one item. GDP, household consumption, and actual individual consumption have corresponding PPPs. Match the conversion to the aggregate; [[汇率与购买力比较|choosing exchange-rate or purchasing-power comparisons]] explains the decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PPP 国际元是比较单位，不是能够按这个汇率在银行换到的货币。全国平均转换率也不意味着不同地区、不同收入家庭面对完全相同的价格；做[[贫困测量的福利口径|贫困比较]]时，还需看福利变量、价格调整和 PPP 版本是否匹配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An international dollar is a comparison unit, not money exchangeable at a bank at the PPP rate. A national average also does not imply identical prices across regions or income groups. For [[贫困测量的福利口径|poverty comparisons]], check the welfare measure, price adjustments, and PPP vintage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
