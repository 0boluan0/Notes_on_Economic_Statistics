# 规模转换把许多小额资金汇集成单笔大额融资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Scale transformation pools many small balances into a large financing claim*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 单个储蓄者愿意投入的金额常小于一套住房、一台设备或一个企业项目所需的融资额。金融中介向许多客户发行金额较小的存款、基金份额、保单或其他索取权，再在资产端形成金额更大的贷款或证券头寸，解决资金单位大小不匹配的问题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> One saver is often willing to commit much less than the financing required for a house, a machine, or a business project. An intermediary issues small deposits, fund shares, insurance policies, or other claims to many customers and forms larger loans or securities positions on the asset side, resolving the mismatch in desired transaction size.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，1,000 名客户各持有 1,000 元索取权，中介便能在保留必要流动性和风险缓冲后，为一笔远大于任何单个客户余额的项目配置资金。每位客户不必寻找 999 名共同贷款人，也不必共同起草和执行一份复杂合同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if 1,000 customers each hold a claim of 1,000, an intermediary can—after maintaining the required liquidity and risk buffers—allocate funding to a project far larger than any single customer's balance. No customer must find 999 co-lenders or jointly draft and enforce a complex contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规模转换回答“金额怎样拼起来”，[[金融交易成本的规模经济|规模经济]]回答“为什么单位处理成本可能下降”，[[信用与风险转换|风险转换]]回答“损失怎样在索取权之间重新分配”。三者经常同时出现，但不能混作一个原因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Scale transformation answers how amounts are assembled; [[金融交易成本的规模经济|economies of scale]] answer why unit processing cost may fall; [[信用与风险转换|risk transformation]] answers how losses are reallocated among claims. They often occur together but are not the same mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 汇集资金本身不会自动分散风险：若全部资金最终投向同一借款人、地区或风险因子，集中度仍然很高。
- 规模转换不等于“中介只能先收齐同额现金才能放贷”；它描述索取权金额的重新组合，不替代银行贷款与支付结算的资产负债表会计。
- 大额项目也可通过拆分成许多标准化证券实现规模匹配，因此规模转换不限于存款银行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Pooling funds does not automatically diversify risk. Concentration remains high if all assets depend on one borrower, region, or common risk factor.
- Scale transformation does not mean an intermediary can lend only after collecting an equal stock of cash. It describes the repackaging of claim sizes and does not replace balance-sheet accounting for bank lending and settlement.
- A large project can also be divided into many standardised securities, so scale transformation is not confined to deposit-taking banks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 共同基金让小额投资者买到一篮子证券，哪些部分是规模转换，哪些部分不是？
>
> **答案：** 把许多小额份额汇成足以购买大额证券头寸是规模转换；交易成本下降属于规模经济，持有不同资产降低特定风险属于分散化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When a mutual fund gives small investors access to a securities portfolio, which part is scale transformation?
>
> **Answer:** Combining small shares into amounts large enough to buy sizeable positions is scale transformation. Lower trading cost is an economy of scale, and holding different assets is diversification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
