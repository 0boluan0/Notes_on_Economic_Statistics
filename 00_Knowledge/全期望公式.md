# Law of total expectation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*全期望公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **law of total expectation** lets us calculate an overall average in two stages: average within each group, then average those group means using the groups' probabilities. This is useful when the groups have simpler descriptions than the whole population. A [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] supplies the first-stage mean; an ordinary [[期望|expectation]] supplies the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**全期望公式**把总体平均分成两步计算：先算各组内部的平均值，再按各组出现的概率平均这些组均值。当每组比总体更容易描述时，这样算就很方便。[[条件期望]]负责第一步的组内均值，普通[[期望]]负责第二步的加权平均。

> [!example] Two bus routes of different sizes · 两组大小不同的公交线路
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ be the route label and $Y$ the waiting time in minutes. Within each route, the two listed waits are equally likely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $X$ 表示线路标签，$Y$ 表示等待分钟数。每条线路内，列出的两个等待时间等概率出现。
>
> | Route / 线路 $x$ | $P(X=x)$ | Possible waits / 等待时间 | $E[Y\mid X=x]$ |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | $0$ | $1/4$ | $0,4$ | $2$ |
> | $1$ | $3/4$ | $4,8$ | $6$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The overall mean is closer to $6$ because route $1$ is more likely:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 线路 $1$ 更常出现，所以总体平均更靠近 $6$：
>
> $$E[Y]=\frac14\cdot2+\frac34\cdot6=5.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Checking individual outcomes gives the same result: $0/8+4/8+3\cdot4/8+3\cdot8/8=5$. Grouping has changed the order of the calculation, not its answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 逐个结果计算也一样：$0/8+4/8+3\cdot4/8+3\cdot8/8=5$。分组只改变了计算顺序，没有改变答案。

## Reading the two expectations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*读懂内外两层期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before the route is revealed, $M=E[Y\mid X]$ is a random variable: it equals $2$ on route $0$ and $6$ on route $1$. The outer expectation averages this new variable, so $E[M]=5$. The compact formula is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线路揭晓前，$M=E[Y\mid X]$ 是随机变量：线路 $0$ 时等于 $2$，线路 $1$ 时等于 $6$。外层期望就是对这个新变量取平均，因此 $E[M]=5$。简写为

$$E[Y]=E\!\left[E[Y\mid X]\right].$$

> [!abstract] The general identity · 一般恒等式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $Y$ is integrable, meaning $E[|Y|]<\infty$, and $\mathcal G$ is a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] representing available information, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $Y$ 可积，即 $E[|Y|]<\infty$，且 [[σ-代数]] $\mathcal G$ 表示已有信息，则
>
> $$E[Y]=E\!\left[E[Y\mid\mathcal G]\right].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a finite partition $B_1,\ldots,B_m$ of all outcomes into disjoint events of positive probability, this becomes
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果把全部结果划成互不重叠、概率为正的有限组 $B_1,\ldots,B_m$，就得到
>
> $$E[Y]=\sum_{i=1}^{m}P(B_i)E[Y\mid B_i].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $P(B_i)$ is a group's weight, and $E[Y\mid B_i]$ is its mean. The groups must cover the whole space. Zero-probability groups contribute nothing and can be omitted; their elementary ratio-based conditional mean is undefined. The general identity also covers continuous conditioning, without pretending that every individual value is a positive-probability group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $P(B_i)$ 是第 $i$ 组的权重，$E[Y\mid B_i]$ 是该组均值。这些组必须覆盖全部结果。零概率组没有贡献，可以省略；它的初等概率比值式条件均值没有定义。一般恒等式也适用于连续条件变量，不需要把每个单点误当成正概率的组。

## 为什么成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it holds*

Conditional expectation preserves the original average contribution on every event detectable from $\mathcal G$. Choose the whole sample space $\Omega$ as that event: its indicator is always $1$, so the defining equality becomes $E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G]]=E[Y]$. It is also the no-information outer case of the [[塔式法则|tower property]], which concerns averaging over nested information sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件期望会在每个由 $\mathcal G$ 辨认的事件上保留原来的平均贡献。取整个样本空间 $\Omega$ 作为事件，它的指示变量恒为 $1$，定义中的等式就成为 $E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G]]=E[Y]$。它也可以看作[[塔式法则]]中外层不保留任何非平凡信息的特例；塔式法则处理逐层嵌套的信息。

> [!warning] Equal weighting needs equal probabilities · 等权平均需要等概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The bus group means $2$ and $6$ do not give $(2+6)/2=4$, because the groups occur with different probabilities. Likewise, means $2$ and $8$ yield overall mean $5$ only when the two exhaustive groups each have probability $1/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 公交两组的均值 $2$ 和 $6$ 不能直接算成 $(2+6)/2=4$，因为两组出现的概率不同。同样，如果两组覆盖全部结果，均值分别为 $2$ 和 $8$，只有每组概率均为 $1/2$ 时总体均值才是 $5$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an equal-weight example, toss a fair coin: on heads, let $R$ be uniform on $\{1,3,5\}$; on tails, let it be uniform on $\{2,4,6\}$. The group means are $3$ and $4$, so $E[R]=(1/2)3+(1/2)4=3.5$. The weights follow the experiment, not the number of group labels we wrote down. Applying the same averaging principle to squared deviations leads to the [[全方差公式|law of total variance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等权例子可以用公平硬币：正面时，令 $R$ 在 $\{1,3,5\}$ 上均匀；反面时，在 $\{2,4,6\}$ 上均匀。两组均值为 $3$ 和 $4$，所以 $E[R]=(1/2)3+(1/2)4=3.5$。权重来自试验本身，而不是写下了几个组名。对平方偏差应用同样的平均原则，就通向[[全方差公式]]。
