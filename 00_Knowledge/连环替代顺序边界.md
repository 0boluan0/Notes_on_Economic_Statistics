# 连环替代的单项贡献通常依赖顺序而贡献总和仍等于总差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Individual chain-substitution effects generally depend on the order, although their sum remains the total change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[连环替代法]]中，若指标包含因素之间的交互项，先后替代会改变交互变动分给谁。总差由起点和终点决定，单项分摊还由所选路径决定，不能称为与顺序无关的“真实贡献”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a [[连环替代法|chain-substitution]] model contains interactions, the update order changes which factor receives the interaction effect. Endpoints determine the total change, but the chosen path also determines individual allocations. They are not order-invariant true contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $Y=ab$，记两因素变化为 $\Delta a,\Delta b$。先 $a$ 后 $b$ 分得 $b_0\Delta a$ 和 $a_1\Delta b$；反序则是 $b_1\Delta a$ 和 $a_0\Delta b$。交互项 $\Delta a\Delta b$ 在两因素间换了归属。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $Y=ab$, updating $a$ first allocates $b_0\Delta a$ to $a$ and $a_1\Delta b$ to $b$. Reversing the order gives $b_1\Delta a$ and $a_0\Delta b$. The interaction term $\Delta a\Delta b$ changes its allocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $a:2\to3$、$b:10\to12$，总量20→36。先 $a$ 得10、再 $b$ 得6；先 $b$ 得4、再 $a$ 得12。两路合计都是16。报告时应写明顺序，并避免把这种分摊与统计因果识别混同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a:2\to3$ and $b:10\to12$, the total rises from 20 to 36. Updating $a$ first gives effects of 10 and 6; updating $b$ first gives 4 and 12. Both sum to 16. State the order and do not confuse this allocation with statistical causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
