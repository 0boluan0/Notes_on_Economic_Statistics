# Binomial coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*组合数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **binomial coefficient** counts how many ways we can choose a fixed number of distinct members when their internal order does not matter. For a two-person committee drawn from $A,B,C$, the possibilities are just $\{A,B\},\{A,C\},\{B,C\}$. The descriptions $\{A,B\}$ and $\{B,A\}$ name the same committee, so they contribute one choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**组合数**计算从不同成员中选出固定人数、且不区分内部顺序时有多少种选择。从 $A,B,C$ 中选两人委员会，只有 $\{A,B\},\{A,C\},\{B,C\}$ 三种。$\{A,B\}$ 与 $\{B,A\}$ 描述的是同一委员会，只算一种。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n$ be the number of available distinct objects and $k$ the number selected. The notation $\binom nk$, read “$n$ choose $k$”, counts the $k$-element **subsets** of that collection: a subset records which objects are present, without positions and without repeating an object. Here $n$ is a nonnegative integer; the ordinary selection problem has integer $0\le k\le n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n$ 为可选的不同对象数，$k$ 为要选出的个数。记号 $\binom nk$ 读作“$n$ choose $k$”，计算原集合中有 $k$ 个元素的**子集**：子集只记录哪些对象在内，没有内部位置，也不会重复同一对象。这里 $n$ 是非负整数，通常的选取问题要求 $k$ 为满足 $0\le k\le n$ 的整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the factorial formula divides by $k!$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么要除以 $k!$*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is easier to choose members one at a time and temporarily keep their order. This produces the [[排列|ordered selections]] counted by $P(n,k)=n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)$. For the three-person example, the six ordered pairs fall into three groups:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以先逐个选择成员，暂时保留顺序。这就得到[[排列|有序选择]]，数量为 $P(n,k)=n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)$。三个人的例子中，六个有序对分成三组：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Committee | Ordered descriptions of that committee |
|---|---|
| $\{A,B\}$ | $(A,B),(B,A)$ |
| $\{A,C\}$ | $(A,C),(C,A)$ |
| $\{B,C\}$ | $(B,C),(C,B)$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every committee has exactly two ordered descriptions, so dividing six by two gives three committees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个委员会都恰好有两个有序描述，所以用六除以二，得到三个委员会。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $k$ selected distinct members, each subset has exactly $k!$ orderings. The factorial $k!$ is the product $k(k-1)\cdots1$, with $0!=1$; it counts ways to arrange those members. Since the number of descriptions is the same for every subset, the [[除法法则|division rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选出 $k$ 个不同成员后，每个子集都恰好有 $k!$ 种排列。阶乘 $k!$ 是乘积 $k(k-1)\cdots1$，并约定 $0!=1$，它计算这些成员的排列数。每个子集的描述数量都相同，因此由[[除法法则]]得到

> [!abstract] The number of $k$-element subsets · $k$ 元子集的数量
> $$\binom nk=\frac{P(n,k)}{k!}=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!},\qquad 0\le k\le n.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This counts all size-$k$ subsets of $n$ distinct objects. Extra eligibility or compatibility restrictions require an additional argument.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个公式计算 $n$ 个不同对象的所有 $k$ 元子集；若还有资格、搭配等额外限制，需要进一步分析。

> [!example] A committee or three different jobs? · 委员会还是三个不同职位？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> From ten people, a three-member committee can be chosen in $\binom{10}{3}=10\cdot9\cdot8/(3\cdot2\cdot1)=120$ ways. If the same group instead fills chair, secretary and treasurer, each committee has six role assignments. There are then $120\cdot6=720$ outcomes. Dividing by $3!$ is justified only when those six descriptions mean the same outcome.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从十个人中选三人委员会，有 $\binom{10}{3}=10\cdot9\cdot8/(3\cdot2\cdot1)=120$ 种。若改为分配主席、秘书、财务，每个委员会还有六种职位安排，于是共有 $120\cdot6=720$ 个结果。只有六份描述代表同一结果时，才应除以 $3!$。

## Empty choices and impossible sizes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*空选择与不可能的大小*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is one way to select no objects: choose the empty subset. There is also one way to select all $n$ objects. Thus $\binom n0=\binom nn=1$, including $\binom00=1$. For a nonnegative integer $n$, it is useful to define $\binom nk=0$ for integer $k<0$ or $k>n$, because no subset has such a size. In those cases use the zero convention, not a factorial of a negative number. Noninteger or negative upper arguments belong to extensions beyond this finite-subset interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不选任何对象只有一种方式：选空集；选中全部 $n$ 个对象也只有一种，所以 $\binom n0=\binom nn=1$，包括 $\binom00=1$。对非负整数 $n$，通常把整数 $k<0$ 或 $k>n$ 时的 $\binom nk$ 定义为零，因为不存在这种大小的子集。这时使用零值约定，不能代入负数的阶乘。非整数或负的上标属于此有限子集解释之外的推广。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same coefficient counts binary strings of length $n$ with exactly $k$ ones: choose the positions occupied by ones, and put zeros elsewhere. For length four with two ones, the six strings are $1100,1010,1001,0110,0101,0011$, matching $\binom42=6$. A position is selected at most once, even though several positions may contain the same bit value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个组合数也计算长度 $n$、恰有 $k$ 个一的二进制串：选定放一的位置，其余放零。长度四、恰有两个一的六个串是 $1100,1010,1001,0110,0101,0011$，与 $\binom42=6$ 一致。每个位置最多被选一次，尽管多个位置可以写相同的比特值。

> [!warning] The model still matters · 仍要核对对象模型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A subset cannot contain the same object twice. Choosing two scoops when a flavour may repeat is a different problem. Also, a binomial coefficient gives a number of selections; turning ratios of such counts into probabilities requires [[古典概型等可能条件|equally likely underlying outcomes]]. Counting does not supply that assumption.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 子集不会把同一对象放进来两次。允许同一种口味重复出现的“两勺冰淇淋”，是另一个问题。此外，组合数给出的是选择数量；把数量之比当成概率，需要[[古典概型等可能条件|底层结果等可能]]，计数本身不会提供这个假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complement interpretation is developed in [[组合数对称性|binomial coefficient symmetry]]. The recurrence for neighbouring coefficients and their appearance in powers are separate ideas explained by [[Pascal恒等式|Pascal's identity]] and the [[二项式定理|binomial theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用补集理解同一选择，见[[组合数对称性]]；相邻组合数的递推关系，以及它们为什么出现在幂的展开中，分别见[[Pascal恒等式]]与[[二项式定理]]。
