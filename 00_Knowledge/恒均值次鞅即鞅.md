# Constant-mean submartingales
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*恒均值次鞅即鞅*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A submartingale may have favourable expected changes in different observed situations. If its overall mean never increases, those nonnegative conditional gains must all vanish, apart from events of probability zero. This turns the submartingale inequality into the martingale equality. The point is that nonnegative gains cannot cancel one another in an average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

次鞅允许在不同的已观察情形下出现有利的期望变化。如果总体均值始终没有增加，这些非负的条件期望收益就必须全部消失，至多例外于零概率事件。于是次鞅不等式变成鞅等式。关键在于：非负收益在平均时不能彼此抵消。

> [!example] Two information states · 两种已知情形
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the current value is $4$ or $6$, each with probability $1/2$, and this value has already been observed. The submartingale condition allows next-step conditional means $4+a$ and $6+b$, with $a,b\ge0$. The current mean is $5$; the next mean is $5+(a+b)/2$. If both means equal $5$, then $a=b=0$. The next realized value can still fluctuate; only its conditional mean is forced to equal the current value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设当前值以各 $1/2$ 的概率为 $4$ 或 $6$，而当前值已经观察到。次鞅条件允许下一步的条件均值分别为 $4+a$ 与 $6+b$，其中 $a,b\ge0$。当前均值是 $5$，下一步均值是 $5+(a+b)/2$。若两个总体均值都等于 $5$，则只能 $a=b=0$。下一步实现值仍可波动，被强制相等的只是条件均值与当前值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Precisely, fix a [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$, the information available at each time. Let $X$ be a real [[次鞅|submartingale]] for that filtration: $X_t$ is [[适应过程|adapted]], $E|X_t|<\infty$, and $E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]\ge X_s$ almost surely for each fixed $s\le t$. Suppose also that $E[X_t]=c$ at every time, for one finite constant $c$. Then $X$ is a [[鞅定义|martingale]] for the same filtration. This argument works for discrete or continuous time; it is a comparison between fixed deterministic times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

准确地说，固定[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$，表示每个时刻已经知道的信息。设实值 $X$ 是该滤过下的[[次鞅]]：$X_t$ [[适应过程|适应]]、$E|X_t|<\infty$，且每对固定 $s\le t$ 都满足 $E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]\ge X_s$ 几乎必然成立。再假设每个时刻 $E[X_t]=c$，其中 $c$ 是同一个有限常数，则 $X$ 就是同一滤过下的[[鞅定义|鞅]]。论证对离散和连续时间都成立；比较的始终是固定的确定时刻。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A nonnegative conditional gain cannot have zero mean unless it vanishes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非负差的期望只能为零

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $s\le t$ and define the conditional expected gain $D_{s,t}$. The [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] uses only the information at $s$; subtracting $X_s$ removes the value already known then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $s\le t$，定义条件期望收益 $D_{s,t}$。[[条件期望]]只使用 $s$ 时的信息，再减去当时已经知道的 $X_s$：

$$D_{s,t}=E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]-X_s\ge0\quad\text{a.s.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This difference is integrable because both terms are integrable. By the [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两项都可积，所以差也可积。由[[全期望公式]]，

$$
E[D_{s,t}]=E\!\left[E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]\right]-E[X_s]
=E[X_t]-E[X_s]=c-c=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does this force $D_{s,t}=0$ almost surely? For any positive integer $m$, nonnegativity gives $E[D_{s,t}]\ge m^{-1}\Pr(D_{s,t}\ge m^{-1})$. Its left side is zero, so each event on the right has probability zero. The event $D_{s,t}>0$ is the countable union of these events, hence also has probability zero. We have proved
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这为什么迫使 $D_{s,t}=0$ 几乎必然成立？对任意正整数 $m$，非负性给出 $E[D_{s,t}]\ge m^{-1}\Pr(D_{s,t}\ge m^{-1})$。左边为零，所以右边每个事件的概率为零。而事件 $D_{s,t}>0$ 恰是这些事件的可数并，因此其概率也为零。于是得到

$$E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=X_s\quad\text{a.s.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Together with the original adaptedness and integrability, this is the martingale definition. In discrete time, it is enough to run the argument for adjacent times and use the [[塔式法则|tower property]] for larger gaps. More locally, even without constant means at all times, equality $E[X_t]=E[X_s]$ at a particular pair already forces the conditional equality for that pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结合原有的适应性与可积性，这就是鞅定义。离散时间只需先对相邻时刻论证，再用[[塔式法则]]处理较大间隔。更局部地，即使不是所有时刻的均值都恒定，只要某一对时刻满足 $E[X_t]=E[X_s]$，就足以迫使该对时刻的条件等式成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, start at $5$ and add independent fair $+1$ or $-1$ coin changes. After the first step the value is $4$ or $6$, and every next-step conditional mean equals the current value. The process has constant mean $5$ but nonconstant sample paths. “Constant mean” must not be read as “constant value”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如从 $5$ 开始，每步独立地加入公平的 $+1$ 或 $-1$ 硬币变化。第一步后为 $4$ 或 $6$，之后每一步的条件均值都等于当前值。过程的均值恒为 $5$，样本路径却不是常数。“均值恒定”不能读成“取值恒定”。

> [!warning] The one-sided condition does the work · 单向条件是关键
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Without the submartingale inequality, positive and negative conditional gains can cancel. [[恒定均值不推鞅|Constant overall means alone do not imply a martingale]]. This theorem requires the submartingale property for the same filtration throughout; it does not recover that property from the means. In continuous time, the conclusion is almost-sure equality for each fixed pair $(s,t)$, with a possibly pair-dependent null event. No simultaneous equality for all uncountably many pairs is asserted.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果没有次鞅不等式，正负条件期望收益可能抵消，所以[[恒定均值不推鞅|仅有总体均值恒定不能推出鞅]]。本定理从始至终要求同一滤过下的次鞅性质，不能从均值反推这条前提。在连续时间中，结论是每对固定 $(s,t)$ 的等式几乎必然成立，例外零概率事件可能依赖该对时刻；没有宣称对不可数多对时刻同时成立。

## The same conclusion for supermartingales
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 超鞅的对称情形

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[超鞅|supermartingale]] with constant finite means, use the opposite nonnegative difference $X_s-E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$. Its expectation is again zero, so the conditional inequality becomes equality. Equivalently, apply the submartingale result to $-X$. Thus a submartingale or supermartingale with constant means is a martingale; both the integrability and the one-sided conditional inequality matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于具有恒定有限均值的[[超鞅]]，改用方向相反的非负差 $X_s-E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$，其期望仍为零，条件不等式同样变成等式。等价地，也可以把次鞅结论应用于 $-X$。因此，恒均值的次鞅或超鞅都是鞅；可积性和单向条件不等式都不能省略。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the discrete [[Doob分解|Doob decomposition]] $X_n=M_n+A_n$ with $A_0=0$, this says the nondecreasing predictable part disappears: $E[A_n]=E[X_n]-E[X_0]=0$ and $A_n\ge0$, so $A_n=0$ almost surely. The direct proof above establishes the result without requiring that decomposition first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在离散[[Doob分解]] $X_n=M_n+A_n$、$A_0=0$ 中，这意味着非递减的可预测部分消失：$E[A_n]=E[X_n]-E[X_0]=0$ 且 $A_n\ge0$，所以 $A_n=0$ 几乎必然成立。前面的直接证明不需要先学习该分解。
