# 有效货银对付消除交换的本金风险但不消除重置成本和流动性等风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Effective DvP eliminates principal risk in the exchange, not replacement-cost, liquidity, or all other risks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效运行且法律上成立的[[货银对付]]，防止“一条腿已最终完成、另一条腿没有最终完成”造成的本金损失。它不承诺所有已成交交易都按时交收，也不承诺交易价格不变、无需备付资金或系统不会中断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Legally effective and functioning [[货银对付|DvP]] prevents principal loss from one leg becoming final without the other. It does not promise that every executed trade settles on time, prices remain unchanged, prefunding is unnecessary, or systems never fail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设买方约定付 1,000 元取得一批证券，交收前同批证券市价升至 1,080 元。卖方未能交券，DvP 阻止买方最终付出 1,000 元；但若买方仍要获得证券，替代购买需多付 80 元。这是未履约合同的重置成本暴露，不是已经付出 1,000 元却收不到券的本金暴露。是否最终损失 80，还取决于索赔、担保和回收。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a buyer agrees to pay 1,000 for securities now worth 1,080 before settlement. If the seller fails to deliver, DvP prevents final payment of 1,000; obtaining the securities elsewhere nevertheless costs an extra 80. This is replacement-cost exposure, not exposure from having finally paid 1,000 without receiving securities. The eventual loss also depends on claims, guarantees, and recovery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若参与者依赖这批证券继续交付，或依赖卖券所得支付另一笔债务，交收延迟还会产生借券或融资需求。系统中断、法律不确定、结算银行和托管环节的风险也须分别管理；不能用 DvP 标签代替对整个流程的核验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Participants relying on the incoming securities for another delivery, or sale proceeds for another payment, can face borrowing or funding needs when settlement is delayed. Operational outages, legal uncertainty, and settlement-bank or custody risks require separate controls. A DvP label does not verify the whole process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，DvP 与[[中央清算与多边净额|CCP 接纳和净额安排]]解决不同问题：前者联结交收腿，后者改变合同对手及可净额集合。两者不能互相推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DvP and [[中央清算与多边净额|CCP acceptance and netting]] address different questions: the former links settlement legs, while the latter changes contractual counterparties and eligible netting sets. Neither implies the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
