# 三只乌鸦是上涨后连续三根较长阴线接近各期低位收盘的组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three black crows is a formation after an advance consisting of three relatively long down candles closing near their period lows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三只乌鸦是在上涨之后或相对高位出现的三根连续较长阴[[K线]]：价格连续下移，各根收盘接近本期最低价。它不是任何位置上的“三根阴线”；先前走势和阴线长度、收盘位置都是识别背景。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three black crows consists of three consecutive, relatively long down [[K线|candlesticks]] after an advance or at a relatively high level, with prices moving lower and closes near each period's low. Three down candles at an arbitrary location are insufficient: context, body length and closing position matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第三根收盘后才完整可见。K线学派将它归为看跌候选形态，这既不等于能够事先认定最高点，也不保证第四根继续下跌。向上方向的对应结构见[[白色三兵]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formation is complete only at the third close. Its bearish classification neither identifies a high in advance nor guarantees a fourth declining candle. See [[白色三兵|three white soldiers]] for the corresponding upward structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
