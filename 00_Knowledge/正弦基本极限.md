# 弧度制下 $\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\sin h}{h}=1$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In radians, $\lim_{h\to0}\sin h/h=1$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 当角度用弧度表示时，
> $$
> \lim_{h\to0}\frac{\sin h}{h}=1.
> $$
> 它把圆周上的小弧长与对应弦的竖直分量联系起来，是从三角函数定义推到其导数的关键桥梁。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When angles are measured in radians, $\lim_{h\to0}\sin h/h=1$. It compares a short circular arc with the vertical component of its chord and is the key bridge from trigonometric definitions to trigonometric derivatives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $0<h<\pi/2$，单位圆面积比较给出
$$
\sin h<h<\tan h.
$$
除以 $h$ 并整理可得
$$
\cos h<\frac{\sin h}{h}<1.
$$
两端在 $h\to0^+$ 时都趋于 $1$，由 [[夹逼定理]] 得右极限；利用奇偶性得到左极限。另有
$$
\frac{1-\cos h}{h}=\frac{\sin h}{h}\frac{\sin h}{1+\cos h}\to0.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $0<h<\pi/2$, the unit-circle area comparison gives $\sin h<h<\tan h$, hence $\cos h<\sin h/h<1$. The squeeze theorem yields the right-hand limit and symmetry gives the left-hand limit. Rationalising with $1+\cos h$ also gives $(1-\cos h)/h\to0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

弧度条件不能省略。若输入用度数 $	heta$，则 $sin(\theta^\circ)=\sin(\pi\theta/180)$，差商极限会多出比例 $\pi/180$。公式等于 $1$ 正是因为弧度把单位圆上的角直接量成弧长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The radian condition is essential. With degrees, $sin(\theta^\circ)=\sin(\pi\theta/180)$ and the limit acquires the factor $\pi/180$. The value 1 arises because radians measure the angle by arc length on the unit circle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
