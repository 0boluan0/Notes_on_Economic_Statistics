# The fundamental sine limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正弦基本极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a very small angle, its sine is almost the same number as the angle **measured in radians**. For example, $\sin(0.1)\approx0.0998334$. The fundamental sine limit makes “almost the same” precise: the ratio of these two numbers approaches $1$ as the angle approaches zero. This is the starting point for understanding small oscillations and for proving [[正弦余弦求导|the derivatives of sine and cosine]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个很小的角，它的正弦值与这个角的**弧度数**很接近。例如 $\sin(0.1)\approx0.0998334$。正弦基本极限把“很接近”说得更精确：当角趋近零时，两者的比值趋近 $1$。它是理解小幅振动、证明[[正弦余弦求导|正弦与余弦导数]]的起点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a circle of radius $1$, an angle of $h$ radians subtends a signed arc of length $h$; one complete turn is $2\pi$ radians. Starting from $(1,0)$ and turning by $h$ reaches the point $(\cos h,\sin h)$. Thus $\sin h$ is the vertical coordinate, while $h$ measures travel along the circle. A [[函数极限|limit]] describes what happens as nonzero inputs get arbitrarily close to the specified point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在半径为 $1$ 的圆上，$h$ 弧度对应有向弧长 $h$；一整圈是 $2\pi$ 弧度。从 $(1,0)$ 转过角 $h$，到达的点是 $(\cos h,\sin h)$。因此 $\sin h$ 是纵坐标，而 $h$ 衡量沿圆弧走过的距离。[[函数极限]]描述的是非零输入任意接近指定点时的行为。

> [!abstract] The ratio tends to one · 比值趋近一
> $$
> \lim_{h\to0}\frac{\sin h}{h}=1
> \qquad\text{(angles in radians)}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The quotient is undefined at $h=0$; the limit concerns nearby nonzero $h$. Equivalently, $(\sin h-h)/h\to0$: the error in replacing $\sin h$ by $h$, relative to $h$, disappears. It does not assert that $\sin h=h$ for a fixed nonzero angle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 比值在 $h=0$ 没有定义；极限考察附近的非零 $h$。等价地，$(\sin h-h)/h\to0$：用 $h$ 代替 $\sin h$ 的误差，相对于 $h$ 本身趋于零。这并不表示固定的非零角满足 $\sin h=h$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why geometry forces the limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*几何为什么能确定这个极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First take $0<h<\pi/2$. Let $O=(0,0)$, $A=(1,0)$ and $B=(\cos h,\sin h)$. Extend the ray $OB$ until it meets the vertical tangent at $A$; call the intersection $T=(1,\tan h)$, where $\tan h=\sin h/\cos h$. The triangle $OAB$ lies inside the circular sector $OAB$, which lies inside the triangle $OAT$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先取 $0<h<\pi/2$。设 $O=(0,0)$、$A=(1,0)$、$B=(\cos h,\sin h)$。把射线 $OB$ 延长，与过 $A$ 的竖直切线相交于 $T=(1,\tan h)$，其中 $\tan h=\sin h/\cos h$。三角形 $OAB$ 包含在扇形 $OAB$ 内，而扇形又包含在三角形 $OAT$ 内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two triangles have base $OA=1$ and heights $\sin h$ and $\tan h$. The sector occupies $h/(2\pi)$ of the unit disk, so its area is $h/2$. Comparing the three areas gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个三角形的底都是 $OA=1$，高分别为 $\sin h$ 和 $\tan h$。扇形占单位圆盘的 $h/(2\pi)$，所以面积为 $h/2$。比较这三个面积得到

$$
\frac{\sin h}{2}<\frac h2<\frac{\tan h}{2},
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
\cos h<\frac{\sin h}{h}<1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The right-angle range matters: $h$, $\sin h$ and $\cos h$ are positive, so the divisions keep the inequality directions. Also $0<\sin h<h$ implies $\sin h\to0$. Since $\cos h=\sqrt{1-\sin^2h}$ in this range, $\cos h\to1$ without using any trigonometric derivative. The [[夹逼定理|squeeze theorem]] says that a quantity between two expressions approaching the same number approaches that number too. It therefore gives the right-hand limit $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个角度范围很重要：$h$、$\sin h$、$\cos h$ 都为正，做除法时不等号方向才保持不变。又因为 $0<\sin h<h$，所以 $\sin h\to0$；在此范围内 $\cos h=\sqrt{1-\sin^2h}$，于是 $\cos h\to1$，并不需要先使用三角函数的导数。[[夹逼定理]]说明：夹在两个趋于同一个数的表达式之间的量，也趋于那个数。因此右极限是 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the other side, write $h=-t$ with $t>0$. The identity $\sin(-t)=-\sin t$ gives $\sin h/h=\sin t/t$. Thus the left-hand limit is also $1$, completing the two-sided proof. Also $\cos(-t)=\cos t$, so the cosine limit from the left is $1$ as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从另一侧接近时，令 $h=-t$，其中 $t>0$。由 $\sin(-t)=-\sin t$，有 $\sin h/h=\sin t/t$。所以左极限也等于 $1$，双侧极限得到证明。另外 $\cos(-t)=\cos t$，所以余弦从左侧接近时的极限也为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The companion cosine limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一起得到的余弦极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying numerator and denominator by $1+\cos h$ turns a cosine difference into a sine square. For nonzero $h$ sufficiently close to zero, $1+\cos h$ is nonzero, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分子分母同乘 $1+\cos h$，就能把余弦之差变为正弦的平方。对充分接近零的非零 $h$，$1+\cos h$ 不为零，且

$$
\frac{1-\cos h}{h}
=\frac{\sin h}{h}\frac{\sin h}{1+\cos h}
\longrightarrow 1\cdot\frac02=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently $(\cos h-1)/h\to0$ as well. These two limits enter the difference quotients for sine and cosine; using those derivatives to prove these limits would reverse the intended proof order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $(\cos h-1)/h\to0$ 也成立。这两个极限会进入正弦、余弦的差商；若反过来先用它们的求导公式证明这些极限，就颠倒了这里的证明顺序。

> [!warning] Degrees change the scale · 度数会改变比例
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\theta$ is the numerical angle in degrees, the same angle in radians is $\pi\theta/180$. Therefore $\sin(\theta^\circ)$ means $\sin(\pi\theta/180)$, and the relevant limit is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\theta$ 是用度数表示的角度数值，同一个角的弧度数为 $\pi\theta/180$。所以 $\sin(\theta^\circ)$ 表示 $\sin(\pi\theta/180)$，此时极限为
>
> $$
> \lim_{\theta\to0}\frac{\sin(\theta^\circ)}{\theta}
> =\frac{\pi}{180}.
> $$
