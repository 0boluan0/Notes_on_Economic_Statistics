# Random vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*随机向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **random vector** records several numerical features of the same random outcome. Selecting one person and recording both height and weight produces one two-coordinate output. The coordinates belong together: they describe the same selected person, not two unrelated selections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**随机向量**把同一个随机结果的多个数值特征一起记录。抽取一人，同时记录身高与体重，得到的是一个有两个坐标的输出。两个坐标配对属于同一名被抽中的人，而不是两次无关的抽取。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One random object, one joint output
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个随机对象，一次联合输出

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a toy population with two possible profiles, selected with equal probability. Let $H$ record height in centimetres and $W$ weight in kilograms. The [[随机变量|random variables]] $H$ and $W$ are recording rules; after a selection, each rule returns a number. Write the combined output as the column $X=(H,W)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑一个只含两种可能档案的简化总体，两种档案被选中的概率相同。用 $H$ 记录身高（厘米），$W$ 记录体重（千克）。[[随机变量]] $H$、$W$ 是记录规则；一次选择后，每条规则返回一个数。把联合输出写成列向量 $X=(H,W)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。

| Outcome / 结果 | $H$ (cm) | $W$ (kg) | Probability / 概率 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $a$ | $160$ | $50$ | $1/2$ |
| $b$ | $180$ | $80$ | $1/2$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If outcome $a$ occurs, the realised column is $X(a)=(160,50)^T$. The value $(160,80)^T$ does not occur in this model: taking one coordinate from each row would combine two different outcomes. The table describes possible outcomes and their probabilities; it is not a table of two observed samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若结果 $a$ 发生，就得到实现值 $X(a)=(160,50)^T$。这个模型中不会出现 $(160,80)^T$：从两行各取一个坐标，会把两个不同结果混在一起。表格描述可能结果及其概率，并不是两条已观察样本的数据表。

> [!important] The vector and its realisation · 随机向量与实现值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $X$ denotes the whole random rule. A particular output $x=X(\omega)$ is a fixed numerical vector once the outcome $\omega$ is specified. The number of coordinates, $p$, describes how much is recorded per outcome; it is not a sample size.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $X$ 表示完整的随机记录规则；指定结果 $\omega$ 后，$x=X(\omega)$ 才是一个确定的数值向量。坐标个数 $p$ 表示每次结果记录多少个量，不是样本量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The formal definition uses one probability space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正式定义使用同一个概率空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[概率空间|probability space]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ specifies possible outcomes, which collections of outcomes are events, and their probabilities. For a positive integer $p$, a real random vector is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[概率空间]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ 指定可能结果、哪些结果集合是事件，以及事件概率。对正整数 $p$，实随机向量写作

$$X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T:\Omega\longrightarrow\mathbb R^p.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where every $X_j$ is a real random variable on that **same** probability space. Thus $X(\omega)=(X_1(\omega),\ldots,X_p(\omega))^T$ evaluates all coordinates at the same outcome. Each coordinate is measurable: questions about its value correspond to events to which $P$ assigns probabilities. Equivalently, $X$ is a measurable map into $\mathbb R^p$ with its Borel sets, the sets generated from open subsets by complements and countable unions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中每个 $X_j$ 都是定义在**同一个**概率空间上的实随机变量。因此 $X(\omega)=(X_1(\omega),\ldots,X_p(\omega))^T$ 在同一个结果处计算全部坐标。每个坐标都可测：关于其取值的问题对应于可以由 $P$ 赋予概率的事件。等价地，$X$ 是到 $\mathbb R^p$ 及其 Borel 集族的可测映射；Borel 集族由开集经过补集与可数并运算生成。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the joint question “height exceeds $170$ cm and weight exceeds $70$ kg” is the intersection $\{H>170\}\cap\{W>70\}$. It is an event on the original sample space, with probability $1/2$ in the table. More generally, intersections of coordinate events make rectangular regions in $\mathbb R^p$ measurable; these rectangles generate the Borel sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，“身高超过 $170$ 厘米并且体重超过 $70$ 千克”对应交事件 $\{H>170\}\cap\{W>70\}$。它是原样本空间上的事件，在表中概率为 $1/2$。一般地，把坐标事件取交，就能检验 $\mathbb R^p$ 中的矩形区域；这些区域生成 Borel 集族。

> [!warning] Joint does not mean independent · 放在一起不等于独立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The coordinates need not be [[随机变量独立|independent]]. In the table, learning the height determines the weight. Their [[联合分布|joint distribution]] records how the coordinates occur together; their separate marginal distributions need not determine that joint law, as explained in [[边际分布不定联合分布|the marginal-versus-joint distinction]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 坐标不必[[随机变量独立|独立]]。在表中，一旦知道身高，就知道体重。[[联合分布]]记录坐标怎样共同出现；分别给出各坐标的边际分布，未必能确定联合规律，详见[[边际分布不定联合分布]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Observing $n$ selected people gives $n$ realised vectors $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, each with $p$ coordinates. Whether the underlying selections are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]] is an additional modelling assumption; [[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|a rectangular data table does not establish it]]. A random vector also need not have finite moments. Its [[均值向量|mean vector]] requires finite first moments, while its finite [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] requires finite second moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察 $n$ 名被抽中的人，才得到 $n$ 个实现向量 $x_1,\ldots,x_n$，每个都含 $p$ 个坐标。底层抽样是否[[独立同分布]]，仍是额外的模型假设，[[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|数据表呈矩形并不能证明它]]。随机向量也不必具有有限矩；有限[[均值向量]]需要有限一阶矩，有限[[协方差矩阵]]需要有限二阶矩。
