# 事中信念条件于玩家自己的类型，信息集信念条件于动态博弈中已到达的信息集或历史
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An interim belief conditions on a player's own type, whereas an information-set belief conditions on the information set or history reached in a dynamic game.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种信念都可以是条件概率，却回答不同问题。事中信念 $p_i(t_{-i}\mid t_i)$ 问“知道自己的类型后，其他玩家是什么类型”；信息集信念 $\mu_i(x\mid I)$ 问“到达当前信息集后，我处于其中哪个节点”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both beliefs may be conditional probabilities, but they answer different questions. The interim belief $p_i(t_{-i}\mid t_i)$ asks, “After learning my own type, what are the other players' types?” The information-set belief $\mu_i(x\mid I)$ asks, “Having reached this information set, which node within it am I at?”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件信息不同
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*They condition on different information.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事中阶段的新增信息是玩家自己的私人类型，常用于静态贝叶斯博弈中的条件期望收益。信息集信念还会条件于动态历史中已经观察到的信号或行动，常用于计算该信息集上各行动的序贯期望收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the interim stage, the newly learned information is the player's private type, and the resulting belief is commonly used for conditional expected utility in a static Bayesian game. An information-set belief also conditions on signals or actions observed along a dynamic history and is used to evaluate sequential expected payoffs at that information set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同一底层概率可以产生二者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The same underlying probability model can generate both.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同先验可以先通过 $t_i$ 条件化产生事中信念；再与策略诱导的信号似然结合，条件于观察到的历史产生信息集信念。在信号博弈中，接收者看到信号 $s$ 后的后验通常取决于“各类型发送 $s$ 的概率”，因此不能把它直接等同于任何玩家只知道自身类型时的事中信念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common prior may first be conditioned on $t_i$ to produce an interim belief; combined with signal likelihoods induced by strategies, it may then be conditioned on an observed history to produce an information-set belief. In a signaling game, the receiver's posterior after signal $s$ generally depends on the probability with which each type sends $s$, so it cannot simply be identified with a player's interim belief based only on knowing their own type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
