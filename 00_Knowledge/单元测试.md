# 单元测试检查一个明确划定的程序组件是否满足自身契约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Unit testing checks whether a clearly bounded program component meets its own contract.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单元可以是函数或模块；关键是明确被测责任、所需状态和依赖，使失败能较直接地归到这个组件的行为。单元不是按固定行数划分，也不要求每一个内部调用都被替换成假对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A unit may be a function or module. Its responsibility, required state, and dependencies should be explicit so failures can be connected to its behavior. Units are not defined by a fixed line count, nor must every internal call be replaced with a fake object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如直接调用回文判断 `is_pal(['a', 'b'])` 并检查 `False`，是在检查判断组件；通过键盘收集两项、转换为列表、调用判断并打印文字，则把多个组件的连接也放进了被测范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calling `is_pal(['a', 'b'])` directly and checking for `False` tests the checker. Reading two keyboard inputs, building a list, invoking the checker, and printing text also exercises connections among components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单元测试通过不意味着这些连接正确；[[集成测试]]检查组合行为。一次单元检查也可以在修复后成为[[回归测试]]，因为“测试哪个层级”与“为何重跑”是两个维度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Passing unit tests does not establish that the connections work; [[集成测试|integration testing]] checks combined behavior. A unit check can also become a [[回归测试|regression test]], because scope and the purpose of rerunning it are different dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
