# Open sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*开集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a point inside the interval $(0,1)$ on the real line. It is possible to move a little in either direction and remain inside the interval. An **open set** generalizes this point-by-point room: every member has a whole small neighbourhood that stays in the set. The amount of room can depend on the chosen point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实数区间 $(0,1)$ 内任选一点，都可以向两边稍微移动而仍留在区间内。**开集**把这种逐点的余量推广出去：每个成员周围都有一整块仍在集合内的小邻域。余量可以随所选点变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A ball around every member
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每个成员周围都要有球*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$: $X$ is the collection of allowed points, and $d$ gives their distances. Its open ball $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$ consists of points less than $r$ away from $x$. For a subset $U\subseteq X$, the metric definition is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先给定[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$，其中 $X$ 是允许的全部点，$d$ 给出点间距离。开球 $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$ 收集距 $x$ 不到 $r$ 的点。对子集 $U\subseteq X$，度量中的定义是：

> [!important] The pointwise test · 逐点检验
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $U$ is **open in $X$** if for every $x\in U$ there exists a radius $r_x>0$ with $B_{r_x}(x)\subseteq U$. Choose the point first, then its radius. No single positive radius is required to work for all points.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若每个 $x\in U$ 都存在半径 $r_x>0$，使 $B_{r_x}(x)\subseteq U$，就称 $U$ **在 $X$ 中开**。先选点，再选适合它的半径；不要求全部点共用一个正半径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $U=(0,1)$ and $x\in U$, the distances to the two excluded endpoints are $x$ and $1-x$. Take $r_x=\tfrac12\min\{x,1-x\}$. This is positive, and $|y-x|<r_x$ keeps $y$ between $0$ and $1$. At $x=0.2$, for example, $r_x=0.1$ gives the ball $(0.1,0.3)$. Near either endpoint, the available radius becomes smaller.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $U=(0,1)$ 中的 $x$，到两个不属于集合的端点的距离分别为 $x$ 和 $1-x$。取 $r_x=\tfrac12\min\{x,1-x\}$，它为正，且 $|y-x|<r_x$ 保证 $0<y<1$。例如 $x=0.2$ 时可取 $r_x=0.1$，得到球 $(0.1,0.3)$；越靠近端点，可用半径越小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no uniform positive radius for this interval. Given any proposed $r>0$, choose $x=\min\{r/2,1/2\}$. Then $x\in(0,1)$ but $|0-x|<r$, so $0\in B_r(x)$ and that ball leaves the interval. The failure of a uniform radius does not affect openness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个区间没有统一正半径。对任意候选 $r>0$，取 $x=\min\{r/2,1/2\}$，便有 $x\in(0,1)$，但 $|0-x|<r$，所以 $0\in B_r(x)$，球已越出区间。统一半径不存在，不影响开性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One failed point is enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个点失败就足够否定开性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To show a set is not open, find one of its members around which **every** positive-radius ball leaves the set. For $[0,1]\subseteq\mathbb R$, use the member $0$: for any $r>0$, the point $-r/2$ lies in $B_r(0)$ but outside $[0,1]$. The same argument shows that $\{0\}$ is not open in the usual real line. By contrast, in the discrete metric, a ball of radius $1/2$ is a singleton, so every subset is open.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要证明集合不开，找出其中一个成员，使它周围**每个**正半径球都会越出集合即可。对 $[0,1]\subseteq\mathbb R$，取成员 $0$：任意 $r>0$ 都有 $-r/2\in B_r(0)$，但 $-r/2\notin[0,1]$。同样可证明通常实数距离下的 $\{0\}$ 不开。相反，离散度量下半径 $1/2$ 的球只有一个点，因此每个子集都开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The empty set $\varnothing$ is open because there is no member at which the test could fail. The whole space $X$ is open because balls are defined using points of $X$. An open ball is itself open: the positive-room argument in [[度量空间|the metric-space definition]] puts a smaller ball around every point inside it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空集 $\varnothing$ 开，因为没有成员能使条件失败；整个空间 $X$ 开，因为球本来只收集 $X$ 中的点。开球本身也是开集：[[度量空间|度量空间中的余量论证]]在球内每一点周围给出仍留在原球内的小球。

> [!example] The ambient space changes the test · 环境空间会改变检验
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep the usual distance, but now allow only points in $X=[0,1]$. Then $B_{1/4}^{X}(0)=[0,1/4)$, since negative numbers are no longer in the ambient space. The whole interval $[0,1]$ is open in this $X$, although it is not open in $\mathbb R$. More generally, $[0,1/2)$ is open in $X$; the [[子空间拓扑|subspace-topology definition]] expresses it as $X\cap(-1,1/2)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 保留通常距离，但只允许 $X=[0,1]$ 中的点，则 $B_{1/4}^{X}(0)=[0,1/4)$，因为环境中已没有负数。整个 $[0,1]$ 在这个 $X$ 中开，尽管它在 $\mathbb R$ 中不开。$[0,1/2)$ 在 $X$ 中也开；[[子空间拓扑]]用 $X\cap(-1,1/2)$ 表达它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Beyond spaces with a distance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不必先有数值距离*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A general [[拓扑|topology]] specifies a consistent family $\tau$ of subsets of $X$; its members are called open. In that setting, $U$ is open exactly when $U\in\tau$, and an **open neighbourhood** of $x$ means an open set containing $x$. There need not be any distance or balls. If $\tau$ is induced by a metric, this agrees with the ball test above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般[[拓扑]]直接指定一族满足一致规则的子集 $\tau$，把其中的成员称为开集。此时 $U$ 开就是 $U\in\tau$；$x$ 的**开邻域**是包含 $x$ 的开集，不一定存在数值距离或球。若拓扑由度量诱导，这就与前面的开球检验一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Openness is not a drawing convention such as “no endpoints”, and it is not the opposite of [[闭集|closedness]]. A closed set has an open complement. Both $\varnothing$ and $X$ are open and closed; $[0,1)$ in the usual real line is neither. For a general set, the points passing the local open-neighbourhood test form its [[集合内部|interior]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开性不是“不含端点”之类的画图约定，也不是[[闭集|闭性]]的反面。闭集要求补集为开集；$\varnothing$ 和 $X$ 同时开闭，通常实数线中的 $[0,1)$ 则两者都不是。对一般集合，通过局部开邻域检验的点构成它的[[集合内部|内部]]。
