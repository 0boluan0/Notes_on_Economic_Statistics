# 共同先验是对收益参数与类型剖面的事前联合分布，其条件化生成所有玩家的事中信念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A common prior is an ex-ante joint distribution over payoff parameters and type profiles whose conditioning generates every player's interim beliefs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一场博弈有**共同先验**，是指存在一份 $\pi\in\Delta(\Theta\times T)$，使每名玩家观察自身类型后采用的[[事中信念]]都能由同一份 $\pi$ 条件化得到。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A game has a **common prior** when there exists a distribution $\pi\in\Delta(\Theta\times T)$ such that every player's [[事中信念|interim belief]] after observing their own type is obtained by conditioning that same $\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件化关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Conditioning Relation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有正概率的类型，common-prior assumption 可写为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a type with positive probability, the common-prior assumption can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)
=
\pi(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)
=
\frac{\pi(\theta,t_i,t_{-i})}{\pi_i(t_i)}.
$$

同一联合分布包含两层信息：类型的 ex-ante 发生概率，以及观察自身类型后应怎样用[[Bayes法则]]更新。即使两位玩家共享 $\pi$，他们观察不同的 $t_i$ 后也可能得到不同 interim beliefs；“posterior 不同”本身不证明共同先验失败。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The joint distribution contains both the ex-ante probabilities of types and the conditional beliefs obtained after a player observes their own type. Two players can therefore share $\pi$ yet hold different interim beliefs after receiving different information; different posteriors alone do not refute a common prior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用共同先验时还要检查什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What Else Must Be Checked When Using a Common Prior?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[贝叶斯博弈不要求共同先验]]说明这一假设为何不是 interim-belief 表述的定义条件；[[零概率类型的条件信念]]处理条件化分母为零时的缺口；[[共同先验不等于数据生成分布]]区分玩家共享的主观概率与研究者面对的真实数据过程。有共同先验时，才可进一步使用[[Harsanyi转换]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[贝叶斯博弈不要求共同先验|Why a common prior is not required]] explains its status in an interim-belief formulation. [[零概率类型的条件信念|Conditional beliefs at zero-probability types]] handles the failure of ordinary conditioning, and [[共同先验不等于数据生成分布|a common prior versus a data-generating distribution]] separates shared subjective beliefs from the researcher's empirical process. A [[Harsanyi转换|shared ex-ante Nature draw]] becomes available only once a common prior has been supplied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两名玩家共享同一 $\pi$，但一人观察到高信号、另一人观察到低信号，更新后对高成本状态给出不同概率。这是否自动违反共同先验？
>
> **答案：** 不违反。共同先验要求两人的事中信念来自同一事前联合分布的条件化，并不要求不同信息产生相同 posterior。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** Two players share the same $\pi$, but one observes a high signal and the other a low signal, so they assign different posterior probabilities to the high-cost state. Does this automatically violate the common-prior assumption?
>
> **Answer:** No. A common prior requires both players' interim beliefs to arise by conditioning the same ex-ante joint distribution; it does not require different information to produce the same posterior.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
