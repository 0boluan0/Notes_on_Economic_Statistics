# Orthogonal trajectories
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正交轨线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **orthogonal trajectory** is a curve that crosses a given family of curves at right angles. For curved lines, the angle at a crossing is the angle between their tangent lines at that point. The whole curves do not have to be straight. The requirement concerns every intersection under consideration, not just one convenient crossing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正交轨线**是与给定曲线族以直角相交的一条曲线。曲线的相交角度，指交点处两条切线之间的角度，而不是要求整条曲线为直线。这个要求需要在所讨论的每个交点成立，不能只检查一个方便的交点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A family means a collection indexed by a parameter, such as all circles $x^2+y^2=R^2$ with radius $R>0$. The parameter selects a member; moving along that member changes the point $(x,y)$ while its radius stays fixed. We discuss ordinary Euclidean plane geometry and regular crossings, where each participating branch has a definite tangent direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

曲线族是由参数标记的一组曲线，例如半径 $R>0$ 的所有圆 $x^2+y^2=R^2$。参数选择其中一条曲线；沿这条曲线移动时，点 $(x,y)$ 改变，半径保持不变。这里讨论普通欧氏平面几何，以及各条参与分支都具有明确切线方向的正规交点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use tangent directions to measure the angle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用切线方向判断角度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[参数曲线切线的速度条件|nonzero tangent vector]] is an arrow along the tangent line. For a differentiable parametrisation $(x(t),y(t))$ with nonzero velocity, the arrow $(x'(t),y'(t))$ supplies that direction. Its length and orientation can change without changing the tangent line. If the two curves have nonzero tangent vectors $u=(u_1,u_2)$ and $v=(v_1,v_2)$ at their common point, perpendicularity is equivalent to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[参数曲线切线的速度条件|非零切向量]]是沿切线方向的一支箭头。若可导参数表示 $(x(t),y(t))$ 的速度非零，那么 $(x'(t),y'(t))$ 就提供了这个方向；改变箭头长度或反转方向，并不会改变切线本身。若两条曲线在共同点的非零切向量为 $u=(u_1,u_2)$、$v=(v_1,v_2)$，则垂直等价于

$$u\cdot v=u_1v_1+u_2v_2=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This [[向量点积|dot product]] multiplies matching coordinates and adds them. For nonzero vectors it also equals $\|u\|\|v\|\cos\theta$, where the norms are their lengths and $\theta$ their angle; it vanishes exactly at a right angle. A zero vector has no direction, so its automatically zero dot product cannot prove perpendicular tangents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个[[向量点积]]把对应坐标相乘后相加。对非零向量，它也等于 $\|u\|\|v\|\cos\theta$，其中范数是长度，$\theta$ 是夹角；因此恰在直角时为零。零向量没有方向，所以它与任何向量点积都为零，并不能证明切线垂直。

> [!example] Circles and radial lines · 圆与径向直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At a point $(x,y)$ on a circle $x^2+y^2=R^2$, the line joining the point to the origin has direction $(x,y)$. The circle has tangent direction $(-y,x)$: differentiating $(R\cos t,R\sin t)$ gives exactly that vector. Their dot product is $-xy+yx=0$. Since $R>0$, neither vector vanishes. Thus every circle centred at the origin crosses each line through the origin orthogonally, including the vertical line.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在圆 $x^2+y^2=R^2$ 上的点 $(x,y)$，连接它与原点的直线方向是 $(x,y)$。圆的切向方向为 $(-y,x)$：对参数式 $(R\cos t,R\sin t)$ 求导，恰好得到这个向量。两者点积是 $-xy+yx=0$，且 $R>0$ 保证两个向量都非零。因此每个以原点为圆心的圆，都与所有过原点的直线正交，包括竖直直线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a numerical check, the radius-five circle passes through $(3,4)$. The radial and circular directions are $(3,4)$ and $(-4,3)$; their dot product is $-12+12=0$. At $(5,0)$, the radial direction is horizontal and the circular direction is vertical. The vector check handles both cases with the same rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体核对：半径为五的圆经过 $(3,4)$，径向与切向分别为 $(3,4)$、$(-4,3)$，点积为 $-12+12=0$。在 $(5,0)$，径向水平而圆的切线竖直；向量检验用同一个规则处理两种情形。

## The negative reciprocal is a special coordinate formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*负倒数是一个有条件的坐标公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both tangents have finite slopes $m$ and $q$, direction vectors are $(1,m)$ and $(1,q)$. Their dot product gives $1+mq=0$. Thus, when the original slope is nonzero, the perpendicular slope is $q=-1/m$. For example, slope $2$ pairs with slope $-1/2$, not $-2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两条切线的斜率都有限，分别为 $m,q$，可取方向向量 $(1,m)$ 与 $(1,q)$。点积给出 $1+mq=0$。因此原斜率非零时，垂直斜率为 $q=-1/m$；例如斜率 $2$ 对应 $-1/2$，而不是 $-2$。

> [!warning] Horizontal and vertical tangents still form a right angle · 水平与竖直仍然正交
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $m=0$, no finite $q$ satisfies $mq=-1$. The perpendicular tangent is vertical, so it cannot be described by a finite slope $dy/dx$. The geometry has not failed; only the chosen slope representation has. At a point with no well-defined tangent, first analyse the relevant branches instead of applying a reciprocal formula.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $m=0$ 时，没有有限 $q$ 能满足 $mq=-1$。垂直方向是竖直切线，不能用有限的 $dy/dx$ 表示。失效的是所用的斜率表示，不是几何关系。若某点没有明确切线，应先分析相关分支，不能直接套负倒数公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[正交轨线求解|Solving for orthogonal trajectories]] turns these tangent conditions into a differential equation. It must retain the region where the original family supplies directions and check any axes or branches removed by division. Being a geometric orthogonal curve is distinct from being one global function graph $y(x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正交轨线求解]]把这些切线条件变成微分方程；它需要保留原曲线族实际提供方向的区域，并检查被除法排除的坐标轴或分支。作为几何上的正交曲线，与作为全局的单一函数图像 $y(x)$，是两件不同的事。
