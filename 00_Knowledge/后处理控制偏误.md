# 当目标是处理的总效应时，控制受处理影响的变量通常会改变目标量，并可能破坏原有可比性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When the target is the total treatment effect, conditioning on a treatment-affected variable usually changes the estimand and may destroy prior comparability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] “发生在处理之后”首先是因果角色，不只是时间戳
> 处理总效应先定义为
> $$
> TE=E[Y(1)-Y(0)].
> $$
> 只有当 $M$ 是定义清楚、可以放入联合干预的中介，并且 $Y(a)=Y(a,M(a))$ 这一 composition 关系成立时，才能把它展开成 $E\{Y(1,M(1))-Y(0,M(0))\}$。
> 普通条件差
> $$
> E[Y\mid A=1,M=m]-E[Y\mid A=0,M=m]
> $$
> 一般不等于这个总效应：按 $M=m$ 条件化会排除总效应中经 $M$ 传递的变化，还可能打开新的非因果路径；这种观察条件化也不等于把 $M$ 外部设为 $m$ 的干预。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Define the total effect first as $TE=E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$. Only when $M$ is a well-defined mediator that admits a joint intervention, and the composition relation $Y(a)=Y(a,M(a))$ holds, may this be expanded as $E\{Y(1,M(1))-Y(0,M(0))\}$. An ordinary contrast conditional on $M=m$ generally does not equal that total effect. Conditioning on $M=m$ excludes variation transmitted through $M$ from the total-effect comparison and may open new noncausal paths. This observational conditioning is also not the same as intervening to set $M$ to $m$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“后处理变量”指可能被处理因果影响的变量，不只是记录时间较晚。实验结束后从档案抄录的出生年份仍是基线属性；相反，依从性、退出、是否通过检查、早期反应和后续服务，只要会被处理改变，就不能当作普通基线控制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A post-treatment variable is one that treatment may causally affect, not simply one recorded late. A birth year copied from an archive after an experiment is still baseline; compliance, attrition, passing a check, an early response, or receipt of later services may be post-treatment even when recorded carefully.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A\to M\to Y$，控制 $M$ 会阻断总效应所包含的中介路径，因此调整后的比较不再以总效应为目标；它也不会因此自动成为直接效应。若另有 $U\to M$ 且 $U\to Y$，则 $M$ 又位于 $A\to M\leftarrow U\to Y$ 上；按 $M$ 分层会使原本可比的 $A$ 与 $U$ 相关。删除未通过处理后检查的样本、只分析坚持治疗者或完成随访者，都是条件化，不会因为没有把 $M$ 写进回归式就变安全。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A\to M\to Y$, conditioning on $M$ blocks a mediated pathway contained in the total effect, so the adjusted comparison no longer targets that total effect; it does not thereby become an identified direct effect. If an unmeasured $U$ also causes both $M$ and $Y$, then conditioning on $M$ opens $A\to M\leftarrow U\to Y$. Dropping units who fail a post-treatment check or analysing only adherers or follow-up completers is still conditioning even when $M$ never appears as a regression covariate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不是“处理后信息永远不能用”。若研究问题本来就是在外部固定 $M=m$ 时的直接效应，可以定义
$$
CDE(m)=E[Y(1,m)-Y(0,m)].
$$
但这已经换了 estimand，还要说明 $m$ 是否可干预，并为处理与 $M$ 两个阶段分别给出一致性、可交换性和正值性。中介分析和纵向 g-method 也可能有意使用后处理信息；它们不能由一条普通回归中的“加入 $M$ 后的 $A$ 系数”自动代替。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a ban on all post-treatment information. A controlled direct effect $CDE(m)=E[Y(1,m)-Y(0,m)]$ may be the intended target, but that changes the estimand and requires a meaningful intervention on $m$ plus identification conditions for both treatment and mediator stages. Mediation analysis and longitudinal g-methods can also use post-treatment information deliberately; an ordinary coefficient on $A$ after adding $M$ does not automatically acquire those interpretations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 处理已经随机分配。把处理后的依从性加入回归后，$R^2$ 提高了。若研究目标仍是分配的总效应，这个处理系数还能直接采用吗？请指出至少两种可能失真的机制。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Treatment was randomised, and adding later compliance to the regression raises $R^2$. If the research target remains the total effect of assignment, can the adjusted treatment coefficient be used directly? Name at least two mechanisms that may distort it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能直接采用。依从性可能是分配影响结果的中介，控制它会阻断总效应的一部分；它也可能同时受分配与健康状态等结果原因影响，条件化后会打开选择路径。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Not directly. Compliance may mediate part of the assignment effect, so adjustment blocks part of the total effect. It may also be caused jointly by assignment and health-related causes of the outcome, so conditioning can open a selection path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Rosenbaum (1984), “The Consequences of Adjustment for a Concomitant Variable That Has Been Affected by the Treatment”](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssa/article/147/5/656/7106019)：核对总效应分析中处理后调整的目标改变与偏误。
- [Schisterman, Cole and Platt (2009), “Overadjustment Bias and Unnecessary Adjustment in Epidemiologic Studies”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2744485/)：核对中介控制、过度调整与直接效应边界。
- [Hernán and Robins, *Causal Inference: What If*, Chapters 19–21](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/wp-content/uploads/sites/1268/2024/04/hernanrobins_WhatIf_26apr24.pdf)：核对处理后信息可在重新定义目标量并补充纵向识别条件后进入 g-method，而非被一律禁用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Rosenbaum (1984) was checked for how adjustment for a treatment-affected variable changes or biases a total-effect analysis.
- Schisterman, Cole, and Platt (2009) was checked for mediator adjustment, overadjustment, and the boundary between total and direct effects.
- Hernán and Robins, *Causal Inference: What If*, Chapters 19–21, was checked for deliberate use of post-treatment information in g-methods after redefining the estimand and supplying longitudinal identification conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
