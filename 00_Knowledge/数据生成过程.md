# 数据生成过程描述观察数据如何由随机机制和模型条件产生

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A data-generating process specifies the random mechanism and conditions producing observed data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 数据生成过程（DGP）规定哪些量随机、它们如何依赖、参数取什么值，以及抽样、分组、测量或筛选怎样产生观测数据。估计量的性质总是相对于这些条件而言。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A DGP specifies random quantities, their dependence, parameter values, and the sampling, assignment, measurement, or selection mechanisms producing observations. Estimator properties are conditional on this specification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

$X_i\overset{iid}{\sim}N(\theta,1)$ 指定同分布、独立性、均值和方差。仅写 $Y_i=\alpha+\beta X_i+\varepsilon_i$ 还没说明误差外生性或观察间依赖，不能直接推出 OLS 无偏或一致。见[[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An iid normal model specifies independence, common distribution, mean, and variance. A regression equation alone does not specify exogeneity or dependence across rows and cannot establish OLS unbiasedness or consistency; see [[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|why a data matrix does not determine a sampling model]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
