# 宽平稳要求有限二阶矩常均值且协方差只依赖滞后
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Wide-sense stationarity requires finite second moments, a constant mean, and lag-only covariance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 实值过程 $\{X_t\}$ 宽平稳，当且仅当：
> 1. 每个 $X_t$ 的二阶矩有限；
> 2. $E[X_t]=\mu$ 不随 $t$ 改变；
> 3. $\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},X_t)=\gamma(h)$ 只依赖滞后 $h$。
> 因而 $\operatorname{Var}(X_t)=\gamma(0)<\infty$ 也不随时间改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real process is wide-sense stationary when its second moments are finite, its mean is constant, and $\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},X_t)=\gamma(h)$ depends only on the lag. Hence $\operatorname{Var}(X_t)=\gamma(0)$ is finite and time-invariant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“图上围绕某个水平波动”不是定义。趋势、结构突变和随时间变化的无条件方差都可能破坏宽平稳；把原序列变换成平稳序列，也不等于原序列本身平稳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Visual fluctuation around a level is not the definition. Trends, structural breaks, or time-varying unconditional variance can violate WSS, and stationarity of a transformed series does not transfer back to the original level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 只验证 $E[X_t]=0$ 和 $\operatorname{Var}(X_t)=1$，是否足以判断宽平稳？
>
> **答案：** 不足。还必须验证任意两期协方差在共同平移后只由两期的间隔决定。
