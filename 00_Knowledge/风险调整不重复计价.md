# 对现金流作确定性等价调整后应使用匹配无风险率而不重复加入同一风险溢价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Certainty-equivalent cash flows require matching risk-free rates without repeating the same risk premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果已把风险现金转换成与其价值相等的确定金额，即确定性等价现金（CE），就应使用同币种、同期限口径的无风险率折现。已经在 CE 中扣减风险补偿后，再用原来的风险调整贴现率，会把同一风险补偿重复计入。这是[[贴现率口径匹配]]的一个边界，而不是说任何保守预测都应改用无风险率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When risky cash is converted into an equally valuable guaranteed amount—a certainty equivalent (CE)—discount it at a matching-currency, matching-maturity risk-free rate. Using the original risk-adjusted rate after deducting risk compensation in CE counts the same compensation twice. This is a boundary of [[贴现率口径匹配|discount-rate matching]], not a reason to discount every conservative forecast at a risk-free rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把好坏情景按概率加权，只是在求期望现金流；把坏结果纳入预测并没有自动完成风险溢价调整。因此，[[企业现金流估值]]使用的期望现金流仍需与其风险匹配的资本成本。只有另作有定价依据的调整、真正转换成 CE，才切换分母；任意打一个“谨慎折扣”并不自动得到 CE。名义／实际口径还须遵守[[名义与实际贴现]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Probability-weighting good and bad scenarios only estimates expected cash flow; including adverse outcomes does not automatically account for the risk premium. Expected cash in [[企业现金流估值|FCFF valuation]] still requires a matching cost of capital. Change the denominator only after a justified pricing adjustment genuinely produces CE; an arbitrary cautious haircut is insufficient. [[名义与实际贴现|Nominal–real consistency]] still applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个单期数例可以分清两条路线。下一期的期望现金为120，匹配风险回报要求为20%，同币种同期限无风险率为5%。给定这组定价条件，与它现值相同的 CE 是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A one-period example separates the two routes. Next-period expected cash is 120, its matching required return is 20%, and the matching risk-free rate is 5%. Under these pricing assumptions, the equally valuable CE is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$CE_1=120\frac{1.05}{1.20}=105,\qquad \frac{120}{1.20}=\frac{105}{1.05}=100.$$

若把已经调整过的105再除以1.20，会得到87.5，而非这组假设下的100。这里105是从给定风险定价推出来的等价金额，不是额外挑一个悲观情景来替代期望值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discounting the adjusted 105 again at 20% gives 87.5 rather than the value of 100 under these assumptions. The 105 is derived from the specified risk pricing, not chosen as a pessimistic scenario to replace the expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [[贴现率口径匹配]]、[[名义与实际贴现]]：复用已有风险、币种、期限与名义／实际的口径约束，不重写其一般原则。

Section 4 of Damodaran's paper distinguishes probability-weighted expectations from CE adjustments and matches CE with risk-free rather than risk-adjusted rates. The one-period numbers are independently derived from the stated pricing assumptions; the rest of the distress model is outside this atom. The shared matching atoms provide the remaining measurement constraints.
