# Differentials
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*微分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **differential** is the output change predicted by a function’s tangent model for a chosen input change. It turns a local slope into a usable estimate: multiply the input change by the slope at the starting point. The predicted change is exactly defined even though it usually only approximates the change in the original function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**微分**是切线模型对给定输入增量预测的输出变化。它把局部斜率变成可用的估计：用起点处的斜率乘以输入变化。预测量本身有精确的定义，但通常只是原函数真实变化的近似。

> [!example] A square grows slightly · 一个平方值的小变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $y=x^2$, start at $x=3$ and increase the input by $0.01$. The [[导数|derivative]], the local rate of change, is $f'(3)=6$. The tangent model therefore predicts an output change of $6(0.01)=0.06$. The actual change is slightly larger:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $y=x^2$，从 $x=3$ 出发，把输入增加 $0.01$。[[导数]]表示局部变化率，这里 $f'(3)=6$，所以切线模型预测输出增加 $6(0.01)=0.06$。真实变化稍大：
>
> $$
> \Delta y=(3.01)^2-3^2=0.0601,\qquad dy=6(0.01)=0.06.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The extra $0.0001=(0.01)^2$ is the quadratic contribution omitted by the linear model. The symbol $dy$ names the prediction; $\Delta y$ names the actual output change.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 多出的 $0.0001=(0.01)^2$ 是线性模型省略的二次项。$dy$ 表示预测量，$\Delta y$ 表示真实输出变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Fix a base point, then choose an increment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先固定基点，再选择增量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f$ be differentiable at an interior point $a$ of its real domain. The number $f'(a)$ is its slope there. The differential at $a$, written $df_a$, is the linear rule that sends an input increment $h$ to $f'(a)h$. It is linear in the increment: doubling $h$ doubles the prediction, and predictions for increments add.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实函数 $f$ 在定义域内点 $a$ 可导，数 $f'(a)$ 是该点的斜率。$a$ 处的微分记作 $df_a$，是把输入增量 $h$ 送到 $f'(a)h$ 的线性规则。这里“线性”针对增量：$h$ 翻倍，预测量也翻倍；增量相加，预测量也相加。

> [!important] The differential is an exact linear rule · 微分是精确定义的线性规则
> $$
> df_a(h)=f'(a)h.\qquad \text{Writing }dx=h,\quad dy=df_a(dx)=f'(a)\,dx.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The base point $a$ and the chosen increment $dx$ have different jobs. The base point selects the slope; the increment is fed into that slope. Here $dx$ can be an ordinary real number, including zero. To compare with a function value, $a+dx$ must remain in the domain; a useful local approximation additionally requires a suitably small increment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 基点 $a$ 与所选增量 $dx$ 分工不同：基点决定用哪个斜率，增量则送入这个斜率。在这里，$dx$ 可以是普通实数，包括零。要与函数值比较，必须保证 $a+dx$ 仍在定义域内；要获得有用的局部近似，还需要增量足够小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[线性近似|linear approximation]] predicts the whole output, $L_a(a+h)=f(a)+df_a(h)$. The differential predicts only its change from $f(a)$. For $f(x)=x^2$ at $a=3$, the rule is $df_3(h)=6h$; at $a=5$, it becomes $df_5(h)=10h$. A differential always belongs to a specified base point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[线性近似]]预测完整输出：$L_a(a+h)=f(a)+df_a(h)$；微分只预测相对于 $f(a)$ 的变化。对 $f(x)=x^2$，基点 $a=3$ 时规则为 $df_3(h)=6h$，改成 $a=5$ 后则为 $df_5(h)=10h$。微分始终对应一个指定基点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What makes the prediction accurate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么它能近似真实变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the actual input change is $\Delta x=h$, choose $dx=h$ for the tangent model. Differentiability means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真实输入变化为 $\Delta x=h$ 时，在切线模型中取 $dx=h$。可导性的含义是

$$
\Delta y=f(a+h)-f(a)=f'(a)h+r(h),\qquad\frac{|r(h)|}{|h|}\longrightarrow0\quad(h\to0,\ h\ne0).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $\Delta y=dy+r(h)$. The [[小o记号|notation $r(h)=o(h)$]] says that the error becomes small relative to the input step, not that the error is exactly zero. At $a=0$ for $f(x)=x^2$, the differential is $dy=0$, while the actual change is $h^2$. This is still a valid first-order model because $h^2/|h|\to0$; it need not be a good relative approximation to a nonzero output change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $\Delta y=dy+r(h)$。[[小o记号|记号 $r(h)=o(h)$]]表示误差相对于输入步长趋于零，并不表示误差恰好为零。对 $f(x)=x^2$ 在 $a=0$ 处，微分为 $dy=0$，实际变化却为 $h^2$。由于 $h^2/|h|\to0$，这仍是正确的一阶模型，但它未必能对非零输出变化给出好的相对近似。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a numerical guarantee at a particular step size, use an interval estimate such as [[导数界控制函数差|a derivative bound]] or a suitable [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder]]. The differential by itself is a local model, not a certified error bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要对某个具体步长给出数值保证，需要[[导数界控制函数差]]这样的区间估计，或适用的 [[Taylor余项]]。微分本身提供局部模型，并不自动提供误差上界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Pass changes through a composition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让增量经过复合函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $u=g(x)$ and $y=f(u)$, take the input base point $x=a$ and the intermediate base point $u=g(a)$. Assuming $g$ is differentiable at $a$ and $f$ at $g(a)$, the two linear predictions compose:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $u=g(x)$、$y=f(u)$，取输入基点 $x=a$，中间基点便是 $u=g(a)$。假设 $g$ 在 $a$ 可导，$f$ 在 $g(a)$ 可导，则两次线性预测可以复合：

$$
du=g'(a)\,dx,\qquad dy=f'(g(a))\,du=f'(g(a))g'(a)\,dx.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the [[链式法则|chain rule]] expressed through differentials. The intermediate $du$ is determined by $dx$; it is not an unrelated increment chosen afterwards. The familiar fraction-like notation can organize the calculation, but cancelling symbols never replaces checking the functions, their base points and differentiability. With several inputs, the corresponding linear rule is the [[全微分|total differential]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这正是用微分写出的[[链式法则]]。中间的 $du$ 由 $dx$ 决定，不是之后随意另选的无关增量。像分数一样的记号可以帮助组织计算，但约掉符号不能代替对函数、基点和可导性的检查。多个输入时，对应的线性规则是[[全微分]]。
