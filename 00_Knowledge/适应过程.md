# Adapted process
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*适应过程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A process is **adapted** to an information flow when its value at each time can already be determined from the information available then. A running total of tosses already observed is adapted. A value chosen from a still hidden next toss need not be.

The reference information is part of the statement. A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ tells us which events are decidable at each time. Calling a process adapted without specifying the filtration leaves out what the observer knows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过程对一个信息流**适应**，是说在每个时刻，已经掌握的信息就足以确定过程当时的值。已经观察到的抛掷结果的累计和是适应的；由尚未揭晓的下一次抛掷决定的值，则未必适应。

所参照的信息是陈述的一部分。[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ 说明每个时刻哪些事件可以判定。只说“过程适应”而不说明滤过，就省略了观察者到底知道什么。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Measurability makes “already known” precise
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用可测性表达“当前已知”*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X=(X_t)_{t\in I}$ be a real-valued [[随机过程|stochastic process]] on a fixed probability space, and let $(\mathcal F_t)$ be a filtration on that space. The process is adapted precisely when each $X_t$ is $\mathcal F_t$-measurable. For real-valued variables, this means
$$\{\,\omega:X_t(\omega)\le c\,\}\in\mathcal F_t
\qquad\text{for every }t\in I,\ c\in\mathbb R.$$
Every threshold question about the current value can be answered from current information. For variables in other measurable state spaces, the corresponding inverse-image condition uses their measurable sets.

A [[随机变量|random variable]] is a function on outcomes, while a sigma-algebra contains sets of outcomes. Thus writing $X_t\in\mathcal F_t$ is sometimes used as a shorthand for measurability, but does not literally mean that a numerical variable is an event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X=(X_t)_{t\in I}$ 是固定概率空间上的实值[[随机过程]]，$(\mathcal F_t)$ 是该空间上的滤过。过程适应，当且仅当每个 $X_t$ 都对 $\mathcal F_t$ 可测。对实值变量，这意味着上式对每个 $t$、每个实数阈值 $c$ 都成立：关于当前值的所有阈值问题，都可以用当前信息回答。其他可测状态空间则对其中可测集合的原像提出相应要求。

[[随机变量]]是定义在结果上的函数，而 σ-代数包含的是结果集合。因此，有时写 $X_t\in\mathcal F_t$ 只是可测性的简写，不是说一个数值变量在字面上就是一个事件。

> [!example] A hidden toss is not measurable yet · 隐藏的抛掷尚不可测
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use two independent fair tosses with outcomes $HH,HT,TH,TT$, and increments $\xi_i=1$ for heads and $-1$ for tails. Let $\mathcal F_0$ be trivial, $\mathcal F_1=\sigma(\xi_1)$ and $\mathcal F_2=\sigma(\xi_1,\xi_2)$. The partial sums $S_0=0$, $S_1=\xi_1$, $S_2=\xi_1+\xi_2$ are adapted: their values can be computed at the stated times.
>
> Consider instead $Y_0=0$, $Y_1=\xi_2$, $Y_2=\xi_2$. At time one, the outcomes $HH$ and $HT$ produce the same observation of the first toss, but $Y_1$ takes values $1$ and $-1$ on them. In fact
> $$\{Y_1\le0\}=\{HT,TT\}\notin\mathcal F_1.$$
> This one threshold event is enough to disprove adaptation. If a different filtration reveals the second toss already at time one, $Y$ can become adapted to that larger information flow.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取两次独立公平抛掷，结果为 $HH,HT,TH,TT$，令正面对应增量 $1$、反面对应 $-1$。$\mathcal F_0$ 为平凡 σ-代数，$\mathcal F_1=\sigma(\xi_1)$，$\mathcal F_2=\sigma(\xi_1,\xi_2)$。部分和 $S_0=0$、$S_1=\xi_1$、$S_2=\xi_1+\xi_2$ 是适应的，因为每个时刻都能用已有观察算出对应值。
>
> 改看 $Y_0=0$、$Y_1=\xi_2$、$Y_2=\xi_2$。时刻一，$HH$ 与 $HT$ 给出相同的第一次观察，$Y_1$ 却分别取 $1$ 和 $-1$。事件 $\{Y_1\le0\}=\{HT,TT\}$ 不属于 $\mathcal F_1$；仅这一个阈值事件，就足以否定适应性。如果另一滤过在时刻一就揭示第二次结果，$Y$ 可以对那个更大的信息流适应。

## Larger information preserves adaptation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信息扩大保留适应性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every process is adapted to its [[自然滤过|natural filtration]], generated by all its observations up to the present. More generally, if $X$ is adapted to $(\mathcal F_t)$ and another filtration satisfies $\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal G_t$ for every $t$, each threshold event already in $\mathcal F_t$ is also in $\mathcal G_t$. Thus $X$ remains adapted. Shrinking the filtration may remove a needed event.

Equivalently,
$$X\text{ is adapted to }(\mathcal G_t)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal F_t^X\subseteq\mathcal G_t\text{ for every }t.$$
The forward direction uses the fact that all past $X_u$ must remain measurable in the larger time-$t$ information; the reverse direction follows because $X_t$ is measurable in its own natural history.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过程总是对自己的[[自然滤过]]适应，该滤过由截至当前的全部过程观察生成。一般地，若 $X$ 对 $(\mathcal F_t)$ 适应，并且另一滤过逐时满足 $\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal G_t$，那么原先在 $\mathcal F_t$ 中的阈值事件也都在 $\mathcal G_t$ 中，适应性仍成立。缩小滤过则可能丢掉所需事件。

等价地，$X$ 对 $(\mathcal G_t)$ 适应，当且仅当每个时刻的自然历史 $\mathcal F_t^X$ 都包含在 $\mathcal G_t$ 中。正向使用“过去每个 $X_u$ 的可测性会保留到时刻 $t$”；反向使用“$X_t$ 对自己的自然历史可测”。

> [!warning] Observable does not mean predictable or fair · 可观测不等于可预测或公平
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Adaptation concerns current information. In discrete time, a [[离散可预测过程|predictable process]] requires the time-$n$ value to be measurable already with respect to $\mathcal F_{n-1}$, before the current result arrives. For example, $\xi_n$ is observable after toss $n$ but cannot be predicted from the preceding independent tosses.
>
> A [[鞅定义|martingale]] additionally needs integrability and a conditional-average equality; adaptation alone supplies neither. Even adding integrability does not make adaptation sufficient. The separate [[适应不推鞅|adaptation-versus-martingale boundary]] shows how expanding information can preserve adaptation while destroying the martingale property.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 适应性针对当前信息。离散时间的[[离散可预测过程|可预测过程]]更早要求第 $n$ 期的值对 $\mathcal F_{n-1}$ 可测，即在本期结果到来前已经确定。例如，$\xi_n$ 在第 $n$ 次抛掷后可观察，却不能由此前独立的抛掷预测。
>
> [[鞅定义|鞅]]还要求可积性和条件平均等式，适应性本身不提供其中任何一项；即使再加可积性也仍不充分。[[适应不推鞅]]具体解释了扩大信息为什么可以保留适应性，同时破坏鞅性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In continuous time, adaptation is a separate-time measurability statement. It does not by itself give continuity of paths or joint measurability in time and outcome. Results needing stronger regularity state it separately. Also, “future” is relative to the chosen information model: if a signal genuinely reveals a later process value early, an adapted observer may use it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续时间的适应性逐个时刻要求可测，本身不保证路径连续，也不保证对时间与随机结果的联合可测性。需要更强正则性的结果应另外说明。“未来”也相对于所选信息模型：如果一个信号确实提前揭示了较晚的过程值，适应的观察者可以使用这个已经进入信息流的信号。
