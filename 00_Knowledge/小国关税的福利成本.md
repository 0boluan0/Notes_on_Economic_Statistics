# 在无其他扭曲的小国竞争模型中进口关税造成生产与消费两项净损失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In an otherwise undistorted competitive small-country model, an import tariff causes production and consumption losses.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进口关税是向进口商品征税。这里的“小国”指本国买卖量不能改变世界价格 $p_w$，不是地理面积小。假设国内外商品同质、市场竞争、无运输成本和其他扭曲，征收每单位税 $t>0$ 后仍有进口，则国内价格变为 $p_w+t$。这时消费者少买，国内企业多生产，进口减少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An import tariff taxes imported goods. “Small country” means domestic trade cannot alter the world price, not a small geographical area. With homogeneous goods, competition, no transport costs or other distortions, and imports still positive, a specific tariff raises the domestic price from the world price by the full tax. Consumption falls, domestic production rises and imports decline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税款并没有全部消失：生产者得到部分消费者损失，政府取得税收。把消费者剩余、生产者剩余和政府收入放进同一个国内账户后，净损失来自两处：新增国内产量的资源成本高于进口成本；被放弃的消费原本估值高于进口成本。税收不能在这些资源损失之外再算一次成本，见 [[转移支付核算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not all consumer losses disappear: some accrue to producers and some become government revenue. In a common domestic account, net losses arise because extra domestic output costs more than imports and forgone consumption was valued above import cost. Do not count the tax payment again as a resource loss; see [[转移支付核算|Transfer accounting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自拟线性例：$D(p)=100-p$、$S(p)=p$、$p_w=20$、$t=10$。采用无收入效应的货币剩余口径，固定成本不随政策改变：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the constructed linear example below, using monetary surplus without income effects and fixed costs unchanged by policy:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 状态 | 国内价格 | 需求 | 国内供给 | 进口 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 自由贸易 | 20 | 80 | 20 | 60 |
| 每单位关税 10 | 30 | 70 | 30 | 40 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Free trade gives price 20, consumption 80, domestic output 20 and imports 60. The tariff changes these to 30, 70, 30 and 40 respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta CS&=-\tfrac12(80+70)\times10=-750,\\
\Delta PS&=\tfrac12(20+30)\times10=250,\\
R&=10\times40=400,\\
\Delta W&=-750+250+400=-100.
\end{aligned}
$$

净损失也等于两个三角形：$\tfrac12\times10\times10=50$ 的生产损失，加上同样为 50 的消费损失。这个结论依赖上述条件：大国的贸易条件变化、学习外部性、投入品传导或报复措施须另建分析，不能直接把 100 当作现实关税的全部代价；存在 [[学习外部性]] 也不自动证明关税是最合适的工具。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same loss is the sum of two triangles of 50, one for production and one for consumption. Terms-of-trade effects in a large country, learning externalities, input-output effects and retaliation require additional analysis. The existence of a [[学习外部性|learning externality]] does not establish that a tariff is the best instrument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
