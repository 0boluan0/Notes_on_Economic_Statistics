# When moments are finite
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样判断所需的矩是否有限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A probability distribution can be valid while its mean or variance fails to be finite. Total probability measures how much probability there is; a moment also multiplies that probability by the size of the values. Very rare but very large values can make the weighted total diverge. Before using a mean, variance or a theorem involving them, check the particular weighted total that it needs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个概率分布可以完全合法，但均值或方差仍然不是有限数值。总概率只累计概率有多少；矩还要把概率乘以取值的大小。很罕见却非常大的取值，可能使加权后的总量发散。使用均值、方差或依赖它们的定理前，要检查实际需要的那个加权总量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[原点矩与中心矩|raw moment]] of order $k$ is $E[X^k]$, the [[期望|expectation]] of the $k$th power of a random variable. Here $k$ is a positive integer. For a finite real moment, the decisive condition is the finite **absolute moment** $E[|X|^k]<\infty$. The absolute value removes signs before we ask whether the weighted magnitudes have a finite total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个 $k$ 阶[[原点矩与中心矩|原点矩]]是 $E[X^k]$，即随机变量 $k$ 次幂的[[期望]]，这里 $k$ 为正整数。若要得到有限实数矩，关键条件是**绝对矩** $E[|X|^k]<\infty$。绝对值先去掉正负号，再检查加权后的大小是否累积成有限总量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A finite mean with an infinite variance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值有限，方差却可以无限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a nonnegative variable with [[概率密度函数|density]] $f(x)=2/x^3$ for $x\ge1$, and zero elsewhere. This is a valid density: $\int_1^\infty 2x^{-3}\,dx=1$. The tail extends to arbitrarily large values, although its density gets small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑一个非负变量，其[[概率密度函数|密度]]在 $x\ge1$ 时为 $f(x)=2/x^3$，其余位置为零。这是合法密度，因为 $\int_1^\infty 2x^{-3}\,dx=1$。它能取到任意大的值，只是远处的密度越来越小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To calculate its mean and second moment, [[LOTUS|average the functions]] $x$ and $x^2$ under this density. Integrating to infinity means first integrating up to a finite bound $R$, then letting $R$ increase without limit:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求均值和二阶矩时，使用 [[LOTUS|函数期望的计算规则]]，分别按该密度平均 $x$ 和 $x^2$。积分到无穷，表示先积到有限上界 $R$，再让 $R$ 不断增大：

$$\int_1^R x\frac{2}{x^3}\,dx=2-\frac2R\longrightarrow2,\qquad \int_1^R x^2\frac{2}{x^3}\,dx=2\log R\longrightarrow+\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So $E[X]=2$, but $E[X^2]=+\infty$. The first weighted total settles at a finite number; the second keeps growing. Its [[方差|variance]] is infinite as well: for $x\ge4$, $(x-2)^2\ge x^2/4$, so the average squared deviation still has a divergent tail. A finite mean therefore does not justify a finite-variance formula or theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $E[X]=2$，但 $E[X^2]=+\infty$。第一个加权总量趋于有限数值，第二个却不断增长。它的[[方差]]也无限：当 $x\ge4$ 时，$(x-2)^2\ge x^2/4$，因此平均平方偏离的尾部仍会发散。均值有限，不能保证要求有限方差的公式或定理可以使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which condition to check
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*具体应该检查哪个条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a [[概率质量函数|PMF]] $p_X$, compute or bound $\sum_x|x|^k p_X(x)$. With a density $f_X$, use $\int |x|^k f_X(x)\,dx$. If neither representation covers the full distribution, use its general probability integral. Checking only that the probabilities sum or integrate to one verifies the distribution, not its moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有[[概率质量函数|PMF]] $p_X$ 时，计算或估计 $\sum_x|x|^k p_X(x)$；有密度 $f_X$ 时，检查 $\int |x|^k f_X(x)\,dx$。如果两种表示都不能覆盖整个分布，就使用一般的概率积分。只检查概率相加或积分为一，核验的是分布，并没有核验矩。

| Quantity · 所需量 | Finite-value condition · 有限性条件 |
|---|---|
| Mean · 均值 | $E[\lvert X\rvert]<\infty$ |
| Variance · 方差 | $E[X^2]<\infty$ |
| Raw moment of order $k$ · $k$ 阶原点矩 | $E[\lvert X\rvert^k]<\infty$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a variable with finitely many finite values, these conditions hold at every finite integer order. More generally, if $E[|X|^k]$ is finite and $1\le j\le k$, the lower moment is finite too: $|x|^j\le1+|x|^k$. The reverse implication can fail, as the example above shows. Finite $k$th absolute moment also gives a finite mean and finite central moments up to that order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果变量只取有限个有限数值，那么每个有限整数阶都满足条件。更一般地，若 $E[|X|^k]$ 有限，且 $1\le j\le k$，较低阶矩也有限，因为 $|x|^j\le1+|x|^k$。反方向未必成立，上面的例子已经说明这一点。有限的 $k$ 阶绝对矩，也保证均值以及不超过该阶的中心矩有限。

> [!warning] Undefined is different from positive infinity · 没有定义与正无穷不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a nonnegative variable, an infinite expectation is the well-defined extended value $+\infty$. For a signed variable, add positive contributions and the magnitudes of negative contributions separately. If both totals are infinite, their difference is undefined. Symmetry cannot turn $\infty-\infty$ into zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 非负变量的无限期望，是扩展数值中有定义的 $+\infty$。对于有正有负的变量，应分别累计正值贡献和负值贡献的大小；如果两边都是无穷，相减就没有定义。对称性不能把 $\infty-\infty$ 变成零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The standard Cauchy distribution illustrates the second case. Its density is $1/[\pi(1+x^2)]$ on the real line. On the positive side, the contribution to the mean up to $R$ is $\log(1+R^2)/(2\pi)$, which grows without bound; the negative side has the same infinite magnitude. Its mean is undefined, so the usual variance about that mean is undefined too. A [[Cauchy 主值与反常积分|symmetric principal value]] is a different operation and does not supply a missing expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准 Cauchy 分布说明第二种情况：它在实数轴上的密度为 $1/[\pi(1+x^2)]$。正半轴上累积到 $R$ 的均值贡献为 $\log(1+R^2)/(2\pi)$，会无限增大；负半轴上的贡献大小同样无限。因此它的均值没有定义，围绕该均值定义的通常方差也没有定义。[[Cauchy 主值与反常积分|对称主值]]是另一种运算，不能补出一个不存在的期望。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, match these checks to the actual result being used. [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev bounds]], a [[有界方差弱大数定律|finite-variance weak law]], a [[强大数定律|strong law]], and a [[经典中心极限定理|classical central limit theorem]] have their own assumptions. Finite moments do not provide independence. A finite [[协方差|covariance]] requires finite means and an integrable product of the centred variables; [[相关系数|correlation]] also needs nonzero finite standard deviations. A familiar formula is a reason to check its assumptions, not evidence that they hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，把检查结果与实际使用的结论对应起来：[[Chebyshev不等式]]、[[有界方差弱大数定律]]、[[强大数定律]]和[[经典中心极限定理]]各有自己的假设；矩有限不会自动提供独立性。有限的[[协方差]]需要两个均值有限，且中心化变量的乘积可积；[[相关系数]]还要求标准差有限且非零。见到熟悉公式，应当去核对其条件，而不能由公式的存在推断条件已经满足。
