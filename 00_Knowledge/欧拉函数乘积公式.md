# Euler's totient product formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*欧拉函数乘积公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
How can we count the integers coprime to seventy-two without testing all seventy-two remainders? Its factorization $72=2^3\cdot3^2$ separates two kinds of prime obstruction: divisibility by two and divisibility by three. The totient product formula computes the count from the prime factorization, using one correction factor for each **different** prime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

怎样不逐个测试七十二个余数，就数出其中与七十二互素的整数？分解 $72=2^3\cdot3^2$ 把障碍分成两类：被二整除，以及被三整除。欧拉函数乘积公式从素因数分解计算这个数，对每一种**不同**素数使用一个修正因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[欧拉函数|totient]] $\varphi(n)$ counts $r=0,\ldots,n-1$ with $\gcd(r,n)=1$, for a positive integer $n$. By [[唯一素因数分解|unique prime factorization]], every integer $n\ge2$ can be written as a finite product of prime powers. A [[素数|prime]] is an integer greater than one with no positive divisors other than one and itself. We group all copies of the same prime into one power before applying the formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $n$，[[欧拉函数]] $\varphi(n)$ 统计 $r=0,\ldots,n-1$ 中满足 $\gcd(r,n)=1$ 的个数。由[[唯一素因数分解]]，每个整数 $n\ge2$ 都能写成有限个素数幂的乘积。[[素数]]是大于一、正因子只有一与自身的整数。应用公式前，要把同一种素数的全部出现次数合并成一个幂。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One factor for each distinct prime
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每种不同素数对应一个因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the prime factorization of $n\ge2$, with distinct primes and positive exponents, the displayed formula holds. Each distinct prime contributes once; its multiplicity is already contained in $n$. The empty-product convention also gives $\varphi(1)=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge2$ 的素因数分解为 $n=\prod_{i=1}^r p_i^{\alpha_i}$，其中 $p_i$ 两两不同、$\alpha_i\ge1$。则
$$
\varphi(n)=n\prod_{i=1}^r\left(1-\frac1{p_i}\right).
$$
乘积中每个不同素因子只出现一次；它在分解中的重数已经包含在前面的 $n$ 里。$n=1$ 时把空乘积约定为 $1$，也与 $\varphi(1)=1$ 一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r\ge1$ is the number of distinct prime factors, the index $i$ identifies one of them, and the positive integer $\alpha_i$ counts its occurrences. The symbol $\prod$ means multiply the indicated factors. For seventy-two, $r=2$, with primes $2,3$ and exponents $3,2$. The three copies of two do not create three separate factors $1-1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $r\ge1$ 是不同素因子的种数，下标 $i$ 标识其中一种，正整数 $\alpha_i$ 记录其出现次数；符号 $\prod$ 表示把指定各项相乘。对七十二，$r=2$，素数为 $2,3$，指数为 $3,2$。三个二并不对应三个独立的 $1-1/2$ 因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the prime-power counts multiply
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么素数幂的计数能够相乘*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $q_i=p_i^{\alpha_i}$. Different $q_i$ share no prime divisor. Indeed, a common divisor greater than one would have a prime factor appearing in both prime-power factorizations, forcing their underlying primes to be equal. The same reasoning shows that the product $q_1\cdots q_j$ is coprime to $q_{j+1}$: the new prime occurs in none of the preceding factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $q_i=p_i^{\alpha_i}$。不同 $q_i$ 没有公共素因子：若存在大于一的公因数，它的某个素因子就会同时出现在两边的素数幂分解中，迫使底下的素数相同。同理，已合并的乘积 $q_1\cdots q_j$ 与下一个 $q_{j+1}$ 也互素，因为新的素数没有出现在前面任何一块中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[欧拉函数互素乘法性|coprime multiplicativity theorem]] applies at every step. Start with $q_1$; adjoining $q_2$ multiplies its totient by $\varphi(q_2)$, and adjoining each later block multiplies by that block's totient. For $r=1$ there is no combining step. Thus $\varphi(n)=\prod_i\varphi(q_i)$. Each block then uses the independently proved [[素数幂欧拉函数|prime-power formula]], valid for a prime base and a positive integer exponent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一步都满足[[欧拉函数互素乘法性]]的条件。从 $q_1$ 开始，并入 $q_2$ 时乘上 $\varphi(q_2)$，之后每并入一块就乘上该块的欧拉函数。$r=1$ 时不需要合并。因此 $\varphi(n)=\prod_i\varphi(q_i)$。再对每块使用已经独立证明的[[素数幂欧拉函数]]，其条件是底数为素数、指数为正整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinct prime-power factors are coprime. Applying [[欧拉函数互素乘法性|multiplicativity]] and then the [[素数幂欧拉函数|prime-power formula]] gives the displayed derivation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同素数的幂彼此互素，因此先应用 [[欧拉函数互素乘法性]]，再对每块应用 [[素数幂欧拉函数]]：
$$
\varphi(n)=\prod_i\varphi(p_i^{\alpha_i})
=\prod_i p_i^{\alpha_i}\left(1-\frac1{p_i}\right)
=n\prod_i\left(1-\frac1{p_i}\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last equality collects all the factors $p_i^{\alpha_i}$ into their product $n$. No assumption says that arbitrary positive integers are coprime; the special prime-power blocks make each use of multiplicativity valid. Another form keeps every factor an integer:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个等号把全部 $p_i^{\alpha_i}$ 合并为乘积 $n$。这里并没有假设任意正整数都互素，而是这些特定的素数幂块保证每次乘法性都可用。也可写成每个因子都是整数的形式：

$$\varphi(n)=\prod_{i=1}^r p_i^{\alpha_i-1}(p_i-1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This follows from $p_i^{\alpha_i}(1-1/p_i)=p_i^{\alpha_i-1}(p_i-1)$. Because $\alpha_i\ge1$, the exponent $\alpha_i-1$ is nonnegative. The fractions in the first form therefore combine to an integer count; they are not a rounding approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是因为 $p_i^{\alpha_i}(1-1/p_i)=p_i^{\alpha_i-1}(p_i-1)$，且 $\alpha_i\ge1$ 保证 $\alpha_i-1$ 非负。因此第一种形式中的分数最终给出整数计数，并不是取整近似。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compute seventy-two, then check what was assumed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*计算七十二，并核对用到的条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $72=2^3\cdot3^2$ has totient twenty-four. For distinct primes in [[RSA公钥加密|RSA]], the formula gives $(p-1)(q-1)$. The calculation assumes the prime factorization is known; the formula itself does not supply a fast way to find it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $72=2^3\cdot3^2$，所以 $\varphi(72)=72(1-1/2)(1-1/3)=24$。在 [[RSA公钥加密]] 的不同素数 $n=pq$ 情形，公式直接给出 $(p-1)(q-1)$。这个算法以已知素因数分解为前提；知道公式并不自动提供一种从大合数 $n$ 快速找出其素因子的办法。

> [!example] Keep multiplicities inside the prime powers · 重数放在素数幂中
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $72=8\cdot9$, the separate counts are $\varphi(8)=8-4=4$ and $\varphi(9)=9-3=6$. The blocks eight and nine are coprime, so the result is $4\cdot6=24$. In the compact form, $72(1-1/2)(1-1/3)=72\cdot(1/2)\cdot(2/3)=24$. Repeating the correction once for every prime occurrence instead would give $72(1/2)^3(2/3)^2=4$, which is a different, incorrect count.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $72$ 拆成 $8\cdot9$，两块计数分别为 $\varphi(8)=8-4=4$、$\varphi(9)=9-3=6$。八与九互素，因此结果是 $4\cdot6=24$。紧凑公式给出 $72(1-1/2)(1-1/3)=72\cdot(1/2)\cdot(2/3)=24$。若误按素数每次出现都重复修正，会得到 $72(1/2)^3(2/3)^2=4$，并不是正确计数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For distinct primes $p,q$, the two corrections give $pq(1-1/p)(1-1/q)=(p-1)(q-1)$. Distinctness matters: if the input is $p^2$, there is only one distinct prime, so its totient is $p^2(1-1/p)=p(p-1)$ instead. For example, $\varphi(9)=6$, not $(3-1)^2=4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对不同素数 $p,q$，两次修正得到 $pq(1-1/p)(1-1/q)=(p-1)(q-1)$。不同这一点很重要：若输入是 $p^2$，只有一种素数，因此欧拉函数为 $p^2(1-1/p)=p(p-1)$。例如 $\varphi(9)=6$，不是 $(3-1)^2=4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One and the domain of the formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一与公式的输入范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $n=1$, the prime list is empty. An empty product has value one, the identity for multiplication, so the formula reads $1\cdot1=1$. This agrees with the totient definition: the only candidate is zero and $\gcd(0,1)=1$. One is not a prime and must not be inserted as a factor $1-1/1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $n=1$ 时，素数列表为空。空乘积的值是一，即乘法单位元，所以公式给出 $1\cdot1=1$。这与欧拉函数定义一致：唯一候选是零，且 $\gcd(0,1)=1$。一不是素数，不能人为加进一个 $1-1/1$ 因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a prime input, there is one prime and exponent one, recovering $\varphi(p)=p-1$. For any positive prime power, there is still only one distinct prime. Zero and negative integers are outside the positive-input totient definition used here. To evaluate the product numerically, one needs the actual prime factors; the identity alone is not an algorithmic guarantee for finding the factorization of a large integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入是素数时，只有一个素数且指数为一，恢复 $\varphi(p)=p-1$；输入是正指数的素数幂时，也仍然只有一种素数。零与负整数不在这里采用的正整数欧拉函数定义范围内。要数值计算这个乘积，需要知道实际素因子；等式本身不保证能快速找到大整数的分解。
