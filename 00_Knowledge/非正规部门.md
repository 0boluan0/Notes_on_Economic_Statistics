# 非正规部门由主要面向市场营利但未被政府正式承认为独立市场生产者的经济单位组成

按 ILO 2023 年第 21 届 ICLS 决议，非正规部门（informal sector）的分类对象是经济单位：其生产主要面向市场、旨在为所有者创造收入或利润，但未被正式承认为区别于所有者家庭自用生产的市场生产者。这些单位属于非正规的家庭非公司市场企业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the ILO's 2023 resolution adopted by the 21st ICLS, the informal sector classifies economic units. They produce mainly for the market to generate income or profit for their owners, but are not formally recognized as market producers distinct from their owners' household own-use production. They are informal household unincorporated market enterprises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能只凭“没办某张证”判断。政府所有或控制、与所有者分立的法律身份、为税务目的保留完整账目、符合规定的政府登记，或雇有至少一名正规岗位雇员，都可以使市场单位获得正规部门身份；登记应具有相应权利义务，不能把任何名册或土地登记都当作企业正规身份。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Missing one certificate is insufficient. Government ownership or control, separate legal status, complete accounts for tax purposes, qualifying government registration, or employing at least one employee with a formal job can establish a market unit's formal status. Registration must carry relevant rights and obligations; an arbitrary list or land register is not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个部门可包括农业和非农业生产；主要为家庭自用而生产的单位属于另一部门。生产率高低不是定义条件，须另看[[非正规标签与经营约束]]。这里的“非正规”也不等于“非法”：决议排除产品或服务本身被法律禁止的活动，但纳入通常合法的产品因生产者未获许可而违法生产的情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sector can include agriculture and non-agricultural production; units producing mainly for household own use belong elsewhere. Productivity is not a defining criterion; see [[非正规标签与经营约束|informal status and business constraints]]. Informality is not identical to illegality: the resolution excludes goods or services prohibited by law, but includes normally legal products supplied illegally by unauthorized producers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业部门身份与[[非正规就业|岗位的正规性]]是不同判定。例如，正规公司可以雇用非正规岗位员工；反过来，按这份决议，一家面向市场的单位若雇有正规岗位雇员，就不再归入非正规部门。两条轴不能互换，也不能不加条件地画成任意四种组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A unit's sector and the [[非正规就业|formality of a job]] are different classifications. A formal corporation may employ workers in informal jobs. Conversely, under this resolution a market unit employing an employee with a formal job is no longer classified in the informal sector. The two axes are neither interchangeable nor an unrestricted four-cell grid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
