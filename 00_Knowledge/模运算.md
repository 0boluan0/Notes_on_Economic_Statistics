# Modular arithmetic
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*模运算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a twelve-position dial labelled $0,1,\ldots,11$, starting at $9$ and moving forward five steps ends at $2$: the integer sum is $14$, whose remainder on division by $12$ is $2$. **Modular arithmetic** performs calculations while keeping the remainder information. To use this safely, we must show that choosing a different integer for the same remainder class does not change the resulting class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标着 $0,1,\ldots,11$ 的十二格圆盘上，从 $9$ 向前走五格会到 $2$：整数和是 $14$，除以 $12$ 余 $2$。**模运算**在计算时保留余数信息。要安全地这样计算，必须证明：同一个类换用不同整数作代表，不会改变结果所属的类。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Classes are the objects; remainders name them
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*运算对象是类，余数是它们的代表*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an integer $n>1$, **arithmetic modulo $n$** operates on the [[同余|residue classes]] in $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$, usually represented by $0,1,\ldots,n-1$. Addition, subtraction and multiplication are performed on integers and then reduced to standard remainders modulo $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数 $n>1$，**模 $n$ 运算**是在 [[同余]] 类组成的集合 $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ 上进行的运算；计算时通常用 $0,1,\ldots,n-1$ 代表这些类。加、减、乘都先按整数计算，再取除以 $n$ 的标准余数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[同余|residue class]] $[a]_n$ is the set of all integers whose difference from $a$ is a multiple of $n$. The symbol $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ means the set of these $n$ classes; it is not an ordinary numerical quotient. Define their operations by taking representatives, applying the integer operation, and taking the class of the result:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[同余|同余类]] $[a]_n$ 收集与 $a$ 相差 $n$ 的整数倍的所有整数。$\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ 表示这 $n$ 个类组成的集合，并不是一个普通数值商。运算的定义是：先取代表做整数运算，再取结果所属的类：

$$[a]_n+[b]_n=[a+b]_n,\qquad [a]_n-[b]_n=[a-b]_n,\qquad [a]_n[b]_n=[ab]_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete answer in $0,\ldots,n-1$, use the standard remainder supplied by [[带余除法|division with remainder]]. Modulo $5$, for example, $4+3=7$ reduces to $2$, $2-4=-2$ reduces to $3$, and $3\cdot4=12$ reduces to $2$. Thus $[4]_5+[3]_5=[2]_5$; it does not follow that the ordinary integer sum $4+3$ equals $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要输出 $0,\ldots,n-1$ 中的具体数值，就用[[带余除法]]给出的标准余数。例如模 $5$ 时，$4+3=7$ 取余得 $2$，$2-4=-2$ 取余得 $3$，$3\cdot4=12$ 取余得 $2$。所以 $[4]_5+[3]_5=[2]_5$；这并没有把普通整数等式改成 $4+3=2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why changing representatives is legitimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么可以更换代表元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The issue is not just whether the operations work for one chosen pair of representatives. If $[a]_n=[a^{\prime}]_n$ and $[b]_n=[b^{\prime}]_n$, every permitted choice must lead to the same output class. This independence of the choice is called being **well-defined**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

问题不只是用某一组代表能否算出结果。如果 $[a]_n=[a^{\prime}]_n$、$[b]_n=[b^{\prime}]_n$，所有允许的代表选择都必须给出同一个结果类。这种“不依赖代表选择”的性质叫作**良定义**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition does not depend on the chosen representatives: if $a\equiv a'\pmod n$ and $b\equiv b'\pmod n$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种定义与代表元选择无关：若 $a\equiv a'\pmod n$、$b\equiv b'\pmod n$，则

$$
a+b\equiv a'+b',\qquad
a-b\equiv a'-b',\qquad
ab\equiv a'b'\pmod n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed congruences all use the same modulus $n$. Write $a-a^{\prime}=kn$ and $b-b^{\prime}=\ell n$ for integers $k,\ell$. Then the differences between the two sums and between the two differences are integer multiples of $n$. For the products, expand so that each term contains one of the known differences:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面三条同余式都采用同一个模数 $n$。写成 $a-a^{\prime}=kn$、$b-b^{\prime}=\ell n$，其中 $k,\ell$ 是整数。两组和的差、两组差的差，都是 $n$ 的整数倍；乘法则展开成每项都含一个已知差的形式：

$$\begin{aligned}(a+b)-(a^{\prime}+b^{\prime})&=(k+\ell)n,\\(a-b)-(a^{\prime}-b^{\prime})&=(k-\ell)n,\\ab-a^{\prime}b^{\prime}&=a(b-b^{\prime})+b^{\prime}(a-a^{\prime})=(a\ell+b^{\prime}k)n.\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every coefficient on the right is an integer, so each line proves the required divisibility. No inverse or coprimality assumption was used. The operations therefore work for composite as well as prime moduli, and for negative representatives as well as standard ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右边每个系数都是整数，因此每一行都证明了所需的整除关系。论证没有使用逆元，也没有要求互素，所以素数模数、合数模数都适用，负代表与标准代表也都适用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Addition and subtraction follow from divisibility of the differences; multiplication follows from $ab-a'b'=a(b-b')+b'(a-a')$. Thus, in calculations using integer addition, subtraction and multiplication, operands and intermediate results may be replaced by smaller congruent values at any stage. Fixed nonnegative integer powers also preserve congruence; a zeroth power here denotes the empty product, one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加减法来自 $n$ 对差的整除性；乘法来自 $ab-a'b'=a(b-b')+b'(a-a')$。因此，在只含整数加减乘的计算中，可以随时把操作数和中间结果换成同余的小数值。固定的非负整数次幂也保持同余；零次幂在这里表示空乘积 1。

> [!example] Two representatives, one result · 代表不同，结果类相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Modulo $5$, $12$ and $2$ represent the same class, as do $-4$ and $1$. Computing with $12$ and $-4$ gives $12+(-4)=8$, with remainder $3$, and $12(-4)=-48$, with remainder $2$. Computing with $2$ and $1$ gives remainders $3$ and $2$ again. The integer answers differ, but the remainder answers agree exactly as the proof requires.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 模 $5$ 时，$12$ 与 $2$ 代表同一个类，$-4$ 与 $1$ 也代表同一个类。用前一组算，$12+(-4)=8$ 余 $3$，$12(-4)=-48$ 余 $2$；用 $2$ 与 $1$ 算，余数仍分别是 $3$ 与 $2$。整数结果不同，但余数结果相同，正是一般证明保证的性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reducing during a calculation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在计算过程中取余*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At each addition, subtraction or multiplication, the compatibility just proved preserves the output class. Applying it successively proves the rule for any finite expression built from these operations. For a fixed nonnegative integer exponent $k$, induction gives $a^k\equiv b^k\pmod n$ whenever $a\equiv b\pmod n$: at $k=0$ both products are $1$, and the next power follows by multiplying the current congruence by $a\equiv b$. This uses the same exponent on both sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每次加、减、乘都由刚才证明的兼容性保持结果类；依次应用，就得到任何由这些运算组成的有限表达式的逐步取余规则。对固定非负整数指数 $k$，归纳可得：若 $a\equiv b\pmod n$，则 $a^k\equiv b^k\pmod n$。$k=0$ 时两个空乘积都是 $1$；下一次幂则把当前同余再乘上 $a\equiv b$。这里两边使用的是同一个指数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $287\equiv3\pmod4$ and $3^2\equiv1\pmod4$, so $287^9\equiv(3^2)^4\cdot3\equiv3\pmod4$, without first computing the large integer. [[模重复平方法|Modular repeated squaring]] makes this approach systematic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $287\equiv3\pmod4$，而 $3^2\equiv1\pmod4$，所以 $287^9\equiv(3^2)^4\cdot3\equiv3\pmod4$，无需先算出大整数。反复平方的系统做法见 [[模重复平方法]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this convention even a zero base has zeroth power $1$, because no factors are multiplied; this is an algebraic empty-product convention. For positive $k$, repeated multiplication determines the power. In the example, the exponent remains nine: $9=2\cdot4+1$ groups the same nine factors, rather than replacing nine by a remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这里的约定中，即使底数为零，零次幂也取 $1$，因为没有任何因子参加相乘；这是代数中的空乘积约定。正指数则由反复乘法决定。上例中指数始终是九：$9=2\cdot4+1$ 只是把同样的九个因子重新分组，并没有把九替换成某个余数。

## Division and exponent reduction need more information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*除法与指数约简还需要额外条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These rules do not permit arbitrary division or reduction of an exponent modulo the same modulus. For example, $2^5\equiv2\pmod5$, whereas replacing the exponent five by its remainder zero gives $2^0\equiv1\pmod5$. Division must be interpreted as multiplication by a [[模逆元|modular inverse]]; exponent reduction requires a separate result, such as [[欧拉定理|Euler's theorem]], with its hypotheses satisfied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些规则不允许任意除法，也不允许把指数直接对同一个模数取余。例如 $2^5\equiv2\pmod5$，但把指数 5 换成余数 0 会得到 $2^0\equiv1\pmod5$。除法需转成乘 [[模逆元]]；合法缩减指数需另用带条件的 [[欧拉定理]] 等结论。

> [!warning] A nonzero factor need not cancel · 非零因子未必能约去
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Modulo $6$, $2\cdot1\equiv2\cdot4$ because the products are $2$ and $8$. Cancelling the common nonzero factor would assert $1\equiv4\pmod6$, which is false. By contrast, subtracting the same term from both sides is always permitted: it is an application of the subtraction rule already proved.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 模 $6$ 时，$2\cdot1\equiv2\cdot4$，因为两个乘积是 $2$ 与 $8$。若约去共同的非零因子，就会得到错误的 $1\equiv4\pmod6$。相反，两边减去同一项始终允许，因为这正是已证明的减法规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[模逆元|modular inverse]] of a factor $c$ is a class represented by an integer $u$ with $cu\equiv1\pmod n$. Multiplying by it makes cancellation valid when it exists. The [[模逆元存在条件|existence criterion]] is $\gcd(c,n)=1$: the [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]] of $c$ and $n$ must be one. A noncoprime factor cannot be cancelled as an unrestricted rule with the modulus unchanged; a particular conclusion may still happen to be true. The exact reduced-modulus rule belongs to [[同余消去律|cancellation in congruences]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因子 $c$ 的[[模逆元]]是由某个整数 $u$ 代表、满足 $cu\equiv1\pmod n$ 的类；存在时，乘这个逆元就能合法约去 $c$。[[模逆元存在条件|存在判据]]是 $\gcd(c,n)=1$，也就是 $c,n$ 的[[最大公因数]]为一。非互素因子不能作为“不改变模数、普遍约去”的规则，虽然某个具体结论可能碰巧成立；准确的缩小模数规则见[[同余消去律]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponent counterexample above uses a prime modulus, so even primality does not justify reducing an exponent modulo that same number. The exponent counts factors; it is not one of the factors being replaced. Use a separately justified theorem and all of its conditions before changing it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的指数反例已经使用素数模数，因此即使模数为素数，也不能据此把指数按同一个数取余。指数是在数因子的个数，并不是被替换的那个因子；改变指数前，必须另有定理并满足它的全部条件。
