# 估值日每股价值须先分配既有期权等权利再按一致的普通股数计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Value per common share at the valuation date requires allocating existing option and other claims before using a consistent common-share count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每股估值先问“这份股权价值究竟由哪些权利分享”，再问“分给多少股”。采用权利价值扣减法时，先扣出尚未分配的既有员工期权价值，再用估值日实际发行在外的普通股数作分母。分子与分母必须属于同一组权益，不能只因某个股数叫“完全稀释”就直接使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify which claims share the equity value, then determine the relevant shares. Under the claim-value approach, deduct existing employee-option value not yet allocated, then divide by common shares actually outstanding at the valuation date. Numerator and denominator must describe the same equity claim; a share count labelled fully diluted is not automatically suitable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接[[经营价值转股权价值]]的例子：母公司普通股与既有期权共同对应的股权价值为 800。假设只有一类同权普通股、没有优先股或其他未列权利；既有员工期权按一致模型估值为 40，实际在外普通股为 100 股。此处忽略期权税效应，也没有在前面的现金流中另扣同批既有期权的结算成本，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continue the [[经营价值转股权价值|parent-equity bridge]]: equity shared by common stock and existing options is worth 800. Assume one class of equal-rights common shares, no preferred or other unlisted claims, existing options consistently valued at 40, and 100 common shares outstanding. Ignore option tax effects, and assume settlement costs for these same existing options were not separately deducted in the preceding cash flows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_0=\frac{E_{parent}-V_{options}}{N_{common}}=\frac{800-40}{100}=7.60.$$

40 是本例给定的权利价值，不是期权份数，也不是只看内在价值便能得到的数。若它对应 20 份一股期权，既然分子已经扣过其权利，就不能再除以 $100+20$；那会对同一批权利重复调整。反过来，只把股数增加而不处理行权款和时间价值，也不是完整的期权估值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 40 is an assumed claim value, not an option count or an intrinsic-value-only calculation. If it represents 20 one-share options, dividing the already-adjusted numerator by $100+20$ would adjust twice for the same claims. Conversely, increasing shares alone without accounting for exercise proceeds and time value is not a complete option valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[稀释每股收益]]解决另一个问题：按会计期间的加权股数、归属利润调整和反稀释规则计算每股损益。这里则在一个估值日分配价值。历史期间的稀释 EPS 分母，不能未经核对便替代今天的估值股数；拆股、多股类或估值日附近增发时，也应先统一权利和股数时点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[稀释每股收益|Diluted EPS]] answers a different question using period-weighted shares, attributable earnings adjustments, and antidilution rules. This method allocates value at one date. Its denominator is not automatically the historical diluted-EPS denominator; splits, multiple classes, and nearby issues also require matching rights and dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要区分**已经授予而未消失的存量权利**和**未来将授予的薪酬成本**。若未来授予成本已进入经营利润与现金流预测，不再对同一批未来授予另扣一遍稀释成本；但这并不免除今天既有期权的权利分配。本例的 800 已承担未来正常股权薪酬成本，40 只对应今天既有期权，两者不是同一批。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish outstanding claims already granted from compensation to be granted in the future. When forecast operating profit and cash flow already bear future grant costs, do not deduct dilution again for those same future grants. Existing option claims still require allocation. The example's 800 already bears future normal equity compensation; the 40 concerns only options outstanding today.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真正估计 $V_{options}$ 时，需要处理归属与失效、提前行权、行权价格和剩余期限，并使期权所用股价与最终每股估值一致，必要时迭代。税收利益只能按适用事实计入一次，不能机械把任一期权价值乘以 $(1-T)$。这些测量尚未完成时，7.60 只能作为给定权利价值下的分配示例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Estimating $V_{options}$ requires vesting and forfeiture, early exercise, strike, and remaining life, with the option's share-value input consistent with the final valuation, potentially through iteration. Recognize tax benefits once under applicable facts, not by mechanically applying $(1-T)$ to every option value. Until those measurements are complete, 7.60 remains a conditional allocation example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Damodaran，Employee Stock Options and Restricted Stock，PDF21–28、37–41](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/papers/esops.pdf#page=24)：重开股数简法的局限、既有期权权利扣减、估值一致性及未来授予防双计；PDF24、27、38、41 已目视。800→760→7.60 独立复算；2005 年税收及会计规则描述不作为现行规则采用。
- [[稀释每股收益]]：复用期间加权股数、利润调整及反稀释职责，作为与估值日权利分配的明确对照，不在此重定义会计 EPS。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Damodaran supports the limitations of share-count shortcuts, existing-option allocation, consistent valuation, and avoiding duplicate future-grant costs; the specified pages were reopened and visually checked. The numerical allocation is independently calculated. Historical tax and accounting rules are not treated as current requirements. The shared diluted-EPS atom supplies the distinct accounting responsibility.
