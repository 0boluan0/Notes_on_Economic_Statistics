# 外部融资溢价是借款人外部筹资成本相对内部资金机会成本的额外楔子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The external finance premium is the extra wedge between a borrower's external financing cost and the opportunity cost of internal funds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子概念
> 外部融资溢价（external finance premium）是借款人从外部取得资金的有效成本，相对于使用内部资金的机会成本多出的部分。外部资金提供者看不清借款人的项目质量或后续行动，还要承担筛选、监督、核验和执行契约的成本；这些信息与代理摩擦使外部资金通常比内部资金更贵。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The external finance premium is the part of a borrower's effective external financing cost that exceeds the opportunity cost of internal funds. Outside fund providers cannot perfectly observe project quality or subsequent actions and must bear screening, monitoring, verification, and enforcement costs. These information and agency frictions usually make external funds more expensive than internal funds.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以把定义写成：

$$
\text{外部融资溢价}
=\text{外部资金的有效成本}
-\text{内部资金的机会成本}.
$$

内部资金不是“免费资金”。留存现金本可分红、还债或投入其他项目，因此也有机会成本。外部资金的有效成本则不只是一项票面利率：利息、费用、抵押要求、限制性条款、监督成本和融资额度都可能共同收紧融资条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Internal funds are not free. Retained cash could have been distributed, used to repay debt, or invested elsewhere, so it has an opportunity cost. Nor is the effective cost of external finance just one quoted interest rate: interest, fees, collateral requirements, covenants, monitoring costs, and quantity limits can jointly tighten financing conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

借款人的财务状况会影响这个楔子。净值、流动性和预期现金流较强时，借款人自己承担的项目风险更多，贷方的激励冲突、核验和回收担忧通常较小；财务状况恶化时，溢价和非价格条款可能同时收紧。这个反向关系是 [[金融加速器]] 的核心传导环节。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The wedge varies with the borrower's financial condition. Stronger net worth, liquidity, and expected cash flow give the borrower a larger stake in the project and generally reduce the lender's incentive, verification, and recovery concerns. When financial condition deteriorates, both the premium and non-price terms may tighten. This inverse relationship is the central transmission link in the [[金融加速器|financial accelerator]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与观察到的信用利差的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with an observed credit spread*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外部融资溢价不等于任意一条贷款或债券的“信用利差”。观察到的相对无风险基准利差还可能包含预期违约损失、期限与流动性补偿、系统性风险溢价、税费、市场势力和计量误差。只有明确外部与内部资金的比较基准，并有模型或经验分解把其他成分隔开，才能把某个观测量解释为外部融资溢价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The external finance premium is not identical to any observed loan or bond “credit spread.” A spread over a risk-free benchmark may also contain expected default loss, term and liquidity compensation, systematic risk premia, taxes and fees, market power, and measurement error. An observed variable can be interpreted as the external finance premium only after the external-versus-internal benchmark is specified and the other components are separated by a model or empirical decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“利差上升”本身还不能证明信息摩擦加重；也可能是预期损失或市场流动性变化。反过来，即使票面利差没有变化，更高的抵押要求、费用或额度削减仍可能表示有效外部融资成本上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An increase in a spread alone therefore does not prove that information frictions have worsened; expected loss or market liquidity may have changed instead. Conversely, unchanged quoted spreads do not rule out an increase in effective external financing costs when collateral, fees, or quantity limits tighten.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一笔企业贷款相对国债的利差上升，为什么不能直接断言“外部融资溢价上升了同样幅度”？
>
> **答案：** 国债利差混合了预期信用损失、期限、流动性、风险补偿和其他费用；外部融资溢价还要求用内部资金机会成本作比较。没有分解这些成分，就不能把两者一一对应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can an increase in a corporate-loan spread over government debt not be read directly as an equal increase in the external finance premium?
>
> **Answer:** The observed spread combines expected credit loss, maturity, liquidity, risk compensation, and other costs, while the external finance premium is defined relative to the opportunity cost of internal funds. The two cannot be mapped one-for-one without decomposing those components.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
