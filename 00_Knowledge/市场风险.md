# 市场风险是市场价格或风险因子变动使持仓发生损失的风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market risk is the risk of position losses from changes in market prices or risk factors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场风险（market risk）关心的是：**市场变了，我已经持有的资产或负债会损失多少价值？** 股票价格、汇率、利率、信用利差和波动率都可能是估值输入；无需等到卖出，持仓的盯市价值就会改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market risk concerns losses on existing positions when market prices or valuation factors change. Exposure may involve equity prices, FX rates, interest rates, spreads or volatility; a position can lose marked value before it is sold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如持有 100 股、每股 50 元的股票，价格跌至 48 元，在不交易且没有股息的条件下，持仓损失为 $100(50-48)=200$ 元。同样是“5000 元的头寸”，期权组合的反应可能完全不同：**当前价值不是风险暴露本身**，要先做 [[风险因子映射]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding 100 shares at 50 each produces a loss of 200 when the price falls to 48, with no trades or dividends. An option portfolio worth the same 5,000 can react quite differently: current value is not exposure. Begin with [[风险因子映射|risk-factor mapping]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与交易对手不履约、系统故障或卖不掉资产不是同一个问题，但这些风险可能相互放大。若要把情景损失汇总成 VaR 或 ES，还须明确 [[风险度量口径|持有期、损益口径和概率水平]]；仅有一个 Delta 不能给出整个损失分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counterparty default, operational failure and inability to trade are distinct risks, although they interact with market losses. VaR or ES also requires a [[风险度量口径|horizon, P&L convention and probability level]]; Delta alone does not determine a loss distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
