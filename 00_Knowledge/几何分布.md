# Geometric distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*几何分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **geometric distribution** describes how many attempts we wait for the first success when each attempt has the same success probability and the attempts are independent. Imagine a toy device that passes each test with probability $1/4$. We repeat the test until it passes. Passing immediately takes one attempt; failing twice and then passing takes three. The uncertain number we record is the waiting time, measured in attempts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**几何分布**描述：每次尝试的成功概率相同、各次尝试相互独立时，要等多少次才第一次成功。设想一个示意设备，每次测试通过的概率都是 $1/4$，我们持续测试直到通过。第一次就通过，需要一次；连续失败两次后通过，需要三次。这里记录的随机数是等待时间，单位为“尝试次数”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record attempt $n$ by $X_n=1$ for success and $X_n=0$ for failure. Each record has a [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli distribution]] with success probability $p$. The additional statement that these records are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]] means both that the same $p$ applies every time and that the joint probabilities for any finite collection of attempts factor into their individual probabilities. Write $q=1-p$ for the failure probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把第 $n$ 次尝试记录为 $X_n$：成功记 $1$，失败记 $0$。每个记录都服从成功概率为 $p$ 的 [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli 分布]]；进一步要求它们[[独立同分布]]，意味着每次使用同一个 $p$，且任意有限组尝试的联合概率都能分解为各次概率的乘积。记 $q=1-p$ 为失败概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the probability has this shape
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么概率长成这个样子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T$ count attempts **including the first success**. The event $T=3$ requires exactly the pattern failure, failure, success. Independence lets us multiply its three probabilities: $q\cdot q\cdot p=q^2p$. In general, $T=k$ requires $k-1$ failures followed by one success. No arrangement factor is needed: that final success must occur in position $k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $T$ 统计**包括第一次成功在内**的总尝试次数。$T=3$ 要求结果恰好依次是“失败、失败、成功”。独立性允许把三次概率相乘：$q\cdot q\cdot p=q^2p$。一般地，$T=k$ 要求前 $k-1$ 次全部失败，第 $k$ 次成功。这里不用乘排列方式数，因为第一次成功的位置必须是第 $k$ 次。

> [!abstract] Counting all attempts · 统计全部尝试
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $0<p<1$, the [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]]—the probability of each possible count—is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对于 $0<p<1$，[[概率质量函数]]给出各个次数出现的概率：
>
> $$P(T=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p=q^{k-1}p,\qquad k=1,2,3,\ldots.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All other finite values have probability zero. We also include $p=1$ as the boundary case $T=1$ with probability one. The notation $T\sim\operatorname{Geom}(p)$ is only unambiguous once we state that the count starts at $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其他有限取值的概率均为零。还可以把 $p=1$ 纳入定义，此时 $T$ 以概率一等于 $1$。写 $T\sim\operatorname{Geom}(p)$ 时，必须同时说明这里从 $1$ 开始计数，才不会产生歧义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These probabilities add to one: $p+qp+q^2p+\cdots=p/(1-q)=1$, using the [[几何级数|geometric series]] with $0\le q<1$. This is where the distribution gets its name. We can define a geometric random variable directly by this probability law; independent repeated trials are a mechanism that produces the law, not extra information encoded by the distribution of $T$ alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些概率之和为一：$p+qp+q^2p+\cdots=p/(1-q)=1$，这里用到了 $0\le q<1$ 时的[[几何级数]]，分布名称也由此而来。可以直接通过这份概率规律定义几何随机变量；独立重复尝试是产生这份规律的一种机制，仅凭 $T$ 的分布本身不能反推出整个试验过程的全部依赖关系。

> [!example] Three different questions · 三个不同的问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the device model, $p=1/4$ and $q=3/4$. “First pass on attempt three” means $T=3$, with probability $(3/4)^2(1/4)=9/64$. “Still no pass after three attempts” means $T>3$, with probability $(3/4)^3=27/64$. “Pass within three attempts” is the complementary event $T\le3$, with probability $1-27/64=37/64$. The last event includes passing on attempt one or two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设备模型中，$p=1/4$、$q=3/4$。“第三次才首次通过”是 $T=3$，概率为 $(3/4)^2(1/4)=9/64$；“试了三次仍没通过”是 $T>3$，概率为 $(3/4)^3=27/64$；“三次之内通过”是补事件 $T\le3$，概率为 $1-27/64=37/64$，其中也包括第一次或第二次就通过。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any nonnegative integer $m$, the tail event $T>m$ means that the first $m$ attempts all fail. Thus $P(T>m)=q^m$ and $P(T\le m)=1-q^m$. At $m=0$ there are no trials to fail, so $P(T>0)=1$; at $p=1$ every tail with $m\ge1$ has probability zero. For $p>0$, the tail tends to zero as $m$ grows, so success eventually occurs with probability one. This still gives no fixed deadline that is guaranteed when $p<1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意非负整数 $m$，尾事件 $T>m$ 表示前 $m$ 次尝试都失败，所以 $P(T>m)=q^m$、$P(T\le m)=1-q^m$。$m=0$ 时还没有进行任何尝试，因此 $P(T>0)=1$；$p=1$ 时，所有 $m\ge1$ 的尾概率都为零。只要 $p>0$，随着 $m$ 增大，尾概率趋于零，因此最终会以概率一成功；但当 $p<1$ 时，并不存在保证成功的某个固定截止次数。

## Why some books start at zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么有的书从零开始*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Another convention counts **failures before the first success**. Call that number $F$. Immediate success means $F=0$, and two failures followed by success means $F=2$. Because the successful attempt is omitted, $F=T-1$. For $0<p<1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种约定统计**第一次成功之前的失败次数**，记为 $F$。立即成功时 $F=0$，先失败两次再成功时 $F=2$。由于没有把成功那次算进去，$F=T-1$。对于 $0<p<1$，

$$P(F=k)=q^kp,\qquad k=0,1,2,\ldots.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $p=1$, $F=0$ with probability one. Both conventions are called geometric distributions; the extra power of $q$ comes from which count we are recording. Before using a formula, identify the stopping event, whether that event is included in the count, and whether the parameter denotes its probability or the probability of continuing. For example, a time-homogeneous one-state absorption model that stays put with probability $0\le q<1$ and leaves for an absorbing endpoint with probability $1-q$ has geometric exit time with success parameter $p=1-q$; see the one-state example in [[期望吸收时间为N1|expected absorption time]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$p=1$ 时，$F$ 以概率一等于 $0$。两种约定都被叫作几何分布，公式中的 $q$ 多一幂还是少一幂，来自记录对象的区别。套公式前先说明：遇到什么事件停止，停止的那一次是否计入，以及参数表示停止的概率还是继续的概率。例如，一个时间齐次的单状态吸收模型，以概率 $0\le q<1$ 留在原地、以概率 $1-q$ 离开并进入吸收终点，其离开时间服从成功参数为 $p=1-q$ 的几何分布；见[[期望吸收时间为N1|期望吸收时间]]中的单状态例子。

## How long is the average wait?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*平均要等多久？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|expectation]] is the probability-weighted average count. For this distribution,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望]]是按概率加权的平均次数。对于这个分布，

$$E[T]=\frac1p,\qquad E[F]=\frac{1-p}{p}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One way to understand the first formula is to count waiting one unit at a time. A run lasting three attempts contributes one unit for $T>0$, one for $T>1$, and one for $T>2$. If we cap the count at $N$, then $\min(T,N)$ is exactly the sum of the $N$ [[事件指示变量|indicators]] for $T>0,\ldots,T>N-1$. Taking expectations gives $\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}P(T>m)$. As $N$ increases these nonnegative capped counts grow to $T$; [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence]] permits taking the limit of their expectations. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以把一次等待逐格计数来理解第一个公式：持续三次的记录，为 $T>0$、$T>1$、$T>2$ 各贡献一格。如果把计数截在 $N$，那么 $\min(T,N)$ 恰好等于事件 $T>0,\ldots,T>N-1$ 的 $N$ 个[[事件指示变量]]之和。取期望得到 $\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}P(T>m)$。随着 $N$ 增大，这些非负截断计数逐步增加到 $T$，[[单调收敛定理]]允许把期望也取极限，因此

$$E[T]=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}P(T>m)
=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}q^m=\frac1{1-q}=\frac1p.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting the one successful attempt gives $E[F]=E[T]-1$. For the device with $p=1/4$, the average is four total attempts or three failures. Four is an average over the distribution, not the number each run must take and not the most probable count; when $0<p<1$, each successive point probability is smaller by a factor $q$, so the most probable total count is one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

减掉成功的那一次，便有 $E[F]=E[T]-1$。设备例子中 $p=1/4$，平均总尝试次数为四，平均失败次数为三。四是整个分布的平均值，不是每次一定要试四次，也不是最可能的次数：当 $0<p<1$ 时，相邻次数的概率依次乘上 $q$ 而变小，所以最可能的总次数其实是一。

> [!warning] Repetition alone is not enough · 重复尝试还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $0<p<1$, if every record copies one Bernoulli outcome $Z$, then every attempt still has success probability $p$, but the waiting time is $1$ when $Z=1$ and infinite when $Z=0$. This is not the geometric law above. If independent attempts instead have changing success probabilities $p_1,p_2,\ldots$, the first-success probability becomes $P(T=k)=\bigl[\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}(1-p_j)\bigr]p_k$, which need not be geometric. At $p=0$, success never occurs: $T=\infty$, outside the ordinary geometric distribution on the positive integers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对于 $0<p<1$，如果每次记录都只是复制同一个 Bernoulli 结果 $Z$，每次的成功概率依然是 $p$，但 $Z=1$ 时等待时间为 $1$，$Z=0$ 时则永远等不到；这不是上面的几何分布。如果各次独立但成功概率变成 $p_1,p_2,\ldots$，则 $P(T=k)=\bigl[\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}(1-p_j)\bigr]p_k$，一般也不再是几何分布。当 $p=0$ 时永远不会成功，$T=\infty$，不属于取正整数值的普通几何分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[二项分布|binomial distribution]] answers a different counting question: fix the number of attempts and count their successes. A geometric distribution fixes the target of one success and records how many attempts that target takes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二项分布]]回答另一种计数问题：先固定尝试次数，再统计其中成功了几次。几何分布则固定“首次成功”这个目标，记录达到目标用了多少次尝试。
