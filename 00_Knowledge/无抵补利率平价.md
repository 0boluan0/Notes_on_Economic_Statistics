# 无抵补利率平价是预期回报限制而不是可锁定的套利恒等式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Uncovered interest parity is an expected-return restriction, not a lockable arbitrage identity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子假说
> 在 $S_t$ 报每单位外币的本币价格、外币一期总回报 $R_f$ 于 $t$ 时已知时，未套保外币投资的本币总回报是 $R_fS_{t+1}/S_t$。零货币风险溢价的纯 UIP 要求
> $$R_d=E_t\!\left[R_f\frac{S_{t+1}}{S_t}\right].$$
> 因为 $S_{t+1}$ 尚未锁定，这是一项带假设的条件期望关系，不是今天即可执行并锁定利润的套利等式。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $S_t$ quoted as domestic currency per unit of foreign currency and the one-period foreign gross return $R_f$ known at time $t$, the unhedged foreign investment returns $R_fS_{t+1}/S_t$ in domestic currency. Pure UIP with a zero currency risk premium requires $R_d=E_t[R_fS_{t+1}/S_t]$. Because $S_{t+1}$ is not locked, this is an assumption-dependent conditional-expectation restriction, not an arbitrage profit that can be fixed today.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一期对数近似下，定义外币投资相对本币投资的超额回报
$$
rx_{t+1}=i_f-i_d+\Delta s_{t+1},
\qquad \Delta s_{t+1}=s_{t+1}-s_t.
$$
纯 UIP 取 $E_t[rx_{t+1}]=0$，于是
$$
i_d-i_f=E_t[\Delta s_{t+1}].
$$
在本卡的报价下，本币利率较高对应本币预期贬值，而不是外币预期贬值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a one-period log approximation, the foreign investment's excess return over the domestic investment is $rx_{t+1}=i_f-i_d+\Delta s_{t+1}$. Pure UIP sets $E_t[rx_{t+1}]=0$, giving $i_d-i_f=E_t[\Delta s_{t+1}]$. Under this card's quotation, a higher domestic interest rate is paired with expected domestic depreciation, not expected foreign depreciation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 风险溢价不能悄悄塞进纯 UIP

若投资者要求持有外币风险获得条件期望溢价，明确定义
$$
\pi_t=E_t[rx_{t+1}],
$$
则广义关系为
$$
i_d-i_f=E_t[\Delta s_{t+1}]-\pi_t.
$$
不同文献对风险溢价正负号的定义可能相反，因此必须先定义 $rx$ 与 $\pi_t$。写“UIP 包含风险溢价”会把零溢价假说与风险调整后的资产定价关系混在一起；无套利一般只约束经随机贴现因子调整的价格，并不单独推出原始期望回报相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If investors require a conditional premium for foreign-currency risk, define it explicitly as $\pi_t=E_t[rx_{t+1}]$, so that $i_d-i_f=E_t[\Delta s_{t+1}]-\pi_t$. Some sources use the opposite sign convention, so $rx$ and $\pi_t$ must be defined before interpretation. Saying merely that “UIP contains a risk premium” conflates the zero-premium hypothesis with a risk-adjusted asset-pricing relation. No arbitrage generally restricts stochastic-discount-factor-adjusted prices; it does not by itself equate raw expected returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[抵补利率平价]]使用已锁定的 $F_t$，而 UIP 使用随机的 $S_{t+1}$。只有同时加入 CIP、零 basis、纯 UIP 与相容的预期条件，才会得到远期率接近未来即期率条件期望的推论；不能把 $F_t$ 直接替换成市场的确定预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Covered interest parity uses the locked $F_t$, whereas UIP uses the random $S_{t+1}$. Only after combining CIP, a zero basis, pure UIP, and compatible expectations can one infer that the forward rate approximates the conditional expectation of the future spot rate. The forward cannot be relabelled a certain market forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么高利率货币不保证未套保投资者获得更高实现回报？
>
> **答案：** 期末换汇使用尚未实现的 $S_{t+1}$；货币贬值可以抵消利差，且期望超额回报还可能包含时变风险溢价。
