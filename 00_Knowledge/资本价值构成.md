# 资本价值构成是不变资本价值与可变资本价值之间的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The value composition of capital is the proportion between constant and variable capital values.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本价值构成用价值量比较投入生产资料的[[不变资本]]和投入劳动力的[[可变资本]]。若两部分分别记为 $c$ 和 $v>0$，通常以 $c/v$ 表示；比较时须采用同一资本范围、估值口径与时间基础。这里衡量投入资本的构成，不能把一年的耗用额与某一时点的预付额混在同一个比值中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value composition compares [[不变资本|constant capital]] invested in means of production with [[可变资本|variable capital]] invested in labour power. Writing the parts as $c$ and $v>0$, it is commonly expressed as $c/v$. Comparisons require matched capital scope, valuation and time basis; annual expenditure and an advance measured at one point cannot be mixed within the ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一比值可以因生产方法改变而变，也可以因相同生产资料的单位价值或相同劳动力的价值改变而变。构造例中，原有 $c=80,v=20$，比值为4；如果使用的设备、材料、劳动量均不变，而生产资料价值降至60，$v$仍为20，则比值降为3。数值变动本身尚未告诉我们技术是否改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio can change with production methods, but also with the unit value of unchanged means of production or labour power. In a constructed example, $c=80,v=20$ gives a ratio of four. If equipment, materials and labour quantities remain unchanged while the means of production fall in value to 60 and $v$ stays at 20, the ratio becomes three. A changed ratio alone does not identify technical change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[资本技术构成]]记录实物与劳动的关系；价值构成中由这种技术关系决定并反映其变化的方面，才是[[资本有机构成]]。因此解释 $c/v$ 的变化，要同时检查数量和单位价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[资本技术构成|technical composition]] records the physical relation between means of production and labour. The aspect of value composition determined by and reflecting that relation is the [[资本有机构成|organic composition]]. Explaining a movement in $c/v$ therefore requires examining quantities and unit values together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- Marx，卷I，[第25章§1开头三段、§2物量与价值段](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch25.htm)：三种构成的定义及价值只近似反映实物构成。MIA 的 Moore/Aveling 1887 英译底本，Engels 编。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I supplies the definitions and the qualified correspondence between physical and value composition. Volume III distinguishes changes in input quantities from changes in their values; MIA lists a 1959 Institute of Marxism-Leninism source published by International Publishers. The numbers are constructed.
