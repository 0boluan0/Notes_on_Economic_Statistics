# 曲线间面积通过积分每条切片的非负长度求得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Area between curves is found by integrating the nonnegative length of each slice*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当区域在 $a\le x\le b$ 上的竖直切片恰好从 $g(x)$ 延伸到 $f(x)$，其中 $a<b$、$f\ge g$ 且边界函数可积时，每条细矩形的面积约为 $(f-g)\Delta x$，因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every vertical slice of a region runs from $g(x)$ to $f(x)$ on $[a,b]$, with $a<b$, $f\ge g$, and integrable boundary functions, a thin rectangle has approximate area $(f-g)\Delta x$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A=\int_a^b[f(x)-g(x)]\,dx.
$$

先画区域和代表性切片，再确定切片长度与积分区间。竖切用“上减下”，横切用“右减左”。若同一条竖切片包含几段互不相连的区间，应把各段长度相加，不能把中间空隙也计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sketch the region and a representative slice before choosing the slice length and bounds. Vertical slices use upper minus lower; horizontal slices use right minus left. If a slice contains several disjoint intervals, add their lengths without including the gaps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条图像 $y=f(x)$ 与 $y=g(x)$ 在 $[a,b]$ 内互换上下位置时，几何面积为 $\int_a^b|f-g|\,dx$；实际计算通常在交点处分段。若横切更简单，设左、右边界为 $L(y)$、$R(y)$，改用 $A=\int_c^d[R(y)-L(y)]\,dy$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When two graphs exchange their upper and lower positions, geometric area is $\int_a^b|f-g|\,dx$; split at intersections to evaluate it. If horizontal slices are simpler, use $A=\int_c^d[R(y)-L(y)]\,dy$, where $L$ and $R$ are the left and right boundaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x=y^2$ 与 $y=x-2$ 的交点对应 $y=-1,2$。横切右边界为 $x=y+2$，所以一个积分就给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x=y^2$ and $y=x-2$, the intersections occur at $y=-1,2$. The right boundary is $x=y+2$, so horizontal slicing gives a single integral:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A=\int_{-1}^{2}(y+2-y^2)\,dy=\frac92.
$$

> [!question]- 自检
> 两条曲线相交后，为什么不能继续沿用原来的“上减下”？
>
> **答案：** 原来的差可能变负，正负部分便会抵消；几何面积需要每条切片的非负长度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why can the original upper-minus-lower expression fail after an intersection?
>
> **Answer:** The difference may become negative and cancel positive contributions. Geometric area uses each slice's nonnegative length.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
