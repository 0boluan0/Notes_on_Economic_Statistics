# Area between curves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*曲线间面积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To measure the area of a curved region, imagine covering it with very thin rectangles. A rectangle needs two lengths: its width and the distance across the region. The **area between curves** method first finds that distance from the boundaries, then uses a [[定积分|definite integral]] to add the small areas. The important choice is which region each rectangle must cover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

测量曲边区域的面积，可以先想象用很窄的矩形覆盖它。每个矩形需要两个长度：自身的宽度，以及穿过区域的距离。**曲线间面积**的方法先从边界找出这个距离，再用[[定积分]]累加小面积。关键是确定每个矩形究竟应覆盖哪一块区域。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A vertical strip measures upper minus lower
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*竖条用上边界减下边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the region consists of exactly the points with $a\le x\le b$ and $g(x)\le y\le f(x)$. Here $a<b$ are finite, $f$ is the upper boundary, and $g$ is the lower boundary, so $f(x)\ge g(x)$ throughout. Assume both real-valued boundary functions are Riemann integrable; [[一点连续|continuity]] on the closed interval is a familiar sufficient condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设区域恰好由满足 $a\le x\le b$、$g(x)\le y\le f(x)$ 的点组成。这里 $a<b$ 是有限端点，$f$ 为上边界，$g$ 为下边界，所以全区间内 $f(x)\ge g(x)$。假设两个实值边界函数都黎曼可积；在闭区间上[[一点连续|连续]]是一个常见的充分条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a selected input $x$, the vertical distance is $f(x)-g(x)$, even if both heights are negative. For example, an upper height $-1$ and a lower height $-3$ are two units apart. Multiplying this nonnegative distance by a small horizontal width $\Delta x$ gives a rectangle's area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在选定的输入 $x$ 处，竖直距离为 $f(x)-g(x)$，即使两个高度都为负也如此。例如，上边界为 $-1$、下边界为 $-3$，两者仍相距两个单位。把这个非负距离乘以小的水平宽度 $\Delta x$，就得到矩形面积。

> [!important] Accumulate lengths times widths · 累加长度乘宽度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Divide $[a,b]$ into pieces of widths $\Delta x_i$ and choose a sample point $x_i^*$ in each piece. The resulting [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] approaches the area $A$ as the largest width tends to zero:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $[a,b]$ 分成宽为 $\Delta x_i$ 的小段，每段选一个采样点 $x_i^*$。当最大段宽趋于零时，所得[[黎曼和]]趋于面积 $A$：
>
> $$A=\int_a^b[f(x)-g(x)]\,dx
> =\lim_{\max\Delta x_i\to0}\sum_i[f(x_i^*)-g(x_i^*)]\Delta x_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rectangle approximation is justified by integrability, not just by a convincing sketch. On each piece, an enclosing rectangle and an inner rectangle (possibly of zero height) differ in height by at most the variation of $f$ there plus the variation of $g$ there. Here this range means the [[上确界|least upper bound]] minus the greatest lower bound of the values on that piece. Riemann integrability allows the sum of these height errors times the widths to be arbitrarily small. Thus the inner and outer area estimates have the same limit. Continuity makes this especially easy to picture.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩形近似的依据是可积性，而不只是图画看起来合理。在每一小段上，包住区域的矩形与位于区域内部的矩形（允许高度为零），其高度差至多是 $f$ 与 $g$ 在这段上各自取值跨度之和；这里的跨度指该段取值的[[上确界]]减下确界，即最小上界减最大下界。黎曼可积性允许这些高度误差乘段宽后的总和任意小，因此内外面积估计趋于同一个值。连续情形尤其容易直观理解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, between $y=x$ and $y=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, the line is above the parabola. [[Newton-Leibniz公式|Antiderivative evaluation]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在 $[0,1]$ 上，$y=x$ 位于 $y=x^2$ 上方。用[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]计算：

$$A=\int_0^1(x-x^2)\,dx
=\left[\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1=\frac16.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Crossings change which boundary is upper
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*交点可能改变上下次序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two integrable graphs over a specified finite interval, the region containing all points between them has slice length $|f(x)-g(x)|$. Its area is therefore $\int_a^b|f-g|\,dx$. The absolute value is integrable by the result in [[积分绝对值界|the integral triangle inequality]]. It must be taken before integration: for $f(x)=x$ and $g(x)=0$ on $[-1,1]$, the signed integral is zero but the two triangular areas add to $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对指定有限区间上的两个可积图像，若区域包含两者之间的全部点，切片长度就是 $|f(x)-g(x)|$，所以面积为 $\int_a^b|f-g|\,dx$。绝对值后的可积性由[[积分绝对值界]]中的结论保证。绝对值必须先取：对 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x$、$g(x)=0$，带符号积分为零，但两块三角形面积相加为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solve $f(x)=g(x)$ to locate possible changes, then check the sign between crossings and use [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]]. A touching point need not exchange the order. Finite splitting is a convenient calculation method when there are finitely many relevant pieces, not a requirement for the area formula itself. Bounds may come from stated vertical lines or the selected enclosed region; two equations alone need not specify one finite region.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解 $f(x)=g(x)$ 找出可能改变次序的位置，再检查交点之间的符号，用[[定积分区间可加性]]分段相加。仅仅相切不一定交换上下位置。相关小段只有有限个时，有限分段方便计算，但面积公式本身不要求只有有限个交点。上下限可能来自题设竖线，也可能来自指定的封闭区域；仅有两条方程不一定能确定唯一的有限区域。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn the strips sideways when the boundaries are simpler
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*横切有时能简化边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With horizontal strips, write the left and right boundaries as $x=L(y)$ and $x=R(y)$. If each slice is exactly the interval from $L(y)$ to $R(y)$, the boundaries are integrable, $R\ge L$, and $c<d$ are finite, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用横条时，把左右边界写成 $x=L(y)$、$x=R(y)$。若每条切片恰好从 $L(y)$ 延伸到 $R(y)$，边界可积、$R\ge L$，且 $c<d$ 是有限端点，则

$$A=\int_c^d[R(y)-L(y)]\,dy.$$

> [!example] A sideways parabola and a line · 横向抛物线与直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the region enclosed by $x=y^2$ and $y=x-2$, rewrite the line as $x=y+2$. Intersections satisfy $y^2=y+2$, or $(y-2)(y+1)=0$, giving heights $-1$ and $2$. Between them, $y+2-y^2=(2-y)(y+1)\ge0$, so the line is on the right and the parabola on the left.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $x=y^2$ 与 $y=x-2$ 围成的区域，把直线写成 $x=y+2$。交点满足 $y^2=y+2$，即 $(y-2)(y+1)=0$，所以高度为 $-1$、$2$。两者之间 $y+2-y^2=(2-y)(y+1)\ge0$，直线在右，抛物线在左。
>
> $$A=\int_{-1}^{2}(y+2-y^2)\,dy
> =\left[\frac{y^2}{2}+2y-\frac{y^3}{3}\right]_{-1}^{2}=\frac92.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Vertical slicing gives the same area, but its lower boundary changes at $x=1$: use $-\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,1]$ and $x-2$ on $[1,4]$, with upper boundary $\sqrt{x}$ throughout. The horizontal choice avoids this split.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 竖切也得到相同面积，但下边界在 $x=1$ 改变：$[0,1]$ 上用 $-\sqrt{x}$，$[1,4]$ 上用 $x-2$；上边界始终为 $\sqrt{x}$。横切避免了这次分段。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, a strip must represent the actual region. If one vertical slice contains $[0,1]$ and $[2,3]$, its total length is $1+1=2$, not $3$: the gap is not part of the area. Add lengths of disjoint components or split into suitable regions. With length units on both axes, length times width has square units. For a three-dimensional solid, the corresponding quantity is an entire cross-sectional area times thickness; see [[切片体积|volume by slicing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，切片必须对应实际区域。如果一条竖切片包含 $[0,1]$ 与 $[2,3]$ 两段，总长度为 $1+1=2$，不是 $3$；中间空隙不属于面积。应把不相交部分的长度相加，或把区域适当拆开。两轴均用长度单位时，长度乘宽度得到平方单位。三维立体则应使用整个截面积乘厚度，见[[切片体积]]。
