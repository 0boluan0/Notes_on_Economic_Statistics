# 巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basel Bank Capital and Liquidity Regulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 通过风险加权资本、杠杆后备、流动性和稳定融资约束，使银行在信用、市场、操作和对手损失下仍有吸收能力并降低系统外部性。
> **具体锚点：** 风险加权资本率 12% 不表示银行有 12% 现金；资本是净资产损失缓冲，LCR 中的高质量流动性资产才用于短期现金流压力。
> **核心难点：** 分母依赖风险权重与模型，合规比率不等于真实风险充分；资本、流动性、杠杆和处置能力是互补约束。
> **为什么重要：** 银行高杠杆、短期可赎回负债和支付外部性使私人风险选择可能将损失转给存款人和公共安全网。
> **继续：** 用当前 [Basel Framework](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/) 查口径，不背过时数字；衍生品基础设施见 [[OTC 衍生品清算、保证金与 CCP 风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Through risk-weighted capital, a leverage backstop, liquidity, and stable-funding constraints, it gives banks loss-absorption capacity against credit, market, operational, and counterparty losses and reduces systemic externalities.
> **Concrete anchor:** A 12% risk-weighted capital ratio does not mean the bank holds 12% cash. Capital is a net-asset loss buffer; high-quality liquid assets in the LCR address short-run cash-flow stress.
> **Central difficulty:** The denominator depends on risk weights and models, so compliance does not prove risk adequacy. Capital, liquidity, leverage, and resolvability are complementary constraints.
> **Why it matters:** High bank leverage, short redeemable liabilities, and payment externalities can transfer the cost of private risk-taking to depositors and the public safety net.
> **Continue:** Use the current [Basel Framework](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/) for definitions rather than memorizing obsolete numbers. For derivatives infrastructure, see [[OTC 衍生品清算、保证金与 CCP 风险|OTC Derivatives Clearing, Margin, and CCP Risk]].

## 为什么监管资本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why regulate bank capital?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行杠杆高、负债可赎回且失败有支付/信用外部性，存款保险又削弱市场纪律。资本要求让股东/债权先吸收损失，降低破产概率和公共救助预期，但过高或顺周期要求也可能压缩信贷。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Banks are highly levered, fund with redeemable liabilities, and create payment and credit externalities when they fail, while deposit insurance weakens market discipline. Capital requirements make shareholders and creditors absorb losses first and reduce failure probability and expected public rescue, though excessive or procyclical requirements can compress credit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本不是闲置现金，而是资产减负债后的损失吸收层。资产减值 5 会直接减少权益 5，却未必立即产生现金流；存款集中流出则需要可变现资产或融资，即使账面净值仍为正。监管必须分别处理偿付与流动性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capital is not idle cash; it is the loss-absorbing layer equal to assets minus liabilities. An asset write-down of 5 reduces equity by 5 without necessarily creating immediate cash outflow. A concentrated deposit runoff requires monetizable assets or funding even if book net worth remains positive. Regulation must address solvency and liquidity separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Basel I—III 的结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The structure from Basel I through Basel III*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Basel I 引入统一信用风险权重；Basel II 强化风险敏感、监督审查和市场纪律；危机后 Basel III 提高资本质量，加入资本缓冲、杠杆率、流动性标准、CVA 与交易账簿改革。最终口径应以 BIS 当前整合框架为准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Basel I introduced standardized credit-risk weights. Basel II increased risk sensitivity and added supervisory review and market discipline. After the crisis, Basel III raised capital quality and added capital buffers, a leverage ratio, liquidity standards, CVA, and trading-book reforms. Final conventions should follow the BIS's current consolidated framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史分期帮助理解为什么出现某项约束，但实际计算不能把“Basel III”当单一静态版本。实施日期、司法辖区选择、过渡安排和修订会变化，因此卡片保存逻辑，具体数值回到当前官方框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical phases explain why a constraint appeared, but actual calculation cannot treat Basel III as one static version. Implementation dates, jurisdictional choices, transitions, and revisions change, so this card preserves logic while current numerical requirements must be checked in the official framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三类核心约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three core constraint families*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险加权资本率比较合格资本与 RWA；杠杆率对总敞口设后备；LCR/NSFR 分别关注短期压力流动性与稳定融资。一个机构可资本充足但流动性不足，反之亦然。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk-weighted capital ratios compare eligible capital with RWA; the leverage ratio provides a backstop against total exposure; LCR and NSFR address short-run stressed liquidity and stable funding respectively. An institution can be well capitalized but illiquid, or liquid but undercapitalized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 数值锚点：同一银行的不同分母
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical anchor: different denominators for the same bank*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行合格资本 12、总敞口 200、RWA 100，则风险加权资本率为 12%，杠杆率为 6%。若 RWA 模型把某些资产权重压低，资本率可上升而总敞口不变；杠杆率提供不依赖这些风险权重的后备，但也不区分 200 中资产的风险差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bank has eligible capital 12, total exposure 200, and RWA 100. Its risk-weighted capital ratio is 12% and leverage ratio 6%. If an RWA model lowers weights on some assets, the capital ratio can rise while total exposure is unchanged. The leverage ratio is a backstop independent of those weights, but it also does not distinguish risk differences among the 200 of assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LCR 比较压力期高质量流动性资产与净现金流出，NSFR 比较可用稳定资金与所需稳定资金。两者都依赖流失率、折扣和期限分类；高比率也不能替代特定币种、日内支付和抵押品集中分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LCR compares high-quality liquid assets with stressed net cash outflow, while NSFR compares available stable funding with required stable funding. Both depend on runoff rates, haircuts, and maturity classification. High ratios do not replace analysis of currency-specific, intraday-payment, and collateral concentration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 市场风险与 ES
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market risk and ES*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

FRTB 重划 trading/banking book 边界，标准法和内部模型法处理市场风险，并用 stressed expected shortfall 等捕捉尾部与流动性期限。模型可批准不等于风险可忽略，仍需 backtesting、P&L attribution 和治理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FRTB redraws the trading- and banking-book boundary, provides standardized and internal-model approaches for market risk, and uses stressed expected shortfall and liquidity horizons to capture tails. Model approval does not make risk negligible; backtesting, P&L attribution, and governance remain necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 模型与监管套利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Models and regulatory arbitrage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内部模型、风险权重和法律实体结构可被优化。output floor、披露和监督用于限制差异；评估应同时看比率、绝对敞口、情景和数据质量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Internal models, risk weights, and legal-entity structures can be optimized. Output floors, disclosure, and supervision constrain differences; assessment should examine ratios, absolute exposure, scenarios, and data quality together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从原主题保留的全局定位
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Global orientation retained from the original topic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **它解决什么：** 规定银行用多少资本和流动性吸收信用、市场、操作和对手风险，并通过清算、保证金和披露降低系统外部性。
> **具体锚点：** 风险加权资本率高不等于现金很多；资本吸收损失，流动性资产应付现金流，二者是不同防线。
> **核心难点：** 监管比率依赖风险权重和模型；最低合规不是实际风险充分，对手风险也会迁移到 CCP。
> **为什么重要：** 2008 后改革不仅提高资本，还加入杠杆、流动性、缓冲、处置和衍生品基础设施。
> **继续：** 用当前 Basel Framework 核对口径，不背过时数字；信用/CVA 见对应知识文件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It specifies capital and liquidity to absorb credit, market, operational, and counterparty risk and uses clearing, margin, and disclosure to reduce systemic externalities.
> **Concrete anchor:** A high risk-weighted capital ratio does not mean abundant cash. Capital absorbs loss, while liquid assets meet cash flows; they are different defenses.
> **Central difficulty:** Regulatory ratios depend on risk weights and models. Minimum compliance is not proof of risk adequacy, and counterparty risk can migrate to a CCP.
> **Why it matters:** Post-2008 reform added leverage, liquidity, buffers, resolution, and derivatives infrastructure rather than merely increasing capital.
> **Continue:** Check conventions in the current Basel Framework rather than memorizing obsolete numbers; use the dedicated credit and CVA files for those risks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把资本率当现金储备率，混淆损失吸收和支付能力。
- 只看 RWA 比率，不看总敞口、风险权重来源和集中。
- 用历史最低合规数字评估当前银行，未查实施辖区和缓冲。
- 达到 LCR 便认为长期融资稳定，遗漏 NSFR、币种和日内流动性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating the capital ratio as a cash reserve ratio confuses loss absorption with payment capacity.
- Looking only at the RWA ratio without total exposure, sources of risk weights, and concentration.
- Evaluating a current bank with an old minimum number without checking jurisdictional implementation and buffers.
- Treating LCR compliance as proof of stable long-term funding while omitting NSFR, currency, and intraday liquidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 资本充足和流动性充足为什么不是一回事？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why are capital adequacy and liquidity adequacy not the same?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 资本是损失吸收的净资产缓冲，流动性是按时履行现金义务的资产/融资能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Capital is a net-asset buffer that absorbs loss; liquidity is the asset and funding capacity to meet cash obligations on time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么还需要杠杆率而非只看 RWA 比率？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a leverage ratio needed in addition to the RWA ratio?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 风险权重可能低估、模型化或被套利，杠杆率提供不依赖风险权重的简单后备。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Risk weights can be understated, model-dependent, or arbitraged, so the leverage ratio provides a simple backstop independent of risk weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么多个监管约束需要并存？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why must several regulatory constraints coexist?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 每个指标防范不同漏洞，也有自己的盲点：风险加权资本区分风险却依赖模型，杠杆率简单却不分风险，LCR 管短期现金，NSFR 管期限融资；组合使用才减少绕过空间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Each measure closes a different gap and has its own blind spot. Risk-weighted capital distinguishes risk but depends on models; leverage is simple but risk-insensitive; LCR addresses short-run cash; NSFR addresses funding maturity. Using them together reduces circumvention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
