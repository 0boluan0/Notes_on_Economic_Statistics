# 直观准则把离轨信号后的信念限制在未被该信号均衡支配的发送者类型上
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Intuitive Criterion restricts beliefs after an off-path signal to sender types for whom that signal is not equilibrium dominated*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直观准则是 Cho–Kreps 提出的信号博弈均衡精炼。它从一份 PBE 出发，考察哪些类型即使面对该信号后最有利的理性接收者反应，也不可能达到自己的均衡收益；这些类型不应在观察到该离轨信号后获得正 posterior 概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Intuitive Criterion is an equilibrium refinement for signaling games due to Cho and Kreps. Starting from a PBE, it identifies types that cannot reach their equilibrium payoff from a signal even under their most favorable rational receiver response. Such types should receive no positive posterior probability after that off-path signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 允许的接收者最佳反应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Admissible Receiver Best Responses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定信号 $s$。对类型集合 $\widetilde\Theta\subseteq\Theta$，令 $BR(\widetilde\Theta,s)$ 表示：接收者在某份支持包含于 $\widetilde\Theta$ 的 belief 下，对 $s$ 的所有纯最佳反应的并集。形式上，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a signal $s$. For a set of types $\widetilde\Theta\subseteq\Theta$, let $BR(\widetilde\Theta,s)$ be the union of the receiver's pure best responses to $s$ over beliefs whose support is contained in $\widetilde\Theta$. Formally,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
BR(\widetilde\Theta,s)
=
\bigcup_{p\in\Delta(\widetilde\Theta)}
\arg\max_{a\in A}
\sum_{\theta\in\widetilde\Theta}
p(\theta)u_2(\theta,s,a).
$$

这里允许接收者针对某份 belief 作最佳反应，但不允许发送者假定接收者采取一个在任何相关 belief 下都不理性的行动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This construction allows the receiver to best respond to some admissible belief, but it does not let the sender posit an action that is irrational under every such belief.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 未被均衡支配的类型集合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Types for Whom the Signal Is Not Equilibrium Dominated*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 PBE 策略组合 $\sigma$，并以 $u_1(\theta,\sigma)$ 表示类型 $\theta$ 的均衡收益。定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a PBE strategy profile $\sigma$, and let $u_1(\theta,\sigma)$ denote type $\theta$'s equilibrium payoff. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)
=
\left\{
\theta\in\Theta:
\max_{a\in BR(\Theta,s)}u_1(\theta,s,a)
\ge u_1(\theta,\sigma)
\right\}.
$$

若 $\theta\notin\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)$，则即使从所有可能由理性接收者产生的回应中挑选对该类型最有利的一项，发送 $s$ 仍使其严格低于均衡收益；此时称信号 $s$ 对类型 $\theta$ 是 **equilibrium dominated**。当 $\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)\ne\varnothing$ 时，直观准则要求离轨信号 $s$ 后的 posterior 把概率 1 放在这个集合上。若该集合为空，应使用 [[直观准则空集]] 的单独边界，而不是把概率 1 分给空集。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\theta\notin\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)$, then even the most favorable response that can be rational for the receiver leaves type $\theta$ strictly below its equilibrium payoff. Signal $s$ is then **equilibrium dominated** for type $\theta$. When $\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)\ne\varnothing$, the Intuitive Criterion requires the posterior after $s$ to assign probability one to that set. See [[直观准则空集|the empty-survivor-set boundary]] rather than assigning probability one to an empty set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 通过准则还要检查最不利的允许回应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Passing the Criterion Also Requires Checking the Worst Admissible Response*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支持限制本身还不是完整的通过条件。对每个满足 $\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)\ne\varnothing$ 的相关信号 $s$ 与每个类型 $\theta$，MIT 的定义要求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The support restriction alone is not the complete test. For every relevant signal $s$ with $\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)\ne\varnothing$ and every type $\theta$, MIT's definition requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\min_{a\in BR(\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma),s)}
u_1(\theta,s,a)
\le
u_1(\theta,\sigma).
$$

左侧是 belief 只支持未被均衡支配类型时，类型 $\theta$ 在所有允许的接收者最佳反应中所能保证的最低收益。若这个最低收益仍严格高于均衡收益，该类型必然愿意偏离，原 PBE 不能通过直观准则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The left-hand side is the lowest payoff type $\theta$ can guarantee across receiver best responses allowed when beliefs are supported only on non-dominated types. If even this lowest payoff strictly exceeds the equilibrium payoff, the type must deviate and the PBE fails the Intuitive Criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直观准则限制的是离轨信号的类型解释，而不是用 tremble 极限生成所有信息集 belief；因此它与 sequential equilibrium 的 consistency 要求不是同一个条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Intuitive Criterion restricts the type interpretation of an off-path signal; it does not generate beliefs at all information sets from a common sequence of trembles. It is therefore distinct from the consistency condition of sequential equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
