# 隐藏类型改变谁参与而隐藏行动改变同一参与者怎样做

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Hidden types affect who participates; hidden actions affect what a given participant does*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在常用的金融契约诊断中，先固定比较对象：若风险类型已经存在但银行看不清，利率变化主要改变哪些类型申请，检查[[逆向选择]]；若同一类型的借款人因契约改变而改选项目、努力或资产用途，检查[[道德风险]]。两条机制可以同时存在，必须分别交代。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the standard financial-contract diagnosis, fix what is being compared. If risk types already exist but the bank cannot distinguish them, and rates change which types apply, examine [[逆向选择|adverse selection]]. If the same borrower type changes its project, effort, or use of assets in response to terms, examine [[道德风险|moral hazard]]. Both mechanisms may operate and should be stated separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“签约前／后”是定位问题的辅助线，不是按观测日期分类的规则。违约虽然发生在放款后，却可能来自放款前就存在的高风险类型；拿到钱后改选风险项目则是行动变化。仅观察“较高贷款利率伴随较多违约”，还不能在这两种机制之间作选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before versus after contracting is a guide, not a rule based on the date an outcome is observed. A later default may reflect a high-risk type already present before lending; switching projects after funding is an action change. More defaults associated with higher rates alone do not distinguish these mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要排除另一类解释：若所有借款人都受到同一场衰退冲击，即使类型和行动完全可观察，违约仍可能同时上升。可观察的共同风险、隐藏类型的选择和不可充分订约的行动，是不同判断对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a third explanation: a recession can raise defaults together even if types and actions are fully observable. Common observable risk, selection on hidden types, and actions that cannot be fully contracted on are different objects of diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
