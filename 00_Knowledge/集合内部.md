# Interior of a set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*集合内部*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **interior point** is a point around which a whole small open region still lies in the set. For $A=[0,1]$ on the usual real line, $1/2$ is an interior point: a sufficiently small interval around it stays in $A$. The member $0$ is not an interior point, because every open interval around $0$ also contains negative numbers. The **interior** collects all points that pass this local test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**内点**是这样一种点：它周围有一整小块开区域仍留在集合内部。在通常实数线上的 $A=[0,1]$ 中，$1/2$ 是内点，因为它周围足够小的区间仍在 $A$ 内。成员 $0$ 却不是内点，因为 $0$ 周围每个开区间都包含负数。**内部**就是所有通过这种局部检验的点组成的集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a [[拓扑|topological space]] $X$, meaning that its [[开集|open subsets]] have been specified. An **open neighbourhood** of $x$ means an open set $U$ that contains $x$. For $A\subseteq X$, the notation $\operatorname{int}_X(A)$, or $A^\circ$ when the ambient space is clear, denotes its interior:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定[[拓扑|拓扑空间]] $X$，即已指定哪些子集是[[开集]]。点 $x$ 的**开邻域**是包含 $x$ 的开集 $U$。对于 $A\subseteq X$，用 $\operatorname{int}_X(A)$ 表示其内部；环境明确时也记作 $A^\circ$：

$$x\in\operatorname{int}_X(A)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{there is an open }U\text{ with }x\in U\subseteq A.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The neighbourhood must contain $x$ and stay entirely in $A$. In particular, an interior point is a member of $A$, but mere membership is not enough. We use open neighbourhoods explicitly here; if “neighbourhood” is instead defined as any set containing an open neighbourhood, the interior test is unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个邻域必须包含 $x$，并且整体留在 $A$ 内。因此内点一定属于 $A$，但仅仅属于 $A$ 还不够。这里明确使用开邻域；若另一本书把“邻域”定义为任何包含开邻域的集合，内点判据仍然相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## All the open parts together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 把所有开部分合在一起

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The local definition has an equivalent description: take the union of every open set contained in $A$. The symbol $\bigcup$ collects every point belonging to at least one such set:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

局部定义还有一个等价表述：把包含在 $A$ 中的每个开集取并。符号 $\bigcup$ 收集至少属于其中某个集合的全部点：

$$\operatorname{int}_X(A)=\bigcup\{U\subseteq X:U\text{ is open in }X\text{ and }U\subseteq A\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A point belongs to this union exactly when some qualifying open set contains it, which is the neighbourhood test. The union is open by the topology axiom and remains inside $A$. If $V$ is any other open subset of $A$, it is one of the sets being united, so $V\subseteq\operatorname{int}_X(A)$. This explains the word **largest**: the interior contains every open subset of $A$, not merely one particularly large choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一点属于这个并集，恰好意味着某个合格开集包含它，这就是邻域检验。由拓扑公理，并集本身开，而且仍在 $A$ 内。若 $V$ 是 $A$ 的任何其他开子集，它就是参与取并的集合之一，所以 $V\subseteq\operatorname{int}_X(A)$。这解释了**最大**的含义：内部包含 $A$ 的每一个开子集，而不只是挑出某个看起来较大的选择。

> [!important] The largest open subset · 最大开子集
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The interior is open and lies inside the original set. In particular, $A$ is open exactly when $A=\operatorname{int}_X(A)$. Also, $\operatorname{int}_X(\varnothing)=\varnothing$ and $\operatorname{int}_X(X)=X$. No closedness assumption on $A$ is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 内部是开集，并且包含在原集合中。特别地，$A$ 开当且仅当 $A=\operatorname{int}_X(A)$。此外，$\operatorname{int}_X(\varnothing)=\varnothing$、$\operatorname{int}_X(X)=X$。定义不要求 $A$ 本身闭。

## In a metric space, look for a ball
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 在度量空间中寻找一个球

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$, let $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$ be the open ball of radius $r>0$. An open neighbourhood contains some ball around its point, and the ball itself is open. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$ 中，记 $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$ 为半径 $r>0$ 的开球。开邻域包含中心点周围的某个球，而球本身又开，因此

$$x\in\operatorname{int}_X(A)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{there is }r>0\text{ such that }B_r(x)\subseteq A.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=[0,1]\subseteq\mathbb R$, every $0<x<1$ admits radius $r=\tfrac12\min(x,1-x)>0$. Neither endpoint admits a positive radius, and points outside $A$ cannot be interior points. Thus $\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}([0,1])=(0,1)$. A separate radius is allowed for each point; one radius need not work for the entire interior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A=[0,1]\subseteq\mathbb R$，每个 $0<x<1$ 都可以取半径 $r=\tfrac12\min(x,1-x)>0$。两个端点都不存在合适的正半径，$A$ 外的点又不可能是内点，所以 $\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}([0,1])=(0,1)$。每个点可以分别选择半径，不要求整个内部共用一个半径。

> [!example] Many members, but no interior points · 成员很多，内点却没有
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\mathbb Q$ be the rational numbers, viewed as a subset of $\mathbb R$. For any rational $x$ and any radius $r>0$, choose a positive integer $N$ so large that $\sqrt2/N<r$. The number $x+\sqrt2/N$ is irrational and lies within distance $r$ of $x$. Thus no ball around a rational point stays in $\mathbb Q$, so $\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}(\mathbb Q)=\varnothing$. Interior is not obtained by simply removing a few visible endpoints.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把有理数集 $\mathbb Q$ 看作 $\mathbb R$ 的子集。对任意有理数 $x$ 和任意半径 $r>0$，取足够大的正整数 $N$，使 $\sqrt2/N<r$。数 $x+\sqrt2/N$ 是无理数，与 $x$ 的距离又小于 $r$。因此，没有任何有理数周围的球能完全留在 $\mathbb Q$ 中，故 $\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}(\mathbb Q)=\varnothing$。内部不能只靠删去几个图上可见的端点来求。

## The same set can have a different interior
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同一集合在不同环境中的内部

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Change the ambient space to $Y=[0,1]$ with its [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]]. A ball around $0$ is now intersected with $Y$: for $0<r<1$, it is $[0,r)$. This is open in $Y$ and stays inside $Y$. Every point of $Y$ is interior to $Y$ relative to itself, so $\operatorname{int}_Y(Y)=Y$, whereas $\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}(Y)=(0,1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把环境改成带[[子空间拓扑]]的 $Y=[0,1]$。现在 $0$ 周围的球要与 $Y$ 取交；当 $0<r<1$ 时，它是 $[0,r)$，在 $Y$ 中开，并且留在 $Y$ 内。$Y$ 中每个点都是相对于自身的内点，所以 $\operatorname{int}_Y(Y)=Y$，而 $\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}(Y)=(0,1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the smaller set $A=[0,1/2)\subseteq Y$, the same restriction makes $A$ open in $Y$, so $\operatorname{int}_Y(A)=A$. In the full real line its interior is $(0,1/2)$. Writing the ambient-space subscript is useful whenever more than one space is being considered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于较小的集合 $A=[0,1/2)\subseteq Y$，同样的限制使 $A$ 在 $Y$ 中开，所以 $\operatorname{int}_Y(A)=A$。在整条实数线中，其内部则为 $(0,1/2)$。同时讨论多个环境时，明确写出环境下标可以避免混淆。
