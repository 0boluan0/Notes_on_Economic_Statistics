# 前景决策权重是结果在前景评价中的影响系数而非事件概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prospect-theory decision weights measure an outcome's influence on evaluation rather than its probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前景决策权重是在[[前景理论]]评价式中乘在结果价值上的系数，用来描述该结果对方案吸引力的影响。它不是对事件发生可能性的另一套概率报告；决策者即使接受题目给定的客观概率，选择所反映的权重仍可不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A decision weight is the coefficient multiplying an outcome's value in [[前景理论|prospect theory]]. It describes that outcome's influence on an alternative's attractiveness, not another probability report. Even someone who accepts the stated objective probabilities can exhibit different weights in choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，已知公平硬币的两面概率各为 $0.5$，仍可在适用的简单前景中用 $\pi(0.5)=0.4$ 评价。此时两个权重的和是 $0.8$，而两面概率的和仍为 $1$。因此不能把它当成[[概率质量函数]]，也不能把“权重偏高”直接解释为“相信事件更容易发生”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a known fair coin, each side has probability $0.5$, yet an applicable simple prospect might be evaluated with $\pi(0.5)=0.4$. The two weights then sum to $0.8$ while the probabilities still sum to one. These weights are not a [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]], and overweighting does not by itself mean overestimating occurrence probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1979 年权重函数取 $\pi(0)=0$、$\pi(1)=1$。小概率被高估权重、中高概率被低估权重是其讨论的典型模式，不是对所有人、所有 $p$ 的无例外不等式：不可能与确定事件有端点条件，极小概率还可能被忽略。1992 年多结果版本的单项权重取决于结果排序及[[累积前景权重计算|累积概率的变换差]]，一般不等于对该项 $p_i$ 单独套一次函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 1979 function satisfies $\pi(0)=0$ and $\pi(1)=1$. Overweighting small probabilities and underweighting moderate-to-high probabilities are characteristic patterns, not exceptionless inequalities for every person and probability. Endpoints distinguish impossibility and certainty, and extremely unlikely events may also be ignored. In the 1992 multi-outcome version, individual weights depend on ranks and [[累积前景权重计算|differences of transformed cumulative probabilities]], not generally on transforming each $p_i$ in isolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Kahneman & Tversky (1979), pp. 280–283，PDF 18–21](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf#page=18)：支持权重不是概率或信念、硬币说明、端点和极小概率边界；$0.4$ 是本卡的示意数值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The source supports the distinction from probabilities and beliefs, the coin illustration, endpoint restrictions, and treatment of very unlikely events. The numerical weight $0.4$ is illustrative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Tversky & Kahneman (1992), pp. 300–303，PDF 4–7](https://psych.fullerton.edu/mbirnbaum/teaching/psych466/articles/Tversky_Kahneman_JRU_92.pdf#page=4)：支持按累积概率构建权重、混合前景不要求权重和为一，以及价值和权重共同决定风险态度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cumulative paper supports rank-dependent construction, nonunit weight sums for mixed prospects, and the joint contribution of values and weights to risk attitudes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
