<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Matrix rank
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵秩*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **rank** of a matrix counts the independent directions available among its outputs. Several input coordinates may control the same output direction, so having many columns does not necessarily mean being able to reach a large space. First identify the [[列空间|column space]], the set of all possible outputs; then count its [[维数|dimension]], the number of vectors in a basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵的**秩**计算它的输出中有多少个独立方向。几个输入坐标可能控制同一个输出方向，因此列很多，并不一定能到达很大的空间。先找出[[列空间]]，即全部可能输出的集合，再数它的[[维数]]，也就是一组基中的向量个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three columns, two output directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三列只提供两个输出方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a matrix with real entries. Multiplication gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的实矩阵。相乘得到

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
A\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_3\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}x_1+2x_2\\x_3\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first two input coordinates both affect the first output coordinate; the third input controls the second. Any target $(s,t)^T$ is reachable: choose $x_1=s$, $x_2=0$, $x_3=t$. Here the superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], writing a row of entries as a column. Therefore the column space is all of $\mathbb R^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前两个输入坐标都影响输出的第一坐标，第三个输入控制第二坐标。任意目标 $(s,t)^T$ 都可到达：取 $x_1=s$、$x_2=0$、$x_3=t$ 即可。这里上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把一行数写成列向量。因此列空间就是整个 $\mathbb R^2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first and third columns are a [[基|basis]] of this space: they reach every output and are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]. The second column is twice the first and adds no new direction. Thus $\operatorname{rank}(A)=2$, although $A$ has three columns. One could choose the second and third columns instead; changing the basis does not change its length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一列和第三列构成这个空间的一组[[基]]：既能到达每个输出，又[[线性无关]]。第二列只是第一列的两倍，没有增加新方向。所以尽管有三列，仍有 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=2$。也可以选择第二列和第三列作基；换基不会改变基的长度。

> [!abstract] The definition · 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$, where $m,n$ are positive integers and $\mathbb F$ is the scalar field, usually $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. There are $n$ input coordinates and $m$ output coordinates. Define
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$，其中 $m,n$ 是正整数，$\mathbb F$ 是标量域，通常为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。输入有 $n$ 个坐标，输出有 $m$ 个坐标。定义
>
> $$\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(A):=\dim C(A)},\qquad
> C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb F^n\}\subseteq\mathbb F^m.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All dimensions and linear combinations use the same field $\mathbb F$. The definition measures the reachable output space, not the whole surrounding space unless every output is reachable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所有维数和线性组合都使用同一个标量域 $\mathbb F$。定义衡量的是可达的输出空间；只有每个输出都可达时，它才等于整个环境空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Selecting enough independent columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*选够独立列，而不是数全部列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns always [[张成|span]] $C(A)$: every $Ax$ is their linear combination. From this finite list, keep a column whenever it is outside the span of those already kept. Each addition preserves independence, and each discarded column was already generated. The retained list is therefore a basis of $C(A)$; its length is the rank. Merely selecting some independent columns is insufficient if they do not span the whole column space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各列总能[[张成]] $C(A)$，因为每个 $Ax$ 都是各列的线性组合。依次查看这个有限列表：遇到尚未被已选列张成的列，就保留它。每次加入都保持无关，而被舍去的列已经能由所选列生成。因此最后留下的是 $C(A)$ 的一组基，其长度就是秩。仅仅选到几根无关列还不够，还必须张成整个列空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This immediately gives $0\le\operatorname{rank}(A)\le\min(m,n)$. A basis extracted from $n$ columns has at most $n$ members, and a subspace of $\mathbb F^m$ has dimension at most $m$. Rank is zero exactly when every column is zero: the reachable space is then $\{0\}$, whose basis is empty. Rank $n$ is called **full column rank**: all columns are independent. Rank $m$ is called **full row rank**: every target in $\mathbb F^m$ is reachable, equivalently the rows are independent by the row-rank theorem below. A rectangular matrix may satisfy one of these conditions without the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由此立即得到 $0\le\operatorname{rank}(A)\le\min(m,n)$：从 $n$ 列中抽取的基至多含 $n$ 个成员，而 $\mathbb F^m$ 的子空间维数至多为 $m$。秩为零当且仅当每一列都为零；此时可达空间是 $\{0\}$，其基为空组。秩为 $n$ 称为**满列秩**，表示所有列无关；秩为 $m$ 称为**满行秩**，表示 $\mathbb F^m$ 中每个目标都可达，由下述行秩定理也等价于各行无关。矩形矩阵可以只满足其中一个条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Other ways to compute the same number require results beyond the definition. [[行秩等于列秩|Row rank equals column rank]] permits counting the dimension of the row space. [[主元与自由变量|The pivot–rank relation]] permits counting pivots after elimination; when constructing a column-space basis, [[RREF读取列空间基|use those pivot positions to select columns of the original matrix]]. For real or complex matrices, [[奇异值与秩|the number of positive singular values]] also equals rank. Finally, [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity]] relates the output count to the dimension of the inputs sent to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他计算同一个数的方法，需要定义之外的结论。[[行秩等于列秩]]允许计算行空间维数；[[主元与自由变量]]中的主元与秩关系允许数消元后的主元，而构造列空间基时，要[[RREF读取列空间基|依据这些主元位置返回原矩阵选列]]。对实矩阵或复矩阵，[[奇异值与秩|正奇异值的个数]]也等于秩。最后，[[秩零度定理]]把输出方向数与被送到零的输入所成空间的维数联系起来。

## Size, nonzero entries and numerical resolution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rank is neither matrix size nor the number of nonzero entries. For example, the real matrices below each have four nonzero entries, but the first has equal columns and rank one, whereas the second has independent columns and rank two:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

秩不是矩阵的尺寸，也不是非零元素的个数。例如下面两个实矩阵都含四个非零元素，但左边的两列相同、秩为一，右边的两列无关、秩为二：

$$\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition above is exact algebraic rank. For the real matrix $D=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&\varepsilon\end{bmatrix}$, every nonzero $\varepsilon$ gives two independent columns, however small $|\varepsilon|$ is. A computation may treat the weak second direction as zero under a stated tolerance; that is a [[数值秩|numerical-rank]] decision. Small size and exact zero are different conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的定义是精确代数秩。对于实矩阵 $D=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&\varepsilon\end{bmatrix}$，只要 $\varepsilon\ne0$，两列就无关，无论 $|\varepsilon|$ 多小。数值计算可能按声明的容差把微弱的第二方向当作零；这属于[[数值秩]]的判定。数值小与精确为零，是不同条件。

> [!question]- Two directions really reached · 确实到达两个方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All columns lie in a plane spanned by two independent vectors, and both spanning vectors can themselves be generated from the columns. What is the rank? **Answer:** $2$. Containment gives at most two dimensions, and reaching both independent directions gives at least two. Containment in a plane alone would give only an upper bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所有列都落在两个无关向量张成的平面内，而且这两个张成向量也确实能由各列生成，秩是多少？**答案：** $2$。包含在平面内给出至多二维，能到达两个独立方向又给出至少二维。如果仅知道包含在平面内，就只能得到上界。
