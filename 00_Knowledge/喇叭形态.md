# 喇叭形态是高点抬高与低点降低形成的扩张价格区间

喇叭形态是在选定观察尺度下，价格相继出现更高的高点和更低的低点，使上边界向上、下边界向下发散的多期价格结构。它描述的是振荡区间扩张，而不是单边上涨或单边下跌。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A broadening or megaphone formation is a multi-bar price structure with successively higher highs and lower lows, producing an upward-diverging upper boundary and a downward-diverging lower boundary. It describes an expanding oscillation range, not a one-way rise or decline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如确认的高点依次为110、115、120，低点依次为100、95、90，体现上下两端都向外扩张的结构。高低点须用同一采样、极值和容差规则识别；单根长K线不能代替这个多次往返结构。[[对称三角形]]恰好相反：高点降低、低点抬高，区间收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Confirmed highs at 110, 115 and 120 and lows at 100, 95 and 90 illustrate expansion on both sides. Identify the turning points with consistent sampling, pivot and tolerance rules. One long bar cannot substitute for this repeated structure. A [[对称三角形|symmetrical triangle]] has the opposite geometry: lower highs and higher lows contract the range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程图画出后续下破，只展示一种实现路径。喇叭的几何定义不含“必然下跌”的结论；如果采用突破信号，必须另外记录实际越过哪条边界、用什么条件确认，以及该信息何时可用，见[[交易信号时序]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's later downside break illustrates one realized path. A megaphone's geometric definition does not imply a necessary decline. A breakout signal must separately record the crossed boundary, confirmation criterion and information-availability time under [[交易信号时序|signal timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
