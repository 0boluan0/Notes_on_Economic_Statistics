# 条件分布几乎处处不随条件变量改变当且仅当两变量独立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A conditional law is almost surely constant exactly when the variables are independent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实值随机变量及一个正则条件分布版本，$X\perp Y$ 当且仅当 $Y\mid X=x$ 的条件分布对 $P_X$ 几乎处处的 $x$ 都等于 $Y$ 的边际分布。必须固定整张分布，不只是均值或方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real-valued variables and a regular conditional law, independence is equivalent to the conditional law agreeing with the marginal law for almost every conditioning value. Equality of only one or two moments is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若某个概率测度 $Q$ 对几乎所有 $x$ 都满足 $P(Y\in B\mid X=x)=Q(B)$（作为同一个条件分布核，对所有 Borel 集成立），先用全期望得 $P(Y\in B)=Q(B)$，再得 $P(X\in A,Y\in B)=P(X\in A)Q(B)$。这就恢复独立的事件分解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common conditional probability measure becomes the marginal by total expectation. Averaging over any event of X then factorises the joint event probability, proving independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
