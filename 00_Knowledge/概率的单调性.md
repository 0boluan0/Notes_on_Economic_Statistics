# 事件包含关系使概率保持同方向的大小关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Event inclusion implies the same ordering of probabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A\subseteq B$，则 $P(A)\le P(B)$。因为 $B=A\cup(B\setminus A)$ 是互斥并，
$$P(B)=P(A)+P(B\setminus A)\ge P(A).$$
不要求独立，也不要求结果等可能。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If one event is contained in another, its probability cannot be larger. Decomposing the larger event into disjoint pieces proves the claim using nonnegativity; independence and equiprobability are unnecessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
CDF 的单调不减来自 $\{X\le a\}\subseteq\{X\le b\}$（$a<b$）。比较事件概率之前先比较集合，是比猜概率大小更可靠的方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The CDF is nondecreasing because its threshold events are nested. Comparing the events first supplies the probability ordering.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
