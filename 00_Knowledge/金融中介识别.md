# 金融中介应按负债承诺和资产用途识别而不能只把银行算作中介
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identify financial intermediaries by their liability promises and asset uses, not by treating “bank” as the whole category*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子分类
> 已经确认某个业务构成 [[金融中介|金融中介]] 后，再按负债承诺、资产用途和所做转换分类。银行只是最典型的一类，不是全部；同一家金融集团也可以同时经营多种中介和市场服务。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Once an activity has been identified as [[金融中介|financial intermediation]], classify it by its liability promise, asset use, and transformations. Banks are the clearest example rather than the whole category, and one financial group can operate several intermediary and market-service businesses at once.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以用三组典型负债—资产组合建立直觉：

- **存款机构：** 发行可支付、储蓄或定期存款，持有贷款、证券和结算资产；
- **契约性储蓄机构：** 通过保费或养老金缴款取得合同性资金，持有与预计未来赔付、退休给付相匹配的资产；这些现金流可借助合同和精算估计，但并非没有不确定性；
- **投资中介：** 发行基金份额、商业票据、债券或股权，再持有贷款或证券组合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three typical liability-asset combinations build the intuition:

- **Depository institutions** issue transaction, savings, or time deposits and hold loans, securities, and settlement assets.
- **Contractual savings institutions** receive premiums or pension contributions under contracts and hold assets matched to expected insurance or retirement payments. Contracts and actuarial estimates make these cash flows more analysable, not certain.
- **Investment intermediaries** issue fund shares, commercial paper, bonds, or equity and hold portfolios of loans or securities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分类的重点不是把每家现实机构塞进唯一盒子。大型金融集团会同时经营多种业务，同一产品也可能同时包含支付、投资和保险功能。应在业务或资产负债表层面判断：一个共同基金是中介，但不承诺像银行存款那样按固定面值支取；投资银行承销证券时提供市场服务，却未必在该交易中长期持有信用资产并发行自己的融资负债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The point is not to force every real institution into one exclusive box. A financial group can conduct several businesses, and one product can combine payment, investment, and insurance functions. Classify the activity or balance sheet. A mutual fund is an intermediary but does not make the same fixed-par redemption promise as a bank deposit; an investment bank underwriting a security provides a market service but may not retain the credit asset and finance it with its own liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么功能分类更稳

监管边界和公司名称会变化，信用、期限和流动性转换却可能转移到非银行机构、基金、证券融资交易或相互连接的专门载体中。只监测“银行”会漏掉做相同转换的其他资产负债表，也会把没有相同风险结构的所有非银行机构错误归为一类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regulatory boundaries and company names change, while credit, maturity, and liquidity transformation can migrate to nonbanks, funds, securities-financing transactions, or chains of specialised vehicles. Monitoring only “banks” misses other balance sheets performing the same transformations, while grouping every nonbank together treats unlike risk structures as equivalent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么不能仅凭“基金”和“银行”两个名称判断谁的流动性风险更高？
>
> **答案：** 要看具体负债能否随时赎回、是否承诺固定面值，以及资产能否低损失变现。名称不直接给出期限缺口、赎回规则和底层资产流动性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can the labels “fund” and “bank” alone not determine which has more liquidity risk?
>
> **Answer:** Inspect whether liabilities are redeemable on demand, whether redemption is promised at par, and whether assets can be sold without large losses. The labels do not reveal maturity gaps, redemption rules, or underlying asset liquidity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
