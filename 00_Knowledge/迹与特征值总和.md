# Trace and the sum of eigenvalues
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*迹与特征值总和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **trace** of a square matrix is the sum of its main-diagonal entries. It gives a quick way to find the total of all the matrix’s eigenvalues, even before finding them individually. For example, the diagonal entries of
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方阵的**迹**就是主对角线上元素的总和。它让我们还没逐一求出特征值，就能知道所有特征值加起来是多少。例如

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\1&3\end{bmatrix},\qquad \operatorname{tr}A=3+3=6.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[特征对|eigenvalue]] $\lambda$ is a scalar for which some nonzero vector $v$ satisfies $Av=\lambda v$. Here $A(1,1)^T=4(1,1)^T$ and $A(1,-1)^T=2(1,-1)^T$, so the eigenvalues are $4$ and $2$. Their sum is $6$, matching the trace. The theorem explains why this agreement is necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[特征对|特征值]] $\lambda$ 是满足 $Av=\lambda v$ 的数，其中 $v$ 是某个非零向量。这里 $A(1,1)^T=4(1,1)^T$，$A(1,-1)^T=2(1,-1)^T$，所以特征值是 $4$ 和 $2$，总和为 $6$，与迹相等。下面的定理说明这为什么是必然的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which eigenvalues must be counted?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*应该把哪些特征值加进去？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an $n\times n$ matrix $A=(a_{ij})$, with $n\ge1$, the entry $a_{ii}$ is in row $i$ and column $i$, and $\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_{i=1}^n a_{ii}$. Take real or complex entries, and allow complex eigenvalues. List the $n$ roots of the [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]] $p_A(t)=\det(tI-A)$, repeating each root as many times as its factor occurs. Here $t$ is the scalar variable and $I$ is the identity matrix. This repetition count is its [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic multiplicity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A=(a_{ij})$ 是 $n\times n$ 方阵，$n\ge1$；$a_{ii}$ 是第 $i$ 行第 $i$ 列的元素，$\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_{i=1}^n a_{ii}$。矩阵元素可以是实数或复数，特征值允许取复数。把[[特征多项式]] $p_A(t)=\det(tI-A)$ 的 $n$ 个根列出来，一个根的因子出现几次，就重复列几次。这里 $t$ 是标量变量，$I$ 是单位矩阵；这个重复次数叫[[代数重数与几何重数|代数重数]]。

> [!important] Count repeated roots · 重根要重复计数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the resulting list is $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若得到的列表为 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$，则
>
> $$\operatorname{tr}A=\lambda_1+\cdots+\lambda_n.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This does not require a basis of eigenvectors. For $J=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$, the polynomial is $(t-2)^2$, so the sum is $2+2=4$, although its eigenvectors all lie on the line generated by $(1,0)^T$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个结论不要求有一组特征向量基。对 $J=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$，特征多项式为 $(t-2)^2$，所以总和是 $2+2=4$，尽管它的特征向量全在 $(1,0)^T$ 生成的直线上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the diagonal sum reappears
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么特征值总和恰好回到对角线？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the same polynomial in two ways. Expanding the determinant gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把同一个多项式写成两种形式。展开行列式得到

$$p_A(t)=t^n-(a_{11}+\cdots+a_{nn})t^{n-1}+\text{terms of lower degree}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n\ge2$, the product of diagonal entries of $tI-A$ is $(t-a_{11})\cdots(t-a_{nn})$. To obtain $t^{n-1}$, choose one $-a_{ii}$ and choose $t$ from every other factor; adding these choices gives the displayed coefficient. Every other term in the determinant expansion uses at least two off-diagonal entries, so it contains at most $n-2$ factors of $t$ and cannot change that coefficient. For $n=1$, the identity is simply $p_A(t)=t-a_{11}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $n\ge2$ 时，$tI-A$ 的对角元乘积是 $(t-a_{11})\cdots(t-a_{nn})$。要得到 $t^{n-1}$，就从一个因子选 $-a_{ii}$，从其余因子全选 $t$；把这些选择加起来，就是上式的系数。行列式展开的其他项至少用到两个非对角元，因而至多含 $n-2$ 个 $t$，不能改变这个系数。$n=1$ 时则直接有 $p_A(t)=t-a_{11}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Factoring by its roots instead gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按根分解则得到

$$p_A(t)=\prod_{i=1}^n(t-\lambda_i)
=t^n-(\lambda_1+\cdots+\lambda_n)t^{n-1}+\text{terms of lower degree}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same “choose one constant and all the remaining $t$’s” argument gives this coefficient. Since these are the same polynomial, the coefficients agree. This proves the theorem without assuming diagonalizability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里也用同一个办法：选一个常数项，其余全选 $t$。两种写法是同一个多项式，系数必须相同，因此得到定理，完全没有用到可对角化假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Real matrices can need complex roots
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*实矩阵也可能需要复数根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the quarter-turn matrix $R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$, $p_R(t)=t^2+1$. Its roots are $i,-i$, where $i^2=-1$, and their sum is zero, matching $\operatorname{tr}R=0$. A real matrix’s nonreal roots occur in conjugate pairs, whose imaginary parts cancel in the total. Summing only the real roots would omit part of the required list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对旋转 $90^\circ$ 的矩阵 $R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$，有 $p_R(t)=t^2+1$。根为 $i,-i$，其中 $i^2=-1$，两者总和为零，与 $\operatorname{tr}R=0$ 相等。实矩阵的非实根成共轭对出现，虚部在总和中抵消；只加实根会漏掉应该计入的根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, over another [[特征多项式底层域|scalar field]], the root-sum statement requires a field in which this polynomial splits completely into linear factors. The coefficient argument still applies there. The diagonal sum itself remains defined even when the original field does not contain every root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，换成其他[[特征多项式底层域|标量域]]时，求根之和要在这个多项式能完全分解为一次因子的域中进行，前面的系数论证仍然适用。即使原来的域没有包含全部根，对角元之和本身仍然有定义。

> [!tip] A consistency check · 验算总和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A proposed complete eigenvalue list with the wrong sum is certainly wrong. The right sum alone does not certify it: $1,5$ also sum to $6$, but they are not the eigenvalues of the opening matrix. The [[行列式与特征值乘积|determinant gives the product]] as another check. In size two, trace and determinant together determine the characteristic polynomial; in larger sizes, other coefficients are generally needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若声称已经列出全部特征值，却加不出迹，一定有错。但总和正确也不足以证明列表正确：$1,5$ 同样加起来为 $6$，却不是开篇矩阵的特征值。[[行列式与特征值乘积|行列式给出特征值的乘积]]，可作另一项检查。二维时，迹与行列式合起来确定特征多项式；更高维通常还需要其他系数。

> [!question]- One missing eigenvalue · 补一个特征值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A $3\times3$ matrix has trace $5$, and two entries of its eigenvalue list are $2,-1$. What is the third? **Answer:** $5-2-(-1)=4$, with algebraic multiplicities included throughout.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个 $3\times3$ 矩阵的迹为 $5$，特征值列表中已有两项 $2,-1$，第三项是多少？**答案：** $5-2-(-1)=4$；始终按代数重数计数。
