# 固定永续年金现值等于下一期现金流除以贴现率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The present value of a level perpetuity equals next-period cash flow divided by the discount rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子公式
> 若从下一期末开始，每期永久收到固定现金流 $C$，且每期贴现率恒为 $r>0$，则
> $$PV_0=\frac{C}{r}.$$
> 这是普通年金现值在 $n\to\infty$ 时的极限；永续年金没有有限终止时点，因此通常不定义一个有限的“最终终值”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If a constant cash flow $C$ begins at the end of next period and continues forever at a constant per-period discount rate $r>0$, then
> $$PV_0=\frac{C}{r}.$$
> This is the limit of ordinary-annuity present value as $n\to\infty$. A perpetuity has no finite ending date, so it has no ordinary finite terminal future value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分子必须是下一期的现金流。若第一笔今天立即发生，应把今天的 $C$ 单独加上，或把期末永续年金整体提前一期；若现金流会增长，则应改用增长永续年金而不是 $C/r$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numerator must be the next-period cash flow. If the first $C$ occurs immediately, add today's cash flow separately or shift the end-of-period perpetuity one period earlier. If cash flow grows, use a growing perpetuity rather than $C/r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 永续年金每年末支付 8，贴现率 6%。若第一笔一年后收到，今天价值是多少？若第一笔今天收到呢？
>
> **答案：** 一年后开始时为 $8/0.06=133.33$；今天先收到一笔时为 $8+133.33=141.33$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A perpetuity pays 8 at each year-end and the discount rate is 6%. What is its value if the first payment is in one year? What if the first payment is today?
>
> **Answer:** Starting in one year, $8/0.06=133.33$. With an immediate first payment, $8+133.33=141.33$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
