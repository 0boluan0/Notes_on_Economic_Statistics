# 外部效度是研究结论对明确目标人群、情境、时期和干预版本的适用程度

外部效度（external validity）问研究结论能在多大程度上用于我们真正关心的目标情境。在政策评价中，它关心已估计的效果能否预测另一人群、地点、时期或实施版本的效果。必须先说明“推广到谁、哪里、何时、什么政策”，不能只给一项研究贴上“有外部效度”的永久标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
External validity concerns how far a study's conclusions apply to an explicitly defined target setting. In policy evaluation, it asks whether estimated effects predict effects for another population, place, period or intervention version. Specify the intended target rather than assigning a study a permanent label of external validity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内部的因果识别与向外推广是不同问题。随机分配处理可支持研究样本内的比较，但不把自愿参加试点的学校自动变成全国学校的代表；增加同一批学校内的受访人数也不自动补上未覆盖的地区或机构。前者复用[[因果研究设计检查]]，后者还要比较目标总体、处理版本和作用条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Internal identification and generalization are different questions. Random assignment can support a comparison within the study sample without making volunteer schools representative of all schools. Surveying more people in the same sites does not automatically cover missing regions or institutions. Reuse [[因果研究设计检查|the causal-design audit]] for identification, and compare populations, versions and operating conditions for generalization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，设同一辅导项目对甲类学生的真实平均效果为4分，对乙类为1分；暂假定这两个组别内的效果能适用于目标地区，且没有实施或外溢变化。试点中甲类占80%，总体平均效果为 $0.8\times4+0.2\times1=3.4$ 分；目标地区甲类只占20%，对应平均效果为 $0.2\times4+0.8\times1=1.6$ 分。差异可以只来自人群构成，不说明试点识别错了，也不说明项目一到外地就必然变差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the same tutoring intervention has true average effects of four points for group A and one for group B, and assume these group-specific effects transport to the target with no delivery or spillover changes. A pilot with 80% group A averages 3.4 points; a target with 20% group A averages 1.6. Composition alone can generate the difference without invalidating the pilot's identification or implying that interventions necessarily deteriorate elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个加权例不是说按几个可观测特征重加权就能保证推广。它已经额外假定组别内部的效果可转移；未测差异、目标地区缺乏试点中出现的条件，或政策本身改变，都可能使该假定不成立。对更广人群有代表性的研究通常提供更多信息，但不自动适用于所有国家。扩大实施规模时还须另查[[试点扩张的效应外推|组织与系统反馈]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reweighting a few observed characteristics does not guarantee validity: the example additionally assumes transportable effects within groups. Unmeasured differences, missing enabling conditions or a changed intervention can invalidate that assumption. Representativeness for a broader population adds information without guaranteeing applicability to every country. Scaling implementation also requires checking [[试点扩张的效应外推|organizational and system responses]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
