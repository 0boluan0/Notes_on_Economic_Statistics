# 普通年金现值与终值是等额期末现金流的几何级数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ordinary-annuity present and future values are geometric sums of equal end-of-period cash flows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子公式
> 普通年金是在每期期末支付或收到等额现金流 $C$，持续 $n$ 期。以每期有效率 $r$ 计，时点 0 的现值与第 $n$ 期末的终值分别为
> $$PV=C\frac{1-(1+r)^{-n}}{r},\qquad FV_n=C\frac{(1+r)^n-1}{r}.$$
> 两式分别把同一串现金流移到序列的起点和终点。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> An ordinary annuity pays or receives an equal cash flow $C$ at each period-end for $n$ periods. At an effective rate $r$ per period, its value at time 0 and at the end of period $n$ are
> $$PV=C\frac{1-(1+r)^{-n}}{r},\qquad FV_n=C\frac{(1+r)^n-1}{r}.$$
> The two formulas move the same stream to the beginning and end of the sequence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公式成立的前提是支付间隔、利率期间和金额结构一致。若第一笔立即发生，它是期初年金；若支付额增长、频率不一致或中间有递延期，必须重新画时间线而不是机械套普通年金因子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formulas require payment intervals, rate periods, and payment amounts to align. If the first payment occurs immediately, it is an annuity due. If payments grow, frequencies differ, or payments are deferred, redraw the timeline instead of mechanically applying the ordinary-annuity factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 普通年金最后一期付款为什么在终值求和中乘 $(1+r)^0$？
>
> **答案：** 终值评价时点就是第 $n$ 期末，最后一期付款在该时点刚发生，尚未复利；前面的付款才分别复利一至 $n-1$ 期。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is the final payment of an ordinary annuity multiplied by $(1+r)^0$ in the future-value sum?
>
> **Answer:** The valuation date is the end of period $n$, exactly when the last payment occurs, so it has not compounded. Earlier payments compound for one through $n-1$ periods.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
