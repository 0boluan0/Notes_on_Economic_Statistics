# 艾略特波浪是用不同级别的驱动与修正结构描述价格运动的分析框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Elliott wave framework describes price movements through motive and corrective structures at nested degrees.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

艾略特波浪框架把走势分为顺着更大一级方向的驱动结构与逆该方向的修正结构，并允许一个浪再分成更小级别的子浪。这里的“级别”是嵌套层次，不是任意指定的固定天数；同一个价格段在较小级别的方向可以与整体不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Elliott wave framework distinguishes motive structures aligned with the next larger degree from corrective structures against it, with waves subdividing into smaller-degree waves. Degree means a nesting level, not a fixed number of days. A smaller segment can move against the direction of the larger structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程基本图先用 $1,2,3,4,5$ 表示一个五浪驱动阶段，再用 $A,B,C$ 表示随后的基本修正阶段。上行图中 $1,3,5$ 向上而 $2,4$ 回调；修正阶段的 $A,C$ 向下而 $B$ 反弹。翻转方向可以画出下行版本。这个八段图是入门模式，不是所有修正形态都恰好三段的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The introductory course diagram labels a five-wave motive phase $1$ through $5$, followed by a basic $A,B,C$ correction. In its upward version, $1,3,5$ rise and $2,4$ retrace; $A,C$ fall while $B$ rebounds. Directions reverse in a downward version. This eight-segment illustration does not establish that every correction has exactly three segments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

驱动结构包括 [[标准推动浪]] 与对角线结构等学派类型；[[艾略特修正浪]] 说明修正的变化形式。学派规则约束一套标签是否自洽，不限制真实市场“必须这样走”。实际分析还要记录当时的候选计数和失效条件，不能用完整行情重画后宣称当时已唯一识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Motive structures include the [[标准推动浪|standard impulse]] and diagonals. [[艾略特修正浪|Corrective waves]] have several forms. The school's rules constrain a proposed labeling, not what real prices are allowed to do. Record contemporaneous candidate counts and invalidation conditions rather than relabeling a completed path as though it had been uniquely identified beforehand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
