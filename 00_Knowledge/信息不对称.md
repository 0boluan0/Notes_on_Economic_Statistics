# 信息不对称是参与者掌握的交易相关信息存在差异

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asymmetric information means that participants hold different transaction-relevant information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息不对称指一方掌握另一方无法同样获得的、会影响交易判断或收益的信息。例如借款人知道自己项目的风险类型，银行只能看到不充分的财务指标；这不要求借款人知道项目未来的实际结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Information is asymmetric when one participant holds information relevant to a transaction's decisions or payoffs that another cannot equally access. A borrower may know a project's risk type while a bank observes only incomplete financial indicators. The borrower need not know the project's eventual realised outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若双方都只知道同一套衰退概率、没有任何一方拥有额外相关信息，那是共同面对不确定性，不足以构成信息不对称。一方掌握类型信息、另一方掌握市场信息也可能形成双向的信息差，而不必有一个“什么都知道”的人。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both sides know only the same recession probabilities and neither has additional relevant information, they share uncertainty; that alone is not asymmetric information. Informational advantages can also run in both directions: one side may know its type while the other knows market conditions. No participant must know everything.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息差是问题的起点，不是市场必然消失的结论。需要继续说明它怎样经[[逆向选择|类型选择]]或[[道德风险|行动激励]]影响交易。博弈论中“完全信息”和“完美信息”的技术区分，另见[[完全与完美信息]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An informational difference is a starting point, not proof that a market must disappear. Trace how it affects transactions through [[逆向选择|type selection]] or [[道德风险|action incentives]]. For the distinct game-theoretic meanings of complete and perfect information, see [[完全与完美信息|complete versus perfect information]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
