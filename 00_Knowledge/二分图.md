# Bipartite graphs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二分图*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **bipartite graph** is a graph whose vertices can be put into two sides so that every edge goes from one side to the other. No edge may stay within a side. The sides describe a grouping of vertices, not their positions on the page: moving a drawing cannot change whether such a grouping exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**二分图**是这样一张图：可以把顶点分成两侧，使每条边都从一侧连到另一侧，任何边都不留在同一侧内部。两侧描述顶点的分组，不是纸面上的位置；移动图画不会改变这种分组是否存在。

> [!example] Alternating around a square · 沿正方形交替分组
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the only edges be $ab,bc,cd,da$, where $ab$ denotes the undirected edge $\{a,b\}$. Taking $L=\{a,c\}$ and $R=\{b,d\}$ puts every edge across the two sides. The graph is bipartite even if it is drawn as a square rather than in two columns. If we add the diagonal $ac$, this particular split fails, and in fact no other split works: edges $ab$ and $bc$ force $a,c$ onto the same side, while $ac$ demands opposite sides.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设图中恰有边 $ab,bc,cd,da$，其中 $ab$ 表示无向边 $\{a,b\}$。取 $L=\{a,c\}$、$R=\{b,d\}$，每条边都跨越两侧。即使把图画成正方形而非两列，它仍是二分图。若加上对角线 $ac$，这一划分就失败，而且任何其他划分也不行：边 $ab,bc$ 强迫 $a,c$ 同侧，边 $ac$ 却要求它们异侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The two-side condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两侧划分的条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work first with a finite [[图类型的建模边界|simple undirected graph]] $G=(V,E)$: $V$ is the vertex set, and every edge in $E$ joins two distinct vertices, without parallel copies. The graph is bipartite if there exist subsets $L,R\subseteq V$ satisfying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先考虑有限[[图类型的建模边界|简单无向图]] $G=(V,E)$：$V$ 是顶点集，$E$ 中每条边连接两个不同顶点，且没有平行副本。若存在子集 $L,R\subseteq V$ 满足

$$L\cap R=\varnothing,\qquad L\cup R=V,$$

$$\text{each edge has one endpoint in }L\text{ and one in }R,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then $(L,R)$ is a **bipartition** of the graph. The first two conditions put every vertex on exactly one side; the edge condition is the additional restriction. Here either side may be empty. Thus “two sides” does not require two nonempty groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

则称 $(L,R)$ 为图的一种**二分划分**。前两个条件使每个顶点恰好属于一侧；边的条件是额外限制，即每条边恰有一端在 $L$、另一端在 $R$。本卡允许任意一侧为空，因此“两侧”不要求两个非空组。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, both $L$ and $R$ are [[图的独立集|independent sets]]: neither contains both endpoints of an edge. Labeling $L$ by $0$ and $R$ by $1$ gives a [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper coloring]] with at most two colors. Conversely, the two color classes of such a coloring give a bipartition; one class may be empty. The separate [[二着色与奇环|odd-cycle theorem]] determines when such a split exists without guessing all assignments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地，$L,R$ 都是[[图的独立集|独立集]]：任何一侧都不包含一条边的两个端点。给 $L$ 标 $0$、$R$ 标 $1$，就得到使用至多两色的[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法着色]]；反过来，这种着色的两个颜色类构成二分划分，其中一类可以为空。另见[[二着色与奇环|奇环判据]]，它判断这种划分何时存在，无需猜遍所有分组。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A property versus a chosen split
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*存在划分与指定划分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Bipartite” asserts that at least one suitable split exists; it does not specify a unique one. Exchanging $L$ and $R$ preserves every edge condition. If the graph has several [[无向图连通分量|connected components]], the sides within any component can be exchanged independently because no edge joins different components. An isolated vertex may be placed on either side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“是二分图”断言至少存在一种合适划分，并未指定唯一划分。交换 $L,R$ 后，每条边的条件仍成立。若图有多个[[无向图连通分量|连通分量]]，可以单独交换某个分量内的两侧，因为不同分量之间没有边；孤立顶点可以放在任意一侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[二分图配对关系|bipartite matching model]], the sides may instead be fixed by the application: students on one side and projects on the other, with edges representing permitted pairings. This gives more information than the abstract assertion that a split exists. It still does not select any pairings; a matching is an additional choice of edges. The general definition here does not assign a physical role to either side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[二分图配对关系|二分图配对模型]]中，两侧可能由应用预先指定，例如一侧是学生、另一侧是项目，边表示允许的配对。这比抽象地断言“存在划分”多了角色信息，却仍未选择任何配对；匹配是进一步选取边。本卡的一般定义不替左右两侧指定现实角色。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Empty graphs, loops and repeated edges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*空图、自环与重复边*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An edgeless graph is bipartite: take $L=V$ and $R=\varnothing$, since there are no edges that could violate the rule. If $V=\varnothing$, the permitted split is $L=R=\varnothing$. A single isolated vertex is therefore a valid bipartite graph, although it needs only one color. These cases distinguish the existence of two labels from the need to use both.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无边图是二分图：取 $L=V$、$R=\varnothing$，没有边能够违反规则。若 $V=\varnothing$，允许的划分就是 $L=R=\varnothing$。因此单个孤立顶点也是二分图，尽管只需一种颜色。这些情形说明，允许使用两个标签与必须同时使用两个标签不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same cross-edge definition extends to undirected multigraphs. Parallel edges merely repeat the same endpoint restriction, so they do not change bipartiteness. A self-loop cannot cross disjoint sides because both its endpoints are the same vertex; any graph containing one fails the definition. For directed data, applying this definition to the underlying undirected graph means explicitly ignoring edge directions; it is not a claim about the absence of directed cycles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一跨侧定义可以扩展到无向多重图。平行边只是重复相同的端点限制，不改变二分性质。自环的两个端点是同一个顶点，不可能跨越不交的两侧，因此含自环的图不满足定义。对于有向数据，若把该定义用于其底层无向图，就意味着明确忽略边的方向，而不是在判断有没有有向环。
