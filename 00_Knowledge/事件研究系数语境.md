# 事件研究系数依赖基准期、窗口与处理时序

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Event-study coefficients depend on the reference period, event window, and treatment timing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心解释
> 事件研究把日历时间改写为“距离处理还有或已经多少期”。每个系数都是相对于一个被省略基准期、在特定样本和窗口内构造的比较；它不是脱离规格而存在的绝对效应。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An event study rewrites calendar time as periods before or after treatment. Every coefficient is a comparison relative to an omitted reference period and within a particular sample and event window; it is not an absolute effect independent of the specification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对处理时点的 leads/lags 展示动态效应和处理前差异。系数依赖基准期、窗口和样本构成；处理前系数不显著可能只是功效低。置信区间应考虑多个 event-time 系数的同时推断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Leads and lags relative to treatment timing display dynamic effects and pretreatment differences. The coefficients depend on the reference period, event window, and sample composition; insignificant pretreatment coefficients may merely reflect low power. Confidence intervals should account for simultaneous inference across multiple event-time coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见规格省略 $k=-1$，于是 $k=2$ 的系数表示“处理后第二期相对于处理前一期”的差异，再减去对应对照变化。若改省略 $k=-2$，所有图上的相对高度都会重标；若把远端时期合并成 bin，尾端系数成为多个暴露时长的混合；若要求每个 cohort 都有完整窗口，样本也会随 $k$ 改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common specification omits $k=-1$, so the coefficient at $k=2$ measures the treated-versus-comparison contrast in the second post-period relative to the period immediately before treatment. Omitting $k=-2$ instead renormalises every plotted coefficient. Binning distant periods mixes several exposure lengths, while requiring a complete window can make the contributing sample change with $k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 动态图先要回答“谁和谁比”

在所有处理组同一时期受处理、且有稳定未处理组时，这些比较相对直观。若处理时点错开，传统 TWFE leads/lags 会残差化所有事件时间指标；某个 $k$ 的系数可能混入其他 event time 的处理效应，甚至在没有真实预趋势时画出貌似的预趋势。此时不能只凭图形外观解释，需要使用与 cohort-time 效应相容的估计方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When all treated groups adopt at the same time and a stable untreated group exists, these comparisons are relatively transparent. Under staggered timing, conventional TWFE leads and lags residualise all event-time indicators jointly. A coefficient for one $k$ can absorb treatment effects from other event times and may show an apparent pretrend even when none exists. Visual appearance alone is then insufficient; the estimator must respect cohort-time effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 图上至少说明四件事

说明零点代表何时、哪一期被省略、每个 event time 包含哪些 cohort，以及端点是否合并；同时给出逐点区间和对处理前系数的联合区间或检验。图的解释应围绕预先定义的动态 estimand，而不是从最显眼的一根误差棒倒推故事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State what event time zero means, which period is omitted, which cohorts contribute at each event time, and whether endpoints are binned. Show pointwise intervals together with an appropriate joint assessment of pretreatment coefficients. Interpretation should follow a prespecified dynamic estimand rather than a story inferred from the most striking error bar.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 两篇研究都画出 $k=3$ 的系数，但一篇以 $k=-1$ 为基准，另一篇以所有处理前期平均为基准。能否直接比较两个数值？
>
> **答案：** 不能。它们的归一化、可能的样本与权重不同；先要还原各自系数代表的具体比较。

## 来源与核验

- Sun & Abraham (2021), [*Estimating Dynamic Treatment Effects in Event Studies with Heterogeneous Treatment Effects*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05785)：核验传统 TWFE 事件研究在错位处理与异质效应下的污染，以及 interaction-weighted 替代思路。
- Callaway & Sant’Anna (2021), [*Difference-in-Differences with Multiple Time Periods*](https://psantanna.com/files/Callaway_SantAnna_2020.pdf)，第 3 节：核验由 group-time ATT 按暴露时长聚合为动态参数的方式。
