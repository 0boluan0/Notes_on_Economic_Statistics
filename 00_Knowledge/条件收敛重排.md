# Riemann rearrangement theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*条件收敛实数级数的重排定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite sum, changing the order never changes the answer. An infinite series is different because its answer is a limit of running totals. For a **conditionally convergent real series**, Riemann's rearrangement theorem says that we can choose an order whose running totals approach any specified real number. Every original term is still used exactly once; the sequence of partial sums has changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限求和换个顺序，答案不会改变；无穷级数的答案却是累计值的极限。对**条件收敛的实数级数**，黎曼重排定理说明：可以选一种顺序，让累计值趋于任意指定的实数。原来的每一项仍恰好用一次，改变的是部分和数列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[条件收敛|Conditional convergence]] means that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ has a finite sum in its original order, while $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|$ diverges. A rearrangement is an ordering $a_{\sigma(1)},a_{\sigma(2)},\ldots$ in which each positive integer index occurs exactly once. Such an index map $\sigma$ is called a bijection: it neither repeats nor omits an occurrence, even when different occurrences have equal values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[条件收敛]]指 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ 在原顺序下有有限和，但 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|$ 发散。重排是另一种排列 $a_{\sigma(1)},a_{\sigma(2)},\ldots$，其中每个正整数索引恰好出现一次。索引映射 $\sigma$ 称为双射：原来的每一次出现都不重不漏，即使不同位置的数值相等，也要分别保留。

> [!important] Any prescribed real target · 任意指定的实数目标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a series of finite real terms is conditionally convergent, then for every $L\in\mathbb R$ there is a bijective rearrangement such that
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若有限实数项级数条件收敛，则对每个 $L\in\mathbb R$，都存在不重不漏的重排，使
>
> $$\lim_{N\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{N}a_{\sigma(k)}=L.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is an existence statement about a suitable order, not a statement that every rearrangement changes the sum. The identity order retains the original sum. The theorem here concerns real terms and real targets; it does not assert that arbitrary complex targets are attainable for complex series.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它断言存在合适的顺序，不是说每一种重排都会改变和。保持原顺序当然仍得到原和。本卡处理实数项及实数目标，并未断言复数级数能够重排到任意复数。

## Why there are enough positive and negative terms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么正负两边都还有足够多的量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record the positive and negative contributions as nonnegative magnitudes: $a_n^+=\max(a_n,0)$ and $a_n^-=\max(-a_n,0)$. Thus $a_n=a_n^+-a_n^-$ and $|a_n|=a_n^++a_n^-$. For a finite cutoff $N$, write $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$, $T_N=\sum_{n=1}^N|a_n|$, $P_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n^+$ and $Q_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n^-$. Finite algebra gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把每项分为非负的正部与负部幅度：$a_n^+=\max(a_n,0)$、$a_n^-=\max(-a_n,0)$。于是 $a_n=a_n^+-a_n^-$，而 $|a_n|=a_n^++a_n^-$。有限截断点 $N$ 处，记 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$、$T_N=\sum_{n=1}^N|a_n|$、$P_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n^+$、$Q_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n^-$。有限代数给出

$$P_N=\frac{T_N+S_N}{2},\qquad Q_N=\frac{T_N-S_N}{2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $S_N$ converges and is therefore bounded. Meanwhile $T_N$ increases without bound: it is the divergent partial-sum sequence of a nonnegative series, as explained in [[级数直接比较判别|the monotone-tail argument]]. Hence both $P_N$ and $Q_N$ tend to $+\infty$. The positive terms supply unlimited upward movement; the negative terms supply unlimited downward movement. Removing any finite number of used terms leaves that property intact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $S_N$ 收敛，所以有界；$T_N$ 则无界增大，因为它是发散非负级数的部分和，这一点可沿用[[级数直接比较判别|非负尾和的单调论证]]。所以 $P_N,Q_N$ 都趋于 $+\infty$。正项可以不断向上推动，负项可以不断向下推动；每次去掉有限多个已经用过的项，剩余两边仍有这个性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
List the strictly positive terms in their original order, and do the same for the strictly negative terms. Keep a separate list of zero terms. There are infinitely many terms in each nonzero list. Moreover, their magnitudes tend to zero, because convergence of the original series implies $a_n\to0$ by the [[级数通项判别|nth-term condition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把严格正项按原顺序排成一列，严格负项也按原顺序排成一列，零项另列。正负两列都必须有无穷多项；并且各自幅度都趋零，因为原级数收敛，由[[级数通项判别]]可知 $a_n\to0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Cross the target, then come back
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*越过目标，再折回来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with running total zero. While the total is at most $L$, take the next unused positive term until it is above $L$. Then take unused negative terms in order until it is strictly below $L$. Repeat these upward and downward runs. If zero already exceeds a negative target at the very start, the first upward run is empty; every subsequent run crosses from the opposite side and uses at least one term. Each run finishes after finitely many terms because the remaining positive and negative totals are unbounded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从累计值零开始。如果累计值不超过 $L$，就依次取尚未使用的正项，直到超过 $L$；然后依次取负项，直到严格低于 $L$，不断重复向上、向下的过程。若开头的零已经大于一个负目标，第一次向上阶段可以为空；之后每一段都从另一侧出发，至少使用一项。由于剩余正负总量分别无界，每段都能在有限步内结束。

> [!example] Steering the alternating harmonic series toward one · 把交错调和级数引向一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start from $1-1/2+1/3-1/4+\cdots$, whose original convergence and divergent magnitude sum are established in [[条件收敛|conditional convergence]]. For target $L=1$, the first upward run takes $1$ and $1/3$: equality after the first term is not yet a crossing. The next runs begin
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $1-1/2+1/3-1/4+\cdots$ 出发；其原顺序收敛、幅度之和发散，见[[条件收敛]]。目标取 $L=1$，第一次向上要取 $1$、$1/3$ 两项，因为只取第一项时恰好等于目标，还没有越过。接下来几段为
>
> $$1+\frac13=\frac43>1,\qquad
> \frac43-\frac12=\frac56<1,$$
> $$\frac56+\frac15=\frac{31}{30}>1,\qquad
> \frac{31}{30}-\frac14=\frac{47}{60}<1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The original indices used so far are $1,3,2,5,4$. Continue with the next unused positives and negatives. These first few totals illustrate the construction; the following argument proves its infinite limit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 目前使用的原索引是 $1,3,2,5,4$，接着继续取两列各自尚未使用的下一项。这几个累计值只是展示构造；无穷极限还需要下面的证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every late partial sum approaches the target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么不是只有越界时刻才靠近目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an upward crossing after the initial stage, the total was at most $L$ just before adding the last positive term $p$. The overshoot is therefore at most $p$. At a downward crossing, the undershoot is at most the magnitude $q$ of the last negative term. Because each list is used in its original order and both are used infinitely often, these crossing magnitudes tend to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

初始阶段过后，每次向上越界之前，累计值还不超过 $L$；最后加上的正项若为 $p$，超过目标的距离就至多为 $p$。向下越界同理，低于目标的距离至多为最后一个负项的幅度 $q$。两列都按原顺序推进，又都被无穷多次使用，因此这些越界项的幅度趋零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix any tolerance $\varepsilon>0$. After finitely many runs, all crossing magnitudes are below $\varepsilon$. Each intermediate total in an upward run increases from the preceding lower crossing to the next upper crossing; each downward run decreases between its two crossings. Both endpoints lie in $[L-\varepsilon,L+\varepsilon]$, so every intermediate total does too. This controls the entire sequence of partial sums, proving convergence to $L$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取容差 $\varepsilon>0$。经过有限多段以后，所有越界项幅度都小于 $\varepsilon$。向上阶段的中间累计值，从上一次下方越界点单调走向下一次上方越界点；向下阶段则反向单调走动。两个端点都在 $[L-\varepsilon,L+\varepsilon]$ 内，中间每个累计值也都在其中。这控制了完整的部分和数列，才证明它趋于 $L$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No positive or negative term is omitted: infinitely many runs consume at least one term from each list, always in order, so every fixed entry is eventually reached. Insert the next unused zero, if one remains, after each completed upward-and-downward cycle; this eventually includes every zero and changes no total. The resulting index map is a bijection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正负项都不会遗漏：无穷多段各自至少消耗一项，并始终按各列顺序取，所以任何固定位置最终都会被取到。每完成一轮向上再向下后，若还有零项，就插入下一个尚未使用的零；这样所有零项最终也都出现，而累计值不变。得到的索引映射确实是双射。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite commutativity remains valid. Even swapping finitely many positions leaves the infinite sum unchanged, because sufficiently late partial sums have included all affected terms. The theorem exploits infinitely many ordering decisions. [[绝对收敛级数重排|Absolute convergence]] supplies the stronger guarantee that every bijective rearrangement preserves the sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限交换律仍然成立。即使只交换有限多个位置，无穷和也不变，因为足够晚的部分和已经包含全部受影响的项。本定理依靠的是无穷多次顺序选择；[[绝对收敛级数重排]]则给出更强保证：任意不重不漏的重排都保持原和。
