# 条件收敛的实数级数可以通过重排收敛到任意指定的实数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A conditionally convergent real series can be rearranged to converge to any prescribed real number*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实数级数 $\sum a_n$ 条件收敛。对任意 $L\in\mathbb R$，都存在每项恰好出现一次的重排，使新级数的和为 $L$。这不是说每种重排都会改变和，而是说原和不能仅由“有哪些项”决定，顺序也重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a conditionally convergent real series and any real target $L$, some rearrangement using every term exactly once converges to $L$. Not every rearrangement changes the sum; rather, the list of terms without its order no longer determines the sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把正项按原顺序列出，把负项也按原顺序列出。两边的总量分别为 $+\infty$ 和 $-\infty$：否则原级数收敛会迫使另一边也有限，从而得到绝对收敛，与条件收敛矛盾。于是可以先连续取正项，直到部分和超过 $L$；再取负项，直到低于 $L$，如此反复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
List positive and negative terms separately, retaining their internal orders. Their totals must be positive and negative infinity: if one were finite, convergence of the original series would make the other finite too, contradicting conditional convergence. Add positive terms until the partial sum exceeds $L$, then negative terms until it falls below $L$, and repeat.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每次越过 $L$ 的距离至多是最后加入那一项的绝对值，而由 [[级数通项判别]]，这些项趋于零。每一轮内部的部分和又单调地夹在两次越界附近，因此全部部分和都趋于 $L$；两列都一直向后取，保证没有漏项。若有零项，可依次插入，不改变任何部分和值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each overshoot is no larger than the final added term, whose magnitude tends to zero by the [[级数通项判别|necessary term condition]]. Within a run, partial sums move monotonically between successive crossing levels, so all partial sums approach $L$. Both lists are exhausted in order; zero terms, if present, can be inserted successively without changing any value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交错调和级数就是可实行此构造的例子。有限交换律本身没有失效；改变的是无限部分和所走的序列。若想不受这种顺序影响，需要 [[绝对收敛级数重排]] 的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The alternating harmonic series allows this construction. Finite commutativity remains valid; what changes is the sequence of infinite partial sums. The order-independent guarantee is supplied by [[绝对收敛级数重排|absolute convergence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
