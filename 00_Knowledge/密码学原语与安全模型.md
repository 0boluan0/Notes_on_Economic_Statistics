# 密码学原语与安全模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cryptographic Primitives and Security Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 先明确资产、攻击者和安全目标，再选择提供机密性、完整性或认证的成熟原语与系统控制。
> **具体锚点：** hash 能检测内容变化，却不阻止攻击者替换内容和 hash；需要密钥认证时应使用 MAC 或数字签名。
> **核心难点：** 原语名称相似但安全目标不同，正确算法也会因密钥管理、nonce、权限或恢复流程错误而失效。
> **为什么重要：** 安全不是“加密一下”，而是威胁模型、成熟协议、最小权限和可恢复性的组合。
> **继续：** 写清资产与攻击能力，再按目标选择原语；不要自行拼装密码协议。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Identify assets, attackers, and security goals before selecting mature primitives and system controls for confidentiality, integrity, or authentication.
> **Concrete anchor:** A hash detects a content change but does not stop an attacker from replacing both content and hash; keyed authentication requires a MAC or digital signature.
> **Central difficulty:** Similar-sounding primitives serve different goals, and a correct algorithm can still fail through bad key management, nonces, permissions, or recovery procedures.
> **Why it matters:** Security is not “apply encryption”; it combines a threat model, mature protocols, least privilege, and recoverability.
> **Continue with:** State the asset and attacker capability, select a primitive by goal, and never invent a cryptographic protocol.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 密码学原语
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cryptographic Primitives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

hash 做完整性/指纹，MAC 用共享密钥认证，数字签名用私钥签/公钥验，加密提供机密性；密码存储用带盐慢 KDF。不要自创加密协议。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hash provides a fingerprint for integrity, a MAC authenticates with a shared key, a digital signature signs with a private key and verifies with a public key, and encryption provides confidentiality. Password storage uses a salted, deliberately slow KDF. Do not design a custom cryptographic protocol.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

安全属性不能从原语名字直接继承到系统：加密若没有认证可能被篡改，签名若不验证身份绑定可能信错公钥，密码若用快速 hash 即使加盐也容易离线猜测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Security properties do not transfer automatically from a primitive's name to a complete system. Encryption without authentication may be malleable, a signature can trust the wrong public key if identity binding is not verified, and a password protected by a fast hash remains vulnerable to offline guessing even with a salt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 安全模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Security Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别资产、攻击者能力、入口和影响；最小权限、更新、备份和多因素认证常比复杂机制更有效。验证恢复能力，备份才算存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify assets, attacker capabilities, entry points, and impact. Least privilege, timely updates, backups, and multifactor authentication often outperform elaborate mechanisms. A backup exists only after recovery has been tested.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

威胁模型必须说明信任边界：谁能读磁盘、谁能调用服务、网络是否敌对、管理员或依赖是否可能被攻破。没有边界，“安全”无法被检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A threat model must state trust boundaries: who can read storage, who can call the service, whether the network is hostile, and whether an administrator or dependency can be compromised. Without boundaries, “secure” is not a testable claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：文件下载完整性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Integrity of a Downloaded File*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

网站同时提供文件和普通 SHA-256 值，只能检测传输或存储中的偶然损坏；若攻击者能改网页，也能替换两者。要验证发布者身份，应从已建立信任的渠道获得签名公钥，并验证文件签名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A website that provides both a file and an ordinary SHA-256 value can detect accidental corruption in transport or storage. An attacker able to alter the page can replace both. To authenticate the publisher, obtain the signing public key through an established trust channel and verify the file's signature.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 使用 hash 却声称保密：hash 不可逆不等于隐藏低熵输入，且原文仍可能直接可见；重新明确安全目标。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A hash is claimed to provide secrecy: one-way behavior does not hide low-entropy inputs, and the plaintext may remain directly visible; restate the security goal.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 加密数据仍被篡改：检查方案是否提供认证、nonce 是否唯一，以及验证是否在使用明文前完成。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Encrypted data is still altered: check whether the scheme authenticates, whether nonces are unique, and whether verification occurs before plaintext is used.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 有备份却无法恢复：把恢复演练、密钥可用性、版本和恢复时间纳入同一验证流程。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Backups exist but recovery fails: include restoration drills, key availability, versions, and recovery time in one validation process.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-Check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### hash 与加密的目的差别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How do the purposes of hashing and encryption differ?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> hash 给不可逆指纹/完整性基础，加密用密钥使数据可授权恢复并保护机密。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A hash supplies a one-way fingerprint and an integrity building block; encryption uses a key to protect confidentiality while allowing authorized recovery.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么选择密码学原语之前要先写威胁模型？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why define a threat model before choosing a cryptographic primitive?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 同一种数据可能需要机密性、完整性、来源认证或抗重放中的不同组合；不知道攻击者能力和保护目标，就无法判断该用 hash、MAC、签名还是加密。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The same data may require different combinations of confidentiality, integrity, source authentication, and replay resistance. Without knowing the adversary's capabilities and the protection goal, you cannot decide whether hashing, a MAC, a signature, or encryption is appropriate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 普通 hash 为什么不能认证攻击者可修改的文件？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can an ordinary hash not authenticate a file that an attacker can modify?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 攻击者可同时计算并替换新 hash；认证需要攻击者没有的密钥或可信签名验证链。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The attacker can compute and replace the new hash as well; authentication requires a key unavailable to the attacker or a trusted signature-verification chain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
