# 公共组织实施能力是具体公共机构落实其所承担政策任务的能力

公共组织实施能力指一个公共机构把其职责范围内的政策或项目任务实际完成的能力。这里采用 Kim 等人的 institutional capacity 口径：分析对象包括承担实施任务的部门、机构或国有企业，以及约束该组织的规则与流程；不是把整个国家的所有制度归成一个属性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public-institution implementation capacity is an agency's ability to carry out the policies or projects assigned to it. Following Kim and colleagues, the unit includes the implementing ministry, department, agency or state-owned enterprise together with its governing rules and processes, rather than all institutions of a country treated as one attribute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断必须相对于具体任务。能够按月准确发放工资，不自动表示同一机构也能设计复杂采购合同、协调多部门施工或核验服务质量；同一国家中的机构也可能表现悬殊。这是从[[国家能力|国家层面]]下沉到可检查的组织与职能，并非否认总体比较的用途。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capacity is task-relative. Accurate monthly payroll does not establish an agency's ability to design complex procurement, coordinate construction or verify service quality. Agencies within one country may differ substantially. This moves from [[国家能力|state-level capacity]] to inspectable organizations and functions without denying the usefulness of aggregate comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能把投入、完成率与效果混成能力定义。资金和人员是可能的支撑；交付记录提供机构是否履行任务的证据；居民收入或健康变化还受服务使用、其他条件和政策设计影响。区分[[项目投入产出与结果]]，有助于把“规定要做什么”与“实际做到了什么”的差距说清楚，而不是仅凭结果好坏给机构贴标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inputs, completion and effects are not interchangeable measures. Funds and staff may support capability; delivery records provide evidence of mandate fulfilment; income or health also depends on use, context and policy design. Distinguishing [[项目投入产出与结果|inputs, outputs and outcomes]] makes the mandate–implementation gap inspectable instead of labelling an agency from outcomes alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术上有资源、会办事，也不保证实施符合公共利益。人员、资金、信息与管理还受到问责、独立性和透明度安排的影响；其中“独立”是防止不当利益俘获，不是免于公众监督。目标本身是否合适，与执行者是否偏离任务仍要分别检查。具体诊断步骤见[[实施能力诊断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Resources and technical competence do not guarantee implementation in the public interest. Personnel, finance, information and management are also shaped by accountability, independence and transparency. Independence here means insulation from undue capture, not exemption from public scrutiny. Assess the appropriateness of the objective separately from agents' departure from their mandate; see [[实施能力诊断|implementation-capacity diagnosis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Kim、Kumar、Ramalho、Russell（2026），[Institutional Capacity for Policy Implementation: An Analytical Framework](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099635001072670597/pdf/IDU-3f31d845-5873-45f2-8870-25d1793e57cd.pdf#page=4)，World Bank Policy Research Working Paper11279，2026年1月版。印刷2–4页（PDF4–6）支持组织定义、任务口径与国内机构差异；印刷13–17页（PDF15–19）支持治理激励及独立性边界；印刷25–26页（PDF27–28）明确实施诊断范围及不同职能的要求。工资、采购与施工对比为教学构造，不是该文的实证估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The January 2026 working paper supplies the organizational unit, mandate-relative definition, within-country variation and governance conditions. Its concluding sections delimit implementation diagnosis and variation across functions. The payroll–procurement–construction comparison is illustrative, not an empirical estimate.
