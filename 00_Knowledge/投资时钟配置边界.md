# 投资时钟的阶段偏好不保证顺序轮动或可实现超额收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investment-clock preferences guarantee neither sequential rotation nor realizable excess returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[美林投资时钟]]是条件性的配置框架。阶段可能延长、倒退或跳跃，政策、供给冲击和全球联动也会改变资产反应；即使[[投资时钟阶段识别|阶段判断]]正确，当前价格可能已经反映它，方向判断仍不等于可交易优势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[美林投资时钟|investment clock]] is conditional. Phases may persist, reverse, or skip, while policy, supply shocks, and global linkages alter asset responses. Even a correct [[投资时钟阶段识别|phase diagnosis]] may already be priced in and need not provide a trading advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可用性应以固定规则的实时样本外记录检验，并扣除换仓成本、税费和币种影响；还要明确“债券”的久期与信用、“商品”的投资载体、“现金”的计息和实际购买力。不能用事后最优资产替换当时规则，或把不确定阶段删掉只留下成功轮动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluate a fixed rule with real-time out-of-sample records and account for trading costs, taxes, and currency effects. Define bond duration and credit, commodity instruments, and cash interest and purchasing power. Do not replace real-time choices with ex-post winners or discard uncertain phases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如时钟偏好债券，但所买的是高信用风险债券，衰退引起的违约溢价上升可能超过无风险利率下降的利好。该结果不与“其他条件不变时固定现金流债价随收益率反向”矛盾：信用现金流与利差也在改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a bond preference may fail for high-credit-risk debt if recession-induced credit deterioration outweighs lower risk-free rates. This does not contradict the fixed-cash-flow price–yield relation: expected payments and credit spreads are also changing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
