# Joint and individual significance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*联合显著与逐项显著*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A joint test asks whether a **whole set of restrictions** can hold together. An individual test asks about one restriction. In a regression, “the two coefficients are jointly significant” means that the test rejected their stated joint null; it does not award a significance label to each coefficient. To see why, we must keep both the estimates and the way their sampling errors move together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

联合检验问的是：**一整组限制能否同时成立**。逐项检验每次问一个限制。回归里说“两个系数联合显著”，是说检验拒绝了所声明的联合零假设，并不是给每个系数分别贴上显著标签。要理解差别，必须同时看估计值，以及它们的抽样误差怎样共同变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For coefficients $\beta_1,\beta_2$, the [[零假设与备择假设|joint null]] $H_0:\beta_1=\beta_2=0$ asserts **both** equalities. Its alternative is that **at least one** equality fails. Even a valid rejection is a statistical decision with a possible false-rejection error, not a proof that either particular coefficient is nonzero. [[回归t检验|An individual coefficient test]] uses its own standard error; a [[Wald联合检验|joint Wald test]] also uses the cross-covariances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对系数 $\beta_1,\beta_2$，[[零假设与备择假设|联合零假设]] $H_0:\beta_1=\beta_2=0$ 声明**两个**等式都成立；备择是**至少一个**不成立。即使校准有效，拒绝也是可能犯第一类错误的统计判断，不是某个特定系数非零的证明。[[回归t检验|单系数检验]]使用各自标准误；[[Wald联合检验|联合 Wald 检验]]还要使用交叉协方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two weak coordinate signals can form one precise contrast
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*各自信号不强，也可能组成一个很精确的对比*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a transparent teaching model: the estimator vector is [[联合Gaussian|jointly normal]], $\hat\beta\sim N_2(\beta,V)$, with **known, fixed, [[正定矩阵|positive definite]]** covariance $V$: every nonzero coefficient combination has positive variance. This makes the following normal and chi-square reference laws exact. Suppose the observed estimates and covariance are
$$\hat\beta=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad V=\begin{pmatrix}1&-0.9\\-0.9&1\end{pmatrix}.$$
The diagonal entries are variances, so both [[标准误含义|standard errors]] are $1$. Each zero-null statistic is therefore $1/1=1$, below the two-sided 5% normal critical value $1.96$: neither individual null is rejected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用一个透明的教学模型：估计向量[[联合Gaussian|联合正态]]，$\hat\beta\sim N_2(\beta,V)$，协方差 $V$ **已知、固定且[[正定矩阵|正定]]**，即每个非零系数组合都有正方差，所以这里的正态和卡方参考分布是精确的。观察值与协方差如上。对角元是方差，因此两个[[标准误含义|标准误]]都是 $1$，各自的零假设统计量均为 $1/1=1$，小于双侧 5% 正态临界值 $1.96$，逐项都不拒绝。

> [!example] The sum is measured precisely · 和却估得很精确
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Negative covariance says that the two **estimation errors** tend to offset. For their sum, the [[线性组合推断|complete covariance calculation]] gives
> $$\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_1+\hat\beta_2)=1+1+2(-0.9)=0.2.$$
> The observed sum is $2$, or $2/\sqrt{0.2}\approx4.47$ standard errors above its zero-null value. The joint statistic reaches the same squared distance in this example:
> $$V^{-1}=\frac1{0.19}\begin{pmatrix}1&0.9\\0.9&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad W=\hat\beta^TV^{-1}\hat\beta=20.$$
> Under the joint null, the [[Gaussian马氏平方律|Gaussian squared-distance law]] gives $W\sim\chi^2_2$. Its 5% critical value is about $5.99$, so the joint test rejects; its upper-tail p-value is $e^{-20/2}\approx0.0000454$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 负协方差说明两个**估计误差**倾向于相互抵消。由[[线性组合推断|完整协方差计算]]，它们之和的方差为 $1+1+2(-0.9)=0.2$。观察到的和为 $2$，离零假设值有 $2/\sqrt{0.2}\approx4.47$ 个标准误。本例的联合统计量给出同一个标准化距离的平方 $W=20$。联合零假设下，[[Gaussian马氏平方律|Gaussian 平方距离律]]给出自由度二的卡方分布，其 5% 临界值约为 $5.99$，所以联合拒绝；上尾 p 值为 $e^{-10}\approx0.0000454$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the covariance concerns repeated **coefficient estimates**, not the correlation between the raw regressors. The covariance matrix has eigenvalues $0.1$ and $1.9$, so it is positive definite. If its off-diagonal entries were $+0.9$ instead, the same observed estimates would still give both individual statistics equal to $1$, but $W=20/19\approx1.053$: no joint rejection at 5%. The list of individual results has lost the covariance information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的协方差针对反复抽样的**系数估计值**，不是原始解释变量之间的相关系数。矩阵特征值为 $0.1$ 与 $1.9$，所以正定。若把非对角元改为 $+0.9$，同样的估计值仍让两个逐项统计量都等于 $1$，联合统计量却变成 $20/19\approx1.053$，在 5% 水平不拒绝。只列逐项结果，会丢掉协方差信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Neither decision mechanically determines the other
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两种决定不能机械地互相推导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the same known-normal setup, now take $V=I_2$, meaning variances one and covariance zero. With observed $\hat\beta=(3,0)^T$, $W=9>5.99$: the joint null is rejected, while the second individual statistic is zero. This is a direct counterexample to “joint rejection means every coefficient is significant.” Conversely, $\hat\beta=(2.1,0)^T$ rejects the first individual null because $2.1>1.96$, yet $W=4.41<5.99$ does not reject the joint null. The tests use different rejection regions and allocate their error probability differently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍在协方差已知的正态设定中，改用 $V=I_2$，即方差均为一、协方差为零。观察值若为 $(3,0)^T$，则 $W=9>5.99$，联合拒绝，但第二项的单独统计量为零。这直接否定了“联合拒绝意味着每项都显著”。反过来，若为 $(2.1,0)^T$，第一项因 $2.1>1.96$ 而被拒绝，联合统计量却只有 $4.41<5.99$，不拒绝。两种检验使用不同的拒绝区域，分配错误概率的方式不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Testing two individual nulls at 5% each is also not automatically a 5% test of “at least one rejection.” When their null statistics are independent and each rejection probability is exactly 0.05, that probability is $1-0.95^2=0.0975$. The [[并事件上界|union bound]] explains why allocating at most $0.025$ to each of two prespecified tests controls any false rejection at at most $0.05$, even without independence. Such an adjustment changes the procedure; it need not reproduce a joint Wald test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个单独零假设各按 5% 检验，也不会自动让“至少拒绝一项”的错误率变成 5%。若零假设统计量相互独立，且各自拒绝概率恰为 $0.05$，这个概率是 $1-0.95^2=0.0975$。[[并事件上界]]说明，给两个预先指定的检验各分配至多 $0.025$，即使不独立，也可把任何错误拒绝的概率控制在至多 $0.05$。这改变了检验程序，也不必复现联合 Wald 检验。

> [!important] State the test and then interpret its scope · 先说明检验，再解释它能说明什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For regression with an estimated covariance, replace the exact teaching model by the conditions of the actual method: [[Wald联合检验|Wald]] is commonly asymptotic, while [[嵌套模型F检验|the classical F law]] has its own Gaussian-model and degrees-of-freedom conditions. Report the restrictions, covariance method, calibration and level together. To locate a meaningful direction, report its estimate and interval; searching for that direction after seeing these data raises [[选择后推断|selection]] issues. Joint rejection alone establishes neither [[显著性解释边界|practical importance]] nor a causal interpretation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 回归协方差从数据估计时，要换回实际方法的条件：[[Wald联合检验]]通常使用渐近校准；[[嵌套模型F检验|经典 F 分布]]另有 Gaussian 模型和自由度条件。应一起报告限制、协方差方法、校准和水平。要定位有实质意义的方向，报告该对比的估计与区间；看完同一数据再寻找方向，会引入[[选择后推断|选择问题]]。联合拒绝本身既不建立[[显著性解释边界|实际重要性]]，也不建立因果解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For several response variables, the corresponding localization question is developed in [[多响应联合显著边界|joint versus response-specific significance]]; the causal boundary is discussed in [[多响应因果边界|causal interpretation in multivariate-response models]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有多个响应变量时，对应的定位问题见[[多响应联合显著边界]]；因果解释的边界另见[[多响应因果边界]]。
