# 有效利率下限是现金替代和金融传导约束使进一步下调政策利率受到限制的水平
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The effective lower bound is the level at which cash substitution and financial-transmission constraints limit further policy-rate reductions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

央行不能只靠不断降短期名义利率来无限放松条件。现金的名义利息通常为零，若带息余额的回报变得很负，持有人会考虑转持现金；另一方面，更负的利率如何传到存款、银行利润与贷款，也影响进一步降息的效果。实际下限依制度、成本、持续时间和金融结构而变，不是统一的零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Short nominal rates cannot be cut indefinitely with unchanged effects. Cash normally pays zero nominal interest, making substitution attractive when account returns become sufficiently negative. Transmission to deposits, bank earnings, and lending also matters. The effective bound depends on institutions, costs, duration, and financial structure; it is not universally zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先只看现金替代：假设持有一年现金的安全保管与支付不便总成本为0.5%，银行存款回报为−0.2%，那么在其他条件相同的简化比较中，存款仍优于现金。若存款回报降至−1%，比较方向就改变。这里的−0.5%仅是这组假设下的私人现金选择临界点，不是现实政策利率的精确下限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Isolate cash substitution. If annual security, storage, and payment inconvenience cost 0.5%, a deposit return of −0.2% remains preferable, other things equal. At −1%, the comparison reverses. The implied −0.5% is this hypothetical depositor's cash threshold, not an estimate of a real policy-rate bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金替代的“物理下限”与银行传导开始变弱甚至反向的经济约束也不必重合。接近下限时，[[量化宽松]]和[[前瞻指引]]可以作用于更长期金融条件；它们各有适用条件，不能把“短率难再降”写成“货币政策完全无效”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash-based physical bound need not coincide with an economic threshold at which bank transmission weakens or reverses. Near the bound, [[量化宽松|QE]] and [[前瞻指引|forward guidance]] can influence longer-term conditions under their own assumptions. Limited short-rate space does not imply all monetary policy is ineffective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
