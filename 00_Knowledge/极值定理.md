# Extreme value theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极值定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous real-valued function on a nonempty compact domain has a largest output and a smallest output, and each is produced by at least one input in that domain. This is the extreme value theorem. Its central promise is **attainment**: the best value is reached, rather than merely approached. [[连续性|Continuity]] prevents jumps in the relevant topology, while [[紧致性|compactness]] is the finite-subcover property that keeps the existence argument from escaping the domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个定义在非空紧致空间上的连续实值函数，一定有最大的输出和最小的输出，而且二者都由定义域内的某个输入实际产生。这就是极值定理。关键承诺是**取得**：最好的值确实能达到，而不只是越靠越近。[[连续性]]排除了相应拓扑意义下的跳变，[[紧致性]]则是有限子覆盖性质，它保证存在性证明不会逃出定义域。

> [!example] An interior minimum and an endpoint maximum · 内部最小值与端点最大值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[-2,1]$, the least output is $0=f(0)$ and the greatest is $4=f(-2)$. The domain is a nonempty closed bounded interval, hence compact by the [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|Heine–Borel theorem]]. The minimum occurs inside it, while the maximum occurs at an endpoint. The existence theorem covers both cases without assuming differentiability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[-2,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，最小输出为 $0=f(0)$，最大输出为 $4=f(-2)$。定义域是非空有界闭区间，由 [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|Heine–Borel 定理]]知其紧致。最小值在内部取得，最大值在端点取得；存在性定理同时覆盖这两种情形，不要求可导。

## Values, points and the exact assumptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极值、极值点与准确条件*

> [!important] Extreme value theorem · 极值定理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $K\ne\varnothing$ be a compact topological space and let $f:K\to\mathbb R$ be continuous, with the usual topology on $\mathbb R$. There exist points $x_{\min},x_{\max}\in K$ such that
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $K\ne\varnothing$ 是紧致拓扑空间，$f:K\to\mathbb R$ 连续，实线采用通常拓扑。存在 $x_{\min},x_{\max}\in K$，使
>
> $$f(x_{\min})\le f(x)\le f(x_{\max})\qquad\text{for every }x\in K.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numbers $f(x_{\min})$ and $f(x_{\max})$ are the minimum and maximum **values**; the inputs producing them are minimizing and maximizing **points**. These are global extrema: the comparison is with every input in $K$, not just nearby inputs. The maximizing point need not be unique; a constant function takes its maximum and minimum at every point of a nonempty domain.

Nonemptiness matters because an empty domain has no input at which to attain anything. Real-valuedness supplies the usual order needed to speak of a largest and smallest output. Compactness is a topological assumption; replacing it by “closed and bounded” is valid for subsets of finite-dimensional Euclidean space, not for arbitrary metric spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数 $f(x_{\min})$ 与 $f(x_{\max})$ 是最小值和最大**值**；产生这些输出的输入分别是最小值点和最大值**点**。这里讨论全局极值，要与 $K$ 中每个输入比较，而不只是与附近点比较。极值点未必唯一：非空定义域上的常值函数在每一点都取得最大值和最小值。

非空条件不可少，否则根本没有可用于取得函数值的输入。实值条件提供了比较最大最小所需的通常顺序。紧致性是一条拓扑条件；对有限维欧氏空间的子集，可以把它替换成“闭且有界”，但不能在任意度量空间里这样替换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the upper bound becomes an attained value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么最紧的上界会成为真正取得的值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Look at the set of outputs $S=f(K)$. It is nonempty because $K$ is nonempty. By [[连续像保持紧致|compactness of continuous images]], $S$ is compact in $\mathbb R$, and therefore closed and bounded. Boundedness says there is a finite ceiling above every output. The [[上确界|supremum]] $M=\sup S$ is the least such upper bound; the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property of the real numbers]] guarantees that this real number exists for every nonempty set of real numbers bounded above.

Existence of $M$ does not yet put it in $S$. To do that, fix any $\varepsilon>0$. The number $M-\varepsilon$ cannot also be an upper bound, because it is smaller than the least upper bound. Thus there is some $s\in S$ with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考察所有输出组成的集合 $S=f(K)$。由于 $K$ 非空，$S$ 也非空。由[[连续像保持紧致]]，$S$ 是实线中的紧集，因而闭且有界。有界说明存在一个有限的“天花板”，所有输出都不会超过它。[[上确界]] $M=\sup S$ 是其中最小的上界；[[实数的确界性质]]保证：非空且有上界的实数集合一定存在这样的实数。

仅仅知道 $M$ 存在，还不能断言 $M\in S$。为此，任取 $\varepsilon>0$。$M-\varepsilon$ 比最小上界还小，不可能仍是上界，所以存在 $s\in S$，满足

$$
M-\varepsilon<s\le M.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every open interval around $M$ therefore meets $S$, so $M$ lies in the [[集合闭包|closure]] of $S$. Because $S$ is [[闭集|closed]], its closure is itself: $M\in S$. Being in the image means that some $x_{\max}\in K$ satisfies $f(x_{\max})=M$. Apply the same argument to the continuous function $-f$ to obtain a maximum of $-f$, which is a minimum of $f$.

The roles are distinct: compactness of the image gives closedness and boundedness; boundedness plus completeness of $\mathbb R$ produces a supremum; closedness retains it among the actual outputs. This is why a finite upper bound alone does not establish a maximum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$M$ 周围每个开区间都与 $S$ 相交，也就是 $M$ 属于 $S$ 的[[集合闭包|闭包]]。而 $S$ 是[[闭集]]，等于自己的闭包，所以 $M\in S$。属于像集就意味着存在 $x_{\max}\in K$ 使 $f(x_{\max})=M$。对连续函数 $-f$ 使用同样论证，得到 $-f$ 的最大值，也就得到 $f$ 的最小值。

各条件分工明确：像集紧致给出闭且有界；有界结合实数完备性给出上确界；闭性把上确界留在真实输出集合内。这也说明，单有一个有限上界为什么不够证明最大值存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Approaching a ceiling is different from reaching it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逼近最高处与真正达到最高处不同*

> [!warning] A supremum need not be a maximum · 上确界未必是最大值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x$ on $(0,1)$, the image is $(0,1)$ and its supremum is $1$. The function has **no maximum**: for each allowed $x$, the allowed input $(x+1)/2$ produces a strictly larger output. Thus $1$ is not an “unattained maximum”; it is an unattained supremum. The function is continuous and bounded, but its domain is not compact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $(0,1)$ 上的 $f(x)=x$，像集是 $(0,1)$，上确界为1。函数**没有最大值**：任给合法输入 $x$，另一个合法输入 $(x+1)/2$ 都产生更大的输出。因此1不是“未取得的最大值”，而是未取得的上确界。函数连续且有界，但定义域不紧致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity is also needed for the general guarantee. On the compact interval $[0,1]$, define $g(x)=x$ for $x<1$ and $g(1)=0$. Its image is $[0,1)$, so it has no maximum; the failure is the jump at $1$. Conversely, the theorem's assumptions are sufficient, not necessary for each individual function to have extrema: a constant function on the noncompact domain $\mathbb R$ still attains both.

The theorem establishes existence, not a method for locating all extrema. Derivative tests and optimization algorithms require their own conditions. In particular, an endpoint maximum such as $f(-2)=4$ in the opening example need not satisfy an interior first-order condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性也是普遍保证的一部分。在紧区间 $[0,1]$ 上，令 $g(x)=x$（$x<1$ 时），而 $g(1)=0$。像集为 $[0,1)$，所以没有最大值；问题出在1处的跳变。反过来，定理条件是充分条件，不是每个具体函数取得极值的必要条件：定义在非紧空间 $\mathbb R$ 上的常值函数仍然同时取得最大值和最小值。

定理保证存在性，并不提供找全极值点的算法。导数判别与优化算法还要满足各自的条件。特别是开头例子中的端点最大值 $f(-2)=4$，不必满足内部点的一阶条件。
