# Almost-everywhere differentiability of BV functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有界变差函数几乎处处可导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A function of [[函数总变差|bounded variation]] has only a finite total amount of up-and-down movement on the interval. Surprisingly, this global restriction guarantees a local slope at almost every input. It does not remove every corner or jump. The phrase **almost everywhere** identifies exactly how much exceptional behaviour the theorem allows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[函数总变差|有界变差函数]]在区间上累计上下变化的总量有限。这个整体限制能保证函数在几乎每个输入处都有局部斜率，但不会消除所有尖角或跳点。**几乎处处**这几个字，正是对允许出现多少例外的精确说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What does “almost every time” mean?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*“几乎每个时点”是什么意思？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the input is an ordinary real number $t$ in a finite interval $[a,b]$, with $a<b$. Length is measured by **Lebesgue measure**, which extends the usual length of intervals. A set has length zero if, for every $\varepsilon>0$, it can be covered by countably many open intervals whose total length is less than $\varepsilon$. A single point, or any countable collection of points, has length zero. A zero-length set need not be empty or countable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的输入是有限区间 $[a,b]$ 内普通的实数 $t$，其中 $a<b$。长度采用**勒贝格测度**，它推广了通常的区间长度。如果对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，都能用至多可数个开区间覆盖一个集合，并让这些区间的总长度小于 $\varepsilon$，就说这个集合的长度为零。单个点、任意可数个点都构成零长度集合；零长度集合不一定为空，也不一定可数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A property holds **almost everywhere**, abbreviated **a.e.**, if its exceptional inputs form a set of length zero. This is a statement about length along the input axis. It is not a numerical claim that a small but positive fraction of inputs fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个性质**几乎处处**成立（缩写为 **a.e.**），是说不满足它的输入构成零长度集合。这是在描述输入轴上的长度，并不是说有一小部分正比例的输入不满足它。

> [!abstract] The BV differentiability theorem · 有界变差的可导性定理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ have finite total variation. Then its [[导数|derivative]], the finite limit of local difference quotients, exists at Lebesgue-almost every $t\in(a,b)$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ 的总变差有限。那么，它的[[导数]]——局部差商的有限极限——在 $(a,b)$ 内勒贝格几乎每个 $t$ 处都存在：
>
> $$f'(t)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(t+h)-f(t)}{h}\in\mathbb R\quad\text{for almost every }t\in(a,b).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two endpoints are excluded when taking this two-sided derivative; excluding two points does not change an a.e. assertion. The theorem does not assume continuity beforehand and does not promise differentiability at every input.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取这种双侧导数时排除两个端点；去掉两个点不改变几乎处处的断言。定理没有预先要求连续，也没有保证每个输入处都可导。

^core

> [!example] A permitted corner · 允许存在的尖角
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(t)=|t|$ on $[-1,1]$, the value decreases from $1$ to $0$ and then increases to $1$, so its total variation is $2$. Its derivative is $-1$ for $t<0$ and $1$ for $t>0$. At zero, the two difference quotients disagree, so the derivative fails to exist. The exceptional set $\{0\}$ has length zero, exactly as the theorem permits.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $f(t)=|t|$，函数先从 $1$ 下降到 $0$，再上升到 $1$，总变差为 $2$。在 $t<0$ 时导数为 $-1$，在 $t>0$ 时为 $1$；零点两侧差商不同，所以没有导数。例外集合 $\{0\}$ 的长度为零，符合定理允许的情况。

## Why a bound on movement yields local slopes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么整体变化量的界能保证局部斜率？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key analytic fact is that a real-valued **nondecreasing** function has a finite derivative almost everywhere. Nondecreasing means $u(y)\ge u(x)$ whenever $y>x$. This is the monotone differentiability theorem; its proof uses measure-theoretic covering arguments. We use it here as the cited foundation and explain how bounded variation reduces to that case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键的分析学结论是：取有限实数值的**单调不减**函数几乎处处存在有限导数。“单调不减”指 $y>x$ 时总有 $u(y)\ge u(x)$。这就是单调函数可导性定理，其证明使用测度论中的覆盖论证。这里以引用的该定理为基础，具体说明怎样把有界变差的情况归结到它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each $x\in[a,b]$, let $V(x)$ be the variation accumulated up to $x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个 $x\in[a,b]$，用 $V(x)$ 表示截至 $x$ 累计的总变差：

$$V(a)=0,\qquad V(x)=\operatorname{TV}_{[a,x]}(f)\quad(x>a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is finite because the variation on a shorter interval cannot exceed the variation on the whole interval. For $x<y$, append $y$ to any partition ending at $x$ and then take the supremum over those partitions. This gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它是有限的，因为较短区间上的变差不会超过整个区间上的变差。对于 $x<y$，在任意以 $x$ 结束的分割后面补上 $y$，再对这些分割取上确界，就得到

$$V(y)-V(x)\ge |f(y)-f(x)|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, both $V$ and $W=V-f$ are nondecreasing: the latter follows because
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$V$ 与 $W=V-f$ 都单调不减。后者是因为

$$W(y)-W(x)=V(y)-V(x)-\bigl(f(y)-f(x)\bigr)\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We have expressed $f=V-W$ as the difference of two real-valued nondecreasing functions. Each has a finite derivative outside a zero-length exceptional set. The union of these two exceptional sets still has length zero; everywhere else, subtracting their difference quotients gives $f'=V'-W'$. That proves the BV conclusion from monotone differentiability. Conversely, the triangle inequality shows that a difference $u-v$ of two real-valued nondecreasing functions has total variation at most $u(b)-u(a)+v(b)-v(a)$, so this decomposition also characterises BV functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这样就把 $f=V-W$ 表示成两个取有限实数值的单调不减函数之差。它们各自在一个零长度例外集之外存在有限导数；两个例外集的并仍为零长度。在其余位置，把两者的差商相减，便有 $f'=V'-W'$。这就从单调函数可导性得到了有界变差的结论。反过来，由三角不等式可知，两个取有限实数值的单调不减函数之差 $u-v$ 的总变差至多为 $u(b)-u(a)+v(b)-v(a)$，所以这种分解也刻画了有界变差函数。

## Differentiability does not give the converse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*可导性不能反推出有界变差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define a real function by $f(0)=0$ and $f(t)=t^2\sin(1/t^2)$ for $t\ne0$, and examine it on $[0,1]$. Away from zero it is differentiable by ordinary differentiation rules. At zero,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义实函数：$f(0)=0$，$t\ne0$ 时 $f(t)=t^2\sin(1/t^2)$，并考察它在 $[0,1]$ 上的表现。离开零点后，它按通常求导法则可导；在零点，

$$\left|\frac{f(h)-f(0)}{h}\right|=|h\sin(1/h^2)|\le |h|\longrightarrow0,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
so $f'(0)=0$. Defining the same formula on both sides of zero makes this an ordinary two-sided derivative. Thus the function is differentiable at every point of $[0,1]$, using its extension at the endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $f'(0)=0$。我们在零点两侧都采用同一个公式，因此这里是普通的双侧导数。这样，函数在 $[0,1]$ 的每一点都可导，端点处采用上述延拓后的导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nevertheless, its total variation on $[0,1]$ is infinite. Put $a_k=\pi/2+k\pi$ and $t_k=a_k^{-1/2}$ for integers $k\ge0$. These points decrease to zero, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然而，它在 $[0,1]$ 上的总变差无限。对整数 $k\ge0$，令 $a_k=\pi/2+k\pi$、$t_k=a_k^{-1/2}$。这些点递减趋于零，并且

$$f(t_k)=\frac{(-1)^k}{a_k},\qquad |f(t_k)-f(t_{k+1})|=\frac1{a_k}+\frac1{a_{k+1}}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A partition containing $0<t_N<\cdots<t_0<1$ therefore has a variation sum at least
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，一个包含 $0<t_N<\cdots<t_0<1$ 的分割，其变差和至少为

$$\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\left(\frac1{a_k}+\frac1{a_{k+1}}\right)
\ge\frac1\pi\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac1k\longrightarrow\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last sum is the divergent harmonic series; for example, each block of indices from $2^{j-1}+1$ to $2^j$ contributes at least $1/2$. The oscillations shrink in height but accumulate too much total movement. This verifies that even **everywhere differentiable** does not imply BV. The theorem also implies that a function defined on $[a,b]$ and nowhere differentiable in $(a,b)$ must have infinite total variation on $[a,b]$. Infinite variation alone does not imply nowhere differentiable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个和是发散的调和级数；例如，从 $2^{j-1}+1$ 到 $2^j$ 的每一组下标，对和的贡献都至少为 $1/2$。振荡的高度虽然缩小了，累计变化量却仍然太多。这个例子证明了：即使**处处可导**，也不一定有界变差。定理还说明：定义在 $[a,b]$ 上、在 $(a,b)$ 内处处不可导的函数，在 $[a,b]$ 上的总变差必定无限；总变差无限本身却不推出处处不可导。

## Time exceptions and random-path exceptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*时间点的例外与随机路径的例外*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a stochastic process, fix an outcome $\omega$. Its [[样本路径|sample path]] $t\mapsto X_t(\omega)$ is now an ordinary function, to which the BV theorem applies. If that path has bounded variation, its exceptional **times** have Lebesgue measure zero. A separate assertion that a path property holds **almost surely** (a.s.) means that the exceptional **outcomes** have probability zero. These are measures on different sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对随机过程，先固定一个结果 $\omega$。它的[[样本路径]] $t\mapsto X_t(\omega)$ 就成了一条普通函数，可以应用有界变差定理。如果这条路径有界变差，那么它的例外**时点**具有零勒贝格测度。另一种说法“路径性质**几乎必然**成立”（a.s.），则表示例外的**随机结果**具有零概率。这两个测度作用在不同的集合上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accordingly, if BV paths occur with probability one, the conclusion is: for almost every outcome, the corresponding path is differentiable at almost every time. The exceptional time set may depend on the outcome. Neither phrase licenses dropping its own qualifier and claiming differentiability for every path or every time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，如果有界变差路径以概率一出现，结论就是：对几乎每个随机结果，相应路径在几乎每个时点可导。例外时点集合可以随结果而变化。不能把任一个“几乎”直接删掉，声称每条路径或每个时点都可导。
