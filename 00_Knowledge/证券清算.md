# 证券清算是在交收前核对交易并确定待履行证券与资金义务的处理过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securities clearing processes and reconciles trades before settlement to establish the securities and cash obligations to be discharged*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券清算（clearing）处理成交后的交易资料：传递、核对，并按安排确认交易，确定谁须向谁交付何种证券、多少数量及多少资金。它回答“应当履行什么”，而[[证券结算]]回答“这些义务怎样通过实际转移得到履行”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securities clearing transmits and reconciles trade records and, where applicable, confirms trades. It establishes who must deliver which securities, quantities, and cash amounts to whom. Clearing identifies obligations; [[证券结算|settlement]] discharges them through transfers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

清算可能包含净额计算，但不以净额为定义条件。若规则要求逐笔交收，清算仍须把每笔义务核对清楚。采用净额时，必须先确定允许抵销的交易、证券、币种、日期、账户和法律边界，不能把看起来相反的交易任意相减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Clearing may include netting, but netting is not required by the definition. Gross settlement still needs reconciled obligations. Netting requires a permitted set of trades, securities, currencies, dates, accounts, and legal arrangements; apparently opposite trades cannot simply be subtracted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，同一允许净额的集合中，甲买入同种股票 100 股、卖出 40 股，均每股 10 元且同日交收，忽略费用，可形成净收 60 股、净付 600 元。若不允许净额，则分别履行收 100 股付 1,000 元、交 40 股收 400 元。两种安排都需要清算；它们都尚未证明交收已经完成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose one eligible netting set contains a purchase of 100 shares and a sale of 40 shares in the same stock, both at 10 per share for the same settlement date, with no fees. Net obligations can be receipt of 60 shares and payment of 600. Without netting, the two trades require separate transfers of 100 shares against 1,000 and 40 shares against 400. Both arrangements involve clearing, neither of which establishes completed settlement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

清算也不必由 CCP 接管合同。若采用中央清算，接纳时点和合同替代另按[[中央清算与多边净额|中央清算的法律与净额边界]]判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Clearing need not involve a CCP taking over the contracts. Where central clearing applies, acceptance and contractual substitution follow the [[中央清算与多边净额|legal and netting boundaries of central clearing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
