# 市场流动性是在指定数量和时限内以较小交易成本成交的能力

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market liquidity is the ability to execute a specified quantity within a specified time at relatively low trading cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场流动性是通过买卖证券等资产完成交易的便利程度，具体表现为在所需数量和时限内，能否以较小[[证券交易成本]]、包括较小价格影响成交。评价必须带数量、时间和成本口径；“这个资产很有流动性”并不是对任何规模交易的无条件承诺。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market liquidity is the ease of transacting in an asset, assessed by whether the required quantity can trade within the relevant time at low [[证券交易成本|trading cost]], including limited price impact. Assessment requires size, timing and cost conventions. Calling an asset liquid is not an unconditional promise for every trade size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以分别观察成交速度、[[买卖价差]]、[[市场深度]]，以及压力下交易条件能否持续。各维度可能给出不同信号：两份订单簿的最佳买卖价均为 99.80／100.00，但卖价 100.00 处分别只有 5 股和 5,000 股；对需要立即买 1,000 股的人，两者并不等价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Useful dimensions include execution speed, [[买卖价差|bid–ask tightness]], [[市场深度|depth]] and whether trading conditions can persist under stress. They may send different signals. Two books can share a 99.80/100.00 best quote while offering only 5 versus 5,000 shares at 100.00. They are not equivalent for an immediate 1,000-share purchase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场流动性不同于融资流动性：前者问“资产是否容易交易”，后者问“主体是否容易取得所需资金”。二者可以互相影响，但不能混用。它也不同于[[货币性与流动性|货币性]]；一种股票容易卖出，并不使它成为按面值兑付的货币，或保证[[资产出售、抵押与无损即时变现|无损即时变现]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market liquidity asks whether an asset is easy to trade; funding liquidity asks whether an entity can obtain needed funding. The two can interact but are distinct. Market liquidity is also not [[货币性与流动性|moneyness]]: an easily traded share is neither money redeemable at par nor a promise of [[资产出售、抵押与无损即时变现|immediate lossless conversion to cash]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
