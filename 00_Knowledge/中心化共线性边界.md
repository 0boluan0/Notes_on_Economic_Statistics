# Centering and multicollinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中心化与共线性的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Centering moves a predictor's zero point. If age is recorded as $x$, using $t=x-30$ makes $t=0$ correspond to age 30 instead of age 0. In a polynomial or interaction model, the product and square columns must be rebuilt from that new variable. This can change their correlations and the coefficients' reference meanings. With the required terms retained, it is another way to express the same fitted function, not additional evidence about that function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中心化移动解释变量的零点。年龄记为 $x$ 时，改用 $t=x-30$，零点就从零岁变成三十岁。多项式或交互模型中的乘积、平方列，也要用这个新变量重新构造。这可以改变列间相关和系数的参照含义；保留所需项后，它只是同一拟合函数的另一种表达，没有增加关于函数的证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rewriting a complete quadratic

Let $c$ be a chosen reference value and set $t=x-c$. The reference may be a meaningful fixed value or the observed mean; the following algebra treats its chosen value as given. Write a quadratic prediction rule as $f(x)=b_0+b_1x+b_2x^2$, where $b_0,b_1,b_2$ are its coefficients. Substituting $x=t+c$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 改写完整二次模型

选一个参照值 $c$，令 $t=x-c$。它可以是有意义的固定值，也可以是观察均值；下面的代数把选定值视为给定。把二次预测规则写成 $f(x)=b_0+b_1x+b_2x^2$，其中 $b_0,b_1,b_2$ 为系数。代入 $x=t+c$，得到

$$
f(x)=a_0+a_1t+a_2t^2,\qquad
a_0=b_0+cb_1+c^2b_2,\quad
a_1=b_1+2cb_2,\quad a_2=b_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new intercept $a_0=f(c)$ is the prediction at the reference, and $a_1=f'(c)$ is the slope there. The [[导数|derivative]] $f'(x)=b_1+2b_2x$ describes how the fitted curve changes with $x$; the lower-order coefficient therefore changes meaning when the origin moves. This is the same [[取值依赖边际效应|value-dependent slope]], expressed in new coordinates.

The conversion is reversible: $b_2=a_2$, $b_1=a_1-2ca_2$ and $b_0=a_0-ca_1+c^2a_2$. Hence the design columns $1,x,x^2$ and $1,t,t^2$ generate the same [[列空间|column space]]—the same set of possible fitted vectors. For the same observations and unweighted, unconstrained [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] criterion, the fitted values and residuals are identical in exact arithmetic. This fitted-vector statement does not require unique coefficients; centering preserves the rank as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新截距 $a_0=f(c)$ 是参照点的预测，$a_1=f'(c)$ 是该处斜率。[[导数]] $f'(x)=b_1+2b_2x$ 描述拟合曲线怎样随 $x$ 改变；零点移动后，低阶系数便改变含义。这仍是同一个[[取值依赖边际效应|随取值变化的斜率]]，只是用了新坐标。

转换可以反过来：$b_2=a_2$、$b_1=a_1-2ca_2$、$b_0=a_0-ca_1+c^2a_2$。因此设计列 $1,x,x^2$ 与 $1,t,t^2$ 张成相同的[[列空间]]，也就是同一组可能的拟合向量。在观察相同、使用未加权无约束[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]时，精确算术下的拟合值与残差相同。这个拟合向量结论不要求系数唯一；中心化也保留了原来的秩。

> [!example] Different coefficients, identical predictions / 系数不同，预测相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=2+3x+x^2$ and $c=2$, the new rule is $f(x)=12+7t+t^2$. At $x=1,2,3$, both give $6,12,20$. The slope coefficient changes from 3 to 7 because it now refers to $x=2$, not $x=0$. The slope at $x=2$ was already $3+2(2)=7$ before centering.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $f(x)=2+3x+x^2$、$c=2$，新表达为 $f(x)=12+7t+t^2$。在 $x=1,2,3$ 处，两种写法都给出 $6,12,20$。斜率系数从 3 变成 7，是因为参照点从 $x=0$ 变为 $x=2$；中心化之前，该处斜率也已经是 $3+2(2)=7$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a diagnostic can improve

Take five observed values $x=1,2,3,4,5$ and use their mean $c=3$. The original columns $x,x^2$ have squared sample correlation $180/187$, giving a scalar [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] of $187/7$ in the design $[1,x,x^2]$. After rebuilding the square, $t=(-2,-1,0,1,2)$ and $t^2=(4,1,0,1,4)$ have zero sample correlation, so their VIFs are 1. The fitted quadratic still spans exactly the same space.

Use the model $y_i=f(x_i)+u_i$, where $y_i$ is the response and $u_i$ its error, and hats denote OLS estimates. Under a full-rank fixed design, zero conditional error mean and covariance $\sigma^2I$ with $0<\sigma^2<\infty$, the errors have common variance $\sigma^2$ and zero cross-observation covariances; $I$ is the identity matrix. Then the variance of the centered slope estimate is $\sigma^2/10$. It equals the variance of the original estimate $\hat b_1+6\hat b_2$ for the slope at $x=3$. Comparing it with $\hat b_1$ alone compares slopes at different points. [[线性组合推断|Uncertainty for the same coefficient combination]] must use the full covariance, including its cross terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么诊断数值可能改善

取五个观察值 $x=1,2,3,4,5$，用均值 $c=3$。原列 $x,x^2$ 的样本相关平方为 $180/187$，所以在设计 $[1,x,x^2]$ 中，单列[[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] 为 $187/7$。重新构造平方后，$t=(-2,-1,0,1,2)$ 与 $t^2=(4,1,0,1,4)$ 的样本相关为零，VIF 都为 1；拟合二次曲线的空间仍完全相同。

采用模型 $y_i=f(x_i)+u_i$，其中 $y_i$ 是响应、$u_i$ 是误差，帽号表示 OLS 估计。若设计固定且满秩、误差条件均值为零、协方差为 $\sigma^2I$ 且 $0<\sigma^2<\infty$，即误差具有共同方差 $\sigma^2$ 且观测间协方差为零，$I$ 是单位矩阵，那么中心化后斜率估计的方差为 $\sigma^2/10$。它等于原参数化中 $\hat b_1+6\hat b_2$ 的方差，两者都估计 $x=3$ 处的斜率。若只与 $\hat b_1$ 比较，就在比较不同位置的斜率。[[线性组合推断|同一系数组合的不确定性]]需要完整协方差，包括交叉项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A more convenient coordinate system can also improve numerical calculations; the [[条件数缩放边界|condition-number comparison]] must state the matrix and scaling. Neither a smaller VIF nor improved floating-point computation creates new statistical information. Centering is not guaranteed to reduce every correlation or condition number, especially across different constructed terms or reference choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更方便的坐标也可能改善数值计算；[[条件数缩放边界|条件数比较]]必须说明矩阵与缩放方式。VIF 变小或浮点计算改善，都不会创造新的统计信息；中心化也不保证降低每个相关系数或条件数，尤其不能对不同构造项与参照选择作统一保证。

## What it cannot do
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它不能做什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simply subtracting constants from two existing nonconstant columns leaves their ordinary [[相关系数|Pearson correlation]] unchanged. The change above came from rebuilding the square: $(x-c)^2=x^2-2cx+c^2$, not from merely subtracting a constant from the old square column. Centering distinct age and experience columns therefore does not create variation that separates them. Exact duplicate columns and an intercept with all category indicators remain rank deficient.

The equivalence also depends on the model terms. The family $b_0+b_2x^2$ forces zero slope at $x=0$. Its equivalent centered form includes the linear term $2cb_2t$; fitting only $a_0+a_2t^2$ generally imposes a new restriction, zero slope at $x=c$. Special cases can coincide, but deletion is not generally a harmless recoding. [[交互层级原则|Interaction models have the same issue]]: since $xD=tD+cD$, shifting $x$ may require the lower-order group term $D$.

Finally, the same penalty or coefficient constraint after a coordinate change need not describe the same fitting problem. An unchanged [[岭正则化|ridge penalty]], which penalizes squared coefficient size, can yield different predictions when applied to newly expressed coefficients. The exact OLS equivalence above does not automatically transfer to that procedure. Keep the function, target and fitting criterion explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两条已有的非恒定列，只减去常数不会改变普通[[相关系数|Pearson相关]]。前面的变化来自重新构造平方：$(x-c)^2=x^2-2cx+c^2$，而不是只给旧平方列减常数。因此，分别中心化年龄和经验，不会创造把两者分开的变异。完全重复列、截距加全套类别指示造成的秩亏也仍然存在。

等价还取决于模型包含哪些项。函数族 $b_0+b_2x^2$ 强制 $x=0$ 处斜率为零；它的等价中心化表达包含线性项 $2cb_2t$。若改拟合只有 $a_0+a_2t^2$ 的模型，通常就施加了新限制：$x=c$ 处斜率为零。特殊情形可以重合，但一般不能把删项当成无害重编码。[[交互层级原则|交互模型也有相同问题]]：由 $xD=tD+cD$，平移 $x$ 后可能需要保留低阶组别项 $D$。

最后，换坐标后沿用字面相同的惩罚或系数约束，不一定仍在拟合同一个问题。[[岭正则化|岭惩罚]]惩罚系数大小的平方；直接对新系数使用字面未变的惩罚，可能得到不同预测；上面 OLS 的精确等价不能自动推广到该程序。应明确保留的函数、目标与拟合准则。
