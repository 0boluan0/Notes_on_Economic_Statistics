# 对多项式或交互项先中心化可改变低阶与高阶列的相关性和系数基准，但不能增加样本中原本缺失的独立信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Centering before constructing polynomial or interaction terms can change correlations among design columns and the coefficient reference point, but it cannot add independent information absent from the sample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它能做什么
> 令 $X_c=X-c$。在同时保留常数项和完整低阶项时，用 $X_c,X_c^2$ 代替 $X,X^2$ 只是对同一列空间重新参数化：拟合值不变，但线性项系数现在描述 $X=c$ 附近的斜率。选择有意义的 $c$ 常能降低构造出来的低阶／高阶相关，并改善系数解释。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_c=X-c$. When an intercept and the full lower-order hierarchy are retained, replacing $X,X^2$ with $X_c,X_c^2$ reparameterizes the same column space. Fitted values are unchanged, while the linear coefficient now describes the slope around $X=c$. A meaningful center often reduces construction-induced correlation between lower- and higher-order columns and improves interpretation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

展开
$$
X_c^2=X^2-2cX+c^2
$$
可见，新三列 $(1,X_c,X_c^2)$ 与旧三列 $(1,X,X^2)$ 互为可逆线性组合，因此模型的拟合曲线没有改变。若删除必要的低阶项，这个等价关系和层级解释就不能直接沿用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $X_c^2=X^2-2cX+c^2$, the new columns $(1,X_c,X_c^2)$ and old columns $(1,X,X^2)$ are invertible linear re-expressions of one another. The fitted curve therefore does not change. Dropping required lower-order terms breaks the straightforward hierarchical equivalence and interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它不能做什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What it cannot do*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中心化不会让两个不同经济变量获得新的独立变异。若 treatment 几乎只出现在一个地区，或年龄与实际经验在样本中几乎同步，各列减去均值仍保留同样的狭窄支持。它也不能修复完全重复列或截距加全套虚拟变量造成的秩亏；这些需要正确编码或新设计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Centering does not give distinct economic variables new independent variation. If treatment occurs almost only in one region, or age and actual experience move almost together in the sample, subtracting means leaves the same narrow support. It also does not repair duplicate columns or the rank deficiency from an intercept plus a complete dummy set; those require correct coding or a new design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此“中心化后 VIF 下降”只说明参数化中的某些列相关改变了。要判断科学问题是否更可识别，还要确认目标边际效应、联合项和实际支持是否改变；在同一完整多项式列空间下，它们并没有获得新的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lower VIF after centering shows that some column correlations in the parameterization changed. To decide whether the scientific question became better identified, inspect the target marginal effect, the joint term, and the observed support. Within the same complete polynomial column space, no new information has been created.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 把年龄减去样本均值后，年龄与实际经验仍几乎一一对应。中心化是否解决了二者的本质共线？
>
> **答案：** 没有。它可能改变与截距或构造项的相关，却没有扩大年龄与经验的联合支持。
