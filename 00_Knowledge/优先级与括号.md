# 运算符优先级决定表达式分组而括号可显式改写分组
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Operator precedence groups expressions while parentheses can explicitly override that grouping*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子语法语义
> 没有括号时，Python 按语法规定的运算符优先级和结合方向确定表达式怎样分组；括号可以把某个子表达式明确成一组。优先级回答“哪些操作数属于哪个运算”，不是副作用发生顺序的完整替代品；求值顺序要另按语言规则判断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Without parentheses, Python uses its defined operator precedence and associativity to determine how an expression is grouped. Parentheses can make a chosen subexpression an explicit group. Precedence answers which operands belong to which operation; it is not a complete substitute for the language's separate evaluation-order rules when side effects matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

```python
2 + 3 * 4       # 14: multiplication groups first
(2 + 3) * 4     # 20: parentheses change the grouping
2 ** 3 ** 2     # 512: exponentiation groups right to left
```

同一优先级的多数二元运算从左到右分组，但幂运算和条件表达式从右到左。比较、成员测试与身份测试共享一个优先级并支持比较链；`a < b < c` 会把 `b` 只求值一次，不能机械改读成 `(a < b) < c`。复杂表达式即使不加括号也能按规则运行，括号仍可用来显式表达意图并减少误读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Most binary operators at one precedence level group left to right, while exponentiation and conditional expressions group right to left. Comparisons, membership tests, and identity tests share one level and support chaining; `a < b < c` evaluates `b` once and must not be mechanically read as `(a < b) < c`. Parentheses can still clarify intent even when the unparenthesized expression is valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> `True or False and False` 为什么为 `True`？怎样写才能让 `or` 这一部分先成组？
>
> **答案：** `and` 的优先级高于 `or`，原式分组为 `True or (False and False)`；写成 `(True or False) and False` 才改变分组。
