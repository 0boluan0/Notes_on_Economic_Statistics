# 事中信念是玩家观察自身类型后、采取行动前，对未知收益参数与其他玩家类型的条件概率分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An interim belief is a player's conditional probability distribution over unknown payoff parameters and other players' types after observing their own type and before taking an action*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> 玩家 $i$ 观察到自身类型 $t_i$ 后，以
> $$
> p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)\in\Delta(\Theta\times T_{-i})
> $$
> 表示对收益参数 $\theta$ 与其他玩家类型 $t_{-i}$ 的判断。条件信息是自身类型，时点位于行动之前。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After observing type $t_i$, player $i$ uses $p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)$ to assess the payoff parameter $\theta$ and the other players' types $t_{-i}$. The conditioning information is the player's own type, and the belief is held before actions are chosen.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件对象与时点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditioning Object and Timing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Theta$ 描述影响收益但尚未确定的物理状态，$T_{-i}$ 描述其他玩家可能拥有的私人信息状态。事中信念把二者放在同一个条件分布中，因此玩家可能同时不确定“真实收益环境是什么”和“别人知道什么”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\Theta$ contains payoff-relevant physical states, while $T_{-i}$ contains the other players' possible private-information states. An interim belief treats both as uncertain, so a player may be unsure both about the payoff environment and about what others know.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“事中”位于观察自身类型之后、行动之前。它不同于尚未观察任何私人信息时的事前分布，也不同于动态博弈中观察行动或历史后形成的信息集后验；两种条件对象的完整区别见[[事中与信息集信念]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Interim” means after the player has learned their own type but before acting. It differs both from an ex-ante distribution formed before private information is observed and from a posterior formed after observing actions or histories in a dynamic game; see [[事中与信息集信念|interim versus information-set beliefs]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信念怎样进入决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How the Belief Enters Decisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定其他玩家的类型条件策略，玩家 $i$ 在类型 $t_i$ 下用自己的 $p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)$ 计算各行动的条件期望收益。若存在[[共同先验]]，这些事中信念可以由同一事前分布条件化得到；若类型[[相关类型|相关]]，分布会随观察到的 $t_i$ 改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given the other players' type-contingent strategies, type $t_i$ evaluates actions using conditional expected payoff under its own $p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)$. With a [[共同先验|common prior]], these interim beliefs may be obtained by conditioning one ex-ante distribution; with [[相关类型|correlated types]], the distribution varies with the observed $t_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
