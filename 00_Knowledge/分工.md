# 分工是把生产任务分配给专门承担不同工作的劳动者或职业的组织方式

分工（division of labour）是把生产活动中的不同任务交给不同劳动者或职业，让各方专门承担其中一部分，再把这些工作连接起来。它既可以发生在同一个工场内部，也可以表现为社会上不同职业之间的分化与协作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Division of labour allocates different productive tasks to different workers or occupations. Each specialises in part of the work, and their contributions are connected. It can occur within a workshop or through the separation and coordination of occupations across society.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 的制针工场是一个直观例子：一人拉丝，一人把金属丝弄直，另一人切断，还有人磨尖、装针头。每个人做的都不是一枚完整的大头针，但他们的工作合在一起，才得到最终产品。这里改变的是“谁做哪一步”；如果只是多招几个人，让每个人仍然从头到尾独立制针，那是增加人数，还没有按工序分工。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith's pin factory provides a concrete example: one worker draws the wire, another straightens it, another cuts it, and others sharpen it or attach the head. No worker performs every operation, but their combined work produces the finished pin. What changes is who performs each step. Hiring more workers who each make a complete pin independently increases the workforce without dividing the operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样的道理也适用于工场之外。农民、织工、运输者各做不同工作，通过交换取得自己需要而没有亲自生产的物品。因此，分工不要求所有人归同一家公司管理，也不要求每个人永远只做一个动作。生产阶段跨越国界时，可能形成更具体的 [[全球价值链]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same idea extends beyond the workshop. Farmers, weavers and carriers perform different jobs and obtain goods they do not produce through exchange. Division of labour therefore requires neither a single employer nor one lifelong operation for every worker. When productive stages cross national borders, the arrangement may form a [[全球价值链|global value chain]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义说明的是工作如何组织。为什么这种组织可能让同样人数多生产，见 [[分工增产机制]]；专业化的产出能否卖出去，见 [[分工的市场限制]]。这两个问题都需要进一步解释，不能仅凭“分工”这个名称就得到答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition describes how work is organised. [[分工增产机制|The productivity mechanisms of division of labour]] explain why the same workforce may produce more; [[分工的市场限制|the market constraint on specialisation]] asks whether the resulting output can be sold. Neither answer follows from the label alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
