# Choosing an integration method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*积分方法选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing an integration method means looking for a rewrite that makes the next step possible. An indefinite integral asks for an [[原函数|antiderivative]]—a function that differentiates to the integrand. The methods therefore reverse familiar derivative rules: substitution uses the chain rule, while integration by parts uses the product rule. The useful question is **“What structure can I exploit here?”**, rather than merely “Which named functions appear?”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择积分方法，就是寻找一种改写，让下一步能够算下去。不定积分要求找一个[[原函数]]：对它求导，得到被积函数。因此，积分技巧常常把熟悉的求导规则倒过来用：换元利用链式法则，分部积分利用乘积法则。有效的问题是：**“这里有什么结构可以利用？”**，而不只是“出现了哪些函数名称？”

> [!example] The same radical suggests different first moves · 相似根式，第一步可以不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\int x\sqrt{x^2+1}\,dx$, the factor $x\,dx$ matches half the derivative of $x^2+1$. The [[换元积分|substitution]] $u=x^2+1$ turns the whole integral into a power integral:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\int x\sqrt{x^2+1}\,dx$ 中，$x\,dx$ 与 $x^2+1$ 的导数正好相差一个常数因子。作[[换元积分]] $u=x^2+1$，整个积分变为幂积分：
>
> $$\int x\sqrt{x^2+1}\,dx
> =\frac12\int u^{1/2}\,du
> =\frac13(x^2+1)^{3/2}+C.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\int\sqrt{x^2+1}\,dx$, the extra $x$ is absent. The same substitution no longer gives that simple power integral. The identity $1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$ instead suggests [[三角代换|trigonometric substitution]] $x=\tan\theta$ with $-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2$, so $\sec\theta>0$. This leads to an integral of $\sec^3\theta$ and then integration by parts. Seeing a radical alone does not determine the best first method.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\int\sqrt{x^2+1}\,dx$ 中，额外的 $x$ 不在了，同一个换元便不能直接得到刚才的幂积分。恒等式 $1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$ 则提示[[三角代换]] $x=\tan\theta$，取 $-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2$ 保证 $\sec\theta>0$，随后得到 $\sec^3\theta$ 的积分，再使用分部积分。只看到根号，并不能决定最合适的第一步。

## A useful search order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 一条可执行但非完备的顺序

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start by identifying the task and its domain. An indefinite integral needs a family of functions, including the arbitrary constant $C$ on each interval. A [[定积分|definite integral]] asks for a net accumulation between bounds. Before simplifying either kind, record denominator zeros and real square-root restrictions. For example, cancelling $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ to $x+1$ does not define the original fraction at $x=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先辨认问题与定义域。不定积分要的是函数族，包括每个区间上的任意常数 $C$；[[定积分]]要的是上下限之间的净累积量。两种问题在化简前都要先记录分母零点与实数平方根的限制。例如，把 $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ 约成 $x+1$，并没有给原分式补上 $x=1$ 处的定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next try simple algebra and a known antiderivative. Expanding a short polynomial, splitting a sum or completing a square may expose the answer. If the expression contains a whole derivative pattern $f(g(x))g'(x)$, use [[换元积分|substitution]]: $u=g(x)$ replaces the inner expression, and $du=g'(x)\,dx$ replaces its accompanying derivative factor. Constant factors can be adjusted, but a missing variable factor cannot simply be discarded. For definite integrals, [[定积分换元|change both bounds with the variable]] and keep their resulting order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着尝试简单代数与已经知道的原函数。展开一个短多项式、拆开和式或配方，可能就能看出答案。若出现完整结构 $f(g(x))g'(x)$，就用[[换元积分]]：$u=g(x)$ 替换里面的表达式，$du=g'(x)\,dx$ 替换与它配套的导数因子。常数倍可以调整，缺失的变量因子不能直接丢掉。定积分还要[[定积分换元|随变量同时转换两个界]]，保留转换后的方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When direct recognition does not settle the problem, inspect the remaining structure. For nonnegative integer [[正弦余弦幂积分|powers of sine and cosine]], odd powers can supply a derivative factor; all-even powers suggest power-reducing identities. For [[正切正割幂积分|tangent and secant powers]], look for an available $\sec^2x\,dx$ or $\sec x\tan x\,dx$ and check the corresponding exponent conditions. For a quadratic radical, complete the square if needed, then consider [[三角代换|trigonometric substitution]] with an appropriate angle range and signs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接识别还不能解决时，再看剩余结构。对于非负整数次的[[正弦余弦幂积分]]，奇次幂可以提供导数因子，全偶次幂提示降幂恒等式。对于[[正切正割幂积分]]，寻找确实存在的 $\sec^2x\,dx$ 或 $\sec x\tan x\,dx$，并检查相应指数条件。二次根式需要时先配方，再考虑[[三角代换]]，选定合适的角度范围与符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a rational function—a quotient of polynomials—[[部分分式积分|partial fractions]] may turn the quotient into simpler pieces. Divide first if the numerator's degree is at least the denominator's. Still try a simpler recognition first: $2x/(x^2+1)$ already has the derivative of its denominator in the numerator, so $u=x^2+1$ gives $\ln(x^2+1)+C$. No decomposition is needed. Here $\ln$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], and its input is positive for every real $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有理函数，也就是两个多项式的商，[[部分分式积分]]可以把它拆成较简单的项；分子次数不小于分母时，先做多项式除法。不过仍应先看是否有更直接的结构：$2x/(x^2+1)$ 的分子已经是分母的导数，令 $u=x^2+1$ 就得到 $\ln(x^2+1)+C$，无需分解。这里 $\ln$ 是[[自然对数]]，其输入对所有实数 $x$ 都为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A product suggests [[分部积分|integration by parts]] when one factor becomes simpler after differentiation and an antiderivative of the other is available. In $\int xe^x\,dx$, differentiating $x$ gives $1$ while integrating $e^x$ leaves $e^x$, so the new integral is simpler. A visible product is not necessary: write $\ln x=(\ln x)\cdot1$ for $x>0$. Repetition can also help; [[循环分部积分|cyclic integration by parts]] works when the returning integral forms a new equation that can actually be solved, not merely the identity $I=I$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果乘积中的一个因子求导后变简单，另一个因子又能求出原函数，就可以尝试[[分部积分]]。在 $\int xe^x\,dx$ 中，$x$ 求导变成 $1$，$e^x$ 积分仍是 $e^x$，新积分因此更简单。也不一定非要看见乘号：在 $x>0$ 时可以写成 $\ln x=(\ln x)\cdot1$。重复使用也可能有效；[[循环分部积分]]要求返回的原积分形成一个真正能解的新等式，而不只是 $I=I$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are prompts for choosing the next move, not exclusive categories or a guaranteed algorithm. A problem may fit several methods. After a rewrite, inspect the new integral again: a partial fraction with a quadratic denominator may call for substitution, and a trigonometric substitution may lead to integration by parts. Prefer a route whose next step you can explain and carry out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些提示用来选择下一步，不是互斥的分类，也不是保证成功的算法。同一个问题可能适用多种方法。每次改写后都要重新看新积分：二次分母的部分分式可能接着需要换元，三角代换后也可能需要分部积分。优先选择能够说明理由、也确实能继续执行的路线。

## Check the answer against the original question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*按原问题验算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an indefinite result $F(x)+C$, differentiate $F$ and compare with the **original** integrand on the stated interval. In the opening example, $(\frac13(x^2+1)^{3/2})'=x\sqrt{x^2+1}$ for every real $x$. Answers that look different can still be correct; [[原函数相差常数|two antiderivatives on one interval differ by a constant]]. A formula from a table or computer algebra system needs the same domain and derivative checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对不定积分答案 $F(x)+C$，求 $F$ 的导数，在声明的区间上与**原被积函数**比较。开头例子中，$(\frac13(x^2+1)^{3/2})'=x\sqrt{x^2+1}$ 对所有实数成立。外观不同的答案也可能都正确；[[原函数相差常数|同一区间上的两个原函数只差常数]]。积分表或计算机代数系统给出的公式，也需要相同的定义域与求导检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a definite integral, check the bounds, the variable and the orientation before using the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|endpoint formula]]. A simple sufficient setting is a continuous integrand on the whole finite interval and an antiderivative there. If the integrand is nonnegative and the lower bound is smaller, a negative answer signals a problem. Infinity or a genuine singularity requires [[反常积分|improper-integral limits]]; a formal antiderivative does not by itself establish convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对定积分，使用 [[Newton-Leibniz公式|原函数端点相减]] 前，要核对上下限、变量与方向。一个简单的充分条件是：被积函数在整个有限区间连续，并在这里有原函数。若函数非负、下限小于上限，算出负数就提示出了问题。无穷远或真正的奇点需要检查[[反常积分]]的极限；形式上写出原函数，本身不保证收敛。

> [!warning] A stalled method is not a nonexistence proof · 方法卡住不等于积分不存在
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A continuous function can have an antiderivative without an elementary formula; see [[无初等原函数的积分|integrals without elementary antiderivatives]]. Conversely, an elementary antiderivative does not make every improper integral converge. If only a numerical definite value is needed, a [[中点法|numerical rule]] may be useful, but its error needs its own justification. Method choice, representation and convergence answer different questions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 连续函数可以有原函数，却没有初等公式，见[[无初等原函数的积分]]。反过来，有初等原函数也不能让所有反常积分收敛。如果只需要定积分的数值，[[中点法|数值积分方法]]可能合适，但误差仍需另行说明。方法选择、答案表示与敛散性回答的是不同问题。
