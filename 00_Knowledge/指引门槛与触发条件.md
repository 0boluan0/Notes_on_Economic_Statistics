# 前瞻指引中的维持政策门槛不自动等于改变政策的充分触发条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A threshold for maintaining policy is not automatically a sufficient trigger for changing it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当[[前瞻指引]]说“只要这些条件成立，就至少继续维持当前政策”，越过其中一个门槛只意味着这项维持承诺不再由原条件保证；它不自动要求立刻反向行动。先读清必要条件、充分条件、例外和其他判断信息，不能把单个数字当成自动开关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When guidance promises to maintain policy at least while specified conditions hold, crossing a threshold removes that particular assurance; it does not automatically require a reversal. Read necessary and sufficient conditions, exceptions and other information before treating a number as a switch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把“条件组合成立”记作 $C$，“维持利率”记作 $R$，这类语句给出 $C\Rightarrow R$，而不是 $C\Leftrightarrow R$。因此 $\neg C$ 不能推出 $\neg R$；条件不再成立后，央行仍可能根据其他信息维持利率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $C$ denote the stated combination of conditions and $R$ maintenance of the rate. The guidance gives $C\Rightarrow R$, not equivalence. Thus $\neg C$ does not imply $\neg R$; other information can still justify maintaining the rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史应用：2012 年 12 月 FOMC 指引同时涉及失业率高于 6.5%、未来一至两年通胀预测不超过其 2% 目标 0.5 个百分点，以及长期预期保持锚定。2013 年 Bernanke 明确说明这些是门槛而非触发器。失业率降到 6.4% 不足以推出当天必须加息；6.5% 也不是宣布了一个永恒的充分就业目标。这是当时的案例，不是现行政策规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The December 2012 FOMC guidance combined unemployment above 6.5 percent, one-to-two-year inflation projections no more than 0.5 percentage point above its 2 percent target, and anchored long-run expectations. Bernanke clarified in 2013 that these were thresholds, not triggers. Unemployment of 6.4 percent did not compel an immediate hike, and 6.5 percent was not a timeless employment target. This is a historical case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用时记录指标期限、组合中的“且/或”、例外和有效日期。检查旧指引是否已被替换，并把[[央行法定目标|最终目标]]、预测门槛与实际行动分开；越过门槛后仍应阅读央行对整体判断的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record horizons, logical conjunctions, exceptions and validity dates. Check whether guidance has been replaced, and separate [[央行法定目标|final objectives]], thresholds and actions. After a threshold is crossed, read the explanation of the broader judgment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Bernanke（2013），Communication and Monetary Policy，“Forward Guidance about Policy Interest Rates”](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/bernanke20131119a.htm)：明确区分 thresholds 与 triggers，越线后的就业多指标判断。
- [Federal Reserve，Timeline: Forward Guidance about the Federal Funds Rate，2012-12-12、2013-12-18、2014-03-19 条目](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/timeline-forward-guidance-about-the-federal-funds-rate.htm)：组合条件及后续替换；逻辑记号和 6.4% 情景为教学表达。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bernanke supports the threshold–trigger distinction; the Federal Reserve timeline verifies the historical conditions and later replacements. The logical notation and 6.4 percent scenario are pedagogical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
