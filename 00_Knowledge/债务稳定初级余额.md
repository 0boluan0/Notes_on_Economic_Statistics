# 稳定债务率所需的初级盈余率等于利差债务项加存量流量调节率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The primary surplus required to stabilise the debt ratio equals the interest-growth debt term plus stock-flow adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[债务率动态]]的同口径记号中，令本期债务率不变，即$\Delta b_t=0$，便得到所需初级盈余占本期GDP之比：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the conventions in [[债务率动态|debt-ratio dynamics]], set $\Delta b_t=0$ to obtain the required primary surplus as a share of current GDP:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
pb_t^{\mathrm{stab}}=\frac{i_t-n_t}{1+n_t}b_{t-1}+a_t.
$$

给定这些参数，实际$pb$高于该值，债务率下降；低于该值，债务率上升。例：$b_{t-1}=60\%$、$i=5\%$、$n=3\%$且$a=0$，稳定所需盈余率约为1.165%。若实际盈余只有0.5%，债务率仍上升约0.665个百分点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding the inputs fixed, a larger primary surplus lowers the debt ratio and a smaller one raises it. With opening debt 60% of GDP, interest 5%, growth 3% and no adjustment, the required surplus is about 1.165%. A 0.5% surplus still raises debt by about 0.665 percentage points of GDP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个式子回答的是“账面上需要多少”，不是“政府能否做到”或“应当立刻这样做”。加税减支可能改变增长和利率，或超出政治与执行能力；这些反馈属于[[债务可持续性]]判断。稳定在某个债务率也不等于该水平已经安全。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation gives an accounting requirement, not feasibility or an instruction for immediate adjustment. Tax and spending changes can alter growth and interest rates or exceed political and administrative capacity. Those issues belong to [[债务可持续性|debt sustainability]]. A stable debt ratio need not be safe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
