# Logarithmic growth of harmonic numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*调和数的对数增长*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding smaller and smaller positive terms can still produce an unbounded total. The [[调和数|harmonic number]] $H_n=1+1/2+\cdots+1/n$ is a central example: its growth is slow, but it never settles at a finite limit. More precisely, it grows on the scale of the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] $\ln n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把越来越小的正数不断相加，总量仍可能无界。[[调和数]] $H_n=1+1/2+\cdots+1/n$ 就是典型例子：增长缓慢，却不会停在有限极限。更精确地，它按[[自然对数]] $\ln n$ 的尺度增长。

> [!important] Bounds at every positive integer / 每个正整数处的界
> $$
> \boxed{\ln(n+1)\le H_n\le1+\ln n},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For example, $\ln5\approx1.609\le H_4=25/12\approx2.083\le1+\ln4\approx2.386$. These are bounds for a finite sum, not an equality $H_n=\ln n$. The case $H_0=0$ is defined separately; the logarithmic bounds are not evaluated at zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如，$\ln5\approx1.609\le H_4=25/12\approx2.083\le1+\ln4\approx2.386$。这是对有限和的上下界，不是等式 $H_n=\ln n$。$H_0=0$ 另外定义，不能把零代入这组对数界。

## Compare unit-width rectangles with an area
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用宽度为一的矩形比较面积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function $1/x$ decreases for $x>0$. On $[k,k+1]$, the rectangle of height $1/k$ lies above the curve; its width is one, so its area is exactly $1/k$. The [[定积分|definite integral]] measures the area under this positive curve, and the [[自然对数积分表示|integral representation of the logarithm]] evaluates it:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数 $1/x$ 在正轴上递减。在 $[k,k+1]$ 上，高度 $1/k$ 的矩形覆盖曲线，宽度为一，面积恰为 $1/k$。[[定积分]]表示这条正曲线下的面积，而[[自然对数积分表示]]给出它的数值：

$$
\int_k^{k+1}\frac{dx}{x}\le\frac1k\quad(k\ge1),\qquad\ln(n+1)=\int_1^{n+1}\frac{dx}{x}\le H_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the other direction, use $[k-1,k]$ when $k\ge2$. Its curve is at least as high as $1/k$. Leave the first harmonic term $1$ outside the comparison:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要得到另一方向，对 $k\ge2$ 使用区间 $[k-1,k]$；该区间上的曲线至少有 $1/k$ 那么高。把调和数的第一项 1 留在比较之外：

$$
\frac1k\le\int_{k-1}^{k}\frac{dx}{x},\qquad H_n=1+\sum_{k=2}^{n}\frac1k\le1+\int_1^n\frac{dx}{x}=1+\ln n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $n=1$ the second sum and integral are both zero, so the upper bound remains valid. The two comparisons prove the displayed bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$n=1$ 时，第二个求和与积分都为零，上界依然成立。两边比较就证明了前面的界。

## What “logarithmic” does and does not say
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*“对数增长”具体说明什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n\ge2$, divide the bounds by the positive number $\ln n$. Both outer quantities tend to one:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $n\ge2$，可以除以正数 $\ln n$。两侧比值都趋于 1：

$$
\frac{\ln(n+1)}{\ln n}\le\frac{H_n}{\ln n}\le1+\frac1{\ln n}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\frac{H_n}{\ln n}\longrightarrow1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the [[夹逼定理|squeeze theorem]], this proves $H_n\sim\ln n$. The symbol $\sim$ denotes [[渐近等价|asymptotic equivalence]]: the ratio tends to one. It also implies $H_n=\Theta(\log n)$, where [[大Θ记号|Theta notation]] means eventual upper and lower bounds by positive constant multiples. Any fixed logarithm base greater than one gives the same order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[夹逼定理]]，得到 $H_n\sim\ln n$。符号 $\sim$ 表示[[渐近等价]]，即比值趋于 1；它也推出 $H_n=\Theta(\log n)$，其中[[大Θ记号]]表示最终被两个正常数倍夹住。固定任意大于 1 的对数底数，都有相同的增长量级。

> [!warning] Slow growth is still divergence / 增长慢仍然可以发散
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The lower bound $\ln(n+1)$ tends to infinity, so $H_n\to\infty$. Since $H_n$ is the $n$-th partial sum of the harmonic series, $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}1/k$ diverges. Its terms approach zero, showing why the [[级数通项判别|zero-term condition]] is necessary but insufficient. Asymptotic equivalence also concerns relative error; it does not say that $H_n-\ln n\to0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下界 $\ln(n+1)$ 趋于无穷，因此 $H_n\to\infty$。由于 $H_n$ 是调和级数的第 $n$ 个部分和，$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}1/k$ 发散。它的通项却趋于零，说明[[级数通项判别|通项趋零条件]]只是必要条件，不是充分条件。渐近等价关注相对误差，也不表示 $H_n-\ln n\to0$。

> [!note]- The bounded difference / 有界的差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In fact, $H_n-\ln n$ decreases to a constant $\gamma$, called the Euler–Mascheroni constant, approximately $0.5772$. To see existence without a longer expansion, the integral comparison gives $H_n-\ln n>0$, while
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 事实上，$H_n-\ln n$ 递减到一个常数 $\gamma$，称为 Euler–Mascheroni 常数，约为 $0.5772$。不需要更长的展开也能看出其存在：积分比较给出 $H_n-\ln n>0$，而
>
> $$
> (H_{n+1}-\ln(n+1))-(H_n-\ln n)=\frac1{n+1}-\int_n^{n+1}\frac{dx}{x}<0.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A decreasing sequence bounded below has a limit. This gives the refinement $H_n=\ln n+\gamma+o(1)$, where $o(1)$ denotes a quantity tending to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 递减且有下界的数列存在极限，于是得到更细的关系 $H_n=\ln n+\gamma+o(1)$；这里 $o(1)$ 表示趋于零的量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the one-book-per-layer model described in [[调和数|the harmonic-number example]], unit-length books have top-book overhang $H_n/2$. Thus that model permits unbounded overhang, but obtaining large overhang $d$ requires a book count on an exponential scale in $d$, roughly $n\approx e^{2d-\gamma}$. This concerns the specified ideal balance construction; allowing different pile structures changes the optimisation problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[调和数|调和数例子]]的每层一本模型中，单位长度书的最上方伸出量为 $H_n/2$。这个模型的伸出量理论上无界，但要达到很大的伸出量 $d$，书本数要按 $d$ 的指数尺度增长，约为 $n\approx e^{2d-\gamma}$。这讨论的是指定的理想平衡构造；允许不同堆叠结构，会改变优化问题。
