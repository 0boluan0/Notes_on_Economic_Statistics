# 加权历史模拟用正规化日期权重构造经验损失分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Weighted historical simulation constructs an empirical loss distribution with normalised date weights*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 近期情景可占更大概率质量
> 加权历史模拟保留历史情景及其整行联合冲击，但不再让每个日期等权。权重必须正规化，并在损失排序后跟着原日期情景一起移动。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Weighted historical simulation preserves historical scenarios and each date's whole joint shock but does not give every date equal mass. Weights must be normalised and remain attached to their original scenarios after sorting losses.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $i=1$ 是最近日期、$0<\lambda<1$，有限窗口中的指数权重可写为

$$
w_i=\frac{(1-\lambda)\lambda^{i-1}}{1-\lambda^n},
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^n w_i=1.
$$

将情景损失升序排列后，加权 VaR 是累计权重首次达到 $\alpha$ 的损失。不能先把权重独立排序，也不能让权重脱离原来的联合情景。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After sorting scenario losses in ascending order, weighted VaR is the first loss whose cumulative weight reaches $\alpha$. Weights cannot be sorted independently or detached from their joint scenarios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 损失排序后，权重应按大小重新排序吗？
>
> **答案：** 不应。每个权重必须跟随产生该损失的原历史日期。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** After sorting losses, should the weights themselves be re-sorted by size?
>
> **Answer:** No. Each weight must remain attached to the historical date that generated its loss.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 较大近期权重提高响应性，也降低旧压力期的有效权重。
- $\lambda$ 是模型选择，必须在最终评估期前确定。
- 加权不能创造历史窗口中没有的冲击。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Recency weighting raises responsiveness but reduces the effective mass of older stress periods.
- $\lambda$ is a model choice to be fixed before final evaluation.
- Weighting cannot create shocks absent from the historical window.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
