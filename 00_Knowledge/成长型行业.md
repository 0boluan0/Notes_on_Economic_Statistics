# 成长型行业是在所讨论时期由结构性需求扩张推动较快增长的行业
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A growth industry experiences relatively rapid growth driven by structural demand expansion during the period under study.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成长型行业的较快增长主要来自新需求、技术采用或渗透率提高等持续驱动，而不只是经济复苏后的短期反弹。应说明观察期、增长指标与比较基准；“成长”不是一张永久标签，也不表示完全不受经济周期影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A growth industry's expansion is mainly driven by persistent forces such as new demand, technological adoption, or rising penetration, rather than merely a short recovery rebound. Specify the period, growth measure, and benchmark. Growth is neither a permanent label nor immunity to the business cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一项新服务的用户渗透率由 10% 提高到 20%，在潜在客户数不变时用户数翻倍；这解释需求来源，却尚未说明单价、利润或股票回报。成长与周期暴露的区别见[[成长与周期敏感性]]，价值判断见[[增长不等于增值]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a service's penetration rises from 10% to 20% with an unchanged potential customer base, its users double. This explains demand growth, not prices, profits, or stock returns. See [[成长与周期敏感性|growth versus cyclical sensitivity]] and [[增长不等于增值|why growth need not create value]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
