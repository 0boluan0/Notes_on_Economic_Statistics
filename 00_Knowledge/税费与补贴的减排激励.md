# 排污税费与减排补贴在固定基准和既定参与者下可提供相同的边际减排激励
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An emissions charge and an abatement subsidy can give the same marginal abatement incentive with a fixed baseline and existing participants.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相同单位价格既可以“对剩余排放收费”，也可以“对已经减掉的排放付钱”。在基准、参与者和其他选择都固定时，两者使下一单位减排同样值钱；**相同的边际减排激励不等于相同的利润、财政收支或行业结果**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same unit price can charge remaining emissions or reward emissions already reduced. With a fixed baseline, participants and other choices, another unit abated has the same private value. Equal marginal incentives do not imply equal profits, public budgets or industry outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令基准 $e_0$、减排 $a$ 和成本 $C(a)$ 相同，[[排污费]]取 $t$，[[按量减排补贴]]也取 $s=t$。两种政策下要最小化的私人净成本满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the same baseline $e_0$, abatement $a$ and cost $C(a)$, with [[排污费|charge]] $t$ and [[按量减排补贴|subsidy]] $s=t$. The two private net-cost objectives satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$F_T(a)=C(a)+t(e_0-a),\qquad F_S(a)=C(a)-ta,$$
$$F_T(a)-F_S(a)=te_0.$$

右边不随 $a$ 变化，因此在同一可行减排集合中，两者有相同的最优减排选择；可微内点都满足 $C'(a)=t$。这个等价不要求两种政策的私人净成本相等，甚至不只依靠一阶条件：目标函数整体仅相差一个常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The difference does not depend on $a$, so both objectives have the same minimisers on the same feasible abatement set. A differentiable interior solution satisfies $C'(a)=t$. The argument is a constant shift of the entire objective, not a claim that private net costs are equal or merely a comparison of first-order conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $e_0=100,C(a)=a^2/2,t=s=50$。两者都选 $a=50$；税下净成本 $1250+2500=3750$，补贴下 $1250-2500=-1250$，相差 5000。企业在补贴下的负净成本是本项成本减补助后的余额，不代表生产整个项目无成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $e_0=100$, $C(a)=a^2/2$ and $t=s=50$, both choose $a=50$. Private net cost is 3,750 under the charge and −1,250 under the subsidy, a difference of 5,000. The negative figure is this cost item net of support, not costless production of the entire project.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一旦允许进入退出，补贴带来的利润变化可能维持原本会退出的厂商或吸引新进入者；若基准跟产出增长，$te_0$ 也不再是对产出选择无关的常数。因此单家厂商少排，不保证行业总排放下降同样多，甚至不保证下降。方向与大小需用包含参与、产出和补贴资格的模型判断，而不是把这种可能性说成必然反弹。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With entry and exit, higher profits under a subsidy may retain firms that would otherwise leave or attract entrants. If the baseline rises with output, $te_0$ is no longer constant for the output decision either. Lower emissions per firm therefore need not imply an equally large aggregate reduction, or any aggregate reduction. The sign and magnitude require a model of participation, output and eligibility rather than an assumption of inevitable rebound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府方面，一种工具收取收入，另一种支付补助。社会资源损益不能简单等同这些款项，但筹资扭曲、实施资源与分配后果也不能忽略；相同货币权重下的支付抵消规则见[[转移支付核算]]。实际排放的记录质量可参考[[排放监测与履约|排放计量检查]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One instrument raises government revenue; the other requires expenditure. Neither payment is automatically a social resource loss, but financing distortions, implementation resources and distribution still matter. [[转移支付核算|Transfer accounting]] states when payments cancel; [[排放监测与履约|emissions-measurement checks]] address the quality of actual emissions records.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
