# 债券定价必须把息票金额、贴现率与期数换算到同一付息周期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond valuation must express coupon amounts, discount rates, and period counts on one coupon-period basis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对面值 $F$、年票面利率 $c$、每年付息 $m$ 次、剩余 $T$ 年的规则债券，在付息后估值且剩余 $N=mT$ 是正整数时，每期息票为 $C=Fc/m$。先辨认给出的年收益率，再换成每期有效率 $i$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a regular bond with face value $F$, annual coupon rate $c$, $m$ coupons per year, and $T$ years remaining, value it just after a coupon with a positive integer $N=mT$. Each coupon is $C=Fc/m$. Identify the annual yield convention before converting it to the effective period rate $i$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
i=\begin{cases}
y^{(m)}/m,&\text{名义年收益率，按每年 }m\text{ 次复利报价},\\
(1+R)^{1/m}-1,&\text{有效年收益率 }R.
\end{cases}
$$

随后统一按付息期编号 $k=1,\ldots,N$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then index the payments by coupon periods $k=1,\ldots,N$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{Fc/m}{(1+i)^k}+\frac{F}{(1+i)^N}.$$

指数 $k$ 已经是付息期数，不能再乘一次 $m$。若输入是 EAR，也可以把每期折现因子写成 $(1+R)^{-k/m}$；不能未经换算就写成 $(1+R/m)^{-k}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponent $k$ already counts coupon periods, so do not multiply it by $m$ again. With an EAR input, the factor can instead be written $(1+R)^{-k/m}$; it is not generally $(1+R/m)^{-k}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面值 $100$、年票息 $5\%$、半年付息、两年到期，每期付 $2.5$，共四期。名义年收益率 $6\%$、半年复利时 $i=3\%$，价格约 $98.1415$；若给的是 EAR $6\%$，则 $i=\sqrt{1.06}-1\approx2.9563\%$，价格约 $98.3021$。同一个“年率 6%”标签没有说明口径，就不足以定价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With face value 100, a 5% annual coupon, semiannual payments, and two years remaining, there are four coupons of 2.5. A 6% nominal annual yield compounded semiannually gives $i=3\%$ and price 98.1415. A 6% EAR instead gives $i=\sqrt{1.06}-1\approx2.9563\%$ and price 98.3021. An unexplained “6% annual rate” is insufficient for valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结算日在付息日之间、首末期不规则或日计数有特别规定时，应按合约现金流日期和市场收益率惯例处理分数期，不能直接令 $N=mT$ 后套完整期公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Between-coupon settlement, irregular first or last coupons, and special day-count rules require actual payment dates and market yield conventions. Do not force those cases into the full-period formula merely by setting $N=mT$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
