# Capacitated assignment with slots and flow
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*整数容量的槽化与网络流*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A project with two places can accept two students, but an ordinary [[匹配定义|matching]] uses each vertex at most once. We can represent the two places as two separate vertices, or keep one project vertex and attach a numerical capacity to it. These are two ways to describe the same assignment problem **when each student uses one interchangeable place**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个有两个名额的项目可以接收两名学生，但普通[[匹配定义|匹配]]规定每个顶点至多使用一次。我们可以把两个名额画成两个顶点，也可以保留一个项目顶点，再附上数值容量。**当每名学生只占一个可互换名额时**，这两种写法描述同一个分配问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a finite [[二分图配对关系|allowed-pair graph]] with students $L$, projects $R$, and allowed pairs $E$. Student $\ell$ may receive at most one project. Project $r$ has capacity $q_r\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, meaning it can receive at most $q_r$ students. Capacity counts students, not hours, money or unequal workloads. Requiring every student to receive a project is an additional [[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|left-coverage requirement]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用有限的[[二分图配对关系|允许配对图]]：学生为 $L$，项目为 $R$，允许配对为 $E$。学生 $\ell$ 至多得到一个项目；项目 $r$ 的容量为 $q_r\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$，表示至多接收 $q_r$ 名学生。这里容量数的是人数，不是时长、金额或大小不同的工作量。要求全部学生都有项目，是另加的[[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|覆盖左侧要求]]。

> [!example] Three students, three places · 三名学生与三个名额
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $L=\{a,b,c\}$ and $R=\{p,q\}$, with capacities $q_p=2$ and $q_q=1$. Allow only $ap,bp,bq,cq$; the notation $ap$ means that $a$ is eligible for $p$. Assigning $a,b$ to $p$ and $c$ to $q$ serves everyone. The two selected pairs incident with $p$ would violate ordinary matching on the original graph, yet they obey the intended capacity of two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $L=\{a,b,c\}$、$R=\{p,q\}$，容量为 $q_p=2$、$q_q=1$，只允许 $ap,bp,bq,cq$；其中 $ap$ 表示 $a$ 有资格参加 $p$。让 $a,b$ 去 $p$，$c$ 去 $q$，就完成全部分配。原图中有两条选中边接到 $p$，这会违反普通匹配，却符合项目容量二的实际要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn each place into a vertex
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把每个名额变成一个顶点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace project $r$ by $q_r$ labeled **slots** $r^{(1)},\ldots,r^{(q_r)}$. For every allowed pair $\ell r$, connect $\ell$ to every slot of $r$. A zero-capacity project contributes no slots. Each slot can now be used only once under the ordinary matching rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把项目 $r$ 替换成 $q_r$ 个带编号的**槽位** $r^{(1)},\ldots,r^{(q_r)}$。每个允许配对 $\ell r$ 都改成从 $\ell$ 连到 $r$ 的全部槽位。零容量项目不产生槽位。这样，普通匹配规则就保证每个槽位至多用一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, create $p^{(1)},p^{(2)},q^{(1)}$. Students $a,b$ both connect to the two $p$ slots; $b,c$ connect to the $q$ slot. The matching $\{ap^{(1)},bp^{(2)},cq^{(1)}\}$ is an ordinary matching of size three. Its project interpretation is exactly the assignment above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例中建立 $p^{(1)},p^{(2)},q^{(1)}$。学生 $a,b$ 都连接两个 $p$ 槽，$b,c$ 连接 $q$ 槽。于是 $\{ap^{(1)},bp^{(2)},cq^{(1)}\}$ 是大小为三的普通匹配，解释回项目后，正是前面的分配。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The correspondence works in both directions. Given a slot matching, merge slots with the same project name. No student is repeated, and at most $q_r$ slots of $r$ are occupied. Conversely, if an allowed assignment sends at most $q_r$ distinct students to each $r$, place those students in distinct slots of $r$. Both constructions preserve the number of assigned students and whether all of $L$ is covered. Several slot matchings may describe the same assignment, because slot numbers have no practical meaning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个对应有两个方向。从槽位匹配出发，合并同名项目的槽：学生没有重复，项目 $r$ 至多占用 $q_r$ 个槽。反过来，若一份允许的分配给每个 $r$ 至多安排了 $q_r$ 名不同学生，就把他们放进该项目的不同槽位。两个方向都保持已分配人数，以及是否覆盖全部 $L$。不同槽位匹配可能表示同一实际分配，因为槽位编号没有实际区别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep capacities as numbers instead
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把容量直接写成数值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cloning can create many vertices and edges: there are $\sum_{r\in R}q_r$ project slots and $\sum_{\{\ell,r\}\in E}q_r$ allowed student-slot edges. A **[[网络流|network flow]]** represents those capacities without making all the copies. Flow is a nonnegative amount on each directed arc, bounded by its capacity, with total inflow equal to total outflow at every intermediate vertex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拆槽可能产生很多点和边：项目槽位数为 $\sum_{r\in R}q_r$，允许的学生—槽位边数为 $\sum_{\{\ell,r\}\in E}q_r$。**[[网络流]]**可以直接表示这些容量，不必展开全部副本。流是每条有向弧上的非负数量，不超过弧容量，并且在每个中间顶点保持总流入等于总流出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add a new source $s$ and sink $t$. The source supplies assignment units; the sink collects them. Create only these three kinds of arcs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

添加新的源点 $s$ 和汇点 $t$：源点供应分配单位，汇点接收它们。只建立以下三类弧：

| Arc · 弧 | Capacity · 容量 | Meaning · 含义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $s\to\ell$ for each $\ell\in L$ | $1$ | At most one assignment for each student · 每名学生至多分配一次 |
| $\ell\to r$ for each $\{\ell,r\}\in E$ | $1$ | Only an allowed pair may be used · 只使用允许配对 |
| $r\to t$ for each $r\in R$ | $q_r$ | At most $q_r$ students at that project · 项目至多接收 $q_r$ 人 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **integral flow** has an integer on every arc. Since each $s\to\ell$ arc has capacity one, its flow is either zero or one. Conservation at $\ell$ means that a one must leave along exactly one allowed project arc: several positive integers cannot sum to one. Conservation at $r$ then counts its assigned students, and $f(r,t)\le q_r$ enforces its capacity. Selecting the pairs with $f(\ell,r)=1$ therefore recovers a valid discrete assignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**整数流**指每条弧的流量都是整数。由于 $s\to\ell$ 的容量为一，其流量只能为零或一。由 $\ell$ 处的守恒，一个流入单位必须恰好沿一条允许项目弧流出，因为多个正整数不可能合计为一。在 $r$ 处，守恒使流量等于已分配学生数，而 $f(r,t)\le q_r$ 执行项目容量。因此，选取满足 $f(\ell,r)=1$ 的配对，就恢复了一份离散分配。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, send one unit along $s\to\ell\to r\to t$ for every assigned pair. A valid assignment makes all capacities and conservation equations hold. The flow value is the total leaving $s$, so it equals the number of assigned students. In the running example it is three, with two units on $p\to t$ and one on $q\to t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，对每个已分配配对，沿 $s\to\ell\to r\to t$ 送一单位流。合法分配保证所有容量与守恒式成立。流值是离开 $s$ 的总量，恰等于已分配学生数。贯穿例中流值为三，$p\to t$ 上为二，$q\to t$ 上为一。

> [!important] Integer capacity gives an integral optimum · 整数容量保证存在整数最优解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The **[[最大流整数性|maximum-flow integrality theorem]]** guarantees that a finite network with nonnegative integer capacities has an integral maximum flow. Thus the largest feasible flow value equals the largest number of students assignable here. Full left coverage exists exactly when this value is $|L|$. The theorem says an integral optimum exists; it does not say every feasible or optimal flow is integral. For example, one student eligible for two one-place projects can split one unit into two halves, giving a maximum flow that is not a discrete assignment. Use an integral optimum to extract the assignment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **[[最大流整数性|最大流整数性定理]]**保证：有限网络的容量都是非负整数时，存在一个整数最大流。因此，这里的最大可行流值等于最多可分配学生数；恰在该值达到 $|L|$ 时，能够覆盖全部左侧。定理说的是存在整数最优解，不是每个可行流或最优流都为整数。例如，一名学生同时符合两个单名额项目的资格时，可以把一单位拆成两个半单位，得到非离散分配的最大流。提取实际分配时，应使用整数最优解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An empty left side requires no assignments and has maximum flow zero. A student whose eligible projects all have zero capacity cannot be assigned. The constructions preserve both cases without special fictitious assignments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空左侧无需分配，最大流为零。若某名学生所有合资格项目的容量都为零，就无法给他分配。两种构造都保留这些情况，不需要虚构配对。

## What this transformation assumes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*变换保留了哪些约束？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unit requirement matters. If a machine has capacity three and two indivisible jobs each need two units, two unit slots used by two selected jobs would appear feasible while the true demand is four. Ordinary unit matching has lost the workload constraint. Allowing divisible flow does not by itself restore the requirement that each job be accepted whole or rejected whole.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位需求很关键。例如机器容量为三，两个不可分割任务各需二单位。若把每个任务仍按一槽处理，选中两个任务看似可行，实际需求却是四，超过容量。普通单位匹配丢失了工作量约束；允许流可拆分，也不会自动恢复“整项接收或整项拒绝”的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, slots with different equipment or time availability must have their own eligibility edges; copying every original edge to every slot would be wrong. Joint restrictions on pairs of students, costs, and [[稳定匹配与普通匹配|preference stability]] require their own constraints or objectives. This reduction proves capacity feasibility and preserves the number assigned; it does not choose those additional success criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，若槽位的设备或时间不同，就必须分别记录资格边，不能把原有边复制给所有槽。学生组合之间的联动限制、成本，以及[[稳定匹配与普通匹配|偏好稳定性]]，也需要各自的约束或目标。这个归约证明的是容量可行性并保留分配人数，不替你决定其他成功标准。
