# 单位需求下的右侧整数容量可化为单位槽或网络流

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Integer capacities on the right can be represented by unit slots or network flow when each left assignment consumes one unit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子变换
> 若每个左点的一次分配恰好消耗一个可互换单位、每个左点至多被分配一次，而右点 $r$ 可接收非负整数容量 $q_r$，可以把 $r$ 复制成 $q_r$ 个单位槽，并把每条允许边连接到这些槽。普通 matching 随后自动执行“每个左点至多一次、每个右点至多 $q_r$ 次”的容量约束；若还要求覆盖 $L$，则需让每个左点恰好匹配一次。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose each assignment of a left vertex consumes one interchangeable unit, every left vertex can be assigned at most once, and right vertex $r$ has nonnegative integer capacity $q_r$. Replace $r$ with $q_r$ unit slots and connect every eligible left vertex to those slots. Ordinary matching then enforces at most one assignment per left vertex and at most $q_r$ assignments to $r$; if all of $L$ must be covered, every left vertex must be matched exactly once.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个变换成立，是因为容量只计数使用次数，各槽之间没有额外区别或联动约束。从 expanded graph 中合并同一右点的槽，就得到原问题的容量分配；反过来，原问题中分给 $r$ 的至多 $q_r$ 个左点，可以一一放进这些槽。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The transformation works because capacity merely counts uses; the slots have no further distinctions or coupling constraints. Merging slots belonging to the same right vertex maps any expanded-graph matching back to a capacitated assignment. Conversely, the at most $q_r$ left vertices assigned to $r$ can be placed injectively into its slots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当复制槽会使图膨胀时，可改用 network flow：source 到每个左点的弧容量为 $1$，每条允许边定向为左到右并设容量 $1$，右点到 sink 的弧容量为 $q_r$。整数容量下，integral flow 与离散分配对应。求尽量多的分配就是求 max flow；要求覆盖全部 $L$，则要检验最大流值是否达到 $|L|$。这个写法也更容易继续加入不同容量和全局流量目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If slot cloning would make the graph large, use a flow network instead: source-to-left arcs have capacity one, every eligible edge is directed left to right with capacity one, and the arc from right vertex $r$ to the sink has capacity $q_r$. With integer capacities, integral flows correspond to discrete assignments. Maximising the number assigned is a max-flow problem; covering all of $L$ requires a maximum-flow value of $|L|$. This representation also extends more naturally to varied capacities and global flow objectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界在于“每个任务消耗一个可互换单位”。若任务消耗量不同、若同时选择两个任务会产生联动、或某些槽有独立属性，简单复制不能保留全部约束，必须显式扩展网络或换模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The boundary is that every assignment consumes one interchangeable unit. If tasks consume different amounts, selections interact, or slots have distinct attributes, simple cloning no longer preserves all constraints; the network must be expanded explicitly or the model replaced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 一个项目可收三名学生，且每名学生只占一个名额。怎样把它改成普通 matching？
>
> **答案：** 把项目复制成三个单位槽，并让所有原本符合资格的学生连接到这三个槽。
