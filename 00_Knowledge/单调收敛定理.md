# Monotone convergence theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单调收敛定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a nonnegative quantity is revealed in increasingly complete approximations, and no approximation takes back a contribution already included. The **monotone convergence theorem** says that their probability-weighted averages increase to the average of the final quantity. It justifies passing a limit through an expectation even when the final average is infinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一个非负量通过越来越完整的近似逐步显现，而且后面的近似不会撤回已经计入的贡献。**单调收敛定理**说明：这些近似的概率加权平均，会递增到最终那个量的平均。即使最终平均为无穷，它也能保证极限与期望的交换。

> [!example] Raise a cap on one die roll · 逐步提高同一次掷骰结果的上限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Roll one fair six-sided die and call its result $D$. Let $X_n=\min(D,n)$: record the result, but cap it at $n$. If the roll is $5$, the recorded sequence is $1,2,3,4,5,5,\ldots$. Each outcome gives a nondecreasing sequence ending at its own die value. Their [[期望|expectations]] begin
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 掷一次公平六面骰子，把结果记为 $D$。令 $X_n=\min(D,n)$：记录点数，但最高只记到 $n$。若掷到 $5$，记录序列为 $1,2,3,4,5,5,\ldots$。每个结果都会给出一个不下降的序列，最后到达该次的点数。它们的[[期望]]依次为
>
> $$
> E[X_1]=1,\qquad E[X_2]=\frac{11}{6},\qquad E[X_3]=\frac{15}{6},
> \qquad E[X_n]=\frac{21}{6}=3.5\quad(n\ge6).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For example, capping at $2$ gives the six equally likely values $1,2,2,2,2,2$. The theorem extends this idea to limits that are never reached at a finite stage, and to unbounded quantities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如上限为 $2$ 时，六个等可能的记录值是 $1,2,2,2,2,2$。定理把这个思路推广到有限步永远到不了的极限，也推广到没有固定上界的量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What must increase
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*究竟要求什么递增*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All the [[随机变量|random variables]] must live on the same [[概率空间|probability space]]: $X_n(\omega)$ is the $n$th recorded value for one fixed outcome $\omega$. Write $X_n\uparrow X$ when these values never decrease and tend to $X(\omega)$. The conditions may hold **almost surely**, meaning on one event of probability one, as in [[几乎必然收敛|almost-sure convergence]]. They concern each path, not just the sequence of averages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有[[随机变量]]必须定义在同一个[[概率空间]]上：$X_n(\omega)$ 是固定结果 $\omega$ 下的第 $n$ 个记录值。记号 $X_n\uparrow X$ 表示这些数值从不下降，并趋于 $X(\omega)$。条件可以只在**几乎必然**的意义下成立，即在同一个概率为一的事件上成立，含义见[[几乎必然收敛]]。要求递增的是每条路径，不只是平均数的序列。

> [!important] Nonnegative increasing approximation · 非负且递增的近似
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be nonnegative measurable random variables, with $X_n\uparrow X$ almost surely. Nonnegative extended values are allowed, so the limit may be $+\infty$. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ 为非负可测随机变量，且 $X_n\uparrow X$ 几乎必然成立。这里允许非负扩展值，因此极限可以取 $+\infty$。那么
>
> $$
> E[X_n]\uparrow E[X],\qquad
> \lim_{n\to\infty}E[X_n]=E\!\left[\lim_{n\to\infty}X_n\right].
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All expectations are well-defined in $[0,+\infty]$. Neither finite expectation nor independence is required. There is also no requirement for one fixed integrable upper bound: the increasing nonnegative structure is doing the work.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所有期望都在 $[0,+\infty]$ 中有定义。这里不要求期望有限，也不要求独立；还不要求一个固定的可积上界，保证极限交换的是非负且递增的结构。

## Why the averages cannot stop short
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么平均数不会停在较低的位置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $X_n\le X$, monotonicity of expectation gives $L=\lim_nE[X_n]\le E[X]$. The substantial step is to show that no part of the final expectation is missed. For a nonnegative variable, expectation can be built from below: take every nonnegative **finite-valued** variable $S\le X$, compute its ordinary finite weighted average, and take the supremum of those averages. This supremum is $E[X]$, possibly $+\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $X_n\le X$，期望的保序性给出 $L=\lim_nE[X_n]\le E[X]$。真正需要证明的是：最终期望的任何一部分都不会漏掉。非负变量的期望可以从下方构造：考察每个非负、只取**有限个有限数值**且满足 $S\le X$ 的变量 $S$，计算它的普通有限加权平均，再取这些平均的上确界。这个上确界就是 $E[X]$，可以为 $+\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix one such $S$ and a number $0<c<1$. On each path where $S>0$, the increasing $X_n$ eventually reaches the lower target $cS$, because its limit is at least $S>cS$. Where $S=0$, it already meets that target. Thus the events $A_n=\{X_n\ge cS\}$ increase to an event of probability one, after ignoring the common null exceptional set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定其中一个 $S$，再取 $0<c<1$。在 $S>0$ 的路径上，由于 $X_n$ 递增到至少为 $S$ 的值，而 $S>cS$，所以它最终会达到较低目标 $cS$。在 $S=0$ 的地方，它已经达到目标。因此忽略共同的零概率异常集后，事件 $A_n=\{X_n\ge cS\}$ 递增到一个概率为一的事件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $\mathbf1_{A_n}$ equals one on $A_n$ and zero elsewhere, so $X_n\ge cS\mathbf1_{A_n}$. If $S$ takes the values $s_1,\ldots,s_r$ on disjoint events $B_1,\ldots,B_r$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事件指示变量|指示变量]] $\mathbf1_{A_n}$ 在 $A_n$ 上等于一，在其余地方为零，所以 $X_n\ge cS\mathbf1_{A_n}$。若 $S$ 在互斥事件 $B_1,\ldots,B_r$ 上分别取值 $s_1,\ldots,s_r$，则

$$
E[S\mathbf1_{A_n}]=\sum_{j=1}^r s_jP(B_j\cap A_n)
\longrightarrow\sum_{j=1}^r s_jP(B_j)=E[S].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the probabilities converge because an increasing union collects all its disjoint new pieces; this follows from [[概率三公理|countable additivity of probability]]. The sum has only finitely many terms. Therefore $L\ge cE[S]$. Let $c\uparrow1$ and then take the supremum over $S$: $L\ge E[X]$. Together with the first inequality, this proves the theorem without assuming another expectation-limit interchange.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的概率会收敛，是因为递增的并集收齐了逐次加入的所有互斥部分，这是[[概率三公理|概率可数可加性]]的结果；求和也只有有限项。因此 $L\ge cE[S]$。再令 $c\uparrow1$，并对所有 $S$ 取上确界，得到 $L\ge E[X]$。与起初的不等式合在一起就证明了定理，没有预先假定另一条期望与极限的交换规则。

## Truncations and nonnegative sums
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*截断与非负无穷和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any nonnegative $X$, the capped variables $X\wedge n=\min(X,n)$ increase to $X$. Thus $E[X\wedge n]\uparrow E[X]$. This lets us handle a difficult unbounded quantity through bounded approximations. A random variable that is finite at every outcome can still have infinite expectation: if $U$ is [[连续均匀分布|uniform on $(0,1)$]] and $X=1/U$, then $X$ is finite on every outcome but
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意非负 $X$，截断变量 $X\wedge n=\min(X,n)$ 递增到 $X$，所以 $E[X\wedge n]\uparrow E[X]$。这样可以先处理有界近似，再恢复难处理的无界量。逐个结果都取有限值的变量，仍可能有无穷期望：若 $U$ 在 $(0,1)$ 上[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布]]，$X=1/U$ 在每个结果上有限，但

$$
E[X\wedge n]=\int_0^{1/n}n\,du+\int_{1/n}^{1}\frac1u\,du
=1+\ln n\longrightarrow+\infty.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The density is one, and [[自然对数|the natural logarithm]] supplies the second integral. The theorem identifies the infinite limit as $E[X]$; it does not force a finite answer. Another use is a nonnegative series: its partial sums $S_m=\sum_{k=1}^mY_k$ increase to the full sum. Combining finite additivity of nonnegative expectations with this theorem gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

密度为一，第二段积分由[[自然对数]]算出。定理把这个无穷极限认作 $E[X]$，并不会强迫答案有限。另一个用途是非负级数：部分和 $S_m=\sum_{k=1}^mY_k$ 递增到完整的和。将非负期望的有限可加性与本定理结合，得到

$$
E\!\left[\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}Y_k\right]
=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}E[Y_k],\qquad Y_k\ge0,
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
with $+\infty$ allowed on both sides. [[随机计数的指标和|Counting with sums of indicators]] is a concrete instance; independence is unnecessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两边都允许为 $+\infty$。[[随机计数的指标和]]是一个具体应用，不需要独立性。

> [!warning] Check the direction and the individual paths · 检查方向与逐条路径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Nonnegative variables that merely converge almost surely need not have converging expectations; [[Fatou引理|Fatou's lemma]] explains the lower bound that survives and its rare-spike counterexample. A sequence of averages that increases is not enough to apply the present theorem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 非负变量如果只是几乎必然收敛，其期望未必随之收敛；[[Fatou引理]]说明此时仍能保留的下界，并给出稀有尖峰反例。仅仅平均数构成递增序列，不足以使用本定理。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a decreasing sequence $0\le X_n\downarrow X$, the extra assumption $E[X_1]<\infty$ does give $E[X_n]\downarrow E[X]$: apply this theorem to $X_1-X_n\uparrow X_1-X$, then subtract finite expectations. Without that assumption, $Y_n=U^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}$ decreases to zero for every $U>0$, but $E[Y_n]=+\infty$ for every $n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对递减序列 $0\le X_n\downarrow X$，加上 $E[X_1]<\infty$ 才可保证 $E[X_n]\downarrow E[X]$：对 $X_1-X_n\uparrow X_1-X$ 使用本定理，再做有限期望的减法即可。没有这个额外条件时，$Y_n=U^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}$ 对每个 $U>0$ 都递减到零，但每个 $n$ 都有 $E[Y_n]=+\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expectation is integration against probability: $E[X]=\int X\,dP$. More generally, the same theorem holds for nonnegative measurable functions under a measure $\mu$, with $\int f_n\,d\mu\uparrow\int f\,d\mu$ when $f_n\uparrow f$ almost everywhere. “Almost everywhere” means outside a set of $\mu$-measure zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期望就是按概率积分：$E[X]=\int X\,dP$。更一般地，若非负可测函数在测度 $\mu$ 下满足 $f_n\uparrow f$ 几乎处处成立，同样有 $\int f_n\,d\mu\uparrow\int f\,d\mu$。“几乎处处”是指只允许在 $\mu$ 测度为零的集合上例外。
