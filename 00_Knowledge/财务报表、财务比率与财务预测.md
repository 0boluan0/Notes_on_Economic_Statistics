# 财务报表、财务比率与财务预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Financial Statements, Ratio Analysis, and Forecasting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把三张财务报表还原成企业怎样赚钱、占用资本、生成现金和承担融资压力的连贯故事。
> **具体锚点：** 净利润增长而经营现金流下降，可能来自应收账款和存货上升；只看利润率会错过现金占用。
> **核心难点：** 比率是分子分母共同变化的结果，必须与行业、会计政策、周期和一次性项目对照。
> **为什么重要：** 估值、融资、信用和资本预算都依赖对历史报表质量与未来驱动的判断。
> **继续：** 先走“利润—资产负债—现金”勾稽，再用杜邦和增长模型；估值见 [[股票与企业价值评估|证券估值：债券与股票]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It turns the three financial statements into one coherent account of how a business earns profit, employs capital, generates cash, and bears financing pressure.
> **Concrete anchor:** Net income can rise while operating cash flow falls because receivables and inventory increase; looking only at margins misses the cash absorbed.
> **Central difficulty:** A ratio reflects changes in both numerator and denominator and must be interpreted against industry structure, accounting policy, the cycle, and one-off items.
> **Why it matters:** Valuation, financing, credit analysis, and capital budgeting all depend on judging historical reporting quality and future operating drivers.
> **Continue:** First reconcile profit, balance-sheet movements, and cash; then use DuPont and growth models. For valuation, see [[股票与企业价值评估|Equity and Enterprise Valuation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三张报表的勾稽
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reconciling the three statements*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产负债表是时点存量，利润表是期间经营成果，现金流量表解释现金变化。净利润经非现金项目和营运资本调整形成经营现金流；期末留存收益由期初留存、净利润和分红连接。检查勾稽比孤立背科目更能发现问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The balance sheet reports stocks at a point in time, the income statement reports performance over a period, and the cash-flow statement explains the change in cash. Net income becomes operating cash flow after adjustments for non-cash items and working capital; ending retained earnings connect beginning retained earnings, net income, and dividends. Reconciliation reveals more than memorizing accounts in isolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析顺序应保持因果方向：收入和费用形成利润；赊销、采购、存货与投资活动改变资产负债表；现金流量表说明这些确认何时真正收到或支付现金。若三张表不能勾稽，先查口径或数据错误，不要继续算精细比率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the causal order clear: revenue and expenses produce profit; credit sales, purchases, inventory, and investment change the balance sheet; the cash-flow statement shows when recognized amounts actually become cash receipts or payments. If the statements do not reconcile, investigate definitions or data errors before computing elaborate ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一套最小勾稽检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A minimum reconciliation checklist*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 检查资产是否等于负债加权益。
2. 用“期初现金 + 三类现金流 = 期末现金”连接现金流量表与资产负债表。
3. 用“期初留存收益 + 净利润 − 分红 = 期末留存收益”连接利润表与权益。
4. 从净利润加回折旧等非现金费用，再扣除经营性资产增加、加上经营性负债增加，解释经营现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Check that assets equal liabilities plus equity.
2. Link the cash-flow statement to the balance sheet through beginning cash plus the three categories of cash flow equals ending cash.
3. Link the income statement to equity through beginning retained earnings plus net income minus dividends equals ending retained earnings.
4. Starting from net income, add back non-cash expenses such as depreciation, subtract increases in operating assets, and add increases in operating liabilities to explain operating cash flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 偿债、营运与盈利比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solvency, operating, and profitability ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动/速动比率看短期覆盖，周转率看资产占用，利润率和 ROA 看经营产出。比率应使用匹配的期间流量与平均存量，且要识别季节性、负分母和会计分类变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current and quick ratios examine short-term coverage, turnover ratios examine asset use, and margins and ROA examine operating output. Ratios should match period flows to average stocks and account for seasonality, negative denominators, and accounting reclassifications.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要把比率当作结论；把它拆回驱动。应收周转变慢可能是客户质量恶化、主动放宽信用、期末确认收入或行业季节性。流动比率提高也可能来自滞销存货，而不是偿债能力改善。每个异常比率至少要回到账龄、数量、价格或合同条款核验一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not treat a ratio as a conclusion; unpack its drivers. Slower receivables turnover may reflect weaker customers, deliberately looser credit, period-end revenue recognition, or industry seasonality. A higher current ratio may result from unsold inventory rather than improved debt-paying capacity. Every unusual ratio should be checked at least once against aging, quantities, prices, or contractual terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 杜邦分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*DuPont decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$ROE=(净利润/收入)\times(收入/资产)\times(资产/权益)$，把盈利能力、营运效率和财务杠杆分开。ROE 高可能来自高净利率，也可能只是权益很薄；需同时看风险、现金和可持续性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$ROE=(\text{net income}/\text{revenue})\times(\text{revenue}/\text{assets})\times(\text{assets}/\text{equity})$, separating profitability, operating efficiency, and financial leverage. High ROE may come from a high net margin or simply from a thin equity base; risk, cash, and sustainability must be examined alongside it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若公司净利率从 8% 降到 6%、总资产周转率从 1.0 升到 1.2、权益乘数从 2 升到 3，则 ROE 从 $8\%\times1.0\times2=16\%$ 升到 $6\%\times1.2\times3=21.6\%$。表面改善完全掩盖了利润率下降，并主要来自杠杆增加；这正是分解而非只读总数的价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a company's net margin falls from 8% to 6%, total-asset turnover rises from 1.0 to 1.2, and its equity multiplier rises from 2 to 3, ROE increases from $8\%\times1.0\times2=16\%$ to $6\%\times1.2\times3=21.6\%$. The headline improvement conceals a lower margin and comes mainly from higher leverage; that is why decomposition matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 盈利质量与现金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Earnings quality and cash*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较净利润与经营现金流、应收/存货增长、资本化政策、非经常损益和拨备。现金流也可被延迟付款、保理或一次性营运资本释放美化，因此没有单一“质量指标”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare net income with operating cash flow, growth in receivables and inventory, capitalization policy, non-recurring gains and losses, and provisions. Cash flow can also be flattered by delayed payment, factoring, or one-off working-capital release, so no single quality metric is decisive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

盈利质量不是“利润必须等于现金”。信用销售、存货建立和折旧都会造成正常时点差异；需要判断差异是否能由商业模式解释、是否反复反向结算，以及管理层是否通过估计与交易时点持续推迟坏消息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Earnings quality does not require profit to equal cash. Credit sales, inventory buildup, and depreciation create normal timing differences. The task is to ask whether the differences follow from the business model, whether they reverse over time, and whether management repeatedly uses estimates and transaction timing to defer bad news.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 增长与外部融资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Growth and external financing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

销售增长会带动营运资本和长期资产需求，留存利润提供内部融资。可持续增长率在利润率、周转、杠杆和留存政策稳定等条件下近似 $ROE\times 留存率$；条件变动时不能机械外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sales growth creates demand for working capital and long-lived assets, while retained earnings supply internal finance. Under stable margins, turnover, leverage, and retention policy, the sustainable growth rate is approximately $ROE\times\text{retention ratio}$; it must not be extrapolated mechanically when those conditions change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“可持续”不是道德评价，而是在不发行新股且关键财务政策不变时能与资产和融资同步扩张的增长率。实际增长更快时，缺口必须由更高利润率、更快周转、更高杠杆、更少分红或外部股权中的至少一项填补。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sustainable is not a moral judgment; it is the growth rate at which assets and financing can expand together without issuing new equity and while key financial policies remain unchanged. If actual growth is faster, at least one of higher margins, faster turnover, higher leverage, lower payouts, or external equity must fill the gap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 财务预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Financial forecasting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从收入量价、成本率、周转天数、资本开支和融资假设建立驱动模型，让报表自动勾稽。基准、上行和下行情景比单点直线外推更能显示敏感性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Build a driver model from revenue volume and price, cost ratios, turnover days, capital expenditure, and financing assumptions so that the statements reconcile automatically. Base, upside, and downside scenarios reveal sensitivity better than one-point linear extrapolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个稳健预测按以下顺序推进：先预测经营驱动，再生成利润表；由应收、存货、应付和资本开支驱动资产负债表；计算现金流和融资缺口；最后让利息、债务和现金循环收敛。若先硬填净利润，再倒挤所有科目，模型很难解释也无法做反证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A robust forecast proceeds in this order: forecast operating drivers and build the income statement; use receivables, inventory, payables, and capital expenditure to drive the balance sheet; calculate cash flow and the financing gap; finally iterate interest, debt, and cash until the circularity converges. If net income is imposed first and every other account is forced to fit, the model becomes hard to explain or falsify.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用期末资产除全年收入而同行用平均资产，会制造不可比的周转率。
- ROE 上升但净债务、利息负担和现金转化恶化时，不应把杠杆效果写成经营改善。
- 把营运资本占收入比例固定，却同时预测客户账期或库存策略大改，假设彼此冲突。
- 预测表不满足会计恒等式，通常说明缺少融资、现金或权益连接，而不是“小数误差”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Dividing full-year revenue by ending assets when peers use average assets creates incomparable turnover ratios.
- If ROE rises while net debt, interest burden, and cash conversion deteriorate, do not describe the leverage effect as operating improvement.
- Holding working capital as a fixed share of revenue while forecasting major changes in customer terms or inventory policy creates inconsistent assumptions.
- A forecast that violates accounting identities usually lacks a financing, cash, or equity link; it is not merely a rounding issue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 净利润增长但经营现金流恶化时应先检查什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What should be checked first when net income rises but operating cash flow deteriorates?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 应收、存货、应付等营运资本变化，非现金收益和一次性项目，以及收入确认质量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Changes in receivables, inventory, payables, and other working-capital accounts; non-cash gains and one-off items; and the quality of revenue recognition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### ROE 上升为何不一定是好消息？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is an increase in ROE not necessarily good news?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它可能来自盈利/效率改善，也可能来自权益缩小和杠杆上升；杜邦分解和风险指标可区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It may come from better profitability or efficiency, but it may instead come from a smaller equity base and higher leverage; DuPont decomposition and risk measures distinguish the sources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 财务比率比较前为什么要统一口径？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must ratio definitions be standardized before comparison?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 行业、会计政策、期间、平均/期末存量和一次性项目不同会让比率不可比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Differences in industry, accounting policy, period, average-versus-ending stocks, and one-off items can make the ratios incomparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：预测增长为什么会自动产生融资问题？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does forecasting growth automatically create a financing question?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 增长通常需要更多应收、存货和固定资产；若新增经营现金和留存利润不足以支持这些资产，差额必须由债务、股权或更慢增长填补。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Growth usually requires more receivables, inventory, and fixed assets. If incremental operating cash and retained earnings cannot support them, the gap must be filled by debt, equity, or slower growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
