# 市场集中度描述界定市场的业务量集中在少数经营者手中的程度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market concentration describes how much activity in a defined market is concentrated among a small number of operators.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场集中度描述[[市场份额]]的分布，既与经营者数量有关，也与份额是否悬殊有关。它是一类结构特征，不是某一个固定指标；常用的[[行业集中率]]看前几家合计份额，[[HHI指数]]利用所有经营者的份额平方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market concentration describes the distribution of [[市场份额|market shares]], reflecting both the number of operators and their unequal sizes. It is a structural characteristic rather than one fixed statistic. A [[行业集中率|concentration ratio]] aggregates the largest firms, whereas the [[HHI指数|HHI]] uses all firms' squared shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四家各占 25%，与一家占 70%、其余各占 10%，经营者数量相同，但集中程度不同。比较行业或年份时，产品地域边界、计量指标和企业合并口径必须相容；分支机构不能在一个样本中算独立企业、另一个样本中又按集团合并。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Four equal firms differ from a market with shares of 70%, 10%, 10%, and 10%, despite having the same firm count. Comparisons require compatible product and geographic boundaries, measures, and aggregation rules. Branches should not be treated as independent firms in one sample and consolidated groups in another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集中度是竞争分析的线索，不是垄断利润或违法行为的证明；见[[集中度与竞争强度]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Concentration is evidence for competitive analysis, not proof of monopoly profit or unlawful conduct; see [[集中度与竞争强度|concentration versus competitive intensity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
