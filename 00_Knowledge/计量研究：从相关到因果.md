# 计量研究：从相关到因果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Econometric research: from association to causation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把一个现实问题拆成可观察数据、目标参数、识别假设和估计方法，区分“看到共同变化”与“知道干预会造成什么”。
> **具体锚点：** 受教育年限高的人工资更高，可能是教育作用，也可能是能力、家庭背景或自我选择；回归本身不会替我们区分。
> **核心难点：** 识别问数据加假设能否唯一确定目标因果量，估计问有限样本怎样近似它；两者不是同一步。
> **为什么重要：** 没有研究设计，精确标准误只会精确描述一个不清楚的对象。
> **继续：** 描述性线性关系进入 [[OLS 线性回归]]；因果威胁进入 [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM]] 或 [[双重差分法（DID）]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Econometric research turns a real-world question into observable data, a target parameter, identification assumptions, and an estimation method, separating “variables move together” from “an intervention causes an outcome.”
> **Concrete anchor:** People with more education earn higher wages, but this may reflect education, ability, family background, or self-selection. A regression cannot distinguish these explanations by itself.
> **Central difficulty:** Identification asks whether data plus assumptions uniquely determine the target causal quantity. Estimation asks how a finite sample approximates that quantity. These are different steps.
> **Why it matters:** Without a research design, precise standard errors merely describe an ill-defined object with precision.
> **Continue with:** For descriptive linear relationships, proceed to [[OLS 线性回归|OLS linear regression]]. For threats to causal interpretation, use [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM|endogeneity, identification, IV, 2SLS, and GMM]] or [[双重差分法（DID）|difference-in-differences]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从问题到 estimand
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From a question to an estimand*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把“教育有没有用”改写成对象、干预、结果、时间范围和目标总体，例如平均处理效应 $E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$。estimand 是想知道的量，estimator 是用数据计算它的规则，estimate 是本次样本得到的数值。三者混用会掩盖研究目标变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First rewrite “Does education help?” in terms of units, intervention, outcome, time horizon, and target population—for example, the average treatment effect $E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$. The estimand is the quantity of interest, the estimator is the rule that computes it from data, and the estimate is the value obtained in the present sample. Confusing the three can conceal a change in the research target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反事实与根本缺失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterfactuals and the fundamental missing outcome*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个单位在同一时点不能同时观察处理和未处理结果，这就是因果推断的根本缺失。随机实验通过处理分配使两组在期望上可比；观察数据必须用条件独立、工具变量、平行趋势、断点等设计假设构造反事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The treated and untreated outcomes of the same unit cannot both be observed at the same time; this is the fundamental missing-outcome problem of causal inference. Randomized experiments make groups comparable in expectation through treatment assignment. Observational studies must construct counterfactuals using design assumptions such as conditional independence, instrumental variables, parallel trends, or regression discontinuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件期望与回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional expectations and regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E[Y\mid X=x]$ 描述在数据中 X 取 x 的人平均结果。回归可总结条件均值或最佳线性预测，但只有在外生性/可忽略性等附加条件下，系数才能解释为改变 X 的因果效应。控制变量不是越多越好：后处理变量、碰撞点和测量误差变量可能引入偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$E[Y\mid X=x]$ describes the average observed outcome among units with $X=x$. A regression can summarize a conditional mean or best linear predictor, but its coefficient represents the causal effect of changing $X$ only under additional conditions such as exogeneity or ignorability. More controls are not always better: post-treatment variables, colliders, and variables measured with error can introduce bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 识别、估计与推断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identification, estimation, and inference*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别是在总体分布和假设下能否确定参数；估计处理有限样本误差；推断量化估计不确定性。大样本不能修复未识别，稳健标准误也不能修复遗漏变量偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identification asks whether the parameter is determined by the population distribution and assumptions. Estimation handles finite-sample error. Inference quantifies uncertainty in the estimate. A large sample cannot repair nonidentification, and robust standard errors cannot repair omitted-variable bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 研究设计的证据链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The chain of evidence in a research design*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一项可信研究应连贯回答：数据怎样产生、对照是谁、为何可比、处理时点和暴露如何定义、主要威胁是什么、哪些检验能发现部分失败、结论适用于谁。检验通常不能证明核心不可检验假设，只能寻找与其不相容的迹象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credible study should answer coherently how the data were generated, who serves as the comparison group, why the groups are comparable, how treatment timing and exposure are defined, what the main threats are, which tests can detect particular failures, and to whom the conclusion applies. Tests usually cannot prove a core untestable assumption; they can only seek evidence incompatible with it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 预测与因果的分工
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The distinct roles of prediction and causation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测关心未见样本上的误差，允许使用结果的强代理；因果关心干预效应，需要避免坏控制和反向因果。一个模型可预测很好却给出错误政策效应，也可因果解释清楚但预测能力有限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prediction focuses on error for unseen observations and may use strong proxies for the outcome. Causal analysis focuses on intervention effects and must avoid bad controls and reverse causality. A model can predict well while giving a wrong policy effect, or identify a causal effect clearly while having limited predictive power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分 estimand、estimator 和 estimate。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish estimand, estimator, and estimate in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> estimand 是目标总体量；estimator 是从样本到该量的计算规则；estimate 是规则作用于当前数据后的具体数值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The estimand is the target population quantity. The estimator is the rule mapping a sample to that quantity. The estimate is the specific value obtained when the rule is applied to the current data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 为什么增加样本不能解决遗漏变量导致的因果偏差？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a larger sample not solve causal bias caused by an omitted variable?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 样本增大让估计更精确地靠近错误的总体回归对象，却不会恢复缺失的可比性或识别条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A larger sample makes the estimate converge more precisely to the wrong population regression object. It does not restore the missing comparability or identification condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 面对一个回归结果，判断因果性的第一问是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the first causal question to ask about a regression result?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 处理/解释变量怎样产生，以及在没有处理时比较组是否能代表同一反事实；不是先看 p 值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ask how the treatment or explanatory variable was generated and whether the comparison group represents the same untreated counterfactual. Do not begin with the p-value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
