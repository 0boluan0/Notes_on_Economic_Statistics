# 道氏指数确认要求工业与运输指数都越过各自对应转折位

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dow index confirmation requires both industrial and transportation indexes to cross their own corresponding turning-point levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

道氏指数确认是[[道氏理论]]中的联合识别条件：先在工业与运输指数各自的走势中确定对应前峰或前谷，再要求两者都给出同向突破。看涨确认考察各自向上越过参考峰位；看跌确认考察各自向下跌破参考谷位。仅一个指数突破，还不满足双指数确认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Index confirmation is a joint condition in [[道氏理论|Dow theory]]. Identify the corresponding prior peaks or troughs separately in the industrial and transportation indexes, then require matching directional crossings. A bullish reading crosses each reference peak upward; a bearish reading crosses each reference trough downward. One index alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个参考位不必具有相同数值，突破也不必发生在同一天。原图中工业指数先在A点突破，运输指数到B点才突破：联合条件只能从较晚的B点成立，不能事后回填到A点。采用课程收盘口径时，还须等相应收盘价可得，执行另按[[交易信号时序]]处理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reference values need not be equal and the crossings need not occur on the same day. In the course figure, the industrial index crosses at A and transportation confirms at B. The joint condition is available only at B, not retrospectively at A. A closing-price implementation must also wait for the relevant close, with execution governed separately by [[交易信号时序|trading signal timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程并未给出两次确认之间唯一有效的最大间隔；用于研究时必须事先固定等待期限、参考位更新和失效条件。这是该学派过滤单指数信号的规则，不是两个指数能保证预测未来的证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course supplies no unique maximum interval between confirmations. A study must predefine the waiting period, reference-level updates and invalidation conditions. This is a filter within the school, not proof that two indexes guarantee a forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
