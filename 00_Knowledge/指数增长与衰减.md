# Exponential growth and decay
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指数增长与衰减*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Exponential growth or decay** means that the instantaneous change is proportional to the amount currently present. At the same proportional rate, twice the amount changes twice as fast. For example, a rate of $0.2$ per hour gives an instantaneous increase of $20$ units per hour when the amount is $100$, and $40$ units per hour when it is $200$. The absolute increase is changing even though the proportional rate is fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**指数增长或衰减**表示：瞬时变化速度与当前已有的数量成正比。在相同比例率下，数量翻倍，变化速度也翻倍。例如，比例率为每小时 $0.2$ 时，数量为 $100$ 的瞬时增长速度是每小时 $20$ 单位；数量为 $200$ 时则是每小时 $40$ 单位。比例率虽然固定，绝对增加速度却在变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $t$ be time, $y(t)$ the amount, and $y_0$ its value at the initial time $t_0$. The [[导数|derivative]] $y'(t)=dy/dt$ measures instantaneous change per unit time. With a constant real parameter $r$, the model is the [[常微分方程|ordinary differential equation]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $t$ 为时间，$y(t)$ 为数量，$y_0$ 为初始时刻 $t_0$ 的数量。[[导数]] $y'(t)=dy/dt$ 衡量每单位时间的瞬时变化。取实常数参数 $r$，模型写成[[常微分方程]]

$$y'(t)=ry(t),\qquad y(t_0)=y_0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an amount or a population, take $y_0\ge0$. The parameter $r$ has units of inverse time, so $r(t-t_0)$ is dimensionless. When $y>0$, division gives $y'/y=r$: the rate per unit currently present. A fixed absolute rate $y'=c$ instead gives a straight-line trajectory $y=y_0+c(t-t_0)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

人口或物质量模型取 $y_0\ge0$。参数 $r$ 的单位是时间的倒数，因此 $r(t-t_0)$ 没有单位。$y>0$ 时可写成 $y'/y=r$，即每单位现有数量的变化率。如果固定的是绝对速度 $y'=c$，得到的则是直线轨迹 $y=y_0+c(t-t_0)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the solution is an exponential
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么解是指数函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] $e^u$ is positive and has derivative equal to itself. Applying the [[链式法则|chain rule]] to the input $r(t-t_0)$ shows that the following function satisfies both the equation and the initial value:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自然指数函数]] $e^u$ 为正，且导数等于自身。对内层输入 $r(t-t_0)$ 使用[[链式法则]]，可以核对下面的函数同时满足方程与初值：

$$y(t)=y_0e^{r(t-t_0)},\qquad
y'(t)=r y_0e^{r(t-t_0)}=ry(t),\qquad y(t_0)=y_0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is also the only solution with that initial value on an interval containing $t_0$. For any solution, the [[乘积法则|product rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它也是包含 $t_0$ 的区间上满足该初值的唯一解。对任意解，[[乘积法则]]给出

$$\frac{d}{dt}\left(e^{-r(t-t_0)}y(t)\right)
=e^{-r(t-t_0)}\bigl(y'(t)-ry(t)\bigr)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A function with zero derivative throughout an interval is constant, by the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]]. Evaluating this constant at $t_0$ gives $y_0$, which recovers the stated formula. No division by $y$ was used, so the zero solution is included. If [[分离变量|separation of variables]] is used instead, retain $y\equiv0$ before dividing by $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[拉格朗日中值定理]]，在整个区间上导数为零的函数是常数。在 $t_0$ 代入，常数就是 $y_0$，于是还原出上面的公式。这条证明没有除以 $y$，所以包含零解。如果改用[[分离变量]]，则要先保留 $y\equiv0$，再除以 $y$。

## Read the sign and the time scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*读懂正负号与时间尺度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $y_0>0$, positive $r$ makes $y$ strictly increase without bound as $t\to+\infty$; negative $r$ makes it strictly decrease towards zero while remaining positive at every finite time. If $r=0$, the amount stays at $y_0$. If $y_0=0$, it stays zero for every $r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $y_0>0$ 时，$r>0$ 使 $y$ 严格递增，并在 $t\to+\infty$ 时无界增长；$r<0$ 使它严格递减趋零，但在每个有限时刻仍为正。$r=0$ 时数量保持为 $y_0$；$y_0=0$ 时，无论 $r$ 是多少都保持为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive trajectory and a time interval of length $\Delta$, the ratio $y(t+\Delta)/y(t)=e^{r\Delta}$ is independent of the starting time and amount. Equal time intervals therefore multiply the amount by the same factor. To find the doubling time $T$ for growth, solve $e^{rT}=2$; to find the half-life for decay, solve $e^{rT}=1/2$. Taking the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], the inverse of the exponential, gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正轨迹，长度为 $\Delta$ 的时间间隔满足 $y(t+\Delta)/y(t)=e^{r\Delta}$，与起始时刻和数量无关。因此相同时间间隔把数量乘以相同倍数。增长的倍增时间 $T$ 由 $e^{rT}=2$ 求出，衰减的半衰期由 $e^{rT}=1/2$ 求出。取指数的反函数——[[自然对数]]，得到

$$T_{\mathrm{double}}=\frac{\ln2}{r}\quad(r>0),\qquad
T_{\mathrm{half}}=\frac{\ln2}{|r|}\quad(r<0).$$

> [!example] Same factor, different absolute changes · 相同倍数，不同绝对变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $r=(\ln2)/3$ per day and $y(0)=100$, then $y(3)=200$ and $y(6)=400$: each three-day interval doubles the amount, but the absolute increases are $100$ and $200$. With $r=-(\ln2)/3$ per day, the sequence is instead $100,50,25$. Doubling time and half-life describe positive changing trajectories; a zero or constant trajectory has no unique positive time to double or halve.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $r=(\ln2)/3$ 每天，且 $y(0)=100$，则 $y(3)=200$、$y(6)=400$：每三天翻倍，但两段绝对增加量分别是 $100$ 与 $200$。若 $r=-(\ln2)/3$ 每天，则依次为 $100,50,25$。倍增时间与半衰期描述的是正在变化的正轨迹；零轨迹或常轨迹没有唯一的正倍增时间或半衰期。

> [!warning] An instantaneous percentage is not a finite-period percentage · 瞬时比例不等于整段百分比
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over one time unit, the relative change is $e^r-1$, not $r$. With $r=0.2$ per hour, one hour increases a positive amount by approximately $22.14\%$. A measured finite-period increase of $p$ over duration $\Delta>0$ corresponds to $r=\ln(1+p)/\Delta$ under this model, provided $p>-1$. Use consistent time units.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 经过一单位时间，相对变化是 $e^r-1$，并不是 $r$。比例率为每小时 $0.2$ 时，一小时内正的数量约增加 $22.14\%$。在这个模型下，若时长 $\Delta>0$ 内观测到相对增加量 $p$，则 $r=\ln(1+p)/\Delta$，要求 $p>-1$。时间单位必须一致。

## The mathematical formula and the model assumption
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*数学公式与模型假设*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula solves the mathematical equation for every real $y_0$ and all real times. For $y_0<0$, positive $r$ increases $|y|$ but makes the signed value more negative; negative $r$ makes it rise towards zero from below. Population and material amounts use the nonnegative part of this mathematical family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数学上，同一公式适用于任意实初值 $y_0$，并在全部实数时间上有定义。若 $y_0<0$，正 $r$ 使 $|y|$ 增大，却让带符号的数值更负；负 $r$ 则使它从下方向零上升。人口与物质量模型使用这个数学解族中的非负部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous population variable approximates a discrete count. The model additionally assumes that the proportional rate stays constant; it has no capacity feedback or external forcing. A short exponential-looking segment does not prove that this mechanism persists indefinitely. [[Logistic增长|Logistic growth]] introduces a proportional rate that changes with the current amount.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续人口变量是对离散计数的近似。模型还假设比例率始终不变，没有容量反馈或外部强迫项。一小段近似指数的轨迹，不能证明这种机制会永远持续。[[Logistic增长]]进一步让比例率随当前规模变化。
