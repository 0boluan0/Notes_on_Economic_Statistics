# 指数增长与衰减模型以恒定比例率描述非负规模随时间的变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The exponential growth and decay model describes a nonnegative quantity changing at a constant proportional rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子模型
> 对人口、物质量等非负规模，取初始值 $y_0\ge0$ 和常数比例率 $r$，模型为
> $$
> y'(t)=ry(t),\qquad y(t_0)=y_0
> $$
> 其解为
> $$
> y(t)=y_0e^{r(t-t_0)}.
> $$
> 当 $y_0>0$ 时，沿时间增加的方向，$r>0$ 使规模指数增长，$r<0$ 使规模衰减趋向零；$r=0$ 或 $y_0=0$ 时轨迹恒定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a nonnegative quantity with $y_0\ge0$, the model $y'=ry$, $y(t_0)=y_0$ has solution $y(t)=y_0e^{r(t-t_0)}$. When $y_0>0$, positive $r$ gives forward growth and negative $r$ decay toward zero. If $r=0$ or $y_0=0$, the path is constant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方程在正解上也可写成 $y'/y=r$，所以 $r$ 是每单位现有规模的瞬时增长率，单位是“每时间”。当 $r>0$ 时，解 $e^{rT}=2$ 得倍增时间 $T=\ln2/r$；当 $r<0$ 时，解 $e^{rT}=1/2$ 得半衰期 $T=\ln2/|r|$。这些时间与正的初始规模无关，正是比例变化的特征。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On positive solutions, $y'/y=r$, whose units are inverse time. Solving $e^{rT}=2$ gives the doubling time $\ln2/r$ for $r>0$; solving $e^{rT}=1/2$ gives the half-life $\ln2/|r|$ for $r<0$. Neither depends on the positive initial scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作为数学方程，$y'=ry$ 的同一公式也适用于任意实初值，并在整个实线上有定义。若 $y_0<0$，$r>0$ 时 $y$ 向更负的方向下降而 $|y|$ 增长；$r<0$ 时 $y$ 从下方上升到零而 $|y|$ 衰减。因此“指数幅度增长”与“函数值递增”不能在负轨迹上混用；人口或物质量模型则使用前面的非负范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As a mathematical equation, $y'=ry$ admits the same formula for every real initial value and every real time. For $y_0<0$, positive $r$ makes $y$ more negative while its magnitude grows; negative $r$ makes $y$ increase toward zero while its magnitude decays. Growth of magnitude and increase of the signed value are different statements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型假设 $r$ 恒定且不存在容量、反馈或外部冲击。观察到一段近似指数轨迹不能证明该机制永久成立；若增长率随规模下降，[[Logistic增长]] 等受限模型可能更合适。零初值给零解，分离时不能因除以 $y$ 而遗漏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model assumes a constant proportional rate with no capacity feedback or shocks. A short exponential-looking segment does not establish that mechanism indefinitely; state-dependent rates may call for a bounded model. The zero initial value gives the zero solution and must survive any separation step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么固定的绝对增量不是指数增长？
>
> **答案：** 指数模型固定的是相对增量 $y'/y$；固定绝对增量对应 $y'=c$，产生线性轨迹。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does a constant absolute rate of change not describe exponential growth?
>
> **Answer:** The exponential model holds the proportional rate $y'/y$ constant. A constant absolute rate gives $y'=c$, producing a linear trajectory.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
