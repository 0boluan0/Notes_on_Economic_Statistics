# Response scales and linear transformations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*响应尺度与线性变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing a score from points to tenths of a point changes its numerical coefficient. It need not change the evidence for the same scientific hypothesis. The distinction is between **rewriting the same response information** and **asking a different question with the new numbers**. An invertible linear transformation lets us recover every original response, but hypotheses and uncertainty must be rewritten with it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把成绩从“分”改成“十分之一分”，系数的数值会改变，却未必改变对同一科学假设的证据。关键在于区分**把同一响应信息换一种写法**，与**拿新数值改问另一个问题**。可逆线性变换能恢复全部原始响应，但假设与不确定性也必须一起改写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What changes when the response table is multiplied
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*右乘响应表时，哪些对象跟着改变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[多响应线性回归|multivariate regression]], $Y=XB+U$ has $n$ observations and $q$ response columns. Choose a fixed real invertible $q\times q$ matrix $T$ and set $Y^*=YT$. Each column of $T$ gives one new linear combination of the old responses. Invertibility means an [[逆矩阵|inverse]] $T^{-1}$ recovers $Y=Y^*T^{-1}$. Substitution gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[多响应线性回归]] $Y=XB+U$ 中，$Y$ 有 $n$ 条观测、$q$ 个响应列。选固定、实数、可逆的 $q\times q$ 矩阵 $T$，令 $Y^*=YT$；$T$ 每列给出一个新的响应线性组合。可逆意味着存在[[逆矩阵]] $T^{-1}$，可恢复 $Y=Y^*T^{-1}$。代入得到

$$Y^*=X(BT)+UT,\qquad B^*=BT,\qquad U^*=UT.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A row's error written as a column vector becomes $u_i^*=T^Tu_i$. If its finite covariance is $\Sigma$, the [[协方差矩阵|covariance definition]] therefore gives $\Sigma^*=T^T\Sigma T$. These formulas change response coordinates; they do not rearrange observations or change the predictor design. With [[共享设计的逐响应OLS|shared-design unweighted OLS]], the fitted table and residuals satisfy $\hat Y^*=\hat YT$ and $R^*=RT$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把一行误差写成列向量后，有 $u_i^*=T^Tu_i$；若原有限协方差为 $\Sigma$，[[协方差矩阵|协方差定义]]给出 $\Sigma^*=T^T\Sigma T$。这些公式改变响应坐标，没有重排观测或改变预测设计。使用[[共享设计的逐响应OLS|共享设计的不加权 OLS]]，有 $\hat Y^*=\hat YT$、$R^*=RT$，所以

$$E^*=(R^*)^TR^*=T^TET,\qquad E=R^TR.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $X$ has full column rank, the unique coefficient estimate also satisfies $\hat B^*=\hat BT$. With deficient rank, fitted values remain unique and transforming any OLS coefficient solution gives an OLS solution for the new responses; a separately chosen arbitrary representative need not coincide. This equivariance follows from the shared projection of each response column and needs no Gaussian assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X$ 满列秩时，唯一系数估计也满足 $\hat B^*=\hat BT$。秩亏时，拟合值仍唯一，任意一个 OLS 系数解右乘 $T$ 后都是新响应的解；另外任意选出的系数代表却未必恰好相等。这种对应来自每列共享同一个投影，不要求 Gaussian 假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Carry the hypothesis through the change of units
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把假设一起带到新单位中*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write a fixed [[多响应一般线性假设|linear hypothesis]] as $CBA=D$: $C$ combines predictor-side coefficients, $A$ combines response columns, and $D$ is the proposed value. Since $B=B^*T^{-1}$, the very same hypothesis is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把固定的[[多响应一般线性假设]]写成 $CBA=D$，其中 $C$ 组合预测侧系数，$A$ 组合响应列，$D$ 是假设值。由于 $B=B^*T^{-1}$，完全相同的假设在新坐标中是

$$CB^*A^*=D,\qquad A^*=T^{-1}A.$$

> [!example] A difference must keep its units · 差值必须保留原来的单位
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the estimated programme coefficients for two comparable scores are $(2,5)$. Their original difference is $2-5=-3$. Multiply only the first score by $10$, so $T=\operatorname{diag}(10,1)$ and the new coefficients are $(20,5)$. The old contrast $A=(1,-1)^T$ becomes $A^*=(1/10,-1)^T$, giving $20/10-5=-3$.
>
> Keeping $(1,-1)^T$ instead would give $20-5=15$. That is a different weighted difference of the original scores, not a change in the evidence for their original difference. Similarly, multiplying one response by $1000$ rescales only its shared-design OLS coefficient and residual column; it does not change another response's coefficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设两个可比成绩的项目系数估计为 $(2,5)$，原差值是 $-3$。只把第一项成绩乘 $10$，即 $T=\operatorname{diag}(10,1)$，新系数为 $(20,5)$。原对比 $A=(1,-1)^T$ 必须改成 $A^*=(1/10,-1)^T$，才得到 $20/10-5=-3$。如果仍用 $(1,-1)^T$，就变成 $20-5=15$，问的是原成绩的另一个加权差。类似地，一项响应乘 $1000$，只缩放它在共享设计 OLS 下的系数与残差列，不改变另一响应的系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a full-column-rank design, or an estimable coefficient contrast under rank deficiency, this also protects a lower-dimensional response contrast fixed in advance and transformed correctly: $\Delta^*=C\hat B^*A^*-D=\Delta$, and $(A^*)^TE^*A^*=A^TEA$. Its hypothesis and error SSPs are identical. Coordinate-specific questions can therefore preserve invariance. Leaving their weights unchanged after a coordinate change can, however, change the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在设计满列秩，或秩亏但系数对比可估时，对预先固定的低维响应对比，正确变换同样能保留原问题：$\Delta^*=C\hat B^*A^*-D=\Delta$，且 $(A^*)^TE^*A^*=A^TEA$，假设与误差 SSP 都完全相同。因此具体坐标对比也可以保持不变性；需要检查新坐标下的权重是否仍表示原问题，沿用旧权重可能改变问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the full-response criteria agree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么完整响应空间的统计量相同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a full-response null $CB=D$, use $CB^*=DT$ in the new coordinates. The fitted departure is multiplied by $T$, so the hypothesis SSP becomes $H^*=T^THT$, just as $E^*=T^TET$. Assume the real symmetric error SSP $E$ is positive definite and $H$ positive semidefinite. Applying the [[乘积的逆矩阵|inverse-of-a-product rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对完整响应零假设 $CB=D$，新坐标应写成 $CB^*=DT$。估计偏离右乘 $T$，所以假设 SSP 变成 $H^*=T^THT$，误差 SSP 同样是 $E^*=T^TET$。假设实对称误差 SSP $E$ 正定、$H$ 半正定，由[[乘积的逆矩阵|乘积求逆规则]]得到

$$(E^*)^{-1}H^*=T^{-1}E^{-1}HT.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two comparison matrices are [[相似矩阵|similar]]: the invertible coordinate change preserves their eigenvalues. The [[MANOVA四种统计量|Wilks, Pillai, Hotelling–Lawley and Roy criteria]] depend on those same nonnegative comparison roots, so their values agree. The design, hypothesis dimension, response dimension and residual degrees of freedom also remain the same. Consequently, using the same applicable [[MANOVA检验校准|calibration]] gives the same p-value in exact arithmetic. A numerically ill-conditioned transformation can still affect floating-point calculations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两张比较矩阵[[相似矩阵|相似]]，可逆坐标变化保留特征值。[[MANOVA四种统计量|Wilks、Pillai、Hotelling–Lawley 与 Roy 统计量]]都由这些相同的非负比较根决定，因此数值相同。设计、假设维数、响应维数和残差自由度也保持不变；使用同一个适用的[[MANOVA检验校准|校准方法]]，精确运算下 p 值也相同。数值条件差的变换仍会影响浮点计算。

> [!example] The matrices change, the comparison does not · 矩阵变了，比较结果不变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The four-observation example in [[多响应一般线性假设|the hypothesis card]] gives
> $$H=\begin{bmatrix}4&10\\10&25\end{bmatrix},\qquad E=2I_2.$$
> For $T=\operatorname{diag}(10,1)$,
> $$H^*=\begin{bmatrix}400&100\\100&25\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> E^*=\begin{bmatrix}200&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}.$$
> Both $E^{-1}H$ and $(E^*)^{-1}H^*$ have trace $29/2$ and determinant zero, hence eigenvalues $0$ and $29/2$. Wilks' value is $|E|/|E+H|=4/62=2/31$; after transformation it is $400/6200=2/31$. This equality concerns the same full-response hypothesis, not a claim that this small sample satisfies every scientific assumption.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[多响应一般线性假设|假设卡]]的四条观测给出所列 $H,E$。第一项响应乘 $10$ 后，两张 SSP 分别变成 $H^*,E^*$。变换前后的比较矩阵，迹都为 $29/2$、行列式都为零，所以特征值同为 $0,29/2$。Wilks 从 $4/62$ 变成 $400/6200$，结果都是 $2/31$。这说明同一完整响应假设的数值不变，并不意味着这份小样本自动满足全部科学假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the guarantee stops
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保证的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dropping response columns or keeping only a few [[主成分分析|principal components]] is not invertible: some information cannot be recovered, and the full-space hypothesis has changed. [[PCA尺度选择|Covariance versus correlation PCA]] also changes the weighting of directions. A fixed positive-definite response weight alone still leaves unrestricted shared-design OLS minimizers unchanged, as the [[共享设计的逐响应OLS|OLS card]] proves; response-coupling restrictions or penalties, however, can change that conclusion. Standardization is therefore a modeling choice to interpret, not an automatic cure for unequal units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

删除响应列或只保留少数[[主成分分析|主成分]]不可逆，部分信息无法恢复，完整空间的假设已经改变。[[PCA尺度选择|协方差与相关矩阵 PCA]]也改变方向的权重。仅加入固定正定的响应权重，仍不会改变无约束共享设计 OLS 的最优解，证明见[[共享设计的逐响应OLS|OLS 卡]]；跨响应约束或惩罚则可能改变结论。因此标准化是需要解释的建模选择，不是单位不同的自动补救。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $E$ is singular, the inverse proof above is unavailable. The determinant identity
$$\det\!\left(T^T(H-\lambda E)T\right)=\det(T)^2\det(H-\lambda E)$$
still holds, but the polynomial can even be identically zero; this identity does not create a valid ordinary inverse or a calibrated test. Check the [[误差SSP秩边界|residual-rank boundary]] for the actual procedure. The card assumes fixed linear $T$: nonlinear transformations are different models, and choosing a response direction from the observed strongest result requires [[选择后推断|selection-aware calibration]]. The algebra may still hold for a realized invertible matrix without justifying every subsequent data-selected analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $E$ 奇异，上面的逆矩阵证明不能使用。所列行列式恒等式仍成立，但这个关于 $\lambda$ 的多项式甚至可能恒等于零；等式不会凭空产生普通逆或有效校准。实际程序须检查[[误差SSP秩边界]]。本卡使用固定的线性 $T$；非线性变换属于另一模型，按观察到的最强结果挑响应方向还需要[[选择后推断|选择后的校准]]。某个实际可逆矩阵仍满足代数恒等式，也不代表后续所有依赖数据选择的分析都有推断保证。
