# 科斯定理以明确可交易的权利和无成本有效协商为条件给出外部性内部化的效率基准
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Coase theorem provides an efficiency benchmark for internalisation under clearly defined tradable rights and costless efficient bargaining*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

科斯定理研究这样的基准：相关行为权利明确且可执行，各方能够交易这些权利，并能无成本地达成、执行互利协议。在这些条件下，原本分散在不同人身上的收益和损害可以通过协商进入选择，未利用的互利调整会被完成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Coase theorem studies clearly defined, enforceable and tradable entitlements, with costless completion and enforcement of mutually beneficial agreements. Under these conditions, dispersed benefits and harms can enter the choice through bargaining and available gains from trade are exhausted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推理的核心可以用[[外部性谈判区间]]理解：如果一项减排给渔民带来的好处超过工厂损失，双方就能选择一笔补偿，让工厂愿意改、渔民也愿意付。若已经不存在这样的可行互利调整，才达到这里讨论的效率边界。在准线性、可转移货币收益的模型中，这等价于最大化合并净收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[外部性谈判区间|bargaining range]] explains the mechanism: if fishers gain more than the factory loses, a transfer can make the change acceptable to both. Efficiency is reached when no feasible mutually beneficial adjustment remains. With quasi-linear, transferable monetary returns, this maximises joint net benefit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有效协商”是强条件，不能省略成“产权清楚就必然成功”。识别所有当事人、查明损害、形成协议和执行都可能有[[交易成本]]；私人信息、[[钳制问题]]和[[公共品搭便车|受益者搭便车]]也可能留下谈不成的增益。参与者少、信息容易核实只是更有利于满足条件，不是独立的成功保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficient bargaining is a substantive assumption, not a consequence of clear rights alone. Identifying parties, measuring harm, agreeing and enforcing can incur [[交易成本|transaction costs]]. Private information, [[钳制问题|holdout]] and [[公共品搭便车|free riding]] can leave gains unrealised. Few parties and verifiable information help but do not guarantee success.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个命题不决定初始权利应当归谁，也不决定最终转移额。[[科斯产权不变性|改变权利后数量是否相同]]需要再查财富效应等条件。现实制度选择则比较协商、组织和公共政策各自的收益与成本，而不是从定理直接推出某一种制度永远优越。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem selects neither the initial entitlement nor the final transfer. [[科斯产权不变性|Identical quantities under different entitlements]] require additional conditions such as absent wealth effects. Actual bargaining, organisation and public policy must be compared with their costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
