# 价格与指标出现背离不能单独确定后续价格会持续反转
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Price-indicator divergence alone does not establish a sustained reversal in subsequent prices.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[指标背离]] 是两组已形成转折点之间的比较；没有哪条定义要求下一段价格必须向特定方向运行。因此“顶背离出现”不等于持续下跌已经成为确定结果，“底背离出现”也不等于持续上涨已经确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[指标背离|Divergence]] compares turning points already formed. Its definition places no necessary restriction on the direction of the next price segment. A bearish divergence does not establish a sustained decline, nor does a bullish divergence establish a sustained advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按 [[RSI]] 的三期 Wilder 口径，构造收盘价 $8,10,12,11,13,12,14,13.5,15$。两个价格高点 $13$ 和 $14$ 处的 RSI 分别为 $87.5$ 和约 $80.8824$。两处 RSI 也都高于各自左右一日的有效值：价格高点抬升而指标高点下降，构成顶背离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the three-period Wilder convention in [[RSI]], consider closes of $8,10,12,11,13,12,14,13.5,15$. At the two price highs of $13$ and $14$, RSI is $87.5$ and approximately $80.8824$, respectively. Both RSI readings also exceed their valid neighbours on each side: the higher price high and lower indicator high form a bearish divergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里以左右各一日确认高点，价格与 RSI 的高点在同日对应。价格 $14$ 的高点到下一天收在 $13.5$ 时才确认，而再下一天又收在 $15$。这条完全可计算的路径说明：按已声明的规则确认顶背离后，价格仍可以重新创新高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example confirms highs using one day on each side, with price and RSI peaks aligned on the same dates. The high at $14$ is confirmed only by the following close at $13.5$, yet the next close reaches $15$. Under the stated rule, a confirmed bearish divergence is compatible with a later higher price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是对确定性推断的反例，不是对所有背离策略的绩效评价。若要检验预测价值，需要预先固定识别窗口、对应方式、确认延迟和持有期；看完结果再移动拐点，会让规则选择进入本应独立的测试阶段，见 [[测试集自适应复用]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This refutes a deterministic inference rather than evaluating every divergence strategy. Predictive tests require pre-specified windows, correspondence, confirmation delays, and holding periods. Moving pivots after viewing outcomes brings rule selection into the supposedly independent test phase; see [[测试集自适应复用|adaptive test-set reuse]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
