# Connected subsets of the real line
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*实线连通集的区间刻画*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a line, a missing point between two points of a set creates a way to separate the set. If a set contains $0$ and $2$ but omits $1$, its points below $1$ and its points above $1$ form two nonempty sides. A connected subset of the usual real line therefore has no internal gaps. The converse is also true: containing every intermediate point is enough for connectedness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一条线上，如果集合的两个点之间漏掉一个点，就能由此分开集合。若集合含 $0,2$，却不含 $1$，那么低于 $1$ 与高于 $1$ 的点形成两个非空部分。因此，通常实线的连通子集不能有内部缺口。反向也成立：只要包含任意两点之间的全部点，就足以连通。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State the topology and the interval convention
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*明确拓扑与区间约定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the usual distance $|x-y|$ on $\mathbb R$, and give each subset $C$ its [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]]. Thus a set is open in $C$ when it is $C\cap O$ for some open $O$ of $\mathbb R$. Recall that [[连通性|connectedness]] means that no two disjoint nonempty relatively open sets cover $C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R$ 上使用通常距离 $|x-y|$，并给子集 $C$ 配上[[子空间拓扑]]：$C$ 中的开集是某个实线开集 $O$ 与 $C$ 的交。[[连通性]]意味着，不能用两个不交、非空的相对开集覆盖 $C$。

> [!important] No missing intermediate points · 不遗漏中间点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a nonempty $C\subseteq\mathbb R$ with this topology,
> $$C\text{ is connected}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \forall a,b\in C\;\forall c\in\mathbb R:\quad a<c<b\ \Longrightarrow\ c\in C.$$
> Here an **interval** means a nonempty set with this between-points property; singleton sets are included. The empty set is also connected, but is treated separately to avoid depending on whether a textbook calls it an interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对带上述拓扑的非空 $C\subseteq\mathbb R$，连通当且仅当它包含任意两个点之间的每个实数。这里把具有该性质的非空集合称为**区间**，包含单点集。空集同样连通，但单独处理，不依赖教材是否把空集也称为区间的用词约定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open, closed and half-open intervals all qualify: $(a,b)$, $[a,b]$, $(a,b]$ and $[a,b)$ when $a<b$. Rays such as $[a,\infty)$ and the whole real line also qualify. Endpoint inclusion and boundedness do not decide connectedness. A singleton $\{a\}=[a,a]$ qualifies because there are no two distinct points requiring an intermediate point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开、闭、半开区间都符合：$a<b$ 时的 $(a,b)$、$[a,b]$、$(a,b]$、$[a,b)$ 都连通。$[a,\infty)$ 这样的半直线与整个实线也符合。是否含端点、是否有界，都不是连通性的判据。单点 $\{a\}=[a,a]$ 也符合，因为其中没有两个不同点需要补入中间点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A missing point produces a separation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*缺失点如何产生分离*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $a,b\in C$ and $a<c<b$, but $c\notin C$. Set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,b\in C$、$a<c<b$，但 $c\notin C$。令

$$U=C\cap(-\infty,c),\qquad V=C\cap(c,\infty).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both half-lines are open in $\mathbb R$, so $U,V$ are relatively open. The point $a$ belongs to $U$ and $b$ belongs to $V$, so neither is empty. They are disjoint, and their union is all of $C$ precisely because $c$ is missing. This is a separation, proving that a connected $C$ must have the between-points property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个半直线在 $\mathbb R$ 中开，所以 $U,V$ 相对开。$a\in U$、$b\in V$，保证两边非空；两者不交，而正因为 $c$ 缺失，它们的并覆盖了整个 $C$。这构成分离，证明连通的 $C$ 必须满足包含中间点的性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, any finite real set with at least two points is disconnected. Order its points and choose a real number strictly between two consecutive ones; that number is absent and gives the separation above. This explains why $\{0,2\}$ is disconnected even though it is a closed set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，任何至少含两个点的有限实数集都不连通：将点从小到大排列，在相邻两点之间选一个实数，这个数必然不在集合中，因而产生上述分离。因此 $\{0,2\}$ 虽为闭集，却不连通。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why an interval cannot be separated
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么区间无法分离*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now suppose $C$ has the between-points property but admits a separation $C=U\cup V$. Pick one point from each side; exchange the names of the sides if necessary so that $u\in U$, $v\in V$ and $u<v$. Then the whole segment $[u,v]$ lies in $C$. Look at
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，假设 $C$ 包含任意两点之间的所有点，却能分离成 $C=U\cup V$。各取一边的一个点；必要时交换两边名称，使 $u\in U$、$v\in V$ 且 $u<v$。于是整段 $[u,v]$ 都在 $C$ 中。考察

$$E=U\cap[u,v],\qquad s=\sup E.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The set $E$ is nonempty because it contains $u$, and it is bounded above by $v$. The real numbers have the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property]]: every such nonempty bounded-above set has a real [[上确界|supremum]]. The notation $s=\sup E$ means that every point of $E$ is at most $s$, but for every $\delta>0$ some point of $E$ is greater than $s-\delta$. Otherwise $s-\delta$ would already be a smaller upper bound. The supremum need not belong to $E$; for example $\sup[0,1)=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E$ 含有 $u$，所以非空，又被 $v$ 从上方界住。实数具有[[实数的确界性质|最小上界性质]]：每个这样的非空有上界集合，都有实数[[上确界]]。$s=\sup E$ 的意思是，$E$ 中的点都不大于 $s$，但对每个 $\delta>0$，$E$ 中总有点大于 $s-\delta$；否则 $s-\delta$ 就已经是更小的上界。上确界不必属于 $E$，例如 $\sup[0,1)=1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $u\le s\le v$, we have $s\in C$, hence $s$ lies in either $U$ or $V$. If $s\in U$, then $s<v$ because $v\in V$. Relative openness gives $\delta>0$ such that $C\cap(s-\delta,s+\delta)\subseteq U$. Choose $w=s+\min\{\delta/2,(v-s)/2\}$. Then $s<w<v$, so $w\in E$, contradicting that $s$ is an upper bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $u\le s\le v$，所以 $s\in C$，从而 $s$ 必在 $U,V$ 的一边。如果 $s\in U$，由于 $v\in V$，便有 $s<v$。相对开性给出 $\delta>0$，使 $C\cap(s-\delta,s+\delta)\subseteq U$。取 $w=s+\min\{\delta/2,(v-s)/2\}$，就有 $s<w<v$，因此 $w\in E$，与 $s$ 是上界矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $s\in V$, choose $\delta>0$ with $C\cap(s-\delta,s+\delta)\subseteq V$. The supremum property gives $e\in E$ with $s-\delta<e\le s$. This point lies in the displayed neighbourhood, so it belongs to $V$, but by $e\in E$ it also belongs to $U$. Again there is a contradiction. Neither location for $s$ is possible, and the supposed separation cannot exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $s\in V$，则取 $\delta>0$ 使 $C\cap(s-\delta,s+\delta)\subseteq V$。上确界性质给出 $e\in E$，满足 $s-\delta<e\le s$。这个点处于上述邻域中，因此属于 $V$；但 $e\in E$ 又说明它属于 $U$，仍然矛盾。$s$ 不可能落在任何一边，因此原先假设的分离不存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem uses the usual topology on the **real line**. With the discrete topology, even $[0,1]$ can be separated into the open sets $\{0\}$ and $(0,1]$. The usual real-line theorem, combined with [[连续像保持连通|continuous images preserving connectedness]], explains [[介值定理|why a continuous real function on an interval takes intermediate values]]. It is not a claim that every connected subset of an arbitrary space is a real interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本定理使用的是**实线上的通常拓扑**。若改用离散拓扑，即使 $[0,1]$ 也能分成开集 $\{0\}$ 与 $(0,1]$。通常实线的结论配合[[连续像保持连通]]，解释了[[介值定理|区间上的连续实函数为什么会取到中间值]]；它并不是说任意空间的连通子集都是实区间。
