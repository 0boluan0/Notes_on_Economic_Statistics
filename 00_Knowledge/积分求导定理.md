# Differentiating an accumulation integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*积分求导定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a tank's inflow rate is close to three litres per minute throughout a very short time interval, the added water is close to three times that interval's length. Dividing the added amount by the elapsed time gives an average rate close to three. The **fundamental theorem for accumulation** makes this idea exact: at a point where the integrand is continuous, the derivative of its accumulated integral recovers its value there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一小段时间内，水箱的流入速率始终接近每分钟三升，那么新增水量就接近三乘以这段时间的长度；再除以经过的时间，平均速率就接近三。**积分求导定理**把这一想法变成精确结论：在被积函数连续的点，累积积分的导数会恢复该点的被积函数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[累积函数|accumulation function]] is already defined by integrals; its [[导数|derivative]] asks about the limit of differences between nearby accumulated totals divided by their input increments. These are distinct operations, and continuity is what connects them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[累积函数]]已经通过积分定义；它的[[导数]]则考察相邻累积总量之差除以输入增量后的极限。这是两个不同的运算，连续性把它们连接起来。

> [!important] Continuity at the point is enough · 在所考察点连续即可
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f:[L,R]\to\mathbb R$ be Riemann integrable, with finite $L<R$, and fix $a\in[L,R]$. Define $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$. If $c\in(L,R)$ and $f$ is [[一点连续|continuous at $c$]], then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设实值函数 $f$ 在有限区间 $[L,R]$ 上黎曼可积，其中 $L<R$，固定 $a\in[L,R]$，定义 $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$。若 $c\in(L,R)$ 且 $f$ [[一点连续|在 $c$ 连续]]，则
>
> $$G'(c)=f(c).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $a$ is the fixed starting input, $t$ the dummy variable and $c$ the point where the derivative is taken. Continuity is required at $c$, not at every other point. At $L$ or $R$, relative continuity gives the corresponding **one-sided** derivative from inside the interval; a two-sided derivative would require a domain on both sides.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $a$ 是固定起点，$t$ 是哑变量，$c$ 是求导的位置。这里要求在 $c$ 连续，不要求其他每一点都连续。若在 $L$ 或 $R$ 处相对于区间连续，就得到从区间内部趋近的相应**单侧**导数；双侧导数还需要两侧都有定义域。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common special case starts with $f$ continuous on the whole closed interval. Such a function is [[连续函数可积|Riemann integrable]], so the theorem supplies $G'=f$ at every interior point. Thus integration constructs an [[原函数|antiderivative]] without first finding an elementary expression for it. MIT 18.01SC calls this direction **FTC II**; the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|endpoint-evaluation direction]] is called FTC I there. The displayed statement identifies the direction more reliably than an isolated Roman numeral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常用情形是 $f$ 在整个闭区间连续。这样的函数[[连续函数可积|黎曼可积]]，所以每个内点都满足 $G'=f$。积分因此构造出一个[[原函数]]，不必先找到它的初等表达式。MIT 18.01SC 把这个方向称为 **FTC II**，把[[Newton-Leibniz公式|用端点差计算积分的方向]]称为 FTC I。判断所用方向时，应看完整结论，而不只看孤立的罗马数字编号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The small added interval gives the proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*新增的小区间给出证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a nonzero increment $h$ small enough that $c+h$ stays in the domain. By [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]], the integral from $a$ to $c$ cancels when subtracting $G(c)$ from $G(c+h)$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取非零且足够小的增量 $h$，使 $c+h$ 仍在定义域内。由[[定积分区间可加性]]，$G(c+h)-G(c)$ 中从 $a$ 到 $c$ 的共同部分相消：

$$\frac{G(c+h)-G(c)}h-f(c)
=\frac1h\int_c^{c+h}[f(t)-f(c)]\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\varepsilon>0$ be any desired error bound. Continuity at $c$ provides $\delta>0$ such that $|f(t)-f(c)|<\varepsilon$ whenever $t$ is in the domain and $|t-c|<\delta$. If $0<|h|<\delta$, every point between $c$ and $c+h$ satisfies that bound. By the [[积分绝对值界|integral absolute-value bound]], the absolute integral error is at most height $\varepsilon$ times length $|h|$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任给误差容限 $\varepsilon>0$。在 $c$ 连续，意味着存在 $\delta>0$，只要定义域内的 $t$ 满足 $|t-c|<\delta$，就有 $|f(t)-f(c)|<\varepsilon$。若 $0<|h|<\delta$，则 $c$ 与 $c+h$ 之间的每一点都满足这个界。由[[积分绝对值界]]，积分误差的绝对值至多为高度 $\varepsilon$ 乘以长度 $|h|$，因此

$$\left|\frac{G(c+h)-G(c)}h-f(c)\right|
\le\frac{\varepsilon|h|}{|h|}=\varepsilon.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The error can therefore be made arbitrarily small, proving the derivative limit. The argument uses $|h|$ and oriented integrals, so it covers $h<0$ as well as $h>0$. It never assumes that $f$ is nonnegative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

误差可以任意小，导数极限就得到证明。论证使用 $|h|$ 与有向积分，所以同时覆盖 $h<0$ 和 $h>0$，也从未要求 $f$ 非负。

> [!example] Differentiate without evaluating the integral · 不必先算出积分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $f(t)=e^{-t^2}$ is continuous for every real $t$. Hence $G(x)=\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$ satisfies $G'(x)=e^{-x^2}$ for every real $x$, although $G$ has [[无初等原函数的积分|no elementary expression]] of the usual kind. If the endpoint is instead $x^2$, the function is $H(x)=G(x^2)$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives $H'(x)=2x e^{-x^4}$; simply substituting the upper bound into the integrand would miss the factor $2x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $f(t)=e^{-t^2}$ 在每个实数 $t$ 处都连续，所以 $G(x)=\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$ 对所有实数 $x$ 都满足 $G'(x)=e^{-x^2}$，尽管 $G$ [[无初等原函数的积分|无法写成通常的初等原函数表达式]]。若上端点改成 $x^2$，得到的是 $H(x)=G(x^2)$；[[链式法则]]给出 $H'(x)=2xe^{-x^4}$。只把上限代入被积函数，会漏掉 $2x$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Integrability does not supply every derivative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*可积并不保证每一点都能恢复导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $[-1,1]$, let $f(t)=-1$ for $t<0$ and $f(t)=1$ for $t\ge0$. This bounded step function is Riemann integrable: only a small interval around its single jump contributes to the gap between upper and lower sums, and that gap tends to zero with the interval's length. Its accumulation from zero is $G(x)=|x|$, which has left slope $-1$ and right slope $1$ at zero. So $G'(0)$ does not exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[-1,1]$ 上，令 $t<0$ 时 $f(t)=-1$，$t\ge0$ 时 $f(t)=1$。这个有界阶梯函数黎曼可积：上下和之差只来自包住唯一跳跃点的一小段，随着段长缩小而趋于零。从零开始的累积是 $G(x)=|x|$，在零点的左斜率为 $-1$、右斜率为 $1$，所以 $G'(0)$ 不存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nor does this $f$ have some other antiderivative on $(-1,1)$. If $F'=f$ there, the [[原函数相差常数|constant-difference theorem]] forces $F(x)=-x+C_-$ on the negative side and $F(x)=x+C_+$ on the positive side. [[可导必连续|Differentiability at zero would force continuity there]], making both constants equal to $F(0)$; the two one-sided slopes would still disagree. Thus **Riemann integrability alone does not guarantee an antiderivative on the whole interval**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个 $f$ 在 $(-1,1)$ 上也不存在另一个原函数。若有 $F'=f$，由[[原函数相差常数]]，负半段必须为 $F(x)=-x+C_-$，正半段必须为 $F(x)=x+C_+$。由[[可导必连续]]，零点可导又要求那里连续，使两个常数都等于 $F(0)$，但左右斜率仍然不同。因此，**黎曼可积本身不保证整个区间上存在原函数**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Failure of continuity does not always destroy the derivative either. If $f(0)=1$ and $f(t)=0$ elsewhere, every integral is zero: isolating zero in arbitrarily short partition pieces makes its contribution arbitrarily small. Then $G\equiv0$, so $G'(0)=0\ne f(0)$. At a discontinuity, the theorem gives no automatic conclusion about either existence or equality. Finally, an integral such as $\int_{-1}^x dt/t$ cannot cross zero as an ordinary Riemann integral; the missing integral must not be overlooked before attempting differentiation. See [[变限积分求导|variable-limit differentiation]] when the endpoints themselves move by a specified rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不连续也未必一定破坏可导性。如果 $f(0)=1$，其余点都为零，则每段积分都是零：用任意短的分割小段包住零点，就能使其贡献任意小。此时 $G\equiv0$，所以 $G'(0)=0\ne f(0)$。因此，遇到间断点时，定理既不自动保证导数存在，也不保证导数等于点值。另如 $\int_{-1}^x dt/t$，作为普通黎曼积分不能跨过零点；在尝试求导之前，不能漏查积分是否存在。端点按指定规则移动的情形，可继续读[[变限积分求导]]。
