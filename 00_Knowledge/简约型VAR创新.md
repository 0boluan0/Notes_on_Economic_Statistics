# 简约型 VAR 创新是给定系统过去后的当期不可预测部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A reduced-form VAR innovation is the part of the current system that cannot be predicted from its past*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 简约型 VAR 创新 $u_t$ 是系统在时点 $t$ 新到达、无法由已经观察到的系统过去线性预测的那部分。它是简约型动态的预测误差对象，不是尚未经过识别就已经拥有经济名称的冲击。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] What it is
> The reduced-form VAR innovation $u_t$ is the part of the system newly revealed at date $t$ that cannot be linearly predicted from the observed system history. It is the forecast-error object of the reduced-form dynamics, not an economically named shock before identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在
$$
y_t=c+\sum_{i=1}^p A_i y_{t-i}+u_t
$$
中，若 $\mathcal H_{t-1}$ 是由常数与系统过去张成的闭线性空间，二阶创新可写为
$$
u_t=y_t-P_{\mathcal H_{t-1}}y_t,
$$
因而 $u_t$ 与该线性空间正交。若进一步指定条件均值 VAR，并假定 $E(u_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=0$，则还可写成
$$
u_t=y_t-E(y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}).
$$
后一口径要求的马氏差条件比“创新跨期不相关”更强；阅读模型时要先看作者采用哪一种预测口径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the second-order formulation, $u_t=y_t-P_{\mathcal H_{t-1}}y_t$ is orthogonal to the closed linear space generated by constants and the system's past. If the VAR is additionally specified as a conditional-mean model with $E(u_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=0$, then $u_t=y_t-E(y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})$. This martingale-difference formulation is stronger than serial uncorrelatedness, so the forecasting convention must be read from the maintained assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种口径都通常记
$$
\operatorname{Cov}(u_t)=\Sigma_u.
$$
这个矩阵可以非对角：不同方程在同一期可以产生相关的预测误差。这不改变“它们都是相对过去的新信息”，却意味着不能把每个坐标分量自动命名为一个独立经济冲击；这个独立边界由 [[简约型创新不是结构冲击]] 承担。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The innovation covariance $\Sigma_u$ need not be diagonal: equations may make correlated forecast errors in the same period. They are still new information relative to the past, but their coordinate components cannot therefore be named as independent economic shocks without further identification. That separate boundary is developed in [[简约型创新不是结构冲击|the reduced-form/structural-shock distinction]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

简约型创新是多步预测误差的基本积木，也是 VMA 中沿时间传播的输入。它因此是一个完整的统计学对象，即使研究者只做联合预测、并不企图识别经济结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reduced-form innovations are the building blocks of multi-step forecast errors and the inputs propagated by the VMA. They remain complete statistical objects for joint forecasting even when no economic structure is identified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 给定一个已估计 VAR，$u_t$ 在概念上是“真实结构冲击”还是“相对已用信息的预测新息”？
>
> **答案：** 先是后者。只有在另外建立并识别结构映射后，才能把它的某种变换解释为结构冲击。
