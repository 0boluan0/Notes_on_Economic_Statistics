# What non-spanning inputs leave undetermined
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*非张成输入留下的未定方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing the output of a [[线性映射|linear map]] on some inputs also tells us its output on every linear combination of them. It gives no such formula for a direction outside their [[张成|span]]. For independent inputs in a finite-dimensional domain, the missing directions can receive freely chosen outputs. If the codomain contains a nonzero vector, this produces different maps agreeing on all the specified inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道一个[[线性映射]]对若干输入的输出，也就知道了它对这些输入的全部线性组合的输出。但在它们的[[张成]]以外，没有这样的确定公式。对于有限维定义域中的无关输入，缺失方向的输出可以自由选择；只要陪域含非零向量，就能得到多个映射，它们在所有已指定输入上都一致。

> [!example] One known direction in the plane · 平面中只知道一个方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Seek $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$, and prescribe only $T(e_1)=1$, where $e_1=(1,0)$. Linearity forces $T(s,0)=s$, so every point on the horizontal axis is settled. The value at $e_2=(0,1)$ is still free. For each real number $c$, the rule
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 希望构造 $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$，只规定 $T(e_1)=1$，其中 $e_1=(1,0)$。线性性迫使 $T(s,0)=s$，所以横轴上每个输入的输出都确定了。但 $e_2=(0,1)$ 的输出仍然自由。对每个实数 $c$，规则
>
> $$T_c(s,t)=s+ct$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> is linear and satisfies $T_c(e_1)=1$, while $T_c(e_2)=c$. In particular, $T_0(s,t)=s$ and $T_1(s,t)=s+t$ meet the same data but are different maps. More observations along the horizontal axis cannot determine $c$; an input with a nonzero second coordinate would supply the missing information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 都线性，且满足 $T_c(e_1)=1$，同时 $T_c(e_2)=c$。特别地，$T_0(s,t)=s$ 与 $T_1(s,t)=s+t$ 符合同一份数据，却是不同映射。继续添加横轴上的观测无法确定 $c$；第二坐标非零的输入才会带来缺少的信息。

## What can be freely prescribed?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些输出仍可自由选择？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional [[向量空间|vector space]] over $\mathbb F$, with [[维数|dimension]] $n\ge1$, and let $W$ be a vector space over the same field. Let $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ be [[线性无关|linearly independent]], with $0\le k<n$, and prescribe arbitrary $w_1,\ldots,w_k\in W$. Independence means that a zero combination of the inputs has only all-zero coefficients; it ensures that arbitrary outputs create no conflicting relations. Their span $U$ is a proper part of $V$ because $k<n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V$ 是标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的有限维[[向量空间]]，[[维数]]为 $n\ge1$；$W$ 是同一域上的向量空间。取[[线性无关]]的 $u_1,\ldots,u_k$，其中 $0\le k<n$，并任意指定 $w_1,\ldots,w_k\in W$。无关指输入的零组合只能使用全零系数，因此任意输出不会造成关系冲突。由于 $k<n$，它们的张成 $U$ 只是 $V$ 的一部分。

> [!important] An extension exists, but need not be unique · 延拓存在，但未必唯一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There is a linear map $T:V\to W$ with $T(u_j)=w_j$ for every $j$. If $W\ne\{0_W\}$, there are at least two such maps. All of them agree on $U$; the freedom concerns inputs outside $U$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 存在线性映射 $T:V\to W$ 满足全部 $T(u_j)=w_j$。若 $W\ne\{0_W\}$，这样的映射至少有两个。它们在 $U$ 上全部相同，自由度在于 $U$ 以外的输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here an **extension** is a map on the larger domain $V$ that retains the specified values. There is no assumption that $W$ is finite-dimensional, and the chosen $w_j$ may be zero or dependent. When $k=0$, no images are prescribed and $U=\{0_V\}$; the same statement still applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的**延拓**是在更大的定义域 $V$ 上定义映射，同时保留已有指定值。不要求 $W$ 有限维，所选 $w_j$ 也可以是零或相关的。$k=0$ 表示尚未指定任何像，此时 $U=\{0_V\}$，结论仍成立。

## Complete the inputs to a basis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把已有输入补成一组基*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[基|basis]] gives every input exactly one coefficient expansion. Extend the independent list to a basis of $V$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[基]]使每个输入都有唯一的系数展开。将已有无关组补成 $V$ 的一组基：

$$\mathcal B=(u_1,\ldots,u_k,v_{k+1},\ldots,v_n).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why is this possible? Take a finite spanning list of $V$ and inspect it one vector at a time. Add a vector only if it is outside the span of the vectors already kept. This preserves independence: a relation with a nonzero coefficient on the added vector would express it in the old span, a contradiction. After the finite list is exhausted, every inspected vector is in the resulting span, so the list now spans $V$ as well. None of the original $u_j$ was removed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么可以补基？取 $V$ 的一个有限张成组，依次检查其中向量；只有不在当前已保留向量的张成中时，才将它加入。这会保持无关性：若某条关系中新加入向量的系数非零，就能把它表示成旧组的组合，与选择条件矛盾。有限组检查完毕后，每个被检查的向量都在最终张成中，所以此时也张成 $V$。原先的 $u_j$ 一个都没有移除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now choose any extra outputs $z_{k+1},\ldots,z_n\in W$. The [[线性映射的基向量像|basis-image theorem]] gives exactly one linear map for this completed choice. For the unique coordinates $a_j,b_\ell$ of an input, its rule is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在任意选择补基向量的输出 $z_{k+1},\ldots,z_n\in W$。[[线性映射的基向量像|基像定理]]使这份完整选择恰好对应一个线性映射。设某输入的唯一坐标为 $a_j,b_\ell$，则规则为

$$T\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^k a_ju_j+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^n b_\ell v_\ell\right)
=\sum_{j=1}^k a_jw_j+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^n b_\ell z_\ell.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every extension has this form, because its values on the extra basis vectors must be some choices of the $z_\ell$. For $x\in U$, all the $b_\ell$ are zero, so the choice has no effect on $T(x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个延拓都具有这个形式，因为它在补基向量上的值总会给出某一组 $z_\ell$。若 $x\in U$，全部 $b_\ell$ 为零，所以这些选择不会影响 $T(x)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $k<n$, at least $v_{k+1}$ is available. If $W$ contains a vector $w\ne0_W$, choose all extra outputs zero for one map, and change only $T(v_{k+1})$ to $w$ for a second. They retain every prescribed value and differ at $v_{k+1}$. This proves nonuniqueness without requiring any output dimension or an infinite scalar field.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $k<n$，至少存在补基向量 $v_{k+1}$。若 $W$ 含 $w\ne0_W$，第一个映射把所有补基向量都送到零；第二个只把 $v_{k+1}$ 的输出改为 $w$。它们保留全部指定值，却在 $v_{k+1}$ 处不同。这证明了非唯一性，并不要求特定的输出维数或无限标量域。

## Separate absence of information from inconsistency
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*区分信息不足与数据冲突*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition $W\ne\{0_W\}$ matters. If the codomain consists only of zero, every input must be sent to zero, so there is exactly one linear map regardless of how few images were specified. There is then no alternative output to assign to a missing direction. The zero domain also causes no counterexample: no list can have span strictly smaller than $\{0_V\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件 $W\ne\{0_W\}$ 很重要。若陪域只有零，每个输入都只能被送到零，因此无论之前指定了多少像，都恰好只有一个线性映射。缺失方向也没有另一种输出可选。零定义域同样不会构成反例：任何向量组都不可能张成一个严格小于 $\{0_V\}$ 的空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the prescribed inputs are dependent, non-spanning alone does not guarantee that an extension exists. For example, asking $T(e_1)=1$ and $T(2e_1)=3$ in $\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ is impossible despite leaving the vertical direction unspecified. First apply [[相关向量像需保持关系|the relation-consistency condition]]. When that condition holds, the data define one linear map on their span; choose a basis of that span and repeat the completion argument. For finite-dimensional $V$, a proper span and nonzero $W$ then again give multiple extensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若指定输入相关，仅凭不张成并不能保证延拓存在。例如在 $\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ 中要求 $T(e_1)=1$、$T(2e_1)=3$，即使还没指定纵向的作用，也已不可能实现。必须先检查[[相关向量像需保持关系|关系一致性条件]]。条件成立时，数据在其张成空间上定义唯一映射；选该空间的一组基，再用补基论证。在有限维 $V$ 中，只要张成空间仍是严格的一部分且 $W$ 非零，就再次得到多个延拓。

> [!question]- Which additional value is needed? · 还需要哪个值？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R$, suppose $T(e_1)$ and $T(e_2)$ are known. Would knowing $T(e_1+e_2)$ determine the whole map?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R$，已知 $T(e_1)$ 与 $T(e_2)$。再知道 $T(e_1+e_2)$，能确定整个映射吗？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. That value is already their sum. The value on a third independent direction, such as the standard vector $e_3=(0,0,1)$, is still needed. Choosing it completes the basis data and determines the whole map uniquely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不能。那个值已经等于前两个像之和。仍需第三个独立方向的像，例如标准向量 $e_3=(0,0,1)$ 的像；指定它才补齐基上的数据，从而唯一确定整个映射。

## Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, fourth edition, 2.30–2.32, PDF pp.54–55](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=55): completing an independent list to a finite basis. The add-only construction above spells out preservation of independence and eventual spanning.
- [Axler, §3A, especially 3.4, PDF pp.66–68](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=68): arbitrary basis images give a unique linear map. Applying this to the extra basis vectors proves the stated existence and freedom; the zero-codomain exception follows directly from the zero-map definition on p.66.
- [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/05_Session_Summaries/MIT18_06SCF11_Ses3.6sum.pdf#page=3|MIT 18.06SC Session 3.6 summary, p.3]]: known images determine the map on the span of their inputs. The plane family and the three-dimensional information check are verified by substitution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 2.30–2.32，PDF54–55：核对无关组补成有限基；正文逐步说明只加入张成外向量为何保持无关且最终覆盖整个空间。
- Axler 第3A节，特别是3.4，PDF66–68：核对任意基像唯一确定线性映射。将它用于补基向量，得到存在性和自由度；零陪域例外直接由第66页的零映射定义核验。
- MIT Session 3.6 summary，第3页：核对已知像确定输入张成空间上的映射；平面映射族和三维信息检查均直接代入核验。
