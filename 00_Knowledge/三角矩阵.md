# Triangular matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三角矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **triangular matrix** is a square matrix with zeros on one entire side of its main diagonal. A square matrix has the same number of rows and columns; its main diagonal runs from the top-left entry to the bottom-right entry. The name tells you where nonzero entries are allowed, so you can recognize the structure by looking at positions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**三角矩阵**是一种方阵：主对角线的一侧全部为零。方阵的行数和列数相等，主对角线从左上角一直延伸到右下角。这个名称告诉你哪些位置允许出现非零元素，因此先看元素的位置，就能识别它的结构。

> [!example] The upper and lower shapes · 上三角与下三角的形状
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Compare these two matrices. Their diagonal entries are $2,3,5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 比较下面两个矩阵；它们的对角元都是 $2,3,5$。
>
> $$
> U=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&0\\0&3&4\\0&0&5\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad
> L=\begin{pmatrix}2&0&0\\-1&3&0\\0&4&5\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $U$ is **upper triangular**: everything below the diagonal is zero, so any nonzero entries lie on or above it. $L$ is **lower triangular**: everything above the diagonal is zero, so any nonzero entries lie on or below it. The upper-right zero in $U$ is allowed too; the upper region is not required to be full of nonzero numbers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $U$ 是**上三角矩阵**：主对角线下方全部为零，非零元素只能出现在对角线及其上方。$L$ 是**下三角矩阵**：上方全部为零，非零元素只能出现在对角线及其下方。$U$ 右上角的零也完全允许；“上三角”不要求上方每个位置都非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn the picture into an index test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把位置图像写成下标条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ have $n$ rows and $n$ columns, where $n$ is a positive integer and entries are real or complex numbers. The first index $i$ selects the row, counted downward; the second index $j$ selects the column, counted rightward. On the diagonal the indices agree. Below it, the row number is larger: for example, $(3,1)$ is below the diagonal because $3>1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A=(a_{ij})$ 有 $n$ 行、$n$ 列，$n$ 为正整数，元素可以是实数或复数。第一个下标 $i$ 是从上往下数的行号，第二个下标 $j$ 是从左往右数的列号。对角位置的两个下标相等；下方位置的行号更大，例如 $(3,1)$ 在下方，因为 $3>1$。

$$
\begin{aligned}
A\text{ is upper triangular}&\iff a_{ij}=0\quad\text{whenever }i>j,\\
A\text{ is lower triangular}&\iff a_{ij}=0\quad\text{whenever }i<j.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These conditions impose no restriction on the diagonal values $a_{11},\ldots,a_{nn}$. A diagonal entry may be zero, negative or, when working over the complex numbers, nonreal. All-zero matrices qualify. A $1\times1$ matrix is both upper and lower triangular because it has no off-diagonal entries to check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些条件不限制对角元 $a_{11},\ldots,a_{nn}$ 的取值。对角元可以为零、为负数；在复数范围内也可以不是实数。全零矩阵同样符合定义。一阶矩阵同时是上三角和下三角，因为它根本没有需要检查的非对角位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] exchanges row and column positions, so it swaps the upper and lower shapes. In the displayed example, $L=U^T$. A matrix that is both upper and lower triangular has zeros on both sides; it is a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵转置]]交换行列位置，因此会把上三角形状变成下三角形状，反之亦然。上例中恰好有 $L=U^T$。如果一个矩阵同时上三角且下三角，两侧都必须为零，它就是[[对角矩阵]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why this shape helps solve equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这种形状为什么便于解方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the same $U$ in $Ux=b$, where $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$ is the unknown column and $b=(0,14,10)^T$. The [[矩阵乘法|row-by-column multiplication rule]] turns it into
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把同一个 $U$ 用于 $Ux=b$，其中未知列向量为 $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$，右端为 $b=(0,14,10)^T$。按[[矩阵乘法|行乘列的计算规则]]，它就是

$$
\begin{cases}
2x_1-x_2=0,\\
3x_2+4x_3=14,\\
5x_3=10.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last equation contains only $x_3$, so $x_3=2$. Substitute upward: $3x_2+8=14$ gives $x_2=2$, then $2x_1-2=0$ gives $x_1=1$. This is back substitution. With a lower triangular system, the corresponding process starts at the first equation and moves downward. This structure is why triangular matrices appear in [[消元与LU分解|Gaussian elimination and LU factorization]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一条方程只含 $x_3$，所以 $x_3=2$。向上代入：由 $3x_2+8=14$ 得到 $x_2=2$，再由 $2x_1-2=0$ 得到 $x_1=1$。这就是回代。对下三角方程组，相应过程从第一条方程开始，向下推进。正因这种结构便于逐个求未知数，三角矩阵会出现在[[消元与LU分解]]中。

> [!warning] The zero pattern does not guarantee usable pivots · 零的位置不保证主元可用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The example divides by $5,3,2$, which are all nonzero. The definition of triangularity does not promise that: a zero diagonal entry may stop this step-by-step division. Likewise, the diagonal entries of $L$ in some LU conventions are all $1$; that is an extra normalization, not part of the general definition of a lower triangular matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上例依次除以 $5,3,2$，它们都非零。三角矩阵的定义并不保证这一点：对角元为零时，这样逐步除法的过程可能无法继续。同样，某些 LU 约定里 $L$ 的对角元全为 $1$，这是额外的规范，不是一般下三角矩阵的定义条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero pattern is the concept here. Its consequences have their own precise statements: [[下三角乘积封闭性|closure of lower triangular matrices under multiplication]] and [[三角矩阵行列式|the determinant formula for triangular matrices]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡定义的是这种零元素位置结构。它的具体性质分别见[[下三角乘积封闭性]]与[[三角矩阵行列式]]。
