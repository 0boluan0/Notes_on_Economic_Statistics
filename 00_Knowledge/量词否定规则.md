# 否定量词时必须交换全称与存在并否定谓词
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Negating a quantified statement swaps the quantifier and negates the predicate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子规则
> $$
> \neg\forall x\,P(x)\equiv\exists x\,\neg P(x),
> \qquad
> \neg\exists x\,P(x)\equiv\forall x\,\neg P(x).
> $$
> 否定穿过一个量词时，$\forall$ 与 $\exists$ 互换，同时把否定继续作用到内部命题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $$
> \neg\forall x\,P(x)\equiv\exists x\,\neg P(x),
> \qquad
> \neg\exists x\,P(x)\equiv\forall x\,\neg P(x).
> $$
> When a negation moves across a quantifier, $\forall$ and $\exists$ exchange roles and the negation continues inward to the predicate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“并非每个人都提交了作业”表示“至少有一个人没有提交”，而不是“所有人都没有提交”。同样，“不存在失败的测试”表示“每个测试都没有失败”。对多层量词要一层一层移动否定，不能一次凭语感改写。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Not everyone submitted” means “someone did not submit,” not “nobody submitted.” Likewise, “there is no failing test” means that every test does not fail. With nested quantifiers, move the negation inward one layer at a time rather than rewriting the sentence by intuition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 否定 $\forall x(P(x)\Rightarrow Q(x))$ 后还要否定内部蕴含，得到 $\exists x(P(x)\land\neg Q(x))$；只换量词而不处理谓词不完整。
- 论域保持不变。不能在否定时顺便把“所有实数”缩成“所有正实数”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** Negating $\forall x(P(x)\Rightarrow Q(x))$ also requires negating the implication, yielding $\exists x(P(x)\land\neg Q(x))$. Merely changing the quantifier is incomplete.<br>
**2.** The domain remains fixed. Negation does not permit silently shrinking “all real numbers” to “all positive real numbers.”<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 写出“不是每个整数都有一个比它小的非负整数”的形式否定。
>
> **答案：** 若原式为 $\forall n\in\mathbb Z\exists m\in\mathbb N:m<n$，其否定是 $\exists n\in\mathbb Z\forall m\in\mathbb N:m\ge n$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Formally negate “every integer has a nonnegative integer smaller than it.”
>
> **Answer:** If the original statement is $\forall n\in\mathbb Z\exists m\in\mathbb N:m<n$, its negation is $\exists n\in\mathbb Z\forall m\in\mathbb N:m\ge n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
