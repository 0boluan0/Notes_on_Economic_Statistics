# Negating quantified statements
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*量词否定规则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A statement about everyone can fail because of just one person. If Ada submitted her assignment and Ben did not, “everyone submitted” is false, while “nobody submitted” is also false. Its negation is the different claim “someone did not submit.” Negating a sentence means stating exactly when the **whole original sentence is false**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关于所有人的陈述，可能只因一个人而失败。若 Ada 交了作业、Ben 没交，“所有人都交了”为假，“所有人都没交”也为假。原句的否定是另一条陈述：“至少有人没交”。否定一个句子，就是准确说出**整个原句什么时候为假**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work in ordinary classical logic. A predicate is a condition whose truth is evaluated with its inputs and interpretation fixed; see [[命题与谓词|propositions and predicates]]. Fix a domain $D$ and the meaning of a condition $P(x)$. The symbols $\forall$, $\exists$, and $\neg$ mean “for every,” “there exists at least one,” and “not.” The symbol $\equiv$ denotes logical equivalence: the two sides have the same truth value under every permitted interpretation and assignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里使用通常的经典逻辑。谓词是关于输入的条件；固定输入与解释后，才评价其真假，见[[命题与谓词]]。固定论域 $D$ 以及条件 $P(x)$ 的含义。$\forall,\exists,\neg$ 分别表示“对每个”“至少存在一个”“非”；$\equiv$ 表示逻辑等价，即两边在每个允许的解释与赋值下具有相同真值。

> [!important] Keep the domain and negate the condition · 论域不动，否定向内
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $$
> \neg\forall x\,P(x)\equiv\exists x\,\neg P(x),
> \qquad
> \neg\exists x\,P(x)\equiv\forall x\,\neg P(x).
> $$
> When a negation moves across a quantifier, $\forall$ and $\exists$ exchange roles and the negation continues inward to the predicate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $$
> \neg\forall x\,P(x)\equiv\exists x\,\neg P(x),
> \qquad
> \neg\exists x\,P(x)\equiv\forall x\,\neg P(x).
> $$
> 否定穿过一个量词时，$\forall$ 与 $\exists$ 互换，同时把否定继续作用到内部命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The domain $D$ has been omitted only to shorten these two formulas: the $x$ on each side ranges over that same $D$. The first left side means $\neg(\forall x\,P(x))$, not $\forall x\,\neg P(x)$. Parentheses identify the statement being negated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两式只是为简短而省去论域 $D$：左右两边的 $x$ 始终取自同一个 $D$。第一式左侧表示 $\neg(\forall x\,P(x))$，不是 $\forall x\,\neg P(x)$。括号用来确定被否定的整个陈述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why both rules hold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两条规则为什么成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If not every allowed $x$ satisfies $P$, then at least one allowed $x$ makes $P$ false; that is a witness for $\exists x\in D\,\neg P(x)$. Conversely, such a witness prevents $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ from being true. For the second rule, no allowed witness for $P$ means that $P$ is false at every allowed input, exactly $\forall x\in D\,\neg P(x)$. Conversely, if every input fails $P$, no witness for $P$ exists. These arguments give both directions of each equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若并非每个允许的 $x$ 都满足 $P$，就至少有一个允许的 $x$ 使 $P$ 为假；这正是 $\exists x\in D\,\neg P(x)$ 的见证。反过来，这样的见证会阻止 $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ 为真。第二条中，不存在满足 $P$ 的允许见证，意味着 $P$ 在每个允许输入处都为假，即 $\forall x\in D\,\neg P(x)$；反过来，每个输入都不满足 $P$，当然就不存在满足它的见证。这分别说明了两条等价式的两个方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Not everyone submitted” means “someone did not submit,” not “nobody submitted.” Likewise, “there is no failing test” means that every test does not fail. With nested quantifiers, move the negation inward one layer at a time rather than rewriting the sentence by intuition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“并非每个人都提交了作业”表示“至少有一个人没有提交”，而不是“所有人都没有提交”。同样，“不存在失败的测试”表示“每个测试都没有失败”。对多层量词要一层一层移动否定，不能一次凭语感改写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Follow a nested statement one layer at a time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逐层处理嵌套陈述*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D$ be a set of students, $E$ a set of advisers, and $R(s,t)$ mean that adviser $t$ supervises student $s$. Negate the claim that every student has an adviser:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $D$ 为学生集合，$E$ 为导师集合，$R(s,t)$ 表示导师 $t$ 指导学生 $s$。要否定“每名学生都有一位导师”，依次处理：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\neg\bigl[\forall s\in D\,\exists t\in E\,R(s,t)\bigr]\\
&\quad\equiv\exists s\in D\,\neg\bigl[\exists t\in E\,R(s,t)\bigr]\\
&\quad\equiv\exists s\in D\,\forall t\in E\,\neg R(s,t).
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is at least one student who is supervised by no adviser. This does not say that there is one adviser who supervises nobody: that would exchange the variable order, which is a separate and generally invalid operation. Negation flips the quantifier kinds while preserving their nesting and [[量词次序|dependence structure]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结论是：有一名学生不受任何导师指导。它不是“有一位导师没有指导任何学生”；后者交换了变量次序，这是另一种一般不合法的操作。否定交换的是量词种类，同时保留它们的嵌套次序与[[量词次序|依赖结构]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a numerical example, let $\mathbb Z$ be the integers and $\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ the nonnegative integers. Negating $\forall n\in\mathbb Z\,\exists m\in\mathbb N:m<n$ gives $\exists n\in\mathbb Z\,\forall m\in\mathbb N:m\ge n$. The original is false at $n=0$: no permitted $m$ is smaller than $0$. Thus $n=0$ is a witness for its negation, since every permitted $m$ satisfies $m\ge0$. The witness is one integer, and the inner claim still covers every nonnegative integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取数值例子：$\mathbb Z$ 为整数集，$\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 为非负整数集。$\forall n\in\mathbb Z\,\exists m\in\mathbb N:m<n$ 的否定是 $\exists n\in\mathbb Z\,\forall m\in\mathbb N:m\ge n$。原句在 $n=0$ 处失败：没有允许的 $m$ 比 $0$ 小。因此 $n=0$ 是否定命题的见证，因为每个允许的 $m$ 都满足 $m\ge0$。外层只需一个整数见证，内层仍须覆盖每个非负整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Negate the complete conditional
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*否定完整的条件命题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inner condition may itself contain logical operations. An [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] $A(x)\Rightarrow B(x)$ is false exactly when $A(x)$ is true and $B(x)$ is false. The connective $\land$ means “and”: both parts must hold. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内部条件还可能含有逻辑运算。[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]] $A(x)\Rightarrow B(x)$ 为假，恰好意味着 $A(x)$ 真而 $B(x)$ 假。连接词 $\land$ 表示“且”，要求两部分都成立。因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\neg\bigl[\forall x\in D\,(A(x)\Rightarrow B(x))\bigr]\\
&\quad\equiv\exists x\in D\,\neg(A(x)\Rightarrow B(x))\\
&\quad\equiv\exists x\in D\,(A(x)\land\neg B(x)).
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To negate “every even integer is divisible by $4$,” take an integer that is even but not divisible by $4$, such as $2$. An odd integer does not refute the conditional: it fails the antecedent. Negation has not changed the integer domain or removed the condition “even.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要否定“每个偶整数都能被 $4$ 整除”，须找一个偶数却不能被 $4$ 整除的整数，例如 $2$。奇整数不能反驳这个条件句，因为它不满足前件。否定既没有改变整数论域，也没有删除“为偶数”这个条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same reasoning handles a conditional with a quantified conclusion. If $A(x)$ is an eligibility condition and $R(x,y)$ says that $y$ is a valid choice for $x$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件句的后件含量词时，也用同样的步骤。若 $A(x)$ 表示资格条件，$R(x,y)$ 表示 $y$ 是适合 $x$ 的选择，那么

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\neg\bigl[\forall x\in D\,(A(x)\Rightarrow\exists y\in E\,R(x,y))\bigr]\\
&\quad\equiv\exists x\in D\,\bigl(A(x)\land\forall y\in E\,\neg R(x,y)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The failure is an eligible object with no valid choice. Merely finding an ineligible object proves nothing against the original claim. More generally, identify the whole target with brackets, pass the negation through each quantifier, and then negate the remaining inner statement by its truth conditions. The operation is different from rewriting a conditional as its equivalent contrapositive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

失败情形是：有一个具备资格的对象，却没有任何有效选择。只找到一个不具备资格的对象，不能反驳原句。一般而言，先用括号圈定整个目标，再逐层让否定通过量词，最后按真值条件否定剩下的内部陈述。这与把条件句改写成等价的逆否命题是不同操作。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Negating $\forall x(P(x)\Rightarrow Q(x))$ also requires negating the implication, yielding $\exists x(P(x)\land\neg Q(x))$. Merely changing the quantifier is incomplete.
- The domain remains fixed. Negation does not permit silently shrinking “all real numbers” to “all positive real numbers.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 否定 $\forall x(P(x)\Rightarrow Q(x))$ 后还要否定内部蕴含，得到 $\exists x(P(x)\land\neg Q(x))$；只换量词而不处理谓词不完整。
- 论域保持不变。不能在否定时顺便把“所有实数”缩成“所有正实数”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Other free variables, parameters and relation meanings also stay fixed. If $P(x,a)$ still depends on a parameter $a$, the negation rule holds separately for each fixed $a$; it does not insert a new quantifier for $a$. Likewise, if $y$ is restricted to a set $E(x)$ depending on $x$, keep that same restriction when moving the negation inward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他自由变量、参数与关系含义也保持固定。如果 $P(x,a)$ 仍含参数 $a$，否定规则是对每个固定 $a$ 分别成立的，并不会替 $a$ 自动添加量词。同样，若 $y$ 的范围是依赖 $x$ 的集合 $E(x)$，否定向内移动时应保留相同限制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The source uses a nonempty first-order domain. For restricted quantification over an empty set, the usual convention makes a universal claim true and an existential claim false. The two negation laws remain consistent with those values: $\neg\forall x\in\varnothing\,P(x)$ and $\exists x\in\varnothing\,\neg P(x)$ are both false; the other pair are both true. This is a boundary check, not permission to change a nonempty domain into an empty one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所引教材使用非空一阶论域。若讨论空集上的限制量化，通常约定全称为真、存在为假。两条否定规则与这些真值一致：$\neg\forall x\in\varnothing\,P(x)$ 和 $\exists x\in\varnothing\,\neg P(x)$ 同为假；另一对同为真。这是在检查边界，不是允许把原来的非空论域改成空集。

> [!question]- Identify the original claim first · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Formally negate “every integer has a nonnegative integer smaller than it.”
>
> **Answer:** If the original statement is $\forall n\in\mathbb Z\exists m\in\mathbb N:m<n$, its negation is $\exists n\in\mathbb Z\forall m\in\mathbb N:m\ge n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 写出“每个整数都有一个比它小的非负整数”的否定，论域与前文相同。
>
> **答案：** 若原式为 $\forall n\in\mathbb Z\exists m\in\mathbb N:m<n$，其否定是 $\exists n\in\mathbb Z\forall m\in\mathbb N:m\ge n$。
