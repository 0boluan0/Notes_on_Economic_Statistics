# Choosing an error covariance method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*误差协方差决策*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose monthly sales are regressed on advertising expenditure. Large residuals in high-sales months suggest unequal noise; runs of positive residuals suggest persistence. These observations raise an uncertainty question, but they may also reveal an omitted seasonal mean. **Choosing an error covariance method means deciding what uncertainty to estimate and which observations may share information, then choosing a method whose assumptions fit that problem.** It is not simply selecting the smallest reported standard error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设用广告支出解释月度销售额。高销售月份的残差更大，可能提示噪声大小不同；连续出现正残差，可能提示冲击持续。这些现象引出不确定性问题，也可能暴露遗漏的季节性均值。**误差协方差决策，就是先明确要估计哪种不确定性、哪些观测可能共享信息，再选择假设与问题相符的方法。** 它并非从软件输出中挑最小的标准误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Identify the target before changing the error bars
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确目标，再改变误差条*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write down the [[估计对象三分|estimand]]: for example, a descriptive linear-projection slope, a correctly specified conditional-mean coefficient, or an identified causal effect. These are different claims. A [[条件均值与线性投影|linear projection]] is the best linear summary under its stated squared-error criterion; it need not be the full conditional mean. If that is the intended target, a nonlinear conditional mean does not by itself invalidate every robust analysis. It does change which assumptions and interpretation need justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写清[[估计对象三分|估计对象]]：例如描述性的线性投影斜率、正确设定的条件均值系数，或已识别的因果效应。这些是不同主张。[[条件均值与线性投影|线性投影]]是在指定平方误差准则下的最佳线性概括，不一定等于完整条件均值。如果它就是目标，条件均值非线性并不自动推翻所有稳健分析，但必须据此明确假设和解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next describe the experiment that could produce another dataset: independent people, repeatedly observed firms, one time series, or treatment assigned to whole schools. Sampling and assignment can matter as much as the appearance of residuals. A company name is not, by itself, proof that the company is the right clustering unit. [[标准误口径匹配|Matching the covariance to the design]] connects these choices to the uncertainty of the actual coefficient or contrast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着说明另一份数据会怎样产生：独立抽取个人、重复观察企业、观测一条时间序列，还是把处理分配给整所学校。抽样与处理分配的重要性不低于残差外观；有一个“企业”标签，本身并不证明企业就是正确聚类单位。[[标准误口径匹配]]把这些选择与实际系数或对比的不确定性联系起来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the claim concerns a conditional mean or a causal coefficient, check whether omitted nonlinearities, trends, seasons, lags, breaks or endogeneity undermine that claim. [[残差协方差诊断|Residual diagnostics]] supply evidence to investigate; they do not identify the cause by themselves. Changing the covariance estimator cannot repair an estimator centred on the wrong target. Keep this [[设定问题分层|separation of mean, covariance and identification]] explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果主张涉及条件均值或因果系数，应检查遗漏的非线性、趋势、季节、滞后、结构变化或内生性是否破坏该主张。[[残差协方差诊断]]提供调查线索，不会自行识别原因。改变协方差估计量，不能修复中心已经偏离研究目标的估计量；应明确[[设定问题分层|均值、协方差与识别的层次区别]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Match the method to its job
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让方法承担对应的职责*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a justified regression target, the following are common routes, not an exhaustive classification or automatic test-based algorithm. Each row retains its own moment, design and large-sample requirements.

| Situation to justify | Method and responsibility |
|---|---|
| Independent observations with unknown, possibly unequal variances | [[异方差稳健协方差\|HC]] keeps OLS coefficients and estimates their covariance; standard asymptotics require suitable moments and no dominant observation. |
| One ordered, equally spaced series with suitably decaying dependence | [[HAC协方差\|HAC/Newey–West]] keeps OLS coefficients and estimates long-run covariance, using a justified kernel and bandwidth sequence. |
| Dependence follows sampling, assignment or a defensible grouping structure | [[聚类标准误边界\|Cluster-robust inference]] allows within-group dependence under its framework; ordinary cluster asymptotics need enough independent groups (or a separately justified extension), not merely many rows. |
| A credible diagonal variance shape is available | [[加权最小二乘\|WLS]] changes the fit to use different observation precisions. |
| A credible full error covariance shape is available or consistently estimable | [[广义最小二乘\|GLS]] or [[可行GLS\|FGLS]] changes the fit using that structure; efficiency and model-based standard errors require the claimed covariance model and other assumptions. |
| Predictable mean dynamics are omitted, or volatility is itself the research object | Revise the mean or study a model such as [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas\|ARCH/GARCH]] for conditional variance; merely changing the reported standard error does not explain these dynamics. |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对已论证的回归目标，以下是常见路线，不是穷尽分类，也不是检验结果驱动的自动算法。每一行都有自己的矩条件、设计条件和大样本要求。

| 需要论证的情形 | 方法与职责 |
|---|---|
| 观测独立，方差未知且可能不同 | [[异方差稳健协方差\|HC]] 保留 OLS 系数并估计其协方差；常规渐近理论需要适当矩条件，且不能由某个观测主导。 |
| 单一等间隔序列，依赖以适当速度衰减 | [[HAC协方差\|HAC/Newey–West]] 保留 OLS 系数，用有依据的核和带宽序列估计长期协方差。 |
| 依赖来自抽样、处理分配或可论证的分组结构 | [[聚类标准误边界\|聚类稳健推断]] 在其框架内允许组内依赖；普通聚类渐近理论需要足够多独立的组，或另有理论支持的推广，而不只是很多行。 |
| 对角方差形状可信 | [[加权最小二乘\|WLS]] 利用不同观测精度改变拟合。 |
| 完整误差协方差形状可信，或可以一致估计 | [[广义最小二乘\|GLS]] 或 [[可行GLS\|FGLS]] 利用该结构改变拟合；效率和模型标准误都依赖所声明的协方差模型等假设。 |
| 遗漏可预测的均值动态，或波动率本身就是研究对象 | 重设均值，或用[[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas\|ARCH/GARCH]] 等模型研究条件方差；只改标准误不能解释这些动态。 |

> [!example] Changing uncertainty versus changing the estimate · 改误差条与改估计值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two independent measurements share an unknown mean $\mu$, with known variances $1$ and $4$. Their OLS mean is $(y_1+y_2)/2$, with variance $(1+4)/4=5/4$. Correctly reporting that variance changes no coefficient. Inverse-variance WLS instead uses weights $1$ and $1/4$: its estimate is $(4y_1+y_2)/5$, with variance $4/5$. If the observed values are $0$ and $4$, the two estimates are $2$ and $0.8$. Both target the same $\mu$ under the stated common-mean model; the weighted estimate uses the more precise measurement more heavily.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个独立测量共享未知均值 $\mu$，已知方差分别为 $1$、$4$。OLS 均值是 $(y_1+y_2)/2$，方差为 $(1+4)/4=5/4$；正确报告这个方差，并不改变任何系数。逆方差 WLS 使用权重 $1$、$1/4$，估计值变为 $(4y_1+y_2)/5$，方差为 $4/5$。如果实际观测是 $0$、$4$，两个估计分别为 $2$、$0.8$。在所声明的共同均值模型下，两者都以同一 $\mu$ 为目标；加权估计更多使用较精确的测量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The common target in the example matters. In a general correctly specified linear conditional-mean model, valid fixed weights can preserve the coefficient target while changing efficiency. With a misspecified mean and a descriptive projection target, reweighting can instead change which observations the best linear approximation prioritises, and hence change the target. Estimated weights in FGLS require their own justification; a fitted covariance model is not automatically the true one. Covariance-based robust inference can also be developed for weighted estimators, so “change the estimator” and “use robust uncertainty” are not mutually exclusive choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例子中的共同目标很关键。一般正确设定的线性条件均值模型中，有效的固定权重可以保留系数目标而改变效率。如果均值设定不正确、目标是描述性投影，重新加权却可能改变最佳线性近似优先照顾哪些观测，进而改变目标。FGLS 的估计权重需要单独论证，拟合出的协方差模型并不自动等于真实模型。也可以为加权估计量构造稳健协方差，所以“改变估计量”与“使用稳健不确定性”并非互斥选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check which pairs may share a shock
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检查哪些观测对可能共享冲击*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the maintained panel design allows both persistent shocks within a firm and common shocks across firms in the same year. Grouping by the **intersection** “firm × year” does not capture either whole pattern: two rows from the same firm in different years receive different group labels, as do two firms in the same year. If there is one row per firm-year, this grouping has only singleton groups and adds no cross-row products. Under a suitable multiway framework, separate firm and year dimensions can account for either shared label; it also needs adequate information in each dimension. Many firms cannot compensate automatically for very few years.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设所依据的面板设计允许同一企业内部的持续冲击，也允许同一年不同企业之间的共同冲击。按**交叉单元**“企业×年份”分组，不能覆盖这两种完整模式：同一企业不同年份被赋予不同组标签，同一年不同企业也如此。若每个企业年份只有一行，这样分组全部是单元素组，不会加入任何跨行乘积。在适当的多维聚类框架下，把企业与年份分别作为两个聚类维度，可以纳入共享任一标签的关系，但各维度也都需要足够信息；企业很多并不能自动弥补年份极少。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This example is a conditional design argument, not a rule to cluster every panel in two dimensions. Check what the sampling and assignment actually randomise, what dependence remains after modelling, and whether a proposed covariance method supports it. Nor does stacking panel rows into one long series justify ordinary time-series HAC.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子是给定设计后的判断，不是所有面板都必须双向聚类的规则。应核对抽样和处理分配实际随机化什么、建模后还保留哪些依赖，以及方法是否支持它们；把面板行串成一条长序列，也不能据此为普通时间序列 HAC 辩护。

> [!warning] Diagnostics inform a plan; they do not license convenient inference · 诊断帮助修正方案，不许可挑选结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A preliminary test that does not reject heteroskedasticity or serial correlation has not established their absence. For example, [[Breusch-Pagan检验|Breusch–Pagan (BP)]] tests specified variance patterns, while [[Breusch-Godfrey检验|Breusch–Godfrey (BG)]] tests specified residual lags. Avoid a mechanical rule such as “use robust only if BP rejects” or “use HAC only if BG rejects”, and do not try many clustering levels and keep the most favourable $p$-value. Start from the target, sampling/assignment and permitted dependence; use diagnostics to question the assumptions and report justified sensitivity analyses.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 初步检验未拒绝异方差或序列相关，并不证明它们不存在。例如，[[Breusch-Pagan检验|Breusch–Pagan（BP）检验]]检查指定的方差模式，[[Breusch-Godfrey检验|Breusch–Godfrey（BG）检验]]检查指定的残差滞后。不要机械规定“BP 拒绝才用 robust”或“BG 拒绝才用 HAC”，更不能尝试多个聚类层级后只保留最有利的 $p$ 值。应从目标、抽样／处理分配及容许依赖出发，用诊断质疑假设，并报告有依据的敏感性分析。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful report states the original and final mean model, target and information units, chosen diagnostics and their settings, covariance or weighting method, HC version or HAC kernel/bandwidth/correction or cluster counts, the covariance model used for FGLS, and the reference distribution and degrees-of-freedom calibration used for inference. Then distinguish whether a change moved the coefficient, changed its uncertainty, or changed the statistical target. This makes the reasoning reviewable without pretending that one diagnostic can choose the entire model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有用的报告应写出原始和最终均值模型、目标与信息单位、诊断及其设置、协方差或加权方法、HC 版本或 HAC 核／带宽／修正或各维聚类数，FGLS 使用的协方差模型，以及推断采用的参考分布与自由度校准。然后分清改动究竟移动了系数、改变了不确定性，还是改变了统计目标。这样判断过程才可复核，而不是让一个诊断替代全部模型选择。
