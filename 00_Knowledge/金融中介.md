# 金融中介是以自己名义发行金融索取权并持有另一组金融资产的机构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A financial intermediary issues financial claims in its own name and holds another set of financial assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 金融中介以自己的资产负债表站在资金两端之间：它向资金提供者发行存款、基金份额、保单、债券或其他索取权，再用取得的资金持有贷款或证券。客户直接持有的是对中介的索取权，而不是对每个最终融资者的索取权。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A financial intermediary stands between the two funding sides with its own balance sheet. It issues deposits, fund shares, insurance contracts, debt, or other claims to fund suppliers and uses the proceeds to hold loans or securities. Its customers hold claims on the intermediary rather than direct claims on every ultimate borrower.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例子是银行：储户的存款是银行负债，企业贷款是银行资产。两份合同在金额、期限、流动性和风险上都可不同，这正是“中介”不只是代为转交资金的原因。共同基金也是中介：投资者持有基金份额，基金持有一组底层证券，但它的负债承诺与银行存款不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bank is the simplest example: a deposit is the bank's liability and a business loan is its asset. The two contracts can differ in size, maturity, liquidity, and risk, so intermediation is more than forwarding the same money unchanged. A mutual fund is also an intermediary: investors hold fund shares and the fund holds underlying securities, although its promises differ from those of a bank deposit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与金融服务机构的边界

承销商、经纪商、托管人和清算机构可以帮助发行、交易或履约，却不必在该段交易中发行自己的融资索取权并长期持有最终信用资产。因此“有金融机构参与”不等于“存在资产负债表中介”；具体区分见 [[直接融资的服务链]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Underwriters, brokers, custodians, and clearing institutions can help issue, trade, or settle a security without issuing their own funding claim and retaining the ultimate credit asset on that leg. The presence of a financial institution is therefore not sufficient to establish balance-sheet intermediation; see [[直接融资的服务链|the direct-finance service chain]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 家庭持有银行存款，银行持有企业贷款。哪一层结构使银行成为金融中介？
>
> **答案：** 家庭持有的是银行负债，而银行持有企业资产；银行以自己名义把两份不同索取权连接起来。
