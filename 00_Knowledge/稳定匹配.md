# Stable matchings
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*稳定匹配*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **stable matching** is a feasible complete pairing in which no two people who are not currently partners would both prefer to become partners. Everyone must first have a valid assignment; stability then checks whether any cross-side pair would mutually prefer to depart from it. “Stable” describes this preference test, rather than how many pairs were selected or the total cost of the assignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**稳定匹配**是一份可行的完整配对，而且不存在这样两个人：当前没有配给彼此，却双方都更愿意成为伙伴。首先，每个人必须得到合法分配；然后再检查是否有跨侧的一对人都更愿意离开现有分配。“稳定”描述的是这个偏好检验，而不是选了多少对，或分配的总成本。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Feasibility and absence of blocking
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先可行，再检查没有阻挡对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use two disjoint finite sides $L,R$ of the same size $n\ge0$. Every participant ranks all members of the opposite side, with no ties and with every candidate acceptable. A feasible assignment is a [[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|perfect matching]]: an edge set $M$ with exactly one selected cross-side edge at each vertex. Thus it satisfies the endpoint-disjointness rule of a [[匹配定义|matching]] and covers everyone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
取两个不交有限集合 $L,R$，两侧大小同为 $n\ge0$。每个人都对另一侧全部成员给出没有并列的完整排序，并接受清单中的所有候选人。可行分配是一个[[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|完美匹配]]：边集 $M$ 使每个顶点恰接一条选中的跨侧边。因此它既满足[[匹配定义|匹配]]的端点互斥规则，又覆盖所有人。

> [!important] Stable in the complete strict model · 完整严格偏好模型中的稳定性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A feasible perfect matching $M$ is **stable** if it has no [[阻挡对|blocking pair]]. For every cross-side pair not selected in $M$, at least one of its two participants prefers their current partner to the proposed alternative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 可行完美匹配 $M$ 若没有[[阻挡对]]，就称为**稳定**。对每一对未被 $M$ 选中的跨侧组合，至少有一方更喜欢自己的当前伙伴，而不是拟更换的对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second sentence uses strict preferences: for two different candidates, one must be preferred. If $p_M(v)$ denotes $v$'s assigned partner and $u\succ_v w$ means that $v$ prefers $u$ to $w$, then a possible blocker $(\ell,r)$ would require both $r\succ_\ell p_M(\ell)$ and $\ell\succ_r p_M(r)$. Stability rules out their conjunction. One participant's unwillingness suffices to rule out that pair; one pair with both comparisons true suffices to refute stability of the entire matching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
后一句使用了严格偏好：两个不同候选人之间必有一个更受偏好。若 $p_M(v)$ 表示 $v$ 的伙伴，$u\succ_v w$ 表示 $v$ 更喜欢 $u$，则潜在阻挡对 $(\ell,r)$ 要同时满足 $r\succ_\ell p_M(\ell)$ 与 $\ell\succ_r p_M(r)$。稳定性排除这两个条件同时成立。只要有一方不愿更换，就排除了这一对；只要有一对的两条件都成立，就否定了整份匹配的稳定性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This definition is relative to the preference lists. The same selected pairs can be stable for one set of lists and unstable for another. Feasibility must be checked independently: an empty or incomplete assignment on a nonempty instance does not qualify merely because one has not specified how unmatched participants compare their options.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
定义依赖给定的偏好清单。同一组选中配对，对一套清单可能稳定，对另一套则可能不稳定。可行性必须单独检查：在非空实例中，空分配或不完整分配不能因为“尚未说明未匹配者怎样比较选项”就被算作稳定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Perfect can still be unstable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*完美匹配仍可能不稳定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $L=\{a,b\}$, $R=\{x,y\}$, and the complete lists
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
取 $L=\{a,b\}$、$R=\{x,y\}$，完整偏好为

$$y\succ_a x,\qquad x\succ_b y,\qquad
b\succ_x a,\qquad a\succ_y b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagonal assignment $M=\{\{a,x\},\{b,y\}\}$ is perfect and has the maximum possible number of pairs, two. Yet $(a,y)$ blocks it: each prefers the other to their current partner. The cross assignment $M'=\{\{a,y\},\{b,x\}\}$ is stable. Every participant gets their first choice, so no participant strictly prefers any alternative; in particular, neither of the two unselected cross-side pairs can block it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对角分配 $M=\{\{a,x\},\{b,y\}\}$ 是完美匹配，也达到了最多的两对。但 $(a,y)$ 阻挡它，因为双方都更喜欢彼此。交叉分配 $M'=\{\{a,y\},\{b,x\}\}$ 则稳定：所有人都得到首选，没人严格更愿意换人，因此另外两对未选的跨侧组合都不可能阻挡它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stability need not give everyone a first choice and need not select a unique outcome. To see both points with four people, use a different preference profile:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
稳定性不要求人人得到首选，也不保证只有一个结果。另换一组四人偏好，就能同时看到这两点：

$$x\succ_a y,\qquad y\succ_b x,\qquad
b\succ_x a,\qquad a\succ_y b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now both perfect assignments are stable. In the diagonal assignment, each left participant has their first choice, so every unselected pair is rejected by its left member. In the cross assignment, each right participant has their first choice, so every unselected pair is rejected by its right member. Each assignment leaves one side with second choices, while no pair wants to change together. These examples verify the definition; they do not specify an algorithm for finding stable matchings in larger instances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
此时两份完美分配都稳定。对角分配让每位左侧参与者得到首选，因此任何未选组合都被其左侧成员拒绝；交叉分配让每位右侧参与者得到首选，因此任何未选组合都被其右侧成员拒绝。两份分配分别让一侧得到次选，但都没有一对人愿意共同改换。这些例子是在验证定义，并没有给出一般大实例的求解算法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rankings are not an additive objective
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*偏好排序不是可相加的目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ranking states which partner one participant prefers; it does not supply a common numerical scale to add across people. Even if an independent cost is provided for every pair, minimizing total cost is a separate requirement. In the first preference profile, assign cost $0$ to $ax,by$ and cost $10$ to $ay,bx$. Among perfect matchings, the diagonal assignment has minimum cost $0$ but is unstable; the stable cross assignment has cost $20$ and is not cost-minimizing. Thus neither property implies the other when these costs are given independently of the rankings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
排序说明某个人更喜欢谁，并未提供可在不同人之间相加的统一数值尺度。即使另给每个配对一个成本，总成本最小仍是另一项要求。在第一组偏好下，给 $ax,by$ 各设成本 $0$，给 $ay,bx$ 各设成本 $10$。在完美匹配中，对角分配总成本最低，为 $0$，却不稳定；稳定的交叉分配成本为 $20$，又不最小。在这些独立给定的成本下，两个性质互不推出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
See [[稳定匹配与普通匹配|stable versus ordinary matching]] for the distinction between allowed-edge feasibility and preferences. A stable matching in this complete model is automatically maximum in cardinality because it is perfect; that fact does not make maximum-cardinality matching a stability test. Nor is “no blocking pair” a claim about resilience to every group rearrangement, changing preferences or physical disruptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
允许边可行性与偏好的区别另见[[稳定匹配与普通匹配]]。本完整模型中的稳定匹配既然完美，当然也达到最大配对数；但这不意味着最大基数匹配能替代稳定性检验。“没有阻挡对”也没有承诺对任意集体重排、偏好改变或现实扰动都保持不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Small and changed models
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*小规模情形与模型改变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $n=0$, the empty matching is perfect and there are no blocking pairs, so it is stable under the empty-instance convention. If $n=1$, the single available pair is the unique feasible perfect matching and is stable. With ties, incomplete lists, unequal side sizes or multiple slots, one must first specify acceptability, unmatched outcomes and what kind of preference improvement counts as blocking. This card's complete strict one-to-one definition does not silently extend to those variants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$n=0$ 时，空匹配完美，且不存在阻挡对，因此按空实例约定它稳定。$n=1$ 时，唯一可选的一对构成唯一可行完美匹配，并且稳定。若加入偏好并列、不完整清单、两侧不等大或多个名额，必须先规定可接受性、未匹配结果以及什么偏好改进算作阻挡；不能把本卡完整严格一对一定义悄悄套到这些变体上。
