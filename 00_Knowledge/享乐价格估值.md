# 享乐价格估值从复合商品价格对属性的边际变化识别隐含属性价格

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hedonic valuation identifies implicit attribute prices from marginal changes in a bundled good's price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

享乐价格估值把住宅、工作等视为一组属性的组合，通过可比较的市场价格表，研究某一属性微小变化对应的价格变化。它属于[[显示偏好估值]]；价格表的局部斜率是隐含边际价格，只有满足相应选择条件，才等于在该点选择者的边际支付意愿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hedonic valuation treats housing or jobs as bundles of attributes and studies marginal price changes along a comparable market schedule. As a [[显示偏好估值|revealed-preference method]], it identifies an implicit marginal price. Equating that slope with a chooser's marginal willingness to pay additionally requires the appropriate choice conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以有益属性 $z$ 为例，固定其他属性与市场环境，消费者可在光滑价格表 $P(z)$ 上自由选择，消费其他商品 $c=m-P(z)$。若效用可微、$u_c>0$ 且最优选择为内点，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a desirable attribute $z$, hold other attributes and the market environment fixed. A consumer chooses freely along a smooth schedule $P(z)$ with other consumption $c=m-P(z)$. If utility is differentiable, $u_c>0$ and the optimum is interior:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\max_z u(m-P(z),z),\qquad P'(z^*)=\frac{u_z}{u_c}.$$

自造价格表 $P(z)=100+2z+z^2$ 在 $z=3$ 的隐含价格为8；从3增加到3.01的实际价差为0.0801，局部线性近似为0.08。这个斜率不是价格水平115，也不能直接用于任意大幅改善或所有住户。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the constructed schedule $P(z)=100+2z+z^2$, the implicit price at $z=3$ is 8. Increasing the attribute to 3.01 changes price by 0.0801, compared with the local approximation 0.08. The slope is not the price level of 115 and does not directly value arbitrarily large improvements or all households.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计时要处理遗漏属性与人群排序：不同家庭偏好和不同供给者成本共同决定均衡价格表。识别出价格表不等于识别出所有人的完整需求曲线；大项目改变价格表时，不能把原局部斜率直接乘总规模当作总福利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Estimation must address omitted attributes and sorting. Heterogeneous preferences and supplier costs jointly shape the equilibrium schedule. Identifying that schedule does not identify everyone's full demand curve; a large project that changes the schedule cannot be valued simply by multiplying its scale by the original local slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
