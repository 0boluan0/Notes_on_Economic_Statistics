# 楔形是两条同向倾斜边界相互收敛的价格形态

楔形是在选定观察尺度下，价格高低点分别形成两条同向倾斜、彼此间距逐渐缩小的边界，从而围成收敛区间的多期价格形态。两线都上倾是上升楔形，两线都下倾是下降楔形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A wedge is a multi-bar price formation bounded by two lines that slope in the same direction while moving closer together. Both lines slope upward in a rising wedge and downward in a falling wedge, at a specified observation scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一线性坐标下，设上界 $U(t)=a+bt$、下界 $L(t)=c+dt$，且识别区间内 $U(t)>L(t)$。收敛要求间距变化率 $b-d<0$；上升楔形为 $0<b<d$，下降楔形为 $b<d<0$。这些不等式是几何定义的表达，不是一个已经验证有收益的交易算法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On common linear axes, let the upper and lower lines be $U(t)=a+bt$ and $L(t)=c+dt$, with $U(t)>L(t)$ throughout the identified interval. Contraction requires $b-d<0$: a rising wedge has $0<b<d$, while a falling wedge has $b<d<0$. These inequalities express geometry, not a profit-validated trading algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $U(t)=120+t$、$L(t)=100+2t$ 在 $0\le t\le5$ 内都上升，间距从20缩到15，符合上升楔形边界。[[旗形]]的两边界近似平行，[[对称三角形]]则上界下降、下界上升；三者不能只凭“越走越窄”的印象混称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $U(t)=120+t$ and $L(t)=100+2t$ both rise over $0\le t\le5$, while their gap shrinks from 20 to 15. A [[旗形|flag]] instead has approximately parallel boundaries; a [[对称三角形|symmetrical triangle]] has a falling upper and rising lower boundary. These structures are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别还需固定价格口径、极值与触及规则，并区分候选楔形与已确认的边界突破。“上升楔形”中的上升描述两条边界，不保证随后上涨；下降楔形也不保证随后下跌。课程示意的反向离开路径不构成必然结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identification also requires fixed price, pivot and touch conventions, and a distinction between a candidate wedge and a confirmed breakout. “Rising” describes its boundaries, not a guaranteed subsequent rise; “falling” does not guarantee a subsequent fall. The course's opposite-direction exits are illustrative rather than necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
