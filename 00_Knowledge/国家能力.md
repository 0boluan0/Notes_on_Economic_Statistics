# 国家能力是国家落实目标并履行基本职能的能力

国家能力（state capacity）指国家依靠资源、组织与实际触达范围，把目标或政策落实为行动、履行基本职能的能力。本卡采用 Hanson 与 Sigman 的比较研究口径，关注筹集收入、维护内外安全秩序，以及组织行政和基本基础设施供给等职能；它不同于政府选择介入多少领域，也不由政策目标是否值得赞同来定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State capacity is the state's ability to implement goals or policies and perform core functions through its resources, organization and effective reach. Following Hanson and Sigman's comparative approach, these functions include revenue extraction, security and order, administration and basic infrastructure. Capacity is distinct from the scope of government activity and from approval of its objectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以从三个相互联系的维度理解这种能力：**汲取能力**提供税收等资源，**强制能力**支持维护秩序和执行法律，**行政能力**涉及专业人员、信息、协调和服务组织。三者是分析视角，不是互不相干的三个分数；只有预算或人员数量，而没有相应的组织和执行能力，也不能概括整体能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three interrelated dimensions help organize the concept: extractive capacity mobilizes revenue, coercive capacity supports order and enforcement, and administrative capacity concerns personnel, information, coordination and service delivery. They are analytical lenses, not three unrelated scores. Budget or staffing levels alone do not capture the organizational ability to act.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

能力、选择与结果要分别观察。税收收入较少，可能反映较低的征税选择，而非收不上应纳税款；政府没有开展某项服务，也可能是没有优先安排，而非没有能力。反过来，落实了一项政策不自动证明其目标合理或公众受益，评价还需明确福利标准与[[公共项目反事实|比较基准]]。[[制度塑造发展激励|规则和权力约束]]会影响这种能力服务于谁。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate capability, choice and outcome. Low revenue may reflect a choice to tax less rather than inability to collect, and an absent service may reflect priorities rather than incapacity. Conversely, implementing a policy establishes neither a desirable objective nor public benefit; evaluation needs a welfare criterion and a [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual]]. [[制度塑造发展激励|Rules and constraints on power]] influence whose interests capacity serves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

国家层面的概念适合提出总体比较问题，但不能直接诊断某个项目。若问一条道路为什么没有按期修复，应转到负责机构的[[公共组织实施能力]]，核对具体任务、地区与时期。同一国家内部，不同机构与地区的能力可以不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The national concept frames broad comparisons but does not diagnose an individual project. A delayed road repair requires examining the responsible agency's [[公共组织实施能力|implementation capacity]] for that task, place and period. Capabilities can vary across agencies and regions within one country.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
