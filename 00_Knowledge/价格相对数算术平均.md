# 价格相对数算术平均先计算各项价格比再按给定权重相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An arithmetic average of price relatives first forms individual price ratios and then adds them with specified weights.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较同一组对象在基期 $0$ 和报告期 $t$ 的价格，先对每项算 $R_i=p_{it}/p_{i0}$。基期价格须为正，价格单位与公司行动处理须可比。给定非负权重且 $\sum_iw_i=1$，基值为 $B$ 时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same objects in periods $0$ and $t$, form $R_i=p_{it}/p_{i0}$ with positive base prices and comparable units and corporate-action treatment. For nonnegative weights summing to one and base value $B$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$I_A=B\sum_iw_iR_i;\qquad w_i=1/n\text{ 时 }I_A=\frac{B}{n}\sum_i\frac{p_{it}}{p_{i0}}.$$

两股从 10、20 变为 12、18，价格比分别是 1.2、0.9；等权算术指数为 105。若先把价格加总再作比，则为 $100(12+18)/(10+20)=100$，因为后者给高基期价格更大隐含权重。先平均“倍数”和先平均“价格”，回答的不是同一个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prices of 10 and 20 becoming 12 and 18 give relatives 1.2 and 0.9, so the equally weighted arithmetic index is 105. The ratio of price sums is instead 100, because it implicitly gives the higher base price more weight. Averaging ratios and comparing price totals answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若从基期开始各投相同金额、持股不变且忽略股息和费用，这一固定基期公式对应该组合价格价值的变化。若每期重设等权，则要按[[等权指数]]的再平衡规则逐期连接，不能混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With equal amounts invested at the base date, unchanged holdings and no dividends or fees, the fixed-base formula tracks that portfolio's price value. Periodic equal-weight rebalancing instead requires the chaining rule described in [[等权指数|equal-weighted indices]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
