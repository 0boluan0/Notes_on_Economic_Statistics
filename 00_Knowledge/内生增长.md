# 内生增长是由模型内部的积累或创新机制支撑的持续人均产出增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Endogenous growth is sustained growth in output per person supported by accumulation or innovation mechanisms within the model.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内生增长模型要解释的是：**经济中的积累、学习或创新过程，为什么能持续提供增长动力？** 它把相应过程及其资源约束写进模型，让长期增长由这些关系决定。在带外生技术的 Solow 基准中，模型可以解释资本怎样调整，却把维持长期人均增长的技术增长率留作外部输入，见 [[Solow 的外生技术边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An endogenous-growth model explains how accumulation, learning or innovation can sustain growth. It specifies the relevant processes and resource constraints so that their interaction determines long-run growth. The Solow benchmark with exogenous technology explains capital adjustment while taking the technological growth rate that sustains per-capita growth as given; see [[Solow 的外生技术边界|Solow's exogenous-technology boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积累与创新是不同的办法。例如，AK 模型可以保持技术系数不变，依靠不会因资本增加而趋于零的资本回报维持积累；研发模型则说明研究资源怎样创造设计或改善质量。前一种办法已经可以产生内生增长，却没有因此解释新技术怎样被发明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accumulation and innovation are distinct routes. An AK model can keep its technology coefficient fixed while sustaining accumulation through a capital return that does not approach zero. An R&D model instead explains how research creates designs or improves quality. The first route can generate endogenous growth without explaining invention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“内生”是相对于所选模型的解释范围而言的，不表示所有参数都由模型决定。一个模型仍可把偏好、人口路径、研发效率或制度作为给定条件；改变政策是否永久改变增长率，也必须从具体方程判断。一次提高产出水平，或一段追赶期增长加快，都还不足以说明长期增长机制改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Endogeneity is relative to the model's explanatory scope, not a requirement that it determine every parameter. Preferences, population, research productivity or institutions may remain given. Whether a policy permanently changes growth must be established from that model's equations. A higher output level or faster transitional catch-up alone does not establish a changed long-run growth mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断时先找到三件事：要解释的是否是人均长期增长；哪种资源或知识在持续积累；哪些关系决定积累速度。技术来自国内还是国外是另一条分类轴，见 [[技术来源与内生性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the long-run per-capita outcome, what resource or knowledge accumulates, and which relationships determine its accumulation rate. Domestic versus foreign origin is a separate distinction; see [[技术来源与内生性|technology origin versus model endogeneity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
