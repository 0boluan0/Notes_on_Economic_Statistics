# Smith 把国防、司法和私人收益不足以支撑的公共工程与机构列为政府的基本职责
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Smith assigns the sovereign basic duties of defence, justice, and public works and institutions that private returns cannot sustain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 的市场社会需要公共责任支撑。在《国富论》第五卷中，他依次讨论：保护社会免受外部暴力与侵略的**国防**，保护成员免受他人不公和压迫的**司法**，以及对社会有重要益处、却不能指望个别人从收益中收回费用的**公共工程和机构**。第三类特别涉及便利商业的工程与促进教育的机构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith's commercial society depends on public responsibilities. Book V discusses **defence** against external violence and invasion, **justice** protecting members from injustice and oppression by others, and **public works and institutions** whose benefits to society are important but whose returns do not make it reasonable to expect a few individuals to finance them. The third category particularly concerns works facilitating commerce and institutions promoting instruction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里有一条直接接回增长主线的关系。道路、桥梁、运河和港口降低运输困难，使更多生产者和购买者能相互交易；而[[分工的市场限制|市场范围又限制着分工能细到什么程度]]。因此，公共工程不是放在“市场之外”的附录，它可能改变市场和专业化本身的可行范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This connects directly to the growth argument. Roads, bridges, canals, and harbours reduce transport difficulties and connect more producers and buyers; [[分工的市场限制|the extent of that market limits how far specialisation can proceed]]. Public works can therefore change the feasible reach of markets and specialisation rather than merely sit outside them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不过，负有公共职责还没有自动决定**怎样筹钱、由谁经营、怎样维护**。Smith 随后讨论路桥通行费、港口费，并比较不同工程的维护激励。例如，运河失修会使通航和收费收入一起停止，道路却可能在维护很差时仍能收费；他据此对管理安排作不同判断。公共责任在这里需要落实成具体制度，而不是只贴一个“由政府提供”的标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public responsibility does not itself determine **financing, operation, or maintenance**. Smith goes on to discuss road and bridge tolls, harbour dues, and differences in maintenance incentives. A neglected canal may become unusable and lose its toll revenue, whereas a poorly maintained road can remain passable and continue collecting tolls. He uses that difference to assess alternative management arrangements. Responsibility must therefore be translated into institutions, not merely labelled government provision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现代可以用[[公共品的筹资、生产与治理|筹资、生产和治理的角色区分]]继续分析这些安排。但不要因此把 Smith 的道路、港口和教育一律改称严格非排他的纯公共品；他的主要切入点是社会受益与个别经营者可回收收益之间的差别，并结合收费和监督讨论办法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Modern analysis can pursue these arrangements through the [[公共品的筹资、生产与治理|distinction between financing, production, and governance]]. That does not make all of Smith's roads, ports, and educational institutions strictly non-excludable pure public goods. His central concern is the difference between benefits to society and returns recoverable by individual operators, followed by concrete questions of charging and oversight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读[[看不见的手的原语境|私人利益与公共结果]]时，可以把这组职责一同放在眼前：一边考察私人选择怎样引导资本，另一边考察权利保护、交通和教育怎样支撑这些选择。两者共同构成 Smith 对市场社会的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read these duties alongside [[看不见的手的原语境|private interest and public consequences]]: examine both how private choices direct capital and how protection of rights, transport, and education support those choices. Together they form Smith's account of commercial society.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
