# 基金份额净值是特定估值时点的基金净资产除以对应存续份额数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Net asset value per fund unit divides the fund's net assets at a valuation time by the corresponding outstanding units*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基金份额净值把资产池的[[净值]]换算到一份基金单位。先固定估值日期、币种、资产负债覆盖与估值规则，再用同一时点的存续份额数相除。总净资产与每份净值不是同一个数量；英文 NAV 有时指前者，有时是 per-share NAV 的简称，须由语境辨认。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Per-unit NAV converts the pool's [[净值|net assets]] into a value per fund unit. Fix the valuation time, currency, coverage, and valuation rules before dividing by outstanding units at that same time. Total net assets and per-unit NAV are different quantities; English usage sometimes abbreviates either as NAV, so the convention must be explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$v_t=\frac{A_t-L_t}{N_t},\qquad N_t>0.$$

单一同权份额基金的资产为 110 万元、负债为 10 万元，存续 50 万份，则总净资产为 100 万元，每份净值为 2 元。若有不同份额类别，应使用分配给该类别的净资产与该类别份额数，不能把全基金净资产除以其中一类的份额数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a single-class fund with assets of 1.1 million, liabilities of 0.1 million, and 0.5 million units, total net assets are 1 million and NAV is 2 per unit. Multiple classes require the net assets attributable to a class and its own unit count, not total fund assets divided by one class's units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净值是估值结果，不是保证即时成交的报价。普通[[开放式基金]]按适用估值时点的净值及费用规则办理申赎；[[ETF]]或上市[[封闭式基金]]的二级市场成交价由订单形成，可能出现[[基金折溢价]]。昨天公布的净值也不是今天每一时刻都可兑现的资产价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NAV is a valuation result, not a guaranteed executable quote. Ordinary [[开放式基金|open-end mutual funds]] process subscriptions and redemptions using the applicable valuation time and fee rules. Secondary-market prices of [[ETF|ETFs]] or listed [[封闭式基金|closed-end funds]] arise from orders and may show [[基金折溢价|premiums or discounts]]. Yesterday's reported NAV is not an asset price guaranteed to be realizable at every moment today.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
