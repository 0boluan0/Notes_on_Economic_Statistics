# Ordinary least squares
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*普通最小二乘（OLS）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Ordinary least squares** is a rule for fitting a linear combination of chosen predictors to observed outcomes. For every candidate set of coefficients, compute the gap between each observed outcome and its fitted value, square each gap, and add them. OLS chooses coefficients that make this total as small as possible. The gaps are [[回归残差|residuals]]: observed minus fitted, with a positive residual meaning that the fit is too low at that observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**普通最小二乘**是一条拟合规则：用选定解释变量的线性组合拟合观测结果。对每组候选系数，计算每个观测结果与拟合值的缺口，把缺口平方后相加，再选使总和尽可能小的系数。这里的缺口叫[[回归残差|残差]]，按“观测减拟合”计算；正残差表示该点的拟合值偏低。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A line is judged by all its gaps
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose three illustrative households have incomes $x=(0,1,2)$ and consumptions $y=(1,2,2)$, measured in common monetary units. Fit consumption with $a+cx$, where $a$ is the intercept and $c$ the change in the fitted value for one unit of income. Each pair $(a,c)$ gives three fitted values. The candidate line $1+x$ passes through the first two observations, but misses the third by $-1$; its sum of squared residuals is $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设三个示意家庭的收入为 $x=(0,1,2)$，消费为 $y=(1,2,2)$，统一使用相同货币单位。用 $a+cx$ 拟合消费；$a$ 是截距，$c$ 是收入增加一单位时拟合值的改变量。每组 $(a,c)$ 都给出三个拟合值。候选直线 $1+x$ 经过前两个观测，却在第三点留下 $-1$ 的残差，残差平方和为 $1$。

> [!example] Smaller total error without passing through a point / 没经过任何点，总平方误差反而更小
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For these data, OLS gives $a=7/6$ and $c=1/2$. The three fitted values are $(7/6,5/3,13/6)$, so the residuals are $(-1/6,1/3,-1/6)$. Their squared total is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本例 OLS 得到 $a=7/6$、$c=1/2$。三个拟合值为 $(7/6,5/3,13/6)$，残差为 $(-1/6,1/3,-1/6)$，平方和为
>
> $$(-1/6)^2+(1/3)^2+(-1/6)^2=1/6.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This line passes through none of the three observations, but its total is smaller than $1$. OLS optimizes the sum, not the number of points touched. The [[OLS正规方程|normal equations]] show how to obtain these coefficients and certify the minimum over every candidate line.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这条线没有经过三个观测中的任何一个点，但总平方误差小于 $1$。OLS 优化的是总和，而不是经过的点数。[[OLS正规方程]]说明怎样算出这组系数，并验证它在全部候选直线中达到最小值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the scatterplot these are **vertical gaps**, measured along the outcome axis while the predictor positions remain fixed. They are not the shortest perpendicular distances from the two-dimensional points to the line. There is also a compatible vector picture: the three vertical gaps form one residual vector, whose squared length is the same sum. That is the [[线性最小二乘|linear least-squares problem]] in the space of outcome vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在散点图上，这些是沿结果轴计算的**竖直缺口**，解释变量的位置保持不变；不是二维点到直线的最短垂直距离。另一个相容的向量视角是：三个竖直缺口组成一个残差向量，它的长度平方就是同一个平方和。这便是结果向量空间中的[[线性最小二乘]]问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Write the same rule for several predictors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把同一规则写给多个解释变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $n\ge1$ observed rows and $k\ge1$ fitted coefficients. The real **design matrix** $X$ has $n$ rows and $k$ columns: row $i$ records the predictor values for observation $i$, and each column records one predictor term across all observations. An intercept contributes a column of ones. The real column vector $y$ contains the $n$ outcomes. A candidate coefficient vector $b\in\mathbb R^k$ supplies one multiplier for each column, so [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] produces the fitted vector $Xb$.

For the three-household example, this is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用 $n\ge1$ 个观测行和 $k\ge1$ 个拟合系数。实数**设计矩阵** $X$ 有 $n$ 行、$k$ 列：第 $i$ 行记录第 $i$ 个观测的解释变量取值，每列记录一个解释项在所有观测中的取值。截距对应全为一的一列。实列向量 $y$ 存放 $n$ 个结果。候选系数向量 $b\in\mathbb R^k$ 为每列提供一个乘数，通过[[矩阵乘法]]得到拟合向量 $Xb$。

三个家庭的例子写成

$$X=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
b=\begin{bmatrix}a\\c\end{bmatrix},\qquad
y=\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\\2\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, write $x_i'$ for row $i$ of $X$; the prime denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so $x_i$ itself is a $k$-component column vector. The scalar $x_i'b$ is the candidate fitted value for row $i$. Define the sum-of-squares function $Q$ by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，用 $x_i'$ 表示 $X$ 的第 $i$ 行；撇号表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，所以 $x_i$ 本身是有 $k$ 个分量的列向量。标量 $x_i'b$ 就是第 $i$ 行的候选拟合值。把平方和函数记为 $Q$：

$$Q(b)=\sum_{i=1}^n(y_i-x_i'b)^2=\lVert y-Xb\rVert_2^2,\qquad
\hat\beta\in\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{b\in\mathbb R^k}Q(b).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] $\lVert v\rVert_2$ is the square root of the sum of squared entries of $v$. The symbol $\operatorname{arg\,min}$ means the **set of coefficient vectors** attaining the smallest value; $\hat\beta$ denotes one chosen minimizer, not the minimum value of $Q$. A hat marks a quantity obtained by fitting. The resulting fitted outcomes and residuals are $\hat y=X\hat\beta$ and $\hat u=y-\hat y$.

“Ordinary” here means that every squared residual enters with the same weight. Dividing the whole sum by the same positive number $n$ leaves its minimizers unchanged. In contrast, assigning different observation weights defines a [[加权最小二乘|weighted least-squares]] criterion. The chosen columns can include a squared predictor or an interaction: the fitted expression is linear in the coefficients even when its graph is curved in an original predictor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[欧氏范数]] $\lVert v\rVert_2$ 是向量各分量平方和的平方根。$\operatorname{arg\,min}$ 表示使目标达到最小值的**系数向量集合**；$\hat\beta$ 是其中选取的一个最小化解，不是 $Q$ 的最小数值。帽号表示通过拟合得到的量。相应拟合结果和残差为 $\hat y=X\hat\beta$、$\hat u=y-\hat y$。

这里“普通”表示每个残差平方以相同权重进入目标。把整个和除以同一个正数 $n$，不会改变最小化解；给不同观测不同权重，则形成[[加权最小二乘]]准则。选定的列可以包含某个解释变量的平方或交互项；即使相对原变量的图形是弯曲的，拟合式仍可以对系数保持线性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the criterion does and does not require
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用条件与含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite real data and unrestricted coefficients, a least-squares minimum exists. The fitted outcome vector on the observed design is unique, even when several coefficient vectors produce it. Whether the coefficients themselves are unique is the separate [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full-column-rank condition]]. Thus the inverse-matrix formula is a way to solve a suitable case, not the definition of OLS.

The calculation needs no assumption that observations are independent, errors are Gaussian, or the proposed line is a true population mean. Those assumptions concern interpretation and inference. [[条件均值与线性投影|A conditional mean and a population linear projection]] are distinct targets; [[估计量无偏性|unbiasedness]], [[估计量一致性|consistency]] and causal interpretation require arguments beyond minimizing the sample loss. Sample residuals are computed gaps, not observed values of the underlying model errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限实数数据、系数不受约束时，最小二乘最小值一定存在。原设计上的拟合结果向量唯一，即使多个系数向量都能产生它。系数本身是否唯一，由另一个[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩条件]]决定。因此，逆矩阵公式是适用情形下的求解方法，不是 OLS 的定义。

计算本身不要求观测独立、误差正态，或拟合直线就是真实总体均值。这些假设涉及解释和推断。[[条件均值与线性投影|条件均值与总体线性投影]]是不同目标；[[估计量无偏性|无偏性]]、[[估计量一致性|一致性]]和因果解释，都需要样本损失最小化之外的论证。样本残差是计算得到的缺口，不是底层模型误差的直接观测值。

> [!note] Squaring changes what the fit emphasizes / 平方决定了拟合重视什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A gap of $10$ contributes $100$ to the objective, whereas a gap of $1$ contributes $1$. Large gaps can therefore strongly affect the chosen fit. This explains a sensitivity of squared loss; it does not prove that any particular observation is erroneous or should be deleted.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 缺口为 $10$ 时，对目标贡献 $100$；缺口为 $1$ 时只贡献 $1$。大缺口因此可能显著影响选出的拟合。这解释了平方损失的一种敏感性，并不证明某个观测错误或应被删除。
