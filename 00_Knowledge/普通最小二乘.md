# 普通最小二乘选择平方残差和最小的系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ordinary least squares chooses coefficients that minimise the sum of squared residuals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 在线性回归中，给定设计矩阵 $X$ 和结果向量 $y$，普通最小二乘法（OLS）把系数估计定义为任一平方残差和最小化解
> $$
> \hat\beta\in\arg\min_b\lVert y-Xb\rVert^2
> =\arg\min_b\sum_{i=1}^n(y_i-x_i'b)^2.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a linear regression with design matrix $X$ and outcome vector $y$, ordinary least squares defines the coefficient estimate as any minimiser $\hat\beta\in\arg\min_b\lVert y-Xb\rVert^2=\arg\min_b\sum_{i=1}^n(y_i-x_i'b)^2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用条件与含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use condition and meaning boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[回归残差|残差]] $\hat u_i=y_i-x_i'\hat\beta$ 是观测结果与拟合结果之差。因此在一元散点图里，OLS 最小化的是竖直距离平方，而不是点到直线的欧几里得最短距离。OLS 是估计规则，不是关于数据如何产生的模型；仅凭这个优化问题，不能推出系数无偏、一致或具有因果意义。平方损失还会让大残差获得更大的权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[回归残差|regression residual]] $\hat u_i=y_i-x_i'\hat\beta$ is the observed outcome minus its fitted value. In a simple scatterplot, OLS therefore minimises squared vertical distances rather than Euclidean point-to-line distances. OLS is an estimation rule, not a data-generating model, and this optimisation alone does not guarantee unbiasedness, consistency, or causality. Squaring also gives large residuals disproportionate influence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟合消费 $C_i=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_i+u_i$ 时，每个候选 $(\beta_0,\beta_1)$ 都产生一组残差；OLS 选择残差平方和最小的截距与斜率，而不是选择经过最多数据点的直线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When fitting consumption as $C_i=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_i+u_i$, each candidate pair $(\beta_0,\beta_1)$ produces a set of residuals. OLS selects the intercept and slope with the smallest residual sum of squares, not the line passing through the largest number of observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> OLS 最小化的“距离”是哪一个方向上的什么量？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What distance, and in which direction, does OLS minimise?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 结果变量方向上的残差平方和；不是点到拟合线的欧几里得距离。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The sum of squared residuals along the outcome axis, not Euclidean distance to the fitted line.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
