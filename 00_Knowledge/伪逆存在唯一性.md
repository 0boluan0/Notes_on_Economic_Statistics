# 每个实或复矩阵都存在唯一的 Moore–Penrose 伪逆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every real or complex matrix has a unique Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心定理
> 对每个 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$，其中 $\mathbb F\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$，恰有一个 $G\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ 满足四个 Penrose 方程。因此，每个实或复矩阵的 Moore–Penrose 伪逆都存在且唯一。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ with $\mathbb F\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$, there is exactly one $G\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ satisfying the four Penrose equations. Hence every real or complex matrix has a unique Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 存在性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Existence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[伪逆SVD公式]] 在完整 SVD 坐标中构造一个候选矩阵：只逆转正奇异值对应的对角块，并在其余方向取零。直接相乘后，$AG$ 与 $GA$ 分别成为由奇异向量张成空间上的正交投影，所以它们为 Hermitian；同样的分块乘法还给出 $AGA=A$ 与 $GAG=G$。因此，这个候选矩阵满足四个 Penrose 方程，存在性得证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[伪逆SVD公式|SVD formula for the pseudoinverse]] constructs a candidate in full-SVD coordinates by inverting the diagonal block associated with positive singular values and setting all remaining directions to zero. Direct multiplication makes $AG$ and $GA$ the orthogonal projectors onto the corresponding singular-vector subspaces, so both are Hermitian; the same block multiplication also gives $AGA=A$ and $GAG=G$. Thus the candidate satisfies all four Penrose equations, proving existence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 唯一性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取完整 SVD
$$
A=U\Sigma V^*,
\qquad
\Sigma=
\begin{bmatrix}
\Sigma_r&0\\
0&0
\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb F^{m\times n},
$$
其中 $\Sigma_r$ 是可逆的 $r\times r$ 对角矩阵。对任意满足四个 Penrose 方程的候选矩阵 $G\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$，令
$$
C=V^*GU
=\begin{bmatrix}
C_{11}&C_{12}\\
C_{21}&C_{22}
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
四个块的尺寸依次为 $r\times r$、$r\times(m-r)$、$(n-r)\times r$ 与 $(n-r)\times(m-r)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a full SVD as displayed above, where $\Sigma_r$ is an invertible $r\times r$ diagonal matrix. For any candidate $G\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ satisfying the four Penrose equations, introduce the displayed block matrix $C=V^*GU$. The four blocks have sizes $r\times r$, $r\times(m-r)$, $(n-r)\times r$, and $(n-r)\times(m-r)$, respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这些坐标中，$AGA=A$ 等价于 $\Sigma C\Sigma=\Sigma$，先迫使 $C_{11}=\Sigma_r^{-1}$。$AG$ 与 $GA$ 的 Hermitian 条件分别迫使 $C_{12}=0$ 与 $C_{21}=0$。最后，$GAG=G$ 等价于 $C\Sigma C=C$，它迫使 $C_{22}=0$。所以四个方程把 $C$ 的每一个块都唯一确定，进而把 $G=VCU^*$ 唯一确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In these coordinates, $AGA=A$ becomes $\Sigma C\Sigma=\Sigma$, which first forces $C_{11}=\Sigma_r^{-1}$. The Hermitian conditions on $AG$ and $GA$ force $C_{12}=0$ and $C_{21}=0$, respectively. Finally, $GAG=G$ becomes $C\Sigma C=C$ and forces $C_{22}=0$. The four equations therefore determine every block of $C$, and hence determine $G=VCU^*$ uniquely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary cases*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 是可逆方阵，唯一的 Moore–Penrose 伪逆就是普通逆 $A^{-1}$。若 $A$ 为矩形或秩亏矩阵，普通双侧逆不存在，但上述定理仍保证 Moore–Penrose 伪逆存在且唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ is square and invertible, its unique Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse is the ordinary inverse $A^{-1}$. If $A$ is rectangular or rank deficient, an ordinary two-sided inverse does not exist, but the theorem still guarantees a unique Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在完整 SVD 坐标中，四个 Penrose 方程分别怎样排除 $C$ 的四个块的自由度？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In full-SVD coordinates, how do the four Penrose equations eliminate the freedom in the four blocks of $C$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** $AGA=A$ 给出 $C_{11}=\Sigma_r^{-1}$；两个 Hermitian 条件给出 $C_{12}=C_{21}=0$；$GAG=G$ 最后给出 $C_{22}=0$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The equation $AGA=A$ gives $C_{11}=\Sigma_r^{-1}$; the two Hermitian conditions give $C_{12}=C_{21}=0$; and $GAG=G$ finally gives $C_{22}=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
