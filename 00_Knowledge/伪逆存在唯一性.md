# Existence and uniqueness of the pseudoinverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*伪逆的存在与唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose definition]] asks one matrix to satisfy four rules. A definition alone does not guarantee that those rules are compatible, or that they select only one answer. The **existence-and-uniqueness theorem** supplies both guarantees: every finite real or complex matrix has exactly one Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse, including rectangular and singular matrices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose 定义]]要求一个矩阵同时满足四条规则。仅仅写出定义，并不能保证这些规则彼此相容，也不能保证它们只选出一个答案。**存在唯一性定理**补上这两个保证：每个有限维实或复矩阵都有且只有一个 Moore–Penrose 伪逆，包括长方矩阵和奇异矩阵。

> [!example] One reversible direction, one lost direction / 一个可逆方向，一个丢失方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$ sends an input $(x_1,x_2)^T$ to $(2x_1,0)^T$. Its first coordinate is recoverable by dividing by 2; its second input coordinate has disappeared. The pseudoinverse is $A^+=\begin{bmatrix}1/2&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$. It reverses the first stretch and assigns zero to the unobserved direction. The theorem says this rule extends to every matrix and is forced by the four conditions. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上述 $A$ 把输入 $(x_1,x_2)^T$ 变成 $(2x_1,0)^T$：第一坐标可以除以2恢复，第二输入坐标却已消失。因此 $A^+$ 撤销第一方向的伸缩，并把没有信息的方向置零。定理说明，每个矩阵都能这样构造，而且四条件会唯一确定这个选择。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Precisely, let $A$ have $m$ rows and $n$ columns, with positive integers $m,n$. There is exactly one $n\times m$ matrix $G$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

准确地说，设 $A$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，其中 $m,n$ 为正整数。恰有一个 $n\times m$ 矩阵 $G$ 满足

$$
AGA=A,\qquad GAG=G,\qquad (AG)^*=AG,\qquad (GA)^*=GA.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $*$ means [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose and conjugate every entry, changing $a+bi$ to $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$. For real matrices it is just transpose. The last two equations say that the square products are [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]]. The unique matrix $G$ is denoted $A^+$; the plus sign names this operation and is not addition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$*$ 表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]：交换行列，并把每个 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$；实矩阵时就是普通转置。后两式要求两个方阵乘积为 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]。唯一的 $G$ 记作 $A^+$，加号是这个操作的记号，不是加法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Constructing an answer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 存在性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonzero $A$, a [[紧SVD|compact singular value decomposition]] separates its $r$ active directions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非零矩阵 $A$，[[紧SVD]]把它拆成 $r$ 个真正起作用的方向：

$$
A=U_r\Sigma_rV_r^*,\qquad
\Sigma_r=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_r),\qquad \sigma_i>0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r$ is the [[矩阵秩|rank]], the number of independent output directions. The notation $\operatorname{diag}$ places the listed values on the diagonal and zeros elsewhere. The columns of $U_r$ and $V_r$ are [[标准正交组|orthonormal lists]] in the output and input spaces; each list has unit-length, mutually perpendicular vectors. The factor sizes are $m\times r$, $r\times r$, and $n\times r$. Reversing each positive stretch gives the candidate from the [[伪逆SVD公式|SVD formula]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r$ 是[[矩阵秩|秩]]，即独立输出方向的数量；$\operatorname{diag}$ 把所列值放在对角线上，其余位置为零。$U_r$、$V_r$ 的列分别是输出空间与输入空间中的[[标准正交组]]，每个向量长度为1，同组不同向量互相垂直。三个因子的尺寸分别为 $m\times r$、$r\times r$、$n\times r$。逐个撤销正伸缩，得到[[伪逆SVD公式]]中的候选：

$$
G=V_r\Sigma_r^{-1}U_r^*,\qquad
AG=U_rU_r^*,\qquad GA=V_rV_r^*.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $U_r^*U_r=V_r^*V_r=I_r$, multiplying once more gives $AGA=A$ and $GAG=G$. Here $I_r$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Both displayed square products equal their conjugate transposes, so the other two conditions hold as well. We have produced an answer, rather than merely assumed one exists. If $A=0$, choose the zero matrix of reversed size; all four conditions hold directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $U_r^*U_r=V_r^*V_r=I_r$，继续相乘便得到 $AGA=A$ 和 $GAG=G$；这里 $I_r$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。显示的两个方阵乘积都等于自身共轭转置，另外两条件也成立。这样就真正构造出了答案。如果 $A=0$，直接取反向尺寸的零矩阵即可，不需要对任何零奇异值取倒数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why no other answer survives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 唯一性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return briefly to the opening diagonal matrix and write a completely unspecified candidate as $G=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}$. The equation $AGA=A$ forces $a=1/2$. The symmetry or Hermitian conditions on $AG$ and $GA$ force $b=0$ and $c=0$. Finally $GAG=G$ forces $d=0$. No entry remains free.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先回到开头的对角矩阵，把任意候选写成 $G=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}$。$AGA=A$ 迫使 $a=1/2$；$AG$ 和 $GA$ 的对称或 Hermitian 条件分别迫使 $b=0$、$c=0$；最后 $GAG=G$ 迫使 $d=0$。每个元素都被确定了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general nonzero matrix, a [[奇异值分解|full SVD]] makes exactly this argument possible in rotated coordinates. Write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般非零矩阵也能借助[[奇异值分解|完整 SVD]]，在换过的坐标中执行同样的论证。写成

$$
A=U\Sigma V^*,\qquad
\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}\Sigma_r&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
C=V^*GU=\begin{bmatrix}C_{11}&C_{12}\\C_{21}&C_{22}\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$U$ and $V$ are square orthogonal or unitary coordinate matrices, of sizes $m$ and $n$, so their inverses are $U^*$ and $V^*$. The matrix $C$ is simply the same proposed map $G$ expressed in those coordinates. Its four blocks have sizes $r\times r$, $r\times(m-r)$, $(n-r)\times r$, and $(n-r)\times(m-r)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$U$、$V$ 分别为 $m$ 阶、$n$ 阶正交或酉坐标矩阵，逆分别为 $U^*$、$V^*$。$C$ 就是把同一个候选映射 $G$ 改写到这些坐标中。四个块的尺寸依次为 $r\times r$、$r\times(m-r)$、$(n-r)\times r$ 和 $(n-r)\times(m-r)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now $AGA=A$ becomes $\Sigma C\Sigma=\Sigma$. Its upper-left block is $\Sigma_rC_{11}\Sigma_r=\Sigma_r$, hence $C_{11}=\Sigma_r^{-1}$. Next,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$AGA=A$ 在新坐标中变成 $\Sigma C\Sigma=\Sigma$。左上块满足 $\Sigma_rC_{11}\Sigma_r=\Sigma_r$，所以 $C_{11}=\Sigma_r^{-1}$。接着，

$$
\Sigma C=\begin{bmatrix}I_r&\Sigma_rC_{12}\\0&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
C\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}I_r&0\\C_{21}\Sigma_r&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the first product to be Hermitian, its upper-right block must be the conjugate transpose of its zero lower-left block. Thus $C_{12}=0$. The second product similarly forces $C_{21}=0$. The remaining condition $C\Sigma C=C$ then forces $C_{22}=0$. Therefore $C$, and consequently $G=VCU^*$, are completely determined. When a block has zero rows or columns, it contains no unknowns; this covers full-rank rectangular matrices too. For $A=0$, uniqueness follows immediately from $GAG=G$, which reads $0=G$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一个乘积若为 Hermitian，右上块必须等于左下零块的共轭转置，因此 $C_{12}=0$；第二个乘积同理迫使 $C_{21}=0$。剩下的条件 $C\Sigma C=C$ 再迫使 $C_{22}=0$。所以 $C$ 完全确定，$G=VCU^*$ 也完全确定。零行或零列的块不含任何未知量，这也覆盖满秩长方矩阵。若 $A=0$，由 $GAG=G$ 直接得到 $0=G$，唯一性同样成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What uniqueness does and does not say
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an invertible square matrix, the ordinary inverse satisfies the four conditions, so uniqueness identifies $A^+=A^{-1}$. For a rectangular or singular matrix, a two-sided inverse is unavailable but $A^+$ still exists. This does not make every equation $Ax=b$ solvable, or make all least-squares coefficient vectors identical. It selects one [[最小范数最小二乘解|minimum-norm least-squares solution]] for each target. Nor does uniqueness guarantee numerical insensitivity: [[小奇异值放大噪声|small positive singular values]] can still magnify changes in the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可逆方阵的普通逆满足四条件，因此唯一性说明 $A^+=A^{-1}$。长方矩阵或奇异矩阵没有双侧逆，但 $A^+$ 仍存在。这不意味着每个 $Ax=b$ 都能精确求解，也不意味着所有最小二乘系数都相同；它对每个目标选定一个[[最小范数最小二乘解]]。唯一性也不保证数值上不敏感，[[小奇异值放大噪声|很小的正奇异值]]仍可能放大数据变化。

- R. Penrose, [“A generalized inverse for matrices” (1955), printed pp.406–407, Theorem 1](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100030401): existence and uniqueness for arbitrary rectangular complex matrices, including zero. The original proof uses algebraic identities; the block-SVD proof above is a direct derivation of the same theorem.
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 6.67–6.70 and 7.75/7.78, PDF pp.234–236 and 289](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=234): the uniquely defined geometric pseudoinverse and reciprocal singular-direction construction; also checked against the [[伪逆SVD公式|formula card]].
- [[03_Positive Definite Matrices and Applications#3.8.4 Moore–Penrose 四条件|Course §3.8.4]]: course notation and four-condition statement. The diagonal example and rectangular block sizes were verified by direct multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Penrose 原论文第406–407页定理1核对任意长方复矩阵（含零矩阵）的存在唯一性；原证明使用代数恒等式，本文的 SVD 分块证明是同一定理的直接推导。
- Axler 第6.67–6.70条、第7.75条及公式7.78核对几何定义与正奇异方向倒数构造，并与[[伪逆SVD公式]]对照。
