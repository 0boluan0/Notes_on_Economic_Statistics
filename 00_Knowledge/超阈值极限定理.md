# 连续母分布属于极大值吸引域等价于其高阈值超额可由同形状广义Pareto分布逼近
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a continuous parent distribution, membership in a maximum domain of attraction is equivalent to GPD approximation of high-threshold excesses with the same shape parameter*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设母分布的 CDF $F$ 连续，右端点为 $x_F=\sup\{x:F(x)<1\}$，$F_u$ 是 $u<x_F$ 时的[[超阈值法|条件超额 CDF]]。对于给定 $\xi\in\mathbb R$，$F\in\operatorname{MDA}(H_\xi)$ 当且仅当存在正的可测尺度函数 $\beta(u)>0$，使阈值从下方趋向 $x_F$ 时，下式成立。这里的“同形状”是指 GPD 与[[极大值吸引域|极大值极限 GEV]] 具有相同的 $\xi$，并不要求尺度不随阈值改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the parent CDF $F$ be continuous, with right endpoint $x_F=\sup\{x:F(x)<1\}$, and let $F_u$ be the [[超阈值法|conditional excess CDF]] for $u<x_F$. For a fixed $\xi\in\mathbb R$, $F\in\operatorname{MDA}(H_\xi)$ if and only if a positive measurable scale function $\beta(u)$ exists such that the following limit holds as $u$ approaches $x_F$ from below. “Same shape” means that the GPD and the [[极大值吸引域|GEV maxima limit]] share $\xi$; it does not mean a threshold-invariant scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\lim_{u\uparrow x_F}\ \sup_{0\le y<x_F-u}
\left|F_u(y)-G_{\xi,\beta(u)}(y)\right|=0.
$$

其中 $G$ 按[[广义Pareto分布]]的完整 CDF（包括支持边界外的 0 或 1）取值。该结果称 Pickands–Balkema–de Haan 定理：它连接归一化最大值的极限与高阈值条件超额的近似。阈值若趋近有限端点，不能写成 $u\to\infty$；若固定一个有限阈值，定理也不直接给出近似误差已经足够小的保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $G$ is the complete [[广义Pareto分布|GPD CDF]], including its zero or one values outside the support. The Pickands–Balkema–de Haan theorem connects normalised maxima limits with high-threshold conditional excess approximations. A finite endpoint requires $u\uparrow x_F$, not $u\to\infty$. At any fixed threshold, the theorem alone supplies no guarantee that the approximation error is already acceptably small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本页采用连续母分布版本；连续性是这条充要表述的前提，不是定义条件超额的前提。离散分布只要 $P(X>u)>0$ 也能定义 $F_u$，但不能只凭上述截断区间内的误差趋零，就套用本页的逆命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This page uses the continuous-parent version. Continuity is a premise of this equivalence, not of conditional excesses themselves. A discrete distribution also defines $F_u$ whenever $P(X>u)>0$, but vanishing error on the displayed truncated interval alone does not justify this converse without its premise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个有限端点例是 $X\sim U(0,1)$。对 $0\le u<1$，直接使用条件概率可得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite-endpoint example is $X\sim U(0,1)$. For $0\le u<1$, conditional probability gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F_u(y)=\frac{y}{1-u}=G_{-1,\,1-u}(y),\qquad 0\le y\le1-u.
$$

这里 GPD 已是精确分布，$\xi=-1$ 与[[极大值极限定理|该均匀分布的最大值极限]]一致，但 $\beta(u)=1-u$ 随 $u\uparrow1$ 缩小。不能把这个特殊例子的精确等式推广为所有母分布在某个固定阈值上的等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The GPD is exact in this example. Its shape $\xi=-1$ agrees with the [[极大值极限定理|uniform maxima limit]], while $\beta(u)=1-u$ shrinks as $u\uparrow1$. This special exact equality does not justify an exact GPD law at a fixed threshold for every parent distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Martin Haugh，[Extreme Value Theory，PDF 第 20、22 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/QRM/EVT_MasterSlides.pdf#page=22)：目视核对条件超额、上确界误差、右端点、尺度函数和 MDA 的充要关系。
- McNeil–Frey，[1999-06-19 作者稿，PDF／文内第 7 页、式 (7) 与脚注 4](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=7)：核对 Balkema–de Haan（1974）与 Pickands（1975）的归属及吸引域条件；均匀分布例由条件 CDF 独立推导。
- Paul Embrechts，[Statistics and Quantitative Risk Management，Ottawa 2008，PDF 第 25–26 页](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~embrecht/ftp/Ottawa2008_english.pdf#page=25)：本页连续母分布版本的直接依据；重新目视核对第 25 页的数据前提“F continuous”、可测尺度和比较区间，第 26 页支持收敛可以任意慢的边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Haugh's notes, PDF pp. 20 and 22, are visually checked for the conditional excess, supremum error, endpoint, scale function, and equivalence with the MDA condition.
- The McNeil–Frey author draft, 19 June 1999, PDF and internal p. 7, equation (7) and footnote 4, supports the Balkema–de Haan (1974)/Pickands (1975) attribution and domain condition. The uniform example is independently derived from the conditional CDF.
- Embrechts' Ottawa 2008 slides directly support the continuous-parent version: p. 25 was reopened for the continuous-CDF premise, measurable scale and comparison interval; p. 26 supports the arbitrarily slow convergence caveat.
