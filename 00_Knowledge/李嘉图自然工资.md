# 李嘉图的自然工资是按社会习惯维持劳动者及其家庭延续所需的工资

**自然工资**是 Ricardo 用来描述劳动者及其家庭能够维持生活、使劳动力人口延续而不增不减的工资水平。它涉及食物和其他必需品，也包括社会习惯已经视为必要的生活条件。因此，自然工资不是固定的一笔钱，也不只是维持个体当下不挨饿的生理下限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo's **natural wage** supports workers and their families so that the labouring population can reproduce without increasing or decreasing. It covers food and other necessities, including conditions of life made necessary by social custom. It is neither a fixed money amount nor merely the physiological minimum for an individual's immediate survival.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个固定生活篮子理解它的计价：一名劳动者对应家庭每期需要 $b$ 单位粮食，另需 $m$ 单位工业品；两者价格为 $P_F,P_M$。在这份篮子暂时不变时，货币工资要求和工业品计价的要求分别是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed consumption basket makes the units clear. Suppose the household associated with a worker needs $b$ units of food and $m$ units of manufactured goods per period, priced at $P_F,P_M$. Holding that basket fixed gives the following money-wage requirement and its manufactured-goods equivalent:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
W_n=bP_F+mP_M,
\qquad
w_n=\frac{W_n}{P_M}=b\frac{P_F}{P_M}+m.
$$

例如 $b=2,m=1,P_M=1$，食品价格从 1 升至 1.5，维持同一篮子的工资从 3 升至 4。工人并未因此多得到食品或工业品；提高的是购买原篮子需要支付的价格。[[食品价格与工业工资]] 还提供另一种模型中的招工条件与计价区分，但其农业收入挂钩假设不等于这里的固定生活篮子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $b=2,m=1,P_M=1$, raising the food price from 1 to 1.5 raises the wage needed for the same basket from 3 to 4. The household receives no more food or manufactured goods; maintaining its basket costs more. [[食品价格与工业工资|Food prices and industrial wages]] discusses another model's recruitment and wage units, whose link to agricultural income differs from the fixed basket used here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“固定篮子”只是这次比较的假设。Ricardo 明确说，自然工资即使用实物衡量，也会因时代、国家与生活习惯而不同。把人口课堂里的水平生存工资线解释为某一阶段维持标准不变是可以的；把它解释为永远不变的自然常数则超出了原论述。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fixed basket is an assumption for this comparison. Ricardo explicitly states that the natural wage can differ across periods, countries and customs even in real terms. A horizontal subsistence-wage line can represent a maintained standard during one teaching exercise, but not an immutable natural constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际成交的**市场工资**还受劳动供需影响，可能高于或低于自然工资。两者之间怎样调整，需要另看 [[工资与人口调整]]；定义了自然工资，并没有证明每一天的工资都等于它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **market wage** actually paid also depends on labour demand and supply and may lie above or below the natural wage. Their adjustment is the separate mechanism in [[工资与人口调整|wages and population adjustment]]. Defining the natural wage does not establish that daily wages equal it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》V.1–3、5、16](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=6#5.1)：支持自然工资、生活资料价格、市场工资的区别及社会习惯的作用。V.16 明确否定自然工资的实物数量绝对固定。
- 双商品篮子是将该计价逻辑写清的教学构造，不是原书估计出的家庭预算；3→4 已按固定数量、变动食品价格回算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter V supports the definition, price dependence, market-wage distinction and role of custom. The two-good basket is a constructed accounting illustration, not an estimated historical household budget; the change from 3 to 4 has been recalculated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
