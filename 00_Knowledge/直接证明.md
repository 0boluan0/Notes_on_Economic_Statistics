# Direct proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*直接证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **direct proof** starts with what a claim gives us and explains why its conclusion must follow. For “if an integer is odd, then its square is odd,” we may use the integer's oddness as an assumption. We may not assume that its square is odd: that is the result we are trying to establish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**直接证明**从命题给出的条件出发，说明结论为什么必然跟随。例如要证明“若一个整数是奇数，则它的平方也是奇数”，可以把这个整数为奇数当作假设；不能把平方为奇数也当作假设，因为那正是待证结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] is an “if–then” statement, written $P\Rightarrow Q$. Here $P$ is the **antecedent**, or assumed condition, and $Q$ the **consequent**, or desired conclusion. The implication fails only if $P$ is true and $Q$ false. Thus a direct proof examines the situation in which $P$ holds and rules out that failure by deriving $Q$. Assuming $P$ for this purpose does not assert that $P$ always holds or that there exists an object satisfying it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]]是“如果……那么……”形式的陈述，记为 $P\Rightarrow Q$。$P$ 是**前件**，即假设条件；$Q$ 是**后件**，即希望得到的结论。蕴含只有在 $P$ 真而 $Q$ 假时失败，所以直接证明考察 $P$ 成立的情形，再推出 $Q$，排除这种失败。为了证明而假设 $P$，并不宣称 $P$ 总成立，也不宣称一定存在满足 $P$ 的对象。

> [!important] Start from the given condition · 从给定条件出发
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To prove $P\Rightarrow Q$ directly, assume $P$ and derive $Q$ using only that assumption, explicit definitions, and established results available in the context. Do not assume $Q$, or an equivalent form of $Q$, along the way.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要直接证明 $P\Rightarrow Q$，先假设 $P$，再只用该假设、明示定义和已经允许使用的结论推到 $Q$。不能在中途把 $Q$ 或与 $Q$ 等价的内容当作已知。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The warning concerns using the conclusion as an unproved premise. Once a statement has actually been established from the allowed assumptions, it may be used, even if it happens to be equivalent to the goal. What matters is the [[数学证明的有效推理|justification of the reasoning]], not the shape of a sentence alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里禁止的是把结论作为尚未证明的前提。若某个陈述确实已经从允许的假设推出，即使它恰与目标等价，也可以使用。关键是[[数学证明的有效推理|推理有没有依据]]，不是仅看一句话的外形。

## An arbitrary odd integer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*任意奇整数的平方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We prove: for every integer $n$, if $n$ is odd, then $n^2$ is odd. An integer is **even** if it has the form $2k$, and **odd** if it has the form $2k+1$, for an integer $k$. The set $\mathbb Z$ consists of all integers, including zero and negative integers.

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
证明目标是：对每个整数 $n$，若 $n$ 为奇数，则 $n^2$ 为奇数。整数若能写成 $2k$，就叫**偶数**；若能写成 $2k+1$，就叫**奇数**，这里 $k$ 为整数。$\mathbb Z$ 表示包括零和负整数在内的全部整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, take an arbitrary odd integer $n$. By definition, $n=2k+1$ for some $k\in\mathbb Z$. Then
$$
n^2=4k^2+4k+1=2(2k^2+2k)+1,
$$
so $n^2$ is twice an integer plus one and is therefore odd. The backbone is “arbitrary object satisfying the antecedent → unfold its definition → reach the definition of the consequent.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，取任意奇整数 $n$。由定义，存在 $k\in\mathbb Z$ 使 $n=2k+1$。于是
$$
n^2=4k^2+4k+1=2(2k^2+2k)+1,
$$
所以 $n^2$ 能写成 $2$ 乘整数再加 $1$，按定义也是奇数。这个证明的主干是“任意满足前件的对象 → 展开前件定义 → 到达后件定义”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here an [[全称证明与存在见证|arbitrary object satisfying the hypothesis]] means that no special property of $n$ beyond oddness is used. The integer $k$ may depend on $n$: for $n=3$ it is $1$, while for $n=-3$ it is $-2$. The proof does not require the same $k$ for all inputs. These numerical examples illustrate the definition; the symbolic argument establishes the universal claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里[[全称证明与存在见证|任意满足前提的对象]]意味着没有使用 $n$ 在奇数性质之外的特殊条件。整数 $k$ 可以依赖于 $n$：$n=3$ 时取 $k=1$，$n=-3$ 时取 $k=-2$；证明并不要求所有输入共用同一个 $k$。这些数值例子帮助理解定义，真正建立全称结论的是前面的符号论证。

## Turn scratchwork into a proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把探索整理成证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Working backward can suggest what to try. For example, to prove $-x^3+4x+1>0$ whenever $0\le x\le2$, notice the factorization

$$-x^3+4x+1=x(2-x)(2+x)+1.$$

The final proof now runs forward: the hypothesis gives $x\ge0$, $2-x\ge0$ and $2+x\ge0$. Their product is nonnegative; adding one makes it strictly positive. The endpoints $x=0$ and $x=2$ both give the value $1$, so neither is lost. This example from MIT shows how an observation becomes a proof once each sign is justified by the assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
倒着想可以帮助找到路线。例如，要在 $0\le x\le2$ 时证明 $-x^3+4x+1>0$，先观察分解

$$-x^3+4x+1=x(2-x)(2+x)+1.$$

正式证明就能正向展开：由题设得到 $x\ge0$、$2-x\ge0$、$2+x\ge0$；三者乘积非负，加一后严格为正。端点 $x=0$ 与 $x=2$ 都给出数值 $1$，因此没有遗漏。MIT 的这个例子说明：把每个符号判断都落实到假设，观察才成为证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful drafting habit is to name the domain and hypothesis, unpack a definition, and check that the last line really is the requested conclusion. These are obligations rather than a mandatory writing template. A proof can use words, inequalities, constructions or established theorems; its steps must all follow from what is already justified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
写草稿时，可以先列论域与假设，展开定义，再检查最后一句是否真是要求的结论。这些是证明义务，不是必须照搬的写作模板。证明可以使用文字、不等式、构造或已证定理；每一步都须由已获依据的内容推出。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Backward reasoning from the goal can be useful scratchwork, but a final proof must verify that each backward step is reversible or rewrite the argument as valid reasoning from premises to conclusion.
- A direct proof is not merely algebra. Its prose should identify the objects, quantifiers, definitions, and justification for each decisive transformation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 从目标倒推可以用于草稿探索，但正式证明必须确认每一步可逆，或改写成从前提向结论的有效推导。
- 直接证明不等于“只做代数”。文字必须说明对象、量词、定义和每个关键变形为何成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, $x=1$ implies $x^2=1$, but squaring is not reversible without additional conditions: $x=-1$ also satisfies $x^2=1$. Reaching a true statement after assuming a goal does not by itself prove the goal. Likewise, “take an arbitrary odd integer” covers all such integers, while “take $n=3$” covers only one; the difference is explained in [[全称命题与反例|universal claims and counterexamples]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，$x=1$ 能推出 $x^2=1$，但在没有额外条件时平方不可逆，因为 $x=-1$ 也满足 $x^2=1$。从假设目标出发算到一个真命题，不能单凭这一点证明目标。同样，“取任意奇整数”覆盖全部此类整数，“取 $n=3$”则只覆盖一个；[[全称命题与反例]]解释了这种区别。

> [!question]- Identify the assumed conclusion · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a direct proof of “if $n^2$ is even, then $n$ is even,” why may one not begin with $n=2k$? **Answer:** $n=2k$ is precisely the definition of the desired consequent, so beginning there assumes what must be proved. One may instead try the [[逆否证明|contrapositive]], proving that odd $n$ has odd square.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要直接证明“若 $n^2$ 偶，则 $n$ 偶”，为什么不能开头就写 $n=2k$？**答案：** $n=2k$ 正是后件“$n$ 偶”的定义，开头这样写等于预设待证结论。可以另选[[逆否证明]]，证明奇整数的平方仍为奇数。
