# Trigonometric substitution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三角代换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trigonometric substitution changes an algebraic variable into a sine, tangent or secant so that a square root becomes easier to handle. It is a use of [[换元积分|integration by substitution]]: replace both the expression in the integral **and its differential**, then integrate in the new variable. The reason it works is that the trigonometric identities can turn a sum or difference of squares into a single square.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三角代换把原来的代数变量写成正弦、正切或正割，让难处理的平方根变得简单。它是 [[换元积分]] 的一种用法：既要替换被积表达式，**也要替换微分**，然后对新变量积分。背后的依据是三角恒等式能把两个平方的和或差改写成一个平方。

$$1-\sin^2\theta=\cos^2\theta,\qquad
1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta,\qquad
\sec^2\theta-1=\tan^2\theta.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\theta$ is an angle measured in radians, $\tan\theta=\sin\theta/\cos\theta$, and $\sec\theta=1/\cos\theta$. The angle's allowed range matters: $\sqrt{z^2}=|z|$, so knowing the square alone does not determine the sign of the expression outside the root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\theta$ 是以弧度计量的角，$\tan\theta=\sin\theta/\cos\theta$，$\sec\theta=1/\cos\theta$。角度的取值范围很重要：$\sqrt{z^2}=|z|$，只知道平方，还不能直接确定去掉根号后的符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Watch the radical and the differential change together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同时看根式和微分怎样变化*

> [!example] A root that cancels · 根式刚好消去
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a>0$ and consider $\int dx/\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$ for $-a<x<a$. Choose $x=a\sin\theta$ with $-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2$. Then $\cos\theta>0$, so the radical is $a\cos\theta$ and $dx=a\cos\theta\,d\theta$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $a>0$，在 $-a<x<a$ 上计算 $\int dx/\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$。取 $x=a\sin\theta$，限制 $-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2$。此时 $\cos\theta>0$，根式是 $a\cos\theta$，同时 $dx=a\cos\theta\,d\theta$：
> $$\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}
> =\int\frac{a\cos\theta\,d\theta}{a\cos\theta}
> =\theta+C=\arcsin(x/a)+C.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[反三角函数求导|inverse sine]] returns the unique angle in that range whose sine is $x/a$. Choosing the range made both the cancellation and the return to $x$ unambiguous.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[反三角函数求导|反正弦]]返回指定范围内正弦值为 $x/a$ 的唯一角度。选定范围以后，约去因子和最后回到 $x$ 都有了明确依据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A root in the numerator leaves a trigonometric integral instead. For example, on $-3<x<3$, use $x=3\sin\theta$ with the same principal range:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根式在分子时，代换后会留下三角函数积分。例如，在 $-3<x<3$ 上，仍选上述主值范围，令 $x=3\sin\theta$：

$$\begin{aligned}
\int\sqrt{9-x^2}\,dx
&=9\int\cos^2\theta\,d\theta\\
&=\frac92\theta+\frac94\sin(2\theta)+C\\
&=\frac92\arcsin(x/3)+\frac x2\sqrt{9-x^2}+C.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The middle step uses the [[正弦余弦幂积分|power-reduction identity]] $\cos^2\theta=(1+\cos2\theta)/2$. To return to $x$, use $\sin\theta=x/3$, $\cos\theta=\sqrt{9-x^2}/3$, and $\sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta$. These signed identities work throughout the chosen range; a triangle drawn with positive side lengths alone is not enough to decide signs when $x<0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中间一步使用 [[正弦余弦幂积分|降幂恒等式]] $\cos^2\theta=(1+\cos2\theta)/2$。回代时用 $\sin\theta=x/3$、$\cos\theta=\sqrt{9-x^2}/3$ 和二倍角公式。带符号的恒等式适用于所选范围的全部角度；若 $x<0$，只画一个边长为正的三角形，并不足以判断各函数的符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose the range with the substitution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*代换和角度范围一起选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a>0$, the following choices make the radical nonnegative explicitly. Endpoints in the table describe the radical's domain; if the original integrand has that radical in a denominator, any zero of the denominator must still be excluded.

| Radical and real branch | Substitution and angle range | Radical and differential |
|---|---|---|
| $\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$, $-a\le x\le a$ | $x=a\sin\theta$, $-\pi/2\le\theta\le\pi/2$ | $a\cos\theta$; $dx=a\cos\theta\,d\theta$ |
| $\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$, all real $x$ | $x=a\tan\theta$, $-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2$ | $a\sec\theta$; $dx=a\sec^2\theta\,d\theta$ |
| $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$, $x\ge a$ | $x=a\sec\theta$, $0\le\theta<\pi/2$ | $a\tan\theta$; $dx=a\sec\theta\tan\theta\,d\theta$ |
| $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$, $x\le-a$ | $x=-a\sec\theta$, $0\le\theta<\pi/2$ | $a\tan\theta$; $dx=-a\sec\theta\tan\theta\,d\theta$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

表中均要求 $a>0$。差平方根选正弦，和平方根选正切；$\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$ 的正、负两支分别用 $x=a\sec\theta$ 和 $x=-a\sec\theta$。正弦主值范围保证余弦非负，正切主值范围保证正割为正；后两行用第一象限的角，保证正切非负。负支的微分额外带负号，不能漏掉。表中的端点只说明根式本身有定义；如果根式位于原被积函数的分母，分母为零的位置仍要排除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A shifted quadratic may first need completing the square. For instance, $x^2+2x+5=(x+1)^2+4$ suggests $x+1=2\tan\theta$, not $x=2\tan\theta$. For a definite integral, convert both bounds using the same branch and retain their resulting order. On the negative secant branch, increasing $x$ decreases $\theta$; the negative differential and the transformed bounds already account for that reversal. If an endpoint is singular, apply the substitution to finite truncated integrals and then take the [[反常积分|improper limit]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

带平移的二次式可能要先配方。例如 $x^2+2x+5=(x+1)^2+4$，提示使用 $x+1=2\tan\theta$，而不是直接令 $x=2\tan\theta$。定积分的两个界必须用同一分支转换，并保留转换后的次序。在负正割分支上，$x$ 增大时 $\theta$ 反而减小；负微分和转换后的上下限已经反映这个方向。端点若为奇点，应先对截断后的有限积分换元，再取 [[反常积分]] 的极限。

> [!warning] Check whether a simpler substitution already works · 先检查简单换元
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\int x\sqrt{x^2-9}\,dx$, the factor $x\,dx$ already matches the derivative of the expression inside the root. Setting $u=x^2-9$ gives $(x^2-9)^{3/2}/3+C$ on each real branch with $|x|>3$. A trigonometric substitution is possible, but does not simplify this calculation further.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $\int x\sqrt{x^2-9}\,dx$，$x\,dx$ 已经配合根号内部的导数。令 $u=x^2-9$，在 $|x|>3$ 的各实分支上直接得到 $(x^2-9)^{3/2}/3+C$。三角代换虽然可行，却没有让这个计算更简单。
