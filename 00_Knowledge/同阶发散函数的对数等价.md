# 最终为正的同阶函数若趋于正无穷则其对数渐近等价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Eventually positive functions of the same order have asymptotically equivalent logarithms when they tend to positive infinity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f,g$ 定义在同一个趋向无穷的输入域上，最终严格为正，满足 $f=\Theta(g)$ 且 $g\to+\infty$。则 $f$ 也趋于正无穷，并且 $\ln f\sim\ln g$。这里的[[大Θ记号|同阶]]不要求 $f/g$ 收敛；取对数后，固定倍数范围变成有界加性误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f,g$ share an input domain tending to infinity, be eventually positive, and satisfy $f=\Theta(g)$ with $g\to+\infty$. Then $f\to+\infty$ too, and $\ln f\sim\ln g$. Having the [[大Θ记号|same order]] does not require $f/g$ to converge: logarithms turn a bounded range of positive multiplicative factors into bounded additive error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由同阶存在固定 $0<c\le C$，使最终 $c\le f/g\le C$。因此 $\ln(f/g)$ 的绝对值被固定常数控制，而 $\ln g\to+\infty$，所以

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal order supplies fixed constants $0<c\le C$ with $c\le f/g\le C$ eventually. Thus $\ln(f/g)$ is bounded in magnitude, while $\ln g\to+\infty$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\ln f}{\ln g}
=1+\frac{\ln(f/g)}{\ln g}
\longrightarrow1.
$$

这正是[[渐近等价]]。例如 $f(n)=n(2+\sin n)$ 与 $g(n)=n$ 满足 $1\le f/g\le3$，故其对数等价，无需先求 $f/g$ 的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is exactly [[渐近等价|asymptotic equivalence]]. For example, $f(n)=n(2+\sin n)$ and $g(n)=n$ satisfy $1\le f/g\le3$, so their logarithms are equivalent without first finding a limit of $f/g$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能直接删掉趋于正无穷的条件：常数函数 $f(n)=4$ 与 $g(n)=2$ 同阶且为正，但 $\ln f/\ln g=2$，并不趋于 $1$。本命题给出一个充分条件，不声称只有发散函数的对数才可能等价。反方向的指数运算也有独立边界，见[[渐近等价不保留指数运算]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The divergence condition cannot simply be dropped: the positive constant functions $f(n)=4$ and $g(n)=2$ have the same order, but $\ln f/\ln g=2$, not a limit of one. The theorem gives a sufficient condition; it does not claim logarithmic equivalence is possible only for diverging functions. Exponentiation has a separate boundary; see [[渐近等价不保留指数运算|asymptotic equivalence under exponentiation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
