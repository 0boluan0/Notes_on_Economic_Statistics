# Uniqueness of sequence limits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*序列极限的唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A convergent sequence in a metric space has one possible destination. It cannot eventually stay within $1/3$ of $0$ and also within $1/3$ of $1$ on the usual number line: those two intervals do not meet. The uniqueness theorem generalizes this separation argument to any distance satisfying the metric axioms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在度量空间中，一个收敛序列只可能有一个极限。在通常数轴上，它不可能最终一直距 $0$ 不到 $1/3$，同时又最终一直距 $1$ 不到 $1/3$，因为两个区间不相交。极限唯一性定理把这一分离论证推广到满足度量公理的任意距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(X,d)$ be a [[度量空间|metric space]], with $x_n\in X$ for integers $n\ge1$. Recall that [[序列收敛|convergence to $x$]] means that every ball about $x$ contains all sufficiently late terms. The candidate points $x$ and $y$ below both belong to $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $(X,d)$ 是[[度量空间]]，$x_n\in X$，索引为整数 $n\ge1$。[[序列收敛|收敛到 $x$]]指以 $x$ 为中心的每个球都包含足够靠后的全部项。下面的候选点 $x,y$ 都属于 $X$。

> [!important] At most one limit · 至多一个极限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $x_n\to x$ and $x_n\to y$ in the same metric space, then $x=y$. This is a uniqueness statement conditional on convergence; it does not assert that every sequence has a limit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若同一度量空间中 $x_n\to x$ 且 $x_n\to y$，则 $x=y$。这是关于收敛后的唯一性陈述，不保证每个序列都有极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use a cutoff that works for both candidates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*选一个对两个候选都有效的门槛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose instead that $x\ne y$. Their distance $D=d(x,y)$ is positive. Choose the tolerance $\varepsilon=D/3$. Convergence to $x$ supplies a cutoff $N_x$ after which $d(x_n,x)<D/3$; convergence to $y$ supplies a possibly different cutoff $N_y$. For any $n\ge\max\{N_x,N_y\}$, both bounds hold. The triangle inequality, together with symmetry of distance, gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反设 $x\ne y$，则两点距离 $D=d(x,y)>0$。取容许误差 $\varepsilon=D/3$。收敛到 $x$ 给出门槛 $N_x$，之后 $d(x_n,x)<D/3$；收敛到 $y$ 给出可能不同的门槛 $N_y$。当 $n\ge\max\{N_x,N_y\}$ 时，两条界同时成立。由三角不等式和距离的对称性，

$$D=d(x,y)\le d(x,x_n)+d(x_n,y)<\frac D3+\frac D3=\frac{2D}{3}<D.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive number cannot be strictly smaller than itself. The assumption $x\ne y$ was therefore impossible. The same calculation says that the two radius-$D/3$ balls are disjoint: a point in both would already contradict the triangle inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个正数不可能严格小于自身，因此 $x\ne y$ 不成立。同一个计算也说明两个半径 $D/3$ 的球不相交：若有一点同时属于两个球，就已经违反三角不等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The choice $D/3$ is convenient, not mandatory. Radius $D/2$ also works because membership in an open ball gives a **strict** inequality: the sum of two distances each smaller than $D/2$ is smaller than $D$. One must keep track of these strict inequalities rather than treating the radius choice itself as a necessary condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $D/3$ 方便，但不是必须。半径 $D/2$ 同样可用，因为属于开球给出的是**严格**不等式：两个各自小于 $D/2$ 的距离之和仍小于 $D$。关键是保留严格不等号，而不是把某个半径选择当成必要条件。

## What uniqueness does not promise
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*唯一性没有保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The real sequence $(-1)^n$ has no limit: arbitrarily late terms remain at both $-1$ and $1$. Its two constant subsequences have different limits, as explained in [[聚点不等于收敛|cluster points versus full convergence]]. There is no contradiction with the theorem, which says “at most one”, allowing no limit at all. The proof uses only the metric axioms and the two convergence assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实序列 $(-1)^n$ 没有极限，因为任意晚的时候仍会出现 $-1$ 和 $1$。它的两个常值子列有不同极限，见[[聚点不等于收敛]]。这不违反定理：“至多一个”允许根本没有极限。证明只使用度量公理和两个收敛假设。

> [!warning] General topologies need a separation argument · 一般拓扑需要分离依据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a general [[拓扑|topological space]], convergence uses open neighbourhoods. If any two distinct points have disjoint open neighbourhoods (the Hausdorff separation condition), eventual membership in both proves uniqueness in the same way. This is a sufficient condition. Uniqueness cannot simply be assumed for every topology: on $X=\{a,b\}$ with open sets only $\varnothing$ and $X$, the constant sequence $x_n=a$ converges to both $a$ and $b$. The only open neighbourhood of either point is $X$, which contains every term.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一般[[拓扑|拓扑空间]]用开邻域定义收敛。若任意两个不同点都有不相交的开邻域，即满足 Hausdorff 分离条件，那么“最终同时属于两个邻域”仍能证明唯一性。这是一个充分条件。不能直接假设每种拓扑都有唯一极限：在 $X=\{a,b\}$ 上，若开集只有 $\varnothing$ 和 $X$，常值序列 $x_n=a$ 就同时收敛到 $a$ 与 $b$。两点各自唯一的开邻域都是 $X$，其中包含序列全部项。
