# A uniform lower bound for a positive-definite form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正定二次型的统一下界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[正定矩阵|Positive definiteness]] says that a quadratic value is positive in every nonzero direction. In a fixed finite-dimensional matrix, it says something stronger too: after accounting for the input's length, the value stays at least a fixed positive distance above zero. The same constant must work for **all** input vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正定矩阵|正定性]]表示每个非零方向上的二次型值都正。对于一个固定的有限维矩阵，它还保证更强的结论：把输入长度考虑进去以后，二次型值与零之间有一个统一的正下界。**所有**输入向量要共用同一个常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The scale is squared [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]], $\|x\|_2^2=x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2$ for real coordinates. This is the appropriate scale because replacing $x$ by $tx$ multiplies both the [[二次型|quadratic form]] and squared length by $t^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里按[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]的平方比较：实坐标下 $\|x\|_2^2=x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2$。这个尺度恰当，因为把 $x$ 换成 $tx$，[[二次型]]和长度平方都会乘以 $t^2$。

> [!example] The weakest direction determines the bound · 最弱的方向决定下界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$, the listed numbers are on the diagonal and other entries are zero. With $T$ denoting [[矩阵转置|transpose]],
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对[[对角矩阵]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$，所列数位于对角线，其余为零；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，则
>
> $$x^TAx=2x_1^2+5x_2^2\ge2(x_1^2+x_2^2)=2\|x\|_2^2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equality holds at $x=(1,0)^T$. Therefore $c=2$ works, and no larger constant works for every vector. The answer is not $5$ or the average of $2$ and $5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=(1,0)^T$ 上取等号。因此 $c=2$ 可用，任何更大的常数都不能适用于全部向量；答案不是 $5$，也不是 $2$ 与 $5$ 的平均数。

## The statement and its quantifiers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定理与量词*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an $n\times n$ [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric]] matrix $A$, with finite integer $n\ge1$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个 $n\times n$ 的[[实对称矩阵]] $A$，有限整数 $n\ge1$。则

$$A\succ0\iff
\exists c>0\ \text{such that}\ 
x^TAx\ge c\|x\|_2^2\quad\text{for every }x\in\mathbb R^n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The constant $c$ may depend on $A$, but not on $x$. The symbol $A\succ0$ means positive definite. For a [[Hermitian 矩阵|complex Hermitian]] matrix, replace $x^T$ by the conjugate transpose $x^*$ and use $\|x\|_2^2=\sum_i|x_i|^2$. For $z=a+bi$, $|z|^2=a^2+b^2$, with $a,b$ real and $i^2=-1$. The same equivalence holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常数 $c$ 可以依赖 $A$，却不能随 $x$ 改变；$A\succ0$ 表示正定。若 $A$ 为复 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，把 $x^T$ 换成共轭转置 $x^*$，并使用 $\|x\|_2^2=\sum_i|x_i|^2$。对 $z=a+bi$，有 $|z|^2=a^2+b^2$，其中 $a,b$ 实且 $i^2=-1$；相同等价关系仍成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Among the admissible constants, the largest is $\lambda_{\min}(A)$, the smallest [[特征对|eigenvalue]] of $A$. Thus every $0<c\le\lambda_{\min}(A)$ works, and every larger $c$ fails. The finite-dimensional hypothesis matters: a finite list of positive eigenvalues has a positive minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可用常数中最大的是 $A$ 的最小[[特征对|特征值]] $\lambda_{\min}(A)$。所以每个 $0<c\le\lambda_{\min}(A)$ 都可用，任何更大的 $c$ 都失败。有限维前提在这里起作用：有限多个正特征值一定有正的最小值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Spectral coordinates reveal the minimum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*谱坐标揭示最小值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the [[实对称矩阵谱定理|real spectral theorem]], write $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$, where the columns of the [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrix]] $Q$ are perpendicular unit eigenvectors and $\Lambda$ is diagonal with entries $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$. Set $y=Q^Tx$. Orthogonality preserves length, so $\sum_i y_i^2=\|x\|_2^2$. The [[正定与半正定的谱判据|sign criterion]] makes every $\lambda_i>0$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]，写成 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$，其中[[正交矩阵]] $Q$ 的列为互相垂直的单位特征向量，$\Lambda$ 是对角元为 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ 的对角矩阵。令 $y=Q^Tx$。正交变换保持长度，故 $\sum_i y_i^2=\|x\|_2^2$；[[正定与半正定的谱判据]]保证每个 $\lambda_i>0$。因此

$$x^TAx=\sum_i\lambda_i y_i^2
\ge\lambda_{\min}(A)\sum_i y_i^2
=\lambda_{\min}(A)\|x\|_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a unit eigenvector $q$ of the smallest eigenvalue, $Aq=\lambda_{\min}q$ and $\|q\|_2=1$, so $q^TAq=\lambda_{\min}$. This gives equality and proves that the constant cannot be improved. In the complex case, the [[Hermitian 谱定理|Hermitian spectral theorem]] gives $A=U\Lambda U^*$ with [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary]] $U$; the same calculation uses $y=U^*x$ and $\sum_i\lambda_i|y_i|^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对最小特征值对应的单位特征向量 $q$，有 $Aq=\lambda_{\min}q$ 且 $\|q\|_2=1$，于是 $q^TAq=\lambda_{\min}$。这给出了等号，证明常数不能再提高。复数情形由[[Hermitian 谱定理]]得到 $A=U\Lambda U^*$，$U$ 为 [[Unitary 矩阵]]；同一计算改用 $y=U^*x$ 和 $\sum_i\lambda_i|y_i|^2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, suppose the uniform bound holds for some $c>0$. If $x\ne0$, its squared norm is positive, so $x^*Ax\ge c\|x\|_2^2>0$, exactly the positive-definite condition. Equivalently, applying the bound to each unit eigenvector gives $\lambda_i\ge c>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，假设某个 $c>0$ 给出统一下界。只要 $x\ne0$，长度平方就为正，因此 $x^*Ax\ge c\|x\|_2^2>0$，恰好满足正定定义。等价地，把每个单位特征向量代入，也能得到 $\lambda_i\ge c>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A bound for one matrix need not cover a family
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个矩阵的下界不一定适用于整族矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A_\delta=\operatorname{diag}(\delta,1)$ with $0<\delta<1$, each matrix is positive definite and its best constant is $\delta$. As $\delta$ approaches zero there is no single positive constant valid for every matrix in this family. At $\delta=0$, the first coordinate is a nonzero zero-value direction, which rules out every positive lower bound. More generally, a singular positive-semidefinite matrix cannot have such a bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $A_\delta=\operatorname{diag}(\delta,1)$，$0<\delta<1$。每个矩阵都正定，各自的最优常数为 $\delta$；但 $\delta$ 趋零时，没有一个正的常数能对整族矩阵共同成立。$\delta=0$ 时，第一坐标给出非零零值方向，排除任何正下界。一般而言，奇异半正定矩阵都不可能有这样的正下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\lambda_{\min}(A)=0.01$, choosing $c=0.02$ fails at a unit eigenvector for $0.01$; choosing $c=0.005$ succeeds. In dimension one, $A=[a]$ has optimal constant $a$ when $a>0$. The lower bound describes the quadratic form of this fixed matrix; applying it to a Hessian in a [[Hessian 局部极小判据|local-minimum test]] still requires that theorem's differentiability and stationarity assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\lambda_{\min}(A)=0.01$，则 $c=0.02$ 会在相应单位特征向量上失败，$c=0.005$ 则可用。一维 $A=[a]$ 且 $a>0$ 时，最优常数就是 $a$。这里讨论固定矩阵的二次型；把结论用于 Hessian 的[[Hessian 局部极小判据|局部极小检验]]，仍需保留该定理的可微性和驻点前提。
