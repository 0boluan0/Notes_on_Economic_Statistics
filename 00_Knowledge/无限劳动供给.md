# 无限劳动供给是一定阶段内可按近乎不变的工资获得额外劳动的条件

[[Lewis 二元经济模型]]中的无限劳动供给，指在有关阶段与工资附近，资本主义部门能够继续招到所需的额外非熟练劳动，劳动短缺尚未迫使工资随雇佣量上升。它是近水平劳动供给的条件，不是人口在数学上无穷，也不是任意技能和地点都不缺人。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Lewis 二元经济模型|Lewis model]], unlimited labour supply means that, during the relevant phase and around the prevailing wage, the capitalist sector can recruit the additional unskilled labour it needs without labour scarcity forcing wages upward. It describes approximately horizontal supply, not an infinite population or an absence of shortages at every skill level and location.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定消费篮子和相关相对价格的简化版本中，设生计部门可得收入为 $s$，工资溢价为 $\mu\ge0$，在有足够可招募劳动的一段就业范围内，可以写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified version with a fixed consumption basket and relevant relative prices, let $s$ be earnings available in the subsistence sector and $\mu\ge0$ a wage premium. Over an employment range with sufficient recruitable labour, one may write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
w_M(L_M)=\bar w=(1+\mu)s.
$$

这里 $s$ 与 $\mu$ 在所讨论阶段保持稳定，是额外条件而非由[[剩余劳动力|零边际产出]]自动推出；溢价可反映生活成本、迁移与工作安排等差别，本式不估计其大小。若按家庭平均收入重新分配，[[边际产出与退出收入|留下的人变少]]就可能使 $s$ 上升，即使农业总产出没有下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stability of $s$ and $\mu$ over this phase is an additional condition, not an automatic implication of [[剩余劳动力|zero marginal product]]. A premium may reflect living costs, relocation or work arrangements; this expression does not estimate its size. With equal sharing of household output, [[边际产出与退出收入|fewer remaining recipients]] may raise $s$ even if agricultural output is unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要核对工资单位。以消费篮子衡量的实际工资不变，不保证以资本主义产品衡量的工资成本不变：设货币工资为 $W$，消费篮子价格为 $P_C>0$，资本主义产品价格为 $P_M>0$，则 $W/P_M=(W/P_C)(P_C/P_M)$。两类产品相对价格变化时，企业面对的工资成本可以变；农业版本的反馈见[[食品价格与工业工资]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the wage unit as well. A constant consumption real wage need not mean constant labour cost in units of capitalist output. For money wage $W$, consumption-basket price $P_C>0$ and capitalist-output price $P_M>0$, $W/P_M=(W/P_C)(P_C/P_M)$. Changing relative prices can alter the wage cost faced by firms; see [[食品价格与工业工资|food prices and industrial wages]] for the agricultural mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当可招募劳动不足、原部门收入或工资标准上升时，水平段可能结束。Lewis 本文明确允许工资在剩余劳动尚未完全消失前上升，因此不能把“水平工资结束”“农业边际产出刚转正”和[[农业短缺点与商业化点|商业化点]]不加条件地认作同一个门槛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The horizontal phase can end when recruitable labour becomes scarce or outside earnings or wage norms rise. Lewis explicitly allows wages to rise before surplus labour has entirely disappeared. The end of flat wages, the first positive agricultural marginal product and the [[农业短缺点与商业化点|commercialization point]] must therefore not be treated as one threshold without additional assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lewis supplies the supply condition, skill scope, outside-income benchmark and reasons wages may rise before labour surplus disappears. The proportional-premium notation and price identity are teaching expressions, not estimated equations from the paper.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
