# Outer-product expansion of a matrix product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵乘积的外积展开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix product can be assembled by adding several whole matrices of the same size. Each contribution pairs one column of the left matrix with the corresponding row of the right matrix. This **outer-product expansion** shows how each intermediate coordinate contributes to every output entry, rather than computing the entries one at a time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵乘积可以通过把几张同样大小的完整矩阵相加来组装。每个贡献项把左矩阵的一列与右矩阵对应的一行配对。这种**外积展开**说明每个中间坐标怎样参与所有结果元素，而不只是逐个计算元素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build two complete contributions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先构造两张完整的贡献矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the rectangular example from the [[矩阵乘积逐列作用|columnwise reading]]. The matrix $A$ has three rows and two columns; $B$ has two rows and three columns:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用[[矩阵乘积逐列作用|逐列读法]]中的矩形例子。$A$ 有三行两列，$B$ 有两行三列：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\3&1&4\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first column of $A$ is $a_1=(2,1,0)^T$; the first row of $B$ is $r_1=(1,2,0)$. Here $T$ means ordinary [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Their [[秩一矩阵外积刻画|outer product]] $a_1r_1$ is a column times a row: entry $(i,j)$ multiplies entry $i$ of the column by entry $j$ of the row. Thus it is a $3\times3$ matrix, not a scalar. Form the corresponding second contribution and add:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A$ 的第一列为 $a_1=(2,1,0)^T$，$B$ 的第一行为 $r_1=(1,2,0)$；$T$ 表示普通[[矩阵转置|转置]]。它们的[[秩一矩阵外积刻画|外积]] $a_1r_1$ 是列乘行，第 $(i,j)$ 个元素等于列的第 $i$ 项乘行的第 $j$ 项，所以结果是 $3\times3$ 矩阵，不是标量。再构造第二个对应项并相加：

$$\begin{aligned}
a_1r_1&=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}(1\ \ 2\ \ 0)
=\begin{pmatrix}2&4&0\\1&2&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\\
a_2r_2&=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}(3\ \ 1\ \ 4)
=\begin{pmatrix}-3&-1&-4\\0&0&0\\3&1&4\end{pmatrix},\\
AB&=a_1r_1+a_2r_2
=\begin{pmatrix}-1&3&-4\\1&2&0\\3&1&4\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two contributions because the shared inner dimension is two. Every contribution already has the full output size. For instance, entry $(1,2)$ receives $2\cdot2=4$ from the first pair and $(-1)\cdot1=-1$ from the second, giving $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同的内维数为二，所以有两个贡献项；每一项已经具有完整的结果尺寸。例如位置 $(1,2)$ 从第一对得到 $2\cdot2=4$，从第二对得到 $(-1)\cdot1=-1$，相加为 $3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Pair the same intermediate index
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*沿同一个中间指标配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ and $B\in\mathbb F^{n\times p}$, where $m,n,p$ are positive integers and $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Write $a_k$ for column $k$ of $A$, and $r_k$ for row $k$ of $B$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$、$B\in\mathbb F^{n\times p}$，其中 $m,n,p$ 为正整数，$\mathbb F$ 为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。用 $a_k$ 表示 $A$ 的第 $k$ 列，$r_k$ 表示 $B$ 的第 $k$ 行，则

> [!important] Add column–row products · 把列乘行的结果相加
> $$\boxed{AB=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_kr_k.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each term has size $(m\times1)(1\times p)=m\times p$. If $r_k$ is written as $b_k^T$ for a column vector $b_k$, this is the same formula $AB=\sum_{k=1}^n a_kb_k^T$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每项尺寸为 $(m\times1)(1\times p)=m\times p$。若把行 $r_k$ 写成某列向量 $b_k$ 的转置 $b_k^T$，就得到同一公式 $AB=\sum_{k=1}^n a_kb_k^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove it, let $A_{ik}$ and $B_{kj}$ denote the indicated entries. At each output position $(i,j)$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明时，用 $A_{ik}$、$B_{kj}$ 表示相应位置的元素。对每个结果位置 $(i,j)$，有

$$\left(\sum_{k=1}^n a_kr_k\right)_{ij}
=\sum_{k=1}^n A_{ik}B_{kj}=(AB)_{ij}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last equality is the definition of [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]]. Since every entry agrees, the matrices agree. This explains the matching index: column $k$ must meet row $k$ to produce $A_{ik}B_{kj}$. Arbitrarily crossing the pairs changes those summands and generally changes the product. The [[矩阵乘积逐行作用|rowwise]], columnwise and outer-product readings regroup one calculation, rather than define different operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一步就是[[矩阵乘法]]的定义。每个元素都相等，所以矩阵相等。这也解释了为什么指标必须匹配：第 $k$ 列要与第 $k$ 行配对，才能产生 $A_{ik}B_{kj}$；任意交叉配对会改变求和项，一般也会改变乘积。[[矩阵乘积逐行作用|逐行]]、逐列与外积和读法，是同一计算的不同组织方式，不是不同运算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Nonzero terms need not contribute independent directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*非零项不一定贡献独立方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both $a_k$ and $r_k$ are nonzero, every column of $a_kr_k$ is a scalar multiple of $a_k$, and at least one is nonzero. Its [[矩阵秩|rank]], the dimension of its [[列空间|column space]], is therefore one. If either factor is zero, the term has rank zero. This is the [[秩一矩阵外积刻画|rank-one outer-product characterization]]. It does not say that ranks add when matrices are added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a_k$ 与 $r_k$ 均非零，$a_kr_k$ 的各列都是 $a_k$ 的倍数，而且至少一列非零。因此其[[矩阵秩|秩]]，即[[列空间]]的维数，为一；任一因子为零时，该项的秩为零。这就是[[秩一矩阵外积刻画]]，并不表示矩阵相加时秩也相加。

> [!warning] Count directions, not written terms · 数方向，不是数写出的项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Put $u=(1,0)^T$ and $r=(1,0)$, so $E=ur=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. Two nonzero outer products can repeat a direction: $E+E=2E$ still has rank one. They can also cancel: $E+(-E)=0$ has rank zero. These arise from $A=[u\ u]$ and $B$ with rows $r,r$ or $r,-r$, respectively. Thus two written nonzero contributions do not imply rank two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $u=(1,0)^T$、$r=(1,0)$，则 $E=ur=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$。两个非零外积可以重复同一方向：$E+E=2E$ 仍为秩一；也可以抵消：$E+(-E)=0$ 的秩为零。分别取 $A=[u\ u]$，并让 $B$ 的两行为 $r,r$ 或 $r,-r$，就得到这两个例子。因此写出两个非零贡献项，并不意味着秩为二。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sharper bound in terms of the two factors is the [[矩阵乘积秩上界|product-rank bound]]. No square shape, independence or invertibility is required for the expansion itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按两个因子本身给出的更精确限制见[[矩阵乘积秩上界]]。外积展开本身不要求方阵、无关性或可逆性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over complex numbers, the row used here is exactly the row already present in $B$. The symbol $T$ does not conjugate it. For example, $u=(1,i)^T$ and $r=(1,i)$ give $ur=\begin{pmatrix}1&i\\i&-1\end{pmatrix}$, where $i^2=-1$. Replacing $r$ by $(1,-i)$ gives a different matrix. For any matrix $U$ with columns $u_k$, the right factor in $UU^*$ is itself a conjugate transpose. Its rows already contain conjugated entries, and the same expansion gives $\sum_k u_ku_k^*$. The star denotes [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]; it comes from the specified factor, not an extra rule in multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数情形中，这里使用的行就是 $B$ 原本的行，符号 $T$ 不取共轭。例如 $u=(1,i)^T$、$r=(1,i)$ 给出 $ur=\begin{pmatrix}1&i\\i&-1\end{pmatrix}$，其中 $i^2=-1$；把 $r$ 换成 $(1,-i)$ 会得到不同矩阵。对任意列为 $u_k$ 的矩阵 $U$，乘积 $UU^*$ 的右因子本来就是共轭转置，其各行已经含有共轭元素，同一展开便给出 $\sum_k u_ku_k^*$。星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]，它来自指定的因子，不是乘法额外添加的规则。
