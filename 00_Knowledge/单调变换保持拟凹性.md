# 拟凹函数经过非减的实值变换后仍然拟凹

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A nondecreasing real-valued transformation preserves quasiconcavity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在凸域拟凹，$g$ 在其值域所处区间上非减，则 $g\circ f$ 拟凹。由[[拟凹性线段判据]]，
$$
g(f(tx+(1-t)y))\ge g(\min\{f(x),f(y)\})
=\min\{g(f(x)),g(f(y))\}.
$$
无需 $g$ 可导。若 $g$ 严格递增，则完整排序也保留；仅非减可能把不同值压为相等。集合等式 $\{f\ge k\}=\{g(f)\ge g(k)\}$ 需要严格递增，不能机械用于有平台的变换。

正象限上的 $\log x+\log y$ 凹，经指数变换成为 $xy$，仍拟凹却不凹。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the nondecreasing transformation to the [[拟凹性线段判据|segment inequality]]. It commutes with taking the smaller of two values, proving preservation without differentiability. Strict increase also preserves full ordering and supports the matching-threshold set equality; weak increase may collapse distinct values. Exponentiating a sum of logarithms shows that concavity itself need not survive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
