# Monotone transformations of quasiconcave functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凹函数的单调变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a function assigns a score to each input. A **nondecreasing transformation** rescales those scores without reversing their order: if $u\le v$, then $g(u)\le g(v)$. It may make different scores equal. If the original function is [[拟凹函数|quasiconcave]], a mixture of two inputs never scores below the worse endpoint. Applying the same nondecreasing rescaling to all three scores preserves that comparison, so the transformed function is also quasiconcave.

Write the new function as $h(x)=g(f(x))$, or $h=g\circ f$: first compute the original score $f(x)$, then apply $g$. The inputs and their feasible domain stay the same. This result concerns the shape of the sets of inputs passing a threshold; it does not say that all curvature properties survive the rescaling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一个函数给每个输入打分。**非减变换**只重标这些分数而不颠倒顺序：若 $u\le v$，则 $g(u)\le g(v)$；它可以把不同分数变成同一个分数。原函数若[[拟凹函数|拟凹]]，混合输入的得分就不会低于较差的端点；把这三个分数都送入同一个非减变换，这项比较仍成立，所以变换后的函数仍拟凹。

新函数写成 $h(x)=g(f(x))$，也记 $h=g\circ f$：先算原分数 $f(x)$，再算 $g$。输入及可行定义域保持不变。这个结论保护的是“达到门槛的输入集合”的形状，并不保证所有曲率性质都保留。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the minimum-endpoint comparison survives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么较小端点的比较会保留

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A nondecreasing real-valued transformation preserves quasiconcavity*

Assume $f:U\to\mathbb R$ is quasiconcave on a [[凸集|convex domain]] $U$, and $g:I\to\mathbb R$ is nondecreasing on an interval $I$ containing every value of $f$. Requiring real values ensures that the new score is defined and finite for every allowed input. Take any $x,y\in U$ and any $t\in[0,1]$.

The [[拟凹性线段判据|segment criterion]] gives $f(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{f(x),f(y)\}$. Applying a nondecreasing $g$ keeps this inequality pointing the same way. Also, $g(\min\{u,v\})=\min\{g(u),g(v)\}$: if $u\le v$, both sides equal $g(u)$; if $v\le u$, both equal $g(v)$. These two facts give the displayed proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在凸域拟凹，$g$ 在其值域所处区间上非减，则 $g\circ f$ 拟凹。由[[拟凹性线段判据]]，
$$
g(f(tx+(1-t)y))\ge g(\min\{f(x),f(y)\})
=\min\{g(f(x)),g(f(y))\}.
$$
无需 $g$ 可导。若 $g$ 严格递增，则完整排序也保留；仅非减可能把不同值压为相等。集合等式 $\{f\ge k\}=\{g(f)\ge g(k)\}$ 需要严格递增，不能机械用于有平台的变换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The resulting lower bound is exactly $h(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{h(x),h(y)\}$ for every pair and every weight, proving quasiconcavity of $h$. Neither continuity nor differentiability of $g$ is needed. Strict increase gives more: $u<v$ if and only if $g(u)<g(v)$, so it preserves the full ranking and allows the quasiconcavity inequality to be read in both directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $U$ 是[[凸集|凸定义域]]，$I$ 是包含 $f$ 全部取值的区间，$g:I\to\mathbb R$ 在这些值上都有有限实数输出。取较小值与应用 $g$ 可以交换，是因为当 $u\le v$ 时，两边都等于 $g(u)$；另一个次序同理。推得的下界正是 $h=g\circ f$ 的拟凹性线段判据，覆盖任意两点与任意权重。$g$ 不必连续，也不必可导。严格递增还使大小关系可以反向推出，因此在严格递增变换下，$f$ 拟凹当且仅当 $g\circ f$ 拟凹。

## Plateaus and jumps
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平台和跳跃为何仍被允许

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!example] A jump can preserve quasiconcavity
> Let $f(x)=x$ on $\mathbb R$ and let $g(s)=0$ for $s<0$, while $g(s)=1$ for $s\ge0$. The transformed score $h$ jumps at zero. Its upper contour set is the whole line when the threshold is at most $0$, the ray $[0,\infty)$ when the threshold is above $0$ and at most $1$, and the empty set above $1$. Each is convex, so $h$ is quasiconcave despite its jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 跳跃不会自动破坏拟凹性
> 在 $\mathbb R$ 上取 $f(x)=x$；令 $g(s)$ 在 $s<0$ 时为 $0$，在 $s\ge0$ 时为 $1$。$h$ 在零点跳跃，但门槛不高于 $0$ 时上轮廓集是整条实线，门槛位于 $(0,1]$ 时是 $[0,\infty)$，门槛超过 $1$ 时为空集。它们都是凸集，所以 $h$ 仍拟凹。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!warning] Equal transformed scores do not imply equal original scores
> With $f(x)=x$ on $[-1,1]$ and $g(s)=\max\{s,0\}$, take $k=-1/2$. The input $x=-1$ fails $f(x)\ge k$, yet satisfies $g(f(x))\ge g(k)$ because both transformed values are $0$. Thus the matching-threshold set equality is not a general rule for nondecreasing transformations with plateaus.

Strict increase is a sufficient assumption for using that equality for every matching threshold in $I$. It is not necessary for equality at a particular threshold: a plateau elsewhere may be irrelevant. The segment proof avoids both this issue and the need to represent every transformed threshold as $g(k)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 变换后的分数相同，不代表原分数相同
> 在 $[-1,1]$ 上取 $f(x)=x$，以及 $g(s)=\max\{s,0\}$，令 $k=-1/2$。输入 $x=-1$ 不满足 $f(x)\ge k$，但因 $g(f(x))=g(k)=0$，它满足 $g(f(x))\ge g(k)$。因此有平台的非减变换不能普遍使用上述门槛集合等式。

前文“需要严格递增”说的是保证所有对应门槛都可安全使用等式的统一条件，并非说某个特定门槛处的等式只有在严格递增时才能碰巧成立。平台若出现在别处，未必影响这个门槛。线段证明同时避免了这一问题，以及变换后的门槛未必能写成 $g(k)$ 的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Quasiconcavity can survive while concavity fails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 拟凹性保留时凹性仍可能丢失

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the positive quadrant, let $f(a,b)=\log a+\log b$, where $\log$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. Each logarithm is concave and their sum is concave, hence quasiconcave. Apply the strictly increasing [[自然指数函数|exponential function]] $g(s)=e^s$. Then $h(a,b)=e^{\log a+\log b}=ab$. Quasiconcavity survives, but concavity does not: the values at $(1,1)$ and $(3,3)$ are $1$ and $9$, whereas the midpoint value is $4<5$. The full threshold proof appears in [[凹性推出拟凹性|concavity and quasiconcavity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正象限上的 $\log x+\log y$ 凹，经指数变换成为 $xy$，仍拟凹却不凹。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the nondecreasing transformation to the [[拟凹性线段判据|segment inequality]]. It commutes with taking the smaller of two values, proving preservation without differentiability. Strict increase also preserves full ordering and supports the matching-threshold set equality; weak increase may collapse distinct values. Exponentiating a sum of logarithms shows that concavity itself need not survive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $\log$ 是[[自然对数]]，$g(s)=e^s$ 是[[自然指数函数]]。变换后 $(1,1)$ 与 $(3,3)$ 的值是 $1$、$9$，中点值却为 $4<5$，故不凹；完整的上轮廓集验证见[[凹性推出拟凹性|凹性与拟凹性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The direction of monotonicity matters too. The concave function $f(x)=-x^2$ is quasiconcave on $\mathbb R$, but the decreasing transformation $g(s)=-s$ produces $x^2$. Its level-$1$ upper contour set is $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)$, which contains $-1$ and $1$ but not their midpoint. A decreasing transformation therefore has no general quasiconcavity-preservation guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单调的方向也不能随意改：$f(x)=-x^2$ 在实线上凹，因而拟凹；但递减变换 $g(s)=-s$ 把它变成 $x^2$。门槛为 $1$ 的上轮廓集是 $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)$，含 $-1$、$1$ 而不含中点，所以递减变换没有一般的拟凹性保留保证。
