# 失业率是失业人数占就业与失业人口合计的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unemployment rate is the share of unemployed people in the combined employed and unemployed population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令就业人数为 $E$、失业人数为 $U$，劳动力为 $L=E+U$，则失业率 $u=U/L$（$L>0$）。失业的统计判定还要求满足适用调查的未就业、求职和可工作条件；年龄范围、回溯期与例外须按该国口径核对。分母不是全部人口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For employment $E$, unemployment $U$, and labour force $L=E+U>0$, the unemployment rate is $u=U/L$. Classification also requires the applicable survey's joblessness, search, and availability criteria; age limits, reference periods, and exceptions are jurisdiction-specific. The denominator is not total population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

失业率下降未必来自新增就业。若 $E=95,U=5$，失业率为 5%；两名失业者停止求职而退出劳动力后，$E$ 不变、$U=3$，失业率降为 $3/98\approx3.06\%$。因此判断劳动市场改善还应看就业人数和劳动参与情况；退出也不能自动称为“自愿失业”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lower rate need not reflect job creation. With $E=95,U=5$, the rate is 5%. If two unemployed people stop searching and leave the labour force, employment is unchanged but the rate becomes $3/98\approx3.06\%$. Employment and participation therefore matter, and exit does not automatically mean voluntary unemployment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中国城镇调查失业率按城镇常住人口调查，并非仅涵盖城镇户籍或等同登记失业率。跨口径比较需回到[[经济数据的实时口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
China's surveyed urban unemployment rate concerns urban residents, not only urban household registrations, and is distinct from registered unemployment. Cross-series comparisons require [[经济数据的实时口径|consistent statistical definitions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
