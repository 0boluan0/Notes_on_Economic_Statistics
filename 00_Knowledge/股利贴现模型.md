# 股利贴现模型是将预期现金股利及持有期末剩余股权价值折现的股票估值模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dividend discount model values a share by discounting expected cash dividends and the remaining equity value at the end of the holding period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股利贴现模型（DDM）从持有者的[[股权]]出发：把预计收到的现金股利，以及出售时仍然拥有的股票权利价值，折算到今天，得到模型下的[[股票内在价值]]。它折现的是股东收到的现金，而不是直接把公司的营业收入或全部净利润当成股利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The DDM starts from the holder's [[股权|equity claim]]. It discounts expected cash dividends and the value of the rights sold at the holding horizon to obtain model-implied [[股票内在价值|intrinsic share value]]. It values cash reaching the shareholder, not company revenue or all accounting profit treated as dividends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定一期末的权利：今天为 $t=0$，预计一期末收到每股现金股利 $D_1$，**收到这笔股利之后**的预计卖价为 $P_1$。在匹配股权风险、币种与期间的恒定必要报酬率 $k_e$ 下，忽略税费：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the rights at the end of the period. Today is $t=0$; $D_1$ is the expected cash dividend at $t=1$, and $P_1$ is the expected sale price **after that dividend has been received**. Using a constant required equity return $k_e$ matched to risk, currency, and period, and ignoring taxes and costs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_0=\frac{D_1+P_1}{1+k_e}.$$

例如预计一年后收股利 1 元并以除息价 22 元卖出，必要报酬率为 15%，则 $V_0=(1+22)/1.15=20$ 元。如果给的卖价已经包含同一笔股利权，就不能再把它加一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An expected dividend of 1 and ex-dividend sale price of 22 in one year, discounted at 15%, give $V_0=(1+22)/1.15=20$. If a quoted sale value already includes the same dividend right, adding the dividend again would double-count it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有 $H$ 期时，把每期股利按自己的日期折现，并在最后一期加上收到 $D_H$ 后的卖价 $P_H$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a holding period of $H$ periods, discount each dividend at its own date and add the final sale price $P_H$ after receipt of $D_H$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_0=\sum_{t=1}^{H}\frac{D_t}{(1+k_e)^t}
+\frac{P_H}{(1+k_e)^H}.$$

$P_H$ 表示届时剩余股东权利的价值，不要求公司在那一天清算。若用后续股利继续解释这个[[估值终值]]，并满足折现终端项趋于零的条件，有限式才可写为无限期股利之和：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$P_H$ values the remaining shareholder rights; the company need not liquidate on that date. If subsequent dividends explain this [[估值终值|terminal value]] and the discounted terminal term tends to zero, the finite expression becomes the infinite dividend sum:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\lim_{H\to\infty}\frac{P_H}{(1+k_e)^H}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_0=\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}\frac{D_t}{(1+k_e)^t}.$$

因此，有限持有并没有让出售价值“消失”；无限式是把那份剩余权利继续展开，并排除了不由未来分配支持的终端残项。已经用无限股利覆盖后续权利时，不再另加一笔相同权利的卖价。现实中暂不派息不等于没有价值；若分配政策、回购或可分配现金与实际股利不一致，应接着判断[[股利与自由现金流估值差异]]，而不是机械把全部未来股利设为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite holding horizon does not make the sale value disappear. The infinite form expands the remaining rights and excludes a residual terminal term unsupported by future distributions. Once future dividends already cover those rights, do not add their sale value again. No current dividend does not imply no value. When payout policy, repurchases, or distributable cash differ from actual dividends, examine [[股利与自由现金流估值差异|dividend versus free-cash-flow valuation]] rather than mechanically assuming all future dividends are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
