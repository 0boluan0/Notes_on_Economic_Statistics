# Conditional probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*条件概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conditional probability** describes how likely an event is when some information is already known. If someone rolls a fair die and tells us that the result is even, the chance of a six is no longer $1/6$: only $2,4,6$ remain possible, and the chance is $1/3$. We have changed the information used to calculate the probability, without changing the roll itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**条件概率**描述在已经知道某些信息时，一件事发生的可能性。别人掷了一枚公平骰子，并告诉我们点数是偶数，此时出现六点的概率就不再是 $1/6$：剩下的可能点数只有 $2,4,6$，所以概率是 $1/3$。这里改变的是计算概率时掌握的信息，骰子那次投掷本身没有改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To write this carefully, an **event** is a set of outcomes that satisfy a description. In this [[离散概率空间|discrete probability model]], the possible outcomes are $1,2,3,4,5,6$, each with probability $1/6$. Let $A=\{6\}$ mean “a six is rolled”, and let $B=\{2,4,6\}$ mean “the result is even”. The symbol $P(A\mid B)$ reads “the probability of $A$, given $B$”; the event after the bar is the information we are taking as known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了把它写清楚，先把**事件**理解为满足某个描述的一组结果。在这个[[离散概率空间|离散概率模型]]里，可能结果是 $1,2,3,4,5,6$，每个结果的概率都是 $1/6$。令 $A=\{6\}$ 表示“掷出六点”，$B=\{2,4,6\}$ 表示“点数是偶数”。$P(A\mid B)$ 读作“给定 $B$ 时 $A$ 的概率”；竖线后面的事件就是已知信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Restricting attention, then rescaling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先保留符合信息的结果，再重新缩放*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once $B$ is known, outcomes outside $B$ are ruled out. Among the remaining outcomes, we want those that also satisfy $A$. The intersection $A\cap B$ means “both $A$ and $B$ occur”. Its original probability tells us how much probability belongs to the desired outcomes, while $P(B)$ tells us how much original probability remains in total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道 $B$ 发生后，$B$ 外的结果就被排除了。在剩下的结果中，我们关心同时满足 $A$ 的部分。交集 $A\cap B$ 表示“$A$ 和 $B$ 同时发生”。它的原概率告诉我们目标结果占了多少概率，而 $P(B)$ 告诉我们剩下的结果总共占了多少原概率。

> [!abstract] The conditional probability formula · 条件概率公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $P(B)>0$, divide the probability shared by $A$ and $B$ by the probability of the known event:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $P(B)>0$ 时，用两个事件重叠部分的概率，除以已知事件的概率：
>
> $$P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The division makes the entire remaining event $B$ have conditional probability $1$. This rescaling is called **normalisation**.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这样相除后，整个剩余事件 $B$ 的条件概率就变成了 $1$。这种重新缩放叫作**归一化**。

> [!example] A six, given an even result · 已知偶数后出现六点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the die example, $A\cap B=\{6\}$, so $P(A\cap B)=1/6$ and $P(B)=3/6$. Therefore
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 骰子的例子中，$A\cap B=\{6\}$，所以 $P(A\cap B)=1/6$，而 $P(B)=3/6$。因此
>
> $$P(A\mid B)=\frac{1/6}{3/6}=\frac13.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Counting one favourable result among three works here because the die's outcomes were equally likely. With unequal probabilities, use their probability weights in the same formula.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里可以数成“三个结果中有一个符合要求”，是因为原来的六种结果等可能。若各结果概率不同，就要在同一公式中使用各自的概率权重。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator is $P(B)$ because $B$ defines which outcomes remain under consideration. Reversing the condition asks a different question: $P(B\mid A)=1$, since knowing the result is six guarantees it is even. Thus $P(A\mid B)$ and $P(B\mid A)$ usually differ. [[Bayes法则|Bayes' formula]] relates these two directions when the required probabilities are positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母是 $P(B)$，因为决定“现在还考虑哪些结果”的是 $B$。反过来问，$P(B\mid A)=1$，因为知道点数是六，就一定知道它是偶数。因此 $P(A\mid B)$ 和 $P(B\mid A)$ 通常不同。在所需概率为正时，[[Bayes法则]]把这两个方向联系起来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rearranging the definition gives $P(A\cap B)=P(B)P(A\mid B)$: first account for $B$, then for the fraction of $B$ where $A$ also occurs. This is the [[概率乘法法则|multiplication rule]]. If knowing $B$ leaves the probability of $A$ unchanged, so $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$, the two events are [[事件独立|independent]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把定义变形，就得到 $P(A\cap B)=P(B)P(A\mid B)$：先计算 $B$ 的概率，再计算其中同时满足 $A$ 的比例。这就是[[概率乘法法则]]。如果知道 $B$ 并不改变 $A$ 的概率，即 $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$，这两个事件就是[[事件独立|独立的]]。

> [!warning] The condition must have positive probability · 条件事件必须有正概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The ratio above is undefined when $P(B)=0$. For example, conditioning an ordinary die on “the result is seven” leaves no possible outcomes in that model. There is no denominator by which to normalise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $P(B)=0$ 时，上面的比值没有定义。例如，要求普通骰子满足“点数是七”，在这个模型中不会留下任何可能结果，也就没有可用来归一化的分母。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a continuous model, an exact value can have probability zero without being impossible. Conditioning on such values requires an additional construction, such as the [[条件分布|conditional distributions]] obtained from joint densities. More general regular conditional distributions assign probabilities consistently under appropriate assumptions; they need not determine a unique value at every zero-probability condition. These constructions do not divide by $P(B)=0$, and the elementary ratio alone cannot determine them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在连续模型里，一个精确取值的概率可能为零，却并非不可能。要以这种取值为条件，需要额外的构造，例如从联合密度得到[[条件分布]]；更一般的正则条件分布也在适当假设下一致地赋予条件概率，但不一定在每个零概率条件处都给出唯一值。这些构造都不是除以 $P(B)=0$，仅靠上面的初等比值也无法确定它们。
