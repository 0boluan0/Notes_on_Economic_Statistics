# 一致估计量序列随样本量增长依概率收敛到目标参数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A consistent estimator sequence converges in probability to its target as sample size grows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 估计量序列 $T_n$ 对目标 $\theta$ 一致，是指对每个固定 $\varepsilon>0$，$P_\theta(|T_n-\theta|>\varepsilon)\to0$ 随 $n\to\infty$ 成立。一般模型中的一致性要求对每个参数值成立。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consistency means that, for every fixed positive tolerance, the probability of exceeding it tends to zero as sample size grows. A model-wide claim is made at every parameter value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

这是[[依概率收敛]]用于估计问题。它不要求一条样本路径上的误差单调下降，也不要求有限样本无偏。若[[均方误差]]趋零，利用 Markov/Chebyshev 型概率界可推出一致；这个充分条件不是无条件的必要条件。[[一致不推出无偏]]给出具体反例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This applies [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]] to estimation. It does not require monotone realised errors or finite-sample unbiasedness. Vanishing [[均方误差|MSE]] is sufficient through a probability bound, but is not necessary without further assumptions. See [[一致不推出无偏|consistency without unbiasedness]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
