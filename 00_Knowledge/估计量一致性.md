# Consistency of an estimator
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*估计量的一致性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Consistency** asks whether an estimation rule becomes reliably close to its target as the amount of data grows. Choose any fixed positive error tolerance. A consistent rule makes the chance of exceeding that tolerance tend to zero as the sample size increases. This is a property of a sequence of rules and their sampling distributions, rather than a guarantee about one observed estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**一致性**问的是：数据越来越多时，一条估计规则能否可靠地接近目标。任选一个固定的正误差容忍值，一致规则会让超过这个容忍值的概率随样本量增长而趋于零。这是一个规则序列及其抽样分布的性质，不是对某次已观察估计值的保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## More observations, a more concentrated average
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*更多观察，让平均数更集中*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a [[总体与样本|population]] that takes $0,2,4,6$ with equal probabilities. Its mean is $3$ and its [[方差|variance]], the mean squared distance from $3$, is $(9+1+1+9)/4=5$. Draw $n$ times independently with replacement and estimate the mean by $\bar X_n=(X_1+\cdots+X_n)/n$. Here $n$ counts observations in each sample, not how many times we simulate the same-sized sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑等概率取 $0,2,4,6$ 的[[总体与样本|总体]]。均值是 $3$，[[方差]]是与 $3$ 的平方距离的平均，即 $(9+1+1+9)/4=5$。独立、有放回地抽 $n$ 次，用 $\bar X_n=(X_1+\cdots+X_n)/n$ 估计均值。这里 $n$ 是每份样本包含的观察数，不是把同样大小的样本模拟多少遍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample average has mean $3$ and variance $5/n$. Independence removes cross-covariances in the [[和的方差协方差项|variance of the sum]], while division by $n$ divides that variance by $n^2$. The variance therefore tends to zero as $n$ grows; we can turn this into a probability bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

样本平均的均值是 $3$，方差是 $5/n$。独立性使[[和的方差协方差项|求和方差]]中的交叉协方差为零，而整体除以 $n$ 会把方差除以 $n^2$。因此，$n$ 增长时方差趋于零；接下来可以把它转化成概率界。

> [!example] Turn concentration into an error probability · 把集中程度变成超差概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev's inequality]] bounds the chance of a deviation using the variance. For every fixed $\varepsilon>0$,
> $$P(|\bar X_n-3|>\varepsilon)\le\frac{5}{n\varepsilon^2}\longrightarrow0.$$
> For a tolerance of $0.5$, the bound is $20/n$. At $n=400$ it is $0.05$: the probability of an error larger than $0.5$ is at most five percent. This is an upper bound, not an exact probability; bounds above one give no useful extra information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[Chebyshev不等式]]利用方差给偏离概率设上界。任意固定 $\varepsilon>0$ 都有上式。若容忍误差为 $0.5$，上界是 $20/n$；$n=400$ 时为 $0.05$，即误差超过 $0.5$ 的概率至多为百分之五。这只是上界，不是精确概率；超过一的上界不能提供额外信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same argument works for [[独立同分布|independent, identically distributed draws]] from any population with a finite variance $\sigma^2$: replace $3$ by its mean $\mu$ and $5$ by $\sigma^2$. This proves consistency of the sample mean throughout that model. Finite variance is sufficient for this proof; it is not part of the definition of consistency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任何具有有限方差 $\sigma^2$ 的总体进行[[独立同分布]]抽样，证明都一样：把 $3$ 换成均值 $\mu$，把 $5$ 换成 $\sigma^2$。这证明样本均值在该模型中一致。有限方差是这条证明的充分条件，不是一致性的定义要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What must tend to zero?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*究竟什么量要趋于零？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T_n$ be the [[估计对象三分|estimation rule]] using $n$ observations, let $\theta$ index the data model, and let $g(\theta)$ be the finite real target. The symbol $P_\theta$ calculates probabilities under the model with that fixed parameter. The event $|T_n-g(\theta)|>\varepsilon$ says that the estimate misses the target by more than the chosen tolerance, on either side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $T_n$ 表示使用 $n$ 个观察的[[估计对象三分|估计规则]]，$\theta$ 标记数据模型，$g(\theta)$ 是有限实数目标。$P_\theta$ 按这个固定参数对应的模型计算概率。事件 $|T_n-g(\theta)|>\varepsilon$ 表示估计值偏离目标超过给定容忍值，可以偏高，也可以偏低。

> [!definition] Consistency in probability · 依概率意义的一致性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sequence $(T_n)$ is consistent for $g(\theta)$ throughout the model if, for **each** allowed fixed $\theta$ and **every** fixed $\varepsilon>0$,
> $$\lim_{n\to\infty}P_\theta\bigl(|T_n-g(\theta)|>\varepsilon\bigr)=0.$$
> This is [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]] to the target, written $T_n\xrightarrow{P_\theta}g(\theta)$. If the target is $\theta$ itself, replace $g(\theta)$ by $\theta$. This ordinary notion is also called weak consistency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若**每个**允许的固定 $\theta$ 以及**每个**固定 $\varepsilon>0$ 都满足上式，则序列 $(T_n)$ 对目标 $g(\theta)$ 在整个模型中一致。这就是向目标[[依概率收敛]]，记作 $T_n\xrightarrow{P_\theta}g(\theta)$；目标为 $\theta$ 本身时，把 $g(\theta)$ 换成 $\theta$。这种通常的一致性也称弱一致性。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a given parameter value and tolerance, the probability is an ordinary number at each $n$; it is this sequence of numbers that tends to zero. Success at only one parameter value is weaker than a model-wide claim. For example, the constant rule $T_n=7$ is consistent when the target is $7$, but fails whenever the target differs from $7$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定参数值和容忍值，每个 $n$ 对应的概率都是一个普通数；趋于零的是这串概率数值。只在一个参数值成功，弱于对整个模型成立。例如，常数规则 $T_n=7$ 在目标为 $7$ 时一致，但目标不是 $7$ 时就失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The required sample size may depend on $\theta$. This definition does not promise one error-probability bound that works uniformly over all parameter values. It also keeps $\varepsilon$ fixed as $n$ grows: replacing it by a shrinking tolerance asks for additional information about the rate of convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所需样本量可以依赖 $\theta$。这个定义没有承诺一个同时适用于所有参数值的统一超差概率界。它也要求 $n$ 增长时 $\varepsilon$ 保持固定；若让容忍值也不断缩小，就额外询问了收敛速度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A useful sufficient condition: vanishing MSE
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个常用充分条件：均方误差趋零*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[均方误差|mean squared error]] is $E_\theta[(T_n-g(\theta))^2]$. If this expectation tends to zero, [[Markov不等式|Markov's inequality]] applied to the nonnegative squared error gives
$$P_\theta(|T_n-g(\theta)|>\varepsilon)\le\frac{E_\theta[(T_n-g(\theta))^2]}{\varepsilon^2}\longrightarrow0.$$
Check this at every parameter value for a model-wide consistency result. No independence assumption is used in this implication itself; such assumptions may be needed to establish the MSE bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[均方误差]]是 $E_\theta[(T_n-g(\theta))^2]$。若它趋于零，对非负的平方误差使用[[Markov不等式]]就得到上式。要推出整个模型上的一致性，应在每个参数值检查这一条件。这个推论本身不需要独立性；证明具体 MSE 上界时，才可能需要相应假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse is false in general. A very large error on an increasingly rare event can keep the squared-error average away from zero even while every fixed-tolerance probability vanishes. The [[均方收敛推出概率收敛|mean-square/probability comparison]] constructs such a sequence explicitly. Therefore consistency alone does not imply vanishing MSE or variance, and the definition itself does not require finite moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般不能反推。越来越罕见的事件上若出现越来越巨大的误差，可能让平方误差的平均始终不趋于零，即使每个固定容忍值的超差概率都趋于零。[[均方收敛推出概率收敛|均方收敛与概率收敛的比较]]给出明确构造。因此，一致性本身不推出 MSE 或方差趋零，定义本身也不要求有限矩。

> [!warning] An asymptotic claim is not a pathwise promise · 渐近结论不是逐次保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consistency does not require each newly observed sample to give a smaller realized error, nor exact equality with the target at a finite $n$. It does not require [[估计量无偏性|finite-sample unbiasedness]] either: adding $1/n$ to an unbiased, consistent sample mean leaves a finite-sample bias that disappears with $n$. The separate [[一致不推出无偏|counterexample]] shows the calculation. Conversely, an unbiased rule that ignores all but the first observation need not be consistent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一致性不要求每次增加数据后，实现误差都更小，也不要求某个有限 $n$ 时与目标完全相等。它还不要求[[估计量无偏性|有限样本无偏]]：给无偏且一致的样本均值加上 $1/n$，会产生随 $n$ 消失的有限样本偏差；[[一致不推出无偏|独立反例卡]]展示了计算。反过来，无偏规则若永远只用第一个观察，也不一定一致。
