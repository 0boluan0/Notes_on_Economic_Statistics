# 商业模式是企业创造、交付并获取价值的经营逻辑
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A business model is the operating logic through which a firm creates, delivers, and captures value.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

商业模式说明企业为谁解决什么问题，如何组织资源与活动交付产品或服务，以及由谁付款、收入怎样覆盖成本并留下企业可获取的价值。它把客户、供给组织和经济可行性连起来；“卖软件”只说明产品，“按月订阅”只说明一种收费安排，都还不是完整的商业模式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A business model explains whose problem the firm solves, how resources and activities deliver the offering, and who pays enough to cover costs and leave value for the firm. It connects customers, operating arrangements, and economic viability. “Selling software” names a product; “monthly subscription” names a charging arrangement. Neither alone describes the complete model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一家虚构的餐馆排班服务商，为小餐馆减少人工排班时间，通过云端软件和在线客服交付，由餐馆按门店订阅付费，并承担开发、服务器和支持成本。客户得到的好处是[[价值主张]]，交付活动是[[价值链]]，付费门店数和单店收费则可以进入[[收入驱动预测]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a fictional scheduling service saves small restaurants time through cloud software and online support. Restaurants pay per-location subscriptions, while the provider bears development, server, and support costs. The customer benefit is its [[价值主张|value proposition]], delivery activities form its [[价值链|value chain]], and paying locations and fees can enter a [[收入驱动预测|revenue forecast]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

商业模式描述不等于商业成功证明：获客、留存、收费与成本假设仍须验证；相同收费方式的两家公司也可能拥有不同客户、活动系统与竞争地位。商业计划书是承载计划的文档，不是商业模式本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Describing a model does not establish its success: acquisition, retention, pricing, and cost assumptions still require evidence. Firms using the same charging arrangement may have different customers, activity systems, and competitive positions. A business plan is a planning document, not the model itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
