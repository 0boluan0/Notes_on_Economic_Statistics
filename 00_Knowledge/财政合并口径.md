# 财政合并先确定单位范围再抵销范围内相互对应的交易和债权债务
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consolidating fiscal accounts requires a defined perimeter and elimination of reciprocal internal flows and claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的consolidation指合并统计，不是“财政紧缩”。把一组单位当作一个整体报告时，要抵销它们相互之间的交易和债权债务，只留下与该组以外主体的关系。因此，先选[[一般政府与公共部门|统计范围]]，再识别交易双方，最后在双方口径、时点和金额一致的基础上抵销。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here consolidation means combining accounts, not fiscal tightening. Reporting units as one group requires eliminating internal transactions and reciprocal claims. First set the [[一般政府与公共部门|perimeter]], identify counterparties, and reconcile timing, valuation and amounts before elimination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中央政府欠债100，地方政府欠债40；其中地方持有中央发行的债券20。简单相加得到140，但一般政府合并后的债务是120，因为内部那笔20在中央是负债、在地方是资产。没有抵销居民或外国人持有的债务；债权人属于境内，不等于属于政府范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Central debt is 100 and local debt 40, with local government holding 20 of central bonds. Aggregation gives 140; consolidation gives debt of 120 after eliminating the internal claim. Debt held by domestic households or foreign investors is not eliminated merely because a holder is domestic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同理，中央拨给地方10，地方再购买公共服务10，合并报表不能把内部拨款和最终支出都算成对外使用资源20。抵销是报表处理，不是取消债券、免除地方履约或抹去政府层级之间的责任。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the centre grants 10 to a local government that spends 10 on services, consolidated accounts do not report 20 of external resource use. Consolidation does not cancel legal debt or remove responsibilities between levels of government.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [IMF, GFSM 2014](https://www.imf.org/external/np/sta/gfsm/pdf/text14.pdf)，§3.152–3.163：支持先合计再抵销内部对应关系及其分析用途；100/40/20和拨款例为自造。以雇员为实际交易人的代扣税等需按真实交易主体处理，不能见两个政府账户就抵销。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  These sections support consolidation and its purpose. Both examples are constructed. Transactions rerouted through employees, such as withheld employee taxes, require the true counterparty rather than automatic elimination.
