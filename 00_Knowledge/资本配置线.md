# 资本配置线描述一个固定风险组合与无风险资产的收益—波动组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A capital allocation line describes the mean–volatility combinations of one fixed risky portfolio and a risk-free asset*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本配置线（CAL）是在同一单期、同一计价单位下，把一个固定风险组合 $P$ 与 [[无风险资产]] 配置所得的期望收益—标准差轨迹。通常的直线公式描述对 $P$ 非负配置的分支；$P$ 本身不必有效，也不必是市场组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A capital allocation line (CAL) traces the expected return and standard deviation obtained by allocating between one fixed risky portfolio $P$ and a [[无风险资产|risk-free asset]], for one common period and unit of account. The usual straight-line formula describes the branch with a nonnegative allocation to $P$. The chosen portfolio need be neither efficient nor the market portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $P$ 的期望收益为 $\mu_P$、标准差 $\sigma_P>0$，权重 $y$ 在期初确定；其余 $1-y$ 投入无风险资产。在忽略税费且借贷同率 $r_f$ 的模型中，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $P$ have expected return $\mu_P$ and standard deviation $\sigma_P>0$. Choose its weight $y$ at the start and invest $1-y$ in the risk-free asset. With taxes and fees omitted and borrowing and lending at the same rate $r_f$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
R_C=r_f+y(R_P-r_f),\qquad
\mu_C=r_f+y(\mu_P-r_f),\qquad
\sigma_C=|y|\sigma_P.
$$

当 $y\ge0$ 时，消去 $y$ 得到下式，斜率是 $P$ 的 [[Sharpe比率]]。$0\le y<1$ 表示同时持有现金与风险组合，$y=1$ 表示全额风险投资，$y>1$ 表示借款加杠杆。若允许 $y<0$，标准差仍非负，应使用绝对值公式，此时得到另一分支而不是把横轴画成负标准差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y\ge0$, eliminating $y$ gives the following equation, whose slope is $P$'s [[Sharpe比率|Sharpe ratio]]. Weights $0\le y<1$ combine cash and the risky portfolio, $y=1$ invests entirely in it, and $y>1$ uses borrowing. If $y<0$ is allowed, standard deviation remains nonnegative: the absolute-value formula gives another branch, not a negative horizontal standard deviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mu_C=r_f+\frac{\mu_P-r_f}{\sigma_P}\sigma_C\qquad(y\ge0).
$$

例如 $r_f=3\%$、$\mu_P=9\%$、$\sigma_P=20\%$：$y=0.5$ 给出 $(\mu_C,\sigma_C)=(6\%,10\%)$；$y=1.5$ 给出 $(12\%,30\%)$；$y=-0.5$ 则给出 $(0\%,10\%)$。只有进一步选择合适的 [[切点组合]]，才得到同一机会集的有效配置射线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $r_f=3\%$, $\mu_P=9\%$, and $\sigma_P=20\%$, $y=0.5$ gives $(\mu_C,\sigma_C)=(6\%,10\%)$; $y=1.5$ gives $(12\%,30\%)$; and $y=-0.5$ gives $(0\%,10\%)$. Choosing an appropriate [[切点组合|tangency portfolio]] is an additional step needed to obtain the efficient allocation ray for that opportunity set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
