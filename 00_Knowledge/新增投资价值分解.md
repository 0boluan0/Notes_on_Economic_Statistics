# 在明确现金流时序下经营价值可分为既有业务价值与未来新增投资净现值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With explicit cash-flow timing, operating value decomposes into existing-business value and the NPV of future investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把“公司有增长机会”变成可核算的价值判断，可以先估计不追加扩张投资时既有业务的价值，再逐项加入未来扩张投资的[[净现值决策|净现值]]。加的是新项目收益现值减投资成本后的余额，不是未来投入资本的总额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To value growth opportunities, first value existing operations without expansion, then add the [[净现值决策|NPV]] of future expansion investments. Add benefits net of investment cost, not the capital spending itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一个简化模型中，既有业务从第一年末起每年可分配 $N_1$；必要维护投资已经处理，所以这里的税后经营利润也等于该基准业务现金流。第 $t$ 年末再投入 $I_t$，从第 $t+1$ 年末起每年永久增加税后现金 $R_tI_t$。假设项目可加总、风险与恒定折现率 $w>0$ 匹配、没有额外相互影响且级数收敛，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose existing operations distribute $N_1$ each year from year 1; maintenance is already accounted for, so baseline NOPAT equals distributable cash. Investment $I_t$ at year-end $t$ generates perpetual annual after-tax cash $R_tI_t$ from year $t+1$. Assume additive projects, a matching constant rate $w>0$, no further interactions, and convergence:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_0=\frac{N_1}{w}+\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}\frac{(R_t/w-1)I_t}{(1+w)^t}.$$

第二项很直接：站在投资发生的第 $t$ 年末，后续收入值 $R_tI_t/w$，立即付出的成本是 $I_t$；再把净额折回今天。单笔投资 100、一年后起每年产生 15、必要回报 10%，其投资时点净现值为 $15/0.10-100=50$。若同一投资一年后才发生，今天的贡献是 $50/1.10$，而不是 50。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At investment time, future receipts are worth $R_tI_t/w$ and the immediate cost is $I_t$. Discount their difference to today. An investment of 100 paying 15 annually from the following year has NPV 50 at a 10% rate; if made one year from now, its present contribution is $50/1.10$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$R_t=w$ 的新增投资在此模型中只扩大规模；$R_t>w$ 才带来正增长价值。现实项目若寿命有限、回报会变或资本分期投入，应重新逐期算 NPV，不能拿一年会计 ROIC 代替上述永久现金回报。[[增长不等于增值]]保留一般判断，本卡给出其特定现金流模型的展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here investment earning $R_t=w$ expands scale without adding NPV; $R_t>w$ creates value. Finite lives, changing returns, or staged spending require explicit cash-flow NPV rather than a single accounting ROIC. [[增长不等于增值|The general growth/value rule]] remains the broader decision principle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
