# 集合竞价汇集订单并在指定撮合时点按规则产生统一成交价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A call auction accumulates orders and applies its rules at a designated matching event to determine a single execution price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合竞价是先在一段时间内汇集买卖订单，再在规定的撮合时点统一确定该轮成交价和配量的交易机制。并不是每笔新单到来就立即成交；同一轮实际匹配的交易使用统一价格。如果规则下不能形成成交，则不凭空产生交易。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A call auction collects orders over an interval and determines the round's price and allocations at a specified matching event. A new order does not immediately execute merely upon arrival. Matched trades in the round share one price; if the rules yield no match, no transaction is fabricated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在只含普通限价单、没有其他约束的简化例子中，买单为 101 元 40 股、100 元 60 股；卖单为 99 元 30 股、100 元 50 股。对候选价 $p$，累计买量 $B(p)$ 包括限价不低于 $p$ 的买单，累计卖量 $S(p)$ 包括限价不高于 $p$ 的卖单，可撮合量为两者较小值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified book containing only ordinary limit orders and no further constraints, bids are 40 shares at 101 and 60 at 100; asks are 30 at 99 and 50 at 100. At candidate price $p$, $B(p)$ includes bids with limits at least $p$, while $S(p)$ includes asks with limits no greater than $p$. Executable quantity is the smaller total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Q(p)=\min\{B(p),S(p)\}.
$$

| 候选价 Candidate price | 累计买量 Cumulative bids | 累计卖量 Cumulative asks | 可成交量 Executable quantity |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 99 | 100 | 30 | 30 |
| 100 | 100 | 80 | 80 |
| 101 | 40 | 80 | 40 |

若允许的候选价为表中三价，按最大成交量规则应选 100 元，成交 80 股。买卖委托中原报 101 或 99 元的限价，不表示它们必须分别按那些价格成交。若最大量对应多个候选价，还需使用该市场的失衡、参考价等决胜规则；“最大量”单独未必确定唯一价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If these three prices are the admissible candidates, maximising executable volume selects 100 and trades 80 shares. Limits of 101 or 99 do not require those orders to trade at their submitted prices. When multiple candidates maximise volume, venue-specific imbalance, reference-price or other tie-breaks are still needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报单期间显示的参考撮合价可随新增或撤销订单而改变，并非最终成交承诺。集合竞价也不天然意味着“密封投标”；是否披露价格、数量或失衡，以及何时允许撤单，属于另行规定的信息和时段规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An indicative auction price may change as orders enter or leave and is not a final execution promise. A call auction is not inherently a sealed-bid mechanism. Disclosure of prices, quantities or imbalance and cancellation windows are separate information and session rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
