# 多阶段股利估值是在明确切分时点后将显性股利与后续股利价值一起折现的方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multistage dividend valuation discounts explicit dividends and the value of later dividends after choosing a precise cutoff date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当股利尚不能从下一期开始按一个固定增长率永久延续时，先逐期预测过渡阶段的股利，再将剩余股利在切分日的价值作为[[估值终值]]，一起折现到今天。这是[[股利贴现模型]]的分段计算；切分日不是凭空增加的一笔现金，也不表示公司届时必须停止经营。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When dividends cannot yet grow at one constant rate forever from next period, forecast transitional dividends individually, then value the remaining stream at the cutoff as a [[估值终值|terminal value]]. Discount both to today. This is a segmented calculation within the [[股利贴现模型|DDM]], not an additional cash payment or an assumption that the company must cease operating at the cutoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若显性期覆盖 $D_1,\ldots,D_H$，从下一笔 $D_{H+1}$ 起的后续股利按固定率 $g$ 永续增长，并采用恒定且匹配的股权必要报酬率 $k_e>g$，则收到 $D_H$ 后的续值与今天价值分别为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the explicit period covers $D_1,\ldots,D_H$ and the remaining dividend stream beginning with $D_{H+1}$ grows perpetually at $g$, with a constant matched required equity return $k_e>g$, the continuation value after receipt of $D_H$ and today's value are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_H=\frac{D_{H+1}}{k_e-g},\qquad
V_0=\sum_{t=1}^{H}\frac{D_t}{(1+k_e)^t}
+\frac{V_H}{(1+k_e)^H}.$$

先在时间线上写清“续值覆盖的第一笔股利”，再定位续值：用下一笔现金流计算的[[戈登增长模型|戈登价值]]位于那笔股利的**前一期末**。续值与显性期必须覆盖同一条现金流且不重叠；换一种合法切分不会改变今天价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First mark the **first dividend covered by the continuation value**, then place that value one period before the dividend. A [[戈登增长模型|Gordon value]] using the next cash flow belongs at the preceding period-end. Explicit dividends and continuation value must cover the same stream without overlap; another valid cutoff leaves today's value unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教材例3-25中，刚支付 $D_0=2$ 元，未来三期股利增长20%，之后每期增长12%，必要报酬率为15%。因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In textbook example 3-25, $D_0=2$ has just been paid. Dividends grow by 20% for the next three periods and by 12% thereafter; the required return is 15%. Thus:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$D_1=2.4,\quad D_2=2.88,\quad D_3=3.456,\quad D_4=3.87072.$$

**在第3期末切分。** 单独计算前三笔股利，再用 $D_4$ 求第3期末的续值：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Cut off at the end of period 3.** Value the first three dividends explicitly and use $D_4$ for the continuation value at $t=3$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_3=\frac{3.87072}{0.15-0.12}=129.024,$$
$$V_0=\frac{2.4}{1.15}+\frac{2.88}{1.15^2}
+\frac{3.456+129.024}{1.15^3}
=91.3724007561\ldots$$

**教材改在第2期末切分。** 把 $D_3$ 当作尾段第一笔股利，因为从它之后 $D_4/D_3$、$D_5/D_4$ 等都为1.12，可以直接写：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The textbook instead cuts off at the end of period 2.** Take $D_3$ as the first terminal-stream dividend. Since subsequent ratios $D_4/D_3$, $D_5/D_4$, and so on all equal 1.12:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_2=\frac{3.456}{0.15-0.12}=115.2,$$
$$V_0=\frac{2.4}{1.15}+\frac{2.88+115.2}{1.15^2}
=91.3724007561\ldots$$

这里 $D_3/D_2-1=20\%$ 并不妨碍后一种切分：尾段只用已知首笔 $D_3$ 及其**后**各期12%的增长。115.2属于第2期末，不能再折现3期；它已经覆盖 $D_3$，也不能在第二种算式里把 $D_3$ 再加一次。教材最后显示91.37元，与两种未舍入复算一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fact that $D_3/D_2-1=20\%$ does not invalidate the second cutoff: the terminal stream uses the known first dividend $D_3$ and 12% growth **after it**. The value 115.2 belongs at $t=2$, not three periods from today, and already includes $D_3$. Adding $D_3$ again would double-count it. Both unrounded calculations agree with the textbook's displayed value of 91.37.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际分析还需判断何时确实进入稳定期。若各期风险或折现率不同，应使用各期匹配的累计折现因子，不能照抄本例恒定15%；若后续股利仍不稳定，就继续显性预测，不能为了缩短表格而强套永续增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An actual valuation must justify when stable growth begins. If risk or discount rates vary by period, use matching cumulative discount factors rather than copying the constant 15% example. Continue explicit forecasting when subsequent dividends remain unstable instead of imposing a perpetuity merely to shorten the table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
