# 二元模型的预测概率是在给定协变量配置下由完整拟合模型给出的事件概率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A binary model's predicted probability is the event probability supplied by the complete fitted model at a stated covariate configuration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对二元结果 $Y\in\{0,1\}$，预测概率是模型在给定协变量配置 $x$ 下给出的
> $$
> \hat p(x)=\widehat{P}(Y=1\mid X=x).
> $$
> LPM 给出 $\hat p(x)=x'\hat\beta$；logit 与 probit 给出 $\hat p(x)=G(x'\hat\beta)$，其中分别有 $G=\Lambda$ 与 $G=\Phi$。这个量属于拟合模型，并不自动是真实风险或因果效应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a binary outcome $Y\in\{0,1\}$, a predicted probability is the fitted model's value $\hat p(x)=\widehat P(Y=1\mid X=x)$ at a stated covariate configuration. An LPM gives $x'\hat\beta$; logit and probit give $G(x'\hat\beta)$ with $G=\Lambda$ and $G=\Phi$, respectively. This is a model-implied quantity, not automatically the true risk or a causal effect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测概率回答“按这套模型和这个 $x$，事件概率是多少”。报告时要说明 $x$ 的取值来自真实个体、政策相关配置、样本分布还是人为构造的均值人。不同配置的预测差异属于不同问题，不能用一个“代表性概率”替代。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A predicted probability answers: “Under this model and for this $x$, what is the probability of the event?” A report should say whether the values in $x$ come from an actual individual, a policy-relevant configuration, the sample distribution, or an artificially constructed person at the means. Predictions for different configurations answer different questions and cannot be replaced by one supposedly “representative probability.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 三种模型怎样产生预测
> 某个 LPM 若在已声明的 $x$ 上给出 $x'\hat\beta=0.6$，模型内概率就是 $0.6$；某个 logit 若完整指数为 $0.6$，概率是 $\Lambda(0.6)\approx0.65$；某个 probit 若完整指数为 $0.6$，概率是 $\Phi(0.6)\approx0.73$。这里的 $0.6$ 在三种模型中处于不同尺度，例子只演示各自的映射，不是在比较模型优劣。LPM 还可能产生区间外拟合值，见 [[LPM预测越界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!example] How the three models generate predictions
> If an LPM gives $x'\hat\beta=0.6$ at a stated $x$, its model-implied probability is $0.6$. If a logit's complete index is $0.6$, its probability is $\Lambda(0.6)\approx0.65$; if a probit's complete index is $0.6$, its probability is $\Phi(0.6)\approx0.73$. The value $0.6$ is on a different scale in each model, so this example illustrates the mappings rather than ranking the models. The LPM can also produce fitted values outside the probability range; see [[LPM预测越界|out-of-range LPM predictions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 logit 或 probit 中，得到正确的 $x'\hat\beta$ 必须包括模型里的全部变换、类别项和交互；这条独立的计算边界见 [[预测须用完整指数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For logit or probit, constructing the correct $x'\hat\beta$ requires every transformation, categorical term, and interaction in the fitted model. The separate [[预测须用完整指数|complete-index boundary]] develops this calculation rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Logit 与 Probit 的 CDF 保证模型内预测落在 $(0,1)$；LPM 没有这个保证，可能产生 [[LPM预测越界|区间外拟合值]]。即使数值处于合法概率区间，也不保证它是可靠风险。若用于预测，应在完整 pipeline 的未见数据上检查严格适当概率损失（proper loss，例如 log loss 或 Brier score）、区分能力和 [[分类概率校准|概率校准]]；若用于因果解释，还需另行定义干预与识别条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The logit and probit CDFs keep their model-implied predictions in $(0,1)$; the LPM has no such guarantee and can produce [[LPM预测越界|out-of-range fitted values]]. Even a numerically admissible probability is not necessarily a reliable risk estimate. For prediction, evaluate the full pipeline on unseen data using a strictly proper probability loss, such as log loss or the Brier score, together with discrimination and [[分类概率校准|probability calibration]]. For causal interpretation, the intervention and identification conditions must also be defined separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
