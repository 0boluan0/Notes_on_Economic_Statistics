# 启明星是下降后由长阴线低位小实体与回升阳线组成的三线形态

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A morning star is a three-candle formation after a decline: a long down candle, a lower small body and a recovering up candle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

启明星是下降走势之后的三根[[K线]]组合：第一根是较长阴线；第二根为较小实体，并与第一实体向下分离形成星线；第三根是阳线，收盘明显进入第一根阴线实体。第二实体可阴可阳，关键是其较小及所处位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A morning star consists of three [[K线|candlesticks]] after a decline: a long down candle, a small second body gapping below the first body, and an up candle closing well inside the first down body. The second body may have either direction; its small size and position matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的星线跳空比较的是实体，不要求两根K线的全部影线也互不重叠。本课把星线与前后两实体都跳空称为理想形态；第二根出现时还只是候选星线，第三根收盘后才能判断三线组合是否完成。与之对应的上涨后形态是[[黄昏星]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The star gap concerns real bodies, not necessarily disjoint full ranges. The course calls gaps on both sides of the star ideal. The second candle alone is only a candidate star; the third close is needed to assess the completed formation. Its counterpart after an advance is the [[黄昏星|evening star]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

启明星是该学派的潜在反转形态名称，不是事先知道底部已形成。把“明显进入”转成自动规则时，应先固定穿入阈值；不能看完后续涨跌再改变定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Morning star names a potential reversal formation rather than foreknowledge of a bottom. An automated rule must specify how far the third body penetrates before observing subsequent returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
