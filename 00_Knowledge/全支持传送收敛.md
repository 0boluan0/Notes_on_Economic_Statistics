# Full-support teleportation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*全支持传送的加强性质*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A teleportation distribution has **full support** when every allowed page receives a positive chance of being chosen. In finite PageRank, this gives more than a common long-run distribution: every page can be reached directly, every page can stay put for one step, and every page receives positive stationary mass. These are useful guarantees about which states remain present in the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传送分布具有**全支持**，指每个允许的页面都有正概率被选中。在有限 PageRank 中，这不只带来共同的长期分布，还保证每个页面都能一步到达、每个页面都可以停留一步，并且每个页面的稳态质量都严格为正。这些保证说明了模型中的哪些状态会持续占有概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the finite [[PageRank随机传送|PageRank model]] with an already-repaired [[Markov矩阵|row-stochastic matrix]] $P$, a probability column $v$ and $0\le\alpha<1$. Let $G=\alpha P+(1-\alpha)\mathbf1v^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and $\mathbf1$ is the column of ones. Full support is the additional requirement $v_j>0$ for every state $j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用有限[[PageRank随机传送|PageRank 模型]]：$P$ 是已经完成悬空修补的[[Markov矩阵|行随机矩阵]]，$v$ 是概率列，$0\le\alpha<1$。令 $G=\alpha P+(1-\alpha)\mathbf1v^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，$\mathbf1$ 是全一列。全支持是额外要求：每个状态 $j$ 都有 $v_j>0$。

> [!important] Positive transitions and positive stationary mass · 转移全正，稳态也全正
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Full support gives
>
> $$G_{ij}\ge(1-\alpha)v_j>0\quad\text{for every }i,j.$$
>
> The chain is [[不可约链|irreducible]] and [[状态周期|aperiodic]]. Its unique stationary probability row $\pi$, already guaranteed by [[PageRank阻尼收敛|damping convergence]], satisfies
>
> $$\pi_j\ge(1-\alpha)v_j>0\quad\text{for every }j.$$
>
> Every initial probability row converges to this same $\pi$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 全支持给出每个 $G_{ij}\ge(1-\alpha)v_j>0$，因此链[[不可约链|不可约]]且[[状态周期|非周期]]。[[PageRank阻尼收敛]]已经保证了唯一稳态概率行 $\pi$ 的存在与任意初始概率行的收敛；全支持还进一步给出每个 $\pi_j\ge(1-\alpha)v_j>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read each guarantee from one entry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从一个元素读懂各个保证*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Irreducibility means that every state can reach every other state by a positive-probability route. Here the route takes just one step because $G_{ij}>0$. In particular $G_{ii}>0$ is a self-loop: a positive-time return is possible after one step. The greatest common divisor of possible return lengths is therefore one, which is the definition of period one, or aperiodicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不可约要求每个状态都能沿正概率路线到达其他任意状态。这里因为 $G_{ij}>0$，一步就足够。尤其是 $G_{ii}>0$ 给出了自环：一步就有可能正时间返回自身。因此可能回返步数的最大公约数为一，也就是周期为一，即非周期。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the stationary lower bound, use the [[Markov稳态分布|fixed-point equation]] $\pi G=\pi$. Its $j$-th component is

$$\pi_j=\alpha\sum_i\pi_iP_{ij}+(1-\alpha)v_j\ge(1-\alpha)v_j.$$

The first term is nonnegative. The second is a guaranteed inflow from teleportation, present regardless of which pages send ordinary links to $j$. With uniform teleportation $v_j=1/n$, the lower bound is $(1-\alpha)/n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

稳态下界来自[[Markov稳态分布|固定点方程]] $\pi G=\pi$。第 $j$ 个分量是 $\pi_j=\alpha\sum_i\pi_iP_{ij}+(1-\alpha)v_j\ge(1-\alpha)v_j$：第一项非负，第二项则是传送保证的流入，无论哪些页面向 $j$ 提供普通链接都存在。均匀传送 $v_j=1/n$ 时，下界就是 $(1-\alpha)/n$。

> [!example] A page with no incoming links can still receive mass · 没有入链，也可以有稳态质量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, so the ordinary walk always moves to page 1. With $\alpha=1/2$ and uniform $v$, both rows of $G$ become $(3/4,1/4)$. Every initial law reaches $\pi=(3/4,1/4)$ in one step. Page 2 has no incoming $P$ links, yet receives exactly the lower bound $(1-\alpha)v_2=1/4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$，普通游走总是前往页面 1。取 $\alpha=1/2$ 和均匀 $v$ 后，$G$ 的两行都变成 $(3/4,1/4)$，所以任意初始分布一步就到达 $\pi=(3/4,1/4)$。页面 2 没有来自 $P$ 的入链，但仍恰好得到下界 $(1-\alpha)v_2=1/4$ 的质量。

## Which conclusion actually needs full support?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪些结论真正用到了全支持？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general [[PageRank阻尼收敛|damping theorem]] only requires $v_j\ge0$ and total mass one. It still gives uniqueness and convergence when some $v_j=0$. What is then lost is the automatic proof that every entry of $G$ and every stationary component is positive, and that all declared states communicate. Some particular graphs can retain those properties, but the zero entries of $v$ alone do not ensure them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般的[[PageRank阻尼收敛|阻尼定理]]只要求 $v_j\ge0$ 且总质量为一；即使部分 $v_j=0$，唯一性与收敛仍然成立。此时失去的是自动保证：不能单凭这些条件断言 $G$ 逐项正、稳态每个分量正，或者所有声明的状态互通。某些具体图仍可能保留这些性质，但 $v$ 中的零项本身无法保证它们。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, with the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $P=I_2$, $v=(1,0)^T$ and $\alpha=1/2$ give $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1/2&1/2\end{pmatrix}$ and the unique limiting row $(1,0)$. Conversely, even a full-support $v$ has no effect at $\alpha=1$, because its coefficient is zero. The two-page swap $P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ then keeps alternating when started entirely at page 1. The restart must actually be used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，取[[单位矩阵]] $P=I_2$、$v=(1,0)^T$、$\alpha=1/2$ 给出 $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1/2&1/2\end{pmatrix}$，唯一极限是 $(1,0)$。反过来，即使 $v$ 全支持，在 $\alpha=1$ 时也毫无作用，因为它的系数为零；两页面交换链 $P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 从全部质量在页面 1 的初态出发，仍会不断交替。模型必须真的使用重新开始这一分支。
