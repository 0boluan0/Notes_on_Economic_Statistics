# 统一排放税率与竞争性配额价格都能用共同边际价格分散实现成本有效减排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A uniform emissions charge and a competitive allowance price can both decentralise a cost-effective abatement allocation through a common marginal price.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多家企业面对同一单位排放价格时，每家都会比较下一单位减排成本与少排一单位省下的价格。若企业是成本最小化者、完整履约且能自由作相应调整，内点企业会把自己的边际减排成本调到这个共同价格，从而实现[[成本有效减排|给定总减排量下的成本有效分担]]。这个机制既适用于统一按量收费，也适用于竞争性配额交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common emissions price makes each firm compare the next unit of abatement cost with the payment avoided by emitting less. Cost-minimising, compliant firms able to adjust equate interior marginal abatement costs with that price, implementing a [[成本有效减排|least-cost allocation of the resulting total abatement]]. Both uniform charges and competitive allowance trading can supply this mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定基准排放 $e_{0i}$、企业集合及凸成本函数 $C_i(a_i)$。使用私人成本作选择时，还须确认它代表相同的社会资源牺牲，没有遗漏的其他扭曲。[[排污费]]税费率 $t$ 与[[总量排放交易|配额市场]]价格 $p$ 对应的个体目标分别为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix baseline emissions, firms and convex abatement costs. Private costs must represent the relevant social resource sacrifice without omitted distortions. A rate $t$ under an [[排污费|emissions charge]] and a competitive price $p$ in [[总量排放交易|cap and trade]] give the respective firm objectives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\min_{a_i}\{C_i(a_i)+t(e_{0i}-a_i)\},
\qquad
\min_{a_i}\{C_i(a_i)+p(e_{0i}-a_i-l_i)\}.
$$

这里 $l_i$ 是固定、无条件的初始配额，企业把 $p$ 当作给定。两式在内点分别给 $C_i'=t$ 和 $C_i'=p$；边界企业按[[成本有效减排|角点不等式]]处理。初始配额为什么可能只改财务负担，见[[初始配额中性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $l_i$ is a fixed, unconditional endowment and the firm takes $p$ as given. Interior choices satisfy $C_i'=t$ and $C_i'=p$ respectively; use [[成本有效减排|corner inequalities]] at bounds. [[初始配额中性|Initial-allocation neutrality]] explains when the endowment changes financial burdens without changing choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种制度固定的变量不同。收费先定 $t$，总减排由各家响应加总；交易先给总量预算，再由市场出清决定 $p$。若要用收费恰好实现预定 $A$，必须找到使 $\sum_i a_i(t)=A$ 的适当费率；不知道成本时，统一收费不保证事前数量。共同价格下应横向加总各家的减排量，而非纵加同一减排量处的 MAC。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The instruments fix different variables. A charge fixes $t$ and sums firms' responses; trading fixes the emissions budget and lets the market determine $p$. Hitting a predetermined target with a charge requires a rate satisfying $\sum_i a_i(t)=A$. Unknown costs prevent an advance quantity guarantee. Aggregate abatement by adding quantities at a common price, not marginal costs at a common abatement quantity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $C_1=a_1^2/2$、$C_2=3a_2^2/2$，两家基准排放各100且角点未触及。价格75使两家减排75和25，总减排100；若交易总配额为100，竞争出清也给价格75。两种制度的减排成本均为3750，但政府收入和企业净付款未必相同；[[转移支付核算|转移与资源成本]]应分开核算，成本有效也不自动意味着公平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the stated quadratic costs and baseline emissions of one hundred per firm, a price of seventy-five induces abatement of seventy-five and twenty-five. A cap of one hundred emissions units also clears at seventy-five. Resource costs are $3,750$ under either instrument, but public revenue and firms' net payments can differ. Apply [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]] separately; cost-effectiveness does not establish fairness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
