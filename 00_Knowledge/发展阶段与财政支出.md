# 发展阶段论用生产能力与社会需求的变化解释财政支出重点的转移
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A stages-of-development account explains shifts in spending priorities through changes in productive capacity and social needs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发展阶段论从“经济正在解决什么发展问题”解释公共支出结构，而不仅问支出总额。基础设施不足、产业技术扩散和居民对社会保障的要求，并不对应完全相同的支出重点。Rostow 的历史框架把这些变化放在从起飞准备、起飞、走向成熟到大众消费的发展过程中理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A development-stage account relates spending composition to the economy's development problems. Infrastructure shortages, technological diffusion and demand for social protection need not imply the same priorities. Rostow's historical framework locates these changes across preparation for take-off, take-off, maturity and mass consumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这一框架中，起飞准备阶段重视交通、通信和教育等支持生产能力的投入；随着技术向更广领域扩散，产业结构和投资机会改变；在技术成熟以后，社会可能通过政治选择把更多资源用于福利和保障。最后一步包含分配与政治选择，不是技术成熟自动触发的一项会计规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The account emphasises transport, communications and education before take-off, changing industries and investment opportunities during technological diffusion, and politically chosen welfare provision after technical maturity. The final transition involves distribution and politics, not an accounting rule triggered automatically by maturity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用它阅读预算时，可以问：“当前主要瓶颈是什么，这项支出为什么在这个阶段更重要？”例如，先建成供水设施，之后稳定供水需要人员、维护和收费管理；支出重点改变，并不意味着原有公共资本不再需要资金。这个例子是机制说明，不是一份适用于所有国家的阶段时间表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask which bottleneck matters and why a particular expenditure becomes more important. Building a water system is followed by staffing, maintenance and administration to keep it operating. A shift in emphasis does not eliminate funding needs for existing capital. This is a constructed mechanism example, not a universal timetable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它也不能直接给出最优支出比例。私人投资增加不自动消除 [[市场失灵]]；基础设施建设多也不自动证明项目值得。具体支出仍要说明 [[政府干预的效率与分配理由|效率或分配理由]]，并检查资金、实施能力和受益者。课堂的早期/中期/成熟三段式，是支出重点的教学概括，不宜当作所有国家都必须依次完成的预算规律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The account does not determine an optimal spending share. Private investment need not eliminate [[市场失灵|market failures]], and more construction does not establish value. Individual interventions still need [[政府干预的效率与分配理由|efficiency or distributional justification]] and feasible finance and implementation. The course's three-stage summary is a teaching framework, not a mandatory sequence of budgets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
