# 税收归宿与超额负担
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Tax Incidence and Excess Burden*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 追踪税负最终由谁承担，以及税改变行为后损失了多少互利交易。
> **具体锚点：** 法律要求雇主缴工资税，不表示经济负担都由雇主承担；工资和就业调整会把一部分转给劳动者。
> **核心难点：** 法定纳税人和经济归宿不同；归宿由供需弹性和市场调整决定，不能从税单名字判断。
> **为什么重要：** 公平评价和税制设计必须看行为后的真实收入与价格，而不是只看征收环节。
> **继续：** 先做局部均衡弹性分析，再考虑一般均衡、时间范围和市场力量；设计原则见 [[最优税收：效率、公平与信息约束|最优税收、财政收入与政府预算]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Trace who ultimately bears a tax and how many mutually beneficial transactions disappear after behavior adjusts.
> **Concrete anchor:** Requiring employers to remit a payroll tax does not mean employers bear the full economic burden; wage and employment adjustments can shift part of it to workers.
> **Central difficulty:** The statutory taxpayer and economic incidence differ. Incidence is determined by supply and demand elasticities and market adjustment, not by the name on the tax bill.
> **Why it matters:** Equity and tax design must examine post-adjustment income and prices rather than only the collection point.
> **Continue with:** Begin with local elasticities, then add general equilibrium, time horizon, and market power; for design principles see [[最优税收：效率、公平与信息约束|optimal taxation, fiscal revenue, and government budgets]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 法定与经济归宿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Statutory and Economic Incidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税楔子使买方支付价与卖方收到价相差税额。无论形式上向哪一方征收，在竞争市场标准模型中均衡楔子相同；经济负担通过价格变化分配。制度摩擦、规避和显著性可使法律形式仍影响调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tax wedge creates a difference between the price paid by buyers and the price received by sellers. In the standard competitive model, the equilibrium wedge is the same regardless of which side remits the tax; economic burden is allocated through price changes. Institutional frictions, avoidance, and salience can nevertheless make legal form matter in practice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

归宿比较的是税前反事实与税后均衡中的实际福利或收入，不是税款从谁账户汇出。短期合同、最低工资、价格管制和征管边界可能暂时阻止价格调整，因此应声明市场、时间和制度条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Incidence compares actual welfare or income in the post-tax equilibrium with the no-tax counterfactual; it is not determined by whose account remits the payment. Short-term contracts, minimum wages, price controls, and enforcement boundaries may temporarily block price adjustment, so the market, time horizon, and institutional setting must be stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 弹性决定短期归宿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Elasticities Determine Short-run Incidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对更缺乏弹性的一方承担更多，因为更难改变数量。完全无弹性的一方承担全部，完全弹性的一方不承担。这里比较的是相关市场和时间范围的补偿弹性，不是曲线“更陡”的视觉判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relatively less elastic side bears more because it is less able to change quantity. A perfectly inelastic side bears the entire burden, while a perfectly elastic side bears none. The relevant comparison is between compensated elasticities in the relevant market and time horizon, not which curve merely looks steeper on a graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在简单竞争市场中，买方负担比例近似与供给弹性成正比，卖方负担比例近似与需求弹性的绝对值成正比。直觉是更容易退出市场的一方迫使价格向另一方调整；横纵轴单位改变会改变斜率，却不会改变弹性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simple competitive market, the buyers' burden share is approximately proportional to supply elasticity, while the sellers' share is approximately proportional to the absolute demand elasticity. The side that can exit more readily forces price adjustment onto the other side. Changing graph units changes slopes but not elasticities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一般均衡与资本化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*General Equilibrium and Capitalization*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个市场的税会改变要素需求、替代品和资产价格。地税可资本化进房价，资本税可通过投资和工资部分转移。短期固定要素与长期可移动要素归宿可能相反。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tax in one market changes factor demand, substitutes, and asset prices. Property taxes can be capitalized into house values, while capital taxes may partly shift through investment and wages. Incidence on fixed factors in the short run may differ from incidence on mobile factors in the long run.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 超额负担
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Excess Burden*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税收除转移收入给政府外，还扭曲边际选择，减少总剩余；该净损失是 deadweight loss。小税的超额负担近似随税率平方和补偿弹性增长，因此加倍税率可能超过加倍扭曲。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In addition to transferring revenue to government, taxation distorts marginal choices and reduces total surplus; this net loss is deadweight loss. For a small tax, excess burden rises approximately with the square of the tax rate and with compensated elasticity, so doubling a tax rate can more than double the distortion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税收收入本身不是社会损失，因为它可用于支出或转移；超额负担来自被放弃的交易和资源用于规避、征管等。比较税种时应在相同收入目标下看边际超额负担，而不是只比较税率高低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tax revenue itself is not a social loss because it can finance spending or transfers. Excess burden comes from forgone transactions and resources used in avoidance or administration. Compare tax instruments at a common revenue target through marginal excess burden rather than simply comparing statutory rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 劳动、储蓄与收入效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Labor, Saving, and Income Effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税后工资下降同时有替代效应（闲暇相对便宜）和收入效应（实际收入下降），劳动供给总反应不一定同号。储蓄税也需区分替代、收入和跨期流动性约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lower after-tax wage creates both a substitution effect, because leisure becomes relatively cheaper, and an income effect, because real income falls; the total labor-supply response need not have a fixed sign. Taxes on saving likewise require separating substitution, income, and intertemporal liquidity-constraint effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实证归宿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Empirical Incidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要识别税制变化的外生差异，并追踪工资、价格、数量和利润的动态。只观察名义缴税者会错过调整渠道。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empirical incidence requires plausibly exogenous variation in tax policy and dynamic tracking of wages, prices, quantities, and profits. Observing only the party that formally remits the tax misses adjustment channels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：工资税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Payroll Taxation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若劳动供给短期很不弹性，而企业劳动需求更有弹性，新增工资税即使法定由企业缴纳，也会主要通过较低净工资落到劳动者。若最低工资约束阻止工资下调，调整可能转为就业、工时或非工资福利下降；长期职业和地区流动又会改变弹性与归宿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If short-run labor supply is highly inelastic while firms' labor demand is more elastic, a new payroll tax remitted by employers will still fall mainly on workers through lower net wages. If a binding minimum wage prevents wage adjustment, the response may instead take the form of lower employment, hours, or non-wage benefits. Long-run occupational and geographic mobility can change elasticities and incidence again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用曲线斜率代替弹性会受坐标单位误导；只看税前税后平均价格会把同期冲击当归宿；只追踪一个市场会漏掉资本化和要素替代；把税收收入加入 deadweight loss 又会重复计算社会成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using slopes instead of elasticities makes results depend on graph units. Comparing average prices before and after a tax confuses concurrent shocks with incidence. Tracking only one market misses capitalization and factor substitution. Adding tax revenue to deadweight loss double-counts social cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么把工资税写在企业端不保证企业承担全部？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does levying a payroll tax on firms not ensure that firms bear the full burden?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 企业会通过工资、就业、价格和利润调整；真实负担取决于劳动供需弹性和制度摩擦。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Firms adjust wages, employment, prices, and profits. The true burden depends on labor supply and demand elasticities and institutional frictions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 哪一方通常承担更多税负？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which side usually bears more of a tax?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 相对缺乏弹性、较难逃离被税活动的一方。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The relatively less elastic side, which has greater difficulty leaving the taxed activity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 税收收入为什么不等于社会损失？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is tax revenue not equal to social loss?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 税收收入主要是私人向政府的转移；社会净损失是行为扭曲造成的互利交易减少及其他资源成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Tax revenue is mainly a transfer from private parties to government. Net social loss is the reduction in mutually beneficial transactions and other resource costs caused by distorted behavior.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
