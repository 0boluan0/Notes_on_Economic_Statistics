# 加权平均资本成本是与所估业务及融资政策匹配的各类资本必要回报的市值加权平均
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
WACC is the market-value-weighted required return on capital matched to the business and its financing policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加权平均资本成本（WACC）把债务、普通股等资本提供者所要求的回报，按其资本价值比例合成一个与经营现金流匹配的折现率。它衡量相应资本的机会成本；股东没有收到股利，不代表股权资本的成本为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
WACC combines required returns on debt, common equity, and other capital according to their value weights to obtain a rate matched to operating cash flow. It measures opportunity cost; the absence of dividends does not make equity free.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在只有普通股与债务、利息税盾可按 $T$ 使用的常见框架中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the usual framework with only common equity and debt and usable interest tax shields at rate $T$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$WACC=\frac{E}{D+E}k_e+\frac{D}{D+E}k_d(1-T).$$

$E,D$ 是匹配业务及目标融资政策的市值权重依据，$k_e,k_d$ 是相应的当前必要回报，不是历史融资票面数字的机械平均。假设股权占 60%、债务占 40%，股权成本 10%、债务成本 5%、税率 25%，则 $WACC=0.6(10\%)+0.4(5\%)(0.75)=7.5\%$。已有债务的票面利率只有在能代表当前债务回报要求时才可代用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$E,D$ are market-value weights appropriate to the business and target financing policy; $k_e,k_d$ are current required returns. A 60/40 equity/debt mix, costs of 10% and 5%, and a 25% tax rate give 7.5%. An old coupon rate is usable only when representative of the current debt return requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重稳定还不够：跨年使用一个 WACC，也要求经营风险、资本回报要求和税盾处理足够稳定。业务或债务比例明显改变时，应重新估计分期资本成本，或采用把融资影响分开估值的框架。[[项目Beta匹配]]解释为什么不能把一家公司的 WACC 无条件套给所有项目；[[WACC 与无杠杆 FCF]]说明分子为何不再扣利息、本金与股利。有优先股等资本时，要加入相应权重和成本项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stable weights alone are insufficient: a constant WACC also needs sufficiently stable business risk, required returns, and tax-shield treatment. Material changes require period-specific estimates or a framework valuing financing effects separately. [[项目Beta匹配|Project risk matching]] explains why one company rate is not universal; [[WACC 与无杠杆 FCF|cash-flow matching]] explains the exclusion of financing payments. Add matching terms for preferred or other capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
