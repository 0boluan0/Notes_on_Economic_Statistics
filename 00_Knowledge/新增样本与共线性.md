# More observations and multicollinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*新增样本与共线性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More observations can improve a regression estimate without making its predictors less correlated. To see why, separate **how much independent information has accumulated** from **what fraction of a predictor’s variation remains after adjusting for other predictors**. The first affects precision; the second enters the [[方差膨胀因子|variance inflation factor (VIF)]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增加观测可以让回归估计更精确，同时并不降低解释变量的相关程度。关键是分开**总共积累了多少独立信息**和**扣除其他解释变量后还剩多少变异比例**。前者影响精度，后者进入[[方差膨胀因子|方差膨胀因子（VIF）]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Repeating a design can add information

Consider $y_i=\beta_0+\beta_xx_i+\beta_zz_i+u_i$, with an intercept and two nonconstant predictors $x,z$. The estimate $\hat\beta_x$ is the fitted coefficient of $x$. Let $S_{xx}=\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2$ measure total variation in $x$, and let $r$ be their sample correlation. For a full-column-rank [[设计矩阵|design]] $D=[1,x,z]$ with $E(u\mid D)=0$ and error covariance $\sigma^2I$, meaning a common positive finite variance and zero cross-observation covariances, [[FWL残差化定理|FWL residualization]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 重复设计也能增加信息

考虑回归 $y_i=\beta_0+\beta_xx_i+\beta_zz_i+u_i$，含截距及两个非恒定解释变量 $x,z$；$\hat\beta_x$ 是拟合得到的 $x$ 系数。$S_{xx}=\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2$ 表示 $x$ 的总变异，$r$ 是两变量的样本相关系数。若[[设计矩阵|设计]] $D=[1,x,z]$ 满列秩，$E(u\mid D)=0$，误差协方差为 $\sigma^2I$ 即共同的正有限方差及观测间零协方差，[[FWL残差化定理]]给出

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_x\mid D)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{S_{xx}(1-r^2)},
\qquad \mathrm{VIF}_x=\frac{1}{1-r^2}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator of the variance is the total squared residual variation in $x$ after fitting it on $1,z$. VIF measures the loss as a proportion; it does not include the total scale $S_{xx}$. Repeat every design row $m$ times with genuinely new, conditionally independent errors of the same variance. Then $S_{xx}$ multiplies by $m$, $r$ and VIF stay unchanged, and the true coefficient variance divides by $m$. The conditional standard deviation divides by $\sqrt m$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方差分母是将 $x$ 对 $1,z$ 回归后剩余变异的平方总和。VIF 衡量比例上的损失，不包含总量 $S_{xx}$。若每个设计行重复 $m$ 次，并获得真正新鲜、给定设计后相互独立且方差相同的误差，$S_{xx}$ 变成原来的 $m$ 倍，$r$ 与 VIF 不变，真实系数方差除以 $m$，条件标准差除以 $\sqrt m$。

> [!warning] New observations versus copied records / 新观测与复制记录
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Copying the same recorded outcomes adds no independent information. Repeated measurements on the same unit can also have correlated errors, so the $1/m$ variance result needs the stated error condition. An estimated standard error uses an estimated noise variance and need not fall in every realized sample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 复制同一批已经记录的结果不会增加独立信息。同一对象的重复测量也可能误差相关，因此方差除以 $m$ 需要上述误差条件。报告的标准误还使用估计的噪声方差，不保证每份实际样本都下降。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A design you can count by hand

Use the four predictor combinations $(-1,-1),(-1,1),(1,-1),(1,1)$ in that order. Counts $(3,1,1,3)$ give eight observations. Both means are zero, $S_{xx}=8$ and $r=1/2$, so VIF is $4/3$ and the true slope variance is $\sigma^2/6$. Keeping the same model and spherical error variance gives three different extensions:

| Counts of the four combinations | Observations | Correlation | VIF | True variance of $\hat\beta_x$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $(6,2,2,6)$: repeat the original mix | 16 | $1/2$ | $4/3$ | $\sigma^2/12$ |
| $(3,3,3,3)$: add the underrepresented combinations | 12 | $0$ | $1$ | $\sigma^2/12$ |
| $(9,1,1,9)$: add the common combinations | 20 | $4/5$ | $25/9$ | $5\sigma^2/36$ |

The last row has a higher VIF but a smaller true variance than the original $\sigma^2/6$. The middle row lowers VIF without introducing any previously unseen combination: changing frequencies within the existing support is enough. Thus “new combinations are necessary” is too strong. What matters for VIF is the resulting relative geometry, not the row count or the set of distinct combinations alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一组可以手数的设计

按顺序使用四种解释变量组合 $(-1,-1),(-1,1),(1,-1),(1,1)$。频数 $(3,1,1,3)$ 共八个观测，两变量均值都为零，$S_{xx}=8$、$r=1/2$，所以 VIF 为 $4/3$，真实斜率方差为 $\sigma^2/6$。保持同一模型与球形误差方差，可作三种扩充：

| 四种组合的频数 | 观测数 | 相关系数 | VIF | $\hat\beta_x$ 的真实方差 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $(6,2,2,6)$：重复原来的比例 | 16 | $1/2$ | $4/3$ | $\sigma^2/12$ |
| $(3,3,3,3)$：补充原来较少的组合 | 12 | $0$ | $1$ | $\sigma^2/12$ |
| $(9,1,1,9)$：补充原来较多的组合 | 20 | $4/5$ | $25/9$ | $5\sigma^2/36$ |

最后一行的 VIF 上升，真实方差却比原来的 $\sigma^2/6$ 小。中间一行没有引入任何此前未见的组合，也降低了 VIF：改变已有支持内的频数就够了。因此，“必须增加新组合”这个要求过强。决定 VIF 的是最终的相对几何关系，不能只看行数或不同组合的集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Broader support can change the target population
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 新支持也会改变目标总体

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If two columns are exactly identical, repeating those rows never restores [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]]. A coding identity needs a new parameterization; a sample-specific identity may be broken by feasible new predictor combinations. Algebraic rank restoration alone does not establish a causal interpretation.

Collecting from new populations, periods or predictor regions may change the question the sample answers. Even changing frequencies within an existing support can change a descriptive best linear projection when the conditional mean is nonlinear. Under a common, correctly specified linear conditional mean, changing the design need not change its coefficient. Report the sampling scheme and target, and assess whether the added observations share the required error structure. The distinction between [[结构性与数据性共线|construction-induced and data-based dependence]] helps decide which intervention is plausible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两列完全相同，重复这些行永远不能恢复[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]。编码恒等式需要重新参数化；只在当前样本成立的恒等式则可能被可行的新组合打破。代数上恢复秩本身不证明因果解释成立。

从新人群、新时期或新的解释变量区域取样，可能改变样本所回答的问题。条件均值非线性时，即使只改变已有支持内的频数，也可能改变描述性的最佳线性投影。若共同的线性条件均值设定正确，改变设计则不一定改变其系数。应报告抽样方式与目标，并检查新增观测是否满足所需的误差结构。[[结构性与数据性共线]]的区别有助于判断什么干预可行。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Hansen, *Econometrics*, §§4.4 and 4.11](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~xshi28/Econometrics_Hansen.pdf): the conditional covariance formula and the separate roles of sample size and regressor correlation. The replication result and four-combination examples are derived directly from this formula.
- [Penn State STAT 501, §12.5](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson12): collecting data with different predictor relationships and reporting the resulting population coverage. Its design advice is distinguished here from the stronger, unnecessary requirement of previously unseen combinations.
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/06_多重共线性.md#5.2. 增大样本容量|Course section 5.2]]: sample expansion as a possible remedy; the examples above specify its conditions and limitations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hansen §4.4、4.11 核对条件协方差，以及样本量和解释变量相关性的不同作用；重复设计结论与四组合例由公式直接推导。
- Penn State §12.5 核对改变解释变量关系的数据收集方式及总体覆盖说明；本卡区分这种建议与“不出现全新组合就不可能改善”的过强要求。
- 课程 §5.2 提供扩充样本这一处理选项；上面的例子明确其条件与边界。
