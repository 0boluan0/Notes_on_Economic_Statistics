# 增加同类样本可以降低抽样噪声，却只有在新观测扩大回归量的独立变异时才会降低设计中的共线程度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*More observations of the same design can reduce sampling noise, but only new observations that broaden independent regressor variation reduce the collinearity of the design itself*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 两种“帮助”要分开
> 若新观测来自相同的狭窄联合分布，样本量增大通常会增加 $\tilde x_j'\tilde x_j$ 并缩小标准误，但辅助回归的 $R_j^2$ 和 VIF 未必下降。要修复“两个回归量几乎总是一起变化”的设计问题，新数据必须包含此前缺少的组合。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If new observations come from the same narrow joint distribution, a larger sample usually increases $\tilde x_j'\tilde x_j$ and can reduce standard errors, while the auxiliary $R_j^2$ and VIF need not fall. Repairing a design in which two regressors almost always move together requires observations containing combinations that were previously missing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 treatment 在原样本中几乎只出现在 London。再收集许多同样“London 接受处理、其他地区不接受”的观测，可以降低同一模式下的抽样噪声，却仍无法可靠区分 treatment effect 与 London difference。真正扩大独立变异需要观察 London 内的处理变化、其他地区的处理，或由研究设计产生的可比反事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose treatment occurs almost only in London. Collecting many more observations with the same pattern—treated London units and untreated units elsewhere—can reduce noise around that pattern but still cannot reliably separate a treatment effect from a London difference. Broader independent variation requires treatment variation within London, treatment elsewhere, or a design that creates credible comparable counterfactuals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 新支持也会改变目标总体
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Broader support can change the target population*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

刻意收集此前缺少的组合可能改变样本对应的人群、时间或制度范围。这个改变往往正是识别所需，但必须报告：系数现在依靠哪些新增区域，目标总体是否扩大，以及外推假设是否变化。不能把“更多数据”当作不改变 estimand 的中性按钮。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deliberately collecting missing combinations may change the population, period, or institutional range represented by the sample. That change may be exactly what identification needs, but it must be reported: which new regions now identify the coefficient, whether the target population expanded, and which extrapolation assumptions changed. “More data” is not always a neutral button that leaves the estimand untouched.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全共线性若来自恒等编码，重复任何数量的相同设计都不会恢复秩；应先修正参数化。近似共线时，则同时报告样本量带来的总精度改善和设计几何是否真正改变，避免把标准误下降误称为共线程度下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If exact collinearity comes from an identity in the coding, any number of repetitions of the same design leaves the rank deficient and the parameterization must be corrected. Under near collinearity, report both the overall precision gain from sample size and whether the design geometry actually changed; a smaller standard error is not necessarily a smaller degree of collinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 样本量翻倍后教育系数标准误下降，但教育对其他控制的辅助回归 $R^2$ 几乎不变。是否能说 VIF 问题已被解决？
>
> **答案：** 不能。总精度可能提高了，但独立变异占比没有明显改变；应分别报告标准误与 VIF／支持结构。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Penn State STAT 501, Lesson 12.5](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson12)：明确指出只收集更多同类数据不足以降低数据性共线，新增数据必须扩大相关预测变量的基础支持。
- [[近似共线性精度]]与[[方差膨胀因子]]：核对总残差变异可以随样本量增加而增长，而 VIF 取决于残差变异占总变异的比例。
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/06_多重共线性.md#5.2. 增大样本容量|本地课程：增加样本]]：核对课程把增加样本列为可能补救而非保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Penn State explicitly warns that collecting more of the same kind of data does not reduce data-based multicollinearity; the predictor support must be broadened. The linked source-checked atoms distinguish total residual variation from its VIF ratio, and the local course presents sample expansion as a possible rather than guaranteed remedy.
