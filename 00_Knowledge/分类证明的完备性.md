# Proof by cases
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分类证明的完备性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **proof by cases** handles different situations separately when one argument would be awkward. Its conclusion is reliable only if every permitted situation is handled. For example, an integer is even or odd; proving a claim for an arbitrary integer of each kind covers every integer, including zero and negative integers. Checking just a few even and odd examples does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**分类证明**把不便统一处理的情形分开论证。要得到可靠结论，每一种允许出现的情形都必须受到覆盖。例如，一个整数非偶即奇；分别对任意偶整数和任意奇整数证明结论，就覆盖包括零和负整数在内的全部整数。只检查几个奇偶数例子则不够。

> [!important] Coverage and a proof in every branch · 覆盖全部情形并逐支证明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A proof by cases divides a target into several situations and proves the same conclusion in each. Its key obligation is exhaustive coverage: every object satisfying the original hypotheses must lie in at least one proved case. Cases may overlap, because overlap merely covers some objects more than once rather than leaving any uncovered.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 分类证明把一个目标拆成若干情形，并在每个情形中证明同一结论。正确性的关键是完备覆盖：任意满足原前提的对象至少落入一个已经证明的分支。分支可以重叠，因为重叠对象只会被重复覆盖，不会造成遗漏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the case split works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*共同前提与分支条件各做什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $H$ denote the original hypotheses, $Q$ the desired conclusion, and $C_1,\ldots,C_k$ the chosen case conditions, for a positive integer $k$. The symbols $\land$ and $\lor$ mean “and” and inclusive “or.” The proof has two obligations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
令 $H$ 表示原命题的共同前提，$Q$ 表示待证结论，$C_1,\ldots,C_k$ 表示所选的分支条件，其中 $k$ 为正整数。$\land$ 表示“并且”，$\lor$ 表示可兼有的“或”。证明有两项义务：

$$H\Rightarrow(C_1\lor\cdots\lor C_k),\qquad (H\land C_i)\Rightarrow Q\quad\text{for each }i=1,\ldots,k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first statement supplies **coverage**: under $H$, at least one case applies. The second supplies a [[数学证明的有效推理|valid proof of the conclusion within each case]], retaining $H$. Any valid proof method may be used inside a branch. To see why the two together suffice, take an [[全称证明与存在见证|arbitrary object]] satisfying $H$. Coverage places it in some $C_i$, and that branch proves $Q$ for it. No step requires it to belong to only one case.

Coverage is required only for objects satisfying the original hypotheses. If $H$ is $x\ne0$ for real $x$, the cases $x<0$ and $x>0$ cover the permitted inputs; if $H$ allows every real number, they do not. An empty or impossible branch may be harmless, but it cannot compensate for a missing possible branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
第一式保证**覆盖**：在 $H$ 下至少有一个分支适用。第二式要求在每个分支中保留 $H$，再以[[数学证明的有效推理|可靠推理证明所需结论]]；分支内部可以使用任何有效证明方法。两者为何足够？取任意一个满足 $H$ 的[[全称证明与存在见证|对象]]；覆盖性保证它落在某个 $C_i$ 中，该分支的证明便对它给出 $Q$。整个推理不要求它只能属于一个分支。

覆盖只针对满足原前提的对象。如果实数 $x$ 的共同前提是 $x\ne0$，那么 $x<0$ 与 $x>0$ 已覆盖允许的输入；如果允许所有实数，就没有覆盖。空分支或不可能分支可以无害，但不能弥补漏掉的可能情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove a claim about every integer $n$, one may split into even and odd cases; these are disjoint and exhaustive. One may instead use $n\le 0$ and $n\ge 0$; they overlap at zero but still cover all integers, so the case split remains valid once both branches are proved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
要证明关于任意整数 $n$ 的结论，可以分成“$n$ 偶”与“$n$ 奇”；二者互斥且穷尽。也可以使用“$n\le 0$”与“$n\ge 0$”；它们在 $n=0$ 处重叠，但仍覆盖全部整数，所以只要两个分支都成立，分类仍有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A complete parity example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把奇偶两支都证明完*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We prove that $n(n+1)$ is even for every integer $n$. An even integer has the form $2r$ and an odd integer the form $2r+1$, where $r$ is an integer. [[带余除法|Division by two with remainder]] guarantees that exactly one of these forms applies. This establishes coverage before either branch is used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
证明：对每个整数 $n$，$n(n+1)$ 都为偶数。偶整数可写成 $2r$，奇整数可写成 $2r+1$，其中 $r$ 是整数。[[带余除法|除以二的带余除法]]保证二者恰有一种成立；这先确立了覆盖性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $n$ is even, write $n=2r$. Then

$$n(n+1)=2\bigl(r(2r+1)\bigr).$$

The quantity inside parentheses is an integer, so the product is even. If $n$ is odd, write $n=2r+1$. Then

$$n(n+1)=(2r+1)(2r+2)=2\bigl((2r+1)(r+1)\bigr).$$

Again the parenthesized quantity is an integer. Both possible cases give the same conclusion, so the claim holds for every integer. For orientation, $n=0$ gives $0$, while $n=-3$ gives $6$; neither boundary is excluded by the argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若 $n$ 为偶数，写成 $n=2r$，则

$$n(n+1)=2\bigl(r(2r+1)\bigr).$$

括号内是整数，所以乘积为偶数。若 $n$ 为奇数，写成 $n=2r+1$，则

$$n(n+1)=(2r+1)(2r+2)=2\bigl((2r+1)(r+1)\bigr).$$

括号内仍是整数。两种可能情形都给出同一结论，因此结论对每个整数成立。用数值帮助理解：$n=0$ 时乘积为 $0$，$n=-3$ 时为 $6$；论证并没有排除这两类边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Overlapping cases are allowed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零点可以同时属于两支*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every real $x$, prove $x^2\ge0$ using the overlapping cases $x\ge0$ and $x\le0$. In the first case, $x\cdot x$ is a product of nonnegative numbers. In the second, $-x\ge0$ and $x^2=(-x)^2$ is such a product. At $x=0$, both proofs apply and both give $0\ge0$. Repeating a valid argument at an overlap does not damage the conclusion.

Contrast the false claim $x^2>0$ for every real $x$. It is true on the branches $x<0$ and $x>0$, but their union omits $x=0$, where the claim fails. The branch calculations are not the problem; the missing coverage is.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对每个实数 $x$，用有重叠的两支 $x\ge0$ 与 $x\le0$ 证明 $x^2\ge0$。第一支中，$x\cdot x$ 是非负数的乘积；第二支中，$-x\ge0$，且 $x^2=(-x)^2$ 也是非负数的乘积。$x=0$ 时两套论证都适用，也都得到 $0\ge0$。在重叠处重复一次有效证明，不会损害结论。

对比错误命题“每个实数都满足 $x^2>0$”：它在 $x<0$ 和 $x>0$ 两支上成立，但两支的并集遗漏了 $x=0$，而命题恰在此处失败。问题不在分支计算，而在覆盖不完整。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Every branch retains the hypotheses shared by the original proposition; a case split does not license dropping an inconvenient premise.
- Proving only the “typical cases” is incomplete unless the omitted situations are shown to be impossible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 每个分支都必须保留原命题的共同前提；分情况不是把不方便的前提丢掉。
- 如果只证明“典型情形”而没有说明其他情形为何不可能，论证就不完备。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each branch must prove the requested conclusion for **every** object satisfying its conditions. [[全称命题与反例|A representative example]] is not a substitute unless it exhausts that branch. Nested cases are allowed, but every split must cover its parent case; an omitted subcase leaves a hole even when the top-level split is complete.

A fact established only in one branch cannot be combined with a fact established only in another as though both held for every input. For example, an even-branch calculation and an odd-branch calculation do not make an arbitrary integer both even and odd. What joins at the end is their common conclusion $Q$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
每个分支都必须对满足该支条件的**全部**对象证明所求结论。除非已经穷尽该分支，否则[[全称命题与反例|代表性例子]]不能替代证明。可以继续嵌套分类，但每次划分都必须覆盖其父分支；即使顶层完备，漏掉子情形也会留下缺口。

不能把只在某一支成立的事实，与只在另一支成立的事实拼起来，当成对所有输入都同时成立。例如，偶数分支和奇数分支的计算，并不会让任意整数同时变成奇数和偶数。最后能够合并的是各支共同得到的结论 $Q$。

> [!question]- Find the uncovered input · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A proof of a property for every real $x$ handles only $x<0$ and $x>0$. What is missing?
>
> **Answer:** The case $x=0$. The two branches are not exhaustive; add that case or use a covering split such as $x\le 0$ and $x>0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 证明所有实数 $x$ 的某性质时，只处理 $x<0$ 与 $x>0$ 两种情形，缺了什么？
>
> **答案：** 缺少 $x=0$。两个分支没有覆盖全部实数；必须增加该情形，或把分支改成例如 $x\le 0$ 与 $x>0$。
