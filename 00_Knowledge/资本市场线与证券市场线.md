# 资本市场线按总波动描述有效组合而证券市场线按Beta描述均衡期望收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The CML relates efficient portfolios to total volatility; the SML relates equilibrium expected returns to beta*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一标准 CAPM 中，[[资本市场线]]（CML）回答有效组合如何在期望收益与总波动之间配置；[[证券市场线]]（SML）回答单资产或任意组合的均衡期望收益如何随市场 Beta 变化。二者都不是已实现收益的承诺；横轴和适用对象不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within the same standard CAPM, the [[资本市场线|capital market line]] describes allocation between expected return and total volatility for efficient portfolios. The [[证券市场线|security market line]] describes how equilibrium expected return varies with market beta for an asset or any portfolio. Neither promises realized returns, and their horizontal axes and domains are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 比较 | CML | SML |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 横轴 | $\sigma_P$，总收益标准差 | $\beta_P$，相对市场的协方差暴露 |
| 对象 | 无风险资产与正向市场配置形成的有效组合 | 单项资产及所有组合，包括有效与无效组合 |
| 斜率 | $(\mu_M-r_f)/\sigma_M$ | $\mu_M-r_f$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Comparison | CML | SML |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal axis | $\sigma_P$, total return standard deviation | $\beta_P$, covariance exposure to the market |
| Domain | Efficient combinations of the risk-free asset and a nonnegative market allocation | Assets and all portfolios, both efficient and inefficient |
| Slope | $(\mu_M-r_f)/\sigma_M$ | $\mu_M-r_f$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例：$r_f=3\%$、$\mu_M=9\%$、$\sigma_M=20\%$。若股票 $i$ 的 $\beta_i=1$，CAPM 给出 $\mu_i=9\%$；若它另有正交残余风险，令总波动为 30%，它仍可在 SML 上，但低于同波动处期望收益为 12% 的 CML。低于 CML 说明它不作为整体有效组合，不单凭此判定错误定价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $r_f=3\%$, $\mu_M=9\%$, and $\sigma_M=20\%$. A stock with $\beta_i=1$ has CAPM expected return 9%. If orthogonal residual risk raises its total volatility to 30%, it can remain on the SML while lying below the CML's 12% expected return at that volatility. Being below the CML makes it inefficient as a complete portfolio, not automatically mispriced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“没有特质残差”也不能脱离方向条件就替代有效性：$R_P=2r_f-R_M$ 对市场的 Beta 为 $-1$，残差为零，却有期望收益 $-3\%$、标准差 20%。它符合 SML，但不在有效 CML 上。因此应复用 [[Beta与总波动]] 的分解来分析风险，不能把“只剩系统风险”单独当作有效组合的充分定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Having no idiosyncratic residual is not sufficient without the direction condition. For $R_P=2r_f-R_M$, beta is $-1$ and residual risk is zero, yet expected return is $-3\%$ with 20% volatility. It satisfies the SML but not the efficient CML. Use [[Beta与总波动|the beta/total-volatility decomposition]] to analyze risk; “only systematic risk remains” is not by itself a sufficient definition of efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
