# k 阶偏自相关衡量剔除中间滞后后本期与第 k 阶滞后的线性关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The lag-k partial autocorrelation measures the linear relation between the present and lag k after removing the intervening lags*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对宽平稳序列，把 $X_t$ 线性投影到 $X_{t-1},\ldots,X_{t-k}$：
> $$X_t=a_k+\phi_{k1}X_{t-1}+\cdots+\phi_{kk}X_{t-k}+u_{t,k}.$$
> 第 $k$ 阶偏自相关 $\alpha(k)$ 就是最后一个总体投影系数 $\phi_{kk}$。它衡量在已经控制滞后 $1,\ldots,k-1$ 后，$X_{t-k}$ 还为 $X_t$ 增加多少线性关系。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Project $X_t$ linearly on lags $1$ through $k$. The lag-$k$ partial autocorrelation is the final population projection coefficient $\phi_{kk}$: the remaining linear relation with $X_{t-k}$ after the intervening lags have been removed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地，可分别从 $X_t$ 与 $X_{t-k}$ 中投影掉中间变量 $X_{t-1},\ldots,X_{t-k+1}$，再计算两组残差的相关。$k=1$ 时没有中间滞后可剔除，所以 $\alpha(1)=\rho(1)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, remove the linear effects of the intervening lags from both $X_t$ and $X_{t-k}$ and correlate the two residuals. At lag one there is nothing to remove, so the PACF equals the ACF.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

AR(1) 说明 PACF 与 ACF 为什么不是同一张图。若
$$X_t=\phi X_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,\qquad |\phi|<1,$$
则 $\rho(2)=\phi^2$ 通常不为零；但一旦控制 $X_{t-1}$，$X_{t-2}$ 不再增加线性预测信息，所以 $\alpha(2)=0$。这正是“相关可经中间滞后传递”与“直接线性关系”的差别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For AR(1), $\rho(2)=\phi^2$ is generally nonzero, yet $\alpha(2)=0$ because lag two adds no linear predictive content once lag one is controlled. The ACF includes dependence transmitted through intervening lags; the PACF removes it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

样本 PACF 是总体投影系数的估计，不是数据生成机制本身。有限样本尖峰有抽样误差，多个滞后同时查看还会产生偶然越界；如何把 ACF 与 PACF 用于提出 AR/MA 阶数候选，见[[ACF-PACF阶数识别]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample PACF estimates the population projection coefficients; it is not the data-generating mechanism itself. Sampling variation and inspection of many lags can create isolated spikes. Its limited role in proposing AR/MA orders is handled separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> AR(1) 的二阶自相关 $\rho(2)$ 不为零，为什么二阶偏自相关仍可为零？
>
> **答案：** 二阶自相关包含经 $X_{t-1}$ 传递的关系；二阶偏自相关先剔除这条中间路径，检验 $X_{t-2}$ 是否还提供额外线性信息。
