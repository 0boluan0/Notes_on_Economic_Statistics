# 股票与企业价值评估
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equity and Enterprise Valuation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把公司未来经营、再投资、融资和风险假设转换成股权价值或企业价值，并反推当前价格要求什么结果才合理。
> **具体锚点：** 明年股利 5、长期增长 3%、股权成本 8% 时，稳定增长模型给出价值 100；股权成本只升到 9%，价值就降到约 83.33。
> **核心难点：** 现金流归属与贴现率必须匹配，增长需要再投资支持，终值不能让企业永久增长得快于经济。
> **为什么重要：** 它把“公司很好”变成可检查的收入、利润率、再投资、资本成本与价格条件。
> **继续：** 经营假设从 [[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测]] 和 [[宏观、行业与公司基本面分析]] 来；股权成本可从 [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本]] 取得。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It converts assumptions about future operations, reinvestment, financing, and risk into equity or enterprise value and works backward from the current price to the outcomes it requires.
> **Concrete anchor:** With next year's dividend of 5, perpetual growth of 3%, and cost of equity of 8%, a stable-growth model gives value 100. Raising the cost of equity only to 9% lowers value to about 83.33.
> **Central difficulty:** Cash-flow ownership must match the discount rate, growth requires supporting reinvestment, and terminal value cannot assume that the firm forever outgrows the economy.
> **Why it matters:** It turns “this is a good company” into testable conditions for revenue, margin, reinvestment, cost of capital, and price.
> **Continue:** Operating assumptions come from [[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测|Financial Statements, Ratio Analysis, and Forecasting]] and [[宏观、行业与公司基本面分析|Macro, Industry, and Company Fundamental Analysis]]. Cost of equity can come from [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|CAPM, Systematic Risk, and the Cost of Capital]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 统一现金流折现框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A unified discounted-cash-flow framework*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$V_0=\sum_t E(CF_t)/(1+r_t)^t$。名义现金流配名义贴现率，实际配实际；股权现金流配股权成本，企业自由现金流配 WACC。风险既可进期望现金流，也可进贴现因子，但不能无意识双重扣减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$V_0=\sum_t E(CF_t)/(1+r_t)^t$. Match nominal cash flows to nominal discount rates and real cash flows to real rates; match equity cash flow to cost of equity and free cash flow to the firm to WACC. Risk can enter expected cash flows or discount factors, but it must not be deducted twice without intention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估值的主干只有三步：预测可分配现金流，选择归属与风险匹配的贴现率，把显性期和持续经营期加总。复杂模型只是把这三步展开；若无法说明每个输入属于哪一步，模型越细越可能掩盖错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Valuation has only three backbone steps: forecast distributable cash flow, choose a discount rate matching ownership and risk, and add the explicit forecast period to continuing value. Complex models merely expand these steps. If each input cannot be assigned to one of them, more detail is likely to conceal rather than solve errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 股利贴现与增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dividend discounting and growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

稳定增长 Gordon 模型 $P_0=D_1/(r-g)$ 需 $r>g$ 且增长长期可持续。多阶段模型把高增长过渡到稳定期。分红不是唯一股东现金流，回购和留存再投资回报也重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stable-growth Gordon model $P_0=D_1/(r-g)$ requires $r>g$ and sustainable long-run growth. A multi-stage model transitions high growth toward a stable stage. Dividends are not the only shareholder cash flow; repurchases and returns on retained reinvestment also matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长不是免费输入。长期上可用 $g=\text{reinvestment rate}\times ROIC$ 连接增长与再投资效率。若预测高增长却不增加营运资本和资本开支，或让增量投资回报无限高，现金流与增长假设彼此冲突。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth is not a free input. In the long run, $g=\text{reinvestment rate}\times ROIC$ links growth to reinvestment efficiency. Forecasting high growth without additional working capital and capital expenditure, or assuming infinitely high returns on incremental investment, makes cash-flow and growth assumptions inconsistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## FCFF 与 FCFE
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*FCFF and FCFE*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

FCFF 是对债权和股权共同可分配现金流，用 WACC 折现后减净债务得股权价值；FCFE 直接对股东，用股权成本折现。债务政策变化大时 FCFF 往往更稳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFF is cash flow distributable jointly to debt and equity holders; discount it at WACC and subtract net debt to obtain equity value. FCFE is cash flow directly to shareholders and is discounted at cost of equity. FCFF is often more stable when debt policy changes substantially.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条防错链是：从经营价值到企业价值，再桥接到普通股价值。桥接时除净债务外，还需按事实处理少数股东权益、非经营资产、养老金缺口、优先股和稀释性期权。不要把企业价值倍数直接与每股价格相比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful error-prevention chain moves from operating value to enterprise value and then bridges to common-equity value. Besides net debt, the bridge may need to account for minority interests, non-operating assets, pension deficits, preferred stock, and dilutive options. Do not compare an enterprise-value multiple directly with price per share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 相对估值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relative valuation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

P/E、P/B、EV/EBITDA 等倍数把价格与基本面标尺比较，但可比公司需在增长、风险、资本密集度和会计口径上相近。倍数差异应被驱动解释，不能只用行业中位数盖章。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P/E, P/B, and EV/EBITDA compare price with a fundamental scale, but comparable firms should be similar in growth, risk, capital intensity, and accounting convention. Differences in multiples should be explained by drivers, not validated merely by an industry median.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择倍数时要匹配分子分母归属：企业价值配息税前、对全部资本提供者的经营指标；股权价值配净利润或账面权益。负利润使 P/E 失去通常解释，资本化政策差异会扭曲 EBITDA 可比性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Match ownership in a multiple's numerator and denominator. Enterprise value goes with pre-interest operating measures available to all capital providers; equity value goes with net income or book equity. Negative earnings remove the usual interpretation of P/E, while differing capitalization policies can distort EBITDA comparability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 敏感性与终值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sensitivity and terminal value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告增长、利润率、再投资和贴现率的二维敏感性，检查终值占比和隐含长期经济规模。估值是条件区间，不是精确点数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report two-way sensitivities for growth, margins, reinvestment, and discount rates; inspect the terminal-value share and the implied long-run economic scale. Valuation is a conditional range, not an exact point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 数值锚点：终值为什么敏感
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical anchor: why terminal value is sensitive*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若稳定期下一年 FCFF 为 50、WACC 为 8%、增长率为 3%，终值为 $50/(0.08-0.03)=1{,}000$。WACC 只升到 9% 时终值变为约 833.33；增长率升到 4% 时也变为 1,250。分母 $r-g$ 很小时，小幅假设变化会造成大幅价值变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If next year's stable-period FCFF is 50, WACC is 8%, and growth is 3%, terminal value is $50/(0.08-0.03)=1{,}000$. Raising WACC only to 9% lowers terminal value to about 833.33; raising growth to 4% instead increases it to 1,250. When the denominator $r-g$ is small, modest assumption changes produce large value changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用 WACC 折 FCFE 或用股权成本折 FCFF，会错配现金流归属与风险。
- 高增长期结束后仍假设公司永远快于整体经济，终值经济含义不成立。
- 用同行 EV/EBITDA 中位数乘目标公司的 EBITDA 后直接称为股权价值，遗漏净债务桥接。
- 终值占比极高却只报告一个点数，不展示 $r$ 与 $g$ 的联合敏感性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Discounting FCFE at WACC or FCFF at cost of equity mismatches cash-flow ownership and risk.
- Assuming the firm permanently grows faster than the overall economy after the high-growth stage gives terminal value no coherent economic meaning.
- Multiplying the peer median EV/EBITDA by target EBITDA and calling the result equity value omits the net-debt bridge.
- Reporting one point estimate when terminal value dominates, without joint sensitivity to $r$ and $g$, hides the main uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么现金流口径和贴现率必须匹配？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must the cash-flow definition match the discount rate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不同现金流归属于不同资本提供者并含不同风险；错配会重复或遗漏债务和风险影响。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Different cash flows belong to different capital providers and carry different risks; mismatch duplicates or omits the effects of debt and risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 终值占估值很高时应怎样处理？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What should be done when terminal value is a large share of valuation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 核验稳定增长、回报率和规模假设，做敏感性和隐含预期分析，并降低点估计确信度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Verify stable-growth, return, and scale assumptions; perform sensitivity and implied-expectations analysis; and reduce confidence in a point estimate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么好公司不一定是便宜股票？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why is a good company not necessarily a cheap stock?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 公司质量影响未来现金流，但价格已经反映市场对这些现金流的预期；只有未来结果相对价格隐含预期更好，或要求回报下降，投资者才获得超出基准的回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Company quality affects future cash flow, but the price already reflects market expectations. Investors outperform only if future outcomes exceed price-implied expectations or required returns fall.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一家公司用 FCFF 估值后，怎样得到每股价值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*After valuing a company with FCFF, how is value per share obtained?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 先以 WACC 折现得到经营/企业价值，再加非经营资产、减净债务及其他优先索取权并处理少数股东权益，得到普通股权价值，最后除以完全稀释股数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Discount at WACC to obtain operating or enterprise value, add non-operating assets, subtract net debt and other senior claims, account for minority interests, obtain common-equity value, and divide by fully diluted shares.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
