# 方差缩减是在保持估计目标与偏差口径不变时，通过重新设计估计量提高单位计算成本精度的方法族
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Variance reduction is a family of estimator-design methods that improves precision per unit computational cost while preserving the estimand and stated bias convention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 方差缩减不是让随机数“更随机”，而是在目标量不变、偏差已说明的前提下，利用相关配对、已知期望或改变抽样分布等结构，使相同成本下的估计更精确。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Variance reduction does not make random numbers “more random.” It exploits dependence, known expectations, or a changed sampling law to improve precision at comparable cost while preserving the target and making any bias explicit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本图先展开四条常用路径：

- [[共同随机数]]：让两个方案共享输入，降低**差值**的噪声；
- [[对偶变量]]：把一个样本与保持同一边际分布的“反向”样本配对；
- [[控制变量]]：利用另一个已知期望修正目标输出；
- [[重要性抽样]]：改变抽样分布，再用似然比恢复目标积分。

分层抽样、条件化等也属于方差缩减方法族，只是暂不在这张图中展开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This map first expands four common routes: common random numbers target the noise of a paired difference; antithetic variables pair a draw with a distribution-preserving opposite; control variates exploit a known expectation; importance sampling changes the sampling law and restores the target with likelihood-ratio weights. Stratification, conditioning, and other variance-reduction methods remain outside this particular map rather than outside the method family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“方差更小”只有在估计同一对象、偏差口径一致且成本可比时才有意义，具体检查见 [[方差缩减比较口径]]。每种方法还拥有自己的失败条件：配对相关方向可能不利，control 均值可能写错，importance proposal 可能漏支持或产生无限方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lower variance is meaningful only for the same estimand, an explicit bias convention, and comparable cost. Each method also has its own failure mode, so the family label never replaces method-specific conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方差缩减只改善给定模型与实现下的数值效率，不会修复模型、参数、代码或离散化错误；这一共同边界由 [[路径数不修模型]] 承载。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variance reduction improves numerical efficiency conditional on a model and implementation. It does not repair misspecification, parameter error, code defects, or discretisation bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Art B. Owen, [*Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples*, Chapter 8, §8.1](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-basic.pdf)：核对方差缩减的定义、主要方法族与按方差和计算成本共同衡量效率的口径。
- Herman Kahn and Albert W. Marshall (1953), [“Methods of Reducing Sample Size in Monte Carlo Computations”](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.1.5.263)：核对相关抽样、重要性抽样与统计估计三类早期方差缩减思想。
