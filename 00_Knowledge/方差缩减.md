# Variance reduction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方差缩减*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simulation can answer the same question in more than one way. Some ways produce much less variable answers than others. **Variance reduction** redesigns the sampling or the recorded output so that repeated estimates fluctuate less while still targeting the intended quantity. The practical aim is more accuracy for the computation spent; a method's name alone does not guarantee that it succeeds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个模拟问题可以有不止一种算法；不同算法在重复运行时，答案的波动可能相差很大。**方差缩减**通过改变抽样方式或记录的输出，让估计仍针对原来的量，同时减少重复估计的波动。实际目的是让付出的计算得到更高精度；采用某个方法的名字，并不保证已经实现改善。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo estimation]] often approximates a target [[期望|expectation]] $\mu=E[f(X)]$ by averaging simulated outputs $Y=f(X)$. Here $X$ is the random input and $f$ says what to record. For a fixed number $N$ of [[独立同分布|iid]] outputs with finite variance $\sigma^2$, the mean has [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|variance $\sigma^2/N$ and standard error $\sigma/\sqrt N$]]. The [[Monte Carlo平方根律|square-root law]] makes extra digits expensive. Instead of only increasing $N$, we can look for another output with the same expectation and less variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Monte Carlo估计]]常把目标[[期望]] $\mu=E[f(X)]$ 写成模拟输出 $Y=f(X)$ 的平均：$X$ 是随机输入，$f$ 规定记录什么。固定 $N$ 个[[独立同分布|iid]]输出、且方差 $\sigma^2$ 有限时，均值的[[Monte Carlo均值标准误|方差为 $\sigma^2/N$、标准误为 $\sigma/\sqrt N$]]。[[Monte Carlo平方根律]]说明继续增加精度很贵。除了增大 $N$，还可以寻找具有相同期望、却更少波动的输出。

## A pair that cancels some noise
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让一对输出抵消一部分波动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $U$ [[连续均匀分布|uniformly distributed on $[0,1]$]], and suppose we want $E[U^2]$. The answer is $1/3$; knowing it here lets us check the simulation. A plain method evaluates $f(u)=u^2$ at independent uniform inputs. An [[对偶变量|antithetic method]] instead evaluates both $u$ and $1-u$, then records their average:
$$Z=\frac{U^2+(1-U)^2}{2}=U^2-U+\frac12.$$
Since $1-U$ has the same uniform distribution as $U$, both function evaluations have expectation $1/3$, and so does $Z$. The two values within a pair are dependent; the original target is preserved by their marginal distributions and linearity of expectation, not by within-pair independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U$ 在 $[0,1]$ 上服从[[连续均匀分布]]，要计算 $E[U^2]$。答案是 $1/3$，此处先知道答案，是为了核验模拟。普通方法在独立均匀输入处计算 $f(u)=u^2$；[[对偶变量]]方法则同时计算 $u$ 与 $1-u$，记录两项平均 $Z$。因为 $1-U$ 与 $U$ 有相同分布，两项期望都是 $1/3$，平均后仍为 $1/3$。一对内部存在依赖；保留目标靠的是相同边际分布和期望线性性，并不要求对内独立。

> [!example] Compare the same number of function evaluations · 比较相同的函数计算次数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $u=0.1$, the outputs are $0.01$ and $0.81$, giving $Z=0.41$. At $u=0.4$, they are $0.16$ and $0.36$, giving $Z=0.26$. A low output is paired with a high one.
>
> The uniform moments satisfy $E[U^k]=\int_0^1 u^k\,du=1/(k+1)$. Expanding $Z^2$ gives $E[Z^2]=7/60$, hence
> $$\operatorname{Var}(U^2)=\frac15-\frac19=\frac4{45},\qquad
> \operatorname{Var}(Z)=\frac7{60}-\frac19=\frac1{180}.$$
> With $R$ independent pairs, the paired estimate has variance $1/(180R)$. The fair plain comparison uses $2R$ independent evaluations, whose mean has variance $2/(45R)=8/(180R)$. Thus this example reduces variance eightfold at the same function-evaluation count. Its SE is smaller by $\sqrt8$, not by $8$. Actual runtime also includes the cost of generating, pairing and evaluating inputs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $u=0.1$，两输出为 $0.01,0.81$，平均为 $0.41$；当 $u=0.4$，两输出为 $0.16,0.36$，平均为 $0.26$。低值配上高值。均匀分布的幂次期望为 $1/(k+1)$；展开平方得到上面的两个方差。独立生成 $R$ 对时，配对均值方差为 $1/(180R)$；公平的普通方法使用 $2R$ 次独立函数计算，方差为 $8/(180R)$。同样函数计算次数下，本例方差缩为八分之一、标准误缩为 $1/\sqrt8$。实际运行时间还要计入生成、配对和函数计算的成本。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The trick helps here because the squared outputs move in opposite directions as $u$ increases. It is not automatic for every $f$. Pairing can leave variance unchanged or increase it; the [[配对方差缩减的协方差方向|covariance direction]] decides the effect. The independent replication unit is now a complete pair, so its uncertainty must be calculated from pair outputs rather than by pretending all $2R$ values are independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例有用，是因为随着 $u$ 增大，两项平方输出朝相反方向变化；换一个 $f$ 就不保证如此。配对也可能不改变甚至增加方差，要检查[[配对方差缩减的协方差方向|协方差方向]]。独立重复单位现在是一整对，因此应从配对输出计算不确定性，不能假装 $2R$ 个值全部独立。

## Different methods use different structure
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不同方法利用不同结构*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The method must fit the question. [[共同随机数|Common random numbers]] compare two alternatives under shared inputs: positive covariance between their outputs can reduce the variance of their difference. [[对偶变量|Antithetic variables]] instead seek negative covariance when averaging paired outputs. The desired sign changes because a difference and an average combine covariance differently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方法必须匹配问题。[[共同随机数]]让两个方案使用共享输入；若输出正相关，差值的方差可以降低。[[对偶变量]]在对配对输出求平均时则希望负相关。符号不同，是因为求差与求平均的方差公式以不同方式包含协方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[控制变量|Control variates]] use an auxiliary output whose expectation is known, subtracting a centered version to remove some fluctuation. A wrong auxiliary mean can introduce bias. [[重要性抽样|Importance sampling]] draws more often from selected regions and reweights each output to preserve the original expectation; support coverage and finite variance need separate checks. An unsuitable proposal may increase variance or make it infinite. Stratification and conditioning also belong to this family; the four linked methods are starting points, not an exhaustive definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[控制变量]]利用期望已知的辅助输出，减去其中心化后的部分来消除波动；辅助均值写错可能引入偏差。[[重要性抽样]]让某些区域更常被抽中，再用权重恢复原期望；覆盖所需支持与方差有限要分别核对，错误提议分布可能让方差增加甚至无穷。分层抽样、条件化也属于这个方法族；以上四条是阅读入口，不是穷尽定义。

> [!important] A smaller variance needs a comparison · 方差更小需要比较对象
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep the target and data-generating model fixed, state any bias, and compare complete estimators at a common computational budget or at the cost of a common accuracy. Lower per-output variance can be outweighed by higher per-output cost. If a method introduces bias, compare [[均方误差|mean squared error]] or another stated loss; concentration around the wrong value is not accuracy. [[方差缩减比较口径|Comparing variance-reduction efficiency]] works through the cost calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定目标与生成模型，说明偏差，并比较同一预算下的完整估计量，或达到同一精度的成本。单次输出方差降低，可能抵不过单次成本上升。若引入偏差，应比较[[均方误差]]或明确的其他损失；集中在错误位置不等于准确。[[方差缩减比较口径]]展开成本计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variance reduction improves a numerical estimator under its chosen model. It does not, by itself, fix wrong parameters, model assumptions, program logic or discretisation. More stable output can hide those problems; see [[Monte Carlo误差分层|the different sources of simulation error]] and [[路径数不修模型|why additional paths do not repair a model]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方差缩减改善的是选定模型内的数值估计量，本身不会修复错误参数、模型假设、程序逻辑或离散化。更稳定的输出还可能遮住这些问题，见[[Monte Carlo误差分层]]与[[路径数不修模型]]。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Art B. Owen, Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples, Chapter 8, pp.3–7, §§8.1–8.2](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-basic.pdf#page=3): redesigning the problem with the same expectation, cost-aware comparison, and dependence within antithetic pairs. The uniform-square example is derived here from exact moments.
- [Owen, Chapter 8, §§8.6 and 8.9](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-basic.pdf#page=17): shared-input differences and centered auxiliary outputs; [Chapter 9, introduction and §9.1](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-is.pdf): reweighting and its support and variance conditions.
- [Kahn and Marshall (1953), Methods of Reducing Sample Size in Monte Carlo Computations](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.1.5.263): the publisher's abstract documents the historical grouping into correlated samples, importance sampling and statistical estimation. Only that abstract is used here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Owen 第8章第3–7页：同目标的估计量重设计、成本比较与对偶配对内部依赖；均匀平方例由矩直接核算。
- 第8章第8.6、8.9节核对共同输入差值与中心化辅助输出；第9章开头及第9.1节核对加权、支持与方差条件。
- Kahn–Marshall 的出版社摘要仅用于三类早期思想的历史分类，不作为本文公式的来源。
