# 农业就业份额下降本身不能保证跨部门重配提高总劳动生产率

“离开农业”只告诉我们就业从哪里减少，没有告诉我们增加在哪里。重配对总[[劳动生产率]]的贡献，要同时看来源部门、目的部门和转移的就业份额；“非农”“城市”或“服务业”的名字不能代替这些数值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Leaving agriculture identifies where employment is shrinking, not where it is expanding. The reallocation contribution to aggregate [[劳动生产率|labour productivity]] depends on the origin, destination and change in employment shares. Labels such as non-agricultural, urban or service work cannot substitute for those quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先做一个把部门生产率固定住的核算实验。就业总人数不变，所有劳动者仍在所列部门就业；将总就业的 $\delta$ 份额从农业 $A$ 转往部门 $B$，其他部门不变。由[[劳动生产率加总]]得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an accounting experiment with fixed sectoral productivities and total employment. Everyone remains employed in the listed sectors. Move a share $\delta$ of total employment from agriculture $A$ to sector $B$, leaving other sectors unchanged. The [[劳动生产率加总|aggregation identity]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta P=\delta(p_B-p_A).
$$

设 $\delta=0.1$、$p_A=2$。目的部门生产率若为 8，总劳动生产率提高 $0.1(8-2)=0.6$；若为 1.5，则降低 $0.05$；若仍为 2，则不变。三个例子都有同样的农业就业份额下降。这里的 0.1 是总就业的 **10 个百分点**，不是农业原有就业的 10%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\delta=0.1$ and $p_A=2$. A destination productivity of 8 raises aggregate productivity by 0.6; a destination productivity of 1.5 lowers it by 0.05; a destination productivity of 2 leaves it unchanged. Agricultural employment shares fall equally in all three cases. Here 0.1 means **10 percentage points of total employment**, not 10% of initial agricultural employment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现实中，各部门生产率也会变化，应用[[劳动生产率分解]]把部门内变化和重配项放在同一个总账里。若有人从就业转为失业，上面的固定就业实验就不适用；失业不能直接塞进就业份额表，当作一个生产率为零的就业部门。更不能把这个固定生产率实验直接当作某名迁移者的收益预测，原因见[[平均生产率与迁移收益]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In observed data, sectoral productivities also change. Use the [[劳动生产率分解|productivity decomposition]] to account for within-sector changes and reallocation together. A move from employment to unemployment is outside the fixed-employment experiment; unemployment cannot simply be inserted as an employed sector with zero productivity. Nor does this experiment predict an individual migrant's gain; see [[平均生产率与迁移收益|average productivity and migration gains]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- McMillan、Rodrik、Verduzco-Gallo（2014），[Globalization, Structural Change, and Productivity Growth, with an Update on Africa](https://drodrik.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum7106/files/dani-rodrik/files/globalization_structural_change_productivity_growth_with_africa_update.pdf)，印刷第16、18–19页：固定部门生产率的反事实及重配项的符号；原文的历史区域样本不作为当下行业排序。
- 三个数例由就业份额加权恒等式构造并计算；它们只反驳“离农必然提高总劳动生产率”，不估计实际政策效果。
- IMF（2018），[WEO 第3章 Manufacturing Jobs: Implications for Productivity and Inequality，独立章节 PDF 第1、12页](https://www.imf.org/~/media/Files/Publications/WEO/2018/April/c3.ashx)：服务业子行业生产率有明显差异，不能只按制造业或服务业标签判断就业转移效果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
McMillan and coauthors support the fixed-productivity counterfactual and the sign of the reallocation term. The IMF chapter supports distinguishing service subsectors. The examples are constructed from the weighted identity; they refute an unconditional claim without estimating any policy effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
