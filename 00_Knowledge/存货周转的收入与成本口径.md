# 存货成本周转次数等于收入周转次数乘营业成本率而两种天数不能混比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cost-based inventory turnover equals revenue-based turnover times the cost-to-sales ratio; their implied days are not directly interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一期间和同一存货 $I$，收入 $S>0$、成本 $C>0$，[[存货周转率]]有 $T_S=S/I$ 与 $T_C=C/I$ 两个不同口径。设成本率 $c=C/S$，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same period and inventory $I$, with positive revenue $S$ and cost $C$, [[存货周转率|inventory turnover]] has distinct revenue and cost versions. With $c=C/S$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$T_C=T_Sc,\qquad D_C=\frac{D}{T_C}=\frac{D_S}{c}.$$

ABC的 $S=3000,C=2644,I=119$，收入次数25.2101、成本次数22.2185；一年365天下分别是14.4783天与16.4278天。差异来自毛利口径，不是库存真的变快或变慢。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For ABC, $S=3000,C=2644,I=119$ gives revenue turnover of 25.2101 and cost turnover of 22.2185. With 365 days, the implied periods are 14.4783 and 16.4278 days. The difference is due to the margin basis, not an actual change in inventory speed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成本分子匹配成本计量的存货，常用于库存管理；收入分子可用于同一收入尺度的[[资产周转分解]]。跨期或跨公司必须同口径，不能把毛利率变化误归因于库存效率变化。$I=0$时次数不可除，公式也不能用来制造有限周转天数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cost matches cost-valued inventory and is commonly used for inventory management; revenue supports [[资产周转分解|asset-turnover decomposition]] on a common sales base. Keep the basis consistent across firms and periods rather than misattributing margin changes to inventory efficiency. Turnover is undefined at zero inventory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
