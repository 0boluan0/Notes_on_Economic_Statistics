# Determinants, volume, and orientation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*行列式体积与取向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix can stretch, shear, or flatten a region. The [[行列式|determinant]] summarizes one part of that change: for a real square matrix, its absolute value measures the ordinary volume scale, and its sign records orientation. It does not describe every individual length or angle.

Start with $A=\begin{pmatrix}-2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$. The [[线性映射|linear map]] $(x,y)\mapsto(-2x,3y)$ sends the unit square to a rectangle of side lengths $2$ and $3$. The area becomes $6$. Its determinant is $-6$: the minus sign records the reversal of the ordered coordinate directions, not a negative ordinary area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵可以拉伸、剪切或压扁一个区域。[[行列式]]概括其中一部分变化：对实方阵，绝对值衡量普通体积的缩放，符号记录取向；它不描述每个方向的长度和夹角。

先看 $A=\begin{pmatrix}-2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$。[[线性映射]] $(x,y)\mapsto(-2x,3y)$ 把单位正方形变成边长为 $2$ 和 $3$ 的矩形，面积为 $6$。行列式等于 $-6$：负号记录有序坐标方向的取向翻转，不表示普通面积为负。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which solid does the matrix produce?

Let $n\ge1$ be an integer and $A=[a_1\ \cdots\ a_n]\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$. Here $a_i$ is column $i$. The unit cube $[0,1]^n$ consists of vectors whose coordinates all lie between zero and one. Its image is the parallelepiped
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*矩阵产生的是哪个实体？*

设整数 $n\ge1$，$A=[a_1\ \cdots\ a_n]\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$，其中 $a_i$ 是第 $i$ 列。单位立方体 $[0,1]^n$ 中每个坐标都在零与一之间，其像是平行多面体

$$P=A[0,1]^n=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n t_i a_i:0\le t_i\le1\right\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each coefficient is bounded: this is a filled, bounded solid, not the unrestricted linear span of the columns. In dimension one it is a segment; in dimension two a parallelogram; in dimension three a possibly skewed box. Ordinary $n$-dimensional volume, denoted $\operatorname{Vol}_n$, gives length, area, and volume in these three cases. The rule is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个系数都受到限制：这里是有界的、填满的实体，不是各列的任意线性组合构成的线性空间。一维时是线段，二维时是平行四边形，三维时是可以倾斜的平行六面体。普通 $n$ 维体积记为 $\operatorname{Vol}_n$，这三种情形分别就是长度、面积和体积。规则为

$$\operatorname{Vol}_n(P)=|\det A|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same factor applies to bounded regions $S\subset\mathbb R^n$ having ordinary volume, such as boxes, balls, and simplexes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对具有普通体积的有界区域 $S\subset\mathbb R^n$，例如盒子、球和单纯形，同样有

$$\operatorname{Vol}_n(AS)=|\det A|\operatorname{Vol}_n(S).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For precision, bounded Jordan-measurable regions suffice here: their inner and outer approximations by small boxes approach the same volume. For invertible $A$, images of disjoint small boxes remain disjoint and every box is scaled by the same factor, so the approximation gives the formula. For singular $A$, the whole image lies in a lower-dimensional linear subspace and has zero ambient $n$-volume. Translation changes position but not volume. No assertion about volumes of arbitrary sets is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格说，这里取有界 Jordan 可测区域就足够：用小盒子从内部和外部逼近时，两种体积之和趋向同一数值。$A$ 可逆时，不交的小盒子之像仍不交，而每个盒子都乘以相同体积因子，逼近便给出公式。$A$ 奇异时，整个像落在低维线性子空间中，环境 $n$ 维体积为零。平移只改变位置，不改变体积；这里不需要对任意集合的体积作断言。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the absolute determinant appears

We can establish the parallelepiped formula directly from base times height and [[行操作对行列式的影响|elementary row operations]]. First arrange the edge vectors as **rows** of a matrix. If they are dependent, the solid is flat and both its ambient volume and determinant are zero. If they are independent, row operations reduce the matrix to the identity.

Adding a multiple of one row to another moves that edge parallel to the opposite base, preserving the base and perpendicular height. Multiplying one row by a nonzero real $c$ multiplies the height, and hence volume, by $|c|$. Swapping rows merely reorders the same edges and leaves the solid unchanged. The identity gives the unit cube of volume one. The absolute determinant follows exactly these rules, so undoing the operations from the identity gives equal volume and absolute determinant for the original rows. Finally, [[行列式转置不变性|transpose invariance]] gives the same result for columns. This proof does not assume a change-of-variables formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么出现行列式的绝对值？*

用底乘高和[[行操作对行列式的影响|初等行操作]]就能建立平行多面体公式。先把边向量排成矩阵的**行**。如果它们相关，实体被压扁，环境维数的体积与行列式都为零；如果独立，就能用行操作化为单位矩阵。

把一行的倍数加到另一行上，是把对应边沿着对面的底平移，底和垂直高度都不变。一行乘非零实数 $c$，高度及体积都乘 $|c|$。交换行只是给同一组边重新排序，不改变实体。单位矩阵对应体积为一的单位立方体。行列式的绝对值恰好遵守这几条规则，因此从单位矩阵逆向撤销操作，就得到原矩阵行向量围成的体积等于行列式绝对值。最后由[[行列式转置不变性]]，结论也适用于列向量。这项证明没有预先使用换元积分公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Orientation and composition

For independent real columns, their order determines an ordered coordinate frame. Relative to the standard frame, positive determinant means the same orientation and negative determinant the opposite orientation. In two dimensions this distinguishes the two senses in which one ordered edge turns toward the other; in three dimensions it matches the right-hand convention. Exchanging two columns reverses orientation while leaving the underlying parallelepiped and its ordinary volume unchanged. When the determinant is zero, the columns do not form a full frame, so there is no full-dimensional orientation to preserve or reverse.

[[行列式乘法性|Multiplicativity]] makes composition consistent: $B$ acts first and $A$ second, so the signed factor of $AB$ is $\det A\det B$ and its ordinary factor is $|\det A||\det B|$. A shear such as $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ has determinant one; it changes the shape while preserving area. A [[单纯形|simplex]] fills a different part of the region between common-vertex edges; its factor $1/n!$ is explained in [[单纯形行列式体积|the simplex volume formula]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*取向与复合*

对线性无关的实列向量，列的次序确定一个有序坐标架。相对于标准坐标架，行列式为正表示取向相同，为负表示取向相反。二维中，它区别两个有序边之间的转动方向；三维中，它对应右手约定。交换两列会翻转取向，却不改变平行多面体本身和普通体积。行列式为零时，各列不能组成完整坐标架，也就没有满维取向可供保持或翻转。

[[行列式乘法性]]使复合的解释一致：$B$ 先作用，$A$ 后作用，所以 $AB$ 的有向因子是 $\det A\det B$，普通体积因子是 $|\det A||\det B|$。例如剪切矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ 的行列式为一，改变形状却保持面积。[[单纯形]]填出同顶点边之间的另一种区域；其 $1/n!$ 因子见[[单纯形行列式体积]]。

## Boundaries of the interpretation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary volume is never negative. Also, $|\det A|=1$ preserves volume but does not force lengths, angles, or shapes to remain fixed. The [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $\operatorname{diag}(2,1/2)$ stretches one coordinate by two and compresses the other by one half; its area factor is one. If $\det A=0$, zero ambient volume does not mean every output is zero: $\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ still maps the unit square onto a segment of length one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通体积不会为负。此外，$|\det A|=1$ 只保持体积，不保证长度、夹角或形状不变。例如[[对角矩阵]] $\operatorname{diag}(2,1/2)$ 把一个坐标放大两倍、另一个压缩为一半，面积因子仍为一。$\det A=0$ 时，环境体积为零也不表示所有输出都为零：$\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ 仍把单位正方形送到长度为一的线段。

> [!note]- Complex coordinates count twice · 每个复坐标对应两个实坐标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A complex determinant has no real positive-versus-negative orientation sign. For $A=X+iY\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$, where $X,Y$ are real and $i^2=-1$, write an input as $x+iy$. The corresponding real map on $\mathbb R^{2n}$ has matrix
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 复行列式没有实数的正负取向意义。设 $A=X+iY\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$，其中 $X,Y$ 为实矩阵、$i^2=-1$。把输入写成 $x+iy$，对应的 $\mathbb R^{2n}$ 上实映射矩阵是
> $$R=\begin{pmatrix}X&-Y\\Y&X\end{pmatrix}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Its real volume factor is $\det R=|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2$. To verify this even for singular $A$, let $I$ be the $n\times n$ [[单位矩阵|identity]] and use the invertible complex block matrix
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 它的实体积因子是 $\det R=|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2$。为验证奇异 $A$ 也成立，令 $I$ 是 $n\times n$ [[单位矩阵]]，取可逆的复分块矩阵
> $$S=\begin{pmatrix}I&iI\\I&-iI\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> SR=\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\0&\overline A\end{pmatrix}S.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The map $S$ is invertible because the two outputs $u=x+iy$ and $v=x-iy$ recover $x=(u+v)/2$, $y=(u-v)/(2i)$. Taking determinants and cancelling the nonzero $\det S$ gives $\det R=\det A\det\overline A$. The bar means entrywise complex conjugation; the determinant formula implies $\det\overline A=\overline{\det A}$, so the product is the squared modulus. Since $R$ is real, its determinant is the same real scalar whether computed over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. For example, $z\mapsto2z$ doubles both real coordinates and multiplies area by four, although its complex determinant is two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $S$ 可逆，因为由两个输出 $u=x+iy$、$v=x-iy$ 可恢复 $x=(u+v)/2$、$y=(u-v)/(2i)$。两边取行列式，约去非零的 $\det S$，得到 $\det R=\det A\det\overline A$。横线表示逐项复共轭；行列式公式给出 $\det\overline A=\overline{\det A}$，故乘积就是模的平方。$R$ 为实矩阵，无论在实数域还是复数域计算，其行列式都是同一个实数。例如 $z\mapsto2z$ 把两个实坐标都放大两倍，所以面积乘四，虽然复行列式是二。

> [!question]- Read both pieces of information · 同时读出两种信息
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What does $\operatorname{diag}(-2,3)$ do to area and orientation? Its determinant is $-6$, so ordinary area is multiplied by six and orientation is reversed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $\operatorname{diag}(-2,3)$ 怎样改变面积与取向？其行列式为 $-6$，所以普通面积放大六倍，取向翻转。
