# 行列式的绝对值是体积缩放而符号记录取向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The absolute determinant is a volume scale factor, while its sign records orientation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 几何解释
> 对实线性变换 $T(x)=Ax$，其中 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$，$A$ 把单位立方体送到由列向量 $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ 张成的平行多面体。其普通 $n$ 维体积是
> $$
> |\det A|.
> $$
> $\det A>0$ 表示保留取向，$\det A<0$ 表示翻转取向，$\det A=0$ 表示至少一个维度被压扁。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real linear map $x\mapsto Ax$, with $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$, sends the unit cube to the parallelepiped spanned by the columns of $A$. Its ordinary $n$-dimensional volume is $|\det A|$. The sign records whether orientation is preserved or reversed, while zero means that at least one dimension is flattened.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么交换两列会变号？列的顺序规定了一个有序坐标架。交换两列并不改变平行多面体本身及其普通体积，却把坐标架的取向翻转一次，所以 signed volume 变号。为什么给一列加另一列的倍数不变？几何上这是沿某个面平行剪切，底和相应高度都不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exchanging two columns leaves the geometric parallelepiped and its ordinary volume unchanged but reverses the orientation of the ordered frame, so signed volume changes sign. Adding a multiple of one column to another is a shear parallel to a face; it preserves the relevant base and height.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘法公式也因此有清楚的几何意义：$B$ 先按 $\det B$ 缩放有向体积，$A$ 再按 $\det A$ 缩放，复合变换 $AB$ 的总因子就是两者乘积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplicativity has the same geometric meaning: $B$ first scales signed volume by $\det B$, then $A$ by $\det A$, so the composite map $AB$ scales it by their product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由同一顶点出发的边向量还可以围成三角形、四面体与一般单纯形；它们相对平行多面体的体积换算见[[单纯形行列式体积]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Edge vectors from a common vertex can also form a triangle, tetrahedron, or general simplex; their volume conversion from the corresponding parallelepiped is given by the [[单纯形行列式体积|simplex determinant formula]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- “负体积”不是普通几何体积；负号只记录取向。
- $|\det A|=1$ 只说明总体积保持，不说明长度、角度或形状保持。例如 $\operatorname{diag}(2,1/2)$ 保面积却拉长一个方向、压缩另一个方向。
- $\det A=0$ 表示 $n$ 维体积为零，但图像仍可在更低维子空间里有非零长度或面积。
- 上述正负取向只针对实方阵。若把复矩阵 $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$ 看成 $\mathbb R^{2n}$ 上的实线性映射，其实 $2n$ 维体积因子是 $|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2$；不能把复 determinant 的“正负”当作取向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Ordinary geometric volume is never negative; a negative determinant records orientation only.
- $|\det A|=1$ preserves total volume, not lengths, angles, or shape. For example, $\operatorname{diag}(2,1/2)$ preserves area while stretching one direction and compressing the other.
- A zero determinant means zero full-dimensional volume, although the image may retain nonzero lower-dimensional length or area.
- The sign-and-orientation statement is for real matrices. A complex matrix $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$ viewed as a real map on $\mathbb R^{2n}$ scales real $2n$-dimensional volume by $|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2$; a complex determinant has no positive-versus-negative orientation sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $A=\operatorname{diag}(-2,3)$ 对面积和取向分别做了什么？
>
> **答案：** $\det A=-6$，所以面积放大 6 倍，同时取向翻转。
