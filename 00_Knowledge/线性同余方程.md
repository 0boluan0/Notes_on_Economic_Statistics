# Linear congruences
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*线性同余方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Which integers, when multiplied by three, leave remainder one on division by seven? Five works because $3\cdot5=15=2\cdot7+1$. So do twelve and minus two. A **linear congruence** expresses this kind of question: we seek integer inputs that meet a remainder condition, rather than an ordinary equality between integer values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哪些整数乘以三后，除以七余一？五可以，因为 $3\cdot5=15=2\cdot7+1$；十二和负二也可以。**线性同余方程**表达的就是这类问题：寻找满足余数条件的整数输入，而不是要求两个整数值普通相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Given data and an unknown integer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*已知数据与未知整数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **linear congruence** is an equation of the form $ax\equiv b\pmod n$, where integers $a,b$ and an integer modulus $n>1$ are given, and the integer $x$ is unknown. It requires $n\mid(ax-b)$: multiplying $x$ by $a$ must give the same remainder modulo $n$ as $b$. The degenerate form with $a=0$ is also allowed here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**线性同余方程**是形如 $ax\equiv b\pmod n$ 的方程，其中整数 $a,b$ 和整数模数 $n>1$ 已给定，整数 $x$ 未知。它要求 $n\mid(ax-b)$，也就是寻找乘以 $a$ 后与 $b$ 有相同模 $n$ 余数的整数；这里也允许 $a=0$ 的退化形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\equiv$ is the [[同余|congruence relation]], not the equality sign. The notation $n\mid(ax-b)$ means [[整除|divisibility]]: there is an integer $q$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\equiv$ 表示[[同余]]关系，并不是等号。记号 $n\mid(ax-b)$ 表示[[整除]]，也就是存在某个整数 $q$，使

$$ax-b=qn.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numbers $a,b,n$ are fixed for the question. The integer $q$ is a witness that a proposed $x$ works; it may change when $x$ changes. The coefficient $a$ and the target $b$ may be positive, negative or zero. The modulus is a positive integer greater than one, and the unknown must be an integer. No coprimality or existence-of-solution condition is part of this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一个问题，$a,b,n$ 都是固定的。整数 $q$ 是证明某个候选 $x$ 有效的见证，随 $x$ 改变时也可能改变。系数 $a$ 和目标 $b$ 都可以为正、为负或为零；模数是大于一的正整数，未知量则必须取整数。互素条件或“已经有解”都不是定义的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, in $3x\equiv1\pmod7$, the given data are $a=3,b=1,n=7$. Substituting $x=5$ gives $3\cdot5-1=14$, which is divisible by seven, so five is an integer solution. Twelve and five belong to the same solution class modulo seven. See [[线性同余求解|solving a linear congruence]] for solvability and the complete set of solution classes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在 $3x\equiv1\pmod7$ 中，$a=3,b=1,n=7$ 已知。代入 $x=5$ 得 $3\cdot5-1=14$，被 7 整除，因此 5 是整数解；12 与 5 属于同一个模 7 解类。判定是否有解、求出全部解类的方法见 [[线性同余求解]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The negative solution $x=-2$ gives $3(-2)-1=-7$, also a multiple of $7$. By contrast, $x=1$ fails because $3-1=2$ is not a multiple of $7$. Solving the ordinary equation $3x=1$ would give $x=1/3$, but that is outside the integer domain here; it does not answer the congruence question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负整数解 $x=-2$ 给出 $3(-2)-1=-7$，同样是 $7$ 的整数倍。相反，$x=1$ 不成立，因为 $3-1=2$ 不是 $7$ 的整数倍。普通方程 $3x=1$ 的解是 $x=1/3$，但它不属于这里的整数论域，因此没有回答同余方程的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Integer solutions and solution classes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*整数解与解类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **integer solution** is a particular integer that makes the congruence true when substituted. A **solution class modulo $n$** groups solutions differing by an integer multiple of $n$: if $x$ is a solution, then $a(x+kn)-b=(ax-b)+akn$, so $x+kn$ is also a solution for every $k\in\mathbb Z$. One solution class does not mean a single integer solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个**整数解**是代入后使同余成立的具体整数。一个**模 $n$ 解类**则把相差 $n$ 的整数倍的解视为同一类：若 $x$ 是解，则 $a(x+kn)-b=(ax-b)+akn$，所以 $x+kn$ 对任何 $k\in\mathbb Z$ 也是解。说“一个解类”不等于“只有一个整数解”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[同余|residue class modulo the fixed modulus]] collects integers differing by multiples of $n$. Each integer has exactly one standard representative in $0,1,\ldots,n-1$, supplied by [[带余除法|division with remainder]]. Substitution is constant on each class by the displayed calculation above. Thus we may list the classes using those $n$ representatives without losing any integer solutions. If even one integer solution exists, there are infinitely many integer solutions $x+kn$, since $n>0$ and different integers $k$ give different values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定模数的[[同余|同余类]]收集相差 $n$ 的整数倍的整数。由[[带余除法]]，每个整数在 $0,1,\ldots,n-1$ 中恰有一个标准代表；上面的计算证明同一个类中的整数代入后同真同假。因此可以用这 $n$ 个代表列出解类，而不遗漏任何整数解。只要有一个整数解，就有无穷多个整数解 $x+kn$，因为 $n>0$，不同整数 $k$ 会给出不同的整数值。

> [!example] Two classes of solutions · 两个解类
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $2x\equiv2\pmod6$, substituting the representatives $x=0,1,2,3,4,5$ gives remainders $0,2,4,0,2,4$. Exactly $x=1$ and $x=4$ pass. Hence there are two solution classes modulo six: all integers $1+6k$ and all integers $4+6k$, with $k\in\mathbb Z$. These classes are different because $4-1=3$ is not divisible by six.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $2x\equiv2\pmod6$，依次代入标准代表 $x=0,1,2,3,4,5$，所得余数是 $0,2,4,0,2,4$，恰有 $x=1$ 和 $x=4$ 通过。因此模六有两个解类：所有 $1+6k$ 和所有 $4+6k$，其中 $k\in\mathbb Z$。两类不同，因为 $4-1=3$ 不能被六整除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The equation can have no solutions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方程也可能无解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expression $2x\equiv1\pmod6$ is still a linear congruence, but it has no integer solution. For every integer $x$, the number $2x-1$ is odd, whereas every multiple of six is even. Failing to have a solution does not make an equation cease to fit the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$2x\equiv1\pmod6$ 仍是线性同余方程，但没有整数解。对每个整数 $x$，$2x-1$ 都是奇数，而六的每个整数倍都是偶数。一个方程没有解，并不意味着它不再符合定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $a=0$, the condition is $n\mid(-b)$, equivalently $n\mid b$, and does not depend on $x$. If it holds, every integer is a solution and all $n$ residue classes occur; otherwise there are none. For example, $0x\equiv0\pmod5$ admits all integers, while $0x\equiv1\pmod5$ admits none. This is why the allowed degenerate coefficient must be stated explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a=0$，条件变成 $n\mid(-b)$，等价于 $n\mid b$，与 $x$ 无关。如果成立，每个整数都是解，全部 $n$ 个同余类都是解类；否则无解。例如 $0x\equiv0\pmod5$ 允许所有整数，而 $0x\equiv1\pmod5$ 无解。这也说明为什么需要明确允许零系数的退化情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition tells us what counts as an equation and as a solution. A general criterion for existence and a method for obtaining every solution class belong to [[线性同余求解|solving linear congruences]]. Such a criterion is a theorem about the equations just defined, not an extra assumption needed to call them linear congruences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义负责说明什么是这种方程、什么算一个解；普遍的存在判据及求全部解类的方法由[[线性同余求解]]负责。这样的判据是关于已定义方程的定理，不是让它们成为线性同余方程的额外假设。
