# The unconstrained first-order condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无约束一阶条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an interior local minimum, no sufficiently small allowed move can lower the function. If the function has a linear approximation there, that linear part must vanish: any nonzero linear slope would decrease the function in one direction and increase it in the opposite direction. The **first-order necessary condition** turns this observation into equations for candidate points. Solving those equations does not yet prove optimality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在内部局部最小点，任何足够小的合法移动都不能降低函数值。如果函数在那里有线性近似，其线性部分就必须为零：非零的线性斜率会沿一个方向使函数下降、沿相反方向使它上升。**一阶必要条件**把这个观察变成寻找候选点的方程，但解出这些方程还没有证明最优。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $f(x,y)=(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2$ has [[偏导数|partial derivatives]] $2(x-1)$ and $2(y+2)$, the rates obtained by varying one input at a time. Setting them to zero gives $(1,-2)$. In this particular problem the sum of two squares proves that this point is a global minimum. The derivative equations locate the candidate; the nonnegative sum establishes its optimality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x,y)=(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2$ 的[[偏导数]]分别是 $2(x-1)$、$2(y+2)$，即每次只改变一个输入所得的变化率。令它们等于零，得到 $(1,-2)$。在这个具体问题中，两个平方之和证明该点是全局最小：导数方程负责找候选，非负平方和负责证明最优。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$, with $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ [[开集|open]], $n\ge1$ a finite integer, and $a\in U$. Openness gives a whole small ball of allowed moves around $a$. A [[局部极值与绝对极值|local minimum]] means $f(a+h)\ge f(a)$ for all sufficiently small $h$; a local maximum reverses the inequality. These comparisons need not be strict. Assume that $f$ is [[全微分|differentiable at $a$]], so one linear map $Df(a)$ approximates all small changes. Its [[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|gradient]] $\nabla f(a)$ is the column of first partial derivatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:U\to\mathbb R$，$U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 为[[开集]]，$n\ge1$ 是有限整数，且 $a\in U$。开放性保证 $a$ 周围整个小球内都允许移动。[[局部极值与绝对极值|局部最小]]表示所有足够小的 $h$ 都满足 $f(a+h)\ge f(a)$，局部最大把不等号反向；这里不要求严格比较。假设 $f$ 在 $a$ 处[[全微分|可微]]，即存在同一个线性映射 $Df(a)$ 近似所有微小变化。它的[[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|梯度]] $\nabla f(a)$ 是一阶偏导组成的列向量。

> [!important] Necessary at an interior extremum · 内部极值的必要条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $a$ is a local minimum or maximum under these assumptions, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述假设下，若 $a$ 是局部最小点或局部最大点，则
> $$Df(a)=0,\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad\nabla f(a)=0.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus $a$ is a [[驻点|stationary point]]. Differentiability at this one point suffices; the theorem does not require continuous derivatives nearby, second derivatives, or strictness of the extremum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因而 $a$ 是[[驻点]]。只需在这一点可微，不要求附近导数连续，不要求二阶导数，也不要求极值严格。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the gradient must vanish
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么梯度必须为零

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a nonzero vector $v\in\mathbb R^n$ and follow the straight line $a+tv$, where the real number $t$ can be positive or negative. Since $a$ is interior, this line stays in $U$ for all sufficiently small $|t|$. Define the one-variable function $\phi(t)=f(a+tv)$. Differentiability gives its finite two-sided derivative through the [[方向导数梯度公式|directional derivative formula]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个非零向量 $v\in\mathbb R^n$，沿直线 $a+tv$ 移动，其中实数 $t$ 可正可负。因为 $a$ 是内点，当 $|t|$ 足够小时直线仍留在 $U$ 内。令一元函数 $\phi(t)=f(a+tv)$，可微性通过[[方向导数梯度公式]]给出其有限双侧导数：

$$\phi'(0)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}t=Df(a)[v]=\nabla f(a)^Tv.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so the last product is the sum of each partial derivative times its corresponding component of $v$. No unit-length requirement is needed: $t$ simply parametrizes this fixed line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，最后的乘积就是每个偏导乘以 $v$ 的对应分量后求和。这里不需要 $v$ 为单位长度，$t$ 只是给这条固定直线作参数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $a$ is a local minimum, then $\phi(t)-\phi(0)\ge0$ for small $t$. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a$ 是局部最小点，足够小的 $t$ 满足 $\phi(t)-\phi(0)\ge0$。于是

$$\frac{\phi(t)-\phi(0)}t\ge0\quad(t>0),\qquad \frac{\phi(t)-\phi(0)}t\le0\quad(t<0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both one-sided limits equal the same number $\phi'(0)$. That number is therefore at least zero and at most zero, hence zero. A local maximum reverses both inequalities and has the same conclusion. Thus $Df(a)[v]=0$ for every nonzero $v$; a linear map also sends $v=0$ to zero. It follows that $Df(a)$ is the zero map. Taking $v$ to be each coordinate unit vector shows directly that every component of the gradient is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两侧极限都等于同一个数 $\phi'(0)$，因此这个数既不小于零又不大于零，只能为零。局部最大把两边的不等式同时反向，结论相同。故每个非零 $v$ 都满足 $Df(a)[v]=0$，而线性映射本来就把 $v=0$ 映到零，所以 $Df(a)$ 是零映射。依次取各坐标单位向量，也可直接看出梯度的每个分量都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three boundaries of the condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个不能遗漏的边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**An optimum on a restricted boundary can have a nonzero derivative.** Take the smooth function $f(x)=x$ on $\mathbb R$, but maximize it only for $x\in[0,1]$. The maximizing input is $1$, and the derivative of the ambient function there is $f'(1)=1$. The missing move to $x>1$ is exactly why the two-sided proof cannot be applied to this constrained problem. Likewise, $f(x,y)=y$ is constant on the feasible line $y=0$ even though its ambient gradient is $(0,1)^T$. Interiority relative to a line does not give all directions in $\mathbb R^2$. Feasible directions must be checked; [[KKT条件|KKT conditions]] offer a separate framework under their own hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**受限边界上的最优点可以有非零导数。** 取定义在 $\mathbb R$ 上的光滑函数 $f(x)=x$，但只允许 $x\in[0,1]$，最大点是 $1$，原函数在那里的导数却为 $f'(1)=1$。恰恰因为不能再向 $x>1$ 移动，上面的双侧证明不能用于这个约束问题。同样，$f(x,y)=y$ 在可行直线 $y=0$ 上恒定，原函数的梯度却是 $(0,1)^T$。相对于直线是内点，并不意味着允许 $\mathbb R^2$ 中的全部方向。此时要检查可行方向；[[KKT条件]]在其自身假设下提供另一套框架。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**A nondifferentiable point can be optimal.** The function $|x|$ has a strict global minimum at zero because $|x|>0$ when $x\ne0$. Its right derivative is $1$ and its left derivative is $-1$, so the finite two-sided derivative is absent. A one-variable [[临界点|critical-point search]] retains such points as well as stationary ones. In several variables, even having all partial derivatives is not by itself enough to establish the [[偏导不可推可微|differentiability assumption]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**不可微点可以是最优点。** $|x|$ 在零点有严格全局最小，因为 $x\ne0$ 时 $|x|>0$。右导数为 $1$、左导数为 $-1$，因此有限双侧导数不存在。一元[[临界点]]搜索除了驻点，也保留这类位置。多元时，即使全部偏导存在，也不足以单独证明这里的[[偏导不可推可微|可微假设]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Zero gradient is not sufficient.** For $f(x,y)=x^2-y^2$, $\nabla f(0,0)=0$. Yet $f(t,0)=t^2>0$ and $f(0,t)=-t^2<0$ whenever $t\ne0$, so arbitrarily close values lie above and below $f(0,0)=0$: this is a saddle. In one variable, $x^3$ has derivative zero at zero and likewise no local extremum. Classification can use direct comparisons or, when their assumptions hold, the [[Hessian 局部极小判据|Hessian minimum]] and [[Hessian 局部极大判据|maximum]] criteria. Their extra conditions perform a different job from first-order necessity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**零梯度并不充分。** 对 $f(x,y)=x^2-y^2$，有 $\nabla f(0,0)=0$；但任意 $t\ne0$ 都给出 $f(t,0)=t^2>0$、$f(0,t)=-t^2<0$。因此任意近处都有高于和低于 $f(0,0)=0$ 的值，这是鞍点。一元 $x^3$ 在零点导数为零，同样没有局部极值。后续分类可以直接比较函数值，也可以在满足各自假设时使用 [[Hessian 局部极小判据]]与[[Hessian 局部极大判据]]；这些额外条件与一阶必要性承担不同任务。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition also does not ensure that an optimum exists. For example, $f(x)=x$ on $\mathbb R$ has neither maximum nor minimum. For a continuous function on a nonempty compact set, the [[极值定理|extreme value theorem]] guarantees attainment, but the attaining points can be on the boundary. After locating candidates, one still needs to compare values or establish an appropriate sufficient condition; [[凸优化全局性|convexity or concavity on a suitable domain]] can sometimes supply a global argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个条件也不保证最优点存在。例如 $\mathbb R$ 上的 $f(x)=x$ 既没有最大也没有最小。非空紧集上的连续函数可由[[极值定理]]保证取得极值，但取得的位置可能在边界。找到候选之后，仍需比较函数值或证明适当的充分条件；[[凸优化全局性|适当定义域上的凸性或凹性]]有时能提供全局论证。

> [!question]- What remains after solving the equations? · 解完方程后还剩什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Does solving $\nabla f=0$ finish an optimization problem? No: first check whether an optimum is attained and whether the domain has boundary or nondifferentiable candidates. Then classify the stationary points and compare the relevant values. A necessary condition filters candidates; it does not certify them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 解出 $\nabla f=0$ 是否已经完成优化？没有：先检查极值是否取得、定义域是否还有边界或不可微候选，再分类驻点并比较相关函数值。必要条件只筛选候选，不能单独证明最优。
