# 可微函数在开放域内的局部极值点必有零梯度，但边界点与不可微点不受此条件约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A differentiable function has zero gradient at an interior local extremum, but the condition does not cover boundary or nondifferentiable points*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 一阶条件只筛出候选点
> 设 $D\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 是开集，$f:D\to\mathbb R$ 在 $x^*\in D$ 可微。若 $x^*$ 是局部最大点或局部最小点，则
> $$\nabla f(x^*)=0.$$
> 反过来不成立：零梯度可能对应最大点、最小点、鞍点，甚至不是极值点。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $D\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be open and let $f:D\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable at $x^*\in D$. If $x^*$ is a local maximum or minimum, then $\nabla f(x^*)=0$. The converse is false: a stationary point may be a maximum, a minimum, a saddle, or no extremum at all.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么梯度必须为零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the gradient must vanish*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开放域内点允许沿任意方向 $d$ 做足够小的正、负移动。若 $x^*$ 是局部最大点，一元函数 $\phi(t)=f(x^*+td)$ 在 $t=0$ 也局部最大，所以
$$
\phi'(0)=\nabla f(x^*)^Td=0.
$$
这个等式对每个方向 $d$ 成立，只能有 $\nabla f(x^*)=0$。局部最小点的论证相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an interior point of an open domain, one can move a sufficiently small distance in either sign along every direction $d$. If $x^*$ is a local maximum, then $\phi(t)=f(x^*+td)$ has a local maximum at $t=0$, so $\phi'(0)=\nabla f(x^*)^Td=0$. Since this holds for every $d$, the gradient must be zero. The argument for a local minimum is identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个不能遗漏的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three boundaries that must not be omitted*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一，定义域边界上的最优点不必有零梯度。最大化 $f(x)=x$、约束 $x\in[0,1]$ 时，最优点是 $x=1$，但 $f'(1)=1$。此时可行方向不是双向的，应改用边界条件或 [[KKT条件]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, an optimum on the boundary need not have zero gradient. Maximising $f(x)=x$ over $[0,1]$ gives the optimum $x=1$ even though $f'(1)=1$. Feasible directions are no longer two-sided, so boundary conditions or the [[KKT条件|KKT conditions]] are needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二，不可微点可能是极值点。$f(x)=|x|$ 在零点取得全局最小，但导数不存在。第三，零梯度只是必要条件：$f(x,y)=x^2-y^2$ 在原点梯度为零，却沿 $x$ 方向上升、沿 $y$ 方向下降，是鞍点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Second, a nondifferentiable point can be optimal: $|x|$ has a global minimum at zero although its derivative does not exist there. Third, a zero gradient is only necessary: $f(x,y)=x^2-y^2$ has zero gradient at the origin but rises along the $x$ direction and falls along the $y$ direction, so the point is a saddle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一阶条件也不负责保证最优点存在。存在性要从定义域与连续性入手；例如非空紧集上的连续函数可由 [[极值定理]] 保证取得最大值和最小值。找到驻点后，再用曲率、凸性或直接比较判断其性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-order condition does not establish existence either. Existence comes from the domain and continuity—for example, the [[极值定理|extreme-value theorem]] guarantees attainment for a continuous function on a nonempty compact set. After finding stationary points, use curvature, convexity, or direct value comparison to classify them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“解出 $\nabla f=0$”还不能宣布优化结束？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does solving $\nabla f=0$ not finish an optimisation problem?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 它会漏掉边界点与不可微点，也不能区分驻点是最大、最小还是鞍点；还必须检查存在性、可行域和充分条件。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The equation misses boundary and nondifferentiable candidates and does not distinguish maxima, minima, and saddles. Existence, feasibility, and sufficient conditions still have to be checked.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
