# Centering matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中心化矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Centering a data column** means subtracting that column's average from every entry. It changes the reference point: a positive result now means above the observed average, and a negative result means below it. A **centering matrix** performs this subtraction for every column at once, using the same fixed matrix for all datasets with the same number of rows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**一列数据的中心化**，就是从该列每个元素中减去这一列的平均值。它改变参照点：正数表示高于已观察到的平均值，负数表示低于平均值。**中心化矩阵**用一次矩阵乘法对所有列完成这一步；只要行数相同，使用的矩阵就相同。

> [!example] Subtract a separate mean from each variable · 每个变量减去自己的均值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider three observations of study hours and test scores. The [[多元数据矩阵|data matrix]] and its [[样本均值向量|sample mean vector]] are
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑三份学习时长与测验分数的观测，其[[多元数据矩阵]]与[[样本均值向量]]为
>
> $$X=\begin{bmatrix}2&60\\4&70\\6&80\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> \bar x=\begin{bmatrix}4\\70\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Subtract $4$ from the first column and $70$ from the second:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一列减去 $4$，第二列减去 $70$：
>
> $$X_c=\begin{bmatrix}-2&-10\\0&0\\2&10\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each column sums to zero. The first column still measures hours and the second still measures points; their units have not been combined or rescaled.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每列的元素和都为零。第一列仍以小时计，第二列仍以分数计，并未混合或缩放单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A fixed operation on the observation axis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在观测方向上进行的固定运算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ and $p\ge1$ be integers, with observations stored as the rows of a complete real matrix $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$. Let $I_n$ be the $n\times n$ identity matrix, which leaves vectors unchanged, and let $\mathbf1_n$ be the column of $n$ ones. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令整数 $n\ge1$、$p\ge1$，完整实数矩阵 $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$ 以行存放观测。令 $I_n$ 为不改变向量的 $n\times n$ 单位矩阵，$\mathbf1_n$ 为含 $n$ 个一的列向量，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]。定义

$$\boxed{H=I_n-\frac1n\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T},\qquad X_c=HX.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every entry of $\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$ is $1/n$. Its product with a column $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)^T$ is the constant column $\bar v\mathbf1_n$, where $\bar v=n^{-1}\sum_i v_i$. Therefore $Hv=v-\bar v\mathbf1_n$. Applied to every column of $X$, and writing $\bar x_j=n^{-1}\sum_i x_{ij}$ for its column means, this gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$ 的每个元素都是 $1/n$；它乘以列向量 $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)^T$，得到各项均为平均值的常数列 $\bar v\mathbf1_n$，其中 $\bar v=n^{-1}\sum_i v_i$。所以 $Hv=v-\bar v\mathbf1_n$。把它用于 $X$ 的每一列，并记各列均值为 $\bar x_j=n^{-1}\sum_i x_{ij}$，就得到

$$HX=X-\mathbf1_n\bar x^T,\qquad
(HX)_{ij}=x_{ij}-\bar x_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dimensions explain the side of multiplication: $H$ is $n\times n$, so left multiplication combines the $n$ observations within each variable. It does not subtract a mean across the $p$ different variables within a row. In the three-row example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尺寸说明了为什么要左乘：$H$ 是 $n\times n$，所以它在每个变量内部组合 $n$ 份观测；并不是对一行中的 $p$ 个不同变量求平均再相减。三行例子中，

$$H=\frac13\begin{bmatrix}2&-1&-1\\-1&2&-1\\-1&-1&2\end{bmatrix},$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
which produces exactly the $3\times2$ centered table above. For fixed $n$, $H$ depends only on the number of observations, not on their realized values, even though the column means themselves depend on those values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它恰好得到上面的 $3\times2$ 中心化表。固定 $n$ 后，$H$ 只取决于观测数，不取决于本次记录的数值，尽管列均值本身取决于这些数值。

## Why centering twice changes nothing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么中心化两次不再改变结果*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the first subtraction, a column has mean zero. Subtracting its mean again therefore leaves it unchanged. Algebraically, $H$ is symmetric and idempotent:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一次相减后，一列的均值已为零，再减一次均值不会改变它。代数上，$H$ 对称且幂等：

$$H^T=H,\qquad H^2=H.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check the second identity, write $J=\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$. Since $\mathbf1_n^T\mathbf1_n=n$, multiplication gives $J^2=J$. Hence $(I_n-J)^2=I_n-2J+J^2=I_n-J=H$. Symmetry follows by transposing $I_n$ and $\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

验证第二式时，记 $J=\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$。由于 $\mathbf1_n^T\mathbf1_n=n$，相乘得到 $J^2=J$，因此 $(I_n-J)^2=I_n-2J+J^2=I_n-J=H$。对称性则由 $I_n$ 和 $\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T$ 转置后不变得到。

> [!important] The output space consists of sum-zero columns · 输出空间是元素和为零的列
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[列空间|range]] of $H$, meaning the vectors it can output, is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $H$ 的[[列空间|像空间]]，即它能输出的向量集合，为
>
> $$\operatorname{range}(H)=\{v\in\mathbb R^n:\mathbf1_n^Tv=0\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every output has sum zero, and any sum-zero vector is unchanged by $H$, so every such vector is an output. The [[零空间|kernel]] consists exactly of constant columns $a\mathbf1_n$, which are removed entirely. The sum-zero condition leaves $n-1$ free coordinates: the last must be minus the sum of the others. Thus
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所有输出的元素和都为零，而任意元素和为零的向量又会被 $H$ 保持不变，因此这些向量全部都是可能输出。[[零空间]]恰好由常数列 $a\mathbf1_n$ 组成，它们会被完全去掉。和为零这一约束留下 $n-1$ 个自由坐标，最后一个必须等于前面各项之和的相反数，所以
>
> $$\operatorname{rank}(H)=n-1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[矩阵秩|rank]] counts independent output directions. Geometrically, $v=\bar v\mathbf1_n+Hv$ splits a column into its constant component and a perpendicular sum-zero component. Thus $H$ is the [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] onto the sum-zero subspace; the generic [[正交投影矩阵判别|symmetric-idempotent criterion]] gives the same conclusion. This is geometry in $\mathbb R^n$, the space of values across observations, rather than in the $p$-dimensional space of variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[矩阵秩|秩]]计算独立输出方向。几何上，$v=\bar v\mathbf1_n+Hv$ 把一列分成常数分量和与其垂直的零和分量。因此，$H$ 是到零和子空间的[[正交投影]]；一般的[[正交投影矩阵判别|对称幂等判别]]也给出相同结论。这是在“跨观测取值”的 $\mathbb R^n$ 空间中进行的几何操作，而不是在 $p$ 维变量空间中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n=1$, $H=[0]$: subtracting a single observation's own mean produces zero, and the rank is $0=n-1$. For $n=0$, the displayed formula is undefined. A valid centering operation at $n=1$ does not make the usual [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance]] with denominator $n-1$ defined there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$n=1$ 时，$H=[0]$：唯一观测减去自己的均值后为零，秩为 $0=n-1$。$n=0$ 时，这个公式无定义。$n=1$ 可以中心化，并不意味着分母为 $n-1$ 的通常[[样本协方差矩阵]]此时也有定义。

## What centering does not provide
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中心化本身没有完成什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Centering changes location, not scale. Z-score standardization additionally divides by a suitable positive [[标准差|standard deviation]], a measure of spread; a constant column has zero standard deviation and cannot be standardized by that division. In the example, the second centered column is still five times the first, so the observed linear association remains. Centering imposes no independence assumption on a statistical model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中心化改变位置，不改变尺度；z分数标准化还要除以适当的正[[标准差]]，它描述分散程度。常数列的标准差为零，不能这样相除。例子中，第二个中心化列仍是第一个的五倍，可见数据中的线性关联依然存在。中心化不会给统计模型增加独立假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nor does centering manufacture independent observations. With two scalar random observations $Z_1,Z_2$, the centered residuals are $(Z_1-Z_2)/2$ and its negative. Even if $Z_1,Z_2$ were independent fair signs taking $-1$ and $1$, the residuals would be nonconstant and tied together by that exact relation. Algebraic centering therefore supplies no replacement for a [[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|sampling model]] or its dependence assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中心化也不会制造独立观测。两个标量随机观测 $Z_1,Z_2$ 中心化后，残差分别为 $(Z_1-Z_2)/2$ 及其相反数。即使 $Z_1,Z_2$ 原来是独立、等概率取 $-1$ 与 $1$ 的随机符号，残差也会非常数，并受这一精确关系约束。因此，代数中心化不能替代[[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|抽样模型]]及其依赖假设。
