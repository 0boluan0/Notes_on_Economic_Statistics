# Surface area of revolution and repeated coverage
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*旋转曲面面积与重复覆盖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rotate a vertical line segment $(R,t)$, $0\le t\le H$, around the $y$-axis, where $R,H>0$. It sweeps out the **side** of a cylinder. A narrow strip has circumference $2\pi R$ and small height $dt$, so adding the strips gives $2\pi RH$. This measures a surface, in square units. It does not include the two end disks, and it is different from the volume $\pi R^2H$ enclosed by the cylinder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把竖直线段 $(R,t)$、$0\le t\le H$ 绕 $y$ 轴旋转，其中 $R,H>0$，就扫出圆柱的**侧面**。一个窄带的周长为 $2\pi R$，小高度为 $dt$，累加得到 $2\pi RH$。这里测量的是表面，单位是长度的平方；不包含两个端面圆盘，也不同于圆柱内部的体积 $\pi R^2H$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a curved profile, the strip's width follows the slant of the curve. The relevant width is the [[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|arc-length element]] $ds$, not merely its vertical or horizontal projection. We must also check whether different parts of the profile generate the same surface patch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对弯曲的母线，窄带宽度要沿着曲线的倾斜方向测量，使用的是[[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|弧长微元]] $ds$，而不是仅取竖直或水平投影。还要检查母线的不同部分是否生成了同一块表面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Circumference times slant length
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先辨认半径，再用圆周长乘弧长微元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the [[参数曲线|profile curve]] be $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$ on a finite interval $[a,b]$ with $a<b$. Assume it is continuous and piecewise $C^1$: finitely many pieces have continuous coordinate derivatives extending one-sidedly to their endpoints. Put $v(t)=\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2}$. This is the speed, so $ds=v(t)\,dt$ for increasing $t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设[[参数曲线|母线]]为有限区间 $[a,b]$ 上的 $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$，其中 $a<b$。假设曲线连续、分段 $C^1$：有限个分段内坐标导数连续，并可向各段端点单侧连续延伸。令 $v(t)=\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2}$，它是速率，所以参数递增时 $ds=v(t)\,dt$。

> [!important] The axis determines the radius · 旋转轴决定半径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Around the $y$-axis, the radius is the perpendicular distance $|x(t)|$; around the $x$-axis it is $|y(t)|$. The parameter-area formulas are
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 绕 $y$ 轴时，半径是垂直距离 $|x(t)|$；绕 $x$ 轴时则为 $|y(t)|$。参数面积公式为
>
> $$A_y=2\pi\int_a^b|x(t)|v(t)\,dt,\qquad
> A_x=2\pi\int_a^b|y(t)|v(t)\,dt.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These integrals count surface coverage with multiplicity. They give the geometric area of a piecewise smooth generated surface when its positive-area patches are covered once. Boundary circles, a closing seam, or isolated axis points may be shared without adding a positive area. Repeating a whole surface patch adds its area again.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这些积分按表面覆盖次数计数。若生成的是分片光滑曲面，且有正面积的各块只被覆盖一次，结果就是几何表面积。边界圆、闭合接缝或孤立轴上点可以被共享，却不增加正面积；重复扫过整块表面，才会再次计入该块面积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The absolute value makes the radius nonnegative; the square root makes the slant length nonnegative. Neither factor keeps an orientation sign. For rotation about $y=k$ rather than the $x$-axis, use $|y(t)-k|$ as the radius; about $x=k$, use $|x(t)-k|$. The coordinate scales must represent the same physical length units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绝对值使半径非负，平方根使弧长非负；两者都不保留方向符号。若旋转轴不是 $x$ 轴而是 $y=k$，半径应改为 $|y(t)-k|$；绕 $x=k$ 则用 $|x(t)-k|$。各坐标尺度应表示同一种物理长度单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the area element uses the full speed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么面积微元需要完整速率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a straight profile segment on one side of the axis, rotation gives a conical frustum, a cylinder, or a planar annulus. Its lateral area is $2\pi$ times the average radius times the segment's slant length. Subdividing a curved profile motivates summing circumference times short arc length, giving $dA=2\pi r\,ds$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对位于轴同一侧的一小段直母线，旋转得到圆台侧面、圆柱侧面或平面圆环；其面积等于 $2\pi$ 乘平均半径，再乘该线段的斜长。把曲线分细，就得到圆周长乘短弧长的累加动机，即 $dA=2\pi r\,ds$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The local area factor can also be checked directly. Around the $y$-axis, use two parameters: profile position $t$ and angle $\theta$ in radians. The surface position is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还可以直接检查局部面积因子。绕 $y$ 轴旋转时，用母线位置 $t$ 与以弧度计的角度 $\theta$ 两个参数描述表面位置：

$$F(t,\theta)=(x(t)\cos\theta, y(t), x(t)\sin\theta),\qquad0\le\theta\le2\pi.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing $t$ while fixing $\theta$ gives the tangent vector $F_t=(x'\cos\theta,y',x'\sin\theta)$, of length $v(t)$. Changing $\theta$ gives $F_\theta=(-x\sin\theta,0,x\cos\theta)$, of length $|x(t)|$. Where both tangent vectors are nonzero, their [[向量点积|dot product]] is zero, so these two infinitesimal directions are perpendicular. The tangent rectangle therefore has area factor $|x(t)|v(t)$; integrating its angular width from $0$ to $2\pi$ gives the boxed formula. This is the usual tangent-area derivation on regular patches; where either factor vanishes, the parameter area density is zero. The examples below include axis endpoints and a zero-speed point explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $\theta$ 改变 $t$，切向量为 $F_t=(x'\cos\theta,y',x'\sin\theta)$，长度为 $v(t)$；固定 $t$ 改变角度，切向量为 $F_\theta=(-x\sin\theta,0,x\cos\theta)$，长度为 $|x(t)|$。当两个切向量都非零时，它们的[[向量点积]]为零，所以这两个微小变化方向垂直，切平面小矩形的面积因子就是 $|x(t)|v(t)$。再把角宽从 $0$ 积到 $2\pi$，便得到框中公式。这是在正则曲面片上的通常切面积推导；若任一因子为零，参数面积密度就是零。下面的例子会具体包含轴上端点与零速度点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sphere needs one semicircle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个球面只需一段半圆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $x(t)=R\sin t$, $y(t)=R\cos t$, $R>0$, for $0\le t\le\pi$, and rotate around the $y$-axis. This is the right semicircle from the north pole to the south pole. Its speed is $R$ and its radius is $R\sin t$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $x(t)=R\sin t$、$y(t)=R\cos t$，其中 $R>0$、$0\le t\le\pi$，绕 $y$ 轴旋转。这是从北极到南极的右半圆。速率为 $R$，旋转半径为 $R\sin t$，因此

$$A=2\pi R^2\int_0^\pi\sin t\,dt=4\pi R^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the whole circle, $0\le t\le2\pi$, instead gives $2\pi R^2\int_0^{2\pi}|\sin t|\,dt=8\pi R^2$. The planar circle itself was traversed once, but its left and right halves generate the **same sphere**. Thus surface repetition can occur even without retracing a planar arc.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改用整个圆，即 $0\le t\le2\pi$，公式得到 $2\pi R^2\int_0^{2\pi}|\sin t|\,dt=8\pi R^2$。平面中的圆虽然只描了一次，但左右两半生成了**同一个球面**。因此，即使没有重复描画平面弧段，也可能重复覆盖旋转曲面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course ellipse $x=2\sin t$, $y=\cos t$ behaves the same way. Its right half, $0\le t\le\pi$, generates the ellipsoid once. Here $v=\sqrt{4\cos^2t+\sin^2t}$, so [[定积分换元|substituting]] $u=\cos t$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程椭圆 $x=2\sin t$、$y=\cos t$ 也是如此。只取右半边 $0\le t\le\pi$，便生成一次椭球面。此时 $v=\sqrt{4\cos^2t+\sin^2t}$，[[定积分换元|换元]] $u=\cos t$ 得到

$$A=4\pi\int_0^\pi\sin t\sqrt{1+3\cos^2t}\,dt
=4\pi\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1+3u^2}\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reversed $u$ endpoints and $du=-\sin t\,dt$ compensate each other. Adding the left half would double the same geometric area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$u$ 的端点反向与 $du=-\sin t\,dt$ 中的负号相互补偿。再加左半边，会把同一几何面积算两次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check coverage, not just the sign of a coordinate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检查覆盖，而不只看坐标正负*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Around the $y$-axis, two profile points generate the same circle exactly when they have the same height $y$ and the same radius $|x|$. Repeated circles over a whole surface patch cause overcounting. Crossing the axis alone does not: $\gamma(t)=(t,t)$ on $[-1,1]$ generates two cone sides meeting only at their tip. The negative and positive halves have different heights. Its area is $2\pi\sqrt2\int_{-1}^1|t|\,dt=2\pi\sqrt2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绕 $y$ 轴时，两个母线点恰在高度 $y$ 相同、半径 $|x|$ 相同时生成同一个圆。若一整块曲面上的圆被重复扫过，就会多算。仅仅跨轴不够：$[-1,1]$ 上的 $\gamma(t)=(t,t)$ 生成两个只在顶点相接的圆锥侧面；负半段和正半段的高度不同。面积为 $2\pi\sqrt2\int_{-1}^1|t|\,dt=2\pi\sqrt2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero speed need not invalidate area either. The profile $(R,t^3)$, $-1\le t\le1$, $R>0$, generates a cylinder side of height $2$. Although its speed vanishes at $0$, the integral is $2\pi R\int_{-1}^1 3t^2\,dt=4\pi R$. A profile portion lying on the rotation axis has radius zero and contributes no surface area. A one-to-one monotone smooth change of parameter preserves the integral by the same chain-rule and substitution argument as for arc length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零速度也未必使面积公式失效。母线 $(R,t^3)$、$-1\le t\le1$、$R>0$ 生成高为 $2$ 的圆柱侧面。虽然 $t=0$ 处速率为零，积分仍为 $2\pi R\int_{-1}^1 3t^2\,dt=4\pi R$。若一段母线位于旋转轴上，其半径为零，不贡献表面积。一一、单调、光滑的参数改变也保持该积分不变，理由与弧长相同：链式法则加换元。

> [!warning] Count each area-bearing patch once · 每块有面积的表面只算一次
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When the goal is the area of the geometric union, choose profile pieces that generate it once, apart from shared sets of zero area, or explicitly remove repeated patches. Dividing by $2$ is justified only if the surface really is covered twice almost everywhere with respect to surface area; coverage need not be uniform. The analogous [[旋转体重复覆盖|overlap problem for volumes]] concerns three-dimensional volume elements, not the surface element used here.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若目标是几何并集的面积，应选出只生成一次表面的母线片段（允许共享零面积集合），或明确扣除重复曲面片。只有整个表面除零面积部分外确实都覆盖两次，才能直接除以 $2$；各处覆盖次数未必相同。类似的[[旋转体重复覆盖]]问题处理的是三维体积微元，不是这里的曲面面积微元。
