# 扩大经营规模只有在新增投资创造正净现值时才增加企业价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding operations adds firm value only when the additional investment has positive net present value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

销量、资产或利润变大，只说明规模变化；股东为新增经营投入资源后是否更富，要按匹配风险的资本机会成本计算[[净现值决策|新增净现值]]。好行业也可能因争夺市场、过度投资或支付过高收购价而消耗价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Higher sales, assets, or earnings describe scale. Whether investors become wealthier after committing additional resources depends on [[净现值决策|incremental NPV]] at a risk-matched opportunity cost. Even an attractive industry can destroy value through market-share competition, overinvestment, or overpriced acquisitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个明确的永久项目看区别：今天投入 100，从下一年末起，每年净收 8；所有维持性支出已扣，现金流永续不变，必要回报率 10%。其新增价值是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a specified perpetual project: invest 100 today and receive net cash of 8 at each year-end starting next year. Maintenance spending is already deducted, cash flows remain constant, and the required return is 10%. Its value added is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$NPV=-100+\frac{8}{0.10}=-20.$$

现金利润增加了，价值却少了 20。若相同条件下每年净收 12，NPV 则为 20。常说的“新增 ROIC 要高于资本成本”依赖回报与投入的正确测量；项目寿命有限、回报路径变化时，完整现金流 NPV 更可靠，不能只比较某一年的会计比率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash earnings increase, but value falls by 20. Under identical conditions, annual net cash of 12 yields NPV of 20. The shorthand “new ROIC must exceed the cost of capital” requires appropriate measurement. With finite lives or changing returns, evaluate the full cash-flow path rather than one year's accounting ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
