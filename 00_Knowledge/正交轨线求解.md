# Solving for orthogonal trajectories
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正交轨线求解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find curves that cross a given family at right angles, first find the family’s tangent direction at each covered point. Then rotate that direction by a right angle and solve for curves following the new direction. The unknown trajectory is a new curve; its parameter is not the parameter that selected an original family member.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要找出与给定曲线族成直角相交的曲线，先确定原族在所覆盖点的切线方向，再把方向旋转直角，求出沿新方向行进的曲线。待求轨线是一条新曲线，它的参数与选择原曲线族成员的参数并不是同一个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正交轨线|orthogonality condition]] uses tangent vectors. If the original family supplies a single finite nonzero slope $m(x,y)$ at a point, a trajectory written as $y(x)$ must have slope $q$ satisfying $1+mq=0$. Hence its [[常微分方程|differential equation]] is $y'=-1/m(x,y)$ on that region. If several family members give different tangent lines at the same point, a single slope field cannot represent all of them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正交轨线|正交条件]]使用切向量。若原族在某点提供唯一的有限非零斜率 $m(x,y)$，写成 $y(x)$ 的轨线斜率 $q$ 必须满足 $1+mq=0$，因此该区域上的[[常微分方程|微分方程]]为 $y'=-1/m(x,y)$。若同一点的不同族成员提供不同切线，就不能用一个斜率场代表它们全部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Eliminate the old family parameter
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*消去原曲线族参数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the family $y=ax^2$, with any fixed real $a$ for each member; $a=0$ supplies the horizontal axis. Differentiating along one member gives $y'=2ax$. For $x\ne0$, the original equation determines $a=y/x^2$, so the family slope becomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取曲线族 $y=ax^2$，每个成员对应一个固定实数 $a$；其中 $a=0$ 给出水平轴。沿一个成员求导，得 $y'=2ax$。在 $x\ne0$ 时，原式给出 $a=y/x^2$，因此原族斜率变成

$$m(x,y)=\frac{2y}{x}\qquad(x\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The parameter has disappeared: at each point with $x\ne0$, there is exactly one such parabola, and its slope is now expressed in the coordinates of that point. On the smaller region $x\ne0$, $y\ne0$, this slope is finite and nonzero, giving the orthogonal equation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数已经消去：对每个 $x\ne0$ 的点，恰好有一条这样的抛物线经过它，其斜率现在用点的坐标表示。在更小的区域 $x\ne0$、$y\ne0$，原斜率有限且非零，因此正交方程为

$$y'=-\frac{x}{2y}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Solve the new equation and verify its directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求解新方程，再验证方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[分离变量|separation of variables]] works. Multiplying by $2y$ is legitimate in the stated region, and integrating gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此时可以[[分离变量]]。在所述区域中乘以 $2y$ 合法，积分得到

$$2y\,dy=-x\,dx\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y^2+\frac{x^2}{2}=C.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $C>0$, these are ellipses with horizontal semiaxis $\sqrt{2C}$ and vertical semiaxis $\sqrt C$. These lengths are the distances from the centre to the farthest points along each coordinate axis. The constant $C$ labels the new family independently of $a$. When $C=0$, there is only the origin, not a regular trajectory; when $C<0$, there are no real points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $C>0$，得到椭圆：水平半轴为 $\sqrt{2C}$，竖直半轴为 $\sqrt C$，即从中心沿两条坐标轴到最远点的距离。常数 $C$ 独立于 $a$，用于标记新曲线族。$C=0$ 只给原点，不是一条正规轨线；$C<0$ 则没有实数点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[隐式求导|Implicit differentiation]] gives $2yy'+x=0$, recovering the orthogonal equation wherever $y\ne0$. Where also $x\ne0$, the product of slopes is $(2y/x)(-x/(2y))=-1$. For example, the original parabola $y=x^2$ and the ellipse $y^2+x^2/2=3/2$ meet at $(1,1)$, with slopes $2$ and $-1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[隐式求导]]得到 $2yy'+x=0$，在 $y\ne0$ 处还原出正交方程。若同时 $x\ne0$，两斜率乘积为 $(2y/x)(-x/(2y))=-1$。例如，原抛物线 $y=x^2$ 与椭圆 $y^2+x^2/2=3/2$ 在 $(1,1)$ 相交，斜率分别为 $2$ 与 $-1/2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the axes that the calculation excluded
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检查计算中排除的坐标轴*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an ellipse point with $y=0$ and $x\ne0$, the original family member is $a=0$, a horizontal line. The ellipse has a vertical tangent there, so orthogonality still holds even though the reciprocal-slope formula cannot be evaluated. For a direct vector check, the original tangent is $(1,2ax)$ and an ellipse tangent is $(2y,-x)$. At a shared point $y=ax^2$, their dot product is $2y-2ax^2=0$. The ellipse tangent is nonzero because $C>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在椭圆上满足 $y=0$、$x\ne0$ 的点，原族成员是 $a=0$ 的水平线，椭圆切线则为竖直，因此仍然正交，只是负倒数公式无法在那里求值。可以直接用向量核对：原切向量是 $(1,2ax)$，椭圆切向量是 $(2y,-x)$；在共同点 $y=ax^2$，点积为 $2y-2ax^2=0$。$C>0$ 保证椭圆切向量非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=0$, $y\ne0$, no original parabola passes through the point. An ellipse may continue there, but there is no original-family intersection at which to impose a tangent condition. The simplified equation $y'=-x/(2y)$ is defined there and extends the derived direction field to that part of the vertical axis. This is an explicit extension of the equation’s domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x=0$、$y\ne0$ 处，没有原抛物线经过。椭圆可以在那里继续，但没有原族交点需要施加切线条件。化简后的 $y'=-x/(2y)$ 在这里有定义，把推导出的方向场扩展到了这一部分纵轴。这是在明确扩展方程的定义域。

> [!note] A geometric trajectory can be missed by y(x) · 函数图像表示也会遗漏几何轨线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The vertical axis $x=0$ is itself orthogonal to every original parabola at their sole intersection, the origin: all parabolas have horizontal tangent there. It is not an ellipse with $C=0$, and it is not the graph of a function $y(x)$ on an open interval. Thus the ellipses found by the slope equation should not be presented as an exhaustive list of all geometric trajectories, including excluded axes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 竖直轴 $x=0$ 本身也与每条原抛物线正交：它们唯一的交点是原点，而所有抛物线在那里切线都水平。它不是 $C=0$ 的椭圆，也不是某个开区间上的函数 $y(x)$ 图像。因此不能把斜率方程找到的椭圆，宣称为包含被排除坐标轴在内的所有几何轨线的完整清单。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State the interval of each function solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*写清每个函数解的区间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the extended equation on $y\ne0$, the ellipse splits into two function branches:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对定义在 $y\ne0$ 上的扩展方程，椭圆分为两条函数分支：

$$y_\pm(x)=\pm\sqrt{C-\frac{x^2}{2}},\qquad
x\in(-\sqrt{2C},\sqrt{2C}),\quad C>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are the [[微分方程最大区间|maximal intervals]] for the respective upper and lower branches. At the endpoints, $y$ tends to zero and the original right-hand side of this extended equation is undefined; the curve also has a vertical tangent. The geometric ellipse continues, but neither branch continues through those endpoints as the same differentiable function $y(x)$. If we keep the stricter derivation domain $x,y\ne0$, each interval must additionally be split at zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这分别是上下分支的[[微分方程最大区间|最大区间]]。端点处 $y$ 趋零，扩展方程的右端无定义，曲线也具有竖直切线。几何上的椭圆可以继续，但任一分支都不能作为同一个可导函数 $y(x)$ 穿过端点。若保留更严格的推导区域 $x,y\ne0$，每个区间还需要从零处分开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general method therefore ends with a domain check: identify where the original family supplies a direction, where reciprocal slopes are valid, which cases division excluded, and which solution branch matches any specified initial point. Direct tangent checks settle geometric cases that the slope equation does not cover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，这种方法最终需要检查定义域：原族在哪里提供方向、负倒数斜率在哪里有效、除法排除了哪些情形，以及哪条解支符合指定初始点。斜率方程未覆盖的几何情形，要直接检查切线。
