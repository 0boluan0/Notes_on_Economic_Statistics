# 正交轨线求解先消去曲线族参数再由垂直条件建立微分方程并核对解分支
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solving for orthogonal trajectories eliminates the family parameter, forms an ODE from perpendicular tangents, and checks the solution branches*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 怎样做
> 从给定曲线族消去族参数，得到原切线斜率 $m(x,y)$。在斜率有限且非零的区域，[[正交轨线]]满足 $y'=-1/m(x,y)$。用适合该方程的方法求解，再核对原曲线族覆盖的区域、参数范围、解区间和消元排除的点。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Eliminate the family parameter to obtain the original tangent slope $m(x,y)$. Where it is finite and nonzero, an [[正交轨线|orthogonal trajectory]] satisfies $y'=-1/m(x,y)$. Solve the resulting ODE, then check the region covered by the original family, parameter range, solution intervals, and points excluded by elimination.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负倒数来自两个切向量的垂直条件：若原切线方向为 $(1,m)$，新切线方向为 $(1,q)$，那么 $1+mq=0$，所以 $q=-1/m$。原斜率为零时，正交方向是竖直方向，不能再用有限的 $q$ 表示；曲线族在某点没有唯一切线时，也不能硬写一个斜率场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The negative reciprocal follows from perpendicular tangent vectors: $(1,m)\cdot(1,q)=1+mq=0$. If $m=0$, the orthogonal direction is vertical and cannot be represented by a finite $q$. If the family supplies no unique tangent at a point, it does not define a single slope field there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如抛物线族 $y=ax^2$，先求导，再用原式消去 $a$：
$$
y'=2ax=\frac{2y}{x}\qquad(x\ne0).
$$
在 $x,y\ne0$ 的区域，正交方程为
$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{x}{2y}.
$$
此时恰好可以[[分离变量]]：
$$
2y\,dy=-x\,dx
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y^2+\frac{x^2}{2}=C.
$$
当 $C>0$，这给出椭圆族。代回隐式求导所得斜率，确实与原斜率相乘为 $-1$；$C=0$ 只是一个点，不能当作有切线的轨线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y=ax^2$, differentiating and eliminating $a$ gives $2y/x$ for $x\ne0$. On the region where $x,y\ne0$, the orthogonal ODE separates and integrates to $y^2+x^2/2=C$. For $C>0$ these are ellipses, and implicit differentiation verifies the product-of-slopes condition. For $C=0$ the set is only a point, not a trajectory with a tangent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

化简后的斜率方程 $y'=-x/(2y)$ 在 $y\ne0$ 时也能定义于 $x=0$；下面讨论这个自然延拓后的方程。原抛物线族不经过纵轴上的非零点，所以在那里虽可延续解曲线，却没有原族交点需要核验正交性。若严格限制在推导时的区域 $x,y\ne0$，则还须把解区间从 $x=0$ 分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simplified slope equation $y'=-x/(2y)$ extends naturally to $x=0$ when $y\ne0$; the following discussion uses this extended equation. The original parabola family does not pass through nonzero points on the vertical axis, so the solution curve can continue there without an original-family intersection at which to test orthogonality. If the original derivation region $x,y\ne0$ is retained strictly, each solution interval must also be split at zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

隐式曲线还不等于一个全局函数解。写成
$$
y=\pm\sqrt{C-\frac{x^2}{2}}
$$
后，两个 $y(x)$ 解支的最大区间均为 $(-\sqrt{2C},\sqrt{2C})$；端点处切线竖直，方程 $y'=-x/(2y)$ 也因 $y=0$ 而未定义。椭圆作为几何曲线仍可延伸，但不再是同一个函数图形。这正是[[微分方程最大区间]]与几何曲线延伸的区别。若要在推导排除的轴上讨论正交性，须回到原曲线的切线方向另查，不能把负倒数公式当作覆盖整平面的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An implicit curve is not a global function solution. The upper and lower branches have maximal $x$-intervals $(-\sqrt{2C},\sqrt{2C})$; their endpoints have vertical tangents, and the ODE is undefined there because $y=0$. The geometric ellipse can continue, but not as the same function graph. This separates a [[微分方程最大区间|maximal solution interval]] from geometric continuation. Orthogonality on axes excluded during derivation must be checked from the original tangent directions, not inferred from a reciprocal-slope formula that was undefined there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么求得 $y^2+x^2/2=C$ 后，还不能直接写“任意常数对应全实线上的一个解”？
>
> **答案：** $C>0$ 才给非退化椭圆；椭圆包含两个函数分支，每支的 $x$ 区间有限，端点处原斜率方程没有定义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does $y^2+x^2/2=C$ not justify saying that every constant gives one solution on the whole real line?
>
> **Answer:** Only $C>0$ gives a nondegenerate ellipse. It contains two function branches, each with a finite $x$-interval; the slope equation is undefined at the endpoints.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
