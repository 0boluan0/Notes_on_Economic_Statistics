# 差异化战略是以客户重视的独特价值建立竞争优势的战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Differentiation seeks competitive advantage through distinctive value that customers appreciate.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差异化战略在客户重视的产品、服务、交付或其他属性上形成有意义的区别，使客户愿意选择企业的提供物，并可能支付溢价。它不是“任何与别人不同”，也不局限于奢侈品；不同之处必须与[[价值主张|客户需求]]有关，并由企业活动实际兑现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiation creates meaningful distinctions in valued product, service, delivery, or other attributes, encouraging customer choice and potentially a price premium. It is not arbitrary difference or a strategy confined to luxury goods. The distinction must address [[价值主张|customer needs]] and be delivered through actual activities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差异不自动带来高利润。自拟单件比较：基础方案售价 100、成本 70，利润 30；改进服务后售价 115、成本 80，利润 35。若服务成本升至 90，利润反降至 25。该例固定销售数量，只说明溢价必须与额外成本比较；真实判断还要考虑销量、客户留存和竞争者模仿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinctiveness does not automatically increase profit. In a constructed per-unit comparison, a base offer sells for 100 and costs 70, earning 30. Improved service priced at 115 and costing 80 earns 35; at a cost of 90, profit instead falls to 25. Volume is fixed here. Actual evaluation also considers demand, retention, and imitation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果差异化只针对狭窄细分市场，就是[[集聚战略|差异化集聚]]；与低成本并非逻辑上绝对互斥，但同时追求时需说明活动选择如何兼容，而不是把所有功能都堆上去。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiation directed only at a narrow segment is [[集聚战略|differentiation focus]]. Differentiation and low cost are not logically incompatible, but combining them requires compatible activity choices rather than indiscriminately adding features.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
