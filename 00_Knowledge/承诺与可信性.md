# 承诺只有在对手决策前可观察地改变后续行动集合、收益或信息时，才可能使原本不可信的威胁变得可信
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A commitment can make a previously non-credible threat credible only by observably changing future actions, payoffs, or information before the opponent decides*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**承诺**不是把同一句威胁说得更坚定，而是在对手选择之前改变后续博弈。例如，不可逆投资可以改变阻击成本；合同可以删除反悔选项；自动执行规则可以把后续行动从人的自由选择变成机制结果。改变后，再到相应续局检查威胁行动是否成为[[可信威胁|最佳反应]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **commitment** is not a more emphatic repetition of the same threat. It changes the continuation game before the opponent chooses. Irreversible investment may alter the cost of fighting, a contract may remove the option to reverse course, and an automatic rule may replace a later discretionary action with a mechanism outcome. Credibility is then reassessed by asking whether the threatened action is a [[可信威胁|best response]] in the modified continuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种真正改变博弈的渠道
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three Channels That Actually Change the Game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **改变行动集合：** 销毁退路、签订不可撤销合同，或把控制权交给无法临时改写的规则。
- **改变续局收益：** 提前投资、抵押品、违约金或声誉后果，使执行与反悔的相对收益改变。
- **改变信息：** 让对手在行动前观察到不可逆选择或可验证合同，从而改变其对后续行为的合理预期。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Action set:** remove the option to retreat, sign an irreversible contract, or delegate control to a rule that cannot be rewritten at the last moment.
- **Continuation payoffs:** investment, collateral, penalties, or reputational consequences change the relative payoff from carrying out versus abandoning the threat.
- **Information:** make the irreversible action or verifiable contract observable before the opponent moves, so that the opponent can rationally revise expectations of later behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 时点与可观察性缺一不可
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Timing and Observability Both Matter*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

承诺必须先于对手要被影响的选择生效。若企业在竞争者已经进入后才锁定产能，它不能解释先前的威慑。承诺还必须进入对手的信息：一项完全秘密、又不通过价格或行动留下可观察后果的安排，可能改变承诺者自己的后续选择，却不能让对手在此前据此改变行动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The commitment must take effect before the opponent makes the choice it is meant to influence. Capacity locked in only after entry cannot explain prior deterrence. The commitment must also enter the opponent's information. A completely secret arrangement with no observable implication may change the committing player's later action, but it cannot induce the opponent to alter an earlier choice on that basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先改模型，再重新求解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Modify the Model, Then Solve Again*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

承诺改变了行动集合、收益、时序或信息集中的至少一项，因此得到的是新的[[博弈模型]]。不能保留原树和原收益不变，同时在答案里写“因为已经承诺，所以威胁可信”。正确步骤是画出承诺阶段，写明可观察性与不可逆性，再从新的续局向前求[[子博弈完美均衡|SPE]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A commitment changes at least one of the action set, payoffs, timing, or information sets, so it creates a new [[博弈模型|game model]]. One cannot leave the original tree and payoffs unchanged and merely declare the threat credible. The correct procedure is to add the commitment stage, state observability and irreversibility, and solve the new game for [[子博弈完美均衡|SPE]] from its continuation games.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 进入威慑
> 进入后，在位者若阻击得 $-1$、容纳得 $1$，则“进入就阻击”不可信。若在进入前进行可观察且不可逆的产能投资，使进入后阻击得 $2$、容纳得 $1$，阻击才可能成为最佳反应。第二个结论来自新收益与新时序，不是语言更强硬。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After entry, suppose fighting gives the incumbent $-1$ while accommodating gives $1$. The threat to fight is then not credible. If an observable and irreversible capacity investment made before entry changes those continuation payoffs to $2$ from fighting and $1$ from accommodating, fighting may become a best response. The second conclusion follows from the new payoffs and timing, not from firmer language.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 企业公开说“若竞争者进入，我会发动价格战”，但声明没有合同、投资、声誉或其他后果；进入后容纳仍比价格战多赚 10。公开性是否足够？
>
> **答案：** 不够。公开性只让对手听见声明，没有改变后续最佳反应。必须存在会改变行动、收益或可执行约束的承诺装置。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A firm publicly says, “If a rival enters, I will start a price war,” but the statement creates no contract, investment, reputational consequence, or other cost; after entry, accommodation still earns 10 more than fighting. Is publicity enough?
>
> **Answer:** No. Publicity lets the rival hear the statement, but it does not change the continuation best response. A commitment device must change feasible actions, payoffs, or an enforceable constraint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
