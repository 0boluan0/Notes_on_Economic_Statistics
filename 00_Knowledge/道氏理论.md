# 道氏理论以市场指数的分层趋势和相互确认识别市场主要方向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dow theory identifies the market's major direction through a hierarchy of index trends and confirmation between indexes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

道氏理论是以代表性股票价格指数研究市场整体方向的技术分析框架：把市场运动分层，以价格行为为主要依据，并要求不同指数相互确认。它研究的是市场整体走势，不是由指数直接推出每只个股的未来价格。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dow theory is a technical-analysis framework for studying broad market direction through representative stock-price indexes. It distinguishes scales of movement, gives primary weight to price behavior, and requires confirmation between indexes. A market-level reading does not determine each individual stock's future price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其最小结构是：用[[道氏趋势层级]]区分主要方向与内部折返；用[[道氏指数确认]]判断工业与运输指数是否给出一致证据。课程以收盘价为重要观测口径、以成交量配合为辅助，并在明确反转信号之前维持原趋势判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The framework uses [[道氏趋势层级|Dow's trend hierarchy]] to separate the primary direction from internal reactions, and [[道氏指数确认|index confirmation]] to compare industrial and transportation indexes. The course emphasizes closing prices, treats volume as supporting evidence, and retains the current trend interpretation until a reversal signal is established.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些是该学派组织证据的原则。重视收盘价不等于证明它完整包含全部信息；保持原趋势判断不等于趋势必然持续；指数确认也不是盈利保证。具体检验仍须固定识别规则与[[交易信号时序]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These principles organize evidence within the school. Emphasizing the close does not prove that it contains all information; retaining a trend interpretation does not guarantee continuation or profit. Testing requires explicit recognition rules and [[交易信号时序|signal and execution timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
