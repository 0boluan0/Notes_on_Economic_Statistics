# 导数与求导规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Derivatives and differentiation rules*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把“当输入发生极小变化时，输出怎样变化”变成可计算的对象。
> **具体锚点：** 汽车在某一瞬间的速度不是一段路程除以一段时间，而是平均速度在时间间隔趋近于零时的极限。
> **核心难点：** 导数依赖极限；公式只是压缩后的规则，不能替代对局部线性变化的理解。
> **为什么重要：** 优化、近似、微分方程、统计估计和经济学边际分析都以此为语言。
> **继续：** 先读“从差商到导数”，再进入 [[导数的应用#优化：把目标变成一阶条件|优化]] 或 [[定积分与微积分基本定理#两个基本定理怎样把导数与积分接起来|积分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** A derivative turns “how does the output respond to an infinitesimal input change?” into a computable local quantity.
> **Concrete anchor:** Instantaneous speed is not distance over one fixed interval; it is the limit of average speed as the interval shrinks to zero.
> **Central difficulty:** Derivatives depend on limits. Product, quotient, and chain rules compress limit arguments but do not replace the local-linear meaning.
> **Why it matters:** Optimisation, approximation, differential equations, statistical estimation, and marginal analysis use derivatives as a common language.
> **Continue with:** Start from the difference quotient, then move to [[导数的应用#优化：把目标变成一阶条件|optimisation]] or [[定积分与微积分基本定理#两个基本定理怎样把导数与积分接起来|the connection between derivatives and integrals]].

## 从差商到导数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From difference quotient to derivative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数在 $x$ 附近的平均变化率是 $[f(x+h)-f(x)]/h$。令 $h\to0$，若极限存在，就得到 $f'(x)$。它同时有三种读法：图像切线的斜率、随时间的瞬时变化率、以及局部线性近似 $f(x+h)\approx f(x)+f'(x)h$ 的系数。尖点、跳跃和竖直切线提醒我们：函数有值不等于可导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The average rate of change near $x$ is $[f(x+h)-f(x)]/h$. If its limit exists as $h\to0$, it is $f'(x)$. The same quantity is the slope of a tangent line, an instantaneous rate of change, and the coefficient in the local approximation $f(x+h)\approx f(x)+f'(x)h$. Corners, jumps, and vertical tangents show that being defined does not imply differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 求导规则为何成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why differentiation rules work*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积、商与链式法则都来自差商的代数重排。链式法则 $(f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x)$ 的实质是两层局部缩放相乘。隐函数求导不是把 $y$ 当常数，而是对关系 $F(x,y(x))=0$ 两边对 $x$ 求导；其局部形式为 $dy/dx=-F_x/F_y$，前提是 $F_y\ne0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product, quotient, and chain rules arise from algebraic rearrangements of difference quotients. The chain rule $(f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x)$ multiplies two successive local scale factors. Implicit differentiation does not treat $y$ as constant; it differentiates $F(x,y(x))=0$, giving $dy/dx=-F_x/F_y$ locally when $F_y\ne0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $y=(3x^2+1)^5$ 有内层 $g(x)=3x^2+1$ 与外层 $f(u)=u^5$，因而 $y'=5(3x^2+1)^4\cdot6x$。漏掉 $6x$ 意味着只算了外层对中间输入的反应，没有计入中间输入自身对 $x$ 的反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $y=(3x^2+1)^5$ has inner map $g(x)=3x^2+1$ and outer map $f(u)=u^5$, so $y'=5(3x^2+1)^4\cdot6x$. Omitting $6x$ accounts only for the outer response to its intermediate input and misses how that input responds to $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 导数的信息边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limits of the information in a derivative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一阶导数描述局部方向，二阶导数描述斜率如何改变。导数为零只是内部极值的候选条件，不是充分条件；端点、不可导点也必须检查。单位同样重要：若 $f$ 是成本、$x$ 是产量，$f'(x)$ 的单位是“成本/产量”，这正是边际成本而不是总成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first derivative describes local direction, and the second describes how the slope changes. A zero derivative is only a candidate condition for an interior extremum, not a sufficient condition; endpoints and nondifferentiable points also require checking. Units matter: if $f$ is cost and $x$ output, then $f'(x)$ has units of cost per unit of output—marginal, not total, cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：用隐式求导找圆的切线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: tangent to a circle by implicit differentiation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对圆 $x^2+y^2=25$，两边对 $x$ 求导得 $2x+2y\,dy/dx=0$，因而 $dy/dx=-x/y$。在 $(3,4)$ 处斜率为 $-3/4$。当 $y=0$ 时这个公式分母为零，与圆在 $(\pm5,0)$ 有竖直切线一致；这不是简单的代数故障，而是局部不能把 $y$ 写成 $x$ 的光滑函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the circle $x^2+y^2=25$, differentiation with respect to $x$ gives $2x+2y\,dy/dx=0$, hence $dy/dx=-x/y$. At $(3,4)$ the slope is $-3/4$. When $y=0$, the denominator vanishes, matching the vertical tangents at $(\pm5,0)$; this is not merely an algebraic failure but a point where $y$ cannot locally be represented as a smooth function of $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么导数不是把 $h$ 直接代成 0？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why a derivative does not substitute $h=0$ directly.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 差商在 $h=0$ 时没有定义；导数研究的是 $h$ 趋近于 0 时差商是否稳定到一个极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The difference quotient is undefined at $h=0$. A derivative asks whether the quotient approaches a stable limit as $h$ tends to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 看到一个隐式关系时，什么时候可以局部把 $y$ 看成 $x$ 的函数？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When can an implicit relation locally define $y$ as a function of $x$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 在相应点附近关系足够光滑且 $F_y\ne0$ 时；此时隐函数定理给出局部函数和斜率 $-F_x/F_y$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> When the relation is sufficiently smooth near the point and $F_y\ne0$; the implicit function theorem then provides a local function with slope $-F_x/F_y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 链式法则中为什么要乘内层导数？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the chain rule multiply by the inner derivative?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 外层导数是“输出对中间量”的变化率，内层导数是“中间量对原输入”的变化率；两次局部缩放必须相乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The outer derivative measures output change per unit of the intermediate quantity, and the inner derivative measures intermediate change per unit of the original input. The two local scale factors multiply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 在闭区间上找最大值时，为什么不能只解 $f'(x)=0$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is solving $f'(x)=0$ insufficient for a maximum on a closed interval?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 最大值还可能出现在端点或不可导点；而导数为零的内部点也可能是最小值或平坦拐点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A maximum may occur at an endpoint or a nondifferentiable point, while an interior point with zero derivative may be a minimum or a flat inflection point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
