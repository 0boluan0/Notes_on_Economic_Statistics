# Interpreting a joint multivariate test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*多响应联合检验的解释*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A training programme is evaluated using maths, reading, and science scores. A **joint multivariate test** asks whether a specified collection of claims about these outcomes can all hold together. It gives one answer for that collection. “All three mean effects are zero” is a different claim from “each of the three effects is nonzero.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一项培训用数学、阅读和科学成绩评价。**多响应联合检验**问的是：关于这些结果的一组指定限制，能否同时成立？它对整组限制给出一个检验结果。“三个均值效应全为零”和“三个均值效应各自都不为零”，是不同的命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the null before reading the p-value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先读零假设，再读 p 值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[多响应线性回归|multiple-response linear model]] $Y=XB+U$, rows of $Y$ are observations, its columns are responses, $X$ records predictor values, and $U$ contains model errors. A row of $B$ contains one predictor's coefficients across the responses. Suppose the predictor is a programme indicator and its three coefficients are $\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3$. A common [[零假设与备择假设|null and alternative hypothesis]] pair is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[多响应线性回归]]模型 $Y=XB+U$ 中，$Y$ 的行是观测，列是响应，$X$ 记录解释变量的取值，$U$ 包含模型误差。$B$ 的某一行包含同一个解释变量在各响应上的系数。假设这个变量表示是否参加项目，三个系数为 $\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3$。常见的一组[[零假设与备择假设]]是

$$
H_0:\beta_1=\beta_2=\beta_3=0,
\qquad
H_1:\text{at least one }\beta_j\ne0.
$$

> [!important]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **“At least one” is the alternative.** The population coefficient vector $(5,0,0)$ violates this null even though the second and third coefficients are zero. A valid rejection supplies statistical evidence against the joint null; it neither proves the alternative with certainty nor identifies which coefficients differ.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **备择是“至少一个”。** 总体系数向量 $(5,0,0)$ 已经违反这个零假设，尽管第二、第三个系数仍为零。有效的拒绝结果提供了反对联合零假设的统计证据；它既不是对备择的绝对证明，也没有直接指出哪些系数不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The more general [[多响应一般线性假设|linear hypothesis]] is $CBA=D$: $C$ selects predictor contrasts, $A$ selects response contrasts, and $D$ specifies their proposed values. Its alternative is $CBA-D\ne0$, meaning that at least one entry of this matrix is nonzero. If $A$ selects “maths minus reading,” the test concerns that contrast; it is not automatically a test of each original response separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般的[[多响应一般线性假设]]写成 $CBA=D$：$C$ 选择解释变量的对比，$A$ 选择响应的对比，$D$ 给定这些对比的假设值。备择 $CBA-D\ne0$ 表示这个矩阵至少一个元素不为零。如果 $A$ 选择的是“数学减阅读”，检验对象就是该对比，并不会自动变成对每个原始响应的分别检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the test and significance level $\alpha$ before looking at the results. Under its stated model, a level-$\alpha$ test controls the probability of rejecting a true null at at most $\alpha$. The [[p值条件解释|p-value]] measures a tail probability under that null and reference model; it is not the probability that the null is true. A non-rejection does not establish that every coefficient is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验方法和显著性水平 $\alpha$ 应在查看结果之前确定。在其所要求的模型下，水平为 $\alpha$ 的检验把“零假设为真却被拒绝”的概率控制在不超过 $\alpha$。[[p值条件解释|p 值]]是零假设及参考模型下的尾部概率，不是零假设为真的概率。不拒绝也不能确立每个系数都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A combination can be unusual when neither coordinate is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*单列都不突出，组合却可能很异常*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Joint tests use both each estimate's uncertainty and their [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]], which describes how estimation errors move together. If two errors usually have the same sign, an observed difference between them may be much more unusual than either estimate considered alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

联合检验既使用各估计量的不确定性，也使用描述估计误差如何共同变化的[[协方差矩阵]]。如果两个误差通常同向变化，那么观测到两者相差很远，可能比单独看其中任意一个都更异常。

> [!example]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Two individually modest estimates.** Let $\delta$ be the vector of two population contrasts. Suppose its estimator has the exact [[联合Gaussian|bivariate normal]] sampling law $\widehat\delta\sim N_2(\delta,V)$, with mean $\delta$ and known covariance $V$. The observed estimate is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **两个单独看并不突出的估计。** 设 $\delta$ 是两个总体对比组成的向量，估计量具有精确的[[联合Gaussian|二元正态]]抽样分布 $\widehat\delta\sim N_2(\delta,V)$，均值为 $\delta$，协方差 $V$ 已知。观测值为
>
> $$
> V=\begin{pmatrix}1&0.95\\0.95&1\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad
> \widehat\delta=\begin{pmatrix}1.5\\-1.5\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each [[标准误含义|standard error]], the square root of the corresponding sampling variance, is $1$. Neither coordinate rejects a zero mean in its separate two-sided $5\%$ normal test, since $1.5<1.96$. Under the joint null $\delta=0$, however, the [[Gaussian马氏平方律|Gaussian squared Mahalanobis statistic]] is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个[[标准误含义|标准误]]都是相应抽样方差的平方根，这里等于 $1$。由于 $1.5<1.96$，分别进行双侧 $5\%$ 正态检验时，两列都不拒绝零均值。但在联合零假设 $\delta=0$ 下，[[Gaussian马氏平方律|高斯马氏平方统计量]]为
>
> $$
> Q=\widehat\delta^{\mathsf T}V^{-1}\widehat\delta=90,
> \qquad Q\sim\chi^2_2\ \text{under }H_0.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This exceeds the $95\%$ cutoff, approximately $5.991$. The contrast $\widehat\delta_1-\widehat\delta_2$ equals $3$, while its variance is only $1+1-2(0.95)=0.1$. The covariance explains why the joint test rejects.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个值超过约为 $5.991$ 的 $95\%$ 分位数。对比 $\widehat\delta_1-\widehat\delta_2$ 等于 $3$，方差却只有 $1+1-2(0.95)=0.1$。协方差解释了为什么联合检验会拒绝。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This example uses a **known sampling covariance** to make the geometry transparent. It does not license a chi-square reference distribution when a MANOVA procedure estimates an unknown covariance. That procedure needs its own [[MANOVA检验校准|calibration and assumptions]]. Joint rejection and separate coordinate rejections are not interchangeable labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子使用**已知的抽样协方差**，以便直接看清几何原因。它并不允许在 MANOVA 中估计了未知协方差后，仍直接套用这里的卡方分布；实际程序需要相应的[[MANOVA检验校准|校准与假设]]。联合拒绝和逐坐标拒绝不能互相替换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Locate the differences with a defined follow-up procedure
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用事先明确的后续分析定位差异*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To say which outcomes differ, define the comparisons you want to report. A [[置信区间覆盖率|confidence interval]] is a procedure designed to cover its target at a stated rate across repeated samples. For a fixed family of $m$ comparisons, [[Bonferroni对比区间|Bonferroni-adjusted intervals or tests]] allocate error probability $\alpha/m$ to each comparison. The [[并事件上界|union bound]] then controls the probability of at least one false rejection across the family by $\alpha$, without requiring the tests to be independent. The individual tests or intervals still need valid sampling assumptions; the adjustment cannot repair an invalid standard error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要说明哪些结果不同，需要先明确要报告哪些比较。[[置信区间覆盖率|置信区间]]是按重复抽样中给定的目标覆盖率设计的程序。对于预定的 $m$ 个比较，[[Bonferroni对比区间|Bonferroni 调整区间或检验]]把每个比较的错误概率设为 $\alpha/m$。[[并事件上界]]由此将整个比较族中至少一次误拒绝的概率控制在 $\alpha$，不要求各检验独立。不过，每个检验或区间仍须有有效的抽样依据；多重性调整不能修复无效的标准误。

> [!warning]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Passing the joint test does not generally authorize an unrestricted search.** A rule such as “if the joint test rejects, try many unadjusted comparisons and report the smallest p-values” needs its own error-control justification. Some prespecified gatekeeping procedures are valid, but that validity belongs to the whole procedure. A newly chosen direction after seeing the data may require [[选择后推断|selection-aware inference]] or confirmation with independent data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **总体检验通过，并不普遍授权无限制的搜索。** “总体拒绝后，尝试许多未经调整的比较，再报告最小 p 值”的规则需要独立的错误率控制依据。有些预先规定的门控程序确实有效，但有效性属于整个程序。看过数据后才新选的方向，可能需要[[选择后推断]]或独立数据确认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same care applies to choosing among [[MANOVA四种统计量|Pillai, Wilks, Hotelling–Lawley, and Roy statistics]]. They can address the same null through different summaries and calibrations; selecting whichever gives the smallest p-value after seeing all four changes the testing procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[MANOVA四种统计量|Pillai、Wilks、Hotelling–Lawley 和 Roy 统计量]]之间选择时也应如此。它们可用不同的汇总方式与校准检验同一个零假设；看完四个 p 值后再挑最小者，已经改变了检验程序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, report estimated contrasts in meaningful units, their uncertainty, and the size that matters in the application. A very small p-value does not establish a large practical difference, and Wilks or Pillai statistics are not universal measures of practical importance. Whether a difference is caused by the programme is a further question about [[多响应因果边界|causal identification]]. For the analogous distinction among predictors of a single response, see [[回归联合显著边界|joint significance in single-response regression]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，还应在有意义的单位上报告对比估计、不确定性，以及应用中值得关注的量级。很小的 p 值不代表实际差异很大，Wilks 或 Pillai 统计量也不是通用的实质重要性度量。差异是否由项目造成，还需要回答[[多响应因果边界|因果识别]]问题。单个响应下多个解释变量的对应区别见[[回归联合显著边界]]。
