# 均值方差有效前沿由不被其他可行组合均值方差支配的组合构成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The mean-variance efficient frontier consists of portfolios not mean-variance dominated by any other feasible portfolio.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定资产、约束、期间和收益口径的[[投资组合可行集]]中，一个组合是**均值方差有效的**，当且仅当不存在另一个可行组合，在期望收益不低、方差不高的同时至少一项严格更好。所有这类组合的风险收益点构成均值方差有效前沿；这里要求比较所需二阶矩有限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within a [[投资组合可行集|portfolio opportunity set]] with fixed assets, constraints, horizon, and return convention, a portfolio is mean-variance efficient exactly when no other feasible portfolio has no lower mean and no higher variance, with at least one strict improvement. Their risk-return points form the efficient frontier; the required second moments must be finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有效”排除的是一项能变好且另一项不变坏的配置，不是替投资者选出唯一答案。一个有效组合可以收益较高而风险也较高，另一个则相反；投资者仍需在这类不可支配的权衡之间选择，不能从有效性直接推出所有人的最佳组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficiency excludes allocations for which one dimension can improve without worsening the other; it does not choose a unique portfolio for the investor. One efficient portfolio may have both higher mean and higher risk than another. Preferences are still needed to choose among these nondominated trade-offs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如只允许两种方案：$a$ 的期望收益与标准差为 $(8\%,15\%)$，$b$ 为 $(10\%,20\%)$。二者互不支配，都有效；若再加入 $(10\%,15\%)$ 的可行方案 $c$，则 $c$ 支配前两者。因此有效性总是相对于指定可行集而言。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose only two allocations are allowed: $a$ has mean/standard deviation $(8\%,15\%)$, and $b$ has $(10\%,20\%)$. Neither dominates the other, so both are efficient. If a feasible allocation $c$ with $(10\%,15\%)$ is added, it dominates both. Efficiency is always relative to the specified feasible set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在经典正定、允许卖空的模型中，有效前沿恰好是[[最小方差边界的有效上支|最小方差边界的上支]]；这是一条带条件的结论，不是有效前沿的定义。均值方差有效也不自动排除所有尾部风险或保证一般效用意义的最优，见[[均值方差偏好边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the classical positive-definite model with unrestricted short selling, efficiency selects the [[最小方差边界的有效上支|upper minimum-variance branch]]. That is a conditional result, not the definition. Mean-variance efficiency does not eliminate all tail risk or guarantee optimality under unrestricted preferences; see the [[均值方差偏好边界|preference boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
