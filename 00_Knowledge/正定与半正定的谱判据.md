# Eigenvalue tests for positive definiteness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正定与半正定的谱判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real symmetric matrix, eigenvalues reveal the signs of its quadratic form in a complete set of perpendicular directions. This converts a requirement about **every vector** into a finite sign test. [[正定矩阵|Positive definiteness]] requires a positive value for every nonzero vector; [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefiniteness]] allows zero values and includes the positive-definite case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实对称矩阵的特征值，会给出二次型在一整组互相垂直方向上的符号。于是，关于**所有向量**的要求可以化为有限个数的符号检查。[[正定矩阵|正定]]要求每个非零向量对应的值都正；[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]允许零值，也包含正定情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, take $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$. An [[特征对|eigenpair]] satisfies $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$: $\lambda$ is the eigenvalue and $v$ its eigenvector. Here $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$ have eigenvalues $3$ and $1$. Superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], turning the displayed row of coordinates into a column. Both weights are positive, so the test below proves that $A$ is positive definite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，取 $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$。[[特征对]]满足 $Av=\lambda v$ 且 $v\ne0$，其中 $\lambda$ 是特征值，$v$ 是对应的特征向量。本例的 $(1,1)^T$、$(1,-1)^T$ 分别对应特征值 $3$、$1$；上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把横写坐标变成列向量。两个权重均正，下面的判据便说明 $A$ 正定。

> [!important] Keep the symmetry assumption · 保留对称性前提
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer. Suppose $A$ is either [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric]], $A=A^T$, or [[Hermitian 矩阵|complex Hermitian]], $A=A^*$. The star means conjugate transpose. Its $n$ eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$, listed with multiplicity, are real. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A$ 是[[实对称矩阵]] $A=A^T$，或复 [[Hermitian 矩阵]] $A=A^*$；星号表示共轭转置。计入重数后，它的 $n$ 个特征值 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ 都是实数，并且
>
> $$A\succ0\iff\lambda_i>0\text{ for every }i,
> \qquad A\succeq0\iff\lambda_i\ge0\text{ for every }i.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbols $\succ0$ and $\succeq0$ denote positive definiteness and positive semidefiniteness respectively. The strict version excludes a zero eigenvalue; the nonstrict version does not require one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\succ0$ 与 $\succeq0$ 分别表示正定与半正定。严格版本排除零特征值；非严格版本并不要求一定存在零特征值。

## Why these directions cover every vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么只检查这些方向就足够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[实对称矩阵谱定理|real spectral theorem]] supplies $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$. The columns $q_i$ of the [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrix]] $Q$ are unit eigenvectors, perpendicular to one another; $\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ is a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]], with the listed entries on its diagonal and zeros elsewhere. Write $y=Q^Tx$ for the coordinates of a vector $x$ in that basis. Since $Q$ is invertible, $x\ne0$ exactly when $y\ne0$. Matrix multiplication gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]给出 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$。[[正交矩阵]] $Q$ 的列 $q_i$ 是彼此垂直的单位特征向量；$\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ 是[[对角矩阵]]，所列数位于对角线，其余元素为零。令 $y=Q^Tx$，表示向量 $x$ 在这组基下的坐标。$Q$ 可逆，所以 $x\ne0$ 恰好等价于 $y\ne0$。矩阵乘法给出

$$x^TAx=y^T\Lambda y=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i y_i^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If all weights are nonnegative, every summand is nonnegative. If all weights are positive and $x\ne0$, at least one $y_i$ is nonzero, so at least one summand is strictly positive. This proves sufficiency in both tests. Conversely, insert the unit eigenvector $x=q_k$. The quadratic value is exactly $\lambda_k$: a negative eigenvalue rules out semidefiniteness, and a zero eigenvalue rules out positive definiteness. That proves necessity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若全部权重非负，每一项都非负。若全部权重正且 $x\ne0$，至少一个 $y_i\ne0$，因此至少一项严格为正。这证明两个判据的充分性。反过来，代入单位特征向量 $x=q_k$，二次型值恰好为 $\lambda_k$：负特征值否定半正定，零特征值否定正定。这就证明了必要性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the opening example the normalized eigenvectors are $q_1=(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ and $q_2=(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$. Thus $y_1=(x_1+x_2)/\sqrt2$, $y_2=(x_1-x_2)/\sqrt2$, and $x^TAx=3y_1^2+y_2^2$. The cross term has disappeared because the coordinates follow the eigenvectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头例子的单位特征向量是 $q_1=(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$、$q_2=(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$。因此 $y_1=(x_1+x_2)/\sqrt2$、$y_2=(x_1-x_2)/\sqrt2$，且 $x^TAx=3y_1^2+y_2^2$。沿特征向量选择坐标以后，交叉项消失了。

## The complex calculation uses squared magnitudes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复数计算使用模平方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Hermitian 谱定理|Hermitian spectral theorem]] gives $A=U\Lambda U^*$, where the [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary matrix]] $U$ has inverse $U^*$. For $y=U^*x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Hermitian 谱定理]]给出 $A=U\Lambda U^*$，其中 [[Unitary 矩阵]] $U$ 的逆为 $U^*$。令 $y=U^*x$，有

$$x^*Ax=y^*\Lambda y=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i|y_i|^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a complex number $a+bi$, with $a,b$ real and $i^2=-1$, its squared magnitude is $|a+bi|^2=a^2+b^2$. These nonnegative weights make exactly the same proof work. Ordinary squares $y_i^2$ would not suffice for complex coordinates. For example, $H=\begin{pmatrix}2&i\\-i&2\end{pmatrix}$ is Hermitian and has eigenvectors $(i,1)^T$, $(-i,1)^T$ with eigenvalues $3$, $1$; it is positive definite even though two entries are imaginary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a,b$ 为实数且 $i^2=-1$，则复数 $a+bi$ 的模平方是 $|a+bi|^2=a^2+b^2$。这些非负权重使同一证明继续成立；复坐标下不能换成普通平方 $y_i^2$。例如 $H=\begin{pmatrix}2&i\\-i&2\end{pmatrix}$ 为 Hermitian，特征向量 $(i,1)^T$、$(-i,1)^T$ 分别对应特征值 $3$、$1$；尽管含虚数元素，它仍然正定。

## Zero values and the limits of the test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零值与判据边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A symmetric matrix with eigenvalues $4,1,0$ is positive semidefinite but not positive definite: a nonzero eigenvector for $0$ has zero quadratic value. The zero matrix is also semidefinite. A one-entry matrix $[a]$ reduces to $a>0$ or $a\ge0$, respectively. Repeated positive eigenvalues cause no difficulty. If both a positive and a negative eigenvalue occur, the corresponding eigenvectors exhibit the two signs of an [[不定二次型|indefinite form]]; failure of positive definiteness alone does not imply indefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特征值为 $4,1,0$ 的对称矩阵半正定但非正定，因为零特征值对应的非零特征向量给出零二次型值。零矩阵也半正定。一阶矩阵 $[a]$ 的两种判据分别退化为 $a>0$、$a\ge0$；重复的正特征值不会造成问题。若同时存在正、负特征值，对应特征向量就展示了[[不定二次型]]的两种符号；仅仅“不正定”并不能推出“不定”。

> [!warning] Positive eigenvalues without symmetry are insufficient · 非对称时正谱不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvalues $1,1$, but at $x=(1,-1)^T$ it gives $x^TBx=1-4+1=-2$. For a general real matrix, its quadratic expression depends on its [[二次型对称化|symmetric part]] $(B+B^T)/2$, not on the eigenvalue signs of $B$ itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 反例 $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ 的特征值为 $1,1$，但取 $x=(1,-1)^T$，得到 $x^TBx=1-4+1=-2$。一般实矩阵的二次型由[[二次型对称化|对称部分]] $(B+B^T)/2$ 决定，不能直接看原矩阵 $B$ 的特征值符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statements above concern exact matrices and exact eigenvalues. A tiny positive number returned by floating-point software is not by itself a certificate of strict positivity: near zero, rounding and input uncertainty can change the sign. A numerical decision needs a stated scale, tolerance and error justification; merely setting small values to zero changes the decision rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以上结论针对精确矩阵与精确特征值。浮点软件返回一个很小的正数，本身不是严格正性的证明；接近零时，舍入与输入误差可能改变符号。数值判断需要说明尺度、容差和误差依据；直接把小数当作零，改变的是判断规则。
