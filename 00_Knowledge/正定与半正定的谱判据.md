# 实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵正定当且仅当特征值全正，半正定当且仅当特征值全非负
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix is positive definite or semidefinite exactly when its eigenvalues are all positive or nonnegative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 谱判据
> 对实对称 $A$，
> $$A\succ0\iff \lambda_i>0\ \text{对所有 }i,$$
> $$A\succeq0\iff \lambda_i\ge0\ \text{对所有 }i.$$
> 复 Hermitian 矩阵有完全相同的结论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real symmetric matrix is positive definite exactly when all eigenvalues are positive, and positive semidefinite exactly when all eigenvalues are nonnegative. The same statements hold for Hermitian matrices.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$ 并令 $y=Q^Tx$，
$$
x^TAx=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i y_i^2.
$$
若所有权重严格正或非负，结论直接成立；反向若某个 $\lambda_k<0$，取 $x=q_k$ 就找到负方向。判断正定时，若 $\lambda_k=0$，同一选择给出非零零方向。
复 Hermitian 情形用 $A=U\Lambda U^*$ 与 $y=U^*x$，对应式子是
$$
x^*Ax=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i|y_i|^2,
$$
因此必须保留共轭模平方，但符号判据完全相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Real spectral coordinates give $x^TAx=\sum_i\lambda_i y_i^2$. Positive or nonnegative weights prove the forward direction. Conversely, an eigenvector for a negative eigenvalue is a negative direction, while an eigenvector for a zero eigenvalue is a nonzero flat direction that rules out positive definiteness. In the Hermitian case, $A=U\Lambda U^*$ gives $x^*Ax=\sum_i\lambda_i|y_i|^2$ with $y=U^*x$, so the same sign test applies with conjugate moduli.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非对称矩阵，这个判据不成立。$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&4\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$ 的特征值都正，但 $x^TAx$ 有负方向；应先检查对称部分。数值计算中“$\lambda_{\min}>0$”也必须配合明确容差，因为舍入会把近零特征值显示成微小正数或负数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The criterion fails without symmetry. The displayed triangular matrix has positive eigenvalues but an indefinite quadratic form. In floating-point work, a smallest computed eigenvalue must also be interpreted with a stated tolerance because near-zero values can change sign under rounding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个实对称矩阵的特征值为 $4,1,0$。它属于哪一类？
>
> **答案：** 正半定但不正定；零特征向量给出非零零方向。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** A real symmetric matrix has eigenvalues $4,1,0$. Which definiteness class does it belong to?
>
> **Answer:** It is positive semidefinite but not positive definite; an eigenvector for the zero eigenvalue gives a nonzero zero direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
