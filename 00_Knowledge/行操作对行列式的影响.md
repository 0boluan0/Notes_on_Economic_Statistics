# 换行、行缩放与倍加行分别使行列式变号、同倍缩放与不变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Row exchange, row scaling, and row replacement respectively negate, scale, and preserve the determinant*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心规则
> 三类初等行操作必须分别记账：
> - $R_i\leftrightarrow R_j$：行列式乘 $-1$；
> - $R_i\leftarrow cR_i$（作为可逆的初等行操作时 $c\ne0$）：行列式乘 $c$；
> - $R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$（$i\ne j$）：行列式不变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Track the three elementary row operations separately: exchanging two rows multiplies the determinant by $-1$; scaling one row by a nonzero $c$ multiplies it by $c$; adding $c$ times another row to a row leaves it unchanged. The determinant scaling identity itself also holds for $c=0$, although that replacement is not an invertible elementary operation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第三条来自逐行线性：新行列式等于原值，加上 $c$ 乘以一个第 $i$、$j$ 行相同的行列式；后者为零。它并不是说“任何行相加都不影响行列式”：若把两行同时改掉，或把一行替换成它自己的倍数加别行，就要把缩放因素另外算进去。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row-replacement rule follows from separate row linearity. The new determinant is the old one plus $c$ times a determinant with two equal rows, and that extra term is zero. This does not mean that every operation involving addition preserves the determinant: changing two rows simultaneously or scaling the target row requires separate accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一行计算

若
$$
B=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&5\end{bmatrix},
$$
做 $R_2\leftarrow R_2-3R_1$ 得 $\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&-1\end{bmatrix}$，行列式不变，故 $\det B=-1$。若随后交换两行，新矩阵的行列式就是 $1$；若再把第一行乘 $4$，则变为 $4$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $B=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&5\end{bmatrix}$, the replacement $R_2\leftarrow R_2-3R_1$ gives an upper-triangular matrix without changing the determinant, so $\det B=-1$. A subsequent row exchange changes the value to $1$, and scaling one row by four changes it to $4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 消元时若为了得到主元 $1$ 而除以主元，就已经缩放了整行，必须把该因子还原。
- 当 $i\ne j$ 时，$R_i\leftarrow cR_i+R_j$ 可拆成“先缩放、再倍加行”，总效果不是保持不变，而是把行列式乘 $c$；若 $c=0$，它制造重复行，结果也确为零。
- 行列式为零时，“变号”看不出数值变化，但规则仍然成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Dividing a row by its pivot in order to create a leading one scales that row, so the factor must be restored when computing the original determinant.
- For $i\ne j$, the operation $R_i\leftarrow cR_i+R_j$ combines scaling and row replacement; it multiplies the determinant by $c$ rather than preserving it. When $c=0$, it creates two equal rows and the resulting determinant is indeed zero.
- A zero determinant is numerically unchanged by a sign reversal, but the rule still applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $R_2\leftarrow R_2+5R_1$ 不改变行列式，而 $R_2\leftarrow5R_2+R_1$ 会把它乘以 $5$？
>
> **答案：** 第一式保留了 $R_2$ 的系数 $1$，新增部分因重复行而贡献零；第二式先把原 $R_2$ 缩放了 $5$ 倍。
