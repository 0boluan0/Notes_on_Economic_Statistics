# Row operations and determinants
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行操作对行列式的影响*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When you simplify a matrix by changing its rows, you also need to know what happened to its [[行列式|determinant]], the scalar attached to a square matrix. Some row operations change that number, while one especially useful operation leaves it alone. Keeping track lets you calculate with a simpler matrix and recover the original determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改变矩阵的行来简化计算时，还需要知道它的[[行列式]]——方阵对应的那个标量——发生了什么变化。有些行操作会改变这个数，而一种特别有用的操作会保留它。记清这些变化，就能先计算较简单矩阵的行列式，再还原原矩阵的值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $R_i$ for row $i$. In the [[行等价#The allowed operations|elementary row operations]], $R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$ means “replace row $i$ by its old value plus $c$ times row $j$”; other rows stay fixed. Below, the original $A$ and resulting $B$ are $n\times n$ matrices over the same field, $n\ge1$, and $c$ is a scalar in that field. Real and complex matrices are the usual cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $R_i$ 表示第 $i$ 行。在[[行等价#The allowed operations|初等行操作]]中，$R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$ 的意思是“把第 $i$ 行替换为它原来的值加上第 $j$ 行的 $c$ 倍”，其他行保持原样。下面的原矩阵 $A$ 与操作后的 $B$ 都是同一域上的 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$，$c$ 是该域的标量；实矩阵和复矩阵是常用情形。

> [!important] Track the operation on the whole row · 记录对整行的操作
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - Exchange distinct rows: $R_i\leftrightarrow R_j$ gives $\det B=-\det A$.
> - Scale one row: $R_i\leftarrow cR_i$ gives $\det B=c\det A$.
> - Add another row's multiple: $R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$, $i\ne j$, gives $\det B=\det A$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> - 交换不同的两行：$R_i\leftrightarrow R_j$，得到 $\det B=-\det A$。
> - 缩放一行：$R_i\leftarrow cR_i$，得到 $\det B=c\det A$。
> - 加上另一行的倍数：$R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$，$i\ne j$，得到 $\det B=\det A$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The scaling identity holds at $c=0$ too, but scaling is invertible only for $c\ne0$. The other two operations need distinct rows and are unavailable when $n=1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 缩放恒等式对 $c=0$ 也成立，但缩放操作只有在 $c\ne0$ 时才可逆。另外两种操作需要两行不同，因此 $n=1$ 时不能做。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the rules follow from the definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这些规则怎样来自定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The determinant is linear in one row with all other rows fixed, and it vanishes when two rows agree. Write $D(\ldots,r_i,\ldots,r_j,\ldots)$ for the determinant with those rows. Linearity gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式在其余行固定时对一行线性，并且两行相同时为零。用 $D(\ldots,r_i,\ldots,r_j,\ldots)$ 表示具有这些行的矩阵的行列式。线性给出

$$
D(\ldots,r_i+cr_j,\ldots,r_j,\ldots)
=D(\ldots,r_i,\ldots,r_j,\ldots)
+cD(\ldots,r_j,\ldots,r_j,\ldots).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The final determinant has $r_j$ twice and is zero, leaving the original value. The scaling rule follows directly from the same linearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个行列式出现两次 $r_j$，所以为零，剩下的就是原值。缩放规则也直接来自同一个线性条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the exchange rule, put $r+s$ into both row positions. This determinant is zero. Expanding those two positions gives four terms; the two with repeated $r$ or repeated $s$ vanish. Hence $D(\ldots,r,\ldots,s,\ldots)+D(\ldots,s,\ldots,r,\ldots)=0$, exactly the exchange rule. No division by two is used. In characteristic two, where $-x=x$, the same identity holds even though there is no visible numerical sign change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明换行规则时，在两个行位置都放入 $r+s$，这个行列式为零。对这两个位置展开得到四项，重复 $r$ 或重复 $s$ 的两项为零，所以 $D(\ldots,r,\ldots,s,\ldots)+D(\ldots,s,\ldots,r,\ldots)=0$，正是换行规则。这里没有除以二。在特征二的域上，$-x=x$，同一恒等式仍成立，只是看不到数值的正负变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Follow three operations in sequence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 一行计算

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work over the real numbers in this example. Start with $B$ below. Its two-by-two determinant is $1\cdot5-2\cdot3=-1$. Remove its lower-left entry, exchange rows, then scale the first row:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例使用实数。从下面的 $B$ 开始，二阶行列式为 $1\cdot5-2\cdot3=-1$。先消去左下角元素，再换行，最后缩放第一行：

$$
\begin{gathered}
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&5\end{pmatrix}
\xrightarrow{R_2\leftarrow R_2-3R_1}
U=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\\[4pt]
U=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}
\xrightarrow{R_1\leftrightarrow R_2}
V=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\\[4pt]
V=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}
\xrightarrow{R_1\leftarrow4R_1}
W=\begin{pmatrix}0&-4\\1&2\end{pmatrix}.
\end{gathered}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The determinants are $-1,-1,1,4$: first unchanged, then negated, then multiplied by four. If only the last value were known, $\det W=-4\det B$ would recover $\det B=-\det W/4=-1$. The [[三角矩阵行列式|diagonal-product rule for triangular matrices]] explains why $U$ is convenient in larger problems; [[消元计算行列式|determinant computation by elimination]] develops that procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式依次为 $-1,-1,1,4$：先不变，再取负，再乘以四。如果只知道最终值，由 $\det W=-4\det B$ 就能还原 $\det B=-\det W/4=-1$。[[三角矩阵行列式|三角矩阵的对角元乘积规则]]说明为什么中间的 $U$ 在高阶问题中很方便；[[消元计算行列式]]继续讲解这一过程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the old target-row coefficient in view
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 易错边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For distinct rows, $R_i\leftarrow cR_i+R_j$ multiplies the determinant by $c$: expansion gives $c\det A$ plus a repeated-row term. This remains valid at $c=0$, when the two rows become equal. In contrast, $R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$ keeps coefficient one on the target row and preserves the determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对不同的两行，$R_i\leftarrow cR_i+R_j$ 会把行列式乘以 $c$：展开后是 $c\det A$ 加上一个重复行为零的项。$c=0$ 时也成立，此时两行相同。相比之下，$R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$ 保留了目标行的系数一，所以行列式不变。

> [!warning] Simultaneous changes use different inputs · 同时改行使用的输入不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replacing both rows of $B$ by the sum of its **old** rows gives $\begin{pmatrix}4&7\\4&7\end{pmatrix}$, with determinant zero. This is not two successive determinant-preserving additions: the second step of a sequence uses the already updated matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $B$ 的两行都同时替换成**旧两行**之和，得到 $\begin{pmatrix}4&7\\4&7\end{pmatrix}$，行列式为零。这不是连续做两次保持行列式的倍加行：连续操作的第二步使用的是第一步更新后的矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividing a row by its nonzero pivot to make the pivot one also scales the determinant; restore the factor when finding the original value. Scaling every row by $c$ contributes $n$ factors, giving [[整体缩放的行列式|$\det(cA)=c^n\det A$]]. A determinant that is already zero remains zero after an exchange, despite the valid negation rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了把非零主元变成一而除以它，同样缩放了行列式，求原值时要还原这个因子。把每一行都乘以 $c$ 会贡献 $n$ 个因子，得到[[整体缩放的行列式|$\det(cA)=c^n\det A$]]。行列式已经为零时，换行后仍是零，但取负规则依然成立。
