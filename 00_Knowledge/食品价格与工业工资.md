# 工业工资若维持对农业食品收入的固定比例则食品相对价格上升会提高工业品计价的招工工资

检验[[无限劳动供给]]时，“农业边际产出为零”并不单独保证工厂能以固定工业品工资招工。如果工业报酬须与劳动者在农业可得的食品收入挂钩，食品相对工业品变贵就会增加工厂维持这一招工条件所需的工业品价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When assessing [[无限劳动供给|unlimited labour supply]], zero agricultural marginal product alone does not guarantee a constant industrial-goods recruitment wage. If industrial remuneration must track food income available in agriculture, more expensive food relative to industrial goods raises the industrial-goods value needed to preserve that recruitment condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设农业食品收入为 $b_A>0$，食品和工业品价格为 $P_A>0,P_M>0$，工业货币工资为 $W_M$。在明确假定工业工资按食品购买力保持农业收入的固定比例 $\theta>0$ 时，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let agricultural food income be $b_A>0$, food and industrial-goods prices $P_A>0,P_M>0$, and the industrial money wage $W_M$. If industrial wages maintain a fixed ratio $\theta>0$ to agricultural income in food purchasing power,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{W_M}{P_A}=\theta b_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\frac{W_M}{P_M}=\theta b_A\frac{P_A}{P_M}.
$$

所以当 $b_A$ 与 $\theta$ 不变、$P_A/P_M$ 上升时，工业品计价工资必然上升。比如 $b_A=8$、$\theta=1$，相对价格从1升至1.25，工业品工资从8升至10；它在食品单位上仍为8。一般消费篮子的实际工资是另一口径，不能把不同“实际工资”混为一谈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $b_A$ and $\theta$ fixed, a higher $P_A/P_M$ raises the industrial-goods wage. For $b_A=8$ and $\theta=1$, a relative price rise from 1 to 1.25 raises that wage from 8 to 10, while it remains 8 in food units. A real wage defined using a broader consumption basket is a different measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[费景汉–拉尼斯模型]]指出：当[[平均农业剩余]]下降，工业劳动者对应的食品供给趋紧，商品市场可能通过食品相对价格上涨调整。若上述工资挂钩仍维持，这会在农业食品收入尚未上升时便抬高工业用工成本。在工业技术、产品收入及其他成本给定时，增加的工资成本会压缩可用于积累的剩余；实际利润变化仍须把产出和其他价格变化一同核算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[费景汉–拉尼斯模型|Fei–Ranis model]] argues that declining [[平均农业剩余|average agricultural surplus]] tightens food availability per industrial worker, potentially raising food's relative price through the goods market. If the wage linkage is maintained, industrial labour costs rise even before agricultural food income increases. Given industrial technology, sales revenue and other costs, higher wage costs reduce the surplus available for accumulation. Actual profit changes still require accounting for output and other price changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里必须分清两步：工资计价恒等式能够给出**已经发生相对价格变化之后**的条件性效果，却不能仅由AAS下降唯一求出价格。需求偏好、消费替代、农业供给、跨部门交换及可能的食品进口都影响市场如何调整。固定总人口既不固定这些条件，也不等于固定粮价；从两点图推具体工资曲线，须说明采用了哪些价格与工资假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate two steps. The wage conversion establishes a conditional effect after a relative-price change has occurred; falling AAS alone does not uniquely determine that price. Preferences, consumption substitution, agricultural supply, intersectoral exchange and possible food imports affect adjustment. Constant population fixes neither these conditions nor food prices. A wage curve inferred from a two-point diagram therefore needs explicit price and wage assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也解释了[[农业短缺点与商业化点]]为何可能对应两种工资压力：先有食品价格反馈，后有农业边际产出达到制度收入后的用工竞争。若教学图先固定相对价格，就暂时关闭了第一种反馈，并未证明它在真实经济或原模型中不存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This explains two potential sources of wage pressure at the [[农业短缺点与商业化点|shortage and commercialization points]]: food-price feedback first, and agricultural wage competition once marginal product reaches institutional income. A teaching diagram that fixes relative prices temporarily shuts down the first feedback; it does not establish its absence from actual economies or the original model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ranis、Fei（1961），[A Theory of Economic Development](https://www.depfe.unam.mx/actividades/11/desarrollo-crecimiento/11-2_catcdejr_04_ranis_fei_1961.pdf#page=8)，印刷第539–540页、注8–9及第543页：食品相对价格、农业／工业工资的计价差别、工资相等或固定溢价，以及农业生产率改变食品剩余的渠道。第540页注9明确需要农业实际工资、AAS和偏好，未给出完整商品市场的严格推导。
- 上述工资换算与8→10的例子直接依固定比例假设推导；需求、供给和进口是判定商品市场如何调整所需的条件提示，不作为原文已经解出的价格方程或经验效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ranis and Fei support the relative-price mechanism and distinguish wage units. Footnotes 8–9 state the wage-linkage assumptions and the need for preferences, without a complete rigorous price derivation. The conversion and example follow directly from the stated fixed ratio; the market conditions are scope requirements, not an estimated or fully solved price equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
