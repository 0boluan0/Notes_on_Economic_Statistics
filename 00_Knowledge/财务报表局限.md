# 财务报表的确认范围与计量判断使其不能完整替代企业经济价值评估
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recognition boundaries and measurement judgments prevent financial statements from fully replacing an assessment of economic value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务报表提供受报告主体、期间和会计规则约束的信息，并不直接列出企业全部未来现金流的价值。确认哪些资源、如何估计回收与折旧、何时确认收入费用，都会影响账面金额；因此即使报表如实遵循准则，也不能把[[净值|账面权益]]直接当企业市场价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial statements report information bounded by entity, period, and accounting rules, not the value of every future cash flow. Recognition, recovery and depreciation estimates, and income timing affect carrying amounts. Even faithfully prepared statements therefore do not identify [[净值|book equity]] with market value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如两家公司各有账面资产 100、负债 40，账面权益都为 60；若未来客户需求、设备用途或管理能力不同，未来现金流和风险仍可能不同。这个比较只说明报表汇总金额不足以唯一决定价值，不说明任何估值结果都合理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two firms may each report assets of 100 and liabilities of 40, giving book equity of 60, while differences in demand, asset use, or management imply different future cash flows and risks. The example shows that aggregate accounting amounts do not uniquely determine value, not that any valuation is defensible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用时补读附注、行业和经营证据，并核查重大估计。也不要从“需要估计”跳到“数据不可信”：合理估计是会计的正常部分，是否失真须由[[盈利质量核查]]提供证据。资产并非全按历史成本计量，某些项目采用其他计量基础；比较必须识别具体项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read notes and business evidence and examine material estimates. Needing estimates does not itself make a report unreliable; [[盈利质量核查|earnings-quality checks]] supply evidence about distortion. Not every asset uses historical cost, so comparisons must identify the actual measurement basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
