# 非线性二元模型中连续变量的概率边际效应等于链接密度乘以完整指数对该变量的导数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a nonlinear binary model, the probability marginal effect of a continuous variable equals the link density times the derivative of the complete index with respect to that variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 完整导数
> 若 $p(X)=G(\eta(X))$，$g=G'$，则连续变量 $X_j$ 在给定 $X=x$ 处的边际效应是
> $$
> \frac{\partial p(x)}{\partial x_j}
> =g[\eta(x)]\frac{\partial\eta(x)}{\partial x_j}.
> $$
> 只有当 $X_j$ 只以 $\beta_jX_j$ 进入指数时，才简化为 $g(x'\beta)\beta_j$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $p(X)=G(\eta(X))$ and $g=G'$, then the marginal effect of a continuous variable $X_j$ at $X=x$ is $g[\eta(x)]\,\partial\eta(x)/\partial x_j$. It simplifies to $g(x'\beta)\beta_j$ only when $X_j$ enters the index solely as $\beta_jX_j$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个乘积有两层取值依赖。第一层是链接密度：同一个指数斜率在概率曲线中部通常对应较大概率变化，在尾部对应较小变化。第二层是完整指数导数：若 $\eta=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2+\beta_3XZ$，则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This product depends on the evaluation point in two ways. First, through the link density: the same index slope usually produces a larger probability change near the middle of the probability curve and a smaller change in its tails. Second, through the derivative of the complete index: if $\eta=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2+\beta_3XZ$, then that derivative must include every term involving $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial X}=\beta_1+2\beta_2X+\beta_3Z.
$$

因此单个 $\beta_1$ 的符号既不必等于完整指数斜率的符号，也不必等于概率边际效应的符号。logit 的 $g(z)=\Lambda(z)[1-\Lambda(z)]$，probit 的 $g(z)=\phi(z)$；两者密度为正，所以概率方向与完整指数导数方向相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, the sign of $\beta_1$ alone need not match either the sign of the complete index slope or the sign of the probability marginal effect. For logit, $g(z)=\Lambda(z)[1-\Lambda(z)]$; for probit, $g(z)=\phi(z)$. Both densities are positive, so the direction of the probability effect matches the direction of the derivative of the complete index.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边际效应是局部导数，只适合可作小幅连续变化的变量。0/1 或少量类别变量应使用 [[二元离散效应|完整离散变化]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A marginal effect is a local derivative and is appropriate only for a variable that can undergo a small continuous change. A 0/1 variable or a variable with only a few categories should instead use a [[二元离散效应|full discrete change]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
