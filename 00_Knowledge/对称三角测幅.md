# 对称三角测幅用形态价格高度从突破参考价投射候选目标

对称三角测幅是在已经识别[[对称三角形]]并按预定条件确认突破后，把形态的价格高度沿突破方向加到或减去突破参考价，得到一个候选价格目标。它是技术分析中的测幅约定，不是最低必达空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A symmetrical-triangle measured move projects the pattern's price height from a reference breakout price after a [[对称三角形|symmetrical triangle]] and its breakout have been identified under predefined criteria. The result is a candidate price target, not a guaranteed minimum move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定采样周期、形态起止、极值选择、线性价格口径和突破条件。令形态内最高峰价为 $P_H$、最低谷价为 $P_L$，价格高度为 $h=P_H-P_L>0$；两者通常位于较宽的起始区域，但不必发生在同一期。令 $P_b$ 为按既定规则取得的突破参考价，则：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the sampling interval, pattern endpoints, extrema, linear price convention and breakout rule. Let $P_H$ and $P_L$ be the pattern's highest peak and lowest trough, so $h=P_H-P_L>0$. They are usually in its wider initial region but need not occur in the same bar. For the predefined breakout reference price $P_b$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P_{\text{target}}=
\begin{cases}
P_b+h,&\text{upward breakout},\\
P_b-h,&\text{downward breakout}.
\end{cases}
$$

这里量的是纵轴价差，不是把时间与价格混在一起的斜线段长度，也不是屏幕上量得的像素距离。例如最高峰120、最低谷100，故 $h=20$；上破参考价112对应候选132，下破参考价108对应候选88。若改用对数价格轴，图上高度表示对数差，不能不换算便套用线性价差公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measure a price-axis difference, not the length of a diagonal combining time and price or a pixel distance. A peak of 120 and trough of 100 give $h=20$: reference breakouts at 112 upward or 108 downward give candidate targets of 132 or 88. Height on a logarithmic price axis is a log difference and cannot be inserted unchanged into this linear-price formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课另画了过形态上端、平行于下边界的斜辅助目标线。那条线给出的参考价格随时间变化，与固定的 $P_b+h$ 不是同一个对象。突破也不保证到达任一目标；目标的计算与实际成交时点要分开，见[[交易信号时序]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course also draws a sloping auxiliary target line through the upper part of the pattern, parallel to its lower boundary. Its reference price varies with time and is not the same object as the fixed value $P_b+h$. Neither target is guaranteed to be reached. Target calculation and actual execution timing remain separate under [[交易信号时序|signal timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
