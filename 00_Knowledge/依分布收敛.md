# Convergence in distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*依分布收敛 · 弱收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes the useful question is whether an approximation has the right **distribution of possible values**, even when its realised value is not close to a particular target on the same trial. Convergence in distribution describes this agreement of probability laws. It is the kind of limit used when a [[经典中心极限定理|central limit theorem]] approximates a standardised sum by a normal distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时我们关心的是一个近似是否具有正确的**取值分布**，即使在同一次试验中，它的实际值并不接近某个指定目标。依分布收敛描述的是概率律的这种趋近。[[经典中心极限定理]]用正态分布近似标准化的和时，用的就是这种极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[随机变量|random variable]] $Y$, its [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function (CDF)]] is $F_Y(x)=P(Y\le x)$: the total probability to the left of a cutoff $x$, including the cutoff. Comparing CDFs lets us compare both discrete and continuous laws without requiring densities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[随机变量]] $Y$，[[累积分布函数|累积分布函数（CDF）]]为 $F_Y(x)=P(Y\le x)$：它累计了截止点 $x$ 左侧、包含该点的全部概率。比较 CDF 可以同时处理离散和连续分布，不需要先有概率密度。

> [!important] Compare CDFs at continuity points · 在连续点比较 CDF
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $F_{X_n}$ and $F_X$ be the CDFs of the real-valued variables $X_n$ and $X$. We write $X_n\Rightarrow X$ or $X_n\xrightarrow dX$ when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$F_{X_n}(x)\longrightarrow F_X(x)$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> for every $x$ at which the **limiting** CDF $F_X$ is continuous. Only the individual laws matter, so the variables need not live on the same probability space. In this setting “weak convergence” is another name for convergence in distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 条件必须在**极限** CDF $F_X$ 的每个连续点 $x$ 成立；记为 $X_n\Rightarrow X$ 或 $X_n\xrightarrow dX$。这里只比较各自的概率律，因此变量不必在同一概率空间上。在这里，“弱收敛”也是依分布收敛的名称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why jumps are excluded
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么只检查连续点

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the deterministic variables $X_n=1/n$ and $X=0$. Their point masses move towards zero. For $x<0$, both CDFs equal zero. For a fixed $x>0$, eventually $1/n\le x$, so both CDFs equal one. At the cutoff $x=0$, however,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取确定性变量 $X_n=1/n$ 与 $X=0$，它们的单点概率质量逐渐移向零。若 $x<0$，两者的 CDF 都为零；若固定 $x>0$，最终会有 $1/n\le x$，因此两个 CDF 都为一。但在截止点 $x=0$，

$$F_{X_n}(0)=0,\qquad F_X(0)=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limiting CDF jumps at zero, so this disagreement is allowed. At all its continuity points we have convergence, giving $X_n\Rightarrow0$. Requiring convergence at the jump itself would reject even this elementary example of numbers approaching their limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限 CDF 在零点跳跃，所以允许这一点不一致。在它的所有连续点上都成立收敛，因此 $X_n\Rightarrow0$。如果连跳点也必须收敛，就会错误地排除这个普通数列趋近极限的例子。

> [!example] Same distribution, different realised value · 分布相同，实际值不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $B$ take zero and one with equal probability, and set $X=B$, $X_n=1-B$ for every $n$. Every $X_n$ has exactly the same CDF as $X$, so $X_n\Rightarrow X$ immediately. But $|X_n-X|=1$ on both outcomes. Thus $P(|X_n-X|>1/2)=1$, and the sequence does not converge to this $X$ [[依概率收敛|in probability]]. Matching laws cannot identify which outcomes should be paired.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $B$ 等概率取 0 和 1，取 $X=B$，并让每个 $X_n=1-B$。每个 $X_n$ 与 $X$ 的 CDF 完全相同，立即得到 $X_n\Rightarrow X$。但两种结果下都有 $|X_n-X|=1$，因此 $P(|X_n-X|>1/2)=1$，序列并不[[依概率收敛]]到这个 $X$。概率律相同并没有确定结果之间如何配对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the general distinction, see [[分布收敛不推概率收敛|why distributional convergence does not imply probability convergence]]. A [[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|constant limiting value]] is an important exception. In the other direction, [[概率收敛推出分布收敛|convergence in probability always implies convergence in distribution]]. Distributional convergence alone also makes no promise about convergence of means or variances; those involve the size of tail values as well as their probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般区别见[[分布收敛不推概率收敛]]，而[[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|极限为常数]]是重要的例外。另一个方向上，[[概率收敛推出分布收敛|依概率收敛总能推出依分布收敛]]。依分布收敛本身也不保证均值或方差收敛；这些量除了尾部概率，还受到尾部取值大小的影响。

> [!question]- A normal approximation · 一个正态极限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X_n\sim N(0,1+1/n)$, does $X_n\Rightarrow Z$ for $Z\sim N(0,1)$? Yes. By [[正态标准化|normal standardisation]], $F_{X_n}(x)=\Phi(x/\sqrt{1+1/n})\to\Phi(x)$, where $\Phi$ is the continuous CDF of $Z$. This calculation specifies only the distributions. It does not establish probability convergence to an arbitrarily chosen $Z$ on an existing probability space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X_n\sim N(0,1+1/n)$，是否依分布收敛到 $Z\sim N(0,1)$？是。由[[正态标准化]]，$F_{X_n}(x)=\Phi(x/\sqrt{1+1/n})\to\Phi(x)$，其中 $\Phi$ 是 $Z$ 的连续 CDF。但计算只指定了分布，没有证明它依概率收敛到既有概率空间上任意选定的 $Z$。
