# 人口惯性是既有年龄结构在生育死亡与迁移条件给定后仍对未来人口规模变化产生的影响

人口惯性指过去出生、死亡与迁移留下的年龄结构继续影响后续人口变化：每位女性生育得少一些，不代表处于生育年龄的女性总数也立即减少。相反，较老的年龄结构也可能使人口在生育回升后仍继续缩小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Population momentum is the continuing influence of the age distribution inherited from past births, deaths and migration. Fewer births per woman need not immediately mean fewer women at reproductive ages. Conversely, an older starting population can continue shrinking even after fertility rises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用年龄别生育率 $f_a$ 乘以同期女性暴露 $F_a$ 人年，出生总数为 $B=\sum_a f_aF_a$。即使 $f_a$ 下降，较大的出生队列进入生育年龄仍可能推高出生数。最终人口增减还要扣除死亡、加入净迁移，见[[人口变动恒等式]]；这解释了为什么单看[[总和生育率]]不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying age-specific fertility $f_a$ by female exposure $F_a$ in woman-years gives $B=\sum_a f_aF_a$. Even if $f_a$ falls, a larger cohort reaching reproductive ages can raise births. Population change must also subtract deaths and add net migration through the [[人口变动恒等式|balancing equation]], so the [[总和生育率|total fertility rate]] alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要把惯性单独量化，需要指定反事实。WPP2024 的“惯性情景”同时设定：生育立即达到更替水平、死亡条件固定、净迁移为零，再观察初始年龄结构带来的变化。更替水平意指考虑生存至生育年龄的死亡后，每代恰好替代自身，不是把任何人口的生育率一律设成 2。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quantifying momentum separately requires a counterfactual. WPP2024’s momentum scenario combines immediate replacement-level fertility, constant mortality and zero migration to isolate the initial age structure’s effect. Replacement means that each generation replaces itself after allowing for mortality up to reproductive ages; it is not universally equivalent to setting fertility to 2.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“人口会继续增长多少年”不能仅由惯性的名称推断，必须结合完整年龄结构和明确的生育、死亡、迁移情景计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The label “momentum” alone cannot tell how many more years growth will continue. That requires the full age distribution and specified fertility, mortality and migration assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
