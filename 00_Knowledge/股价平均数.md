# 股价平均数是在同一时点对样本股票价格按指定权重求得的平均值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An average stock price is the weighted average of constituent prices at a given time.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股价平均数回答“一组样本股当前的平均价格是多少”，不是单只股票的跨日平均。对至少一只样本股，简单平均给各股票相同数量权重；加权平均先声明权数 $q_i$ 代表发行股数、成交量还是其他给定数量，并要求 $q_i\geq0$、$\sum_iq_i>0$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This statistic averages across at least one sample stock, not across dates for one stock. The simple mean uses equal quantity weights; a weighted mean must specify what $q_i$ represents, such as outstanding shares or traded quantities, with $q_i\geq0$ and $\sum_iq_i>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar p=\frac1n\sum_i p_i,
\qquad \bar p_q=\frac{\sum_i p_iq_i}{\sum_iq_i}.
$$

课堂四只股票为 10、15、25、30 元，简单平均是 20 元。这个数保留价格单位，受每股面额和拆股影响；若要衡量可比较的市场变化，还需要[[股票价格指数|指数化]]及必要的[[指数除数调整]]。这里 $q_i/\sum q_j$ 是价格的权重，不是[[市值加权指数|收益率的市值权重]] $p_iq_i/\sum p_jq_j$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course prices 10, 15, 25 and 30 have a mean of 20. The result has price units and is sensitive to share denomination and splits. Comparable market changes require [[股票价格指数|index construction]] and appropriate [[指数除数调整|divisor adjustments]]. The weights on prices here, $q_i/\sum q_j$, differ from the [[市值加权指数|market-value weights on returns]], $p_iq_i/\sum p_jq_j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
