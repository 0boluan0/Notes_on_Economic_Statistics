# Expected time to absorption
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*期望吸收时间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing where a process will end does not tell us how long it will take. The **expected absorption time** averages the number of transitions until the first entry into any absorbing state. For a finite absorbing chain, it can be found by adding the expected visits to all intermediate states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道过程最终停在哪里，并不等于知道要等多久。**期望吸收时间**是首次进入任一吸收状态以前，所走转移步数的平均。对有限吸收链，把所有中间状态的期望访问次数加起来，就能得到它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Count steps along one path first
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先沿一条路径数步数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $u,v$ are intermediate states and $a,b$ are [[吸收状态|absorbing states]], which cannot be left once entered. On the path $u\to u\to v\to b$, the first absorption occurs at time $3$. The states occupied at times $0,1,2$ are $u,u,v$: exactly three visits before absorption. The visit to $b$ at time $3$ does not add another waiting step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $u,v$ 是中间状态，$a,b$ 是一旦进入就不能离开的[[吸收状态]]。对路径 $u\to u\to v\to b$，首次吸收发生在时刻 $3$。时刻 $0,1,2$ 所在状态是 $u,u,v$，恰好有三次吸收前访问；时刻 $3$ 已经到达 $b$，不会再多贡献一步等待。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This explains both ends of the counting convention. Starting in a transient state and moving straight into absorption takes one step. Starting in an absorbing state takes zero steps. Including the initial transient visit is exactly what makes the visit count equal the number of transitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也说明了计数的两个端点：从暂态状态下一步直接吸收，用一步；从吸收状态出发，用零步。计入初始的暂态访问，恰好使访问次数等于转移步数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The finite absorbing-chain setting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限吸收链中的设置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a finite, time-homogeneous [[吸收链|absorbing Markov chain]], with every nonabsorbing state able to reach an absorbing state along a positive-probability path. Let $A$ be the set of absorbing states and define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用有限、时间齐次的[[吸收链]]，每个非吸收状态都能沿正概率路径到达某个吸收状态。令 $A$ 为吸收状态集合，定义

$$\tau=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\in A\},\qquad t_i=E_i[\tau].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $X_n$ is the state at time $n$, and $E_i$ is [[期望|expectation]] starting at $X_0=i$. The time counts discrete steps, and $t_i$ need not be an integer: it is an average across paths. Finiteness and the reachability condition guarantee [[吸收链必然吸收|almost-sure absorption]]. In fact they give a geometric bound in fixed-length blocks, so every $t_i$ is finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X_n$ 是时刻 $n$ 的状态，$E_i$ 是固定从 $X_0=i$ 出发时的[[期望]]。时间按离散步数计算，$t_i$ 不必是整数，因为它是跨路径的平均。有限性与可达条件保证[[吸收链必然吸收|几乎必然吸收]]，而且能在固定长度的时间段上给出几何尾界，因此每个 $t_i$ 都有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put the $m\ge1$ transient states first, using the [[吸收链标准形|row-convention block form]] $P=\begin{pmatrix}Q&R\\0&I\end{pmatrix}$. Here $Q$ records transitions among transient states, $R$ records exits to absorbing states, and the identity block $I$ keeps each absorbing state fixed. The $m\times m$ [[吸收链基本矩阵|fundamental matrix]] is $N=(I_m-Q)^{-1}$, where $I_m$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Its entry $N_{ij}$ gives the expected number of visits to transient state $j$ before absorption, from transient start $i$, including the initial time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $m\ge1$ 个暂态状态排在前面，采用[[吸收链标准形|行约定的分块形式]] $P=\begin{pmatrix}Q&R\\0&I\end{pmatrix}$。其中 $Q$ 记录暂态间转移，$R$ 记录进入吸收状态的出口，单位矩阵块 $I$ 使每个吸收状态停在自身。$m\times m$ 的[[吸收链基本矩阵|基本矩阵]]为 $N=(I_m-Q)^{-1}$，其中 $I_m$ 为[[单位矩阵]]。元素 $N_{ij}$ 表示从暂态起点 $i$ 出发、吸收前访问暂态状态 $j$ 的期望次数，包括初始时刻。

> [!important] Add an entire row · 把整行相加
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $t=(t_1,\ldots,t_m)^{\mathsf T}$ be the column of mean times for transient starting states; $\mathsf T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Let $\mathbf1_m$ be the column with $m$ entries all equal to one. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $t=(t_1,\ldots,t_m)^{\mathsf T}$ 为各暂态起点的平均时间列向量；$\mathsf T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。令 $\mathbf1_m$ 为 $m$ 个元素全等于一的列向量，则
>
> $$t=N\mathbf1_m,\qquad t_i=\sum_{j=1}^{m}N_{ij}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every transient state visited consumes time. Reading only $N_{ii}$ counts returns and visits to the starting state, while missing time spent in the other transient states.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 经过任何暂态状态都会耗费时间。只读 $N_{ii}$，只能数到对起点的访问和回访，却会漏掉在其他暂态状态花费的时间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the identity on each path, let $V_j=\sum_{n=0}^{\tau-1}\mathbf1_{\{X_n=j\}}$ count pre-absorption visits to state $j$. The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] is one when $X_n=j$ and zero otherwise. Before absorption, each time belongs to exactly one transient state, so $\sum_jV_j=\tau$. Taking expectations and using finite [[期望线性性|linearity]] gives $E_i\tau=\sum_jE_iV_j=\sum_jN_{ij}$. No independence among the visit counts is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐条路径看，令 $V_j=\sum_{n=0}^{\tau-1}\mathbf1_{\{X_n=j\}}$ 统计吸收前访问状态 $j$ 的次数；其中[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]在 $X_n=j$ 时为一，否则为零。吸收以前，每个时刻都恰好属于一个暂态状态，所以 $\sum_jV_j=\tau$。取期望，再用有限求和的[[期望线性性]]，就有 $E_i\tau=\sum_jE_iV_j=\sum_jN_{ij}$。这里不要求各状态的访问次数独立。

## A two-stage calculation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个阶段的计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the same chain as in [[吸收概率矩阵为NR|absorption probabilities]]: from $u$, stay with probability $1/2$, move to $v$ with probability $1/4$, and finish at $a$ with probability $1/4$; from $v$, stay with probability $1/2$, or finish at $a,b$ with probability $1/4$ each. Its transient block and fundamental matrix are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用[[吸收概率矩阵为NR|吸收概率]]中的同一条链：从 $u$ 以 $1/2$ 留下、以 $1/4$ 到 $v$、以 $1/4$ 在 $a$ 结束；从 $v$ 以 $1/2$ 留下，以各 $1/4$ 在 $a,b$ 结束。暂态块与基本矩阵为

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/4\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
N=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
t=N\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}3\\2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $u$, the average path has two visits to $u$ and one to $v$ before absorption, for three steps in total. This is an expectation statement, not a claim that every path follows that pattern. From $v$, the average wait is two steps; $u$ is never visited.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $u$ 出发，吸收前平均访问 $u$ 两次、访问 $v$ 一次，总计三步。这是期望陈述，并不是说每条路径都按这个模式行走。从 $v$ 出发，平均等两步，且不会访问 $u$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望命中时间方程|first-step mean-time equations]] provide a direct check: after paying one step, only transitions into transient states leave further waiting. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望命中时间方程|平均命中时间的第一步方程]]给出直接核对：先用掉一步，只有进入暂态状态时还需要继续等待。因此

$$t=\mathbf1_m+Qt,\qquad
 t_v=1+\tfrac12t_v=2,\qquad
 t_u=1+\tfrac12t_u+\tfrac14t_v=3.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, solving $(I_m-Q)t=\mathbf1_m$ gives the same $N\mathbf1_m$. The two absorbing destinations affect where the path ends; for waiting until either one, the immediate-exit contribution to the remaining time is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，解 $(I_m-Q)t=\mathbf1_m$ 就得到同一个 $N\mathbf1_m$。两个吸收终点决定路径最终去哪里；若只问何时到达其中任意一个，则直接退出之后的剩余等待贡献都是零。

> [!example] One transient state · 一个暂态状态
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the only transient state is revisited with probability $0\le q<1$, absorption occurs on the next step with probability $1-q$. The row-sum formula becomes
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若唯一的暂态状态以概率 $0\le q<1$ 留在自身，则下一步以概率 $1-q$ 吸收。行和公式化为
>
> $$N=(1-q)^{-1},\qquad E\tau=(1-q)^{-1}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $q=0$, absorption takes exactly one step; for $q=1/2$, the mean is two. This [[几何分布|waiting time]] has probabilities $\Pr(\tau=k)=q^{k-1}(1-q)$ for integers $k\ge1$: wait for $k-1$ steps, then exit. It counts the final successful step as well. Equivalently, $\Pr(\tau>n)=q^n$ for integers $n\ge0$, so the tail-sum formula gives the [[几何级数|geometric series]] $E\tau=\sum_{n\ge0}q^n=(1-q)^{-1}$. At $q=1$, the formerly transient state itself becomes absorbing, so this transient-block setup no longer applies. If the target is any absorbing state of the current model, starting there gives time zero. If the target remains the original external endings, it is never reached and that different hitting time has infinite mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $q=0$ 时恰好一步吸收；当 $q=1/2$ 时平均两步。这个[[几何分布|等待时间]]的概率为 $\Pr(\tau=k)=q^{k-1}(1-q)$（整数 $k\ge1$）：先等待 $k-1$ 步，再退出，最后成功的那一步也计入。等价地，$\Pr(\tau>n)=q^n$（整数 $n\ge0$），所以尾概率和公式给出[[几何级数]] $E\tau=\sum_{n\ge0}q^n=(1-q)^{-1}$。当 $q=1$ 时，原先的暂态状态自身变成吸收状态，以上暂态块设置失效。若目标是当前模型的任意吸收状态，从这里出发时间为零；若目标仍固定为原来的外部终点，则永远不到达，那个不同的命中时间均值才是无穷。

## Keep the target and the scope fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保持目标与范围一致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the unconditional mean time to **any** absorbing state. It is not the conditional mean given that a selected destination is reached, nor the time to a selected absorbing state when another one can be reached first. Starting already absorbed gives time zero, outside the transient vector $t$; if all states are absorbing, every starting time is zero and no transient calculation is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是到达**任一**吸收状态的无条件平均时间，不是已知最终到达某个指定终点后的条件平均，也不是在可能先到其他吸收状态时、等待某个指定吸收状态的时间。若起点已经吸收，则时间为零，不属于暂态向量 $t$；若全部状态都吸收，任何起点的时间都为零，无须计算暂态块。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite absorbing-chain assumptions ensure the [[基本矩阵存在条件|existence and finite entries of $N$]]. If a nonabsorbing closed class is included in $Q$, the inverse formula is unavailable. For infinite-state chains, even almost-sure arrival can coexist with an infinite mean, as shown by [[必达不推有限期望|the fair-walk counterexample]]. The general expected-hitting-time equations still make sense with nonnegative infinite values; this finite matrix formula does not replace their assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限吸收链条件保证[[基本矩阵存在条件|$N$ 存在且元素有限]]。若把非吸收闭类塞入 $Q$，求逆公式就不可用。对无限状态链，即使几乎必达，平均时间也可能无穷，见[[必达不推有限期望|公平游走反例]]。一般的期望命中时间方程仍可允许非负无穷值，但这个有限矩阵公式不能替代对条件的检查。
