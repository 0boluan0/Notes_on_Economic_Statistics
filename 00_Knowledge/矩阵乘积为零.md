# A matrix product AB is zero exactly when the column space of B lies in the nullspace of A
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵乘积 AB 为零当且仅当 B 的列空间包含于 A 的零空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For compatible real matrices $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ and $B\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对尺寸相容的实矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$、$B\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$，

$$
AB=0\iff C(B)\subseteq N(A).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $B=[b_1\ \cdots\ b_p]$. By [[矩阵乘积逐列作用|columnwise multiplication]], $AB=[Ab_1\ \cdots\ Ab_p]$. The product vanishes exactly when every column $b_j$ belongs to [[零空间|$N(A)$]]. Since the nullspace is a subspace, this is equivalent to all of their span, [[列空间|$C(B)$]], lying in it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写成 $B=[b_1\ \cdots\ b_p]$。根据[[矩阵乘积逐列作用|逐列乘法]]，$AB=[Ab_1\ \cdots\ Ab_p]$。乘积为零当且仅当每个 $b_j$ 属于[[零空间|$N(A)$]]；零空间对线性组合封闭，所以这也等价于它们的整个张成，即[[列空间|$C(B)$]]，包含于其中。

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both factors here are nonzero: the second produces only multiples of $(1,1)^T$, which the first sends to zero. Thus a zero product does not force a factor to be zero. If $A$ has trivial nullspace, however, the same criterion forces $B=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例子中两个因子都非零：后面的矩阵只产生 $(1,1)^T$ 的倍数，而前面的矩阵将这一方向映成零，所以不能由零乘积断言某个因子为零。不过若 $A$ 的零空间只有零向量，同一判据便迫使 $B=0$。
