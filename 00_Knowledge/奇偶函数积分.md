# Integrating odd and even functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*奇偶函数积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On an interval symmetric about zero, opposite inputs can make equal or opposite contributions to an integral. For $f(x)=x$, the part below the axis on $[-1,0]$ cancels the part above it on $[0,1]$, giving zero. For $f(x)=x^2$, the two halves contribute equally, so computing one half and doubling gives $2\int_0^1x^2\,dx=2/3$. These are statements about signed [[定积分|definite integrals]], not about always adding positive geometric areas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在关于零对称的区间上，互为相反数的输入可以给积分带来相同或相反的贡献。例如 $f(x)=x$ 在 $[-1,0]$ 上位于横轴下方，其贡献与 $[0,1]$ 上的正贡献抵消，积分为零。$f(x)=x^2$ 的两半贡献相同，只算一半再加倍就得到 $2\int_0^1x^2\,dx=2/3$。这里讨论的是带符号的[[定积分]]，不是一律取正的几何面积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real-valued function defined on $[-a,a]$, **odd** means $f(-x)=-f(x)$ for every $x$ in that interval, while **even** means $f(-x)=f(x)$. Oddness in particular forces $f(0)=0$. The number $a>0$ is finite and is the distance of either endpoint from zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对定义在 $[-a,a]$ 上的实值函数，**奇函数**是指区间内每个 $x$ 都满足 $f(-x)=-f(x)$，**偶函数**则满足 $f(-x)=f(x)$。奇函数尤其必须有 $f(0)=0$。这里 $a>0$ 是有限数，表示两端点到零的距离。

> [!abstract] Cancellation or doubling · 抵消或加倍
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Assume $f$ is Riemann integrable on the **whole** interval $[-a,a]$. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $f$ 在**整个** $[-a,a]$ 上黎曼可积，则
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> f(-x)=-f(x)&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\int_{-a}^{a}f(x)\,dx=0,\\
> f(-x)=f(x)&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\int_{-a}^{a}f(x)\,dx=2\int_0^a f(x)\,dx.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[连续函数可积|Continuity on the closed interval]] is sufficient for integrability, but is not required by these identities. At $a=0$, the interval is degenerate and every integral displayed is zero by the equal-bound convention.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[连续函数可积|闭区间上的连续性]]足以保证可积，但这两条恒等式并不要求连续。若 $a=0$，区间退化，按相同端点积分为零的约定，上述积分都为零。

## Reflect the small pieces, not just the symbols
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把小区间逐一反射*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[定积分区间可加性|Interval additivity]] splits the integral at zero; integrability on the whole interval also gives integrability on each half. The key identity is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[定积分区间可加性]]允许在零点拆开积分；整段可积也保证两个半段各自可积。关键恒等式是

$$\int_{-a}^{0}f(x)\,dx=\int_0^a f(-u)\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For continuous $f$, this follows from [[定积分换元|substitution]] with $u=-x$. To justify the full Riemann-integrable case, use the definition by [[黎曼和|Riemann sums]]. Partition $[0,a]$ as $0=u_0<u_1<\cdots<u_n=a$, and choose a sample point $\xi_i$ in the $i$th piece. Reflect that piece to $[-u_i,-u_{i-1}]$ and its sample point to $-\xi_i$. The width is still $u_i-u_{i-1}$, so the sum for $u\mapsto f(-u)$ is exactly the sum for $f$ on the reflected partition:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$f$ 连续时，可用 $u=-x$ 的[[定积分换元]]得到它。要覆盖一般黎曼可积函数，则直接使用[[黎曼和]]定义。把 $[0,a]$ 分成 $0=u_0<u_1<\cdots<u_n=a$，在第 $i$ 段内选采样点 $\xi_i$。将该段反射为 $[-u_i,-u_{i-1}]$，采样点反射为 $-\xi_i$。宽度仍是 $u_i-u_{i-1}$，因此 $u\mapsto f(-u)$ 的和，恰好就是 $f$ 在反射分割上的和：

$$\sum_{i=1}^n f(-\xi_i)(u_i-u_{i-1}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reflection preserves the largest piece width and pairs **every** partition and sample choice on the right with one on the left. Since all sufficiently fine left-half sums approach the same integral, so do all these right-half sums. This proves both integrability of $f(-u)$ and the displayed identity without assuming continuity. Adding the right-half integral now gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反射保持最大段宽不变，并把右半段的**每一种**分割与采样选择对应到左半段。左半段所有充分细的和都趋于同一个积分，右半段这些和也就如此。这同时证明了 $f(-u)$ 的可积性及上面的恒等式，没有假设连续。再加上右半段积分，便得到

$$\int_{-a}^{a}f(x)\,dx=\int_0^a\bigl[f(-u)+f(u)\bigr]\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an odd function the bracket is zero at every point; for an even function it is $2f(u)$. This proves cancellation and doubling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

奇函数的方括号在每一点都是零；偶函数的方括号为 $2f(u)$。抵消与加倍由此得到证明。

> [!example] A discontinuous function still works · 不连续也可以使用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[-1,1]$, take $f(x)=-1$ for $x<0$, $f(0)=0$, and $f(x)=1$ for $x>0$. This is odd and Riemann integrable: only pieces touching zero can have differing upper and lower rectangle heights; their total width is at most twice the largest piece width, and their height difference is at most two. The upper/lower-sum gap therefore tends to zero. Its integral is $-1+1=0$ although it jumps at zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[-1,1]$ 上，令 $x<0$ 时 $f(x)=-1$，$f(0)=0$，$x>0$ 时 $f(x)=1$。它是奇函数，也黎曼可积：只有接触零点的小段可能有不同的上、下矩形高度；这些段的总宽度不超过最大段宽的两倍，高度差不超过二。所以上下和之差趋于零。尽管零点有跳跃，积分仍是 $-1+1=0$。

## Check the whole integrand and the whole interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检查整个被积函数与整个区间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product $x^3e^{-x^2}$ is odd because replacing $x$ by $-x$ changes only the cubic factor's sign. Its integral over $[-2,2]$ is therefore zero without finding an [[原函数|antiderivative]]. The Gaussian integrand $e^{-x^2}$ is even, so its integral there is $2\int_0^2e^{-x^2}\,dx$; symmetry reduces the work but does not supply an elementary antiderivative. The sum $x+x^2$ is neither odd nor even: integrating its two terms separately on $[-1,1]$ gives $0+2/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积 $x^3e^{-x^2}$ 是奇函数，因为把 $x$ 换成 $-x$ 只改变三次幂因子的符号。因此在 $[-2,2]$ 上的积分为零，不必先找[[原函数]]。高斯被积函数 $e^{-x^2}$ 是偶函数，同一区间上的积分为 $2\int_0^2e^{-x^2}\,dx$；对称性减少了工作，却没有给出初等原函数。和式 $x+x^2$ 既不奇也不偶：在 $[-1,1]$ 上分别积分两项，得到 $0+2/3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limits matter too: oddness of $x$ does not make $\int_0^1x\,dx=1/2$ vanish. The cancellation rule needs a symmetric interval about the same zero used in the parity identities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上下限也很重要：$x$ 是奇函数，并不会使 $\int_0^1x\,dx=1/2$ 消失。抵消要求积分区间关于奇偶关系中使用的同一个零点对称。

> [!warning] A singularity cannot be cancelled away · 不能把奇点直接抵消掉
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Although $1/x$ changes sign under reflection, it is undefined and unbounded near zero. The ordinary [[反常积分|improper integral]] across zero requires both one-sided integrals to converge separately. Here $\int_\varepsilon^1dx/x=-\log\varepsilon\to+\infty$ and $\int_{-1}^{-\varepsilon}dx/x=\log\varepsilon\to-\infty$. Their symmetric truncated sum is zero, but that coordinated cancellation is not convergence of the ordinary improper integral.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 虽然 $1/x$ 反射后变号，它在零点无定义且在附近无界。跨过零点的普通[[反常积分]]要求左右两侧分别收敛。这里 $\int_\varepsilon^1dx/x=-\log\varepsilon\to+\infty$，而 $\int_{-1}^{-\varepsilon}dx/x=\log\varepsilon\to-\infty$。对称截断之和虽为零，这种把两个截断绑定在一起的抵消，却不表示普通反常积分收敛。
