# 对称区间上的可积奇函数积分为零而偶函数积分可折半后加倍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*On a symmetric interval, an integrable odd function cancels and an even function reduces to twice a half-interval integral*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认 $a>0$、$f$ 在整个 $[-a,a]$ 可积，再检查**完整被积函数**：
$$f(-x)=-f(x)\Rightarrow\int_{-a}^{a}f=0,\qquad
f(-x)=f(x)\Rightarrow\int_{-a}^{a}f=2\int_0^af.$$
把左半段作 $u=-x$ 的[[定积分换元]]，就得到 $\int_{-a}^0f(x)dx=\int_0^af(-u)du$，正负抵消或加倍由此而来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First establish integrability on the whole symmetric interval, then test parity of the entire integrand. Substituting the negative variable on the left half turns it into the reflected integrand on the right half, yielding cancellation or doubling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x^3e^{-x^2}$ 是奇函数，所以在 $[-2,2]$ 上积分为零，不必寻找原函数。$e^{-x^2}$ 是偶函数，所以同一区间的积分是 $2\int_0^2e^{-x^2}dx$，但这并不自动给出初等闭式。$1/x$ 跨过零点则必须先按[[反常积分]]检查收敛，不能只用奇性宣布积分为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first integrand is odd and integrates to zero over symmetric bounds; the Gaussian integrand is even and halves the work without producing an elementary closed form. For $1/x$ across zero, [[反常积分|improper convergence]] must be checked before any symmetry shortcut is valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
