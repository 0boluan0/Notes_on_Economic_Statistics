# 国债期货转换因子是按合约标准收益率折算可交割债券价格的无量纲系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Treasury futures conversion factor is a dimensionless coefficient that normalizes a deliverable bond's price at the contract's standard yield*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

国债期货允许交付的债券可能有不同票息和剩余期限。转换因子（CF）把这些差异折算为交割报价的乘数：按合约指定的标准收益率和日期规则，计算可交割债券每单位面值的理论净价。用每百元面值报价时，其基本量纲关系是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deliverable Treasury bonds can differ in coupon and remaining maturity. A conversion factor turns those differences into a delivery-price multiplier: the theoretical clean price per unit of face value under the contract's standard yield and date conventions. For prices quoted per 100 face value,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$CF=\frac{P^{\mathrm{clean},*}_{100}}{100}.$$

星号表示**按标准规则计算**，不是市场今天的到期收益率。标准收益率为 6% 不代表可交割债券本身的票面利率都是 6%，也不意味着市场实际能赚到 6%。本课程原 PDF 用中金所 3% 的框架；CME 的美国国债期货说明采用 6%。具体合约的可交割条件、月份取整、付息和系数精度必须分别查规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The asterisk means a standard-rule calculation, not today's market yield. A 6% standard does not make every deliverable bond's coupon 6%, nor promise a 6% realized return. The course PDF uses a 3% CFFEX framework; CME's US Treasury documentation uses 6%. Eligibility, date rounding, payment frequency, and factor precision remain contract-specific.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只为看清价格与系数的区别，取一个**恰在付息后、无应计利息**的简化例：面值 100，年票息率 5%，每半年付 2.5，剩余三年，共六期。假设标准名义年收益率为 6%、半年复利，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To isolate the units, consider a simplified bond immediately after a coupon payment, with no accrued interest: face value 100, annual coupon 5%, six remaining semiannual payments of 2.5, and a 6% nominal annual standard yield compounded semiannually.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P^*_{100}=\sum_{j=1}^{6}\frac{2.5}{1.03^j}+\frac{100}{1.03^6}
\approx97.2914043,\qquad CF\approx0.9729140.$$

若按四位小数展示，CF 为 0.9729。97.2914 是每百元面值的理论价格，不能直接当 CF 乘期货报价。这个算例没有指定真实债券代码和交割月份，不能冒作某一在市合约的官方 CF。交割时怎样使用系数见[[国债期货交割货款]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At four decimal places the factor is 0.9729. The number 97.2914 is a price per 100 face value, not the multiplier. No actual bond code or delivery month is specified, so this is not an official factor for a listed contract. Its delivery use is explained in [[国债期货交割货款|Treasury futures invoice amounts]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
