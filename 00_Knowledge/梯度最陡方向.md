# Steepest ascent in Euclidean geometry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*梯度最陡方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine standing at a point on a height map and choosing the direction of a very small step. To compare directions fairly, give each step the same length. The gradient identifies the direction with the greatest **first-order** increase when length is measured by the usual Euclidean rule. It answers a local rate question, so an arbitrarily large step need not behave the same way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象站在一张高度图的某点，选择一个很小的迈步方向。为了公平比较，要让每一步长度相同。用通常的欧氏规则衡量长度时，梯度给出**一阶**上升最快的方向。这回答的是局部变化率问题，任意大的步子不一定有同样表现。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be [[全微分|differentiable]] at $a$, where $U$ is an [[开集|open subset]] of $\mathbb R^n$ and $n\ge1$. An open domain allows sufficiently small steps in every direction. Differentiability means one linear approximation describes all small displacements. Write $g=\nabla f(a)$: this column vector collects the [[偏导数|coordinate partial derivatives]], each measuring change while the other inputs stay fixed. The [[方向导数梯度公式|gradient formula]] then gives $D_vf(a)=g^Tv$, the dot product of $g$ and the step vector $v$; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Mere existence of the partial derivatives is not the assumption here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:U\to\mathbb R$ 在 $a$ 处[[全微分|可微]]，其中 $U$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 的[[开集]]，$n\ge1$。开域允许朝每个方向迈出足够小的步子；可微则表示同一个线性近似能描述所有小位移。记 $g=\nabla f(a)$，这是把各[[偏导数]]排成列的向量，每个偏导数考察其他输入不动时的变化。[[方向导数梯度公式]]给出 $D_vf(a)=g^Tv$，也就是梯度与步向量的点积；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。这里的假设不只是偏导数存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] is $\|v\|_2=\sqrt{v_1^2+\cdots+v_n^2}$. We compare **unit vectors**, $\|v\|_2=1$. Otherwise multiplying a favorable vector by a larger positive number would increase its parameter rate without changing its geometric direction; [[方向导数尺度|directional-derivative scaling]] explains that distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]为 $\|v\|_2=\sqrt{v_1^2+\cdots+v_n^2}$。这里比较**单位向量**，即 $\|v\|_2=1$。否则，把一个有利向量乘以更大的正数，就能在不改变几何方向的情况下增大参数变化率；[[方向导数尺度]]解释了这个区别。

> [!important] The maximizing unit direction · 最优单位方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $g\ne0$, the unique maximizing unit vector is $v_*=g/\|g\|_2$, and its rate is $\|g\|_2$. The unique minimizing vector is $-v_*$, with rate $-\|g\|_2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $g\ne0$，唯一的最大化单位向量为 $v_*=g/\|g\|_2$，最大变化率为 $\|g\|_2$。唯一的最小化向量为 $-v_*$，变化率为 $-\|g\|_2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the maximum and uniqueness hold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么最大值与唯一性成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz inequality]] bounds a dot product by the product of the lengths. For a unit vector,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]用两个向量的长度乘积控制点积。对单位向量，有

$$-\|g\|_2\le g^Tv\le\|g\|_2\|v\|_2=\|g\|_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the upper bound, equality in Cauchy–Schwarz requires the two nonzero vectors to be parallel, and a positive dot product requires the same orientation. The unit constraint fixes the multiplier to $1/\|g\|_2$. This proves both attainment and uniqueness. Reversing the vector gives the lower bound. In dimension $n\ge2$, every intermediate rate occurs: choose a unit $w$ perpendicular to $g$ and vary $v=\cos\theta\,v_*+\sin\theta\,w$. Then $g^Tv=\|g\|_2\cos\theta$. In dimension $1$, the only unit vectors are $1$ and $-1$, so for $g\ne0$ only the two endpoint rates occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

达到上界时，Cauchy–Schwarz 的等号要求两个非零向量平行；点积为正又要求同向。单位长度把比例固定为 $1/\|g\|_2$，因此最大值确实取得，而且方向唯一。反向给出下界。在 $n\ge2$ 时，每个中间变化率也能取得：取与 $g$ 垂直的单位向量 $w$，让 $v=\cos\theta\,v_*+\sin\theta\,w$ 随角度变化，就有 $g^Tv=\|g\|_2\cos\theta$。在一维，单位向量只有 $1,-1$，所以当 $g\ne0$ 时只能取得两个端点变化率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A unit vector perpendicular to $g$ gives zero first-order change. That says nothing by itself about higher-order change. For example, $f(x,y)=x+y^2$ at the origin has gradient $(1,0)^T$. The vertical unit vector gives rate zero, although $f(0,t)-f(0,0)=t^2>0$ for every nonzero $t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与 $g$ 垂直的单位向量给出零一阶变化，但不能据此排除高阶变化。例如，$f(x,y)=x+y^2$ 在原点的梯度为 $(1,0)^T$；竖直单位方向的一阶变化率为零，可是每个非零 $t$ 都满足 $f(0,t)-f(0,0)=t^2>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the gradient is zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 梯度为零时没有唯一方向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $g=0$, every unit vector gives rate zero and all tie. There is no normalized zero vector and no unique first-order best direction. At the origin, $x^2+y^2$, $-x^2-y^2$ and $x^2-y^2$ all have zero gradient, yet respectively have a minimum, a maximum and a saddle. [[多元Taylor近似|Higher-order local information]] is needed to distinguish such cases; a zero gradient alone makes no classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $g=0$，所有单位向量的变化率均为零，并列最优。零向量不能归一化，也没有唯一的一阶最佳方向。在原点，$x^2+y^2$、$-x^2-y^2$、$x^2-y^2$ 的梯度都为零，却分别是极小值、极大值与鞍点。区分这些情形需要[[多元Taylor近似|更高阶的局部信息]]，零梯度本身不能完成分类。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compute a direction and interpret the rate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x,y)=3x+4y$ the gradient is $(3,4)^T$ everywhere. Its length is $5$, so the maximizing unit vector is $(3/5,4/5)^T$ and the rate is $5$. The opposite direction has rate $-5$, and $(4/5,-3/5)^T$ has rate $0$. Here a step of length $0.01$ in the best direction changes $f$ by exactly $0.05$ because this function is linear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x,y)=3x+4y$，处处有梯度 $(3,4)^T$，长度为 $5$，所以最优单位方向为 $(3/5,4/5)^T$，变化率为 $5$。反方向的变化率为 $-5$，而 $(4/5,-3/5)^T$ 的变化率为 $0$。由于这个函数是线性的，沿最佳方向走 $0.01$，函数恰好增加 $0.05$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general differentiable function and a fixed unit $v$, $f(a+hv)-f(a)=h\,g^Tv+o(h)$ as $h\downarrow0$; the remainder divided by $h$ tends to zero. A positive rate therefore guarantees an increase for sufficiently small positive steps, not for every step size. For $f(x,y)=x-100x^2+y^2$ at the origin, the steepest first-order direction is $(1,0)^T$, but a step of length $0.02$ gives $0.02-100(0.02)^2=-0.02$. Even among steps of that fixed length, the vertical direction gives $0.0004$, a larger actual change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般可微函数和固定单位向量 $v$，当 $h\downarrow0$ 时，$f(a+hv)-f(a)=h\,g^Tv+o(h)$，其中余项除以 $h$ 后趋零。因此，正变化率保证足够小的正向步长会让函数增加，却不保证任意步长。对原点处的 $f(x,y)=x-100x^2+y^2$，一阶最陡方向为 $(1,0)^T$，但走 $0.02$ 得到 $0.02-100(0.02)^2=-0.02$。即使只比较这个固定长度，竖直方向的实际增加量也有 $0.0004$，反而更大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result compares all directions in an open domain, using Euclidean length. Constraints may exclude the maximizing direction. Changing the units or the length rule changes the unit directions being compared; [[最陡方向依赖度量|metric dependence of steepest ascent]] gives the corresponding result. With gradient $(6,8)^T$, the same Euclidean calculation gives direction $(3/5,4/5)^T$ and maximum rate $10$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个结论在开域中比较所有方向，采用欧氏长度；额外约束可能排除这里的最佳方向。改变单位或长度规则，也会改变参与比较的单位方向，详见[[最陡方向依赖度量]]。若梯度为 $(6,8)^T$，同样计算得到方向 $(3/5,4/5)^T$，最大变化率为 $10$。
