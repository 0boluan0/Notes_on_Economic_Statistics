# Self-normalised importance sampling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*自归一化重要性抽样*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Self-normalised importance sampling**, or **SNIS**, estimates an average when the target probabilities are known only in relative proportions. It draws from a distribution we can simulate, gives each observation a correction weight, and divides by the total weight actually observed. This last division removes a common unknown scale. It also makes the estimate a random ratio, with different properties from the ordinary [[重要性抽样|importance-sampling mean]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**自归一化重要性抽样**，简称 **SNIS**，用于估计目标平均值，而目标概率可能只知道相对比例。它从可模拟的分布抽样，给每个观测一个修正权重，再除以这次实际得到的权重总和。最后这次除法会消去共同的未知尺度，也使估计量成为随机比率，与普通[[重要性抽样|重要性抽样均值]]具有不同性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Relative probabilities become relative weights
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从相对概率到相对权重*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a target gives outcome $1$ three times the probability of outcome $0$. Write the unnormalised masses as $\pi(0)=1$, $\pi(1)=3$. Their total is $C=4$, so the actual [[概率质量函数|probabilities]] are $p(0)=1/4$, $p(1)=3/4$. We can compute $C$ in this small example; in a large problem that total or integral may be the difficult part. To estimate the mean of $h(x)=x$, draw each outcome with proposal probability $q(x)=1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设目标分布中，结果 $1$ 的概率是结果 $0$ 的三倍。把未归一化质量写成 $\pi(0)=1$、$\pi(1)=3$，总量为 $C=4$，因此真正的[[概率质量函数|概率]]是 $p(0)=1/4$、$p(1)=3/4$。小例子能直接算出 $C$，大型问题中难算的可能正是这个总和或积分。要估计 $h(x)=x$ 的均值，按 $q(0)=q(1)=1/2$ 在两个结果之间抽样。

| Outcome / 结果 $x$ | Unnormalised mass / 未归一化质量 $\pi(x)$ | Proposal / 提议 $q(x)$ | Weight / 权重 $\widetilde w=\pi/q$ | Recorded value / 记录值 $h(x)$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $0$ | $1$ | $1/2$ | $2$ | $0$ |
| $1$ | $3$ | $1/2$ | $6$ | $1$ |

> [!example] One realised weighted average · 一次实际加权平均
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the three draws are $0,1,1$, their weights are $2,6,6$. SNIS gives $(0\cdot2+1\cdot6+1\cdot6)/(2+6+6)=6/7$. The ordinary unweighted sample mean is $2/3$, and the target mean is $3/4$. A single weighted run need not equal the target. Multiplying every weight by $7$ changes neither the ratio nor the relative weights $1/7,3/7,3/7$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若三次抽到 $0,1,1$，相应权重为 $2,6,6$，SNIS 得到 $(0\cdot2+1\cdot6+1\cdot6)/(2+6+6)=6/7$。未加权样本均值是 $2/3$，目标均值则是 $3/4$。单次加权运行不一定恰好等于目标；把每个权重都乘以 $7$，比率及相对权重 $1/7,3/7,3/7$ 都不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What cancels, and what must be available
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*消去什么，又必须知道什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuous model, let $\pi(x)\ge0$ be a computable unnormalised density, with finite positive normalising constant $C=\int\pi(x)\,dx$. Then $p(x)=\pi(x)/C$ is the target [[概率密度函数|density]]. For a discrete model use sums. The real-valued function $h(x)$ is the recorded output, and the target is its [[期望|expectation]] $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$, with $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续模型中，设可计算的未归一化密度为 $\pi(x)\ge0$，其归一化常数 $C=\int\pi(x)\,dx$ 有限且严格为正。那么目标[[概率密度函数|密度]]是 $p(x)=\pi(x)/C$；离散模型把积分改成求和。实值函数 $h(x)$ 表示记录的输出，目标是它的[[期望]] $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$，并假定 $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a fixed proposal density $q$ from which we can actually generate [[独立同分布|iid]] draws. For the standard guarantee, require $q>0$ wherever $p>0$, apart from sets of zero integration measure. At sampled points with $q(x)>0$, compute $\widetilde w(x)=\pi(x)/q(x)=C p(x)/q(x)$. For a fixed positive integer $N$, define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选定提议密度 $q$，并能实际从中生成[[独立同分布|iid]]观测。为获得通常的保证，要求除积分测度为零的集合外，$p>0$ 处都有 $q>0$。在抽到且 $q(x)>0$ 的点，计算 $\widetilde w(x)=\pi(x)/q(x)=C p(x)/q(x)$。对预先固定的正整数 $N$，定义

$$
S_N=\sum_{i=1}^N\widetilde w(X_i),\qquad
\widehat\mu_{SNIS}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^N h(X_i)\widetilde w(X_i)}{S_N},\qquad S_N>0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same $C$ multiplies the numerator and denominator, so it cancels. We need to evaluate the weights up to one common positive factor, not to know $C$ separately. If the proposal is also available only as $q_u=Dq$ with $0<D<\infty$, the computable ratio $\pi/q_u=(C/D)p/q$ works too, provided the draws really follow $q$. Cancellation does not supply a sampler for an otherwise unavailable proposal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个 $C$ 同时乘在分子和分母上，所以相消。需要的是能够把权重算到一个共同的正比例因子，而不是单独知道 $C$。若提议也仅能算出 $q_u=Dq$，其中 $0<D<\infty$，那么可计算的 $\pi/q_u=(C/D)p/q$ 也可使用，但实际观测仍必须来自 $q$；常数相消不会自动提供一个原本不存在的提议抽样器。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A zero total weight gives no ratio
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*权重总和为零时，比率没有定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full target coverage permits $q$ to sample places where $p=0$, giving zero weights. For example, let the target put all mass at $1$, but let $q$ choose $0$ and $1$ equally. A fixed batch containing only zeros has $S_N=0$, with probability $2^{-N}$. The displayed estimator is then undefined. Reporting this failure, drawing more points, or assigning a fallback value are different procedures; finite-sample claims must specify which procedure is used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

覆盖完整目标，仍允许 $q$ 抽到 $p=0$ 的地方，得到零权重。例如目标全部质量在 $1$，但 $q$ 等概率抽 $0,1$；若固定批次全部抽到零，便有 $S_N=0$，概率为 $2^{-N}$。此时公式中的估计量无定义。报告失败、继续抽样、或者填入备用数值，是不同的程序；讨论有限样本性质时必须说明采用哪一种。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $S_N>0$, the normalised weights $\widetilde w(X_i)/S_N$ are nonnegative and sum to one. Thus SNIS is a weighted average of the observed outputs, and a constant $h$ is reproduced exactly. The observations themselves still follow $q$; weighting does not turn them into independent draws from $p$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $S_N>0$，归一化权重 $\widetilde w(X_i)/S_N$ 非负且总和为一，因此 SNIS 是观测输出的加权平均，常数函数 $h$ 会被精确再现。观测本身仍服从 $q$，加权并不会把它们变成来自 $p$ 的独立样本。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自归一抽样有限样本偏差|finite-sample bias]] of this random ratio and its [[自归一抽样强一致性|strong consistency]] are separate questions. Full target coverage protects the denominator as well as the numerator; the narrower fixed-$h$ condition for [[重要性抽样支持条件|ordinary importance sampling]] is not a substitute. Cancelling a constant establishes neither a precision gain nor a valid confidence interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机比率的[[自归一抽样有限样本偏差|有限样本偏差]]与[[自归一抽样强一致性|强一致性]]是两个问题。完整目标覆盖同时保护分母与分子，不能替换成[[重要性抽样支持条件|普通重要性抽样]]只针对固定 $h$ 的较窄覆盖条件。消去常数本身也不证明精度改善或置信区间有效。
