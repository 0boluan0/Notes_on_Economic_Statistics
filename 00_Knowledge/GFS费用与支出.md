# GFS费用记录交易引起的净值减少而支出还包括非金融资产净投资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*GFS expense records transaction-induced reductions in net worth; expenditure also includes net investment in nonfinancial assets.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 IMF《政府财政统计手册 2014》的权责发生制业务表中，费用（expense）是交易引起的净值减少；支出（expenditure）则是费用加上非金融资产净投资。两者都有明确统计含义，不能都凭“付出去的钱”理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the GFSM 2014 accrual-based Statement of Operations, expense is a transaction-induced decrease in net worth. Expenditure adds net investment in nonfinancial assets. Neither is defined simply as cash paid out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，用于日常服务的工资、消耗和赠款会进入费用；固定资本消耗（CFC）也计入费用，虽然当期没有相应现金付款。按等值取得一项固定资产则只是用现金换成资产，取得时不因这笔交换减少净值，因而不直接计为费用。资产因市场价格变化而产生的损益，不计入这里的费用，而另列为持有损益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wages and consumption for ordinary services, together with grants, enter expense, as does consumption of fixed capital even without a cash payment. Acquiring an asset for equivalent value exchanges cash for an asset without reducing net worth. Price-driven holding gains or losses are not transaction expense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“非金融资产净投资”包括固定资产取得减处置、再减固定资本消耗，加上存货变动，以及贵重物品和非生产资产的净取得。写成汇总式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net investment comprises fixed-asset acquisitions less disposals and consumption of fixed capital, changes in inventories, and net acquisitions of valuables and nonproduced assets. In aggregate:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
I_{\mathrm{NFA}}^{\mathrm{net}}
=\text{非金融资产取得}-\text{非金融资产处置}-CFC,
$$
$$
\text{Expenditure}=\text{Expense}+I_{\mathrm{NFA}}^{\mathrm{net}}.
$$

设日常费用与赠款合计 160，固定资本消耗为 8；非金融资产取得 100、处置 10，无其他相关流量。费用为 $160+8=168$，净投资为 $100-10-8=82$，支出为 $168+82=250$。CFC 在两处抵消，所以不能再把 8 加到支出总额上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With operating expense and grants of 160, CFC of 8, acquisitions of 100 and disposals of 10, expense is 168, net investment is 82 and expenditure is 250. CFC cancels between the two components; adding it again would double count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另行发放可收回的贷款或偿还债务本金，是金融资产或负债交易，不计入上述支出总额。资本赠款则不同：如果资产属于受助者而非政府，它可能减少政府净值并记为费用，不能仅因“资本”二字就列为政府资产。具体判断参见 [[购买支出与转移支出]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recoverable lending and debt-principal repayment are financial-asset or liability transactions, outside this expenditure aggregate. A capital grant for an asset owned by the recipient can instead reduce government net worth and enter expense. See [[购买支出与转移支出|purchases and transfers]] for the transaction boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本国预算中的“经常/资本支出”或现金付款表，可能采用不同范围和记录时点。跨表比较先把口径衔接好；不能把 GFS 费用、预算支出与现金流出简单画等号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
National current/capital budgets and cash-flow statements may differ in coverage and timing. Reconcile them before comparison instead of equating GFS expense, budget expenditure and cash outflow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
