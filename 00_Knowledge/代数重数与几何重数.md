<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Algebraic and geometric multiplicity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*代数重数与几何重数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An eigenvalue can appear repeatedly in a polynomial without providing that many independent eigenvector directions. **Algebraic multiplicity** counts the repeated root. **Geometric multiplicity** counts the independent directions on which the matrix acts by that eigenvalue. Keeping these counts separate explains why a repeated eigenvalue sometimes causes a shortage of eigenvectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个特征值可以在多项式里重复出现，却未必提供同样多的独立特征方向。**代数重数**数这个根重复几次；**几何重数**数矩阵按这个特征值作用的独立方向有几个。分清这两种计数，才能理解为什么重复特征值有时会造成特征向量不足。

> [!example] Two root occurrences, one independent direction · 两次根，只有一个独立方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the real matrix
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑实矩阵
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Its [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]] is $p_A(t)=\det(tI_2-A)=(t-2)^2$. Here $t$ is a polynomial variable, $I_2$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]], and $\det$ is the [[行列式|determinant]]. The factor $t-2$ occurs twice, so the algebraic multiplicity of $2$ is two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它的[[特征多项式]]为 $p_A(t)=\det(tI_2-A)=(t-2)^2$。其中 $t$ 是多项式变量，$I_2$ 是[[单位矩阵]]，$\det$ 表示[[行列式]]。因子 $t-2$ 出现两次，所以2的代数重数为2。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To count eigenvector directions, solve $Av=2v$. Writing $v=(x,y)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要数特征向量方向，则解 $Av=2v$。把 $v$ 写成 $(x,y)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，得到
>
> $$\begin{pmatrix}2x+y\\2y\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}2x\\2y\end{pmatrix}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y=0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every nonzero solution is a multiple of $(1,0)^T$. There are infinitely many such real vectors, but only one independent direction. The geometric multiplicity is one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个非零解都是 $(1,0)^T$ 的倍数。这样的实向量有无穷多个，却只有一个独立方向，所以几何重数为1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Define the two counts in the same field
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在同一个域中定义两种计数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over a fixed field $\mathbb F$, with $n\ge1$, and let $\lambda\in\mathbb F$ be an [[特征对|eigenvalue]]. The algebraic multiplicity $m_a(\lambda)$ is the positive integer $a$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为固定域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 方阵，$n\ge1$，且 $\lambda\in\mathbb F$ 是一个[[特征对|特征值]]。代数重数 $m_a(\lambda)$ 是满足下式的正整数 $a$：

$$p_A(t)=(t-\lambda)^a q(t),\qquad q(\lambda)\ne0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $q(t)$ is the remaining polynomial after all factors $t-\lambda$ have been removed. The condition $q(\lambda)\ne0$ says that no further copy of that factor remains. A simple root has $m_a=1$; a repeated root has $m_a\ge2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$q(t)$ 是把全部 $t-\lambda$ 因子取走后剩下的多项式；$q(\lambda)\ne0$ 表示里面不再藏着这个因子。单根的 $m_a=1$，重根的 $m_a\ge2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The geometric multiplicity is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几何重数为

$$m_g(\lambda)=\dim E_\lambda(A)=\dim\ker(A-\lambda I_n)=n-\operatorname{rank}(A-\lambda I_n).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[特征空间|eigenspace]] $E_\lambda(A)$ consists of all solutions of $Av=\lambda v$, including zero; $\ker$ means the [[零空间|nullspace]], or solutions mapped to zero. Its [[维数|dimension]] is the number of vectors in a [[基|basis]], an independent list that generates every vector in the space. The last equality is the [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]]. The zero vector belongs to the space but is not an eigenvector and adds no independent direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[特征空间]] $E_\lambda(A)$ 包含 $Av=\lambda v$ 的全部解，也包括零向量；$\ker$ 表示[[零空间]]，即被映到零的解集。空间的[[维数]]是其一组[[基]]的向量个数：基是独立且能生成空间中全部向量的一组向量。最后一个等号来自[[秩零度定理]]。零向量属于这个空间，却不是特征向量，也不增加独立方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For comparison, the real matrix $2I_2$ has the same polynomial $(t-2)^2$, but $(2I_2-2I_2)v=0$ imposes no restriction on $v$. Its eigenspace is the whole plane, with basis $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T$. Thus its two multiplicities are both two. The polynomial alone cannot distinguish these two geometric situations; the separate [[同谱不必相似|same-spectrum counterexample]] uses this difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作个对照，实矩阵 $2I_2$ 也有多项式 $(t-2)^2$，但 $(2I_2-2I_2)v=0$ 对 $v$ 没有任何限制。特征空间是整个平面，基可取 $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T$，所以两种重数都是2。仅看多项式分不出这两种几何情况；独立的[[同谱不必相似|同谱反例]]正是使用这一区别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the geometric count cannot exceed the algebraic count
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么几何重数不会超过代数重数*

> [!important] The multiplicity bound · 重数的上下界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For an eigenvalue in the working field,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对当前域中的一个特征值，始终有
>
> $$1\le m_g(\lambda)\le m_a(\lambda).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first inequality comes from the existence of a nonzero eigenvector. The second says that each independent eigenvector direction requires a copy of $t-\lambda$ in the characteristic polynomial.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一个不等式来自非零特征向量的存在；第二个说明，每个独立特征方向至少需要特征多项式中的一个 $t-\lambda$ 因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the second inequality, put a basis of $E_\lambda(A)$ first in a basis of the whole space. Write $g=m_g(\lambda)$. If $g=n$, every vector is multiplied by $\lambda$, so $A=\lambda I_n$ and $p_A(t)=(t-\lambda)^n$. Otherwise extend the $g$ eigenvectors by $n-g$ further basis vectors: keep choosing a vector outside the span already reached until the whole space is covered. In these coordinates the matrix is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了看清第二个不等式，先取 $E_\lambda(A)$ 的一组基，再把它放在整个空间的一组基的最前面。记 $g=m_g(\lambda)$。如果 $g=n$，每个向量都被乘上 $\lambda$，所以 $A=\lambda I_n$，$p_A(t)=(t-\lambda)^n$。否则，在这 $g$ 个特征向量后补上 $n-g$ 个基向量：每次选一个尚未被当前向量组张成的向量，直到覆盖全空间。新坐标下矩阵为

$$\begin{pmatrix}\lambda I_g&B\\0&C\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first $g$ columns express the fact that each chosen eigenvector is multiplied by $\lambda$ and has no component in the added directions. The block $B$ has size $g\times(n-g)$, and $C$ has size $(n-g)\times(n-g)$; they describe the images of the added basis vectors. [[相似保持特征多项式|Changing this basis preserves the characteristic polynomial]]. Expanding the determinant along its first $g$ columns, whose lower entries are zero, gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前 $g$ 列表示：每个选出的特征向量都只被乘上 $\lambda$，不会产生后来补入方向的分量。块 $B$ 的尺寸为 $g\times(n-g)$，$C$ 为 $(n-g)\times(n-g)$，两者记录补入基向量的像。[[相似保持特征多项式|换这组基不改变特征多项式]]。沿下半部分为零的前 $g$ 列展开行列式，得到

$$p_A(t)=(t-\lambda)^g\det(tI_{n-g}-C).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are therefore at least $g$ copies of $t-\lambda$, proving $m_a(\lambda)\ge g$. This argument does not require every other root of $p_A$ to belong to $\mathbb F$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此至少有 $g$ 个 $t-\lambda$ 因子，即 $m_a(\lambda)\ge g$。这个论证不要求 $p_A$ 的其他根也都属于 $\mathbb F$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What a mismatch tells us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两种重数不相等意味着什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $m_g(\lambda)<m_a(\lambda)$ for even one eigenvalue, ordinary eigenvectors cannot form a basis of the whole space, so the matrix is not diagonalizable over that field. The full decision, including the need for all roots to lie in the field, is explained in [[重根对角化条件|diagonalization with repeated eigenvalues]]. When the polynomial splits, [[广义特征向量|generalized eigenvectors]] in [[Jordan链|Jordan chains]] describe the extra layers missing from the ordinary eigenspace; they do not turn those extra vectors into ordinary eigenvectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要某个特征值有 $m_g(\lambda)<m_a(\lambda)$，普通特征向量就无法构成全空间的基，因此矩阵不能在该域上对角化。完整判断还要求根都在该域中，详见[[重根对角化条件]]。当多项式在该域中分裂时，[[广义特征向量]]组成的[[Jordan链]]描述普通特征空间缺少的层级；这些额外向量并不会因此变成普通特征向量。

> [!question]- Count a repeated root · 读出一个重根的两种计数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, suppose $p_A(t)=(t-1)^3(t+2)$ and $\dim E_1(A)=2$. Then $m_a(1)=3$ and $m_g(1)=2$. The root occurs three times, but there are only two independent ordinary eigenvector directions for it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实数或复数域上，若 $p_A(t)=(t-1)^3(t+2)$，且 $\dim E_1(A)=2$，则 $m_a(1)=3$、$m_g(1)=2$：根重复三次，却只有两个独立的普通特征方向。
