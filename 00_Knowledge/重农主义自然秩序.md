# 重农主义的自然秩序是一套可由理性认识并由稳定法权保护的物理与道德社会秩序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Physiocratic natural order is a physical and moral social order knowable by reason and protected by stable law and rights*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 重农主义认为，经济与社会生活存在人类可以认识的物理与道德秩序。主权者的职责不是随意重写这套秩序，而是保障人身与财产权、维持稳定法律，并使耕作投入和商品交易免受破坏性任意干预。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Physiocracy holds that economic and social life has a physical and moral order that human reason can know. The sovereign does not arbitrarily rewrite that order, but secures persons and property, maintains stable law, and protects cultivation advances and commodity exchange from destructive discretionary intervention.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不是“政府消失”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*It is not the disappearance of government*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自然秩序要求有可执行的权利、稳定的规则和维护秩序的主权者。因此，它不等于“政府完全不作为”。它反对的是破坏农业预付、财产安全和交易连续性的任意命令，而不是法权、行政和公共秩序本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Natural order requires enforceable rights, stable rules, and a sovereign that maintains order. It is therefore not a doctrine of government disappearing. It opposes arbitrary commands that disrupt agricultural advances, property security, and continuity of exchange, not law, administration, or public order as such.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它怎样进入经济政策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How it enters economic policy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一旦把可预期的财产权、稳定法律和不受任意干扰的耕作视为维持再生产的条件，税收就不能仅按“哪里最容易征”来设计。它还必须避免侵蚀预付和破坏下一轮再生产。这一秩序前提如何与农业净产品、税负转嫁一起导向直接地税，由 [[单一地税的重农推导]] 单独说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once predictable property rights, stable law, and cultivation protected from arbitrary interference are treated as conditions of reproduction, taxation cannot be designed merely by asking where collection is easiest. It must also avoid eroding advances and the next production cycle. The separate [[单一地税的重农推导|derivation note]] shows how this premise combines with agricultural net product and tax shifting to support direct land taxation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“自然秩序”不能缩写成“政府什么都不做”？
>
> **答案：** 这套秩序需要主权者保障人身与财产权、执行稳定法律并维持交易秩序；它排斥的是破坏这些条件的任意干预。
