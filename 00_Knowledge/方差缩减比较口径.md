# Comparing variance-reduction efficiency
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方差缩减比较口径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simulation method can produce quieter outputs and still be a worse use of computation. If each output costs much more, we may afford so few of them that the final estimate is less precise. To compare [[方差缩减|variance-reduction methods]], ask what it costs to estimate the same target to the same accuracy, or what accuracy each method reaches under the same budget.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种模拟方法的输出可以更稳定，却更不划算：若每个输出的成本大增，预算能支付的输出太少，最终估计反而更不精确。比较[[方差缩减]]方法时，应问达到同一目标、同一精度各要多少成本，或者同一预算下各能达到什么精度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with the whole replication
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先说清一次完整重复是什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the model and scalar target $\mu$. For method $j$, one complete replication returns $Y_j$, [[估计量无偏性|unbiased]] in the sense that $E[Y_j]=\mu$, with positive finite variance $\sigma_j^2$. The label $j=0$ denotes the baseline, and $j=1$ the alternative. Replicate each method independently under its own fixed output law, taking a preselected positive integer count $n_j$. Its mean estimate then has [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|variance and standard error]]
$$\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\mu_j)=\frac{\sigma_j^2}{n_j},\qquad
\operatorname{SE}(\widehat\mu_j)=\frac{\sigma_j}{\sqrt{n_j}}.$$
Independence is between complete replications; outputs inside an antithetic pair or a shared-input comparison need not be independent. The two methods need not use the same replication count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定模型与标量目标 $\mu$。方法 $j$ 的一次完整重复输出 $Y_j$，对 $\mu$ [[估计量无偏性|无偏]]，即 $E[Y_j]=\mu$，方差 $\sigma_j^2$ 正且有限；$j=0$ 表示基准，$j=1$ 表示替代方法。每种方法按各自固定的输出分布独立重复，预先选定正整数次数 $n_j$，均值估计的[[Monte Carlo均值标准误|方差和标准误]]便如上。独立性针对完整重复；一对对偶值或共享输入比较内部不必独立。两种方法也不必使用相同重复次数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, an antithetic replication evaluates a function twice and returns the pair average. Its $n_j$ counts pairs, and its cost must include both evaluations. Calling it one output does not make it cost one function evaluation. If runs share a fitted control coefficient or use other dependencies, the displayed variance formula must first be justified for that design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一次对偶重复计算两次函数，再返回配对平均。此时 $n_j$ 数的是对数，成本必须包含两次函数计算；叫它“一个输出”不等于只付一次计算。若各次重复共享拟合出的控制系数或存在其他依赖，要先证明上面的方差式确实适用于该设计。

> [!example] One method saves work; another loses it · 一个省成本，一个更贵
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Measure cost in fixed work units. Suppose the baseline has per-replication variance $4$ and cost $1$. Alternative A has variance $1$ and cost $3$; alternative B has variance $2$ and cost $10$. All estimate the same $\mu$ without bias. To achieve SE at most $0.1$, the target variance is $0.01$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用固定工作单位计成本。基准每次方差为 $4$、成本为 $1$；替代 A 的方差为 $1$、成本为 $3$；替代 B 的方差为 $2$、成本为 $10$，三者对同一目标无偏。要让标准误不超过 $0.1$，目标方差就是 $0.01$：
>
> | Method · 方法 | Replications · 重复数 | Total cost · 总成本 |
> |---|---:|---:|
> | Baseline · 基准 | $400$ | $400$ |
> | Alternative A · 替代 A | $100$ | $300$ |
> | Alternative B · 替代 B | $200$ | $2000$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A is cheaper despite a higher cost per replication. B halves per-replication variance but multiplies cost by ten: equal precision costs five times as much. Neither a variance percentage nor a replication count alone decides the comparison.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> A 每次更贵，但达到目标的总成本更少。B 的单次方差虽减半，单次成本却变成十倍，达到同一精度的成本为基准五倍。只看方差百分比或重复数，都不足以判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the cost–variance product comes from
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*成本乘方差为什么出现*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume each complete replication costs a deterministic $c_j>0$, total cost is $n_jc_j$, and startup cost is negligible. For a target error variance $v>0$, the exact minimum integer count under these assumptions is
$$n_j=\left\lceil\frac{\sigma_j^2}{v}\right\rceil,\qquad
C_j(v)=c_j\left\lceil\frac{\sigma_j^2}{v}\right\rceil.$$
The ceiling rounds up to the smallest integer at least as large as its argument. Ignoring that rounding when counts are large gives $C_j(v)\approx c_j\sigma_j^2/v$. Thus the usual relative efficiency is
$$\mathcal E=\frac{c_0\sigma_0^2}{c_1\sigma_1^2}.$$
An efficiency above one means the alternative has the lower cost–variance product. Under this approximation the baseline needs $\mathcal E$ times the work. With small counts, compare the integer costs directly: rounding can affect the ranking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设每次完整重复的确定成本为 $c_j>0$，总成本为 $n_jc_j$，启动成本可忽略。目标误差方差为 $v>0$ 时，最小整数重复数和精确成本如上；上取整表示不小于该数的最小整数。重复数大到可以忽略取整时，成本近似为 $c_j\sigma_j^2/v$，得到通常的相对效率 $\mathcal E$。它大于一，表示替代方法的成本—方差乘积更小；在此近似下，基准需要它的 $\mathcal E$ 倍工作。次数很小时应直接比较整数成本，因为取整可能改变排序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed budget $B$ that funds at least one replication, the exact count is $n_j=\lfloor B/c_j\rfloor$ and variance is $\sigma_j^2/n_j$. The floor rounds down. For large counts this is approximately $c_j\sigma_j^2/B$, giving the same comparison. These are work-budget calculations; random runtimes and stopping when a clock expires require additional analysis because the completed sample count may depend on the simulated outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若固定预算 $B$ 至少够一次重复，次数精确为 $n_j=\lfloor B/c_j\rfloor$，方差为 $\sigma_j^2/n_j$；下取整表示向下取整。次数很大时近似为 $c_j\sigma_j^2/B$，得到相同的比较。这些是工作量预算公式；运行时间若随机，或时间一到就停止，完成次数可能与模拟结果有关，需要额外分析，不能直接套式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Bias and overhead can reverse the choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*偏差与额外成本会改变选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[估计量偏差|estimator's bias]] is its expected answer minus $\mu$. With finite second moments, [[MSE偏差方差分解|mean squared error is variance plus squared bias]]. A method with fixed bias $0.2$ has MSE at least $0.04$, however many replications we average; it cannot meet an MSE target of $0.01$ by repetition alone. If bias changes with a tuning parameter or sample count, evaluate that dependence explicitly. Variance ratios compare accuracy directly only when the bias conditions justify it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[估计量偏差]]是期望答案减去 $\mu$。二阶矩有限时，[[MSE偏差方差分解|均方误差等于方差加偏差平方]]。若固定偏差为 $0.2$，无论平均多少次，MSE 都至少为 $0.04$，无法仅靠重复达到 $0.01$ 的目标。若偏差随调节参数或次数改变，应显式评估这种关系；只有偏差条件允许时，方差比才能直接代表精度比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the table, A saved $100$ work units. If A also requires a one-off setup of $200$ units, this single calculation costs $500$, exceeding the baseline's $400$. Reusing that setup across many target calculations can change the decision again. Memory, implementation and checking effort, and wall-clock time also matter. Hardware, parallelism, language and implementation affect timing ratios, so they are not universal mathematical constants of the methods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

表中的 A 省下 $100$ 个工作单位；若它还需要一次性的 $200$ 单位准备工作，本次总成本就变为 $500$，高于基准的 $400$。如果多次目标计算能共用准备过程，选择又可能改变。内存、实现与检查的人力、实际运行时间也应计入；硬件、并行、语言和实现都会影响时间比，它不是方法固有的数学常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero-variance output is outside the positive-variance ratio above. If it really has the correct expectation and is constant almost surely, one replication already gives the target; its construction cost still matters. Conversely, an infinite-variance output has no finite SE of the displayed form. Do not turn either case into a misleading numerical efficiency ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零方差输出不属于上述正方差比值的范围。若它确实具有正确期望且几乎必然恒定，一次重复就给出目标，但构造成本仍重要。反过来，无穷方差输出没有上式那样的有限标准误；这两类都不应硬算一个容易误导的效率数字。

> [!tip] Make the comparison reproducible · 让比较可以复核
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> State the target, model, bias assumptions and replication unit. Report the estimate, design-matched estimated SE, replication count, total function evaluations, setup cost, measured runtime and computing environment. Separate known theoretical variances from pilot estimates: an estimated efficiency ratio is uncertain, and selecting a count from pilot data is not the same fixed-count experiment as the derivation above. A reproducible seed helps rerun the comparison; it does not prove correctness or independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 说明目标、模型、偏差假设与重复单位，报告估计值、匹配设计的估计标准误、重复数、总函数计算次数、准备成本、实测时间与计算环境。区分已知理论方差和预实验估计：估计的效率比有不确定性，按预实验选择次数也不是上面推导的同一个固定次数实验。记录种子有助复跑，但不证明正确性或独立性。
