# 比较方差缩减方法必须固定估计目标、说明偏差，并按达到同等精度所需的总成本衡量效率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Variance-reduction methods must be compared for the same estimand with bias stated and efficiency measured by the total cost of reaching equal precision*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 单看“方差下降百分比”可能选错方法
> 先确认两个估计量针对同一目标并说明偏差，再用设计匹配的标准误、函数评估次数和实际运行成本比较达到同等精度要付出多少。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First ensure that the estimators target the same quantity and make their bias explicit. Then compare design-matched uncertainty, function evaluations, and the actual cost required to reach equal precision.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两个无偏估计量分别有

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\mu_j)=\frac{\sigma_j^2}{n},
\qquad
\operatorname{Cost}_j(n)=n c_j,
\qquad j\in\{0,1\},
$$

则新方法相对基准的效率可写成

$$
\mathcal E=\frac{c_0\sigma_0^2}{c_1\sigma_1^2}.
$$

$\mathcal E>1$ 表示在这些线性成本与无偏假设下，新方法达到同一误差方差所需成本更低。若方法带偏，应比较均方误差或与任务一致的损失，而不能只比较方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For unbiased estimators with linear per-replication cost, relative efficiency is the ratio $c_0\sigma_0^2/(c_1\sigma_1^2)$. If bias is present, variance alone is insufficient; use mean squared error or the task-relevant loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最低报告项是：估计值、偏差口径、与抽样设计匹配的标准误、配对或有效样本数量、总函数评估次数、墙钟时间和额外实现复杂度。不同机器上的墙钟比率不是方法固有常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report the estimate, bias convention, design-matched standard error, number of pairs or effective observations, total function evaluations, wall-clock time, and additional implementation burden. Timing ratios are environment-dependent rather than intrinsic constants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 一个方法把方差减半，却让每次复制成本增加十倍。在其他条件相同且成本近似线性时，它的单位成本效率是否提高？
>
> **答案：** 没有。新成本—方差乘积约为原来的五倍，达到同等精度反而更贵。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. Its cost–variance product is about five times the baseline, so equal precision is more expensive.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
