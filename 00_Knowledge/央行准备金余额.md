# 准备金余额是中央银行负债与合格机构资产而公众通常不能直接持有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reserve balances are central-bank liabilities and assets of eligible institutions, not accounts generally available to the public*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 准备金余额是合格机构在中央银行账户中持有的余额：它列在中央银行负债端、持有机构资产端，用于支付结算、流动性管理和政策实施。普通家庭和非金融企业通常不能直接开立这种账户。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Reserve balances are balances that eligible institutions hold in accounts at the central bank. They are liabilities of the central bank and assets of the holding institutions, used for payment settlement, liquidity management, and policy implementation. Households and non-financial firms generally cannot open such accounts directly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在美国，IORB 适用于由合格机构持有或代表其持有的 Federal Reserve master-account 余额；没有 master account 的机构可通过 correspondent 结算。依法具备申请 Federal Reserve account 的资格不等于自动获批，Reserve Bank 仍须按账户准入规则审查。于是“只有银行能持有准备金”也是简化说法：精确边界是符合适用资格、其余额按规则直接持有或由他人代持的机构，而不是公众。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the United States, IORB applies to balances maintained by or on behalf of eligible institutions in Federal Reserve master accounts; institutions without a master account may settle through a correspondent. Statutory eligibility to request a Federal Reserve account does not guarantee approval, because the Reserve Bank still applies the account-access review. Thus, “only banks hold reserves” is shorthand: the precise boundary is an eligible institution whose balance is maintained directly or on its behalf under the applicable rules—not the general public.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 对商业银行而言，准备金是资产；对中央银行而言，同一余额是负债。
- 客户的商业银行存款不是客户在央行的准备金份额。
- “准备金增加”不表示公众可支配存款自动同额增加；两者是不同机构的负债。
- Federal Reserve 还为财政部、外国官方机构等记录其他存款负债；不能把央行账上的每一种存款、每个获准账户或每笔 master-account 余额都自动归为 H.6 的 reserve balances 或视为可获得 IORB。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A reserve balance is an asset of the commercial bank and the same balance is a liability of the central bank.
- A customer's commercial-bank deposit is not the customer's share of a central-bank reserve account.
- More reserves do not automatically mean an equal rise in deposits spendable by the public; they are liabilities of different institutions.
- The Federal Reserve also records other deposit liabilities, including deposits of the U.S. Treasury and foreign official institutions. Not every central-bank deposit, approved account, or master-account balance is automatically an H.6 reserve balance or eligible for IORB.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么一家银行的准备金增加时，不能直接说某个家庭的银行存款也增加？
>
> **答案：** 准备金是央行对合格机构的负债；家庭存款是商业银行对家庭的负债，必须有另一笔记账事件才会改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does an increase in a bank's reserves not by itself prove that a household's bank deposit rose?
>
> **Answer:** Reserves are central-bank liabilities to eligible institutions, while household deposits are commercial-bank liabilities. A separate accounting event is needed to change the household deposit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
