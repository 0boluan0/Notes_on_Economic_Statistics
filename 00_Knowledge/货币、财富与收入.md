# 货币是时点存量财富包含货币而收入是期间流量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Money is a point-in-time stock, wealth includes money, and income is a flow over a period*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 货币是某一时点持有的货币性资产存量；财富是该时点各种资产价值的总和，范围包括但不限于货币；收入则是在一段期间内获得的资源流量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Money is a stock of monetary assets held at a point in time. Wealth is the value of all assets held at that time and includes, but is not limited to, money. Income is a flow of resources received over a period.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“某人本月收入很高”不推出月末货币余额很高：收入可能已被消费、投资或用于还债。“某人很富有”也不推出其持有很多货币：财富可能主要是房屋、股票或企业权益。比较时必须同时对齐对象和计量时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, a high monthly income does not imply a high end-of-month money balance: the income may have been consumed, invested, or used to repay debt. Being wealthy also does not imply holding much money, because wealth may consist mainly of property, shares, or business equity. A valid comparison must align both the object measured and the time dimension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 货币与财富都是存量概念，但财富的资产集合更广。
- 收入是流量，必须带期间；货币余额是存量，必须带观察时点。
- 银行贷款同时创造存款和债务时，借款人的货币资产增加不等于净财富同额增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Money and wealth are stocks, but wealth covers a wider asset set.
- Income is a flow and requires a period; a money balance is a stock and requires an observation date.
- When a bank loan creates a deposit and a matching debt, the borrower's monetary asset rises without an equal rise in net wealth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一名学生本周收到工资后立刻用来还贷。为什么“本周收入增加”和“周末货币存量增加”不是同一结论？
>
> **答案：** 工资是本周的流量；还贷会减少其存款余额，所以周末的货币存量未必增加。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A student receives wages and immediately repays debt. Why are “income rose this week” and “the money stock held at week-end rose” different claims?
>
> **Answer:** Wages are a flow during the week, while repayment reduces the deposit balance, so the end-of-week money stock need not rise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
