# Asymptotic stability in continuous time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续时间的渐近稳定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a continuous-time system, the state changes at every instant. **Asymptotic stability of zero** means that a sufficiently small initial departure stays small for all future time and eventually vanishes. For a constant linear system, eigenvalues tell us whether this happens: their real parts control whether the corresponding modes decay, persist or grow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续时间系统的状态在每个时刻都发生变化。**零点渐近稳定**表示足够小的初始偏离，在以后所有时刻都保持小，并最终消失。对常系数线性系统，特征值能告诉我们是否如此：它们的实部控制相应模式衰减、持续存在还是增长。

> [!example] Rotation with a shrinking radius · 半径逐渐缩小的旋转
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $x(t)$ be a two-coordinate state at real time $t\ge0$. For the matrix and initial state below, the trajectory is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $x(t)$ 表示实时间 $t\ge0$ 下的二维状态。对下面的矩阵和初值，轨迹为
>
> $$x'(t)=Ax(t),\qquad A=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-4\\4&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad x(0)=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad x(t)=e^{-t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos(4t)\\\sin(4t)\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The prime denotes the time [[导数|derivative]], taken in each coordinate. Direct differentiation verifies the equation and the initial value. The state turns at angular speed $4$ while its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] is $e^{-t}$. The eigenvalues are $-1\pm4i$, where $i^2=-1$: the imaginary part produces oscillation, and the real part $-1$ produces decay.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 撇号表示逐坐标对时间求[[导数]]，直接求导可以核对方程和初值。状态以角速度4旋转，[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]却是 $e^{-t}$。特征值为 $-1\pm4i$，其中 $i^2=-1$：虚部产生振荡，实部 $-1$ 产生衰减。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State the system and the strict condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确系统，再写严格条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a constant real or complex $n\times n$ matrix, with integer $n\ge1$. Consider the homogeneous system $x'(t)=Ax(t)$, with no external input. Zero is an **equilibrium**: its derivative and its later value are both zero. The [[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|matrix-exponential solution theorem]] gives the unique trajectory from $x_0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是常量实或复 $n\times n$ 矩阵，整数 $n\ge1$。考虑没有外部输入的齐次系统 $x'(t)=Ax(t)$。零是一个**平衡点**：导数为零，以后也始终为零。[[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|矩阵指数解定理]]给出从 $x_0$ 出发的唯一轨迹：

$$x(t)=e^{tA}x_0.$$

> [!important] Strictly in the left half-plane · 严格位于左半平面
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Zero is asymptotically stable exactly when every complex eigenvalue of $A$ satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当且仅当 $A$ 的每个复特征值都满足下式，零点渐近稳定：
>
> $$\operatorname{Re}\lambda<0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\operatorname{Re}(a+ib)=a$ is the real part. Thus all eigenvalues must lie strictly to the left of the imaginary axis. Such a matrix is called **Hurwitz stable**. This is equivalent to $e^{tA}\to0$ as $t\to\infty$, and to $x(t)\to0$ for **every** initial vector. Diagonalization is not required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中 $\operatorname{Re}(a+ib)=a$ 表示实部，所以全部特征值必须严格位于虚轴左侧。这样的矩阵称为 **Hurwitz 稳定**。它等价于 $t\to\infty$ 时 $e^{tA}\to0$，也等价于**所有**初值的轨迹都趋零；不要求矩阵可对角化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why negative real parts are enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么负实部足够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[特征对|eigenvector]] is a nonzero vector $v$ with $Av=\lambda v$. Starting from this vector gives $x(t)=e^{\lambda t}v$. For $\lambda=a+ib$, the scalar factor has magnitude $|e^{\lambda t}|=e^{at}$, so its size decays exactly when $a<0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[特征对|特征向量]]是满足 $Av=\lambda v$ 的非零向量 $v$。从这个向量出发，得到 $x(t)=e^{\lambda t}v$。若 $\lambda=a+ib$，标量因子的模为 $|e^{\lambda t}|=e^{at}$，因此恰好在 $a<0$ 时衰减。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There may not be enough eigenvectors for a basis. Nevertheless, every complex square matrix has a [[Jordan标准形|Jordan form]] $A=SJS^{-1}$, with invertible $S$; a real matrix can be viewed over the complex numbers for this argument. Its exponential is $Se^{tJ}S^{-1}$, by applying the similarity to every power in the convergent series. Each entry inside a [[Jordan块的指数|Jordan-block exponential]] is a constant multiple of $t^q e^{\lambda t}$, where $q$ is a fixed nonnegative integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵可能没有足够多的特征向量组成基，但每个复方阵都有 [[Jordan标准形|Jordan形]] $A=SJS^{-1}$，其中 $S$ 可逆；实矩阵也可以暂时放到复数域进行这个论证。对收敛指数级数中的每一项幂施加相似变换，得到其指数为 $Se^{tJ}S^{-1}$。[[Jordan块的指数]]中，每个元素都是 $t^q e^{\lambda t}$ 的常数倍，其中 $q$ 是固定的非负整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\operatorname{Re}\lambda=-c<0$, the magnitude is $t^q e^{-ct}$. This approaches zero even when $q>0$. One explicit reason comes from the [[指数函数幂级数|exponential series]]: for $t>0$, its positive term $(ct)^{q+1}/(q+1)!$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\operatorname{Re}\lambda=-c<0$，大小就是 $t^q e^{-ct}$，即使 $q>0$ 也趋零。可以从[[指数函数幂级数]]直接看出：当 $t>0$ 时，级数的正项 $(ct)^{q+1}/(q+1)!$ 给出

$$0\le t^q e^{-ct}\le\frac{(q+1)!}{c^{q+1}t}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $m!$ is the product of the positive integers up to $m$. Every Jordan block therefore decays; multiplication by the fixed finite matrices $S$ and $S^{-1}$ preserves that limit. For example, the nondiagonalizable matrix $J=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ has $e^{tJ}=e^{-t}\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\to0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $m!$ 是从1到 $m$ 的正整数乘积。每个 Jordan 块因而都衰减；左右乘固定的有限矩阵 $S$、$S^{-1}$，仍保持这个极限。例如，不能对角化的 $J=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ 满足 $e^{tJ}=e^{-t}\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\to0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This also gives stability in the “stays small” sense. The continuous function $e^{tA}$ is bounded on every finite time interval; because it tends to zero, there is a single finite $C\ge1$ bounding its [[谱范数|induced Euclidean matrix norm]] for all $t\ge0$. Here $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$ is the length induced by the standard coordinate [[内积|inner product]], with $|x_j|$ the real or complex modulus of coordinate $j$. Hence $\|x(t)\|_2\le C\|x_0\|_2$. Starting within $\varepsilon/C$ keeps the state within any chosen radius $\varepsilon>0$, and the state eventually tends to zero. Linearity lets a local attraction statement extend to every initial vector by scaling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也给出“始终保持小”的稳定性：连续函数 $e^{tA}$ 在每个有限时间区间上有界；又因它最终趋零，存在一个有限常数 $C\ge1$，在全部 $t\ge0$ 上界住它的[[谱范数|诱导欧氏矩阵范数]]。这里 $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$ 是标准坐标[[内积]]给出的长度，$|x_j|$ 表示第 $j$ 个实或复坐标的模。于是 $\|x(t)\|_2\le C\|x_0\|_2$。从半径 $\varepsilon/C$ 内出发，就会一直留在任意指定的半径 $\varepsilon>0$ 内，而且最终趋零。线性还允许通过缩放，把局部吸引的结论推广到所有初值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why one nondecaying mode defeats the conclusion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么一个不衰减模式就足以否定结论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If some eigenvalue has positive real part, the trajectory $e^{\lambda t}v$ grows in magnitude. If its real part is zero, this nonzero trajectory keeps a constant magnitude. Either case prevents convergence to zero for all initial states. For a real matrix and a complex eigenvector, write $v=a+ib$ with real vectors $a,b$. If both real trajectories $e^{tA}a$ and $e^{tA}b$ approached zero, their complex combination would too, contradicting the nondecaying eigenvector trajectory. Thus at least one real initial state also fails to decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某个特征值实部为正，轨迹 $e^{\lambda t}v$ 的大小会增长；若实部为零，这条非零轨迹的大小保持常数。两种情况都否定“所有初值趋零”。对实矩阵和复特征向量，把 $v$ 写成实向量的组合 $a+ib$。如果 $e^{tA}a$、$e^{tA}b$ 两条实轨迹都趋零，它们的复数组合也应趋零，这与不衰减的特征向量轨迹矛盾。因此至少有一个实初值也不会衰减。

> [!warning] On the imaginary axis, distinguish boundedness from decay · 虚轴上要区分有界与衰减
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A pure rotation, with matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, stays bounded but does not approach zero from a nonzero initial state. A nontrivial Jordan block at zero can even grow polynomially. The exact boundedness test belongs to [[连续系统有界判据|the boundary criterion]]. Negative real parts do not promise monotone shrinking. For $B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&4\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ and $x_0=(0,1)^T$, the trajectory is $e^{-t}(4t,1)^T$. Its length at $t=1$ is $\sqrt{17}/e>1$, larger than its initial length, although it eventually vanishes. Compare the related [[非正规矩阵瞬态|discrete-time transient-growth example]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 产生纯旋转：轨迹有界，但从非零初值出发不会趋零。零特征值处的非平凡 Jordan 块甚至可能产生多项式增长。精确的有界条件见[[连续系统有界判据]]。负实部不保证长度单调下降。例如 $B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&4\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$、$x_0=(0,1)^T$ 给出轨迹 $e^{-t}(4t,1)^T$。在 $t=1$ 时，长度为 $\sqrt{17}/e>1$，大于起始长度，却最终仍会消失。可以对照相关的[[非正规矩阵瞬态|离散时间暂时放大例子]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem concerns all initial states, not one observed path; see [[单轨迹与系统稳定性|the single-trajectory limitation]]. It also concerns a constant matrix and a homogeneous equation. For [[离散系统谱稳定性|discrete time]], compare $|\lambda|$ with $1$ instead: a scalar value $-2$ gives continuous decay but discrete growth in magnitude. Variable coefficients, external inputs and nonlinear systems require their own hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理针对全部初值，不能只凭一条观测轨迹判断，见[[单轨迹与系统稳定性]]。它还要求常矩阵与齐次方程。对[[离散系统谱稳定性|离散时间]]，应比较 $|\lambda|$ 与1：标量值 $-2$ 在连续时间给出衰减，在离散时间却给出大小增长。变系数、外加输入和非线性系统各有自己的适用条件。
