# 历史冲击只有经由可说明的再生产机制才构成制度路径依赖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A historical shock constitutes institutional path dependence only through an explicit mechanism of persistence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 历史机制
> 历史冲击可以改变初始制度，但“过去发生过”不会自动使结果持续。路径依赖需要指出再生产环：既有制度改变资源与政治权力的分配，受益者用这些权力维持规则；正式与非正式制度也可以相互强化。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A historical shock can alter initial institutions, but persistence does not follow merely because something happened in the past. Path dependence needs a reproduction loop: institutions alter the distribution of resources and political power, beneficiaries use that power to maintain the rules, and formal and informal institutions may reinforce one another.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以殖民制度为例，有完整意义的证据链不是“殖民历史→今日收入”一根箭头，而是要问：初始的土地、劳动和政治安排如何延续，哪些组织与精英从中受益，后续改革为何被阻止，以及这套规则如何继续改变投资和市场进入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For colonial institutions, a meaningful chain is not a single arrow from colonial history to current income. It asks how initial land, labour, and political arrangements were reproduced, which organizations and elites benefited, why later reforms were blocked, and how the surviving rules continued to affect investment and market entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

路径依赖不等于不可改变。权力平衡、经济机会、内部动员或外部压力改变时，制度也可能发生突变或渐进改革。因此“为什么持续”和“为什么此刻改变”必须放进同一个动态解释，不能把持续当成定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Path dependence is not immutability. Shifts in political power, economic opportunities, internal mobilization, or external pressure can produce gradual or abrupt institutional change. Persistence and change must be explained within one dynamic account rather than treating persistence as a definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 两个地区的土地制度从殖民时期到现代都不同，为什么还不能只凭这一点证明路径依赖？
>
> **答案：** 还需说明中间的制度、组织、权力和激励如何一代代再生产差异，并排除其他持续的共同原因。

- [Acemoglu, Johnson & Robinson, “Reversal of Fortune”](https://doi.org/10.1162/003355302320935025)：核对前殖民地中历史制度反转与后续工业化差异的具体经验论证，不将该样本外推为全球历史定律。
