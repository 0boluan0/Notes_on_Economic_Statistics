# Lagrange 乘子由驻点与约束系统决定，并不天然等于使违约不再有利的最小精确罚金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Lagrange multiplier is determined by the stationarity and constraint system and is not inherently the smallest exact penalty for violation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] Lagrangian 与罚函数是两个构造
> 普通 Lagrangian
> $$
> \mathcal L(x,\nu)=f(x)-\nu^Th(x)
> $$
> 用乘子把约束的一阶法向信息写进驻点系统；求解时仍要同时保留 $h(x)=0$。精确罚函数则另造一个无约束目标，例如在最大化问题中使用
> $$
> f(x)-\rho\|h(x)\|_1,
> $$
> 并在额外条件下证明某些 $\rho$ 能复制原问题的局部解。两者不能只凭“都在惩罚违约”就认成同一对象。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An ordinary Lagrangian inserts constraint normals into a stationarity system while feasibility remains an explicit equation. An exact-penalty method constructs a different unconstrained objective and proves, under additional assumptions, that sufficiently large penalty parameters reproduce constrained solutions. Similar intuition does not make the two constructions identical.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 乘子数值回答的是什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What the multiplier value actually answers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Lagrange必要条件]] 中，乘子是把目标梯度表示成约束梯度线性组合的系数。约束被缩放、重复或改写时，乘子数值可能变化，即使可行集和最优选择不变。若要赋予经济含义，还需把约束右端参数化，并通过价值函数的局部导数得到 [[影子价格]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the Lagrange necessary condition, multipliers are coefficients representing the objective gradient by constraint normals. Rescaling, duplicating, or rewriting constraints can change those coefficients without changing the feasible set or optimizer. An economic interpretation instead requires a parameterised constraint and the local value derivative described by the shadow price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 精确罚理论可以使用乘子，但仍不是定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exact-penalty theory may use multipliers without identifying the concepts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在适当正则性下，乘子大小有时能帮助给出精确罚参数的充分阈值；这是一条另需证明的定理。它不意味着每个乘子都自动是唯一或最小阈值，也不意味着光滑的二次罚项会在有限参数下精确复制约束解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under suitable regularity, multiplier magnitudes can sometimes provide sufficient exact-penalty thresholds. That is a separate theorem. It does not make every multiplier the unique or smallest threshold, and smooth quadratic penalties generally need not reproduce the constrained solution at a finite parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么从 Lagrange 驻点方程解出 $\nu=5$，不能立即宣布“5 是最小精确罚金”？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does solving the Lagrange stationarity equation and obtaining $\nu=5$ not establish that five is the smallest exact penalty?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 驻点方程只确定约束法向量的系数；精确罚金还需指定另一套罚函数并证明其复制原解的阈值性质。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Stationarity determines only a coefficient on the constraint normal. Exactness requires a specified penalty function and a separate proof of the threshold that reproduces the constrained solution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
