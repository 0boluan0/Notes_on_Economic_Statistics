<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Lagrange multipliers and exact penalties
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Lagrange 乘子与精确罚参数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Lagrange multiplier describes how a constrained optimum balances the objective against the constraint. A penalty parameter is a coefficient that we choose in a new objective to discourage violations. They can be related by a theorem, but finding a multiplier does not by itself tell us a smallest penalty that reproduces the desired solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lagrange 乘子描述约束最优点处目标与约束怎样平衡；罚参数是我们在一个新目标里自行选择的系数，用来压低违约方案的收益。两者可以通过定理建立关系，但算出乘子，并不等于已经证明某个最小罚参数能复制原解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a maximization problem with equality constraints $h(x)=0$, where $h(x)\in\mathbb R^m$ is the vector of constraint residuals. A residual is zero when that constraint is satisfied. At a regular optimum the [[Lagrange必要条件|Lagrange condition]] says that $\nabla f$ is a combination of the constraint gradients, with coefficients $\nu\in\mathbb R^m$. A penalty method instead searches over infeasible as well as feasible $x$, subtracting a cost based on how large $h(x)$ is.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑带等式约束 $h(x)=0$ 的最大化问题；$h(x)\in\mathbb R^m$ 把各约束的残差放在一起，残差为零表示满足相应约束。在正则最优点，[[Lagrange必要条件]]把 $\nabla f$ 表示成约束梯度的线性组合，系数为 $\nu\in\mathbb R^m$。罚函数方法则允许在可行点和不可行点中一起搜索，并按 $h(x)$ 的大小扣除收益。

> [!important] Two constructions · 两个构造
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An ordinary Lagrangian inserts constraint normals into a stationarity system while feasibility remains an explicit equation. An exact-penalty method constructs a different unconstrained objective and proves, under additional assumptions, that sufficiently large penalty parameters reproduce constrained solutions. Similar intuition does not make the two constructions identical.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 普通 Lagrangian
> $$
> \mathcal L(x,\nu)=f(x)-\nu^Th(x)
> $$
> 用乘子把约束的一阶法向信息写进驻点系统；求解时仍要同时保留 $h(x)=0$。精确罚函数则另造一个无约束目标，例如在最大化问题中使用
> $$
> f(x)-\rho\|h(x)\|_1,
> $$
> 并在额外条件下证明某些 $\rho$ 能复制原问题的局部解。两者不能只凭“都在惩罚违约”就认成同一对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\nu^Th=\sum_i\nu_i h_i$ is a signed linear term. In contrast, $\|h(x)\|_1=\sum_i|h_i(x)|$ measures total absolute violation and $\rho\ge0$ is a chosen penalty weight. In general the ordinary Lagrangian is not maximized jointly over $x$ and $\nu$; solving its stationarity equations still includes feasibility. The absolute-value penalty has a corner at zero, so an arbitrarily small violation can incur a first-order cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\nu^Th=\sum_i\nu_i h_i$ 是带符号的线性项；$\|h(x)\|_1=\sum_i|h_i(x)|$ 则是各残差绝对值之和，$\rho\ge0$ 是自行选择的惩罚权重。通常不能把普通 Lagrangian 当成对 $x,\nu$ 同时求最大值的问题；其驻点系统仍包含可行性方程。绝对值罚项在零处有尖角，因此任意小的违约也可能受到一阶惩罚。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the multiplier value actually answers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 乘子数值回答的是什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the Lagrange necessary condition, multipliers are coefficients representing the objective gradient by constraint normals. Rescaling, duplicating, or rewriting constraints can change those coefficients without changing the feasible set or optimizer. An economic interpretation instead requires a parameterised constraint and the local value derivative described by the shadow price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Lagrange必要条件]] 中，乘子是把目标梯度表示成约束梯度线性组合的系数。约束被缩放、重复或改写时，乘子数值可能变化，即使可行集和最优选择不变。若要赋予经济含义，还需把约束右端参数化，并通过价值函数的局部导数得到 [[影子价格]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, maximize $5x-x^2-y^2$ subject to $x=0$. Among feasible points the unique optimum is $(0,0)$. With $\mathcal L=5x-x^2-y^2-\nu x$, stationarity gives $5-2x-\nu=0$ and $-2y=0$, so $\nu=5$. Writing the same constraint as $2x=0$ changes the coefficient to $\widetilde\nu=5/2$. Nothing about the feasible points or their objective values changed. A penalty $\rho|2x|$ also uses twice the violation scale of $\rho|x|$; thresholds cannot be compared without specifying those units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如最大化 $5x-x^2-y^2$，约束为 $x=0$。可行点中唯一最优解是 $(0,0)$。令 $\mathcal L=5x-x^2-y^2-\nu x$，驻点条件给出 $5-2x-\nu=0$、$-2y=0$，所以 $\nu=5$。若把同一约束写成 $2x=0$，相应乘子变成 $\widetilde\nu=5/2$，可行集与各点的收益都没有改变。罚项 $\rho|2x|$ 的残差尺度同样是 $\rho|x|$ 的两倍；没有固定单位就不能比较阈值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Say what exactness means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先说明要复制哪一种最优解

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a specified penalty $P_\rho(x)=f(x)-\rho\|h(x)\|_1$, **local exactness at $x^*$** means that a constrained local maximizer $x^*$ is also a local maximizer of $P_\rho$. Global exactness requires a global conclusion. One may demand only that an original optimizer remains optimal, or the stronger statement that the entire optimizer sets coincide with no infeasible ties. Those demands can behave differently at the threshold. In particular, a strict inequality $\rho>r$ does not establish that $\rho=r$ works, or even that a smallest working parameter exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对已经指定的 $P_\rho(x)=f(x)-\rho\|h(x)\|_1$，**在 $x^*$ 处局部精确**表示：原约束问题的局部最大点 $x^*$，仍是 $P_\rho$ 的局部最大点。全局精确则需要全局结论。还要区分“原最优点仍然最优”与更强的“两个最优解集合完全相同，没有不可行的并列解”。阈值处这两种要求可能不同。特别是，证明 $\rho>r$ 有效，不能推出 $\rho=r$ 有效，也不能推出存在最小有效参数。

> [!example] A threshold that is attained · 能取到的阈值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the preceding concave objective, $P_\rho(x,y)=5x-x^2-y^2-\rho|x|$. If $x\ge0$, it equals $(5-\rho)x-x^2-y^2$; if $x<0$, it equals $(5+\rho)x-x^2-y^2$. For every $\rho\ge5$, both branches are nonpositive, with equality only at $(0,0)$. If $0\le\rho<5$, the infeasible point $x=(5-\rho)/2$, $y=0$ gives payoff $(5-\rho)^2/4>0$. Thus this specified penalty has smallest globally exact parameter five. That conclusion came from examining the penalized objective, not merely reading off $\nu=5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对刚才的凹目标，$P_\rho(x,y)=5x-x^2-y^2-\rho|x|$。当 $x\ge0$ 时，它等于 $(5-\rho)x-x^2-y^2$；当 $x<0$ 时，它等于 $(5+\rho)x-x^2-y^2$。若 $\rho\ge5$，两支都不为正，只有 $(0,0)$ 取零；若 $0\le\rho<5$，不可行点 $x=(5-\rho)/2,y=0$ 的收益为 $(5-\rho)^2/4>0$。因此，这个指定罚函数的最小全局精确参数确实是5，但结论来自对罚目标的分析，不是仅仅看到了 $\nu=5$。

> [!warning] The same multiplier need not give a minimum penalty · 同一乘子不保证最小罚参数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Now change the objective to $5x+x^2-y^2$, keeping $x=0$. The constrained optimizer is still $(0,0)$ and the multiplier is still five. For any $\rho>5$, let $\delta=\rho-5>0$. On $0<x<\delta$, the penalized value is $-\delta x+x^2-y^2<0$; for sufficiently small $x<0$ it is also negative, and at $x=0$, $y\ne0$ it is $-y^2<0$. Thus $(0,0)$ is a strict local maximum. But at $\rho=5$, points $(x,0)$ with arbitrarily small $x>0$ give $x^2>0$, so local exactness fails. Here the working local parameters are $(5,\infty)$: infimum five, no smallest parameter. Globally, $x^2$ dominates any finite linear penalty as $x\to+\infty$, so no finite $\rho$ is globally exact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 现在把目标改为 $5x+x^2-y^2$，仍约束 $x=0$。约束最优解还是 $(0,0)$，乘子还是5。对任意 $\rho>5$，令 $\delta=\rho-5>0$；在 $0<x<\delta$ 时罚目标为 $-\delta x+x^2-y^2<0$，对足够小的负 $x$ 也为负，而 $x=0,y\ne0$ 时为 $-y^2<0$，所以 $(0,0)$ 是严格局部最大点。但若 $\rho=5$，任意小的正 $x$、$y=0$ 都给出 $x^2>0$，局部精确性失败。有效的局部参数是 $(5,\infty)$：下确界为5，却没有最小值。全局上，$x\to+\infty$ 时二次项最终超过任意有限线性罚项，所以不存在有限的全局精确参数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Exact-penalty theory may use multipliers without identifying the concepts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 精确罚理论可以使用乘子，但仍不是定义

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under suitable regularity, multiplier magnitudes can sometimes provide sufficient exact-penalty thresholds. That is a separate theorem. It does not make every multiplier the unique or smallest threshold, and smooth quadratic penalties generally need not reproduce the constrained solution at a finite parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在适当正则性下，乘子大小有时能帮助给出精确罚参数的充分阈值；这是一条另需证明的定理。它不意味着每个乘子都自动是唯一或最小阈值，也不意味着光滑的二次罚项会在有限参数下精确复制约束解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A particularly transparent sufficient result uses a global Lagrangian certificate. Suppose $x^*$ is feasible and, for fixed $\nu^*$, satisfies $\mathcal L(x,\nu^*)\le\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)=f(x^*)$ for all $x$ in the search domain. This is an extra global hypothesis, as in [[KKT充分性|KKT sufficiency]], not a consequence of stationarity alone. Set $a=\max_i|\nu_i^*|$. Since $\nu^{*T}h(x)\le a\|h(x)\|_1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个容易直接证明的充分结论来自全局 Lagrangian 证书。假设 $x^*$ 可行，且对固定的 $\nu^*$，搜索域内所有 $x$ 都满足 $\mathcal L(x,\nu^*)\le\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)=f(x^*)$。这是与[[KKT充分性]]中相似的额外全局条件，不是仅凭驻点条件就成立。令 $a=\max_i|\nu_i^*|$，利用 $\nu^{*T}h(x)\le a\|h(x)\|_1$ 得到：

$$
P_\rho(x)=\mathcal L(x,\nu^*)+\nu^{*T}h(x)-\rho\|h(x)\|_1
\le f(x^*)-(\rho-a)\|h(x)\|_1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\rho>a$, every infeasible point has a strictly smaller penalized value, while feasible points retain their original values. Thus the global optimizer sets coincide. At $\rho=a$ the original optimizer still works, but this inequality no longer rules out infeasible ties. General nonlinear local exactness theorems need their own regularity hypotheses; they must not be silently substituted for this global certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\rho>a$，每个不可行点的罚目标都严格更小，可行点的值又与原目标相同，因而两个全局最优解集合相同。若 $\rho=a$，原最优点依然有效，但这个不等式不再排除不可行的并列点。一般非线性问题的局部精确罚定理各有正则性条件，不能悄悄拿来替代这里的全局证书。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a quadratic penalty, return to $f=5x-x^2-y^2$ and subtract $\rho x^2$. The unconstrained maximizer is $x_\rho=5/[2(1+\rho)]$, $y_\rho=0$. It violates $x=0$ for every finite $\rho\ge0$, although $x_\rho\to0$ as $\rho\to\infty$. Approaching feasibility in a limit is different from an exact feasible optimum at a finite parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对二次罚项，回到 $f=5x-x^2-y^2$ 并减去 $\rho x^2$。无约束最大点为 $x_\rho=5/[2(1+\rho)]$、$y_\rho=0$。每个有限的 $\rho\ge0$ 都违反 $x=0$，尽管当 $\rho\to\infty$ 时 $x_\rho\to0$。极限中接近可行，与有限参数下精确得到可行最优解，是不同结论。

> [!question] Recall · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does solving the Lagrange stationarity equation and obtaining $\nu=5$ not establish that five is the smallest exact penalty?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 为什么从 Lagrange 驻点方程解出 $\nu=5$，不能立即宣布“5 是最小精确罚金”？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Stationarity determines only a coefficient on the constraint normal. Exactness requires a specified penalty function and a separate proof of the threshold that reproduces the constrained solution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 驻点方程只确定约束法向量的系数；精确罚金还需指定另一套罚函数并证明其复制原解的阈值性质。
