# 社会必要劳动时间是在正常生产条件和平均熟练程度及强度下生产商品所需的劳动时间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Socially necessary labour time is the time required to produce a commodity under normal conditions with the prevailing average skill and intensity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**社会必要劳动时间（socially necessary labour time）**是在当时社会正常的生产条件、平均熟练程度和劳动强度下，生产某类商品所需的劳动时间。它是[[Marx商品价值|商品价值量]]的尺度：单件商品被视为其同类的平均样品，其价值量按社会标准衡量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Socially necessary labour time** is the time required to produce a kind of commodity under the normal production conditions and average skill and intensity prevailing in society. It measures the magnitude of [[Marx商品价值|commodity value]]. An individual commodity is treated as an average specimen of its class and measured against the social standard.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假定生产一件标准商品通常需要两小时。甲按正常方法用两小时，乙因不熟练用了四小时；乙的产品不会仅因个人多花两小时就具有两倍价值。这里比较的工时须先按[[抽象劳动|同质社会劳动]]计量，商品种类、质量和有用性也保持可比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a standard commodity normally takes two hours to produce. One producer takes two hours using the normal method, while an inexperienced producer takes four. The second product does not acquire twice the value merely because its maker took longer. The hours must be expressed as [[抽象劳动|homogeneous social labour]], with commodity type, quality and usefulness held comparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

社会标准还会随生产条件改变。原文以动力织机说明：当社会生产同量布所需时间降低，继续使用旧方法的织工即使个人耗时未变，其产品所代表的社会劳动也会下降。因而价值分析既不能奖励个人低效率，也不能把过去的生产条件永久固定下来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The social standard changes with production conditions. Marx's power-loom example shows that when society requires less time to produce a given quantity of cloth, a weaver retaining the old method represents less social labour in the product even though personal working time is unchanged. Value measurement therefore neither rewards individual inefficiency nor freezes past production conditions indefinitely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
