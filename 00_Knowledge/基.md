# Basis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*基*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **basis** is a list of vectors that gives every vector in a chosen space exactly one coefficient representation. It gives us a coordinate system for that space: specify how much of each basis vector to use, then add the contributions. The list must be sufficient to reach every target and contain no redundant vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基**是一组向量，使指定空间中的每个向量都恰好有一套系数表示。它为这个空间提供坐标系统：指定使用多少份每个基向量，再把贡献相加。要求既足够拼出每个目标，又没有多余向量。

> [!example] Coordinates in a different basis · 换一组基读坐标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\mathbb R^2$, let $v_1=(1,0)$ and $v_2=(1,1)$, in that order. The coefficients $c_1,c_2$ multiply the first and second whole vectors:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中，按顺序取 $v_1=(1,0)$、$v_2=(1,1)$。系数 $c_1,c_2$ 分别乘第一个、第二个完整向量：
>
> $$c_1v_1+c_2v_2=(c_1+c_2,c_2).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any target $(a,b)$, the second coordinate requires $c_2=b$, and the first then requires $c_1=a-b$. These values both exist and are forced, so the pair is a basis. For example, $(3,2)=1v_1+2v_2$ has basis coordinates $(1,2)$, although its usual coordinate pair is $(3,2)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意目标 $(a,b)$，第二坐标要求 $c_2=b$，第一坐标再要求 $c_1=a-b$。这组系数既确实存在，又被唯一确定，因此这一对向量是一组基。例如，$(3,2)=1v_1+2v_2$ 在这组基下的坐标是 $(1,2)$，通常的坐标对却是 $(3,2)$。

> [!abstract] Coverage and no redundancy · 覆盖且无冗余
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite list $B=(v_1,\ldots,v_k)$ in a vector space $V$ over $\mathbb F$ is a basis of $V$ when it [[张成|spans]] $V$ and is [[线性无关|linearly independent]]. Spanning means every $x\in V$ can be expressed as $x=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k$. Independence means that $c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k=0$ forces every $c_j=0$. The coefficients are taken from the stated scalar field $\mathbb F$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上向量空间 $V$ 中的有限向量组 $B=(v_1,\ldots,v_k)$ 是 $V$ 的基，要求它[[张成]] $V$ 并且[[线性无关]]。张成表示每个 $x\in V$ 都能写成 $x=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k$；无关表示 $c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k=0$ 迫使每个 $c_j=0$。系数取自指定的标量域 $\mathbb F$。

## 直觉
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the two conditions give one representation*

Spanning supplies existence. For uniqueness, suppose two coefficient lists describe the same vector. Subtract the representations: their coefficient differences combine the basis vectors to zero. Independence forces every difference to vanish. Thus a basis is exactly what is needed to turn a vector into an unambiguous list of coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

张成提供存在性。对于唯一性，假如两组系数描述同一个向量，把两种表示相减：系数之差把这些基向量组合成零。无关性迫使每个差值为零。因此，基恰好能把一个向量变成一份没有歧义的系数列表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order matters for coordinates: in $B=(v_1,v_2)$ the first coordinate multiplies $v_1$; if we reverse the basis order, the coefficient positions reverse too. Choosing a different basis changes the coordinates, not the vector or the space. It also need not give perpendicular or unit-length vectors: neither property appears in the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读取坐标时顺序很重要：在 $B=(v_1,v_2)$ 中，第一坐标乘 $v_1$；基的顺序倒过来，系数位置也要倒过来。换一组基改变的是坐标，不是向量或空间本身。基向量也不必互相垂直，长度也不必为一，定义中没有这两项要求。

## 最小例子与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Both requirements refer to a specified space*

The standard basis of $\mathbb R^2$ is $e_1=(1,0),e_2=(0,1)$, because $(a,b)=ae_1+be_2$ uniquely. Adding $e_1+e_2=(1,1)$ preserves coverage but destroys independence: the third vector can be replaced by the first two. For instance, $(1,1)$ then has coefficients $(1,1,0)$ and $(0,0,1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\mathbb R^2$ 的标准基是 $e_1=(1,0),e_2=(0,1)$，因为 $(a,b)=ae_1+be_2$ 且表示唯一。再加入 $e_1+e_2=(1,1)$，覆盖性仍在，无关性却消失了：第三个向量能由前两个替代。例如，此时 $(1,1)$ 既有系数 $(1,1,0)$，又有 $(0,0,1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use three-coordinate vectors $u_1=(1,0,0),u_2=(0,1,0)$. They are a basis of the $xy$-plane $S=\{(x,y,0):x,y\in\mathbb R\}$, but not a basis of $\mathbb R^3$: they cannot produce $(0,0,1)$. “Is this a basis?” therefore needs a target space. The [[线性无关与张成|independence-versus-spanning distinction]] explains these two ways to fail separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再看三个坐标的向量 $u_1=(1,0,0),u_2=(0,1,0)$。它们是 $xy$ 平面 $S=\{(x,y,0):x,y\in\mathbb R\}$ 的基，却不是 $\mathbb R^3$ 的基，因为无法得到 $(0,0,1)$。所以问“是不是一组基”，必须说清目标空间。[[线性无关与张成]]分别解释这两种失败方式。

> [!note] Zero space and scalar field · 零向量空间与标量域边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The zero vector space $\{0\}$ has the **empty basis**: the empty list spans zero and is independent. The one-vector list $(0)$ is not a basis, because it is dependent. Also, $1,i$ form a basis of $\mathbb C$ over real scalars: $a+bi$ needs two real coefficients. Over complex scalars, the single vector $1$ is already a basis, because any $z\in\mathbb C$ equals $z\cdot1$ uniquely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 零向量空间 $\{0\}$ 的基是**空向量组**：空组张成零，并且无关。只含一个零向量的向量组 $(0)$ 不是基，因为它相关。此外，$1,i$ 是 $\mathbb C$ 在实标量下的一组基，$a+bi$ 需要两个实系数；允许复标量时，单个向量 $1$ 已是一组基，因为任意 $z\in\mathbb C$ 唯一地等于 $z\cdot1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card uses finite bases. A space that has a finite spanning list is finite-dimensional; redundant members can be removed until a basis remains. Every basis of that space has the same length, which defines its [[维数|dimension]]. A space with no finite spanning list cannot be described by a finite basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡讨论有限基。存在有限张成组的空间叫有限维空间，可以不断移除冗余成员，最终留下基。该空间每组基都含相同数量的向量，这个数量定义了[[维数]]。没有任何有限张成组的空间，无法用有限基描述。
