# Topologically equivalent metrics
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拓扑等价度量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two distance rules can disagree about how far apart points are while agreeing on which sets are open. Such metrics are **topologically equivalent**. On the real line, measuring distance by $|x-y|$ or by $2|x-y|$ changes every positive reading, but it does not change which points can be approached by arbitrarily small neighbourhoods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两套距离规则可以对“相隔多远”给出不同数值，却对“哪些集合是开集”完全一致。这样的度量叫作**拓扑等价度量**。在实数线上，用 $|x-y|$ 或 $2|x-y|$ 测距，会改变每个正的距离读数，却不会改变通过任意小邻域接近一个点的方式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[度量空间|metric]] $d$ on a set $X$ determines open balls $B_d(x,r)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$. A set is [[开集|open]] when each of its points has such a ball staying inside it. Write $\tau_d$ for the family of all these open sets, the [[拓扑|topology]] generated by $d$. Two metrics $d$ and $\rho$ on the **same set** are topologically equivalent when $\tau_d=\tau_\rho$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合 $X$ 上的[[度量空间|度量]] $d$ 决定开球 $B_d(x,r)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$。若集合中每个点都各有一个留在集合内的球，这个集合就[[开集|开]]。用 $\tau_d$ 表示这些开集组成的族，即 $d$ 生成的[[拓扑]]。**同一个集合**上的两个度量 $d,\rho$ 拓扑等价，就是 $\tau_d=\tau_\rho$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compare small balls in both directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在两个方向比较小球*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To show that the open sets agree, it is enough to check two local containments. At every $x\in X$ and for every radius $r>0$, a sufficiently small $\rho$-ball must fit in the $d$-ball of radius $r$; the same must hold after exchanging the metrics:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要证明开集族相同，只需检查两个局部包含关系：对每个 $x\in X$ 和每个半径 $r>0$，都能找到足够小的 $\rho$-球，放进半径为 $r$ 的 $d$-球；交换两个度量后也要成立：

$$\begin{aligned}
&\forall x\in X\;\forall r>0\;\exists s>0:
 &&B_\rho(x,s)\subseteq B_d(x,r),\\
&\forall x\in X\;\forall r>0\;\exists t>0:
 &&B_d(x,t)\subseteq B_\rho(x,r).
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if $U$ is $d$-open and $x\in U$, first fit a $d$-ball inside $U$, then a $\rho$-ball inside that ball. Thus $U$ is $\rho$-open. Reversing the roles proves the other inclusion. Conversely, equal open-set families make every ball in one metric open in the other, giving these containments. The radii $s,t$ may depend on both $x$ and $r$; no common scale factor is required. Equivalently, the identity map $x\mapsto x$ is [[连续性|continuous]] in both directions between the two metric spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，若 $U$ 是 $d$-开集且 $x\in U$，先在 $U$ 内放一个 $d$-球，再在这个球内放一个 $\rho$-球，就说明 $U$ 是 $\rho$-开集。交换角色得到另一方向。反过来，开集族相同，就使一种度量的每个球在另一种度量下也开，从而得到上述包含。半径 $s,t$ 可以同时依赖 $x,r$，不要求存在统一缩放比例。等价地，恒等映射 $x\mapsto x$ 在这两个度量空间之间的两个方向都[[连续性|连续]]。

> [!example] Keep small distances, cap large ones · 保留小距离，截断大距离
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Starting from any metric $d$, put $\rho(x,y)=\min\{1,d(x,y)\}$. Distinct points still have positive distance and symmetry is preserved. For nonnegative $a,b$,
> $$\min\{1,a+b\}\le\min\{1,a\}+\min\{1,b\}.$$
> If either $a$ or $b$ is at least $1$, the right side is at least $1$; if both are below $1$, the right side is $a+b$. Combining this inequality with the triangle inequality for $d$ proves that $\rho$ is a metric. For every $0<r\le1$, $B_\rho(x,r)=B_d(x,r)$, so the metrics generate the same topology.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从任意度量 $d$ 出发，令 $\rho(x,y)=\min\{1,d(x,y)\}$。不同点的距离仍为正，对称性也保留。上式对非负 $a,b$ 成立：若其中一个至少为 $1$，右侧就至少为 $1$；若两者都小于 $1$，右侧就是 $a+b$。把它与 $d$ 的三角不等式合用，便证明 $\rho$ 仍是度量。对每个 $0<r\le1$，两个开球完全相同，因此生成同一个拓扑。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For usual distance on $\mathbb R$, $d(0,2)=2$ and $d(0,100)=100$, whereas both corresponding $\rho$-distances are $1$. The whole real line is unbounded under $d$ but bounded under $\rho$. No single positive constant $c$ can satisfy $\rho=cd$ for all pairs: the pair $(0,1/2)$ forces $c=1$, while $(0,2)$ rules that out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在带通常距离的 $\mathbb R$ 上，$d(0,2)=2$、$d(0,100)=100$，对应的 $\rho$-距离却都为 $1$。整条实数线在 $d$ 下无界，在 $\rho$ 下有界。也不存在一个正数 $c$ 使所有点对都满足 $\rho=cd$：点对 $(0,1/2)$ 强迫 $c=1$，点对 $(0,2)$ 又否定这个值。

## Which conclusions survive the change?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*换度量后哪些结论保持*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal open sets give equal closed sets, [[集合内部|interiors]], [[集合闭包|closures]] and [[集合边界|boundaries]]. They also give the same [[序列收敛|convergence to each specified point]]: eventually entering every open neighbourhood is the same test. Continuous maps into or out of the space are unchanged when all other spaces are kept fixed, because the open-preimage test is unchanged. [[紧致性|Compactness]], defined using open covers, and [[连通性|connectedness]], defined using open separations, are unchanged for the same reason.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开集相同，闭集、[[集合内部|内部]]、[[集合闭包|闭包]]和[[集合边界|边界]]也相同。[[序列收敛|序列是否收敛到某个指定点]]同样不变，因为最终进入每个开邻域的检验相同。固定其他空间后，映入或映出这个空间的连续映射也不变，因为开集原像判据不变。用开覆盖定义的[[紧致性]]、用开集分割定义的[[连通性]]也因此保留。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quantitative conclusions still require the actual distance. Diameter means the supremum of pairwise distances, and a Lipschitz bound compares output distance with a fixed multiple of input distance. Neither a diameter nor such a fixed constant is determined just by the open sets. For instance, the identity from $(\mathbb R,\rho)$ to $(\mathbb R,d)$ in the capped example is continuous but has no finite Lipschitz constant: $d(0,n)=n$ while $\rho(0,n)=1$ for positive integers $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

涉及数值的结论仍要看实际度量。直径是点对距离的上确界；Lipschitz 界则要求输出距离不超过输入距离的某个固定倍数。开集族本身不能确定直径或这个固定常数。例如截断例中的恒等映射 $(\mathbb R,\rho)\to(\mathbb R,d)$ 连续，却没有有限的 Lipschitz 常数：对正整数 $n$，$d(0,n)=n$ 而 $\rho(0,n)=1$。
