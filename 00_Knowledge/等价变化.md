# 等价变化以原价格衡量达到变化后效用所需调整的收入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equivalent variation uses original prices to measure the income adjustment that delivers final utility.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价变化（EV）是在保持原价格的反事实中，为达到变化后效用而需要施加的收入变化。本卡采用“福利改善为正”的符号：正 EV 是原预算应增加的收入；负 EV 是原预算应扣去的收入。它与所比较的状态变化在效用上等价，并非保持原购买组合不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EV is the income change at original prices that delivers final utility. Under the gain-positive convention, positive EV adds income to the original budget, while negative EV removes income. It is equivalent in utility to the state change being compared, rather than preserving the original consumption bundle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $(p_0,m_0)$ 变到 $(p_1,m_1)$，记 $u_1=v(p_1,m_1)$。在 [[普通与补偿需求|支出函数的标准对偶条件]] 下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a change from $(p_0,m_0)$ to $(p_1,m_1)$, let $u_1=v(p_1,m_1)$. Under the [[普通与补偿需求|standard expenditure duality conditions]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
EV=e(p_0,u_1)-m_0,
\qquad v(p_0,m_0+EV)=u_1.
$$

例如 $u(x,y)=\sqrt{xy}$、收入固定为 $100$、$p_y=1$，$p_x$ 从 $10$ 升到 $20$。新选择 $(2.5,50)$ 给出 $u_1=\sqrt{125}$；在原价格达到这个效用只需 $e(10,1,u_1)=100/\sqrt2$，所以：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $u(x,y)=\sqrt{xy}$, fixed income $100$, $p_y=1$, and $p_x$ rising from $10$ to $20$, the new bundle $(2.5,50)$ gives utility $\sqrt{125}$. At original prices, the expenditure needed for that utility is $100/\sqrt2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
EV=\frac{100}{\sqrt2}-100\approx-29.289.
$$

在原价格下扣去 $29.289$，与不补偿地经历涨价一样差；这个金额不同于在新价格下恢复原效用所需的 $41.421$，后者等于 $-CV$，见 [[补偿变化]]。税收课以原价格下的一次总付税与商品税比较效用时，使用的是这类等价收入比较，不能直接换成普通需求面积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Removing $29.289$ at original prices leaves the consumer as badly off as the uncompensated price increase. Restoring initial utility at new prices instead requires $41.421$, the negative of [[补偿变化|CV]]. Comparing a commodity tax with an equally harmful lump-sum tax at original prices uses this equivalent-income comparison, not automatically an ordinary-demand area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
