# 可导函数复合的导数等于外层导数在内层取值处乘以内层导数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The derivative of a composition is the outer derivative evaluated at the inner value times the inner derivative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 若 $g$ 在 $x$ 可导，$f$ 在 $g(x)$ 可导，则
> $$
> (f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x).
> $$
> 外层先把中间量的微小变化缩放一次，内层再说明原输入怎样产生这个中间变化；两次局部缩放相乘。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $g$ is differentiable at $x$ and $f$ is differentiable at $g(x)$, then $(f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x)$. The inner map converts an input increment into an intermediate increment, and the outer map converts that intermediate increment into an output increment; the two local scale factors multiply.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $u=g(x)$ 当作中间变量可写成 $dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$，但这只是帮助记忆的记号。严格证明要处理 $\Delta u$ 可能为零的情形，不能把它当普通分数无条件约掉；本质依据是两层可导各自给出的线性余项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mnemonic $dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$ is useful, but it is not unconditional cancellation of ordinary fractions. A proof must also cover increments for which $\Delta u=0$; the real argument composes the local-linear remainders supplied by differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $y=(3x^2+1)^5$，令 $u=3x^2+1$，则
$$
y'=5u^4\cdot6x=30x(3x^2+1)^4.
$$
漏掉 $6x$ 只计算了输出对中间量的反应，没有计算中间量对原输入的反应。多层复合从外向内逐层相乘，并在每层检查定义域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y=(3x^2+1)^5$, set $u=3x^2+1$ to obtain $y'=5u^4(6x)=30x(3x^2+1)^4$. Omitting $6x$ measures only the outer response to the intermediate value. A deeper composition multiplies one derivative per layer and retains every domain restriction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $d[\sin(10x)]/dx$ 与 $d[(\sin x)^{10}]/dx$ 为什么不同？
>
> **答案：** 两者的内外层不同：前者为 $10\cos(10x)$，后者为 $10(\sin x)^9\cos x$。
