# Chain rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*链式法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **chain rule** follows a change through successive functions. Suppose $x$ first determines $u=3x^2+1$, and that intermediate value then determines $y=u^5$. To find how fast $y$ changes with $x$, we need both links: how $x$ changes $u$, and how $u$ changes $y$. A [[导数|derivative]] measures each local rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**链式法则**追踪一个变化怎样经过连续的函数作用。假设先由 $x$ 算出 $u=3x^2+1$，再由这个中间值算出 $y=u^5$。要知道 $y$ 随 $x$ 变化得多快，两段关系都不能漏：$x$ 如何改变 $u$，以及 $u$ 如何改变 $y$。每一段的局部变化率由[[导数]]衡量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep track of the input to each function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先分清每一层接收什么输入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the inner function as $g(x)$ and the outer function as $f(u)$. Their **composition** is $y=f(g(x))$, also written $(f\circ g)(x)$: compute $g$ first, then feed its result into $f$. Here $x$ is the original independent input; $u$ and $y$ depend on it. When calculating $f'(u)$, we temporarily treat $u$ as the input of $f$, then evaluate this derivative at the actual value $u=g(x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把内层函数写成 $g(x)$，外层写成 $f(u)$。它们的**复合**是 $y=f(g(x))$，也写作 $(f\circ g)(x)$：先算 $g$，再把结果交给 $f$。这里 $x$ 是最初的自变量，$u$、$y$ 都依赖它。求 $f'(u)$ 时，暂把 $u$ 当作 $f$ 的输入；随后要在实际中间值 $u=g(x)$ 处取这个导数。

> [!abstract] Two local rates multiply · 两段局部变化率相乘
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $g$ is differentiable at $x_0$, and $f$ is differentiable at $u_0=g(x_0)$, then the composition is differentiable at $x_0$ and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $g$ 在 $x_0$ 可导，且 $f$ 在 $u_0=g(x_0)$ 可导，则复合函数在 $x_0$ 可导，并且
>
> $$(f\circ g)'(x_0)=f'(g(x_0))g'(x_0).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The outer derivative is evaluated at the **inner value**, not at the inner derivative. In the notation $u=g(x)$ and $y=f(u)$, the same rule is $dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 外层导数代入的是**内层函数值**，不是内层导数。用 $u=g(x)$、$y=f(u)$ 的记号，同一条规则写成 $dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$。

> [!example] Differentiate both links · 两段关系都要求导
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $y=(3x^2+1)^5$, the [[整数幂求导|integer power rule]] gives $df/du=5u^4$ and $dg/dx=6x$; the constant term contributes zero by [[导数线性法则|linearity and the constant rule]]. Therefore
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $y=(3x^2+1)^5$，[[整数幂求导]]给出 $df/du=5u^4$、$dg/dx=6x$；根据[[导数线性法则]]中的常数法则，常数项的导数为零。因此
>
> $$y'=5(3x^2+1)^4\cdot6x=30x(3x^2+1)^4.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $x=1$, the intermediate value is $u=4$. The inner rate is $6$ and the outer rate is $5\cdot4^4=1280$, so the overall rate is $7680$. A small change $h$ in $x$ produces approximately $6h$ in $u$, then approximately $1280(6h)$ in $y$. Omitting $6x$ would answer a different question: change per unit of $u$, rather than per unit of $x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=1$ 处，中间值为 $u=4$。内层变化率是 $6$，外层是 $5\cdot4^4=1280$，总变化率就是 $7680$。$x$ 的小改变量 $h$ 先使 $u$ 改变约 $6h$，再使 $y$ 改变约 $1280(6h)$。漏掉 $6x$，算出的会是每单位 $u$ 的变化，而不是每单位 $x$ 的变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the rule also works when the intermediate change is zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中间量没有变化时，规则为什么仍成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rate-multiplication picture is useful, but $dy/du$ and $du/dx$ are not ordinary fractions that may always be cancelled. For a nonzero increment $h$ in $x$, the intermediate increment $k=g(x_0+h)-g(x_0)$ can be zero. Dividing by $k$ would then fail. The [[导数|local approximation definition of differentiability]] gives a proof without that division.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

变化率相乘的直觉很有用，但 $dy/du$、$du/dx$ 不是可以无条件约分的普通分数。即使 $x$ 的增量 $h$ 非零，中间增量 $k=g(x_0+h)-g(x_0)$ 仍可能为零；这时不能除以 $k$。[[导数]]中的局部近似定义能避开这一步。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=f'(u_0)$ and $B=g'(x_0)$. Differentiability of $g$ says $k/h\to B$, so also $k\to0$. Differentiability of $f$ lets us write its output change as $[A+\varepsilon(k)]k$, where the error coefficient $\varepsilon(k)\to0$ as $k\to0$. Set $\varepsilon(0)=0$; the identity is then still true when $k=0$. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $A=f'(u_0)$、$B=g'(x_0)$。$g$ 可导意味着 $k/h\to B$，也就有 $k\to0$。$f$ 可导意味着它的输出增量可以写成 $[A+\varepsilon(k)]k$，其中误差系数 $\varepsilon(k)$ 随 $k\to0$ 趋于零。令 $\varepsilon(0)=0$，则在 $k=0$ 时这个等式仍成立。于是

$$\frac{f(g(x_0+h))-f(g(x_0))}{h}
=[A+\varepsilon(k)]\frac{k}{h}\longrightarrow AB.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proves the rule even for a constant inner function, or at a point where $g'(x_0)=0$. More layers work the same way: for $f(g(q(x)))$, multiply $f'(g(q(x)))$, $g'(q(x))$ and $q'(x)$, checking that every layer is defined and differentiable at the value it receives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也覆盖内层为常数函数，或 $g'(x_0)=0$ 的情形。更多层同样处理：对 $f(g(q(x)))$，把 $f'(g(q(x)))$、$g'(q(x))$、$q'(x)$ 相乘，并逐层检查收到的输入是否属于定义域、该处是否可导。

> [!question] Which operation happens first? · 哪一步先发生？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Compare $\sin(10x)$ with $(\sin x)^{10}$. In the first, multiply by $10$ before applying sine; in the second, apply sine before taking the tenth power. Using [[正弦余弦求导|the sine derivative in radians]], their derivatives are respectively $10\cos(10x)$ and $10(\sin x)^9\cos x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 比较 $\sin(10x)$ 与 $(\sin x)^{10}$：前者先乘 $10$ 再取正弦，后者先取正弦再求十次幂。结合[[正弦余弦求导|弧度制下的正弦导数]]，两者的导数分别是 $10\cos(10x)$ 和 $10(\sin x)^9\cos x$。
