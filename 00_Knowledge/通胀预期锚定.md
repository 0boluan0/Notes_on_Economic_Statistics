# 通胀预期锚定是公众的中长期通胀判断围绕可信目标且较少随短期消息漂移的状态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inflation expectations are anchored when medium- and long-run beliefs remain near a credible objective and respond relatively little to short-run news.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预期锚定不是要求每个人对下个月通胀作出完全相同的预测，而是当短期物价受到冲击时，公众仍相信中长期通胀会回到相应目标附近。判断既要看预期水平是否与目标一致，也要看对消息的敏感度、期限和人群分歧；一条稳定但远高于目标的预测不能证明目标得到信任。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Anchoring does not require identical forecasts of next month's inflation. It concerns confidence that inflation will return near the relevant objective after short-run shocks. Assess levels relative to the objective, sensitivity to news, horizons and disagreement. A stable forecast far above the target does not establish trust in that target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工资、合同和价格决策可能参考未来通胀；若一次成本上升同时推动长期预期持续上移，它可经这些决策延长通胀压力。但具体传导取决于信息、议价、竞争和合同安排，长期调查指标也不能直接替代具体锁价或定薪期间的预期。不能仅凭调查预期上升就认定已经形成工资—价格螺旋；[[供给冲击下的政策权衡]]须同时评估实际收入和政策时滞。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wages, contracts and prices may reflect expected inflation, so a persistent shift in long-run beliefs can extend a cost shock. Transmission depends on information, bargaining, competition and contracts; a long-horizon survey cannot substitute for expectations over a particular price- or wage-setting period. A higher survey forecast alone does not establish a wage–price spiral; [[供给冲击下的政策权衡|supply-shock policy analysis]] must also assess real income and policy lags.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教学对照：能源涨价后，同一调查的一年预期从 3% 升至 4%，五至十年预期仍为 2.1%。这与长期预期较稳相容，不能据此证明完全锚定；还应检查样本、分歧和多次消息反应。若[[盈亏平衡通胀]]同期上升，也要先区分预期、风险与流动性补偿，不把市场补偿当成纯预期读数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a survey's one-year forecast rises from 3 to 4 percent after an energy shock while its five-to-ten-year forecast remains 2.1 percent. This is consistent with stable long-run beliefs, not proof of perfect anchoring. Check samples, dispersion and responses to repeated news. A simultaneous rise in [[盈亏平衡通胀|breakeven inflation]] also requires separating expectations from risk and liquidity compensation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[央行透明与问责]]和一致的行动有助于公众理解政策，但发布目标本身不是锚定已经成功的证据。[[前瞻指引]]描述未来政策路径，预期锚定则描述公众的通胀判断，两者不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[央行透明与问责|Transparency, accountability]] and consistent actions can aid understanding, but publishing a target does not establish successful anchoring. [[前瞻指引|Forward guidance]] concerns future policy, whereas anchoring concerns public inflation beliefs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Bernanke（2007），Inflation Expectations and Inflation Forecasting，锚定定义与研究问题部分](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/Bernanke20070710a.htm)：对短期消息的相对不敏感、期限和人群测量差异、工资定价联系与测量限制。
- [Bandera 等（2023），Monetary policy in the face of supply shocks，§3 及结论](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/conferences/ecbforum/shared/pdf/2023/Tenreyro_paper.pdf)：预期形成及经济影响的证据限制，避免把预期指标直接作为可靠中间目标。数值对照为教学构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bernanke supplies the anchoring concept and measurement questions; Bandera and coauthors qualify the evidence on formation and effects. The numerical contrast is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
