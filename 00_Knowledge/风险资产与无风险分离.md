# 共同机会集下均值—方差有效配置可分为共同风险基金与无风险资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With a common opportunity set, mean–variance-efficient allocation separates into a common risky fund and a risk-free asset*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设投资者面对相同的单期 $\mu,\Sigma,r_f$，$\Sigma$ 正定，能够无税费地无限卖空风险资产并按同一 $r_f$ 借贷，而且 [[切点组合闭式解|有限正 Sharpe 切点]] $T$ 存在。则风险／无风险联合机会集的均值—方差有效配置都可写成 $yT+(1-y)f$、$y\ge0$。采用 [[均值方差偏好边界|相容均值—方差偏好]] 的投资者先选共同风险基金，再按风险偏好选 $y$；不是仅凭“风险厌恶”就能得出的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose investors share one-period $\mu,\Sigma,r_f$, with positive-definite $\Sigma$, unrestricted costless short sales, borrowing and lending at the same $r_f$, and an existing [[切点组合闭式解|finite positive-Sharpe tangency portfolio]] $T$. Every mean–variance-efficient allocation in the joint risky/risk-free opportunity set has the form $yT+(1-y)f$, $y\ge0$. Investors with [[均值方差偏好边界|compatible mean–variance preferences]] first choose the common risky fund and then select $y$ according to risk preferences. Risk aversion alone does not establish this result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么能分离？令 $a=\mu-r_f\mathbf1$、$d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}a>0$。对任意风险头寸向量 $x$，无风险权重为 $1-\mathbf1^\top x$，组合超额均值为 $a^\top x$、方差为 $x^\top\Sigma x$。固定波动 $s>0$ 时，Cauchy–Schwarz 给出的最高超额均值由同一正方向达到：$x=s\Sigma^{-1}a/\sqrt{a^\top\Sigma^{-1}a}$。因为 $d>0$，这正是对 $w_T$ 的非负倍数；$s=0$ 时全持无风险资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does separation hold? Define $a=\mu-r_f\mathbf1$ and $d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}a>0$. For any risky position vector $x$, the risk-free weight is $1-\mathbf1^\top x$, mean excess return is $a^\top x$, and variance is $x^\top\Sigma x$. At a fixed volatility $s>0$, the largest excess mean allowed by Cauchy–Schwarz is attained in one positive direction: $x=s\Sigma^{-1}a/\sqrt{a^\top\Sigma^{-1}a}$. Since $d>0$, this is a nonnegative multiple of $w_T$. At $s=0$, the allocation is entirely risk-free.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若进一步明确采用 $U=\mu_C-\frac\gamma2\sigma_C^2$、$\gamma>0$，把 [[资本配置线|配置公式]] 代入并最大化这一严格凹二次式，可得下式。偏好参数改变基金规模，不改变 $T$ 内部的相对权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If preferences specifically take the form $U=\mu_C-\frac\gamma2\sigma_C^2$ with $\gamma>0$, substituting the [[资本配置线|allocation formulas]] and maximizing the strictly concave quadratic gives the expression below. The preference parameter changes fund size, not the relative weights inside $T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y^*=\frac{\mu_T-r_f}{\gamma\sigma_T^2}.
$$

例如，假定已经找到 $\mu_T=9\%$、$\sigma_T=20\%$ 的切点且 $r_f=3\%$：$\gamma=3$ 给 $y^*=0.5$，$\gamma=1$ 给 $y^*=1.5$。后者需要借入初始财富的 50%；若借款受限，这个无约束解便不再是可实施方案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an already identified tangency portfolio with $\mu_T=9\%$, $\sigma_T=20\%$, and $r_f=3\%$, $\gamma=3$ gives $y^*=0.5$, while $\gamma=1$ gives $y^*=1.5$. The latter requires borrowing 50% of initial wealth; a borrowing restriction makes that unconstrained solution infeasible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条“一个风险基金加无风险资产”的结果不同于仅有风险资产时的 [[双基金分离]]。投资者的资产范围、预期、约束或融资成本不同时，共同基金结论也不能直接照搬；把共同切点认作市场组合还需 [[CAPM假设|市场出清]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This one-risky-fund-plus-cash result differs from [[双基金分离|two-fund separation with risky assets alone]]. Different asset universes, beliefs, constraints, or financing costs prevent direct reuse of the common-fund conclusion. Identifying the common tangency portfolio with the market additionally requires [[CAPM假设|market clearing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*

- [Zivot，Portfolio Theory with Matrix Algebra，PDF 第 34–38 页](https://faculty.washington.edu/ezivot/econ424/portfoliotheorymatrixslides.pdf#page=34)：支持切点符号、风险／无风险基金分离与配置比例；本卡的共同正方向证明由其矩阵设定独立推导。
- [Bollerslev，Duke Econ 471/571，PDF 第 29–30、40–45、79–84 页](https://public.econ.duke.edu/~boller/Econ.471-571.F19/Lec1_471-571_F19.pdf#page=29)：支持两阶段决策与二次均值—方差准则下的最优配置；$\gamma=3,1$ 算例独立复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Zivot, Portfolio Theory with Matrix Algebra, PDF pp. 34–38](https://faculty.washington.edu/ezivot/econ424/portfoliotheorymatrixslides.pdf#page=34) supports tangency signs, risky/risk-free fund separation, and allocation shares. The common-positive-direction proof is independently derived from its matrix setup.
- [Bollerslev, Duke Econ 471/571, PDF pp. 29–30, 40–45, and 79–84](https://public.econ.duke.edu/~boller/Econ.471-571.F19/Lec1_471-571_F19.pdf#page=29) supports two-stage choice and optimal allocation under the quadratic mean–variance criterion. The $\gamma=3,1$ example is independently checked.
