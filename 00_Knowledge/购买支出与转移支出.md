# 购买支出换取商品服务或非金融资产而转移支出不取得直接对应的对价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government purchases obtain goods, services or nonfinancial assets; transfers provide no directly corresponding consideration to the payer.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在财政学的购买性/转移性二分中，购买支出是政府为取得商品、服务、劳动或非金融资产支付的款项；转移支出则把资金或资产交给其他主体，不换取对方直接提供的对应对价。判断看的是交易关系，不是政策“有没有社会好处”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the textbook purchases/transfers distinction, purchases obtain goods, services, labour or nonfinancial assets. Transfers convey funds or assets without directly corresponding consideration from the recipient. Classification follows the transaction, not whether the policy benefits society.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府付钱雇教师上课，是购买劳动服务；政府发放助学金，学生受教育虽然有益，仍不是向政府出售等额劳动或商品。购买也不限于当期消费：买办公用品和购建政府拥有的校舍，分别涉及当期使用与 [[经常支出与资本支出|资本形成]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paying a teacher purchases labour services. A student grant remains a transfer even though education is beneficial: the student is not selling equivalent labour or goods to government. Purchases include both current-use items and [[经常支出与资本支出|capital formation]], such as a government-owned school building.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若政府补助私人机构建造归该机构所有的设施，这笔款项可以是资本转移；它并不因此成为政府直接取得的固定资产。反过来，实物救助也可以是转移，不能以“是否现金”代替“是否有直接对价”的判断。正式统计还有利息等单列科目，不能把课堂二分法直接当作完整的 [[GFS费用与支出|GFS 科目表]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A grant for a privately owned facility can be a capital transfer, without creating a government-owned fixed asset. Transfers can also be in kind. Cash versus in-kind form does not determine whether consideration is received. Formal statistics separately classify items such as interest; the textbook dichotomy is not the full [[GFS费用与支出|GFS classification]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做社会成本分析时，这个区别只是起点。购买金额要与资源的机会成本核对；纯转移能否抵消取决于核算边界和福利权重，按 [[转移支付核算]] 逐笔对齐。转移政策引起的管理耗费或行为变化，仍可能占用真实资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For social appraisal, purchase payments must be checked against opportunity costs. Whether pure transfers cancel depends on the accounting boundary and welfare weights; apply [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]]. Administration and behavioural responses can still use real resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
