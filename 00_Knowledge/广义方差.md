# Generalized variance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*广义方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Generalized variance summarizes the spread of several measurements by multiplying the variances along their perpendicular principal directions. It gives one number for the combined spread, while allowing a direction with no variation to make that number zero. Its name extends ordinary variance: with just one variable, it is exactly that variable's variance.

For a real [[随机向量|random vector]] $X$ with $p\ge1$ coordinates and finite second moments, its [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] $\Sigma$ records each coordinate's variance on the diagonal and pairwise covariances off the diagonal. Generalized variance is the [[行列式|determinant]] of this matrix:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

广义方差把多个测量量在相互垂直的主方向上的方差相乘，用一个数概括联合散布。只要有一个方向完全没有变化，这个数就会变成零。名称延伸了普通方差：只有一个变量时，它就是该变量的方差。

设实[[随机向量]] $X$ 有 $p\ge1$ 个坐标且二阶矩有限。它的[[协方差矩阵]] $\Sigma$ 在对角线上记录各坐标的方差，在非对角位置记录成对协方差。广义方差定义为这张矩阵的[[行列式]]：

$$G(X)=\det(\Sigma).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $|\Sigma|$ is also common here; it means a determinant, not the sum of absolute entries. For a dataset with $n\ge2$ complete observations, the sample version is $\det(S)$, where $S$ is the [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance matrix]] with divisor $n-1$. Both are nonnegative because covariance matrices are [[协方差矩阵半正定性|positive semidefinite]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也常写成 $|\Sigma|$，这里表示行列式，不是矩阵元素绝对值的总和。对 $n\ge2$ 个完整观测，样本版本为 $\det(S)$，其中 $S$ 是以 $n-1$ 为分母的[[样本协方差矩阵]]。协方差矩阵[[协方差矩阵半正定性|半正定]]，所以这两个量都非负。

> [!example] Same marginal variances, different joint spread · 相同边际方差，不同联合散布
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose two coordinates have variances $4$ and $9$ and covariance $c$. The two-by-two determinant is
> $$\det\begin{bmatrix}4&c\\c&9\end{bmatrix}=36-c^2.$$
> For $c=0$ it is $36$; for $c=3$ it is $27$; for $c=6$ it is $0$. These all retain the same individual variances. At $c=6$, the centered second coordinate is $3/2$ times the first almost surely, so the points have only one direction of variation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个坐标的方差分别为 $4$ 和 $9$，协方差为 $c$ 时，广义方差为 $36-c^2$。取 $c=0,3,6$，分别得到 $36,27,0$；各变量单独的方差始终相同。$c=6$ 时，中心化后的第二坐标几乎必然等于第一坐标的 $3/2$ 倍，因此只剩一个变化方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, if both scalar standard deviations $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$ are positive and $\rho$ is their [[相关系数|correlation coefficient]], then the covariance is $\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2$ and
$$\det(\Sigma)=\sigma_1^2\sigma_2^2(1-\rho^2).$$
Holding the marginal variances fixed, increasing the magnitude of correlation reduces this two-dimensional spread. Zero correlation gives the largest determinant for those fixed variances, but does not by itself establish independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，若两个标准差 $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$ 均为正，[[相关系数]]为 $\rho$，则协方差为 $\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2$，于是
$$\det(\Sigma)=\sigma_1^2\sigma_2^2(1-\rho^2).$$
固定各自方差后，相关系数的绝对值越大，这个二维散布量越小。零相关在这些固定方差下给出最大行列式，但本身不建立独立性。

## Reading the principal-direction product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 用特征值读取这个数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[实对称矩阵谱定理|spectral theorem]] chooses perpendicular unit directions in which $\Sigma$ is diagonal. Along its $j$th direction the variance is the nonnegative eigenvalue $\lambda_j$. The [[行列式与特征值乘积|determinant product rule]] therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]允许选择相互垂直的单位方向，使 $\Sigma$ 在这些坐标下成为对角矩阵。沿第 $j$ 个方向的方差就是非负特征值 $\lambda_j$。由[[行列式与特征值乘积|行列式乘积公式]]，

$$\det(\Sigma)=\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_p.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $\operatorname{diag}(4,9)$ gives $36$, and three principal variances $(9,4,1)$ also give $36$. These examples have different dimensions, so their equal numerical answers are not an equal-spread comparison. Within two dimensions, $(9,1)$ and $(3,3)$ both give $9$, yet one shape is elongated and the other has equal spread in all directions. A product cannot recover the individual factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $\operatorname{diag}(4,9)$ 给出 $36$；三个主方向方差 $(9,4,1)$ 也相乘为 $36$。这两个例子的维度不同，数值相同不能解释成散布相同。即使同在二维，$(9,1)$ 和 $(3,3)$ 都给出 $9$，前者形状拉长，后者各方向等宽。乘积不能还原每一个因子。

> [!warning] Zero does not mean no variation · 零不代表处处没有变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A zero covariance determinant means at least one nonzero linear combination is constant almost surely; the distribution is confined to a lower-dimensional affine subspace. Other directions may still vary greatly. For instance, $\operatorname{diag}(100,0)$ has determinant zero but first-coordinate variance $100$. See [[协方差奇异性|singular covariance]] for this precise equivalence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 协方差行列式为零，表示至少有一个非零线性组合几乎必然为常数，分布局限在更低维的仿射子空间内；其余方向仍可能变化很大。例如 $\operatorname{diag}(100,0)$ 的行列式为零，第一坐标方差却为 $100$。准确的等价关系见[[协方差奇异性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use this summary with its boundaries in view. It changes with [[广义方差尺度敏感性|measurement units]], and its square root—not the determinant itself—sets [[广义方差与椭球体积|fixed-radius covariance-ellipsoid volume]]. It also cannot diagnose [[行列式与条件数边界|numerical conditioning]] by itself. Finally, an unbiased $S$ does not generally make $\det(S)$ an unbiased determinant estimate: for example, a two-observation sample in two dimensions always has $\det(S)=0$ by the [[样本协方差秩边界|rank bound]], even when the population covariance is positive definite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用这个摘要时，还须明确它的边界：它随[[广义方差尺度敏感性|测量单位]]改变；决定[[广义方差与椭球体积|固定半径协方差椭球体积]]的是它的平方根；单靠它也无法判断[[行列式与条件数边界|数值条件性]]。此外，$S$ 无偏通常不意味着 $\det(S)$ 也是行列式的无偏估计。例如二维中只有两个观测时，由[[样本协方差秩边界|秩界]]必有 $\det(S)=0$，即使总体协方差正定。
