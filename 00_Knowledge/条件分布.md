# 条件分布描述给定信息后剩余的不确定性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A conditional distribution describes uncertainty remaining after given information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定 $X=x$ 后，$Y$ 的条件分布把 $Y$ 的事件映到该信息下的概率。离散时，对 $p_X(x)>0$，
$$p_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)=\frac{p_{x,y}}{p_X(x)}.$$
它先保留一个 $X$ 切片，再除以该切片总概率，使对所有 $y$ 的和为 1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditional law of Y given X=x assigns probabilities after that information is supplied. In a discrete model, retain the relevant slice and divide by its total probability to restore normalisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有联合密度时，对 $f_X(x)>0$ 可取 $f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)=f_{X,Y}(x,y)/f_X(x)$。这不是除以通常为 0 的 $P(X=x)$；条件分布版本只需对 $P_X$ 几乎处处确定。条件密度还须使用该切片的支持范围，例如三角形例给 $Y\mid X=x\sim U[0,x]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a joint density, normalise the slice by the marginal density. This does not divide by the usually zero point probability. Versions are determined only almost everywhere under the marginal law. The slice’s support remains essential, as in the conditional uniform distribution on [0,x].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
