# Conditional distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*条件分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **conditional distribution** describes the possible values of a variable and their probabilities after some information is taken as known. If we learn that it is raining, we may revise the probability that someone carries an umbrella. We still need a whole distribution: the probabilities of both carrying and not carrying one must account for every remaining possibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**条件分布**描述在已经知道某些信息之后，一个变量还可能取哪些值，以及这些值各有多大概率。如果知道当天下雨，我们可能会调整对某人带伞概率的判断；但仍需要一份完整分布，让“带伞”和“不带伞”的概率包含所有剩下的可能性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[条件概率|conditional probability]] answers one question under the given information. A conditional distribution collects those answers for all values or events of the variable. The bar $\mid$ means “given”: $Y\mid X=x$ refers to the distribution of $Y$ when the value $x$ of $X$ is supplied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[条件概率]]回答的是在已知信息下某一件事的概率；条件分布把这个变量的所有取值或事件对应的答案组织起来。竖线 $\mid$ 表示“给定”：$Y\mid X=x$ 指的是已知 $X$ 取值为 $x$ 时，$Y$ 的分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the relevant row, then rescale it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*保留符合信息的一行，再重新缩放*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[联合分布|joint distribution]] from the rain-and-umbrella model. Here $X=1$ means rain and $X=0$ means no rain; $Y=1$ means carrying an umbrella and $Y=0$ means not carrying one. A joint probability describes one combination, such as rain and an umbrella together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用雨天与带伞模型的[[联合分布]]：$X=1$ 表示下雨，$X=0$ 表示没下雨；$Y=1$ 表示带伞，$Y=0$ 表示没带伞。联合概率描述一种组合，例如“下雨且带伞”。

| $X\backslash Y$ | $Y=0$ | $Y=1$ | $P(X=x)$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $X=0$ | $0.45$ | $0.15$ | $0.60$ |
| $X=1$ | $0.10$ | $0.30$ | $0.40$ |

> [!example] Given that it rains · 已知下雨
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Knowing $X=1$ leaves the bottom row. Its entries $0.10$ and $0.30$ sum to $0.40$, the original probability of rain. To describe only rainy days, divide each entry by $0.40$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已知 $X=1$，就只保留下面一行。里面的 $0.10$ 和 $0.30$ 合计为 $0.40$，这是原模型中下雨的概率。要描述下雨时的情况，需要把每一项除以 $0.40$：
>
> $$P(Y=0\mid X=1)=\frac{0.10}{0.40}=0.25,$$
> $$P(Y=1\mid X=1)=\frac{0.30}{0.40}=0.75.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These two values are the entire conditional distribution of $Y$ given $X=1$. They are nonnegative and sum to one. The $0.30$ in the original table was a joint probability; $0.75$ is a conditional probability with a different reference set.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这两个数构成 $X=1$ 时 $Y$ 的完整条件分布：各项非负，并且相加为一。原表中的 $0.30$ 是联合概率；$0.75$ 是条件概率，计算时所参照的范围已经改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[边际分布|marginal distribution]] instead combines rainy and dry days: $P(Y=1)=0.15+0.30=0.45$. If we learn that it does not rain, the other row gives $P(Y=1\mid X=0)=0.15/0.60=0.25$. A conditional distribution can therefore change with the value we condition on.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[边际分布]]则把下雨和没下雨的日子合在一起，得到 $P(Y=1)=0.15+0.30=0.45$。如果已知没下雨，另一行给出 $P(Y=1\mid X=0)=0.15/0.60=0.25$。可见，条件分布可以随已知的条件值改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The discrete formula divides by the retained probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*离散公式的分母是保留下来的总概率*

> [!abstract] Conditional PMF · 条件概率质量函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $p_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X=x,Y=y)$ be the joint [[概率质量函数|PMF]], and let $p_X(x)=P(X=x)$ be the marginal PMF. For a fixed $x$ with $p_X(x)>0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 记联合[[概率质量函数|PMF]] 为 $p_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X=x,Y=y)$，边际 PMF 为 $p_X(x)=P(X=x)$。固定一个满足 $p_X(x)>0$ 的 $x$，就有
>
> $$p_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)=\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_X(x)}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The numerator selects the desired cell and the denominator is the total of the selected row. Summing over all $y$ gives $p_X(x)/p_X(x)=1$. If $p_X(x)=0$, this ratio does not define a distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 分子选出目标格子，分母是所选整行的总概率。对所有 $y$ 求和，得到 $p_X(x)/p_X(x)=1$。如果 $p_X(x)=0$，这个比值就不能定义分布。

## With a joint density, normalise a slice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有联合密度时，把切片归一化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For variables with a joint [[概率密度函数|density]], a table is replaced by a surface $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$. Fix $x$ and look at the curve as $y$ varies. Its area is the marginal density $f_X(x)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy$. Dividing that curve by its area produces a conditional density with area one. For $0<f_X(x)<\infty$, this gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当变量有联合[[概率密度函数|密度]]时，概率表变成曲面 $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$。固定 $x$，沿着 $y$ 的方向看一条曲线；它下面的面积是边际密度 $f_X(x)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy$。把曲线除以这个面积，就得到面积为一的条件密度。当 $0<f_X(x)<\infty$ 时，

$$f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)=\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_X(x)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator is a **density**, not $P(X=x)$, which is zero in this setting. Once the conditional density has been formed, a conditional probability is an area:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母是**密度**，不是 $P(X=x)$；在这里，后者等于零。得到条件密度后，条件概率仍然要通过面积计算：

$$P(a<Y<b\mid X=x)=\int_a^b f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)\,dy.$$

> [!example] The same triangle, viewed at a fixed $x$ · 固定 $x$ 看同一个三角形
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the [[边际分布|marginal-distribution example]], $f_{X,Y}(x,y)=2$ on $0<y<x<1$ and zero elsewhere. At a fixed $0<x<1$, the permitted $y$ values run from $0$ to $x$. The slice has area $2x$, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在[[边际分布]]的例子中，$0<y<x<1$ 时 $f_{X,Y}(x,y)=2$，其余位置为零。固定 $0<x<1$ 后，允许的 $y$ 从 $0$ 到 $x$，切片下面积为 $2x$，因此
>
> $$f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)=\frac{2}{2x}=\frac1x,\qquad 0<y<x.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The conditional density is zero outside $(0,x)$. Thus $Y\mid X=x$ is [[连续均匀分布|uniform on $(0,x)$]]: height $1/x$ times width $x$ is one. Using this density-ratio version at $x=1/2$, the density is $2$ on $(0,1/2)$, giving
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 条件密度在 $(0,x)$ 以外为零，所以 $Y\mid X=x$ 在 $(0,x)$ 上服从[[连续均匀分布]]：高度 $1/x$ 乘宽度 $x$ 等于一。使用这个密度比值所指定的版本，在 $x=1/2$ 时，密度在 $(0,1/2)$ 上等于 $2$，于是
>
> $$P(Y<1/4\mid X=1/2)=\int_0^{1/4}2\,dy=\frac12.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The threshold $1/4$ cuts the remaining interval in half. The support matters as much as the height: writing $1/x$ without restricting $y$ to $(0,x)$ would not describe this conditional distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 门槛 $1/4$ 把剩余区间分成两半。允许的取值范围与高度同样重要：如果只写 $1/x$ 而不把 $y$ 限制在 $(0,x)$，就没有写出这个条件分布。

> [!note]- Conditioning at a zero-probability value · 在单点概率为零的值上条件化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The density ratio specifies a version of the conditional distribution. In general, a joint law determines conditional distributions only up to changes on a set of conditioning values whose marginal probability is zero. This is what “determined $P_X$-almost everywhere” means, where $P_X$ denotes the distribution of $X$. Changing the assigned conditional distribution at one fixed zero-probability value of $X$ can leave the joint law unchanged. The specified density-ratio formula provides the version used in the triangle calculation; a zero denominator does not license division by zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 密度比值指定的是条件分布的一个版本。一般而言，联合分布只能确定条件分布到以下程度：在一组边际概率合计为零的条件值上，可以改变指定的条件分布，而不改变联合分布。这就是“只在 $P_X$ 几乎处处意义下确定”，其中 $P_X$ 表示 $X$ 的分布。特别地，在某个单点概率为零的固定 $X$ 值上改动条件分布，可以保持联合分布不变。三角形计算使用的是本例指定的密度比值公式所给出的版本；分母为零时仍然不能做除法。
