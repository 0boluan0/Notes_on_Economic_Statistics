# 替代品威胁是行业外满足相同基本需求的其他方式对行业盈利施加的压力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Substitution pressure comes from alternatives outside the defined industry that meet the same underlying need.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在五力框架中，替代品用不同方式满足客户的相同基本需求，可能限制本行业价格或分走需求。威胁取决于替代方案的相对价格、性能与转换成本，而不是产品名称是否相似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the five-forces framework, substitutes meet the same underlying need differently and may constrain industry prices or divert demand. Their threat depends on relative price, performance, and switching costs, not similar product names.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，视频会议可以替代部分商务出行。必须先固定边界：若研究整个乘用车行业，电动车与燃油车通常是行业内竞争产品；若只研究燃油动力方案，电动方案才可作为外部替代。改变边界后应重画竞争关系，不能让同一例子永久固定在“替代品”一栏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Videoconferencing can replace some business travel. Boundaries come first: in a passenger-car industry analysis, electric and combustion-engine cars are usually rival products within the industry; with combustion propulsion as the focal boundary, electric propulsion can be an outside substitute. Redraw relationships when the boundary changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
