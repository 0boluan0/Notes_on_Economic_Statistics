# Discrete probability space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*离散概率空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **discrete probability space** is a model of an uncertain experiment: it lists the possible outcomes and assigns a probability to each one. Once that list is in place, we can answer questions such as “What is the chance of an even number?” by adding the probabilities of the outcomes that count as an even number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**离散概率空间**是描述一次不确定试验的模型：先列出可能发生的结果，再给每个结果分配概率。有了这份表，就能回答“掷出偶数的概率是多少”这样的问题——把符合条件的结果所占的概率加起来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Outcomes and events
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个结果，与一组符合条件的结果*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose we roll one six-sided die. The **sample space**, written $\Omega$ (the Greek letter omega), is the set of possible outcomes. Braces list the members of a set:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设想掷一次六面骰子。所有可能结果组成的集合叫**样本空间**，记作 $\Omega$（希腊字母 omega）；下面的大括号把集合里的成员列出来：

$$
\Omega=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One particular result, such as $4$, is an **outcome**. An **event** is a set of outcomes that satisfy a condition we care about. For example, “the result is even” is the event $A=\{2,4,6\}$. The event occurs whenever the actual outcome belongs to this set. Thus an outcome records exactly what happened; an event states which outcomes would answer our question with “yes”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中一个具体结果，例如 $4$，叫作一个**基本结果**。**事件**则是符合某个条件的一组结果。例如，“掷出偶数”对应事件 $A=\{2,4,6\}$；只要实际结果属于这个集合，事件就发生了。基本结果记录具体发生了什么，事件说明哪些结果能让我们关心的问题得到“是”的回答。

> [!example] An even result on a fair die · 公平骰子掷出偶数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fair die, each of the six outcomes has probability $1/6$. The event $A$ contains three of them, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 公平骰子的六个结果各有 $1/6$ 的概率。事件 $A$ 包含其中三个，所以
>
> $$P(A)=P(\{2\})+P(\{4\})+P(\{6\})=\frac16+\frac16+\frac16=\frac12.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $P(A)$ means the probability of event $A$. The singleton $\{2\}$ is the event “the result is exactly $2$”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $P(A)$ 表示事件 $A$ 发生的概率；只含一个成员的集合 $\{2\}$ 就是“结果恰好为 $2$”这个事件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What makes the model valid?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*怎样的概率分配才有效？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Discrete” allows either finitely many outcomes or a countably infinite list: outcomes that can be listed as first, second, third, and so on, even if the list never ends. For example, a model for the number of taxis passing during a class can use $0,1,2,3,\ldots$, without building in a fixed maximum. In both cases, probabilities are assigned to individual outcomes and added to obtain event probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“离散”允许结果只有有限个，也允许**可数无穷**个：可以按第一个、第二个、第三个……逐一列出，只是这份清单没有尽头。例如，为一节课期间经过的出租车数量建模，可以使用 $0,1,2,3,\ldots$，不在模型里预设一个固定上限。这两种情形都能为单个结果分配概率，再相加得到事件概率。

> [!abstract] Assign probabilities, then add · 分配概率，再相加
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each outcome $\omega$ must have a nonnegative probability, and the probabilities of all outcomes must sum to $1$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个基本结果 $\omega$ 的概率必须非负，所有基本结果的概率之和必须为 $1$：
>
> $$P(\{\omega\})\geq 0,\qquad \sum_{\omega\in\Omega}P(\{\omega\})=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any event $A\subseteq\Omega$, add only the probabilities of the outcomes in $A$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任何事件 $A\subseteq\Omega$，只把属于 $A$ 的那些基本结果的概率加起来：
>
> $$P(A)=\sum_{\omega\in A}P(\{\omega\}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\sum$ means “add the following terms”; $\omega\in A$ tells us which outcomes to include. The total is $1$ because the model says that some outcome in $\Omega$ occurs. An impossible event contains no outcomes, so its probability is $0$. Textbooks sometimes abbreviate the single-outcome probability $P(\{\omega\})$ as $P(\omega)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\sum$ 表示“把后面的项加起来”；$\omega\in A$ 告诉我们要包括哪些结果。总和为 $1$，是因为模型规定一定会发生 $\Omega$ 中的某个结果。不可能事件不包含任何结果，所以概率为 $0$。教材有时会把单个结果的概率 $P(\{\omega\})$ 简写成 $P(\omega)$。

> [!warning] Counting alone is not enough · 不能总靠数个数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $\Omega=\{a,b,c\}$ with probabilities $0.2,0.3,0.5$. For $A=\{a,c\}$, we get $P(A)=0.2+0.5=0.7$, even though $A$ contains two of the three outcomes. For this counting shortcut to work for every event in a finite sample space, the outcomes must be equally likely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\Omega=\{a,b,c\}$，三个结果的概率分别为 $0.2,0.3,0.5$，那么事件 $A=\{a,c\}$ 的概率是 $0.2+0.5=0.7$。虽然它包含三个结果中的两个，概率却不是 $2/3$。要把“符合条件的个数除以总个数”作为对任意事件通用的捷径，有限样本空间中的基本结果必须等可能。

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where the discrete model applies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a model with a whole interval of possible outcomes, we generally cannot obtain probabilities by summing the individual point probabilities. The general [[概率空间|probability space]] specifies which sets are events and assigns probabilities to those sets. Its [[概率三公理|probability axioms]] retain nonnegativity, total probability $1$, and addition for any finite or countable collection of disjoint events—events that cannot occur together. The discrete construction above satisfies these rules by adding outcome probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当模型允许一整个区间内的结果时，通常不能通过逐点概率求和得到事件概率。一般的[[概率空间]]会明确哪些集合是事件，再给这些集合分配概率。它的[[概率三公理]]仍要求概率非负、全集概率为 $1$，并且对有限个或可数个互斥事件——不能同时发生的事件——可以把概率相加。上面的离散构造通过对基本结果的概率求和，满足了这些规则。
