# 离散概率空间给基本结果赋非负且总和为一的概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A discrete probability space assigns nonnegative probabilities summing to one to its elementary outcomes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 样本空间 $\Omega$ 是一次随机试验所有可能基本结果的集合，事件 $A$ 是 $\Omega$ 的子集。离散概率空间给每个 $\omega\in\Omega$ 赋值 $P(\omega)\ge 0$，且
> $$\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}P(\omega)=1.$$
> 事件概率因此为
> $$P(A)=\sum_{\omega\in A}P(\omega).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> The sample space $\Omega$ is the set of all elementary outcomes of one random experiment, and an event $A$ is a subset of $\Omega$. A discrete probability space assigns each $\omega\in\Omega$ a probability $P(\omega)\ge 0$ such that
> $$\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}P(\omega)=1.$$
> Consequently,
> $$P(A)=\sum_{\omega\in A}P(\omega).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“把事件概率写成基本结果概率之和”是离散模型的表达。连续样本空间通常不能逐点相加；那里需要概率测度和积分。无论离散还是连续，概率都必须非负、全集概率为 $1$，并对两两互斥事件满足可列可加性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Writing an event probability as a sum over elementary outcomes is specific to a discrete model. In a continuous sample space, point probabilities generally cannot be added this way; a probability measure and integration are needed. In either setting, probabilities are nonnegative, the whole space has probability one, and countable additivity holds for pairwise disjoint events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $\Omega=\{a,b,c\}$ 且三个基本结果的概率分别为 $0.2,0.3,0.5$，事件 $A=\{a,c\}$ 的概率是多少？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\Omega=\{a,b,c\}$ and the three elementary outcomes have probabilities $0.2,0.3,0.5$, what is the probability of $A=\{a,c\}$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** $P(A)=0.2+0.5=0.7$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** $P(A)=0.2+0.5=0.7$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
