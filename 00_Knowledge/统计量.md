# 统计量是由观测样本和已知常数确定的可测函数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A statistic is a measurable function of the observed sample and known constants*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 统计量写作 $T_n=g(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$，计算规则不能依赖未知参数。样本给定后应能算出它的值；“是统计量”不保证它对某个估计目标有用。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A statistic is a measurable sample function whose rule does not depend on unknown parameters. Once the sample is given, its value is computable. Being a statistic does not guarantee usefulness for estimation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

$\bar X_n$、样本最大值和常数 $7$ 都是统计量。若 $\mu$ 未知，$\bar X_n-\mu$ 不能仅由数据计算。若检验中把 $\mu_0$ 指定为已知假设值，则 $\bar X_n-\mu_0$ 可以是统计量。选定估计目标后的角色见[[估计对象三分]]；反复取样时的分布见[[抽样分布]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample mean, maximum, and constant seven are statistics. Subtracting an unknown $\mu$ is not a computable rule; subtracting a specified null value $\mu_0$ can be. See [[估计对象三分|estimand, estimator, and estimate]] and [[抽样分布|sampling distributions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
