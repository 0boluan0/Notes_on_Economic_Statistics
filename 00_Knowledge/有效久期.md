# 有效久期是在既定重估模型下衡量基准曲线平行移动的一阶相对价格敏感度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective duration measures first-order relative price sensitivity to a parallel benchmark-curve shift under a specified repricing model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效久期（effective duration）衡量：选定基准利率曲线整体上移一点时，模型价格相对减少多少。先固定估值日、曲线报价及复利单位、利差和其他模型假设；以小数年率的加法位移 $x$ 表示曲线从 $r_0(t)$ 变为 $r_0(t)+x$。令 $P(x)$ 是每次扰动后重新计算的全价，$P_0=P(0)>0$。在价格可微处，其局部定义与常用中央差分估计分别为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective duration asks how much model price falls proportionally when the chosen benchmark curve rises slightly. Fix the valuation date, curve quotation and compounding units, spreads, and other model assumptions. A decimal annual-rate shift $x$ changes $r_0(t)$ to $r_0(t)+x$. Let $P(x)$ be the fully repriced dirty price, with $P_0=P(0)>0$. At a differentiable point, the local measure and its usual central-difference estimate are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
D_{\mathrm{eff}}=-\frac{P'(0)}{P_0},\qquad
\widehat D_{\mathrm{eff}}(h)=\frac{P(-h)-P(h)}{2hP_0},\quad h>0.
$$

因此小幅变化满足 $\Delta P/P_0\approx-D_{\mathrm{eff}}\Delta x$。例如固定现金流模型在该冲击下给出 $P(x)=100e^{-5x}$，精确局部久期为 5；用 $h=0.001$（10bp）重估得 $P(-h)=100.501252$、$P(h)=99.501248$，差分久期约为 5.000021。这里“5”并不保证上涨 1 个百分点就精确损失 5%。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a small move, $\Delta P/P_0\approx-D_{\mathrm{eff}}\Delta x$. If fixed cash flows give $P(x)=100e^{-5x}$ under this shock, local duration is exactly 5. Repricing at $h=0.001$ (10bp) gives $P(-h)=100.501252$ and $P(h)=99.501248$, hence a difference estimate of about 5.000021. A duration of 5 does not promise an exact 5% loss for a one-percentage-point rise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有效”不要求现金流一定改变。固定现金流照常重新贴现；若模型含赎回、[[提前还款选择权|提前还款]]或浮息重定价，则必须按同一模型重新处理相应支付与行权决策，不能强行冻结原支付路径。重估价格是模型情景输出，并非已经观察到的未来成交价；它也不是单券 [[修正久期]] 的自动替代名称，见 [[收益率久期与曲线久期]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Effective” does not require cash flows to change. Fixed payments are simply rediscounted. When the model includes calls, [[提前还款选择权|prepayment]], or floating-rate resets, repricing must update those payments and decisions consistently instead of freezing the original path. Scenario prices are model outputs, not observed future trades. Nor is this automatically another name for a bond's [[修正久期|modified duration]]; see [[收益率久期与曲线久期|yield versus curve duration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告数值时同时报告 $h$ 与固定参数。价格足够光滑时，中央差分向局部导数收敛；步长过大会混入非线性，过小又可能被价格舍入或模拟噪声主导。行权边界若造成不可微，仍可报告指定步长的情景差分，但不能把它冒称为唯一局部导数。大幅冲击应检查完整重估。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report the bump size and frozen parameters with the result. For sufficiently smooth prices, central differences converge to the local derivative. Large bumps mix in nonlinear effects; very small bumps can be dominated by rounding or simulation noise. At a nondifferentiable exercise boundary, a stated scenario difference remains reportable but is not a unique local derivative. Large shocks require a full-repricing check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
