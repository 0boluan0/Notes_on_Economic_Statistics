# 平均处理效应 ATE 是目标总体中个体潜在结果之差的期望

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The ATE is the expected individual potential-outcome contrast in the target population*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 对定义明确的二元处理，平均处理效应为 $\mathrm{ATE}=E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$。若潜在结果可积，由[[期望线性性]]，它等于 $E[Y(1)]-E[Y(0)]$。两项都面向同一个目标总体。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a well-defined binary treatment, the ATE averages the individual contrast over the target population. With integrable potential outcomes, [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] makes it equal to the difference of their population means.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

四个单位的效应为 $10,12,8,12$ 时，有限总体 ATE 为 $(10+12+8+12)/4=10.5$。这一定义不等于直接可观测：每个单位缺失一个反事实，识别需要额外设计。另见[[已处理者平均处理效应]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effects of 10, 12, 8, and 12 give a finite-population ATE of 10.5. Definition does not imply observability: one counterfactual is missing for each unit. Identification needs additional design assumptions. Compare the [[已处理者平均处理效应|effect among treated units]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
