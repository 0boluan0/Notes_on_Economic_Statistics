# 正态变量减去均值再除以标准差后服从标准正态分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A normal variable becomes standard normal after centring and scaling by its standard deviation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$、$\sigma>0$，则 $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma\sim N(0,1)$。由逆变换 $x=\mu+\sigma z$，
$$f_Z(z)=\sigma f_X(\mu+\sigma z)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-z^2/2}.$$
所以 $P(X\le c)=\Phi((c-\mu)/\sigma)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Centring and dividing a normal variable by its positive standard deviation gives the standard normal density through the inverse scale factor. Threshold probabilities therefore reduce to the standard normal CDF.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$N(10,4)$ 的标准差是 2，所以 13 对应 $z=1.5$。任意有限正方差变量都可标准化成均值 0、方差 1，但原分布非正态时不因此变成正态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For N(10,4), standard deviation is two and the threshold thirteen becomes 1.5. A nonnormal variable can be standardised to mean zero and variance one without becoming normal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
