# Normal standardisation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正态标准化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Normal standardisation** changes the coordinate used to describe a normal variable. We subtract its mean and then divide by its standard deviation, so a value is expressed as “how many standard deviations above or below the mean”. All nondegenerate normal distributions then become the same standard normal distribution, allowing one probability table to serve them all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正态标准化**是在改变描述正态变量所用的坐标：先减去均值，再除以标准差，把取值改写成“在均值上方或下方多少个标准差”。经过这一步，所有非退化正态分布都会变成同一个标准正态分布，因此可以共用一张概率表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $X$ has a [[正态分布|normal distribution]] $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$. Here $\mu$ is its [[期望|mean]], $\sigma$ is its [[标准差|standard deviation]], and $\sigma^2$ is its variance. The requirement $\sigma>0$ allows division by the standard deviation. Define the new variable $Z$ by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X$ 服从[[正态分布]] $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$，其中 $\mu$ 是[[期望|均值]]，$\sigma$ 是[[标准差]]，$\sigma^2$ 是方差。要求 $\sigma>0$，才能用标准差作除数。定义新变量 $Z$：

> [!abstract] Centre, then rescale · 先中心化，再缩放
> $$Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $Z=0$ corresponds to the mean. A positive $Z$ lies above the mean; a negative one lies below. Since the numerator and denominator have the same units, $Z$ is dimensionless.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $Z=0$ 对应均值；$Z$ 为正表示在均值上方，为负表示在均值下方。分子、分母具有相同单位，所以 $Z$ 不带单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Transform the event as well as the variable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*变量换了坐标，事件阈值也要一起变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $c$ be a numerical threshold, and let $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$ be the [[累积分布函数|standard normal CDF]]. Subtracting $\mu$ and dividing by positive $\sigma$ on both sides of an inequality preserves its direction. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $c$ 是一个数值阈值，$\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$ 是标准正态的[[累积分布函数|CDF]]。对不等式两边同时减去 $\mu$、再除以正数 $\sigma$，不等号方向保持不变，因此

$$P(X\le c)
=P\!\left(\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\le\frac{c-\mu}{\sigma}\right)
=\Phi\!\left(\frac{c-\mu}{\sigma}\right).$$

> [!example] A threshold of thirteen · 阈值为十三
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X\sim N(10,4)$, the standard deviation is $2$, so the standardised threshold for $13$ is $(13-10)/2=1.5$. Consequently $P(X\le13)=\Phi(1.5)\approx0.93319$. A value of $13$ is one and a half standard deviations above the mean; the cumulative probability includes everything below it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X\sim N(10,4)$，标准差是 $2$，所以 $13$ 对应的标准化阈值为 $(13-10)/2=1.5$。因此 $P(X\le13)=\Phi(1.5)\approx0.93319$。数值 $13$ 位于均值上方一个半标准差，累计概率把它下方的取值都包括进去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an interval with endpoints $a<b$, standardise **both** endpoints and subtract the cumulative probabilities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对端点为 $a<b$ 的区间，要把**两个**端点都标准化，再把累计概率相减：

$$P(a<X\le b)=
\Phi\!\left(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\right)
-\Phi\!\left(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the same $N(10,4)$ variable, $8$ becomes $-1$ and $13$ becomes $1.5$. Thus $P(8<X\le13)=\Phi(1.5)-\Phi(-1)\approx0.77454$. For the upper tail, use $P(X>13)=1-\Phi(1.5)\approx0.06681$. Inclusion of a finite endpoint does not change a normal probability because each single point has probability zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍用 $N(10,4)$，$8$ 变成 $-1$，$13$ 变成 $1.5$，所以 $P(8<X\le13)=\Phi(1.5)-\Phi(-1)\approx0.77454$。若求上尾，则用 $P(X>13)=1-\Phi(1.5)\approx0.06681$。由于单点概率为零，是否包括某个有限端点不会改变正态概率。

## Why the whole distribution becomes standard normal
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么改变的是整个分布，而不仅是均值和方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] and [[方差的仿射变换|variance scaling rule]] already show that $Z$ has mean zero and variance one. To establish that it is also normal, use the shape of the original density. This extra step matters: many distributions share a mean and variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望线性性]]与[[方差的仿射变换|方差缩放规则]]已经能说明 $Z$ 的均值为零、方差为一；但要进一步证明它仍是正态，还需要使用原密度的形状。这一步不可省略，因为很多分布都可能有相同均值和方差。

> [!note]- Checking the density transformation · 核对密度变换
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write $f_X$ and $f_Z$ for the two densities. The inverse change of coordinates is $x=\mu+\sigma z$. A small width in $z$ corresponds to $\sigma$ times that width in $x$, so the [[密度换元的尺度因子|density change-of-variable rule]] supplies the factor $\sigma$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $f_X,f_Z$ 分别表示两个密度。逆向换坐标为 $x=\mu+\sigma z$，$z$ 中的一小段宽度对应 $x$ 中 $\sigma$ 倍的宽度，所以[[密度换元的尺度因子|密度换元规则]]给出因子 $\sigma$：
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> f_Z(z)&=\sigma f_X(\mu+\sigma z)\\
> &=\frac{\sigma}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}
> e^{-\frac{(\mu+\sigma z-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}\\
> &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-z^2/2}=\phi(z).
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\phi$ is the standard normal density. The location and scale have disappeared from the entire density formula, which establishes $Z\sim N(0,1)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中 $\phi$ 是标准正态密度。位置与尺度已经从整个密度公式中消失，这才建立了 $Z\sim N(0,1)$。

## What standardisation can and cannot guarantee
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*标准化能保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Any variable with a finite, positive variance can be centred and divided by its standard deviation. The result has mean zero and variance one, but it is normal only if the original variable was normal. For example, a variable taking $-1$ and $1$ with equal probability already has mean zero and variance one. Standardising it leaves the same two-point distribution, not a continuous bell curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任何具有有限正方差的变量，都可以先减均值再除以标准差，得到均值零、方差一的变量；但只有原变量正态时，结果才是标准正态。例如，一个以相同概率取 $-1$ 和 $1$ 的变量，本来就有均值零、方差一。标准化后仍是原来的两点分布，并不会变成连续钟形曲线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two checks prevent common mistakes: use $\sigma$, not the variance $\sigma^2$, as the divisor; and verify that a positive finite standard deviation exists. A constant variable has $\sigma=0$, so this division is undefined. An infinite or undefined variance also prevents this finite-variance standardisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用时要检查两点：除数是 $\sigma$，而不是方差 $\sigma^2$；还要确认标准差有限且为正。常数变量的 $\sigma=0$，不能这样相除；方差无穷或没有定义时，也不能进行这里的有限方差标准化。
