# 推理性能比较必须先对齐工作负载、资源、质量要求与指标口径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Comparing inference performance requires aligned workloads, resources, quality requirements, and metric definitions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要判断某种改动是否使推理更好，先说清“完成什么工作、使用多少资源、满足什么质量与延迟条件”，再比较测量值。相同的 token/s 标签，可能统计不同长度、不同数量的请求，甚至采用不同时间起点；不能直接拿来排优劣。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To judge an inference change, first specify the work, resources, quality requirements, and latency constraints. Identically labeled token/s figures may use different request lengths, request counts, or timing boundaries and cannot automatically be ranked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于一次受控实现对比，除正在研究的改动外，至少固定下面四层。若比较的是不同模型，则模型本身是决策变量，任务质量约束更不能省略。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a controlled implementation comparison, hold the following layers fixed apart from the intended change. When comparing different models, model choice itself is the variable, making the task-quality requirement especially important.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 层次 | 要记录的内容 |
|---|---|
| 工作 | 提示集合、输入与实际输出长度分布、对话历史、特殊 token、采样和停止规则；计数用同一 [[Token口径]] |
| 负载 | 到达过程与请求率，或固定在途并发；两种实验不能仅凭一个数字互换；同时记录调度、batch/token 预算和缓存命中状态 |
| 资源与实现 | 设备数量与型号、功耗约束、模型/adapter 版本、权重与 KV 精度、runtime/kernel 版本、并行/卸载配置 |
| 验收 | 目标任务质量、[[首Token延迟\|TTFT]]、[[平均输出Token时间\|TPOT]] 或 [[Token间隔\|ITL]] 的边界和聚合方式、[[推理吞吐率]]、显存峰值、错误/取消/超时 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record the workload and actual token counts; the arrival process or fixed outstanding concurrency; hardware, model, precision, and implementation; and quality, latency, throughput, memory, and failure criteria. Arrival rate, outstanding requests, and the batch actually executing are different quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 A 在并发 1、短提示、冷缓存下得到 40 token/s，B 在并发 16、长提示、共享前缀命中下得到 300 token/s。这还不能说明 B 的 kernel 快了 7.5 倍，也不能保证 B 的单个用户等待更短。若要隔离 kernel 收益，就重跑相同形状与缓存状态；若要比较整套服务能力，则在相同需求和 [[达标吞吐量|服务条件]] 下比较可交付结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose A reports 40 token/s at concurrency one with short prompts and cold caches, while B reports 300 at concurrency sixteen with long prompts and prefix hits. That does not establish a 7.5-fold kernel improvement or shorter individual waits. Isolate kernel effects with matched shapes and cache state, or compare complete services under matched demand and [[达标吞吐量|service constraints]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将模型加载、编译和预热单独报告，稳态测试才排除它们；若实际需求关心首次启动，则冷启动本身就是结果。固定输出长度可用于隔离成本，但强制忽略 EOS 已改变真实生成工作，必须注明，并另验正常停止时的任务质量。重复运行并保留分布与原始请求记录；平均 TPOT 的改善不能代替最慢间隔或超时率的检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report loading, compilation, and warmup separately; exclude them only for a steady-state test. Cold start is itself a result when relevant. Forced output lengths can isolate cost, but ignoring EOS changes the workload and requires separate normal-stopping quality checks. Repeat runs and preserve distributions and request records; better mean TPOT does not establish better stalls or timeout rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量化对比另验 [[量化输出误差|输入相关的质量变化]]，困惑度须遵循 [[困惑度可比性]]。测量完成后，只有当目标质量和服务条件也成立，性能提升才有该任务下的使用意义；这与 [[生命周期最优|训练加部署的长期成本]] 又是不同层次的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quantization comparisons additionally check [[量化输出误差|input-dependent quality effects]] and follow [[困惑度可比性|perplexity comparability]] where applicable. Performance gains become useful for the task only when quality and service constraints hold. [[生命周期最优|Lifecycle cost]] remains a separate decision layer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [NVIDIA, *LLM Inference Benchmarking: Fundamental Concepts*](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/llm-benchmarking-fundamental-concepts/)，LLM inference metrics、Benchmarking parameters and best practices：支持工具计时差异、序列长度、并发/请求率、batch、采样和 EOS 对测量的影响。本卡不把文章的强制长度建议用于所有真实任务。
- [NVIDIA, *GenAI-Perf*](https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/triton-inference-server/user-guide/docs/perf_benchmark/genai-perf-README.html)，Metrics 和运行示例：支持可记录的输入、负载与测量时间口径。四层清单、稳态与冷启动的分开记账及 A/B 反例是据这些依赖整理的比较方法，不是工具自动保证的公平性。
- 质量和生命周期的条件分别复用上述共享原子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NVIDIA's benchmarking materials document metric and workload controls. The checklist and counterexample organize these dependencies into a comparison method; no tool automatically guarantees fairness. Quality and lifecycle conditions use the linked shared atoms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
