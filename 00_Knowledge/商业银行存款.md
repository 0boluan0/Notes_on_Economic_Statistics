# 商业银行存款是客户资产与银行负债且可充当公众货币
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A commercial-bank deposit is the customer's asset, the bank's liability, and a form of public money*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 客户账户中的存款对客户是可支配的金融资产，对商业银行是必须按约兑现提现或转账请求的负债。它能在支付网络内按面值使用，因此虽不是央行纸币，也可以构成公众持有的货币。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A deposit in a customer's account is a spendable financial asset of the customer and a liability against which the commercial bank must honour withdrawal or transfer requests as agreed. Because it can be used at par within the payment network, it can be money held by the public even though it is not a central-bank note.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一金额必须从两张资产负债表同时看：客户的“银行余额”对应银行的存款负债。银行用贷款、证券、准备金等资产支持其整体负债，但特定存款并不是金库里为该客户单独封存的一叠现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same amount must be viewed on two balance sheets: the customer's bank balance corresponds to the bank's deposit liability. Loans, securities, reserves, and other assets support the bank's liabilities as a whole, but a particular deposit is not a separately sealed bundle of cash in a vault for that customer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 存款是私人发行的商业银行货币，不是中央银行准备金。
- 银行卡、支票和转账指令通常是访问或移动存款的支付工具，不是另一笔独立货币。
- 存款可按面值使用依赖银行偿付能力、监管、存款保障和与中央银行货币的可兑换性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A deposit is privately issued commercial-bank money, not a central-bank reserve balance.
- Cards, cheques, and transfer instructions normally access or move the deposit; they are not an additional stock of money.
- Use of deposits at par relies on bank solvency, regulation, deposit protection, and convertibility into central-bank money.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“我在银行有一笔存款”和“银行欠我一笔钱”是同一事实的两面？
>
> **答案：** 该余额在我的资产端记录，在银行的负债端记录；银行承诺按约让我提现或转账。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why are “I hold a bank deposit” and “the bank owes me money” two sides of the same fact?
>
> **Answer:** The balance is recorded as my asset and the bank's liability; the bank promises withdrawal or transfer according to the account terms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
