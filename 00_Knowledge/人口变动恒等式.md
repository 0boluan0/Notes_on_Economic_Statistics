# 同一地域与统计口径下人口增量等于出生减死亡加净迁入

人口变动恒等式把某段时间内的人口增减分成自然变动和迁移：出生增加人口，死亡减少人口，迁入与迁出之差是净迁入。期初、期末和各项事件必须对应同一地域、人口定义与时间区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The population balancing equation separates population change over an interval into natural change and migration. Births add people, deaths remove people, and immigration minus emigration gives net migration. Stocks and events must share the same territory, population definition and time interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设期初、期末人口为 $N_0,N_1$，期间活产、死亡、迁入、迁出人数分别为 $B,D,I,O$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $N_0,N_1$ be the opening and closing populations, and let $B,D,I,O$ count live births, deaths, immigration and emigration during the interval. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
N_1-N_0=(B-D)+(I-O).
$$

例如期初 1,000 人，期间出生 20、死亡 8、迁入 5、迁出 7，则自然增加 12、净迁入 $-2$，期末为 1,010 人。只有在净迁入为零时，迁移项才在总量恒等式中消失；这不表示没有迁入迁出，也不保证年龄结构不受迁移影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting with 1,000 people, 20 births, 8 deaths, 5 immigrants and 7 emigrants give natural increase of 12 and net migration of $-2$, leaving 1,010 people. The migration term vanishes from the aggregate identity only when net migration is zero. This neither rules out inward and outward flows nor ensures that migration leaves age composition unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

恒等式不是预测模型。预测还需要对未来生育、死亡与迁移作出假设，并考虑[[人口惯性]]所代表的初始年龄结构。调查遗漏、登记误差或行政边界改变也可能使观测人口差额不能直接与记录事件相抵；残差不能未经核查就全称为迁移。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity is not a forecasting model. Forecasts require assumptions about fertility, mortality and migration, together with the starting age distribution captured by [[人口惯性|population momentum]]. Survey omissions, registration errors or boundary changes can prevent observed stocks from reconciling with recorded events; an unexplained residual cannot automatically be labelled migration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[粗出生率]]与[[粗死亡率]]把事件数除以人口暴露量；它们与事件总数、[[总和生育率]]回答的不是同一个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[粗出生率|crude birth rate]] and [[粗死亡率|crude death rate]] divide event counts by population exposure. They answer different questions from event totals and the [[总和生育率|total fertility rate]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
