# Termwise differentiation of power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*幂级数逐项求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A power series represents a function by adding powers of the signed displacement $x-a$ from a fixed centre $a$. **Termwise differentiation** finds the rate of change of that sum by differentiating each power. For a finite polynomial this follows from ordinary differentiation rules. For an infinite sum, we must also justify passing to the limit: a small error in function values alone need not give a small error in slopes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

幂级数把相对于固定中心 $a$ 的带符号偏移量 $x-a$ 的各次幂相加，用来表示一个函数。**逐项求导**就是分别对这些幂求导，从而求整个和函数的变化率。有限多项式可以直接使用普通求导法则；无穷多项还必须说明极限过程合法，因为函数值的误差很小，并不自动表示斜率误差也很小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with a familiar series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从熟悉的级数开始*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[几何级数|geometric series]] gives $1/(1-x)=1+x+x^2+\cdots$ for $|x|<1$. The constant has derivative zero; the [[整数幂求导|integer power rule]] turns $x^n$ into $nx^{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$. The theorem below makes the following calculation valid:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[几何级数]]给出 $1/(1-x)=1+x+x^2+\cdots$，条件是 $|x|<1$。常数的导数为零；[[整数幂求导]]把 $x^n$ 变为 $nx^{n-1}$，其中 $n\ge1$。下面的定理保证这次计算合法：

$$
\frac1{(1-x)^2}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}nx^{n-1}
=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(k+1)x^k
=1+2x+3x^2+\cdots,\qquad |x|<1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $k=n-1$ is only a change of index: both the coefficient and the exponent change. At $x=1/2$, the sum is $4$; its first four terms give $1+1+3/4+1/2=13/4$. The remaining terms supply $3/4$. The infinite identity does not say that a finite truncation is already exact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $k=n-1$ 只是换了求和下标，系数和幂次都要随之改变。在 $x=1/2$ 处，和为 $4$；前四项只有 $1+1+3/4+1/2=13/4$，剩余项再贡献 $3/4$。无穷级数的恒等式不表示截取有限项就已经精确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The interior theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*严格内部的定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a$ and the coefficients $c_n$ be real numbers, and let the [[幂级数|power series]] below have [[幂级数收敛半径|radius of convergence]] $R>0$, allowing $R=\infty$. The centre $a$ and coefficients stay fixed while the real input $x$ varies. Define $f$ to be the sum on $|x-a|<R$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设中心 $a$ 和系数 $c_n$ 都是实数，下面的[[幂级数]]具有[[幂级数收敛半径|收敛半径]] $R>0$，允许 $R=\infty$。变化的是实数输入 $x$，中心和系数保持不变。在 $|x-a|<R$ 上，把级数的和记为 $f$。

$$
f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n.
$$

> [!important]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At every such interior point, $f$ has a finite [[导数|derivative]], and
> $$f'(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n c_n(x-a)^{n-1}.$$
> The derivative series has exactly the same radius $R$. Its first term is the constant $c_1$, including at $x=a$. The original constant $c_0$ disappears.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个这样的内点都存在有限的[[导数]]，并满足上式。导数级数的收敛半径仍恰好是 $R$。第一项是常数 $c_1$，在 $x=a$ 处也如此；原来的常数项 $c_0$ 消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result can be applied repeatedly, so the sum has derivatives of every finite order throughout the interior. If $R=\infty$, this means every finite real input. If $R=0$, there is no open interval around the centre on which this theorem supplies a derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以反复使用这个结论，所以和函数在严格内部具有任意有限阶导数。若 $R=\infty$，这里的内部就是所有有限实数输入；若 $R=0$，本定理不能提供中心周围的开区间及其导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the infinite operation is legitimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么可以对无穷和求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an interior point $x_0$. Choose two finite positive distances $r,s$ with $|x_0-a|<r<s<R$. We will control every nearby input in the smaller closed interval $[a-r,a+r]$ using convergence at the more distant point $a+s$. Since $\sum c_ns^n$ converges, its terms are bounded: there is a finite $M\ge0$ such that $|c_n|s^n\le M$ for every $n$. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个内点 $x_0$，选两个有限正距离 $r,s$，使 $|x_0-a|<r<s<R$。我们要用更远一点 $a+s$ 处的收敛，控制较小闭区间 $[a-r,a+r]$ 中所有附近输入。由于 $\sum c_ns^n$ 收敛，其通项有界，所以存在有限 $M\ge0$，使每个 $n$ 都满足 $|c_n|s^n\le M$。因此，

$$
n|c_n|r^{n-1}\le\frac{M}{s}\,n\left(\frac rs\right)^{n-1}\qquad(n\ge1).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put $q=r/s$, so $0<q<1$. The series $\sum_{n\ge1}nq^{n-1}$ converges: its adjacent positive-term ratio is $q(n+1)/n\to q<1$, so the [[级数比值判别|ratio test]] applies without using the derivative identity we are proving. Hence the numerical tail
$E_N=\sum_{n>N}n|c_n|r^{n-1}$ tends to zero. This one bound works for the whole smaller interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $q=r/s$，则 $0<q<1$。级数 $\sum_{n\ge1}nq^{n-1}$ 收敛，因为相邻正项之比为 $q(n+1)/n\to q<1$，可以使用[[级数比值判别]]，不必借用正在证明的求导公式。因此，数值尾和 $E_N=\sum_{n>N}n|c_n|r^{n-1}$ 趋于零。这个统一的界对整个较小区间都有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $S_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^N c_n(x-a)^n$ for the finite polynomial and $g(x_0)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n c_n(x_0-a)^{n-1}$ for the proposed derivative. For nonzero $h$ small enough that $x_0+h\in[a-r,a+r]$, the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] applied to each monomial gives
$|((x_0+h-a)^n-(x_0-a)^n)/h|\le nr^{n-1}$.
Sum this bound first over finitely many tail terms and then take their convergent limit. It bounds the tail difference quotient by $E_N$; the tail of $g(x_0)$ is also bounded by $E_N$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把有限多项式记为 $S_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^N c_n(x-a)^n$，把准备证明为导数的和记为 $g(x_0)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n c_n(x_0-a)^{n-1}$。取充分小的非零 $h$，使 $x_0+h\in[a-r,a+r]$。对每个单项式使用[[拉格朗日中值定理]]，得到 $|((x_0+h-a)^n-(x_0-a)^n)/h|\le nr^{n-1}$。先对有限多项尾部相加，再取其收敛极限，尾部差商便不超过 $E_N$；$g(x_0)$ 的尾部也不超过 $E_N$。于是

$$
\left|\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}h-g(x_0)\right|
\le 2E_N+
\left|\frac{S_N(x_0+h)-S_N(x_0)}h-S_N'(x_0)\right|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given any error tolerance, first choose $N$ so that $2E_N$ is small. Keep that $N$ fixed and then let $h\to0$; the remaining term tends to zero because $S_N$ is a polynomial. This proves the derivative formula. The order of these choices is what justifies the infinite operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定任意误差要求，先选 $N$ 使 $2E_N$ 足够小；固定这个 $N$，再令 $h\to0$。剩余项因为 $S_N$ 是多项式而趋于零，这就证明了求导公式。先控制尾部、再取差商极限的顺序，正是无穷运算合法的理由。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bound proves convergence of the derivative series at every distance less than $R$. Its radius cannot be larger: if it converged absolutely at some distance $t>R$, then
$|c_n|t^n\le t\,n|c_n|t^{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$ would make the original series converge absolutely there too, a contradiction. If a larger radius existed, such a $t$ could be chosen strictly inside it. For $R=\infty$, the interior argument already covers every finite distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的界证明导数级数在每个小于 $R$ 的距离处都收敛。其半径也不能更大：若在某个 $t>R$ 处绝对收敛，由 $|c_n|t^n\le t\,n|c_n|t^{n-1}$（$n\ge1$），原级数也会在那里绝对收敛，产生矛盾。若新半径更大，就能在它严格内部选这样的 $t$。当 $R=\infty$ 时，内部论证已经覆盖所有有限距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same result holds for complex coefficients with a real input. For a complex number $u+iv$, $i^2=-1$ and $|u+iv|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$; differentiation means differentiating real and imaginary parts. The monomial difference estimate above, multiplied by $|c_n|$, remains valid. This also justifies applying the rule entry by entry to a matrix power series with a real parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数为复数、输入仍为实数时，同样成立。复数 $u+iv$ 中 $i^2=-1$，其模为 $|u+iv|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$；求导指分别对实部和虚部求导。上面单项式的差商界乘以 $|c_n|$ 后仍有效，因此也能对具有实参数的矩阵幂级数逐个元素使用本规则。

## An endpoint is a new question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*端点是另外的问题*

> [!warning]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Preserving the radius does not preserve the endpoints. The [[对数函数幂级数|logarithm series]] $\sum_{n\ge1}(-1)^{n+1}x^n/n$ represents $\ln(1+x)$ for $|x|<1$. At $x=1$ the series converges by the [[交错级数判别|alternating series test]]. Its termwise derivative becomes $1-1+1-\cdots$ there, whose terms do not tend to zero. Yet the ordinary function $\ln(1+x)$ has derivative $1/2$ at $1$. A function's differentiability at that point does not validate a divergent derivative series.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 半径保持不变，不表示端点也保持。[[对数函数幂级数]] $\sum_{n\ge1}(-1)^{n+1}x^n/n$ 在 $|x|<1$ 表示 $\ln(1+x)$；在 $x=1$，由[[交错级数判别]]仍然收敛。但逐项导数级数在那里变成 $1-1+1-\cdots$，通项不趋于零。普通函数 $\ln(1+x)$ 在 $1$ 处的导数却是 $1/2$；函数在该点可导，不能让发散的导数级数变得有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply [[幂级数端点判别|endpoint tests]] to the new numerical series separately. Even convergence at an endpoint and identification of its sum with an endpoint function value are separate claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应对新的数项级数单独做[[幂级数端点判别]]。即使端点级数收敛，其和是否等于目标函数的端点值，也仍是另一项需要论证的结论。
