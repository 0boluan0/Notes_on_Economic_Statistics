# 幂级数在收敛半径内部可逐项求导且导数级数保持原收敛半径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Inside its radius, a power series can be differentiated termwise without changing that radius*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$ 的收敛半径为 $R>0$，则在 $|x-a|<R$ 内
$$
f'(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n c_n(x-a)^{n-1}.
$$
导数级数的收敛半径仍是 $R$。可重复使用本定理，故幂级数的和在内部无限可微。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the displayed series defines $f$ with positive radius $R$, its derivative is obtained term by term for $|x-a|<R$, with the same radius. Repeating the theorem shows that its sum is smooth in the interior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这使一个已知展开产生新的展开。例如对 [[几何级数|$1/(1-x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$]] 求导，得到
$$
\frac1{(1-x)^2}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n x^{n-1}
=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(k+1)x^k,
\qquad |x|<1.
$$
第二个写法只是令 $k=n-1$；系数和幂次必须一起改。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating the [[几何级数|geometric series]] gives the displayed expansion for $(1-x)^{-2}$. Reindexing with $k=n-1$ changes both the coefficient and exponent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持的是半径，端点须 [[幂级数端点判别|重新检查]]。例如 $\ln(1+x)$ 的级数在 $x=1$ 收敛，求导后的 $1-x+x^2-\cdots$ 在 $x=1$ 却因通项不趋零而发散；即使函数导数在那里存在，也不能在该端点使用逐项导数级数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The radius is preserved, but [[幂级数端点判别|endpoint behaviour must be retested]]. The logarithm series converges at $x=1$, whereas its differentiated series has nonvanishing terms there. Existence of the function's derivative at an endpoint does not extend this theorem to that point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
