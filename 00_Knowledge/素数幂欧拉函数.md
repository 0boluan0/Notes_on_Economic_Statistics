# Totient of a prime power
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*素数幂欧拉函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To count the integers coprime to $9$ among $0,1,\ldots,8$, cross out $0,3,6$. The six remaining numbers are $1,2,4,5,7,8$. A power of a single prime has the same useful pattern: the only obstruction to coprimality is divisibility by that prime, so counting the excluded multiples is enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要在 $0,1,\ldots,8$ 中数出与 $9$ 互素的整数，划去 $0,3,6$，就剩 $1,2,4,5,7,8$ 六个数。单个素数的幂具有同样的规律：不互素的障碍恰好是被该素数整除，因此只需数清排除的倍数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[素数|prime]] $p$ is an integer greater than one whose only positive divisors are one and itself. For an integer $k\ge1$, the power $p^k$ is a product of $k$ copies of $p$. The [[欧拉函数|totient]] $\varphi(p^k)$ counts representatives $r$ from $0$ through $p^k-1$ with [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]] $\gcd(r,p^k)=1$; this condition is called coprimality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[素数]] $p$ 是大于一、正因子只有一与自身的整数。对整数 $k\ge1$，$p^k$ 是 $k$ 个 $p$ 的乘积。[[欧拉函数]] $\varphi(p^k)$ 统计从 $0$ 到 $p^k-1$ 的代表中，满足[[最大公因数]] $\gcd(r,p^k)=1$ 的个数；这个条件称为互素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Subtract exactly one out of every p candidates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每 p 个候选恰好排除一个*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a prime $p$ and integer $k\ge1$, the displayed formula holds because a residue fails to be coprime to $p^k$ exactly when it is divisible by $p$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $p$ 为素数、$k\ge1$ 为整数，则
$$
\varphi(p^k)=p^k-p^{k-1}=p^k\left(1-\frac1p\right).
$$
因为一个整数与 $p^k$ 不互素，当且仅当它是 $p$ 的倍数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Among the $p^k$ residues, the multiples of $p$ are indexed from zero through $p^{k-1}-1$, so there are $p^{k-1}$ of them. Removing these gives the [[欧拉函数|totient count]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $0,1,\ldots,p^k-1$ 中，$p$ 的倍数恰为 $0,p,2p,\ldots,(p^{k-1}-1)p$，共 $p^{k-1}$ 个。从全部 $p^k$ 个余数里剔除它们，就得到 [[欧拉函数]] 的计数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why these and only these integers are excluded
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么恰好排除这些整数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $p\mid r$, then $p$ divides both $r$ and $p^k$, so their gcd is at least $p>1$ and the representative is not counted. Conversely, suppose $d=\gcd(r,p^k)>1$. By the existence part of [[唯一素因数分解|prime factorization]], $d$ has a prime divisor $q$. Then $q$ divides $r$ and $p^k$. [[素数整除乘积|Euclid’s lemma for a finite product]] makes $q$ divide one of the $k$ factors $p$. Since $p$ is prime and $q>1$, this forces $q=p$, so $p\mid r$. Both directions are now proved; in particular, zero is excluded because $p\mid0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $p\mid r$，则 $p$ 同时整除 $r$ 与 $p^k$，所以它们的 gcd 至少为 $p>1$，这个代表不计入。反过来，若 $d=\gcd(r,p^k)>1$，由[[唯一素因数分解|素因数分解的存在性]]，$d$ 有素因子 $q$。于是 $q$ 整除 $r$ 和 $p^k$。把[[素数整除乘积]]用于有限乘积，$q$ 必整除 $k$ 个因子 $p$ 中的一个。由于 $p$ 为素数且 $q>1$，只能有 $q=p$，所以 $p\mid r$。两个方向都已证明；特别地，因为 $p\mid0$，零也应排除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It remains to count rather than merely list the excluded values. Write $r=jp$. The range $0\le r<p^k$ is equivalent to $0\le j<p^{k-1}$ because $p>0$. The integer indices are therefore $0,1,\ldots,p^{k-1}-1$: there are exactly $p^{k-1}$, including zero. Distinct indices give distinct multiples since $p\ne0$. Subtract this count from the $p^k$ candidates to obtain $p^k-p^{k-1}$. Factoring out $p^k$ gives the displayed equivalent form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着需要准确计数，而不只是列举。把排除的数写成 $r=jp$。由于 $p>0$，范围 $0\le r<p^k$ 等价于 $0\le j<p^{k-1}$。所以整数下标恰为 $0,1,\ldots,p^{k-1}-1$，共 $p^{k-1}$ 个，包括零；又因为 $p\ne0$，不同下标对应不同倍数。从全部 $p^k$ 个候选中减去这个数，就得到 $p^k-p^{k-1}$。再提出 $p^k$，便得到上式的另一种写法。

> [!example] Count twenty-seven step by step · 逐步计算二十七
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $p=3$ and $k=3$. There are $27$ candidates, from $0$ through $26$. Exclude $0,3,6,9,12,15,18,21,24$, corresponding to the nine indices $j=0,\ldots,8$. Every excluded candidate has a common factor three, and the preceding proof shows no other candidates fail. Hence $27-9=18$ are counted. Stopping the list at $24$ matters: $27$ is outside the chosen representative range.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $p=3,k=3$，候选为 $0$ 到 $26$，共 $27$ 个。排除 $0,3,6,9,12,15,18,21,24$，对应九个下标 $j=0,\ldots,8$。被排除者都有公因子三，前面的证明又保证没有其他候选不合格，所以计入 $27-9=18$ 个。倍数列表止于 $24$，因为 $27$ 已超出所选代表范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the conditions before substituting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*代入前检查条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $\varphi(27)=18$. Exponent one gives $\varphi(p)=p-1$. Exponent zero gives modulus one and uses the definition $\varphi(1)=1$, outside the stated formula's range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\varphi(27)=27-9=18$。$k=1$ 时恢复 $\varphi(p)=p-1$；$k=0$ 时模数为 $1$，应使用定义中的 $\varphi(1)=1$，不能代入含 $p^{k-1}$ 的本公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $p=2$ and any integer $k\ge1$, precisely the even representatives are excluded, leaving $2^{k-1}$ odd ones. At $k=1$, the range is $\{0,1\}$: exclude zero and keep one, so $\varphi(2)=1$. At $k=3$, the surviving representatives are $1,3,5,7$, giving $\varphi(8)=4$. The formula does not require an odd prime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $p=2$、整数 $k\ge1$ 时，恰好排除偶数代表，留下 $2^{k-1}$ 个奇数代表。$k=1$ 时范围为 $\{0,1\}$，排除零、保留一，所以 $\varphi(2)=1$；$k=3$ 时留下 $1,3,5,7$，所以 $\varphi(8)=4$。公式并不要求素数为奇数。

> [!warning] The base must be prime · 底数必须是素数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Using a composite base in the same expression can miss excluded numbers. If one puts $p=6,k=1$, the expression gives $6-1=5$, but only $1,5$ are coprime to six, so $\varphi(6)=2$. The obstruction is not just divisibility by six: two and three already share smaller prime factors with it. For general inputs, use the separately justified [[欧拉函数乘积公式|prime-factor product formula]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把同一个式子的底数换成合数，可能漏掉应排除的数。例如代入 $p=6,k=1$ 会得到 $6-1=5$，但与六互素的代表只有 $1,5$，所以 $\varphi(6)=2$。障碍不只是被六整除：二和三已经与六共有更小的素因子。一般输入应使用另行证明的[[欧拉函数乘积公式]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponent condition is substantive. With $k=0$, $p^k=1$ has totient one by definition, whereas the formal expression $1-1/p$ is not that count. Negative exponents do not even give positive integer moduli. The count above establishes this one-prime-power result without assuming the general totient product formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数条件有实质作用。$k=0$ 时，$p^k=1$ 按定义的欧拉函数值为一，而形式代入所得 $1-1/p$ 不是这个计数；负指数甚至不产生正整数模数。上面的计数独立证明了单个素数幂的结论，没有预先假定一般的欧拉函数乘积公式。
