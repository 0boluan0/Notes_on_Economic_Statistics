# Diagonalization and row reduction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对角化与行化简：换坐标和改写方程的区别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simpler-looking matrix can result from two very different tasks. [[行等价|Row reduction]] rewrites a system of equations so that its solutions are easier to find. [[对角化|Diagonalization]] describes the same linear transformation using a basis of eigenvectors, so that each coordinate is multiplied separately. Reaching a diagonal array by row operations does not, by itself, perform a diagonalization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
矩阵变得更简单，可能来自两种完全不同的任务。[[行等价|行化简]]把方程组改写成更容易求解的形式；[[对角化]]则用特征向量基描述同一个线性变换，使各坐标分别乘上相应的倍数。通过行操作得到一个对角数组，本身不等于完成了对角化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Row reduction keeps the unknowns and changes the equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行化简保留未知数，改变方程的写法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real system $Ax=b$ below. The column $x$ contains the two unknowns, and $b$ contains the two right-hand sides. Subtracting the second equation from the first is represented by left multiplication with the invertible [[初等矩阵|elementary matrix]] $E$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
考虑下面的实方程组 $Ax=b$。列向量 $x$ 装着两个未知数，$b$ 装着两个方程右端。用第一行减去第二行，可以表示成左乘可逆[[初等矩阵]] $E$：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
b=\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
EA=I_2,\qquad Eb=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $I_2$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. The equations $x_1+x_2=3$, $x_2=1$ become $x_1=2$, $x_2=1$. The unknown vector is unchanged, and the answer is still $x=(2,1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Both sides must be transformed: replacing $A$ by $I_2$ while leaving $b=(3,1)^T$ would instead give the wrong solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $I_2$ 为[[单位矩阵]]。原方程 $x_1+x_2=3$、$x_2=1$，变成了 $x_1=2$、$x_2=1$。未知向量没有改换坐标，答案仍是 $x=(2,1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]。必须同步改变两侧：若只把 $A$ 换成 $I_2$，却保留 $b=(3,1)^T$，就会得到错误答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, a sequence of reversible row operations gives $EA$, where $E$ is a product of elementary matrices. Since $E$ is invertible, $Ax=b$ and $EAx=Eb$ have [[行操作的解集不变性|exactly the same solutions]]. Row operations recombine rows reversibly, preserving their [[行空间|row space]] and its dimension, the [[矩阵秩|rank]]. They also preserve the [[零空间|null space]], because $EAx=0$ is equivalent to $Ax=0$. These statements apply to rectangular matrices too; no eigenvector basis is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
更一般地，一串可逆行操作给出 $EA$，其中 $E$ 是若干初等矩阵的乘积。因为 $E$ 可逆，$Ax=b$ 与 $EAx=Eb$ [[行操作的解集不变性|具有完全相同的解]]。行操作可逆地重新组合各行，因此保持[[行空间]]及其维数，即[[矩阵秩|秩]]；也保持[[零空间]]，因为 $EAx=0$ 等价于 $Ax=0$。这些结论对矩形矩阵也适用，不需要特征向量基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Diagonalization changes both coordinate descriptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对角化同时改变输入与输出的坐标表示*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now let $A$ be square, of size $n\times n$ over a chosen field. Suppose it has a [[基|basis]] of eigenvectors $v_1,\ldots,v_n$, with eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ in that field. Put those vectors in the columns of $S$. The coordinates of a vector in this new basis form a column $c$, and $x=Sc$ converts them into old coordinates. This [[换基矩阵的方向|direction of conversion]] follows from the columns of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
现在令 $A$ 是所选数域上的 $n\times n$ 方阵，并且具有特征向量[[基]] $v_1,\ldots,v_n$，对应的特征值 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ 也在该域中。把这些向量依次放入 $S$ 的列。一个向量在新基中的坐标组成列 $c$，而 $x=Sc$ 将它还原成旧坐标。这个[[换基矩阵的方向|转换方向]]由 $S$ 的列定义决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply $A$ to the old coordinates, then express the output in the same new basis. The three operations, read from right to left, are $S$ (new to old), $A$ (apply the map), and $S^{-1}$ (old to new). Thus the new matrix is $S^{-1}AS$. Since $Av_j=\lambda_jv_j$, its $j$th column acts on just the $j$th coordinate:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
先用 $A$ 作用于旧坐标，再把输出写回同一个新基。从右往左，三步依次是 $S$（新坐标变旧坐标）、$A$（施加变换）、$S^{-1}$（旧坐标变新坐标）。所以新矩阵为 $S^{-1}AS$。由于 $Av_j=\lambda_jv_j$，第 $j$ 列对应的作用只缩放第 $j$ 个新坐标：

$$AS=S\Lambda,\qquad
S^{-1}AS=\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\operatorname{diag}$ constructs the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with the listed entries and zeros elsewhere. This is a [[相似矩阵|similarity transformation]], using the same basis on the input and output spaces. It requires $S$ to be invertible, which is exactly why independent eigenvectors forming a full basis are needed. The [[可对角化判据|diagonalizability criterion]] decides when such a basis exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$\operatorname{diag}$ 表示把所列数字放在主对角、其他位置为零的[[对角矩阵]]。这是一种[[相似矩阵|相似变换]]，在输入和输出两侧使用同一组基。它要求 $S$ 可逆，这正是必须拥有一整组线性无关特征向量基的原因。是否存在这样的基，由[[可对角化判据]]判断。

> [!example] A diagonal result from elimination need not be a similar matrix · 消元得到对角结果，也未必相似
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The opening $A$ row-reduces to $I_2$. Both have only eigenvalue $1$, counted twice, but $(A-I_2)x=0$ requires $x_2=0$, while $(I_2-I_2)x=0$ permits every vector. Their [[特征空间|eigenspaces]] for $1$ have dimensions $1$ and $2$. Since [[相似保持特征空间维数|similarity preserves these dimensions]], $A$ is not similar to $I_2$ and cannot be diagonalized. In this example even the eigenvalues happen to agree; that coincidence does not make row reduction a change to an eigenvector basis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 开头的 $A$ 可以行化简为 $I_2$。二者都只有特征值 $1$，各计两次，但 $(A-I_2)x=0$ 要求 $x_2=0$，而 $(I_2-I_2)x=0$ 允许任意向量。因此二者在 $1$ 下的[[特征空间]]维数分别为 $1$、$2$。[[相似保持特征空间维数|相似会保持这些维数]]，所以 $A$ 与 $I_2$ 不相似，且 $A$ 不可对角化。这个例子中特征值还碰巧相同；这种巧合并不能把行化简变成特征向量换基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row operations need not even preserve the eigenvalues. Over $\mathbb R$, scaling the rows of $\operatorname{diag}(2,3)$ by $1/2$ and $1/3$ gives $I_2$, changing the eigenvalues $2,3$ to $1,1$. Thus row equivalence does not imply similarity. Particular operations can happen to leave a matrix unchanged or produce a similar matrix; the distinction is what each procedure guarantees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
行操作甚至不保证保持特征值。在实数域中，把 $\operatorname{diag}(2,3)$ 的两行分别乘以 $1/2$、$1/3$，便得到 $I_2$，特征值从 $2,3$ 变成 $1,1$。所以行等价不推出相似。某些具体行操作可能碰巧保持矩阵不变，或得到相似矩阵；区别在于这两类过程各自保证什么。

> [!question] Why is AS alone incomplete? · 为什么只算 AS 还不完整？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $ASc$ applies the transformation to a vector supplied in new coordinates, but the output is still in old coordinates. The final $S^{-1}$ converts that output into the same new basis. By contrast, solving a row-reduced system keeps the original unknown $x$ and changes the equations and their right-hand sides together.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $ASc$ 接收新坐标输入并施加变换，但输出仍是旧坐标。最后的 $S^{-1}$ 才把输出转换到同一个新基。相比之下，解行化简后的方程组时，未知数仍是原来的 $x$，同步改变的是方程与右端。
