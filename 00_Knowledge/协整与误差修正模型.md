# 协整与误差修正模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cointegration and error-correction models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当多个非平稳序列各自漂移，却被某个稳定长期关系约束时，同时保留长期均衡和短期调整。
> **具体锚点：** 两个价格水平都像随机游走，但价差长期围绕常数波动；它们不是普通相关，而是共同趋势被稳定组合抵消。
> **核心难点：** 协整是变量组合的性质，不能由高相关或每条序列都是 I(1) 推出；误差修正系数要与协整向量的符号一起解释。
> **为什么重要：** 它避免对水平做伪回归，也避免只差分而丢掉长期信息。
> **继续：** 两变量先学 Engle–Granger，再进多变量 Johansen/VECM；因果措辞另需识别。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When several nonstationary series drift individually but a stable long-run relation constrains them, cointegration preserves both long-run equilibrium and short-run adjustment.
> **Concrete anchor:** Two price levels may each resemble a random walk while their spread fluctuates around a constant. A stable combination cancels their common stochastic trend.
> **Central difficulty:** Cointegration is a property of a combination, not a consequence of high correlation or of every series being $I(1)$. Adjustment coefficients must be interpreted jointly with the sign convention for the cointegrating vector.
> **Why it matters:** It avoids spurious level regression without discarding long-run information through pure differencing.
> **Continue with:** Learn Engle–Granger for a single relation, then Johansen/VECM for multiple relations. Structural causal language still requires separate identification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 协整与共同随机趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cointegration and common stochastic trends*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $y_t$ 各分量为 I(1)，存在非零 $\beta$ 使 $\beta^Ty_t$ 为 I(0)，则它们协整。$\beta^Ty_t$ 是长期偏离；协整秩决定独立长期关系数，共同随机趋势数为变量数减协整秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If each component of $y_t$ is $I(1)$ and a nonzero $\beta$ makes $\beta^Ty_t$ $I(0)$, the variables are cointegrated. The term $\beta^Ty_t$ is a long-run deviation; cointegration rank is the number of independent long-run relations, and the number of common stochastic trends is the number of variables minus that rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协整向量只在非零缩放下定义：$\beta$ 与 $c\beta$ 给出同一稳定关系。因此必须归一化，例如将某一变量系数设为 1，才能报告“长期关系”。反转 $\beta$ 的符号也会反转调整系数 $\alpha$ 的直观符号，但不改变系统。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cointegrating vector is defined only up to nonzero scale: $\beta$ and $c\beta$ describe the same stable relation. A normalisation, such as setting one variable's coefficient to one, is therefore required before reporting a long-run equation. Reversing the sign of $\beta$ also reverses the intuitive sign of adjustment coefficients $\alpha$ without changing the system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Engle–Granger 两步法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Engle–Granger two-step method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先估计长期关系并取残差，再对残差做专用单位根检验；残差临界值不同于普通 ADF，因为协整向量已估计。若协整，第二步估计误差修正模型。方法简单，但多变量中只能处理一个协整关系且结果可能受归一化影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First estimate a long-run relation and retain its residuals, then apply a specialised residual unit-root test. Critical values differ from an ordinary ADF because the cointegrating vector was estimated. If cointegration is found, estimate an error-correction model. The method is simple but handles only one relation in a multivariate system and can depend on normalisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两变量步骤是：

1. 分别检查 $y_t,x_t$ 的整合阶数与确定性项；
2. 估计 $y_t=\beta_0+\beta_1x_t+e_t$，保留 $\hat e_t$；
3. 用 Engle–Granger 临界值检验 $\hat e_t$ 是否平稳；
4. 若平稳，估计含 $\hat e_{t-1}$ 与差分滞后的 ECM，再检查调整符号、残差与参数稳定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two variables:

**1.** Check the integration order and deterministic terms of $y_t$ and $x_t$ separately.<br>
**2.** Estimate $y_t=\beta_0+\beta_1x_t+e_t$ and retain $\hat e_t$.<br>
**3.** Test stationarity of $\hat e_t$ using Engle–Granger critical values.<br>
**4.** If stationary, estimate an ECM with $\hat e_{t-1}$ and lagged differences, then inspect adjustment signs, residuals, and parameter stability.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 误差修正表示
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Error-correction representation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Delta y_t=\alpha\beta^Ty_{t-1}+\sum_i\Gamma_i\Delta y_{t-i}+u_t$。$\beta^Ty_{t-1}$ 给昨期长期偏离，$\alpha$ 给各变量纠偏速度。短期差分项描述瞬时动态；某个 $\alpha$ 为零可对应弱外生性语境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\Delta y_t=\alpha\beta^Ty_{t-1}+\sum_i\Gamma_i\Delta y_{t-i}+u_t$. The term $\beta^Ty_{t-1}$ is yesterday's long-run deviation and $\alpha$ gives each variable's adjustment speed. Lagged differences describe short-run dynamics; a zero row or component of $\alpha$ can correspond to weak exogeneity in a specified parameter context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两变量且定义 $e_{t-1}=y_{t-1}-\beta x_{t-1}$，若 $e_{t-1}>0$ 表示 $y$ 高于长期关系，则 $\Delta y_t$ 方程中的调整系数应为负才会将 $y$ 往下拉。如果把协整项改写为 $\beta x_{t-1}-y_{t-1}$，同一纠偏机制的系数符号就应反转。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two variables, define $e_{t-1}=y_{t-1}-\beta x_{t-1}$. If $e_{t-1}>0$ means $y$ is above the long-run relation, a negative adjustment coefficient in the $\Delta y_t$ equation pulls $y$ downward. Rewriting the cointegrating term as $\beta x_{t-1}-y_{t-1}$ requires the coefficient sign to reverse for the same mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Johansen 方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Johansen method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Johansen 从 VAR 的秩约束同时估计多个协整向量，用 trace/max-eigenvalue 等检验协整秩。需要选择 VAR 阶数、确定性项和样本区间；不同选择可能改变秩判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Johansen estimates multiple cointegrating vectors simultaneously through rank restrictions on a VAR and tests rank using trace and maximum-eigenvalue statistics. VAR order, deterministic terms, and sample window must be selected; different choices can change the inferred rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将 VAR($p$) 改写为
$$
\Delta y_t=\Pi y_{t-1}+\sum_{i=1}^{p-1}\Gamma_i\Delta y_{t-i}+u_t,
$$
关键是 $\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)$：秩 0 表示没有水平约束，满秩表示水平本身平稳，$0<r<K$ 时 $\Pi=\alpha\beta^T$ 并有 $r$ 个协整关系。trace 检验的原假设是 rank $≤ r$，max-eigenvalue 比较 rank $=r$ 与 $r+1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rewrite a VAR($p$) as
$$
\Delta y_t=\Pi y_{t-1}+\sum_{i=1}^{p-1}\Gamma_i\Delta y_{t-i}+u_t.
$$
The key is $\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)$: rank zero means no level restrictions, full rank means levels are stationary, and $0<r<K$ permits $\Pi=\alpha\beta^T$ with $r$ cointegrating relations. The trace test has null rank at most $r$, while the maximum-eigenvalue test compares rank $r$ with $r+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 解释和边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interpretation and boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协整说明统计长期约束，不自动证明经济均衡或因果。结构突变、制度变化和非线性调整可能使固定协整向量失效。高相关既非必要也非充分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cointegration establishes a statistical long-run restriction, not automatically an economic equilibrium or causal relation. Structural breaks, institutional change, and nonlinear adjustment may invalidate a fixed cointegrating vector. High correlation is neither necessary nor sufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另外，某个调整系数为零不等于该变量在所有意义下外生。弱外生性是针对特定长期参数的推断概念；“不对长期偏离调整”也不排除它受短期滞后或同期冲击影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nor does a zero adjustment coefficient make a variable exogenous in every sense. Weak exogeneity is defined relative to inference on specified long-run parameters. Failure to adjust to long-run disequilibrium does not rule out effects from short-run lags or contemporaneous shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：价格与成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: price and cost*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设产品价格 $p_t$ 与投入成本 $c_t$ 均为 I(1)，但 $e_t=p_t-1.2c_t$ 平稳。若
$$
\Delta p_t=-0.3e_{t-1}+0.2\Delta c_{t-1}+u_{pt},
$$
则当上期价格比长期比例高 1 个单位时，其他项相同下本期价格变化减少 0.3，即纠正 30% 的偏离。这个解释依赖 $e_t$ 的符号定义，也不证明成本的变化是外生因果冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose product price $p_t$ and input cost $c_t$ are both $I(1)$, while $e_t=p_t-1.2c_t$ is stationary. If
$$
\Delta p_t=-0.3e_{t-1}+0.2\Delta c_{t-1}+u_{pt},
$$
then when price is one unit above its long-run proportion in the previous period, current price growth falls by 0.3, holding other terms fixed: 30% of the deviation is corrected. This interpretation depends on the sign definition of $e_t$ and does not show that a change in cost is an exogenous causal shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 两个 I(1) 序列高度相关，是否足以说明协整？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Is high correlation between two $I(1)$ series sufficient for cointegration?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不足。必须存在一个线性组合为 I(0)；共同趋势也能让无关序列样本相关很高。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. A linear combination must be $I(0)$; common trends can also make unrelated series highly correlated in a sample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 误差修正项的系数表示什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does an error-correction coefficient represent?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 当上一期偏离长期关系时，本期各变量差分朝哪个方向、以多快速度调整；符号要结合协整项定义解释。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It gives the direction and speed with which a variable's current difference responds to last period's long-run deviation; its sign must be read with the cointegrating-term definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么协整系统不宜只对所有变量差分后做 VAR？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why should a cointegrated system not be modelled only as a VAR in first differences?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 差分会消除单位根，却也丢掉长期约束和纠偏机制，造成模型遗漏。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Differencing removes unit roots but also discards the long-run restriction and adjustment mechanism, leaving the model misspecified.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释 Johansen 方法为什么看 $\Pi$ 的秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why the Johansen method studies the rank of $\Pi$.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $\Pi y_{t-1}$ 是水平变量进入差分方程的长期部分；每一个独立非零方向都对应一个平稳线性组合及其调整，所以秩就是独立协整关系数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $\Pi y_{t-1}$ is the long-run level component in the difference equations. Each independent nonzero direction corresponds to a stationary linear combination and its adjustment, so the rank counts independent cointegrating relations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
