# 准备金数量变化本身不足以判断货币政策是在宽松还是收紧
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A change in reserve quantity alone does not identify monetary easing or tightening.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

准备金是结算资产，政策立场关心货币金融条件如何支持或约束支出与通胀。央行可能为维持同一个利率目标而补充准备金，也可能在准备金不变时提高管理利率。只看央行资产负债表的一列数量，就会把实施操作和政策意图混在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reserves are settlement assets; the policy stance concerns monetary and financial conditions supporting or restraining spending and inflation. A central bank can replenish reserves to maintain an unchanged target, or raise administered rates without draining reserves. A quantity series alone conflates implementation with policy intent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，公众增加现金持有使准备金减少10，央行又购券补回10。这两笔结算合起来可以只是抵销数量扰动，不能仅凭购券宣称一次新增刺激。反过来，在[[充足准备金框架]]中维持供给不变、同时把相关管理利率上调，仍可提高市场融资价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If public currency demand drains 10 of reserves and a purchase restores 10, the combined operations can merely offset a quantity disturbance. The purchase alone is not proof of additional stimulus. Conversely, an [[充足准备金框架|ample-reserves system]] can raise market funding prices by increasing administered rates while keeping reserve supply unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断时至少补齐三个背景：当前操作目标是否改变；预期中的利率路径和其他金融条件如何改变；数量操作是在补充结算需要，还是通过[[资产购买的组合再平衡]]等渠道改变更长期条件。[[货币乘数的因果边界]]进一步解释，准备金与贷款或广义货币之间也没有固定倍数因果关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check whether the operating target changed, how the expected rate path and wider conditions changed, and whether the quantity operation met settlement needs or affected longer-term conditions through [[资产购买的组合再平衡|portfolio rebalancing]]. The [[货币乘数的因果边界|money-multiplier boundary]] separately rejects a fixed causal reserve-to-lending multiple.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

管理利率的每一次微调也不必都表示立场变化。例如，目标区间保持不变，只为让市场利率更好地留在该区间内而调整实施利率，可以是技术性操作。应将调整目的和整个目标设置一起读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every administered-rate adjustment changes the stance. With the target range unchanged, an implementation-rate adjustment to keep market rates within that range can be technical. Read its purpose together with the full target setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
