# 毛利率是同一业务口径的收入扣除对应销售成本后占该收入的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gross profit margin is revenue less the corresponding cost of sales, divided by revenue on the same business basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

毛利率衡量在扣除对应已售商品或服务的成本后，每单位收入还剩多少毛利。设同期间、同业务范围的净收入为 $S>0$、对应销售成本为 $C$，则 $g=(S-C)/S$。这里的成本不是全部期间费用，也不是仅本期采购付现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gross margin measures the gross profit remaining per unit of revenue after the cost of the goods or services sold. For matched period and business scope, net revenue $S>0$ and cost of sales $C$ give $g=(S-C)/S$. Cost of sales is neither all period expenses nor cash purchases alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入 100、对应销售成本 70，则毛利 30、毛利率 30%。若销售、管理等期间费用再花 20，剩余营业利润不是 30；这由[[营业利润率]]另行衡量。毛利率也不同于以成本为分母的加价率 $30/70\approx42.86\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue of 100 and cost of sales of 70 give gross profit of 30 and a 30% gross margin. If selling and administrative expenses add 20, operating profit is not 30; [[营业利润率|operating margin]] captures that further layer. Gross margin also differs from the cost-based markup of approximately 42.86%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较时需看收入和成本分类、产品组合与退折让。若集团“营业总收入”包含金融业务利息，但成本只取制造业务营业成本，就没有完成[[财务比率口径匹配]]。毛利率上升可来自价格、成本或组合变化，本身不证明销量、现金或股东价值都增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparisons require attention to classification, product mix, returns, and discounts. Combining group revenue that includes financial interest with manufacturing cost alone fails [[财务比率口径匹配|ratio-basis matching]]. Higher gross margin may reflect pricing, costs, or mix; it does not independently prove greater sales volume, cash, or shareholder value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
