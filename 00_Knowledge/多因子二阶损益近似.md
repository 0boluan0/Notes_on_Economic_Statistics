# 多因子二阶损益近似须包含各自曲率与混合二阶项并保持因子坐标一致
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A multi-factor second-order P&L approximation includes both own-factor and mixed curvature in consistent factor coordinates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先冻结估值时点与持仓，写清本币价值 $V(x)$ 的全部相关输入、单位及保持不变的模型参数。若 $V$ 在当前点邻域为 $C^2$，令 $g=\nabla V$、$H=\nabla^2V$，由 [[多元Taylor近似]] 得到同刻情景价值变化：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Freeze valuation time and holdings, and specify the inputs, units, and fixed model parameters of domestic value $V(x)$. If $V$ is $C^2$ near the current point, with gradient $g$ and Hessian $H$, [[多元Taylor近似|multivariable Taylor expansion]] gives the same-time scenario change:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta V=g^T\Delta x+\frac12\Delta x^TH\Delta x+o(\|\Delta x\|^2).
$$
$$
\frac12\Delta x^TH\Delta x
=\frac12\sum_iH_{ii}(\Delta x_i)^2
+\sum_{i<j}H_{ij}\Delta x_i\Delta x_j.
$$

第二式使用对称 Hessian。把每对不同因子只列一次后，交叉项前不再有 $1/2$，因为矩阵形式中两个求导顺序已经各出现一次。只给每个因子各加一项 Gamma 平方，仍然不是完整二阶展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second identity uses Hessian symmetry. Once each distinct factor pair appears only once, its cross term has no remaining factor of $1/2$: the matrix form contains both orders. Adding only each factor's squared gamma term is not a complete second-order expansion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如持有 100 股、每股 50 欧元，汇率为每欧元 1.20 美元，价值为 6000 美元。股价变为 52 欧元、汇率变为 1.25 后，完整价值为 6500。由 $V=100SX$，一阶项和交叉项分别为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold 100 shares priced at EUR 50, with USD 1.20 per euro: value is USD 6,000. At EUR 52 and USD 1.25 per euro, value becomes USD 6,500. For $V=100SX$, the first-order and mixed terms are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
100(1.20)(2)+100(50)(0.05)=240+250=490,
\qquad100(2)(0.05)=10.
$$

所以完整二阶结果是 $500$，在这个双线性例子中恰好精确。两个自身 Gamma 都为零，遗漏 [[交叉Gamma]] 仍会漏算 10 美元。一般非二次估值函数则保留余项，不能由这个例子推出全重估总与二阶结果相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complete second-order result is 500, exact for this bilinear example. Both own-factor gammas are zero, yet omitting [[交叉Gamma|cross-gamma]] misses USD 10. General nonquadratic valuation functions retain a remainder; the example does not make quadratic approximation universally exact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若输入包括现货 $S$ 与波动率 $\sigma$，完整二阶还可能包括 $V_{S\sigma}\Delta S\Delta\sigma$ 和 $\frac12V_{\sigma\sigma}(\Delta\sigma)^2$，而非仅 Delta、Gamma 与线性 Vega。若时间也推进，可把 $(t,x)$ 一起展开；只添 $\Theta\Delta t$ 而省略时间交叉项，是另行采用的截断约定，不是完整多元二阶式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With spot and volatility inputs, complete quadratic expansion can include $V_{S\sigma}\Delta S\Delta\sigma$ and $\frac12V_{\sigma\sigma}(\Delta\sigma)^2$, not just delta, gamma, and linear vega. If time advances, expand jointly in $(t,x)$. Adding only theta times elapsed time while omitting time cross-terms is a separate truncation convention, not the complete multivariable second-order formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格变化、简单收益率与对数收益率是不同坐标；更换坐标时必须转换导数和二阶项，不能只把数字换进原公式。有限大冲击、跳跃或行权边界附近应按 [[估值近似验证]] 比较完整重估；二阶项不保证涵盖所有损益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price changes, simple returns, and log returns are different coordinates. Changing coordinates requires transforming derivatives and quadratic terms rather than substituting new numbers into the old formula. For large shocks, jumps, or exercise boundaries, compare against full repricing using [[估值近似验证|valuation-approximation validation]]. Quadratic terms do not guarantee complete P&L coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
