# 衍生工具是按标的资产或其他约定变量确定支付的金融合约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A derivative is a financial contract whose payments depend on an underlying asset or another specified variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

衍生工具把某个变量的变化写进合约：它可以是股票价格、汇率、利率、商品价格，也可以是指数或约定信用事件。买到的是一组合同权利与义务，未必是标的资产本身。例如，持有股票看涨期权，并不会因此自动取得该公司的股东表决权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A derivative contract ties payments to a variable such as an equity price, exchange rate, interest rate, commodity price, index, or specified credit event. It creates contractual rights and obligations, not necessarily ownership of the underlying. Holding an equity call, for example, does not itself confer shareholder voting rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别时先分清双方是否都承担约定的未来履约义务，还是一方拥有行权选择。[[远期合约]]、[[期货合约]]和普通[[金融互换]]属于前一类；[[期权]]把选择权交给持有人。复杂产品可以同时含有这些成分，应按条款拆解支付，而不是按产品名称猜风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First ask whether both parties are committed to future performance or one party holds an exercise choice. [[远期合约|Forwards]], [[期货合约|futures]], and plain [[金融互换|swaps]] belong to the former group; [[期权|options]] give their holder a choice. A structured product can combine these components, so its terms—not its marketing name—determine its payments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一合约既可用于[[套期保值]]，也可用于主动承担风险。企业已有外币收入时卖出外汇远期，可能是在减少汇率敞口；没有对应收入而建立同样头寸，则是在新增敞口。用途取决于**合约与原有资产负债合在一起**的结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same contract can be used for [[套期保值|hedging]] or for taking risk. Selling foreign currency forward may reduce exposure when a firm already expects foreign-currency receipts; the same position without those receipts creates exposure. Its purpose depends on the combined position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
