# 矩阵有右逆当且仅当它满行秩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A matrix has a right inverse if and only if it has full row rank*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心等价
> 设 $\mathbb F\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$ 且 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$。存在 $R\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ 使
> $$
> AR=I_m
> $$
> 当且仅当 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=m$。因此右逆只可能在 $n\ge m$ 时存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\mathbb F\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$ and $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$. A matrix $R\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ satisfying $AR=I_m$ exists if and only if $\operatorname{rank}(A)=m$. Hence a right inverse can exist only when $n\ge m$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么两边等价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the two conditions are equivalent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $AR=I_m$，则任意 $b\in\mathbb F^m$ 都可写成 $b=A(Rb)$。所以 $C(A)=\mathbb F^m$，即 $A$ 是满射，因而 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=m$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $AR=I_m$, then every $b\in\mathbb F^m$ can be written as $b=A(Rb)$. Thus $C(A)=\mathbb F^m$, so $A$ is surjective and $\operatorname{rank}(A)=m$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若 $A$ 满行秩，则它把 $\mathbb F^n$ 映满整个 $\mathbb F^m$。对 $\mathbb F^m$ 的每个基向量 $e_i$ 选取一个原像 $x_i$ 使 $Ax_i=e_i$，并定义 $Re_i=x_i$；线性延拓后便有 $AR=I_m$，所以 $R$ 是一个右逆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, if $A$ has full row rank, it maps $\mathbb F^n$ onto all of $\mathbb F^m$. For each basis vector $e_i$ of $\mathbb F^m$, choose a preimage $x_i$ with $Ax_i=e_i$ and define $Re_i=x_i$. Extending linearly gives $AR=I_m$, so $R$ is a right inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 解的含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary for solutions of $Ax=b$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满行秩保证 $Ax=b$ 对每个 $b$ **至少**有一个解，但不保证解唯一。若 $n>m$，则 $\dim N(A)=n-m>0$，每个特解都可加上零空间向量；右逆也通常不唯一。标准内积进一步选出的规范右逆见 [[满行秩伪逆公式]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full row rank guarantees at least one solution to $Ax=b$ for every $b$, but not uniqueness. When $n>m$, the nullity is $n-m>0$, so a null-space vector can be added to any particular solution; right inverses are generally non-unique as well. The canonical right inverse selected by the standard inner product is given in [[满行秩伪逆公式|the full-row-rank pseudoinverse formula]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 一个 $2\times5$ 满行秩矩阵为什么可以让每个 $b\in\mathbb R^2$ 都可达，却不能唯一恢复五维输入？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can a full-row-rank $2\times5$ matrix reach every $b\in\mathbb R^2$ without uniquely recovering a five-dimensional input?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 它是满射，但零空间维数为 3；多个输入会产生同一输出。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** It is surjective, but its null space has dimension three, so multiple inputs produce the same output.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
