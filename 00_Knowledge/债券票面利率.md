# 债券票面利率是年化约定息票金额与计息本金之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A bond's coupon rate is its annualised contractual coupon amount divided by the interest-calculation principal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券票面利率（coupon rate）规定每单位计息本金所对应的年化合同息票。对面值 $F$ 不变、规则等额付息的普通债券，若每年约定息票总额为 $C_{\rm year}$，则票面利率为 $c=C_{\rm year}/F$；一年付息 $m$ 次时，每期息票为 $C=Fc/m$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coupon rate specifies the annualised contractual coupon per unit of interest-calculation principal. For an ordinary bond with constant face value $F$ and regular equal coupons, an annual coupon amount $C_{\rm year}$ gives $c=C_{\rm year}/F$. With $m$ payments per year, each coupon is $C=Fc/m$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面值 $100$、年票面利率 $6\%$、半年付息，意味着每半年支付 $3$，不是每半年支付 $6$。这一合约报价不会因二级市场价格变成 $95$ 而改成 $6/95$；它也不是已计算复利的[[有效年利率]]，更不是估值时自动采用的[[票面利率与贴现率|市场贴现率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Face value 100 with a 6% annual coupon paid semiannually means 3 every half-year, not 6. A secondary-market price of 95 does not change that contractual rate to $6/95$. Nor is the quote an already-compounded [[有效年利率|effective annual rate]] or automatically the [[票面利率与贴现率|market discount rate]] used for valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
