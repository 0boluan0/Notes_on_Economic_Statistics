# Matrix space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix space treats an entire matrix as one vector. Two such vectors are added entry by entry, and a scalar multiplies every entry. The [[向量空间|vector-space]] rules concern these two operations; matrix multiplication is not needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵空间把整个矩阵当作一个向量。两个这样的向量相加，就是对应元素相加；数乘就是每个元素乘同一个数。[[向量空间]]的规则关心这两种运算，不需要使用矩阵乘法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a real number such as $2$ scales the whole matrix, and addition matches entries in the same position:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，实数 $2$ 会把整个矩阵的各元素都放大两倍；相加则把相同位置的元素配对：

$$
2\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}
+\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\4&-1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\4&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result is another $2\times2$ matrix. We can choose all four entries freely; there is no requirement that the rows or columns be independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结果仍是一个 $2\times2$ 矩阵。四个元素可以自由选择；这里并不要求行或列线性无关。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $M_{m,n}(\mathbb R)$ for all real matrices with $m$ rows and $n$ columns. The size is fixed so that addition is defined. Its zero vector is the zero matrix, and negating each entry supplies an additive inverse. The remaining vector-space rules follow from the corresponding rules for real numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $M_{m,n}(\mathbb R)$ 表示全部 $m$ 行、$n$ 列的实矩阵。固定尺寸才能定义相加。零向量是零矩阵，各元素变号得到加法逆元，其余运算公理由实数运算逐项继承。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A basis with one entry at a time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*每次只控制一个位置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $2\times2$ matrices, the four building blocks are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $2\times2$ 矩阵，先把四个基本构件写出来：

$$
E_{11}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
E_{12}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
E_{21}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
E_{22}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $aE_{11}+bE_{12}+cE_{21}+dE_{22}$, the coefficient $a$ controls only the top-left entry, $b$ only the top-right entry, and similarly for $c,d$. The result is $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$. Each coefficient can be read back from its own position, so two different coefficient lists cannot give the same matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $aE_{11}+bE_{12}+cE_{21}+dE_{22}$ 中，系数 $a$ 只控制左上角，$b$ 只控制右上角，$c,d$ 同理。结果就是 $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$。每个系数都能从对应位置读回，因此两组不同的系数不可能得到同一矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $E_{ij}$ have a single $1$ in position $(i,j)$ and zeros elsewhere. Each matrix has the unique expansion below: its entries are exactly the coefficients. Thus the $mn$ matrices $E_{ij}$ both span the space and are independent, so they form a [[基|basis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $E_{ij}$ 只在第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列为 $1$，其余位置为零。每个矩阵都有下面的唯一展开，矩阵元素正是组合系数。因此这 $mn$ 个矩阵既张成全空间，又线性无关，构成一组[[基]]。

$$
X=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{ij}E_{ij},
\qquad \dim M_{m,n}(\mathbb R)=mn.
$$

> [!example] A matrix as one vector · 一个矩阵就是一个向量
> $$
> \begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}
> =2E_{11}-E_{12}+3E_{22}.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The space of $2\times2$ matrices has dimension $4$, even though any individual matrix in it has rank at most $2$. [[维数|Dimension]] counts independent directions in a specified space; [[矩阵秩|matrix rank]] counts independent columns of one specified matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 全部 $2\times2$ 矩阵组成的空间维数为 $4$，其中任何单个矩阵的秩却至多为 $2$。[[维数]]数一个指定空间的独立方向；[[矩阵秩]]数一个指定矩阵的独立列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The scalar field is part of the specification. Over $\mathbb C$, the same $E_{ij}$ form a complex basis of $M_{m,n}(\mathbb C)$, so its complex dimension is $mn$. If those complex matrices are instead viewed as a real vector space, each entry has two real parameters and the real dimension is $2mn$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标量域也是定义的一部分。对 $\mathbb C$，同样的 $E_{ij}$ 构成 $M_{m,n}(\mathbb C)$ 的复基，复维数为 $mn$。若把这些复矩阵当成实向量空间，每个元素都有两个实参数，实维数就是 $2mn$。
