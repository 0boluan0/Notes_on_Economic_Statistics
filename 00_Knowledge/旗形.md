# 旗形是较快行情后在狭窄平行通道内短暂整理的价格形态

旗形是在一段较快的上涨或下跌之后，价格进入狭窄、近似平行通道作短暂整理的多期形态。前面的较快行情称为旗杆，后面的通道称为旗面；典型旗面向原行情的反方向倾斜。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A flag is a multi-bar pattern in which a relatively rapid advance or decline is followed by brief consolidation in a narrow, approximately parallel channel. The preceding move is the flagpole and the channel is the flag. The flag typically slopes against the preceding move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上涨后向下缓倾的旗面与下跌后向上缓倾的旗面互为方向对照。识别需说明采样周期，以及相对旗杆如何判定“较快”“短暂”和“狭窄”，不能只看一个倾斜区间就省略前序行情。旗面的几何基础是[[趋势线通道]]，两边界大致平行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A gently descending flag after a rise mirrors a gently ascending flag after a fall. Specify sampling and how “rapid,” “brief” and “narrow” are assessed relative to the flagpole; an isolated sloping interval is insufficient. The flag's geometry is a [[趋势线通道|trend-line channel]] with broadly parallel boundaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两条同向倾斜边界明显收敛，应按[[楔形]]的结构辨认；若形成短小的收敛三角旗，则不是这里的平行旗面，其收敛关系接近[[对称三角形]]。传统分析把旗形视为延续候选，但只有实际突破后才知道这次是否沿原方向离开，不能把候选轮廓写成必然延续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Clearly converging boundaries sloping in the same direction describe a [[楔形|wedge]], not a parallel flag. A small triangular pennant is also distinct, with contraction resembling a [[对称三角形|symmetrical triangle]]. Traditional analysis treats flags as continuation candidates; the actual breakout is needed to establish whether this instance exits in the prior direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
