# 排序复杂度陈述必须说明规模、成本单位与情形

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A sorting complexity claim must state size, cost unit, and case*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区别
> “这个排序是 $O(n^2)$”至少还缺三项：$n$ 表示什么、计算的是比较/移动/写入/时间还是空间，以及结论属于最好、最坏、某个分布或随机选择下的期望，还是一串操作上的摊还成本。算法变体也必须固定。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “This sort is $O(n^2)$” omits at least three things: what $n$ denotes; whether the cost counts comparisons, moves, writes, elapsed work, or space; and whether the bound is best-case, worst-case, expected under a distribution or random choice, or amortized over an operation sequence. The algorithm variant must also be fixed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 自然解释

- 带“无交换即停止”的 bubble sort：已排序输入为 $\Theta(n)$，逆序输入为 $\Theta(n^2)$。
- 经典 selection sort：比较次数对所有输入都是 $n(n-1)/2$，但交换至多 $n-1$ 次。
- 标准 top-down array merge sort：输入顺序不改变其 $\Theta(n\log n)$ 结构，但还要报告线性辅助空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Bubble sort with a “stop after a swap-free pass” rule takes $\Theta(n)$ on sorted input and $\Theta(n^2)$ on reverse input.
- Classic selection sort performs $n(n-1)/2$ comparisons on every input but at most $n-1$ swaps.
- Standard top-down array merge sort retains its $\Theta(n\log n)$ structure regardless of input order, but its linear auxiliary space must also be reported.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 边界
> 期望成本必须写明随机性来自[[随机算法期望复杂度|算法]]还是[[平均情况复杂度|输入分布]]。[[摊还复杂度|摊还成本]]把一串操作的总成本分配到各次操作，并不是“平均输入”；一次独立排序通常没有理由只因运行很多轮就改称摊还分析。实测平均延迟又是另一个工作负载统计量。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An expected bound must state whether randomness comes from the [[随机算法期望复杂度|algorithm]] or an [[平均情况复杂度|input distribution]]. [[摊还复杂度|Amortized cost]] distributes the total cost of an operation sequence and is not an “average input”; a standalone sort does not become amortized merely because it is benchmarked many times. Measured average latency is yet another workload statistic.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> “selection sort 和 insertion sort 都是 $O(n^2)$，所以代价相同。”这句话漏了什么？
>
> **答案：** 它没固定输入情形和成本单位。selection sort 始终做平方级比较但交换少；insertion sort 的比较与移动会随逆序对变化。
