# 财富渠道是资产价值变化通过家庭可支配的终身资源影响消费选择的机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The wealth channel links asset-value changes to consumption through households' lifetime resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财富渠道指资产价值变化使家庭重新评价可供一生消费的资源，进而调整消费计划。货币政策可以经资产价格进入这条链，但“政策怎样改变价格”和“价格怎样影响消费”是两个步骤：第一步复用[[政策传导的估值渠道]]，第二步还取决于财富持有者、负债、流动性和消费偏好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The wealth channel revises consumption plans as asset values change households' lifetime resources. Monetary policy may enter through asset prices, but valuation and spending responses are separate steps. Reuse [[政策传导的估值渠道|policy valuation analysis]] for the first; the second depends on ownership, liabilities, liquidity and preferences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

隔离一个最小财富效应：家庭只有两期，当前可用净资源为 $W$，没有其他收入、负债、遗赠或借贷限制，实际毛回报固定为 1，最大化 $\log c_0+\log c_1$。预算为 $c_0+c_1=W$，最优消费各为 $W/2$。给定资产价值把 $W$ 从 100 提高到 110，两期消费分别从 50 到 55。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Isolate a two-period wealth effect with current available net resources $W$, no other income, liabilities, bequests or credit constraints, and a fixed real gross return of one. Maximising $\log c_0+\log c_1$ subject to $c_0+c_1=W$ gives $W/2$ in each period. A given asset-value increase raising $W$ from 100 to 110 raises each consumption amount from 50 to 55.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的当期反应 5 来自所设偏好和两期寿命，不是“一元资产升值必带来五角消费”。真实政策还可能同时改变未来收益率、劳动收入和希望储备的财富；资产升值若伴随未来投资回报降低，消费反应可能被部分抵销。[[货币、财富与收入]]还提醒：账面财富不是同额新增可用货币或当期收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The current response of 5 follows from these preferences and two-period horizon, not a universal propensity to consume half of a capital gain. Policy can also change future returns, labour income and desired buffers. Lower prospective returns may offset part of the spending response to asset appreciation. [[货币、财富与收入|Money, wealth and income]] are different objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

房价尤其需要另看住房服务：未来买更大房屋的人也面对更高购置成本，持有房屋并不说明涨价使每户都同样变富。以房产增加借款额度的机制则是[[财富效应与抵押效应]]中的另一条链，不能从消费与房价同涨就认定财富渠道已经识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Housing also provides services: future purchasers of larger homes face higher acquisition costs, so owners do not benefit equally from appreciation. Borrowing capacity against housing belongs to the distinct [[财富效应与抵押效应|collateral mechanism]]. Co-movement between prices and consumption does not identify a wealth effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
