# Closed-interval method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*闭区间极值法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find the largest and smallest outputs of a continuous function on a finite closed interval, you do not need to inspect every input separately. The **closed-interval method** identifies every place where an absolute extremum could occur, then compares the function values at those places. Endpoints matter just as much as interior turning points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要找连续函数在有限闭区间上的最大、最小输出，不必逐个检查所有输入。**闭区间极值法**先找出绝对极值可能出现的全部位置，再比较这些位置的函数值。端点与内部转折点同样重要。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $f(x)=x^3-3x$ on $[-2,3]$. The [[导数|derivative]] is $f'(x)=3x^2-3=3(x^2-1)$, so its interior zeros are $-1$ and $1$. The derivative exists everywhere, so there are no extra nondifferentiable candidates. Add the two endpoints and evaluate the original function:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在 $[-2,3]$ 上考察 $f(x)=x^3-3x$。[[导数]]为 $f'(x)=3x^2-3=3(x^2-1)$，内部零点是 $-1$ 与 $1$。导数处处存在，没有额外的不可导候选。加入两个端点，再代回原函数：

$$\begin{array}{c|rrrr}x&-2&-1&1&3\\\hline f(x)&-2&2&-2&18\end{array}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The smallest output is $-2$, attained at **both** $x=-2$ and $x=1$. The largest is $18$, attained at $x=3$. Solving only $f'(x)=0$ would miss the absolute maximum and one location of the absolute minimum. Report the extremal value and every input attaining it; these are different kinds of answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小输出为 $-2$，在 $x=-2$ 和 $x=1$ **两处**取得；最大输出为 $18$，在 $x=3$ 取得。只解 $f'(x)=0$，会漏掉绝对最大点和一个绝对最小点。答案应分别说明极值与取得该值的全部输入，它们不是同一种对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the candidate set cannot miss an extremum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*候选集合为什么不会漏掉答案*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a,b$ be finite real numbers with $a<b$, and let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be [[连续性|continuous]] on the whole closed interval, including its endpoint limits from inside the interval. The [[极值定理|extreme value theorem]] guarantees that $f$ actually attains an absolute maximum and an absolute minimum. This is an existence statement, not yet a way to locate them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,b$ 是有限实数，且 $a<b$；实值函数 $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ 在整个闭区间上[[连续性|连续]]，包括端点从区间内部趋近的连续性。[[极值定理]]保证函数真正取得绝对最大值与绝对最小值。这先回答“存在吗”，还没有指出答案在哪里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An attaining input is either an endpoint or an interior point. At an interior local extremum, if the finite derivative exists, the [[无约束一阶条件|first-order necessary condition]] forces it to equal zero. If that derivative does not exist, the point must still be retained. Under the vault's [[临界点|critical-point convention]], these are precisely the two kinds of interior critical input. An absolute extremum is also local, so every attaining input belongs to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取得极值的输入，不是端点，就是内点。在内部局部极值处，如果有限导数存在，[[无约束一阶条件|一阶必要条件]]要求它等于零；如果该导数不存在，也必须保留这个点。按本库的[[临界点]]约定，这正是两类内部临界输入。绝对极值同时也是局部极值，因此取得答案的位置必属于

$$C=\{a,b\}\cup\{c\in(a,b):f'(c)=0\text{ or no finite }f'(c)\text{ exists}\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $C$ is the set of **candidate inputs**. It can contain points that attain neither global extremum. The theorem guarantees that taking the largest and smallest values of $f$ over $C$ gives exactly the extrema over $[a,b]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $C$ 是**候选输入集合**，其中可能有既非全局最大点也非全局最小点的位置。但上述定理保证：比较 $C$ 上的函数值所得的最大、最小值，恰好也是整个 $[a,b]$ 上的极值。

> [!important] Compare original values after finding all candidates · 找全候选后比较原函数值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First check the domain and continuity. Include $a,b$, all interior zero-derivative points, and all interior points where the finite derivative is absent. If this set is finite, evaluate $f$ at every candidate and compare the outputs. Endpoints need not have zero derivatives. Local classification is optional for this final comparison: it does not replace evaluating the values.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先核对定义域与连续性，再加入 $a,b$、全部内部导数零点，以及全部内部不存在有限导数的点。若候选有限，就逐点计算 $f$ 并比较输出。端点不需要满足导数为零。最终比较不强制先给每个点作局部分类；局部分类也不能代替计算函数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Corners belong in the comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不可导的尖角也必须比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $g(x)=|x|$ on $[-1,2]$, the function is continuous but has no derivative at zero: the left and right difference quotients are $-1$ and $1$. Its derivatives away from zero never vanish. The candidate set is nevertheless $\{-1,0,2\}$, giving values $1,0,2$. Thus the minimum is $0$ at zero, and the maximum is $2$ at the right endpoint. Differentiability throughout the interval is not required for this method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $[-1,2]$ 上的 $g(x)=|x|$，函数连续，但零点的左右差商分别为 $-1$ 与 $1$，所以不可导；零点以外的导数从不为零。候选仍然是 $\{-1,0,2\}$，对应函数值 $1,0,2$。最小值为零点处的 $0$，最大值为右端点处的 $2$。本方法不要求函数在整个区间处处可导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An infinite candidate set changes the computation, not attainment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*候选无限时，变化的是计算方式，不是存在性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The hypotheses do not promise finitely many critical points. For the constant function $f(x)=7$ on $[0,1]$, every interior point is critical; every input attains both the maximum and minimum value $7$. There is no useful finite list of all candidates, but the extreme value theorem is fully valid. With an infinite candidate set, use an argument that controls the whole set, such as a formula or a proven monotonicity or range bound. A finite table is a convenient implementation when available, not an extra conclusion of continuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些假设没有保证临界点有限。例如，$[0,1]$ 上的常函数 $f(x)=7$ 的每个内点都是临界点，每个输入都同时取得最大、最小值 $7$。全部候选无法列成有用的有限清单，但极值定理仍完全成立。候选无限时，要用公式、已证明的单调性或取值范围界等论证控制整个集合。有限表格只是条件合适时的计算方式，不是连续性额外保证的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the interval or continuity assumptions change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区间与连续性条件改变时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $(0,1)$, $f(x)=x$ has no largest or smallest attained value. The limits $0$ and $1$ are bounds approached at excluded inputs, not values to enter in a candidate table. In particular, $0^+$, $1^-$ and infinity describe limiting behavior, not additional points where a real-valued function has necessarily been evaluated. A formula may extend to an excluded endpoint, but that does not add the point to the original optimization domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $(0,1)$ 上，$f(x)=x$ 不取得最大或最小值。趋近得到的 $0$、$1$ 是排除端点附近的界，不是可以填进候选表的已取得函数值。特别地，$0^+$、$1^-$ 与无穷远描述极限行为，不是额外的、已经代入实值函数求值的位置。即使公式能延伸到排除的端点，也不会自动把端点加入原优化定义域。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A closed interval alone is also insufficient. On $[0,1]$, set $f(0)=1$ and $f(x)=x$ for $x>0$. It is discontinuous at zero and has no minimum: every positive value $f(x)=x$ can be lowered by replacing $x$ with $x/2$, while the output at zero is $1$. The maximum $1$ is still attained. Losing a hypothesis removes the guarantee; it does not prove that all extrema disappear. If $a=b$, the domain has one input and its value is trivially both extrema; the nondegenerate procedure above assumes $a<b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有闭区间也不够。在 $[0,1]$ 上令 $f(0)=1$，对 $x>0$ 令 $f(x)=x$。它在零点不连续，而且没有最小值：每个正输入产生的值 $x$，都可用 $x/2$ 降低，而零点的输出是 $1$。但最大值 $1$ 仍然取得。失去某个假设意味着保证失效，不意味着所有极值都消失。若 $a=b$，定义域只含一个输入，其函数值自然同时是最大、最小；上面的非退化步骤假设 $a<b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This separates three jobs: the extreme value theorem gives attainment, the first-order condition and nondifferentiable-point check prevent omissions, and value comparison determines the global winners. A [[二阶导数检验|second derivative test]] or [[一阶导数检验|first derivative test]] answers a different, local classification question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此要分清三件事：极值定理保证取得；一阶必要条件与不可导检查防止遗漏；函数值比较决定全局答案。[[二阶导数检验]]与[[一阶导数检验]]回答的则是另一个局部分类问题。
