# 金融危机、挤兑与金融加速器
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Financial Crises, Bank Runs, and the Financial Accelerator*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释一个原本有限的损失，为什么会经借款人净值、抵押品价格、融资收缩、火售和集中提款变成全系统危机。
> **具体锚点：** 房价下跌使抵押品缩水，银行收紧贷款，企业和家庭被迫卖资产，进一步压低价格；即使一家银行长期资产最终足值，也可能因所有存款人同时取款而倒下。
> **核心难点：** 必须区分偿付能力损失、流动性短缺与协调失败，同时识别它们怎样相互转化，而不能把任何危机都归结为单一原因。
> **为什么重要：** 这决定最后贷款人、存款保险、资本重组和损失处置分别该在何时使用，也决定救助怎样避免制造更大的事前道德风险。
> **继续：** 先补金融契约的微观基础见 [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约]]；单家银行的期限与流动性约束见 [[商业银行资产负债管理]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains how an initially limited loss can become a system-wide crisis through borrower net worth, collateral prices, contracting finance, fire sales, and concentrated withdrawals.
> **Concrete anchor:** Falling house prices erode collateral, banks tighten credit, and households and firms are forced to sell assets, depressing prices further. Even a bank whose long-term assets will ultimately pay in full may fail if all depositors withdraw at once.
> **Central difficulty:** Solvency losses, liquidity shortages, and coordination failure must be distinguished while also tracing how one can turn into another; no single cause explains every crisis.
> **Why it matters:** The diagnosis determines when to use lender-of-last-resort support, deposit insurance, recapitalization, or loss resolution, and how to keep rescue policy from creating greater ex ante moral hazard.
> **Continue:** For the microeconomic foundation in financial contracts, see [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约|Asymmetric Information and Contracting in Financial Markets]]. For the maturity and liquidity constraints of an individual bank, see [[商业银行资产负债管理|Commercial Bank Asset–Liability Management]].

## 一条完整的危机放大链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A complete crisis-amplification chain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

危机不是“坏消息出现”这个单点事件，而是一组状态变量相互反馈。一个常见链条是：收入或资产价格受到冲击 → 借款人净值和抵押品价值下降 → 外部融资溢价上升、贷款条件收紧 → 投资与消费收缩、资产被迫出售 → 资产价格进一步下跌 → 银行损失和提款压力加重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A crisis is not the single event of bad news arriving; it is feedback among state variables. A common chain is: an income or asset-price shock → lower borrower net worth and collateral value → a higher external-finance premium and tighter lending conditions → weaker investment and consumption plus forced asset sales → further asset-price declines → larger bank losses and withdrawal pressure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把叙述变成分析，每一箭头都应说出约束：抵押贷款成数、资本要求、保证金、短期融资到期、存款可随时赎回，或投资者的风险限额。若找不到哪一个约束真正绑定，就还没有证明存在放大机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To turn the narrative into analysis, name the constraint behind every arrow: loan-to-value limits, capital requirements, margin calls, maturing short-term funding, demandable deposits, or investor risk limits. If no binding constraint can be identified, an amplification mechanism has not yet been established.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 金融加速器
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The financial accelerator*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产价格下降削弱净值与抵押品，提高外部融资溢价，迫使削减投资和出售资产，进一步压低价格。小冲击因此经资产负债表放大到实体经济。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Falling asset prices weaken net worth and collateral, raise the external-finance premium, and force cuts in investment and asset sales, which depress prices further. A small shock is therefore amplified through balance sheets into the real economy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“外部融资溢价”是企业从外部筹资相对于用内部资金的额外成本。净值较低时，企业自身承担的损失缓冲更小，贷方更担心隐藏行动、违约和回收不足，于是提高利差、追加抵押或减少额度。融资成本反过来压低投资与未来现金流，使最初冲击更持久。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The external-finance premium is the extra cost of raising funds externally relative to using internal funds. When net worth is low, the firm's own loss buffer is smaller, so lenders become more concerned about hidden action, default, and inadequate recovery; they raise spreads, demand more collateral, or cut credit limits. Financing costs then depress investment and future cash flow, making the initial shock more persistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 数值锚点：抵押约束怎样放大价格下跌
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical anchor: how a collateral constraint amplifies a price decline*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一家企业持有市值 150 的资产、债务 100，净值为 50。贷款人允许债务不超过抵押品价值的 80%。若资产价格下跌 20%，资产只值 120，可支持的债务降到 96；企业至少要偿还 4 或补充抵押。若它只能卖同类资产还款，销售又压低市场价格，其他受同样约束的企业也会被迫出售。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A firm owns assets worth 150 and owes 100, so its net worth is 50. Lenders allow debt of no more than 80% of collateral value. After a 20% asset-price fall, the assets are worth only 120 and support debt of 96; the firm must repay at least 4 or post more collateral. If it can repay only by selling the same type of asset, its sale depresses the market price and forces other constrained firms to sell as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 挤兑、流动性与偿付能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Runs, liquidity, and solvency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

短期可赎回负债支持流动性服务，却可能在协调失败时挤兑。流动性不足和资不抵债会互相转化：火售造成损失，损失又触发更多提款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Short-term redeemable liabilities provide liquidity services but can be vulnerable to runs when coordination fails. Illiquidity and insolvency can turn into one another: fire sales create losses, and those losses trigger more withdrawals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **流动性不足：** 资产长期能够偿付全部债务，但现在无法在不承受巨大折价的情况下变现。
- **资不抵债：** 即使等到资产到期，其可回收价值也低于负债。
- **协调性挤兑：** 每个存款人单独提款是理性的，因为预期别人先取；集体行动却迫使银行提前清算长期资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Illiquidity:** Assets can cover all debts in the long run, but cannot be converted into cash now without a severe discount.
- **Insolvency:** Even if assets are held to maturity, their recoverable value is below liabilities.
- **Coordination run:** With each depositor expecting others to withdraw first, individual withdrawal is rational even though the collective action forces premature liquidation of long-term assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Diamond–Dybvig 模型说明，活期存款既能让有早期消费需要的人取得流动性，又会因先到先得的偿付结构产生多个均衡，其中之一是自我实现的挤兑。它不是说所有历史挤兑都与基本面无关；现实中坏资产信息、流动性担忧与协调机制可以同时存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Diamond–Dybvig model shows that demand deposits can both provide liquidity to people with early consumption needs and, because of sequential service, generate multiple equilibria, one of which is a self-fulfilling run. It does not claim that every historical run is unrelated to fundamentals; information about bad assets, liquidity concerns, and coordination mechanisms can coexist in practice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 危机政策与道德风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Crisis policy and moral hazard*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存款保险、流动性支持和资本重组可阻止系统崩溃，但预期救助削弱事前纪律。监管、处置和损失承担规则应与安全网配套。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deposit insurance, liquidity support, and recapitalization can prevent systemic collapse, but expected rescue weakens ex ante discipline. Regulation, resolution, and loss-allocation rules should accompany the safety net.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政策工具应匹配诊断：短期现金缺口但资产足值时，抵押贷款或最后贷款人支持可争取时间；已资不抵债时，仅增加贷款会扩大最终损失，需要确认损失、减记资本或重组；协调性零售挤兑可由可信存款保险缓解；整个市场火售时，流动性工具还要兼顾抵押品估值和风险承担边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Policy tools should match the diagnosis. When assets are sound but cash is temporarily short, collateralized lending or lender-of-last-resort support can buy time. When the institution is already insolvent, more lending alone enlarges ultimate losses, so losses must be recognized and capital written down or restructured. Credible deposit insurance can mitigate a coordination-driven retail run. During a market-wide fire sale, liquidity tools must also address collateral valuation and the boundary of public risk-bearing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“救助债权人”和“救助股东”不是一回事。为了保持关键支付与信用功能，可以保护某些短期债权或转移良好资产，同时让原股东和管理层承担损失。只有把受益者、损失承担者和事前规则分开，才能严肃讨论道德风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rescuing creditors is not the same as rescuing shareholders. To preserve critical payment and credit functions, policy can protect certain short-term claims or transfer sound assets while imposing losses on original shareholders and management. Moral hazard can be discussed seriously only after separating beneficiaries, loss bearers, and ex ante rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断：三种常见误判
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis: three common misclassifications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把“账面有资产”当作有偿付能力，而不检查资产在正常持有和压力出售下分别值多少。
- 把央行提供流动性当作已经补足资本；流动性贷款改变现金时点，不自动消除资产损失。
- 把价格下跌后的同步出售都叫恐慌，而不检查保证金、资本或赎回约束是否机械触发了销售。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating the mere presence of booked assets as proof of solvency without checking their value under orderly holding and stressed sale.
- Treating central-bank liquidity as if it had replenished capital; a liquidity loan changes the timing of cash, not the underlying asset loss.
- Calling all synchronized selling after a price decline panic without checking whether margin, capital, or redemption constraints mechanically triggered sales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 金融加速器怎样把资产价格下跌传到实体投资？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How does the financial accelerator transmit an asset-price decline to real investment?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 抵押品和净值下降提高外部融资成本、收紧借贷，企业削减投资并可能火售，进一步压低价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Lower collateral values and net worth raise external financing costs and tighten credit; firms cut investment and may conduct fire sales, depressing prices further.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：一家银行为什么可能“长期有偿付能力、今天却没有流动性”？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: how can a bank be solvent in the long run but illiquid today?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它的长期贷款和证券到期价值足以覆盖负债，但当前提款必须立即支付；若这些长期资产只能以巨大折价出售，银行会在价值实现前耗尽现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Its long-term loans and securities may have enough maturity value to cover liabilities, while withdrawals must be paid immediately. If those assets can be sold only at a steep discount, the bank can run out of cash before their value is realized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么火售会把流动性问题变成偿付能力问题？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a fire sale turn a liquidity problem into a solvency problem?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 为取得现金而折价出售会实现损失、削弱资本；价格下跌还会压低剩余资产估值并触发其他机构的约束，最终使资产价值低于负债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Selling at a discount to obtain cash realizes losses and erodes capital. The price decline also marks down remaining assets and triggers constraints at other institutions, potentially pushing total asset value below liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 面对挤兑，怎样判断应优先提供流动性还是重组资本？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*During a run, how do you decide whether liquidity provision or capital restructuring should come first?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 先在合理的持有到期价值和压力估值下判断资产是否覆盖负债。资产足值而现金错配时以流动性工具为主；存在真实净资产缺口时必须确认损失并重组资本，流动性只能辅助处置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> First assess whether assets cover liabilities under reasonable hold-to-maturity and stressed valuations. If assets are sound but cash timing is mismatched, use liquidity tools; if there is a genuine negative-net-worth gap, recognize losses and restructure capital, with liquidity only supporting resolution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
