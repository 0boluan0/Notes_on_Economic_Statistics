# 个人效用的严格递增改标保留个人排序但固定加总公式未必保留社会排序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strictly increasing rescaling preserves personal utility rankings but an unchanged sum need not preserve social rankings*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[社会福利函数]]中的个人效用刻度与评价公式须配套指定。对每人的效用分别作严格递增变换，仍保留各自的个人偏好排序；但若改标后继续使用固定权重的加总公式，它表示的社会排序可能变化。个人排序不变，不等于继续表达了同一个规范标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify individual utility scales together with the formula of a [[社会福利函数|social welfare function]]. Applying a strictly increasing transformation separately to each person's utility preserves each personal preference ranking. If the same fixed-weight sum is retained after rescaling, however, its social ranking may change. Unchanged personal rankings do not establish that the same normative criterion is still being represented.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小自设例：两种方案的效用为 $A=(4,1)$ 与 $B=(2,4)$。原规则为等权和 $W(u_1,u_2)=u_1+u_2$，得到 $W(A)=5<6=W(B)$，所以选 $B$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Minimal constructed example: profiles are $A=(4,1)$ and $B=(2,4)$. The original rule is the equal-weight sum $W(u_1,u_2)=u_1+u_2$, giving $W(A)=5<6=W(B)$ and favouring $B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只把第一人的效用改标为 $\tilde u_1=2u_1$，并令 $\tilde u_2=u_2$。两人的变换都严格递增，各自的个人排序未变；方案的新效用向量为 $\tilde A=(8,1)$、$\tilde B=(4,4)$。若仍用 $\widehat W(\tilde u_1,\tilde u_2)=\tilde u_1+\tilde u_2$，便得到 $\widehat W(\tilde A)=9>8=\widehat W(\tilde B)$，改选方案 $A$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rescale the first person's utility to $\tilde u_1=2u_1$ and leave the second at $\tilde u_2=u_2$. Both transformations are strictly increasing, so both personal rankings are preserved. The new utility profiles are $\tilde A=(8,1)$ and $\tilde B=(4,4)$. Retaining the formula $\widehat W(\tilde u_1,\tilde u_2)=\tilde u_1+\tilde u_2$ nevertheless gives $\widehat W(\tilde A)=9>8=\widehat W(\tilde B)$, now favouring alternative $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要在新刻度中表示原来的社会判断，应相应改写为 $\widetilde W(\tilde u_1,\tilde u_2)=\tilde u_1/2+\tilde u_2$，于是两方案仍为 $5$ 与 $6$。这只是把同一评价改写到新坐标；保持原公式却让排名反转，则改变了评价规则与效用单位的配合，不能将其当作新的福利证据。单纯的个人严格递增改标仍保留 [[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto 支配比较]]，因为该比较只用每个人自己的排序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To represent the original social judgement in the new scales, rewrite it as $\widetilde W(\tilde u_1,\tilde u_2)=\tilde u_1/2+\tilde u_2$, restoring values 5 and 6. This expresses the same evaluation in new coordinates. Keeping the old formula while reversing the ranking changes how the rule interacts with utility units; it is not new welfare evidence. Strictly increasing personal rescaling still preserves [[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto dominance]], which uses only each person's own ranking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Ted Bergstrom, UCSB, *A Primitive Public Economy*, 印刷第 5 页／PDF 第 6 页](https://faculty.econ.ucsb.edu/~tedb/Courses/UCSBpf/pflectures/chap1.pdf#page=6)：序数效用允许保持偏好的单调重标，效用空间的形状可随刻度变化；本卡排序反转例为据此自设并验算。Pareto 比较的保持由严格递增变换保留不等号直接推出。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports preference-preserving rescaling of ordinal utility and its effect on utility-space geometry. The ranking-reversal example is constructed and checked directly. Preservation of Pareto comparisons follows because strictly increasing transformations preserve the relevant inequalities.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [MIT 14.42, *Social Choice*, PDF 第 21–24 页](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-42-environmental-policy-and-economics-spring-2011/43779e39d6fa6d8473fbd24c21ea8d6a_MIT14_42S11_lec02.pdf#page=21)：社会福利函数以个人效用为输入，效用测量、权重及社会价值是须说明的输入，不由个人偏好排序自动确定。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports individual utilities as inputs to welfare functions and the need to specify utility measurement, weights, and social values rather than infer them automatically from personal rankings.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
