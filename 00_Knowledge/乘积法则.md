# 两个可导函数的乘积满足 $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The product of two differentiable functions satisfies $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 若 $u$ 与 $v$ 在 $x$ 可导，则
> $$
> (uv)'(x)=u'(x)v(x)+u(x)v'(x).
> $$
> 乘积的一阶变化来自“$u$ 变而 $v$ 暂取原值”和“$v$ 变而 $u$ 暂取原值”两部分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $u$ and $v$ are differentiable at $x$, then $(uv)'(x)=u'(x)v(x)+u(x)v'(x)$. The first-order change of a product has one contribution from changing $u$ while retaining the old $v$, and another from changing $v$ while retaining the old $u$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在乘积差商的分子中加减同一项 $u(x+h)v(x)$：
$$
\frac{u(x+h)v(x+h)-u(x)v(x)}{h}
=\frac{u(x+h)-u(x)}h v(x)
+u(x+h)\frac{v(x+h)-v(x)}h.
$$
取极限时 $u(x+h)\to u(x)$，这里用到了 [[可导必连续]]。这也解释了为何公式不是 $u'v'$：同时改变两因子的乘积项属于二阶小量，不是唯一的一阶贡献。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add and subtract $u(x+h)v(x)$ in the numerator of the product difference quotient. The result separates into two ordinary difference quotients, and differentiability supplies $u(x+h)\to u(x)$. This also explains why the answer is not $u'v'$: the product of both increments is second order rather than the sole first-order contribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如
$$
\frac{d}{dx}(x^2\sin x)=2x\sin x+x^2\cos x.
$$
把三个因子展开会得到三项，每一项恰有一个因子被求导；一般 $n$ 阶版本见 [[高阶乘积法则]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $d(x^2\sin x)/dx=2x\sin x+x^2\cos x$. With three factors, the first derivative has three terms, each differentiating exactly one factor. The general higher-order version is recorded in [[高阶乘积法则|the higher-order product rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
