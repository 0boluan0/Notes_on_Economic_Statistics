# Product rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘积法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a product changes, either factor can contribute. A rectangle's area changes if its width changes, if its height changes, or if both change. The **product rule** calculates the local rate of that combined change from the [[导数|derivatives]] of the two factors. It applies to any two differentiable real-valued functions, even when an area picture is not appropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积的变化可能来自任一因子。矩形的宽改变、高改变，或者两者都改变，面积都会变化。**乘积法则**根据两个因子的[[导数]]，计算这个整体的局部变化率。它适用于任意两个可导的实值函数，不要求函数值都能解释为几何长度。

> [!example] One side grows while the other shrinks · 一边变长，另一边变短
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Near time $t=0$, let a rectangle have width $u(t)=3+2t$ metres and height $v(t)=4-t$ metres, with $t$ measured in seconds. Its width grows at $2$ m/s and its height shrinks at $1$ m/s. Expanding the area gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在时刻 $t=0$ 附近，设矩形的宽为 $u(t)=3+2t$ 米，高为 $v(t)=4-t$ 米，$t$ 以秒计。宽每秒增加 $2$ 米，高每秒减少 $1$ 米。展开面积得到
>
> $$A(t)=(3+2t)(4-t)=12+5t-2t^2,
> \qquad A'(0)=5\ \mathrm{m}^2/\mathrm{s}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The same rate is $2\cdot4+3\cdot(-1)=5$: widening contributes $8$ m²/s and shrinking contributes $-3$ m²/s. Multiplying the two side rates, $2\cdot(-1)$, would miss both contributions and even have the wrong units.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一变化率也可写成 $2\cdot4+3\cdot(-1)=5$：变宽贡献每秒 $8$ 平方米，变矮贡献每秒 $-3$ 平方米。直接相乘两个边长变化率 $2\cdot(-1)$，会漏掉这两部分，连单位也不对。

## Separate the two first-order changes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分开两个一阶变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an input $x$ and let it change by $h$. Write $\Delta u=u(x+h)-u(x)$ and $\Delta v=v(x+h)-v(x)$. Expanding the new product gives an exact identity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个输入 $x$，让它改变 $h$。记 $\Delta u=u(x+h)-u(x)$、$\Delta v=v(x+h)-v(x)$。把新乘积展开，得到精确恒等式：

$$(u+\Delta u)(v+\Delta v)-uv
=v\Delta u+u\Delta v+\Delta u\Delta v.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $u$ and $v$ without an argument mean their original values at $x$. Divide by $h$ and let $h\to0$. The first two terms tend to $vu'$ and $uv'$. The cross term vanishes after division: $(\Delta u/h)\Delta v\to u'\cdot0$, because $\Delta u/h$ has a finite limit and [[可导必连续|differentiability implies continuity]], so $\Delta v\to0$. This makes precise why the product of both small changes does not contribute to the derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里不带自变量的 $u$、$v$ 表示在 $x$ 处的原值。除以 $h$ 后令 $h\to0$，前两项分别趋于 $vu'$、$uv'$。交叉项除以 $h$ 后趋于零：$(\Delta u/h)\Delta v\to u'\cdot0$，因为 $\Delta u/h$ 有有限极限，而[[可导必连续]]保证 $\Delta v\to0$。这就严格解释了为什么两个小增量的乘积不贡献导数。

> [!abstract] Differentiate one factor in each term · 每一项只求导一个因子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $u$ and $v$ are differentiable at $x$, then their product is differentiable there and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $u$、$v$ 在 $x$ 可导，则它们的乘积在该处可导，且
>
> $$(uv)'(x)=u'(x)v(x)+u(x)v'(x).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All four values are evaluated at the same input. Neither factor needs to be nonzero. If $u$ is a constant, its derivative vanishes and this becomes the [[导数线性法则|constant-multiple rule]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 四个函数值都在同一个输入处求值。两个因子都不必非零。如果 $u$ 为常数，它的导数消失，就得到[[导数线性法则|常数倍法则]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The usual difference-quotient proof packages the same algebra by adding and subtracting $u(x+h)v(x)$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见的差商证明把同样的代数重排写成加减 $u(x+h)v(x)$：

$$\frac{u(x+h)v(x+h)-u(x)v(x)}h
=\frac{u(x+h)-u(x)}h v(x)
+u(x+h)\frac{v(x+h)-v(x)}h.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now $u(x+h)\to u(x)$, again by continuity, and the two difference quotients become the two derivatives. No division by either function value is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此时由连续性有 $u(x+h)\to u(x)$，两个差商则分别趋于两个导数。整个证明不需要除以任一函数值。

## Use it without expanding the functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不展开函数也能计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $u=x^2$ and $v=\sin x$, the [[整数幂求导|power rule]] gives $u'=2x$, and [[正弦余弦求导|the sine derivative in radians]] gives $v'=\cos x$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $u=x^2$、$v=\sin x$。[[整数幂求导]]给出 $u'=2x$，[[正弦余弦求导|弧度制下的正弦求导]]给出 $v'=\cos x$。于是

$$\frac d{dx}(x^2\sin x)=2x\sin x+x^2\cos x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three differentiable factors can be grouped as $u(vw)$. Applying the rule twice gives $(uvw)'=u'vw+uv'w+uvw'$. Each term differentiates one factor and keeps the others unchanged. Taking several derivatives of the same product is a separate extension: the [[高阶乘积法则|higher-order product rule]] counts how often each allocation of derivatives appears.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个可导因子可以先看成 $u(vw)$。两次使用规则，得到 $(uvw)'=u'vw+uv'w+uvw'$：每项对一个因子求导，其余因子保留。对同一个乘积连续求多阶导数则是另一种推广；[[高阶乘积法则]]会计算每种求导次数分配出现了多少次。
