# 影子银行是传统存款银行体系之外伴随类银行脆弱性的信用中介活动或链条
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Shadow banking is credit intermediation outside the traditional deposit-banking system that carries bank-like vulnerabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> “影子银行”没有唯一的永久机构清单。一个实用的狭义是：在传统存款银行体系之外进行信用中介，同时伴随期限或流动性转换、杠杆、短期融资、不完全信用风险转移或挤兑风险等类银行脆弱性的活动或机构链。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Shadow banking” has never denoted one permanent list of institutions. A useful narrow meaning is credit intermediation outside traditional deposit banks that involves bank-like vulnerabilities such as maturity or liquidity transformation, leverage, short-term funding, imperfect credit-risk transfer, or run risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

典型链条可以包括贷款发起人、证券化载体、用回购融资库存的交易商、发行可赎回份额的货币市场基金，以及显性或隐性流动性支持。类银行功能被分布在多个法律实体中，不等于这些风险已消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A typical chain can include loan originators, securitisation vehicles, dealers funding inventory with repos, money market funds issuing redeemable shares, and explicit or implicit liquidity support. Bank-like functions are distributed across legal entities rather than eliminated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 这个名称的使用边界

金融稳定理事会现在使用更宽的“非银行金融中介”（NBFI）来描述多样的非银行生态，再从中识别具有较高系统性风险或监管套利可能的狭义子集。因此阅读论文、数据或监管文件时，必须先看它把“影子银行”画到哪个范围；不能把这个词当成所有非银行机构的同义词。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Financial Stability Board now uses the broader term non-bank financial intermediation (NBFI) for the diverse nonbank ecosystem and then identifies a narrower subset with elevated systemic-risk or regulatory-arbitrage potential. Always check the perimeter used by a paper, dataset, or rule; “shadow banking” is not a synonym for every nonbank institution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一家养老基金用稳定长期负债持有公开交易债券。能否仅因它不是银行就称为影子银行？
>
> **答案：** 不能。还要检查它是否进行类银行信用中介，以及是否存在期限、流动性、杠杆、短期融资或不完全风险转移等具体脆弱性。
