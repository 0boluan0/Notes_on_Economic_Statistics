# 净金融负债是所划定金融负债减金融资产后的净额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net financial debt is financial liabilities less financial assets under the stated classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净金融负债（NFD）把融资性负债与经营以外的金融资产放在同一侧看净额：$NFD=FL-FA$。金融资产在这个分析中抵减净融资占用，并不因此消灭具体债权人的法律请求权。项目范围须先按[[管理用报表分类]]确定，不能用所有负债减所有现金作未经说明的替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NFD nets financing liabilities against financial assets outside operations: $NFD=FL-FA$. This reduces net financing exposure analytically; it does not extinguish individual creditors' legal claims. Establish the scope through [[管理用报表分类|functional classification]] rather than silently substituting all liabilities less all cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ABC 的金融负债为 $60+12+450+240=762$，金融资产为 0，因此 NFD 为 762。这里包括按教材划入金融侧的应付利息 12，而货币资金 44 留在经营侧。若另一个明确口径下金融负债 100、金融资产 130，则 NFD 为 −30，表示净金融资产头寸。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's financial liabilities total 762, including accrued interest of 12 classified on the financial side; financial assets are zero. Its cash of 44 remains operating. Under another stated classification, financial liabilities of 100 and assets of 130 give NFD of −30, a net financial asset position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净额不能替代流动性核查：金融资产可能受限、难以即时变现，期限和币种也可能与债务不同。用它计算[[净财务杠杆]]可以说明净融资结构，但到期付款仍要检查总额与[[偿债能力的报表外核查]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Netting does not replace liquidity analysis. Financial assets may be restricted or illiquid, with maturities and currencies unlike those of debt. [[净财务杠杆|Net financial leverage]] describes the net funding structure, while payment capacity still requires gross obligations and [[偿债能力的报表外核查|additional solvency checks]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
