# 奇异值分解与低秩近似
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Singular value decomposition and low-rank approximation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把任意矩阵分成输入正交方向、非负缩放和输出正交方向，从而读出秩、病态方向、伪逆与最佳低秩近似。
> **具体锚点：** 一张图像矩阵的奇异值若快速下降，保留前几个模式就能用很少参数近似原图。
> **核心难点：** SVD 不是普通特征分解；$V$ 描述输入方向，$U$ 描述输出方向，它们在非方阵时属于不同空间。
> **为什么重要：** PCA、数据压缩、潜在语义、最小二乘、伪逆和数值稳定性都以它为核心工具。
> **继续：** 先读“旋转输入 → 分方向缩放 → 旋转输出”，再用奇异值谱决定秩、压缩和稳定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** SVD decomposes any matrix into orthogonal input directions, nonnegative scale factors, and orthogonal output directions, revealing rank, instability, pseudoinverses, and optimal low-rank approximations.
> **Concrete anchor:** If an image matrix's singular values decay rapidly, a few leading modes can approximate it with far fewer parameters.
> **Central difficulty:** SVD is not an ordinary eigendecomposition. $V$ describes input directions and $U$ output directions, which belong to different spaces for a rectangular matrix.
> **Why it matters:** PCA, compression, latent semantics, least squares, pseudoinverses, and numerical stability use SVD centrally.
> **Continue with:** Read it as “rotate input → scale each direction → rotate output,” then use the singular spectrum to choose rank and assess stability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## SVD 的三层结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The three-layer structure of SVD*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任意 $m\times n$ 矩阵都有 $A=U\Sigma V^T$。V 的列给输入正交方向，A 把它们按奇异值缩放到 U 的对应输出方向。非零奇异值的个数是秩；其平方是 $A^TA$ 的非零特征值。SVD 不要求 A 方阵或可对角化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every $m\times n$ matrix has $A=U\Sigma V^T$. Columns of $V$ are orthogonal input directions; $A$ scales them by singular values into corresponding output directions in $U$. The number of nonzero singular values is the rank, and their squares are the nonzero eigenvalues of $A^TA$. SVD requires neither a square matrix nor diagonalisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 低秩近似与压缩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Low-rank approximation and compression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留最大的 k 个奇异值和对应向量，得到 Frobenius 范数和谱范数下的最佳秩 k 近似。压缩有效表示信号主要集中在少数模式；若奇异值下降缓慢，强行低秩会损失结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retaining the largest $k$ singular values and corresponding vectors gives the best rank-$k$ approximation in both Frobenius and spectral norms. Compression works when signal is concentrated in a few modes. If singular values decay slowly, aggressive truncation discards substantial structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件数与数值稳定性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Condition numbers and numerical stability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满秩问题的 2-范数条件数为 $\kappa_2(A)=\sigma_{max}/\sigma_{min}$。最小奇异值很小意味着某些输入方向几乎被压扁，数据或舍入误差会在求逆时放大。病态是问题本身的敏感性，不等同于算法写错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a full-rank problem, the 2-norm condition number is $\kappa_2(A)=\sigma_{max}/\sigma_{min}$. A very small minimum singular value means that some input direction is almost flattened, so data or rounding errors are amplified by inversion. Ill-conditioning is sensitivity of the problem itself, not necessarily an implementation error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：从奇异值判断可压缩性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: judging compressibility from singular values*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某矩阵的奇异值为 $(10,2,0.1)$，最佳秩 1 近似的 Frobenius 误差是 $\sqrt{2^2+0.1^2}\approx2.002$，最佳秩 2 近似的误差是 $0.1$。在 Frobenius 能量上，前两个模式保留了 $(10^2+2^2)/(10^2+2^2+0.1^2)>99.99\%$。但是否选秩 2 还取决于任务：对逆问题，$0.1$ 方向可能虽小却很关键。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a matrix has singular values $(10,2,0.1)$. The best rank-one approximation has Frobenius error $\sqrt{2^2+0.1^2}\approx2.002$, while the best rank-two approximation has error $0.1$. The first two modes retain more than $99.99\%$ of squared Frobenius energy. Whether rank two is appropriate still depends on the task: in an inverse problem, the small $0.1$ direction may be scientifically important despite its low energy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与失败模式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and failure modes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应同时报告奇异值谱、选定秩、近似误差与原任务上的验证。“解释方差高”不等于保留了与预测或因果目标最相关的方向。奇异向量的符号不唯一；在重复奇异值子空间中，基的旋转也不唯一，因此不应过度解读单个坐标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report the singular spectrum, selected rank, approximation error, and validation on the original task. High explained variance does not ensure retention of directions most relevant for prediction or causal interpretation. Singular-vector signs are arbitrary, and within a repeated-singular-value subspace the basis can rotate; individual coordinates should not be overinterpreted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### SVD 与对称矩阵特征分解的用途差别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How does SVD differ in use from the eigendecomposition of a symmetric matrix?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 特征分解描述方阵的不变方向；SVD 对任意矩阵描述输入方向、缩放和输出方向，并直接支持秩、条件数与低秩近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Eigendecomposition describes invariant directions of a square matrix. SVD applies to any matrix and separates input directions, scaling, and output directions while directly revealing rank, conditioning, and low-rank approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 小奇异值为什么会让最小二乘系数不稳定？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can small singular values make least-squares coefficients unstable?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 对应方向的信号几乎被 A 消除，反演时必须除以很小的数，因此很小的数据扰动会造成很大的系数变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> $A$ almost erases those input directions, so inversion divides by a very small number and magnifies small data perturbations into large coefficient changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么截断到秩 $k$ 能给 Frobenius 范数下的最佳近似？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does truncating to rank $k$ give the best Frobenius-norm approximation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> SVD 把矩阵分解成两两正交的秩一成分，每个成分的 Frobenius 能量为 $\sigma_i^2$；在只能保留 k 个时，丢掉最小的奇异值使剩余能量最小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> SVD decomposes the matrix into orthogonal rank-one components with squared Frobenius energies $\sigma_i^2$. Keeping the largest $k$ leaves the smallest possible discarded energy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明为什么高解释方差不保证好预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain why high explained variance does not guarantee good prediction.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> SVD 只按输入数据中的总变异排序，不看目标变量；预测信号可能处在方差小但与目标高度相关的方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> SVD ranks directions by variation in the inputs without using the target. Predictive signal may lie in a low-variance direction that is strongly related to the outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
