# The full-row-rank pseudoinverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*满行秩伪逆公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With more input coordinates than independent equations, many inputs can produce the same target. The **full-row-rank pseudoinverse formula** chooses the shortest exact input, measured in the usual Euclidean norm. Full row rank guarantees that every target is attainable; minimum norm resolves the remaining freedom in the inputs. These are two distinct parts of the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当输入坐标多于独立方程时，很多输入可以产生同一个目标。**满行秩伪逆公式**按通常欧氏范数选出最短的精确输入。满行秩保证每个目标都可达；最小范数再处理输入中剩余的自由度。这是结论中的两层含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One equation, many possible inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

> [!example] Choose the shortest point on the solution line / 在解直线上选最短点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\end{pmatrix}$ and let $b$ be a real scalar target. The equation is $x_1+2x_2=b$. One exact solution is $(b,0)^T$, but the [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|pseudoinverse]] chooses
>
> $$A^+=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> x_\star=A^+b=\begin{pmatrix}b/5\\2b/5\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> Every solution is $x=x_\star+t(-2,1)^T$ for a real number $t$. The added direction changes neither $x_1+2x_2$ nor the fit, and is perpendicular to $x_\star$. Therefore
>
> $$\|x\|_2^2=b^2/5+5t^2.$$
>
> The unique shortest solution has $t=0$. For $b=5$, it is $(1,2)^T$ of length $\sqrt5$, compared with length 5 for $(5,0)^T$. Here $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and $\|\cdot\|_2$ means [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $A=[1\ 2]$，实标量目标为 $b$，方程为 $x_1+2x_2=b$。$(b,0)^T$ 是一个精确解，但[[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|伪逆]]选出上面的 $x_\star=(b/5,2b/5)^T$。全部解可写成 $x=x_\star+t(-2,1)^T$，$t$ 为实数；新增方向既不改变拟合，又垂直于 $x_\star$，因此长度平方为 $b^2/5+5t^2$，仅在 $t=0$ 最小。$b=5$ 时，最短解 $(1,2)^T$ 长度为 $\sqrt5$，而 $(5,0)^T$ 长度为5。$T$ 是[[矩阵转置|转置]]，$\|\cdot\|_2$ 表示[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real or complex $m\times n$ matrix with $n\ge m\ge1$. **Full row rank** means its $m$ rows are [[线性无关|linearly independent]], so its [[矩阵秩|rank]] is $m$. Write $A^*$ for conjugate transpose, which is ordinary transpose for real entries. Then

$$\boxed{A^+=A^*(AA^*)^{-1}.}$$

The row [[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram matrix]] $AA^*$ has size $m\times m$ and is invertible under this assumption; the result $A^+$ has size $n\times m$. The superscript $+$ denotes the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse and $^{-1}$ the ordinary inverse. For a complex vector, squared length is the sum of squared absolute values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge m\ge1$。**满行秩**指 $m$ 个行[[线性无关]]，所以[[矩阵秩]]为 $m$。$A^*$ 表示共轭转置，实矩阵时就是普通转置。此时伪逆由上式给出。行 [[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram 矩阵]] $AA^*$ 为 $m\times m$，在上述假设下可逆；结果 $A^+$ 为 $n\times m$。上标 $+$ 表示 Moore–Penrose 伪逆，$^{-1}$ 表示普通逆；复向量的长度平方使用坐标绝对值的平方和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the chosen input is shortest
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么公式成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full row rank makes the columns of $A^*$ independent. For every nonzero $m$-coordinate vector $y$,

$$y^*AA^*y=\|A^*y\|_2^2>0.$$

Thus $AA^*$ is [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] [[正定矩阵|positive definite]] and invertible. Set $G=A^*(AA^*)^{-1}$. Then $AG=I_m$, so $G$ is a [[右逆|right inverse]] and $x_\star=Gb$ solves $Ax=b$ for every target $b$. The matrix $I_m$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]].

The shortest-solution claim needs one more fact. The vector $x_\star=A^*y$ for $y=(AA^*)^{-1}b$ lies in the column space $C(A^*)$. If $z$ is in the [[零空间|null space]] $N(A)$, meaning $Az=0$, then

$$z^*x_\star=z^*A^*y=(Az)^*y=0.$$

Every other solution differs from $x_\star$ by such a $z$. Consequently

$$\|x_\star+z\|_2^2=\|x_\star\|_2^2+\|z\|_2^2,$$

with equality to $\|x_\star\|_2^2$ only when $z=0$. This proves unique minimum norm, including over the complex numbers. For real matrices, $C(A^T)$ is the usual [[行空间|row space]] written with column-vector coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满行秩使 $A^*$ 的列独立，故每个非零的 $m$ 维向量 $y$ 都满足上面的严格正二次型。于是 $AA^*$ 是可逆的 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] [[正定矩阵]]。令 $G=A^*(AA^*)^{-1}$，有 $AG=I_m$，所以 $G$ 是[[右逆]]，对每个目标 $b$，$x_\star=Gb$ 都解 $Ax=b$；$I_m$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。

最短解还需要额外一步。取 $y=(AA^*)^{-1}b$，则 $x_\star=A^*y$ 属于列空间 $C(A^*)$。若 $z$ 属于[[零空间]] $N(A)$，即 $Az=0$，就有 $z^*x_\star=(Az)^*y=0$。每个其他解与 $x_\star$ 的差都是这样的 $z$；因此长度平方分成上面的两项，只有 $z=0$ 才不增加长度。这证明了最小范数解唯一，复数情形也成立。对实矩阵，$C(A^T)$ 就是把坐标排成列的通常[[行空间]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The other product $GA=A^*(AA^*)^{-1}A$ is the [[A+A行空间投影|orthogonal projector onto $C(A^*)$]]. It removes precisely the unused null-space component. Together with $AG=I_m$, this verifies all four Moore–Penrose conditions. A general right inverse supplies an exact solution, but need not remove that extra component; the opening solution $(b,0)^T$ illustrates the difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向乘积 $GA=A^*(AA^*)^{-1}A$ 是[[A+A行空间投影|到 $C(A^*)$ 的正交投影]]，正好去掉不影响输出的零空间分量；结合 $AG=I_m$，便验证了四个 Moore–Penrose 条件。一般右逆只需给出一个精确解，并不一定去掉额外分量；开头的 $(b,0)^T$ 就体现了区别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rank, dimensions and numerical calculation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A wide full-row-rank matrix has many exact solutions, but is not rank deficient under the rectangular convention; see [[欠定不等于秩亏|shape versus rank]]. For a square full-rank matrix, the formula reduces to the ordinary inverse. A genuinely tall matrix ($m>n$) cannot have full row rank; its full-rank counterpart uses the [[满列秩伪逆公式|full-column-rank formula]]. If rows are dependent, including a zero matrix with positive dimensions, $AA^*$ is singular. The displayed ordinary inverse then fails to exist, while the [[伪逆SVD公式|SVD pseudoinverse]] still exists.

Use conjugation for complex entries. For the row $A=[1\ i]$ with $i^2=-1$, $AA^*=2$ and $A^+=(1,-i)^T/2$. Indeed $AA^+=1$. Replacing $A^*$ with $A^T$ incorrectly gives $AA^T=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

宽且满行秩的矩阵有很多精确解，但按矩形矩阵口径并不秩亏，见[[欠定不等于秩亏|形状与秩的区别]]。满秩方阵时公式退化为普通逆；真正的高矩阵 $m>n$ 不可能满行秩，其对应满秩情形使用[[满列秩伪逆公式]]。若行相关，包括正尺寸的零矩阵，$AA^*$ 就奇异，上式中的普通逆不存在，但[[伪逆SVD公式|SVD 伪逆]]仍存在。

复数必须取共轭。例如 $A=[1\ i]$、$i^2=-1$ 时，$AA^*=2$，伪逆为 $(1,-i)^T/2$，确有 $AA^+=1$；误用普通转置则会得到 $AA^T=0$。

> [!tip] Compute through a factorization / 用分解实现求解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This closed form explains the geometry; it does not require explicitly forming an inverse in software. For full row rank, $\kappa_2(AA^*)=\kappa_2(A)^2$, where $\kappa_2(A)$ is the ratio of largest to smallest of the $m$ positive singular values. Thus forming the Gram matrix can worsen rounding sensitivity. A [[欠定不等于秩亏#算法边界|thin QR factorization of $A^*$]], an LQ factorization of $A$, or an SVD can compute the minimum-norm solution without forming $AA^*$. Accurate numerical calculation still cannot remove intrinsic sensitivity from small positive singular values.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 闭式解释几何，不要求软件显式求逆。满行秩时，$\kappa_2(AA^*)=\kappa_2(A)^2$，其中 $\kappa_2(A)$ 是 $m$ 个正奇异值中最大与最小者之比。因此形成 Gram 矩阵可能放大舍入影响。可用 [[欠定不等于秩亏#算法边界|$A^*$ 的薄 QR 分解]]、$A$ 的 LQ 分解或 SVD，不形成 $AA^*$ 就计算最小范数解；准确计算仍不能消除小正奇异值带来的内在敏感性。
