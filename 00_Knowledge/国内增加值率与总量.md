# 国内增加值率下降不等于出口创造的国内增加值总量下降

出口中的国内增加值率是国内增加值金额占出口总额的比例。只看比例，不能判断出口所含的国内增加值总量是否增减：进口投入占比上升时，出口规模也可能扩大。先按[[出口额与国内增加值]]的同一核算边界定义金额 $D$、出口额 $X>0$ 和比例 $s$，则 $D=sX$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The domestic value-added share of exports is domestic value added divided by gross exports. A change in the share alone does not determine the change in the amount: exports may expand while imported inputs account for a larger share. Using the same [[出口额与国内增加值|accounting boundary]], define domestic value added $D$, gross exports $X>0$, and share $s$; then $D=sX$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例：在一致价格和口径下，初期出口100、国内增加值率40%，国内增加值为40；后来出口200、比例30%，金额为60。比例下降10个百分点，金额却增加20，即50%。若后来出口只有120，同样30%的比例则对应36，金额反而下降。因此，比例变化的方向不够决定金额变化的方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At consistent prices and on a consistent basis, exports of 100 with a 40% domestic share contain 40 of domestic value added. Later exports of 200 with a 30% share contain 60: the share falls by 10 percentage points while the amount rises by 20, or 50%. If later exports were only 120, the same 30% share would imply 36 instead. The direction of the share change is therefore insufficient to determine the direction of the amount change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这只是核算关系，不证明降低本地含量会使出口增长，也不证明应尽量提高或降低这一比例。评价技术学习、居民所得或福利，分别需要生产与能力证据、[[国民总收入|收入归属]]和[[GDP与福利|福利口径]]；跨期金额若使用现价，还须区分价格变化与实际数量变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This accounting identity does not establish that reducing domestic content causes exports to grow, or that the share should be maximized or minimized. Assessing learning, resident income and welfare requires evidence on capabilities, [[国民总收入|income accrual]], and the [[GDP与福利|welfare boundary]], respectively. Comparisons at current prices must also distinguish price changes from real changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
