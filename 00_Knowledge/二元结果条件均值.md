# Conditional mean of a binary outcome
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二元结果的条件均值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an outcome records whether an event happens, code “yes” as $1$ and “no” as $0$. Averaging that outcome gives the probability of the event. A [[条件期望|conditional mean]] uses the probabilities appropriate to the information $X$, so the average of a zero-one outcome within a group is that group’s event probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一个结果记录某事件是否发生，把“是”编码为 $1$、“否”编码为 $0$，那么平均这个结果就得到事件概率。[[条件期望|条件均值]]使用给定信息 $X$ 下的概率，因此零一结果在某一组内的平均，就是该组的事件概率。

> [!example] A default indicator · 一个违约指标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Y=1$ if a borrower defaults during a specified period and $Y=0$ otherwise. Suppose the model gives a particular group default probability $0.2$. Its average indicator is $1\times0.2+0\times0.8=0.2$. An individual outcome is still either zero or one; the average is a probability, not a fractional occurrence for that individual.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $Y=1$ 表示借款人在指定期间违约，$Y=0$ 表示没有违约。若模型给某组的违约概率为 $0.2$，则该组指标平均为 $1\times0.2+0\times0.8=0.2$。个人结果仍只能是零或一；平均值是概率，不表示某个人发生了零点二次违约。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $p(X)=P(Y=1\mid X)$, a probability that may change with the information. Since $P(Y=0\mid X)=1-p(X)$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $p(X)=P(Y=1\mid X)$，它可以随信息变化。因为 $P(Y=0\mid X)=1-p(X)$，

$$
E[Y\mid X]=1\cdot p(X)+0\cdot[1-p(X)]=p(X)\qquad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the conditional version of the [[指示变量期望|indicator-expectation identity]]: $Y$ is the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] of the event $\{Y=1\}$. Because $0\le Y\le1$, integrability is automatic. For a discrete $X$, compute group probabilities only when $P(X=x)>0$. For continuous information, the equality is between conditional random variables; it does not require dividing by the probability of a single observed point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是[[指示变量期望]]的条件版本：$Y$ 就是事件 $\{Y=1\}$ 的[[事件指示变量]]。由于 $0\le Y\le1$，可积性自动成立。离散 $X$ 只在 $P(X=x)>0$ 时使用分组概率；连续信息下，等式比较的是两个条件随机变量，不需要除以某个观测单点的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## For zero-one outcomes, one probability determines the law
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零一结果的分布由一个概率确定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional on the information, $Y$ has a [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli distribution]] with parameter $p(X)$: its only probabilities are $p(X)$ at one and $1-p(X)$ at zero. Consequently, a constant conditional mean fixes the whole conditional distribution in this special case. An integrable general outcome can be [[均值独立不推独立|mean independent without being independent]] because it has more than these two fixed possible values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定信息后，$Y$ 服从参数为 $p(X)$ 的 [[Bernoulli分布]]：在一处的概率为 $p(X)$，在零处为 $1-p(X)$。因此，对零一结果，条件均值恒定就固定了整个条件分布。一般结果可以[[均值独立不推独立|均值独立但不独立]]，因为它并不受这两个固定可能值的限制。

## 为什么这一等式是建模入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why this identity starts binary-outcome modelling*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[线性概率模型|The linear probability model]] uses a linear function of chosen regressors to represent or approximate $p(X)$. [[Logit模型|Logit]] and [[Probit模型|probit]] instead apply a logistic or standard-normal [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]] to a linear index, keeping their finite-index model probabilities between zero and one. They use different shapes and coefficient scales to describe the same conditional-probability target; an arbitrary fitted curve is not guaranteed to equal the true $p(X)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[线性概率模型]]用选定回归量的线性函数表示或逼近 $p(X)$；[[Logit模型]]和[[Probit模型]]则把线性指数放入 logistic 或标准正态[[累积分布函数]]，让有限指数对应的模型概率落在零与一之间。不同函数形式和系数尺度描述的是同一个条件概率目标；任意拟合曲线并不保证恰好等于真实的 $p(X)$。

> [!warning] The outcome must be coded zero and one · 必须看结果的编码
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The identity concerns the outcome $Y$, not a binary explanatory variable. If an outcome takes values $a$ and $b$ instead, its conditional mean is $a+(b-a)P(Y=b\mid X)$, generally not a probability itself. For example, coding “no” as $1$ and “yes” as $2$ gives mean $1+p(X)$ rather than $p(X)$. The meaning of the event and its coding must be specified first.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等式针对的是结果 $Y$，不是某个二元解释变量。若结果编码为 $a,b$，条件均值是 $a+(b-a)P(Y=b\mid X)$，一般不再直接是概率。例如把“否”编码为 $1$、“是”编码为 $2$，平均就变为 $1+p(X)$，而不是 $p(X)$。首先要明确事件及其编码。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A binary regressor changes a prediction through a [[二元离散效应|full zero-to-one comparison]]; it does not make an arbitrary outcome’s mean a probability. Likewise, an observed group probability is not automatically an intervention effect: the [[条件均值因果边界|causal interpretation]] needs separate identifying assumptions. The amount of remaining zero-one variation is described by the [[二元结果条件方差|binary conditional variance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二元解释变量的作用要用[[二元离散效应|从零到一的完整比较]]衡量，它不会让任意结果的均值成为概率。同样，观察到的组别概率不自动是干预效应，[[条件均值因果边界|因果解释]]需要额外识别假设。零一结果还剩多少波动，由[[二元结果条件方差]]描述。
