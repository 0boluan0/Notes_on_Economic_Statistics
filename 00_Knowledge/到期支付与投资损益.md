# 到期支付描述合约在终点给付什么而投资损益还要计入建仓成本和期间现金流
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A payoff describes the terminal contractual payment; investment profit also includes entry cost and interim cash flows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到期支付（payoff）是在已给定终点状态后，合约分配给你的金额或经济价值。它还没有回答你为这项权利付了多少钱。例如，[[股债到期分配|股东到期分得 20]]不等于赚了 20；若买入这项股权花了 30，忽略其他现金流和利息，反而亏了 10。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A payoff is the contractual amount or economic value in a specified terminal state. It does not include what you paid for the right. [[股债到期分配|Receiving 20 as a shareholder]] is not a profit of 20: paying 30 to obtain that claim produces a loss of 10 before other flows or interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若单一合约期初成本为 $C_0$、到期支付为 $X_T$，期间无其他现金流，忽略利息时损益为 $X_T-C_0$。若明确把初始成本按实际融资或指定机会成本积累到 $T$，令积累因子为 $a(0,T)$，则**按该成本口径调整后的期末损益**为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For initial cost $C_0$, terminal payoff $X_T$, and no interim flows, profit ignoring interest is $X_T-C_0$. If the cost is carried to $T$ at an explicitly specified financing or opportunity-cost factor $a(0,T)$, terminal cost-adjusted profit is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\Pi_T=X_T-C_0a(0,T).$$

一份看涨期权到期支付 5，期初权利金 8：不计利息亏 3；若这笔 8 在整段期间的融资成本为 5%，则亏 $5-8(1.05)=-3.4$。这说明[[看涨期权|实值、有正支付]]仍可能亏钱；也说明不同评价时点和成本口径必须注明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A call paying 5 after an initial premium of 8 loses 3 before interest. If financing the 8 costs 5% over the full period, the loss is 3.4. A [[看涨期权|positive in-the-money payoff]] can therefore coexist with an investment loss, and cost conventions must be stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若途中有息票、股利、保证金结算、费用或追加资金，先列完整现金流并选择[[现金流评价时点|共同评价时点]]，不能只改一项终值公式。[[持有期收益率]]进一步把投资增益除以明确的投入基数；到期支付、金额损益、收益率又是三个不同量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coupons, distributions, margin settlements, fees, or additional funding require a complete dated cash-flow account and a [[现金流评价时点|common valuation date]]. [[持有期收益率|Holding-period return]] further divides the gain by a specified investment base. Payoff, monetary profit, and return rate are distinct quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
