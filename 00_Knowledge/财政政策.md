# 财政政策是政府通过税收、支出和转移等预算安排影响经济活动的政策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fiscal policy uses government taxation, spending, transfers, and related budget decisions to influence economic activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政政策改变政府购买、家庭可支配收入或企业税后回报，也可通过公共投资影响长期生产能力。它不同于中央银行的[[货币政策]]，不能都用“市场上的钱变多或变少”定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fiscal policy changes government purchases, household disposable income, or firms' after-tax returns and can affect productive capacity through public investment. It differs from central-bank [[货币政策|monetary policy]]; neither is adequately defined as simply more or less money in markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政作用既包括主动修改税支，也包括既有规则随经济变动而自动调整，例如失业人数上升导致符合条件的福利支出增加。政府购买计入支出法 GDP 的 $G$，转移支付则先改变收款人的预算，不能把两者重复计作产出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fiscal effects include discretionary changes and automatic responses under existing rules, such as higher benefit payments when eligible unemployment rises. Government purchases enter expenditure GDP as $G$; transfers first change recipients' budgets and are not themselves additional output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扩张性税支不保证任何规模的固定乘数，更不保证股价上涨。融资约束、进口、私人反应、货币条件及实施时间都会影响结果；证券应用须经过[[政策传导的估值渠道]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expansionary budgets guarantee neither a fixed multiplier nor higher stock prices. Financing, imports, private responses, monetary conditions, and implementation timing affect outcomes. Security valuation requires [[政策传导的估值渠道|explicit transmission channels]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
