# 多元正态分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The multivariate normal distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 为多个连续变量提供一个可计算的联合分布，并描述样本协方差在正态抽样下如何波动。
> **具体锚点：** 身高和体重可各自近似正态，但真正的多元模型还要说明椭圆方向和相关性。
> **核心难点：** 每个边际都正态不必推出联合正态；条件分布与独立性结论依赖协方差块结构。
> **为什么重要：** Hotelling $T^2$、MANOVA、判别分析和协方差推断都建立在这里。
> **继续：** 均值检验见 [[Hotelling T² 与多元均值推断|多元均值推断]][[MANOVA 多元方差分析|与 MANOVA]]；分类见 [[判别分析：Bayes、LDA 与 QDA|判别分析]][[聚类：层次聚类与 K-means|与聚类]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It provides a tractable joint distribution for several continuous variables and supports analysis of sampling variation in covariance under normal sampling.
> **Concrete anchor:** Height and weight may each be approximately normal, but a genuine multivariate model must also specify their elliptical orientation and correlation.
> **Central difficulty:** Normal marginal distributions do not imply joint normality. Conditional distributions and independence results depend on the block structure of the covariance matrix.
> **Why it matters:** Hotelling's $T^2$, MANOVA, discriminant analysis, and covariance inference are built on this distribution.
> **Continue with:** For mean tests, see [[Hotelling T² 与多元均值推断|multivariate mean inference]] and [[MANOVA 多元方差分析|MANOVA]]. For labelled classification, see [[判别分析：Bayes、LDA 与 QDA|discriminant analysis]]; for unlabelled grouping, see [[聚类：层次聚类与 K-means|clustering]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 密度与椭球几何
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Density and ellipsoidal geometry*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非奇异 $p$ 维正态写作 $N_p(\mu,\Sigma)$，密度等高线由 Mahalanobis 距离常数定义，是以 $\mu$ 为中心的椭球。$\Sigma$ 的特征向量给主轴，特征值给各轴方差。奇异协方差对应分布落在低维仿射子空间上，不能使用普通密度公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A non-singular $p$-dimensional normal distribution is written $N_p(\mu,\Sigma)$. Its density contours are ellipsoids centred at $\mu$ and defined by constant Mahalanobis distance. Eigenvectors of $\Sigma$ give the principal axes and eigenvalues the variances along them. A singular covariance places the distribution on a lower-dimensional affine subspace, so the ordinary density formula does not apply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差矩阵 $\Sigma$ 决定椭球 $\{x:(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)=c\}$ 的形状：椭球沿大特征值方向更长；相关性旋转椭球，边际方差改变轴长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The covariance matrix $\Sigma$ determines the shape of the ellipsoid $\{x:(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)=c\}$. The ellipsoid is longer along eigenvectors with larger eigenvalues. Correlation rotates it, while marginal variances change its axis lengths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 线性组合与刻画
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Linear combinations and characterisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多元正态最有用的刻画是：任意线性组合 $a^TX$ 都是一元正态。线性变换仍正态，$AX+b\sim N(A\mu+b,A\Sigma A^T)$。仅逐个检查边际正态无法验证这一联合性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The most useful characterisation of multivariate normality is that every linear combination $a^TX$ is univariate normal. Linear transformations remain normal: $AX+b\sim N(A\mu+b,A\Sigma A^T)$. Checking each marginal separately cannot establish this joint property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，这一刻画允许不写密度地处理奇异正态，也让均值向量、回归条件分布和二次型推导变得直接。若某个线性组合明显非正态，联合正态立即被否定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, this characterisation handles singular normal distributions without a density and makes derivations involving mean vectors, conditional regressions, and quadratic forms direct. A clearly non-normal linear combination immediately rules out joint normality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边际、条件与独立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marginal, conditional, and independent components*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分块正态的边际仍正态，条件分布的均值对已知块线性调整，条件协方差为 Schur complement。联合正态下零协方差等价于独立；离开正态族后这一等价一般失效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marginals of a partitioned multivariate normal remain normal. A conditional mean adjusts linearly for the observed block, and the conditional covariance is a Schur complement. Under joint normality, zero covariance is equivalent to independence; outside the normal family, that equivalence generally fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X=(X_1^T,X_2^T)^T$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X=(X_1^T,X_2^T)^T$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[X_1\mid X_2=x_2]=\mu_1+\Sigma_{12}\Sigma_{22}^{-1}(x_2-\mu_2),
$$

$$
Cov(X_1\mid X_2)=\Sigma_{11}-\Sigma_{12}\Sigma_{22}^{-1}\Sigma_{21}.
$$

条件协方差不依赖具体 $x_2$ 是 Gaussian 模型的特殊结构，不应无条件推广到一般联合分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fact that conditional covariance does not depend on the realised value $x_2$ is special to the Gaussian model and should not be generalised to arbitrary joint distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二次型与卡方
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Quadratic forms and chi-square distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X\sim N_p(\mu,Sigma)$ 且 $Sigma$ 可逆，则 $(X-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(X-\mu)\sim\chi_p^2$。这把椭球距离转为概率阈值，是置信区域和异常检测的基础。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X\sim N_p(\mu,Sigma)$ and $Sigma$ is invertible, then $(X-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(X-\mu)\sim\chi_p^2$. This converts ellipsoidal distance into a probability threshold and underlies confidence regions and anomaly detection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该结论通过白化得到：令 $Z=\Sigma^{-1/2}(X-\mu)$，则 $Z\sim N_p(0,I)$，二次型等于 $\sum_jZ_j^2$。若 $\mu$、$\Sigma$ 由同一数据估计，直接使用 $\chi^2$ 阈值忽略参数不确定性，尤其小样本会失真。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result follows by whitening. With $Z=\Sigma^{-1/2}(X-\mu)$, we have $Z\sim N_p(0,I)$ and the quadratic form becomes $\sum_jZ_j^2$. If $\mu$ and $\Sigma$ are estimated from the same data, a direct $\chi^2$ threshold ignores parameter uncertainty and can be distorted, particularly in small samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：二维条件分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: a bivariate conditional distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $(X,Y)$ 标准正态且相关系数为 $\rho=0.6$。则 $Y\mid X=x\sim N(0.6x,1-0.6^2)$。观察到 X=2 后，Y 的条件均值为 1.2，条件方差为 0.64；相关信息既移动中心也降低剩余不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $(X,Y)$ is standard bivariate normal with correlation $\rho=0.6$. Then $Y\mid X=x\sim N(0.6x,1-0.6^2)$. After observing $X=2$, the conditional mean of $Y$ is 1.2 and its conditional variance is 0.64. Correlated information shifts the centre and reduces remaining uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 正态性诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnosing multivariate normality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断结合单变量图、Mahalanobis 距离 Q–Q 图和异常点检查。大样本检验容易把微小偏离判显著，小样本又缺乏功效；应同时判断方法对偏离的敏感性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagnostics combine univariate plots, a Q–Q plot of Mahalanobis distances, and examination of outliers. Large-sample tests may flag negligible deviations, while small samples lack power. The sensitivity of the intended method to the observed departure must also be assessed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各边际看似正态仍需检查线性组合、椭圆结构和尾部。异常点既可能破坏正态诊断，也可能是错误记录或重要子群；不应为通过检验而自动删除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apparently normal marginals still require examination of linear combinations, ellipsoidal structure, and tails. Outliers may violate normal diagnostics, but they may also be data errors or important subgroups and should not be deleted automatically to pass a test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 各分量都服从正态，为什么仍可能不是多元正态？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can normally distributed components fail to be jointly multivariate normal?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 联合依赖结构可能使某些线性组合不正态；多元正态要求所有线性组合都正态。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The joint dependence structure may make some linear combination non-normal. Multivariate normality requires every linear combination to be normal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 联合正态中协方差为 0 为什么更强？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is zero covariance stronger under joint normality?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 正态联合分布完全由均值和协方差决定，零交叉协方差使联合密度分解，因此得到独立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A joint normal distribution is fully determined by its mean and covariance. Zero cross-covariance factorises the joint density and therefore implies independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用估计的均值和协方差做异常检测时，为什么不能无条件套 $\chi_p^2$ 阈值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a $\chi_p^2$ threshold not be used uncritically for anomaly detection when the mean and covariance are estimated?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 同一数据估计参数会改变距离的抽样分布，并可能被异常点反向拉动；应使用相应有限样本结果、稳健估计或独立基准样本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Estimating parameters from the same data changes the sampling distribution of the distances and lets outliers distort the reference centre and covariance. Use an appropriate finite-sample result, robust estimates, or an independent reference sample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
