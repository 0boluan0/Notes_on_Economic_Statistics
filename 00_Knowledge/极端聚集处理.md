# 处理极端聚集应先确定每日边际风险还是事件簇风险再选择依赖建模或去簇
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Handling extreme clusters starts by distinguishing daily marginal risk from event-cluster risk before choosing dependence modelling or declustering.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一损失序列中的高阈值超越可能连续或在短时间内成簇出现。此处关注时间上的极端聚集：多个大损失可能来自一个持续状态，不能仅因逐个观测不同就视为独立尾部证据。先明确要估计的是一个随机日的损失分布、给定当前信息的下一日风险，还是一次独立极端事件的最大损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
High-threshold exceedances in one loss series can occur consecutively or in short clusters. The issue here is temporal clustering: several large losses may arise from one persistent state and do not become independent tail evidence merely because they are distinct observations. First specify whether the target is a randomly selected day's loss, next-day risk conditional on current information, or the maximum loss of an independent extreme episode.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目标是条件每日风险，可先用[[波动过滤EVT]]解释动态并继续检查残差聚集。若目标仍是边际每日尾部，可保留每日观测，但估计不确定性要反映相应时间依赖；适用时采用依赖模型或与过程相容的区块重抽方案。若目标是事件簇的严重程度，可以明确去簇规则，提取每簇最大值，并另行估计簇出现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For conditional daily risk, [[波动过滤EVT|volatility-filtered EVT]] can model dynamics, followed by checks for residual clusters. For a marginal daily tail, retain daily observations while accounting for temporal dependence in uncertainty, using an appropriate dependence model or compatible block-resampling scheme when justified. For episode severity, specify a declustering rule, retain each cluster maximum, and estimate the cluster occurrence rate separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种 runs 去簇规则是：两个超越之间少于 $r$ 个连续非超越观测时，归入同一簇；至少 $r$ 个非超越观测才分开。$r$ 是需要说明和做敏感性检查的选择，改变它会改变簇数、簇峰值与估计目标。去簇后也不能不经检查就宣称有限样本的簇已经独立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One runs rule groups exceedances when fewer than $r$ consecutive non-exceedances separate them; at least $r$ non-exceedances separate clusters. State $r$ and assess its sensitivity: changing it changes cluster counts, maxima, and the resulting target. Declustering alone does not establish independence of finite-sample clusters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 1000 日中有 50 个超越，被分成 10 簇。每日经验超越比例仍为 $50/1000=5\%$；$10/1000$ 是每观测日的簇出现率，不能替换前者，再把簇峰值分布塞入原来的[[POT尾概率|每日尾概率公式]]。同样，把极端聚集消除的某个诊断当成通过，也不等于整个过程 iid；尾部之外仍可能有依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose 50 exceedances among 1,000 days form 10 clusters. The empirical daily exceedance proportion remains $50/1000=5\%$. The rate $10/1000$ counts clusters per observed day; it cannot replace the former while inserting cluster-maximum severity into the original [[POT尾概率|daily POT probability formula]]. Likewise, a diagnostic indicating no extreme clustering does not imply that the entire process is iid; dependence may remain elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Ferro–Segers（2003）《Inference for clusters of extreme values》，印刷第 545–547 页／PDF 第 1–3 页](https://mistis.inrialpes.fr/docs/EXTREMES/Ferro2003.pdf#page=1)：支持时间超越簇、runs 规则、去簇参数敏感性及渐近独立的条件性；已重开正文。每日比例与簇率的数字对照按各自计数定义独立核算。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Printed pp. 545–547 / PDF pp. 1–3 support temporal exceedance clusters, the runs rule, sensitivity to declustering parameters, and the conditional nature of asymptotic independence. The daily-proportion versus cluster-rate example is independently calculated from the respective count definitions.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [McNeil–Frey，作者版 PDF 第 6、9 页](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=9)：支持对原始序列与过滤残差分别检查依赖。区块重抽的机制复用[[历史模拟Bootstrap]]；本卡不把重抽次数解释成新增独立历史。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  These pages support separate dependence checks for raw observations and filtered residuals. [[历史模拟Bootstrap|Historical-simulation bootstrap]] supplies the block-resampling mechanism; resampling repetitions are not additional independent history.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
