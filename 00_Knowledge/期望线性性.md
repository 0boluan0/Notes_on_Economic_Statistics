# Linearity of expectation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*期望的线性性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Linearity of expectation** lets us find the mean of a total by adding the means of its parts. This can save us from finding the full distribution of the total. The parts may depend on each other: [[随机变量独立|independence]] is not required for this rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**期望的线性性**允许我们把各部分的均值相加，得到总量的均值。因此，即使还没有求出总量的完整分布，也可能已经能算出它的期望。各部分可以相互依赖；这条规则不要求[[随机变量独立|独立]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|expectation]] $E[X]$ is the probability-weighted average of $X$. For two variables with finite expectations, adding their values before averaging gives the same result as adding their averages:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望]] $E[X]$ 是 $X$ 按概率加权后的平均数。对于两个具有有限期望的变量，先把它们相加再平均，与先分别平均再相加，得到相同结果：

$$E[X+Y]=E[X]+E[Y].$$

> [!example] Copy the same die roll · 把同一个骰子结果抄两遍
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Roll one fair die and call its value $X$. Set $Y=X$, so the second value is a copy, completely determined by the first. Both means are $3.5$, and the total is $X+Y=2X$. Its expectation is $3.5+3.5=7$. The dependence is as strong as it can be, yet linearity still applies.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 掷一枚公平骰子，把点数记作 $X$，再令 $Y=X$：第二个数完全由第一个决定。两者均值都是 $3.5$，总量是 $X+Y=2X$，所以它的期望为 $3.5+3.5=7$。即使存在这种完全确定的依赖，线性性仍然成立。

## Fixed weights and shifts also pass through the average
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*固定权重和平移也能移到平均运算之外*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n$ be a finite number of variables $X_1,\ldots,X_n$. Each must be integrable, meaning $E[|X_i|]<\infty$. Let $a_i$ be a fixed multiplier for the $i$th variable, and let $b$ be a fixed amount added to the total. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一共有有限个变量 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$，每个都可积，即 $E[|X_i|]<\infty$。让 $a_i$ 是第 $i$ 个变量的固定乘数，$b$ 是额外加上的固定数，则

> [!abstract] Finite linearity · 有限线性性
> $$E\!\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_iX_i+b\right]=\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_iE[X_i]+b.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The summation sign means to include the terms $i=1$ through $i=n$. The coefficients and offset are constants; dependence among the variables is unrestricted. A constant remains itself when averaged, which explains the final $b$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 求和符号表示包括从 $i=1$ 到 $i=n$ 的各项。乘数和偏移量必须是常数，变量之间的依赖关系不受限制。常数平均后仍是它自身，所以最后留下 $b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the copied die, $E[3X-10]=3E[X]-10=0.5$. This does not assert that $3X-10$ actually takes the value $0.5$; it computes the centre of its distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对刚才的骰子，$E[3X-10]=3E[X]-10=0.5$。这仍不表示 $3X-10$ 真的能取到 $0.5$，而是在计算它的分布中心。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does dependence not enter? At each original outcome, ordinary algebra distributes the probability weight across the sum. Adding these weighted contributions preserves that equality. In a general model the same argument is the linearity of integration. At no step do we replace a joint probability by a product of marginal probabilities, which would require an independence condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么这里不需要独立？对每个原始结果，普通代数已经允许把概率权重分配到相加的各项上；再把这些加权贡献累积起来，等式仍然成立。在一般模型中，这就是积分的线性性。整个过程没有把联合概率改成边际概率的乘积，因此不需要那类独立性条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Counting events without their joint distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不用先求联合分布，也能计算事件个数的期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[事件指示变量|indicator variable]] is $1$ when its event happens and $0$ otherwise, so its [[指示变量期望|expectation equals that event's probability]]. If $I_i$ indicates whether person $i$ is late, the count $N=I_1+\cdots+I_n$ records how many people are late. Therefore $E[N]$ is the sum of their individual lateness probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事件指示变量]]在事件发生时为 $1$，否则为 $0$，所以它的[[指示变量期望|期望等于事件发生的概率]]。若 $I_i$ 表示第 $i$ 个人是否迟到，$N=I_1+\cdots+I_n$ 就是迟到人数。因此，$E[N]$ 等于每个人迟到概率之和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For ten people, each with lateness probability $0.2$, the expected count is $10\times0.2=2$. They may share a delayed bus, so the indicators can be dependent. The expected count still follows from linearity; a claim about a [[Bernoulli和的二项分布|binomial distribution]] for the whole count would need additional assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，十个人各有 $0.2$ 的迟到概率，迟到人数的期望就是 $10\times0.2=2$。他们可能乘坐同一辆晚点的公交车，因此指示变量之间可能有依赖；但期望人数仍由线性性给出。若进一步声称整个人数服从[[Bernoulli和的二项分布|二项分布]]，就需要额外假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the rule does not automatically extend to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*使用范围要随运算一起检查*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linearity concerns sums and multiplication by constants. It does not generally let a product, a square, or another nonlinear function pass through $E[\cdot]$. For example, $E[X^2]$ and $(E[X])^2$ are different quantities; see [[期望与非线性变换不可交换|expectation and nonlinear transformations]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性性适用于相加和乘以常数，一般不能让乘积、平方或其他非线性函数穿过 $E[\cdot]$。例如，$E[X^2]$ 与 $(E[X])^2$ 是不同的量，见[[期望与非线性变换不可交换]]。

> [!note]- Infinite sums need an interchange argument · 无穷求和需要额外的交换条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The finite rule does not by itself justify moving an infinite sum through expectation. One sufficient condition is that the total of the average magnitudes is finite: $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}E[|X_i|]<\infty$. Under that condition, $E[\sum_iX_i]=\sum_iE[X_i]$. For nonnegative terms, [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence]] also allows the exchange, possibly with value $+\infty$. These are extensions with their own conditions, not a consequence of ignoring the word “finite”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 有限规则本身不足以保证能交换无穷求和与期望。一个充分条件是各项平均大小的总和有限，即 $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}E[|X_i|]<\infty$；此时可以写 $E[\sum_iX_i]=\sum_iE[X_i]$。对于非负项，[[单调收敛定理]]也允许交换，结果可以为 $+\infty$。这些扩展各有条件，不能把有限公式里的“有限”直接略掉。
