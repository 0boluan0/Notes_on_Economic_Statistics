# Continuing a chain after explosion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*爆炸后延拓*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A jump-rate rule says what happens next while the process occupies an ordinary state. If infinitely many jumps finish in finite time, that rule does not automatically say what happens afterwards. A **post-explosion extension** supplies that extra boundary behaviour. Different valid choices can have the same local rates and initial state but different probabilities at later times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跳转速率规则告诉我们：过程还处于普通状态时，下一步怎样发生。如果无限次跳跃在有限时间内完成，这条规则不会自动告诉我们之后怎么办。**爆炸后延拓**补充这部分边界行为。不同的合法选择可以具有相同局部速率和初始状态，却给出不同的后续概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here a [[生成矩阵约束|stable conservative generator]] $Q$ has finite exit rate at every state and zero row sums on a countable state set $S$. The [[CTMC爆炸|explosion time]] $\zeta$ is the accumulation time of genuine jumps. A [[最小CTMC|minimal process]] sends the path to an absorbing cemetery label at $\zeta$. That is one complete convention. A non-minimal extension can instead allow re-entry into $S$, but it must define a consistent process, not just assign arbitrary values after each explosion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[生成矩阵约束|稳定、保守生成矩阵]] $Q$ 定义在可数状态集 $S$ 上，各状态出口率有限、每行和为零。[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸时刻]] $\zeta$ 是真实跳时的聚积时刻。[[最小CTMC|最小过程]]在 $\zeta$ 把路径送往吸收的墓地标签，这是一种完整约定。非最小延拓可以允许重新进入 $S$，但必须定义出一致的过程，不能只是任意填写爆炸以后的取值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two continuations of the same birth mechanism
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个纯生机制的两种继续方式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use states $S=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and start at $0$. From $n$, wait an independent [[指数分布|exponential time]] of rate $2^n$, then move to $n+1$. In row notation the nonzero generator entries are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取状态 $S=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$，从 $0$ 出发。在 $n$ 独立等待速率为 $2^n$ 的[[指数分布|指数时间]]，再前往 $n+1$。采用行约定，生成矩阵的非零元素为

$$q_{n,n+1}=2^n,\qquad q_{nn}=-2^n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The successive mean waits are $1,1/2,1/4,\ldots$. Their sum is $2$, so the nonnegative total lifetime is finite almost surely, as proved in the [[CTMC爆炸|explosion example]]. Up to that first explosion, consider exactly the same path under both choices below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各段平均等待为 $1,1/2,1/4,\ldots$，总和为 $2$，因此非负总寿命几乎必然有限，论证见[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸例子]]。在首次爆炸之前，让下面两种选择使用完全相同的路径。

> [!example] Kill the run or restart it independently · 终止，或独立重启
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Minimal choice:** at the first explosion, enter $\Delta\notin S$ and stay there forever.
>
> **Restart choice:** at each explosion, set the state to $0$ and start a fresh independent copy of the same birth mechanism. Repeat this rule after every subsequent explosion. Each cycle uses new exponential clocks; it does not reuse the clocks or hidden history of the preceding cycle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **最小选择：** 首次爆炸时进入 $\Delta\notin S$，以后永远停在那里。
>
> **重启选择：** 每次爆炸时把状态设为 $0$，开始一份全新、独立的同样纯生过程；后续每次爆炸都重复此规则。每轮使用新的指数时钟，不沿用上一轮的时钟或隐藏历史。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This particular restart is well-defined for all finite times. Every cycle contains an independent rate-one wait at $0$, with mean $1$. By the [[强大数定律|strong law]], the sum of those waits over infinitely many cycles is infinite almost surely; hence infinitely many whole cycles cannot themselves accumulate in finite time. Within each cycle, however, infinitely many genuine jumps still occur before its endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一特定重启规则在每个有限时刻都有定义。每轮都包含在 $0$ 处的一段独立、速率为一的等待，均值为 $1$。由[[强大数定律]]，无限多轮的这些等待之和几乎必然为无穷，所以无限多整个轮次不会再于有限时刻聚积。但每一轮内部，仍会在该轮终点之前完成无限次真实跳跃。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The restart process is time homogeneous and Markov: from a currently observed finite state, [[指数分布无记忆性|exponential memorylessness]] gives a fresh remaining holding time, and both subsequent clocks and future restart cycles use independent randomness. Thus the past cycle number is not needed to predict the future. This verifies the specified independent restart; it is not a guarantee for every imaginable return rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重启过程时间齐次且具有 Markov 性：观察到当前有限状态后，[[指数分布无记忆性|指数分布的无记忆性]]使剩余停留时间具有全新的等待分布；其后的时钟与未来重启轮次都使用独立随机性。因此预测未来无需额外知道过去已经经历几轮。这个论证核验的是指定的独立重启，不能保证所有想得到的返回规则都成立。

## Why the local generator stays the same
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么局部生成率仍然相同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an ordinary starting state $i$. To explode by a very small time $h$, the process must at least finish the first two independent waits, of rates $2^i$ and $2^{i+1}$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个普通出发状态 $i$。要在很短的时长 $h$ 内爆炸，至少必须完成前两段独立等待，其速率分别为 $2^i$ 和 $2^{i+1}$。所以

$$\Pr_i(\zeta\le h)
\le(1-e^{-2^i h})(1-e^{-2^{i+1}h})
\le 2^{2i+1}h^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last inequality uses $1-e^{-a h}\le a h$ for $a,h\ge0$. The minimal and restarting paths agree until explosion, so their probabilities of any specified endpoint differ by at most this bound. Divide by $h$ and let $h\downarrow0$: the difference tends to zero. Their [[生成矩阵约束|right derivatives at time zero]], and hence their entries $q_{ij}$ on $S$, are the same. The bound is for each fixed $i$; no uniform rate bound over all states was assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个不等式用了 $a,h\ge0$ 时的 $1-e^{-ah}\le ah$。最小路径与重启路径在爆炸之前一致，所以它们到达任一指定终点的概率之差不超过这个上界。除以 $h$ 再令 $h\downarrow0$，差异趋于零，因此两者在[[生成矩阵约束|时长零处的右导数]]相同，也就是原状态上各项 $q_{ij}$ 相同。这个界针对每个固定 $i$，没有假设所有状态上的速率一致有界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their later laws still differ. The minimal kernel has row sum $\Pr_i(t<\zeta)$ on $S$, while the restart process stays somewhere in $S$ and has row sum one. Whenever explosion by $t$ has positive probability, these transition laws cannot agree. With $\widetilde X$ denoting the restart process and $p_{ij}(t)$ the minimal probability, the common pre-explosion construction gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但两者之后的概率律仍然不同。最小核在 $S$ 上的行和为 $\Pr_i(t<\zeta)$，重启过程则始终处于 $S$ 中某个状态，行和为一。只要截至 $t$ 爆炸的概率为正，两种转移律就不可能相同。记 $\widetilde X$ 为重启过程，$p_{ij}(t)$ 为最小概率，同一份爆炸前构造给出

$$\Pr_i(\widetilde X_t=j)
=p_{ij}(t)+\Pr_i(\widetilde X_t=j,\ \zeta\le t),\qquad i,j\in S.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extra term counts returns after the **first** explosion. It may include several later cycles. This explains how the same initial state and the same local Q-matrix can lead to different transition probabilities and [[有限维分布|finite-dimensional distributions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

额外项计入**首次**爆炸后的返回，也可能包含后续多轮。这解释了为什么同一个初始状态、同一个局部 Q 矩阵，可以产生不同转移概率和[[有限维分布]]。

> [!warning] Check the whole extension · 核验整个延拓
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A proposed return mechanism needs a state/entrance rule, a timing rule and a consistent probability law. If it retains hidden history that affects the future, the displayed state alone may no longer have the [[Markov性|Markov property]]; [[状态扩充|enlarging the state]] may be necessary. A deterministic delay before re-entry can likewise require recording elapsed delay. Preserving local rates alone does not establish all Markov, semigroup or differential-equation properties.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 提出的返回机制需要说明进入哪个状态、何时进入，以及怎样形成一致概率律。如果保留了会影响未来的隐藏历史，仅显示当前状态就可能不再具有[[Markov性]]，有时需要[[状态扩充]]。若重入之前设置确定延迟，也可能必须记录已等待的年龄。只保留局部速率，不能保证全部 Markov 性、半群或微分方程性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The restart example is right-continuous at each reset, but it has no left limit there in the original discrete space: the preceding states run off through $0,1,2,\ldots$. It is a non-minimal continuation, not a claim that the entire path is a nonexplosive jump path with finitely many changes on every bounded interval. If the original minimal construction is nonexplosive from the chosen initial law, no finite observation reaches this boundary, and post-explosion choices cannot change its finite-time laws.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重启例在每个重置时刻右连续，但在原离散空间中没有该处的左极限，因为之前的状态不断走过 $0,1,2,\ldots$。它是非最小延拓，并不是说整条路径已经变成“每个有界区间内只有有限次变化”的非爆炸跳跃路径。如果原最小构造在所选初始分布下非爆炸，任何有限观测都不会到达这个边界，爆炸后选择也就不能改变其有限时间概率律。

## 来源与核验

- [Norris, Markov Chains, §2.9, Example 2.9.1, printed p.91 / preview PDF p.9](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~jrn10/Markov/s28.pdf#page=9): the $2^n$ birth process restarted at zero after each explosion, its Markov transition law, and the need to specify re-entry in addition to the initial law and $Q$. The cycle-clock and small-time bounds above spell out why this particular construction is legitimate and preserves the local rates.

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Norris 第2.9节例2.9.1，印刷第91页／预览PDF第9页：核对 $2^n$ 纯生过程每次爆炸后重启于零、相应 Markov 转移律，以及初始分布与 $Q$ 之外还须指定重入规则。正文展开轮次时钟与短时概率界，说明这一特定构造为何合法且保留局部速率。
- Cambridge 第1.8节例1.30，PDF第19–20页：核对独立重启构造，以及“爆炸前概率加返回路径”的分解。
