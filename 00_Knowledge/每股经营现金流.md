# 每股经营现金流是期间经营活动现金流量净额按明确普通股数口径折算的每股金额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating cash flow per share scales period net operating cash flow by a stated ordinary-share denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每股经营现金流是分析中把经营活动现金流量净额 $CFO$ 按普通股数 $N_*>0$ 标准化的指标：$CFPS=CFO/N_*$。必须声明采用期末股数还是期间加权股数，并说明报表主体与现金流分类；它没有[[基本每股收益]]那样可直接照搬的一套统一每股损益规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating cash flow per share is an analytical scaling measure, $CFPS=CFO/N_*$ with $N_*>0$. State whether shares are measured at period-end or weighted over the period, as well as the reporting entity and cash-flow classification. The accounting rules for [[基本每股收益|basic EPS]] cannot simply be transferred to this measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如公司只有一类普通股，全年股数不变为 100 万股，CFO 为 300 万元，则为 3 元／股。若期内增发，采用期末数和加权数可能得不同结果，比较时先做[[财务比率口径匹配]]。合并 CFO 也并非已分配给母公司普通股股东的现金，不能把这个缩放数当股东账户的收款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With one ordinary share class, 1 million shares unchanged throughout the year, and CFO of 3 million, the measure is 3 per share. New issues may make end-period and weighted denominators differ; first apply [[财务比率口径匹配|ratio-basis matching]]. Consolidated CFO is not cash already allocated to parent ordinary shareholders, so this scaling measure is not their cash receipt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本页定义分析用法，不表示在所有市场都可作为公司披露指标使用；例如美国 SEC 对每股流动性指标的披露有专门限制。它也不等于自由现金流每股或最大股利，见[[每股经营现金流不限定股利]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This defines an analytical use, not universal permission for issuer disclosure; the US SEC, for example, specifically restricts per-share liquidity measures. It is neither free cash flow per share nor a maximum dividend; see [[每股经营现金流不限定股利|the dividend boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
