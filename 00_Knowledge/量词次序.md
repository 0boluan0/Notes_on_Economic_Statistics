# Quantifier order and dependence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*量词次序与依赖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Every student has an adviser” allows different students to have different advisers. “One adviser advises every student” asks for someone who works for the whole group. The difference is not the names of the people: it is **whether the choice may depend on the student already under consideration**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“每名学生都有一位导师”允许不同学生有不同导师；“有一位导师指导所有学生”则要求找出一位对整个群体都适用的人。差别不在于人名，而在于**选择能否依赖已经给定的学生**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[命题与谓词|predicate]] is a condition on inputs. Write $P(x,y)$ for a condition on $x$ and $y$, with their meanings fixed. Here $\forall$ means “for every,” $\exists$ means “there exists at least one,” and a **witness** is a permitted value that makes an existential claim true. Initially take fixed nonempty domains $D$ for $x$ and $E$ for $y$; they need not be the same set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[命题与谓词|谓词]]是关于输入的条件。用 $P(x,y)$ 表示关于 $x,y$ 的条件，并固定符号含义。$\forall$ 表示“对每个”，$\exists$ 表示“至少存在一个”；**见证**是能使存在断言成立的允许取值。先约定 $x$ 来自固定非空论域 $D$，$y$ 来自固定非空论域 $E$；两者不必是同一个集合。

> [!summary] A separate choice or one shared choice · 分别选择，还是共同选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\forall x\exists y\,P(x,y)$, the choice of $y$ may depend on $x$. In $\exists y\forall x\,P(x,y)$, one fixed $y$ must work for every $x$. Exchanging universal and existential quantifiers therefore usually changes the claim.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $\forall x\exists y\,P(x,y)$ 允许 $y$ 随 $x$ 改变；$\exists y\forall x\,P(x,y)$ 要求一个固定的 $y$ 同时适用于所有 $x$。把全称量词和存在量词交换，通常会改变命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the outer choice first
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先读外层选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\forall x\in D\,\exists y\in E\,P(x,y)$, take any allowed $x$ first. For that $x$, at least one suitable $y$ must exist. A new $x$ may use a different $y$. In $\exists y\in E\,\forall x\in D\,P(x,y)$, first find one $y$; after it has been fixed, it must satisfy the condition for every allowed $x$. This reading describes logical dependence, not a claim that a fast algorithm for finding the witness is available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\forall x\in D\,\exists y\in E\,P(x,y)$ 中，先给定任意允许的 $x$；对这个 $x$，必须至少存在一个合适的 $y$。换一个 $x$，可以另选 $y$。在 $\exists y\in E\,\forall x\in D\,P(x,y)$ 中，先找出一个 $y$；固定它之后，条件必须对所有允许的 $x$ 成立。这种读法说明逻辑依赖，不是在断言存在快速求出见证的算法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word **may** matters. The first formula permits dependence; it does not require different inputs to receive different witnesses. If one adviser really advises everyone, both statements hold. What fails is the general inference from separate choices to a shared choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的**允许**很重要。第一式允许依赖，却不要求不同输入必须使用不同见证。如果确实有一位导师指导所有学生，两种陈述都成立。不能成立的是从“可以分别选择”一般地推出“能共同选择”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two small models make the difference visible
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用两个小模型看清差别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over the integers, $\forall x\exists y:y>x$ is true: after receiving $x$, choose $y=x+1$. But $\exists y\forall x:y>x$ is false because no fixed integer exceeds every integer. The predicate is identical; the difference lies entirely in the order of choices and permitted dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在整数论域中，$\forall x\exists y:y>x$ 为真：给定 $x$ 后可取 $y=x+1$。而 $\exists y\forall x:y>x$ 为假，因为不存在大于所有整数的固定整数。两式使用相同谓词，差异完全来自选择顺序与依赖关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the failure explicitly, suppose someone proposes the fixed integer $y_0$. The universal requirement includes $x=y_0$, but $y_0>y_0$ is false. Testing a few smaller integers would not answer this requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要明确看出第二式为什么失败，假设有人提出固定整数 $y_0$。全称要求也包含 $x=y_0$，但 $y_0>y_0$ 为假。只检验几个比它小的整数，不能满足这个要求。

> [!example] Two students and two advisers · 两名学生与两位导师
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the students be Ada and Ben and the advisers be Lee and Noor. Suppose Lee advises only Ada, and Noor advises only Ben. Each student has a witness, but neither adviser works for both students. If Lee also advises Ben, then Lee is a shared witness and both quantified statements become true.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 设学生是 Ada、Ben，导师是 Lee、Noor。若 Lee 只指导 Ada，Noor 只指导 Ben，每名学生都有见证，但没有哪位导师同时指导两人。如果 Lee 也指导 Ben，Lee 就成为共同见证，两种量化陈述都为真。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which direction of implication is valid?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪个方向可以推出？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A shared witness always supplies the separate witnesses. Thus, with the stated fixed domains,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同见证总能充当每次分别选择的见证。因此，在上述固定论域下，

$$
\bigl(\exists y\in E\,\forall x\in D\,P(x,y)\bigr)
\ \Rightarrow\
\bigl(\forall x\in D\,\exists y\in E\,P(x,y)\bigr).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, if $y_0$ works for every $x$, take any $x$ and reuse $y_0$. It belongs to $E$ and satisfies $P(x,y_0)$, exactly what the inner existential statement asks for. The integer example disproves the reverse implication. “Not equivalent in general” does not mean the two formulas must have different truth values in every model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $y_0$ 对所有 $x$ 都适用，就任取一个 $x$ 并继续使用 $y_0$。它属于 $E$，也满足 $P(x,y_0)$，这正是内层存在断言要求的内容。整数例子反驳了反方向的蕴含。“一般不等价”并不是说两式在每个模型中都必须有不同真值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Scope determines the permitted dependence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*作用域决定允许怎样依赖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quantifier governs its **scope**, the part of the formula to which it applies. Parentheses make that scope explicit. In $\forall x\in D\,(\exists y\in E\,P(x,y))$, the inner condition is still about the currently given $x$. In $\exists y\in E\,(\forall x\in D\,P(x,y))$, the same $y$ remains in force for all those $x$. Variable names alone do not record this difference; the nesting does.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量词约束自己的**作用域**，即公式中受它作用的部分；括号可以把这个范围写清楚。在 $\forall x\in D\,(\exists y\in E\,P(x,y))$ 中，内层条件仍然讨论当前给定的 $x$。在 $\exists y\in E\,(\forall x\in D\,P(x,y))$ 中，同一个 $y$ 对所有这些 $x$ 都保持固定。单看变量名称不能辨认这种差别，嵌套结构才说明依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a longer prefix $\forall x\,\exists y\,\forall z\,R(x,y,z)$, the chosen $y$ may depend on the preceding $x$, but it must work for every later $z$ without being changed to suit that $z$. Replacing this with $\forall x\,\forall z\,\exists y\,R(x,y,z)$ gives the witness more freedom. For example, over the integers with $R(x,y,z)$ meaning $y>z$, the first statement is false, while the second is true using $y=z+1$. The unused input $x$ does not change those truth values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在更长的前缀 $\forall x\,\exists y\,\forall z\,R(x,y,z)$ 中，所选 $y$ 可以依赖之前给定的 $x$，但必须对后面每个 $z$ 都适用，不能再随 $z$ 修改。换成 $\forall x\,\forall z\,\exists y\,R(x,y,z)$，见证就有了更多选择自由。例如在整数上令 $R(x,y,z)$ 表示 $y>z$，第一式为假，第二式则可取 $y=z+1$ 而为真；没有实际使用的输入 $x$ 不影响这两个真值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Adjacent quantifiers of the same kind may be exchanged: $\forall x\forall y\,P(x,y)$ is equivalent to $\forall y\forall x\,P(x,y)$. The main danger is swapping different kinds of quantifier.
- In prose, say whether a witness may depend on a previously given object; otherwise “everyone has a choice” may be misread as “there is one shared choice for everyone.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 相邻的同类量词可以交换，例如 $\forall x\forall y\,P(x,y)$ 与 $\forall y\forall x\,P(x,y)$ 等价；风险主要来自交换不同种类的量词。
- 写自然语言时应说明 witness 是否允许依赖先前给定的对象，否则“每个人都有一个选择”可能被误读成“所有人共享同一个选择”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed independent domains, two universal quantifiers inspect the same set of pairs in either order. Two existential quantifiers likewise ask for one suitable pair, so they also commute. These statements do not license a blind swap in a dependent restriction such as $y\in E(x)$: changing order must preserve which pairs are permitted. All other free parameters and symbol meanings must remain fixed as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于固定且彼此独立的论域，两个全称量词无论次序如何，都检查同一批有序对；两个存在量词也都只要求一个合适的有序对，所以同样可交换。但不能据此盲目交换带有 $y\in E(x)$ 这种依赖限制的量词：改写时必须保留哪些有序对被允许。其他自由参数与符号解释也须保持固定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The choice reading above used nonempty domains. If restricted quantification over an empty set is allowed, “for every element of the empty set” is true and “there exists an element of the empty set” is false. For example, with $D=E=\varnothing$, the separate-choice formula is true and the shared-choice formula is false. The shared-to-separate implication still holds; a vacuously true outer universal does not supply a witness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的选择读法使用非空论域。若允许对空集作限制量化，则“对空集的每个元素”为空真，“空集中存在元素”为假。例如 $D=E=\varnothing$ 时，分别选择式为真，共同选择式为假。共同见证推出分别见证的方向仍成立；外层全称空真不会凭空提供一个见证。

> [!question]- Check the dependence · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Which quantifier structure naturally expresses “every student has an adviser”? Does it imply that one adviser supervises every student?
>
> **Answer:** It normally means $\forall s\exists t\,Mentors(t,s)$, where the adviser may vary with the student. It does not imply $\exists t\forall s\,Mentors(t,s)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> “每名学生都有一位导师”更自然对应哪一种结构？它是否推出“有一位导师指导所有学生”？
>
> **答案：** 它通常对应 $\forall s\exists t\,Mentors(t,s)$，导师可随学生改变；这不推出 $\exists t\forall s\,Mentors(t,s)$。
