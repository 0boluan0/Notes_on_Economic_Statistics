# 价格加权指数使成分股对指数收益的影响与其每股价格成比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a price-weighted index, each constituent's return weight is proportional to its share price.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的价格加权指数把各成分股价格相加，再除以除数 $D_t$。在成分、除数不变且没有公司行动的一期内，它的收益等于以期初股价为权重的个股价格收益之和：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic construction divides the sum of constituent prices by a divisor. With unchanged constituents and divisor and no corporate actions during a period, its return uses beginning-of-period price weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
I_t=\frac{\sum_i p_{it}}{D_t},\qquad
\frac{I_t}{I_0}-1=\sum_i\frac{p_{i0}}{\sum_jp_{j0}}\left(\frac{p_{it}}{p_{i0}}-1\right).
$$

两只股票初价 10、30 元，高价股的权重是 75%，即使它的公司总市值更小也是如此。两股分别涨 10% 和跌 10%，指数跌 5%。它不是[[等权指数|每只股票等金额]]；它接近每只股票持有相同股数的价格组合。拆股时通常需要[[指数除数调整]]，不能把价格机械减半解释成投资损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For prices of 10 and 30, the higher-priced stock has a 75% weight even if its company's capitalization is smaller. Returns of +10% and -10% give an index return of -5%. This resembles equal share quantities, not [[等权指数|equal amounts invested]]. Splits require the appropriate [[指数除数调整|divisor treatment]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
