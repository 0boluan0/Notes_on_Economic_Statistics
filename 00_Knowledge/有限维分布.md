# Finite-dimensional distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有限维分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **finite-dimensional distribution** describes the probabilities of a [[随机过程|stochastic process]] at several selected times **together**. For a queue, it can answer how likely it is to be empty at time $1$ and have three people at time $3$. That joint question asks more than the two separate questions about time $1$ and time $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**有限维分布**描述一个[[随机过程]]在选定的几个时点**共同取值**的概率。对于排队模型，它可以回答“时点 $1$ 没有人、同时在时点 $3$ 有三个人”的概率。这个联合问题，比单独询问时点 $1$ 和时点 $3$ 的情况包含更多信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose finitely many indices $t_1,\ldots,t_n$ and group the observations into the [[随机向量|random vector]] $(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_n})$ for a real-valued process. Its [[联合分布|joint distribution]] is the finite-dimensional distribution at those indices. For real values it can be specified by the joint cumulative probabilities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选取有限多个指标 $t_1,\ldots,t_n$。对实值过程，把观测排成[[随机向量]] $(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_n})$；它的[[联合分布]]就是这些指标上的有限维分布。可以用下列联合累积概率来指定：

$$F_{t_1,\ldots,t_n}(a_1,\ldots,a_n)
 =\Pr(X_{t_1}\le a_1,\ldots,X_{t_n}\le a_n).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The thresholds $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ are ordinary numbers. The comma inside the probability means that **all** the inequalities hold in the same outcome. Allowing every choice of thresholds determines the law of that vector; no density or independence is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阈值 $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ 是普通数值。概率里的逗号表示这些不等式在同一个结果下**全部**成立。让阈值遍历所有可能值，就确定这个向量的分布；不要求存在密度，也不要求各坐标独立。

> [!example] Same marginals, different two-time law · 边际相同，二维分布不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In model A, toss one fair coin $Z\in\{0,1\}$ and keep $X_t=Z$ for every integer time. In model B, use independent fair tosses $Y_t$ at different integer times. At any single time both models assign probability $1/2$ to each value. But at times $1$ and $3$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 模型 A 只抛一次公平硬币 $Z\in\{0,1\}$，在所有整数时点都令 $X_t=Z$；模型 B 在不同整数时点采用独立公平投掷 $Y_t$。单独看任意一个时点，两模型都以各 $1/2$ 的概率取 $0$ 或 $1$。但在时点 $1$ 和 $3$，
>
> $$\Pr(X_1=0,X_3=1)=0,\qquad
> \Pr(Y_1=0,Y_3=1)=\tfrac14.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first model allows only pairs $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$, each with probability $1/2$. The second allows all four pairs, each with probability $1/4$. Their one-dimensional distributions agree; their two-dimensional distributions do not.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一个模型只允许 $(0,0)$ 与 $(1,1)$，各以 $1/2$ 的概率出现；第二个模型允许全部四种配对，各以 $1/4$ 的概率出现。它们的一维分布相同，二维分布不同。

## One finite selection, or the whole family?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个有限时点组，还是整族分布？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Finite-dimensional” describes how many coordinates are selected **at a time**. It does not require the entire index set $T$ to be finite. Even when $T=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ or $T=[0,\infty)$, the pair $(X_1,X_3)$ has a two-dimensional law and $(X_1,X_3,X_{10})$ has a three-dimensional law. A one-dimensional law is a [[边际分布|marginal distribution]]; dimensions two and above retain dependence among the selected positions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有限维”说的是**一次**选取多少个坐标，并不要求整个指标集 $T$ 有限。即使 $T=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 或 $T=[0,\infty)$，$(X_1,X_3)$ 也有二维分布，$(X_1,X_3,X_{10})$ 也有三维分布。一维分布就是[[边际分布]]；二维及以上的分布保留所选位置之间的依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **family of all finite-dimensional distributions** includes every finite selection, of every size $n\ge1$. It is much more than all one-time marginals. For an existing real-valued process, this family determines its law on the canonical product sigma-algebra. Conversely, constructing a process from a proposed family requires consistency: deleting or reordering coordinates must give the corresponding smaller or reordered law. These existence and uniqueness statements belong to the [[有限维分布决定律|Kolmogorov extension theorem]]. They do not automatically impose [[有限维分布不定路径正则性|regularity on a particular continuous-time version]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**全部有限维分布族**包含每种大小 $n\ge1$ 的每个有限指标组，远不止所有单时点边际。对已经给定的实值过程，这整族分布确定其在典范乘积 σ-代数上的律。反过来，若要从拟定的一族分布构造过程，就需要一致性：删去或重排坐标后，必须得到相应较小或重排的分布。存在与唯一性的结论属于[[有限维分布决定律|Kolmogorov 扩张定理]]，并不会自动给[[有限维分布不定路径正则性|某个连续时间版本附上路径正则性]]。

> [!note]- General state-space notation · 一般状态空间记号
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the states are not real numbers, the same definition uses the joint law on $(S^n,\mathcal S^{\otimes n})$. Here $S$ is the state set, $\mathcal S$ its [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] of allowed events, and $\mathcal S^{\otimes n}$ is generated by rectangles $A_1\times\cdots\times A_n$ with each $A_j\in\mathcal S$. Such a rectangle asks whether all $X_{t_j}\in A_j$. Ordering the indices orders the coordinates; repeating an index repeats the same random variable, not an independent copy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当状态不是实数时，同一定义使用 $(S^n,\mathcal S^{\otimes n})$ 上的联合律。$S$ 是状态集合，$\mathcal S$ 是允许讨论的事件构成的[[σ-代数]]；$\mathcal S^{\otimes n}$ 由各 $A_j\in\mathcal S$ 构成的矩形 $A_1\times\cdots\times A_n$ 生成。这样的矩形询问所有 $X_{t_j}\in A_j$ 是否同时成立。指标顺序决定坐标顺序；重复一个指标，是重复同一个随机变量，不是取独立副本。

> [!question]- Does a normal marginal determine the process? · 正态边际能否确定过程？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose every $X_t$ has a standard normal distribution. Is the whole finite-dimensional family known? No: setting every coordinate equal to one standard normal $Z$ differs from using independent standard normals at integer times. In the first model $X_1=X_3$ with probability one; in the second this equality has probability zero. The marginals alone do not specify the joint law.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若每个 $X_t$ 都服从标准正态分布，是否已经知道全部有限维分布？没有：让所有坐标都等于同一个标准正态 $Z$，与在整数时点使用独立标准正态，得到的过程不同。第一种模型中 $X_1=X_3$ 的概率为一，第二种为零。边际分布没有指定联合分布。
