# 样本均值加一个趋零偏移可以有偏而一致

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adding a vanishing shift to the sample mean can yield a biased but consistent estimator*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 若 $X_i\overset{iid}{\sim}N(\theta,1)$，取 $T_n=\bar X_n+1/n$。有限样本偏差为 $1/n\ne0$，但 MSE 为 $1/n+1/n^2\to0$，所以它一致。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Adding $1/n$ to the iid normal sample mean gives nonzero bias at every finite sample size, yet its MSE tends to zero, establishing consistency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

由[[样本均值无偏性]]与[[样本均值协方差|样本均值方差]]，$E[T_n]=\theta+1/n$、$\operatorname{Var}(T_n)=1/n$。对任意 $\varepsilon>0$，$P(|T_n-\theta|>\varepsilon)\le(1/n+1/n^2)/\varepsilon^2\to0$。偏差趋零和方差趋零在这里共同保证一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample mean’s expectation and variance give bias $1/n$ and variance $1/n$. Applying the nonnegative-variable probability bound to squared error makes the error probability at any fixed tolerance tend to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
