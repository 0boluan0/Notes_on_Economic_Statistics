# Consistency without unbiasedness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一致不推出无偏*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An estimate can carry a small systematic shift at every finite sample size, while both that shift and its random error disappear as more observations arrive. It can then be [[估计量一致性|consistent]] without being [[估计量无偏性|unbiased]]. A sample mean with an added $1/n$ makes the distinction visible: the extra amount is never exactly zero, but it becomes smaller with the number $n$ of observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个估计规则在每个有限样本量下都可能带着小的系统偏移，同时，这个偏移和随机误差又都随着数据增加而消失。因此它可以[[估计量一致性|一致]]，却不[[估计量无偏性|无偏]]。给样本均值加上 $1/n$，就能看见这个区别：增加的量永远不严格等于零，但会随着观察数 $n$ 增大而变小。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be [[独立同分布|i.i.d.]] observations with [[正态分布|normal distribution]] $N(\theta,1)$, where the unknown real mean $\theta$ is the target and the variance is $1$. All required second moments are finite. Write $\bar X_n=(X_1+\cdots+X_n)/n$ for the average of the first $n\ge1$ observations, and define
$$T_n=\bar X_n+\frac1n.$$
The target is still $\theta$; adding a number to the rule does not change what we are trying to estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ [[独立同分布]]，每个服从[[正态分布]] $N(\theta,1)$，目标是未知的实数均值 $\theta$，方差为 $1$，所需的二阶矩都有限。记 $\bar X_n=(X_1+\cdots+X_n)/n$ 为前 $n\ge1$ 个观察的平均，规则 $T_n$ 就是在这个平均上再加 $1/n$。目标仍是 $\theta$；改变规则，并不改变要估计的对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The finite-sample shift
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每个有限样本量下都有偏差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[期望线性性|Linearity of expectation]] gives $E_\theta[\bar X_n]=\theta$, where $E_\theta$ means expectation in the model with parameter fixed at $\theta$. The [[估计量偏差|bias]] is the sampling mean minus the target, so
$$E_\theta[T_n]=\theta+\frac1n,\qquad \operatorname{Bias}_\theta(T_n)=\frac1n.$$
For every finite $n$ and every real $\theta$, the bias is positive. The rule is therefore biased throughout this model, even when the numerical bias is tiny.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[期望线性性]]，$E_\theta[\bar X_n]=\theta$，其中 $E_\theta$ 表示参数固定为 $\theta$ 时的模型期望。[[估计量偏差|偏差]]是抽样均值减目标，因此期望为 $\theta+1/n$，偏差为 $1/n$。每个有限 $n$、每个实数 $\theta$ 下，偏差都为正；数值再小，也不是严格无偏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independence makes the cross-covariance terms in the [[和的方差协方差项|variance of the sum]] vanish. Thus $\operatorname{Var}_\theta(\bar X_n)=n/n^2=1/n$. Adding the deterministic amount $1/n$ leaves variance unchanged by the [[方差的仿射变换|variance shift rule]]. Consequently $T_n$ also has variance $1/n$ and [[标准误含义|standard error]] $1/\sqrt n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立使[[和的方差协方差项|求和方差中的交叉协方差]]为零，所以 $\operatorname{Var}_\theta(\bar X_n)=n/n^2=1/n$。由[[方差的仿射变换|方差的平移规则]]，加上确定的 $1/n$ 不改变方差。因此 $T_n$ 的方差也是 $1/n$，[[标准误含义|标准误]]为 $1/\sqrt n$。

> [!example] Two quantities shrinking at different rates · 两个量以不同速度缩小
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $n=4$, the rule adds $0.25$ and has standard error $0.5$. At $n=100$, it adds $0.01$ and has standard error $0.1$. Bias describes the displacement of the sampling centre; standard error describes spread around that centre. Neither is the error of one particular realised answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $n=4$ 时增加 $0.25$，标准误为 $0.5$；$n=100$ 时增加 $0.01$，标准误为 $0.1$。偏差描述抽样中心偏离目标多少，标准误描述围绕该中心的分散程度；二者都不是某一次答案的实际误差。

## Why the error probability still tends to zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把偏差与波动一起控制住*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[均方误差|mean squared error]] averages the squared distance from the target. By its [[MSE偏差方差分解|bias–variance decomposition]],
$$E_\theta[(T_n-\theta)^2]=\frac1n+\frac1{n^2}\longrightarrow0.$$
The first term is variance; the second is squared bias. To connect this calculation to consistency, take any **fixed** tolerance $\varepsilon>0$. The squared error is nonnegative, so [[Markov不等式|Markov's inequality]] bounds the probability of exceeding $\varepsilon^2$ by its mean divided by that threshold:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[均方误差|MSE]]把与目标的平方距离取平均。由[[MSE偏差方差分解]]，它等于 $1/n+1/n^2$ 并趋零，前者是方差，后者是偏差平方。为了把这个计算连接到一致性，取任意**固定**的容忍值 $\varepsilon>0$。平方误差是非负量，[[Markov不等式]]把它超过阈值 $\varepsilon^2$ 的概率，控制在其均值除以阈值之内：

> [!important] Consistency with nonzero finite-sample bias · 有偏规则的一致性证明
> $$P_\theta(|T_n-\theta|>\varepsilon)
> =P_\theta((T_n-\theta)^2>\varepsilon^2)
> \le\frac{1/n+1/n^2}{\varepsilon^2}\longrightarrow0.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The denominator stays positive and fixed, and the numerator tends to zero. This proves consistency for each fixed $\theta$, despite the nonzero bias at every finite $n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 分母保持固定的正数，分子趋零。这对每个固定 $\theta$ 证明了一致性，尽管每个有限 $n$ 的偏差都不为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normality is useful for the exact distribution $T_n\sim N(\theta+1/n,1/n)$, but the argument above only needs i.i.d. observations with mean $\theta$ and a finite variance $\sigma^2$. In that wider case, replace $1/n$ in the variance by $\sigma^2/n$, and the same probability bound tends to zero. These are sufficient conditions for this example. Merely saying “the bias vanishes” would not control random spread; nor does consistency in general force moments to converge. A [[固定偏移破坏一致性|fixed nonzero shift]] behaves differently because it never disappears.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正态假设能给出精确分布 $T_n\sim N(\theta+1/n,1/n)$，但上面的证明只需要 iid 观察具有均值 $\theta$ 和有限方差 $\sigma^2$。在这一更广情形中，把方差项改成 $\sigma^2/n$，同样的概率界仍趋零。这是本例的一组充分条件；只说“偏差趋零”还没有控制随机波动，一致性本身一般也不能保证矩收敛。[[固定偏移破坏一致性|固定非零偏移]]则不同，因为它不会随样本量消失。
