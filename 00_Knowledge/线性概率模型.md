# 线性概率模型把二元结果的条件概率表示为回归量的线性函数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The linear probability model represents a binary outcome's conditional probability as a linear function of the regressors.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 模型入口
> 对 $Y\in\{0,1\}$，线性概率模型（LPM）写成
> $$
> E(Y\mid X)=P(Y=1\mid X)=X'\beta,
> $$
> 它用一个平面或超平面近似事件概率如何随协变量改变。这个式子定义的是概率函数的形状；怎样从样本中求得 $\beta$，由 [[LPM的OLS估计]] 单独处理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $Y\in\{0,1\}$, the linear probability model (LPM) uses the specification above. It approximates the way the event probability changes with the covariates by a plane or hyperplane. This equation defines the shape of the probability function; [[LPM的OLS估计|estimating $\beta$ from a sample]] is a separate step.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种线性表示的直观之处，是所有变化都留在概率尺度上。在只有一个未变换主效应的简单规格中，系数 $0.08$ 对应 8 个百分点的概率差，而不是“增加 8%”。系数何时能这样直读，见 [[LPM系数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The appeal of this linear representation is that changes remain on the probability scale. In a simple specification with one untransformed main effect, a coefficient of $0.08$ corresponds to an eight-percentage-point probability difference, not an “8% increase.” The separate [[LPM系数|LPM-coefficient atom]] states when that direct reading is valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LPM 不会自动把这条直线限制在 $[0,1]$ 内，也默认未变换的连续变量在各处有相同斜率。这是模型形状的两个独立边界，分别见 [[LPM预测越界]] 和 [[LPM常数斜率]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The LPM does not automatically keep this line inside $[0,1]$, and an untransformed continuous regressor has the same slope everywhere in a basic additive specification. These are two distinct shape boundaries, developed in [[LPM预测越界|out-of-range LPM predictions]] and [[LPM常数斜率|the constant-slope boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 先读概率尺度
> 若
> $$
> \widehat P(employed=1)=0.30+0.08\,trained,
> $$
> 则未培训者和培训者的拟合概率分别为 0.30 与 0.38。这里的 0.08 是 8 个百分点；是否具有因果解释仍取决于培训如何分配。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the fitted equation above, the predicted probabilities for an untrained and a trained person are 0.30 and 0.38, respectively. The value 0.08 means 8 percentage points; whether it has a causal interpretation still depends on how training was assigned.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
