# 条件独立与边际独立互不推出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Neither conditional nor marginal independence implies the other*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> [[条件独立]]比较每个给定 $Z$ 后的条件分布；[[随机变量独立|边际独立]]比较把 $Z$ 积分或求和掉之后的联合分布。改变所比较的分布，独立性也可能改变，因此两个方向都不成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Random variables $X$ and $Y$ are conditionally independent given $Z$ when, for relevant values,
> $$P(X=x,Y=y\mid Z=z)=P(X=x\mid Z=z)P(Y=y\mid Z=z).$$
> This is factorisation within each conditioning stratum; unconditional independence instead factorises the marginal joint distribution. Adding or removing a condition can change independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterexamples in both directions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 边际独立未必条件独立：两枚独立公平硬币的结果 $X,Y$ 原本独立；给定“两次相同”后，知道 $X$ 就确定 $Y$。
- 条件独立未必边际独立：若共同原因 $Z$ 决定 $X=Z,Y=Z$，给定 $Z$ 后二者都是确定值并满足条件分解；忽略 $Z$ 后却有 $X=Y$，因而相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Unconditional independence need not survive conditioning: outcomes $X,Y$ of two independent fair coins are independent, but after conditioning on “the two outcomes match”, knowing $X$ determines $Y$.
- Conditional independence need not imply marginal independence: if a common cause $Z$ determines $X=Z,Y=Z$, then within each value of $Z$ both variables are fixed and the conditional distribution factorises; after marginalising over $Z$, however, $X=Y$ and the variables are dependent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么看到 $X\perp Y$ 后不能自动写成 $X\perp Y\mid Z$？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can $X\perp Y$ not automatically be rewritten as $X\perp Y\mid Z$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 条件化会改变所比较的分布；在某个条件层内可能引入此前不存在的约束或选择关系。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Conditioning changes the distribution being compared and can introduce a constraint or selection relationship within a stratum that was absent marginally.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
