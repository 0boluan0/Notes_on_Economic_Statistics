# 进入壁垒是使新进入者难以有效进入并竞争的结构性或制度性障碍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry barriers are structural or institutional obstacles to effective entry and competition by newcomers.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在行业战略分析中，进入壁垒是新进入者建立可竞争业务时必须克服的障碍，例如规模要求、品牌与客户关系、渠道或资源控制、专有技术、许可要求。须说明障碍针对哪类进入者；行业外的大企业与小型创业者面对的资本和能力限制可能不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In industry strategy analysis, entry barriers are obstacles to building a viable competitive business, such as scale requirements, brands and customer relationships, control of channels or resources, proprietary technology, and licensing. Identify the entrant: a large outside firm and a small start-up may face different financing and capability constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若达到竞争性单位成本需要很大产量，[[规模经济]]可能使初入者承受低份额与高成本的矛盾；但仅仅“项目花钱多”还不足以证明所有进入者都受阻。高壁垒可能减轻[[潜在进入者威胁]]，却不保证现有企业不打价格战。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If competitive unit costs require substantial output, [[规模经济|economies of scale]] may leave a newcomer with low share and high cost. A large investment requirement alone does not prove that every entrant is blocked. High barriers may reduce the [[潜在进入者威胁|entry threat]] without preventing price wars among incumbents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
