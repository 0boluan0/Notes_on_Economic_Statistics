# 必要劳动时间不变时延长工作日会增加绝对剩余价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extending the working day with necessary labour time unchanged increases absolute surplus value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绝对剩余价值生产，是在 [[必要劳动|必要劳动时间]] 给定时，通过把工作日延长到这一时点以外，增加由资本占有的 [[剩余劳动]]。比较延长前后时，要固定劳动力价值、生产率、正常劳动强度和社会劳动的价值折算；这样，增加的工作时间就增加剩余劳动及其形成的剩余价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Production of absolute surplus value extends the working day beyond a given amount of [[必要劳动|necessary labour time]], increasing [[剩余劳动|surplus labour]] appropriated by capital. A comparison before and after extension holds constant the value of labour-power, productivity, normal labour intensity and the value expression of social labour. Under these conditions, additional working time increases surplus labour and the surplus value it creates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用教学构造：劳动力按一天400单位的价值支付，平均社会劳动每小时表现为100价值单位。原来8小时工作日中，必要4小时、剩余4小时；延长至10小时，必要劳动仍为4小时，剩余劳动增至6小时。新价值由800增为1000，剩余价值由400增为600，[[剩余价值率]] 由100%增至150%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the common constructed example, labour-power is paid at its daily value of 400 units, and each hour of average social labour is expressed in 100 value units. An eight-hour day initially contains four necessary and four surplus hours. Extending it to ten hours leaves necessary labour at four hours and raises surplus labour to six. New value rises from 800 to 1,000, surplus value from 400 to 600, and the [[剩余价值率|rate of surplus value]] from 100% to 150%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

延长工作日可能同时增加原料耗用和机器耗损；这些转移价值需在产品价值中另外计算。本例只计算活劳动创造的新价值及其分割。若工作日固定，而必要劳动时间下降，则对应 [[相对剩余价值生产]] 的机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extending the day may also increase raw-material consumption and machinery wear. Their transferred values must be calculated separately in the value of the product. This example calculates only new value created by living labour and its division. With a fixed working day and declining necessary labour time, the relevant mechanism is [[相对剩余价值生产|production of relative surplus value]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工作日的延长受人的身体恢复需要与社会生活需要限制。Marx 在工作日章进一步把正常工作日的确定放进资本与劳动之间的斗争中；因此，给定条件下的10小时比较不能向无限延长外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extension is bounded by physical recovery and social needs. In his chapter on the working day, Marx further places the determination of a normal working day within struggle between capital and labour. The conditional comparison with a ten-hour day therefore cannot be extrapolated to unlimited extension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第12章](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch12.htm)，开篇工作日划分及绝对/相对剩余价值命名；[第16章](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch16.htm)，从工作日延长与必要劳动缩短的比较，到给定生产率和正常强度时提高剩余价值率的条件：支持机制和控制变量；[第10章第1节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch10.htm#S1)支持身体、社会与斗争边界。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。8→10小时例为教学构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 12 introduces the division of the working day and names absolute and relative surplus value. Chapter 16 compares extending the day with reducing necessary labour, then states the conditions for increasing the rate with given productivity and normal intensity. Chapter 10, Section 1 supports the physical, social and conflict-related boundaries. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The eight-to-ten-hour comparison is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
