# Adjacency-list search complexity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*邻接表图搜索复杂度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Searching a graph requires work both for its vertices and for the connections we inspect. With [[邻接表与邻接矩阵|adjacency lists]], a full breadth-first or depth-first search can charge a constant amount of work to each vertex and each stored adjacency entry. This is why its time grows with vertices **plus** edges, rather than with vertices alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

搜索图时，既要处理顶点，也要检查它们之间的连接。使用[[邻接表与邻接矩阵|邻接表]]时，完整的广度优先或深度优先搜索可以把常数量的工作分别记在每个顶点和每个存储的邻接项上。因此，时间随“顶点数**加**边数”增长，而非只随顶点数增长。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take five vertices $s,a,b,x,z$ and four directed edges $s\to a,s\to b,a\to x,b\to x$, with $z$ isolated. Their outgoing lists have lengths $2,1,1,0,0$. A full search schedules each of the five vertices once and inspects four adjacency entries, even though $x$ can be reached by two routes. If those four edges are undirected instead, the lists have eight entries in total. The second inspection of an undirected edge changes a constant factor, not the order of growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取五个顶点 $s,a,b,x,z$，以及四条有向边 $s\to a,s\to b,a\to x,b\to x$，其中 $z$ 孤立。各出邻接表长度为 $2,1,1,0,0$。完整搜索会分别安排五个顶点一次，并检查四个邻接项；即使有两条路线到达 $x$，也不会重复安排它。若把四条边都改成无向边，邻接项总数变为八。无向边检查两次只改变常数因子，不改变增长阶。

> [!important] The bound and its implementation conditions · 时间界及实现条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a finite nonempty graph, write $n=|V|$ for its vertex count and $m=|E|$ for its edge count. A full BFS or DFS takes $O(n+m)$ time when adjacency lists are already available, [[搜索标记与父森林|discovery marks prevent repeated scheduling]], each list is scanned once, mark/parent access costs constant time, and queue/stack operations cost constant amortized time. Selecting new roots uses one pass through the vertex list. [[大O记号|Big O]] bounds the total by a constant multiple for sufficiently large inputs; it is not an exact count of instructions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对有限非空图，记 $n=|V|$ 为顶点数、$m=|E|$ 为边数。在邻接表已经可用、[[搜索标记与父森林|发现标记避免重复安排]]、每张表只扫描一次、标记与父记录访问为常数时间、队列/栈操作为常数摊还时间的条件下，完整 BFS 或 DFS 耗时 $O(n+m)$。选择新根只对顶点列表做一遍扫描。[[大O记号|大 O]]表示输入足够大时总工作被固定常数倍控制，并不是精确的指令条数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Count vertices and adjacency entries separately
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 计数论证

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, every vertex is marked and scheduled at most once. A BFS vertex is removed from the queue once; a DFS call is entered and completed once. DFS may pause a parent's scan while visiting children, but it resumes where it stopped instead of rescanning the list. Initializing records and making one pass to find new roots therefore contribute $O(n)$ in a full search.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看顶点：每个顶点至多被标记、安排一次。BFS 顶点只出队一次；DFS 调用只进入和完成一次。DFS 可能在访问子节点时暂停父节点的扫描，但恢复时从原位置继续，而不是重新扫描整张表。因此，初始化记录和用一遍顶点扫描寻找新根，在全图搜索中贡献 $O(n)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next, let $q$ be the total number of stored adjacency entries. In the usual directed representation each edge appears once, in its tail's outgoing list, so $q=m$. For an undirected graph, storing each edge at both ends gives $q=2m$. With constant work per entry, scanning costs $O(q)$. Thus the more direct accounting is $O(n+q)$, giving $O(n+m)$ for these representations. A dense graph may have $m$ of order $n^2$; “linear in the input” then need not mean linear in $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再看邻接项：令 $q$ 为存储的邻接项总数。通常的有向表示把每条边存一次，放在起点的出邻接表中，因此 $q=m$。无向图若把每条边在两端各存一次，则 $q=2m$。每项只做常数工作，所以扫描耗时 $O(q)$。更直接的计数是 $O(n+q)$，在这些表示下便得到 $O(n+m)$。稠密图的 $m$ 可能与 $n^2$ 同阶，因此“相对输入线性”未必意味着“相对顶点数线性”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parallel edges are counted separately in $m$ and in the lists. A self-loop is one outgoing entry in a directed graph. In an undirected incidence-list convention it contributes two entries at the same vertex; an implementation storing it once still has only a constant number of entries per edge and the same asymptotic bound. Marks prevent these extra inspections from causing new discoveries of an already discovered endpoint. The bound concerns the represented graph and its stored entries, not just the number of distinct neighboring vertices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平行边在 $m$ 和邻接表里分别计数。有向自环贡献一个出邻接项；无向关联表若按两个端点存储，自环就在同一个顶点贡献两项。某种实现若只存一次，仍然是每条边常数个邻接项，渐近界不变。发现标记使这些额外检查不会再次发现已经发现的终点。该界针对实际表示的图和存储项，而不只是不同邻居的数量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State which work is included
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 前提与更精确的说法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a single source, let $V_R$ be the vertices reached and $q_R$ the sum of their outgoing-list lengths. If only those records are created and no whole-graph initialization is performed, the search work is $O(|V_R|+q_R)$. This includes the source even when it is isolated and $q_R=0$. Initializing an array for all $n$ vertices adds $O(n)$. A full search retains marks across restarts and advances through one vertex enumeration; repeatedly starting a fresh scan from the beginning to find the next root can cost $O(n^2)$ even on an edgeless graph. An empty graph returns immediately in constant time; $O(1+n+m)$ includes that boundary explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对单源搜索，令 $V_R$ 为到达的顶点集，$q_R$ 为这些点的出邻接表长度之和。若只为这些点创建记录，没有全图初始化，则搜索工作为 $O(|V_R|+q_R)$。即使源点孤立、$q_R=0$，它自身仍计入成本。若为全部 $n$ 个顶点初始化数组，则另加 $O(n)$。全图搜索跨重启保留标记，并沿一份顶点枚举向前扫描；如果每找一个新根都从头重扫，哪怕无边图也可能耗时 $O(n^2)$。空图可以常数时间返回；写作 $O(1+n+m)$ 就能显式包含这一边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant-time lookup is an implementation assumption. Integer vertex labels can index arrays directly. A hash-table implementation generally gives the corresponding **expected** bound under its hashing assumptions; the graph argument alone does not guarantee worst-case constant lookup for arbitrary labels. Amortized constant queue/stack cost means that a sequence of operations has total cost proportional to its length, even if an occasional resizing operation is expensive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常数时间查询是实现前提。整数编号可以直接索引数组；散列表实现通常是在相应散列假设下给出**期望**时间界，图上的计数本身不会保证任意标签都能最坏常数时间查询。“队列/栈操作摊还常数成本”表示一串操作的总成本与操作数成正比，即使偶尔扩容较贵也可以成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an [[邻接表与邻接矩阵|adjacency matrix]], a standard neighbor scan examines all $n$ positions of each processed row. A full traversal therefore takes $O(n^2)$ rather than a sparse-graph $O(n+m)$ guarantee. A queue that removes its first element by shifting every remaining array element also breaks the assumed cost: after a star's center enqueues $n-1$ leaves, the repeated shifts total $(n-2)+(n-3)+\cdots+1$, which is quadratic. Use a queue representation with the required operation bounds. Allowing duplicate scheduling similarly requires a fresh work and memory analysis rather than reusing the one-record proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用[[邻接表与邻接矩阵|邻接矩阵]]时，通常的邻居扫描要检查所处理行的全部 $n$ 个位置，因此全图遍历为 $O(n^2)$，不能保留稀疏图的 $O(n+m)$ 保证。如果队列删除首元素时必须搬动数组中的其余元素，也会破坏操作成本假设：星形图中心把 $n-1$ 个叶子入队后，反复搬动的总数为 $(n-2)+(n-3)+\cdots+1$，是二次增长。应使用满足所需操作界的队列表示。允许重复安排时也要重新分析工作量和内存，不能直接复用每点一份记录的证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The search's marks, parents and frontier require $O(n)$ auxiliary space, excluding the already stored graph. Parent pointers compactly encode routes. Explicitly printing every root-to-vertex route can take longer: on a directed chain of $n$ vertices their total lengths are $0+1+\cdots+(n-1)=n(n-1)/2$. The linear search bound does not include that quadratic-size output or arbitrary work performed at each visited vertex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

搜索标记、父记录和前沿需要 $O(n)$ 辅助空间，不含已存储的图。父指针能紧凑表示路线；若明确打印从根到每个顶点的全部路线，可能耗时更多：在含 $n$ 个顶点的有向链上，总路径长度为 $0+1+\cdots+(n-1)=n(n-1)/2$。线性搜索界不包括这种二次大小的输出，也不包括在每个访问点另外进行的任意工作。

> [!question]- Why does checking an undirected edge twice stay linear? · 无向边检查两次为何仍线性？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two stored entries per edge give $2m$ checks, not $2^m$. Multiplication by the fixed constant $2$ preserves an $O(m)$ bound. By contrast, scanning all $n$ possible neighbors for each of $n$ vertices yields $n^2$ checks, even when most matrix entries record no edge.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每条边两个存储项，只带来 $2m$ 次检查，而不是 $2^m$ 次。乘以固定常数 $2$ 仍是 $O(m)$。相反，若对 $n$ 个顶点各扫描全部 $n$ 个候选邻居，就得到 $n^2$ 次检查，即使矩阵中绝大多数位置都没有边。
