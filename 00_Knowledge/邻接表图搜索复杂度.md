# 邻接表上的完整图搜索在线性于顶点数加边数的时间内完成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A full graph search with adjacency lists runs in time linear in vertices plus edges*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子性能结论
> 在 adjacency-list 表示、发现时标记且每个 frontier 操作是常数摊还时间的条件下，full BFS 或 full DFS 的运行时间是
> $$
> O(|V|+|E|).
> $$
> 这是相对输入表示大小的线性时间，不是“只与顶点数有关”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With adjacency lists, marking on discovery, and constant-amortized-time frontier operations, full BFS and full DFS run in
> $$
> O(|V|+|E|)
> $$
> time. This is linear in the represented graph size, not “linear only in the number of vertices.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 计数论证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Accounting argument*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个顶点至多被发现、入 frontier、取出或完成一次，贡献 $O(|V|)$。处理 $u$ 时只扫描 $Adj(u)$；对 digraph，所有列表长度和是 $|E|$，对 undirected graph，每条边在两端列表各出现一次，总和是 $2|E|$。每个邻接项只做常数工作，因此边扫描贡献 $O(|E|)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every vertex is discovered, inserted, removed, or finished at most once, contributing $O(|V|)$. Processing $u$ scans only $Adj(u)$. In a digraph the total list length is $|E|$; in an undirected graph each edge occurs in both endpoint lists, for total length $2|E|$. Constant work per adjacency entry contributes $O(|E|)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 前提与更精确的说法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assumptions and a more precise statement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 单源 search 若只访问可达子图，可按访问到的 $V_R,E_R$ 写成 $O(|V_R|+|E_R|)$；若还初始化所有顶点的 distance 或检查未访问根，则包含 $O(|V|)$。
- adjacency matrix 下枚举一个顶点的邻居要扫 $|V|$ 个格子，full traversal 通常变成 $O(|V|^2)$；不能脱离表示法宣称 $O(|V|+|E|)$。
- 若 frontier 容器用线性时间删除队首、或允许同一顶点重复入队，数据结构和不变量被破坏，结论也随之失效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** A source search restricted to the reachable subgraph may be written $O(|V_R|+|E_R|)$. Initializing distances for all vertices or selecting roots for a full search adds $O(|V|)$.<br>
**2.** With an adjacency matrix, enumerating the neighbors of one vertex scans $|V|$ entries, so a full traversal is generally $O(|V|^2)$. The $O(|V|+|E|)$ claim is representation-dependent.<br>
**3.** A frontier implementation with linear-time queue removal, or one that admits duplicate insertions, breaks the assumptions and may break the bound.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 为什么无向边在邻接表中检查两次仍是 $O(|E|)$，而不是 $O(2^{|E|})$ 或额外一个数量级？
>
> **答案：**每条边只在两个端点列表各出现一次，邻接项总数恰为 $2|E|$；常数因子 $2$ 被大 O 吸收，总边工作仍与 $|E|$ 成正比。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does examining each undirected edge from both endpoint lists remain $O(|E|)$?
>
> **Answer:** Every edge contributes exactly two adjacency entries, so the total is $2|E|$. The constant factor two is absorbed by big-O notation, leaving work linear in $|E|$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
