# 供给能随报酬差异调整时，Smith 的竞争过程使市场价格趋向自然价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When supply can respond to differences in returns, Smith's competitive process draws market price toward natural price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 用来连接实际市场价与[[Smith自然价格|自然价格]]的，是生产者对报酬差异的反应：某种活动比通常更赚钱，会吸引更多资源；持续低于通常报酬，就会使资源撤出。资源改变用途，送到市场的商品数量随之改变，原先的价差才有缩小的压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith connects actual market price with [[Smith自然价格|natural price]] through responses to differences in returns. An activity paying more than the ordinary return attracts resources, while persistently low returns encourage withdrawal. As resources change use, the quantity brought to market changes, creating pressure for the initial price gap to narrow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个方向怎样连起来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How adjustment works in each direction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若上市量小于[[Smith有效需求|自然价格上能够购买的数量]]，买者竞争可能把市场价推高，使部分工资、利润或地租超过通常率。其他劳动者、资本所有者或地主便有动力增加这类供给。供给扩张后，先前的短缺减轻，超出通常率的报酬及价格受到向下的压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If market supply falls short of the [[Smith有效需求|quantity buyers will purchase at natural price]], competition among buyers may raise market price and some component of wages, profit, or rent above its ordinary rate. Other workers, capital owners, or landowners then have an incentive to expand supply. As the shortage diminishes, extraordinary returns and price face downward pressure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，上市量超过有效需求时，一部分商品只能降价出售，至少某项报酬低于通常率。受到损失的人减少用于该行业的劳动、资本或土地，上市量下降，价格便有回升的压力。这里的“损失”可以是低于其他用途的通常收益，不一定已经出现现金支出大于收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When supply exceeds effectual demand, some goods must sell for less, putting at least one return below its ordinary rate. Those affected reduce the labour, capital, or land devoted to the activity. Supply contracts and price faces upward pressure. The loss may be a shortfall relative to ordinary returns elsewhere, rather than negative cash earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比如，织布需求突然增加时，眼前能买到的布匹还没变多，价格可能先上涨。更多资本和劳动进入织布、产量增加后，原先因短缺而获得的额外报酬才可能被竞争压低。这个教学示例的关键是**供给确实能够增加**，不是假定所有生产者看到价格后当天就能完成调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, an unexpected increase in demand for cloth may initially raise price while available cloth remains unchanged. Extra returns from the shortage may be competed down only after more capital and labour enter weaving and output expands. The essential assumption in this illustration is **that supply can actually expand**, not that producers can adjust fully on the day they observe a price change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 趋向不等于每次都已到达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A tendency is not continuous equality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个过程需要劳动和资本能改变用途。若进入受到限制，或新增供给长期做不到，[[垄断与自然价格|价格偏离就可能维持]]。即使不存在这类阻碍，农业年产量的波动也会使市场价反复偏离；通常报酬改变时，自然价格本身还会移动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The process requires labour and capital to be able to change use. Restrictions on entry or persistent limits to additional supply can [[垄断与自然价格|sustain deviations]]. Even without such barriers, variations in agricultural yields can repeatedly move market price away from its benchmark. Changes in ordinary returns also move natural price itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，这是一个有具体调整环节的竞争机制，不是已经证明了唯一均衡和全局收敛的数学定理。后来 [[Marshall 供求与调整期限|Marshall 按哪些变量来得及调整来区分市场期、短期与长期]]，把时间问题处理得更细；这是后续的发展，不是换个名字重复 Smith 的原论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a competitive mechanism with specific adjustment steps, not a mathematical proof of a unique equilibrium and global convergence. [[Marshall 供求与调整期限|Marshall later distinguished market, short, and long periods by which variables could adjust]], giving the time dimension a more explicit treatment. That is a later development rather than a new name for Smith's original argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*

- [Smith，《国富论》I.VII.9–17、20–21、30、33](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=10)：核对买卖竞争、供给和要素用途的双向调整、重心比喻、农业产出波动、进入障碍以及自然价格自身变动。织布需求冲击为依据这套机制构造的教学示例，不是原书的定量案例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Smith, *The Wealth of Nations*, I.VII.9–17, 20–21, 30, and 33](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=10): competition among buyers and sellers, adjustment of supply and resource use in both directions, the centre-of-gravity analogy, variable agricultural output, entry barriers, and changes in natural price. The cloth-demand shock is a teaching illustration of the mechanism, not a quantitative case from the book.
