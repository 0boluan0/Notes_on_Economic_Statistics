# 潜变量阈值模型把不可观察的连续潜变量是否越过阈值映射为二元结果，并通过误差分布规范化其尺度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A latent-variable threshold model maps whether an unobserved continuous latent variable crosses a threshold into a binary outcome and normalizes its scale through the error distribution.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 共同机制
> 设
> $$
> Y^*=X'\beta+\varepsilon,
> \qquad
> Y=1[Y^*>0].
> $$
> 研究者只观察阈值两侧的 0/1，不观察 $Y^*$ 的绝对数值。若 $\varepsilon$ 的 CDF 为 $F$，则
> $$
> P(Y=1\mid X)=F(X'\beta)
> $$
> （在误差分布关于 0 对称的常用规范化下）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the latent outcome and observed binary outcome be defined by the equations above. The researcher observes only whether the threshold is crossed, not the absolute value of $Y^*$. If the CDF of $\varepsilon$ is $F$, the conditional probability is the expression shown above under the usual normalization in which the error distribution is symmetric about zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若误差采用标准 logistic 分布，就得到 [[Logit模型]]；采用标准正态分布，就得到 [[Probit模型]]。这是一种生成机制解释，并不要求“就业倾向”或“违约压力”真的能被独立测量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A standard logistic error distribution produces the [[Logit模型|logit model]], whereas a standard normal error distribution produces the [[Probit模型|probit model]]. This is an interpretation in terms of a data-generating mechanism; it does not require constructs such as “propensity for employment” or “default pressure” to be independently measurable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于把 $Y^*$、$\beta$ 和 $\varepsilon$ 同时乘以同一个正数不会改变阈值判断，潜变量的尺度不能从二元数据单独识别。logit 固定 logistic 误差尺度，probit 固定误差方差为 1；因此两种模型的原始系数大小不能机械比较。实质比较应回到预测概率、离散变化或边际效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying $Y^*$, $\beta$, and $\varepsilon$ by the same positive constant does not alter which side of the threshold an observation falls on, so binary data alone cannot identify the scale of the latent variable. Logit fixes the scale of the logistic error, while probit fixes the error variance at 1. The raw coefficient magnitudes from the two models therefore cannot be compared mechanically; meaningful comparisons should instead use predicted probabilities, discrete changes, or marginal effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若同一变量经变换、交互或重复进入指数，单个系数的符号也只描述对应基函数的方向；变量的完整方向由 $\partial(X'\beta)/\partial X_j$ 决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the same variable enters the index through transformations, interactions, or repeated terms, the sign of any one coefficient describes only the direction of its corresponding basis function. The variable's overall direction is determined by $\partial(X'\beta)/\partial X_j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
