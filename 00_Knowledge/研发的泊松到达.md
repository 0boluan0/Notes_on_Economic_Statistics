# 在线性泊松研发技术中当前研发劳动决定创新到达强度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With linear Poisson research technology, current research labour determines the arrival intensity of innovations.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Aghion–Howitt 基本模型把研发投入与随机成果连接起来：投入 $n$ 单位熟练劳动进行研发，使成功创新以强度 $z=\lambda n$ 到达，其中 $\lambda>0$ 是研发效率。这里 $n$ 是劳动量，**不是人口增长率**；$z$ 是每单位时间的到达强度，也不是一段任意长度内的成功概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic Aghion–Howitt model connects research inputs to uncertain discoveries. Employing $n$ units of skilled labour generates innovations at intensity $z=\lambda n$, where $\lambda>0$ measures research productivity. Here $n$ is labour, not population growth, and $z$ is an arrival intensity rather than a success probability over an arbitrary period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若研发劳动在整个观察期间恒为 $\bar n>0$，累计创新数 $J(\tau)$ 是率为 $z=\lambda\bar n$ 的 [[齐次泊松过程]]。借助 [[泊松指数等价]]，下一次创新的等待时间 $T$ 满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With research labour fixed at $\bar n>0$ throughout the observation period, the innovation count $J(\tau)$ is a [[齐次泊松过程|homogeneous Poisson process]] with rate $z=\lambda\bar n$. The [[泊松指数等价|Poisson–exponential equivalence]] gives the waiting time $T$ to the next discovery:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Pr(T>h)=e^{-zh},\qquad E[T]=\frac1z,\qquad
\Pr(J(h)\ge1)=1-e^{-zh}.
$$

小区间内成功概率可近似为 $z\,d\tau$；有限区间要用上面的指数式。例如 $\lambda=0.1,\bar n=0.28$ 时，$z=0.028$，一个时间单位内至少一次创新的概率约为 $2.7612\%$，平均等待约为 $35.71$ 个时间单位。研究投入提高成功的到达率，但不能保证某个确定日期完成创新。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over a short interval the success probability is approximately $z\,d\tau$; finite intervals require the exponential formula. With $\lambda=0.1$ and $\bar n=0.28$, the intensity is $0.028$, the probability of at least one innovation within one time unit is about $2.7612\%$, and mean waiting time is about $35.71$ units. More research increases arrival intensity without guaranteeing a completion date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原文允许不同创新代际之间改变研发劳动 $n_j$：每个代际内部的等待率是 $\lambda n_j$，但整个历史就不能直接称为同一恒定率的齐次泊松过程。这个特例的研发技术也没有过去失败投入的记忆；若 $n=0$，到达强度为零，不会产生创新。它怎样在经济中被选择，见 [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发]]；到达次数怎样变成产出增长，见 [[随机创新的增长口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Research can differ across innovation generations, with within-generation waiting rate $\lambda n_j$; the full history then need not have one constant Poisson rate. This research technology has no memory of past unsuccessful inputs. Zero research means zero innovation intensity. See [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|equilibrium research allocation]] for the choice of research and [[随机创新的增长口径|growth measures under random innovation]] for its output consequences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
