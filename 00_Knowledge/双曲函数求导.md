# 双曲正弦的导数是双曲余弦而双曲余弦的导数是双曲正弦
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The derivative of hyperbolic sine is hyperbolic cosine and the derivative of hyperbolic cosine is hyperbolic sine*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\sinh x=\cosh x,
> \qquad
> \frac{d}{dx}\cosh x=\sinh x.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The derivative identities are $d(\sinh x)/dx=\cosh x$ and $d(\cosh x)/dx=\sinh x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 [[双曲函数|定义]]逐项求导即可：
$$
\frac d{dx}\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2
=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2=\cosh x,
$$
其中 $d(e^{-x})/dx=-e^{-x}$ 来自 [[链式法则]]。同理，$\cosh$ 中原有的加号在求导后变成相减，得到 $\sinh$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiate the exponential definitions term by term. The chain rule gives $d(e^{-x})/dx=-e^{-x}$, turning the difference in $\sinh$ into the sum defining $\cosh$, and the sum in $\cosh$ into the difference defining $\sinh$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

注意第二条没有负号。这与 $d(\cos x)/dx=-\sin x$ 不同，因为圆函数来自旋转，而双曲函数来自 $e^x$ 与 $e^{-x}$ 的线性组合。对复合输入 $g(x)$，还要乘 $g'(x)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second identity has no minus sign, unlike the circular cosine derivative. The difference follows from the exponential definitions. For a composite input $g(x)$, the chain rule contributes the additional factor $g'(x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $d[\cosh(3x)]/dx$ 是什么？
>
> **答案：** $3\sinh(3x)$；外层导数给 $\sinh$，内层导数再给因子 $3$。
