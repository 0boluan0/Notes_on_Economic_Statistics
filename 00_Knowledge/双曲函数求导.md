# Derivatives of hyperbolic functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*双曲函数求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic [[双曲函数|hyperbolic functions]] exchange roles when differentiated: the slope of $\sinh x$ is $\cosh x$, and the slope of $\cosh x$ is $\sinh x$. For example, at zero the first graph has slope one, while the second has slope zero. These rates follow from the two exponential terms in each definition, so the signs can be derived instead of memorised by analogy with ordinary trigonometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本[[双曲函数]]求导时会互换角色：$\sinh x$ 的斜率是 $\cosh x$，而 $\cosh x$ 的斜率是 $\sinh x$。例如在零点，前一条曲线的斜率为一，后一条为零。这些变化率来自定义中的两个指数项，因此可以直接推导符号，而不必照搬普通三角函数的记忆。

> [!abstract] The two basic derivatives · 两条基本导数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both functions are differentiable at every real $x$, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个函数在每个实数 $x$ 处都可导，且
>
> $$\frac d{dx}\sinh x=\cosh x,\qquad
> \frac d{dx}\cosh x=\sinh x.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The notation $d/dx$ means the [[导数|rate of change with respect to the input $x$]]. There is no minus sign in the second formula.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 记号 $d/dx$ 表示[[导数|相对于输入 $x$ 的变化率]]。第二条公式没有负号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Follow both signs in the exponential definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*跟踪指数定义里的两个负号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[指数函数求导|exponential derivative]] gives $(e^x)'=e^x$. For $e^{-x}$, the inner input is $-x$, whose derivative is $-1$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] therefore gives $(e^{-x})'=-e^{-x}$. Now the [[导数线性法则|linearity of differentiation]] allows the two terms to be differentiated separately while keeping their constant weights $1/2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[指数函数求导]]给出 $(e^x)'=e^x$。对 $e^{-x}$，内层输入是 $-x$，其导数为 $-1$；因此[[链式法则]]给出 $(e^{-x})'=-e^{-x}$。再用[[导数线性法则]]分别求两项的导数，同时保留常数系数 $1/2$：

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac d{dx}\sinh x
&=\frac d{dx}\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2
=\frac{e^x-(-e^{-x})}{2}
=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2=\cosh x,\\
\frac d{dx}\cosh x
&=\frac d{dx}\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2
=\frac{e^x+(-e^{-x})}{2}
=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2=\sinh x.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\sinh$, subtracting the derivative of $e^{-x}$ cancels two minus signs. For $\cosh$, the original plus sign leaves the inner derivative's minus sign intact. This is the entire sign calculation. In contrast, [[正弦余弦求导|ordinary cosine]] has derivative $-\sin x$; similar function names do not transfer that sign here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\sinh$ 中，减去 $e^{-x}$ 的导数使两个负号相消；在 $\cosh$ 中，原来的加号让内层求导产生的负号保留下来。这就是全部的符号计算。相较之下，[[正弦余弦求导|普通余弦]]的导数是 $-\sin x$，不能因为函数名字相近，就把那个负号移到这里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formulas also explain the shapes. Since $\cosh x\ge1$, the [[导数单调性判据|positive-derivative criterion]] makes $\sinh$ strictly increasing. Since $\sinh x$ is negative for $x<0$ and positive for $x>0$, $\cosh$ decreases to its value one at zero and then increases. Differentiating each formula once more gives $(\sinh x)''=\sinh x$ and $(\cosh x)''=\cosh x$: both solve the differential equation $y''=y$, which asks for a function whose second derivative equals itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公式也解释了图像形状。由于 $\cosh x\ge1$，[[导数单调性判据|导数为正的判据]]说明 $\sinh$ 严格递增。$\sinh x$ 在 $x<0$ 时为负、$x>0$ 时为正，因此 $\cosh$ 先递减到零点处的最小值一，再递增。再各求导一次，得到 $(\sinh x)''=\sinh x$ 与 $(\cosh x)''=\cosh x$。两者都满足微分方程 $y''=y$，即“二阶导数等于自身”的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A changing inner input contributes another factor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*内层输入的变化还会贡献一个因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a real-valued function $g$ is differentiable at a point $x$, the chain rule at that point gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若实值函数 $g$ 在某点 $x$ 可导，链式法则在该点给出

$$\frac d{dx}\sinh(g(x))=\cosh(g(x))g'(x),\qquad
\frac d{dx}\cosh(g(x))=\sinh(g(x))g'(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The outer hyperbolic functions accept every real input; any domain restriction therefore comes from the chosen $g$. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外层双曲函数接受全部实数输入，具体的定义域限制因而来自所选的 $g$。例如，

$$\frac d{dx}\cosh(3x)=3\sinh(3x),\qquad
\frac d{dx}\sinh(x^2)=2x\cosh(x^2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the first calculation, the inner change is three units per unit of $x$; in the second it is $2x$. For $\cosh(\ln x)$, first require $x>0$. The [[对数函数求导|logarithmic derivative]] then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一式的内层每单位 $x$ 改变三单位，第二式的内层变化率是 $2x$。对 $\cosh(\ln x)$，首先要求 $x>0$，然后由[[对数函数求导]]得到

$$\frac d{dx}\cosh(\ln x)=\frac{\sinh(\ln x)}x
=\frac{1-x^{-2}}2,\qquad x>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last identity can also be checked by first writing $\cosh(\ln x)=(x+x^{-1})/2$. Although the derivative expression exists for negative $x$ too, it does not extend the original logarithmic composition there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个等式也可先写 $\cosh(\ln x)=(x+x^{-1})/2$ 再求导来核对。虽然最后的导数表达式在负 $x$ 也能计算，却不能据此把原来的对数复合函数延伸过去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same basic pair yields derivatives of ratios. The definitions $\tanh x=\sinh x/\cosh x$ and $\operatorname{sech}x=1/\cosh x$ have nonzero denominator for all real $x$. Applying the [[商法则|quotient rule]] and the identity $\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两条基本公式还可用于比值求导。$\tanh x=\sinh x/\cosh x$ 与 $\operatorname{sech}x=1/\cosh x$ 的分母对所有实数都非零。使用[[商法则]]与恒等式 $\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1$，得到

$$\begin{aligned}
(\tanh x)'&=\frac{\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x}{\cosh^2x}
=\frac1{\cosh^2x}=\operatorname{sech}^2x,\\
(\operatorname{sech}x)'&=-\frac{\sinh x}{\cosh^2x}
=-\operatorname{sech}x\,\tanh x.
\end{aligned}$$

> [!question]- Which operation comes last? · 最外层是什么运算？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Do $\sinh(x^2)$ and $(\sinh x)^2$ have the same derivative? No. The first squares the input, then applies $\sinh$, giving $2x\cosh(x^2)$. The second applies $\sinh$, then squares its output; the chain rule gives $2\sinh x\cosh x$. Parentheses determine which function is differentiated first.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\sinh(x^2)$ 与 $(\sinh x)^2$ 的导数相同吗？不同。前者先把输入平方，再取 $\sinh$，得到 $2x\cosh(x^2)$；后者先取 $\sinh$，再把输出平方，链式法则给出 $2\sinh x\cosh x$。括号决定了先对哪一层函数求导。
