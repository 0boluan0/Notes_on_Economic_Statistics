# Sample path
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*样本路径（样本函数）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **sample path** is one complete sequence or curve that a [[随机过程|stochastic process]] can produce. Think of a model for a queue throughout a day. The process describes all possible queue histories and their probabilities; one realised day's history is a sample path. A few measurements from that day reveal only part of the path if the model is defined at more times than were observed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**样本路径**是一个[[随机过程]]可能产生的某一整串序列或曲线。想象全天排队人数的模型：过程描述所有可能的排队历史及其概率，某一天实际发生的完整历史则是一条样本路径。如果模型定义的时点比实际观测的时点更多，那么这一天的几次测量只显示了路径的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $X_t(\omega)$ for the state at time $t$ under outcome $\omega$. A [[随机变量|random variable]] is obtained by fixing the time and allowing the outcome to vary. A path is obtained by doing the reverse: **fix one outcome and let the index vary**. Formally,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $X_t(\omega)$ 表示结果 $\omega$ 下时点 $t$ 的状态。固定时间、让结果变化，得到一个[[随机变量]]；路径的读法正好反过来：**固定一个结果，让指标变化**。正式写成

$$x_\omega:T\longrightarrow S,\qquad x_\omega(t)=X_t(\omega).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $T$ is the index set and $S$ the state space. Once $\omega$ has been fixed, $x_\omega$ is an ordinary deterministic function. The uncertainty is about which function the experiment produces. Different outcomes can produce the same path; a path need not identify every detail of the underlying outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $T$ 是指标集，$S$ 是状态空间。一旦固定 $\omega$，$x_\omega$ 就是一条普通的确定性函数；不确定的是实验最终产生哪条函数。不同结果也可能产生同一路径，所以路径未必能反推出实验结果的每个细节。

> [!example] A random choice of slope · 随机选择斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Choose $\omega=1$ or $-1$ with equal probability and put $X_t(\omega)=\omega t$ for $0\le t\le1$. There are two possible paths: the rising line $x_1(t)=t$ and the falling line $x_{-1}(t)=-t$, each with probability $1/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等概率选择 $\omega=1$ 或 $-1$，并在 $0\le t\le1$ 上令 $X_t(\omega)=\omega t$。可能的路径有两条：上升直线 $x_1(t)=t$ 与下降直线 $x_{-1}(t)=-t$，各以 $1/2$ 的概率出现。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At the fixed time $t=1/2$, the random variable $X_{1/2}$ is $1/2$ or $-1/2$. After the outcome $\omega=1$ is chosen, however, the path gives $X_0=0$, $X_{1/2}=1/2$ and $X_1=1$ together. These values belong to one line, not to fresh random choices at each time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定时点 $t=1/2$，随机变量 $X_{1/2}$ 取 $1/2$ 或 $-1/2$。但一旦选定 $\omega=1$，路径就同时给出 $X_0=0$、$X_{1/2}=1/2$、$X_1=1$。这些值属于同一条直线，不是在每个时点重新随机选择。

## What can be said about a whole path?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对整条路径可以问什么？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real-valued process indexed by a time interval, one can ask whether its paths are [[一点连续|continuous]], meaning that their values approach the current value as time approaches the current time, or whether they have a [[导数|derivative]], an instantaneous slope. One can also ask for right continuity, or for right continuity together with a finite limit from the left at every time after the left endpoint (including a finite right endpoint); the latter is called **càdlàg**. Finite variation means that the sum of absolute changes over ordered time partitions has a finite supremum on the interval. These describe the whole function, not just its value at one time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对以时间区间为指标的实值过程，可以问路径是否[[一点连续|连续]]，即时间靠近当前时刻时，取值是否也靠近当前值；也可以问是否存在[[导数]]，即瞬时斜率。还可以要求右连续，或要求右连续且除左端点外的每个时刻都有有限左极限（包括有限的右端点），后者称为 **càdlàg**。有限变差则表示：对区间内各种有序时间划分，绝对增量之和的上确界有限。这些性质描述整条函数，而不是某一个时点的取值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous time index does not itself make the paths continuous: a queue length can jump when someone arrives. A model may impose a path property for every outcome or only with probability one; the quantifier must be stated. Even a complete family of finite-dimensional laws does not automatically select a continuous version of a continuous-time process; [[有限维分布不定路径正则性|path regularity needs an additional path-space or version argument]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间指标连续，本身并不让路径连续：有人到达时，排队人数可以跳变。模型可能要求每个结果的路径都满足某个性质，也可能只要求它以概率一成立，必须说清量词。即使知道连续时间过程的全部有限维分布，也没有自动选定一个连续版本；[[有限维分布不定路径正则性|路径正则性还需要路径空间或版本方面的论证]]。

> [!tip] One observation is not the probability model · 一次实现不是全部概率规律
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Observing the rising line in the example does not reveal whether its probability was $1/2$, $1/10$ or another positive value. All those models could produce that same observation. A path records what happened; the process law describes probabilities across possible paths. Estimating a law from observations requires additional assumptions and statistical reasoning.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 观察到例子中的上升直线，并不能告诉你它出现的概率原本是 $1/2$、$1/10$，还是别的正数。这些模型都可能产生同一次观测。路径记录发生了什么；过程律描述各种可能路径的概率。要从观测估计概率律，还需要额外假设和统计推理。
