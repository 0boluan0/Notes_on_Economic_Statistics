# 正定且仅有预算约束模型的整条最小方差边界由两个不同目标收益的边界组合仿射生成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With positive-definite covariance and only a budget constraint, two frontier portfolios with different target means affinely generate the entire minimum-variance frontier.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $\Sigma\succ0$，$\mu$ 不与 $\mathbf1$ 成比例；只要求完全投资 $\mathbf1^Tw=1$，允许卖空。任选两个不同目标 $r_1\ne r_2$ 的[[目标收益最小方差解]] $w(r_1),w(r_2)$，则任意实数 $\alpha$ 都满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $\Sigma\succ0$, $\mu$ is not proportional to $\mathbf1$, and only full investment $\mathbf1^Tw=1$ is required, with short selling allowed. Choose [[目标收益最小方差解|minimum-variance solutions]] at two distinct targets $r_1\ne r_2$. For every real $\alpha$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\alpha w(r_1)+(1-\alpha)w(r_2)
=w\!\left(\alpha r_1+(1-\alpha)r_2\right).
$$

给定任意目标 $r$，取 $\alpha=(r-r_2)/(r_1-r_2)$ 就得到其最小方差解。这是两个风险资产基金对整条边界的生成性质；它不要求两个基金分别代表股票和债券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any target $r$, choosing $\alpha=(r-r_2)/(r_1-r_2)$ recovers its minimum-variance solution. This is a spanning property of two risky-asset funds; the two funds need not represent stocks and bonds separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一行证明与两个边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A one-line proof and two boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

闭式解可以写成 $w(r)=g+rh$，其中 $g,h$ 不依赖目标。于是 $\alpha(g+r_1h)+(1-\alpha)(g+r_2h)=g+[\alpha r_1+(1-\alpha)r_2]h$，正是上式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The closed-form solution is $w(r)=g+rh$, with $g,h$ independent of the target. Expanding $\alpha(g+r_1h)+(1-\alpha)(g+r_2h)$ directly gives the identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里是**仿射组合**，不是只准 $0\le\alpha\le1$ 的凸组合。凸组合只能覆盖两个基金收益之间的一段；目标在区间外时，需要对其中一个基金取负权重。并且生成的是整条最小方差边界，是否有效仍须检查[[最小方差边界的有效上支|目标不低于 GMV 收益]]。有卖空约束时，上述任意实数混合可能不可行，全局双基金生成也不再由此定理保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an affine combination, not a convex combination restricted to $0\le\alpha\le1$. Convex mixtures cover only the interval between the two means; extrapolation requires a negative fund weight. The theorem spans the entire minimum-variance frontier; efficiency still requires [[最小方差边界的有效上支|a mean at least as high as GMV]]. Short-sale restrictions can make the mixtures infeasible, so this theorem no longer guarantees global two-fund spanning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^T$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$ 为例，取 $w(0.09)=(0.75,0.25)^T$ 与 $w(0.10)=(0.5,0.5)^T$。目标 $8\%$ 需要 $2w(0.09)-w(0.10)=(1,0)^T$；它在边界上，但位于 GMV 以下，因而不有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the stated inputs, choose $w(0.09)=(0.75,0.25)^T$ and $w(0.10)=(0.5,0.5)^T$. Target $8\%$ requires $2w(0.09)-w(0.10)=(1,0)^T$. It lies on the frontier but below the GMV mean, so it is inefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Sigman, *Fund theorems*, PDF p.1, §1.1](https://www.columbia.edu/~ks20/FE-Notes/4700-07-Notes-funds.pdf#page=1)：支持两个不同目标解通过任意实数混合生成目标解。
- [Zivot, *Portfolio Theory with Matrix Algebra*, PDF pp.17–18, Proposition 9](https://faculty.washington.edu/ezivot/econ424/portfolioTheoryMatrix.pdf#page=17)：支持仿射恒等式、线性闭式证明及有效性筛选；p.16 的概述使用 “convex combination”，但 p.17 允许任意 $\alpha$，本卡按精确命题写为仿射组合。例子独立核算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Sigman, p.1, §1.1, supports generating target solutions by arbitrary real mixtures of two distinct-target solutions.
- Zivot, pp.17–18, Proposition 9, supports the affine identity, its closed-form proof, and the efficiency filter. Although the p.16 introduction says “convex combination,” p.17 permits arbitrary $\alpha$; this note follows the precise affine statement. The example is independently verified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
