# 多维贫困是结合多个生活维度的不足来识别和评价的贫困

多维贫困（multidimensional poverty）是结合教育、健康、居住条件等多个生活维度的不足，按照明确规则识别和评价的贫困，而不只以单一收入或消费是否低于货币贫困线判断。它要求说明选了什么维度、怎样判断不足、以个人还是家庭为识别单位，以及怎样把多项信息合成贫困判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multidimensional poverty is identified and assessed by combining shortfalls across several aspects of life, such as education, health and housing, under explicit rules, rather than relying solely on an income or consumption poverty line. Its measurement must state the selected dimensions, deprivation criteria, individual or household identification unit, and rule for combining information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种常见方法先逐项识别剥夺，再依剥夺项数或加权分数判断是否贫困。单项剥夺不必自动等于多维贫困：只要任一项不足即算贫困、全部项都不足才算贫困，以及二者之间的门槛，给出的是不同识别规则。[[全球多维贫困指数]]是其中一套有具体指标、权重和门槛的制度化方案，不是所有多维贫困测量的统称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common approach first identifies deprivations indicator by indicator, then applies a count or weighted-score criterion. One deprivation need not automatically identify someone as multidimensionally poor. Requiring any deprivation, every deprivation or an intermediate threshold produces different identification rules. The [[全球多维贫困指数|global Multidimensional Poverty Index]] is one specified system of indicators, weights and cutoffs, not a generic name for all multidimensional measurement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要看剥夺是否落在同一人身上。设两名等权居民、两个维度，1 表示不足：甲地区的记录为 $(1,1)$、$(0,0)$；乙地区为 $(1,0)$、$(0,1)$。两地每个维度的不足比例都为 50%，但同时两项不足者在甲为 50%、乙为 0%。若本例事先规定“两项都不足才算贫困”，两地贫困比例因此不同。不能从各维度分别的比例恢复重叠情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The overlap of deprivations also matters. With two equally weighted people and two dimensions, let 1 indicate deprivation. Area A has profiles $(1,1)$ and $(0,0)$; area B has $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. Each area's deprivation rate is 50% in each dimension, yet simultaneous deprivation is 50% in A and zero in B. Under a preselected rule requiring both deprivations, their poverty rates differ. Separate dimensional rates cannot recover the overlap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币贫困与多维贫困可补充使用，不要求识别出完全同一群人；货币指标的收入、消费与人口换算仍须独立确定，见[[贫困测量的福利口径]]。一个地区平均教育或平均健康较好，也不足以证明所有人都未陷入多重剥夺。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monetary and multidimensional poverty can complement each other without identifying exactly the same people. Income, consumption and population conventions for monetary indicators must still be specified independently; see [[贫困测量的福利口径|welfare-measure conventions]]. Strong average education or health outcomes do not establish that nobody experiences overlapping deprivation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
