# 消元计算行列式要先化为三角矩阵并还原每次变号或缩放
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Computing a determinant by elimination requires triangular reduction and reversal of every sign or scale change*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 计算流程
> 计算方阵 $A$ 的行列式时，先用初等行操作把它化为三角矩阵 $U$，再用[[三角矩阵行列式|对角元乘积]]读取 $\det U$，最后反向还原每次换行和行缩放造成的因子。只做倍加行和换行时，若共换行 $s$ 次，则
> $$
> \det A=(-1)^s\prod_{i=1}^n u_{ii}.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Reduce a square matrix to triangular form, read the determinant of the triangular result from its diagonal, and then reverse every determinant-changing row operation. If elimination uses only row replacements and $s$ row exchanges, then $\det A=(-1)^s\prod_i u_{ii}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个方法把结构判断和计算放在同一条链上：零主元若最终造成零行，对角乘积立即为零；非零主元则给出完整的缩放因子。实际数值算法通常由 LU 分解完成同一件事。若 $PA=LU$，其中 $L$ 的对角线全为 $1$，则
$$
\det A=\det(P)\prod_i u_{ii},
$$
而 $\det(P)$ 由置换的奇偶性决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This method combines structural diagnosis with computation. A missing pivot that produces a zero row makes the diagonal product zero; nonzero pivots provide all remaining factors. In an LU factorization $PA=LU$ with unit diagonal in $L$, $\det A=\det(P)\prod_i u_{ii}$, where the sign of $\det(P)$ is determined by the row permutation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example

对
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\2&5&1\\0&1&3\end{bmatrix},
$$
先做 $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$，再做 $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2$，得到对角线为 $1,1,2$ 的上三角矩阵。两步都是倍加行，没有换行或缩放，所以 $\det A=1\cdot1\cdot2=2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the displayed matrix, use $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ and then $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2$. Both are determinant-preserving row replacements, leaving an upper-triangular matrix with diagonal $1,1,2$. Hence $\det A=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 不能只乘消元后矩阵的对角元而忽略中途的换行或归一化。
- [[余子式展开]]在稀疏小矩阵上可能更短；稠密大矩阵通常优先消元，而不是递归展开。
- 浮点运算中计算出来的极小非零 determinant 不是可靠的秩判定器；数值秩应结合奇异值和容差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Multiplying the final diagonal without accounting for row exchanges or normalization gives the determinant of the transformed matrix, not necessarily of the original one.
- Cofactor expansion can be shorter for a small sparse matrix; dense large matrices normally favour elimination.
- In floating-point arithmetic, a tiny computed determinant is not a reliable numerical-rank test. Singular values and a stated tolerance are more appropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 消元时交换一次行，并把另一行除以 $4$ 后得到对角乘积 $6$。原矩阵行列式是多少？
>
> **答案：** 交换贡献 $-1$，除以 $4$ 使新矩阵行列式变为原值的 $1/4$，所以 $6=-\det(A)/4$，原值为 $-24$。
