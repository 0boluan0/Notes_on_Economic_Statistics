# Determinant by elimination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*消元计算行列式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To compute a determinant by elimination, simplify the matrix while keeping a record of how each operation changes its determinant. [[高斯消元|Gaussian elimination]] clears entries below successive leading positions. Once the result is upper triangular, its [[三角矩阵行列式|determinant is the product of its diagonal entries]]. Undo the recorded factors to recover the original determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
用消元计算行列式，就是一边简化矩阵，一边记录每种操作怎样改变行列式。[[高斯消元]]逐步清除首项位置下方的元素。结果成为上三角矩阵后，[[三角矩阵行列式|它的行列式就是全部对角元的乘积]]；最后还原记录的因子，就得到原矩阵的行列式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The method applies to a finite $n\times n$ matrix $A$ over one field, with an integer $n\ge1$; real and complex matrices are included. A [[行列式|determinant]] is defined here for square matrices. Each row operation acts on the current matrix, so later operations use rows already changed by earlier ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里处理同一域上的有限 $n\times n$ 方阵 $A$，其中 $n\ge1$ 为整数，包括实矩阵和复矩阵。本文的[[行列式]]针对方阵定义。每次行操作都作用于当前矩阵，因此后续步骤使用的是已经被前面步骤更新过的行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Worked example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*倍加行的完整算例*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the real matrix below. Write $R_i$ for its current row $i$. Subtract twice the first row from the second, then subtract the updated second row from the third:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
从下面的实矩阵出发，$R_i$ 表示当前的第 $i$ 行。先从第二行减去第一行的两倍，再从第三行减去更新后的第二行：

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\2&5&1\\0&1&3\end{bmatrix}\\[4pt]
\xrightarrow{R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1}
\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\0&1&1\\0&1&3\end{bmatrix}\\[4pt]
\xrightarrow{R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2}
U=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\0&1&1\\0&0&2\end{bmatrix}.
\end{gathered}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both operations add a multiple of one row to a different row. By the [[行操作对行列式的影响|row-operation rules]], each preserves the determinant. There were no row exchanges or row scalings, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两步都是把一行的倍数加到另一行上。由[[行操作对行列式的影响|行操作规则]]，它们都保持行列式不变。这里没有换行，也没有行缩放，所以

$$
\det A=\det U=1\cdot1\cdot2=2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recording exchanges and scalings
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*记录换行与缩放*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replacing a row by itself plus a multiple of another row preserves the determinant. Exchanging two rows multiplies it by $-1$. Multiplying one row by a scalar $c$ multiplies it by $c$. For a reversible elimination procedure, a row-scaling factor must be nonzero; scaling by zero loses information about the original matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一行加上另一行的倍数，行列式不变；交换两行，行列式乘以 $-1$；把一行乘以标量 $c$，行列式也乘以 $c$。为了能够反向还原，消元过程中的行缩放因子必须非零；乘以零会丢失原矩阵的信息。

> [!important] Keep the whole multiplier / 保留完整的乘数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose elimination gives an upper-triangular matrix $U=(u_{ij})$, using $s$ row exchanges, $k$ row scalings with nonzero factors $c_1,\ldots,c_k$, and any number of row replacements. Here $s,k$ are nonnegative integers, and $u_{ii}$ is the $i$th diagonal entry of $U$. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 设消元得到上三角矩阵 $U=(u_{ij})$，过程中进行了 $s$ 次换行、$k$ 次非零因子为 $c_1,\ldots,c_k$ 的行缩放，以及任意次数的倍加行；$s,k$ 是非负整数，$u_{ii}$ 是 $U$ 的第 $i$ 个对角元。则
>
> $$
> \det U=(-1)^s\left(\prod_{j=1}^{k}c_j\right)\det A,
> \qquad
> \det A=\frac{(-1)^s\prod_{i=1}^{n}u_{ii}}
> {\prod_{j=1}^{k}c_j}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $\prod$ denotes a product; if there are no row scalings, its denominator is the empty product $1$. In that common case, $\det A=(-1)^s\prod_i u_{ii}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $\prod$ 表示连乘；没有行缩放时，分母是空乘积 $1$。此时常用公式为 $\det A=(-1)^s\prod_i u_{ii}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula follows by multiplying the effect of each operation. Every $c_j$ is nonzero, so division by their product is valid. No diagonal entry of $U$ is being divided out; a singular final matrix is allowed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
公式来自把各次操作的影响相乘。每个 $c_j$ 都非零，因此可以除以它们的乘积。这里并没有除以 $U$ 的任何对角元，所以最终矩阵奇异也可以使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, exchange the rows of a real $2\times2$ matrix and then divide its new first row by $3$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，对一个实 $2\times2$ 矩阵先交换两行，再把新的第一行除以 $3$：

$$
\begin{bmatrix}0&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}
\longrightarrow
\begin{bmatrix}3&4\\0&2\end{bmatrix}
\longrightarrow
\begin{bmatrix}1&4/3\\0&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The final diagonal product is $2$, but the determinant has been multiplied by $(-1)(1/3)$. Hence the original determinant is $2/(-1/3)=-6$, agreeing with $0\cdot4-2\cdot3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
最后的对角乘积是 $2$，但整个过程把行列式乘以了 $(-1)(1/3)$，所以原值为 $2/(-1/3)=-6$，与 $0\cdot4-2\cdot3$ 一致。

> [!question]- Check the bookkeeping / 检查记账
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over the real numbers, one row exchange and division of another row by $4$ lead to a diagonal product of $6$. What was the original determinant?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 对实矩阵交换一次行，并把另一行除以 $4$ 后，对角乘积为 $6$。原行列式是多少？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The relation is $6=-\det(A)/4$, so $\det A=-24$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 关系是 $6=-\det(A)/4$，所以 $\det A=-24$。

## Boundaries and the LU connection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero at the next candidate pivot position does not by itself imply a zero determinant. A [[主元|pivot]] is a nonzero leading entry; search below a zero candidate and exchange rows if possible. For example, $\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$ becomes the identity after one exchange, so its determinant is $-1$. If the completed row echelon form of a square matrix has a missing pivot, it has a zero row and hence a zero diagonal product. More generally, once any triangular form is obtained, a zero diagonal entry already makes its determinant zero, even if it has no zero row.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
下一候选主元位置为零，并不能单独推出行列式为零。[[主元]]指非零首项；遇到零候选值时，应向下寻找可用的非零元素，并在可能时交换行。例如，$\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$ 交换一次行后成为单位矩阵，因此行列式为 $-1$。若方阵完成行阶梯化后缺少主元，就会出现零行，对角乘积也为零。更一般地，只要已得到三角矩阵，任一对角元为零就足以令行列式为零，即使尚未出现零行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same bookkeeping can be stored in an [[消元与LU分解|LU factorization]] $PA=LU$. Here $P$ is a [[置换矩阵|permutation matrix]] recording row exchanges, $L$ is lower triangular with every diagonal entry equal to $1$, and $U$ is upper triangular. Using [[行列式乘法性|multiplicativity of the determinant]] and the triangular rule,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
同样的记账可以保存在 [[消元与LU分解|LU 分解]] $PA=LU$ 中。其中 $P$ 是记录换行的[[置换矩阵]]，$L$ 是全部对角元为 $1$ 的下三角矩阵，$U$ 是上三角矩阵。由[[行列式乘法性]]及三角矩阵公式，

$$
\det(P)\det(A)=\det(L)\det(U)=\prod_{i=1}^{n}u_{ii},
\qquad
\det(A)=\det(P)\prod_{i=1}^{n}u_{ii}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The recovery step uses $\det(P)=(-1)^s$ for a representation by $s$ row exchanges, so $\det(P)^{-1}=\det(P)$. Include $P$ when exchanges occur; the formula does not require $U$ to be invertible. This is a way to reuse a factorization, not an additional assumption in the earlier row-by-row proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
最后一步用到：若 $P$ 由 $s$ 次换行表示，则 $\det(P)=(-1)^s$，从而 $\det(P)^{-1}=\det(P)$。发生换行时要保留 $P$；公式不要求 $U$ 可逆。这是复用分解的方式，不是前面逐次行操作证明所需的额外假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a small matrix with many zeros, [[余子式展开|cofactor expansion]] may be shorter. Dense large matrices usually favour elimination over recursive expansion. In floating-point work, distinguish the exact identity from [[数值秩|numerical rank]], which depends on scale and a stated tolerance. Even the exact matrix $\begin{bmatrix}\varepsilon&0\\0&\varepsilon\end{bmatrix}$ has determinant $\varepsilon^2$ and full rank whenever the real number $\varepsilon\ne0$. Its determinant can be arbitrarily small merely because every entry was scaled down. A tiny computed determinant alone is therefore not a reliable rank decision; singular values and an appropriate tolerance address a different, numerical question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对于零元素很多的小矩阵，[[余子式展开]]可能更短；稠密大矩阵通常适合消元，而非递归展开。浮点计算中，应区分精确恒等式与依赖尺度和容差的[[数值秩]]。即使在精确计算中，矩阵 $\begin{bmatrix}\varepsilon&0\\0&\varepsilon\end{bmatrix}$ 在实数 $\varepsilon\ne0$ 时也是满秩的，行列式却为 $\varepsilon^2$，可以仅因所有元素一同缩小而任意小。因此，单凭一个很小的浮点行列式不足以可靠地判秩；奇异值与合适的容差回答的是另外一个数值问题。
