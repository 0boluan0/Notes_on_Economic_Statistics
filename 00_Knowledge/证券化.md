# 证券化是以一组金融资产的现金流支持向投资者发行证券的融资结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securitisation issues securities to investors whose payments are supported by cash flows from a pool of financial assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 证券化通常把住房按揭、汽车贷款、信用卡应收款或其他会产生现金流的资产转入专门载体，由载体向投资者发行证券。投资者的本息回收主要取决于底层资产池的表现，而不只是发起人的一般偿债承诺。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Securitisation usually transfers mortgages, auto loans, credit-card receivables, or other cash-flow-producing assets to a special-purpose vehicle, which issues securities to investors. Investor payments depend primarily on the performance of the underlying pool rather than solely on the originator's general promise to repay.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条最小链条是：贷款发起人建立资产池，载体买入资产并发行证券，服务机构收取借款人现金流，再按契约顺序支付投资者。证券可被分成先后承担损失的档次，但“分档”是很多监管定义的关键条件，并不意味所有宽泛的资产支持证券都在每个语境下共享完全相同的法律范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal chain runs from an originator that forms a pool, to a vehicle that buys the assets and issues securities, to a servicer that collects borrower cash flows and distributes them under the contractual payment order. Securities may be tranched by loss priority. Tranching is central to many regulatory definitions, but the legal perimeter of “securitisation” must still be checked in the relevant rulebook.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它改变了什么

证券化可以把难以单独交易的贷款现金流转成可交易证券，并在发起、筛选、服务、融资和风险承担之间重新分工。它不自动证明贷款已经会计终止确认、信用风险已实质转移，或发起人已获得监管资本减免；这些是需要另行核对的判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securitisation can turn otherwise illiquid loan cash flows into tradable securities and redistribute origination, screening, servicing, funding, and risk bearing across institutions. It does not by itself establish accounting derecognition, substantive credit-risk transfer, or regulatory-capital relief; each requires a separate test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一组房贷被转入 SPV，SPV 向投资者发行由房贷回款支持的证券。这一结构中，投资者回款的直接经济来源是什么？
>
> **答案：** 底层房贷资产池产生的本息现金流，再由载体按合同顺序分配给投资者。

## 来源与核验

- [Joint Forum, *Report on Asset Securitisation Incentives*](https://www.bis.org/publ/joint26.pdf)：核对专门载体发行证券，付款由金融资产池现金流支持的广义定义。
- [Basel Framework, CRE 40](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/cre/40.htm)：核对传统和合成证券化、风险分档及监管定义的经济实质边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The Joint Forum report was checked for the broad asset-pool, special-purpose-entity, and cash-flow-backed securities definition.
- Basel CRE 40 was checked for traditional and synthetic securitisation, tranching, and the economic-substance boundary of the regulatory definition.
