# 老年消费由劳动收入、净公共与私人转移以及资产收入减储蓄共同筹资

分析老年经济支持，先问消费由谁提供资源，而不是把全部消费归给政府。按国民转移账户的当期流量口径，同一老年年龄组可以同时获得劳动收入、公共转移、私人转移和资产收入，也可以继续储蓄或动用储蓄。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Analysis of old-age support asks who supplies resources for consumption rather than assigning all consumption to government. Under the current-flow conventions of National Transfer Accounts, the same age group may receive labour income, public transfers, private transfers and asset income while saving or dissaving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一时期、同一年龄组，以总量或一致的人均值记账：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same period and age group, using totals or consistently defined per-capita amounts,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C=Y^L+T^G+T^F+Y^A-S,
\qquad
C-Y^L=T^G+T^F+(Y^A-S).
$$

$T^G$、$T^F$ 分别是公共、私人转移流入减流出，私人转移包括家庭内部及家庭之间；$Y^A$ 是资产收入，$S$ 是储蓄。正储蓄减少可用于当前消费的资源，负储蓄则增加。资产重估收益或一次卖出资产的全部价款，不能额外当作当期资产收入再加一遍；用于消费的净资产动用通过储蓄项体现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$T^G$ and $T^F$ are public and private transfer inflows minus outflows; private transfers include within- and between-household flows. $Y^A$ is asset income and $S$ saving. Positive saving reduces resources for current consumption; negative saving increases them. Asset revaluation gains or the full proceeds of an asset sale cannot be added again as current asset income. Net asset use for consumption is reflected through saving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如人均消费 100、劳动收入 20、净公共转移 40、净私人转移 10、资产收入 20、储蓄 $-10$，则 $20+40+10+20-(-10)=100$。[[生命周期赤字]]为 80，但其中净公共转移只是 40，不能把 80 全称为财政负担。公共实物消费须按相同账户口径计入消费与对应公共转移，不能漏记或重复计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consumption of 100 can be funded by labour income of 20, net public transfers of 40, net private transfers of 10, asset income of 20 and saving of $-10$: $20+40+10+20-(-10)=100$. The [[生命周期赤字|life cycle deficit]] is 80, but net public transfers account for only 40. Public in-kind consumption must be recorded consistently in consumption and the corresponding public transfer, without omission or duplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个账户解释资源的分配与筹资，不单独证明资源配置有效或预算可持续。[[转移支付核算]]解释同一福利边界下的转移抵消，但不能因此忽略付款方的财政约束。公共收支的年龄结构判断进一步使用[[财政供养比]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The account explains allocation and financing, not efficiency or fiscal sustainability by itself. [[转移支付核算|Transfer-payment accounting]] explains cancellation within a common welfare boundary, without removing the payer’s budget constraint. Use the [[财政供养比|fiscal support ratio]] for the age structure of public inflows and outflows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [UN，NTA Manual（2013）](https://ntaccounts.org/doc/repository/NTA%20manual%202013.pdf#page=46)，印刷第29页式(2.1)–(2.2)、第31–32页：当期账户、公共／私人转移与资产再配置；第106页：赤字可由资产筹资而非必定依赖他人。构造例采用净转移和负储蓄；不把资产价格变动收入化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NTA Manual supports current-flow accounting, public and private transfers, asset-based reallocations and asset financing without necessary dependence on others. The constructed example uses net transfers and negative saving, not asset-price gains as income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
