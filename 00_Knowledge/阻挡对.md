# Blocking pairs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*阻挡对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An assignment may give everyone a partner yet leave two people who would both rather be paired with each other. A **blocking pair** identifies those two people. They are not currently partners, and each strictly prefers the other to their own assigned partner. It is a test of two individual preferences against a particular assignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一份分配可以让人人都有伙伴，却仍留下两个人都更愿意选择彼此。**阻挡对**就是这两个人：他们当前没有配给彼此，而且双方都严格更喜欢对方，而不是自己的现任伙伴。这个判断要把两个人各自的偏好，与一份具体分配比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The model and the current partners
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*模型与当前伙伴*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here we use the finite one-to-one stable-marriage model. Two disjoint sides $L$ and $R$ each contain $n$ participants, where $n$ is a nonnegative integer. Every participant ranks **all** members of the opposite side in a strict order: no ties and no omitted or unacceptable candidates. Each list is that participant's own ranking; the rankings need not agree or be symmetric.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
本卡采用有限、一对一的稳定婚姻模型。两个不交的参与者集合 $L,R$ 各有 $n$ 人，$n$ 为非负整数。每个人都把另一侧的**全部**成员排成严格顺序：没有并列，也没有遗漏或不可接受的对象。每份清单属于该参与者本人，不要求不同人的排序一致，更不要求双方互相对称地喜欢。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a feasible [[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|perfect matching]] $M$: it uses only cross-side pairs and gives every participant exactly one partner, with no repeated endpoint. Thus it is also a [[匹配定义|matching]] in the ordinary graph sense. Write $p_M(v)$ for the partner assigned to participant $v$. If $v\in L$, then $p_M(v)\in R$, and conversely. The subscript reminds us that partners depend on $M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
固定一个可行的[[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|完美匹配]] $M$：只在两侧之间配对，每人恰好有一个伙伴，不重复使用端点。因此它首先也是普通图意义下的[[匹配定义|匹配]]。用 $p_M(v)$ 表示分配给参与者 $v$ 的伙伴；$v\in L$ 时有 $p_M(v)\in R$，反之亦然。下标 $M$ 提醒我们，伙伴取决于当前分配。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two candidates $u,w$ on the side opposite $v$, the notation $u\succ_v w$ means “participant $v$ strictly prefers $u$ to $w$.” The person making the comparison is the subscript, not either candidate. For example, $y\succ_a x$ says that $a$ ranks $y$ ahead of $x$; it says nothing about how $y$ ranks $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若候选人 $u,w$ 都位于 $v$ 的另一侧，记号 $u\succ_v w$ 表示“参与者 $v$ 严格更喜欢 $u$ 而不是 $w$”。作比较的人是下标，而不是两个候选人。例如 $y\succ_a x$ 表示 $a$ 把 $y$ 排在 $x$ 前面，并没有说明 $y$ 怎样评价 $a$。

> [!important] Both comparisons must hold · 两边的比较必须同时成立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A cross-side pair $(\ell,r)\in L\times R$ **blocks $M$** when $\{\ell,r\}\notin M$ and
> $$r\succ_\ell p_M(\ell)\qquad\text{and}\qquad
> \ell\succ_r p_M(r).$$
> Each participant compares the proposed partner with their own current partner. “Blocking pair” and “rogue pair” name the same concept here.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 跨侧的一对 $(\ell,r)\in L\times R$ **阻挡 $M$**，是指 $\{\ell,r\}\notin M$，并且
> $$r\succ_\ell p_M(\ell)\qquad\text{且}\qquad
> \ell\succ_r p_M(r).$$
> 双方都把拟选择的对象与自己的当前伙伴比较。本卡的 blocking pair 与 rogue pair 是同一概念。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordered notation $(\ell,r)$ only records which participant belongs to which side; it does not make the graph edge directed. Nor does “not matched to each other” mean that either person is unmatched: in this model both already have assigned partners. Replacing the current assignment changes the comparisons even when the preference lists stay fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有序写法 $(\ell,r)$ 只是记录左右角色，不表示图边具有方向。“没有配给彼此”也不意味着其中有人没有匹配：在本模型里，两人已经各有伙伴。即使偏好清单不变，改变当前分配也会改变比较对象。

## A complete four-person example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*四个人的完整偏好例*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $L=\{a,b\}$ and $R=\{x,y\}$. In each row below, the candidate on the left is preferred:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
令 $L=\{a,b\}$、$R=\{x,y\}$。下表每行左边的候选人更受该行参与者偏好：

| Participant / 参与者 | Ranking / 排序 |
|---|---|
| $a$ | $y\succ_a x$ |
| $b$ | $x\succ_b y$ |
| $x$ | $b\succ_x a$ |
| $y$ | $a\succ_y b$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $M=\{\{a,x\},\{b,y\}\}$. It is perfect: all four people appear once. Nevertheless, $(a,y)$ blocks it. Participant $a$ currently has $x$ but prefers $y$; participant $y$ currently has $b$ but prefers $a$. The other unselected cross-pair $(b,x)$ also blocks it. One such pair would already be enough to refute [[稳定匹配|stability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
取 $M=\{\{a,x\},\{b,y\}\}$。它是完美匹配，因为四个人各出现一次。但 $(a,y)$ 阻挡它：$a$ 当前得到 $x$，却更喜欢 $y$；$y$ 当前得到 $b$，却更喜欢 $a$。另一对未选的跨侧组合 $(b,x)$ 也阻挡它；只要发现其中一对，就足以否定[[稳定匹配|稳定性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now change the matching to $M'=\{\{a,y\},\{b,x\}\}$ without changing the lists. The pair $(a,y)$ is now selected, so it is not a blocking pair. The only unselected cross-pairs are $(a,x)$ and $(b,y)$, and neither participant on the left prefers that alternative to their current first choice. Thus neither pair blocks $M'$. Blocking is relative to a matching, not a permanent label on a pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
保持清单不变，改成 $M'=\{\{a,y\},\{b,x\}\}$。$(a,y)$ 现在已经配对，所以不再是阻挡对。剩余未选的跨侧组合只有 $(a,x)$ 与 $(b,y)$，两位左侧参与者都不愿意放弃当前首选去选这个替代对象，因此两对都不阻挡 $M'$。阻挡是相对于一份匹配的判断，不是某一对人永久具有的标签。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One person's wish is insufficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*只有一方想换还不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a separate check, keep the diagonal matching $M$ but change only $y$'s ranking to $b\succ_y a$. Participant $a$ still prefers $y$ to $x$, yet $y$ prefers the current partner $b$ to $a$. The pair $(a,y)$ no longer blocks this matching, because its second comparison fails. This local conclusion does not rule out a different blocking pair elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
另作一次检查：保留对角匹配 $M$，只把 $y$ 的排序改成 $b\succ_y a$。$a$ 仍然更喜欢 $y$ 而不是 $x$，但 $y$ 更喜欢当前伙伴 $b$ 而不是 $a$。此时 $(a,y)$ 不再阻挡该匹配，因为第二个比较不成立；这个局部判断并不排除别处还有其他阻挡对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n=1$, the only cross-side pair is already selected in the only perfect matching, so no blocking pair exists. For $n=0$, there are no pairs to test. Tied rankings, incomplete lists, unmatched participants or multiple places per participant require further model conventions; the two comparisons above are stated for complete strict lists and an already perfect assignment. They do not by themselves define those broader models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$n=1$ 时，唯一的跨侧组合已经在唯一完美匹配中，没有阻挡对；$n=0$ 时，没有任何组合要检查。若允许偏好并列、清单不完整、参与者未匹配或每人有多个名额，就需要补充模型约定。上面的两个比较明确用于完整严格清单与已完美配对的情形，不能单靠它们替那些更广模型作定义。
