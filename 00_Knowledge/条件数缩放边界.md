# Scaling and design condition numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件数缩放边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose income is a **predictor column** in a regression [[设计矩阵|design matrix]]. Expressing it in pounds instead of thousands of pounds multiplies that column by $1000$, while the matching coefficient divides by $1000$. The predictions can stay identical, yet the raw [[设计矩阵条件数|design condition number]] can change greatly. This is a change in coefficient coordinates, not new evidence about the people observed. A condition-number comparison therefore needs a stated matrix, norm and scaling convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设收入是回归[[设计矩阵]]中的一个**预测变量列**。从“千英镑”改成“英镑”，该列乘以 $1000$，相应系数除以 $1000$。预测可以完全相同，原始[[设计矩阵条件数]]却可能明显变化。这改变的是系数坐标，并没有增加有关被观察个体的新证据。因此，比较条件数必须说明所用矩阵、范数及缩放口径。

> [!example] Orthogonal columns with condition number one or one thousand · 正交列也能有不同条件数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take an intercept and a predictor observed at $-1$ and $1$:
>
> $$X=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> X_{\mathrm{new}}=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1000\\1&1000\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> The columns remain [[正交|orthogonal]], meaning their inner product is zero. Their Gram matrices contain the pairwise column dot products; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Since $X^TX=\operatorname{diag}(2,2)$ and $X_{\mathrm{new}}^TX_{\mathrm{new}}=\operatorname{diag}(2,2\times10^6)$, their singular values are respectively $(\sqrt2,\sqrt2)$ and $(1000\sqrt2,\sqrt2)$. The 2-norm condition number changes from $1$ to $1000$. Here $\operatorname{diag}$ means the listed diagonal entries with zeros elsewhere. This example concerns the design's algebra, not an error-variance estimate from two observations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 原设计包含截距和取值为 $-1,1$ 的预测列；新设计把预测列乘以 $1000$。两列仍[[正交]]，即内积为零。Gram 矩阵记录各列两两的点积，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。两者的 Gram 矩阵分别为 $\operatorname{diag}(2,2)$、$\operatorname{diag}(2,2\times10^6)$，奇异值分别为 $(\sqrt2,\sqrt2)$ 和 $(1000\sqrt2,\sqrt2)$，二范数条件数由 $1$ 变为 $1000$。$\operatorname{diag}$ 表示列出的数位于对角线上，其余元素为零。这是设计的代数例子，并非从两个观测估计误差方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the old coefficients are $(a,b)$, the new ones $(a,b/1000)$ give exactly the same fitted values. More generally, $X_{\mathrm{new}}=XD$ for an invertible [[对角矩阵|diagonal scaling matrix]] $D$ preserves the [[列空间|column space]], the set of all fitted vectors, because $Xb=(XD)(D^{-1}b)$. With the same outcome and unweighted least-squares criterion, fitted values and residuals are unchanged. A coefficient and its uncertainty must both be converted back to the same units before comparing precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

旧系数若为 $(a,b)$，新系数 $(a,b/1000)$ 就给出完全相同的拟合值。更一般地，对可逆的[[对角矩阵|对角缩放矩阵]] $D$，$X_{\mathrm{new}}=XD$ 保持[[列空间]]，即全部拟合向量组成的集合，因为 $Xb=(XD)(D^{-1}b)$。若结果变量和不加权最小二乘准则不变，拟合值与残差也不变。比较精度前，必须把系数及其不确定性一起换回相同单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rescaling the **outcome $y$ alone** does not change $X$ and therefore cannot change $\kappa_2(X)$. Multiplying the **whole matrix** by a nonzero scalar $c$ is different again: every singular value gains the same factor $|c|$, so $\kappa_2(cX)=\kappa_2(X)$. It is unequal column scaling that can change their ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果只改变**结果变量 $y$ 的单位**，$X$ 没有变化，$\kappa_2(X)$ 就不可能变化。把**整个矩阵**乘以非零标量 $c$ 又是另一回事：每个奇异值都乘以相同的 $|c|$，因此 $\kappa_2(cX)=\kappa_2(X)$。不同列使用不同缩放，才可能改变其比值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Centering is a separate choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中心化是另一项选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Centering a predictor subtracts its sample mean. With an intercept, $a+bx=(a+b\bar x)+b(x-\bar x)$, so the fitted linear functions are unchanged after adjusting the intercept. The coordinate system and its condition number can nevertheless change. For observations $x=(0,2)^T$, the raw design $[\mathbf1,x]$ has Gram matrix $\begin{bmatrix}2&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$, whose [[特征对|eigenvalues]] are $3+\sqrt5$ and $3-\sqrt5$. Its condition number is $(3+\sqrt5)/2\approx2.618$. Centering gives $x_c=(-1,1)^T$ and the first matrix in the example, with condition number $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测变量中心化，就是减去其样本均值。有截距时，$a+bx=(a+b\bar x)+b(x-\bar x)$，调整截距后可表达的拟合直线不变，但坐标及条件数仍可能变化。若观测为 $x=(0,2)^T$，原设计 $[\mathbf1,x]$ 的 Gram 矩阵为 $\begin{bmatrix}2&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$，[[特征对|特征值]]为 $3+\sqrt5$、$3-\sqrt5$，所以条件数为 $(3+\sqrt5)/2\approx2.618$。中心化后 $x_c=(-1,1)^T$，得到上例第一个矩阵，条件数为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without an intercept, centering can change the fitted space and hence the model. With powers or interactions, preserving the space requires the corresponding lower-order terms and coefficient transformation; see [[中心化共线性边界|the boundary of centering]]. None of these operations creates observations at previously missing predictor combinations. Centering can improve a numerical representation without resolving the scientific uncertainty of a chosen comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有截距时，中心化可能改变拟合空间，从而改变模型。存在幂次项或交互项时，要保持空间，还需保留相应低阶项并变换系数，见[[中心化共线性边界]]。这些操作都不会创造原本缺失的预测变量组合上的观测。中心化可以改善数值表达，却不因此消除某项科学比较的不确定性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Specify what was normalized
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*说明究竟标准化了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unit-length scaling divides each nonzero column by its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]; it need not subtract means. In the two orthogonal designs above, this produces the same normalized columns and condition number $1$. Standardizing nonconstant predictors by their standard deviations, with or without centering, is another convention. Report whether the intercept is retained, adjusted out, or scaled separately. Centering the intercept column itself would turn it into zeros; dividing a constant predictor by its zero standard deviation is undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位长度缩放把每个非零列除以其[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]，并不一定减去均值。上面两个正交设计这样处理后，得到相同的归一化列，条件数都为 $1$。把非常数预测变量除以其标准差，以及是否先中心化，又是另一组约定。应报告截距是保留、先剔除，还是单独缩放。截距列本身若中心化会变成零列；常数预测变量也不能除以为零的标准差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, SAS's COLLIN diagnostic scales the design so that its Gram matrix has unit diagonal; COLLINOINT first adjusts out the intercept. For a specified full-column-rank diagnostic matrix $Z$, let its singular values be $s_1\ge\cdots\ge s_p>0$. The condition indices are $\mathrm{CI}_j=s_1/s_j$, equivalently $\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}/\lambda_j}$ for the Gram eigenvalues $\lambda_j=s_j^2$. The largest index is $\kappa_2(Z)$. It is the condition number of **that diagnostic matrix**, which need not equal that of raw $X$; the Gram matrix's own 2-norm condition number would be its square.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 SAS 的 COLLIN 诊断把设计缩放到 Gram 矩阵对角元全为一；COLLINOINT 则先剔除截距的影响。对指定的满列秩诊断矩阵 $Z$，设奇异值为 $s_1\ge\cdots\ge s_p>0$。条件指数是 $\mathrm{CI}_j=s_1/s_j$；若 Gram 特征值为 $\lambda_j=s_j^2$，也可写成 $\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}/\lambda_j}$。最大指数是 $\kappa_2(Z)$，属于**这个诊断矩阵**，未必等于原始 $X$ 的条件数；Gram 矩阵自身的二范数条件数还要再平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Return from the number to the direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 标量之后还要回到方向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After fixing the convention, inspect a right singular vector for the smallest singular value: its coordinates show which scaled columns, with which signs and relative weights, nearly cancel. Interpret those weights in the chosen units and ask whether the weak combination is relevant to the target. A scalar condition number alone cannot identify the variables, prescribe a deletion, or certify precision. If several singular values are equally small, inspect their subspace rather than treating one arbitrary singular vector as a unique diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定口径后，查看最小奇异值对应的右奇异向量：其坐标显示哪些缩放后的列，以何种符号和相对权重几乎相互抵消。应按所选单位解释这些权重，并判断弱组合是否与目标有关。一个条件数本身不能指出具体变量、指定删项，也不能证明精度足够。若多个奇异值同样小，应查看它们张成的子空间，而不能把某个任意选取的奇异向量当成唯一诊断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[行列式与条件数边界|small determinant]] is no substitute. A rectangular design has no determinant; for a square matrix, the **absolute** determinant is the product of all singular values, while the condition number is their extreme ratio. For the two-dimensional identity matrix $I_2$, the matrix $0.01I_2$ has determinant $0.0001$ but condition number $1$. Reporting the actual matrix, scaling and weak directions makes the diagnostic interpretable without confusing volume, relative conditioning and absolute sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[行列式与条件数边界|小行列式]]不能代替这项诊断。矩形设计没有行列式；方阵的行列式**绝对值**等于全部奇异值之积，条件数则是极端值之比。例如令 $I_2$ 为二阶单位矩阵，则 $0.01I_2$ 的行列式为 $0.0001$，条件数却为 $1$。报告实际矩阵、缩放与弱方向，才能把体积、相对条件性和绝对敏感性区分开。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [LAPACK Users' Guide, Norms and Condition Numbers](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node75.html) and [Singular Value Decomposition](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node32.html): norm and singular-value conventions. Column-unit, global-scale and centering examples are calculated explicitly above.
- [SAS/STAT, REG: Collinearity Diagnostics](https://support.sas.com/documentation/cdl/en/statug/67523/HTML/default/statug_reg_details24.htm): unit-diagonal Gram scaling, COLLIN versus COLLINOINT, and condition indices as square roots of Gram eigenvalue ratios.
- [NIST Dataplot, Regression Diagnostics, Multi-collinearity](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/regrdiag.htm): explicitly scales columns to unit sums of squares. Its abbreviated condition-index steps are not used as a complete formula; the ratio is checked against SAS and the SVD identity.
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/05_多元回归模型的矩阵表达.md#1. 多元线性回归模型的向量表达|Course §1, matrix representation]]: the design and intercept convention. [[行列式与条件数边界|Determinants and condition numbers]] and [[中心化共线性边界|centering]] supply the separate boundaries used here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- LAPACK 范数与 SVD 章节核对范数和奇异值口径；列单位、整体缩放及中心化例子均在正文中显式计算。
- SAS/STAT 共线性诊断核对 Gram 单位对角缩放、COLLIN 与 COLLINOINT 的区别，以及条件指数等于 Gram 特征值比的平方根。
- NIST 共线性部分明确先将列缩放到单位平方和；其简略步骤不作为完整条件指数公式，实际比值另以 SAS 和 SVD 恒等式核对。
- 本地课程矩阵表达第 1 节核对设计与截距约定；相关原子分别支持行列式及中心化的独立边界。
