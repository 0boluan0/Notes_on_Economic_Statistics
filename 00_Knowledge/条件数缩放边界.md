# 设计矩阵的原始条件数会随变量单位和中心化方式改变，因此诊断必须声明缩放口径并检查弱奇异方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A raw design-matrix condition number changes with variable units and centering, so a diagnosis must state the scaling convention and inspect the weak singular direction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 使用边界
> 把收入从“英镑”改成“千英镑”会把对应设计列除以一千，进而改变奇异值和原始条件数，却没有增加或减少任何实质信息。脱离单位和预处理步骤的条件数阈值，不能直接成为共线性结论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Expressing income in pounds rather than thousands of pounds rescales one design column, changes the singular values and raw condition number, but adds no substantive information. A condition-number cutoff detached from units and preprocessing cannot by itself diagnose multicollinearity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归诊断常先把非截距列中心化并按列范数或标准差缩放，再计算奇异值或 condition indices。这样比较的是相对列形状，而不是计量单位大小；但缩放规则仍是分析者选择，必须报告。若包含截距，是否中心化还会影响截距列与其他列的关系，不能把不同软件口径的数值直接横比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regression diagnostics often center non-intercept columns and scale them by column norm or standard deviation before computing singular values or condition indices. This focuses the comparison on relative column geometry rather than measurement units, but the convention remains an analyst choice and must be reported. With an intercept, centering also changes its relation to the other columns, so values from incompatible software conventions are not directly comparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 标量之后还要回到方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Return to the direction after reading the scalar*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使缩放后的条件数很大，下一步也不是删除“尺度最大”的变量，而是检查最小奇异方向中哪些列以什么符号共同出现，并问这个组合是否对应目标比较。条件数只负责发现最弱方向；变量语义、估计目标和样本支持决定它为何弱、是否需要修复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even when a consistently scaled condition number is large, the next step is not to delete the variable with the largest raw magnitude. Inspect which columns enter the weakest singular direction, with what signs, and whether that combination corresponds to the target comparison. The condition number locates weak geometry; variable meaning, estimand, and support explain it and determine the response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同理，[[行列式与条件数边界|小行列式]]也不能替代条件诊断：行列式看所有奇异值的乘积，条件数看最强与最弱方向之比，而且二者都要尊重尺度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same reason, a small determinant cannot replace conditioning diagnostics. A determinant multiplies all singular values, whereas a condition number compares the strongest and weakest directions; both must be interpreted with scale in view.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 同一数据只把工资单位从英镑改为便士，原始条件数变大。能否说数据突然更共线？
>
> **答案：** 不能。信息结构没有改变；应先采用并报告一致的列缩放，再解释弱方向。
