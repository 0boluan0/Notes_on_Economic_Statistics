# 做市商是以自身账户按报价买卖并提供交易对手的交易商

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A market maker is a dealer that trades on its own account at quoted prices and provides a trading counterparty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做市商是经常报出愿意买入或卖出的价格，并以自身账户承接相应交易的交易商。它可以暂时持有库存，把不同时刻到来的买卖需求连接起来。这与仅代客户传递或执行委托、并不以自己作为该笔交易本金承担者的[[证券经纪与自营|经纪角色]]不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A market maker regularly quotes prices at which it is willing to buy or sell and takes the corresponding trades on its own account. It can hold inventory between arrivals of buying and selling interest. This differs from the [[证券经纪与自营|agency role]] of handling client orders without acting as principal to that transaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，做市商按 100 买入一单位后，未必能按原先 100.20 的卖价卖出；若其随后只能按 99.50 平仓，价格损益为 −0.50。[[买卖价差]]不是已锁定的净利润：库存价格变化、交易费用以及与更知情交易者成交的[[逆向选择]]风险都会影响结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After buying one unit at 100, a market maker may fail to sell it at its earlier ask of 100.20. An eventual sale at 99.50 produces a price loss of 0.50. The [[买卖价差|bid–ask spread]] is not locked-in net profit: inventory repricing, expenses and [[逆向选择|adverse selection]] against better-informed counterparties affect the outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做市商也可以通过向[[限价订单簿]]提交买卖单提供流动性，因而不只存在于纯[[报价驱动市场]]。是否必须双边报价、报价多久、承担多少数量和享有什么安排，取决于具体制度或协议；做市身份不保证任何时刻、任何数量均有可执行报价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A market maker can also supply liquidity by submitting orders to a [[限价订单簿|limit order book]], so the role is not confined to purely [[报价驱动市场|quote-driven markets]]. Two-sided quoting duties, duration, quantities and privileges depend on the applicable arrangements. The role does not guarantee executable quotes at every time and for every size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
