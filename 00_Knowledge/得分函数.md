# Score function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*得分函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **score function** tells us which way a model’s log likelihood slopes when we slightly change a parameter while keeping the observed data fixed. A positive score means a small increase in that parameter locally increases the log likelihood; a negative score points the other way. It describes a slope, not a probability or a grade assigned to an observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计中的**得分函数（score function）**描述：保持观测数据不动，把模型参数稍微调大时，对数似然向哪边倾斜。得分为正，表示参数小幅增大时，对数似然在局部增大；得分为负则方向相反。它是一个斜率，不是概率，也不是给观测打的成绩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep three outcomes fixed and move the probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*固定三个结果，改变概率参数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose three [[独立同分布|independent, identically distributed]] trials each give $1$ with probability $p$ and $0$ with probability $1-p$, a [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli model]]. Observe $(1,1,0)$. The [[似然函数|likelihood]] keeps those three results fixed and compares candidate values of $p$. For $0<p<1$, it is $L(p)=p^2(1-p)$. Taking the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] gives $\ell(p)=2\log p+\log(1-p)$, so its derivative is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设三个[[独立同分布]]的试验各以概率 $p$ 产生 $1$、以概率 $1-p$ 产生 $0$，即 [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli 模型]]。观察到 $(1,1,0)$ 后，[[似然函数]]保持这三个结果不动，比较不同候选 $p$。在 $0<p<1$ 时，$L(p)=p^2(1-p)$。取[[自然对数]]得到 $\ell(p)=2\log p+\log(1-p)$，其导数为

$$
S_p(1,1,0)=\frac{d\ell(p)}{dp}=\frac2p-\frac1{1-p}.
$$

> [!example] Read the sign at a candidate value · 在候选参数处读斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $p=1/2$, the score is $4-2=2>0$: increasing $p$ slightly makes these data more likely under the model. At $p=2/3$, the score is $3-3=0$. In this example $\ell^{\prime\prime}(p)=-2/p^2-1/(1-p)^2<0$, so this stationary point is the maximum. In general, a zero score alone does not certify a maximum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $p=1/2$ 处，得分为 $4-2=2>0$：稍微增大 $p$，模型赋予这组数据的似然会增大。在 $p=2/3$ 处，得分为 $3-3=0$。本例 $\ell^{\prime\prime}(p)=-2/p^2-1/(1-p)^2<0$，所以这个驻点确为最大值；一般情况下，仅有得分为零并不足以认证最大值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The derivative is with respect to the parameter
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求导对象是参数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f_\theta(x)$ denote a [[概率密度函数|density]] or [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]] for the full observation. The scalar parameter $\theta$ lies in an open interval. Where $f_\theta(x)>0$ and its parameter derivative exists, define the log likelihood $\ell(\theta;x)=\log f_\theta(x)$ and the score
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $f_\theta(x)$ 表示整个观测的[[概率密度函数|密度]]或[[概率质量函数]]，标量参数 $\theta$ 位于一个开区间。在 $f_\theta(x)>0$ 且参数导数存在处，定义对数似然 $\ell(\theta;x)=\log f_\theta(x)$，以及得分

$$
S_\theta(x)=\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\ell(\theta;x)=\frac{\partial_\theta f_\theta(x)}{f_\theta(x)}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $\partial_\theta$ means “differentiate in $\theta$ while holding $x$ fixed”; it is a [[偏导数|partial derivative]]. For a small change $h$, differentiability gives $\ell(\theta+h;x)-\ell(\theta;x)\approx hS_\theta(x)$. In the Bernoulli example, no derivative of the discrete outcomes $0$ or $1$ is taken. The parameter moves continuously even though the observations are discrete.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\partial_\theta$ 表示“固定 $x$，只对 $\theta$ 求导”，是一种[[偏导数]]。参数小幅改变 $h$ 时，可微性给出 $\ell(\theta+h;x)-\ell(\theta;x)\approx hS_\theta(x)$。在 Bernoulli 例子中，并没有对离散结果 $0$ 或 $1$ 求导：连续变化的是参数，而观测可以是离散的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Observed score and random score
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*观测得分与随机得分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After observing data $x$ and selecting a numerical candidate $\theta_0$, $S_{\theta_0}(x)$ is a number. Before the sample is observed, $S_{\theta_0}(X)$ is random because $X$ is random. If the reference value $\theta_0$ is specified without knowing the true parameter, this is a computable [[统计量|statistic]]. By contrast, theoretical expressions such as $S_\theta(X)$ evaluated at the **unknown true** $\theta$ need not be computable from data alone. Keeping these roles separate prevents a parameter-dependent formula from being mistaken for an estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察到数据 $x$ 并选定一个数值候选 $\theta_0$ 后，$S_{\theta_0}(x)$ 是一个数。样本尚未观察时，$S_{\theta_0}(X)$ 随 $X$ 一起随机。如果参考值 $\theta_0$ 无须知道真实参数就能指定，它就是可以计算的[[统计量]]。反之，在**未知真值** $\theta$ 处计算的理论表达式 $S_\theta(X)$，未必只靠数据就能算出。区分这些角色，才能避免把依赖未知参数的公式误认成估计量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why its mean is zero in a regular model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正则模型中为什么均值为零*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume a common support $\mathcal S$ where the density or mass is positive, parameter differentiability there, integrability of the score, and permission to interchange parameter differentiation with integration or summation. Then total probability remains one as $\theta$ changes, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设密度或概率质量为正的支撑集合 $\mathcal S$ 不随参数变化，在其中可对参数求导，得分可积，并且允许交换参数求导与积分或求和。总概率随 $\theta$ 改变仍保持为一，因此

$$
E_\theta[S_\theta(X)]=\int_{\mathcal S}\frac{\partial_\theta f_\theta(x)}{f_\theta(x)}f_\theta(x)\,dx=\frac{d}{d\theta}\int_{\mathcal S}f_\theta(x)\,dx=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one Bernoulli observation, $S_p(X)=X/p-(1-X)/(1-p)$. It equals $1/p$ when $X=1$ and $-1/(1-p)$ when $X=0$. Averaging under that same $p$ gives $p(1/p)+(1-p)(-1/(1-p))=0$. Positive and negative slopes cancel over repeated samples; an individual observed score usually is not zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一个 Bernoulli 观测，$S_p(X)=X/p-(1-X)/(1-p)$。当 $X=1$ 时它等于 $1/p$，当 $X=0$ 时它等于 $-1/(1-p)$。按同一个 $p$ 的分布平均，得到 $p(1/p)+(1-p)(-1/(1-p))=0$。正负斜率在重复抽样平均中抵消，不意味着某次观测的得分通常就为零。

> [!warning] The parameter in the expectation must match · 求期望的分布必须匹配
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the true success probability is $q$ but the score is evaluated at a different candidate $p$, then $E_q[S_p(X)]=(q-p)/[p(1-p)]$, which need not vanish. Nor does the zero-mean identity automatically survive a moving support or an invalid differentiation exchange. The [[Cramér–Rao下界|Cramér–Rao card]] works through a uniform distribution with an unknown endpoint to show this failure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果真实成功概率为 $q$，得分却在另一个候选 $p$ 处计算，那么 $E_q[S_p(X)]=(q-p)/[p(1-p)]$，未必等于零。支撑移动或求导交换不合法时，零均值恒等式也不能自动沿用。[[Cramér–Rao下界]]用端点未知的均匀分布展开了这一失效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For independent observations, the joint likelihood is a product, its logarithm is a sum, and the sample score is the sum of the individual scores. That is why the opening three-observation formula contains two success contributions and one failure contribution. The average squared size of the random score leads to [[Fisher信息|Fisher information]]. Bernoulli endpoints $p=0,1$ describe valid distributions, but they are outside the open-parameter, common-support derivative calculation used here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立观测的联合似然是乘积，取对数后变成和，因此样本得分也是各观测得分的和。这解释了开头三观测公式为何包含两项成功贡献与一项失败贡献。随机得分平方的平均大小进一步给出 [[Fisher信息]]。Bernoulli 的端点 $p=0,1$ 是合法分布，但不属于这里开参数区间、共同支撑的导数计算范围。
