# 大Ω记号表示一个函数最终不小于另一个函数的某个固定正常数倍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Big Omega notation means an eventual lower bound by a fixed positive multiple*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一输入域上最终严格为正的函数 $f,g$，**大 $\Omega$ 记号** $f=\Omega(g)$ 表示存在 $c>0$ 和阈值 $N$，使所有 $n\ge N$ 都满足 $f(n)\ge cg(n)$。它就是把 [[大O记号]] 的比较方向倒过来：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For eventually strictly positive functions $f,g$ on the same input domain, **big Omega notation** $f=\Omega(g)$ means that some $c>0$ and threshold $N$ satisfy $f(n)\ge cg(n)$ for every $n\ge N$. It reverses the direction of [[大O记号|big O notation]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
f=\Omega(g)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad g=O(f).
$$

等价性来自取倒数常数：$g\le Cf$ 当且仅当 $f\ge (1/C)g$。例如对 $n\ge1$，$5n^2+n\ge5n^2$，故 $5n^2+n=\Omega(n^2)$；它也属于 $\Omega(n)$，所以单个下界不一定紧。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equivalence follows by reciprocating the positive constant: $g\le Cf$ exactly when $f\ge (1/C)g$. For $n\ge1$, $5n^2+n\ge5n^2$, so $5n^2+n=\Omega(n^2)$. It is also $\Omega(n)$, showing that a lower bound need not be tight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“正常数倍”与“任意正常数倍”不同：后者是 [[小ω记号]]。这里的 $\Omega$ 只描述函数下界，本身不指定算法的最好、平均或最坏情形；必须先说明被比较的是哪个成本函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Some positive multiple” differs from “every positive multiple”; the latter is [[小ω记号|little omega notation]]. Omega describes a lower bound on a function. It does not itself select an algorithm's best, average or worst case; the cost function must be specified first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
