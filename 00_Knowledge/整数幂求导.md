# Integer power rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*整数幂求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **integer power rule** finds the rate of change of repeated multiplication without expanding a new difference quotient every time. For example, $x^3=x\cdot x\cdot x$ has derivative $3x^2$. At $x=2$, the cube changes at a local rate of $12$: a small input increase $h$ changes the cube by approximately $12h$. The exponent $3$ is fixed while $x$ varies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**整数幂求导法则**让我们求出重复相乘的变化率，不必每次重新展开差商。例如 $x^3=x\cdot x\cdot x$ 的导数为 $3x^2$。在 $x=2$ 处，立方的局部变化率是 $12$：输入增加一个很小的 $h$，立方约增加 $12h$。这里变化的是 $x$，指数 $3$ 保持不变。

> [!abstract] Power, constant and reciprocal cases · 正幂、常数与倒数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fixed positive integer $n$, the familiar rule is $d(x^n)/dx=nx^{n-1}$. For $n\ge2$ this holds at every real $x$, including zero; for $n=1$ it simply says $d(x)/dx=1$. For a fixed negative integer $n$, the same formula holds on $x\ne0$, where $x^n$ means $1/x^{-n}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对固定正整数 $n$，常用公式是 $d(x^n)/dx=nx^{n-1}$。当 $n\ge2$ 时，它在所有实数 $x$ 处成立，包括零；当 $n=1$ 时，它直接表示 $d(x)/dx=1$。对固定负整数 $n$，同一公式在 $x\ne0$ 上成立，此时 $x^n$ 表示 $1/x^{-n}$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The zero-exponent case is the constant function $f(x)=1$, whose derivative is $0$. If that constant function is defined at zero as well, its derivative there is also $0$. Use the constant rule there: substituting $n=0,x=0$ into $nx^{n-1}$ would write $0\cdot0^{-1}$, which is undefined. No value of $0^0$ is needed to differentiate the constant function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 零指数情形对应常数函数 $f(x)=1$，导数为 $0$。如果这个常数函数也定义在零点，那么零点导数同样为 $0$；应直接使用常数法则，而不是把 $n=0,x=0$ 代入 $nx^{n-1}$，得到没有定义的 $0\cdot0^{-1}$。对常数函数求导不需要先给 $0^0$ 规定值。

## Where the factor n comes from
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*系数 n 从哪里来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the [[导数|difference quotient]] for the cube. The increment $h$ is nonzero while we simplify; only afterwards do we take its [[函数极限|limit]] at zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从立方函数的[[导数|差商]]开始。化简时增量 $h$ 非零；化简以后，才取它趋于零的[[函数极限|极限]]：

$$\frac{(x+h)^3-x^3}{h}
=\frac{3x^2h+3xh^2+h^3}{h}
=3x^2+3xh+h^2\longrightarrow3x^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n$ factors of $(x+h)$, a term containing exactly one $h$ can choose that $h$ from any of the $n$ factors; it contributes $nx^{n-1}h$. Every remaining changed term contains at least two factors of $h$. After dividing by $h$, those higher powers still tend to zero. This is the relevant part of the [[二项式定理|binomial expansion]], and it works at negative $x$ as well as positive $x$. For $n=1$, the difference quotient is exactly $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $n$ 个 $(x+h)$ 相乘时，只含一个 $h$ 的项，可以从任意一个因子中选出这个 $h$，因此共有 $nx^{n-1}h$。其余发生变化的项至少含两个 $h$；除以 $h$ 后，剩下的正次幂仍趋于零。这就是[[二项式定理]]中这里需要的部分，在负 $x$ 与正 $x$ 处同样成立。$n=1$ 时，差商直接恒为 $1$。

## Negative powers retain their excluded point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*负幂必须保留被排除的零点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive integer $m$, write $x^{-m}=1/x^m$. The [[商法则|quotient rule]] differentiates this reciprocal on $x\ne0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $m$，写成 $x^{-m}=1/x^m$。在 $x\ne0$ 上用[[商法则]]对这个倒数求导：

$$\frac{d}{dx}x^{-m}
=-\frac{mx^{m-1}}{(x^m)^2}
=-mx^{-m-1}.$$

> [!example] A negative input is allowed; zero is not · 可以取负输入，但不能取零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^{-2}$, $f'(x)=-2x^{-3}$ on $x\ne0$. At $x=-2$, $f(-2)=1/4$ and $f'(-2)=1/4$. At $x=0$, the original function is undefined, so there is no derivative to calculate there. This is different from a defined function with an infinite difference-quotient limit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=x^{-2}$，在 $x\ne0$ 上有 $f'(x)=-2x^{-3}$。在 $x=-2$ 处，$f(-2)=1/4$，$f'(-2)=1/4$。在 $x=0$ 处，原函数没有定义，因此根本没有该点导数可算；这与“原函数有定义但差商极限发散”不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Together with [[导数线性法则|linearity]], the rule differentiates polynomials term by term: $(3x^4-2x+7)'=12x^3-2$. For a whole function raised to a power, such as $(1+x^2)^3$, the [[链式法则|chain rule]] also supplies the derivative of the inner function. For a fixed non-integer exponent, use [[实数幂求导|the real power rule]] with its domain conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结合[[导数线性法则]]，可以逐项求多项式导数，例如 $(3x^4-2x+7)'=12x^3-2$。如果是整个函数取幂，如 $(1+x^2)^3$，还要用[[链式法则]]乘上内层导数。固定的非整数指数则见带定义域条件的[[实数幂求导]]。
