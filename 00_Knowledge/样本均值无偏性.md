# Unbiasedness of the sample mean vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本均值向量的无偏性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sample average can miss the population mean in any particular dataset and still be **unbiased**. Unbiasedness says that, across the possible datasets described by the model, its expectation equals the target mean. For the sample mean vector, common integrable coordinate means are enough; independence is not needed for this expectation calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某一份数据的样本平均可以偏离总体均值，同时仍是**无偏**的。无偏说的是：在模型描述的所有可能数据集之间取期望，结果等于目标均值。对样本均值向量，各次观测有共同且可积的坐标均值就足够；计算这个期望不需要独立性。

> [!example] Copying an observation preserves the mean, not its precision · 复制观测保留均值，却没有提高精度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let a random vector $Z$ equal $(1,3)^T$ or $(3,7)^T$, each with probability $1/2$. Its mean is $(2,5)^T$. Record it twice: $X_1=X_2=Z$. The [[样本均值向量|sample mean]] is $(X_1+X_2)/2=Z$, so it still has mean $(2,5)^T$, even though the two observations are completely dependent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令随机向量 $Z$ 等概率取 $(1,3)^T$ 或 $(3,7)^T$，均值为 $(2,5)^T$。把它记录两遍，即 $X_1=X_2=Z$。[[样本均值向量]]为 $(X_1+X_2)/2=Z$，均值仍是 $(2,5)^T$，尽管两份观测完全依赖。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In either possible dataset the observed mean differs from $(2,5)^T$. Nevertheless, averaging the two possible sample-mean values with their probabilities gives that target exactly. Copying has not made an individual estimate more accurate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两种可能数据集各自算出的均值都不等于 $(2,5)^T$，但把这两个可能的样本均值按概率平均，恰好得到目标。复制并没有让单次估计更精确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n,p\ge1$ be integers and let $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$ be random observation vectors on one probability space. Write $X_{ij}$ for variable $j$ in observation $i$. Assume every coordinate is integrable, $E|X_{ij}|<\infty$. The [[均值向量|mean vector]] $E[X_i]$ then exists as a finite vector. The [[样本均值向量|sample mean vector]] is $\bar X=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $n,p\ge1$ 为整数，$X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$ 是同一概率空间上的随机观测向量，$X_{ij}$ 表示第 $i$ 份观测的第 $j$ 个变量。假设各坐标都可积，即 $E|X_{ij}|<\infty$，则[[均值向量]] $E[X_i]$ 是有限向量。[[样本均值向量]]为 $\bar X=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$。

> [!important] A sufficient common-mean condition · 共同均值这一充分条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If every observation has the same mean vector $E[X_i]=\mu$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若每份观测的均值向量都是 $E[X_i]=\mu$，则
>
> $$\boxed{E[\bar X]=\mu.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Hence the sample mean is an [[估计量无偏性|unbiased estimator]] of $\mu$. In a model allowing several parameter values, the common-mean assumption and this conclusion are understood at each parameter value; correctness at only one accidental value would not establish model-wide unbiasedness.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因而样本均值是 $\mu$ 的[[估计量无偏性|无偏估计量]]。如果模型允许多个参数值，共同均值假设与结论是在每个参数值下理解的；只在某个偶然取值处碰巧相等，不足以证明整个模型中的无偏性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof is [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]], applied to each coordinate and then collected into a vector:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明就是逐坐标使用[[期望线性性]]，再把结果组合成向量：

$$
E[\bar X]
=E\!\left[\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right]
=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nE[X_i]
=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\mu
=\mu.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each sum is finite, and the integrability assumption justifies taking its expectation. Nothing in this argument factors a product of probabilities or expectations, so no independence is used. Finite second moments are also unnecessary for this particular statement; they enter the separate covariance calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里都是有限项求和，可积性保证可以取期望。推导没有分解任何概率乘积或期望乘积，因此没有用到独立性。这个结论也不需要二阶矩有限；二阶矩属于另外的协方差计算所需条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which assumptions the expectation calculation actually uses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## iid 是方便的充分条件，不是这里的最小条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[独立同分布|I.i.d. observations]] have a common law, but that law must still have finite coordinate means before the preceding calculation applies. With integrability supplied, i.i.d. sampling is one sufficient way to obtain the common-mean setting. Correlated repeated measurements can also satisfy it, as the copied-vector example demonstrates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[独立同分布|i.i.d.观测]]具有共同分布，但仍须保证该分布的各坐标均值有限，才能使用上述推导。补上可积性后，i.i.d.抽样是达到共同均值设定的一种充分方式；相关的重复测量也可以满足，如复制向量例子所示。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even equality of all the observation means is sufficient rather than necessary for unbiasedness at a given target. If $E[X_i]=\mu_i$, the more general identity is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

甚至“每份观测均值都相同”本身也只是对指定目标无偏的充分条件，并非必要条件。若 $E[X_i]=\mu_i$，更一般的恒等式为

$$E[\bar X]=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\mu_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The average is unbiased for that average of means. For example, if two observation means are $\mu-d$ and $\mu+d$ for a fixed vector $d$, their average still has expectation $\mu$. It is not automatically unbiased for a different mean selected without reference to the sampling model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，它无偏估计这些均值的平均。例如，两份观测的均值分别为 $\mu-d$ 与 $\mu+d$，其中 $d$ 为固定向量，平均后的期望仍是 $\mu$。但它不会自动对脱离抽样模型另选的某个均值无偏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unbiasedness controls the center of the sampling distribution, not its spread or its behavior as $n$ grows. The [[样本均值协方差|covariance of the sample mean]] depends on cross-observation dependence, and [[无偏不推出一致|unbiasedness alone does not imply consistency]]. In particular, five correlated measurements with the same finite mean still have an unbiased average, but their number alone does not determine the error's size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无偏性控制抽样分布的中心，不控制它有多分散，也不控制随 $n$ 增大时的行为。[[样本均值协方差]]受观测之间的依赖影响，而且[[无偏不推出一致]]。特别是，五次相关测量只要有同一个有限均值，平均后仍无偏，但仅凭“五次”不能确定误差大小。
