# 事前实际利率是在决策时依据未来物价预期衡量的利息购买力回报
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ex ante real interest rate measures the purchasing-power return to interest using expectations of future prices at the decision date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事前实际利率把未来收到的钱换算为预期能买到的商品，因此既取决于名义利率，也取决于同一期间的通胀预期。常用的低利率、低通胀近似是 $r_t^{ea}\approx i_t-E_t\pi_{t+1}$；这里的预期只能使用时点 $t$ 已知的信息，不能事后换成实际发生的通胀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ex ante real rate concerns the goods that future money is expected to buy, so it depends on both the nominal rate and expected inflation over the same horizon. A common small-rate approximation is $r_t^{ea}\approx i_t-E_t\pi_{t+1}$. Expectations use information available at $t$, not inflation subsequently realised.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一年名义利率从 4% 升到 5%，而一年预期通胀从 2% 升到 4%，近似事前实际利率反而从 2% 降到 1%。所以名义加息不单独证明这个实际回报上升；比较时还须保持期限、价格指数和借贷工具一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the one-year nominal rate rises from 4% to 5% while expected one-year inflation rises from 2% to 4%, the approximate ex ante real rate falls from 2% to 1%. A nominal increase alone does not establish a higher real return. Keep the horizon, price index and financial instrument consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确计算还要区分确定性情景与随机通胀。若一期名义总回报 $1+i_t$ 已知、无违约与费用，且 $1+\pi_{t+1}>0$，实现实际回报与其条件算术期望分别为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exact calculations distinguish a deterministic scenario from uncertain inflation. With a known one-period nominal gross return, no default or fees, and a positive gross inflation factor, the realised real return and its conditional arithmetic expectation are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
r_{t+1}^{post}=\frac{1+i_t}{1+\pi_{t+1}}-1,
\qquad
E_t[r_{t+1}^{post}]=(1+i_t)E_t\!\left[\frac{1}{1+\pi_{t+1}}\right]-1.
$$

随机通胀下，后式一般不等于 $(1+i_t)/(1+E_t\pi_{t+1})-1$。例如名义利率 4%，通胀以各半概率为 0% 或 8%，两状态实际回报平均约为 0.1481%，并非把平均通胀 4% 代入所得的 0%。这是预期实现购买力回报，不是含风险补偿的资产定价回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under uncertain inflation, the expectation generally differs from $(1+i_t)/(1+E_t\pi_{t+1})-1$. At a nominal rate of 4%, with equally likely inflation of 0% or 8%, the average realised real return is about 0.1481%, not the zero obtained by substituting average inflation. This is an expected purchasing-power return, not a risk-adjusted pricing return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[名义与实际贴现]]负责确定性或一致情景下的现金流／贴现率匹配；本卡负责决策时的预期对象。实际回报怎样改变消费和投资，再由[[利率渠道]]讨论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
