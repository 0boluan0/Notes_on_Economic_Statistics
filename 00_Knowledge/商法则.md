# Quotient rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*商法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ratio changes when its numerator changes, but also when its denominator changes. If a fixed amount is divided among more units, the amount per unit falls. The **quotient rule** keeps track of both effects. It gives the [[导数|derivative]] of $q(x)=u(x)/v(x)$ from the derivatives of $u$ and $v$, at a point where the denominator is nonzero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个比值的变化不仅来自分子，也来自分母。把固定总量分到更多单位中，每单位分得的数量就会减少。**商法则**同时计算这两个影响：在分母非零的点，根据 $u$、$v$ 的导数求出 $q(x)=u(x)/v(x)$ 的[[导数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the simplest example, $q(x)=1/x$ decreases on $x>0$, even though its numerator has derivative zero. Thus dividing numerator and denominator derivatives, $0/1$, cannot give the answer. The missing contribution comes from the changing denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的例子是 $q(x)=1/x$：它在 $x>0$ 上递减，虽然分子的导数为零。所以把分子、分母的导数相除成 $0/1$，不可能是正确答案；漏掉的是分母变化的贡献。

> [!abstract] The denominator contributes a minus sign · 分母变化带来减号
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $u$ and $v$ are differentiable at $x_0$, with $v(x_0)\ne0$. Then $u/v$ is defined near $x_0$, is differentiable there at $x_0$, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $u$、$v$ 在 $x_0$ 可导，且 $v(x_0)\ne0$。于是 $u/v$ 在 $x_0$ 附近有定义，并在 $x_0$ 可导，且
>
> $$\left(\frac uv\right)'(x_0)
> =\frac{u'(x_0)v(x_0)-u(x_0)v'(x_0)}{v(x_0)^2}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep the numerator order: derivative of the top times the bottom, **minus** the top times the derivative of the bottom. The denominator is the square of the original bottom, not its derivative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 分子顺序要固定：分子的导数乘分母，**减去**分子乘分母的导数；下面是原分母的平方，不是分母导数的平方。

## Prove the reciprocal is differentiable first
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先证明倒数确实可导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since [[可导必连续|differentiability implies continuity]], $v(x_0+h)\to v(x_0)$. A nonzero limit stays away from zero for sufficiently small $h$: for example, require $|v(x_0+h)-v(x_0)|<|v(x_0)|/2$. This guarantees the reciprocal is defined throughout a small neighbourhood. Its difference quotient can now be calculated directly:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于[[可导必连续]]，有 $v(x_0+h)\to v(x_0)$。非零极限保证 $h$ 足够小时分母不会碰到零：例如要求 $|v(x_0+h)-v(x_0)|<|v(x_0)|/2$。因此倒数在一个小邻域内有定义，可以直接计算它的差商：

$$\frac{1/v(x_0+h)-1/v(x_0)}h
=-\frac{[v(x_0+h)-v(x_0)]/h}{v(x_0+h)v(x_0)}
\longrightarrow-\frac{v'(x_0)}{v(x_0)^2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This limit **establishes** differentiability of $1/v$. Now apply the [[乘积法则|product rule]] to $u/v=u(1/v)$ to obtain $u'/v-uv'/v^2$, which combines into the displayed quotient formula. The identity $v(1/v)=1$ is also a useful check after reciprocal differentiability is known; differentiating it without first establishing that fact would assume the missing step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个极限**证明了** $1/v$ 可导。现在对 $u/v=u(1/v)$ 使用[[乘积法则]]，得到 $u'/v-uv'/v^2$，通分后就是上面的商法则。确认倒数可导以后，也可以对 $v(1/v)=1$ 求导来检查结果；如果事先没有证明可导性就直接这样做，会把待证明的一步当成已知。

> [!example] Track the minus signs · 看清两个减号
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $q(x)=x/(1-x^2)$, take $u=x$, $v=1-x^2$. The [[整数幂求导|power rule]] and [[导数线性法则|linearity]] give $u'=1$ and $v'=-2x$. Hence
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $q(x)=x/(1-x^2)$，取 $u=x$、$v=1-x^2$。由[[整数幂求导]]和[[导数线性法则]]，得到 $u'=1$、$v'=-2x$。所以
>
> $$q'(x)=\frac{1(1-x^2)-x(-2x)}{(1-x^2)^2}
> =\frac{1+x^2}{(1-x^2)^2},\qquad x\ne\pm1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The subtraction in the rule combines with the negative derivative $-2x$ to produce $+2x^2$. At $x=0$, the derivative is $1$; at $x=1$ and $x=-1$, the original function is undefined.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 法则中的减号与分母导数 $-2x$ 的负号相乘，产生 $+2x^2$。在 $x=0$ 处导数为 $1$；在 $x=1$、$x=-1$ 处，原函数没有定义。

> [!warning] Simplification does not fill a hole · 约分不会自动补上定义域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $q(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ agrees with $x+1$ only for $x\ne1$. Its derivative is $1$ on that domain, but $q'(1)$ does not exist because $q(1)$ was never defined. Defining a new function with value $2$ at $1$ fills the hole; that extended function does have derivative $1$ there. Always distinguish the original function from an extension.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 函数 $q(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ 只在 $x\ne1$ 时等于 $x+1$。它在原定义域上的导数为 $1$，却没有 $q'(1)$，因为原来的 $q(1)$ 根本未定义。另定义一个函数，在 $1$ 处补上值 $2$，才得到没有这个缺口的新函数；新函数在该点的导数确实为 $1$。原函数与补定义后的函数需要分清。
