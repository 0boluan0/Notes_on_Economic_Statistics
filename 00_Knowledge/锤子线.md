# 锤子线是下降走势后实体靠近区间上端且下影线较长的K线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hammer is a candle after a decline with a small body near the top of its range and a long lower shadow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

锤子线是出现在下降走势之后的一根[[K线]]：实体较小且靠近本期价格区间上端，下影线较长，上影线没有或很短。按本课的经典识别口径，下影线至少为实体长度的2倍；实体可阴可阳。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hammer is a [[K线|candlestick]] after a decline, with a small body near the top of the period's range, a long lower shadow and little or no upper shadow. The course's classical criterion requires a lower shadow at least twice the body length. Either body direction is allowed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形状必须与先前走势一起判断：同样的形状在上涨后称为[[上吊线]]。实体为零时不要用影线与实体的倍数相除；应按十字线的识别口径另行判断。锤子线在该期结束后才完整可见，在K线学派中被当作潜在止跌线索，不意味着已经知道未来最低点或确定即将上涨。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding trend is part of the classification: the same shape after an advance is a [[上吊线|hanging man]]. A zero body must not be used as the denominator of a shadow-to-body ratio; assess it under a doji convention. A hammer is fully observable only after its period closes. It is treated as a possible bottoming clue, not knowledge of a future low or a guaranteed advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
