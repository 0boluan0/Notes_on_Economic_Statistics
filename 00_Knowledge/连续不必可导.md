# Continuity does not imply differentiability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续不必可导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A graph can join continuously at a point while changing direction sharply there. Continuity controls how close the **output values** are; differentiability also compares an output change with the **size and sign of the input change**. These are different requirements. The absolute-value graph gives a complete example: its two branches meet at zero, but their slopes disagree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条图像可以在某点连续接上，却在那里突然转向。连续性控制**输出值**有多接近；可导性还把输出变化与**输入变化的大小和正负方向**作比较。这是不同的要求。绝对值图像给出一个完整例子：两支在零点接上，斜率却不一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an interior point $a$ of a real domain, [[一点连续|continuity at a point]] says $f(a+h)-f(a)\to0$ as $h\to0$. A finite [[导数|derivative]] requires the stronger statement that the following ratio approaches one finite real number:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实定义域的内点 $a$，[[一点连续]]要求 $h\to0$ 时 $f(a+h)-f(a)\to0$。有限[[导数]]还要求下面的比率趋于同一个有限实数：

$$\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}h\quad(h\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $h$ is the signed input increment. A numerator tending to zero says nothing by itself about the quotient when its denominator also tends to zero. We must inspect the quotient, including its two sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $h$ 是带正负号的输入增量。分子趋于零，本身并不能决定分母也趋于零时的比率。必须继续检查这个比率，包括它的左右两侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The branches meet, but their slopes disagree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小反例：绝对值函数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define $f(x)=|x|$ for all real $x$. Absolute value is distance from zero: $f(x)=x$ when $x\ge0$ and $f(x)=-x$ when $x<0$. In particular, $f(0)=0$. Both positive and negative inputs close to zero produce small positive outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在全体实数上定义 $f(x)=|x|$。绝对值表示到零的距离：$x\ge0$ 时 $f(x)=x$，$x<0$ 时 $f(x)=-x$。特别地，$f(0)=0$。无论正输入还是负输入，只要靠近零，输出都会是很小的非负数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity follows directly from the error comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性可以由误差直接得到：

$$|f(x)-f(0)|=\bigl||x|-0\bigr|=|x|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every desired accuracy $\varepsilon>0$, choose the input radius $\delta=\varepsilon$. Then $|x|<\delta$ gives $|f(x)-f(0)|<\varepsilon$, exactly the continuity requirement. This proves continuity, rather than relying only on how the graph looks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定任意精度 $\varepsilon>0$，取输入半径 $\delta=\varepsilon$。只要 $|x|<\delta$，便有 $|f(x)-f(0)|<\varepsilon$，恰好满足连续性的要求。这样证明了连续性，而不只是凭图像作判断。

> [!example] Small changes, incompatible rates · 变化虽小，变化率却不相容
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At zero, the difference quotient is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在零点，差商为
>
> $$\frac{f(h)-f(0)}h=\frac{|h|}h=
> \begin{cases}1,&h>0,\\-1,&h<0.\end{cases}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $h=0.01$, the output change is $0.01$ and the ratio is $1$. For $h=-0.01$, the output change is again $0.01$, but division by a negative input change gives $-1$. Making either increment smaller never removes this disagreement: the right quotient remains $1$, and the left quotient remains $-1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $h=0.01$ 时，输出变化为 $0.01$，比率是 $1$；当 $h=-0.01$ 时，输出变化仍为 $0.01$，但除以负的输入变化后，比率为 $-1$。把增量继续缩小也不能消除差异：右差商始终为 $1$，左差商始终为 $-1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the [[双侧极限判定|two-sided limit criterion]], there is no common difference-quotient limit. Thus $f$ is continuous but not differentiable at zero. This single example disproves the universal claim “every continuous function is differentiable at every point.” It does not say that continuous functions are never differentiable, or that this example fails to be differentiable elsewhere: away from zero, $|x|$ is locally either $x$ or $-x$, with derivative $1$ or $-1$ respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[双侧极限判定]]，差商不存在共同极限。所以 $f$ 在零点连续，却不可导。一个反例就足以推翻“连续函数在每一点都可导”这个全称命题。但它不表示连续函数永远不可导，也不表示这个例子在别处都不可导：离开零点后，$|x|$ 局部就是 $x$ 或 $-x$，导数分别为 $1$ 或 $-1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Continuity does not control slope size
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 连续没有排除什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity excludes a jump in function values, but it need not exclude a corner, a cusp or a vertical tangent. The underlying issue is the same: a small output change need not have one finite limiting ratio to the input change. The corner above fails because of two different finite ratios; another failure is an unbounded ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性排除了函数值的跳跃，却不一定排除尖角、尖点或竖直切线。根本问题相同：输出变化虽小，它与输入变化之比却未必趋于同一个有限数。上面的尖角因为两个有限比率不同而失败；另一种失败是比率无界增大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete vertical-tangent example, let $g(x)=\sqrt[3]{x}$, the real cube root, with $g(0)=0$. Given $\varepsilon>0$, the choice $\delta=\varepsilon^3$ makes $|x|<\delta$ imply $|g(x)|<\varepsilon$, so $g$ is continuous at zero. But, for $h\ne0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作为一个具体的竖直切线例子，令 $g(x)=\sqrt[3]{x}$，取实立方根，并有 $g(0)=0$。给定 $\varepsilon>0$，取 $\delta=\varepsilon^3$，则 $|x|<\delta$ 保证 $|g(x)|<\varepsilon$，所以 $g$ 在零点连续。但对 $h\ne0$，

$$\frac{g(h)-g(0)}h=\frac{\sqrt[3]{h}}{(\sqrt[3]{h})^3}
=\frac1{(\sqrt[3]{h})^2}\longrightarrow+\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator is positive and tends to zero from both sides. A quotient tending to $+\infty$ does not give a finite real derivative under the convention used here. This example still is continuous; it illustrates why testing continuity alone cannot settle differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母为正，并且从左右两侧都趋于零。在这里采用的约定下，差商趋于 $+\infty$ 不算存在有限实数导数。这个例子仍然连续，说明仅检查连续性无法决定可导性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The allowed directions matter. Restrict $|x|$ to $[0,\infty)$ and consider its endpoint $0$. It is then just $x$, and its right derivative is $1$. The original counterexample concerns the function on both sides of an interior point; deleting one side changes the question. An isolated domain point is automatically continuous, but has no nearby distinct inputs on which to define the usual unique limiting slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许的趋近方向也很重要。如果把 $|x|$ 限制到 $[0,\infty)$，考察端点 $0$，它就只是 $x$，右导数为 $1$。原反例讨论的是内点两侧都有定义的函数；删掉一侧就改变了问题。孤立的定义域点自动连续，但附近没有不同的合法输入可用来定义通常的唯一极限斜率。

> [!abstract] Necessary does not mean sufficient · 必要不等于充分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[可导必连续|Differentiability implies continuity]] at the same point. Therefore discontinuity rules out differentiability. Passing the continuity check, however, leaves the difference-quotient test to be done. For $|x|$ at zero, values approach $0$ from both sides while slopes approach $-1$ and $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[可导必连续]]说的是同一个点，因此不连续可以排除可导。但通过连续性检查之后，仍须检查差商。对于零点的 $|x|$，函数值从左右两侧都趋于 $0$，斜率却分别趋于 $-1$ 与 $1$。
