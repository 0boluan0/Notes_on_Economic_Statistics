# 函数在一点连续并不能推出它在该点可导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuity at a point does not imply differentiability there*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 连续只要求函数值接上附近趋势；可导还要求差商从各个允许方向趋于同一个有限值。因此连续是可导的必要条件，但不是充分条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Continuity requires function values to join their nearby trend. Differentiability additionally requires the difference quotient to approach one finite value from every permitted direction. Continuity is therefore necessary, but not sufficient, for differentiability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例：绝对值函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample: the absolute-value function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$f(x)=|x|$ 在 $0$ 连续，但
$$
\frac{|h|-|0|}{h}
=\begin{cases}
1,&h>0,\\
-1,&h<0.
\end{cases}
$$
右、左差商极限分别为 $1$ 与 $-1$，没有共同导数。因此
$$
\text{连续}\centernot\Longrightarrow\text{可导}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$f(x)=|x|$ is continuous at $0$, but its right and left difference quotients tend to $1$ and $-1$. No common derivative exists, so continuity does not imply differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连续没有排除什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What continuity does not rule out*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续排除了函数值的跳跃，却没有排除尖角、尖点或竖直切线。这些现象都可能保留连续性，同时破坏有限差商极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity rules out jumps in function values, but not corners, cusps, or vertical tangents. Each may preserve continuity while destroying a finite derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $|x|$ 在 $0$ 连续却不可导？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is $|x|$ continuous but not differentiable at $0$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 函数值从两侧都趋于 $0$，但左右差商分别趋于 $-1$ 与 $1$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The function values approach $0$ from both sides, but the one-sided difference quotients approach $-1$ and $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
