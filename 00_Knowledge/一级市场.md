# 一级市场是发行人向投资者出售新发行证券并取得募集资金的市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A primary market is a market in which issuers sell newly issued securities to investors and receive the proceeds.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断一级市场，关键是看**是否新发行证券，以及资金是否流向发行人**。它包括股票融资，也包括政府或企业发行[[债券]]；不是只有首次公开发行股票才算一级市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify a primary-market transaction by asking whether securities are newly issued and whether proceeds reach the issuer. It includes both equity finance and government or corporate [[债券|bond]] issuance, not just initial public offerings of shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司新发 100 股、每股 10 元，忽略发行费用，公司取得 1,000 元，投资者取得新股票。若随后投资者以每股 12 元把这 100 股转让给另一人，1,200 元归卖方，而非再次归公司：后一步属于[[二级市场]]。同一发行活动若同时包含新股发行和老股出售，应分别追踪两部分资金，不能仅凭“IPO”三个字判断每一元都进入公司。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An issuer selling 100 new shares at 10 receives 1,000 before issuance expenses. If an investor later sells those shares to another investor at 12, the 1,200 goes to the seller: that is a [[二级市场|secondary-market]] transfer. When an offering combines new shares and existing holders' shares, trace the two sets of proceeds separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
