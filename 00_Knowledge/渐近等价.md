# 渐近等价表示两个函数的比值趋于一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asymptotic equivalence means that the ratio of two functions tends to one*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f,g$ 定义在同一个趋向无穷的输入域上，且 $g$ 最终非零。**渐近等价** $f\sim g$ 表示 $f(n)/g(n)\to1$。它控制的是相对误差，而不是绝对差；主增长比较语境通常取 $f,g$ 最终正，但这个比值定义也适用于最终同号的负函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f,g$ share an input domain tending to infinity, with $g$ eventually nonzero. **Asymptotic equivalence** $f\sim g$ means $f(n)/g(n)\to1$. It controls relative error rather than absolute difference. Growth comparisons usually use eventually positive functions, but the ratio definition also allows eventually negative functions of the same sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
f\sim g
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac{f-g}{g}\longrightarrow0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
f=g(1+\varepsilon),\qquad \varepsilon(n)\longrightarrow0.
$$

例如 $n^2+n\sim n^2$，因为比值 $1+1/n\to1$，尽管两者之差 $n\to\infty$。而 $2n$ 与 $n$ 只是 [[大Θ记号|同阶]]，不是渐近等价。[[斯特林公式]] 的符号 $n!\sim\sqrt{2\pi n}(n/e)^n$ 同样表达相对误差趋零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $n^2+n\sim n^2$ because the ratio $1+1/n$ tends to one, even though their difference tends to infinity. By contrast, $2n$ and $n$ have the [[大Θ记号|same order]] but are not asymptotically equivalent. The symbol in [[斯特林公式|Stirling's formula]], $n!\sim\sqrt{2\pi n}(n/e)^n$, likewise expresses vanishing relative error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在最终正函数上，比值趋 1 保证最终如 $\frac12g\le f\le\frac32g$，因此渐近等价蕴含 $\Theta$，反向不成立。相对误差小也不保证任意复合运算后仍等价，见 [[渐近等价不保留指数运算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For eventually positive functions, a ratio tending to one eventually gives bounds such as $\frac12g\le f\le\frac32g$, hence asymptotic equivalence implies Theta, but not conversely. Small relative error is not preserved by arbitrary composition; see [[渐近等价不保留指数运算|the boundary under exponentiation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
