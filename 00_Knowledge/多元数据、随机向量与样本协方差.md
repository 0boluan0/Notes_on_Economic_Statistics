# 多元数据、随机向量与样本协方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multivariate data, random vectors, and the sample covariance matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当每个观察对象同时有多个变量时，用向量和矩阵表达中心、离散、方向与变量间共同变化。
> **具体锚点：** 只看身高和体重的各自方差，无法知道二者是否一起升高；协方差矩阵把这种联动保留下来。
> **核心难点：** 数值大小受单位影响，协方差不等于因果，距离也取决于尺度和相关结构。
> **为什么重要：** 多元正态、回归、PCA、判别和聚类都从均值向量与协方差矩阵出发。
> **继续：** 分布假设见 [[多元正态分布|多元正态分布]][[Wishart 分布与样本协方差推断|与 Wishart 分布]]；降维见 [[主成分分析 PCA|主成分分析]][[因子分析|与因子分析]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When each observational unit has several variables, vectors and matrices represent centre, dispersion, direction, and joint variation among variables.
> **Concrete anchor:** Separate variances for height and weight cannot show whether the two rise together; a covariance matrix preserves that co-movement.
> **Central difficulty:** Numerical magnitudes depend on units, covariance is not causation, and distance depends on scale and correlation structure.
> **Why it matters:** Multivariate normal theory, regression, PCA, discriminant analysis, and clustering all begin with mean vectors and covariance matrices.
> **Continue with:** See [[多元正态分布|multivariate normal distributions]] for distributional assumptions, [[Wishart 分布与样本协方差推断|Wishart distributions and covariance inference]] for sampling theory, and [[主成分分析 PCA|PCA]] for dimension reduction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数据矩阵与随机向量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Data matrices and random vectors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 n 个观察、p 个变量写成 $n\times p$ 数据矩阵 X；一行是一个对象，一列是一个变量。随机向量 $\mathbf X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ 的联合分布包含所有边际分布和依赖结构。先标清维度和单位，能避免把观察方向与变量方向写反。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $n$ observations on $p$ variables as an $n\times p$ data matrix $X$: each row is one observational unit and each column is one variable. The joint distribution of the random vector $\mathbf X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ contains every marginal distribution and their dependence structure. Recording dimensions and units first prevents rows of observations from being confused with columns of variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对第 $i$ 个对象，$\mathbf x_i\in\mathbb R^p$ 是一个点；整个样本是 $p$ 维空间里的点云。“多元”不只是同时记录多列，而是把每个对象的多个测量当成一个整体，因而可以问整体方向、联合异常和组间多维差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For unit $i$, $\mathbf x_i\in\mathbb R^p$ is one point, and the sample is a point cloud in $p$-dimensional space. "Multivariate" means more than storing several columns: it treats all measurements on a unit jointly, allowing questions about dominant directions, joint outliers, and multidimensional group differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 均值、协方差与相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mean, covariance, and correlation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值向量给每个变量的中心；协方差矩阵 $\Sigma=E[(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^T]$ 的对角元是方差，非对角元是协方差。相关矩阵将协方差按标准差缩放到无量纲范围。零协方差只表示无线性共同变化，一般不推出独立；联合正态是常见例外。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean vector gives the centre of each variable. In the covariance matrix $\Sigma=E[(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^T]$, diagonal entries are variances and off-diagonal entries are covariances. The correlation matrix rescales covariances by standard deviations and is dimensionless. Zero covariance rules out only linear co-movement and does not generally imply independence; joint normality is a common exception.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意方向 $a$，$a^T\Sigma a=\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)\ge 0$，所以协方差矩阵必定对称半正定。这个式子给了几何解释：沿 $a$ 方向投影后的方差，就是协方差椭球在该方向上的尺度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every direction $a$, $a^T\Sigma a=\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)\ge 0$, so a covariance matrix is necessarily symmetric positive semidefinite. Geometrically, this quadratic form is the projected variance along direction $a$, hence the scale of the covariance ellipsoid in that direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 样本协方差矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The sample covariance matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$S=\frac1{n-1}\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)(x_i-\bar x)^T$ 是对称半正定矩阵，秩至多为 $\min(p,n-1)$。当 $p\ge n$ 时 S 必然奇异，许多需要求逆的经典方法必须正则化或降维。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$S=\frac1{n-1}\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)(x_i-\bar x)^T$ is symmetric positive semidefinite and has rank at most $\min(p,n-1)$. When $p\ge n$, $S$ is necessarily singular, so classical methods requiring its inverse must use regularisation or dimension reduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵写法也说明为什么分母是 $n-1$：中心化后所有行向量之和为零，丢失一个自由度。若软件返回最大似然版本 $n^{-1}\sum_i(\cdot)$，必须说清分母口径；两者不应在同一推断中混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix expression also explains the denominator $n-1$: after centring, the row vectors sum to zero and one degree of freedom is lost. If software returns the maximum-likelihood version $n^{-1}\sum_i(\cdot)$, its denominator convention must be stated; the two conventions should not be mixed within one inferential calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 距离与几何
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distance and geometry*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Euclidean 距离把各维同等对待，标准化距离消除单位，Mahalanobis 距离 $(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)$ 还考虑相关方向。若协方差估计不稳或奇异，Mahalanobis 距离也不可靠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Euclidean distance treats every coordinate equally, standardised distance removes units, and Mahalanobis distance $(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)$ also accounts for correlated directions. If covariance estimation is unstable or singular, Mahalanobis distance is unreliable as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两个高度正相关的变量沿 $(1,1)$ 方向自然有很大变异。一个点沿这个方向离均值很远，在 Euclidean 距离下可能显得异常；Mahalanobis 距离会因该方向本来就宽而降低惊讶程度，却会对垂直于主轴的小偏离更敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, two strongly positively correlated variables naturally vary greatly along the $(1,1)$ direction. A point far from the mean along that direction may look unusual under Euclidean distance. Mahalanobis distance discounts it because the covariance ellipse is already wide there, while reacting more strongly to a smaller displacement perpendicular to the main axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 广义方差与可视化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Generalised variance and visualisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|S|$ 衡量协方差椭球的总体体积，但变量重标度会改变它。散点矩阵、平行坐标和低维投影用于发现非线性、分组和异常点；摘要矩阵不能替代对原始结构的检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$|S|$ measures the overall volume of a covariance ellipsoid, but rescaling variables changes it. Scatterplot matrices, parallel coordinates, and low-dimensional projections can reveal nonlinearity, groups, and outliers; a summary matrix cannot replace inspection of the underlying structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际工作中，先画每个变量的边际分布与成对散点图，再计算均值、标准差、相关和条件数。若发现尺度极不均、异常点、缺失机制或近共线，应在进入下游模型前处理，而不是等检验失败后再补救。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In practice, inspect marginal distributions and pairwise scatterplots before computing means, standard deviations, correlations, and a condition number. Severe scale imbalance, outliers, missingness mechanisms, or near-collinearity should be addressed before downstream modelling rather than after an inferential procedure fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么协方差矩阵一定半正定？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must every covariance matrix be positive semidefinite?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 任意向量 a 都有 $a^T\Sigma a=\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)\ge0$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every vector $a$, $a^T\Sigma a=\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)\ge0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么时候相关系数为 0 仍不能说变量独立？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When does zero correlation still fail to imply independence?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 一般分布中只排除了线性相关，仍可能存在非线性依赖；只有附加如联合正态等条件才可推出独立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a general joint distribution, zero correlation excludes only linear association; nonlinear dependence may remain. Independence follows only under additional conditions such as joint normality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 高维小样本为何让经典多元方法困难？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do high-dimensional, small-sample data undermine classical multivariate methods?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 样本协方差秩不足或极不稳定，涉及 $S^{-1}$ 的距离、检验和判别规则随之失效。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sample covariance matrix is rank deficient or highly unstable, so distances, tests, and classification rules involving $S^{-1}$ fail with it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释为什么 Mahalanobis 距离不只是“标准化的 Euclidean 距离”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why Mahalanobis distance is more than merely standardised Euclidean distance.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它不仅用每个变量的标准差缩放，还使用协方差矩阵旋转坐标系，因而会根据联合变异的主方向调整距离。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It rescales by marginal standard deviations and also rotates through the covariance matrix, so distance is adjusted for the principal directions of joint variation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
