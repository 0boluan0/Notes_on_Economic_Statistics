# Network flow
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*网络流*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **network flow** describes how much material moves along each allowed one-way connection, subject to two rules: a connection cannot carry more than its capacity, and an intermediate junction cannot create or consume material. Two distinguished vertices, the **source** $s$ and the **sink** $t$, supply and receive the net transfer. Think of the numbers as amounts per unit time, all measured in the same units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**网络流**描述每条允许的单向连接上通过多少物料，遵守两个规则：连接不能超过容量，中间节点不能凭空产生或消耗物料。两个指定顶点——**源点** $s$ 与**汇点** $t$——负责净输入和净输出。可以把各数值理解为单位时间内的数量，并统一计量单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From a directed graph to a flow
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从有向连接到可行流量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a finite [[图类型的建模边界|directed graph]] with vertex set $V$ and arc set $E$. An **arc** is a directed edge with a tail and a head. Choose distinct $s,t\in V$. Each arc $e$ has a finite real capacity $c(e)\ge0$. Distinct parallel arcs, and arcs pointing in opposite directions, retain separate identities and capacities. Self-loops may be included; they will count once as incoming and once as outgoing at their vertex.

For a vertex $v$, let $\operatorname{out}(v)$ and $\operatorname{in}(v)$ be the sets of arcs leaving and entering $v$. A **feasible flow** assigns a real number $f(e)$ to every arc and satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
使用有限[[图类型的建模边界|有向图]]，顶点集为 $V$，弧集为 $E$。**弧**就是有起点与终点的有向边。选取不同的 $s,t\in V$，并为每条弧 $e$ 指定有限实容量 $c(e)\ge0$。平行弧以及方向相反的弧分别保留身份和容量。也可以允许自环；在其顶点处，它同时计作一条入弧和一条出弧。

对顶点 $v$，用 $\operatorname{out}(v)$、$\operatorname{in}(v)$ 分别表示离开和进入它的弧集。**可行流**为每条弧指定实数 $f(e)$，满足

$$
0\le f(e)\le c(e)\qquad(e\in E),
$$
$$
\sum_{e\in\operatorname{in}(v)}f(e)
=\sum_{e\in\operatorname{out}(v)}f(e)
\qquad(v\in V\setminus\{s,t\}).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first line imposes nonnegativity and the **capacity constraint**. The second is **flow conservation** at every intermediate vertex. Empty sums are zero, so an isolated intermediate vertex causes no problem. Zero capacity forces zero flow. Sending zero on every arc always gives a feasible flow.

The arrow is an allowed direction, not merely a sign convention: $f(e)<0$ is forbidden. Sending material the other way requires a separate opposite arc. This differs from the signed edge coordinates in the [[回路空间与守恒流|real circulation space]], where negative coordinates can represent movement against reference arrows and no capacity bounds are imposed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
第一行是非负性与**容量限制**，第二行是每个中间顶点的**流量守恒**。空和等于零，因此孤立的中间顶点没有问题。零容量强迫零流量，而所有弧均取零总是可行流。

箭头表示允许的方向，并非仅用于约定正负号：这里禁止 $f(e)<0$。反向运输需要另一条反向弧。这不同于[[回路空间与守恒流|实数域环流空间]]的带符号边坐标；后者允许负坐标表示逆参考箭头流动，也没有容量限制。

## The value measures net delivery
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*流值计算净送达量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **value** of a flow is its net outflow from the source. Write it as $\operatorname{val}(f)$, to distinguish it from the sum of all arc flows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
流的**值**是源点的净流出量，记为 $\operatorname{val}(f)$，它不同于所有弧流量之和：

$$
\operatorname{val}(f)
=\sum_{e\in\operatorname{out}(s)}f(e)
-\sum_{e\in\operatorname{in}(s)}f(e)
=\sum_{e\in\operatorname{in}(t)}f(e)
-\sum_{e\in\operatorname{out}(t)}f(e).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why do the two net amounts agree? Sum “outflow minus inflow” over all vertices. Every arc occurs once with each sign, including a self-loop, so the total is zero. Every intermediate balance is already zero. The source balance must therefore be the negative of the sink balance.

We allow incoming arcs at $s$ and outgoing arcs at $t$. Under the stated feasibility definition, the net value can consequently be negative: an arc $t\to s$ carrying one unit gives value $-1$. That records net transport in the reverse direction; its arc flow is still nonnegative. If a model also requires nonnegative delivery, add $\operatorname{val}(f)\ge0$. This extra restriction does not change the maximum possible value, since the zero flow is available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两种净量为何相等？把所有顶点的“流出减流入”相加，每条弧都以正负号各出现一次，包括自环，所以总和为零。中间顶点的差已经各为零，因此源点之差必为汇点之差的相反数。

这里允许源点有入弧、汇点有出弧。按上述可行性定义，净流值因此可能为负：弧 $t\to s$ 输送一单位时，流值为 $-1$。这表示净运输方向相反，弧上的流量本身仍非负。若具体模型还要求净送达量非负，可另加 $\operatorname{val}(f)\ge0$；由于零流可行，这项限制不会改变可达到的最大流值。

> [!example] Count delivery once, not once per connection / 送达量不按经过的连接重复累加
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take vertices $s,a,b,t$ and the five arcs below. Each pair is “flow / capacity.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 取四个顶点 $s,a,b,t$，弧上的一对数表示“流量／容量”。
>
> $$s\to a:3/3,\quad s\to b:2/2,\quad a\to b:1/1,$$
> $$a\to t:2/2,\quad b\to t:3/3.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $a$, three units arrive and $1+2=3$ leave. At $b$, $2+1=3$ arrive and three leave. The flow is feasible, with value $3+2=5$ at $s$ and $2+3=5$ at $t$. Adding the five arc flows gives $11$, which counts some transported material on several successive arcs and is not the delivery value. If only $f(a\to b)$ is changed to zero, the individual capacities still hold, but conservation fails at both $a$ and $b$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在 $a$，流入三单位，流出 $1+2=3$；在 $b$，流入 $2+1=3$，流出三。这个流可行，源点的流值为 $3+2=5$，汇点也为 $2+3=5$。五条弧的流量总和是 $11$，它把同一物料经过连续多条弧的情况重复累加，并非送达量。若只把 $f(a\to b)$ 改为零，各弧仍符合容量限制，但 $a,b$ 两处都会违反守恒。

## What the maximum-flow question asks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*最大流要最大化什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **maximum flow** is a feasible flow whose value is at least that of every feasible flow in the same network. It need not maximize the flow on each individual arc, and it need not be unique. In the example, no value can exceed $3+2=5$, the total capacity leaving $s$, so the displayed feasible flow is maximum.

The basic definition permits fractional amounts. Integral capacities do not force a particular feasible flow to be integral: a single arc of capacity $1$ can carry $1/2$. The additional [[最大流整数性|maximum-flow integrality theorem]] guarantees the existence of an integral maximum flow under integer capacities; it does not change the definition of feasible flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**最大流**是在同一个网络的所有可行流中，流值不小于任何其他流值的可行流。它不要求每条弧的流量各自最大，也不一定唯一。在上例中，流值不可能超过离开 $s$ 的总容量 $3+2=5$，所以展示的可行流就是最大流。

基本定义允许分数流量。容量为整数，并不强迫某个指定可行流为整数：容量为 $1$ 的单弧可以输送 $1/2$。另一个独立的[[最大流整数性]]定理保证，在整数容量下**存在**整数最大流，并不改变可行流的定义。
