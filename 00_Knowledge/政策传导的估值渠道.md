# 政策估值判断须分别追踪现金流和所需回报再判断净影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Policy valuation analysis must trace cash flows and required returns separately before assessing the net effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把[[财政政策]]或[[货币政策]]消息转成估值判断时，先标明变化相对原预期有多大，再逐项追踪：销量、售价、投入成本、税收、营运资金与投资；另查利率期限、风险溢价和融资合同。最后用相匹配的现金流与贴现率比较，不从“宽松”直接跳到“股价上涨”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Translate [[财政政策|fiscal]] or [[货币政策|monetary]] news by first identifying the surprise, then tracing volume, prices, costs, taxes, working capital, and investment. Separately examine the rate term structure, risk premia, and financing contracts. Compare matched cash flows and discount rates rather than jumping from “easing” to higher share prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一期教学模型 $V=CF/(1+k)$ 中，基准 $CF=100,k=10\%$，价值为 90.91。若只有 $k$ 降到 5%，价值升至 95.24；若同一消息也令 $CF$ 降到 90，价值反而为 85.71。这里的 $CF$ 是该一期权利全部支付，不把这种简化当成永续股票的完整估值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the one-period teaching model $V=CF/(1+k)$, $CF=100,k=10\%$ gives 90.91. Reducing only $k$ to 5% raises value to 95.24; if the same news reduces $CF$ to 90, value instead falls to 85.71. Here $CF$ is the claim's entire one-period payment, not a full perpetual-equity model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融资影响还取决于固定或浮动合同、再融资时间和对冲。采用[[企业自由现金流]]时，不把债务利息再从经营现金流中重复扣减；采用股东现金流时则须匹配股权所需回报。更完整的公司情景计算见[[宏观情景分析]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financing effects depend on fixed or floating contracts, refinancing dates, and hedges. With [[企业自由现金流|free cash flow to the firm]], do not subtract interest again from operating cash flow; equity cash flow instead requires an equity discount rate. See [[宏观情景分析|macro scenario analysis]] for a fuller company calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
