# 波特五力是用五类竞争关系分析行业盈利条件的框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Porter's five forces is a framework for analyzing industry profitability through five competitive relationships.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

波特五力把行业中的价值分配放在五类关系下分析：[[潜在进入者威胁]]、[[供应商议价能力]]、[[买方议价能力]]、[[替代品威胁]]与[[行业内竞争]]。竞争不仅发生在现有同行之间；上下游、潜在进入者和替代方式也会影响可保留的利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Porter's five forces examines value capture through [[潜在进入者威胁|entry threats]], [[供应商议价能力|supplier power]], [[买方议价能力|buyer power]], [[替代品威胁|substitutes]], and [[行业内竞争|rivalry]]. Competition extends beyond current rivals: upstream and downstream parties, potential entrants, and alternative solutions also affect retained profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一家经营良好的咖啡店仍可能同时受到房东提租、顾客改去邻店和居家冲泡的压力。使用框架时先固定行业边界，再为每种力量提供具体参与者、作用渠道及证据；五个标题不是五项可以任意相加的分数，也不是对单家公司护城河的自动证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A well-run coffee shop may face higher rent, customers switching to another shop, and home brewing as an alternative. Fix the industry boundary and identify the actors, mechanisms, and evidence for each force. The headings are not arbitrarily additive scores and do not automatically establish a firm's competitive advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
