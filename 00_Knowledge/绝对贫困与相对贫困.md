# 绝对贫困按固定实际标准识别而相对贫困按社会参照标准识别

绝对贫困在一次比较中固定[[贫困线]]代表的实际生活标准；相对贫困让标准依赖所比较社会的福利分布，常见形式是中位收入的某个比例。区别在于参照标准是否随分布变化，不在于金额用美元还是本币表示。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute poverty holds the real living standard represented by the [[贫困线|poverty line]] fixed for a comparison. Relative poverty links the standard to the society's welfare distribution, commonly as a fraction of median income. The distinction concerns how the reference standard changes, not whether the amount is quoted in dollars or local currency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“固定实际标准”不等于冻结名义金额。若维持同样生活条件的价格整体上升 10%，原来名义 100 的线可能需要变为 110；这只是保持购买力，而不是自动提高实际标准。选择另一套需求标准或重新估计价格，则必须说明版本并将比较放回一致口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding the real standard fixed does not freeze its nominal amount. If the cost of maintaining those living conditions rises by 10%, a nominal line of 100 may need to become 110. That preserves purchasing power rather than automatically raising the real standard. A change in the needs standard or price estimates requires an explicit version and a consistent comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设相对线为 $z=0.5m$，其中 $m>0$ 是同口径中位收入。若每个人的非负实际收入都翻倍，$m$ 和 $z$ 也翻倍，[[贫困人口比例]]保持不变；固定实际线下，贫困人数则不会因这次普遍增收而上升。这并不矛盾：前者在问相对落后，后者在问是否达到原来的实际标准。0.5 是教学选择，不是所有制度的统一比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the relative line is $z=0.5m$, where $m>0$ is median income on a consistent basis. Doubling everyone's nonnegative real income doubles both $m$ and $z$, leaving the [[贫困人口比例|headcount ratio]] unchanged. Under a fixed real line, this universal income increase cannot raise the number in poverty. The measures answer different questions: relative disadvantage versus attainment of the original real standard. The factor 0.5 is illustrative, not universal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际制度也可结合最低实际标准与随社会收入上升的部分。读统计时应查所用函数，不能仅凭“社会贫困”或“国际贫困”名称推断它是纯粹固定线还是纯粹中位数比例线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actual systems may combine a real minimum with a component that rises with societal income. Read the threshold rule itself: a label alone does not establish that it is either fully fixed or a pure fraction of the median.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Foster et al. (2013), 第27–29页](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/281001468323965733/pdf/A-unified-approach-to-measuring-poverty-and-inequality-theory-and-practice.pdf#page=45)：固定实际线、收入参照线及混合线；倍增结论是对该定义的代数应用。
- [World Bank, PIP Methodology, Derivation of other global poverty lines](https://github.com/worldbank/PIP-Methodology/blob/master/03-Converting-welfare-aggregates.Rmd)：支持区分绝对线和含相对成分的 societal line；此卡不固化现行数额。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Foster and colleagues support the fixed, relative, and hybrid threshold distinctions; the doubling example applies their definitions. PIP documents an operational distinction between absolute and societal lines. No current monetary threshold is fixed in this card.
