# Support in importance sampling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重要性抽样的支持条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sampling distribution cannot tell us about a contribution it can never visit. In [[重要性抽样|importance sampling]], a large weight can compensate for an outcome sampled rarely, but no finite weight can repair an outcome that is never sampled. The relevant coverage depends on the particular average we want.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

抽样分布无法提供它永远不会访问的那部分贡献。[[重要性抽样]]中，大权重可以补偿抽到次数少的结果，却不能修复根本抽不到的结果。需要覆盖哪些区域，取决于我们要算哪个平均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An unsampled outcome can matter—or contribute zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*没有抽到的结果可能重要，也可能本来贡献为零*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the target model gives failure probability $0.01$ and success probability $0.99$. To estimate failure probability, use the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $h=1$ on failure and $0$ on success. A proposal that samples success only produces the weighted output zero every time. Its expectation is $0$, so its bias relative to the target $0.01$ is $-0.01$. More draws cannot change that.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设目标模型的失败概率为 $0.01$，成功概率为 $0.99$。要估计失败概率，就用失败时为 $1$、成功时为 $0$ 的[[事件指示变量]] $h$。如果提议分布只抽成功，加权输出每次都是零，期望为 $0$，相对于目标 $0.01$ 的偏差为 $-0.01$；多抽多少次都不会改善。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, a proposal sampling failure only gives $p/q=0.01/1=0.01$ and the constant output $0.01$. Omitting success causes no problem for this particular $h$, because success contributes zero to the target integral. It would fail for the success indicator. In a realistic problem, constructing a proposal confined to a rare event and computing its normalisation can itself require the unknown probability; this example demonstrates coverage, not a universal shortcut.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若提议分布只抽失败，则 $p/q=0.01/1=0.01$，加权输出恒为 $0.01$。对这个特定 $h$，漏掉成功无妨，因为成功对目标积分贡献为零；换成成功的指示变量就不行了。在实际问题里，构造只抽稀有事件的分布并算出其归一化常数，本身可能就需要未知概率；这个例子说明覆盖条件，并不是通用捷径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Write exactly what the sampler can see
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*准确写出抽样器能够看到的部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $p,q$ be densities on the same space, let $h(x)$ be the recorded quantity, and write the target as $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$, with $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$. Put $Q=\{x:q(x)>0\}$, the region assigned positive density by the proposal. Here $Q$ means this positivity set, not the topological closure sometimes called a distribution’s support. For a fixed sample count and draws with marginal density $q$, the ordinary estimate satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $p,q$ 是同一空间的密度，$h(x)$ 是记录的量，目标记为 $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$，且 $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$。记 $Q=\{x:q(x)>0\}$，即提议分布密度为正的区域。这里的 $Q$ 指这个正值集合，并非有时也叫“分布支撑”的拓扑闭包。抽样次数固定、每个观测的边际密度为 $q$ 时，普通估计量满足

$$E_q[\widehat\mu_{IS}]=\int_Q h(x)p(x)\,dx,$$
$$\operatorname{Bias}(\widehat\mu_{IS})=E_q[\widehat\mu_{IS}]-\mu=-\int_{Q^c}h(x)p(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second equality simply subtracts the full target integral from the visible part. For discrete outcomes, replace each integral with a sum. No independence is required for this expectation calculation. The bias is zero exactly when the omitted signed integral is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二个等式只是用“看得见的部分”减去完整目标积分。离散结果把积分换成求和；这个期望计算不要求观测彼此独立。偏差为零的准确条件，就是遗漏的带符号积分为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A dependable sufficient coverage condition is $h(x)p(x)=0$ wherever $q(x)=0$, apart from a set of zero integration measure. This “almost everywhere” qualification matters for continuous densities: changing a density at one isolated point does not change any probability. For a discrete outcome with positive target probability, omitting that outcome can matter. Under this condition, $E_q|h(X)p(X)/q(X)|=E_p|h(X)|$, so the corrected output has a well-defined finite mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可靠的充分覆盖条件是：除积分测度为零的集合外，只要 $q(x)=0$，就有 $h(x)p(x)=0$。“几乎处处”这一限定对连续密度很重要：只改一个孤立点的密度值，不会改变任何概率；离散情形漏掉一个目标概率为正的结果，则可能改变积分。在此条件下，$E_q|h(X)p(X)/q(X)|=E_p|h(X)|$，修正输出有定义良好的有限均值。

> [!example] Signed omissions can cancel · 正负遗漏可能抵消
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $p=(1/4,1/4,1/2)$ on outcomes A, B, C, and $h=(4,-4,2)$. The target is $1-1+1=1$. A proposal $q=(0,0,1)$ sees only C and always produces $2(1/2)/1=1$. It misses two nonzero contributions but remains unbiased because they cancel. Change only $h(B)$ to $0$: the target becomes $2$, while the same estimator still returns $1$. The coincidence is specific to the original integrand.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 A、B、C 三个结果上，取 $p=(1/4,1/4,1/2)$、$h=(4,-4,2)$，目标为 $1-1+1=1$。提议 $q=(0,0,1)$ 只看到 C，每次输出 $2(1/2)/1=1$。它漏掉两份非零贡献，仍因正负抵消而无偏。仅把 $h(B)$ 改成 $0$，目标就变为 $2$，估计量却仍返回 $1$；这个巧合只对原来的被积函数成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonnegative $h$, an omitted region with strictly positive integral produces strictly negative bias; cancellation is unavailable. Thus “missing coverage always biases every integrand” is too strong, while “more samples repair missing support” is false.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $h$ 非负，遗漏区域只要具有严格正的积分，就产生严格负偏差，没有正负抵消。因此，“漏支持必然使所有被积函数有偏”过强，而“多抽样能修复漏支持”则错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One reusable proposal may need broader coverage
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个重复使用的提议可能需要更广的覆盖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the same sample will estimate many functions, covering the whole target distribution is a simple sufficient rule: $q>0$ wherever $p>0$, up to null sets. [[自归一化重要性抽样|Self-normalisation]] also estimates a denominator corresponding to the function $1$, so this broader condition protects its normalising integral as well. In the failure-only example the ordinary failure estimate is $0.01$, but its self-normalised ratio is $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果同一批样本要估计许多函数，覆盖整个目标分布是一个简单的充分规则：除零测集外，$p>0$ 处都有 $q>0$。[[自归一化重要性抽样]]还要估计对应常数函数 $1$ 的分母，这个更广的条件也保护其归一化积分。前面的“只抽失败”例子中，普通失败概率估计为 $0.01$，自归一化比率却是 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under mathematical sampling from $q$, the draw lies in $Q$ with probability one. Outside $Q$, where $q(x)=0$, the ratio is undefined; writing that ratio does not recover a contribution the sampler never visits. A point with tiny positive $q(x)$ is a different issue: coverage may hold while [[重要性抽样权重方差|weighted-output variance]] is enormous or infinite. Finally, numerical underflow that rounds a positive density to zero is an implementation error to diagnose separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数学上从 $q$ 抽样，观测以概率一落在 $Q$ 内；因此在 $q(x)=0$ 处写一个无定义比率，不能补回遗漏贡献。$q(x)$ 很小但为正是另一问题：覆盖可能正确，[[重要性抽样权重方差|加权输出方差]]却极大或无穷。数值下溢把正密度算成零，则是需要另外诊断的实现问题。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Owen, [Chapter 9, §9.1, PDF p. 4, §9.2, p. 8, and Exercises 9.1–9.2, p. 40](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-is.pdf#page=4): the proposal positivity set, the fixed-integrand coverage identity, and the broader normalising-constant condition. The omitted-integral formula follows by splitting the absolutely integrable target; discrete examples are exact sums.
- Ghahramani, [CMU Sampling and MCMC, PDF p. 9](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~larry/=sml2008/lect2.pdf#page=9): the stronger whole-target support rule. It is sufficient, rather than necessary for every individual integrand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Owen 第 9 章 §9.1（PDF p. 4）、§9.2（p. 8）、习题 9.1–9.2（p. 40）核对提议正值集合、固定被积函数覆盖与归一化常数所需的更广条件。遗漏积分公式由绝对可积的目标直接分区得到；离散例均精确求和。
- Ghahramani 的 CMU 讲义（PDF p. 9）提供更强的全目标覆盖规则；它是充分条件，并非对每个特定被积函数都必要。
