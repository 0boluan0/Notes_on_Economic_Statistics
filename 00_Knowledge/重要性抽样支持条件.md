# 普通重要性抽样只有在 q 覆盖所有 h·p 非零区域时才保证换测度恒等式；漏失支持造成的偏差等于遗漏积分的负值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ordinary importance sampling guarantees the change-of-measure identity only when q covers every region where h times p is nonzero; missing support creates bias equal to the negative omitted integral*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] proposal 漏支持首先是目标正确性问题，不只是“权重有点大”
> 对一个固定被积函数 $h$，方便的充分条件是：只要 $h(x)p(x)\neq0$，就有 $q(x)>0$。否则估计量只能看到 $q$ 支持内的积分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fixed integrand $h$, a convenient sufficient condition is $q(x)>0$ wherever $h(x)p(x)\neq0$. Otherwise the estimator sees only the part of the integral inside the proposal support.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令

$$
Q=\{x:q(x)>0\}.
$$

从 $q$ 抽样的普通重要性估计量满足

$$
E_q[\widehat\mu_{IS}]
=\int_Q h(x)p(x)\,dx.
$$

相对于目标

$$
\mu=\int h(x)p(x)\,dx,
$$

其偏差为

$$
\operatorname{Bias}(\widehat\mu_{IS})
=-\int_{Q^c}h(x)p(x)\,dx.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The estimator integrates only over $Q$, the support of the proposal. Its bias is the negative contribution omitted from $Q^c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“只要漏掉任何 $|h|p>0$ 区域就必然有偏”对可正可负的 $h$ 说得过强：不同漏失区域的正负积分可能恰好抵消。准确结论是，漏支持后恒等式不再有保证；只要遗漏积分非零，就有偏。若 $h\ge0$，遗漏区域上 $h p$ 有正积分，则偏差必为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a signed integrand, positive and negative omitted contributions can cancel, so missing any region does not logically force nonzero bias. The exact statement is that the identity is no longer guaranteed, and bias occurs whenever the omitted integral is nonzero. For nonnegative $h$, omitted positive mass gives downward bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自归一化估计要同时估计归一化常数，通常需要更强的 $q>0$ wherever $p>0$ 条件，见 [[自归一化重要性抽样]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Self-normalisation also estimates the normalising constant and generally needs the stronger support condition $q>0$ wherever $p>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> proposal 漏掉两个区域，一个对积分贡献 $+2$，另一个贡献 $-2$。估计量是否一定有偏？
>
> **答案：** 不一定；这两个遗漏积分恰好抵消。但换测度恒等式已失去结构性保证，换一个 $h$ 就可能产生偏差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Not necessarily; the omitted contributions cancel. But the change-of-measure identity has lost its support guarantee and can fail for another integrand.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
