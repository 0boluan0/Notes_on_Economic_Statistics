# Does a strict optimum imply nonsingularity?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*严格最优能推出非奇异吗？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **strict optimum** compares objective values: all other sufficiently nearby feasible choices are worse. **Nonsingularity** describes a square matrix: its linear action is [[逆矩阵|invertible]], so no nonzero input is lost. These are different statements. A very flat peak can still be strict, while its second derivatives fail to detect that peak. This matters whenever we want to differentiate an optimizer with respect to a parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**严格最优**比较的是目标值：其他足够近的可行选择都更差。**非奇异**描述的则是方阵：它的线性作用[[逆矩阵|可逆]]，不会把非零输入丢失。两者是不同的陈述。一个很平的峰仍然可以是严格的，而二阶导数可能无法识别这个峰。想对最优选择关于参数求导时，这一区别尤其重要。

> [!important] Strictness and invertibility · 严格性与可逆性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Strict definiteness can imply a strict local optimum, but the converse fails. Higher-order terms can create a strict optimum while the second-order matrix remains singular. Nonsingularity of the system Jacobian required by the implicit function theorem is therefore a separate regularity condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> Hessian 严格定号可以推出严格局部最优，但反向不成立。严格最优可能由四阶或更高阶项形成，此时二阶矩阵仍可奇异。因而 [[隐函数定理]] 所需的系统 Jacobian 非奇异性，是另一项正则条件，不能从“解是严格的”自动获得。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Minimal counterexample: a strict maximum with zero Hessian
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例：严格最大但 Hessian 为零

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=-x^4$, the origin is a strict global maximizer even though $f''(0)=0$. Thus strict optimality does not imply a nonsingular Hessian even in one unconstrained dimension, and the same invalid converse cannot be transferred to reduced Hessians or bordered systems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对
$$
f(x)=-x^4,
$$
$x^*=0$ 是严格全局最大点，但 $f''(0)=0$。这说明“严格最优 $\Rightarrow$ Hessian 非奇异”即使在无约束一维问题中也已经错误；约束问题不能把同一逆推搬到约化 Hessian 或边框矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, $f(0)=0$ and $f(x)<0$ for every $x\ne0$, which proves [[局部极值与绝对极值|strict global maximality]] directly. But $f'(x)=-4x^3$ and $f''(x)=-12x^2$, so the $1\times1$ Hessian at zero is the singular matrix $(0)$. The first nonzero change in the objective is fourth-order, not quadratic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地，$f(0)=0$，而每个 $x\ne0$ 都有 $f(x)<0$，这直接证明[[局部极值与绝对极值|严格全局最大]]。但 $f'(x)=-4x^3$、$f''(x)=-12x^2$，所以零点的 $1\times1$ Hessian 是奇异矩阵 $(0)$。目标的首个非零变化是四阶项，不是二次项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Even a regular equality constraint does not fix it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*等式约束满秩仍不能补上这一步*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a genuine constrained example, maximise $f(x,y)=-x^4-y^2$ subject to $h(x,y)=y=0$. On the feasible line the objective is $-x^4$, so the origin is the unique strict global maximizer. The constraint Jacobian is $A=(0,1)$, which has full row rank. The [[Lagrange必要条件|Lagrange equation]] gives the unique multiplier $\nu^*=0$. Nevertheless the tangent direction is horizontal, and the reduced Hessian is $(0)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个真正带约束的例子是：在 $h(x,y)=y=0$ 下最大化 $f(x,y)=-x^4-y^2$。可行直线上的目标为 $-x^4$，所以原点是唯一的严格全局最大点。约束 Jacobian 为 $A=(0,1)$，具有满行秩。[[Lagrange必要条件|Lagrange 方程]]给出唯一乘子 $\nu^*=0$。然而切方向是水平方向，约化 Hessian 为 $(0)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To test an implicit branch, collect stationarity and feasibility into one system, using unknowns $z=(x,y,\nu)$. With $\mathcal L=f-\nu h$, the system and its [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian]] at the optimum are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要检验隐式解支，需要把驻点方程与可行性合成一个系统，以 $z=(x,y,\nu)$ 为待解变量。采用 $\mathcal L=f-\nu h$，该系统及其在最优点的[[Jacobian矩阵]]为

$$F(z)=\begin{pmatrix}-4x^3\\-2y-\nu\\y\end{pmatrix}=0,\qquad
D_zF(0,0,0)=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\0&-2&-1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying this matrix by $(1,0,0)^T$ gives zero, so it is singular. Thus full-rank constraints, a unique multiplier and a strict optimum still do not imply an invertible optimality-system Jacobian. Using the constraint row $-h$ and ordering the multiplier first yields a symmetric bordered Hessian instead; row signs and permutations preserve singularity, though they can change the determinant's sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该矩阵乘以 $(1,0,0)^T$ 得到零，所以它是奇异的。因此，约束满秩、乘子唯一以及严格最优同时成立，仍然不能推出最优性系统 Jacobian 可逆。如果把约束行改成 $-h$，并把乘子排在最前，则得到对称边框 Hessian；行变号与排列不改变奇异性，却可能改变行列式的符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the three matrices distinct. For the simpler objective $-x^2$ with the same constraint $y=0$, the full Lagrangian Hessian is $\operatorname{diag}(-2,0)$, which is singular, but the reduced Hessian is $(-2)$ and the system Jacobian below is invertible. The [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] tests the derivative block of the equations with respect to the variables being solved for, not just any matrix called a Hessian.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要把三种矩阵区分开。对更简单的目标 $-x^2$，仍使用约束 $y=0$，完整 Lagrangian Hessian 为 $\operatorname{diag}(-2,0)$，是奇异的；但约化 Hessian 为 $(-2)$，下面的系统 Jacobian 则可逆。[[隐函数定理]]检验的是方程关于待解变量的导数块，不是任意一个名为 Hessian 的矩阵。

$$D_{(x,y,\nu)}F=
\begin{pmatrix}-2&0&0\\0&0&-1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det D_{(x,y,\nu)}F=-2\ne0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Full rank plus strict second-order curvature does suffice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*满秩加上严格二阶曲率，确实足以保证可逆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is a stronger conclusion when the full hypotheses of the [[等式约束二阶条件|regular second-order sufficient condition]] have actually been checked. Let $A=Dh(x^*)$ have full row rank and $H=\nabla^2_{xx}\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)$ be strictly positive or negative on every nonzero vector in $\ker A$. Then the Jacobian of stationarity and equality feasibility, in the order $(x,\nu)$, is nonsingular:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果已经核实[[等式约束二阶条件|正则二阶充分条件]]的全部假设，就有更强的结论。设 $A=Dh(x^*)$ 满行秩，$H=\nabla^2_{xx}\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)$ 在 $\ker A$ 的每个非零向量上严格为正或严格为负，那么按 $(x,\nu)$ 排列的驻点与等式可行性系统 Jacobian 非奇异：

$$J=\begin{pmatrix}H&-A^T\\ A&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see this, suppose $J(v,w)^T=0$. Then $Av=0$ and $Hv-A^Tw=0$. Multiplication by $v^T$ gives $v^THv=0$, so strict definiteness on $\ker A$ forces $v=0$. Now $A^Tw=0$ and full row rank force $w=0$. A square matrix with zero kernel is invertible. Thus **strict optimality alone** is insufficient, while **full rank together with strict tangent curvature** is sufficient. This is a direct implication from stronger hypotheses, not a reverse inference from the word “strict”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明如下：若 $J(v,w)^T=0$，则 $Av=0$、$Hv-A^Tw=0$。左乘 $v^T$ 得到 $v^THv=0$；由于 $H$ 在 $\ker A$ 上严格定号，必有 $v=0$。再由 $A^Tw=0$ 与满行秩得到 $w=0$。方阵的核只有零向量，因此可逆。所以，**只有严格最优**并不充分，而**满秩加切空间严格曲率**是充分的。这是从更强假设直接推导的结论，不是从“严格”二字反推。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The practical consequence for comparative statics
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对比较静态的实际后果

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the parameterised problem $\max_x\{-x^4+\theta x\}$, the optimizer at zero is strict, but stationarity gives $x^*(\theta)=\sqrt[3]{\theta/4}$, which is not differentiable at zero. A strict and well-defined optimizer therefore need not satisfy the ordinary implicit comparative-statics formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑参数化问题
$$
\max_x\{-x^4+\theta x\}.
$$
在 $\theta=0$，严格最优仍是 $x^*=0$；驻点方程为 $-4x^3+\theta=0$，所以
$$
x^*(\theta)=\sqrt[3]{\theta/4}.
$$
这条最优分支在零点不可微。最优选择存在且严格，并不保证普通 [[隐函数比较静态]] 的导数公式可用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each real $\theta$, the derivative $-4x^3+\theta$ is strictly decreasing in $x$, positive before its unique zero and negative after it. The objective also tends to $-\infty$ as $|x|\to\infty$. Hence the displayed root is the unique global maximizer for every parameter. Its difference quotient at zero is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个实数 $\theta$，导数 $-4x^3+\theta$ 随 $x$ 严格下降，在唯一零点之前为正，之后为负；且 $|x|\to\infty$ 时目标趋于 $-\infty$。因此，上述根对每个参数都是唯一全局最大点。零点处的差商为

$$\frac{x^*(\theta)-x^*(0)}{\theta}
=\frac{1}{4^{1/3}|\theta|^{2/3}}\longrightarrow+\infty
\qquad(\theta\to0,\ \theta\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no finite derivative at zero. The stationarity system is $F(x,\theta)=-4x^3+\theta$, and $F_x(0,0)=0$, so the usual formula $dx^*/d\theta=-F_x^{-1}F_\theta$ is not available there. This is failure of an IFT hypothesis, not failure of the optimizer to exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此零点没有有限导数。驻点系统为 $F(x,\theta)=-4x^3+\theta$，且 $F_x(0,0)=0$，所以通常公式 $dx^*/d\theta=-F_x^{-1}F_\theta$ 在这里不可用。这是 IFT 假设失败，不是最优选择不存在。

> [!warning] Singular does not mean no smooth branch · 奇异也不等于没有光滑解支
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\max_x -(x-\theta)^4$, the unique strict global optimizer is $x^*(\theta)=\theta$, a smooth function. Yet the stationarity derivative $F_x=-12(x-\theta)^2$ vanishes everywhere on that branch. The [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|singular-block boundary]] is therefore two-sided in its possibilities: after the theorem's hypothesis fails, an optimizer may be smooth or nonsmooth. Determine that behaviour from additional information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $\max_x -(x-\theta)^4$，唯一严格全局最优选择为 $x^*(\theta)=\theta$，它是光滑函数；但驻点方程的导数 $F_x=-12(x-\theta)^2$ 在整条解支上都为零。因此，[[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|奇异块的边界]]允许不同结果：定理的假设失败以后，最优选择可能光滑，也可能不光滑，必须用额外信息判断。

> [!question] What must be checked? · 还需要检查什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $x^*$ is known to be a strict local maximizer, can that fact alone prove that the optimality-system Jacobian is invertible?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已知 $x^*$ 是严格局部最大点，能否把这一事实直接当作最优性系统 Jacobian 可逆的证明？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. Strictness may come from higher-order terms; the Hessian, reduced Hessian, or full KKT Jacobian must still be checked directly for nonsingularity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。严格性可能来自高阶项；仍需独立检查 Hessian、约化 Hessian或完整 KKT Jacobian 的非奇异性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In that check, choose the matrix appropriate to the proposed conclusion: for an equality-constrained solution-and-multiplier branch, it is the full solved-variable Jacobian. A nonsingular objective Hessian alone is not a replacement for that check; the constrained example above shows why the full and reduced matrices must not be conflated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做这项检查时，应选取与结论对应的矩阵：对于等式约束的解与乘子分支，要检查完整待解变量 Jacobian。目标 Hessian 非奇异本身不能替代这一步；上面的约束例说明，完整矩阵与约化矩阵不能混为一谈。
