# 严格局部最优并不推出 Hessian 或最优性系统 Jacobian 非奇异；隐函数比较静态需要单独的非奇异性假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A strict local optimum does not imply a nonsingular Hessian or optimality-system Jacobian, so implicit comparative statics needs a separate nonsingularity assumption*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 充分条件不能倒推
> Hessian 严格定号可以推出严格局部最优，但反向不成立。严格最优可能由四阶或更高阶项形成，此时二阶矩阵仍可奇异。因而 [[隐函数定理]] 所需的系统 Jacobian 非奇异性，是另一项正则条件，不能从“解是严格的”自动获得。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Strict definiteness can imply a strict local optimum, but the converse fails. Higher-order terms can create a strict optimum while the second-order matrix remains singular. Nonsingularity of the system Jacobian required by the implicit function theorem is therefore a separate regularity condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例：严格最大但 Hessian 为零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample: a strict maximum with zero Hessian*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对
$$
f(x)=-x^4,
$$
$x^*=0$ 是严格全局最大点，但 $f''(0)=0$。这说明“严格最优 $\Rightarrow$ Hessian 非奇异”即使在无约束一维问题中也已经错误；约束问题不能把同一逆推搬到约化 Hessian 或边框矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=-x^4$, the origin is a strict global maximizer even though $f''(0)=0$. Thus strict optimality does not imply a nonsingular Hessian even in one unconstrained dimension, and the same invalid converse cannot be transferred to reduced Hessians or bordered systems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对比较静态的实际后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The practical consequence for comparative statics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑参数化问题
$$
\max_x\{-x^4+\theta x\}.
$$
在 $\theta=0$，严格最优仍是 $x^*=0$；驻点方程为 $-4x^3+\theta=0$，所以
$$
x^*(\theta)=\sqrt[3]{\theta/4}.
$$
这条最优分支在零点不可微。最优选择存在且严格，并不保证普通 [[隐函数比较静态]] 的导数公式可用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the parameterised problem $\max_x\{-x^4+\theta x\}$, the optimizer at zero is strict, but stationarity gives $x^*(\theta)=\sqrt[3]{\theta/4}$, which is not differentiable at zero. A strict and well-defined optimizer therefore need not satisfy the ordinary implicit comparative-statics formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知 $x^*$ 是严格局部最大点，能否把这一事实直接当作最优性系统 Jacobian 可逆的证明？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $x^*$ is known to be a strict local maximizer, can that fact alone prove that the optimality-system Jacobian is invertible?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。严格性可能来自高阶项；仍需独立检查 Hessian、约化 Hessian或完整 KKT Jacobian 的非奇异性。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. Strictness may come from higher-order terms; the Hessian, reduced Hessian, or full KKT Jacobian must still be checked directly for nonsingularity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
