# 行为金融中的噪声交易者依据未获基本信息支持的错误信念作出资产需求决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In behavioral finance, a noise trader chooses asset demand using mistaken beliefs not warranted by fundamental information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡采用 De Long 等行为金融模型中的噪声交易者（noise trader）含义：交易者把噪声当作有用信号，或对未来价格、收益和风险形成错误判断，并据此选择持仓。模型中的错误信念可以随机变化；“随机”描述信念过程，不表示每笔订单都毫无目的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card uses “noise trader” in the behavioral-finance sense of De Long and coauthors: a trader mistakes noise for useful information or forms erroneous beliefs about future prices, returns, or risk and chooses holdings accordingly. Mistaken beliefs can vary stochastically; randomness describes the belief process, not an absence of purpose behind each order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个最小对照是：两人面对相同的可得基本信息，一人仍无依据地把下一期预期价格多估 10，并因此增加需求。在这个设定里，多出的 10 是信念误差；若他确实掌握关于现金流的新信息，或者只是为支付账单出售资产，就不能仅凭交易方向给他贴上这个标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a minimal contrast, suppose two traders face the same available fundamental information, but one unjustifiably adds 10 to the expected next-period price and increases demand. That extra 10 is a belief error in this setup. Possessing genuine new cash-flow information, or selling to pay a bill, does not warrant the label merely from the trade's direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个概念不是散户、亏损者或没有内幕消息者的同义词；机构也可能持有错误信念，理性交易者也可能亏损。其他模型也可能直接用随机交易量刻画噪声交易者，而不从这里的错误信念过程推导；读论文时须先核对作者的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The concept is not synonymous with retail investors, losing traders, or people without inside information. Institutions can hold mistaken beliefs, and rational traders can lose. Other models can represent noise traders directly through random trading quantities rather than derive them from the mistaken-belief process used here. Check the author's definition before transferring conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单个噪声交易者的存在并不保证价格受到可观测影响。其需求是否有足够规模、是否相互抵消，以及专业交易者能否吸收，仍需另外说明；未来合成需求的不确定性如何限制纠偏，见[[噪声交易者风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The presence of an individual noise trader does not guarantee an observable price effect. Demand scale, cancellation across traders, and absorption by specialists require separate analysis. [[噪声交易者风险|Noise-trader risk]] addresses how uncertainty in future aggregate demand can limit corrective trading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- De Long–Shleifer–Summers–Waldmann, PDF pp. 3 and 5–8 / printed pp. 704 and 706–709, reopened, supports mistaking noise for information, belief-driven demand, stochastic beliefs, and the importance of aggregate noise. This card uses its behavioral definition. Adding 10 is an independently constructed illustration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Stambaugh（2014），Investment Noise and Trends，作者稿 PDF 第 2、46 页／稿件第 1、45 页，脚注 1](https://repository.upenn.edu/bitstreams/9a822e05-ce86-4885-8d44-132d1a903187/download#page=46)：已下载并重开大学托管作者稿，支持区分行为金融中的噪声解释与 Kyle 模型中的随机交易量设定；仅用于术语口径，不采用该文全部投资管理模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Stambaugh's university-hosted author manuscript, PDF pp. 2 and 46 / manuscript pp. 1 and 45, footnote 1, downloaded and reopened, supports distinguishing behavioral interpretations of noise from the random-trading specification in Kyle's model. It is used only for terminology, not the paper's full asset-management model.
