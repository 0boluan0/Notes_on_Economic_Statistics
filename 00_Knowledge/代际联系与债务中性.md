# 有限寿命本身不破坏债务中性而须检查未来纳税人是否进入当前可调整的家庭计划
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite lives do not alone break debt neutrality; examine whether future taxpayers enter the current family's adjustable plan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用代际结构检验[[李嘉图等价]]时，关键不是一个人能否活到偿债日，而是未来税负是否由当前决策者计入，并能通过储蓄或私人转移作相应调整。有限寿命的父母若把子女资源纳入计划，且遗赠选择处于可以调整的内点，延后税收可以被增加遗赠抵消。未来纳税人若与当前受益者没有这种有效联系，中性则不由同一家庭的预算抵消推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[李嘉图等价|Ricardian equivalence]], the issue is not simply whether a person survives until repayment. Future liabilities must enter current decisions and permit adjustment through saving or private transfers. Finite-lived parents who internalise children's resources and have an operative interior bequest can offset deferred taxation. If future taxpayers lack such links to current beneficiaries, neutrality does not follow from the same family budget.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设利率为零，父母在今天、子女在明天消费；税前资源分别为 $110$ 与 $20$。政府今天购买固定为 $10$，原来向父母征税 $10$，改为举债并明天向子女征税 $10$。令父母遗赠 $b\geq0$，家庭最大化 $\ln c_P+\ln c_C$。两种方案分别为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let interest be zero, with parents consuming today and children tomorrow, and pre-tax resources of $110$ and $20$. Current government purchases are fixed at $10$. Replace a tax of $10$ on parents with debt and a later tax of $10$ on children. With bequest $b\geq0$, maximise $\ln c_P+\ln c_C$ under:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{当期征税：}&\quad c_P=100-b,\quad c_C=20+b;\\
\text{延期征税：}&\quad c_P=110-b,\quad c_C=10+b.
\end{aligned}
$$

两方案家庭总资源均为 $120$，最优消费都是 $(60,60)$；遗赠分别为 $40$ 与 $50$，都严格大于零。父母把减税所得 $10$ 全部增加给子女，恰好抵消子女未来税款。有限寿命没有阻断这条联系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Family resources are $120$ in either case, and optimal consumption is $(60,60)$. Bequests are $40$ and $50$, both strictly positive. The extra bequest of $10$ exactly offsets the child's future tax. Finite parental life does not interrupt this link.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若父母只关心自身消费且不作遗赠，在这个同样的资源例子中，父母消费从 $100$ 升至 $110$，子女消费从 $20$ 降至 $10$。这说明未被当前决策者内生化的未来税负可以产生代际变化；它不是所有家庭行为的经验描述。更完整的[[公债代际负担|代际负担核算]]还须查资本和公共服务路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If parents care only about their own consumption and leave no bequest, the same example instead changes consumption from $(100,20)$ to $(110,10)$. Future taxes not internalised by current decision-makers can therefore change cohort allocations. This is a conditional example, not a description of all families. A full [[公债代际负担|intergenerational account]] also checks capital and public services.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有遗产”或“有一些利他关怀”都不自动保证所需调整：遗赠若出于交换服务、留名等动机，或已在零遗赠边界，未来税款未必诱发等额抵消。相反，即使现有个人寿命无限，出现未与其联系的新纳税人，也可能破坏简单的同户抵消。应核对具体偏好、转移约束和税负归属，而不是把有限寿命当作充分判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inheritance or some altruism does not automatically deliver the required adjustment. Strategic or other bequest motives, or a zero-bequest corner, need not produce a full offset. Conversely, even infinitely lived existing people may not internalise taxes on newly arriving, unlinked taxpayers. Check preferences, transfer constraints and tax incidence rather than using finite life as a sufficient test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
