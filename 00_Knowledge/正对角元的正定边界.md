# Positive diagonal entries and positive definiteness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正对角元与正定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive diagonal entries check what a matrix does to individual coordinate directions. [[正定矩阵|Positive definiteness]] asks a stronger question: is the [[二次型|quadratic value]] positive for every nonzero input, including inputs that change several coordinates together? The diagonal checks are necessary, but mixed directions can still produce a negative value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角元全正，只检查了单独沿每个坐标方向的情况。[[正定矩阵|正定性]]要求更强：每个非零输入的[[二次型]]值是否都正，包括几个坐标同时变化的输入？对角检查是必要的，混合方向却仍可能产生负值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a real [[实对称矩阵|symmetric matrix]] $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$, where $n\ge1$ is finite. For a real column $x$, the value is $q(x)=x^TAx$; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Let $e_i$ be the vector with a $1$ in coordinate $i$ and zeros elsewhere. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 为[[实对称矩阵]]，$n\ge1$ 为有限整数。对实列向量 $x$，二次型为 $q(x)=x^TAx$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。令 $e_i$ 为第 $i$ 个坐标是 $1$、其余为零的向量，则

$$q(e_i)=e_i^TAe_i=a_{ii},\qquad q(te_i)=t^2a_{ii}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $a_{ii}$ is a diagonal entry and $t$ is a real number. Thus $a_{ii}>0$ checks every nonzero input on that coordinate axis, not merely the single vector $e_i$. A positive-definite matrix must pass all these tests. If any diagonal entry is zero or negative, that one axis already disproves positive definiteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $a_{ii}$ 是对角元，$t$ 为实数。因此 $a_{ii}>0$ 检查的是这条坐标轴上的每个非零输入，不只是单个 $e_i$。正定矩阵必须通过全部这些检查；只要某个对角元为零或负，那一条坐标轴就足以否定正定性。

> [!example] A mixed direction fails · 混合方向未通过
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the symmetric matrix below. Both diagonal entries are $1$, yet combining the two coordinate directions exposes the cross term.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 看下面的对称矩阵：两个对角元都是 $1$，但把两个坐标方向组合起来，就会显露交叉项。
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2+4x_1x_2+x_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=(1,-1)^T$, the positive square terms total $2$, while the cross term equals $-4$. Therefore $q(1,-1)=1-4+1=-2<0$. In contrast, $q(1,1)=6>0$. The matrix is [[不定二次型|indefinite]], despite having a positive diagonal. Its [[特征对|eigenvalues]] are $3$ and $-1$: multiplying the vectors $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$ by $A$ scales them by those factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $x=(1,-1)^T$，两个正平方项合计 $2$，交叉项却为 $-4$，所以 $q(1,-1)=1-4+1=-2<0$。而 $q(1,1)=6>0$，说明矩阵对应[[不定二次型]]，尽管对角元都正。它的[[特征对|特征值]]为 $3$ 和 $-1$：用 $A$ 乘 $(1,1)^T$、$(1,-1)^T$，恰好分别按这两个倍数缩放。

## What completes the test in two dimensions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二维还缺什么条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real symmetric $2\times2$ matrix, the complete condition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称二阶矩阵，完整条件为

$$M=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}\succ0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a>0\ \text{and}\ ac-b^2>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second quantity is the [[行列式|determinant]]. When $a>0$, completing the square explains why it matters:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二个量就是[[行列式]]。当 $a>0$ 时，配方可以解释它的作用：

$$x^TMx=a\left(x_1+\frac ba x_2\right)^2+\frac{ac-b^2}{a}x_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both coefficients are positive, the sum is positive for every nonzero $x$. Conversely, positive definiteness forces $a=q(e_1)>0$. Testing $x=(-b/a,1)^T$ makes the first square vanish and forces $(ac-b^2)/a>0$. In the counterexample, the determinant is $1-4=-3$, which reveals the failed mixed direction. The separate [[二维二次型配方|two-dimensional completion-of-squares card]] develops this calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两个系数都正，则每个非零 $x$ 都使总和严格为正。反过来，正定性先要求 $a=q(e_1)>0$；再测试 $x=(-b/a,1)^T$，第一项平方消失，于是必须有 $(ac-b^2)/a>0$。前面反例的行列式为 $1-4=-3$，揭示了混合方向的失败。[[二维二次型配方]]单独展开这项计算。

## When diagonal checks do suffice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪些情形只看对角就足够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an actual [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]], the off-diagonal entries are zero and $q(x)=\sum_i a_{ii}x_i^2$. In that restricted class, a positive diagonal is sufficient. It is also sufficient for a one-entry matrix. For a general symmetric matrix of dimension at least two, use a complete test such as the [[正定与半正定的谱判据|spectral criterion]] or [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester’s criterion]]. Merely seeing a [[正行列式的正定边界|positive determinant]] does not by itself complete the test in higher dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若矩阵本身是[[对角矩阵]]，非对角元全为零，$q(x)=\sum_i a_{ii}x_i^2$，此时对角元全正就是充分条件。一阶矩阵也属于这一情形。对至少二维的一般实对称矩阵，应使用完整的[[正定与半正定的谱判据|谱判据]]或[[Sylvester 正定判据]]。在更高维中，再看到一个[[正行列式的正定边界|正行列式]]，本身也不能完成判断。
