# 实对称矩阵的对角元全正仍不足以推出正定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Positive diagonal entries cannot rule out a negative direction created by cross-terms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 最小反例
> 正定矩阵必有 $a_{ii}=e_i^TAe_i>0$，所以正对角元是必要条件；但它不是充分条件。反例
> $$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{bmatrix}$$
> 对角元全正，却有特征值 $3,-1$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Positive diagonal entries are necessary for positive definiteness because $a_{ii}=e_i^TAe_i>0$, but they are not sufficient. The displayed matrix has positive diagonal entries and eigenvalues $3$ and $-1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角元只测试 $e_1,e_2$ 两条坐标轴。取斜方向 $x=(1,-1)^T$，
$$
x^TAx=1-4+1=-2<0.
$$
负值来自交叉项 $4x_1x_2$，而逐个观察对角元看不到它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagonal tests only the coordinate axes. Along $x=(1,-1)^T$, the quadratic form is $-2$. The negative value comes from the cross-term, which no individual diagonal entry records.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二维实对称矩阵还必须控制 determinant：$\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{bmatrix}\succ0$ 当且仅当 $a>0$ 且 $ac-b^2>0$。高维则需完整的特征值、主元或 Sylvester 判据，不能只增加几个对角检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a $2\times2$ real symmetric matrix, one also needs $a>0$ and $ac-b^2>0$. In higher dimensions use the complete eigenvalue, pivot, or Sylvester criterion rather than adding a few more diagonal checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 正对角元究竟验证了正定定义中的哪些向量？
>
> **答案：** 只验证了标准基向量 $e_i$，没有验证它们的线性组合方向。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** Which vectors in the definition of positive definiteness are actually tested by positive diagonal entries?
>
> **Answer:** Only the standard basis vectors $e_i$ are tested; directions formed from their linear combinations remain unchecked.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
