# Spectral radius
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*谱半径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **spectral radius** of a square matrix is the largest magnitude of any of its eigenvalues. An [[特征对|eigenvalue]] is a scalar $\lambda$ for which $Av=\lambda v$ holds for some nonzero vector $v$. The radius keeps only the largest magnitude among these scalars. It is useful for understanding repeated multiplication, but it does not describe every possible amplification in a single step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方阵的**谱半径**是全部特征值中最大的模。[[特征对|特征值]]是使某个非零向量满足 $Av=\lambda v$ 的标量 $\lambda$；谱半径只保留这些标量里最大的模。它帮助理解反复相乘的行为，但不能概括一步作用中所有可能的放大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Measure distance from zero, not signed order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*量到零的距离，不按正负大小排序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real number, its modulus is its absolute value. For a complex number $a+bi$, where $a,b$ are real and $i^2=-1$, the modulus is $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$: its distance from zero in the complex plane. Thus $|-2|=2$, $|1/2|=1/2$ and $|i|=1$. In a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]], all entries outside the main diagonal are zero. The notation $\operatorname{diag}$ lists the diagonal entries, so the matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数的模就是绝对值。复数 $a+bi$ 的模为 $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$，其中 $a,b$ 是实数、$i^2=-1$；这就是它在复平面上到零的距离。所以 $|-2|=2$、$|1/2|=1/2$、$|i|=1$。[[对角矩阵]]的主对角线以外元素都为零，$\operatorname{diag}$ 依次列出主对角元。因此矩阵

$$A=\operatorname{diag}(-2,1/2,i)$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
has these three eigenvalues and spectral radius $2$. The negative sign of $-2$ does not make it small. If a real matrix has eigenvalues $-3$ and $1\pm2i$, compare $3$ with $\sqrt5$; its spectral radius is $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

的三个特征值正是这些对角元，谱半径为2。$-2$ 带负号并不表示它的模小。若实矩阵的特征值为 $-3$ 和 $1\pm2i$，应比较3与 $\sqrt5$，谱半径为3。

> [!important] Include the complex spectrum / 计入复数域中的全部特征值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ real or complex matrix, $n\ge1$. Its **spectrum** $\sigma(A)$ is the set of its complex eigenvalues, equivalently the complex roots of its [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]]. Define
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A$ 为 $n\times n$ 实矩阵或复矩阵，$n\ge1$。它的**谱** $\sigma(A)$ 是全部复特征值组成的集合，也就是[[特征多项式]]在复数域中的根集。定义
>
> $$\boxed{\rho(A)=\max_{\lambda\in\sigma(A)}|\lambda|.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Greek letter $\rho$ names the radius; $\sigma(A)$ here is a set, not a singular value or a standard deviation. The complex spectrum is nonempty and finite, so the maximum exists. Repeating an eigenvalue does not change this maximum. For a real matrix, include nonreal eigenvalues as well; changing to complex scalars makes those directions available without changing the matrix entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

希腊字母 $\rho$ 表示这个半径；这里的 $\sigma(A)$ 是集合，不是奇异值或标准差。复谱非空且有限，所以最大值存在；一个特征值重复出现不会改变最大值。对实矩阵也必须计入非实特征值：允许复标量后，这些方向可以出现，而矩阵元素并没有改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the real quarter-turn matrix $R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ has characteristic polynomial $t^2+1$. Its eigenvalues are $i,-i$, so $\rho(R)=1$. Looking only for real eigenvalues would miss both. The [[Jordan标准形的域条件|field conditions]] for other matrix decompositions are separate from this convention of using the full complex spectrum for the radius.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如实矩阵 $R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 表示四分之一圈旋转，特征多项式为 $t^2+1$，特征值是 $i,-i$，所以 $\rho(R)=1$。只寻找实特征值会把二者都漏掉。其他分解能否在某个[[Jordan标准形的域条件|指定域上成立]]，与谱半径按全部复谱计算的约定是不同问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Radius and largest one-step stretch answer different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*谱半径与一步最大伸长回答不同问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[谱范数|spectral norm]] $\|A\|_2$ is the largest ratio of output length to input length over all nonzero inputs. It includes directions that are not eigenvectors. Hence $\rho(A)\le\|A\|_2$: for any complex eigenvector $v\ne0$, $\|Av\|_2/\|v\|_2=|\lambda|$. Here vector length is the square root of the sum of squared absolute coordinate values, as in the standard [[内积|inner product]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[谱范数]] $\|A\|_2$ 比较所有非零输入，取输出长度与输入长度之比的最大值，其中包括并非特征向量的方向。因此 $\rho(A)\le\|A\|_2$：任取复特征向量 $v\ne0$，就有 $\|Av\|_2/\|v\|_2=|\lambda|$。这里向量长度是坐标绝对值平方和再开平方，与标准[[内积]]一致。

> [!example] Radius zero does not mean a zero matrix / 谱半径为零不表示矩阵为零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, both eigenvalues are zero, so $\rho(N)=0$. Yet $N(0,1)^T=(1,0)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]; its spectral norm is $1$. A second application kills every input because $N^2=0$. This nonzero matrix with radius zero shows that spectral radius is not a matrix norm.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$，两个特征值都为零，所以 $\rho(N)=0$；但 $N(0,1)^T=(1,0)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，而且 $\|N\|_2=1$。第二次作用才把所有输入消去，因为 $N^2=0$。非零矩阵的谱半径可以为零，说明谱半径不是矩阵范数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵幂趋零判据|vanishing-power theorem]] gives its exact long-run role: $A^k$ tends to zero precisely when $\rho(A)<1$, without requiring diagonalization. That does not make every step smaller. For $B=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&3\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$, the radius is $1/2$, but the unit input $(0,1)^T$ becomes $(3,1/2)^T$, whose length exceeds $3$. Its later powers still tend to zero. This distinction is developed in [[非正规矩阵瞬态|finite-time transient amplification]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵幂趋零判据]]给出谱半径对长期行为的精确作用：当且仅当 $\rho(A)<1$ 时，$A^k$ 趋于零，不要求可对角化。但这不表示每一步都变小。例如 $B=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&3\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$ 的谱半径为 $1/2$，单位输入 $(0,1)^T$ 却变为 $(3,1/2)^T$，长度超过3；其后矩阵幂仍最终趋零。两者的区别详见[[非正规矩阵瞬态|有限时瞬态放大]]。
