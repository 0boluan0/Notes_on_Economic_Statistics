# 价格弹性是无量纲局部响应而其数值依赖区间期限与市场边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Price elasticity is a unit-free local response whose value depends on interval, horizon, and market boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子度量
> 当 $p>0$、$q>0$ 且 $q(p)$ 在所考察点可微时，点价格弹性写成
> $$\varepsilon_{q,p}=\frac{dq}{dp}\frac{p}{q}=\frac{d\log q}{d\log p},$$
> 表示价格变化 $1\%$ 附近时数量约变化多少百分比。它消除了“元、便士、千克、吨”等计量单位的影响，但不是一个脱离价格点、时间期与市场定义的常数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $p>0$, $q>0$, and $q(p)$ is differentiable at the point under study, point price elasticity is
> $$\varepsilon_{q,p}=\frac{dq}{dp}\frac{p}{q}=\frac{d\log q}{d\log p},$$
> the approximate percentage quantity response to a one-per-cent price change near a given point. It removes dependence on units such as pounds versus pence or kilograms versus tonnes, but it is not a constant independent of price, horizon, and market definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿同一条非恒定弹性需求曲线，不同价格点可有不同弹性。对两个满足 $p_i,q_i>0$ 且 $p_2\ne p_1$ 的离散点 $(p_1,q_1)$、$(p_2,q_2)$，用起点百分比会产生方向不对称；常见的 midpoint arc 口径是

$$\varepsilon^{\mathrm{arc}}_{q,p}
=\frac{(q_2-q_1)/[(q_1+q_2)/2]}{(p_2-p_1)/[(p_1+p_2)/2]}.$$

它概括两点之间的割线响应，不是某一点的导数；点弹性与弧弹性回答的近似问题不同，不能混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Elasticity may vary across points on the same non-constant-elasticity demand curve. For two discrete observations $(p_1,q_1)$ and $(p_2,q_2)$ with $p_i,q_i>0$ and $p_2\ne p_1$, percentages based on the starting point are direction-dependent. A common midpoint arc convention is

$$\varepsilon^{\mathrm{arc}}_{q,p}
=\frac{(q_2-q_1)/[(q_1+q_2)/2]}{(p_2-p_1)/[(p_1+p_2)/2]}.$$

It summarises a secant response between two points, not a derivative at one point. Point and arc elasticities answer different approximation questions and should not be mixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需求价格弹性通常为负，报告时必须说明保留符号还是报告绝对值。短期内替代、库存和产能调整有限，长期弹性往往不同；“某品牌汽水”与“所有饮料”也因替代品集合不同而有不同弹性。把一次短期局部估计直接当作长期政策参数，会改变问题而不只是改变数字精度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Demand price elasticity is usually negative, so a report must state whether it retains the sign or gives an absolute value. Substitution, inventories, and capacity adjust differently over time, and a narrow brand market has a different substitute set from the market for all beverages. Treating one short-run local estimate as a long-run policy parameter changes the question, not merely numerical precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 把价格单位从英镑改成便士会改变正确计算的点弹性吗？把市场从“某品牌咖啡”改成“全部热饮”呢？
>
> **答案：** 单纯换计量单位不会；改变市场边界会改变替代集合和响应对象，弹性通常会变。
