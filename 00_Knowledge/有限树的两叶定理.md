# Two leaves in a finite tree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限树的两叶定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite tree with more than one vertex always has at least two ends where only one edge is attached. These degree-one vertices let us remove a small terminal piece while keeping the rest connected. That is why leaves are useful in proofs by induction and in simplifying tree structures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一棵不止一个顶点的有限树，总有至少两个末端位置，每处只接着一条边。这样的度一顶点允许我们删去一个末端小部分，同时保持剩下的图连通，因此常用于归纳证明和树结构的简化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[无向树|undirected tree]] is nonempty, connected and acyclic. In this graph the [[度数、入度与出度|degree]] $\deg(v)$ is the number of edges incident to a vertex $v$. The theorem concerns degree exactly one. We call such a vertex a **leaf**; a textbook convention that also calls isolated vertices leaves is discussed below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无向树]]非空、连通且无环。在这张图中，顶点 $v$ 的[[度数、入度与出度|度数]] $\deg(v)$ 是与它关联的边数。本定理讨论度数恰为一的顶点，称其为**叶子**；有的教材也把孤立顶点叫叶子，下文会区分这一术语约定。

> [!important] Two distinct degree-one vertices · 两个不同的度一顶点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every finite tree with at least two vertices has **at least two** distinct vertices of degree one. It may have more: the path $a-b-c-d$ has two leaves, $a,d$, whereas a star with center $s$ joined to three other vertices has three leaves.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每棵至少含两个顶点的有限树，都有**至少两个**不同的度一顶点。叶子可以更多：路径 $a-b-c-d$ 的叶子是 $a,d$ 两个；一个中心 $s$ 分别连接另外三个顶点的星形树则有三片叶子。

## Look at both ends of a longest path
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*看一条最长路径的两端*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a [[游走路径与环|path]] of maximum length and write its vertices as $v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_k$. Such a choice exists: with $n$ finite vertices, a path visits each vertex at most once and has at most $n-1$ edges. Connectedness and $n\ge2$ ensure that some edge exists, so a longest path has $k\ge1$ and two different endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选一条长度最大的[[游走路径与环|路径]]，把其顶点记为 $v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_k$。这个选择确实存在：顶点数 $n$ 有限，路径不重复顶点，因此至多有 $n-1$ 条边。连通且 $n\ge2$ 保证至少有一条边，所以最长路径满足 $k\ge1$，两端是不同顶点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first endpoint $v_0$ is adjacent to $v_1$. Suppose it had another neighbor $w\ne v_1$. If $w$ lies outside the path, the sequence $w,v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_k$ is a longer path, a contradiction. If $w$ lies on the path, then $w=v_j$ for some $j\ge2$; the path segment $v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_j$ together with the edge back to $v_0$ is a cycle, also a contradiction. Thus $v_1$ is its only neighbor and $\deg(v_0)=1$. The same reasoning at the other end gives $\deg(v_k)=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

起点 $v_0$ 与 $v_1$ 相邻。假设它还有另一个邻点 $w\ne v_1$：若 $w$ 不在路径上，$w,v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_k$ 就是更长路径，矛盾；若 $w$ 已在路径上，则 $w=v_j$，其中 $j\ge2$，路径段 $v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_j$ 再接回到 $v_0$ 的边，便构成环，也矛盾。因此 $v_1$ 是唯一邻点，$\deg(v_0)=1$。另一端同理，得到 $\deg(v_k)=1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two cases are both necessary. A new edge to a vertex already on the path does not lengthen a simple path; it is excluded because it creates a cycle. The proof uses maximum length, not merely a path that looks long in a drawing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述两种情况都必须处理。额外连到路径上已有顶点的边，并不会延长一条简单路径；排除它的理由是它会成环。证明使用真正的最大长度，而非图上看起来较长的一条路线。

## What removing a leaf preserves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*删去叶子后保留什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Remove a degree-one leaf and its unique incident edge from a tree with $n\ge2$ vertices. The remaining graph has $n-1\ge1$ vertices and is still acyclic. It is also connected: a path between two remaining vertices could not have used the leaf internally, because an internal path vertex needs two distinct incident path edges. Their original connecting path therefore remains. The result is a smaller tree, including a single vertex when $n=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $n\ge2$ 个顶点的树中删去一片度一叶子及其唯一关联边，剩下 $n-1\ge1$ 个顶点，仍然无环。它也仍连通：连接两个剩余顶点的路径不可能把该叶子当作内部顶点，因为内部路径顶点需要两条不同的关联路径边。因此原来的连接路径仍在。所得是更小的树；$n=2$ 时，剩下单顶点树。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This justifies the leaf-removal step in many finite-tree inductions. For example, when restoring the leaf in a [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper vertex coloring]], where adjacent vertices must have different colors, only its one neighbor constrains its color. It also explains why leaf elimination is available in a forest component containing an edge; an isolated component needs no such elimination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这说明了许多有限树归纳证明中“删叶”步骤为何合法。例如，在[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法顶点着色]]中，相邻顶点必须异色；恢复叶子时，只有它的唯一邻点限制其颜色。这也解释了为什么森林中含边的分量可以逐步删叶，而孤立分量无需这样的消去步骤。

## Finiteness, singleton trees and the word leaf
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限性、单点树与叶子的术语约定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A one-vertex tree has degree zero, so it has no degree-one vertex. MIT's 2015 Session 21 reading defines a leaf to have degree one; its 2018 textbook instead permits degree at most one in a forest. Thus the singleton has no leaf under the first convention and one under the second. Neither convention gives it two degree-one vertices. The theorem's $n\ge2$ condition removes this ambiguity because a connected graph with more than one vertex has no isolated vertex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单点树的唯一顶点度数为零，所以没有度一顶点。MIT 2015 Session 21 reading 把叶子定义为度一顶点；2018 主教材则把森林中度数至多一的顶点都算作叶子。因此单点树在前一种约定下没有叶子，后一种约定下有一片；两种约定都不会让它有两个度一顶点。定理要求 $n\ge2$ 后，连通图没有孤立顶点，术语歧义便不影响结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finiteness matters too. Consider the infinite graph with vertex set $\mathbb Z$, the integers, and an edge joining each integer $j$ to $j+1$. It is connected and acyclic, but every vertex has degree two. There is no longest finite path and no degree-one vertex. Conversely, merely having two leaves does not prove that a finite graph is a tree: attach one terminal vertex to each of two vertices of a triangle. The two new vertices have degree one, while the triangle remains a cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限性也不能省。取以整数集合 $\mathbb Z$ 为顶点的无限图，每个整数 $j$ 都与 $j+1$ 相连。它连通且无环，却每个顶点的度都为二；没有最长的有限路径，也没有度一顶点。反过来，只有两片叶子也不能证明有限图是树：在三角形的两个顶点上各接一个末端新顶点，新顶点的度都为一，但三角形的环依然存在。
