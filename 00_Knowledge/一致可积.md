# Uniform integrability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一致可积

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A family of random variables is **uniformly integrable** when very large values make only a small contribution to the average absolute size, using one cutoff that works for every member of the family. It controls both how rare large values are and how much they weigh.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一族随机变量**一致可积**，意味着可以选同一个“大值阈值”，使族中每个变量在阈值之外贡献的平均绝对大小都很小。它同时关心大值出现得多罕见，以及那些大值究竟有多大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[期望|expectation]] is a probability-weighted average. A variable is *integrable* if $E|X|<\infty$: its average absolute size is finite. The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $\mathbf1_{\{|X|>K\}}$ is one when the value exceeds the cutoff $K$ in absolute size, and zero otherwise. Thus $|X|\mathbf1_{\{|X|>K\}}$ keeps the large values and replaces all other values by zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[期望]]是按概率加权的平均。$E|X|<\infty$称为可积，即平均绝对大小有限。[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]$\mathbf1_{\{|X|>K\}}$在绝对大小超过阈值$K$时等于1，否则等于0；乘上它就只保留大值，其他值全部变成零。

> [!example] A rare value can still carry a large average · 罕见不等于贡献小
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $X$ equals 1000 with probability 0.001 and zero otherwise. For a cutoff $K=100$, the tail probability is 0.001, but its tail expectation is $1000\times0.001=1$. The quantity to make small is this weighted contribution, not just the probability of exceeding the cutoff.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若$X$以0.001概率取1000，其余时候为零，阈值$K=100$之外的概率只有0.001，但尾部期望是$1000\times0.001=1$。一致可积要压低的是这份加权贡献，不能只看超阈值概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One threshold for the whole family
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个阈值控制整个族

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\{X_i:i\in I\}$ be a nonempty family of integrable real random variables. The index $i$ labels members; it need not be a time index. Write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设$\{X_i:i\in I\}$是非空的一族可积实随机变量，$i$只是区分成员的指标，不一定表示时间。记

$$
T(K)=\sup_{i\in I}E\!\left[|X_i|\mathbf1_{\{|X_i|>K\}}\right].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The supremum is the least upper bound across all members: it measures the largest tail contribution, even when no member attains it. The family is uniformly integrable when $T(K)\to0$ as $K\to\infty$. In quantifiers: for every tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, there is one $K$ such that the tail expectation is below $\varepsilon$ **for every** $i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上确界是所有成员尾部贡献的最小上界，即使没有哪个成员恰好达到它也有定义。一致可积要求$K\to\infty$时$T(K)\to0$。量词顺序是：给定任意容差$\varepsilon>0$，能找到同一个$K$，使**每一个**$i$的尾部期望都小于$\varepsilon$。

> [!warning] Tail contribution is not a conditional average · 尾部贡献不是条件平均
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $E[|X|\mathbf1_{\{|X|>K\}}]$ averages over the entire probability space, putting zero outside the tail. When the tail has positive probability, it equals $P(|X|>K)E[|X|\mid |X|>K]$. Dropping the probability factor changes the quantity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $E[|X|\mathbf1_{\{|X|>K\}}]$是在整个概率空间上求平均，尾部之外按零计算。若尾部概率为正，它等于$P(|X|>K)E[|X|\mid |X|>K]$，不能漏掉前面的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the definition is easy to satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哪些情况容易满足

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If all variables satisfy $|X_i|\le10$ almost surely, taking $K\ge10$ makes every tail contribution zero. More generally, if one integrable random variable $Y\ge0$ bounds all the $|X_i|$, each tail contribution is at most $E[Y\mathbf1_{\{Y>K\}}]$, which tends to zero because $Y$ is integrable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若所有变量都几乎必然满足$|X_i|\le10$，取$K\ge10$就让全部尾部贡献变成零。更一般地，若同一个可积非负变量$Y$控制所有$|X_i|$，则每个尾部贡献都不超过$E[Y\mathbf1_{\{Y>K\}}]$；$Y$可积保证它随$K$增大趋于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite family of integrable variables is also uniformly integrable. Each member has a cutoff for a given tolerance; the maximum of these finitely many cutoffs works for all of them. An infinite family has no such automatic maximum: the large values can keep moving outward as the index changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限个可积变量组成的族也一致可积：针对给定容差，分别选出各成员的阈值，再取有限个阈值的最大值即可。无限族就没有这个自动保证，因为随着指标改变，大值可能不断向更远处移动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This explains why a [[L1有界不推一致可积|uniform bound on average absolute size can still fail]]. A [[Lp有界推出一致可积|uniform higher-moment bound]] is a convenient sufficient condition. When a sequence already [[依概率收敛|converges in probability]], the [[一致可积与L1收敛|uniform-integrability criterion]] tells us exactly when this becomes [[L1收敛|convergence of mean absolute error]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也解释了为何[[L1有界不推一致可积|统一控制平均绝对大小仍然不够]]。[[Lp有界推出一致可积|统一的高阶矩界]]是一个方便的充分条件。序列已经[[依概率收敛]]时，[[一致可积与L1收敛|一致可积判据]]能判断它是否进一步达到[[L1收敛|平均绝对误差趋零]]。
