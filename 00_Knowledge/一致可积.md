# 可积随机变量族一致可积，当且仅当其大值尾部的绝对期望可被同一个阈值一致压到零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A family of integrable random variables is uniformly integrable exactly when one common threshold makes every absolute tail expectation small*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 设 $\{X_i:i\in I\}$ 是一族可积随机变量。若
> $$
> \lim_{K\to\infty}
> \sup_{i\in I}
> E\!\left[|X_i|\mathbf1_{\{|X_i|>K\}}\right]=0,
> $$
> 就称这族随机变量**一致可积**（uniformly integrable, UI）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A family is uniformly integrable when the largest absolute tail expectation beyond a common threshold $K$ tends to zero as $K$ grows.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“一致”是定义的核心：先选一个共同阈值 $K$，再要求族中每个随机变量超过该阈值的绝对期望都小。不能为不同的 $X_i$ 各选一个不同阈值；那只是在分别使用每个可积变量自己的尾部收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order of quantifiers matters. One threshold must control every member of the family; choosing a separate threshold for each variable is only ordinary integrability, not uniform integrability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任何只含有限多个可积随机变量的族都一致可积，因为可以分别压低有限多个尾部后取最大的阈值。困难只会在无限族中出现，此时尾部质量可能随着指标不断向外移动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every finite family of integrable variables is uniformly integrable: finitely many individual tail bounds can be controlled by one maximum threshold. The issue arises for infinite families whose tail mass can move outward with the index.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统一的一阶矩界为什么仍不够，见 [[L1有界不推一致可积]]；一个常用的高阶矩充分条件见 [[Lp有界推出一致可积]]；怎样把路径收敛升级为平均绝对误差收敛，见 [[一致可积与L1收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A uniform first-moment bound may fail to control the tails, a higher-moment bound can provide sufficient control, and uniform integrability can upgrade pathwise convergence to $L^1$ convergence under the stated conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若对每个 $X_i$ 都能找到自己的阈值 $K_i$，使其阈值外绝对期望很小，是否已经证明这族变量一致可积？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If every $X_i$ has its own threshold making its tail expectation small, has uniform integrability been proved?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 没有。一致可积要求同一个 $K$ 同时控制整个族，并且这种统一尾部上界在 $K\to\infty$ 时趋于零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. One common threshold must control the entire family, and the resulting uniform tail bound must vanish as the threshold grows.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
