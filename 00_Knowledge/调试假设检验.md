# 调试应提出符合现有证据的故障假设并用预先解释结果的可重复实验检验它
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Debugging should propose a fault hypothesis consistent with existing evidence and test it through a repeatable experiment whose outcomes are interpreted in advance.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先同时查看失败与通过的用例，再写出能够解释这些观测的假设。运行实验之前说明：在哪个位置观察什么状态，哪些结果会反驳该假设。打印或断点是获取证据的工具；不断改动代码直到某次通过，不会自动解释原因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Examine both failing and passing cases before proposing an explanation. Before the experiment, specify where to observe which state and what result would refute the hypothesis. Prints and breakpoints collect evidence; repeated edits until a case passes do not automatically explain the cause.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，对 `['a', 'b']` 错误返回 `True`，假设“补上 `reverse()` 的调用括号就能修复”。补后观察到 `temp` 和 `x` 都变成 `['b', 'a']`，比较仍为真，便否定了“这一步足以修复”的判断。再检查 `temp is x`，可检验二者是否共享同一列表，并用[[对象别名]]解释为什么两个观察值一起改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose `['a', 'b']` is wrongly accepted. Test the hypothesis that adding the invocation parentheses to `reverse()` is sufficient. If both `temp` and `x` become `['b', 'a']` and equality remains true, that refutes sufficiency. Checking `temp is x` then tests whether they share one list; [[对象别名|aliasing]] explains why both observations change together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将观察点放在流程中部，可以先判断某个中间状态是否已经偏离预期，再向前或向后缩小调查范围。一个状态看起来正常不证明此前代码全都正确；故障可能尚未显现，也可能还有另一个故障。记录实验和结果，每次修改后重跑相关用例及[[回归测试]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An observation point near the middle of a flow can show whether an intermediate state has already diverged, guiding the next investigation earlier or later. A normal-looking state does not prove all earlier code correct: a fault may remain latent, or another fault may coexist. Record experiments and rerun relevant cases and [[回归测试|regression tests]] after changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
