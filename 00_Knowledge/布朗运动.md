# 布朗运动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Brownian motion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 布朗运动提供连续时间随机波动的基准；鞅刻画在当前信息下没有可预测漂移的“公平”过程。
> **具体锚点：** 标准布朗运动在长度 h 的未来增量服从 $N(0,h)$，但路径连续且几乎处处不可导。
> **核心难点：** 连续路径不等于平滑路径；鞅是相对于信息流定义的，换信息集后性质可能改变。
> **为什么重要：** 随机微积分、金融定价、扩散模型和序贯统计都从这两个对象出发。
> **继续：** 先掌握定义与条件期望，再接触停时和 Itô 积分；当前课程未展开的内容保留为入口而非伪装成已掌握。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Brownian motion is the benchmark for continuous-time random fluctuation, while a martingale describes a “fair” process with no predictable drift given current information.
> **Concrete anchor:** A future increment of standard Brownian motion over an interval of length $h$ is $N(0,h)$, yet its paths are continuous and almost everywhere non-differentiable.
> **Central difficulty:** A continuous path need not be smooth. Martingales are defined relative to an information flow, and changing the information set can change the martingale property.
> **Why it matters:** Stochastic calculus, financial pricing, diffusion models, and sequential statistics begin with these two objects.
> **Continue with:** First master the definition and conditional expectation, then move to stopping times in [[鞅、停时与可选停止|martingales, stopping times, and optional stopping]] and to Itô integration. Material not developed in the present course remains an entry point rather than being presented as mastered content.

## 标准布朗运动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Standard Brownian motion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$W_0=0$，具有独立平稳增量，$W_t-W_s\sim N(0,t-s)$，且样本路径连续。由此 $E[W_t]=0$、$\operatorname{Cov}(W_s,W_t)=\min(s,t)$。尺度性质 $W_{ct}\overset d=\sqrt c,W_t$ 连接时间与波动幅度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$W_0=0$, Brownian motion has independent stationary increments, $W_t-W_s\sim N(0,t-s)$, and continuous sample paths. Consequently, $E[W_t]=0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(W_s,W_t)=\min(s,t)$. The scaling property $W_{ct}\overset d=\sqrt c,W_t$ links elapsed time to fluctuation size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差公式来自共享历史：当 $s\le t$，写 $W_t=W_s+(W_t-W_s)$，后一个增量与 $W_s$ 独立，所以 $Cov(W_s,W_t)=Var(W_s)=s$。这也显示布朗运动本身不平稳，因为方差随 t 增长；平稳的是它的增量分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The covariance formula comes from shared history. For $s\le t$, write $W_t=W_s+(W_t-W_s)$. The later increment is independent of $W_s$, so $Cov(W_s,W_t)=Var(W_s)=s$. This also shows that Brownian motion itself is not stationary because its variance grows with time; its increment distribution is stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连续但不可导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuous but non-differentiable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

布朗增量典型大小为 $\sqrt{h}$，差商约为 $1/\sqrt h$，随 h 缩小不稳定。因此路径几乎处处不可导，普通微积分链式法则不能直接应用；这正是 Itô 校正项出现的直觉来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Brownian increment over length $h$ is typically of order $\sqrt h$, so its difference quotient is of order $1/\sqrt h$ and becomes unstable as $h$ shrinks. Paths are therefore almost everywhere non-differentiable, and the ordinary chain rule cannot be applied directly. This is the intuition behind the Itô correction term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性说明短时间内绝对位移趋于零，却没有规定位移与时间成线性比例。布朗运动具有非零二次变差 $[W]_t=t$；普通光滑路径的二次变差为零。正是这个尺度差异使随机微积分不同于普通微积分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity says that absolute displacement tends to zero over a short interval, but it does not make displacement proportional to elapsed time. Brownian motion has non-zero quadratic variation, $[W]_t=t$, whereas an ordinary smooth path has zero quadratic variation. This scaling difference is what separates stochastic calculus from ordinary calculus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 漂移与尺度变换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Drift and scale transformations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过程 $X_t=\mu t+\sigma W_t$ 在长度 h 上的增量服从 $N(\mu h,\sigma^2h)$。$\mu$ 控制平均漂移，$\sigma$ 控制波动尺度；二者的时间阶分别是 h 与 $\sqrt h$。在极短期限，随机项相对于漂移可以占主导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $X_t=\mu t+\sigma W_t$, an increment over length $h$ is $N(\mu h,\sigma^2h)$. The parameter $\mu$ controls average drift and $\sigma$ the scale of fluctuation. Their time orders are $h$ and $\sqrt h$ respectively, so random fluctuation can dominate drift over very short horizons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：首次越过与反射直觉
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: first passage and the reflection intuition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $M_t=\max_{0\le s\le t}W_s$。反射原理给 $P(M_t\ge a)=2P(W_t\ge a)$（$a>0$）。例如 t=1、a=1 时，标准布朗运动在一年内曾越过 1 的概率约为 $2[1-\Phi(1)]\approx0.317$，大于终点 $W_1\ge1$ 的概率约 0.159。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $M_t=\max_{0\le s\le t}W_s$. The reflection principle gives $P(M_t\ge a)=2P(W_t\ge a)$ for $a>0$. With $t=1$ and $a=1$, the probability that standard Brownian motion has crossed 1 at some time during the year is about $2[1-\Phi(1)]\approx0.317$, greater than the endpoint probability $P(W_1\ge1)\approx0.159$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

终点分布不能回答整条路径是否触碰边界；反射原理通过把首次触碰后的一段路径镜像，建立越界路径与终点尾部之间的对应。遇到 barrier 或 hitting-time 问题时，必须区分“最终在上方”和“途中曾到达”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The endpoint distribution alone cannot answer whether the path touched a boundary. The reflection principle mirrors the path after its first hit, linking boundary-crossing paths to an endpoint tail probability. In barrier or hitting-time problems, “ending above” must be distinguished from “having reached at some earlier time”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 随机积分入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry point to stochastic integration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Itô 积分以左端可预测 integrand 的平方可积极限定义，满足均值 0 和 Itô 等距。Itô 公式比普通链式法则多出二阶变差项；在未系统学习前应把它视为下一层，而不是背公式替代理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Itô integral is defined as an $L^2$ limit using predictable left-endpoint integrands and has mean zero together with the Itô isometry. Itô's formula contains an additional quadratic-variation term relative to the ordinary chain rule. Before studying it systematically, treat it as the next layer rather than memorising formulas in place of understanding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要因路径看起来“锯齿”就确认 Brownian；应检查连续路径假设、独立平稳增量和增量方差与时间长度成比例。离散观测无法直接验证连续路径，也容易把微观结构噪声、跳跃或条件异方差误认为 Brownian 波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A jagged-looking path does not establish Brownian motion. Check continuity, independent stationary increments, and variance proportional to elapsed time. Discrete observations cannot directly verify continuous paths and may confuse microstructure noise, jumps, or conditional heteroskedasticity with Brownian fluctuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 布朗路径连续，为什么仍几乎处处不可导？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why are Brownian paths almost everywhere non-differentiable despite being continuous?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 长度 h 的增量量级是平方根 h，除以 h 后差商量级为 $1/\sqrt h$，不会稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> An increment over length $h$ is of order $\sqrt h$. Dividing by $h$ gives a difference quotient of order $1/\sqrt h$, which does not stabilise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Brownian motion 本身平稳吗？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Is Brownian motion itself stationary?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不是，$Var(W_t)=t$ 随时间变化；它具有平稳独立增量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. Its variance $Var(W_t)=t$ changes with time. It is the increments that are stationary and independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么普通链式法则不能直接用于 $f(W_t)$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can the ordinary chain rule not be applied directly to $f(W_t)$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Brownian 路径不可导且具有非零二次变差；二阶小量累积后不消失，产生 Itô 公式中的二阶校正项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Brownian paths are non-differentiable and have non-zero quadratic variation. Second-order terms accumulate rather than vanish, producing the second-order correction in Itô's formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
