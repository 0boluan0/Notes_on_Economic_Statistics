# Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Lewis–Fei–Ranis Dual-Economy Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释传统部门存在低边际产出劳动力时，现代部门如何通过利润再投资扩大资本和就业，直到剩余劳动力耗尽。
> **具体锚点：** 若一个家庭农场减少一名成员却几乎不降低总产出，这名成员进入现代部门可提高全经济产出；但城市扩张还需要足够食品供给。
> **核心难点：** “无限供给”不是劳动力真的无穷，而是在某一阶段内，现代部门可以按近乎不变的实际工资吸纳更多劳动。
> **为什么重要：** 它把资本积累、利润份额、农业剩余、工业就业和工资转折放进同一个过程。
> **继续：** 如果城乡之间的关键不是剩余劳动，而是正式岗位概率，进入 [[Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains how a modern sector can expand capital and employment through reinvested profits when a traditional sector contains workers with low marginal product, until surplus labor is exhausted.
> **Concrete anchor:** If a family farm loses one member with almost no reduction in total output, moving that worker into a modern job can raise economy-wide output. Yet urban expansion still requires enough food supply.
> **Central difficulty:** “Unlimited supply” does not mean infinitely many workers. It means that during a phase, the modern sector can hire additional labor at an approximately constant real wage.
> **Why it matters:** It puts capital accumulation, the profit share, agricultural surplus, industrial employment, and the wage turning point into one process.
> **Continue:** If the key rural–urban margin is the probability of obtaining a formal job rather than surplus labor, see [[Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门|Todaro Migration, Urban Unemployment, and Informality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Lewis 二元部门
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Lewis's two sectors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传统部门有共享收入/低边际产出劳动力，现代部门按制度工资雇佣并使用资本。现代利润再投资扩大资本和劳动需求，在工资近稳定时提高利润份额和积累。模型强调结构重配而非单纯人口移动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The traditional sector contains shared-income labor or workers with low marginal product, while the modern sector hires at an institutional wage and uses capital. Reinvestment of modern profits expands capital and labor demand; while wages remain approximately stable, the profit share and accumulation rise. The model emphasizes structural reallocation rather than population movement alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现代部门工资 $w_M$ 通常必须高于传统部门的实际可得收入 $w_S$，用以补偿迁移、城市生活和工作纪律成本。企业在劳动边际产品价值等于 $w_M$ 处雇佣；利润再投资把劳动需求向外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The modern wage $w_M$ generally must exceed income available in the subsistence sector $w_S$ to compensate for migration, urban living, and work-discipline costs. Firms hire until the value of labor's marginal product equals $w_M$; reinvested profit shifts labor demand outward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 剩余劳动力与转折点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Surplus labor and the turning point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要从农业抽离劳动力不显著降低产出，现代部门面临近水平劳动供给。当农业边际产出上升、农村收入和食品价格推动现代工资上升时到达 Lewis turning point。现实测量需看季节、性别和家庭生产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As long as withdrawing labor from agriculture does not materially reduce output, the modern sector faces an approximately horizontal labor supply. The Lewis turning point arrives when agricultural marginal product rises and rural income or food prices push up modern wages. Measurement must account for seasonality, gender, and household production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转折点不是“农业人口归零”，而是继续转移一名劳动者已有可观的机会成本。此后现代部门扩张需要更高工资、更高农业生产率或更多技术节省劳动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The turning point does not mean that agriculture employs nobody. It means that moving one more worker now has a material opportunity cost. Further modern expansion then requires higher wages, higher agricultural productivity, or more labor-saving technology.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Fei–Ranis 对农业约束的补充
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fei–Ranis and the agricultural constraint*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该框架更细分农业剩余、制度工资与商品市场，强调必须有足够农业产出支持城市扩张。若粮食供给跟不上，价格上升会挤压工业利润和积累。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Fei–Ranis framework distinguishes agricultural surplus, institutional wages, and commodity markets more carefully and stresses that enough agricultural output must support urban expansion. If food supply lags, rising food prices squeeze industrial profit and accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这补上了一个常见漏洞：劳动力能从农村移出，不代表食品也能无成本地支持城市。若农业市场剩余下降，名义工资必须随食品价格上调，原本预期的利润—投资环会变弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This closes a common gap: labor can leave rural areas without food becoming costlessly available to cities. If marketed agricultural surplus falls, nominal wages must rise with food prices and the expected profit–investment loop weakens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从原主题保留的全局定位
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Global orientation retained from the original topic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **它解决什么：** 描述传统低生产率部门与现代高生产率部门并存时，资本积累、工资和迁移怎样推动或阻滞结构转型。
> **具体锚点：** 农村存在边际产出很低的劳动力，城市企业以高于农村平均收入的制度工资吸纳；利润再投资扩大就业，直到剩余劳动力耗尽。
> **核心难点：** “无限供给劳动力”是阶段性假设；城市高工资也会诱发迁移和失业，不能只比较实际工资。
> **为什么重要：** 它把增长、分配、城市化和就业放进同一转型过程。
> **继续：** 先读 Lewis，再用 Fei–Ranis 补农业剩余与转折，用 [[Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门|Todaro]] 解释预期收入迁移。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It describes how capital accumulation, wages, and migration advance or impede structural transformation when traditional low-productivity and modern high-productivity sectors coexist.
> **Concrete anchor:** Rural labor with very low marginal product is absorbed by urban firms at an institutional wage above average rural income; reinvested profit expands employment until surplus labor is exhausted.
> **Central difficulty:** “Unlimited labor supply” is a phase-specific assumption. High urban wages can also induce migration and unemployment, so observed wages alone are insufficient.
> **Why it matters:** It unifies growth, distribution, urbanization, and employment in one transformation process.
> **Continue:** Begin with Lewis, add Fei–Ranis for agricultural surplus and the turning point, and use [[Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门|Todaro]] for expected-income migration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把农业平均收入低误当成每一名劳动者边际产出都为零。
- 看到工业就业扩大便认为利润会自动再投资，忽略资本外流、寻租和进口。
- 只看农村劳动供给，不检查粮食剩余和城市实际工资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Mistaking low average agricultural income for zero marginal product of every worker.
- Assuming that expanding industrial employment makes profit reinvestment automatic while ignoring capital flight, rent extraction, and imports.
- Looking only at rural labor supply without checking food surplus and the urban real wage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Lewis 转折点意味着什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does the Lewis turning point mean?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 剩余劳动力基本耗尽，继续从农业转移会提高农村边际产出/工资，现代部门劳动供给不再近水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Surplus labor is largely exhausted. Further agricultural withdrawal raises rural marginal product or wages, so modern-sector labor supply is no longer approximately horizontal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释，为什么 Lewis 模型里的“无限”不是字面无穷。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why “unlimited” in the Lewis model does not literally mean infinite.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它指在剩余劳动力存在的阶段，现代部门可按近似不变的实际工资持续招工；到转折点后该条件消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> During the surplus-labor phase, the modern sector can keep hiring at an approximately constant real wage. That condition ends at the turning point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么农业生产率可能是工业扩张的条件？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can agricultural productivity be a condition for industrial expansion?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 城市劳动者需要食品；农业剩余不足会推高食品价格和工业名义工资，挤压利润与投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Urban workers require food. Insufficient agricultural surplus raises food prices and nominal industrial wages, squeezing profit and investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
