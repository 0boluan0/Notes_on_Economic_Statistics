# Linear least squares
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*线性最小二乘*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Linear least squares** chooses adjustable coefficients so that the outputs of a fixed matrix come as close as possible to a target vector. It measures the mismatch by squaring each coordinate error and adding the squares. An exact match may be impossible: one number cannot simultaneously equal both $1$ and $3$, but we can still choose the best compromise under this rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**线性最小二乘**选择一组可调系数，让固定矩阵产生的输出尽可能接近目标向量。它把每个坐标的误差平方后相加，用这个总和衡量不匹配程度。精确匹配可能做不到：一个数不能同时等于 $1$ 和 $3$，但仍可以按这条规则找到最好的折中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One coefficient, two target values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the adjustable coefficient be $x$. Suppose the two outputs are both $x$, while their targets are $1$ and $3$. In [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] notation,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设可调系数为 $x$，两个输出都等于 $x$，但目标分别是 $1$ 和 $3$。用[[矩阵乘法]]写成

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
b=\begin{bmatrix}1\\3\end{bmatrix},\qquad
Ax=\begin{bmatrix}x\\x\end{bmatrix}.
$$

> [!example] Completing the square finds the compromise · 配方找到折中
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sum of squared coordinate errors is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个坐标误差的平方和为
>
> $$
> (x-1)^2+(x-3)^2=2(x-2)^2+2.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first term is nonnegative and becomes zero exactly at $x=2$. Thus the minimizing coefficient is $\hat x=2$, the fitted vector is $p=A\hat x=(2,2)^T$, and the minimum squared error is $2$. The hat marks a minimizing choice. The residual $r=b-p=(-1,1)^T$ records target minus fitted output, coordinate by coordinate; it has length $\sqrt2$, not $2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一项非负，恰好在 $x=2$ 时为零。因此最优系数为 $\hat x=2$，拟合向量为 $p=A\hat x=(2,2)^T$，最小误差平方和为 $2$。帽子表示选出的最优系数。残差 $r=b-p=(-1,1)^T$ 逐坐标记录“目标减拟合输出”；它的长度为 $\sqrt2$，不是 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From the example to the general problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从例子到一般问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ be fixed, where the positive integers $m,n$ count output coordinates and adjustable coefficients. Let $b\in\mathbb R^m$ be the target and $x\in\mathbb R^n$ the coefficient vector. If $a_j$ is column $j$ of $A$, then $Ax=x_1a_1+\cdots+x_na_n$: each coefficient $x_j$ scales one column, and the scaled columns are added. These possible outputs form the [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设固定矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，正整数 $m,n$ 分别表示输出坐标数和可调系数数。目标为 $b\in\mathbb R^m$，系数向量为 $x\in\mathbb R^n$。若 $a_j$ 是 $A$ 的第 $j$ 列，则 $Ax=x_1a_1+\cdots+x_na_n$：每个系数 $x_j$ 缩放一列，再把缩放后的列相加。这些可能输出构成[[列空间]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] $\|v\|_2$ is the square root of the sum of the squared coordinates of $v$. Linear least squares asks for
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[欧氏范数]] $\|v\|_2$ 是向量各坐标平方和的平方根。线性最小二乘要求解

$$
\min_{x\in\mathbb R^n}\|Ax-b\|_2^2
=\min_{x\in\mathbb R^n}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\bigl((Ax)_i-b_i\bigr)^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $i$ indexes output coordinates. Any $\hat x$ attaining the smallest value is a **least-squares solution**. Minimizing length or its square gives the same solutions because squaring preserves the order of nonnegative lengths. Choosing the residual as $b-Ax$ instead of $Ax-b$ also leaves the squared norm unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $i$ 遍历输出坐标。任何达到最小值的 $\hat x$ 都叫**最小二乘解**。长度非负，平方不会改变它们的大小顺序，所以最小化长度或长度平方得到相同的解。把残差写成 $b-Ax$ 或 $Ax-b$，也不改变范数平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the problem is a projection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么它就是投影问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing $x$ moves the output only within $C(A)$. The [[最佳逼近与正交残差|nearest-point theorem for a finite-dimensional subspace]] guarantees a unique closest output $p$ for every target $b$. This output is the [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] of $b$ onto $C(A)$, written $p=P_{C(A)}b$. Its residual $r=b-p$ is [[正交|orthogonal]] to every column: its dot product with each column is zero. In the example, $(1,1)\cdot(-1,1)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改变 $x$ 只能让输出在 $C(A)$ 内移动。[[最佳逼近与正交残差|有限维子空间的最近点定理]]保证：对每个目标 $b$，都有唯一的最近输出 $p$。这个输出就是 $b$ 在 $C(A)$ 上的[[正交投影]]，记为 $p=P_{C(A)}b$。残差 $r=b-p$ 与每一列[[正交]]，即与每一列的点积为零。上例中 $(1,1)\cdot(-1,1)=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $p$ belongs to the column space, at least one coefficient vector produces it. Thus this finite-dimensional, unconstrained problem always has a solution, even when $Ax=b$ has none. If $b\in C(A)$, the minimum is zero and the minimizers are precisely the exact solutions. Otherwise the minimum is positive. The [[最小二乘正规方程|normal equations]] calculate the minimizing coefficients; [[最小二乘解唯一性|coefficient uniqueness]] is a separate question, since several coefficient vectors can produce the same $p$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $p$ 属于列空间，至少有一组系数能产生它。因此，这个有限维、无约束的问题总有解，即使 $Ax=b$ 无解。若 $b\in C(A)$，最小值为零，最小二乘解恰好就是精确解；否则最小值为正。[[最小二乘正规方程]]用于求最优系数；[[最小二乘解唯一性|系数是否唯一]]则是另一个问题，因为几组系数可能产生同一个 $p$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the definition fixes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficients here are unrestricted and the distance is Euclidean. [[加权最小二乘|Weighted least squares]] changes the mismatch weights; nonlinear least squares replaces $Ax$ by a possibly nonlinear output rule. These changes require their own conditions. For complex matrices and vectors, the same definition uses $\sum_i|(Ax)_i-b_i|^2$, the sum of squared magnitudes, so the objective remains real and nonnegative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的系数不受额外约束，距离采用欧氏度量。[[加权最小二乘]]会改变误差权重；非线性最小二乘则把 $Ax$ 换成可能非线性的输出规则。这些变化需要各自的条件。对于复矩阵和复向量，同一定义使用模的平方和 $\sum_i|(Ax)_i-b_i|^2$，因此目标仍为非负实数。

> [!note] A fitting rule does not supply a probability model · 拟合规则不自带概率模型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Interpreting $A$ and $b$ as regression data gives [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares]]. The geometric minimization alone assumes no random sampling or Gaussian errors, and it does not establish unbiasedness, consistency or a causal interpretation. Those are additional statistical questions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $A,b$ 解释为回归数据，就得到[[普通最小二乘]]。几何最小化本身不要求随机抽样或 Gaussian 误差，也不证明无偏性、一致性或因果含义；这些是额外的统计问题。
