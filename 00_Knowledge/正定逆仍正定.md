# The inverse of a positive-definite matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正定矩阵的逆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] reverses a matrix's action: applying $A$ and then $A^{-1}$ returns the original vector. If $A$ is [[正定矩阵|positive definite]], this inverse exists and is itself positive definite. Reversing the linear map preserves the positivity of its quadratic form, even though individual entries can change sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[逆矩阵]]撤销原矩阵的作用：先用 $A$，再用 $A^{-1}$，便回到原向量。若 $A$ [[正定矩阵|正定]]，它的逆一定存在，而且逆矩阵仍然正定。撤销线性映射以后，二次型的正性得到保留，尽管个别元素可能改变符号。

> [!example] Positive scale factors become positive reciprocals · 正的缩放因子变成正的倒数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$, the diagonal entries are $2,5$ and all other entries are zero. Its inverse is $\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/5)$. With $T$ denoting [[矩阵转置|transpose]],
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对[[对角矩阵]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$，对角元为 $2,5$，其余为零；其逆为 $\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/5)$。$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，于是
>
> $$x^TA^{-1}x=\frac12x_1^2+\frac15x_2^2>0\qquad(x\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Scope and why the inverse exists
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*适用范围与逆的存在*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric]] or [[Hermitian 矩阵|complex Hermitian]] matrix, with finite integer $n\ge1$. In the real case positive definiteness means $x^TAx>0$ for every nonzero real column. In the complex case it means $x^*Ax>0$ for every nonzero complex column, with $*$ the conjugate transpose. The theorem is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为 $n\times n$ 的[[实对称矩阵]]或复 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，有限整数 $n\ge1$。实数情形的正定要求每个非零实列向量都满足 $x^TAx>0$；复数情形要求每个非零复列向量都满足 $x^*Ax>0$，其中 $*$ 是共轭转置。定理为

$$A\succ0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A^{-1}\text{ exists and }A^{-1}\succ0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $Ax=0$, then $x^*Ax=0$. Positive definiteness forces $x=0$, so $A$ has no nonzero [[零空间|nullspace]] direction. A finite square matrix with this property is invertible, as explained by [[正定与半正定可逆性|the invertibility criterion]]. Taking conjugate transposes in the inverse equations shows $(A^{-1})^*=(A^*)^{-1}=A^{-1}$, so the inverse is Hermitian; the real argument uses $T$ and proves symmetry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Ax=0$，就有 $x^*Ax=0$；正定性迫使 $x=0$，所以 $A$ 没有非零[[零空间]]方向。有限方阵满足此条件便可逆，见[[正定与半正定可逆性|可逆性判据]]。对逆矩阵等式取共轭转置，得到 $(A^{-1})^*=(A^*)^{-1}=A^{-1}$，所以逆仍 Hermitian；实数情形用 $T$，得到逆仍对称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Positivity follows by changing the input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*换一个输入即可证明正性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take any nonzero $x$ and set $y=A^{-1}x$. Then $x=Ay$, and $y\ne0$ because an invertible map cannot send a nonzero vector to zero. Using $A^*=A$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取任意非零 $x$，令 $y=A^{-1}x$，于是 $x=Ay$；可逆映射不能把非零向量变成零，因此 $y\ne0$。利用 $A^*=A$，

$$x^*A^{-1}x=(Ay)^*y=y^*Ay>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The final inequality is the positivity of the original matrix, now applied to $y$. It proves the result without computing any eigenvalue. For a real symmetric matrix replace every star by a transpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后的不等式，就是把原矩阵的正定性用于 $y$。因此不必求出特征值，就能证明逆的正性；实对称情形把星号全部换成转置即可。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Eigenvalues become reciprocals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*特征值取倒数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The spectral explanation gives more quantitative information. Write $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$ using the [[实对称矩阵谱定理|real spectral theorem]], where $Q$ is [[正交矩阵|orthogonal]] and $\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ lists the [[特征对|eigenvalues]] on its diagonal. The [[正定与半正定的谱判据|positive-definite sign test]] gives every $\lambda_i>0$. Multiplying the proposed inverse by $A$ yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

谱解释还能给出定量信息。利用[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]写成 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$，其中 $Q$ [[正交矩阵|正交]]，$\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ 在对角线上列出各个[[特征对|特征值]]。[[正定与半正定的谱判据]]保证每个 $\lambda_i>0$。把所写的逆与 $A$ 相乘即可验证

$$A^{-1}=Q\Lambda^{-1}Q^T,\qquad
\Lambda^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1/\lambda_1,\ldots,1/\lambda_n).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the same eigenvectors have positive reciprocal eigenvalues. For complex Hermitian $A=U\Lambda U^*$, the [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary]] matrix $U$ gives $A^{-1}=U\Lambda^{-1}U^*$. Reciprocation reverses the order of positive numbers, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此特征向量保持不变，对应特征值变成正的倒数。复 Hermitian 情形 $A=U\Lambda U^*$，由 [[Unitary 矩阵]] $U$ 得到 $A^{-1}=U\Lambda^{-1}U^*$。正数取倒数会反转大小顺序，所以

$$\lambda_{\min}(A^{-1})=\frac1{\lambda_{\max}(A)},
\qquad
\lambda_{\max}(A^{-1})=\frac1{\lambda_{\min}(A)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\min$ and $\max$ denote the smallest and largest eigenvalues. For the opening example the inverse's smallest eigenvalue is $1/5$, not $1/2$. By the [[正定谱下界|uniform lower-bound theorem]], $x^*A^{-1}x\ge\|x\|_2^2/\lambda_{\max}(A)$, where $\|x\|_2$ is [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] (using squared magnitudes for complex coordinates).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\min$、$\max$ 分别表示最小和最大特征值。开头例子中，逆的最小特征值为 $1/5$，不是 $1/2$。由[[正定谱下界|统一下界定理]]，有 $x^*A^{-1}x\ge\|x\|_2^2/\lambda_{\max}(A)$，其中 $\|x\|_2$ 为[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]，复坐标使用模平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep positivity and invertibility separate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分清正性与可逆性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$, one checks $B^{-1}=\tfrac13\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$. The negative off-diagonal entries do not contradict positive definiteness: the eigenvalues of $B$ are $3,1$, and those of its inverse are $1/3,1$. Positive definite never meant that every entry is positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$，可验得 $B^{-1}=\tfrac13\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$。负的非对角元不违背正定性：$B$ 的特征值为 $3,1$，逆的特征值为 $1/3,1$。正定从来不是说每个元素都正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A singular semidefinite matrix such as $\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$ has no ordinary inverse, so the theorem cannot be applied to it. Conversely, invertibility alone is not enough: $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ equals its own inverse and has both positive and negative quadratic directions. In dimension one the theorem is just $a>0\Rightarrow1/a>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$ 这样的奇异半正定矩阵没有普通逆，不能套用本定理。反过来，仅仅可逆也不够：$\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 等于自身的逆，却同时有正、负二次型方向。一维时，定理就是 $a>0\Rightarrow1/a>0$。

## Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Axler, Linear Algebra Done Right, 4th ed., 3.65, PDF p.98](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=98): zero kernel implies invertibility for equal finite dimensions. The inverse-adjoint and quadratic-input identities are verified directly above.
- [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Appendix A.5.2, PDF pp.660–661](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=660) and [Axler 7.34–7.38, PDF pp.265–266](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=265): real symmetric and complex self-adjoint spectral sign conditions. The reciprocal spectrum and its ordering follow from explicit multiplication, not an extra assumption about entries.
- [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/05_Session_Summaries/MIT18_06SCF11_Ses3.1sum.pdf#page=2|MIT Session3.1, pp.2–3]]: the course positive-eigenvalue convention. The diagonal and two-dimensional inverse examples are recomputed exactly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 3.65（PDF98页）：相同有限维数下，零核保证可逆；逆与共轭转置及二次型输入恒等式在正文直接验证。
- Boyd附录A.5.2（PDF660–661页）和Axler 7.34–7.38（PDF265–266页）：实对称与复自伴的谱符号条件；倒数谱及顺序由直接相乘得到，不依赖元素逐项为正。
- MIT Session3.1第2–3页：课程使用的正谱约定；对角与二维逆例子精确重算。
