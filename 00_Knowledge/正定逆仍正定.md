# 实对称或复 Hermitian 正定矩阵的逆矩阵仍然正定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The inverse of a real symmetric or complex Hermitian positive-definite matrix is also positive definite*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心结论
> 若 $A\succ0$，则 $A$ 可逆，并且
> $$
> A^{-1}\succ0.
> $$
> 这里实数情形要求 $A=A^T$；复数情形要求 $A=A^*$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real symmetric or complex Hermitian positive-definite matrix is invertible, and its inverse is again positive definite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若实对称 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$ 且每个 $\lambda_i>0$，则
$$
A^{-1}=Q\Lambda^{-1}Q^T,
\qquad
\Lambda^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1^{-1},\ldots,\lambda_n^{-1}).
$$
所有倒数仍严格为正，所以谱判据立即给出 $A^{-1}\succ0$。复 Hermitian 情形用 $A=U\Lambda U^*$，同样得到 $A^{-1}=U\Lambda^{-1}U^*$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Spectral decomposition sends each positive eigenvalue $\lambda_i$ to the positive reciprocal $1/\lambda_i$, while preserving the orthonormal eigenvectors. The same calculation uses a unitary decomposition in the Hermitian case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$ 时，
$$
A^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac12,\frac15\right),
$$
且 $x^TA^{-1}x=\frac12x_1^2+\frac15x_2^2>0$ 对每个 $x\ne0$ 成立。注意：结论依赖正定性；奇异的半正定矩阵没有普通逆矩阵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$, its inverse is $\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/5)$ and satisfies $x^TA^{-1}x=\tfrac12x_1^2+\tfrac15x_2^2>0$ for every nonzero $x$. The conclusion relies on positive definiteness: a singular positive-semidefinite matrix has no ordinary inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $A\succ0$ 的最大、最小特征值分别为 $\lambda_{\max}$、$\lambda_{\min}$，$A^{-1}$ 的最小特征值是什么？
>
> **答案：** $1/\lambda_{\max}>0$。取倒数会反转特征值的大小顺序。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the largest and smallest eigenvalues of $A\succ0$ are $\lambda_{\max}$ and $\lambda_{\min}$, what is the smallest eigenvalue of $A^{-1}$?
>
> **Answer:** $1/\lambda_{\max}>0$. Taking reciprocals reverses the ordering of the eigenvalues.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
