# 风险因子映射是把持仓对应到估值驱动变量及其作用关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk-factor mapping identifies the variables driving position values and how their changes affect valuation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险因子映射（risk-factor mapping）是为一笔持仓确定：**哪些变量改变其价值，以及如何把这些变化送入估值**。它不只是给头寸贴“股票”或“外汇”的标签；同一持仓往往同时依赖几类变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk-factor mapping identifies the variables driving a position's value and how their changes enter valuation. It is more than assigning an asset-class label: one position can depend on several factor categories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若持有 $n$ 股欧元计价股票，以美元记账，设 $S$ 为欧元股价、$X$ 为每欧元的美元数，则 $V=nSX$。两个输入分别是股价与汇率；只记录 $S$ 就漏掉换汇影响。进入 [[多因子二阶损益近似]] 后，还会看到两者共同变化的交叉项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n$ euro-denominated shares valued in dollars, let $S$ be the euro share price and $X$ dollars per euro. Then $V=nSX$: omitting $X$ omits FX exposure. The [[多因子二阶损益近似|multifactor expansion]] also captures their interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际建映射时，先定持仓方向、合约数量、支付和记账币种；再写出 $V(x,t)$ 的输入及单位。债券可依赖多个曲线节点，期权可依赖现货、波动率曲面和利率曲线。映射的输出交给 [[风险模拟估值层]]，不是把所有非线性价值都强行写成固定因子载荷。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify signed holdings, contract size, payments and reporting currency before recording the inputs and units of $V(x,t)$. Bonds may depend on several curve nodes and options on spot, volatility surfaces and rates. The mapping feeds the [[风险模拟估值层|valuation layer]]; it need not reduce nonlinear valuation to fixed loadings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
