# 合并内部化通过统一决策把原先分属不同主体的相关收益和损害纳入同一目标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Integration internalises an effect by placing previously separate benefits and harms within one decision objective*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合并内部化是让原先分别决策的相关业务或权利进入一个共同决策组织，使它选择活动时考虑合并后的净收益。例如同一所有者同时经营上游工厂与下游渔场，工厂多生产所损失的鱼就会减少自己的另一项收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integration puts related activities or rights under common decision-making so that choices reflect combined returns. An owner operating both an upstream factory and a downstream fishery loses fishing income when the factory's emissions kill fish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若合并前工厂只最大化 $B(q)$，而渔场承担 $D(q)$；合并后所有者确实最大化 $B(q)-D(q)$，则内点条件成为 $B'(q)=D'(q)$。这里起作用的是决策目标改变，不是把两家公司名称写在一起，也不是损害在物理上消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the factory previously maximised $B(q)$ and the fishery bore $D(q)$, maximising $B(q)-D(q)$ after integration yields $B'(q)=D'(q)$ at an interior solution. The mechanism is a changed objective, not a shared name or physical elimination of damage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还必须界定哪些影响真正被合并。对其他渔民或居民的损害仍可能在组织之外；内部管理可能有信息和激励问题；管理成本及产品市场竞争也可能变化。因此比较的是“纳入更多相关得失后带来的收益”与这些新增成本，而不是假定合并天然最好。更广的制度比较复用[[政府失灵]]和[[福利定理边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify which effects are actually brought inside: harms to other residents can remain external, management can face information or incentive problems, and administrative costs or product-market competition may change. Compare improved accounting of effects with these consequences, using [[政府失灵|government failure]] and [[福利定理边界|institutional boundaries]] where relevant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
