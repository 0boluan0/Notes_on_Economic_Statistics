# Total variation of a function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*函数总变差与有界变差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Total variation** measures how much a real-valued function moves up and down over an interval, counting both directions positively. A value that rises from $0$ to $1$ and then falls back to $0$ has net change zero, but it has accumulated two units of change. Total variation records this accumulated movement. It is useful for describing an ordinary function or one fixed [[样本路径|sample path]] of a random process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**总变差**衡量一个实值函数在一段区间里累计上下变化了多少，上升和下降都按正的数量计算。一个值从 $0$ 上升到 $1$，再下降到 $0$，净变化为零，却累计变化了两个单位。总变差记录的就是这种累计变化。它既可以描述普通函数，也可以描述随机过程的一条固定[[样本路径]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Observe changes along a partition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*沿一个分割观察变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be a real-valued function, with finite endpoints $a<b$. A **partition** is a finite ordered selection of input points, including both endpoints:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ 是实值函数，两个有限端点满足 $a<b$。**分割**是在区间内选出有限个从小到大排列的输入点，并包括两个端点：

$$\Pi:\quad a=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_m=b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are $m$ subintervals. On the $j$th one, $f(t_j)-f(t_{j-1})$ is the signed change; its absolute value counts the size of that change without cancellation. The amount visible at this resolution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个分割产生 $m$ 个小区间。在第 $j$ 个小区间上，$f(t_j)-f(t_{j-1})$ 是带正负号的变化；取绝对值后，上下变化就不会互相抵消。当前观察精度下看到的累计变化为

$$V_\Pi(f)=\sum_{j=1}^{m}|f(t_j)-f(t_{j-1})|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding a point to a partition is called **refinement**. It cannot decrease this sum: inserting $u$ between two neighbouring points $s<t$ replaces one term by two, and the triangle inequality gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在分割里加入一个点叫作**细化**。它不会使这个和减小：在相邻点 $s<t$ 之间插入 $u$，就是把一个项换成两个项，而三角不等式给出

$$|f(t)-f(s)|\le |f(u)-f(s)|+|f(t)-f(u)|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coarse observation can miss a reversal. Refinement may reveal that reversal, but the definition must allow every finite partition, not just one chosen observation grid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

粗略观测可能漏掉一次方向反转。细化可能把它显示出来，所以定义要允许所有有限分割，而不只考察某一套选定的观测网格。

> [!abstract] Total variation and BV · 总变差与有界变差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The **total variation of $f$ on $[a,b]$** is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **$f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上的总变差**定义为
>
> $$\operatorname{TV}_{[a,b]}(f)=\sup_\Pi V_\Pi(f)\in[0,\infty].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The supremum is the least upper bound of the sums over all finite partitions. It need not be attained by one partition. If no finite number bounds every such sum, the total variation is $\infty$. The function has **bounded variation**, written $f\in BV([a,b])$, precisely when this supremum is finite. “Bounded” here means one finite bound that works for every partition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上确界是所有有限分割所得的和的最小上界，不一定由某一个分割取到。如果不存在能约束全部这些和的有限数，总变差就是 $\infty$。当且仅当这个上确界有限时，称函数**有界变差**，记为 $f\in BV([a,b])$。这里的“有界”指存在一个对所有分割都有效的有限上界。

^core

> [!example] One complete rise and fall · 一次完整的上升与下降
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,2]$, let $f(t)=t$ for $0\le t\le1$, and $f(t)=2-t$ for $1\le t\le2$. Looking only at $0$ and $2$ gives a sum of $0$. Including $1$ gives $|1-0|+|0-1|=2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,2]$ 上，令 $0\le t\le1$ 时 $f(t)=t$，$1\le t\le2$ 时 $f(t)=2-t$。只看端点 $0$ 和 $2$，得到的和为 $0$；加上时点 $1$，就得到 $|1-0|+|0-1|=2$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No partition can give more than $2$. Insert $1$ if it is missing: the sum cannot decrease, and on each side the function moves in only one direction, so the changes telescope to $1$ and $1$. Thus $\operatorname{TV}_{[0,2]}(f)=2$, even though $f(2)-f(0)=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 任何分割都不可能给出超过 $2$ 的和。如果没有包含 $1$，先把它插进去：和不会减小，而两侧函数分别只朝一个方向变化，各段变化相加后分别为 $1$ 和 $1$。所以 $\operatorname{TV}_{[0,2]}(f)=2$，尽管 $f(2)-f(0)=0$。

## Bounded movement and bounded height are different
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*变化总量有界与取值高度有界不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A monotone function, meaning one that always moves in the same weak direction, has total variation $|f(b)-f(a)|$: the absolute increments simply telescope. Constant functions have variation zero. A single jump from $0$ to $1$ has variation one, so bounded variation does not require continuity. Total variation measures vertical changes of values, not the geometric arc length of the graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单调函数始终朝同一个方向变化（允许保持不变），它的总变差就是 $|f(b)-f(a)|$：绝对增量相加后中间项依次抵消。常数函数的变差为零。只从 $0$ 跳到 $1$ 一次的函数，变差为一，所以有界变差并不要求连续。总变差衡量的是函数值的上下变化，不是图像的几何弧长。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite total variation does bound the values. A partition containing any selected $t$ shows $|f(t)-f(a)|\le\operatorname{TV}_{[a,b]}(f)$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总变差有限确实能约束函数值。对任意选定的 $t$，包含它的分割给出 $|f(t)-f(a)|\le\operatorname{TV}_{[a,b]}(f)$，因此

$$|f(t)|\le |f(a)|+\operatorname{TV}_{[a,b]}(f).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse fails: staying within a fixed height range does not limit how often the function can change direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来不成立：取值始终落在固定高度范围内，并不限制函数能反转多少次。

> [!warning] Bounded values can accumulate infinite variation · 取值有界也能累计无限变差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Set $f(0)=0$ and $f(t)=\sin(1/t)$ for $0<t\le1$, using radians. Its values stay in $[-1,1]$. At the points $t_k=(\pi/2+k\pi)^{-1}$, for integers $k\ge0$, the values alternate between $1$ and $-1$ as the points approach zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $f(0)=0$，并在 $0<t\le1$ 上令 $f(t)=\sin(1/t)$，角度采用弧度。函数值始终位于 $[-1,1]$。在 $t_k=(\pi/2+k\pi)^{-1}$ 这些点上（整数 $k\ge0$），随着这些点趋近零，函数值在 $1$ 和 $-1$ 之间交替。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Include $t_N<\cdots<t_0$ in a finite partition. The $N$ changes between them each have size $2$, giving a sum at least $2N$. Since $N$ can be arbitrarily large, the total variation is infinite. The definition uses a different finite partition for each $N$; it never requires an “infinite partition”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $t_N<\cdots<t_0$ 包含进一个有限分割。这些点之间的 $N$ 次变化每次大小都是 $2$，所以和至少为 $2N$。由于 $N$ 可以任意大，总变差就是无限。这里对每个 $N$ 使用一个不同的有限分割，并不需要所谓“无限分割”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bounded variation also has a local consequence: [[有界变差函数几乎处处可导|a real BV function has a finite derivative at almost every time]]. That result permits exceptional times, including corners or jumps. Conversely, even existence of a derivative everywhere does not guarantee finite total variation; the linked theorem explains the distinction with an oscillating example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有界变差还有一个局部后果：[[有界变差函数几乎处处可导|实值有界变差函数在几乎每个时点都存在有限导数]]。这个结论允许尖角、跳点等例外时点。反过来，即使处处存在导数，也未必有有限总变差；链接中的定理用一个振荡例子解释了这个区别。
