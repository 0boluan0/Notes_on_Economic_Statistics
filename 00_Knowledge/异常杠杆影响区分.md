# Outliers, leverage, and influence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*异常值、杠杆与影响*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A point can look unusual for three different reasons. Its **outcome** may depart from the fitted relationship; its **predictor values** may occupy an unusual position; or including it may materially **change a result you care about**. In regression these are called an outlier, high leverage, and influence. They ask different questions, so one label does not establish the other two.

For example, a household with exceptionally high income might have exactly the consumption that the existing trend predicts. Its income gives it an unusual predictor position, but that alone does not make its consumption an outcome outlier or show that it changes the fitted slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个观测可能从三个不同方面显得特殊：它的**结果值**偏离拟合关系；它的**解释变量组合**处在特殊位置；或者把它纳入分析会明显**改变你关心的结果**。回归中分别称为异常值、高杠杆和影响。三者问的是不同问题，不能由一个标签推定另外两个。

例如，一个家庭的收入特别高，消费却恰好延续原有趋势。收入使它处在特殊的解释变量位置，但这本身既不说明消费是结果方向的异常值，也不说明它改变了拟合斜率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three questions about one observation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对同一个观测提出三个问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **outlier in the outcome direction** is an observation whose response is unusual relative to the relationship and scale being assessed. A [[回归残差|residual]] is observed outcome minus fitted outcome; its size is one clue. A large raw outcome is not automatically an outlier if the predictors explain it. Conversely, an unusual outcome can pull the fitted relationship toward itself and leave a deceptively small residual. [[学生化残差|Studentized residuals]] compare discrepancies with an estimated residual scale, but also need the relevant model conditions.

**Leverage** concerns the predictor design. It asks how much capacity an observation's position gives it to move the fit. In unweighted linear [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares (OLS)]], which chooses coefficients to minimise squared residuals, [[Hat杠杆值|the hat value]] measures this property using all predictor rows. It does not use the observed outcomes. With several predictors, an unusual *combination* can matter even when no single coordinate is extreme.

**Influence** concerns a chosen result after fitting. [[删点影响定义|Delete-one influence]] compares that result with and without the observation, keeping the intended model and comparison clear. The target may be a slope, a particular prediction, or an uncertainty estimate. A point can change one target substantially and another hardly at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**结果方向异常值**是相对于所考察的关系和尺度而言，响应不寻常的观测。[[回归残差|残差]]是观测结果减拟合结果，大小是线索之一。原始结果很大，若解释变量能说明它，就不自动是异常值；反过来，特殊结果可能把拟合关系拉向自身，留下看起来很小的残差。[[学生化残差]]把偏离与估计的残差尺度比较，但也需要相应的模型条件。

**杠杆**关心解释变量设计：该观测的位置使它有多大能力推动拟合？不加权线性[[普通最小二乘|普通最小二乘法（OLS）]]通过最小化残差平方和选择系数，[[Hat杠杆值|Hat 杠杆值]]则利用全部解释变量行衡量这种位置特征，不使用结果值。多个解释变量同时存在时，即使各个坐标都不极端，特殊的*组合*也可能重要。

**影响**关心拟合后的某个指定结果。[[删点影响定义|逐一删点影响]]比较保留和删去观测后的结果，同时保持模型与比较目的清楚。目标可以是一个斜率、一个位置的预测，或不确定性估计。同一个观测可能明显改变其中一项，却几乎不改变另一项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same position can have different effects
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*位置相同，作用却可以不同*

> [!example] Four predictor values / 四个解释变量值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit an intercept and slope to predictor values $x=(-1,0,1,4)$ and outcomes $y=(-1,2,5,14)$. Every point lies on $y=2+3x$. The point at $x=4$ has the largest hat value, $25/28$, compared with an average of $1/2$ for this design. Yet removing it leaves exactly the same fitted line. It has high leverage relative to the other rows, but no delete-one change in the fitted coefficients.
>
> Now keep all predictor values fixed and change only the last outcome from $14$ to $28$. The four-point OLS line becomes $\hat y=2.5+6x$. Removing that last point restores $\hat y=2+3x$: the fitted slope changes from $6$ to $3$. Its leverage remains $25/28$, because the design did not change. Its full-fit residual is only $28-(2.5+6\cdot4)=1.5$; the point at $x=1$ has a larger absolute residual, $|5-8.5|=3.5$. A point that moves the line can partly conceal its own departure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $x=(-1,0,1,4)$、$y=(-1,2,5,14)$ 拟合截距和斜率。所有点都在 $y=2+3x$ 上。$x=4$ 这一点的 Hat 值最大，为 $25/28$，而本设计的平均值是 $1/2$；但删去它后，拟合直线完全不变。它相对其他行有较高杠杆，却没有引起拟合系数的删点变化。
>
> 现在保持所有解释变量不变，只把最后一个结果从 $14$ 改为 $28$。四点的 OLS 直线变成 $\hat y=2.5+6x$；删去最后一点后恢复 $\hat y=2+3x$，斜率从 $6$ 变为 $3$。由于设计不变，杠杆仍是 $25/28$。该点在完整拟合中的残差却只有 $28-(2.5+6\cdot4)=1.5$；$x=1$ 处的绝对残差反而更大，为 $|5-8.5|=3.5$。一个能推动直线的点，可能部分掩盖自身的偏离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These small examples compare fitted values and coefficients; the exact-line example supplies no positive residual variance for a conventional t-test. They do not assign a universal numerical threshold for “unusual” or “large influence.”

A central point illustrates why the target matters. Return to the original four-point data, but change the outcome at $x=1$ from $5$ to $13$. Here $x=1$ is the sample mean of the predictors. The full line becomes $\hat y=4+3x$: the slope remains $3$, while the intercept increases by $2$. That point's residual is $6$. Deleting it restores $2+3x$. Calling it “uninfluential” without naming the target would miss its effect on the intercept and predictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些小例子比较的是拟合值和系数；完全落在直线上的例子没有可用于常规 t 检验的正残差方差。它们也没有给“不寻常”或“影响大”设定通用数值阈值。

中心位置的点进一步说明为什么必须说清目标。回到原始四点，只把 $x=1$ 处的结果从 $5$ 改为 $13$。这里 $x=1$ 正是解释变量样本均值。完整直线变成 $\hat y=4+3x$：斜率仍为 $3$，截距却增加 $2$，该点残差为 $6$。删去它后恢复 $2+3x$。若不说明目标就称它“没有影响”，便漏掉了截距和预测的变化。

## Interpreting a flag
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Outlyingness depends on the fitted relationship and outcome scale. A curved mean forced into a straight line can create apparent outliers; an outcome transformation changes the scale on which departures are judged. [[残差图边界|Residual-plot patterns]] should therefore lead to a model and data check, not an immediate declaration that an observation is wrong.

High leverage describes capacity, while influence measures a realised change for a specified target. Small raw residuals do not settle either question, and deletion can itself remove the variation needed to estimate a coefficient. If the reduced design loses rank, report that loss rather than pretending the same coefficient remains uniquely estimated.

All three labels are prompts for investigation. Check recording errors, the observation's relevance to the target population, model form, and sensitivity of the substantive conclusion. [[异常观测处理原则|Handling unusual observations]] requires a reason for any exclusion and a record of its consequences; a diagnostic flag alone is not that reason.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异常程度依赖拟合关系和结果尺度。把弯曲均值强行拟合成直线，可能制造表面异常值；变换结果变量，也会改变判断偏离的尺度。因此，[[残差图边界|残差图案]]应引导模型和数据核查，而不是立即断言某条观测错误。

高杠杆描述推动拟合的能力，影响衡量指定目标实际发生的变化。小原始残差不能解决这两个问题；删点本身还可能移除识别某个系数所需的变异。如果删点设计失去秩，应报告这一损失，不能假装相同系数仍可唯一估计。

三种标签都只是调查起点。检查记录错误、观测是否属于目标总体、模型形式和实质结论的敏感性。[[异常观测处理原则|处理特殊观测]]时，任何剔除都需要理由并记录后果；诊断标记本身不是这个理由。

Its diagnostic formula list is not used to define numerical cutoffs here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Penn State 上述 §§11.1–11.2、§11.7：支持结果异常、特殊解释变量位置与目标相关影响的区分，以及先调查而非自动删点；四点数据及拟合数字为构造后直接核算。
- NIST 上述开头定义及高杠杆小残差讨论：支持杠杆潜力与删点影响的区分、残差可能被掩蔽；本卡不以该页诊断公式列表定义数值阈值。
