# 底数 $a>0$ 的指数函数是保持指数加法律的连续实数幂映射 $x\mapsto a^x$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For $a>0$, the exponential function is the continuous real-power map $x\mapsto a^x$ that preserves the exponent addition law*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 固定底数 $a>0$，把整数和有理指数的幂运算连续地扩展到所有实数 $x$，并保持
> $$
> a^{x+y}=a^xa^y,\qquad a^0=1,\qquad a^x>0,
> $$
> 得到指数函数 $x\mapsto a^x$。当 $a=1$ 时它是常数函数；当 $a\ne1$ 时它严格单调并有对数反函数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix $a>0$. Extending integer and rational powers continuously to every real $x$ while preserving $a^{x+y}=a^xa^y$, $a^0=1$, and positivity gives the exponential function $x\mapsto a^x$. For $a=1$ it is constant; for $a\ne1$ it is strictly monotone and has a logarithmic inverse.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“指数函数”描述的是变量在指数位置，而不是任何含指数符号的表达式。$x^3$ 是幂函数，$3^x$ 是指数函数，$x^x$ 则同时移动底数与指数，需要额外定义域和 [[对数求导|求导方法]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exponential function places the variable in the exponent. Thus $x^3$ is a power function, $3^x$ an exponential function, and $x^x$ has both a moving base and exponent and requires separate domain and differentiation work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由指数加法律，差商可以分离成
$$
\frac{a^{x+h}-a^x}{h}
=a^x\frac{a^h-1}{h}.
$$
因此每个指数函数的导数都等于自身乘以一个只依赖底数的常数；哪个底数使该常数等于 $1$，引出 [[自然指数函数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponent law factors the difference quotient as $a^x(a^h-1)/h$. Hence the derivative of any exponential is the function itself times a constant depending only on its base. The base that makes this constant equal to one defines the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
