# Exponential functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指数函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **exponential function** describes repeated multiplication by a fixed positive factor. If a quantity doubles whenever time increases by one unit, its multiplying factor after $x$ units is $2^x$. The input $x$ tells us how far to move along the process; the fixed number $2$ is the **base**. Equal additions to the input produce equal multipliers, rather than equal amounts added to the output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**指数函数**描述按一个固定的正倍数反复相乘。某个量每过一单位时间翻倍，经过 $x$ 单位后的倍数就是 $2^x$。输入 $x$ 表示过程向前走了多少，固定的数 $2$ 称为**底数**。输入增加相同的量，输出就乘以相同的倍数，而不是增加相同的数量。

> [!example] Multipliers at different times · 不同时刻的倍数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a model $Q(t)=100\cdot2^t$, the values at $t=0,1,2$ are $100,200,400$. At $t=1/2$, the multiplier is $\sqrt2$: applying it twice must produce a factor of two. At $t=-1$, the multiplier is $1/2$, undoing one doubling. Extending the model between integer times assumes the same exponential rule there; observations of daily doubling alone do not force that interpolation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在模型 $Q(t)=100\cdot2^t$ 中，$t=0,1,2$ 时分别为 $100,200,400$。$t=1/2$ 的倍数是 $\sqrt2$，因为连续作用两次必须合成翻倍；$t=-1$ 的倍数是 $1/2$，相当于倒退一次翻倍。把模型延伸到整数时刻之间，是假定那里也遵守同一指数规律；仅观察到每天翻倍，并不能强迫中间过程如此变化。

## From whole steps to real inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从整步扩展到实数输入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $a>0$. Positive integer powers multiply copies of $a$, zero steps give $a^0=1$, and negative powers undo multiplication: $a^{-n}=1/a^n$. For integers $p$ and positive integers $q$, define $a^{p/q}$ using the positive $q$th root. For example, $9^{1/2}=3$ and $9^{3/2}=27$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $a>0$。正整数次幂是把若干个 $a$ 相乘；零步给出 $a^0=1$；负指数则把乘法倒过来：$a^{-n}=1/a^n$。对整数 $p$ 和正整数 $q$，用正的 $q$ 次方根定义 $a^{p/q}$。例如 $9^{1/2}=3$、$9^{3/2}=27$。

$$a^{p/q}=\sqrt[q]{a^p}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an irrational input, such as $\sqrt2$, take rational inputs approaching it. Their powers approach a single value; that [[函数极限|limit]] defines $a^{\sqrt2}$. This gives the unique [[一点连续|continuous extension]] of rational powers: making a sufficiently small change to the input makes the output arbitrarily close. Thus $a^x$ is defined for every real $x$, including values that cannot mean a literal number of repeated multiplications.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\sqrt2$ 这样的无理数输入，取逐渐逼近它的有理数输入。这些数的幂趋向同一个值；这个[[函数极限|极限]]就定义了 $a^{\sqrt2}$。这样得到有理数幂唯一的[[一点连续|连续扩展]]：输入改变得足够小，输出就会任意接近原值。因此 $a^x$ 对所有实数 $x$ 都有定义，包括不能按字面解释为“乘了几次”的输入。

> [!important] The law behind the model · 模型背后的运算法则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fixed $a>0$ and all real $x,y$,
>
> $$a^{x+y}=a^xa^y,\qquad a^0=1,\qquad a^x>0.$$
>
> Consequently $a^{x+h}/a^x=a^h$: the multiplier for an input increment $h$ does not depend on the starting point $x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定 $a>0$ 后，对任意实数 $x,y$ 都有指数加法律。因此 $a^{x+h}/a^x=a^h$：输入增加 $h$ 所对应的倍数，与起点 $x$ 无关。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $a>1$, the function increases and takes every positive value. If $0<a<1$, it decreases and still takes every positive value; for instance $(1/2)^x=2^{-x}$. Both approach zero in one direction but never equal zero at a finite input. The case $a=1$ gives the constant function $1^x=1$, with range $\{1\}$; it has no inverse on all real inputs. Some textbooks reserve the name “exponential function” for $a\ne1$, but the constant case remains valid in the power formulas here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a>1$，函数递增并取遍所有正值；若 $0<a<1$，函数递减，仍取遍所有正值，例如 $(1/2)^x=2^{-x}$。两种情况都会在一个方向上趋近零，却不会在有限输入处等于零。$a=1$ 给出常数函数 $1^x=1$，值域只有 $\{1\}$，因而不能在全部实数输入上求反函数。有些教材把“指数函数”一词限于 $a\ne1$；这里的幂运算公式仍包含这个常数情形。

> [!warning] Which part is allowed to vary? · 变化的是哪一部分？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $3^x$ has a fixed base and is exponential; $x^3$ has a fixed exponent and is a power function. For $x^x$, both vary, so its derivative needs separate work, such as [[对数求导|logarithmic differentiation]] on $x>0$. Positive bases are essential for a real-valued function on all of $\mathbb R$: $(-2)^{1/2}$ is not real, and zero as a base does not allow negative exponents.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $3^x$ 固定底数，是指数函数；$x^3$ 固定指数，是幂函数。$x^x$ 两者都在变化，求导需要单独处理，例如在 $x>0$ 上使用[[对数求导]]。要在全部实数上得到实值函数，正底数条件不可省：$(-2)^{1/2}$ 不是实数，零底数则无法处理负指数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The particular base $e$ gives the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]], whose instantaneous proportional growth rate is one. How other bases change the rate is explained in [[指数函数求导|the derivative of an exponential]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特殊底数 $e$ 给出[[自然指数函数]]，它把瞬时相对增长率标准化为一。其他底数怎样改变这个速率，见[[指数函数求导]]。
