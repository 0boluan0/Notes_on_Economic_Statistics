# Negative-semidefinite matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*半负定矩阵（负半定矩阵）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **negative-semidefinite matrix** never gives a positive quadratic value, whatever input vector is chosen. Zero is allowed, including at a nonzero vector, but such a nonzero zero direction is not required. This is an inclusive definition: every [[负定矩阵|negative-definite matrix]] is also negative semidefinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**半负定矩阵**对任意输入向量都不会给出正的二次型值。允许取零，也允许在非零向量上取零，但不要求一定有这样的非零零值方向。这是一个宽义定义：每个[[负定矩阵]]也都半负定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $D=\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$: its diagonal entries are $-1,0$ and its other entries are zero. For a real column $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$, with $T$ denoting [[矩阵转置|transpose]], its [[二次型|quadratic form]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看[[对角矩阵]] $D=\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$：对角元依次为 $-1,0$，其余元素为零。对实列向量 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，对应[[二次型]]为

$$x^TDx=-x_1^2\le0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second coordinate makes no contribution. Inputs $(1,0)^T$ and $(0,1)^T$ give $-1$ and $0$ respectively. Thus the matrix is negative semidefinite, but it is not negative definite: a nonzero input can give zero. Varying the second coordinate alone stays on a flat direction of this particular quadratic function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二个坐标没有贡献。输入 $(1,0)^T$ 与 $(0,1)^T$ 分别给出 $-1$ 与 $0$。所以矩阵半负定，却不负定，因为非零输入也能给出零值。只改变第二个坐标，就沿着这个二次函数的一条平坦方向移动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Nonpositive in every direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*所有方向都非正*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite integer $n\ge1$, take a [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric matrix]] $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$, meaning $A=A^T$. The notation and definition are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 为[[实对称矩阵]]，即 $A=A^T$。记号与定义是

$$\boxed{A\preceq0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A=A^T\ \text{and}\ x^TAx\le0\ \text{for every }x\in\mathbb R^n.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\preceq$ concerns quadratic values, not entrywise comparison. Including $x=0$ changes nothing because its value is already zero. For a scalar matrix $[a]$, the condition is simply $a\le0$. As with negative definiteness, the real symmetry convention is part of the definition; for nonsymmetric real matrices, the same scalar expression tests their [[二次型对称化|symmetric part]] instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\preceq$ 比较二次型值，不是逐元素比较。把 $x=0$ 包含进来不改变条件，因为它的值本来就是零。对一阶矩阵 $[a]$，条件就是 $a\le0$。与负定一样，实对称性是定义的一部分；对非对称实矩阵，同一个标量表达式检查的是它的[[二次型对称化|对称部分]]。

> [!example] A zero direction need not be a coordinate axis · 零方向不必是坐标轴
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$. Direct expansion gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$，直接展开得到
>
> $$x^TAx=-x_1^2+2x_1x_2-x_2^2=-(x_1-x_2)^2\le0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each coordinate axis gives a strictly negative value away from zero, yet $x=(1,1)^T$ gives zero. Positive off-diagonal entries are allowed, and checking only the diagonal would miss the flat direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两条坐标轴上的非零向量都给出严格负值，但 $x=(1,1)^T$ 给出零值。非对角元可以为正；只看对角元会漏掉这条平坦方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Negation explains inclusion and zero directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*取负号解释包含关系与零方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $x^T(-A)x=-x^TAx$, negative semidefiniteness is equivalent to [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefiniteness]] of the negative matrix:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $x^T(-A)x=-x^TAx$，半负定等价于负矩阵的[[半正定矩阵|半正定性]]：

$$A\preceq0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad -A\succeq0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three examples distinguish the possibilities. With $I_n$ the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], $-I_n$ is both negative definite and negative semidefinite; it has no nonzero zero direction. The opening $D$ is negative semidefinite but not negative definite. The zero matrix gives zero for every input and is both positive and negative semidefinite. In particular, “semidefinite” does not assert either a flat direction or some strictly negative value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个例子可以区分这些情况。记 $I_n$ 为[[单位矩阵]]，则 $-I_n$ 既负定又半负定，并没有非零零值方向。开头的 $D$ 半负定但非负定。零矩阵在每个输入上都给出零，同时半正定和半负定。特别地，“半定”既不断言一定有平坦方向，也不保证一定取得严格负值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $A\preceq0$, applying the [[半正定零方向|positive-semidefinite zero-direction theorem]] to $-A$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $A\preceq0$ 时，把[[半正定零方向|半正定零方向定理]]用于 $-A$，得到

$$x^TAx=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Ax=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a zero quadratic direction lies in the [[零空间|nullspace]], the set of inputs sent to the zero vector. The distinction between a negative-definite matrix and a negative-semidefinite matrix with a nonzero zero direction also becomes an [[正定与半正定可逆性|invertibility distinction]] after negation. This equivalence uses semidefiniteness: $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ has a zero quadratic value at $(1,1)^T$ but does not send that vector to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，零二次型方向属于[[零空间]]，也就是被矩阵送到零向量的输入集合。负定矩阵与“半负定但存在非零零方向”的区别，取负后也对应[[正定与半正定可逆性|可逆性的区别]]。这个等价使用了半定前提：$\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 在 $(1,1)^T$ 上的二次型为零，却没有把这个向量送到零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the pure quadratic function $q(x)=x^TAx$ with $A\preceq0$, the value zero at the origin is a global maximum. It may also occur elsewhere, as in the opening example. This conclusion describes the entire quadratic function. For a general function with continuous second partial derivatives near an interior [[驻点|stationary point]] (zero derivative), a [[半定Hessian无结论|singular semidefinite Hessian]] alone may leave the local-extremum question undecided. Direct comparison of function values, control of the Taylor remainder, or higher-order expansions when available can supply more information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\preceq0$ 的纯二次函数 $q(x)=x^TAx$，原点取得的零值是全局最大值，也可能在其他地方取得，如开头的例子。这一结论描述整个二次函数。对一般函数，若在内点[[驻点]]（导数为零）附近二阶偏导连续，单凭[[半定Hessian无结论|奇异半定 Hessian]]仍可能无法判定局部极值。可直接比较函数值、控制 Taylor 余项，或在条件允许时使用更高阶展开来补充信息。

## Complex inputs use conjugate transpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复数输入使用共轭转置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$, require [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]] $A=A^*$ and $z^*Az\le0$ for every complex vector $z$. The star denotes [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose and replace each $a+bi$ by $a-bi$, with $i^2=-1$. Hermitian symmetry ensures that the scalar is real. For instance,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$，要求 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 对称性]] $A=A^*$，以及每个复向量 $z$ 都满足 $z^*Az\le0$。星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]：转置并把每个 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。Hermitian 对称性保证结果为实数。例如，

$$H=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-i\\i&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
z^*Hz=-|z_1+iz_2|^2\le0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $|a+bi|^2=a^2+b^2$. The nonzero vector $z=(-i,1)^T$ gives zero. Negation again yields a positive-semidefinite matrix, so the inclusion and zero-direction statements above also hold in this complex setting, with $x^T$ replaced by $z^*$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $|a+bi|^2=a^2+b^2$。非零向量 $z=(-i,1)^T$ 给出零值。取负后仍得到半正定矩阵，因此前面的包含关系与零方向结论对复数情形同样成立，只需把 $x^T$ 换成 $z^*$。
