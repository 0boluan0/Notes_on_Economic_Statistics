# 实对称矩阵半负定是指其二次型在每个向量上都非正

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric matrix is negative semidefinite when its quadratic form is nonpositive on every vector*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称矩阵 $A$，半负定记作 $A\preceq0$，定义为 $x^TAx\le0$ 对所有 $x$ 成立，等价于 $-A$ [[半正定矩阵|半正定]]。采用宽义定义，[[负定矩阵|负定]]包含在半负定中。

$\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$ 半负定但非负定：$x^TAx=-x_1^2$，沿 $(0,1)$ 为零。零矩阵同时半正定和半负定。因此“半定”本身不表示一定有正曲率或负曲率，也不排除定号的严格情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative semidefiniteness requires nonpositive quadratic values in all directions, equivalently [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefiniteness]] of the negative matrix. The inclusive definition contains [[负定矩阵|negative definiteness]]. The diagonal example has a nonzero zero direction, and the zero matrix is both positive and negative semidefinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
