# 用同一数据选择模型后直接套用预先指定模型的普通 p 值与置信区间，不再自动保留名义错误率和覆盖保证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ordinary p-values and confidence intervals applied after selecting a model on the same data do not automatically retain their prespecified-model error-rate and coverage guarantees*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 普通回归推断通常把待检验模型或参数看作分析前已经给定。若先用同一数据查看许多变量、模型或交互，再选择最显著或最合意的结果，选择事件本身已经利用了抽样噪声。随后把获胜模型当作预先指定，会忽略 selection uncertainty；名义 p 值和 confidence interval 不再自动具有原来的错误率与覆盖率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ordinary regression inference typically treats the tested model or parameter as fixed before examining the data. If the same data are first used to inspect many variables, models, or interactions and then select the most significant or attractive result, the selection event has already exploited sampling noise. Treating the winning model as prespecified ignores selection uncertainty, so nominal p-values and confidence intervals no longer automatically have their original error-rate and coverage guarantees.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么挑选会改变参考分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why selection changes the reference distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次预先指定检验问的是“这个统计量在 $H_0$ 下有多极端”。选择后报告问的实际是“在搜索过的统计量中，最有利的那个有多极端”。后一个随机变量是极值，其分布不同于任何一个固定统计量的分布。

在一个只用于说明机制的理想化例子中，若 $m$ 个零假设下的 p 值彼此独立且都均匀分布，则至少一个小于 $\alpha$ 的概率为

$$
P\!\left(\min_{1\le j\le m}p_j<\alpha\right)
=
1-(1-\alpha)^m.
$$

当 $m=30$、$\alpha=0.05$ 时，这个概率约为 $0.785$，而不是 0.05。实际回归规格通常相关，所以 0.785 不是通用数值；例子只说明“搜索后挑最小值”的参考分布已经改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A prespecified test asks how extreme one fixed statistic is under $H_0$. A selected report effectively asks how extreme the most favourable statistic among those searched is. The latter is an extreme order statistic with a different distribution. In a stylised independent-null example, the probability that at least one of $m$ p-values falls below $\alpha$ is $1-(1-\alpha)^m$; with $m=30$ and $\alpha=0.05$, it is about $0.785$. Real regression specifications are usually dependent, so this number is illustrative rather than universal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界不是“选择之后一切作废”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The boundary is not that all inference after selection is impossible*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

问题针对的是：选择规则依赖当前数据，却仍使用把模型当作固定的普通推断。以下做法分别改变或明确推断目标，可以处理其中一部分风险：

- 分析前预先指定主要模型与检验；
- 完整报告搜索空间和 specification sensitivity，而不是只展示获胜结果；
- 用独立数据、sample splitting 或 external validation 分离选择与评估；
- 使用同时推断或专门的 selective/post-selection procedure。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The problem is specifically that the selection rule depends on the current data while the reported inference still treats the model as fixed. Prespecification, transparent specification-sensitivity reporting, independent validation or sample splitting, and simultaneous or selective-inference procedures address different parts of that problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择后推断也不替代模型本身的实质判断。先按 [[模型选择目标]] 固定问题，再从 [[回归模型比较与选择.canvas|模型比较总图]] 选择合适证据；本卡只负责说明为什么选择路径必须进入 p 值和区间的校准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Post-selection inference does not replace substantive model assessment. First fix the question through the [[模型选择目标|model-selection target]], then use the [[回归模型比较与选择.canvas|model-comparison map]] to choose suitable evidence. This atom only explains why the selection path must enter the calibration of p-values and intervals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 分析者 A 在看数据前指定一个模型并得到 $p=0.04$；分析者 B 用同一结果变量试了 30 个规格，只报告其中最小的 $p=0.04$，并按一次预先指定检验解释。为什么两个 0.04 不能自动视为同样的证据？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Analyst A prespecifies one model and obtains $p=0.04$. Analyst B tries 30 specifications on the same outcome, reports only the smallest $p=0.04$, and interprets it as one prespecified test. Why are the two values not automatically equivalent evidence?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** A 的参考分布对应一个固定检验；B 报告的是经过选择的极值，选择事件提高了偶然出现小 p 值的机会。除非 B 的校准显式纳入选择规则，普通 0.04 不再自动具有单次检验的名义错误率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** A's reference distribution belongs to one fixed test. B reports a selected extreme, and the selection event increases the chance of obtaining a small p-value by noise. Unless B's calibration explicitly incorporates that rule, the ordinary value 0.04 does not automatically retain the nominal error rate of a single test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
