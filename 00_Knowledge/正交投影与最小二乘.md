# 正交投影与最小二乘
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Orthogonal projection and least squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当方程 $Ax=b$ 无法精确满足时，在列空间中找离 $b$ 最近的可实现向量。
> **具体锚点：** 一条直线通常穿不过所有数据点；最小二乘让残差向量与模型能产生的所有拟合方向正交。
> **核心难点：** 正规方程给出投影条件，但直接计算 $A^TA$ 可能放大数值病态；几何结论和算法选择要分开。
> **为什么重要：** OLS 回归、信号逼近、数据拟合和伪逆都由同一投影结构统一。
> **继续：** 统计解释见 [[OLS 线性回归]]；秩亏或最小范数问题见 [[广义逆与最小范数解#Moore–Penrose 伪逆|伪逆]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** When $Ax=b$ has no exact solution, least squares finds the attainable vector in the column space closest to $b$.
> **Concrete anchor:** A fitted line rarely passes through every data point; the least-squares residual is orthogonal to every direction the model can fit.
> **Central difficulty:** Normal equations express the geometry, but forming $A^TA$ can amplify numerical ill-conditioning. Mathematical characterisation and computational method are distinct choices.
> **Why it matters:** OLS regression, approximation, signal fitting, and pseudoinverses share this projection structure.
> **Continue with:** See [[OLS 线性回归|OLS linear regression]] for statistical assumptions and [[广义逆与最小范数解#Moore–Penrose 伪逆|the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]] for rank-deficient and minimum-norm solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 正交补与最近点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Orthogonal complements and nearest points*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

子空间 $S$ 中离 $b$ 最近的点 $p$ 由 $b-p\perp S$ 唯一刻画。于是 $\mathbb R^m=S\oplus S^\perp$，每个向量都唯一分成投影和正交残差。这个几何事实比任何具体公式更基本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The point $p\in S$ closest to $b$ is characterised uniquely by $b-p\perp S$. Thus $\mathbb R^m=S\oplus S^\perp$, and every vector decomposes uniquely into a projection plus an orthogonal residual. This geometric fact is more fundamental than any coordinate formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 投影矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Projection matrices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 Q 的列为 S 的标准正交基，投影矩阵是 $P=QQ^T$；一般满列秩 A 给出 $P=A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T$。投影矩阵满足 $P^T=P$、$P^2=P$，特征值只能是 0 或 1。若 A 秩亏，逆矩阵公式失效，但投影本身仍存在，可用 QR 或 SVD 求得。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the columns of $Q$ are an orthonormal basis for $S$, then $P=QQ^T$. For a general full-column-rank $A$, $P=A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T$. Projection matrices satisfy $P^T=P$ and $P^2=P$, so their eigenvalues are zero or one. If $A$ is rank deficient, the inverse formula fails, but the projection still exists and can be computed by QR or SVD.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小二乘与正规方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Least squares and the normal equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小化 $\lVert Ax-b\rVert^2$ 的一阶条件是 $A^T(A\hat x-b)=0$，即 $A^TA\hat x=A^Tb$。这不是强迫残差为零，而是让残差与每一列解释方向正交。满列秩时系数唯一；秩亏时拟合值仍可能唯一而系数不唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-order condition for minimising $\lVert Ax-b\rVert^2$ is $A^T(A\hat x-b)=0$, or $A^TA\hat x=A^Tb$. It does not force the residual to zero; it makes the residual orthogonal to every explanatory column. Full column rank gives unique coefficients. With rank deficiency, the fitted value may still be unique even though coefficients are not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Gram–Schmidt 与 QR
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Gram–Schmidt and QR factorisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Gram–Schmidt 把独立列正交化成 $A=QR$。此时最小二乘变成解较小的上三角系统 $R\hat x=Q^Tb$，避免显式形成 $A^TA$。modified Gram–Schmidt、Householder QR 或 SVD 在数值上通常更稳健。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gram–Schmidt orthogonalises independent columns to obtain $A=QR$. Least squares then reduces to the smaller upper-triangular system $R\hat x=Q^Tb$, avoiding explicit formation of $A^TA$. Modified Gram–Schmidt, Householder QR, and SVD are usually more stable computationally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从几何到统计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From geometry to statistics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性代数只保证对观测向量做最小距离投影；“系数是否因果”“标准误如何算”还需要随机误差与抽样假设。不要把最小二乘几何自动升级为统计或因果结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linear algebra guarantees only a minimum-distance projection of the observed vector. Whether coefficients are causal and how standard errors should be computed require stochastic and sampling assumptions. Least-squares geometry does not automatically imply statistical or causal validity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：拟合经过原点的直线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: fitting a line through the origin*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对数据 $(x,y)=(1,1),(2,2),(3,2)$，拟合 $y\approx\beta x$。此时 $A=(1,2,3)^T$、$b=(1,2,2)^T$，正规方程给
$$
\hat\beta=\frac{A^Tb}{A^TA}=\frac{11}{14}.
$$
残差 $e=b-A\hat\beta$ 满足 $A^Te=0$；它不是每一项都为零，而是整体与唯一拟合方向正交。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For data $(x,y)=(1,1),(2,2),(3,2)$, fit $y\approx\beta x$. Then $A=(1,2,3)^T$ and $b=(1,2,2)^T$, so the normal equation gives
$$
\hat\beta=\frac{A^Tb}{A^TA}=\frac{11}{14}.
$$
The residual $e=b-A\hat\beta$ satisfies $A^Te=0$. Its entries are not all zero; the residual vector as a whole is orthogonal to the sole fitted direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 最小二乘残差与什么正交？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*To what is a least-squares residual orthogonal?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 与 A 的列空间正交，等价于与每个解释变量列的样本向量内积为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It is orthogonal to the column space of $A$, equivalently to every explanatory-variable column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么数值计算常优先 QR 而非直接解正规方程？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does numerical computation often prefer QR to the normal equations?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 形成 $A^TA$ 会把条件数近似平方，放大舍入误差；QR 直接处理原矩阵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Forming $A^TA$ approximately squares the condition number and amplifies roundoff; QR works directly with the original matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 若 A 秩亏，哪些东西可能仍唯一？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If $A$ is rank deficient, what may remain unique?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 到列空间的投影和拟合值 $A\hat x$ 仍唯一，但实现这一拟合值的系数向量可能有无穷多个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The projection onto the column space and the fitted value $A\hat x$ remain unique, while infinitely many coefficient vectors may produce that fit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明为什么正规方程不是在要求残差为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why the normal equations do not require a zero residual.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 当 b 不在列空间时零残差不可能；正规方程只要求剩下的误差无法沿任何可拟合方向继续减小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> If $b$ is outside the column space, a zero residual is impossible. The normal equations require only that no attainable fitted direction can reduce the remaining error further.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
