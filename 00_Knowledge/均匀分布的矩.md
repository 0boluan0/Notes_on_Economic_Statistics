# 区间均匀分布的均值是中点而方差是区间长度平方除以十二

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A uniform interval has midpoint mean and variance equal to squared length divided by twelve*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X\sim U[a,b]$，$a<b$，则
$$E[X]=\frac{a+b}{2},\qquad E[X^2]=\frac{a^2+ab+b^2}{3},\qquad\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{(b-a)^2}{12}.$$
它们由 $\int_a^b x^k/(b-a)\,dx$（$k=1,2$）与 $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-E[X]^2$ 得到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integrate the first two powers against the constant density, then subtract the squared mean from the second moment. The centre controls the mean and the interval length controls variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如给定 $X=x>0$ 后若 $Y\mid X=x\sim U[0,x]$，则条件均值为 $x/2$，条件方差为 $x^2/12$。这里区间上限随给定信息变化，但每次在固定组内使用的都是同一条公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If Y conditional on X=x>0 is uniform on [0,x], its conditional mean and variance are x/2 and x²/12. The range varies across groups but the same formula applies within each fixed group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
