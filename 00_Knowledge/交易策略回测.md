# 交易策略回测是在历史信息与交易约束下重放规则并计算投资账户路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A trading strategy backtest replays rules under historical information and trading constraints to calculate an investment account path.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交易策略回测是把一套已明确的交易规则放回历史环境，依次重放当时可得数据、信号、委托、模拟成交、由成交形成的持仓与账户价值的过程。它回答“在这组数据、规则和实施假设下，账户会怎样变化”。信号命中率、资产未来收益预测与 [[VaR回测损益口径|VaR 预测的例外检验]]各有不同评价对象，不能直接代替账户重放。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trading strategy backtest replays a specified rule through historical data availability, signals, orders, simulated fills, resulting holdings and account values. It asks how the account would evolve under those data, rules and implementation assumptions. Signal hit rates, asset-return forecasts and [[VaR回测损益口径|VaR exception tests]] evaluate different objects and cannot substitute for the account replay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定 [[历史股票池]]的形成方式、参数更新程序和头寸动作，再沿 [[交易信号时序]]处理订单与成交。重放还要给出现金及结算可用性、未成交订单、[[证券交易成本]]、[[复权与成交价格|公司行动]]和期末未平仓资产的估值口径。订单要求买 8 股而只成交 4 股时，账户持仓只能增加 4 股；目标仓位不是已经实现的仓位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify how the [[历史股票池|historical universe]] is formed, how parameters are updated and which position actions follow, then process orders and fills in [[交易信号时序|signal and execution order]]. The replay also needs cash and settlement availability, unfilled orders, [[证券交易成本|trading costs]], [[复权与成交价格|corporate actions]] and a valuation convention for positions still open at the end. An order for eight shares filled for four increases holdings by four: a target position is not an achieved position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在没有公司行动、外部资金流和融资的单次成交中，令带符号成交股数为 $f$（买正卖负）、实际模拟成交价为 $P^{fill}$、显性费用为 $F$。采用交易日经济记账，持股 $q$ 与现金账面余额 $B$ 更新为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one fill without corporate actions, external flows or financing, let signed filled shares be $f$ (positive for buys), simulated execution price be $P^{fill}$ and explicit charges be $F$. Under trade-date economic accounting, holdings $q$ and book cash $B$ update as follows; settlement availability is a separate constraint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$q^+=q^-+f,\qquad B^+=B^- -fP^{fill}-F.$$

用市场标记价 $M_t$ 估值时，只持有一种股票的账户价值为 $V_t=B_t+q_tM_t$；有应收分配、负债或其他资产时须纳入对应项目。标记价用于估值，不自动是清仓可得价格。完整数例见 [[均线交叉规则]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marking one stock at $M_t$ gives account value $V_t=B_t+q_tM_t$; add receivable distributions, liabilities and other assets when present. A valuation mark is not automatically a liquidation price. See [[均线交叉规则|the moving-average crossover rule]] for a worked account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回放算对后，收益能否作为可推广证据仍由 [[训练验证测试划分]]、[[滚动起点评估]]、[[测试集自适应复用]]和 [[市场异象复核]]检验。一个回测数字同时依赖策略与模拟环境；它本身不证明未来盈利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the replay is correct, [[训练验证测试划分|data roles]], [[滚动起点评估|rolling-origin evaluation]], [[测试集自适应复用|adaptive test reuse]] and [[市场异象复核|anomaly validation]] determine what evidence can generalise. A backtest number depends on both the strategy and its simulated environment; it does not establish future profitability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
