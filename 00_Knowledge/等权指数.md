# 等权指数在指定再平衡时点为各成分分配相同的投资价值权重
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An equal-weighted index assigns equal investment-value weights to constituents at specified rebalancing times.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有 $n$ 个成分时，等权重是 $w_i=1/n$。它指每只股票配置相同金额，不是买相同股数。价格变化后权重会漂移，只有按规则再平衡才恢复相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n$ constituents the target is $w_i=1/n$: equal amounts of money, not equal numbers of shares. Price changes cause weights to drift; rebalancing restores equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两股各投入 100 元，分别涨 10% 和跌 10%，一期组合收益是 0%，依据[[单期组合收益]]为两项收益的算术平均。但期末持仓已变成 110 和 90，权重为 55% 和 45%；若要在下一期仍各占一半，须卖出前者 10 元、买入后者 10 元，暂忽略费用。固定基期的[[价格相对数算术平均]]并不会自动完成这种逐期再平衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investing 100 in each of two stocks with returns of +10% and -10% yields 0%, the arithmetic mean given by [[单期组合收益|one-period portfolio returns]]. Ending holdings of 110 and 90 imply weights of 55% and 45%. Restoring equality requires shifting 10 from the winner to the loser before costs. A fixed-base [[价格相对数算术平均|average of price relatives]] does not automatically implement periodic rebalancing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
