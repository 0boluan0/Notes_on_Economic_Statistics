# 复利与贴现是同一跨期等价关系的正反方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Compounding and discounting are opposite directions of the same intertemporal equivalence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子关系
> 每期有效利率为 $r$ 时，今天的 $PV$ 经 $t$ 期复利成为
> $$FV_t=PV(1+r)^t.$$
> 反向求现值就是除以同一积累因子：
> $$PV=\frac{FV_t}{(1+r)^t}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> At an effective rate $r$ per period, present value compounds for $t$ periods to
> $$FV_t=PV(1+r)^t.$$
> Discounting reverses the same equivalence by dividing by the same accumulation factor:
> $$PV=\frac{FV_t}{(1+r)^t}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的指数 $t$ 是现金流经历的计息期数，而不是看到“第几年”就机械照抄。若利率按月有效而时间以年给出，必须先统一期间；若使用连续复利，则换算因子改为指数形式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponent $t$ counts compounding periods; it is not a year label copied mechanically. If the rate is effective per month while time is stated in years, first put them on the same period basis. Under continuous compounding, the conversion factor takes an exponential form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某笔钱按每年有效利率 $6\%$ 两年后为 112.36。它今天值多少，为什么不能除以 $1.12$？
>
> **答案：** 今天值 $112.36/(1.06)^2=100$。两期复利的积累因子是 $(1.06)^2$；把两个 6% 简单相加会漏掉利息再生利息。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A sum grows to 112.36 in two years at an effective annual rate of $6\%$. What is its value today, and why not divide by $1.12$?
>
> **Answer:** It is $112.36/(1.06)^2=100$. The two-period accumulation factor is $(1.06)^2$; adding two 6% rates ignores interest on interest.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
