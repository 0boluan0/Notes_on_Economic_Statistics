# Smith 的自然价格是让生产并运送商品所需的工资、利润和地租按当地通常率得到支付的价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Smith's natural price pays the wages, profit, and rent required to produce and bring a commodity to market at their locally ordinary rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Smith 的价格分析中，**自然价格（natural price）**是恰好足以按通常率支付商品生产、加工和运送过程中应付工资、利润与地租的价格。这里的“通常”对应特定地点、时代、行业及社会状态；“自然”不是“道德上公正”，也不是一个永远不变的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Smith's analysis, **natural price** is exactly sufficient to pay the wages, profit, and rent due in producing, preparing, and bringing a commodity to market at their ordinary rates. These rates belong to a particular place, time, occupation, and condition of society. “Natural” means neither morally just nor permanently fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最容易漏掉的是**正常利润也在里面**。一个商人即使收回原料钱和已付工资，如果长期得不到在其他用途上通常能取得的利润，仍有理由把资本转走。因此，“以自然价格出售”不等于“经营者没有利润”。还要把上游投入算进去：例如面包店购买面粉，面粉价格又涉及磨坊及更早生产环节的成本与收入；只加总面包店自己的工资和利润，不能代表面包的全部生产成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Crucially, **ordinary profit is included**. Recovering materials and paid wages does not necessarily make an activity sustainable if capital consistently earns less than it could ordinarily earn elsewhere. Selling at natural price therefore does not mean that the proprietor receives no profit. Upstream inputs must also be counted: a bakery purchases flour whose price involves the mill and earlier stages. Adding only the bakery's own wages and profit does not capture the full cost of producing bread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与之相对，**市场价格（market price）**就是实际出售商品的价格，可能高于、低于或等于自然价格。自然价格提供比较的参照，但它的定义本身并没有解释实际价格为什么会靠近它；这个问题要靠[[自然价格的趋向机制|供给随报酬差异调整的过程]]来回答。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Market price**, by contrast, is the price at which the commodity is actually sold. It may lie above, below, or at natural price. Natural price supplies the benchmark, but its definition does not itself explain why the observed price should approach it. That requires an account of [[自然价格的趋向机制|supply adjusting to differences in returns]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读到“围绕自然价格波动”，可以先想：这是围绕**当时足以维持通常报酬的价格**波动。若生产条件或通常工资、利润、地租改变，参照本身也会改变；不要把昨天的自然价格当作今天一定要回到的数值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a price is said to fluctuate around natural price, the reference is **the price that currently sustains ordinary returns**. If production conditions or ordinary wages, profit, and rent change, the benchmark moves too. Yesterday's natural price is not a number today's market must necessarily return to.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
