# 固定息票债券按约定的不变票面利率计算息票
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A fixed-coupon bond calculates coupons at a contractually fixed coupon rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定息票债券（fixed-coupon bond）是在约定期间按不变的[[债券票面利率]]计算息票的债券。对本金不摊还、付息期间规则且等长的普通结构，每期承诺息票相同；浮动利率债券则按合约中的重设规则改变票面利率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed-coupon bond calculates its coupons at a contractually unchanged [[债券票面利率|coupon rate]]. In an ordinary structure with no principal amortisation and regular equal coupon periods, each promised coupon is equal. A floating-rate bond instead resets its coupon rate according to its contractual rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，面值 $100$、年票面利率 $6\%$、半年付息、两年后按面值偿还，其承诺现金流为第 $0.5,1,1.5,2$ 年分别支付 $3,3,3,103$。最后一期包含息票和本金，不能漏掉其中之一；不规则首末期则须按实际条款计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a bond with face value 100, a 6% annual coupon, semiannual payments, and redemption at par in two years promises $3,3,3,103$ at years $0.5,1,1.5,2$. The final payment includes both coupon and principal. Irregular first or last periods must follow the actual terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“固定息票”只描述计息规则，不保证履约，也不排除提前赎回等选择权。因此，固定息票不自动意味着实际现金流的金额和日期在所有状态下都不变；使用[[固定现金流债券定价]]还须确认这些额外条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Fixed coupon” describes the interest rule, not guaranteed performance or the absence of options such as early redemption. It does not by itself make actual payment amounts and dates invariant across states. Applying [[固定现金流债券定价|fixed-cash-flow bond valuation]] requires checking those additional conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
