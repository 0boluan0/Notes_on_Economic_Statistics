# 财政规则是对财政总量施加持续性数值约束的制度安排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A fiscal rule is an institutional arrangement imposing a lasting numerical constraint on fiscal aggregates.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的财政规则指约束债务、预算余额、支出或收入等财政总量的数值规则。它持续适用于一系列年度和决策，而非一次性公布某年的预算目标；“报告应及时公开”是重要制度要求，但不属于这个狭义数值定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A numerical fiscal rule constrains aggregates such as debt, the budget balance, expenditure or revenue across successive decisions and years. A one-year target is not necessarily a lasting rule, and timely disclosure is a procedural requirement rather than a numerical rule in this narrow sense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读一条规则，需要知道约束变量、计算口径、机构范围、期限及上限或下限。例如“某范围的债务率不得超过一定界限”与“年度支出增速不得超过一定值”约束的对象不同；它们可以通过[[债务锚与操作规则]]相连接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the variable, measurement basis, coverage, horizon and ceiling or floor. A debt-ratio limit and an annual expenditure-growth limit constrain different objects; [[债务锚与操作规则|debt anchors and operational rules]] explain how they can connect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规则的作用是给财政选择设置可说明、可监督的边界，不替代支出质量、收入设计和风险判断。可信设计还需要处理经济波动、[[财政逃逸条款]]和监督；制定了规则与真正遵守及产生效果，见[[财政规则合规与效果]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rules provide explicit, monitorable boundaries rather than replace judgements about spending quality, revenue design and risks. Credible arrangements address fluctuations, [[财政逃逸条款|escape clauses]] and oversight. Distinguish adoption from [[财政规则合规与效果|compliance and effectiveness]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
