# 费景汉–拉尼斯模型是把农业劳动释放、食品剩余与工业资本积累联结起来的二部门转型模型

费景汉–拉尼斯模型是一种把农业食品供给与工业劳动吸收联结起来的二部门转型模型。农业释放劳动并提供供养工业人口的食品，工业通过[[利润再投与劳动吸收|资本积累和劳动需求扩张]]提供就业及工业品；劳动转移能否持续，还取决于农业产出、收入分配和两部门之间的相对价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Fei–Ranis model is a two-sector transition model linking agricultural food supply with industrial labour absorption. Agriculture releases labour and supplies food for industrial workers, while [[利润再投与劳动吸收|capital accumulation and expanding labour demand]] create employment and industrial goods. Continued labour transfer also depends on agricultural output, income distribution and relative prices between the sectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1961年的基准分析承接[[Lewis 二元经济模型]]的劳动吸收主线，先固定人口与生产技术，把农业视为存在制度收入安排的劳动来源，把工业视为吸纳劳动的资本积累部门。农业中“移出一人不减产”的范围，与“劳动边际产出仍低于制度收入”的范围并不相同；两者产生[[农业短缺点与商业化点]]的区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Building on labour absorption in the [[Lewis 二元经济模型|Lewis model]], the 1961 baseline initially holds population and technology fixed. Agriculture supplies labour under an institutional income arrangement, while industry absorbs it through capital accumulation. The range in which withdrawing a worker leaves output unchanged is not the same as the range in which labour's marginal product remains below institutional income. This distinction underlies the [[农业短缺点与商业化点|shortage and commercialization points]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个模型不仅问“工厂还能招到多少人”，也问“农业在满足留农者消费之后，还能释放多少[[农业剩余]]”。因此，农业技术进步不是工业化之外的旁支：在消费与分配条件给定时，它能够增加可供工业人口使用的食品，影响[[食品价格与工业工资]]之间的传导，并改变工业扩张的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model asks not only how many workers factories can hire, but also how much [[农业剩余|agricultural surplus]] remains after rural workers' consumption. Agricultural technical progress is therefore integral to industrialization. Given consumption and distribution arrangements, it can increase food available to industrial workers, affect the link between [[食品价格与工业工资|food prices and industrial wages]], and change the conditions for industrial expansion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的农业／工业是模型化的二部门配置，不意味着现实中农业全属非资本主义生产、工业全属资本主义生产。原文先研究固定农业生产率的阶段，再引入农业与工业投资；这些是假设下的转型机制，不是所有国家必须依次经历的时间表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Agriculture and industry form a stylized allocation, not a claim that all actual agriculture is non-capitalist or all industry capitalist. The paper first studies phases with fixed agricultural productivity, then introduces investment in both sectors. These are conditional transition mechanisms, not a timetable that every country must follow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ranis、Fei（1961），[A Theory of Economic Development](https://www.depfe.unam.mx/actividades/11/desarrollo-crecimiento/11-2_catcdejr_04_ranis_fei_1961.pdf#page=3)，印刷第533–540页 §I、第542–544页 §II–III；第534页注2明确部门名称不构成现实一一对应。支持模型定义、农业—工业相互依赖、阶段与价格条件；不据此断言某国实际转型日期。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ranis and Fei support the model's definition, sectoral interdependence and conditional phases. Footnote 2 distinguishes the model's shorthand from actual sectoral organization. The source does not establish a turning-point date for any country.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
