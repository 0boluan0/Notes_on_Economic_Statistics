# EV-EBITDA倍数是经营企业价值与相同经营范围息税折旧摊销前利润的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EV/EBITDA compares operating enterprise value with earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation for the same operating scope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

EV/EBITDA把经营业务对资本提供者的价值，与同一业务范围的EBITDA相比。以所选期间 $EBITDA\ne0$ 为分母，$M=EV/EBITDA$；普通高低倍数比较通常要求EBITDA为正。这里EBITDA为经营利润加回同范围折旧摊销，尚未扣资本开支、营运资本投入或现金税，不等于[[企业自由现金流]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EV/EBITDA divides the value of the operating business by non-zero EBITDA for that scope; ordinary multiple rankings normally use positive EBITDA. Operating EBITDA adds matching depreciation and amortisation back to operating profit. It has not deducted capital expenditure, working-capital investment or cash tax and is not [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简情形下，没有优先股、少数股东权益、既有期权或其他非经营持股，且现金及其收益都未计入经营部分，则市场口径 $EV=普通股市值+债务价值-现金价值$。一旦存在其他权利或持股，应按[[经营价值转股权价值|经营价值与股权价值的桥接]]核对范围；不能用账面总负债替代债务价值，也不能只调整分子而把不匹配的现金收益或子公司利润留在分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With no preferred claims, non-controlling interests, outstanding options or other non-operating holdings, and cash and its income outside operations, market-implied EV is equity market value plus debt value less cash value. Other claims and holdings require the [[经营价值转股权价值|operating-to-equity bridge]]. Book total liabilities are not debt value, and numerator adjustments must match the operating income included in EBITDA.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如上述简化范围内，普通股市值800、债务价值300、另列现金100，经营EV为1000；下一预测年度经营EBITDA为100，预测倍数为10。若给目标企业应用10倍、同一预测年度及口径的EBITDA为80，得到的是经营价值800；还须桥接权益及[[估值日每股价值]]，不能直接称为800元股价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under that simplified scope, equity market value 800, debt value 300 and separately valued cash 100 give operating EV of 1000. Next-year forecast EBITDA of 100 gives a forward multiple of 10. Applying 10 to target EBITDA of 80 for the same forecast year and basis estimates operating value of 800, which still needs equity and [[估值日每股价值|per-share]] adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较时统一历史/预测期间、合并范围与会计调整。相同EBITDA不表示相同可分配现金：维持性资本支出或营运资本需求更高的企业，可能只留下更少自由现金。该倍数减少了直接用净利润时的融资口径差异，但没有消除风险、税、增长和再投资差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Match historical or forecast periods, consolidation scope and accounting adjustments. Equal EBITDA can leave different free cash flows when maintenance investment or working-capital needs differ. The multiple reduces the direct financing-basis differences in net income without removing risk, tax, growth or reinvestment differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=651|证券投资学，PDF651]]：支持课程倍数定义；本卡不把行业示例当无条件适用保证。

Damodaran supports matched operating claims and the adjustments between EBITDA and FCFF. The course supplies the multiple's scope; the examples are independently calculated and industry labels are not treated as guarantees.
