# 速度线是把一段行情的价格变动三等分后从原起点连出的参考线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Speed resistance lines divide a price move into thirds and join its origin to the division levels at the ending time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

速度线是在选定行情起点和终点后，将两端的垂直价格变动三等分，再从原起点连接到终点时刻的两个分割价位，并向未来延伸的参考线。完整图形也可保留起终点连线；新增的两条线不是必须经过真实中途峰谷的普通[[趋势线]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After selecting a move's start and end, divide the vertical price change into thirds, join the origin to the two division levels at the ending time, and extend the lines forward. The start-to-end line may also remain visible. The added lines need not pass through actual intermediate turning points as ordinary [[趋势线|trend lines]] do.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性价格坐标下，给定 $(t_0,P_0)$、$(t_1,P_1)$ 且 $t_1>t_0$、$P_1\ne P_0$，两个终点时刻价位是：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a linear price scale, for $(t_0,P_0)$ and $(t_1,P_1)$ with $t_1>t_0$ and $P_1\ne P_0$, the division levels are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Q_1=P_0+\frac{P_1-P_0}{3},\qquad
Q_2=P_0+\frac{2(P_1-P_0)}{3}.
$$

从 $(t_0,P_0)$ 分别连到 $(t_1,Q_1)$、$(t_1,Q_2)$。例如10个时间单位内从100涨到130，两点为终点时刻的110、120，线的斜率分别为1、2价格单位／时间单位；从130跌到100，则两点为120、110，斜率为−1、−2。它们是斜线，不是水平回撤价位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Join $(t_0,P_0)$ to $(t_1,Q_1)$ and $(t_1,Q_2)$. A rise from 100 to 130 over ten time units gives levels 110 and 120, with slopes 1 and 2 price units per time unit. The reverse decline gives 120 and 110, with slopes −1 and −2. These are sloping lines, not horizontal retracement levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

端点若更新，参考线随之变化；端点确认之前不能提前使用最终线。三等分是该工具的绘图约定，不证明价格必在这些线反弹、停顿或逐线移动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the endpoint changes the lines, and a final line is unavailable before its endpoint is identifiable. Division into thirds is a drawing convention, not proof that price must rebound, pause or move from one line to the next.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
