# Least-squares normal equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最小二乘正规方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **normal equations** test whether a candidate has already made the squared error as small as possible in [[线性最小二乘|linear least squares]]. They say that the remaining error is perpendicular to every direction in which changing the coefficients can move the output. This converts a nearest-point problem into linear equations for the coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正规方程**用来检验一个候选系数是否已经让[[线性最小二乘]]的误差平方和达到最小。它要求：改变系数能够移动输出的每个方向，都与剩余误差垂直。这样就把最近点问题写成了关于系数的线性方程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, take $A=(1,1)^T$ and $b=(1,3)^T$. A candidate $x$ produces $(x,x)^T$, so the target-minus-output residual is $r=(1-x,3-x)^T$. Changing $x$ moves the output along $(1,1)^T$. Its [[向量点积|dot product]] with $r$ is $(1-x)+(3-x)=4-2x$. Requiring that dot product to vanish gives $x=2$, the best compromise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=(1,1)^T$、$b=(1,3)^T$。候选 $x$ 产生 $(x,x)^T$，所以“目标减输出”的残差为 $r=(1-x,3-x)^T$。改变 $x$ 会让输出沿 $(1,1)^T$ 移动。这个方向与 $r$ 的[[向量点积|点积]]是 $(1-x)+(3-x)=4-2x$；要求它为零，就得到最好的折中 $x=2$。

> [!important] A necessary and sufficient test · 充要判别
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix a real $m\times n$ matrix $A$ and a target $b\in\mathbb R^m$, with $m,n$ positive integers. A coefficient vector $\hat x\in\mathbb R^n$ minimizes $\|b-Ax\|_2^2$ over all $x\in\mathbb R^n$ exactly when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定实 $m\times n$ 矩阵 $A$ 和目标 $b\in\mathbb R^m$，其中 $m,n$ 为正整数。系数向量 $\hat x\in\mathbb R^n$ 在全部 $x\in\mathbb R^n$ 中最小化 $\|b-Ax\|_2^2$，当且仅当
>
> $$
> A^T(b-A\hat x)=0
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> A^TA\hat x=A^Tb.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] $A^T$ places the columns of $A$ into rows. Thus its $j$th row multiplies the residual to give the dot product with column $j$. The left zero is the $n$-coordinate zero vector. There is one orthogonality equation per column; full column rank is not required for this characterization.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[矩阵转置|转置]] $A^T$ 把 $A$ 的列放到行中，所以第 $j$ 行乘残差，得到的正是残差与第 $j$ 列的点积。左式中的零是 $n$ 个坐标的零向量。每一列对应一条正交方程；这个判别不要求满列秩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the normal equations characterize the minimum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 正规方程从哪里来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The attainable outputs form the [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$. The [[最佳逼近与正交残差|nearest-point theorem]] says that $A\hat x$ is nearest to $b$ exactly when $r=b-A\hat x$ is [[正交|orthogonal]] to that space. Since every vector in $C(A)$ is a combination of the columns, zero dot product with each column is equivalent to zero dot product with the whole space. These individual conditions are exactly $A^Tr=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有可能输出构成[[列空间]] $C(A)$。[[最佳逼近与正交残差|最近点定理]]说明，$A\hat x$ 最近，当且仅当 $r=b-A\hat x$ 与这个空间[[正交]]。因为空间中的每个向量都是各列的组合，与每一列点积为零，就等价于与整个空间正交。这些逐列条件合写起来就是 $A^Tr=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can also see sufficiency by comparing a candidate $\hat x$ with any other $x$. The change in fitted output, $A(\hat x-x)$, belongs to $C(A)$ and is perpendicular to $r$. Splitting the error gives the [[正交向量勾股关系|Pythagorean identity]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还可以直接比较候选 $\hat x$ 与任意另一个 $x$，看清充分性。拟合输出的变化 $A(\hat x-x)$ 属于 $C(A)$，与 $r$ 垂直。分解误差后，用[[正交向量勾股关系]]得到

$$
b-Ax=r+A(\hat x-x),\qquad
\|b-Ax\|_2^2=\|r\|_2^2+\|A(\hat x-x)\|_2^2\ge\|r\|_2^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extra term cannot be negative. Therefore a solution of the normal equations is a global minimizer, not merely a stationary candidate. Equality holds exactly when the two coefficient choices produce the same output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

额外一项不可能为负，所以满足正规方程的系数一定达到全局最小，并不只是一个驻点候选。恰好当两组系数产生相同输出时，才取得等号。

> [!warning] The new system does not assert an exact fit · 新方程不声称精确拟合
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the opening example, $Ax=b$ is impossible, while $A^TAx=A^Tb$ is simply $2x=4$. Multiplication by $A^T$ is not an equivalence transformation of the original inconsistent system. The new system answers a different question: which output is nearest? At $x=2$ the residual is still $(-1,1)^T$, not zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 开头的 $Ax=b$ 无解，但 $A^TAx=A^Tb$ 就是 $2x=4$。左乘 $A^T$ 不是对原来不相容方程作等价变形；新方程回答的是“哪个输出最近”。在 $x=2$ 时，残差仍为 $(-1,1)^T$，并非零。

## Rank, complex entries and numerical computation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normal equations always have at least one solution here because finite-dimensional least squares always attains its minimum. Their coefficient matrix $A^TA$ is square of size $n\times n$, but it need not be invertible. The [[最小二乘解唯一性|uniqueness criterion]] permits $\hat x=(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb$ exactly when $A$ has full column rank. With dependent columns, there are infinitely many coefficient solutions, all producing the same fitted vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的正规方程总至少有一个解，因为有限维最小二乘总能达到最小值。系数矩阵 $A^TA$ 虽是 $n\times n$ 方阵，却未必可逆。根据[[最小二乘解唯一性|唯一性判据]]，恰好在 $A$ 满列秩时才能写 $\hat x=(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb$。若列相关，则有无穷多个系数解，它们产生同一个拟合向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex $A,b,x$, the objective uses squared magnitudes and the condition becomes $A^*(b-A\hat x)=0$, or $A^*A\hat x=A^*b$. Here $A^*=\overline A^T$ means conjugate transpose: transpose and replace each $a+bi$ by $a-bi$. Ordinary transpose does not give the required complex orthogonality. For instance, with $i^2=-1$, $A=(1,i)^T$ has $A^TA=0$ but $A^*A=2$; it has a nonzero, independent column, so the latter is the appropriate Gram matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数 $A,b,x$ 使用模的平方作为目标，条件变为 $A^*(b-A\hat x)=0$，或 $A^*A\hat x=A^*b$。这里 $A^*=\overline A^T$ 表示共轭转置：转置后，把每个 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$。普通转置不能给出所需的复数正交性。例如令 $i^2=-1$，则 $A=(1,i)^T$ 满足 $A^TA=0$、$A^*A=2$；它的唯一一列非零且线性无关，后一种才是适当的 Gram 矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exact characterization is also distinct from a computational recommendation. For real full-column-rank $A$, forming the Gram matrix [[正规方程条件数平方|squares its two-norm condition number]], a measure of sensitivity: $\kappa_2(A^TA)=\kappa_2(A)^2$. Rounding can therefore matter even when the algebra is correct. [[薄QR最小二乘|QR least squares]] offers a route that works directly with $A$. In regression, the same algebra is expressed as the [[OLS正规方程|OLS normal equations]], with its sample-versus-population interpretation handled there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确的数学判别也不同于计算方法推荐。对满列秩的实矩阵 $A$，形成 Gram 矩阵会让衡量敏感性的[[正规方程条件数平方|二范数条件数平方]]：$\kappa_2(A^TA)=\kappa_2(A)^2$。因此即使代数正确，舍入也可能很重要。[[薄QR最小二乘]]提供直接处理 $A$ 的求解路线。在回归中，同一代数写成 [[OLS正规方程]]；样本与总体含义由该卡展开。
