# 线性最小二乘解当且仅当残差满足正规方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A vector solves linear least squares exactly when its residual satisfies the normal equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心判别
> 对 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ 与 $b\in\mathbb R^m$，向量 $\hat x$ 是线性最小二乘解，当且仅当
> $$
> A^T(b-A\hat x)=0,
> $$
> 等价地，
> $$
> A^TA\hat x=A^Tb.
> $$
> 复数情形把 $A^T$ 换成共轭转置 $A^*$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ and $b\in\mathbb R^m$, a vector $\hat x$ is a linear least-squares solution exactly when $A^T(b-A\hat x)=0$, equivalently $A^TA\hat x=A^Tb$. Over the complex numbers, replace $A^T$ by the conjugate transpose $A^*$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 正规方程从哪里来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where the normal equations come from*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A\hat x$ 是列空间 $C(A)$ 中离 $b$ 最近的向量，当且仅当残差 $r=b-A\hat x$ 与整个列空间正交。$A$ 的列张成 $C(A)$，所以“与每一列正交”正好写成 $A^Tr=0$。这不是把原本不相容的方程 $Ax=b$ 随意左乘 $A^T$，而是把最近点的几何条件写成代数方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The vector $A\hat x$ is the point of $C(A)$ nearest to $b$ exactly when the residual $r=b-A\hat x$ is orthogonal to the whole column space. Because the columns of $A$ span that space, orthogonality to every column is precisely $A^Tr=0$. The normal equations do not come from arbitrarily left-multiplying an inconsistent system $Ax=b$; they encode the geometry of the nearest point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

充分性也可直接看出。若 $A^T(b-A\hat x)=0$，则对任意 $x$，两个向量 $b-A\hat x$ 与 $A(\hat x-x)$ 正交，因此
$$
\|b-Ax\|_2^2
=\|b-A\hat x\|_2^2+\|A(\hat x-x)\|_2^2
\ge \|b-A\hat x\|_2^2.
$$
所以满足正规方程的向量一定达到全局最小值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition is also sufficient. If $A^T(b-A\hat x)=0$, then $b-A\hat x$ is orthogonal to $A(\hat x-x)$ for every $x$. Pythagoras gives $\|b-Ax\|_2^2=\|b-A\hat x\|_2^2+\|A(\hat x-x)\|_2^2$, so $\hat x$ attains the global minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正规方程总能刻画最小二乘解，但 $A^TA$ 不一定可逆。只有 $A$ 满列秩时，才能写 $\hat x=(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb$；秩亏时正规方程仍有解，却可能有无穷多个。数值计算中也不应因为公式简短就默认显式形成 $A^TA$，因为这会平方二范数条件数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normal equations always characterise the least-squares solutions, but $A^TA$ need not be invertible. The formula $\hat x=(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb$ is valid only under full column rank. Under rank deficiency the normal equations still have solutions, but generally more than one. Numerically, the compact formula is not a reason to form $A^TA$ explicitly, because doing so squares the 2-norm condition number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么正规方程不是“把无解的 $Ax=b$ 左乘 $A^T$ 后硬说它有解”？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why are the normal equations not an arbitrary attempt to rescue an inconsistent system by multiplying it by $A^T$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 它们表达的是最优残差与 $C(A)$ 正交；这是最近点的充要条件，而不是对原方程做等价变形。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** They state that the optimal residual is orthogonal to $C(A)$, which is the necessary and sufficient nearest-point condition rather than an equivalent transformation of the original system.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
