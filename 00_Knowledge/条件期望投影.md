# Conditional expectation as a least-squares projection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件期望的最小平方误差投影*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **conditional expectation** is the best prediction from the available information when “best” means the smallest average squared error. For each information state, use the [[条件期望|conditional mean]] as the forecast. Another prediction based on the same information cannot lower the expected squared error. This result concerns the model’s true conditional mean; computing or estimating that mean from data is a further problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当“最好”指平均平方误差最小时，**条件期望**给出利用已有信息所能作出的最佳预测：在每个信息状态下，用[[条件期望|条件均值]]预测。任何使用相同信息的其他预测，都不能把平方误差的期望降得更低。结论针对模型中真实的条件均值；如何计算它或从数据中估计它，是下一步问题。

> [!example] Predicting a waiting time · 预测等待时间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two routes are equally likely. Route $0$ has equally likely waits of $1$ and $3$ minutes; route $1$ has waits of $4$ and $8$. If you know the route, predict its mean: $2$ or $6$. The average squared errors within the routes are $1$ and $4$, so the overall mean squared error is $(1+4)/2=2.5$. Always predicting the overall mean $4$ instead gives error $6.5$. Knowing the route lets the prediction adjust to the relevant group.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两条线路等概率出现：线路 $0$ 等概率等待 $1$、$3$ 分钟，线路 $1$ 等概率等待 $4$、$8$ 分钟。已知线路时，用该线路均值预测，即 $2$ 或 $6$。两组内部的平均平方误差分别为 $1$、$4$，总体均方误差为 $(1+4)/2=2.5$；若始终用总体均值 $4$ 预测，误差则为 $6.5$。线路信息让预测能够适应当前组。

## The error identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*比较预测时的误差恒等式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ be the target and let the [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] $\mathcal G$ record the available information. Assume $E[Y^2]<\infty$, also written $Y\in L^2$, and set $m=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$. A competitor $Z$ must be $\mathcal G$-measurable, meaning it uses only this same information, and square integrable. Its mean squared error is $E[(Y-Z)^2]$: square each prediction error and then average over the model. The key identity is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $Y$ 表示预测目标，[[σ-代数]] $\mathcal G$ 表示可用信息。假设 $E[Y^2]<\infty$，也写成 $Y\in L^2$，并令 $m=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$。作为比较的预测 $Z$ 必须对 $\mathcal G$ 可测，即只使用同一套信息，并且平方可积。它的均方误差是 $E[(Y-Z)^2]$：每次预测误差先平方，再按模型求平均。关键恒等式为

$$
E[(Y-Z)^2]=E[(Y-m)^2]+E[(m-Z)^2].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term is the error that remains when predicting with the conditional mean. The second is the extra error from moving away from that mean. It is nonnegative, and equals zero exactly when $Z=m$ with probability one. This gives both optimality and the correct sense of uniqueness. In the route example, predicting $3$ and $5$ instead adds $(1^2+1^2)/2=1$, raising the error from $2.5$ to $3.5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项是用条件均值预测仍会留下的误差，第二项是偏离条件均值所增加的误差。第二项非负，而且恰好在 $Z=m$ 以概率一成立时为零，因此得到最优性和相应的唯一性。在公交例子中，若改为预测 $3$、$5$，就多出 $(1^2+1^2)/2=1$，误差从 $2.5$ 上升为 $3.5$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The moment condition makes these finite comparisons possible. [[条件Jensen不等式|Conditional Jensen]] for the square gives $m^2\le E[Y^2\mid\mathcal G]$, hence $E[m^2]\le E[Y^2]<\infty$. Moreover, any predictor with finite squared error is automatically square integrable here, since $Z^2\le2(Y-Z)^2+2Y^2$. Thus restricting to square-integrable competitors does not omit a finite-error improvement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩条件保证这些比较使用有限数值。对平方函数使用[[条件Jensen不等式|条件 Jensen 不等式]]，有 $m^2\le E[Y^2\mid\mathcal G]$，所以 $E[m^2]\le E[Y^2]<\infty$。此外，由 $Z^2\le2(Y-Z)^2+2Y^2$ 可知，在这里任何具有有限平方误差的预测都自动平方可积，因此这一竞争范围不会漏掉某个有限误差的更优预测。

## 残差为什么与信息空间正交
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the residual is orthogonal to the information space*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $u=Y-m$ for the residual, the target minus its conditional-mean forecast. Its conditional mean is zero: $E[u\mid\mathcal G]=0$. For any square-integrable quantity $H$ known from $\mathcal G$, the product $uH$ is integrable because $|uH|\le(u^2+H^2)/2$. Apply the [[条件期望取出已知量|known-factor rule]] and [[全期望公式|total expectation]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令残差 $u=Y-m$，即目标减去条件均值预测。其条件均值为零：$E[u\mid\mathcal G]=0$。对任何平方可积、能由 $\mathcal G$ 确定的量 $H$，由于 $|uH|\le(u^2+H^2)/2$，乘积 $uH$ 可积。使用[[条件期望取出已知量]]和[[全期望公式]]：

$$
E[uH]=E\!\left[E[uH\mid\mathcal G]\right]=E\!\left[H E[u\mid\mathcal G]\right]=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is what **orthogonal** means here: the average product is zero. Expand $Y-Z=u+(m-Z)$. The cross term has zero expectation by taking $H=m-Z$, leaving the two squared terms in the identity. The collection of square-integrable predictions based on $\mathcal G$ is called $L^2(\mathcal G)$. With average product as the inner product and expected squared difference as squared distance, $m$ is the closest allowed point to $Y$—its projection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的**正交**就是乘积的平均值为零。展开 $Y-Z=u+(m-Z)$，取 $H=m-Z$ 即知交叉项期望为零，剩下恒等式中的两个平方项。所有基于 $\mathcal G$ 的平方可积预测组成 $L^2(\mathcal G)$。以乘积平均作为内积、平方差的期望作为距离平方时，$m$ 就是允许范围内距离 $Y$ 最近的点，也就是投影。

## 与线性预测的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with linear prediction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The allowed predictions include nonlinear functions of observations. If $X$ is equally likely to be $-1,0,1$ and $Y=X^2$, then $E[Y\mid X]=X^2$ predicts perfectly. A rule restricted to $a+bX$ cannot reproduce all three values; its best choice is the constant $2/3$, with squared error $2/9$. The [[条件均值与线性投影|conditional-mean versus linear-projection distinction]] explains the smaller class. This projection is linear as an operation on the target random variable; its answer need not be a linear function of the observed value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许使用的预测包括观测值的非线性函数。若 $X$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，且 $Y=X^2$，则 $E[Y\mid X]=X^2$ 可以完美预测。若只允许 $a+bX$，就无法同时重现三个值；最佳选择是常数 $2/3$，平方误差为 $2/9$。[[条件均值与线性投影]]说明这个较小的竞争范围。投影作为对目标随机变量的运算是线性的，但所得答案不必是观测值的线性函数。

> [!warning] Orthogonal does not mean independent · 正交不等于独立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the route example, residuals are $\pm1$ on route $0$ and $\pm2$ on route $1$. Their conditional means are zero, so they are orthogonal to every square-integrable function of the route. Yet their absolute size reveals the route exactly. Independence is a stronger claim than the zero-product averages used in the proof.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 公交例子中，线路 $0$ 的残差为 $\pm1$，线路 $1$ 的残差为 $\pm2$。各组条件均值均为零，所以残差与线路的每个平方可积函数都正交；但残差的绝对大小却完全暴露了线路。独立性比证明中使用的乘积平均为零更强。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The optimal error equals $E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)]$, the average [[条件方差|conditional variance]]. The [[全方差公式|law of total variance]] compares it with the error of the best constant prediction. If the target is merely integrable, the conditional mean still exists, but this finite-$L^2$ optimality argument needs the stronger [[矩存在性的使用边界|second-moment condition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最优误差等于 $E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)]$，即[[条件方差]]的平均值。[[全方差公式]]将它与最佳常数预测的误差进行比较。如果目标只有可积性，条件均值仍然存在，但这里的有限 $L^2$ 最优性论证需要更强的[[矩存在性的使用边界|二阶矩条件]]。
