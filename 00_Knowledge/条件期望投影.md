# 平方可积变量的条件期望，是给定信息下所有平方可积预测中的唯一最小均方误差预测，且预测残差与该信息空间正交
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The conditional expectation of a square-integrable variable is the unique minimum-mean-square-error predictor among square-integrable predictions based on the information, and its prediction residual is orthogonal to that information space*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定理
> 设 $X\in L^2$，$\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F$，并令 $m=E[X\mid\mathcal G]$。条件 Jensen 不等式保证 $m\in L^2$。对每个 $\mathcal G$-可测且平方可积的预测 $Z$，
> $$
> E[(X-Z)^2]
> =E[(X-m)^2]+E[(m-Z)^2].
> $$
> 因而 $m$ 在几乎处处相等的意义下唯一最小化均方误差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X\in L^2$, let $\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F$, and set $m=E[X\mid\mathcal G]$. Conditional Jensen gives $m\in L^2$. For every square-integrable, $\mathcal G$-measurable predictor $Z$, $E[(X-Z)^2]=E[(X-m)^2]+E[(m-Z)^2]$. Hence $m$ uniquely minimises mean squared error up to almost-sure equality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 残差为什么与信息空间正交
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the residual is orthogonal to the information space*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令残差 $u=X-m$。由条件期望的线性性，
$$
E[u\mid\mathcal G]
=E[X\mid\mathcal G]-m=0.
$$
对任意 $Z\in L^2(\mathcal G)$，Cauchy–Schwarz 不等式保证乘积可积，并且
$$
E[uZ]
=E\!\left[E[uZ\mid\mathcal G]\right]
=E\!\left[Z E[u\mid\mathcal G]\right]
=0.
$$
展开 $X-Z=u+(m-Z)$ 后，交叉项因此消失，得到上面的平方误差分解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $u=X-m$. Linearity gives $E[u\mid\mathcal G]=0$. For any $Z\in L^2(\mathcal G)$, the product is integrable and $E[uZ]=E[E[uZ\mid\mathcal G]]=E[ZE[u\mid\mathcal G]]=0$. Thus the residual is orthogonal to every square-integrable function of the available information. Expanding $X-Z=u+(m-Z)$ then removes the cross term and yields the squared-error decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与线性预测的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with linear prediction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ 在**所有**具有有限均方误差的 $\mathcal G$-可测预测中最优，不要求它对观测量呈线性。最佳线性预测只在一个更小的线性函数类中最优；除非条件均值本身属于该线性类，两者不必相等。反过来，残差与信息空间正交也不意味着残差与信息独立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ is optimal over all $\mathcal G$-measurable predictors with finite mean squared error; it need not be linear in the observations. A best linear predictor is optimal only in a smaller linear class and equals the conditional expectation only when the conditional mean belongs to that class. Orthogonality of the residual also does not imply independence from the information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么这里不能省略 $X\in L^2$？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can the condition $X\in L^2$ not be omitted here?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 该结论比较的是平方误差并使用 $L^2$ 内积；只有可积并不足以保证这些平方误差和正交内积有限。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The theorem compares squared errors and uses the $L^2$ inner product. Mere integrability does not ensure that the squared errors or orthogonality products are finite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
