# 两期总劳动生产率变化可精确拆为初期就业权重的部门内项与末期生产率权重的重配项

两期劳动生产率分解把总体变化分为：部门自身每就业者增加值的变化，以及就业份额在部门间变化的核算贡献。本页固定一种约定：部门内项用初期就业份额，重配项用末期部门生产率；完整保留两项时是精确恒等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two-period decomposition separates changes in value added per worker within sectors from the accounting contribution of changing sectoral employment shares. This note fixes one convention: initial employment shares weight the within component, and final productivity levels weight reallocation. Retaining both terms gives an exact identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先满足[[劳动生产率加总]]的范围、可加增加值和匹配劳动口径。两期部门集合一致，各期 $p_{i,t}$ 均有定义，$P_t=\sum_i s_{i,t}p_{i,t}$，并令 $\Delta x=x_1-x_0$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First satisfy the coverage, additive-value-added and matching-labour requirements in [[劳动生产率加总|productivity aggregation]]. Use the same sectors in both periods, with each $p_{i,t}$ defined, $P_t=\sum_i s_{i,t}p_{i,t}$ and $\Delta x=x_1-x_0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta P
=\underbrace{\sum_i s_{i,0}\Delta p_i}_{\text{部门内项}}
+\underbrace{\sum_i p_{i,1}\Delta s_i}_{\text{重配项}}.
$$

第一项保持初期就业份额，记录每个部门生产率的变化；第二项按末期生产率给就业份额变化计价。重配是份额变化的贡献名称，不预先保证正值；方向的判断见[[离农与生产率重配]]。总就业可以变化，这个恒等式本身不要求两期就业总数相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term holds initial employment shares fixed while recording sectoral productivity changes. The second values changing shares at final productivity. Reallocation names an accounting contribution, not a guaranteed gain; see [[离农与生产率重配|leaving agriculture and productivity reallocation]] for its sign. Total employment may change—the identity does not require equal employment totals across periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $p_{i,1}=p_{i,0}+\Delta p_i$ 展开第二项，得到同一变化的三项写法：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding the second term with $p_{i,1}=p_{i,0}+\Delta p_i$ gives a three-term expression for the same change:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta P
=\sum_i s_{i,0}\Delta p_i
+\sum_i p_{i,0}\Delta s_i
+\sum_i\Delta s_i\Delta p_i.
$$

最后一项是份额与生产率同时变化的交互项。三项全部保留仍然精确；两项式的重配项已经包含它。若用初期生产率计算重配后又省略交互项，才不再是一般的精确分解，不能把两种“重配”数值直接当作同一约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last term is the interaction of changing shares and productivity. Keeping all three terms remains exact; the two-term reallocation component already contains this interaction. Using initial productivity for reallocation while dropping the interaction is not generally exact. The two reallocation figures must not be treated as the same convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设初期 $s_0=(0.6,0.4)$、$p_0=(2,8)$；末期 $s_1=(0.5,0.5)$、$p_1=(3,10)$。先算总体：$P_0=4.4$、$P_1=6.5$，增量为 2.1。部门内项为 $0.6\times1+0.4\times2=1.4$；末期计价重配为 $3\times(-0.1)+10\times0.1=0.7$。三项写法则为 $1.4+0.6+0.1=2.1$，其中初期计价重配为 0.6，交互项为 0.1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $s_0=(0.6,0.4)$ and $p_0=(2,8)$, changing to $s_1=(0.5,0.5)$ and $p_1=(3,10)$. Aggregate productivity rises from 4.4 to 6.5, a change of 2.1. The within component is $0.6\times1+0.4\times2=1.4$; final-productivity-weighted reallocation is $3\times(-0.1)+10\times0.1=0.7$. The three-term version gives $1.4+0.6+0.1=2.1$, separating initial-productivity reallocation of 0.6 from an interaction of 0.1.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些项的单位是生产率水平的变化，不是自动带百分号的增长率；若 $P_0>0$，各项同除以 $P_0$ 才对应总体增长率的贡献。分解也不是某次迁移政策的因果效应：部门内项可包含资本、技术和部门内构成变化，重配项则使用已经观察到的份额与平均生产率。因果边界见[[平均生产率与迁移收益]]，不要把部门内项直接叫作[[增长核算的 TFP 残差|TFP 增长]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The components are changes in productivity levels, not automatically percentage growth rates. If $P_0>0$, dividing every term by $P_0$ produces contributions to overall growth. Nor does the decomposition identify a migration policy's causal effect: within-sector changes can include capital, technology and internal composition, while reallocation uses observed shares and average productivity. See [[平均生产率与迁移收益|average productivity and migration gains]] for the causal boundary; the within component is not simply [[增长核算的 TFP 残差|TFP growth]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [McMillan、Rodrik、Verduzco-Gallo（2014）](https://drodrik.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum7106/files/dani-rodrik/files/globalization_structural_change_productivity_growth_with_africa_update.pdf#page=8)，印刷第18–19页，§3(a)、式(1)：初期就业份额的部门内项和末期生产率的结构项。原文总生产率记为 $Y_t$；本组用 $P_t$，避免与增加值 $Y$ 混淆。
- [OECD，行业劳动生产率分解方法说明](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/support-materials/2023/02/oecd-compendium-of-productivity-indicators-2023_bdbeba7d/oecd-compendium-of-productivity-indicators-2023-methodology.pdf#page=3)，第3页式(2)–(3)：在适用可加价格口径下，工时加权的两项／三项精确展开。数例与单位判断由同一恒等式独立核算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
McMillan and colleagues specify the initial-share within term and final-productivity structural term. Their aggregate productivity notation $Y_t$ becomes $P_t$ here to distinguish it from value added. OECD supplies the corresponding exact two- and three-term hours-based expansion under additive prices. The example and units are checked independently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
