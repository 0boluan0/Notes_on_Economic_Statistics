# Finite-dimensional distributions of Brownian motion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*布朗运动的有限维分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose we record a [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] at times $1$ and $3$. The two readings are not independent: both contain what happened during the first unit of time. A [[有限维分布|finite-dimensional distribution]] gives the joint probabilities of such a finite list of readings, including their dependence. For Brownian motion, every such vector is [[联合Gaussian|jointly Gaussian]], so its mean vector and covariance matrix specify its whole law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设在时刻 $1$ 和 $3$ 记录同一条[[标准布朗运动]]的位置。这两个读数不独立，因为都包含第一单位时间内发生的变化。[[有限维分布]]给出这样一组有限读数的联合概率，包括相互依赖。对布朗运动，每组读数组成的向量都[[联合Gaussian|联合 Gaussian]]，所以均值向量和协方差矩阵就能指定整个联合分布。

> [!example] Build two positions from two increments · 用两个增量构造两个位置
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Z_1,Z_2$ be independent [[正态分布|standard normal]] variables. The pair
>
> $$(W_1,W_3)\ \overset{d}=\ (Z_1,Z_1+\sqrt2\,Z_2)$$
>
> has the Brownian law at these times; $\overset d=$ means equality of distributions, not equality of individual realizations. The first increment has variance $1$, the second variance $2$. Consequently the positions have variances $1,3$ and covariance $1$:
>
> $$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&3\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> The shared $Z_1$ accounts for that covariance. Two independently drawn normals with variances $1$ and $3$ would give the wrong joint law.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取独立[[正态分布|标准正态变量]] $Z_1,Z_2$，上式右边就具有时刻 $1,3$ 的布朗联合分布；$\overset d=$ 表示同分布，不是每次实现逐个相等。第一段增量的方差为 $1$，第二段为 $2$，因此两个位置的方差为 $1,3$，协方差为 $1$，得到上面的 $\Sigma$。共同的 $Z_1$ 解释了这个协方差。若直接独立抽取方差为 $1$ 和 $3$ 的两个正态值，联合分布反而不对。

## One formula for any finite list of times
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*任意有限时刻列表的统一公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any positive integer $n$ and any times $t_1,\ldots,t_n\ge0$, write $\mathbf W=(W_{t_1},\ldots,W_{t_n})^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and makes a column vector. Then

$$\mathbf W\sim N_n(0,\Sigma),\qquad \Sigma_{ij}=\min(t_i,t_j).$$

Here $0$ is the zero mean vector; the [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] $\Sigma$ records the [[协方差|covariance]] between every pair of positions. The function $K(s,t)=\min(s,t)$, also written $s\wedge t$, is called the covariance kernel: it supplies the matrix entry whenever we choose two times.

To see why the law is Gaussian, first sort the distinct positive times. Their independent normal increments form a jointly Gaussian vector, and each position is a sum of earlier increments. Fixed linear transformations preserve joint Gaussianity. Reordering positions, repeating a time, or inserting the constant $W_0=0$ preserves it too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意正整数 $n$ 和任意 $t_1,\ldots,t_n\ge0$，令 $\mathbf W=(W_{t_1},\ldots,W_{t_n})^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，将列表写成列向量。其分布如上式：$0$ 是零均值向量，[[协方差矩阵]] $\Sigma$ 记录每两个位置之间的[[协方差]]。函数 $K(s,t)=\min(s,t)$，也写作 $s\wedge t$，称为协方差核：选定两个时刻，就能由它得到相应矩阵元素。

为什么联合 Gaussian？先把不同的正时刻排序，它们之间独立的正态增量构成联合 Gaussian 向量；各个位置又是之前增量的和，而固定线性变换保持联合 Gaussian 性。随后重排位置、重复某个时刻、插入常数 $W_0=0$，也都保持这一性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $0\le s\le t$, split the later position into the shared past and the new increment:

$$W_t=W_s+(W_t-W_s),\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(W_s,W_t)=\operatorname{Var}(W_s)+0=s.$$

The zero term comes from independent increments with finite moments. Interchanging $s$ and $t$ gives $\min(s,t)$. Geometrically, this is the length shared by $[0,s]$ and $[0,t]$. In particular, $\operatorname{Cov}(W_2,W_5)=2$. The variables $W_2$ and $W_5-W_2$ are independent, while $W_2$ and $W_5$ are not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $0\le s\le t$ 时，把较晚的位置分成共同的过去和新增量，如上式。零项来自有限矩下的独立增量；交换 $s,t$ 就得到 $\min(s,t)$。从时间区间看，这正是 $[0,s]$ 与 $[0,t]$ 的重叠长度。特别地，$\operatorname{Cov}(W_2,W_5)=2$；$W_2$ 与 $W_5-W_2$ 独立，但 $W_2$ 与 $W_5$ 不独立。

## Joint density from independent increments
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从独立增量得到联合密度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now assume strictly ordered positive observation times $0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n$, and set $x_0=0$. Let $\Delta_i=t_i-t_{i-1}$ be interval length. The increments $D_i=W_{t_i}-W_{t_{i-1}}$ are independent with laws $N(0,\Delta_i)$, so their joint [[概率密度函数|density]] is the product of these normal densities. Positions $x_i$ correspond to increments $x_i-x_{i-1}$. This triangular change of coordinates has determinant $1$, so it leaves the volume factor unchanged:

$$f_{W_{t_1},\ldots,W_{t_n}}(x_1,\ldots,x_n)
=\prod_{i=1}^n\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi\Delta_i}}
\exp\!\left(-\frac{(x_i-x_{i-1})^2}{2\Delta_i}\right).$$

The first factor describes the move from zero to $x_1$ and must be included. The product factors in **increment coordinates**, not into independent marginal densities for the positions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在限定观测时刻严格为正且有序：$0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n$，设 $x_0=0$，并用 $\Delta_i=t_i-t_{i-1}$ 表示区间长度。增量 $D_i=W_{t_i}-W_{t_{i-1}}$ 独立且服从 $N(0,\Delta_i)$，所以联合[[概率密度函数|概率密度]]是这些正态密度的乘积。位置 $x_i$ 对应增量 $x_i-x_{i-1}$；这个三角坐标变换的行列式为 $1$，体积因子不变，得到上式。第一个因子描述从零到 $x_1$ 的移动，不能漏掉。这里乘起来的是**增量坐标**的密度，不是各个位置的独立边际密度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For times $1,3$, this becomes

$$f(x_1,x_2)=\frac1{2\pi\sqrt2}\exp\!\left(-\frac{x_1^2}{2}-\frac{(x_2-x_1)^2}{4}\right).$$

The term $x_2-x_1$ expresses dependence: once the first position is specified, the second is described by a fresh displacement from it. Integrating first over that displacement and then over $x_1$ gives total probability one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对时刻 $1,3$，密度化为上式。$x_2-x_1$ 这一项表达依赖：给定第一个位置后，第二个位置由从那里出发的新位移描述。先对该位移积分，再对 $x_1$ 积分，总概率为一。

> [!warning] A Gaussian law need not have a full-dimensional density · Gaussian 律未必有全维密度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The vector $(W_0,W_1,W_1)$ is still jointly Gaussian, but its first coordinate is always zero and its last two coordinates are equal. Its law lies on a line in three-dimensional space. The general covariance formula remains valid, whereas the density product above does not apply to this repeated-time list. The same distinction is explained in [[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑|singular Gaussian support]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 向量 $(W_0,W_1,W_1)$ 仍联合 Gaussian，但第一个坐标恒为零，后两个坐标相等；分布集中在三维空间的一条直线上。一般协方差公式仍有效，但上面的密度乘积不能用于这组重复时刻。这个区别也见[[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing this kernel determines all Brownian finite-dimensional laws because they are Gaussian; covariance alone does not determine arbitrary non-Gaussian laws. It also does not establish continuity of a particular version. For the reverse identification, including that path condition, use [[协方差核刻画布朗运动|the covariance characterization of Brownian motion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个核能确定全部布朗有限维分布，是因为它们联合 Gaussian；单靠协方差不能确定任意非 Gaussian 分布，也不能保证某个版本的路径连续。包含路径条件的反向识别见[[协方差核刻画布朗运动]]。
