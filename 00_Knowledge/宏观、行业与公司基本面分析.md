# 宏观、行业与公司基本面分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Macro, Industry, and Company Fundamental Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 从宏观环境、行业结构到公司现金流逐层形成可检验的盈利和估值假设。
> **具体锚点：** 降息可能提高整体估值倍数，但银行净息差、地产融资和出口企业汇率暴露的方向各不相同。
> **核心难点：** 宏观变量影响资产的路径多重且已可能被价格预期；好行业中的差公司和差行业中的低价公司都存在。
> **为什么重要：** 基本面分析的价值是把故事落到收入、利润率、资本需求和风险，而不是列新闻。
> **继续：** 用“驱动—财务—估值—催化/风险”闭环；报表见 [[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It moves from macro conditions through industry structure to company cash flow to form testable earnings and valuation assumptions.
> **Concrete anchor:** Lower interest rates may raise market valuation multiples overall, but the effects on bank net interest margins, property financing, and exporters' currency exposure can point in different directions.
> **Central difficulty:** Macro variables affect assets through multiple paths and may already be anticipated in prices; weak firms exist in strong industries and cheap firms in weak industries.
> **Why it matters:** Fundamental analysis turns a story into revenue, margin, capital needs, and risk rather than merely listing news.
> **Continue:** Use the loop “driver → financials → valuation → catalyst or risk.” For statements, see [[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测|Financial Statements, Ratio Analysis, and Forecasting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一条可追踪的研究链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A traceable research chain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本面研究不是从宏观新闻一路抄到公司结论，而是把每个判断写成可追踪箭头：外生变化 → 公司业务暴露 → 数量或价格驱动 → 报表科目 → 自由现金流与风险 → 当前价格隐含预期。任何一步没有机制，后面的估值就只是叙事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental research is not the transcription of macro news into a company conclusion. Write each judgment as a traceable arrow: external change → business exposure → volume or price driver → statement line item → free cash flow and risk → expectation embedded in current price. If any step lacks a mechanism, the valuation that follows is merely narrative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 宏观框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Macro framework*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长、通胀、利率、信用、汇率和政策构成环境。区分变量水平、变化率与相对预期：市场反应常来自“实际相对已定价预期的意外”，不是新闻好坏本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth, inflation, interest rates, credit, exchange rates, and policy form the environment. Distinguish a variable's level, rate of change, and surprise relative to expectations already priced: markets often respond to the surprise, not whether the headline sounds good or bad.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一宏观变化至少通过需求、成本、融资、估值贴现率和风险溢价五条路影响公司。利率下降可能降低利息支出并提高远期现金流现值，却也可能是需求恶化的内生反应；方向不能脱离冲击来源和公司资产负债表判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same macro change can affect a company through at least five channels: demand, cost, financing, valuation discount rate, and risk premium. A rate cut may lower interest expense and raise the present value of distant cash flows, yet it may also be an endogenous response to weakening demand. Direction cannot be inferred without the source of the shock and the firm's balance sheet.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 周期与政策传导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cycles and policy transmission*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经济周期影响需求、库存、违约和风险溢价。货币/财政政策通过不同渠道和时滞影响行业；资产久期、杠杆、定价权和外币敞口决定敏感性。不要给所有公司套同一宏观 beta。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The business cycle affects demand, inventory, default, and risk premia. Monetary and fiscal policy affect industries through different channels and lags; asset duration, leverage, pricing power, and foreign-currency exposure determine sensitivity. Do not assign every company the same macro beta.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 行业结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Industry structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场规模与增长只是起点；进入壁垒、替代品、供应商/客户议价、竞争强度、监管和技术决定利润池。行业生命周期影响资本开支、集中度和估值，但阶段不是固定宿命。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market size and growth are only the starting point. Entry barriers, substitutes, supplier and customer bargaining power, competitive intensity, regulation, and technology determine the profit pool. The industry life cycle affects capital expenditure, concentration, and valuation, but no stage is fixed destiny.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业增长不等于股东回报。若增长吸引大量资本、产品差异小且转换成本低，竞争会把收入增长转成更高资本开支和更低利润率。研究应同时预测市场规模、公司份额、单位经济和维持增长所需再投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry growth is not shareholder return. If growth attracts large amounts of capital while products are undifferentiated and switching costs are low, competition can turn revenue growth into higher capital expenditure and lower margins. Research should jointly forecast market size, company share, unit economics, and reinvestment needed to sustain growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 公司商业模式与护城河
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Company business model and moat*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

说明客户是谁、价值主张、收入模式、单位经济、获客/留存和关键资源。竞争优势必须能落到价格、成本、资本周转或增长持续性，并经数据而非口号验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the customer, value proposition, revenue model, unit economics, acquisition and retention, and critical resources. Competitive advantage must appear in price, cost, capital turnover, or growth persistence and be supported by data rather than slogans.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“品牌强”应产生可观察后果，例如更高同类价格、更低促销依赖、更稳定留存或更低获客成本；“网络效应”应随用户规模提高匹配质量或单位价值，并形成难以多归属的反馈。若没有这些证据，护城河只是命名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strong brand should produce observable consequences such as higher like-for-like prices, less dependence on promotion, steadier retention, or lower acquisition cost. A network effect should improve matching quality or unit value as users grow and create feedback that is difficult to multi-home around. Without such evidence, moat is only a label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 盈利与现金流驱动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Earnings and cash-flow drivers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把收入拆成量×价/用户×单价，把利润率拆成变动成本、固定成本和运营杠杆，把现金流拆成利润、营运资本和资本开支。预测应绑定少数可观测驱动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Decompose revenue into volume times price or users times revenue per user; decompose margins into variable cost, fixed cost, and operating leverage; decompose cash flow into profit, working capital, and capital expenditure. Forecasts should be tied to a small set of observable drivers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 数值锚点：收入增长为何不等于现金流增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical anchor: why revenue growth is not cash-flow growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司收入从 1,000 增长 20% 到 1,200，经营利润率维持 10%，新增经营利润为 20。若为增长新增应收 30、存货 25、应付只增加 10，同时资本开支高于折旧 15，则税前新增现金近似为 $20-30-25+10-15=-40$。增长创造会计利润，却在当期消耗现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue rises 20% from 1,000 to 1,200 while operating margin remains 10%, so incremental operating profit is 20. If growth adds 30 of receivables and 25 of inventory, payables rise only 10, and capital expenditure exceeds depreciation by 15, approximate pre-tax incremental cash is $20-30-25+10-15=-40$. Growth creates accounting profit but consumes cash in the period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 治理、会计与资本配置
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Governance, accounting, and capital allocation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查关联交易、激励、稀释、并购、分红/回购和会计估计。高增长若需要低回报再投资，未必创造价值；资本配置回报决定留存利润质量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Examine related-party transactions, incentives, dilution, acquisitions, dividends and repurchases, and accounting estimates. High growth does not necessarily create value if it requires low-return reinvestment; returns on capital allocation determine the quality of retained earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 估值与反证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Valuation and falsification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较基准情景与市场价格隐含的增长/利润率，列出催化剂和会推翻论点的证据。分析应可被未来事实更新。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare a base case with the growth and margin implied by market price, and list catalysts plus evidence that would overturn the thesis. Analysis should be updateable by future facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份最小投资论点应能回答：市场当前相信什么；你在哪个可观测驱动上不同意；差异何时会进入现金流或风险；什么事件让市场看到；哪些事实出现时你承认判断错误。只有目标价而没有反证条件，不能形成学习闭环。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimum investment thesis should answer: what the market currently believes; which observable driver you disagree about; when the difference reaches cash flow or risk; what event reveals it; and which facts would make you admit the thesis is wrong. A target price without falsification conditions cannot create a learning loop.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 从“GDP 增长”直接跳到“公司收入增长”，未检查地区、客户、份额和价格。
- 把行业增长率当公司永续增长率，遗漏竞争和再投资。
- 用护城河口号解释一切，却找不到价格、留存、成本或资本回报证据。
- 估值高于市价后只寻找支持材料，没有预先写出反证与更新时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Jumping directly from GDP growth to company revenue growth without checking geography, customers, share, and price.
- Treating industry growth as the firm's perpetual growth while ignoring competition and reinvestment.
- Using moat slogans to explain everything without evidence in price, retention, cost, or return on capital.
- After estimating value above price, searching only for confirming evidence and failing to predefine falsifiers and update dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么宏观利好不一定带来某只股票上涨？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why need favorable macro news not make a particular stock rise?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 影响路径因行业/资产负债表不同，且价格可能已反映预期；关键是实际相对预期的变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Transmission differs by industry and balance sheet, and price may already reflect expectations; what matters is realized news relative to what was expected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 所谓护城河怎样落到财务上？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How should a claimed moat appear in the financials?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 应表现为持续定价权、成本优势、较高留存/单位经济、资本效率或更长增长期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It should appear as persistent pricing power, cost advantage, stronger retention or unit economics, capital efficiency, or a longer growth period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 基本面论点为什么要写反证条件？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why should a fundamental thesis state falsification conditions?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 使分析可检验和更新，防止任何结果都被事后故事合理化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It makes analysis testable and updateable and prevents every outcome from being rationalized after the fact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么高速增长公司可能毁灭价值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why can a fast-growing company destroy value?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 若每新增一单位收入需要大量资本，而新增资本回报低于资本成本，规模扩大使负 NPV 投资累积；收入增长越快，价值损失反而越大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> If each unit of new revenue requires substantial capital whose incremental return is below the cost of capital, expansion accumulates negative-NPV investment; faster revenue growth then destroys more value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
