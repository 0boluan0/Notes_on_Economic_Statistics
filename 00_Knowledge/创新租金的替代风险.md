# 下一代创新的到达风险缩短当前创新利润流的预期寿命
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The arrival risk of the next innovation shortens the expected lifetime of current innovation profits.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[创造性破坏|新质量会取代旧质量]] 的模型中，专利在法律上永久有效，并不保证企业永久收到利润。设旧技术仍在市场时利润流为常数 $\pi$，利率为常数 $r>0$，下一次创新以强度 $z$ 到达，并使旧利润归零。则这一利润机会的价值为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A perpetual legal patent need not yield perpetual profits when [[创造性破坏|new quality replaces old quality]]. Suppose profits remain constant at flow $\pi$ while the technology survives, the interest rate is $r>0$, and replacement arrives at intensity $z$, reducing old profits to zero. The profit opportunity is worth
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=\int_0^\infty e^{-rs}\pi\Pr(T>s)\,ds
=\int_0^\infty e^{-(r+z)s}\pi\,ds
=\frac{\pi}{r+z}.
$$

其中 $\Pr(T>s)=e^{-zs}$ 来自 [[研发的泊松到达|泊松替代的生存概率]]。$r+z$ 同时反映时间贴现与可能失去市场的风险；$z$ 不是法律规定的专利到期率。在上述常数条件下，也可写成资产价值条件 $rV=\pi-zV$：取得利润的同时，承担概率性损失整个 $V$ 的风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The survival probability $e^{-zs}$ follows from [[研发的泊松到达|Poisson replacement]]. The effective rate $r+z$ combines time discounting with market displacement, not statutory patent expiry. Under these constant conditions, the equivalent asset equation is $rV=\pi-zV$: profit receipts are offset by the risk of losing the entire asset value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，固定 $\pi=10,r=0.05$，若替代强度由 $0.05$ 升为 $0.15$，价值从 $100$ 降为 $50$。这是保持利润流不变的比较；在完整经济中，未来研发还可能影响工资和利润，需要一同解出，见 [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding $\pi=10$ and $r=0.05$ fixed, increasing replacement intensity from $0.05$ to $0.15$ lowers value from $100$ to $50$. This holds the profit flow constant. In the full economy, future research can also change wages and profits; see [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|stationary research equilibrium]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

研发企业今天争取的是成功后下一代的价值。因此 Aghion–Howitt 的创新代际记号是 $V_{j+1}=\pi_{j+1}/(r+\lambda n_{j+1})$：今天成功之后，**再下一次创新**的威胁缩短它未来收租的时间。经济租金会消失，但这次创新留下的生产率基础仍能传给后代，见 [[创新的跨期知识外溢]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A researcher seeks the value obtained after a successful discovery. In innovation-generation notation, $V_{j+1}=\pi_{j+1}/(r+\lambda n_{j+1})$: once the present project succeeds, a subsequent discovery threatens its profits. The rent can disappear while the productivity foundation benefits later generations; see [[创新的跨期知识外溢|intertemporal knowledge spillovers]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
