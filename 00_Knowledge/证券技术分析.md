# 证券技术分析是从价格和成交量等市场行为数据研究价格变化与交易规则的方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Technical analysis studies price movements and trading rules using market-generated data such as prices and trading volume.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券技术分析（technical analysis）以价格、成交量及其随时间变化的记录为主要输入，通过图形识别或数值计算描述市场行为，并提出关于未来价格或交易决策的假说。它的定义说明研究什么、怎样研究，并不预先保证某个图形具有预测力或某条规则能够获利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Technical analysis primarily uses records of prices, trading volume, and their evolution over time. Chart recognition or numerical calculations describe market behaviour and generate hypotheses about future prices or trading decisions. This defines the subject and approach; it does not establish a pattern's predictive power or a rule's profitability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如把每天的开盘、最高、最低、收盘画成 [[K线]]，是对当天行情的编码；把最近二十个收盘价取 [[简单移动平均]]，是对历史数据的平滑。进一步说“收盘上穿均线就买入”，才进入规则设计：还要明确何时知道信号、下什么订单、买多少以及怎样退出，见 [[交易策略回测]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Encoding a day's open, high, low, and close as a [[K线|candlestick]] describes that day's prices. A [[简单移动平均|simple moving average]] of twenty closes smooths past observations. “Buy after a close crosses above the average” goes further into rule design: signal availability, order type, quantity, and exit conditions must also be specified, as explained in [[交易策略回测|trading-strategy backtesting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本面分析通常从企业经营、现金流和折现出发；技术分析从已经形成的交易记录出发。两者可以共同提供一个研究方案的输入，但各自证据的含义仍须分清。价格里出现一段走势，与从当时可得信息中稳定预测到它，是两个不同的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental analysis commonly starts with operations, cash flows, and discounting; technical analysis starts with observed trading records. Both can supply inputs to one research design, but their evidence must be interpreted separately. Seeing a movement in historical prices is different from reliably predicting it using information available beforehand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
