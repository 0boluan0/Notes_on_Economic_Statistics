# 证券技术分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Technical Analysis of Securities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 用价格、成交量和交易行为形成可检验的趋势、反转和风险管理规则，而不是把图形名称当作因果解释。
> **具体锚点：** 移动平均交叉可把趋势规则写成明确入场/退出信号，但参数、成本和样本区间会显著改变结果。
> **核心难点：** 同一图形常有主观边界；反复试指标会产生数据挖掘，回测若偷看未来或忽略成本会虚高。
> **为什么重要：** 技术规则可以是交易信号研究，但必须接受与其他预测模型同样严格的外样本检验。
> **继续：** 先把图形变成无歧义算法，再做时序验证、成本和风险评估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It turns price, volume, and trading behavior into testable trend, reversal, and risk-management rules rather than treating chart-pattern names as causal explanations.
> **Concrete anchor:** A moving-average crossover can define explicit entry and exit signals, but parameters, costs, and the sample period can materially change results.
> **Central difficulty:** The same chart often has subjective boundaries; repeated indicator trials create data mining, and backtests are inflated by look-ahead or omitted costs.
> **Why it matters:** Technical rules can be studied as trading signals, but they must face the same rigorous out-of-sample tests as other predictive models.
> **Continue:** First translate a chart into an unambiguous algorithm, then perform time-ordered validation plus cost and risk assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从图形语言到算法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From chart language to an algorithm*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任何技术规则都应写成六个元素：输入数据、计算窗口、信号时点、入场条件、头寸规模、退出与失效条件。若两个分析者面对同一历史数据不能得到同一信号，它仍是视觉叙事而不是可检验策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every technical rule should specify six elements: input data, calculation window, signal time, entry condition, position size, and exit or invalidation condition. If two analysts cannot derive the same signal from identical historical data, the rule remains a visual narrative rather than a testable strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 趋势、支撑与阻力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trends, support, and resistance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

趋势用一系列高低点或滤波规则定义；支撑/阻力是特定价格附近供需行为假说。若无法预先规定识别和失效条件，图形只能事后叙事。突破规则需包含成交、缓冲和退出机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trend can be defined by sequences of highs and lows or by filtering rules; support and resistance are hypotheses about supply and demand near a price. Without pre-specified identification and invalidation conditions, a chart is only an ex post story. A breakout rule must include execution, a buffer, and an exit mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“突破 100”仍不完整：应说明用盘中触及还是收盘确认，是否要求超过 100 的固定百分比或波动率缓冲，何时下单，遇到跳空按什么价格成交，以及跌回哪一水平退出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Break above 100” is incomplete. State whether an intraday touch or closing confirmation is required, whether price must exceed 100 by a fixed percentage or volatility buffer, when the order is submitted, how a gap is executed, and at what level a reversal exits the position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 移动平均与动量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Moving averages and momentum*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

移动平均平滑噪声但必然滞后；短长均线交叉是趋势跟随。动量指标比较近期价格变化，震荡指标在区间市场可能有用。参数应来自训练期并在未见数据上评估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A moving average smooths noise but necessarily lags; a short–long moving-average crossover is trend following. Momentum indicators compare recent price changes, while oscillators may help in range-bound markets. Parameters should be selected in a training period and evaluated on unseen data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 最小规则例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A minimal rule example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义 $MA_{20,t}$ 和 $MA_{100,t}$ 都只使用 $t$ 日收盘及以前数据。若 $MA_{20,t}>MA_{100,t}$ 且前一日不满足，信号在 $t$ 日收盘后产生，并假设最早在 $t+1$ 日开盘按可成交价格买入；反向交叉时下一交易日退出。这样才没有用收盘后才知道的信号在同一收盘价成交。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define $MA_{20,t}$ and $MA_{100,t}$ using only closing prices through day $t$. If $MA_{20,t}>MA_{100,t}$ and the condition was false the previous day, the signal is generated after day $t$ closes and execution is assumed no earlier than the tradable opening price on day $t+1$; reverse the position at the next trading session after an opposite crossover. This avoids using a signal known only after the close to execute at that same close.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 形态与波浪
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Patterns and waves*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

头肩、三角、缺口和波浪理论试图从路径形状预测。应用必须量化顶点、窗口和目标，否则不同分析者可随意重画。主观自由度越高，越需盲评和多重检验校正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Head-and-shoulders, triangles, gaps, and wave theories seek predictions from path shape. An application must quantify turning points, windows, and targets; otherwise analysts can redraw the same history at will. The greater the subjective flexibility, the greater the need for blind evaluation and multiple-testing correction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 指标计算与解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Indicator calculation and interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

RSI、KD、MACD 等都是价格的确定变换，不创造新信息；多个指标可能高度重复。超买不等于立即下跌，指标阈值只是条件信号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
RSI, stochastic oscillators, MACD, and similar indicators are deterministic transformations of price and do not create new information; multiple indicators may be highly redundant. Overbought does not mean an immediate decline, and an indicator threshold is only a conditional signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指标价值可能来自压缩、降噪、标准化或方便执行，而不是额外观测。检验多个指标前应先检查它们是否只是相同收益窗口的不同变换；高度相关信号不构成独立证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An indicator may add value through compression, denoising, standardization, or ease of execution rather than through an additional observation. Before testing many indicators, check whether they are merely different transformations of the same return window; highly correlated signals are not independent evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 回测陷阱
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Backtest pitfalls*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

避免 look-ahead、survivorship bias、重复调参、未复权价格和错误成交假设。加入 bid–ask、冲击、借券、滑点和容量；使用滚动/扩展窗口而非随机打乱时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Avoid look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, repeated tuning, unadjusted prices, and false execution assumptions. Include bid–ask spread, impact, securities borrowing, slippage, and capacity; use rolling or expanding windows rather than randomly shuffling time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小数据切分是：训练期用于选择规则，验证期用于有限次比较，测试期只在方案冻结后使用。若看过测试结果再回头改参数，测试期已经变成训练数据，需要新的真正外样本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimum data split uses a training period to choose rules, a validation period for limited comparisons, and a test period only after the design is frozen. If parameters are changed after viewing test results, the test period has become training data and a genuinely new out-of-sample period is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 风险与组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk and portfolio context*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告收益分布、最大回撤、换手、尾部、市场 beta 和不同 regime 表现。高 Sharpe 若来自一次幸运路径或隐藏卖期权风险并不稳健。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report the return distribution, maximum drawdown, turnover, tails, market beta, and performance across regimes. A high Sharpe ratio produced by one lucky path or hidden short-option exposure is not robust.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 图形只能在走势结束后画清，说明识别规则使用了未来拐点。
- 参数从数百组合中选最好，却把普通回测显著性当最终证据。
- 策略毛收益集中在小盘、低流动性证券，但成本模型使用大盘平均点差。
- 多个指标同时确认其实来自同一价格序列，证据被重复计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A pattern becomes clear only after the move ends, indicating that its recognition rule uses future turning points.
- Selecting the best among hundreds of parameter combinations while treating ordinary backtest significance as final evidence.
- Gross return is concentrated in small, illiquid securities while the cost model uses an average large-cap spread.
- Several confirming indicators are transformations of the same price series, so evidence is counted repeatedly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 技术指标为什么没有凭空增加信息？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a technical indicator not create information from nothing?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它们是历史价格/成交量的函数，价值在于压缩和规则化，不是创造独立观测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It is a function of historical price or volume; its value lies in compression and rule formation, not in creating an independent observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 回测中最常见的未来信息泄露是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is a common form of future-information leakage in a backtest?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 使用当期收盘后才知道的数据按同一收盘成交、用未来样本选择参数或使用幸存成分股。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Executing at a close using data known only after that close, choosing parameters with future samples, or using only surviving constituents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 图形规则怎样变得可检验？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How does a chart rule become testable?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 预先量化窗口、阈值、入场、退出、成交价格和失效条件，再在未参与设计的数据上验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Pre-specify the window, threshold, entry, exit, execution price, and invalidation condition, then validate on data not used in design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么“超买”不等于马上卖出？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does “overbought” not mean sell immediately?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 阈值只是历史价格变换后的状态；强趋势中指标可长期处于高位。必须有经外样本验证的条件回报、执行与退出规则，才能从状态变成行动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The threshold is only a state derived from past prices, and an indicator can remain elevated throughout a strong trend. It becomes an action only with out-of-sample evidence plus execution and exit rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
