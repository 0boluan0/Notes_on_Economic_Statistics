# Indicator coding for multiple categories
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多类别指示编码*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A categorical predictor tells us which group an observation belongs to. Its labels need not carry numerical distances. **Indicator coding** replaces those labels with separate yes-or-no columns so a regression can assign a different level to each group. With an intercept, reference-group coding uses one fewer indicator than the number of categories; the missing indicator names the reference, rather than removing that group from the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类别预测变量告诉我们观察属于哪一组，标签本身不必具有数值距离。**指示编码**把标签改写成分别回答“是不是这一类”的数列，使回归可以给各组不同的水平。带截距的基准组编码使用比类别数少一条的指示列；没有单列指示的那一类充当基准，并没有被从数据中删除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three education categories, two indicator columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*三种学历，两条指示列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G$ record high school ($H$), bachelor’s ($B$), or postgraduate ($P$) education; each observation belongs to exactly one category. Choose $H$ as the reference. Define $D_B=I(G=B)$ and $D_P=I(G=P)$, where an [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $I(\cdot)$ equals $1$ when the statement is true and $0$ otherwise. The intercept column always equals $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $G$ 记录高中（$H$）、本科（$B$）、研究生（$P$）学历，每个观察恰属一类。选高中作基准，定义 $D_B=I(G=B)$ 和 $D_P=I(G=P)$；[[事件指示变量|指示函数]] $I(\cdot)$ 在括号内命题成立时取 $1$，否则取 $0$。截距列始终为 $1$。

| Group · 类别 | Intercept · 截距 | $D_B$ | $D_P$ | Model level · 模型水平 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High school · 高中 | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ | $\beta_0$ |
| Bachelor’s · 本科 | $1$ | $1$ | $0$ | $\beta_0+\beta_B$ |
| Postgraduate · 研究生 | $1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $\beta_0+\beta_P$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an integrable outcome $Y$, the group [[条件期望|conditional means]] can be written
$$E[Y\mid G]=\beta_0+\beta_BD_B+\beta_PD_P.$$
Here $\beta_0$ is the high-school mean, and $\beta_B,\beta_P$ are the other means minus that reference mean. With no other predictors, this model can represent any three finite group means; it does not constrain the two gaps to be equal. Actual population group means refer to groups with positive probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对可积结果 $Y$，三组[[条件期望|条件均值]]可以写成上式。$\beta_0$ 是高中组均值，$\beta_B,\beta_P$ 分别是另外两组减去高中组的均值差。没有其他预测变量时，模型能表示任意三个有限组均值，不要求两个差相同。实际总体组均值针对出现概率为正的组。

> [!example] Labels are not distances · 标签不是距离
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the three means are $20,26,35$. The coefficients are then $(\beta_0,\beta_B,\beta_P)=(20,6,15)$. Coding the labels as $1,2,3$ in a single numerical term $a+bG$ would require each successive difference to equal $b$. It cannot reproduce both the $6$-unit first gap and the $9$-unit second gap. Such a trend may be a deliberate model restriction, but it does not follow from assigning numerical labels.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若三组均值为 $20,26,35$，系数就是 $(20,6,15)$。若把标签写成 $1,2,3$ 后只放入一个数值项 $a+bG$，相邻两次变化都必须等于 $b$，无法同时复现第一段差 $6$、第二段差 $9$。等距趋势可以是有意采用的模型限制，但不能由标签的数字写法自动推出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the intercept replaces one indicator
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么截距会占掉一条指示列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $m\ge2$ mutually exclusive, exhaustive categories, define $D_g=I(G=g)$. Every observation satisfies $\sum_{g=1}^mD_g=1$. Thus all $m$ indicator columns together already produce the intercept column. In a model $a+\sum_g b_gD_g$, replacing $a$ by $a+c$ and every $b_g$ by $b_g-c$ leaves every fitted value unchanged, for any real $c$. The data cannot choose one unique set of these coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $m\ge2$ 个互斥且覆盖全部观察的类别，令 $D_g=I(G=g)$。每个观察都有 $\sum_{g=1}^mD_g=1$，所以全部 $m$ 条指示列相加，已经给出了截距列。在模型 $a+\sum_g b_gD_g$ 中，对任意实数 $c$，把 $a$ 换成 $a+c$，每个 $b_g$ 都换成 $b_g-c$，所有拟合值不变；数据无法从中选出唯一的一套系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This exact dependence is called the dummy-variable trap. It violates [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|the rank condition for unique OLS coefficients]]. Dropping one indicator and retaining the intercept removes this particular redundancy. The reference observations remain in the fit, with zeros in the retained indicator columns. For four regions, this coding uses three indicators plus the intercept.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种精确依赖称为 dummy-variable trap（虚拟变量陷阱），违反[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|OLS 系数唯一所需的秩条件]]。保留截距、去掉一条指示列，可以消除这一项冗余。基准组观察仍参与拟合，只是其余指示列都为零；四个地区对应三条指示列加截距。

> [!warning] Dropping one column is not a universal rank check · 少放一列不等于全设计自动满秩
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a design containing only these categories, reference coding has full column rank when every category has at least one observed member. An empty category supplies no information about its unrestricted mean. Extra controls can introduce other exact dependencies—for example, a control that is constant within each group is already a combination of the group indicators. Check the entire design, not just the number of category columns. Missing or unclassified observations must be handled explicitly; they must not silently become the all-zero reference group.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对仅含这些类别的设计，每类至少有一个实际观察时，基准组编码才满列秩。空类别没有信息来估计它不受限制的均值。新增控制变量还可能带来其他精确依赖，例如某控制变量在每组内恒定，它已经是组别指示列的组合。因此应检查整个设计，而不只数类别列。缺失或无法分类的观察也应明确处理，不能悄悄当成全零的基准组。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An alternative: one level per group, without an intercept
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*另一种表达：去掉截距，每组单列水平*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Omitting the intercept and retaining all $m$ indicators gives
$$E[Y\mid G]=\sum_{g=1}^m\mu_gD_g.$$
Exactly one indicator is active, so the coefficient $\mu_g$ is that group's level. When every group is observed and there are no additional dependencies, this is another full-rank representation of the same categorical model. For the education example the coefficients are directly $20,26,35$, instead of the reference coefficients $20,6,15$. Unweighted, unconstrained [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] fits each group to its observed sample average when these are the only predictors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

去掉截距、保留全部 $m$ 条指示列，得到上式。每行恰有一条指示为一，所以系数 $\mu_g$ 直接表示该组水平。各组有观察且没有其他依赖时，这也是同一类别模型的满秩表达。学历例子的系数直接为 $20,26,35$，而不是基准组表达的 $20,6,15$。如果只有这些预测变量，未加权、无约束的[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]会把每个组水平拟合为该组实际样本均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $g_0$ name the chosen reference group, $Z$ the vector of extra controls, and $\gamma$ their coefficients, so $\gamma^TZ$ is their weighted sum. With these controls, the interpretation is conditional: in $E[Y\mid G,Z]=\beta_0+\sum_{g\ne g_0}\beta_gD_g+\gamma^TZ$, the intercept is the reference level at $Z=0$, and $\beta_g$ is the gap at common $Z$ when no group interaction is included. These need not be raw group averages. See [[指示变量系数|indicator coefficients]] for that distinction, and [[基准组重编码|changing the reference group]] for translating coefficients while preserving the same fitted model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $g_0$ 为所选基准组，$Z$ 为额外控制变量向量，$\gamma$ 为它们的系数，因而 $\gamma^TZ$ 是各控制值的加权和。加入这些控制后，解释变为有条件的：在 $E[Y\mid G,Z]=\beta_0+\sum_{g\ne g_0}\beta_gD_g+\gamma^TZ$ 中，截距给出 $Z=0$ 时的基准组水平，没有组别交互时 $\beta_g$ 给出共同 $Z$ 处的差；它们不必等于原始组均值。[[指示变量系数]]解释这个区别，[[基准组重编码]]则说明怎样翻译系数而保留同一拟合模型。
