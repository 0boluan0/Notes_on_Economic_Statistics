# 含截距的模型用 m-1 个指示列表示 m 个类别，省略的类别成为比较基准
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With an intercept, an m-category variable is represented by m−1 indicator columns, and the omitted category becomes the reference group*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 编码规则
> 类别变量 $G\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ 不是一条有等距数值含义的连续变量。含截距时，选一类 $g_0$ 作基准，为其余 $m-1$ 类各建一个 0/1 指示列。截距给出基准类的水平，每个指示系数给出该类相对基准类的对比。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A category label $G\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ is not a continuous variable with equally spaced numerical meaning. With an intercept, choose one category $g_0$ as the reference and create one indicator for each of the other $m-1$ categories. The intercept gives the reference level, and each indicator coefficient gives a contrast with that level.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若学历分为高中、本科和研究生，以高中为基准，可写
$$
E[Y\mid G]
=\beta_0+\beta_BI(G=\text{本科})+\beta_PI(G=\text{研究生}).
$$
三个组的拟合均值依次为 $\beta_0$、$\beta_0+\beta_B$ 和 $\beta_0+\beta_P$。类别标签若直接编码成 $1,2,3$ 放入一列，会额外强加“相邻类别差相同”的线性限制，通常不是类别变量本来的含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For high school, bachelor's, and postgraduate education with high school as the reference, the fitted group means are $\beta_0$, $\beta_0+\beta_B$, and $\beta_0+\beta_P$. Entering labels 1, 2, and 3 as one numerical regressor would instead impose equal differences between adjacent categories, a restriction not normally contained in the labels themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把全部 $m$ 个指示列和截距同时放入，逐行都有
$$
I(G=1)+\cdots+I(G=m)=1,
$$
所以指示列之和等于截距列，设计矩阵不满列秩。这就是常说的 dummy-variable trap；软件自动丢掉一列能得到数值解，却不能替代对基准组和系数含义的说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Including all $m$ indicators together with an intercept makes their row-wise sum equal the intercept column, so the design lacks full column rank. This is the dummy-variable trap. Software may drop a column to obtain a numerical solution, but that does not explain the chosen reference group or coefficient meanings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种满秩参数化是去掉截距、保留全部 $m$ 个指示列；此时各系数直接是各组水平。两种写法可以表示同一组拟合均值，但不能混读系数：有截距写法给“基准 + 差异”，无截距写法给“每组水平”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Another full-rank parameterisation omits the intercept and retains all $m$ indicators, making each coefficient a group level. It can represent the same fitted group means, but its coefficients must not be read as though they came from reference-cell coding: one gives group levels, the other a baseline plus differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 四个地区类别、模型含截距时，常规基准组编码需要几条指示列？为什么不是四条？
>
> **答案：** 三条。四条指示列之和恒等于截距列，会造成完全共线；省略的一类成为比较基准。
