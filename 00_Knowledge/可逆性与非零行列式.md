# Determinant and invertibility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*可逆性与非零行列式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A square matrix is **invertible** when its action can be undone for every input and output. Its [[行列式|determinant]] gives an exact test: a nonzero value means that such an undoing is possible; zero means that some information is lost. The value need not be positive or equal to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
方阵**可逆**，就是它的作用对每个输入和输出都能撤销。[[行列式]]提供一个精确判据：非零表示能够撤销，等于零表示丢失了某些信息；这个数不必为正，也不必等于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, over the real numbers, compare
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，在实数范围比较

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
K=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$A$ sends $(x_1,x_2)^T$ to $(2x_1,3x_2)^T$. From any output $(b_1,b_2)^T$, division by $2$ and $3$ recovers exactly one input, and $\det A=6\ne0$. But $K$ always outputs $(2x_1,0)^T$: it erases $x_2$, so no output can reveal that coordinate. Its determinant is zero. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], here used to write the coordinate list as a column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$A$ 把 $(x_1,x_2)^T$ 变成 $(2x_1,3x_2)^T$。从任意输出 $(b_1,b_2)^T$ 出发，分别除以 $2$ 和 $3$，恰好恢复一个输入，而且 $\det A=6\ne0$。但 $K$ 的输出总是 $(2x_1,0)^T$：第二个输入坐标 $x_2$ 被消去了，输出无法告诉我们它原来是多少。此时行列式为零。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，这里用来把坐标列表写成列。

> [!important] The exact criterion · 精确判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer and let $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, with all entries in one field $\mathbb F$, such as the real or complex numbers. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$ 的所有元素来自同一个域 $\mathbb F$，例如实数域或复数域。则
>
> $$
> \det A\ne0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> A\text{ is invertible}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An [[逆矩阵|inverse]] is a matrix $B$ with $AB=BA=I_n$, where the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_n$ leaves every vector unchanged. “Singular” means that no such inverse exists. The same two-sided definition is used over the field stated here.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[逆矩阵]]是满足 $AB=BA=I_n$ 的矩阵 $B$；[[单位矩阵]] $I_n$ 让每个向量保持原样。“奇异”表示不存在这样的逆。这里在所声明的域上，使用同一个双侧逆定义。

## Why elimination proves both directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么消元能证明两个方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[高斯消元|Gaussian elimination]] to put $A$ into row echelon form $U$. A [[主元|pivot]] is the nonzero leading entry of a nonzero row. Row exchanges and nonzero row scalings multiply the determinant by nonzero factors; adding a multiple of another row preserves it. Thus [[消元计算行列式|tracking the operations]] gives $\det U=\gamma\det A$ for some nonzero scalar $\gamma$. In particular, $\det U$ is zero exactly when $\det A$ is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
用[[高斯消元]]把 $A$ 化成行阶梯形 $U$。[[主元]]是非零行的非零首项。换行与非零行缩放让行列式乘上非零因子，倍加另一行则保持行列式。因此，[[消元计算行列式|追踪这些操作]]得到 $\det U=\gamma\det A$，其中标量 $\gamma\ne0$。特别地，$\det U$ 与 $\det A$ 同时为零，或同时非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If there are $n$ pivots, every row and column has one. The echelon matrix is triangular with all diagonal entries nonzero, so its [[三角矩阵行列式|diagonal product]] is nonzero. We can also scale these pivots to one and clear above them, reaching $I_n$. Each row operation is multiplication by an invertible [[初等矩阵|elementary matrix]]. Let $E$ be the product recording all these operations. Then $EA=I_n$. Since $E$ itself is invertible, $A=E^{-1}$, and hence $AE=I_n$ as well. Thus the same operations exhibit a two-sided inverse of $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
如果有 $n$ 个主元，每一行和每一列都有主元。阶梯形矩阵是对角元全非零的三角矩阵，所以[[三角矩阵行列式|对角元乘积]]非零。还可以把各主元化为一，并消去它们上方的元素，最终得到 $I_n$。每个行操作都等于左乘一个可逆的[[初等矩阵]]；用 $E$ 表示记录全部操作的矩阵乘积，就有 $EA=I_n$。因为 $E$ 自己可逆，所以 $A=E^{-1}$，进而也有 $AE=I_n$。这些操作便给出了 $A$ 的双侧逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If there are fewer than $n$ pivots, the square echelon matrix has a zero row, so $\det U=0$ and $\det A=0$. There is also a [[自由变量|free variable]] in the homogeneous equation $Ux=0$. Set one free variable to $1$, the others to $0$, and solve for the pivot variables. This constructs a nonzero vector $z$ with $Uz=0$. Row operations preserve the solution set, so $Az=0$. An inverse $B$ would force $z=BAz=B0=0$, a contradiction. Thus $A$ is singular. These two exhaustive pivot cases establish the criterion without assuming it in the proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
如果主元少于 $n$ 个，方阵的阶梯形就有零行，因此 $\det U=0$、$\det A=0$。齐次方程 $Ux=0$ 中也会出现[[自由变量]]：取一个自由变量为 $1$，其余自由变量为 $0$，再解出主元变量，便构造出非零向量 $z$，满足 $Uz=0$。行操作保持解集，所以 $Az=0$。若存在逆矩阵 $B$，就会推出 $z=BAz=B0=0$，产生矛盾。因此 $A$ 不可逆。主元齐全与主元不足涵盖全部情况，证明没有预先使用待证的判据。

## Rank, lost directions and unique solutions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*秩、丢失的方向与唯一解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵秩|rank]] counts independent output directions and equals the number of pivots. The [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)=\{z:Az=0\}$ collects input changes that disappear. The proof therefore gives the familiar equivalence chain:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[矩阵秩|秩]]计数独立的输出方向，也等于主元数。[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{z:Az=0\}$ 收集会被消去的输入变化。因此，上面的证明给出常见的等价链：

$$
\det A\ne0
\iff A\text{ is invertible}
\iff \operatorname{rank}(A)=n
\iff N(A)=\{0\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full rank means that the $n$ columns are independent and span all of $\mathbb F^n$; in other words, they form a [[基|basis]], a set that expresses each output uniquely as a linear combination. The coefficients of that combination are exactly the unknowns in $Ax=b$. Consequently these conditions are also equivalent to **every** $b\in\mathbb F^n$ having exactly one solution, namely $x=A^{-1}b$. The word “every” matters: finding a solution for one particular target does not establish invertibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
满秩意味着这 $n$ 列无关，并张成整个 $\mathbb F^n$；换句话说，它们构成一组[[基]]，能把每个输出唯一地表示成线性组合。组合系数正是 $Ax=b$ 中的未知数。因此，这些条件还等价于：**每个** $b\in\mathbb F^n$ 都恰有一个解，即 $x=A^{-1}b$。这里的“每个”很重要；只找到某个特定目标的一个解，还不能证明矩阵可逆。

> [!warning] Singular does not mean that every system has no solution · 奇异不等于每个方程都无解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the opening matrix $K$, the target $(4,0)^T$ has solutions $(2,t)^T$ for every real $t$, while $(4,1)^T$ has none. In general, a singular square matrix gives no solution for unreachable targets and more than one for reachable targets. The latter are infinitely many over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$; see [[方程组解分类|solution classification]] for the general field distinction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对开头的 $K$，目标 $(4,0)^T$ 有解 $(2,t)^T$，其中 $t$ 可以是任意实数；目标 $(4,1)^T$ 则无解。一般地，奇异方阵对到不了的目标无解，对可达目标有多个解；在 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$ 上后者为无穷多解。一般域的区别见[[方程组解分类]]。

## Two diagnostic examples
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 两个诊断例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every row sum is zero, multiply by the all-ones column $\mathbf1=(1,\ldots,1)^T$. Each coordinate of $A\mathbf1$ is the corresponding row sum, so $A\mathbf1=0$. Since $n\ge1$, this is a nonzero input sent to zero, proving singularity and $\det A=0$. For example, $\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\2&-2\end{pmatrix}$ sends $(1,1)^T$ to zero. This is a sufficient test, not a necessary one: the singular matrix $K$ above does not have all row sums zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
如果每一行的元素和都为零，就用全一列向量 $\mathbf1=(1,\ldots,1)^T$ 相乘。$A\mathbf1$ 的各个坐标正是对应行的元素和，所以 $A\mathbf1=0$。因为 $n\ge1$，这是一个被送到零的非零输入，足以证明矩阵奇异且 $\det A=0$。例如 $\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\2&-2\end{pmatrix}$ 把 $(1,1)^T$ 送到零。这是充分检验，不是必要条件：上面的奇异矩阵 $K$ 并非每行元素和都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero determinant does not count how many directions were lost. The $4\times4$ zero matrix has rank zero, while $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,0)$ has rank three; both determinants vanish. Here $\operatorname{diag}$ denotes a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with the listed diagonal entries and zeros elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
行列式等于零，并不会告诉我们丢失了几个方向。$4\times4$ 零矩阵的秩为零，而 $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,0)$ 的秩为三，但二者行列式都为零。这里 $\operatorname{diag}$ 表示[[对角矩阵]]：所列元素位于主对角线，其余位置为零。

> [!question]- How much does zero tell us? · 零能告诉我们多少？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a $4\times4$ matrix has determinant zero, must its rank be $3$? No: its rank is less than $4$, but may be $0,1,2$ or $3$. Determine the actual rank by further elimination; for real or complex matrices, count the positive [[奇异值与秩|singular values]], the nonzero stretching factors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已知 $4\times4$ 方阵的行列式为零，能否断定秩为 $3$？不能：只能断定秩小于 $4$，实际可能是 $0,1,2,3$。具体秩可以继续消元判断；对实矩阵或复矩阵，也可以数表示非零伸缩强度的正[[奇异值与秩|奇异值]]。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The criterion concerns finite square matrices over a field. Rectangular matrices have no ordinary determinant of this kind. A [[左逆|left inverse]] can recover inputs without reaching every output, while a [[右逆|right inverse]] can reach every output without uniquely recovering inputs; dimensions must be kept separate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
判据针对域上的有限阶方阵。矩形矩阵没有这里的普通行列式。[[左逆]]可以恢复输入，却未必能到达全部输出；[[右逆]]可以覆盖全部输出，却未必唯一恢复输入，必须分别核对尺寸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The test is exact: zero and a small nonzero value are different. Over the reals, $\varepsilon I_2$ is invertible for every $\varepsilon\ne0$, although its determinant $\varepsilon^2$ can be arbitrarily small. Numerical decisions require scale and error tolerances; see [[数值秩|numerical rank]] and [[行列式与条件数边界|determinants and conditioning]]. A negative real determinant does not prevent invertibility: $\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$ has determinant $-1$ and is its own inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这个检验是精确的：“零”和“很小但非零”不同。在实数域上，$\varepsilon\ne0$ 时 $\varepsilon I_2$ 都可逆，尽管它的行列式 $\varepsilon^2$ 可以任意小。数值判断还需要尺度与误差容差，见[[数值秩]]和[[行列式与条件数边界]]。负的实行列式也不妨碍可逆性：$\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$ 的行列式为 $-1$，逆矩阵却就是它自己。
