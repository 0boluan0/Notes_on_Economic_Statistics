# 税后经营净利润是经营利润扣除按一致口径归属于经营活动的所得税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NOPAT is operating profit less the income tax consistently attributed to operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税后经营净利润（NOPAT）先把经营与融资损益分开，再扣经营活动应承担的所得税，衡量融资分配之前的税后经营成果。设税前经营利润为 $OP$、分配给经营的所得税为 $T_O$，则 $NOPAT=OP-T_O$。它不是直接从报表净利润中改一个名字。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NOPAT separates operating from financing income and expense, then deducts operating income tax to measure after-tax operating performance before financing distribution. With pre-tax operating profit $OP$ and allocated operating tax $T_O$, $NOPAT=OP-T_O$. It is not merely reported net income under a different label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若经营利润的适用税率可统一写成 $\tau$，才可简化为 $OP(1-\tau)$。CPA 的 ABC 经营利润为 304，按 32% 分配经营税负 97.28，得到 NOPAT 206.72。融资前的经营利润还须排除已划为金融的投资收益；本例它并不等于将普通利润总额加回全部利息后得到的 EBIT 310。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only when one applicable rate $\tau$ consistently represents operating tax can this simplify to $OP(1-\tau)$. ABC's operating profit of 304 and allocated tax at 32% give NOPAT of 206.72. Financial investment income must be removed from the operating numerator; here it differs from EBIT of 310 obtained by adding gross interest to reported pre-tax profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用于[[管理用利润表拆分]]时，经营税和融资税效应必须合起来还原报表所得税；用于[[企业自由现金流]]时，还要处理实际现金税与会计所得税的差别。亏损抵扣、递延税或跨地区税率差异存在时，不应不加判断地套一个历史平均税率。与投入资本比较则见[[投入资本回报率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[管理用利润表拆分|income-statement reconciliation]], operating tax and financing tax effects must reproduce reported tax. For [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]], reconcile accounting tax with cash tax as needed. Loss carryforwards, deferred tax, and different jurisdictions can invalidate an unqualified historical average rate. Comparison with operating capital belongs in [[投入资本回报率|ROIC]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
