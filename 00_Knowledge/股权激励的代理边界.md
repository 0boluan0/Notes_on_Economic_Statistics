# 管理层持股可以增加其承担的企业价值变化但不会自动消除代理冲突

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Managerial ownership can increase managers' exposure to changes in firm value without automatically eliminating agency conflicts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若经理人只享受私人好处，却把公司价值损失大部分留给外部股东，就可能过度消费在职福利或减少努力。持有更多同权普通股，使经理人承担更多公司价值变动，可以改变这种取舍；但持股不是一份完整的监督与治理制度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Managers may consume excessive perks or exert too little effort when they receive private benefits while outside shareholders bear most of the value loss. More equal-rights common equity increases managers' exposure to firm value and can change that trade-off, but ownership alone is not a complete governance system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设公司没有债务及其他优先请求权，一次私人消费使股权总价值减少 100，经理人的私人收益折价为 30，其他影响不变。经理人持股 20% 时承担价值损失 20，可能愿意消费；持股 50% 时承担 50，则该消费不划算。这只比较该项行为的现金流激励，不能推出持股比例与所有公司治理结果有统一单调关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the company has no debt or other prior claims, and a perk reduces total equity value by 100 while giving the manager private benefits valued at 30, with other effects unchanged. At 20% ownership the manager bears 20 and may prefer the perk; at 50% ownership the loss is 50 and the perk is unattractive. This isolates one cash-flow incentive, not a universal monotonic relationship between ownership and every governance outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价治理还要看实际控制权、信息获取和执行成本，以及经理人与其他出资人的目标。即使经理人持有全部股权，存在债务时仍可能发生[[股东债权人风险转移]]。把经理人激励与股东对齐，不等于已经对齐所有契约方的利益。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Governance assessment also requires actual control rights, information, enforcement costs, and the objectives of managers and other financiers. Even a manager owning all equity may face [[股东债权人风险转移|shareholder–creditor risk-shifting]] incentives when debt is outstanding. Alignment with shareholders does not align every contracting party.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
