# Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Smith, Ricardo, and Classical Political Economy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** Smith 解释分工、市场范围、资本积累与制度如何扩大国民财富；Ricardo 解释增长过程中工资、利润和地租如何分配。
> **具体锚点：** Smith 的别针工场说明专业化能大幅提高劳动生产率，但分工受市场范围限制；Ricardo 的边际土地分析说明人口与粮食需求扩张可推高地租并挤压利润。
> **核心难点：** 不能把 Smith 缩成“看不见的手”，也不能把 Ricardo 缩成“比较优势”；两人都在处理长期价格、积累、阶级收入与制度。
> **为什么重要：** 现代的生产率、经济增长、贸易和功能分配问题，仍在重复他们建立的许多分析对象。
> **继续：** Marx 如何从劳动力商品、剩余价值与资本循环重写积累，见 [[Marx 的价值、剩余与资本积累]]；价值分析如何转向边际选择，见 [[边际革命与 Marshall 综合]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Smith explains how division of labor, market extent, capital accumulation, and institutions enlarge national wealth. Ricardo explains how wages, profits, and rent are distributed during growth.
> **Concrete anchor:** Smith's pin factory shows specialization sharply raising labor productivity, while the division of labor remains limited by market extent. Ricardo's marginal-land analysis shows expanding population and food demand raising rent and squeezing profit.
> **Central difficulty:** Smith cannot be reduced to the “invisible hand,” nor Ricardo to “comparative advantage.” Both analyze long-run prices, accumulation, class incomes, and institutions.
> **Why it matters:** Modern questions of productivity, growth, trade, and functional income distribution still revisit many objects they established.
> **Continue:** For Marx's reformulation of accumulation through labor power, surplus value, and capital circulation, see [[Marx 的价值、剩余与资本积累|Marx on Value, Surplus, and Capital Accumulation]]. For the shift from value to marginal choice, see [[边际革命与 Marshall 综合|The Marginal Revolution and Marshallian Synthesis]].

## Smith 的分工、市场与积累
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Smith on division of labor, markets, and accumulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 将分工、市场范围、资本积累和制度连接起来；“看不见的手”不是对一切市场结果的无条件辩护。价值讨论区分使用价值和交换价值，长期价格围绕工资、利润、地租等自然率组织。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith connected division of labor, market extent, capital accumulation, and institutions. The “invisible hand” was not an unconditional defense of every market outcome. His value discussion distinguished use value from exchange value, while long-run prices were organized around natural rates of wages, profit, and rent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分工提高生产率的机制包括工人熟练、节约转换任务的时间和发明专用机器。但分工受市场范围限制：没有足够需求和交通，细分工种无法容纳。因此贸易、城市、基础设施和分工是互相加强的增长机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Division of labor raises productivity through dexterity, saved switching time, and invention of specialized machinery. Yet it is limited by market extent: without enough demand and transport, finely specialized occupations cannot be sustained. Trade, cities, infrastructure, and specialization therefore reinforce one another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自然价格不是今日的数学一般均衡，而是使工资、利润和地租获得通常报酬的长期重心。市场价格可因供需偏离而围绕它变动，资本和产出的进退使价格存在回归压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Natural price was not today's mathematical general equilibrium. It was a long-run center that provided ordinary returns to wages, profit, and rent. Market prices could move around it as supply and demand diverged, while entry and exit of capital and output created pressure toward return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Ricardo 的分配、地租与增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ricardo on distribution, rent, and growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Ricardo 聚焦工资、利润和地租在增长中的分配，比较优势说明相对机会成本而非绝对生产率决定贸易收益。边际土地和递减收益连接地租上升与利润压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo focused on the distribution of wages, profits, and rent during growth. Comparative advantage shows that relative opportunity cost rather than absolute productivity determines trade gains. Marginal land and diminishing returns link rising rent to pressure on profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当粮食需求增加使更劣等土地被耕作，边际土地无地租，较优土地与边际土地的产出差成为差额地租。若实际工资受生活资料成本支撑，粮食变贵会提高工资成本并压低利润。这是增长中功能分配的动态，不是单独一条地价理论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As food demand brings inferior land into cultivation, marginal land pays no rent, while the output difference between superior and marginal land becomes differential rent. If real wages are supported by subsistence-goods costs, dearer food raises wage costs and lowers profit. This is a dynamic of functional distribution during growth, not merely a theory of land prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 比较优势的两国算例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A two-country comparative-advantage example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 A 国每单位劳动可生产 10 布或 5 酒，B 国可生产 6 布或 4 酒。A 在两种商品上都有绝对优势，但 A 的 1 酒成本为 2 布，B 的 1 酒成本为 1.5 布。因此 B 在酒上有比较优势，A 在布上有比较优势。若贸易比率介于 1.5 与 2 布/酒之间，两国都可获得消费改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose one unit of labor produces 10 cloth or 5 wine in country A and 6 cloth or 4 wine in country B. A has absolute advantage in both goods, but one wine costs 2 cloth in A and 1.5 cloth in B. B therefore has comparative advantage in wine and A in cloth. A terms of trade between 1.5 and 2 cloth per wine can improve consumption in both countries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该算例只证明给定技术、完全调整和可行贸易比率下的潜在总收益。它不证明国内每个人都受益，也不包括调整时间、失业、分配与技术学习。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example establishes potential aggregate gains given technologies, complete adjustment, and feasible terms of trade. It does not show that every person within each country gains, nor does it include adjustment time, unemployment, distribution, or technological learning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用一句“看不见的手”代替 Smith 对分工、市场范围、垄断、国防与公共工程的复杂论述。
- 把比较优势误解为生产率绝对较低的国家无法出口。
- 把 Ricardo 的地租论与工资—利润动态拆开，丢失其分配问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Replacing Smith's complex arguments about division of labor, market extent, monopoly, defense, and public works with one sentence about the “invisible hand.”
- Misreading comparative advantage as saying a country with lower absolute productivity cannot export.
- Separating Ricardo's rent theory from wage–profit dynamics and losing the distributional problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimum self-check*

### 用自己的话解释“分工受市场范围限制”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why division of labor is limited by market extent.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 专门从事一个极细任务需要足够多购买者吸收产出；交通、城市或贸易扩大需求后，更细分工才可存续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Specializing in a very narrow task requires enough buyers to absorb output. Transport, cities, or trade expand demand and make finer specialization sustainable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 在算例中，为什么 A 在两种商品上都更高效，B 仍可在酒上有比较优势？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can B have comparative advantage in wine even though A is more productive in both goods?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为比较优势看相对机会成本；B 牺牲 1.5 布便可生产 1 酒，少于 A 的 2 布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Comparative advantage uses relative opportunity cost. B gives up 1.5 cloth for one wine, less than A's 2 cloth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Ricardo 的边际土地为什么把地租与利润联系起来？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does Ricardo's marginal land connect rent with profit?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 更劣土地进入使粮食的边际成本上升，较优土地获得差额地租；粮食变贵又可推高维持实际工资的成本，从而挤压利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Cultivating inferior land raises marginal food cost and creates differential rent on better land. Dearer food can also raise the cost of maintaining real wages, squeezing profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
