# 基金中基金是主要通过持有其他基金份额实现投资配置的基金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A fund of funds primarily allocates capital by holding shares of other funds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基金中基金（FOF）以其他基金份额作为主要投资对象，投资者持有的是上层基金份额，上层基金再持有底层基金。它是一只实际汇集资产、管理组合的[[证券投资基金]]，不只是网站推荐清单或投资者自己同时买入几只基金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fund of funds primarily invests in other funds. Investors own shares of the upper-level fund, which in turn owns the underlying funds. It is an actual [[证券投资基金|pooled investment vehicle]], not merely a website's recommended list or an investor independently buying several funds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“主要投资基金”不等于“任何资产都只能是基金份额”。例如中国《公开募集证券投资基金运作管理办法》第 30 条以 $80\%$ 以上基金资产投资其他基金份额作为 FOF 分类标准，2016 年 FOF 指引发布说明进一步明确其他公开募集基金。其余资产仍受适用规则与合同限制，不能从该比例推出可以任意投资，也不能把这一法域阈值当成全球定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Primarily investing in funds does not mean every asset must be a fund share. For example, Article 30 of China's public-fund operating rules uses an at-least-80% allocation to other fund shares to classify an FOF; the 2016 FOF guidance announcement specifies other publicly offered funds. Remaining assets remain subject to applicable rules and the contract. The threshold neither permits unrestricted investment of the remainder nor defines FOFs worldwide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断风险要穿透持仓，而不能数基金名字。忽略现金、负债和衍生工具，若 FOF 一半投资 A 基金、一半投资 B 基金，而 A、B 都把资产的 $80\%$ 投在同一股票，FOF 对该股票的间接资产暴露仍是 $0.5(80\%)+0.5(80\%)=80\%$。多套管理人没有自动产生[[分散化与共同风险|独立风险来源]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk assessment must look through the holdings rather than count fund names. Ignoring cash, liabilities, and derivatives, an FOF split equally between funds A and B remains 80% indirectly exposed to one stock if each underlying fund invests 80% in that stock: $0.5(80\%)+0.5(80\%)=80\%$. Multiple managers do not automatically create [[分散化与共同风险|independent risk sources]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，需合并理解上层直接费用和底层基金资产中承担的间接费用，并核对适用的费用减免或禁止重复收费规则。FOF 的选基和配置服务可以有价值，但“多一层基金必然降成本、降风险或保本”都不是定义所保证的结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Costs likewise require considering upper-level charges and expenses indirectly borne through underlying funds, together with applicable waivers or restrictions on duplicate charges. Fund selection and allocation can provide value, but an extra fund layer does not by definition lower costs, reduce risk, or protect principal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
