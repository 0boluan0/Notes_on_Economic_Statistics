# Distributional convergence and coupling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么分布相同，两个数仍可能始终不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two random quantities can have exactly the same distribution while always disagreeing when read together. A distribution records how often each value occurs; it does not record which value of one quantity is paired with which value of the other. This missing pairing information is why [[依分布收敛|convergence in distribution]] generally cannot guarantee [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]] to a specified random variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个随机量可以有完全一样的分布，却在一起读取时始终不同。分布记录各个取值出现的概率，没有记录一个量的值与另一个量的值如何配对。缺少这份配对信息，正是[[依分布收敛]]通常不能保证向某个指定随机变量[[依概率收敛]]的原因。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A choice of such a pairing is called a **coupling**: put variables on a common probability space and specify their [[联合分布|joint distribution]], while keeping the desired marginal distributions. No special independence assumption is built into the word.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指定这样的配对称为 **coupling（耦合）**：把变量放在共同概率空间上，指定它们的[[联合分布]]，同时保持要求的边际分布。“耦合”这个词本身并不要求独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A two-outcome counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小耦合反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Toss one fair coin. Let $X$ be one on heads and zero on tails, so $X$ has a [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli distribution]] with success probability $1/2$. For **every** index $n$, use the same toss and set $X_n=1-X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只抛一次公平硬币。正面时令 $X=1$，反面时 $X=0$，所以 $X$ 服从成功概率为 $1/2$ 的[[Bernoulli分布]]。对**每个**索引 $n$，都使用同一次抛掷，令 $X_n=1-X$。

| Coin result · 硬币结果 | Probability · 概率 | $X$ | Every $X_n$ · 每个 $X_n$ | $|X_n-X|$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Heads · 正面 | $1/2$ | $1$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| Tails · 反面 | $1/2$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read down either variable's column: both give zero and one with equal probabilities. Thus all their [[累积分布函数|CDFs]] are exactly the same:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

竖着看任一变量列，0 和 1 都各占一半，因此它们的[[累积分布函数]]完全相同：

$$F_n(t)=F_X(t)=\begin{cases}0,&t<0,\\1/2,&0\le t<1,\\1,&t\ge1.\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no distributional discrepancy to remove as $n$ grows: $X_n\Rightarrow X$ already follows from equality of the CDFs. But read across either row: the paired values differ by one. In particular,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随着 $n$ 增大，根本没有需要消除的分布差异；CDF 相同已经保证 $X_n\Rightarrow X$。但横着看任一行，同一次结果下的两个值都相差 1。特别地，

> [!warning] One fixed tolerance already disproves probability convergence · 一个固定阈值即可否定
> $$P(|X_n-X|>1/2)=1\qquad\text{for every }n.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Convergence in probability would require this probability to tend to zero for every positive tolerance. Its failure at $1/2$ is enough to refute the conclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 依概率收敛要求每个正容忍值对应的超差概率都趋于零；在 $1/2$ 处失败，已经足以否定结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same laws can be paired differently
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相同边际分布可以有不同配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the same coin space, define instead $Y_n=X$. The laws of $Y_n$, $X_n$ and $X$ are all the same, but now $|Y_n-X|=0$ always, so $Y_n\xrightarrow pX$. The distributional statements cannot distinguish these two constructions; their joint distributions can.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍在这个硬币空间上，改为定义 $Y_n=X$。$Y_n$、$X_n$ 与 $X$ 的分布都一样，但现在始终 $|Y_n-X|=0$，所以 $Y_n\xrightarrow pX$。只看分布的陈述无法区分这两个构造，联合分布则可以。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original sequence $X_n=1-X$ is itself constant in $n$ and converges almost surely to $1-X$. The claim is that it does not converge in probability to the **specified** variable $X$. Distributional convergence permits any limit variable with the same law; probability convergence compares actual paired values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原序列 $X_n=1-X$ 本身并不随 $n$ 改变，当然几乎必然收敛到 $1-X$。这里否定的是它依概率收敛到**指定的** $X$。依分布收敛允许用任何具有相同概率律的变量表达极限；依概率收敛则比较实际配对的数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not affect [[概率收敛推出分布收敛|the forward implication]]. The important reverse exception is [[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|a constant limiting value]]: when all limiting mass sits at one number $c$, distributional convergence forces the probability of being far from that number to vanish. The nonconstant Bernoulli limit here does not meet that condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这并不影响[[概率收敛推出分布收敛|正向蕴含]]。反向的重要特例是[[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|极限为常数]]：当极限的全部概率质量集中于一个数 $c$，依分布收敛就会迫使远离这个数的概率消失。本例的 Bernoulli 极限不是常数，不满足该条件。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [MIT 6.436J, Lecture 16, §1.2 and §3.2, pp. 2–3 and 12](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-436j-fundamentals-of-probability-fall-2018/b4ebf022b1060fcce920227d934dd6f0_MIT6_436JF18_lec16.pdf): verifies that distributional convergence constrains laws and that the reverse probability implication fails for random limits. The two-outcome coupling above is computed explicitly rather than attributed as the lecture's example.
- [Durrett, *Probability: Theory and Examples*, 5th ed., §3.2, pp. 116 and 118–119](https://math.duke.edu/~rtd/PTE/PTE5_011119.pdf): verifies the CDF definition and the distinction between prescribed variables and a new coupling with the same laws. Exercise 3.2.12 on p. 124 supplies the constant-limit exception, not this counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- MIT 6.436J 第 16 讲第 1.2 与 3.2 节，第 2–3、12 页：核对分布收敛只约束概率律，以及随机极限下反向蕴含失败。本卡的两结果配对由表格直接核算，不冒称讲义原例。
- Durrett 第五版第 3.2 节，第 116、118–119 页：核对 CDF 定义，以及原有变量与另建同分布耦合的区别；第 124 页习题 3.2.12 支持常数极限特例，并不是该反例的出处。
