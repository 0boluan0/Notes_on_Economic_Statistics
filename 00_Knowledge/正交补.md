# Orthogonal complements
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正交补*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **orthogonal complement** collects every vector perpendicular to a specified collection of vectors. It answers a complete-search question: after fixing some directions, which vectors are [[正交|orthogonal]] to **all** of them? In three dimensions, the answer for one line is a whole plane, not just one perpendicular arrow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正交补**收集与指定向量集合垂直的全部向量。它回答的是一个完整搜索问题：固定一些方向后，哪些向量与它们**全都**[[正交]]？在三维中，一条直线的答案是整个平面，并不是随意挑出的一支垂直箭头。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A line leaves a plane of perpendicular directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子与边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work in $V=\mathbb R^3$ with the standard dot product. Let $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$, the [[张成|span]] of one vector: $S$ contains all vectors $t(1,1,0)^T$ with real $t$. For a candidate $x=(a,b,c)^T$, orthogonality to every member means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在采用标准点积的 $V=\mathbb R^3$ 中，令 $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$，即一个向量的[[张成|张成空间]]：它包含实数 $t$ 任意取值时的全部 $t(1,1,0)^T$。对候选向量 $x=(a,b,c)^T$，与每个成员正交意味着

$$x^T\bigl(t(1,1,0)^T\bigr)=t(a+b)=0\quad\text{for every }t\in\mathbb R.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This holds exactly when $a+b=0$; the coordinate $c$ is unrestricted. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这当且仅当 $a+b=0$，而坐标 $c$ 不受限制。所以

$$S^\perp=\{(a,-a,c)^T:a,c\in\mathbb R\}
=\operatorname{span}\{(1,-1,0)^T,(0,0,1)^T\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $\perp$ names the orthogonal complement. The free choices of $a$ and $c$ describe a plane. Keeping only the line spanned by $(1,-1,0)^T$ would miss $(0,0,1)^T$, which is also perpendicular to every vector of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $\perp$ 表示正交补。$a,c$ 两个自由选择描述了一个平面。若只保留 $(1,-1,0)^T$ 张成的直线，就漏掉了 $(0,0,1)^T$；它同样与 $S$ 的每个向量正交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The definition keeps the ambient space fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定义必须固定外部空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an inner-product space $V$, meaning a [[向量空间|vector space]] with a specified [[内积|inner product]]. For any subset $U\subseteq V$, define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定内积空间 $V$，即一个已经指定[[内积]]的[[向量空间]]。对任意子集 $U\subseteq V$，定义

$$U^\perp=\{x\in V:\langle x,u\rangle=0\text{ for every }u\in U\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The candidate $x$ ranges over the surrounding, or ambient, space $V$. The test ranges over **every** member $u$ of $U$. Conjugate symmetry means that putting $u$ in the first argument gives the same zero condition. The set $U$ need not itself be a [[子空间|subspace]]: the single-point set $\{(1,1,0)^T\}$ has the same orthogonal complement as its span $S$ above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

候选 $x$ 遍历外部空间 $V$，检验则遍历 $U$ 的**每个**成员 $u$。共轭对称性保证，把 $u$ 放在第一个自变量也会给出相同的零条件。$U$ 本身不必是[[子空间]]：单点集合 $\{(1,1,0)^T\}$ 的正交补，与上面它张成的 $S$ 的正交补相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $S=\operatorname{span}\{u_1,\ldots,u_k\}$, testing every generator $u_j$ suffices, because inner products distribute over linear combinations. Testing only some of them need not suffice; the tested vectors must span $S$. The real-coordinate equivalence and a missed-direction example are explained in the [[按生成向量检验正交|spanning-set orthogonality test]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $S=\operatorname{span}\{u_1,\ldots,u_k\}$ 时，检验每个生成向量 $u_j$ 就够了，因为内积对线性组合按相应规则展开。但只检验其中一部分未必够，被检验的向量必须张成整个 $S$。实坐标下的等价性和漏掉方向的例子见[[按生成向量检验正交]]。

> [!warning] Orthogonal subspaces need not be complements · 正交的子空间未必互为正交补
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\mathbb R^3$, the lines $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$ and $T=\operatorname{span}\{(1,-1,0)^T\}$ are orthogonal: each vector of one is perpendicular to each vector of the other. But $T$ is only part of the plane $S^\perp$. In the two-dimensional ambient space $\{(a,b,0)^T:a,b\in\mathbb R\}$, the same lines **are** complements. The ambient space and inner product belong to the question.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中，两条直线 $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$ 与 $T=\operatorname{span}\{(1,-1,0)^T\}$ 正交：一边的每个向量都与另一边的每个向量垂直。但 $T$ 只是平面 $S^\perp$ 的一部分。若外部空间改成二维的 $\{(a,b,0)^T:a,b\in\mathbb R\}$，同样的两条直线**就互为**正交补。因此，外部空间与内积都是问题的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Linear combinations stay perpendicular
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么它仍是子空间

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even when $U$ is merely a set, $U^\perp$ is always a subspace. First, it contains zero. Next, suppose $x,y\in U^\perp$ and let $\alpha,\beta$ be any scalars. For every $u\in U$, using the first-argument-conjugate convention,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使 $U$ 只是一个集合，$U^\perp$ 也总是子空间。首先，它包含零向量。再设 $x,y\in U^\perp$，$\alpha,\beta$ 为任意标量。对每个 $u\in U$，采用第一个自变量共轭线性的约定，有

$$\langle\alpha x+\beta y,u\rangle
=\overline\alpha\langle x,u\rangle+\overline\beta\langle y,u\rangle=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus every linear combination stays in $U^\perp$, which is precisely the subspace closure requirement. Over the reals, the conjugates do nothing. With the opposite complex-inner-product convention, remove the conjugates in this displayed expansion; the zero conclusion is unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此任意线性组合仍属于 $U^\perp$，这正是子空间的封闭要求。实数的共轭不改变数值。若采用相反的复内积约定，则这条展开中的系数不取共轭；结果为零的结论不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extreme cases clarify the meaning. Since every vector is orthogonal to zero, $\{0\}^\perp=V$. Since a vector orthogonal to every member of $V$ must be orthogonal to itself, $V^\perp=\{0\}$. Also $\varnothing^\perp=V$: an empty set imposes no tests. This is not the ordinary set complement $V\setminus U$, which means “everything outside $U$”. For a subspace $S$, zero belongs to both $S$ and $S^\perp$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极端情况能帮助理解定义：每个向量都与零正交，所以 $\{0\}^\perp=V$；若与 $V$ 的每个成员都正交，就必须与自身正交，所以 $V^\perp=\{0\}$。此外 $\varnothing^\perp=V$，因为空集没有施加任何检验。这不是普通集合补集 $V\setminus U$，后者表示“不属于 $U$ 的所有元素”。对子空间 $S$，零向量同时属于 $S$ 与 $S^\perp$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a vector $z$ belongs to both $S$ and $S^\perp$, then $\langle z,z\rangle=0$, forcing $z=0$. Hence $S\cap S^\perp=\{0\}$. In finite-dimensional $V$, the [[正交分解定理|orthogonal decomposition theorem]] adds that every vector splits uniquely between these two subspaces, and $\dim S^\perp=\dim V-\dim S$. Here dimension counts independent directions. The definition itself does not require finite dimension; the extra conditions for decomposition or taking complements twice must be checked in their own results, including [[双重正交补|the double-complement theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $z$ 同时属于 $S$ 与 $S^\perp$，则 $\langle z,z\rangle=0$，迫使 $z=0$。因此 $S\cap S^\perp=\{0\}$。当 $V$ 有限维时，[[正交分解定理]]进一步保证每个向量能在这两个子空间之间唯一拆分，并给出 $\dim S^\perp=\dim V-\dim S$；这里维数计算独立方向的个数。正交补的定义本身不要求有限维，但做分解或再次取补时的额外条件要在相应结论中检查，包括[[双重正交补]]。
