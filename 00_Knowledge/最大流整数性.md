# Maximum-flow integrality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*最大流整数性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[网络流|network flow]] is allowed to split into fractions, even when every capacity is a whole number. **Maximum-flow integrality** says that fractions are not needed to attain the best possible delivery value when all capacities are integers: at least one best flow uses a whole number on every arc. It is an existence statement about an optimal choice, not a claim about every feasible choice or every optimal choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[网络流]]即使每条容量都是整数，也允许分成小数输送。**最大流整数性**说的是：当全部容量为整数时，为达到最好的净送达量，并不需要分数流量；至少存在一个最优流，在每条弧上都取整数。这是关于某个最优选择的存在性结论，并非说每个可行选择或每个最优选择都为整数。

> [!important] An integral optimum exists / 存在整数最优流
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a finite directed network with distinct source $s$ and sink $t$, suppose each arc $e$ has a finite nonnegative integer capacity $c(e)$. Then there is a feasible flow $f^*$ such that every $f^*(e)$ is an integer and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在有限有向网络中，源点 $s$ 与汇点 $t$ 不同，每条弧 $e$ 的容量 $c(e)$ 都是有限非负整数。则存在可行流 $f^*$，每个 $f^*(e)$ 都是整数，且
>
> $$\operatorname{val}(f^*)\ge\operatorname{val}(g)
> \quad\text{for every feasible real-valued flow }g.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The comparison includes fractional flows. Separate identities for parallel or opposite arcs are retained. Self-loops are harmless: they contribute nothing to net balances and can be assigned zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 比较范围包含分数流。平行弧或反向弧分别保留身份；自环对净平衡没有贡献，可统一取零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep track of what can be added or undone
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*记录还能增加什么，以及可以撤回什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with zero flow. To improve a current flow $f$, examine each original arc $e:u\to v$. There are two possible adjustments:

- If $f(e)<c(e)$, a forward step $u\to v$ can add at most $c(e)-f(e)$ on $e$.
- If $f(e)>0$, a backward step $v\to u$ can remove at most $f(e)$ from $e$.

These positive adjustment limits are **residual capacities**. The allowed steps form a residual network on the same vertices. A backward step cancels an earlier allocation; it does not send negative flow on an original arc. For example, an arc with capacity $5$ and current flow $3$ permits two more units forward or removal of up to three units backward.

Each step records the original arc and whether to add or subtract. If $v\to u$ is also an original arc, adding on that arc and removing from $e$ are two separate adjustments, even though their residual arrows point in the same direction. This convention also handles parallel arcs without mixing their capacities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
从零流开始。要改进当前流 $f$，逐一查看原弧 $e:u\to v$，它允许两种调整：

- 若 $f(e)<c(e)$，正向步骤 $u\to v$ 可以在 $e$ 上至多增加 $c(e)-f(e)$。
- 若 $f(e)>0$，反向步骤 $v\to u$ 可以从 $e$ 上至多撤回 $f(e)$。

这些正的调整限额称为**剩余容量**，允许的步骤在同一组顶点上组成余量网络。反向步骤是撤回已有分配，并不让原弧输送负流。例如，一条容量为 $5$、已有流量 $3$ 的弧，允许正向再加两单位，或反向撤回至多三单位。

每一步都记录所对应的原弧，以及增加还是撤回。若原图本来还有弧 $v\to u$，在该弧上增加与从 $e$ 上撤回是两种不同调整，尽管余量箭头同向。这种记录也能处理平行弧，不会混淆各自容量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Integer adjustments preserve feasibility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*整数调整保持可行性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the residual network has an $s$-to-$t$ route, choose a [[游走路径与环|simple directed path]] $P$, with no repeated vertex. Such a path can be obtained by removing repeated-vertex segments from a route. Its positive length follows from $s\ne t$. Let $\delta$ be the smallest residual capacity along its steps. Add $\delta$ on each forward original arc and subtract $\delta$ on each backward original arc.

No bound $0\le f(e)\le c(e)$ is broken, by the choice of $\delta$. A simple path does not use the same original arc twice. Either kind of adjustment increases net outflow by $\delta$ at the residual step's tail and decreases it by $\delta$ at its head. At an intermediate vertex of $P$, the entering and leaving steps cancel; outside $P$, nothing changes. Thus intermediate conservation is preserved, while the source's net outflow increases by $\delta$.

If $f$ and $c$ are integral, all residual capacities are integers. Their positive minimum $\delta$ is then an integer at least $1$, and every updated arc flow is still integral. Starting from zero, each improvement therefore produces another feasible integral flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若余量网络中存在从 $s$ 到 $t$ 的路线，就选一条不重复顶点的[[游走路径与环|简单有向路径]] $P$；删去路线中重复顶点之间的片段即可得到这样的路径。由于 $s\ne t$，它至少有一步。令 $\delta$ 为各步剩余容量中的最小值，对正向原弧增加 $\delta$，对反向原弧撤回 $\delta$。

由 $\delta$ 的选择，每条原弧仍满足 $0\le f(e)\le c(e)$。简单路径不会两次使用同一条原弧。无论增加还是撤回，都使相应余量步骤起点的净流出增加 $\delta$，终点的净流出减少 $\delta$。路径内部的每个顶点都有一进一出，两项相消；路径外没有变化。因此，中间顶点仍守恒，而源点的净流值增加 $\delta$。

若 $f,c$ 都为整数，全部剩余容量就是整数，所以其正的最小值 $\delta$ 至少为 $1$，调整后的各弧流量仍为整数。从零流出发，每次改进都得到新的可行整数流。

## Why improvement stops at a maximum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么有限次停止后得到的是最大流*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every feasible flow has value at most $B=\sum_{e\in\operatorname{out}(s)}c(e)$: incoming flow is nonnegative and outgoing flow is capacity-bounded. This is a finite integer. Starting at value zero and gaining at least one each time permits at most $B$ improvements. Therefore the process eventually has no residual $s$-to-$t$ path.

Let $S$ be the set of vertices reachable from $s$ by residual steps, including $s$ itself. At termination, $t\notin S$. Every original arc leaving $S$ is saturated, meaning $f(e)=c(e)$; otherwise its forward step would reach a vertex outside $S$. Every original arc entering $S$ has zero flow; otherwise its backward step would also leave $S$.

For any feasible flow $g$, sum net outflow over all vertices in $S$. Internal arcs cancel, and every vertex in $S$ other than $s$ has zero balance. Let $E_{\rm out}(S)$ and $E_{\rm in}(S)$ denote the original arcs crossing outward and inward. We obtain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
任意可行流的流值至多为 $B=\sum_{e\in\operatorname{out}(s)}c(e)$：流入非负，流出又受容量限制。这是有限整数。从零流值出发，每次至少增加一，因此改进次数至多为 $B$；最终余量网络中必不再有 $s$ 到 $t$ 的路径。

令 $S$ 是从 $s$ 沿余量步骤可达的顶点集，包含 $s$ 本身。停止时 $t\notin S$。每条从 $S$ 向外的原弧都已饱和，即 $f(e)=c(e)$；否则其正向步骤还能到达外部顶点。每条从外部进入 $S$ 的原弧都必须流量为零；否则沿其反向步骤也能离开 $S$。

对任意可行流 $g$，把 $S$ 中所有顶点的净流出相加。内部弧相消，且除 $s$ 以外各顶点的平衡均为零。用 $E_{\rm out}(S)$、$E_{\rm in}(S)$ 分别表示向外和向内跨越边界的原弧，得到

$$
\operatorname{val}(g)
=\sum_{e\in E_{\rm out}(S)}g(e)
-\sum_{e\in E_{\rm in}(S)}g(e)
\le\sum_{e\in E_{\rm out}(S)}c(e).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The set $S$, with $s\in S$ and $t\notin S$, defines a directed $s$–$t$ **cut**; its capacity is the last outward sum. Our terminal flow attains that bound exactly, because outward arcs are saturated and inward arcs carry zero. Hence its value is at least that of every real-valued feasible $g$. It is both maximum and integral, proving the theorem without assuming optimality merely from termination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
包含 $s$、不含 $t$ 的集合 $S$ 确定一个有向 $s$–$t$ **割**，其容量是最后这个向外容量和。终止时的流恰好达到该上界，因为向外弧饱和、向内弧为零。因此它的流值不小于任意实值可行流 $g$ 的流值，既最大又为整数。这证明了定理，而非只凭“停止了”就宣称最优。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Fractional maximum flows can still exist
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分数最大流仍可能存在*

> [!example] One bottleneck, two ways onward / 一处瓶颈，后面分成两路
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Give each of $s\to u$, $u\to v$, $u\to w$, $v\to t$, $w\to t$ capacity $1$. Send one unit on $s\to u$, then $1/2$ along each of $u\to v\to t$ and $u\to w\to t$. Conservation holds and the value is $1$. It is maximum because $s$ has only one outgoing arc, of capacity $1$. This maximum flow is not integral. Sending the whole unit through $v$ instead gives an integral maximum flow of the same value, exactly as the theorem promises.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 给 $s\to u$、$u\to v$、$u\to w$、$v\to t$、$w\to t$ 各设容量 $1$。先在 $s\to u$ 输送一单位，再沿 $u\to v\to t$ 和 $u\to w\to t$ 各送 $1/2$。各处守恒，流值为 $1$；由于 $s$ 唯一出弧的容量就是 $1$，它已经是最大流，却不是整数流。改为整单位全部经过 $v$，便得到同样流值的整数最大流，这恰好是定理保证的存在性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capacity assumption matters: a lone $s\to t$ arc of capacity $1/2$ has maximum value $1/2$ and no integral maximum flow. Conversely, some networks with noninteger capacities still have an integral optimum; the theorem gives a sufficient guarantee. Zero capacities and an empty arc set are included: the zero flow is then an integral optimum when no positive transfer is possible. The source and sink must still be distinct vertices.

Finally, the termination argument depends on integer improvements of at least one and a finite upper bound. It does not claim that arbitrary path choices terminate for all real capacities, nor that the bound $B$ is a fast running-time guarantee when capacities are large.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
容量条件确实重要：若只有一条容量为 $1/2$ 的 $s\to t$ 弧，最大流值就是 $1/2$，不存在整数最大流。反过来，有些含非整数容量的网络仍有整数最优流；定理提供的是充分保证。零容量和空弧集也被包含：无法正向净输送时，零流就是整数最优流，但源汇仍须是两个不同顶点。

最后，有限终止的论证依赖“每次整数改进至少为一”以及有限上界。它没有声称任意实容量下随意选路都会终止，也没有把改进次数上界 $B$ 宣称为容量很大时仍很快的运行时间保证。
