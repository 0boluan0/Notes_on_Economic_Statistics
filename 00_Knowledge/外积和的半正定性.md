# Positive semidefiniteness of outer-product sums
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*外积和的半正定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying a column vector by its own transpose produces a table of pairwise coordinate products. Adding such tables has a useful guarantee: every quadratic form of the result is a sum of squares, so it cannot be negative. This explains why matrices built from squared data vectors are positive semidefinite, even before introducing a probability model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把一个列向量乘以它自己的转置，会得到一张坐标两两相乘的表。把这样的表相加，有一个重要保证：所得矩阵的每个二次型都是平方和，因此不可能为负。这解释了为什么由数据向量的平方乘积构造的矩阵会半正定，甚至不需要先引入概率模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with real columns $v=(1,-1)^T$ and $u=(0,1)^T$. [[矩阵乘法|Column-times-row multiplication]] gives the **outer products** $vv^T$ and $uu^T$, both $2\times2$ matrices:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先取实列向量 $v=(1,-1)^T$、$u=(0,1)^T$。通过[[矩阵乘法|列乘行]]得到两个 $2\times2$ **外积**矩阵：

$$
vv^T=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
uu^T=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
S=vv^T+uu^T=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any test vector $x=(s,t)^T$, $x^TSx=(s-t)^2+t^2\ge0$. A [[二次型|quadratic form]] is the scalar obtained by placing $x$ on both sides of the matrix. [[半正定矩阵|Positive semidefiniteness]] requires this scalar to be nonnegative for every $x$, together with symmetry. It does not require every matrix entry to be nonnegative: $S$ has entries $-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意检验向量 $x=(s,t)^T$，都有 $x^TSx=(s-t)^2+t^2\ge0$。[[二次型]]就是把 $x$ 放在矩阵两侧相乘得到的标量。[[半正定矩阵|半正定性]]要求矩阵对称，且对每个 $x$ 这个标量都非负；它不要求每个矩阵元素非负，上例中就有 $-1$。

> [!important] Finite nonnegative weighted sums · 有限个外积的非负加权和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ be columns in $\mathbb F^p$, where $p$ is a positive integer, $r$ is a finite nonnegative integer, and $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Let $w_1,\ldots,w_r$ be fixed, finite nonnegative real weights. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $v_1,\ldots,v_r\in\mathbb F^p$ 是列向量，其中 $p$ 为正整数，$r$ 为有限非负整数，$\mathbb F$ 为实数域或复数域，权重 $w_i$ 为固定的有限非负实数。则
>
> $$
> \boxed{S=\sum_{i=1}^r w_i v_iv_i^*\succeq0},\qquad
> x^*Sx=\sum_{i=1}^r w_i|v_i^*x|^2\ge0\quad(x\in\mathbb F^p).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $*$ means [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]; for real entries it is ordinary transpose $T$. The weights need not sum to one. Zero weights and zero vectors are allowed, and an empty sum ($r=0$) is the zero matrix. The guarantee is for a finite sum; no limiting interchange is involved.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $*$ 表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]，实数情形就是普通转置 $T$。权重不必加起来等于一，允许零权重和零向量；空和（$r=0$）表示零矩阵。本结论讨论有限和，不涉及交换极限。

## Why each term contributes a square
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每一项为什么都贡献平方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real coordinates, $x^Tvv^Tx=(x^Tv)(v^Tx)=(v^Tx)^2$. For complex coordinates, the two scalar factors are conjugates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数情形有 $x^Tvv^Tx=(x^Tv)(v^Tx)=(v^Tx)^2$。复数情形中，这两个标量互为共轭：

$$x^*vv^*x=\overline{v^*x}\,(v^*x)=|v^*x|^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also $(vv^*)^*=vv^*$, so each term is [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] (symmetric in the real case). Multiplying its nonnegative quadratic value by $w_i\ge0$ preserves the sign, and adding finitely many such values proves the result. For example, $v=(1,i)^T$ gives $vv^*=\begin{bmatrix}1&-i\\i&1\end{bmatrix}$; its quadratic value is $|v^*x|^2$. Using $vv^T$ instead would not give this guarantee over $\mathbb C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

又因为 $(vv^*)^*=vv^*$，每一项都是 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，实数时则对称。非负二次型值乘以 $w_i\ge0$ 后仍非负，再把有限项相加即可。例如 $v=(1,i)^T$ 给出 $vv^*=\begin{bmatrix}1&-i\\i&1\end{bmatrix}$，二次型为 $|v^*x|^2$。复数情形改用 $vv^T$ 就失去了这个保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $r\ge1$, collecting the scaled columns into $B=[\sqrt{w_1}v_1\ \cdots\ \sqrt{w_r}v_r]\in\mathbb F^{p\times r}$ gives $S=BB^*$. This is the [[矩阵乘积外积展开|column–row expansion]] of a product. It explains the same nonnegativity as $x^*BB^*x=\|B^*x\|_2^2$, where the squared norm is the sum of the absolute squares of its coordinates. The [[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram criterion]] treats when such a product is strictly positive definite and invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $r\ge1$，把缩放后的列排成 $B=[\sqrt{w_1}v_1\ \cdots\ \sqrt{w_r}v_r]\in\mathbb F^{p\times r}$，就有 $S=BB^*$。这正是[[矩阵乘积外积展开|矩阵乘积的列—行展开]]，也把同一非负性写成 $x^*BB^*x=\|B^*x\|_2^2$，其中范数平方就是各坐标绝对值平方之和。什么时候这种乘积严格正定、进而可逆，由 [[Gram矩阵正定判据]]处理。

> [!warning] The sign and pairing matter · 权重符号与配对方式很关键
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A negative weight removes the universal guarantee. With one nonzero real $v$ and weight $-1$, choosing $x=v$ gives $x^T(-vv^T)x=-\|v\|_2^4<0$. Some sums with negative weights can still happen to be positive semidefinite, but the displayed theorem cannot certify them. Nor is an arbitrary outer product $uv^*$ covered: the paired factors must be the same vector and its conjugate transpose.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 负权重会破坏普遍保证。只取一个非零实向量 $v$，权重为 $-1$，再选 $x=v$，就有 $x^T(-vv^T)x=-\|v\|_2^4<0$。某些带负权重的和仍可能碰巧半正定，但不能凭上面的定理保证。任意外积 $uv^*$ 也不在此保证之内：这里配对的必须是同一个向量及其共轭转置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Semidefinite also does not mean invertible. A nonzero $vv^*$ is [[秩一矩阵外积刻画|rank one]], and in dimension $p>1$ any nonzero $x$ with $v^*x=0$ is a zero direction. For the opening vector $v=(1,-1)^T$, $x=(1,1)^T$ is such a direction for $vv^T$. Adding the term $uu^T$ removes it in that example, but adding more terms in an already represented direction need not do so.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半正定也不意味着可逆。非零 $vv^*$ 是[[秩一矩阵外积刻画|秩一矩阵]]；在 $p>1$ 时，满足 $v^*x=0$ 的非零 $x$ 是零方向。对开头的 $v=(1,-1)^T$，$x=(1,1)^T$ 就是 $vv^T$ 的零方向。上例加入 $uu^T$ 后消除了这个零方向，但继续加入已有方向的外积未必有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For random vectors, this algebra applies to each realised finite list. Independence or Gaussian assumptions are unnecessary for this positive-semidefiniteness statement. They become relevant to the distribution of a [[Wishart分布|Wishart matrix]], while centering and scaling define a [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance]]. An expected centered outer product is a different population construction, covered by [[协方差矩阵半正定性|the covariance PSD theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若向量是随机的，这个代数结论适用于每一组已经实现的有限取值；半正定性本身不需要独立或 Gaussian 假设。那些假设关系到 [[Wishart分布]]等抽样分布，中心化和缩放则定义了[[样本协方差矩阵]]。对中心化外积取期望是另一个总体构造，由[[协方差矩阵半正定性]]说明。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, §2.2.5, printed pp. 34–35 / PDF pp. 48–49](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=48): the real positive-semidefinite cone is closed under nonnegative linear combinations, proved by quadratic forms. The outer-product specialization and finite extension are derived above.
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 4th ed., 7.5, 7.7 and 7.34–7.38](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf): conjugate-transpose rules and positive-semidefinite operators over real or complex spaces. The displayed absolute-square identity checks the complex extension directly; Axler uses “positive” for nonnegative quadratic forms.
- [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/05_Session_Summaries/MIT18_06SCF11_Ses1.3sum.pdf#page=1|MIT 18.06SC matrix multiplication summary, p. 1]]: column-times-row expansion supports $BB^*=\sum_i w_iv_iv_i^*$. The zero, negative-weight and numerical examples are checked by direct multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Boyd 与 Vandenberghe 第 2.2.5 节（印刷第 34–35 页／PDF 第 48–49 页）：核对非负线性组合保持实对称半正定；正文把它具体化为外积，并扩展到有限项。
- Axler 第 7.5、7.7、7.34–7.38 条：核对共轭转置和实、复半正定约定；复数推广由正文绝对平方恒等式直接核验，该书的 positive 表示二次型非负。
- MIT 矩阵乘法摘要第 1 页：核对列乘行展开；零情形、负权重和具体矩阵都直接相乘检查。
