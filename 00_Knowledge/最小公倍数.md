# 最小公倍数是两个正整数共有的最小正倍数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The least common multiple is the smallest positive multiple shared by two positive integers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $a,b$，$\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ 是同时被 $a,b$ [[整除]]的最小正整数。例如 $4$ 的正倍数是 $4,8,12,\ldots$，$6$ 的正倍数是 $6,12,18,\ldots$，第一个重合点是 $12$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive integers $a,b$, $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ is the smallest positive integer [[整除|divisible]] by both. The positive multiples of $4$ are $4,8,12,\ldots$, and those of $6$ are $6,12,18,\ldots$; the first shared value is $12$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个数一定存在，因为 $ab$ 已是正公倍数。不要与[[最大公因数]]混淆：gcd 找一个能整除两个输入的数，lcm 找一个能被两个输入整除的数。两者的计算联系由[[公因公倍乘积]]给出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Such a number exists because $ab$ is already a positive common multiple. Unlike the [[最大公因数|gcd]], which divides both inputs, the lcm is divisible by both. Their computational connection is given by the [[公因公倍乘积|gcd–lcm product identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对互异素数 $p,q$，[[RSA全消息正确性]]用 $\lambda=\operatorname{lcm}(p-1,q-1)$：只要 $\lambda\mid(ed-1)$，$p-1$ 与 $q-1$ 就都整除 $ed-1$，于是可以分别在两个素数模数下恢复消息。这里需要的是一个同时满足两种整除条件的指数步长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For distinct primes $p,q$, [[RSA全消息正确性|RSA's all-message correctness]] uses $\lambda=\operatorname{lcm}(p-1,q-1)$. If $\lambda\mid(ed-1)$, both $p-1$ and $q-1$ divide $ed-1$, allowing recovery modulo each prime. The lcm supplies an exponent increment meeting both divisibility conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
