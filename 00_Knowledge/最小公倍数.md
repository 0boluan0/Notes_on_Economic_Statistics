# Least common multiple
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最小公倍数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine two signals that flash together at time $0$. One repeats every $4$ seconds and the other every $6$ seconds. They next flash together at $12$ seconds. The **least common multiple** finds this first positive common step: a number made from a whole number of steps of either size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两个信号在时刻 $0$ 同时闪烁，一个每隔 $4$ 秒闪一次，另一个每隔 $6$ 秒闪一次。它们下一次同时闪烁是在 $12$ 秒。**最小公倍数**找的就是这个最早的正共同步长：无论按哪一种步长，都能用整数步恰好到达。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive integers $a,b$, $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ is the smallest positive integer [[整除|divisible]] by both. The positive multiples of $4$ are $4,8,12,\ldots$, and those of $6$ are $6,12,18,\ldots$; the first shared value is $12$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $a,b$，$\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ 是同时被 $a,b$ [[整除]]的最小正整数。例如 $4$ 的正倍数是 $4,8,12,\ldots$，$6$ 的正倍数是 $6,12,18,\ldots$，第一个重合点是 $12$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $a\mid m$, read “$a$ divides $m$,” means $m=ak$ for an integer $k$. Thus a common multiple $m$ satisfies both $a\mid m$ and $b\mid m$. In $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$, the letters name a function of the two inputs; they are not extra factors being multiplied. “Least” compares the positive common multiples by their usual numerical order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $a\mid m$ 读作“$a$ 整除 $m$”，意思是存在整数 $k$ 使 $m=ak$。所以公倍数 $m$ 同时满足 $a\mid m$ 与 $b\mid m$。在 $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ 中，这几个字母是两个输入的函数名称，不是额外相乘的因子。“最小”是按通常的数值大小比较所有正公倍数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a least positive value exists
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么最小正值存在

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Such a number exists because $ab$ is already a positive common multiple. Unlike the [[最大公因数|gcd]], which divides both inputs, the lcm is divisible by both. Their computational connection is given by the [[公因公倍乘积|gcd–lcm product identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个数一定存在，因为 $ab$ 已是正公倍数。不要与[[最大公因数]]混淆：gcd 找一个能整除两个输入的数，lcm 找一个能被两个输入整除的数。两者的计算联系由[[公因公倍乘积]]给出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The existence argument can be made finite. Among the integers $1,2,\ldots,ab$, keep those divisible by both $a$ and $b$. This is a nonempty finite set because it contains $ab$, so it has a least member $L$. Any positive common multiple outside this list exceeds $ab$ and therefore cannot be smaller than $L$. Hence $L$ is least among all positive common multiples, and a least value is unique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在性也可以用有限候选说明：在整数 $1,2,\ldots,ab$ 中，保留同时被 $a,b$ 整除的数。这个有限集合包含 $ab$，因此非空，能够选出最小成员 $L$。列表之外的正公倍数都大于 $ab$，不可能比 $L$ 更小。因此 $L$ 是全部正公倍数中的最小值，而且最小值唯一。

> [!example] Check both divisibility and minimality · 同时检查整除与最小性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $4$ and $6$, the positive multiples of $4$ below or equal to $12$ are $4,8,12$. Of these, only $12$ is divisible by $6$: $12=4\cdot3=6\cdot2$. This proves that $12$ works and that no smaller positive candidate works. The product $4\cdot6=24$ is also a common multiple, but it is not the least one. Zero is a common multiple too; the word **positive** excludes it from this definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $4,6$，不超过 $12$ 的正的 $4$ 倍数是 $4,8,12$，其中只有 $12$ 能被 $6$ 整除：$12=4\cdot3=6\cdot2$。这既证明 $12$ 合要求，也排除了更小的正候选。乘积 $4\cdot6=24$ 也是公倍数，但不是最小的；零也同时是两数的倍数，定义中的**正**排除了它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## All common multiples repeat this step
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 全部公倍数都重复这个步长

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive inputs $a,b$, writing $L=\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ gives the useful characterization below. Here $m$ may be any integer, including zero or a negative integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数输入 $a,b$，记 $L=\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$，还可以得到下面的刻画。其中 $m$ 可以是任意整数，包括零或负数。

$$a\mid m\ \text{and}\ b\mid m\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad L\mid m.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One direction is immediate: since $a\mid L$ and $b\mid L$, every integer multiple of $L$ is a multiple of both. For the converse, suppose $a\mid m$ and $b\mid m$. Apply [[带余除法|division with remainder]] using the positive divisor $L$: write $m=qL+r$ with $0\le r<L$. Both $a$ and $b$ divide $m-qL=r$. If $r>0$, it would be a positive common multiple smaller than $L$, contradicting the definition. Thus $r=0$ and $m=qL$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个方向直接成立：因为 $a\mid L$、$b\mid L$，所以 $L$ 的每个整数倍都是两数的公倍数。反过来，设 $a\mid m$ 且 $b\mid m$。以正数 $L$ 为除数，用[[带余除法]]写成 $m=qL+r$，其中 $0\le r<L$。两数都整除差 $m-qL=r$。如果 $r>0$，它就是比 $L$ 更小的正公倍数，与定义矛盾。因此 $r=0$，即 $m=qL$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $4,6$, the positive common multiples are therefore $12,24,36,\ldots$. The same definition gives $\operatorname{lcm}(1,b)=b$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a,a)=a$. More generally, if $a\mid b$, then $b$ is already a common multiple, and any positive multiple of $b$ is at least $b$, so $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$4,6$ 的正公倍数恰好是 $12,24,36,\ldots$。同一定义还给出 $\operatorname{lcm}(1,b)=b$ 和 $\operatorname{lcm}(a,a)=a$。更一般地，若 $a\mid b$，那么 $b$ 已经是公倍数，而 $b$ 的任何正倍数都不小于 $b$，所以 $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=b$。

## Signs, zero and applications
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 符号、零与应用

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition above uses two positive integers. For nonzero integer inputs, one can extend it by taking absolute values: $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=\operatorname{lcm}(|a|,|b|)$, where $|a|$ is the nonnegative magnitude of $a$. This leaves the set of positive common multiples unchanged; for example, $\operatorname{lcm}(-4,6)=12$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的定义以两个正整数为输入。若要扩展到非零整数，可以先取绝对值：$\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=\operatorname{lcm}(|a|,|b|)$，其中 $|a|$ 是 $a$ 的非负大小。这样没有改变正公倍数的集合；例如 $\operatorname{lcm}(-4,6)=12$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If either input is zero, there is no positive common multiple: $0\mid m$ forces $m=0$. A common separate convention sets $\operatorname{lcm}(a,0)=\operatorname{lcm}(0,a)=0$, including $a=0$. That is a nonnegative extension, not a least positive value supplied by the original definition. It also does not assign any value to division by zero. The [[公因公倍乘积|gcd–lcm product identity]] explains how this convention affects its formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某个输入为零，就没有正公倍数，因为 $0\mid m$ 强制 $m=0$。一种常用的另行约定是 $\operatorname{lcm}(a,0)=\operatorname{lcm}(0,a)=0$，也包括 $a=0$。这是非负取值的扩展，不是原定义给出的最小正值，也没有给除以零赋值。[[公因公倍乘积]]会说明这个约定怎样影响恒等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For distinct primes $p,q$, [[RSA全消息正确性|RSA's all-message correctness]] uses $\lambda=\operatorname{lcm}(p-1,q-1)$. If $\lambda\mid(ed-1)$, both $p-1$ and $q-1$ divide $ed-1$, allowing recovery modulo each prime. The lcm supplies an exponent increment meeting both divisibility conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对互异素数 $p,q$，[[RSA全消息正确性]]用 $\lambda=\operatorname{lcm}(p-1,q-1)$：只要 $\lambda\mid(ed-1)$，$p-1$ 与 $q-1$ 就都整除 $ed-1$，于是可以分别在两个素数模数下恢复消息。这里需要的是一个同时满足两种整除条件的指数步长。
