# Simplex
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单纯形*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simplex is the filled geometric object made by joining a suitable set of vertices: two distinct points give a line segment, three noncollinear points give a triangle including its interior, and four noncoplanar points give a solid tetrahedron. The rule for “filling” is precise: take weighted averages of the vertices, with nonnegative weights whose sum is one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单纯形是把一组位置合适的顶点连接并填满得到的几何对象：两个不同点给出线段，三个不共线点给出包含内部的三角形，四个不共面点给出实心四面体。“填满”的规则是取顶点的加权平均，所有权重非负且总和为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Vertices, weights, and dimension

Work in real coordinate space $\mathbb R^m$, with a positive integer $m$. Choose an integer $k$ with $0\le k\le m$ and points $p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_k$. Require the difference vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*顶点、权重与维数*

在实坐标空间 $\mathbb R^m$ 中，$m$ 为正整数。取整数 $0\le k\le m$ 和点 $p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_k$，要求以下差向量满足线性无关：

$$p_1-p_0,\ldots,p_k-p_0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[线性无关|Linear independence]] means that a linear combination of these differences can be zero only when every coefficient is zero. The condition says that each new vertex contributes a new independent direction relative to the first vertex. It is called **affine independence** of the vertices; it is the differences, not the position vectors $p_i$ themselves, that must be independent. For $k=0$ the list of differences is empty and the condition holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[线性无关]]是指这些差向量的线性组合只有在所有系数都为零时才等于零。这里的条件表示，每个新增顶点相对于第一个顶点提供一个新的独立方向；称这些顶点**仿射无关**。要求独立的是差向量，不是顶点的位置向量 $p_i$ 本身。$k=0$ 时差向量列表为空，条件成立。

> [!definition] The filled simplex · 填满的单纯形
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The **$k$-simplex** with these vertices is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 以这些点为顶点的 **$k$ 维单纯形**是
> $$S=\left\{\sum_{i=0}^{k}\theta_i p_i:\ \theta_i\ge0,\ \sum_{i=0}^{k}\theta_i=1\right\}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The real numbers $\theta_i$ are weights. Such a weighted average is a **convex combination**; the set of all these combinations is the **convex hull** of the vertices. The single point $p_0$ is a $0$-simplex.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 实数 $\theta_i$ 是权重。这种加权平均称为**凸组合**；全部凸组合构成顶点的**凸包**。只有一个点 $p_0$ 时，得到 $0$ 维单纯形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum-one condition lets us write the same point as $p_0+\sum_{i=1}^k\theta_i(p_i-p_0)$, with $\theta_i\ge0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^k\theta_i\le1$. Thus the simplex lies in a translated $k$-dimensional linear space. Its dimension is $k$, even if it sits in a larger ambient space $\mathbb R^m$. It is a [[凸集|convex set]]: a weighted average of two points of $S$ again has nonnegative vertex weights summing to one, so the whole segment between the points stays in $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重总和为一，使同一点还可以写成 $p_0+\sum_{i=1}^k\theta_i(p_i-p_0)$，其中 $\theta_i\ge0$、$\sum_{i=1}^k\theta_i\le1$。因此，单纯形位于一个平移后的 $k$ 维线性空间中；即使放在更大的环境 $\mathbb R^m$ 内，它本身仍是 $k$ 维。它也是[[凸集]]：对其中两点再次加权平均，顶点权重仍非负且总和为一，所以两点之间的整条线段都留在集合内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A triangle is more than three edges

Take $p_0=(1,1)$, $p_1=(3,2)$, and $p_2=(2,3)$. Their differences $(2,1)$ and $(1,2)$ are independent: $2a+b=0$ and $a+2b=0$ imply $a=b=0$. We therefore have a $2$-simplex. Weights $(1/2,1/4,1/4)$ give the interior point $(7/4,7/4)$. Setting the weight of $p_0$ to zero gives the edge joining $p_1$ to $p_2$; allowing all the permitted weights fills the entire triangle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*三角形不只是三条边*

取 $p_0=(1,1)$、$p_1=(3,2)$、$p_2=(2,3)$。差向量 $(2,1)$ 与 $(1,2)$ 线性无关：由 $2a+b=0$、$a+2b=0$ 可得 $a=b=0$，所以这里是二维单纯形。权重 $(1/2,1/4,1/4)$ 给出内部点 $(7/4,7/4)$。令 $p_0$ 的权重为零，得到连接 $p_1,p_2$ 的边；让所有合法权重变化，便填出整个三角形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each point has **unique vertex weights**. Indeed, subtract two weighted representations of the same point. Because both weight sums are one, their difference becomes $\sum_{i=1}^k(\theta_i-\eta_i)(p_i-p_0)=0$. Independence forces $\theta_i=\eta_i$ for $i\ge1$, and the sum-one condition also gives $\theta_0=\eta_0$. For $k=0$ the only weight is already one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个点的**顶点权重唯一**。把同一点的两种加权表示相减，利用两组权重都和为一，可得 $\sum_{i=1}^k(\theta_i-\eta_i)(p_i-p_0)=0$。线性无关迫使 $i\ge1$ 时 $\theta_i=\eta_i$，再由总和为一得到 $\theta_0=\eta_0$。$k=0$ 时唯一的权重本来就是一。

> [!warning] Convex hull is not linear span · 凸包不是线性张成
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Unrestricted linear combinations of two independent edge vectors fill a whole plane. The nonnegative, sum-at-most-one coefficients fill only the triangle. Also, three distinct collinear vertices do not define a $2$-simplex under this definition: their convex hull is a segment, and their vertex weights need not be unique.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 两个独立边向量的任意线性组合填满整个平面；非负且总和不超过一的系数只填出三角形。此外，按照这里的定义，三个互异且共线的顶点不能给出二维单纯形：其凸包只是线段，顶点权重也未必唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Standard and probability simplexes

For a positive integer $n$, the standard $n$-simplex has vertices $0,e_1,\ldots,e_n$ in $\mathbb R^n$, where $e_i$ has a one in coordinate $i$ and zeros elsewhere. Its points satisfy $t_i\ge0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^n t_i\le1$; the unused weight $1-\sum_i t_i$ belongs to the zero vertex.

By contrast, probability vectors on $N\ge1$ outcomes form $\{p\in\mathbb R^N:p_i\ge0,\sum_i p_i=1\}$, whose vertices are $e_1,\ldots,e_N$. This is an **$(N-1)$-simplex**, not an $N$-dimensional filled region: the differences $e_1-e_N,\ldots,e_{N-1}-e_N$ are independent, and the sum constraint leaves $N-1$ directions. For $N=1$ it is the point $(1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准单纯形与概率单纯形*

对正整数 $n$，标准 $n$ 维单纯形的顶点是 $\mathbb R^n$ 中的 $0,e_1,\ldots,e_n$，其中 $e_i$ 只有第 $i$ 个坐标为一，其余为零。集合中的点满足 $t_i\ge0$、$\sum_{i=1}^n t_i\le1$；剩余权重 $1-\sum_i t_i$ 分配给零顶点。

相比之下，$N\ge1$ 种结果的概率向量构成 $\{p\in\mathbb R^N:p_i\ge0,\sum_i p_i=1\}$，顶点是 $e_1,\ldots,e_N$。这是 **$N-1$ 维单纯形**，不是填满 $N$ 维空间的区域：差向量 $e_1-e_N,\ldots,e_{N-1}-e_N$ 线性无关，总和约束留下 $N-1$ 个方向。$N=1$ 时它就是点 $(1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A $k$-simplex with $k<m$ has zero ambient $m$-dimensional volume, although it can have positive intrinsic $k$-dimensional volume—for example, a triangle in $\mathbb R^3$ has area but zero three-dimensional volume. The square [[单纯形行列式体积|determinant volume formula]] applies when the number of independent edge directions equals the ambient dimension; for $1\le k<m$, its intrinsic $k$-volume can instead be measured in orthonormal coordinates of the translated $k$-dimensional space described above, or with a [[矩形Jacobian体积因子|Gram volume factor]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $k<m$，$k$ 维单纯形的环境 $m$ 维体积为零，但自身的 $k$ 维体积可以为正，例如 $\mathbb R^3$ 中的三角形有面积，却没有三维体积。方阵的[[单纯形行列式体积|行列式体积公式]]适用于独立边方向数等于环境维数的情形；当 $1\le k<m$ 时，自身的 $k$ 维体积可改在上述平移后的 $k$ 维空间的标准正交坐标中测量，或使用[[矩形Jacobian体积因子|Gram 体积因子]]。
