# Multivariable chain rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多元链式法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **multivariable chain rule** follows a change through successive functions. An input first changes some intermediate quantities; those quantities then change the output. Each differentiable stage has a [[全微分|linear approximation]]. Composing these approximations gives the first-order response of the whole calculation. When one input reaches the output through several intermediate quantities, all those contributions must be added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**多元链式法则**追踪变化怎样经过前后相接的函数：输入先改变一些中间量，中间量再改变输出。每个可微阶段都有一个[[全微分|线性近似]]，把它们复合，就得到整个计算的一阶响应。如果同一个输入通过几个中间量影响输出，就要把这些路径的贡献全部相加。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let one parameter $t$ determine $u=t$ and $v=t^2$, and let the output be $f(u,v)=uv$. A change in $t$ changes both factors in the product. We will first state the general matrix rule, then use this example to see exactly where its two contributions come from.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一个参数 $t$ 同时决定 $u=t$ 与 $v=t^2$，输出为 $f(u,v)=uv$。改变 $t$ 会同时改变乘积的两个因子。下面先写一般矩阵规则，再用这个例子看清两个贡献分别从哪里来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compose derivatives at the correct base points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在正确的基点复合导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ and $V\subseteq\mathbb R^m$ be [[开集|open sets]]. Let $g:U\to V$ be differentiable at $a\in U$, and let $f:V\to\mathbb R^p$ be differentiable at $b=g(a)$. Here $n,m,p$ are the positive numbers of input, intermediate and output coordinates. The composite $f\circ g$ means $x\mapsto f(g(x))$. Then it is differentiable at $a$ and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$、$V\subseteq\mathbb R^m$ 都是[[开集]]，$g:U\to V$ 在 $a\in U$ 可微，$f:V\to\mathbb R^p$ 在 $b=g(a)$ 可微。正整数 $n,m,p$ 分别是输入、中间量和输出的坐标数。复合 $f\circ g$ 表示 $x\mapsto f(g(x))$。那么它在 $a$ 可微，并且

$$D(f\circ g)(a)=Df(g(a))\circ Dg(a),\qquad
\boxed{J_{f\circ g}(a)=J_f(g(a))J_g(a).}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first formula composes linear maps; the second uses their [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian matrices]] in standard coordinates. Evaluate the inner derivative at the original input $a$ and the outer derivative at the corresponding intermediate value $g(a)$. Differentiability at those two points is sufficient; continuity of the derivatives in a whole neighborhood is not an additional requirement of this theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一式复合的是线性映射；第二式使用标准坐标中的[[Jacobian矩阵]]。内层导数在原输入 $a$ 计算，外层导数在对应的中间值 $g(a)$ 计算。这两个点处的可微性已经足够；定理不额外要求导数在整个邻域内连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the matrix product has this order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么矩阵乘法正是所需操作

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An input step $h$ first becomes the predicted intermediate step $J_g(a)h$ and then the predicted output step $J_f(g(a))[J_g(a)h]$. The [[矩阵乘法|matrix product]] records that order of action: its rightmost factor acts first. The dimensions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入步长 $h$ 先变成预测的中间增量 $J_g(a)h$，再变成预测的输出增量 $J_f(g(a))[J_g(a)h]$。[[矩阵乘法]]记录的正是这个作用顺序：最右边的因子先作用。尺寸关系为

$$\underbrace{J_f(g(a))}_{p\times m}\;
\underbrace{J_g(a)}_{m\times n}
=\underbrace{J_{f\circ g}(a)}_{p\times n}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a map from three inputs to two intermediate quantities and then one output, the multiplication is $(1\times2)(2\times3)=(1\times3)$. Reversing it is not defined. Even when both stages are square, reversing them usually gives a different map; equality in a special commuting case does not change the composition rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若三个输入先变成两个中间量，再得到一个输出，乘法就是 $(1\times2)(2\times3)=(1\times3)$，反过来没有定义。即使两个阶段都是方阵，交换次序通常也会得到不同映射；特殊情况下两个矩阵可交换，不会改变复合的规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To justify the rule beyond a formal approximation, write $A=J_g(a)$ and $B=J_f(b)$. Let the actual intermediate change be $k=g(a+h)-b$. Differentiability gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了证明这不只是形式近似，记 $A=J_g(a)$、$B=J_f(b)$，把真实中间增量记为 $k=g(a+h)-b$。可微性给出

$$k=Ah+r_g(h),\qquad f(b+k)-f(b)=Bk+r_f(k),$$
$$\frac{\|r_g(h)\|_2}{\|h\|_2}\to0,\qquad
\frac{\|r_f(k)\|_2}{\|k\|_2}\to0\quad(k\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fixed matrices have finite stretch bounds: $\|Ah\|_2\le C_A\|h\|_2$ and $\|Bz\|_2\le C_B\|z\|_2$ for constants $C_A,C_B$. For sufficiently small nonzero $h$, the first remainder ratio is at most $1$, so $\|k\|_2\le(C_A+1)\|h\|_2$. In particular $k\to0$. Substitution now gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定矩阵都有有限的伸缩界：存在常数 $C_A,C_B$，使 $\|Ah\|_2\le C_A\|h\|_2$、$\|Bz\|_2\le C_B\|z\|_2$。非零 $h$ 足够小时，第一个余项商不超过 $1$，所以 $\|k\|_2\le(C_A+1)\|h\|_2$，特别地 $k\to0$。代入后得到

$$f(g(a+h))-f(g(a))=BAh+Br_g(h)+r_f(k).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After division by $\|h\|_2$, the first error is bounded by $C_B\|r_g(h)\|_2/\|h\|_2\to0$. If $k\ne0$, the second is bounded by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

除以 $\|h\|_2$ 后，第一个误差不超过 $C_B\|r_g(h)\|_2/\|h\|_2\to0$。当 $k\ne0$ 时，第二个误差不超过

$$\frac{\|r_f(k)\|_2}{\|k\|_2}(C_A+1)\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $k=0$, then $r_f(0)=f(b)-f(b)-B0=0$, so this error is already zero and no division by $\|k\|_2$ is needed. Both errors are negligible relative to the original step. That proves differentiability of the composition with derivative matrix $BA$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $k=0$，则 $r_f(0)=f(b)-f(b)-B0=0$，这一误差已经为零，无需除以 $\|k\|_2$。两个误差相对于原始步长都可忽略，由此证明复合函数可微，导数矩阵为 $BA$。

## Coordinates and the special case of a path
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 坐标公式与路径特例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one output, write the outer function as $f(z_1,\ldots,z_m)$ and the intermediate coordinates as $z_j=g_j(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$. For input $x_i$, the row–column product says
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当只有一个输出时，把外层函数写成 $f(z_1,\ldots,z_m)$，中间坐标写成 $z_j=g_j(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$。对于输入 $x_i$，行列相乘的公式是

$$\frac{\partial(f\circ g)}{\partial x_i}(a)
=\sum_{j=1}^m\frac{\partial f}{\partial z_j}(g(a))
\frac{\partial g_j}{\partial x_i}(a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $j$ lists every intermediate route from input $i$ to the output. In the outer partial derivative, the other $z$ arguments are held fixed. In the derivative of the composite, all $z_j$ that depend on $x_i$ can change; the sum accounts for that difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指标 $j$ 遍历输入 $i$ 通向输出的每条中间路径。计算外层偏导时，其余 $z$ 自变量保持固定；计算复合函数的导数时，所有依赖 $x_i$ 的 $z_j$ 都可能改变，这个求和正是在处理两者的区别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the input is a single parameter $t$ and $g(t)$ is a differentiable path into $\mathbb R^m$ (the planar case is a [[参数曲线|parametric curve]]), its derivative $g'(t)$ is a column of component velocities. For a differentiable scalar $f$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若输入只有一个参数 $t$，$g(t)$ 是进入 $\mathbb R^m$ 的可微路径（平面情形就是[[参数曲线]]），则 $g'(t)$ 是各分量速度排成的一列。对可微实值函数 $f$，

$$\frac{d}{dt}f(g(t))=\nabla f(g(t))^Tg'(t).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the gradient is the column of outer partials, and $T$ transposes it to a row. On the line $g(t)=a+tv$, this gives the [[方向导数梯度公式|gradient formula for directional derivatives]], under differentiability of $f$. The [[方向导数|line derivative definition]] by itself does not supply that hypothesis. The same composition rule also differentiates identities along an already differentiable implicit solution branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里梯度是外层偏导组成的列，$T$ 把它转成行。取直线 $g(t)=a+tv$，在 $f$ 可微的前提下便得到[[方向导数梯度公式]]；单独的[[方向导数|直线导数定义]]并不提供这一假设。同一个复合规则也可对已经可微的隐式解支所满足的恒等式求导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Example: identify the dependency map before multiplying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 例子：先画出依赖关系再相乘

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to $g(t)=(t,t^2)^T$ and $f(u,v)=uv$. The inner derivative is a $2\times1$ column and the outer derivative a $1\times2$ row:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到 $g(t)=(t,t^2)^T$ 与 $f(u,v)=uv$。内层导数是 $2\times1$ 列，外层导数是 $1\times2$ 行：

$$J_g(t)=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2t\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_f(u,v)=\begin{pmatrix}v&u\end{pmatrix},$$
$$\frac{d}{dt}f(g(t))=\begin{pmatrix}t^2&t\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}1\\2t\end{pmatrix}=t^2+2t^2=3t^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first contribution is the change through $u$; the second is the change through $v$. Direct substitution gives $f(g(t))=t^3$, whose derivative agrees. At $t=1$, the total rate is $3$, whereas the outer partial $\partial f/\partial u$ at $(1,1)$ is $1$. These answer different questions: the latter freezes $v$, while the former lets $v=t^2$ move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一个贡献经过 $u$，第二个经过 $v$。直接代入得到 $f(g(t))=t^3$，其导数与上式一致。在 $t=1$ 处，总变化率为 $3$，而 $(1,1)$ 处的外层偏导 $\partial f/\partial u$ 为 $1$。二者回答不同问题：后者固定 $v$，前者允许 $v=t^2$ 随之变化。

> [!warning] Follow the actual dependencies · 按真实依赖关系求导
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A reused variable name does not make an indirect path disappear. If an argument depends on the input, include that path; if it is truly held fixed, do not invent a path for it. Write the intermediate map first, then evaluate each derivative at its own base point. This also organizes economic comparative statics and the multiple paths in backpropagation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 重复使用变量名字，不会让间接路径消失。某个自变量若依赖输入，就要把该路径计入；若它确实固定，就不能凭空添加路径。先写出中间映射，再在各自基点计算导数。经济比较静态与反向传播中的多条路径，也依照同一个原则组织。
