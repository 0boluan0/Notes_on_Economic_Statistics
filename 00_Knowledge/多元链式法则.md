# 复合映射的 Jacobian 等于外层 Jacobian 与内层 Jacobian 的矩阵乘积，先作用的内层导数写在右边
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Jacobian of a composite map is the product of the outer and inner Jacobians, with the derivative that acts first written on the right*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 链式法则连接两个局部线性映射
> 设 $g:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ 在 $a$ 可微，$f:\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^p$ 在 $g(a)$ 可微，则 $f\circ g$ 在 $a$ 可微，而且
> $$
> J_{f\circ g}(a)=J_f(g(a))J_g(a).
> $$
> $J_g(a)$ 先把输入扰动送入中间空间，$J_f(g(a))$ 再把中间扰动送入输出空间，所以内层 Jacobian 写在右边。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $g$ is differentiable at $a$ and $f$ is differentiable at $g(a)$, the Jacobian of their composition is the displayed matrix product. The inner Jacobian first maps an input perturbation into the intermediate space, and the outer Jacobian then maps it into the output space; matrix order records that order of action.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么矩阵乘法正是所需操作
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why matrix multiplication is exactly the required operation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

局部线性化给出
$$
g(a+h)-g(a)=J_g(a)h+o(\|h\|).
$$
把这个中间变化代入 $f$ 的线性近似，主导项成为 $J_f(g(a))J_g(a)h$。链式法则不是需要死记的偏导表格，而是“复合线性映射的矩阵等于矩阵乘积”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first approximation sends $h$ to the intermediate perturbation shown above. Applying the outer linear approximation makes the leading output change $J_f(g(a))J_g(a)h$. The chain rule is therefore the matrix representation of composing two derivative maps, rather than an isolated bookkeeping formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维度同时给出检查：
$$
\underbrace{J_f(g(a))}_{p\times m}
\underbrace{J_g(a)}_{m\times n}
=\underbrace{J_{f\circ g}(a)}_{p\times n}.
$$
如果中间的 $m$ 不能相消，变量顺序或 Jacobian 排列一定有误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed dimensions provide an immediate diagnostic. The intermediate dimension $m$ must match. If it does not, either the order of multiplication or the Jacobian convention is wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 坐标公式与路径特例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The coordinate formula and the path special case*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f:\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R$，$g=(g_1,\ldots,g_m)$，则对输入坐标 $x_i$，
$$
\frac{\partial(f\circ g)}{\partial x_i}(a)
=\sum_{j=1}^m
\frac{\partial f}{\partial z_j}(g(a))
\frac{\partial g_j}{\partial x_i}(a).
$$
当输入只有一个参数 $t$ 时，这变成沿路径 $g(t)$ 的导数
$$
\frac{d}{dt}f(g(t))=\nabla f(g(t))^Tg'(t).
$$
沿直线限制所需的对象由 [[方向导数]] 定义；在函数可微时，这个路径特例给出 [[方向导数梯度公式]]，也用于对隐式恒等式求导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a scalar-valued outer function, the first displayed equation is the coordinate form of the same matrix product. With a one-dimensional input path, it becomes the second displayed identity. The line restriction is defined in [[方向导数|directional derivative]], while differentiability turns this path rule into the [[方向导数梯度公式|gradient formula for directional derivatives]] and later differentiates implicit identities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 例子：先画出依赖关系再相乘
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Example: identify the dependency map before multiplying*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $g(t)=(t,t^2)^T$，$f(u,v)=uv$。于是
$$
J_f(u,v)=\begin{pmatrix}v&u\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
J_g(t)=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2t\end{pmatrix},
$$
所以
$$
\frac{d}{dt}f(g(t))
=\begin{pmatrix}t^2&t\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}1\\2t\end{pmatrix}
=3t^2.
$$
直接代入得到 $f(g(t))=t^3$，导数相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inner map produces two intermediate variables, both of which depend on $t$. The matrix product therefore adds both routes from $t$ to the output and gives $3t^2$, agreeing with direct differentiation of $t^3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] “偏导”与“总导数”取决于依赖结构
> 若表达式中的另一个变量本身依赖 $x$，对 $x$ 求导时必须把这条间接路径计入链式法则。把它错误地当成固定参数，会漏掉一项；反过来，在真正固定该变量的偏导中又不应擅自加入间接项。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Whether a term contributes depends on the dependency structure. If another argument itself depends on $x$, differentiating with respect to $x$ must include that indirect path. Treating it as fixed drops a term, while inventing an indirect term in a genuine partial derivative is equally wrong.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检：为什么顺序不能反过来？
> 在一般维度下，为什么不能写 $J_g(a)J_f(g(a))$？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can the product not generally be written in the reverse order $J_g(a)J_f(g(a))$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** $J_g$ 必须先作用于原输入，$J_f$ 才能接收中间空间中的结果；反向乘积通常维度不匹配，即使偶然都是方阵也表示错误的复合次序。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** $J_g$ must first act on the original input so that $J_f$ can receive a vector in the intermediate space. The reverse product is usually dimensionally invalid and, even when both matrices happen to be square, represents the wrong order of composition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
