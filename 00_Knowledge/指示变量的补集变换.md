# Complement indicators
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*补事件的指示变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To change an indicator from “the event happens” to “the event does not happen”, subtract its record from one. This switches $1$ to $0$ and $0$ to $1$. It is the numerical version of reversing a yes-or-no answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把指示变量从“事件发生”改成“事件没有发生”，只需用一减去原记录：$1$ 变成 $0$，$0$ 变成 $1$。这就是把“是或否”的答案反转后的数值写法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an event, and let $I_A$ be its [[事件指示变量|indicator]], which equals one on $A$ and zero elsewhere. The [[补事件法|complement]] $A^c$ consists of all outcomes in the same sample space that are outside $A$. The superscript $c$ names the complement; it is not an exponent. Write $I_{A^c}$ for its indicator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是一个事件，$I_A$ 是它的[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]：属于 $A$ 的结果记为一，其余记为零。[[补事件法|补事件]] $A^c$ 包含同一个样本空间中所有不属于 $A$ 的结果；右上角 $c$ 表示补集，不是乘方。用 $I_{A^c}$ 表示补事件的指示变量。

> [!abstract] Flip the binary record · 反转零一记录
> $$I_{A^c}=1-I_A.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This holds for each individual outcome: when $A$ happens, both sides are zero; when $A$ does not happen, both sides are one. It is an identity between random variables, before taking any average.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个结果，这个等式都成立：$A$ 发生时两边都是零，$A$ 没有发生时两边都是一。这是随机变量之间的恒等式，尚未进行任何平均运算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a six-sided die, let $A$ mean “greater than four”, so $A=\{5,6\}$ and $A^c=\{1,2,3,4\}$. Write $\omega$ for the die result. The transformation is visible across all six possible outcomes, whether or not the die is fair:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一枚六面骰，令 $A$ 表示“大于四”，则 $A=\{5,6\}$，$A^c=\{1,2,3,4\}$。用 $\omega$ 表示骰子结果。无论骰子是否公平，六个结果上的记录都按下表反转：

| $\omega$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ | $6$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $I_A(\omega)$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1$ |
| $1-I_A(\omega)$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| $I_{A^c}(\omega)$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $\omega=6$, the original record is one and the complement record is zero; at $\omega=3$, they are zero and one. The last two rows agree because they answer the same question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\omega=6$ 时，原记录为一、补事件记录为零；$\omega=3$ 时则分别为零和一。最后两行相同，因为它们回答的是同一个问题。

## From records to probabilities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从记录值过渡到概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking [[期望|expectations]] averages the pointwise identity. By [[期望线性性|linearity]] and the [[指示变量期望|indicator-expectation rule]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取[[期望]]，就是把逐结果恒等式两边分别平均。由[[期望线性性]]与[[指示变量期望]]规则，

$$P(A^c)=E[I_{A^c}]=E[1-I_A]=1-E[I_A]=1-P(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fair die, $P(A)=2/6=1/3$, so $P(A^c)=1-1/3=2/3$. The pointwise identity explains how the records relate; the probability formula explains how their averages relate. Both work without an independence assumption. The indicator identity even needs no choice of probability weights on the outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若骰子公平，$P(A)=2/6=1/3$，于是 $P(A^c)=1-1/3=2/3$。逐结果恒等式说明记录值怎样对应，概率公式说明这些记录的平均怎样对应。两者都不要求独立；指示变量恒等式甚至还不需要为各结果指定概率权重。

> [!warning] Complementing is different from negating the number · 取补事件不同于给记录加负号
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The value $-I_A$ is either zero or minus one, so it does not record the complement. The operation must be $1-I_A$. Also check the event itself: the complement of “greater than four” includes four, so it is “at most four”, not “less than four”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $-I_A$ 只能是零或负一，不能记录补事件；所需运算是 $1-I_A$。还要检查事件本身：“大于四”的补事件包含四，所以是“不大于四”，而不是“小于四”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Applying the operation twice returns the original record: $1-(1-I_A)=I_A$. This matches the fact that the complement of the complement is $A$. When several events are involved, first identify the whole event being complemented; the [[指示变量的并事件恒等式|union indicator identity]] handles “at least one occurs”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续反转两次会回到原记录：$1-(1-I_A)=I_A$，对应“补集的补集仍是 $A$”。涉及多个事件时，要先确认整个被取补的事件是什么；“至少一个发生”对应的记录由[[指示变量的并事件恒等式|并事件指示恒等式]]说明。
