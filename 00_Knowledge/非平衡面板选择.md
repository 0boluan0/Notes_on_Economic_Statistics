# 面板不平衡本身不必然致偏但与冲击相关的进入退出或缺失会选择估计样本

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Panel imbalance does not itself imply bias, but entry, exit, or missingness related to shocks selects the estimation sample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> FE、RE 或 CRE 可以在单位拥有不同 $T_i$ 时计算；真正的边界是观测机制。选择可以依赖已观察协变量和时间不变异质性，但若在给定这些信息后仍系统性依赖当期 idiosyncratic shock $u_{it}$，当前估计样本中的目标矩条件通常不再代表原目标总体。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> FE, RE, and CRE can be computed when units have different $T_i$ values. The substantive boundary is the observation mechanism. Selection may depend on observed covariates and time-invariant heterogeneity, but if it still depends systematically on the current idiosyncratic shock $u_{it}$ after conditioning on that information, moments in the observed estimation sample generally need not represent the original target population.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

去均值只处理已观察数据中的加性单位效应，不会让消失的结果重新出现，也不会阻断“冲击导致退出”这条路径。因此应分别说明目标总体、进入估计样本的规则，以及在该规则下为何仍可相信所需条件。DID 中处理导致样本构成变化的专门版本见 [[DID 的样本构成变化]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Demeaning handles additive unit effects in observed data; it neither recovers missing outcomes nor blocks a shock-to-attrition path. The target population, the rule determining estimation-sample entry, and the assumptions that survive that rule must therefore be stated separately. The DID-specific version is retained in the existing composition-change atom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> “软件允许 unbalanced panel”为什么不能回答退出是否致偏？
>
> **答案：** 软件能力只说明不同 $T_i$ 可以参与计算；偏误取决于谁因什么原因被观察，而不是数据能否排成面板。
