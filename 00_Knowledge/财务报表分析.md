# 财务报表分析是把报表数据与经营背景转化为支持财务决策的信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial statement analysis turns reported data and business context into information for financial decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务报表分析从一个决策问题出发，结合[[财务报表]]、附注及经营背景，判断企业的财务状况、经营成果、现金生成和未来资金需要。它既包含计算，也包含对数字口径、比较基准和经济原因的解释；算出比率并不是分析的终点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial statement analysis starts with a decision question and uses [[财务报表|financial statements]], notes, and business context to assess financial position, performance, cash generation, and future funding needs. Calculations must be interpreted in light of definitions, benchmarks, and economic causes; producing a ratio is not the endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可依次问：企业靠什么业务赚钱、会计如何反映业务、报表表现发生了什么、这些变化对未来意味着什么。这分别对应战略、会计、财务和前景四个分析视角，并不要求每次分析都机械写四份报告。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask how the business earns money, how accounting represents its activities, what changed in the reported results, and what those changes imply for the future. These are strategic, accounting, financial, and prospective perspectives, not a requirement to write four separate reports every time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如销售增长而经营现金流下降，先核对赊销、回款及存货占用，再判断是扩张带来的暂时占款还是回收问题。不能只凭一个差额判定增长失败或报表造假。计算前使用[[财务比率口径匹配]]，比较时使用[[财务报表比较分析]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If sales rise while operating cash flow falls, examine credit sales, collections, and inventory before deciding whether the cash absorption reflects expansion or collection problems. One gap does not establish failed growth or fraud. Use [[财务比率口径匹配|ratio-basis checks]] before calculating and [[财务报表比较分析|comparative analysis]] when choosing benchmarks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
