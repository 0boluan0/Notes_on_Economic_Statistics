# 财政支出的功能经济与行政分类分别说明为何支出怎样支出和由谁负责
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Functional, economic and administrative classifications identify the purpose, nature and responsible institution of expenditure.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一笔 [[财政支出]] 可以沿几个维度观察。功能分类问“用于什么公共目的”，经济分类问“发生了什么类型的交易”，行政分类问“哪个政府或单位负责”。它们不是互相排斥的三类支出，而是同一笔支出的不同坐标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same [[财政支出|fiscal expenditure]] has several coordinates: its public purpose, the nature of the transaction and the responsible government or institution. These are different dimensions, not three mutually exclusive spending categories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以某县公立中学给教师支付当月工资为例：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a county public secondary school paying its teachers for the current month:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 维度 | 本例归属 | 回答的问题 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 功能 | 教育 | 钱用于哪项公共职能？ |
| 经济性质 | 工资报酬 | 钱付出了什么、换来了什么？ |
| 行政 | 该县及其相应教育预算单位 | 谁负责安排、使用和报告？ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The functional classification is education; the economic category is employee compensation; the administrative coordinate is the county and the relevant education budget unit. A transaction can carry all three classifications simultaneously.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，不能把“教育支出 + 工资支出 + 地方支出”相加求总额：这会把同一笔钱算三次。比较不同教育项目的交易方式时，再区分 [[购买支出与转移支出]]、[[经常支出与资本支出]]。同一功能既可能含经常支出，也可能含资本支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding education expenditure, wages and local expenditure would count the same payment three times. To compare transactions within education, distinguish [[购买支出与转移支出|purchases from transfers]] and [[经常支出与资本支出|current from capital expenditure]]. One function can include both current and capital spending.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程中的“中央/地方”是政府层级；行政分类还可以继续细分到部门、机构和责任单位。跨国对照要检查各国分类与统一功能分类的对应，不能只凭科目名称相似就认为范围相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Central versus local identifies the government level; administrative classifications can extend to ministries, agencies and responsible units. International comparisons require a mapping of classifications, not merely similar category names.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
