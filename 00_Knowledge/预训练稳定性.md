# 在长周期大模型训练中，预训练稳定性由优化轨迹有效性和训练作业连续性两条独立状态轴共同刻画
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In long-running large-model training, pretraining stability is jointly characterized by optimization-trajectory validity and training-job continuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> **预训练稳定性**是对训练过程能否持续产生可用更新并按计划推进的运行性质。它至少要分成两条状态轴：**优化轨迹有效性**问“当前参数更新是否仍在一条可继续的轨迹上”；**训练作业连续性**问“计算、通信和状态恢复是否让作业按预期继续前进”。一条轴的状态不能代替另一条。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Pretraining stability** is the operating property of a training process that can keep producing usable updates and make planned progress. It has at least two distinct axes. **Optimization-trajectory validity** asks whether current parameter updates remain on a trajectory that can be continued. **Training-job continuity** asks whether compute, communication, and state recovery let the job keep advancing as intended. The state of one axis cannot stand in for the other.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 优化轨迹有效性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Optimization-trajectory validity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条轴判断的不是进程还在不在，而是当前 checkpoint 及其后续更新是否仍然有效。一次短暂的 loss spike 可能自行恢复，不必然就是发散；反过来，进程没有报错也不证明优化轨迹健康。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This axis does not ask whether the process is still alive. It asks whether the current checkpoint and subsequent updates remain usable. A brief loss spike may recover and need not be a divergence. Conversely, a process that has not crashed may still be following an invalid optimization trajectory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断时应同时看训练与验证 loss、非有限数、loss scaler、gradient norm、activation norm、参数范数以及干预后的趋势。可以把状态记为 `healthy`、`spike-under-observation`、`recovering` 或 `diverged`，但每个标签都要带着观测依据，而不是只凭单个阈值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The judgment should combine training and validation loss, non-finite values, the loss scaler, gradient and activation norms, parameter norms, and the trend after intervention. States may be recorded as `healthy`, `spike-under-observation`, `recovering`, or `diverged`, but every label needs observational evidence rather than a single context-free threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 训练作业连续性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Training-job continuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条轴记录作业是否在运行、为什么停止，以及恢复后是否从一个完整状态继续。它可以标为 `running`、`planned-stop`、`unexpected-stop`、`restoring` 或 `resumed`。作业连续不等于从不中断；可预期、可恢复且没有静默丢失状态或数据进度的中断，仍可以属于可靠的运行过程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This axis records whether the job is running, why it stopped, and whether it resumes from a complete state. Useful states include `running`, `planned-stop`, `unexpected-stop`, `restoring`, and `resumed`. Continuity does not mean zero interruptions. A predictable, recoverable interruption that does not silently lose training state or data position can still belong to a reliable operating process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它的证据来自作业退出原因、GPU 与主机故障、网络和存储日志、计划内维护、checkpoint 新鲜度、恢复耗时，以及重启后是否重复或跳过了 token。只有“重启了多少次”还不足以说明恢复质量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its evidence comes from job exit reasons, GPU and host failures, network and storage logs, planned maintenance, checkpoint freshness, recovery latency, and whether the resumed run repeats or skips tokens. A restart count alone does not describe recovery quality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两条轴怎样组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How the two axes combine*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 优化轨迹 | 作业状态 | 正确解读 |
|---|---|---|
| 健康 | 运行 | 训练正常推进 |
| 健康 | 意外停止 | 系统可靠性事故；不因此判定优化发散 |
| 发散 | 运行 | 进程存活，但参数更新已不可继续信任 |
| 恢复中 | 重启中 | 优化干预与系统恢复同时发生，应分开记录 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Optimization trajectory | Job state | Correct interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy | Running | Training is progressing normally |
| Healthy | Unexpected stop | A reliability incident; it does not establish optimization divergence |
| Diverged | Running | The process is alive, but its parameter updates are no longer trustworthy |
| Recovering | Restarting | An optimization intervention and a systems recovery are occurring together and should be logged separately |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一事件可以共用一个 incident ID，但不能把两条轴压成一个“不稳定次数”。例如，loss 持续恶化后回滚 500 step，同时更换了故障 GPU：优化记录要写轨迹何时失效、从哪个 checkpoint 恢复；系统记录要写哪个组件失效、作业停顿了多久。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One event may share an incident ID across both axes, but the axes must not be collapsed into a single “instability count.” If persistently worsening loss leads to a 500-step rollback while a failed GPU is replaced, the optimization record should state when the trajectory became invalid and which checkpoint restored it; the systems record should state which component failed and how long the job was unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小记录与判定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum record and decision procedure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每条稳定性事件至少保留：时间、global step 与已处理 token、checkpoint、loss 和关键 norm、非有限数与 loss scaler、作业退出原因、故障组件、计划/意外标记、回滚距离、重复或跳过的数据范围、恢复耗时和人工干预。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each stability incident should retain at least the time, global step and processed tokens, checkpoint, loss and key norms, non-finite values and loss scaling, job exit reason, failed component, planned or unexpected status, rollback distance, repeated or skipped data, recovery latency, and manual intervention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判定时分别回答四个问题：轨迹是否仍有效？作业是否仍在推进？若有中断，恢复的状态是否完整？介入后的趋势是否证明恢复成功？四个答案都有证据，才能说清一次预训练事故。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Judge the axes by answering four separate questions: Is the trajectory still valid? Is the job still advancing? If it stopped, was complete state restored? Does the post-intervention trend show that recovery succeeded? An incident is interpretable only when all four answers have evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一次 GPU 故障后，作业从健康 checkpoint 自动恢复，loss 延续中断前的趋势。它在两条轴上应怎样记录？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A GPU failure is followed by automatic recovery from a healthy checkpoint, and loss continues its previous trend. How should the event be recorded on the two axes?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 优化轨迹记为健康；作业连续性记为意外停止后成功恢复。重启是系统事件，不是发散证据。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Record the optimization trajectory as healthy and the job as successfully resumed after an unexpected stop. The restart is a systems event, not evidence of divergence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
