# 观察单位是研究中被编号并记录变量的对象

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An observational unit is the entity indexed and measured in a study*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 观察单位（unit）是下标 $i$ 所指的对象，例如个人、家庭或学校。每个单位可有多个变量；变量个数不等于单位个数。分配单位、测量单位与分析单位可能不同，必须分别说明。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An observational unit is the entity represented by index $i$. A unit can have many measured variables. Treatment assignment, measurement, and analysis can operate at different levels, which must be distinguished.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

EC400 的学校补习例以 school-grade cell（学校—年级单元）说明分配。把每个学生的测量当作独立分配，会误读样本中的独立信息，见[[观测行协方差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The EC400 example assigns tutoring to school-grade cells. Treating every pupil measurement as an independently assigned unit misrepresents the information structure; see [[观测行协方差|covariance across observational rows]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
