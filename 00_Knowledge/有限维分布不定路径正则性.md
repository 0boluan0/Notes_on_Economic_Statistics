# Finite-dimensional laws and path regularity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有限维分布与路径正则性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two continuous-time processes can agree in every finite collection of observations and still have different kinds of sample paths. The subtlety is that we choose those observation times in advance. A defect at a random time can escape every fixed finite inspection, while appearing on every complete path. This is why a claim about continuous paths needs a path-space or version argument in addition to a specification of finite-dimensional laws.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个连续时间过程可以在每组有限观测上有相同分布，却具有不同类型的样本路径。关键在于，这些观测时点是事先固定的。出现在随机时刻的缺陷，可以躲过每次固定的有限检查，却出现在每一条完整路径中。因此，在指定有限维分布之外，声称路径连续还需要路径空间或版本方面的论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[样本路径|sample path]] fixes one outcome $\omega$ and follows $t\mapsto X_t(\omega)$ across all times. [[有限维分布|Finite-dimensional laws]] instead fix a finite list of times and describe their observations jointly. A path is [[一点连续|continuous]] when nearby times have nearby values at every point of its domain, using the appropriate one-sided interpretation at an endpoint. This statement concerns the whole function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条[[样本路径]]固定一个结果 $\omega$，沿所有时刻观察 $t\mapsto X_t(\omega)$。[[有限维分布]]则固定有限个时点，描述这些观测的联合分布。路径[[一点连续|连续]]，意味着在定义域的每一点，足够接近的时间具有足够接近的数值；端点使用相应的单侧理解。这是在对整个函数提出要求。

> [!example] A spike at a random time · 随机时刻的尖峰
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,1]$, choose $U$ from the [[连续均匀分布|uniform distribution]] and define, for every $t\in[0,1]$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上按[[连续均匀分布]]选取 $U$，并对每个 $t\in[0,1]$ 定义：
>
> $$X_t=0,\qquad Y_t=\mathbf1_{\{t=U\}}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] is $1$ exactly at the selected time and $0$ otherwise. For any fixed finite times $t_1,\ldots,t_n$, a uniform draw hits that finite set with probability zero. Consequently $(Y_{t_1},\ldots,Y_{t_n})=(0,\ldots,0)$ with probability one, exactly as for $X$. Their entire finite-dimensional families agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[事件指示变量|指示量]]只在选中的时刻等于 $1$，其余时刻都为 $0$。对任何事先固定的有限时点 $t_1,\ldots,t_n$，均匀抽值命中这个有限集合的概率为零。因此 $(Y_{t_1},\ldots,Y_{t_n})=(0,\ldots,0)$ 以概率一成立，与 $X$ 完全相同。它们的全部有限维分布族一致。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Now fix the outcome $U=u$. The $X$ path stays at zero. The $Y$ path is $1$ at $u$ but $0$ arbitrarily close to $u$, so it is discontinuous there. This remains true when $u$ is an endpoint, using a one-sided limit. Every $Y$ path has this defect, whereas every $X$ path is continuous.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 现在固定结果 $U=u$。$X$ 的路径始终为零；$Y$ 的路径在 $u$ 处为 $1$，但任意接近 $u$ 的其他时刻都为零，所以在那里不连续。即使 $u$ 是端点，使用单侧极限仍然不连续。每条 $Y$ 路径都有这个缺陷，而每条 $X$ 路径都连续。

## The order of the quantifiers matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*“每个时点”与“整条路径”的量词顺序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the same probability space, $Y$ is a **modification** (or version) of $X$ when, for every fixed $t$, $P(X_t=Y_t)=1$. The exceptional probability-zero set is allowed to depend on $t$. They are **indistinguishable** when there is one event $\Omega_0$ of probability one on which $X_t(\omega)=Y_t(\omega)$ for all $t$ simultaneously. The latter uses a single exceptional set for the entire path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一概率空间上，如果每个固定 $t$ 都满足 $P(X_t=Y_t)=1$，就说 $Y$ 是 $X$ 的**修改版本**；例外的零概率集合可以随 $t$ 改变。如果存在同一个概率一事件 $\Omega_0$，使其内每个 $\omega$ 都同时满足所有 $t$ 的 $X_t(\omega)=Y_t(\omega)$，才称两者**不可区分**。后者为整条路径使用同一个例外集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the spike example, each fixed-time exceptional set is $\{U=t\}$ and has probability zero, but their union over all $t\in[0,1]$ is the entire experiment. Thus the processes are modifications but are not indistinguishable. Countably many probability-zero sets have a probability-zero union; an uncountable union need not. Equality of finite-dimensional laws is weaker still: it does not even require two processes to live on the same probability space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尖峰例子中，每个固定时点的例外集合是 $\{U=t\}$，概率为零；但把所有 $t\in[0,1]$ 对应的集合并起来，就覆盖了整个实验。因此两者是修改版本，但并非不可区分。可数个零概率集合的并仍然概率为零，不可数个集合的并却未必如此。有限维分布相同还要更弱：甚至不要求两个过程生活在同一个概率空间。

## What extra argument restores regularity?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*还需要怎样的论证才能得到正则路径？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[有限维分布决定律|Kolmogorov extension theorem]] gives a law on $\mathbb R^T$ equipped with the product sigma-algebra generated by finite-coordinate events. For $T=[0,1]$, the set of all continuous functions is not itself an event in this full product sigma-algebra. This does not prevent a particular process from having a measurable continuity event on its underlying probability space: in the example, that event is the whole space for $X$ and empty for $Y$. It explains why uniqueness on the product sigma-algebra does not decide this path question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[有限维分布决定律|Kolmogorov 扩张定理]]给出 $\mathbb R^T$ 上的律，其事件族是有限坐标事件生成的乘积 σ-代数。当 $T=[0,1]$ 时，全部连续函数构成的集合，并不属于这个完整乘积空间的 σ-代数。这不妨碍某个具体过程在自己的底层概率空间上具有可测的连续性事件：上例中，对 $X$ 该事件是全集，对 $Y$ 则是空集。它解释了为什么乘积 σ-代数上的唯一性，无法替我们回答这个路径问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One route is to construct a probability law directly on a space of continuous functions with an appropriate sigma-algebra. Another is to start with a process and prove that it has a continuous modification. For example, a Kolmogorov continuity theorem uses bounds on moments of increments to establish such a modification. Finite-dimensional information can support this proof; the added conclusion is the existence of a regular version, not continuity of every version having those same finite-dimensional laws.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种路线是在连续函数空间及其合适的 σ-代数上直接构造概率律；另一种是先有过程，再证明它存在连续修改。例如，Kolmogorov 连续性定理利用增量矩的界来建立连续修改。有限维信息可以帮助完成这个证明，但额外得到的是正则版本的存在性，不是说具有这些有限维分布的每个版本都连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[标准布朗运动|Brownian motion]], consistent Gaussian increment laws are the distributional part of the construction; continuous paths still require that extra step. Other whole-path requirements, such as right-continuous paths with left limits or finite variation, likewise need their stated hypotheses and an appropriate path argument. They should not be appended to an extension theorem without justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[标准布朗运动|布朗运动]]，一致的 Gaussian 增量律完成的是构造中的分布部分，连续路径仍需要额外一步。其他整路径要求，例如右连续且有左极限、有限变差，也需要其各自假设和适当的路径论证，不能不加证明就附在扩张定理之后。
