# 反例必须满足全部前提并违反结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A counterexample must satisfy every premise and violate the conclusion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子标准
> 要反驳蕴含 $P\Rightarrow Q$，必须找到同一个对象使 $P$ 为真而 $Q$ 为假。漏掉任何前提，或只展示一个“不像结论”的对象，都没有击中原命题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To refute an implication $P\Rightarrow Q$, one must find a single object for which $P$ is true and $Q$ is false. An object that misses any premise, or merely looks unlike the conclusion, does not refute the statement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

命题“每个大于 $2$ 的偶整数都是两个素数之和”不能用 $9$ 反驳，因为 $9$ 不满足“偶整数”这个前提。若要反驳它，必须给出一个大于 $2$ 的偶整数，并证明它不能写成两个素数之和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statement “every even integer greater than $2$ is the sum of two primes” is not refuted by $9$, because $9$ fails the premise of being even. A counterexample would have to be an even integer greater than $2$ together with a proof that it cannot be written as a sum of two primes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 一个对象即使违反结论，只要它不满足某项前提，就不在命题承诺的范围内，不能成为反例。
- 失败的证明有时提示命题缺少条件。应明确修改陈述并重新证明，不能在推导中偷偷加入“非零”“有限”“连通”等原题没有的假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** Even if an object violates the conclusion, it is not a counterexample when it fails any premise, because it lies outside the claim's scope.<br>
**2.** A failed proof may reveal a missing condition. Revise the statement explicitly and prove the revision instead of smuggling in assumptions such as nonzero, finite, or connected.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 命题是“若实数 $x>1$，则 $x^2>x$”。取 $x=-2$ 能否作为反例？
>
> **答案：** 不能。虽然 $x^2>x$ 在此仍真，更关键的是 $x=-2$ 不满足前提 $x>1$；它不落在命题承诺的范围内。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider: “If a real number $x>1$, then $x^2>x$.” Can $x=-2$ be a counterexample?
>
> **Answer:** No. More importantly than the conclusion being true here, $x=-2$ does not satisfy the premise $x>1$, so it lies outside the claim's scope.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
