# Valid counterexamples
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反例有效性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A proposed counterexample has two jobs: it must be an object the original claim actually covers, and it must make that claim fail. For instance, the claim “every positive real number $x$ satisfies $x^2>x$” fails at $x=\tfrac12$: the input is positive, but its square is $\tfrac14<\tfrac12$. A negative input would not test that particular promise, even if a calculation at it looked surprising.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个候选反例承担两项任务：它必须属于原命题确实承诺处理的对象，而且必须使原命题失败。例如，“每个正实数 $x$ 都满足 $x^2>x$”在 $x=\tfrac12$ 处失败：输入为正，但平方为 $\tfrac14<\tfrac12$。负数输入却不在这条承诺之内，即使代入后的计算看起来出人意料，也没有检验原命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general reason that one failure suffices is explained in [[全称命题与反例|universal claims and counterexamples]]. Here we check a particular candidate against a conditional universal claim. Let $D$ be the allowed domain, $A(x)$ collect **all** of the claim's hypotheses, and $B(x)$ be its conclusion. The statement has the form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
为什么一个失败就足够，见[[全称命题与反例]]；这里负责把一个具体候选与原命题逐项对照。设 $D$ 是允许的论域，$A(x)$ 汇集原命题的**全部**前提，$B(x)$ 是结论。所讨论的命题形如

$$\forall x\in D\,\bigl(A(x)\Rightarrow B(x)\bigr).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\forall$ means “for every.” By the [[蕴含真值条件|truth condition for implication]], an input refutes this statement exactly when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$\forall$ 表示“对每个”。根据[[蕴含真值条件]]，输入 $a$ 能反驳这条命题，当且仅当

$$a\in D,\qquad A(a)\text{ is true},\qquad B(a)\text{ is false}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, [[量词否定规则|negating the quantified claim]] gives $\exists x\in D\,(A(x)\land\neg B(x))$: at least one allowed object satisfies every hypothesis and fails the conclusion. Here $\exists$ means “there exists,” $\land$ means “and,” and $\neg$ means “not.” If the hypotheses are $A_1,A_2,\ldots$, all must hold for the **same** candidate; evidence about different objects cannot be combined into one counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
等价地，按[[量词否定规则]]否定整条命题，得到 $\exists x\in D\,(A(x)\land\neg B(x))$：至少存在一个允许对象，满足全部前提而不满足结论。$\exists$ 表示“存在”，$\land$ 表示“并且”，$\neg$ 表示“非”。若前提分为 $A_1,A_2,\ldots$，它们必须在**同一个**候选处同时成立，不能把几个不同对象的证据拼成一个反例。

> [!important] Enter the scope, then show failure · 先进入范围，再核实失败
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To refute an implication $P\Rightarrow Q$, one must find a single object for which $P$ is true and $Q$ is false. An object that misses any premise, or merely looks unlike the conclusion, does not refute the statement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要反驳蕴含 $P\Rightarrow Q$，必须找到同一个对象使 $P$ 为真而 $Q$ 为假。漏掉任何前提，或只展示一个“不像结论”的对象，都没有击中原命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check all conditions on the same integer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*在同一个整数上核对全部条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the false claim: “Every **positive even integer** is divisible by $4$.” The domain is the integers; positivity and evenness are both hypotheses. These candidates illustrate different outcomes.

| Candidate | Positive? | Even? | Divisible by $4$? | A counterexample? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $-2$ | No | Yes | No | No: positivity fails |
| $3$ | Yes | No | No | No: evenness fails |
| $4$ | Yes | Yes | Yes | No: conclusion holds |
| $2$ | Yes | Yes | No | Yes: both hypotheses hold and conclusion fails |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
考虑这个假命题：“每个**正偶整数**都能被 $4$ 整除。”论域是整数，“为正”和“为偶数”都是前提。表中 $-2$ 不为正，$3$ 不是偶数，二者即使不被 $4$ 整除，也不是反例；$4$ 满足前提但结论同样成立，仍不是反例。只有 $2$ 同时满足这两项前提，却使结论失败，因此构成反例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One fully verified candidate settles falsity; no search for every counterexample is required. In contrast, rejecting a candidate only says that **this attempt** did not refute the claim. It does not prove the claim true. A claim about several inputs requires a whole tuple with the same fixed parameters and interpretation, not one coordinate borrowed from each of several examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个核验完整的候选就能确定命题为假，不必找出全部反例。反过来，排除一个候选只说明**这一次尝试**没有反驳成功，并没有证明原命题为真。若命题涉及多个输入，反例应是一整组输入，并保持参数和解释不变，不能从几组例子中各借一个坐标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Failure of a search is not failure of an existential conclusion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*没有找到表示，不等于证明不存在表示*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statement “every even integer greater than $2$ is the sum of two primes” is not refuted by $9$, because $9$ fails the premise of being even. A counterexample would have to be an even integer greater than $2$ together with a proof that it cannot be written as a sum of two primes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
命题“每个大于 $2$ 的偶整数都是两个素数之和”不能用 $9$ 反驳，因为 $9$ 不满足“偶整数”这个前提。若要反驳它，必须给出一个大于 $2$ 的偶整数，并证明它不能写成两个素数之和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here a [[素数|prime]] is an integer greater than one whose only positive divisors are one and itself. “Is the sum of two primes” is itself an existence statement: some primes $p,q$ must satisfy $n=p+q$. They need not be distinct unless the original statement says so; for example, $6=3+3$ is allowed. At $n=8$, the attempt $8=2+6$ does not use two primes, but that does not make $8$ a counterexample: $8=3+5$ supplies a successful pair.

To refute the existence conclusion at a chosen $n$, one must rule out **every** permitted pair, not just one proposed decomposition. The logical task is “no such pair exists.” This example explains what a refutation would require; it supplies no refutation of the displayed universal statement. Its quantifier order is kept intact throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里的[[素数]]是大于一、正因子只有一和自身的整数。“是两个素数之和”本身就是存在命题：要有某对素数 $p,q$ 满足 $n=p+q$。除非原句另外要求，两素数不必不同，例如 $6=3+3$ 是允许的。对 $n=8$，尝试 $8=2+6$ 没得到两个素数，却不能说明 $8$ 是反例，因为 $8=3+5$ 就给出了成功的一对。

要在选定的 $n$ 处否定存在性结论，必须排除**每一对**允许的素数，而不是只排除一种尝试。逻辑任务是证明“没有这样的配对存在”。这个例子说明反驳需要什么，并没有反驳所展示的全称命题；整个过程中都应保持量词次序不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the original claim fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*保持原命题不变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The opening claim used $x>0$ and failed at $x=\tfrac12$. Replacing it by “if $x>1$, then $x^2>x$” gives a **different** statement. This revised statement is true because $x^2-x=x(x-1)>0$ when $x>1$. That proof does not rescue the broader positive-input claim; it proves the explicitly revised one.

Definitions must also apply at the proposed input. For example, $x=0$ is not a counterexample to a statement restricted to nonzero real $x$ and involving $1/x$. Removing that restriction produces an undefined expression at zero, not an automatic false value in the same well-defined proposition. First state a meaningful domain and conclusion, then test them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
开头的命题使用 $x>0$，在 $x=\tfrac12$ 处失败。把它改为“若 $x>1$，则 $x^2>x$”，得到的是**另一条**命题；后者确实成立，因为 $x>1$ 时 $x^2-x=x(x-1)>0$。这个证明没有挽救原来关于所有正输入的命题，而是证明了已明确改写的版本。

候选输入处的表达式还必须有定义。例如，若命题已经限定 $x$ 为非零实数并涉及 $1/x$，则 $x=0$ 不是反例。去掉限制后，零处得到的是未定义表达式，不能自动当作同一条有明确定义的命题中的假值。应先说清有意义的论域和结论，再作核验。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Even if an object violates the conclusion, it is not a counterexample when it fails any premise, because it lies outside the claim's scope.
- A failed proof may reveal a missing condition. Revise the statement explicitly and prove the revision instead of smuggling in assumptions such as nonzero, finite, or connected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 一个对象即使违反结论，只要它不满足某项前提，就不在命题承诺的范围内，不能成为反例。
- 失败的证明有时提示命题缺少条件。应明确修改陈述并重新证明，不能在推导中偷偷加入“非零”“有限”“连通”等原题没有的假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If no object satisfies the hypotheses, the conditional universal claim has no counterexample and is vacuously true; it makes no existence assertion. Likewise an empty domain supplies no candidate. A genuine counterexample also cannot be dismissed merely because it is unusual: once it meets every stated condition, excluding it requires openly changing the statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
如果没有对象满足全部前提，条件全称命题就没有反例而空真，但它没有作出存在断言；空论域同样不会提供候选。一个真正满足全部已声明条件的反例，也不能只因“不典型”就被排除；要排除它，必须公开修改陈述。

> [!question]- Check the premise first · 先核前提
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider: “If a real number $x>1$, then $x^2>x$.” Can $x=-2$ be a counterexample?
>
> **Answer:** No. More importantly than the conclusion being true here, $x=-2$ does not satisfy the premise $x>1$, so it lies outside the claim's scope.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 命题是“若实数 $x>1$，则 $x^2>x$”。取 $x=-2$ 能否作为反例？
>
> **答案：** 不能。虽然 $x^2>x$ 在此仍真，更关键的是 $x=-2$ 不满足前提 $x>1$；它不落在命题承诺的范围内。
