# Time homogeneity and stationarity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*时间齐次与平稳性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Time homogeneity** says that the same transition rule is used at every step. **Stationarity** says that the process's distribution looks the same after shifting the observation times. A machine can obey the same failure-and-repair rule every day while the probability that it is broken changes from day to day. The rule and the distribution are different objects, so fixing the first does not fix the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**时间齐次**说的是每一步使用同一套转移规则；**平稳性**说的是把观察时间整体平移以后，过程的分布保持相同。机器每天可以使用同一套故障和维修规则，但当天处于故障状态的概率仍然逐日变化。规则和分布是不同对象，固定前者并不自动固定后者。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here consider a [[离散时间Markov链|discrete-time Markov chain]] on a finite or countable state space. Its [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|homogeneous kernel]] $P=(p_{ij})$ specifies the chance of the next state $j$ from the current state $i$. Use the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], and let $\mu_n$ be the probability row at time $n$: its entry $\mu_n(i)$ is $\Pr(X_n=i)$, a one-time [[边际分布|marginal probability]]. Averaging the transition rule over today's distribution gives tomorrow's distribution:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里讨论有限或可数状态空间上的[[离散时间Markov链]]。它的[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|齐次核]] $P=(p_{ij})$ 指定从当前状态 $i$ 到下一状态 $j$ 的概率。采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]，令 $\mu_n$ 为时点 $n$ 的概率行：条目 $\mu_n(i)=\Pr(X_n=i)$ 是一个时点的[[边际分布|边际概率]]。按今天的分布对转移规则加权，得到明天的分布：

$$\mu_{n+1}(j)=\sum_i\mu_n(i)p_{ij},\qquad
\mu_{n+1}=\mu_nP,\qquad \mu_n=\mu_0P^n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum runs over all possible current states. $P^n$ means applying the same rule $n$ times; on a countably infinite space these expressions denote sums and kernel compositions, not an appeal to finite-matrix spectral results. Even though $P$ is fixed, the starting row $\mu_0$ can move under repeated multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和遍历所有可能的当前状态。$P^n$ 表示把同一规则应用 $n$ 次；状态可数无穷时，式子表示求和与核的复合，并不是在引用有限矩阵的谱结论。虽然 $P$ 固定，起始概率行 $\mu_0$ 在反复相乘后仍然可能变化。

> [!example] One rule, two very different starts · 同一规则，两种不同起点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For states $0,1$, take the swap rule
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对状态 $0,1$，采用交换规则
>
> $$P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The next state is always the other one, at every time. Starting with $\mu_0=(1,0)$ gives $(1,0),(0,1),(1,0),\ldots$. This chain is homogeneous but its marginal distribution is not constant. Starting instead with $\pi=(1/2,1/2)$ gives $\pi P=\pi$, so every marginal stays $(1/2,1/2)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不论哪个时点，下一状态一定换到另一边。若从 $\mu_0=(1,0)$ 开始，分布依次为 $(1,0),(0,1),(1,0),\ldots$；链时间齐次，但边际分布并不固定。若改从 $\pi=(1/2,1/2)$ 开始，则 $\pi P=\pi$，每个时点的边际分布都保持 $(1/2,1/2)$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the second experiment, a fair initial choice selects one of the two alternating paths. Every individual path still changes at every step. A stationary distribution is not a claim that the realised state stops moving.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第二种实验中，最初的公平选择决定走哪一条交替路径，每条具体路径仍然每步改变。分布平稳并不是说实现出来的状态停止运动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The initial law must be invariant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*初始分布必须是不变分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[Markov稳态分布|stationary distribution]] for the row kernel is a probability row $\pi$ satisfying $\pi P=\pi$. Therefore a homogeneous chain has the same one-time distribution at every step, starting at step zero, **if and only if** its initial law satisfies $\mu_0P=\mu_0$. Necessity follows by comparing steps zero and one. For sufficiency, repeat the equality: $\mu_0P^2=(\mu_0P)P=\mu_0P=\mu_0$, and continue. The initial law is part of specifying the process, alongside its kernel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行随机核的[[Markov稳态分布|平稳分布]]是满足 $\pi P=\pi$ 的概率行。因此，齐次链从第零步开始在每个时点都有同一个边际分布，**当且仅当**初始分布满足 $\mu_0P=\mu_0$。必要性只需比较第零步与第一步；充分性则反复使用等式：$\mu_0P^2=(\mu_0P)P=\mu_0P=\mu_0$，以后同理。初始律与转移核一样，都是确定过程所需的部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why this also gives strict stationarity here
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么在这里还能得到严平稳*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[严平稳定义|Strict stationarity]] requires more than identical one-time marginals: the joint law of observations at **any finite collection of times** must be unchanged when all times are shifted together. For a homogeneous Markov chain started at an invariant probability row $\pi$, this stronger statement follows from the [[概率乘法法则|multiplication rule]]. For a block of length $\ell+1$, where $\ell\ge0$ is an integer, and any starting time $m\ge0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[严平稳定义|严平稳]]比单个时点的边际相同要求更多：在**任意有限组时点**观察到的联合分布，都必须在这些时点共同平移后保持相同。对从不变概率行 $\pi$ 开始的齐次 Markov 链，这个更强结论可以由[[概率乘法法则]]得到。取长度为 $\ell+1$ 的连续片段，整数 $\ell\ge0$、起点 $m\ge0$，有

$$\Pr(X_m=i_0,\ldots,X_{m+\ell}=i_\ell)
=\pi(i_0)p_{i_0i_1}\cdots p_{i_{\ell-1}i_\ell}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The right side does not contain $m$: the first-state law is always $\pi$ and all following transition factors use the same kernel. For $\ell=0$, there are no transition factors and the probability is just $\pi(i_0)$. Summing over unobserved intermediate states handles a finite set of times with gaps. Thus every finite joint law is shift-invariant. Conversely, strict stationarity includes equality of the step-zero and step-one marginals, so it forces $\mu_0P=\mu_0$ in this homogeneous Markov model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右边没有 $m$：首状态的分布始终是 $\pi$，后续转移因子始终使用同一个核。$\ell=0$ 时没有转移因子，概率就是 $\pi(i_0)$。若所选时点之间有间隔，对未观察的中间状态求和即可，因而任意有限维联合分布都平移不变。反过来，严平稳包含第零步和第一步的边际相同，所以在这个齐次 Markov 模型中也必然要求 $\mu_0P=\mu_0$。

> [!warning] Do not replace a joint-law check by a marginal check · 不能一般地用边际代替联合分布
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The preceding equivalence uses the homogeneous Markov structure. A general process may have the same marginal at every time while its dependence pattern changes. For example, let $U,V$ be independent fair bits, set $Y_0=Y_1=U$ and $Y_n=V$ for $n\ge2$. Every $Y_n$ is fair, but $\Pr(Y_0=Y_1)=1$ whereas $\Pr(Y_1=Y_2)=1/2$. It is not strictly stationary.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上面的等价关系使用了齐次 Markov 结构。一般过程可以每个时点的边际都相同，但依赖模式随时间改变。例如，令 $U,V$ 为独立公平二元结果，设 $Y_0=Y_1=U$，而 $n\ge2$ 时 $Y_n=V$。每个 $Y_n$ 都是公平的，但 $\Pr(Y_0=Y_1)=1$，$\Pr(Y_1=Y_2)=1/2$，所以它并不严平稳。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stationarity also differs from eventual convergence. In the swap example, starting at $\pi$ is stationary from the outset, but starting at $(1,0)$ never converges to $\pi$. Even the [[稳态唯一不推收敛|uniqueness of a stationary distribution does not force convergence]]. [[有限链逐步收敛|Finite-chain convergence]] examines the extra class and period conditions. To check stationarity of a given homogeneous chain, first check its **actual initial law**, not just whether its kernel possesses some stationary distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平稳性也不同于最终收敛。交换例子从 $\pi$ 出发时，从一开始就平稳；从 $(1,0)$ 出发却永远不趋近 $\pi$。甚至[[稳态唯一不推收敛|稳态分布唯一也不能迫使收敛]]。[[有限链逐步收敛]]进一步讨论类结构和周期条件。要判断给定齐次链是否平稳，先检查它**实际采用的初始分布**，不能只问该核是否拥有某个平稳分布。
