# 排放价格走廊以明确的上下限干预规则约束配额价格并允许边界处的排放量调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An emissions price collar uses specified floor and ceiling interventions to bound allowance prices, allowing emissions to adjust when a boundary is reached.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

排放价格走廊是把[[总量排放交易]]与价格下限、上限规则结合的混合制度。区间内由配额总量决定市场出清价格；到达边界时，事先规定的干预维持价格约束并改变允许或实际发生的排放量。地板、上限的数字本身不是执行机制，必须同时说清谁买卖或支付、多少数量，以及是否突破原总量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A price collar combines [[总量排放交易|cap and trade]] with floor and ceiling rules. Within the interval, the cap determines the clearing price; at a boundary, specified intervention supports the price constraint and changes permitted or actual emissions. Numerical bounds alone are not mechanisms: specify the buyer, seller or payer, the quantities available and whether the original cap can be exceeded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个明确的单期构造是：主管机构发出可交易配额总量 $L$；对企业持有但未用来覆盖排放的配额向其按每份 $s$支付，对超出所持配额的排放允许企业按每单位 $h$向主管机构付费，$0\le s\le h$。假定竞争、完整计量、支付可信且边界通道没有额外数量限制。这里的上限付费是制度允许的替代履约通道，不是擅自把违法罚款当成免除缴回义务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In one explicit single-period construction, the regulator issues $L$ tradable allowances, pays firms $s$ for each allowance held but unused for emissions, and lets firms pay it $h$ per emissions unit beyond holdings, with $0\le s\le h$. Assume competition, complete measurement, credible payments and no additional quantity limits on these channels. The ceiling charge is an authorised compliance option, not an assumption that paying a violation fine cancels surrender obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若许可价格为 $q$，企业不会在 $q<s$时放弃买入有支付支持的配额，也不会在 $q>h$时为排放买昂贵配额而不使用上限通道。基础均衡因此有 $s\le q\le h$。以 $M(E)$表示总排放为 $E$时、经过成本有效分担的边际减排成本，且 $M$随排放增加而下降，内点规则为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an allowance price below $s$, firms would buy supported allowances; above $h$, they would use the ceiling option instead of buying expensive allowances. The benchmark equilibrium therefore has $s\le q\le h$. Let $M(E)$ be aggregate marginal abatement cost at emissions $E$, after least-cost allocation, decreasing in emissions. Interior outcomes satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{condition}&\text{price}&\text{emissions}\\
M(L)<s&q=s&M(E)=s,\ E<L\\
s<M(L)<h&q=M(L)&E=L\\
M(L)>h&q=h&M(E)=h,\ E>L
\end{array}
$$

恰好等于边界时可有 $E=L$，不需要凭等号另造跳跃。各企业仍面对共同边际价格，故在相应条件下保持[[共同排放价格与减排分担|成本有效分担]]；走廊是否值得采用及参数如何选，还需比较环境损害、成本不确定性和执行成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At equality with a bound, emissions can equal $L$ without a discontinuous jump. Firms still face a common marginal price and can retain [[共同排放价格与减排分担|cost-effective allocation]]. Whether to adopt a collar and how to choose its parameters require comparing damage, cost uncertainty and implementation costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造例：$L=100,s=60,h=90$，相关范围内 $M(E,\theta)=0.75(200-E)+\theta$。$\theta=0$时价格75、排放100；$\theta=30$时原cap价格会是105，走廊使价格90、排放120；$\theta=-30$时原cap价格45，支持下限使价格60、排放80。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $L=100$, $s=60$, $h=90$ and $M(E,\theta)=0.75(200-E)+\theta$ over the relevant range. At zero shock, price is seventy-five and emissions one hundred. A shock of thirty would give cap price 105, but the collar yields price ninety and emissions 120. A shock of minus thirty would give cap price forty-five, but the floor yields price sixty and emissions eighty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数量能力改变承诺：若高价通道最多只增加10配额，高成本状态到排放110时 $M=97.5$，储备耗尽后90便不是硬上限。只给新拍卖设保留价，也未必能支撑所有已有配额的二级市场价格。按数量触发的稳定储备不是价格走廊的自动同义词，必须另读它的规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capacity changes the promise: if the high-price channel adds at most ten allowances, marginal cost at emissions 110 is 97.5 in the high-cost state, so ninety ceases to be a hard ceiling once the reserve is exhausted. An auction reserve price alone need not support the secondary market for existing allowances. A quantity-triggered stability reserve is not automatically a price collar.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
