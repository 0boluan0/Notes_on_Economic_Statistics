# 同一主体时点和会计口径下资产等于负债与所有者权益之和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under one entity, date, and accounting basis, assets equal liabilities plus owners' equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记同一报告主体在时点 $t$ 确认的资产、负债和所有者权益分别为 $A_t,L_t,E_t$。所有者权益是账面资产扣除负债后的剩余，因此有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A_t,L_t,E_t$ denote the recognised assets, liabilities, and equity of the same entity at date $t$. Equity is the accounting residual after liabilities are deducted from assets, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$A_t=L_t+E_t,\qquad E_t=A_t-L_t.$$

同一口径下，每项交易或调整后的余额都应继续满足恒等式；两期相减便有 $\Delta A=\Delta L+\Delta E$。例如期初 $A=100,L=60,E=40$：借款10并收到现金后为 $110=70+40$；若随后确认一项不影响负债且忽略税收的资产减值5，则为 $105=70+35$。借款不自动创造利润，损失也不要求当时支付现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Balances must continue to satisfy the identity after each transaction or adjustment. Subtracting two dates gives $\Delta A=\Delta L+\Delta E$. Starting from $100=60+40$, borrowing 10 for cash gives $110=70+40$. A subsequent impairment of 5 with no liability or tax effect gives $105=70+35$. Borrowing need not create profit, and a loss need not require a contemporaneous cash payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一项记账和范围一致性约束，不是“资产一定足以清偿”的保证：权益可以为负，账面值也未必能按原额兑现。反过来，报表平衡也不充分证明确认计量正确；同时漏记一项资产和对应负债，仍可能平衡。读[[资产负债表]]时应将恒等式检查与[[财务报表附注|计量和披露核查]]分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a recording and scope-consistency constraint, not a solvency guarantee: equity may be negative, and carrying amounts may not be realised in cash. Balance is also insufficient to establish correct recognition and measurement; omitting both an asset and its corresponding liability can leave the equation intact. Keep the [[资产负债表|balance-sheet]] identity check separate from [[财务报表附注|measurement and disclosure review]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
