# 没有初等原函数的连续函数仍可积分并由积分定义新函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A continuous function without an elementary antiderivative remains integrable and can define a new function by accumulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“不存在初等原函数”是说无法用有限次代数运算、指数、对数、三角函数及其反函数的组合表示一个原函数；不是说原函数不存在，更不是说定积分不存在。若 $f$ 在区间 $I$ 连续，[[连续函数可积]]保证 $I$ 内每个有限闭区间上的积分存在；固定 $a\in I$，[[积分求导定理]]还直接构造出 $G(x)=\int_a^xf(t)dt$，在 $I$ 内部作为原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lacking an elementary antiderivative concerns representability by finite combinations of elementary operations and functions, not the existence of an antiderivative or integral. Continuity on an interval guarantees integration on every finite closed subinterval contained in it; accumulation from a fixed base point constructs an antiderivative on its interior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

典型例子是 $e^{-x^2}$。虽然原函数不是初等函数，$G(x)=\int_0^xe^{-t^2}dt$ 仍可求导、[[累积函数图像|作图]]，其有限区间值可用[[Simpson法]]近似；整个实轴上的积分反而有精确值 $\sqrt\pi$，见[[高斯积分]]。特殊端点的定积分能算出闭式，与原函数没有初等表达式并不冲突。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gaussian accumulation can still be differentiated, [[累积函数图像|sketched]], and [[Simpson法|approximated numerically]]. Its integral over the whole real line even has the exact value given by the [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral]]. A closed form for a special definite integral does not imply an elementary formula for every endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此做题时先判断需要什么：研究变化就求导，比较大小就用积分界，需要数值就用带误差控制的数值积分。不要把“暂时没找到原函数”和“已证明不存在初等原函数”混为一谈；后一个结论需要额外理论，本课程只使用高斯等标准例子，并不证明其非初等性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the tool for the task: derivatives for change, bounds for comparison, and controlled quadrature for numerical values. Failure to find an antiderivative is not a proof of non-elementarity. The latter requires additional theory; the course uses the Gaussian example without proving its non-elementarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
