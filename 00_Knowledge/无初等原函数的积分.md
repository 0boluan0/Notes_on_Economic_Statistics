# Integrals without elementary antiderivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*无初等原函数的积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find an [[原函数|antiderivative]] of $f$, we seek a function whose derivative is $f$. Sometimes such a function exists but cannot be written using the familiar elementary functions. **A limit on the available formulas is different from a failure of the integral to exist.** The bell-shaped function $e^{-x^2}$ is the standard example: its accumulated area is perfectly well defined, even though its antiderivative is not elementary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求 $f$ 的[[原函数]]，就是寻找一个导数等于 $f$ 的函数。有时这个函数存在，却不能用熟悉的初等函数写出。**可用公式的表达能力有限，与积分不存在，是两件不同的事。**钟形函数 $e^{-x^2}$ 就是标准例子：它累积的面积有明确含义，虽然原函数不是初等函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here “elementary” means a finite expression assembled from algebraic functions, exponentials, logarithms, trigonometric functions and their inverses, using arithmetic operations and composition on a suitable domain. For example, $x^2$, $\sqrt{1+x^2}$ and $\ln(1+x^2)$ are elementary. Giving a new function a name, or defining it by an integral, does not automatically put it in this class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“初等”指在合适定义域上，由代数函数、指数、对数、三角函数及其反函数，经过有限次四则运算与复合得到的表达式。例如，$x^2$、$\sqrt{1+x^2}$、$\ln(1+x^2)$ 都是初等函数。给新函数取名字，或用积分定义它，并不会自动让它成为初等函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Continuity still constructs an antiderivative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*连续性仍然能构造原函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $f$ is continuous on a real interval $I$ with nonempty interior, and fix a base point $a\in I$. Every finite closed interval between $a$ and another point $x\in I$ lies in $I$. The [[连续函数可积|continuous-integrability theorem]] therefore makes the following definition valid:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 在内部非空的实区间 $I$ 上连续，固定基点 $a\in I$。对任意另一个点 $x\in I$，$a$ 与 $x$ 之间的有限闭区间都留在 $I$ 中，因此[[连续函数可积|连续可积性定理]]保证下面的定义成立：

$$G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The letter $t$ runs through the integration interval; $x$ chooses the endpoint. By the [[积分求导定理|accumulation differentiation theorem]], $G'(x)=f(x)$ at every interior point of $I$. Thus the integral itself supplies an antiderivative. When $x<a$, the usual reversed-bound sign applies. This construction establishes existence; it makes no promise about an elementary formula. It also says nothing by itself about convergence at an infinite endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$t$ 在积分区间内变化，$x$ 则决定终点。由[[积分求导定理]]，在 $I$ 的每个内点都有 $G'(x)=f(x)$。因此，积分本身就给出了一个原函数；$x<a$ 时按反向积分取符号。这个构造证明存在性，并不承诺初等表达式，也不单独保证无穷端点处收敛。

> [!example] The Gaussian accumulation · 高斯累积函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Define $G(x)=\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$ for every real $x$. Then $G(0)=0$, $G'(x)=e^{-x^2}>0$, and $G''(x)=-2xe^{-x^2}$. These formulas already tell us that $G$ increases everywhere and changes from convex to concave at zero; the reasoning is developed in [[累积函数图像|graphing accumulation functions]]. The [[误差函数|error function]] is a standard normalised version, $\operatorname{erf}(x)=2G(x)/\sqrt\pi$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个实数 $x$，定义 $G(x)=\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$。于是 $G(0)=0$、$G'(x)=e^{-x^2}>0$、$G''(x)=-2xe^{-x^2}$。这些式子已经说明：$G$ 处处递增，并在零点从凸转为凹；推理见[[累积函数图像]]。[[误差函数]]是它的一种标准归一化形式：$\operatorname{erf}(x)=2G(x)/\sqrt\pi$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nearby expression does have an elementary antiderivative:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个很相近的表达式却有初等原函数：

$$\int x e^{-x^2}\,dx=-\tfrac12e^{-x^2}+C.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating the right side by the [[链式法则|chain rule]] produces the factor $x$. Without that factor, the same substitution does not complete the integral. This observation explains why a tempting method fails; it is **not** a proof that every possible elementary formula fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[链式法则]]对右边求导，恰好会产生因子 $x$。少了这个因子，同一个换元就不能直接完成积分。这解释了一个看似合适的方法为什么失效，**不能**据此证明所有可能的初等表达式都失效。

## Choose the information the problem asks for
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*按问题需要的信息选择工具*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A derivative or graph can be studied from the integrand, as above. For a numerical value on a finite interval, [[Simpson法|Simpson's rule]] is one approximation method; a requested accuracy also needs an [[Simpson误差界|error bound]] and its smoothness assumptions. To compare values without calculating them, use [[积分上下界估计|bounds on the integrand]]. For example, $0\le t\le1$ gives $e^{-1}\le e^{-t^2}\le1$, so $e^{-1}\le G(1)\le1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

研究导数或图像，可以像上面那样从被积函数入手。求有限区间上的数值，可以用[[Simpson法]]等近似方法；若要求精度，还须检查[[Simpson误差界]]及其光滑性条件。不想先算数值、只需比较大小时，可以用[[积分上下界估计]]。例如，$0\le t\le1$ 时有 $e^{-1}\le e^{-t^2}\le1$，因此 $e^{-1}\le G(1)\le1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some particular definite integrals even have simple exact values. The separate [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral theorem]] proves that $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-t^2}\,dt=\sqrt\pi$. Knowing this one total is different from an elementary formula for $G(x)$ at every variable endpoint. Both statements can hold together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某些特定端点的定积分甚至有简单的精确值。另一个[[高斯积分|高斯积分定理]]证明了 $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-t^2}\,dt=\sqrt\pi$。知道这一个总量，与能对每个变化的端点 $x$ 都写出 $G(x)$ 的初等公式，是不同要求；两种结论可以同时成立。

> [!warning] An unsuccessful method is not an impossibility proof · 方法失败不是不可能性证明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Gaussian non-elementarity is a standard established result from additional integration theory. The elementary calculus sources below state it but do not prove it. The existence, differentiation, bounds and special definite value discussed here do not depend on reproducing that advanced proof. “I have not found an antiderivative” is a weaker statement and must stay distinct.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 高斯原函数的非初等性是需要额外积分理论的标准结论。下面的初等微积分来源陈述它，却没有给出证明。这里讨论的存在性、求导、估计和特殊定积分值，并不依赖于在此重现那个进阶证明。“我暂时没找到原函数”是更弱的陈述，不能混为一谈。
