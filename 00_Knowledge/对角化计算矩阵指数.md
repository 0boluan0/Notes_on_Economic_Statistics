# Computing a matrix exponential by diagonalization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用对角化计算矩阵指数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵指数|matrix exponential]] $e^{tA}$ turns an initial vector into its later value in a [[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|constant-coefficient system]] $u'=Au$. When $A$ has an eigenvector basis, the calculation separates into independent scalar exponentials: express the initial vector in that basis, evolve each coefficient, then reconstruct the vector. This card explains that calculation; the power series defines the exponential even when such a basis does not exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵指数]] $e^{tA}$ 把初始向量变成[[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|常系数系统]] $u'=Au$ 中之后的向量。若 $A$ 有特征向量基，计算就能拆成独立的标量指数：先用这组基表示初始向量，再分别演化各个系数，最后还原向量。本卡说明这套计算；即使没有这样的基，矩阵指数仍由幂级数定义。

> [!example] Separate two coupled coordinates · 拆开两个耦合坐标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the real matrix $A$ below. Its first coordinate receives a contribution from the second. The two vectors $x_1,x_2$ nevertheless satisfy the [[特征对|eigenvector equations]] $Ax_1=x_1$ and $Ax_2=3x_2$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑下面的实矩阵 $A$。它的第一个坐标会收到第二个坐标的贡献，但两个向量 $x_1,x_2$ 分别满足[[特征对|特征向量方程]] $Ax_1=x_1$、$Ax_2=3x_2$：
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad x_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad x_2=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The vectors form a [[基|basis]]. For the initial vector $u_0=(0,1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], we have $u_0=-\tfrac12x_1+\tfrac12x_2$. Each coefficient evolves at its eigenvalue's rate, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这两个向量组成一组[[基]]。对初始向量 $u_0=(0,1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，有 $u_0=-\tfrac12x_1+\tfrac12x_2$。各个系数按对应特征值的速率演化，因此
>
> $$u(t)=-\tfrac12e^t x_1+\tfrac12e^{3t}x_2=\begin{pmatrix}(e^{3t}-e^t)/2\\e^{3t}\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Organize the calculation for every initial vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把计算组织成适用于全部初值的矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real or complex $n\times n$ matrix, $n\ge1$, with a [[对角化|diagonalization]] $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$. Here $S$ is invertible, its columns are eigenvectors, and $\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ lists their eigenvalues in the same order. For real time $t$, the scalar $e^{t\lambda_i}$ is the ordinary exponential, interpreted over $\mathbb C$ if needed. The identity also holds for complex $t$ by the same convergent series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是实或复的 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$，并有[[对角化]] $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$。这里 $S$ 可逆，各列是特征向量，$\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ 按相同顺序放置对应特征值。对实时间 $t$，标量 $e^{t\lambda_i}$ 是通常的指数；需要时在复数中解释。利用同一个收敛级数，恒等式也适用于复数 $t$。

> [!important] Change coordinates, exponentiate, change back · 换坐标、取指数、换回来
> $$e^{tA}=S\operatorname{diag}(e^{t\lambda_1},\ldots,e^{t\lambda_n})S^{-1}=Se^{t\Lambda}S^{-1}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a vector $u_0$, first find $c=S^{-1}u_0$, then multiply coordinate $c_i$ by $e^{t\lambda_i}$, and finally multiply by $S$. In computation, finding $c$ means solving $Sc=u_0$; it does not require explicitly forming an inverse matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对向量 $u_0$，先求 $c=S^{-1}u_0$，再把第 $i$ 个坐标 $c_i$ 乘上 $e^{t\lambda_i}$，最后乘 $S$。计算 $c$ 时可以直接解方程 $Sc=u_0$，不要求显式构造逆矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the example, the [[逆矩阵|inverse]] and matching diagonal matrix are small enough to display. Multiplying the three factors yields a formula that works for any initial vector:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对开头例子，[[逆矩阵]]和对应对角矩阵很小，可以直接写出。把三个因子相乘，就得到适用于任意初始向量的公式：

$$S=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\quad S^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1/2\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix},\quad \Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},$$

$$e^{tA}=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}e^t&0\\0&e^{3t}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&-1/2\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}e^t&(e^{3t}-e^t)/2\\0&e^{3t}\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its second column is exactly the trajectory already computed from $u_0=(0,1)^T$. The off-diagonal exponential entry is a difference of two exponentials; exponentiating the entries of $A$ separately would give a different matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二列正好就是刚才从 $u_0=(0,1)^T$ 算出的轨迹。指数矩阵的非对角元是两个指数之差；逐个对 $A$ 的元素取指数，会得到另一个矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the formula agrees with the definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么这个公式符合定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each nonnegative integer $k$, cancellation of adjacent $S^{-1}S$ gives $A^k=S\Lambda^kS^{-1}$, as in [[对角化计算矩阵幂|matrix powers by diagonalization]]. At $k=0$, both powers mean the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Thus, for a finite partial sum ending at integer $N\ge0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个非负整数 $k$，相邻的 $S^{-1}S$ 相消，就得到 $A^k=S\Lambda^kS^{-1}$，与[[对角化计算矩阵幂]]相同。$k=0$ 时两个幂都表示[[单位矩阵]]。因此，对截止于整数 $N\ge0$ 的有限部分和，

$$\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{t^kA^k}{k!}=S\left(\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{t^k\Lambda^k}{k!}\right)S^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $k!$ is the factorial, with $0!=1$. On the right, every diagonal entry is a scalar exponential partial sum. As $N\to\infty$, these sums converge to $e^{t\lambda_i}$. Multiplication by the two fixed finite matrices uses only finite sums of entries and therefore preserves this limit. The left side converges to $e^{tA}$ by its definition, proving the formula without treating an infinite sum as a formal rearrangement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $k!$ 是阶乘，约定 $0!=1$。右边每个对角元都是标量指数的部分和；当 $N\to\infty$ 时，分别趋于 $e^{t\lambda_i}$。左右乘两个固定的有限矩阵只涉及元素的有限求和，所以保持这个极限。左边则按定义趋于 $e^{tA}$，由此证明公式，无需把无穷和当成形式符号随意重排。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the result and the scope of the method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检查结果与方法范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the proposed answer as $E(t)$. At zero, an exponential must satisfy $E(0)=I_n$. Its [[导数|derivative]] with respect to real $t$ must satisfy $E'(t)=AE(t)$, in particular $E'(0)=A$. For the example, direct differentiation and multiplication both give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把候选结果记为 $E(t)$。指数在零点必须满足 $E(0)=I_n$；对实时间 $t$ 求[[导数]]，还必须满足 $E'(t)=AE(t)$，特别是 $E'(0)=A$。本例直接求导与矩阵相乘都得到

$$E'(t)=AE(t)=\begin{pmatrix}e^t&(3e^{3t}-e^t)/2\\0&3e^{3t}\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero-time check alone is not sufficient, but it quickly detects many factor-order errors. The initial-value and differential-equation checks together identify the exponential by the [[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|uniqueness theorem]], applied to each column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只检查零时刻还不够，但能快速发现许多因子顺序错误。初值与微分方程两个检查合在一起，则可以逐列使用[[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|唯一性定理]]，确定候选就是矩阵指数。

> [!note] Repeated, zero and missing eigenvectors · 重复、零特征值与缺少特征向量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Repeated eigenvalues are allowed whenever an eigenvector basis exists. A zero eigenvalue contributes $e^{0t}=1$, not zero; in particular, $e^{t0}=I_n$. If $A$ is real but diagonalizable only over $\mathbb C$, the complex calculation still gives a real matrix for real $t$, as the real power series shows. If $A$ has no eigenvector basis even over $\mathbb C$, this shortcut is unavailable; the series and [[Jordan块的指数|Jordan-block exponential formulas]] remain usable. A [[Schur分解|complex Schur decomposition]] gives an upper-triangular matrix instead, whose off-diagonal entries still need to be handled.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 只要存在特征向量基，就允许重复特征值。零特征值贡献的是 $e^{0t}=1$，不是零；特别地，$e^{t0}=I_n$。若 $A$ 为实矩阵但只能在复数域对角化，复数计算对实时间 $t$ 仍给出实矩阵，这可从实系数的幂级数看出。若连复数域中都没有特征向量基，就不能使用这条捷径，但仍可用级数或[[Jordan块的指数|Jordan 块指数公式]]。[[Schur分解|复 Schur 分解]]给出的则是上三角矩阵，其中的非对角元仍需处理。
