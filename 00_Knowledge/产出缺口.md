# 产出缺口是实际产出相对于潜在产出的偏离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The output gap is the deviation of actual output from potential output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用同期间、同数量口径的实际产出 $Y$ 与[[潜在产出]] $Y^*>0$ 比较，常用百分比产出缺口为 $100(Y-Y^*)/Y^*$。正缺口表示高于潜能，负缺口表示低于潜能；有些资料报告水平差 $Y-Y^*$，读取时不能混用单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For actual output $Y$ and [[潜在产出|potential output]] $Y^*>0$ measured consistently, the percentage gap is commonly $100(Y-Y^*)/Y^*$. A positive gap means output above potential; a negative gap means below it. Some sources report the level difference instead, so units must be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Y=96,Y^*=100$，缺口为 −4%；后来 $Y=98,Y^*=102$，缺口约为 −3.92%。实际增长、缺口改善和正缺口是三个不同判断：此例前两项成立，第三项不成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $Y=96,Y^*=100$, the gap is −4%. Later, $Y=98,Y^*=102$ gives about −3.92%. Output growth, an improving gap, and a positive gap are distinct: the first two hold here, but the third does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

潜能是估计量，缺口也随模型与数据版本变化。正缺口可以提示需求压力，不能单独决定通胀；供给、预期和进口价格也可能变动。实时应用应保留当时可得的潜能估计，见[[经济数据的实时口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because potential is estimated, gaps depend on models and data vintages. A positive gap may indicate demand pressure but does not alone determine inflation. Real-time applications must retain the potential estimate available at the decision date; see [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data conventions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
