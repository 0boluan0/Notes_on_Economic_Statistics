# 财务报表是按会计准则呈现主体财务状况、经营成果和现金流量的整套报表与附注
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial statements are the accounting-standard-based set of statements and notes describing an entity's financial position, performance, and cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务报表（financial statements）把一个报告主体的会计信息组织成相互联系的报表与解释材料，供使用者理解资源、义务、收益及现金变化。本卡讨论普通非金融企业的一般目的财务报表；它不是银行账户流水，也不是分析者自行编制的估值模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial statements organise a reporting entity's accounting information into connected statements and explanatory material. They help readers understand resources, obligations, performance, and changes in cash. The scope here is general-purpose reporting by an ordinary non-financial business, rather than bank-account transactions or an analyst's valuation model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读一套报表时，分别找[[资产负债表]]、[[利润表]]及其他综合收益信息、[[现金流量表]]、[[所有者权益变动表]]和[[财务报表附注]]，还要保留所需的比较信息。利润与其他综合收益可以按适用准则合并或分表列报；“三张主表”是学习简称，不表示权益变动和附注可省略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Locate the [[资产负债表|balance sheet]], [[利润表|income statement]] and other-comprehensive-income information, [[现金流量表|cash-flow statement]], [[所有者权益变动表|statement of changes in equity]], and [[财务报表附注|notes]], together with the required comparatives. Profit or loss and other comprehensive income may appear together or separately under the applicable framework. The teaching shorthand “three statements” does not make equity changes or notes dispensable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认这是母公司个别报表还是集团合并报表、覆盖哪一期、使用什么币种和金额单位。将集团利润与母公司资产拼在一起，或将“万元”误读成“元”，即使加减法正确，也不是同一对象的分析。管理层讨论、审计报告与报表相互有用，但不因此都成为财务报表本身的组成项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify whether the entity is a parent alone or a consolidated group, the reporting period, currency, and scale. Combining group profit with parent-only assets, or misreading amounts in ten-thousands as single currency units, produces a scope error despite correct arithmetic. Management commentary and an auditor's report can assist interpretation without thereby becoming components of the statements themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
