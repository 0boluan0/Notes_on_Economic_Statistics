# 阈值策略按一维类型相对临界值选择行动，候选临界值通常由边际类型的事中无差异条件确定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A cutoff strategy selects actions according to whether a one-dimensional type lies above or below a threshold, which is usually identified by the interim indifference of the marginal type*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当类型 $t_i$ 有自然顺序且可选行动为两个时，**阈值策略**用一个临界值 $c_i$ 把类型空间分成两段。例如

$$
s_i(t_i)=
\begin{cases}
a^0,&t_i<c_i,\\
a^1,&t_i>c_i.
\end{cases}
$$

究竟是高类型选择 $a^1$，还是低类型选择 $a^1$，取决于收益差怎样随类型变化；“类型更高”本身不决定行动方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a player's type has a natural order and there are two actions, a **cutoff strategy** partitions the type space at a threshold $c_i$. Whether high or low types choose $a^1$ depends on how the payoff difference varies with type; the ordering of types alone does not determine the direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 由边际类型给出候选临界值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Marginal Type Determines a Candidate Cutoff*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先假设其他玩家采用临界值 $c_{-i}$，再用玩家 $i$ 的[[事中信念]]计算两行动的收益差

$$
\Delta_i(t_i;c_{-i})
=U_i(a^1\mid t_i,c_{-i})-U_i(a^0\mid t_i,c_{-i}).
$$

若 $c_i$ 位于内部，边际类型应满足

$$
\Delta_i(c_i;c_{-i})=0.
$$

对称候选还要令 $c_i=c_{-i}=c$，联立求解。这里用的是给定自身类型后的条件概率；类型相关时，不能把无条件边际代入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume the other players use cutoffs $c_{-i}$ and compute the payoff difference under player $i$'s [[事中信念|interim belief]]. An interior marginal type satisfies $\Delta_i(c_i;c_{-i})=0$; a symmetric candidate additionally imposes $c_i=c_{-i}=c$. With correlated types, this calculation must use the distribution conditional on the player's own type rather than an unconditional marginal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 无差异方程只产生候选
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Indifference Produces Only a Candidate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求出 $\Delta_i(c_i;c_{-i})=0$ 还不等于证明阈值均衡。必须继续检查：收益差在类型上是否单调或满足 single-crossing；临界值两侧是否真的分别偏好规定行动；端点解是否被遗漏；方程是否有多个根。若收益差反复变号，最佳反应可能不是单一阈值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving the indifference equation does not prove a cutoff equilibrium. One must still check monotonicity or single crossing, verify the prescribed preference on both sides of the cutoff, include possible boundary solutions, and inspect multiple roots. If the payoff difference changes sign repeatedly, the best response need not have a single cutoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 课程中的最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Course Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/03_game theory/05_贝叶斯博弈#4. 含噪声的性别博弈|含噪声的性别博弈]]的手写图只给出支持集 $[0,a]$。为复现课堂使用的概率比例，修订后的课程页明确补充 $a>0$ 且 $\varepsilon,\delta\overset{ind}{\sim}U[0,a]$。在玩家 1 低 $\varepsilon$ 选 Pub、玩家 2 低 $\delta$ 选 Cafe 的阈值口径下，

$$
\Delta_1(\varepsilon;\delta^*)
=\varepsilon-2+\frac{6\delta^*}{a},
\qquad
\Delta_2(\delta;\varepsilon^*)
=\delta-2+\frac{6\varepsilon^*}{a}.
$$

对称无差异给出 $c^*=\bar\delta=\bar\varepsilon=2a/(6+a)$，而代回后 $\Delta_1=\varepsilon-c^*$、$\Delta_2=\delta-c^*$，因此两侧行动方向确实正确。但它只是对称阈值均衡：$0<a<6$ 时还有两个非对称阈值解，$a=6$ 时有一整段阈值解，只有 $a>6$ 时裁剪后的阈值最佳反应才只交于这个对称解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The handwritten diagrams for the [[01_Math/03_game theory/05_贝叶斯博弈#4. 含噪声的性别博弈|noisy Battle of the Sexes]] show only the support $[0,a]$. To reproduce the probability ratios used in class, the revised course page explicitly supplements the model with $a>0$ and $\varepsilon,\delta\overset{ind}{\sim}U[0,a]$. Under the convention that player 1 chooses Pub at low $\varepsilon$ and player 2 chooses Cafe at low $\delta$, the payoff differences are those displayed above. Symmetric indifference gives $c^*=\bar\delta=\bar\varepsilon=2a/(6+a)$, and substitution yields $\Delta_1=\varepsilon-c^*$ and $\Delta_2=\delta-c^*$, verifying both sides of the cutoff. It is only the symmetric cutoff equilibrium: two asymmetric cutoff solutions remain when $0<a<6$, a continuum remains at $a=6$, and only for $a>6$ do the clipped cutoff best responses intersect solely at the symmetric solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
