# 结构性共线性由建模构造产生，数据性共线性来自样本支持中不同变量缺少独立变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Structural multicollinearity is created by model construction, whereas data-based multicollinearity comes from insufficient independent variation among distinct variables in the observed support*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 两种来源
> **结构性共线性**是分析者把原变量加工成新的设计列时产生的依赖，例如截距加全套类别虚拟变量、$X$ 与 $X^2$、主效应与交互项。**数据性共线性**来自收集到的样本，例如某项政策几乎只在一个地区发生，或年龄与实际经验在当前样本中几乎同步。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Structural multicollinearity** is induced when the analyst constructs design columns, such as an intercept with a complete set of category indicators, $X$ with $X^2$, or main effects with interactions. **Data-based multicollinearity** arises from the observed support, such as a policy occurring almost exclusively in one region or age moving almost one-for-one with actual experience in the sample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么来源决定修复
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the source determines the repair*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构性问题常能通过正确参数化处理：给类别变量设基准组、删除重复编码、在保留层级原则时重新表达多项式或交互项。这里修的是“同一个模型怎样写”，而不是凭空创造新数据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural problems can often be handled by a correct parameterization: choose a reference category, remove duplicate encodings, or re-express polynomial and interaction terms while preserving hierarchy. This changes how the same model is written; it does not create new information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数据性问题则不能靠改单位解决。若目标要求比较在样本中几乎总是一起变化的两个因素，需要扩大支持、改变研究设计、缩小 estimand，或诚实报告只能识别某个组合。机械删一列也许降低 VIF，却可能把问题改写成另一个问题，而非回答原问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Data-based problems cannot be repaired by changing units. If the estimand requires separating factors that almost always move together in the sample, the response may require broader support, a new design, a narrower estimand, or an honest statement that only a combination is identified. Mechanical deletion may lower a VIF by changing the question rather than answering the original one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两类来源可以同时出现。例如观察数据中 `income` 与 `age` 高度相关，又在模型中加入 `age`、`age²` 和与 treatment 的交互项。诊断时应逐层指出哪个弱方向来自编码、哪个来自样本支持，而不是给整个数据集贴一个“有共线性”的总标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both sources can coexist. Income and age may be highly related in observational data while the analyst also includes age, age squared, and treatment interactions. A useful diagnosis identifies which weak direction comes from coding and which from sample support instead of attaching one undifferentiated label to the dataset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 截距加上四个互斥且穷尽的地区虚拟变量，属于哪一类共线性？最先应做什么？
>
> **答案：** 属于结构性完全共线；先明确基准组或等价约束，而不是收集更多同样编码的数据。
