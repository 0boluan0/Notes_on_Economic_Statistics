# Structural and data-based multicollinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*结构性与数据性共线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When two regression features seem difficult to separate, ask how that relationship arose. Did the model builder create one column from others, or did the observed people, places or occasions provide little separate variation? **Structural** and **data-based multicollinearity** distinguish these sources. They help explain a [[多重共线性|column-dependence problem]]; they do not, by themselves, say whether the dependence is exact, how large an estimation error will be, or which variable should be removed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归中两项特征难以分开时，先问这种关系怎样产生：是建模时用其他列构造了这列，还是实际观察到的人、地点或时点没有提供多少分别变化？**结构性共线**与**数据性共线**区分这两种来源。它们帮助解释[[多重共线性|设计列依赖问题]]，本身不说明依赖是否精确、估计误差有多大，也不直接决定删哪个变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Dependence introduced by constructing features

A [[设计矩阵|design matrix]] has one observation per row and one included feature per column. Some columns are built rather than measured separately. Examples include category indicators, squares and products. Construction can create an exact identity, or it can produce strongly related columns on the particular observed range.

For four mutually exclusive regions covering every observation, let $D_g$ be 1 for region $g$ and 0 otherwise, with $g=1,2,3,4$. Every row satisfies $D_1+D_2+D_3+D_4=1$. Thus all four indicators plus an intercept contain a redundant column. This is an exact relationship forced by the [[多类别指示编码|coding]], whatever the region frequencies. More rows using that same coding cannot remove the identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 构造特征时引入的依赖

[[设计矩阵]]每行对应一个观测，每列对应一个纳入特征。有些列不是分别测量的，而是构造出来的，例如类别指示、平方项与乘积项。构造可能制造精确恒等式，也可能只在实际观察范围内产生很强的列关联。

若四个地区互斥且覆盖全部观察，令 $D_g$ 在属于地区 $g$ 时取 1，否则取 0，其中 $g=1,2,3,4$。每行都有 $D_1+D_2+D_3+D_4=1$。因此四条指示列加截距包含冗余，这是[[多类别指示编码|编码方式]]强制的精确关系，与各地区频数无关。按相同编码增加行数，不能消除恒等式。

> [!example] A square is not automatically a redundant column / 平方项不自动等于冗余列
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For observed values $x=(-1,0,1)$, the square column is $x^2=(1,0,1)$. No constants $a,b$ make $x^2=a+bx$ at all three observations: the middle row forces $a=0$, while the two outer rows would require both $b=-1$ and $b=1$. Hence the columns $1,x,x^2$ are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]. The sample correlation between $x$ and $x^2$ is even zero.
>
> In contrast, if the only observed values are 0 and 1, then $x^2=x$ row by row. The same formula for constructing a square now gives exact redundancy. A deterministic nonlinear relationship is different from a linear combination with fixed coefficients.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若观察值为 $x=(-1,0,1)$，平方列为 $x^2=(1,0,1)$。不存在常数 $a,b$，让三个观察都满足 $x^2=a+bx$：中间一行强制 $a=0$，两端却分别要求 $b=-1$ 与 $b=1$。因此 $1,x,x^2$ 三列[[线性无关]]，$x$ 与 $x^2$ 的样本相关甚至为零。
>
> 若观察值只有 0 和 1，则每行都有 $x^2=x$。同一个平方构造，此时就产生精确冗余。确定的非线性关系与固定系数的线性组合，是两回事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This distinction matters for polynomials and interactions: being computed from the same raw variable does not automatically violate [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|the rank condition]]. On a narrow range far from zero, however, $x$ and $x^2$ may look almost like an affine pair. Changing the reference point can then alter their correlation and the meaning of the coefficients; [[中心化共线性边界|centering]] explains what is preserved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一区分也适用于多项式和交互项：来自同一个原变量，不自动违反[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|秩条件]]。但在远离零点的狭窄范围里，$x$ 与 $x^2$ 可能近似为仿射关系。更换参照点可以改变两列相关性及系数含义；[[中心化共线性边界]]解释哪些量保留不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Dependence in the observed combinations

Suppose a policy indicator $P$ and a region indicator $L$ were recorded separately. If every observed policy recipient is in that region and every observed person in that region receives the policy, then $P=L$ in this sample. The columns are exactly duplicated here, even though the variables have different meanings and the coding did not require that equality. If a few observations break the pattern, the dependence may instead be approximate.

Subtracting each column's mean does not create the missing comparisons: $P-L=0$ remains an identity when $P=L$. A defensible collection plan might add policy variation within a region, or alter the frequencies of combinations already observed. Previously unseen combinations are not always necessary to reduce a VIF; see [[新增样本与共线性|more observations and multicollinearity]]. Whether these comparisons identify a causal effect is a separate design question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实际观测组合中的依赖

设政策指示 $P$ 与地区指示 $L$ 是分别记录的。若样本里每个接受政策的人都在该地区，而且该地区的每个人都接受政策，就有样本内的 $P=L$。两列在这里精确重复，虽然变量含义不同，编码也没有强制它们相等。如果少数观察打破这种模式，依赖则可能是近似的。

逐列减去均值不能创造缺失的比较：当 $P=L$ 时，$P-L=0$ 这一恒等式仍在。有依据的数据收集可以增加地区内部的政策变化，也可以改变已经出现的组合的频数；降低 VIF 不总需要此前未见的组合，见[[新增样本与共线性]]。这些比较能否识别因果效应，仍是另一个设计问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the source matters for a response
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么来源决定修复

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For redundant coding, choose an equivalent full-rank representation and explain what its coefficients mean. For a complete polynomial or interaction model, a new reference point can preserve the fitted function while improving interpretation or computation. It supplies no additional observations. For limited observed combinations, changing units alone cannot make the same target better supported; options include informative new observations, reporting an estimable combination or acknowledging uncertainty. [[共线性补救原则|The response follows the target]], not the label alone.

The two sources can coexist, and the terminology is not a claim that they form a unique exhaustive classification. An observed age–income relationship can coexist with constructed age-squared and interaction columns. State the actual identity or approximate relation and where it comes from. Here “structural” describes feature construction; it does not mean that a structural causal equation has been identified, nor does either label establish [[共线性与内生性|exogeneity or endogeneity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对冗余编码，应选等价的满秩表达，并说明系数代表什么。对完整多项式或交互模型，更换参照点可以保留拟合函数，同时改善解释或计算，但不会提供额外观察。若问题在于实际组合有限，仅换单位不能让同一目标获得更充分的支持；可考虑有信息的新观察、报告可估组合，或承认不确定性。[[共线性补救原则|处理应跟着目标走]]，不能只跟着分类标签走。

两类来源可以同时存在，这套术语也不意味着存在唯一、穷尽的分类。年龄与收入的观察关联，可以和构造出来的年龄平方、交互列同时存在。应写清实际恒等式或近似关系，以及它的来源。这里的“结构性”指特征构造，不意味着已经识别结构因果方程；两类标签也都不能判定[[共线性与内生性|外生或内生]]。
