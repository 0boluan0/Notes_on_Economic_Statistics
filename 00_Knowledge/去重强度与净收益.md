# 更激进的预训练去重同时改变重复暴露、记忆机会与内容覆盖，因此不保证模型质量单调提高
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*More aggressive pretraining deduplication changes repeated exposure, memorization opportunities, and coverage, so model quality need not improve monotonically*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 方法边界
> 去重范围扩大，通常会减少重复样本的暴露和逐字记忆机会；同时也可能删除标准定义、固定术语、独立报道或低资源表达。净效果取决于语料、匹配规则、训练预算、模型和评测目标，不能把某一论文中的收益写成普适定律。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Wider deduplication usually reduces repeated exposure and opportunities for verbatim memorization. It can also remove standard definitions, conventional wording, independent reports, or low-resource expression. Net effects depend on the corpus, matching rule, training budget, model, and evaluation target; a gain in one study is not a universal law.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三条同时变化的路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three pathways change together*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **暴露：** 若 sampler 不补回被删副本，重复内容在 [[预训练分布]] 中的质量下降；若之后重新设权，方向要按新 sampler 重算。
2. **记忆：** 长序列被反复训练通常增加逐字复现机会；Lee et al. 的受控设置中，去重使记忆文本的无提示生成约减少一个数量级。
3. **覆盖：** 相似并不总是冗余。不同来源对同一事实的独立表述、少数语言和规范用语可能被激进规则误删。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, repeated content loses mass in the [[预训练分布]] only when later sampling does not compensate. Second, repeated long sequences create more opportunities for verbatim reproduction; Lee et al. report roughly an order-of-magnitude reduction in memorized emissions in their controlled setting. Third, similarity is not always redundancy: independent accounts, minority languages, and conventional wording can be removed by aggressive rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“更激进”指命中或删除范围更大，不写成“阈值更高”：相似度阈值、距离阈值和 LSH 配置的方向并不相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“More aggressive” means a wider matched or removed set. It should not be rewritten as “a higher threshold,” because similarity thresholds, distance thresholds, and LSH configurations use different directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不同论文结果不矛盾
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why different empirical results need not conflict*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lee et al. 报告更少记忆、更快达到相同或更好准确率，并在部分设置下降低 perplexity。Pythia 在其 MinHashLSH 设置下明确报告没有清晰的语言建模收益。Llama 3 发现激进行去重会删掉部分高质量文本，但整体实验仍有明显净改善。三者的语料、预算、重复结构和指标不同，只能说明去重效果必须在目标设置中验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lee et al. report less memorization, faster attainment of equal or better accuracy, and lower perplexity in some settings. Pythia reports no clear language-modeling benefit under its MinHashLSH setup. Llama 3 notes that aggressive line deduplication removed some high-quality text while its overall evaluation still improved strongly. Different corpora, budgets, duplication structures, and metrics make these setting-specific results rather than contradictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某规则删除率最高。能否据此选它作为最佳去重配置？
>
> **答案：** 不能。应在匹配 token/compute 预算下同时比较各域保留率、干净 held-out loss、下游任务和记忆或抽取指标。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Lee et al. (2022), *Deduplicating Training Data Makes Language Models Better*](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.577/)：受控比较去重对记忆、训练—验证重叠、准确率和 perplexity 的影响。
- [Biderman et al. (2023), *Pythia*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/biderman23a.html)：第 2.2 与 2.6 节记录 0.87 MinHashLSH 设置，并报告该设置下没有清晰语言建模收益。
- [Grattafiori et al. (2024), *The Llama 3 Herd of Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783)：第 3.1.1 节记录激进行去重同时删除部分高质量文本，但在其整体实验中产生净改善。
