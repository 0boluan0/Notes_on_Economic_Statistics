# 信用利差不能直接当作违约概率因为回收、风险价格与非违约成分共同影响它

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credit spread is not a default probability: recovery, the price of risk, and nondefault components jointly affect it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利差是两个收益率的差，[[违约概率口径|PD]] 则是特定事件在指定期限内发生的概率。即使不考虑流动性等差异，同样的违约概率配上不同回收额，也会形成不同债券价格；反过来，同一个价格可以对应不同的概率与回收组合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A spread is a difference between yields, whereas [[违约概率口径|PD]] concerns a specified event over a specified horizon. Even without liquidity differences, equal default probabilities with different recoveries imply different bond prices. Conversely, one price can correspond to different probability–recovery combinations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在到期统一回收的[[单期信用债定价]]模型中，$q^{\mathbb Q}=2\%$、损失率 60%，与 $q^{\mathbb Q}=3\%$、损失率 40%，都使每单位到期承诺的风险中性期望损失为 1.2%。其他条件相同，两者有相同价格和利差，却有不同的定价违约概率。若不知道回收假设，单靠该价格不能区分它们。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the terminal-recovery [[单期信用债定价|one-period pricing model]], $q^{\mathbb Q}=2\%$ with 60% loss severity and $q^{\mathbb Q}=3\%$ with 40% severity both imply a risk-neutral expected loss of 1.2% of the promised terminal payment. Otherwise identical claims have the same price and spread but different pricing default probabilities. The price alone cannot distinguish them without recovery assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格反推的 $\mathbb Q$ 概率还含风险价格，不能直接当作预测实际违约频率的 $\mathbb P$ 概率。现实报价另受流动性、税收、融资和可选性影响；这些因素的分解需要模型与数据，并不是把观测利差减几个固定常数就能唯一识别。[[Merton违约模型]]给出一种有条件的定价与物理概率区分，不是替所有债券提供同一个换算系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price-implied $\mathbb Q$ probabilities also reflect the price of risk and cannot be read as $\mathbb P$ forecasts of actual default frequencies. Liquidity, taxes, funding, and optionality further affect observed quotes. Separating them requires models and data, not a uniquely identified subtraction of fixed constants. The [[Merton违约模型|Merton model]] provides one conditional distinction between pricing and physical probabilities, not a universal conversion factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
