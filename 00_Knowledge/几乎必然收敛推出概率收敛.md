# 几乎必然收敛推出依概率收敛，而反向一般不成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Almost-sure convergence implies convergence in probability, but convergence in probability does not imply almost-sure convergence in general*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 路径稳定强于偏离概率消失
> 若 $X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}X$，则必有 $X_n\xrightarrow{p}X$。反向一般不成立：逐时点的边际偏离概率可以随 $n\to\infty$ 趋于零，但偏离仍可能沿几乎每条路径反复出现。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Almost-sure convergence always implies convergence in probability. The converse can fail: the marginal probability of a visible error at index $n$ may vanish as $n\to\infty$ even though such errors recur along almost every path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么正向一定成立

固定任意 $\varepsilon>0$，并记

$$
A_n=\{|X_n-X|>\varepsilon\},
\qquad
B_N=\bigcup_{n\ge N}A_n.
$$

若 $X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}X$，那么几乎每条路径只会落入有限多个 $A_n$，所以

$$
P\!\left(\bigcap_{N=1}^{\infty}B_N\right)=0.
$$

事件列 $B_1\supseteq B_2\supseteq\cdots$ 递减，因此概率的上连续性给出 $P(B_N)\to0$。对每个 $n\ge N$ 又有 $A_n\subseteq B_N$，从而

$$
0\le P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)=P(A_n)\le P(B_N)\longrightarrow0.
$$

这对每个 $\varepsilon>0$ 都成立，正是依概率收敛的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed $\varepsilon>0$, let $A_n$ be the event that the error exceeds $\varepsilon$, and let $B_N$ be the event that such an error occurs at least once from index $N$ onward. Almost-sure convergence says that the decreasing events $B_N$ shrink to a null event. Continuity of probability from above gives $P(B_N)\to0$, while $A_n\subseteq B_N$ for every $n\ge N$. Hence $P(A_n)\to0$, which is convergence in probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么反向一般不成立

令 $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}[0,1)$。对每个 $m\ge0$ 与 $j=0,\ldots,2^m-1$，定义

$$
X_{2^m+j}
=\mathbf 1\!\left\{
\frac{j}{2^m}\le U<\frac{j+1}{2^m}
\right\}.
$$

索引 $n=2^m,\ldots,2^{m+1}-1$ 构成第 $m$ 个区块。区块中的每个变量都满足

$$
P(X_n=1)=2^{-m}\longrightarrow0,
$$

所以 $X_n\xrightarrow{p}0$。但对每个 $U\in[0,1)$，每个区块恰好有一个 $X_n(U)=1$；从第二个区块起也总有若干个 $X_n(U)=0$。因此同一条路径上会反复出现 $1$ 和 $0$，序列不收敛到 $0$，也不几乎必然收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partition $[0,1)$ into dyadic intervals at each level and let the variables visit those intervals one by one. Every variable in level $m$ equals one with probability $2^{-m}$, so the sequence converges to zero in probability. Yet every outcome belongs to exactly one interval at every level, so a one reappears in every block while zeros also keep appearing. The paths therefore do not converge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)\to0$ 对每个 $\varepsilon>0$ 都成立，为什么还不能说几乎每条路径最终永久靠近 $X$？
>
> **答案：** 边际偏离概率 $P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)$ 随 $n$ 趋于零，只控制各个时点的截面；它没有排除小概率偏离事件沿同一条路径出现无穷多次。要得到几乎必然收敛，还需要控制整条无限路径。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Vanishing marginal error probabilities control one index at a time. They do not rule out rare error events recurring infinitely often along the same path; almost-sure convergence requires control of the whole infinite path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
