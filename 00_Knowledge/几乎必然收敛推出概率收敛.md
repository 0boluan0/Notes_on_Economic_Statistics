<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# From almost-sure to probability convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逐路径收敛与逐时点超差概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sequence that eventually stays close to its target on almost every path must also have a vanishing chance of a large error at a late index. This links [[几乎必然收敛|almost-sure convergence]], which follows an entire realised sequence, to [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]], which checks an error probability at each index separately. The reverse direction can fail because a shrinking set of bad outcomes may keep moving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果几乎每条路径最终都持续靠近目标，那么在足够晚的索引上出现明显误差的概率也必须趋于零。这连接了观察整条已实现序列的[[几乎必然收敛]]，以及逐个索引检查误差概率的[[依概率收敛]]。反方向可能失败，因为越来越小的坏结果集合，可以不断换位置。

> [!important] The implication · 正向保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For real-valued $X_n$ and $X$ on one probability space,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}X\quad\Longrightarrow\quad X_n\xrightarrow pX.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There is no independence or finite-moment assumption. The target $X$ may be random.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一概率空间上的实值变量满足这个蕴含；不要求独立或有限矩，目标 $X$ 可以随机。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Control the whole remaining tail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么正向一定成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$. Let $A_n$ mean “the error is too large at index $n$,” and $B_N$ mean “such an error occurs at least once from index $N$ onwards”:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $\varepsilon>0$。用 $A_n$ 表示“第 $n$ 个索引超差”，用 $B_N$ 表示“从第 $N$ 个索引起，至少还会发生一次超差”：

$$A_n=\{|X_n-X|>\varepsilon\},\qquad B_N=\bigcup_{n\ge N}A_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The events $B_N$ decrease as $N$ grows: a later starting point includes fewer opportunities for an error. Their intersection is the event of errors recurring arbitrarily late. Almost-sure convergence makes that event have probability zero, since on a convergent path only finitely many errors exceed the fixed tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$N$ 增大时，$B_N$ 递减，因为开始得越晚，剩下的超差机会越少。它们的交集表示无论从多晚开始，后面仍会出现超差。几乎必然收敛使这个事件的概率为零，因为一条收敛路径超过固定容忍值的次数只能有限。

$$P\!\left(\bigcap_{N=1}^{\infty}B_N\right)=0,\qquad P(B_N)\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second conclusion uses continuity of probability for decreasing events: their probabilities approach the probability of the intersection. This follows from the countable-additivity rule in a [[概率空间|probability space]] by splitting the successive differences into disjoint events. Finally $A_n\subseteq B_n$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二个结论使用递减事件的概率连续性：事件的概率趋于交集的概率。这可以从[[概率空间]]的可列可加规则推出，把相邻事件的差拆成互不相交的事件即可。最后，由 $A_n\subseteq B_n$，

$$0\le P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)=P(A_n)\le P(B_n)\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the tolerance was arbitrary, this proves convergence in probability. A path can have its own cutoff; the proof never needs one finite index that works for all paths simultaneously.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于容忍值任意，这就证明了依概率收敛。每条路径可以有自己的截止点；证明不需要找到一个适用于所有路径的有限共同索引。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A small bad set that visits everyone
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么反向一般不成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Draw one $U$ [[连续均匀分布|uniformly on $[0,1)$]]. At level $m=0,1,2,\ldots$, cut this interval into $2^m$ equal half-open pieces and visit them from left to right. During a visit, report one if $U$ lies in that piece and zero otherwise. The first levels visit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只抽一次[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布在 $[0,1)$]] 上的 $U$。在第 $m=0,1,2,\ldots$ 层，把区间平分成 $2^m$ 个左闭右开的小区间，依次从左向右访问。访问一个区间时，若 $U$ 落在其中就报告 1，否则报告 0。前几层访问的是

$$
[0,1);\qquad [0,\tfrac12),[\tfrac12,1);\qquad
[0,\tfrac14),[\tfrac14,\tfrac12),[\tfrac12,\tfrac34),[\tfrac34,1).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] formula indexes these visits in a single sequence. For $j=0,\ldots,2^m-1$, set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]把访问过程编号成一条序列。对 $j=0,\ldots,2^m-1$，令

$$X_{2^m+j}=\mathbf1\!\left\{\frac{j}{2^m}\le U<\frac{j+1}{2^m}\right\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The indices in level $m$ run from $2^m$ to $2^{m+1}-1$. Each variable in that level equals one with probability $2^{-m}$. Therefore for $0<\varepsilon<1$, $P(|X_n|>\varepsilon)=2^{-m}\to0$ as $n\to\infty$; for $\varepsilon\ge1$ it is zero. Hence $X_n\xrightarrow p0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 $m$ 层的索引从 $2^m$ 到 $2^{m+1}-1$。该层每个变量等于 1 的概率都是 $2^{-m}$。因此，当 $0<\varepsilon<1$ 时，$P(|X_n|>\varepsilon)=2^{-m}$ 随 $n\to\infty$ 趋于零；当 $\varepsilon\ge1$ 时则为零。所以 $X_n\xrightarrow p0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
But every $U\in[0,1)$ belongs to exactly one piece at each level. Each path therefore has a one in **every** level, and from $m=1$ onwards also has zeros in every level. For example, $U=0.3$ starts with blocks $(1)$, $(1,0)$, $(0,1,0,0)$. Ones never stop recurring, so no path converges to zero. For tolerance $1/2$, the event of an error somewhere after any fixed $N$ is the whole space: $P(B_N)=1$, even while $P(A_n)\to0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但每个 $U\in[0,1)$ 在每一层都恰好属于一个小区间。因此每条路径在**每一层**都会出现一次 1，从 $m=1$ 起每层也都有 0。例如，$U=0.3$ 的前几块为 $(1)$、$(1,0)$、$(0,1,0,0)$。1 永远会再出现，所以没有路径收敛到零。对容忍值 $1/2$，从任意固定 $N$ 往后某处超差的事件都是整个空间：尽管 $P(A_n)\to0$，却始终有 $P(B_N)=1$。

> [!tip] One index versus infinitely many future indices · 一个索引与无限个未来索引
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Small $P(A_n)$ controls each individual visit. Almost-sure convergence requires errors to stop along almost every path for each fixed tolerance. A conclusion about each individual probability cannot simply be transferred to the probability of an infinite union.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 小的 $P(A_n)$ 控制的是每一次单独访问。几乎必然收敛要求对每个固定容忍值，几乎每条路径上的超差最终停止；不能把逐个概率的结论直接转给无限个事件之并的概率。

- [MIT 6.436J, Lecture 16, Theorem 5 and §3.1, pp. 10–12](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-436j-fundamentals-of-probability-fall-2018/b4ebf022b1060fcce920227d934dd6f0_MIT6_436JF18_lec16.pdf): the forward implication and failure of its converse. - MIT 6.436J 第 16 讲定理 5 与第 3.1 节，第 10–12 页：核对正向蕴含与反向失败；本卡直接使用递减事件证明，不依赖控制收敛定理。
- Durrett 第五版第 1.1 节与第 2.3 节：核对单调事件的概率连续性及两种收敛的区别。保留本卡原有的二进分层访问构造，并逐项验证端点和索引。
