# Pivots
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*主元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **pivot** is the leading nonzero coefficient in a row after the equations have been arranged in staircase form. It marks which variable that row can determine during back-substitution. Reading the pivots tells us which variables are determined by equations and which can be chosen as [[自由变量|free variables]] once the system is consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**主元**是方程排成阶梯形后，每条非零行最前面的非零系数。它标记这一行可以在回代时确定哪个变量。读出主元，就能分清哪些变量由方程确定，哪些在系统相容时可以作为[[自由变量]]选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[高斯消元|Gaussian elimination]] uses reversible combinations of rows to make that staircase: zero rows go at the bottom, and each successive leading nonzero entry lies strictly farther right. The entries below each leading entry are zero. A row may begin with several zeros; “leading” means the first **nonzero** entry, not necessarily the first column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[高斯消元]]通过可逆的行组合形成阶梯：零行放在底部，下一条非零行的首个非零位置严格向右移动，每个首非零元的下方都是零。一行开头可以有多个零；“首个”指第一个**非零**元素，不一定是第一列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the entry, position and column separately
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider this coefficient matrix, with columns corresponding in order to $x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的系数矩阵，四列按顺序对应 $x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4$：

$$U=\begin{bmatrix}
2&1&0&3\\
0&0&-4&1\\
0&0&0&0
\end{bmatrix}.$$

> [!example] Follow the first nonzero entry · 沿首个非零元读
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Row 1 has pivot $2$ at position $(1,1)$; row 2 has pivot $-4$ at position $(2,3)$. Thus columns 1 and 3 are **pivot columns**, and $x_1,x_3$ are **pivot variables**. The zero row contributes no pivot. Column 2 contains a nonzero number, but no row starts its nonzero entries there, so it is not a pivot column.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一行的主元是位置 $(1,1)$ 上的 $2$；第二行是位置 $(2,3)$ 上的 $-4$。因此第1、3列为**主元列**，$x_1,x_3$ 为**主元变量**。零行没有主元。第二列虽然含有非零数，却没有任何一行的首非零元落在那里，因此不是主元列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the second equation of $Ux=b$, where $b=(b_1,b_2,b_3)^T$ is the right-hand side and $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], is $-4x_3+x_4=b_2$. Once $x_4$ is chosen, the nonzero pivot allows division by $-4$ to determine $x_3$. The first equation then determines $x_1$ after $x_2,x_4$ are chosen. The last row still requires $b_3=0$; a pivot pattern alone does not establish that a particular right-hand side is possible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$Ux=b$ 的第二条方程是 $-4x_3+x_4=b_2$，其中右端向量为 $b=(b_1,b_2,b_3)^T$，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。选定 $x_4$ 后，非零主元允许除以 $-4$，从而确定 $x_3$。选定 $x_2,x_4$ 后，第一条方程确定 $x_1$。末行仍要求 $b_3=0$；只看主元图案，还不能断言某个右端一定可达。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pivot is an entry, a pivot position is its row-and-column location, and a pivot column is a whole column. In the original matrix, “pivot columns” means the column indices identified after reduction; their entries need not already look like the staircase. Those indices are used when [[RREF读取列空间基|selecting original columns for a column-space basis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主元是一个元素，主元位置是行列坐标，主元列则是一整列。说原矩阵的“主元列”，指的是化简后识别出的列编号；原矩阵中的元素不必已经呈现阶梯形。[[RREF读取列空间基|从原矩阵选列空间基]]时，使用的正是这些编号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Values may change while the positions remain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pivot need not be $1$. Dividing the first row of $U$ by $2$ and the second by $-4$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主元不必等于 $1$。把 $U$ 的第一行除以 $2$、第二行除以 $-4$，得到

$$R=\begin{bmatrix}
1&\tfrac12&0&\tfrac32\\
0&0&1&-\tfrac14\\
0&0&0&0
\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pivot values are now $1$, but the pivot columns remain 1 and 3. This matrix is also in [[行最简形|reduced row echelon form]]: each pivot is the only nonzero entry in its column. For a fixed column order, legal row operations lead to the [[RREF存在唯一性|same final reduced form]] and hence the same pivot-column indices. The numerical pivot values in an intermediate echelon form need not agree. If columns are exchanged, [[列置换后的解向量|keep track of the variable order]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主元值变成了 $1$，主元列仍是第1、3列。这里每个主元所在列的其余元素也都为零，因此已经是[[行最简形]]。列顺序固定时，合法行操作得到[[RREF存在唯一性|同一个最终行最简形]]，所以主元列编号相同；中间阶梯形里的主元数值却可以不同。若交换列，则必须[[列置换后的解向量|追踪变量顺序]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An original diagonal entry is not automatically a pivot. For instance, $\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$ has zero diagonal entries but becomes the identity after swapping its rows, so it has two pivots. Conversely, $\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}$ has two nonzero diagonal entries but only one pivot after elimination. The [[主元与自由变量|number of coefficient pivots]] equals the [[矩阵秩|rank]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原矩阵的对角元并不会自动成为主元。例如 $\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$ 的对角元都是零，交换两行后却成为单位矩阵，因而有两个主元。反过来，$\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}$ 有两个非零对角元，消元后却只有一个主元。[[主元与自由变量|系数主元的个数]]等于[[矩阵秩|秩]]。

> [!warning] The augmented column is not an unknown · 增广列不是未知量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $[A\mid b]$, where $A$ contains the coefficients and $b$ the right-hand sides, a pivot in the final column represents a row $0=c$ with $c\ne0$, so the system is inconsistent. It is not a pivot variable: the right-hand-side column has no corresponding unknown. Count coefficient pivots when discussing the variables of $Ax=b$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[A\mid b]$ 中，$A$ 放系数、$b$ 放右端；末列出现主元，表示一行 $0=c$ 且 $c\ne0$，因此系统无解。它不对应主元变量，因为右端列没有对应未知量。讨论 $Ax=b$ 的变量时，应数系数部分的主元。
