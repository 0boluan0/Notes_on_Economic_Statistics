# 可转换债券赋予持有人按约定条件把债权转换为发行人股权的选择权

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A convertible bond gives its holder the option to exchange debt for the issuer's equity under specified terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里讨论持有人可自主选择转股的普通可转债：在约定时间和条件下，持有人可以放弃相应债权，换取规定数量的发行人股票；不转股时保留合同允许的债务请求权。转股比例、可转股期间和调整条款都属于权利定义的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This entry concerns ordinary voluntary convertibles: the holder may surrender the relevant debt claim for a specified number of issuer shares at the permitted times and under the agreed conditions. Otherwise, the holder retains the debt rights allowed by the contract. The conversion ratio, exercise window, and adjustment provisions are part of the right itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如每张面值 100 的债券可换 2 股，转股比例为 2 股／张。转股不是同时拿回 100 本金又免费得到 2 股，而是以被转换的债权换股票；股票此后的现金流与治理权按[[股权]]规则处理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a bond with face value 100 converts into two shares, its conversion ratio is two shares per bond. Conversion does not return the 100 principal while awarding two free shares: it exchanges the debt claim for stock, whose subsequent distribution and governance rights are those of [[股权|equity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

强制转换、达到监管触发点的资本工具，以及由发行人决定交付股票的反向可转产品，不能直接套用本卡的自愿选择。普通可转债还可能叠加赎回或回售条款，应把各项权利分别读清。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mandatory conversion, regulatory-trigger capital instruments, and reverse convertibles where the issuer can deliver stock do not inherit this voluntary-choice interpretation. An ordinary convertible may also contain calls or puts, which must be read separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [FINRA, Corporate and Agency Bond Data Glossary, Convertible](https://www.finra.org/finra-data/fixed-income/corp-and-agency/glossary)：已重开特定时点可转为股权、通常由持有人选择的定义。
- [Damodaran, Chapter 28 solutions，PDF 第 1 页，题 28-3](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/cfsol/ch28sol.pdf#page=1)：已重开转股使原债券终止、区别于独立认股权证的说明。2 股／张为自拟例。
- [FINRA, Reverse Convertibles: Complex Investments，“What Is a Reverse Convertible?”](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/reverse-convertibles-complex-investments)：已重开该节用于区分把股票交付选择让给发行人的反向结构，不把其名称当作普通转股权。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [FINRA, Convertible data definition](https://www.finra.org/finra-data/fixed-income/corp-and-agency/glossary), reopened, identifies conversion into equity at specified times, usually at holder discretion.
- [Damodaran, Chapter 28 solutions, PDF p. 1, problem 28-3](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/cfsol/ch28sol.pdf#page=1), reopened, explains cancellation of the debt upon conversion, unlike an independent warrant. The ratio example is original.
- [FINRA, Reverse Convertibles](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/reverse-convertibles-complex-investments) supports the distinction from issuer-controlled delivery of stock.
