# Compactness and convergent subsequences
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*紧致性的序列判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sequence in a compact metric set can wander forever without converging as a whole. Compactness promises something more selective: we can keep infinitely many terms, in their original order, so that the selected terms converge to a point still in the set. For example, $(-1)^n$ does not converge, but its even-indexed terms are constantly $1$. Finding one nonconvergent sequence is therefore not enough to disprove compactness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

紧致度量集合中的序列可以一直游走，整列并不收敛。紧致性给出的保证允许选择：我们可以按原有顺序保留无穷多项，使选出的各项收敛到仍在集合内的点。例如，$(-1)^n$ 不收敛，但它的偶数索引项恒为 $1$。因此，找到一条整列不收敛的序列，还不足以否定紧致性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $K$ be a subset of a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$. A [[聚点不等于收敛|subsequence]] $x_{n_k}$ uses strictly increasing positive integer indices $n_1<n_2<\cdots$; it cannot keep reusing one early occurrence. Saying that it [[序列收敛|converges]] to $x$ means $d(x_{n_k},x)\to0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $K$ 是[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$ 的子集。[[聚点不等于收敛|子列]] $x_{n_k}$ 使用严格递增的正整数索引 $n_1<n_2<\cdots$，不能反复使用同一个早期位置。它[[序列收敛|收敛]]到 $x$，指的是 $d(x_{n_k},x)\to0$。

> [!important] A metric-space equivalence · 度量空间中的等价性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The set $K$ is [[紧致性|compact]] if and only if every sequence $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ in $K$ has a subsequence converging in $X$ to some $x\in K$. The latter property is called **sequential compactness**. The limit must remain in $K$. The empty set satisfies both conditions: there is no sequence in it to provide a counterexample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $K$ [[紧致性|紧致]]，当且仅当每一条取值在 $K$ 内的序列 $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ 都有一个子列，在 $X$ 中收敛到某个 $x\in K$。后者称为**序列紧致性**；极限必须留在 $K$ 内。空集满足两者，因为其中没有序列可提供反例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $K=(0,1)$ with the usual distance, take $x_n=1/(n+1)$. Every term is in $K$. Every subsequence still tends to $0$ in $\mathbb R$, and by [[序列极限唯一|uniqueness of metric limits]] it cannot converge to a different point of $K$. Hence $K$ is not compact. By contrast, the alternating sequence above has suitable constant subsequences in the compact finite set $\{-1,1\}$. The issue is the existence of a suitable subsequence, not convergence of all terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在通常距离的 $K=(0,1)$ 中取 $x_n=1/(n+1)$，每项都属于 $K$。每个子列在 $\mathbb R$ 中仍趋于 $0$，由[[序列极限唯一|度量极限唯一性]]，它不能再收敛到 $K$ 内另一个点。因此 $K$ 不紧致。相比之下，上述交替序列在有限紧致集 $\{-1,1\}$ 中有合适的常值子列。问题是能否选出合适子列，不是全部项是否收敛。

## Why an open-cover condition forces a subsequence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*开覆盖条件为什么能迫使子列存在*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $K$ is compact and $(x_n)$ is a sequence in it. If there were no convergent subsequence with limit in $K$, no point of $K$ could be a [[聚点不等于收敛|sequence cluster point]]. Thus for each $x\in K$ there would be a ball about $x$ containing terms at only finitely many indices. These balls cover $K$. Compactness supplies a finite subcover, but a finite union of finite index sets is finite. It cannot account for every index $n\ge1$ of the sequence. This contradiction proves that a cluster point exists, and the radius-$1/k$ construction in the linked card extracts a convergent subsequence. Counting indices handles repeated values as well as distinct ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $K$ 紧致，$(x_n)$ 是其中一条序列。若不存在极限留在 $K$ 的收敛子列，那么 $K$ 中任何点都不是[[聚点不等于收敛|序列聚点]]。于是对每个 $x\in K$，都存在一个以它为中心的球，只包含有限多个索引对应的序列项。这些球覆盖 $K$。紧致性提供有限子覆盖，但有限个有限索引集的并仍有限，不可能包含序列的全部索引 $n\ge1$，矛盾。因此必有聚点，再用链接卡中的半径 $1/k$ 选取法抽出收敛子列。按索引计数也正确处理了取值重复的情况。

## Why the sequence condition forces a finite cover
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*序列条件为什么能迫使有限覆盖存在*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now assume every sequence in $K$ has a subsequence converging within $K$; take $K\ne\varnothing$, since the empty case is immediate. Write $B_r^K(x)=\{y\in K:d(y,x)<r\}$. We need two consequences of the sequence assumption before considering an arbitrary open cover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，假设 $K$ 中每条序列都有在 $K$ 内收敛的子列。以下取 $K\ne\varnothing$，空集情形已直接成立。记 $B_r^K(x)=\{y\in K:d(y,x)<r\}$。处理任意开覆盖之前，先从序列假设推出两个事实。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, at every positive radius $r$, finitely many such balls cover $K$: this is [[全有界性|total boundedness]]. Otherwise choose $x_1\in K$, then repeatedly choose $x_{m+1}$ outside the radius-$r$ balls around all earlier choices. Every pair of chosen points is at distance at least $r$. No subsequence can converge: two sufficiently late terms of a convergent subsequence would each be less than $r/3$ from its limit, making their mutual distance less than $2r/3$. This contradicts the separation. Hence a finite ball cover exists at each radius.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一，对每个正半径 $r$，有限多个这样的球都能覆盖 $K$，这就是[[全有界性]]。否则，先选 $x_1\in K$，以后每次选 $x_{m+1}$ 落在此前所有中心的半径 $r$ 球之外。这样任意两个选出的点距离都至少为 $r$，不可能有收敛子列：若有，则子列中足够靠后的两项都距极限小于 $r/3$，两项之间的距离就小于 $2r/3$，与分离性矛盾。因此每个半径都存在有限球覆盖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Second, fix an arbitrary cover by sets open in $K$. There is a positive radius $r$ such that **every** ball $B_r^K(x)$ is contained in some member of that cover; the chosen member may depend on $x$. To see why, suppose no such radius exists. For each positive integer $n$, choose $x_n\in K$ whose ball $B_{1/n}^K(x_n)$ is not contained in any one cover member. Extract a subsequence $x_{n_k}\to x\in K$. Choose a cover member $U$ containing $x$. Its openness gives $a>0$ with $B_a^K(x)\subseteq U$. For sufficiently large $k$, both $d(x_{n_k},x)<a/2$ and $1/n_k<a/2$. Then the triangle inequality yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二，固定任意一个由 $K$ 中开集组成的覆盖。存在正半径 $r$，使**每个**球 $B_r^K(x)$ 都包含在某一个覆盖成员中；所选成员可以随 $x$ 改变。若不存在这样的半径，则对每个正整数 $n$，都可选出 $x_n\in K$，使 $B_{1/n}^K(x_n)$ 不被任何单个覆盖成员包含。抽出子列 $x_{n_k}\to x\in K$，再选包含 $x$ 的覆盖成员 $U$。由开性，存在 $a>0$ 使 $B_a^K(x)\subseteq U$。当 $k$ 足够大时，同时有 $d(x_{n_k},x)<a/2$ 与 $1/n_k<a/2$，于是三角不等式给出

$$B_{1/n_k}^K(x_{n_k})\subseteq B_a^K(x)\subseteq U,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
contradicting the choice of $x_{n_k}$. Finally, use the first consequence to cover $K$ by finitely many radius-$r$ balls, and the second to place each of these balls inside one member of the original cover. Those finitely many original members cover $K$. This proves compactness. Ambient covers are equivalent to these relative covers by the [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与 $x_{n_k}$ 的选取矛盾。最后，用第一个事实选出有限多个半径 $r$ 的球覆盖 $K$，再用第二个事实为每个球选一个包含它的原覆盖成员。选出的有限多个原成员便覆盖 $K$，证明了紧致性。由[[子空间拓扑]]，这种相对覆盖检验与环境覆盖等价。

> [!warning] The metric hypothesis matters · 度量条件有作用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Sequential compactness can also be defined using neighbourhood convergence in a general topological space. The equivalence proved here is for metric spaces. The proof uses both countable shrinking radii and the triangle inequality; replacing “ball” by an arbitrary neighbourhood does not automatically prove the same equivalence in every topology.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在一般拓扑空间中，也能用邻域收敛定义序列紧致性。但本文证明的等价性限定在度量空间。证明用到了可数的缩小半径和三角不等式，不能仅把“球”换成任意邻域，就声称在每种拓扑中都有同样的等价性。
