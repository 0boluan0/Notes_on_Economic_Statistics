# 财务预测是在明确经营和融资假设下推演未来报表与资金需求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial forecasting projects future statements and funding needs under explicit operating and financing assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务预测把“准备怎样经营”翻译成相互衔接的收入、费用、资产、负债、权益和现金。狭义的融资预测只问还差多少资金；完整财务预测还要说明利润如何形成、资金何时收付，以及各张预计报表能否对得上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial forecasting translates an operating plan into linked revenue, expenses, assets, liabilities, equity, and cash. A financing forecast asks how much additional funding is needed; a full financial forecast also explains profit formation, payment timing, and consistency across projected statements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从[[收入驱动预测]]起步：销量、价格和业务组合给出收入；成本结构给出费用；回款和存货政策决定资金占用；投资、借款和分红再改变资产、利息与现金。各项数字应当来自这些关系，而不是为了填平报表各自猜一个增长率。借款与利息相互影响时，另用[[融资利息循环]]处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with [[收入驱动预测|revenue drivers]]. Volume, price, and mix determine revenue; cost structure determines expenses; collection and inventory policies determine investment needs. Capital spending, borrowing, and dividends then affect assets, interest, and cash. Use these relationships rather than independently guessed growth rates. Resolve feedback through [[融资利息循环|financing-interest circularity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如预计多卖 20% 的产品，不能只把收入乘 1.2：机器是否还有空余产能，客户是否晚两个月付款，供应商是否允许赊购，都会改变资金需求。年度利润仍为正，也可能在采购旺季出现现金缺口，所以还需[[现金预算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A 20% volume increase is more than multiplying revenue by 1.2. Spare capacity, delayed customer payments, and supplier credit change the funding requirement. Positive annual profit can coexist with a seasonal cash shortage, which requires a [[现金预算|cash budget]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测的用途是检查计划及其条件，而不是证明未来必然如此。用[[情景预测]]比较不同的、内部一致的量价与融资路径；实际结果出现后，区分原假设错了还是执行偏离了，再更新模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A forecast tests a plan and its conditions; it does not prove that the future will follow it. Use [[情景预测|scenarios]] to compare coherent operating and financing paths, then distinguish assumption errors from implementation differences when updating the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
