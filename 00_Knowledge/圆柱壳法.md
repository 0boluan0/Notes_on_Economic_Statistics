# Cylindrical shell method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*圆柱壳法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine a solid built from nested thin tubes, like layers around an axis. The **cylindrical shell method** adds the volumes of those tubes. A layer is described by its distance from the axis, its occupied length along the axis and its small radial thickness. It is useful when a strip parallel to the rotation axis has an easy-to-describe length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象把立体拆成围绕一根轴嵌套的薄管。**圆柱壳法**把这些薄管的体积相加。每层由它到轴的距离、沿轴占据的长度，以及很小的径向厚度来描述。如果平行于旋转轴的细条长度容易表达，这种方法通常方便。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Call the nonnegative radius $r$ and the axial length $h$. A very thin shell has circumference $2\pi r$, length $h$ and thickness $\Delta r$, so its volume is approximately $2\pi rh\Delta r$. Here “height” means length parallel to the axis, even when the axis is horizontal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把非负半径记为 $r$，沿轴长度记为 $h$。很薄的一层有周长 $2\pi r$、沿轴长度 $h$、厚度 $\Delta r$，所以体积近似为 $2\pi rh\Delta r$。这里的“壳高”指平行于轴的长度，即使旋转轴是水平的也一样。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why circumference times height appears
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么出现周长乘壳高*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a shell with constant height $h$, inner radius $r$ and outer radius $r+\Delta r$, subtracting two cylinder volumes gives an exact expression:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若壳高固定为 $h$，内半径为 $r$，外半径为 $r+\Delta r$，两个圆柱体积相减给出精确式：

$$
\pi\bigl[(r+\Delta r)^2-r^2\bigr]h
=2\pi rh\Delta r+\pi h(\Delta r)^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term is the thin-shell approximation. For bounded heights on a fixed finite radial interval, the sum of the second terms tends to zero as the largest width tends to zero: $\sum h_i(\Delta r_i)^2\le H\max_i\Delta r_i\sum\Delta r_i$ if $h_i\le H$. When the actual height varies continuously or piecewise continuously, its short-layer approximations also converge. This produces a [[黎曼和|Riemann-sum]] explanation of the formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项就是薄壳近似。固定有限径向区间内，若各壳高有共同上界，最大段宽趋零时，第二项的总和趋零：若 $h_i\le H$，则 $\sum h_i(\Delta r_i)^2\le H\max_i\Delta r_i\sum\Delta r_i$。实际壳高连续或分段连续地变化时，短层内的高度近似也会收敛，由此得到公式的[[黎曼和]]解释。

> [!important] Count each radial layer once · 每个径向层只计一次
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a bounded solid of revolution with a piecewise-smooth boundary, let $h(r)\ge0$ be the total occupied axial length at radius $r$. Over a finite radial range $0\le\alpha<\beta$, assume $h$ is piecewise continuous: it is [[一点连续|continuous]] on finitely many pieces and has finite one-sided limits at their endpoints. With each actual radial layer counted once, the volume is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对一个边界分段光滑的有界旋转体，令 $h(r)\ge0$ 表示半径 $r$ 处沿轴方向实际占据的总长度。在有限径向范围 $0\le\alpha<\beta$ 上，设 $h$ 分段连续，即在有限多个小段内[[一点连续|连续]]，各段端点有有限的单侧极限。每个实际径向层只计一次时，体积为
>
> $$V=2\pi\int_\alpha^\beta r\,h(r)\,dr.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[定积分|definite integral]] sums circumference times occupied length times radial thickness. If a layer contains several separated axial intervals, $h(r)$ is their combined length. Empty radial layers have $h=0$. A zero radius causes no division by zero: its factor $r$ simply vanishes. More general integrable height functions can be used when the corresponding volume and layer integrals are defined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个[[定积分]]累加“周长乘占据长度乘径向厚度”。若某层沿轴有几个分离区间，$h(r)$ 是各段长度之和；没有材料的层取 $h=0$。半径为零不会产生除零问题，因子 $r$ 直接为零。更一般的可积高度函数，在相应体积与分层积分有定义时也能使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A parabola gives the shell height
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*由抛物线读出壳高*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With coordinate units fixed, rotate the region $x^2\le y\le1$ about the $y$-axis. Its right half, $0\le x\le1$, already sweeps out the whole solid. A vertical strip is parallel to the axis. Its radius is $r=x$, its top is $1$ and its bottom is $x^2$, so its height is $1-x^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定坐标单位，把区域 $x^2\le y\le1$ 绕 $y$ 轴旋转。只取右半边 $0\le x\le1$，已经能够扫出整个立体。竖直细条平行于轴，半径为 $r=x$，上端为 $1$，下端为 $x^2$，所以壳高是 $1-x^2$。

$$
V=2\pi\int_0^1x(1-x^2)\,dx
=2\pi\left[\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{x^4}{4}\right]_0^1
=\frac\pi2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|antiderivative formula]] gives the evaluated integral above. The [[圆盘垫圈法|disk method]] checks the same solid in the other direction: at height $0\le y\le1$, the rotated section is a disk of radius $\sqrt y$, so $V=\pi\int_0^1 y\,dy=\pi/2$. The two calculations agree because they cover the same space, although one accumulates in $x$ and the other in $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面用[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]完成积分计算。[[圆盘垫圈法]]可以从另一个方向核对同一立体：在高度 $0\le y\le1$ 处，旋转截面是半径 $\sqrt y$ 的圆盘，所以 $V=\pi\int_0^1 y\,dy=\pi/2$。两种计算覆盖同一空间，因此结果一致；只是一个沿 $x$ 累加，另一个沿 $y$ 累加。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In dimensional notation the parabola can be written $y=kx^2$, where $k$ carries inverse-length units. Taking the numerical coefficient as $1$ fixes the coordinate units. Changing length units also changes that coefficient; keeping the same numbers after a unit change can describe a different solid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

带量纲时，抛物线可写作 $y=kx^2$，其中 $k$ 带长度倒数的单位。把数值系数取为 $1$，就固定了坐标单位；更换长度单位时，这个系数也要改变。换单位后仍保留同一组数值，可能已经是在描述另一个立体。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose a radius and an axial length
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*确定半径与沿轴长度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a vertical axis $x=c$, use vertical strips: the radius is $|x-c|$, and the height is upper $y$ minus lower $y$. If the generating region stays on one side of the axis, each $x$ identifies one radius, and increasing $x$ gives positive thickness $dx$. Thus one may integrate $2\pi|x-c|h(x)\,dx$ between increasing $x$ bounds. For a horizontal axis $y=c$, use horizontal strips, radius $|y-c|$, axial length equal to right $x$ minus left $x$, and thickness $dy$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绕竖直轴 $x=c$ 旋转时，取竖条：半径为 $|x-c|$，壳高为上端 $y$ 减下端 $y$。若生成区域完全位于轴的一侧，每个 $x$ 就对应一个半径；按递增的 $x$ 积分时，$dx$ 表示正厚度，所以可在递增的 $x$ 上下限之间积分 $2\pi|x-c|h(x)\,dx$。绕水平轴 $y=c$ 时，取横条，半径为 $|y-c|$，沿轴长度是右端 $x$ 减左端 $x$，厚度为 $dy$。

> [!example] The left side still has positive volume · 左侧也给正体积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Rotate the rectangle $-2\le x\le-1$, $0\le y\le3$, about the $y$-axis. Radius is $-x$, not $x$, so $V=2\pi\int_{-2}^{-1}(-x)\,3\,dx=9\pi$. In radius coordinates this is $2\pi\int_1^2 3r\,dr$. If substituting $r=-x$, both the reversed bounds and $dr=-dx$ must be accounted for, as in [[定积分换元|definite-integral substitution]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把矩形 $-2\le x\le-1$、$0\le y\le3$ 绕 $y$ 轴旋转。半径为 $-x$，不是 $x$，所以 $V=2\pi\int_{-2}^{-1}(-x)\,3\,dx=9\pi$。改用半径坐标就是 $2\pi\int_1^2 3r\,dr$。若直接代入 $r=-x$，要按[[定积分换元]]同时处理反向上下限和 $dr=-dx$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a generating region crosses the axis, strips on opposite sides may sweep out the same shell. In the parabola example, adding the left half again would double the volume. At a shared radius, overlapping axial intervals must be replaced by their union; disjoint intervals may have their lengths added. See [[旋转体重复覆盖|overlapping rotational coverage]] for that separate decision. Negative radii or signed cancellation cannot repair repeated coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若生成区域跨过轴，两侧细条可能扫出同一个壳层。抛物线例子再把左半边相加，就会把体积算两遍。同一半径处，重叠的轴向区间须先取并集；互不相交的区间则可以把长度相加。这个独立判断见[[旋转体重复覆盖]]。使用负半径或正负抵消，不能修复重复覆盖。
