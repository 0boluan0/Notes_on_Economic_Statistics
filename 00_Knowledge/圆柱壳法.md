# 圆柱壳法沿半径积分周长乘壳高且每个壳层只计一次
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The cylindrical shell method integrates circumference times shell height over radius, counting each shell once*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

圆柱壳法取平行于旋转轴的细条，并把旋转后的立体按非负半径 $r$ 分层。若半径 $r$ 处沿轴方向的总占据长度为 $h(r)\ge0$，且每个实际壳层只计一次，则在可积条件下
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take thin strips parallel to the axis and organise the resulting solid by nonnegative radius $r$. If $h(r)\ge0$ is the total occupied axial length at radius $r$, and each physical shell is counted once, then, assuming integrability,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=2\pi\int_{r_{\min}}^{r_{\max}}r\,h(r)\,dr.
$$

细壳展开后近似为周长 $2\pi r$、高度 $h(r)$、厚度 $dr$ 的薄片，因此三个长度相乘给出体积微元。若沿轴有几段分离的占据区间，$h$ 是各段长度之和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unrolling a thin shell gives an approximate slab with width $2\pi r$, height $h(r)$, and thickness $dr$. Their product is a volume element. If the occupied axial intervals are disjoint, add their lengths to obtain $h$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绕竖直轴 $x=c$ 时，半径为 $|x-c|$。在轴的一侧可用 $x$ 积分，按递增坐标写 $2\pi|x-c|\,h(x)\,dx$；跨轴时须先检查 [[旋转体重复覆盖]]，因为两侧的不同细条可能生成同一个壳层。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For rotation about $x=c$, the radius is $|x-c|$. On one side of the axis, increasing $x$ gives the element $2\pi|x-c|h(x)\,dx$. If the region crosses the axis, check [[旋转体重复覆盖|overlapping rotational coverage]] because strips on opposite sides can generate the same shell.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定坐标单位下，将 $y=x^2$ 与 $y=1$ 之间的区域绕 $y$ 轴旋转，右半区域已生成完整立体。其壳高为 $1-x^2$，所以
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With coordinate units fixed, revolve the region between $y=x^2$ and $y=1$ about the $y$-axis. The right half generates the entire solid. Its shell height is $1-x^2$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=2\pi\int_0^1x(1-x^2)\,dx=\frac\pi2.
$$
