# 央行购债对准备金和存款的影响取决于卖方与结算链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The reserve and deposit effects of a central-bank bond purchase depend on the seller and settlement chain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 在标准的银行准备金结算链中，央行买入证券时，自身证券资产和准备金负债通常同时增加；但公众存款是否同步增加要看证券由谁卖出。向银行买入主要把银行证券资产换成准备金；向非银行卖方买入则通常还会由其银行贷记卖方存款。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the standard settlement chain through a bank reserve account, when a central bank buys a security, its securities asset and reserve liability normally rise together; whether public deposits also rise depends on the seller. A purchase from a bank mainly swaps the bank's security asset for reserves; a purchase from a non-bank seller normally also causes the seller's bank to credit the seller's deposit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非银行卖方链条是：央行获得证券并贷记结算银行准备金；银行增加准备金资产和对卖方的存款负债；卖方减少证券资产、增加存款资产。若卖方本身是银行，则银行减少证券、增加准备金，不需要凭这一步新增公众存款。后续卖方还债、再投资或转账会继续改变余额分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a non-bank seller, the central bank acquires the security and credits the settlement bank's reserves; the bank gains a reserve asset and a deposit liability to the seller; the seller replaces the security with a deposit. If the seller is itself a bank, the bank reduces securities and gains reserves without this step requiring a new public deposit. Subsequent debt repayment, reinvestment, or transfer can further change the distribution of balances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- “央行买债 = 准备金增加”可以是第一步；“= 公众存款必定同额增加”必须先识别卖方。
- 央行资产增加与准备金增加是央行自己的复式记账；商业银行存款是另一张资产负债表上的负债。
- 经 dealer、custodian 或 correspondent 结算时，要追踪最终证券所有者与被贷记的银行账户，不能只看中间机构名称。
- 若最终交易对手直接持有另一类央行账户，央行可能贷记 Treasury、foreign official 或 other deposit，而不是 reserve balance；此时必须沿实际被贷记的央行负债重做结论，不能套用标准银行结算链。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- “Central-bank bond purchase means more reserves” can describe the first step; “it must raise public deposits equally” requires identifying the seller.
- The rise in central-bank assets and reserves is the central bank's double entry; a commercial-bank deposit is a liability on another balance sheet.
- With a dealer, custodian, or correspondent, trace the ultimate security owner and credited bank account rather than stopping at the intermediary's name.
- If the ultimate counterparty directly holds another type of central-bank account, the central bank may credit a Treasury, foreign-official, or other deposit rather than a reserve balance. Trace the liability actually credited instead of imposing the standard bank-settlement chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 央行从商业银行自身投资组合买债，与从养老基金买债，公众存款的初始结果为何不同？
>
> **答案：** 前者主要是银行证券换准备金；后者需要养老基金的银行贷记其存款，因此通常同时新增一笔公众部门存款负债。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why do purchases from a commercial bank's own portfolio and from a pension fund have different initial effects on public deposits?
>
> **Answer:** The first mainly swaps a bank security for reserves. The second requires the pension fund's bank to credit its deposit, normally creating an additional deposit liability to the non-bank sector.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
