# Determinant of a transpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行列式的转置不变性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turning every row of a square matrix into a column changes where its entries sit, but leaves its [[行列式|determinant]] unchanged. This lets us transfer a rule about rows to columns. The operation is ordinary [[矩阵转置|transpose]], denoted $A^T$: the entry $a_{ij}$ moves to position $(j,i)$ without changing its value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把方阵的每一行转成一列，会改变元素的位置，却保留它的[[行列式]]。这个性质让我们把行规则转用于列。这里使用普通[[矩阵转置|转置]]，记作 $A^T$：元素 $a_{ij}$ 移到 $(j,i)$，数值本身不变。

> [!important] Transpose preserves the determinant · 转置保留行列式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every square matrix $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, with $n\ge1$ and $\mathbb F$ any field,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意域 $\mathbb F$ 上的方阵 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，$n\ge1$，都有
>
> $$\boxed{\det(A^T)=\det A.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Neither symmetry nor invertibility is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不要求 $A$ 对称，也不要求可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&5\end{pmatrix}$ and $A^T=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\2&5\end{pmatrix}$ are different, yet their determinants are $1\cdot5-2\cdot3=-1$ and $1\cdot5-3\cdot2=-1$. The same numbers are multiplied in a different order. For larger matrices, we need to match every term and its sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&5\end{pmatrix}$ 与 $A^T=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\2&5\end{pmatrix}$ 不同，但行列式分别为 $1\cdot5-2\cdot3=-1$ 和 $1\cdot5-3\cdot2=-1$。乘积使用相同的数，只是顺序不同。高阶情形还要说明每一项及其符号都能对应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Match terms using the inverse permutation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*通过逆排列逐项对应*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Leibniz 行列式公式|Leibniz formula]] selects one entry from each row and each column. A permutation $\sigma$ records the selected column in each row; $S_n$ is the set of all permutations, and $\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)$ is $+1$ or $-1$ according to its parity. Applying the formula to $A^T$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Leibniz 行列式公式]]在每行、每列各选一个元素。排列 $\sigma$ 记录每一行选哪一列，$S_n$ 是全部排列的集合，$\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)$ 按奇偶性取 $+1$ 或 $-1$。对 $A^T$ 应用公式：

$$
\det(A^T)=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)
\prod_{i=1}^{n}a_{\sigma(i),i}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a product, rename the row index $k=\sigma(i)$. Every index appears once, so this only reorders the factors, and $i=\sigma^{-1}(k)$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对其中一个乘积，把行指标改记为 $k=\sigma(i)$。每个指标恰好出现一次，所以这只是重排因子，而 $i=\sigma^{-1}(k)$。因此

$$
\prod_{i=1}^{n}a_{\sigma(i),i}
=\prod_{k=1}^{n}a_{k,\sigma^{-1}(k)}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A permutation and its inverse have the same sign: reverse a sequence of swaps that performs the permutation, and the same number of swaps undoes it. As $\sigma$ runs through all permutations, its inverse does too, without repetition. Set $\tau=\sigma^{-1}$ to obtain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

排列及其逆排列具有相同符号：把实现排列的一串交换反过来做，就用同样次数的交换撤销它。$\sigma$ 遍历所有排列时，其逆排列也把它们遍历一次。令 $\tau=\sigma^{-1}$，便得到

$$
\det(A^T)=\sum_{\tau\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\tau)
\prod_{k=1}^{n}a_{k,\tau(k)}=\det A.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, selecting $\sigma=(2,3,1)$ in $A^T$ gives $a_{21}a_{32}a_{13}$. Reordering gives $a_{13}a_{21}a_{32}$, the term for $\tau=(3,1,2)$ in $A$. Both permutations are even. The proof never requires a nonzero determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在 $A^T$ 中选 $\sigma=(2,3,1)$，得到 $a_{21}a_{32}a_{13}$；重排为 $a_{13}a_{21}a_{32}$，它正是 $A$ 中 $\tau=(3,1,2)$ 对应的项。两个排列都是偶排列。证明不需要行列式非零。

> [!note]- An elimination proof using the product rule · 使用乘法性的消元证明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Alternatively, [[消元与LU分解|row-pivoted elimination]] gives $PA=LU$, with $P$ recording $s$ row exchanges, $L$ unit lower triangular, and $U$ upper triangular. Counting those swaps in reverse gives $\det P=\det P^{-1}=(-1)^s$, without using transpose invariance. By [[行列式乘法性|multiplicativity]],
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 也可以使用[[消元与LU分解|允许换行的消元]] $PA=LU$。其中 $P$ 记录 $s$ 次换行，$L$ 为单位下三角矩阵，$U$ 为上三角矩阵。把这些交换反序执行得到 $\det P=\det P^{-1}=(-1)^s$，无需使用转置不变性。由[[行列式乘法性]]，
>
> $$\det A=(-1)^s\det L\det U,\qquad A^T=U^TL^TP.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Triangular matrices and their transposes have the same diagonal entries. The [[三角矩阵行列式|triangular determinant rule]] therefore gives $\det L^T=\det L$ and $\det U^T=\det U$. Multiplicativity applied to $U^TL^TP$ yields the desired equality. Singular $U$ is allowed: no diagonal entry is divided out.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三角矩阵及其转置具有相同对角元，因此[[三角矩阵行列式|三角矩阵行列式规则]]给出 $\det L^T=\det L$、$\det U^T=\det U$。再对 $U^TL^TP$ 应用乘法性，即得所需等式。允许 $U$ 奇异，因为没有除以对角元。

## Transfer the row rules to columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 一个常用后果

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A column operation on $A$ becomes the corresponding row operation on $A^T$. Apply the [[行操作对行列式的影响|row-operation rule]] and transpose invariance to the old and new matrices. Exchanging distinct columns negates the determinant; scaling one column by $c$ multiplies it by $c$; adding a multiple of another column leaves it unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A$ 的列做操作，转置后就是对 $A^T$ 的行做对应操作。先用[[行操作对行列式的影响|行操作规则]]，再对操作前后的矩阵用转置不变性。因此，交换不同的两列使行列式取负，把一列乘以 $c$ 使行列式乘以 $c$，给一列加另一列的倍数则不改变它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a column lies in the [[张成|span]] of the others—that is, it is a sum of scalar multiples of those columns—subtract those multiples successively to make it zero. Each column replacement preserves the determinant, while a zero column forces zero. Column dependence therefore forces determinant zero just as row dependence does; for square matrices, [[可逆性与非零行列式|zero determinant is equivalent to noninvertibility]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一列位于其他列的[[张成]]中，即它等于其他列分别数乘后再相加，就能依次减去这些倍数，把它变成零列。每次列倍加保留行列式，零列则使其为零。因此列相关与行相关一样，都会导致行列式为零；对方阵，[[可逆性与非零行列式|行列式为零等价于不可逆]]。

## Ordinary transpose and conjugate transpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal determinants do not imply $A=A^T$, as the opening example shows. Nor do they make the [[行空间|row space]] equal to the [[列空间|column space]]: even for square matrices these subspaces can differ, and transposition exchanges their roles. Rectangular matrices can be transposed, but their ordinary determinants are not defined here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式相同不意味着 $A=A^T$，开头的例子已说明这一点，也不意味着[[行空间]]等于[[列空间]]：即使是方阵，这两个子空间也可能不同，转置交换的是它们的角色。矩形矩阵可以转置，但这里没有为它定义普通行列式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex matrices, the [[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|conjugate transpose]] $A^*=\overline A^{\,T}$ also conjugates the entries. The permutation formula uses real coefficients $+1,-1$, so conjugating every entry conjugates the result. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复矩阵，[[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|共轭转置]] $A^*=\overline A^{\,T}$ 还把元素取共轭。排列公式的系数 $+1,-1$ 都是实数，所以把每个元素取共轭会使最后结果也取共轭。因此

$$\det(A^*)=\overline{\det A}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This need not equal $\det A$. With $i^2=-1$ and $A=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, the ordinary transpose has determinant $i$, while the conjugate transpose has determinant $-i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不一定等于 $\det A$。例如令 $i^2=-1$、$A=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$，普通转置的行列式为 $i$，共轭转置的行列式却为 $-i$。
