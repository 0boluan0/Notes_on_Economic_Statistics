# Marshall 的供求共同决定价格而相对作用随调整期限改变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marshallian price is jointly determined by demand and supply, whose relative roles change with the adjustment horizon*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子框架
> 在 Marshall 的竞争性局部均衡中，价格和数量由需求与供给共同相容决定，不能只归因于效用或只归因于生产成本。哪一侧看起来更约束结果，还取决于给供给调整多长时间。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In Marshallian competitive partial equilibrium, price and quantity are jointly made consistent by demand and supply; they cannot be attributed solely to utility or solely to production cost. Which side appears more constraining also depends on how much time supply has to adjust.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **市场期：** 可出售存量近乎给定，需求变化主要通过价格吸收。
- **短期：** 企业能调整劳动、原料和利用率，但部分厂房、资本与企业数量固定。
- **长期：** 厂房与资本规模、生产组织及进入退出都可改变，供给关系因而不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Market period:** the available stock is nearly fixed, so demand changes are absorbed mainly through price.
- **Short period:** labour, materials, and utilisation can adjust, while some plant, capital, and the number of firms remain fixed.
- **Long period:** plant and capital scale, production organisation, entry, and exit can change, producing a different supply relation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些不是由日历天数固定定义的标签：同样长的日历区间，在不同行业中可能开放完全不同的产能、组织与进入退出调整。判断标准是哪些选择变量来得及调整。也不能把简写“短期需求决定价格、长期成本决定价格”当作单因果定律：各期限仍有需求和供给，只是弹性与可调边界不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The periods are not fixed by calendar length: the same span of calendar time may permit very different capacity, organisational, and entry or exit adjustments across industries. The criterion is which choice variables can adjust. The slogan “demand determines short-run price and cost determines long-run price” is not a one-cause law. Demand and supply operate in every period, but elasticities and adjustment margins differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 同一个需求冲击为什么可能使当天鲜鱼价格暴涨，却在数年后主要表现为船队和产量增加？
>
> **答案：** 当天存量近乎固定；更长时期船只、冷链、劳动和进入退出都能调整，供给对价格更有弹性。
