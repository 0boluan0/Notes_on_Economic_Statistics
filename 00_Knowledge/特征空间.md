# Eigenspaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*特征空间：收集同一个特征值对应的全部方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An eigenvalue tells us a multiplier, but one vector rarely describes every input with that multiplier. The **eigenspace** collects all vectors satisfying $Av=\lambda v$ for one fixed $\lambda$, including zero. Its nonzero members are precisely the [[特征对|eigenvectors]] for that value: each is sent to $\lambda$ times itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
特征值给出一个倍数，但一个向量往往不能描述所有具有这个倍数的输入。**特征空间**收集固定 $\lambda$ 下满足 $Av=\lambda v$ 的全部向量，包括零向量。其中的非零成员恰好是该值对应的[[特征对|特征向量]]：每个向量都被送到自身的 $\lambda$ 倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A whole line, described by one basis vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一根基向量可以描述整条直线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the matrix below and ask which inputs it multiplies by $3$. Here $(x,y)^T$ is a column vector; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
考虑下面的矩阵，问它把哪些输入变成自身的 $3$ 倍。这里 $(x,y)^T$ 是列向量，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]。

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\0&3\end{bmatrix},\qquad
A\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}3x+y\\3y\end{bmatrix}
=3\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coordinate $x$ is free. The answer is the entire horizontal line, not just $(1,0)^T$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
坐标 $x$ 可以自由选择。答案是整条横轴，而不只是 $(1,0)^T$：

$$E_3(A)=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}x\\0\end{bmatrix}:x\in\mathbb F\right\}
=\operatorname{span}\left\{\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}\right\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb F$ is the chosen scalar field, such as $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, and [[张成|span]] means all scalar combinations of the listed vectors. The single vector $(1,0)^T$ is a [[基|basis]]: it generates the whole line without redundancy. Every nonzero point on the line is an eigenvector for $3$. Zero belongs to the space but is not an eigenvector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $\mathbb F$ 是所选标量域，例如 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$；[[张成|span]] 表示所列向量的全部标量组合。单个向量 $(1,0)^T$ 构成一组[[基]]：它无冗余地生成整条直线。直线上每个非零点都是特征值 $3$ 的特征向量。零属于这个空间，但不是特征向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Finding the space means solving a homogeneous system
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*求特征空间，就是求一个齐次方程组的解集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$ with $n\ge1$, and fix a scalar $\lambda\in\mathbb F$. The [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_n$ leaves every vector unchanged, so $\lambda v=\lambda I_nv$. Moving the terms to one side gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，$n\ge1$，并固定标量 $\lambda\in\mathbb F$。[[单位矩阵]] $I_n$ 使每个向量保持不变，所以 $\lambda v=\lambda I_nv$。把等式移到一侧便得到

$$
\boxed{E_\lambda(A)=\{v:Av=\lambda v\}
=\ker(A-\lambda I_n).}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $\ker B$ denotes the [[零空间|null space]] of $B$: all solutions of $Bv=0$. Thus, once $\lambda$ is known, finding its eigenspace is an ordinary null-space calculation. In the example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
记号 $\ker B$ 表示 $B$ 的[[零空间]]，即 $Bv=0$ 的全部解。因此，一旦知道 $\lambda$，求它的特征空间就是一次普通的零空间计算。上例中，

$$A-3I_2=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix},$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
whose equation is exactly $y=0$. The kernel expression is meaningful for any scalar $\lambda$. It is $\{0\}$ when $\lambda$ is not an eigenvalue, and contains a nonzero vector exactly when $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue. In particular, $E_0(A)=\ker A$; zero is an eigenvalue only if this space contains a nonzero vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对应方程恰好是 $y=0$。对任意标量 $\lambda$，这个零空间表达式都有意义。当 $\lambda$ 不是特征值时，它等于 $\{0\}$；当且仅当 $\lambda$ 是特征值时，它包含非零向量。特别地，$E_0(A)=\ker A$；只有当这个空间包含非零向量时，零才是特征值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why it is a subspace, and what its dimension counts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么它是子空间，维数又在数什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $Au=\lambda u$ and $Av=\lambda v$, then for any scalars $a,b$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若 $Au=\lambda u$、$Av=\lambda v$，那么对任意标量 $a,b$，

$$A(au+bv)=aAu+bAv=\lambda(au+bv).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All linear combinations stay in the set, including combinations that cancel to zero. Together with the zero vector, this makes $E_\lambda(A)$ a [[子空间|linear subspace]]. The common value $\lambda$ matters: vectors belonging to different eigenvalues do not in general remain eigenvectors when added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
所有线性组合都留在集合内，包括恰好相消为零的组合。再加上它本就包含零向量，这说明 $E_\lambda(A)$ 是一个[[子空间|线性子空间]]。这里必须是同一个 $\lambda$：属于不同特征值的向量相加，一般不再是特征向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its [[维数|dimension]] is the number of vectors in a basis, not the total number of eigenvectors. The example has dimension $1$. If $\dim E_2(A)=3$, one can choose three independent eigenvectors for $2$ as a basis, and every vector in that eigenspace is their linear combination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
它的[[维数]]是一组基中向量的个数，而不是特征向量的总数。上例的维数是 $1$。若 $\dim E_2(A)=3$，就可以选出三个对应特征值 $2$ 的线性无关向量作为基，该特征空间的每个向量都是它们的线性组合。

> [!example] The same repeated root can leave different amounts of freedom / 重根相同，独立方向仍可能不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both the example matrix and $3I_2$ have characteristic polynomial $(t-3)^2$. The first has only the line $y=0$ as its eigenspace for $3$; the second satisfies $3I_2v=3v$ for every $v$, so its eigenspace is all of $\mathbb F^2$, of dimension $2$. Repetition of a root and dimension of an eigenspace are the two different counts explained in [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic and geometric multiplicity]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上例矩阵与 $3I_2$ 的特征多项式都是 $(t-3)^2$。前者在特征值 $3$ 下的特征空间只有 $y=0$ 这条直线；后者对每个 $v$ 都满足 $3I_2v=3v$，所以特征空间是整个 $\mathbb F^2$，维数为 $2$。根的重复次数与特征空间维数是两种不同的计数，详见[[代数重数与几何重数]]。

> [!warning] Keep the eigenvalue cases when eliminating / 消元时保留特征值分支
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{bmatrix}$, the equations are $(2-\lambda)x=0$ and $(3-\lambda)y=0$. Dividing the first equation by $2-\lambda$ before considering $\lambda=2$ loses the entire $x$-axis eigenspace. A reliable route is to find the eigenvalues from the [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]], then solve the null space for each fixed value. Parameter-based elimination is also possible if every case where a proposed divisor is zero is retained.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{bmatrix}$，方程是 $(2-\lambda)x=0$、$(3-\lambda)y=0$。若还没考虑 $\lambda=2$，就用 $2-\lambda$ 除第一个方程，会丢掉整条 $x$ 轴特征空间。可靠的做法是先由[[特征多项式]]确定特征值，再对每个固定值分别求零空间。也可以带参数消元，但必须保留每个除数为零的分支。
