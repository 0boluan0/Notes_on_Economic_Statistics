# 同一留存权益增长率用期初ROE直接相乘而用期末ROE必须调整分母
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same retained-equity growth rate is retention times beginning-equity ROE, but ending-equity ROE requires a denominator adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定一个期间：期初权益 $E_0>0$、期末权益 $E_1$、本期净利润 $NI$、留存率 $b$。若没有本期留存以外的权益变动，$E_1=E_0+bNI$。权益增长率是新增权益除以**期初**权益：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix one period: beginning equity $E_0>0$, ending equity $E_1$, current net income $NI$, and retention $b$. If retention is the only equity change, $E_1=E_0+bNI$. Equity growth divides the addition by beginning equity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$g_E=\frac{E_1-E_0}{E_0}=\frac{bNI}{E_0}=b\,ROE_{\mathrm{begin}}.$$

如果提供的是 $ROE_{\mathrm{end}}=NI/E_1$，就先用 $E_0=E_1-bNI$ 换回期初基数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the supplied ratio is $ROE_{\mathrm{end}}=NI/E_1$, recover the beginning base using $E_0=E_1-bNI$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$g_E=\frac{bNI}{E_1-bNI}=\frac{b\,ROE_{\mathrm{end}}}{1-b\,ROE_{\mathrm{end}}}.$$

例如 $E_0=100$、$NI=20$、$b=50\%$，$E_1=110$。期初 ROE 为 20%，直接乘留存率得到 10%；期末 ROE 约 18.18%，应算 $0.090909/(1-0.090909)=10\%$。若把期末 ROE 直接乘留存率，就得到 9.09%，它是留存利润占期末权益的比，而非权益相对期初的增长率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $E_0=100$, $NI=20$, and $b=50\%$, ending equity is 110. Beginning-equity ROE is 20%, giving 10% growth. Ending-equity ROE is about 18.18%; its adjusted formula also gives 10%. Simply multiplying the ending ratio by retention gives 9.09%, the addition relative to ending equity rather than growth from the beginning base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $g_E$ 进一步称为销售的[[可持续增长率]]，还需要资本结构、资产周转率等稳定条件。过渡年即使权益桥成立，实际销售增长也可能不同。平均权益 ROE 不属于上面任何一个定义，不能未经换算直接代入；若资料只给“ROE”，先查分母时点及股东范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calling $g_E$ the [[可持续增长率|sustainable sales growth rate]] additionally requires stable capital structure, turnover, and other policies. Actual sales can grow differently in a transition year even when the equity bridge holds. Average-equity ROE is neither definition above and cannot be substituted unchanged. Check timing and shareholder scope when a source merely says “ROE.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若使用期末公式并将结果解释为非负有限的增长能力，还需 $E_1>0$ 且 $0\le bROE_{\mathrm{end}}<1$。权益中有增资、回购或其他综合收益时，先完整重建权益桥，不能用这两个简式把所有权益增长都归给留存利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interpreting the ending-equity formula as finite nonnegative growth capacity requires $E_1>0$ and $0\le bROE_{\mathrm{end}}<1$. Contributions, repurchases, or other comprehensive income require the full equity bridge rather than attributing every equity change to retained profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
