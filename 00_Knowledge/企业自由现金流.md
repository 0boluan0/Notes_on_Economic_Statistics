# 企业自由现金流是经营税后并满足经营再投资后可供全部资本提供者分配的现金流
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Free cash flow to the firm is cash available to all capital providers after operating taxes and operating reinvestment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业自由现金流（FCFF）从整个经营业务出发：先扣经营税负和维持预测经营所需的再投资，再看还剩多少现金可供债权人与股东等资本提供者。这里的“可供”是一种估值口径，不代表当期实际支付股利；FCFF 也可以为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFF starts with the operating business as a whole. Deduct operating taxes and the reinvestment required by the forecast, then measure cash available to debt and equity providers. Availability is a valuation concept, not an actual dividend payment; FCFF may be negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在非金融企业的常用简化模型中，以当期可用的统一税率 $T$ 计算税后经营利润，并使用不含现金和融资性债务的经营营运资本 $NWC$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a common simplified nonfinancial-company model, apply a usable tax rate $T$ to operating profit and define operating working capital $NWC$ excluding cash and financing debt:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$FCFF_t=EBIT_t(1-T)+Dep_t-CapEx_t-\Delta NWC_t.$$

折旧已减少 EBIT 却不是当期付款，所以加回；购置经营资产和增加经营资金占用则扣除。例如 EBIT 为 100、税率 25%、折旧 20、资本开支 35、营运资本增加 10，FCFF 为 $75+20-35-10=50$。若存在递延税、资本化研发等调整，必须把利润、资本与实际现金税的口径一并改好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Depreciation reduced EBIT without a current payment, so it is added back. Capital expenditure and additional operating working capital are deducted. EBIT of 100, tax at 25%, depreciation of 20, capex of 35, and working-capital investment of 10 give FCFF of 50. Deferred taxes or capitalised R&D require consistent adjustments to profit, capital, and cash taxes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要把利息、偿还本金和股利再从这个分子中扣一遍，具体配对见[[WACC 与无杠杆 FCF]]。对银行，存贷款本身属于经营，普通工商企业的上述拆法往往不合适；不能只换公司名称便套用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not deduct interest, principal repayments, and dividends again; see [[WACC 与无杠杆 FCF|the cash-flow/discount-rate matching rule]]. For banks, lending and deposits are operating activities, so the standard nonfinancial-company decomposition cannot simply be transplanted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MIT supports the operating-profit, tax, depreciation, capex, and working-capital construction; the example is independently calculated. Damodaran supports the all-capital/equity distinction and the limits of applying nonfinancial-company debt and reinvestment definitions to financial firms.
