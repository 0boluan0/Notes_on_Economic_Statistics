# 相同均值和方差一般不足以保证风险厌恶者对两个组合无差异
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equal means and variances generally do not make two portfolios indifferent to a risk-averse investor*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险厌恶表示偏好会惩罚风险，并不表示只关心前两个矩。一般期望效用 $E[u(W)]$ 还会受财富分布的形状影响；只知道均值和方差，通常无法复原这个期望。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk aversion does not imply that only the first two moments matter. Expected utility $E[u(W)]$ can depend on the shape of the wealth distribution, which mean and variance generally do not determine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个最小反例：终值财富 X 以各 $1/2$ 概率取 $1,3$；Y 以 $4/13,9/13$ 概率取 $1/2,8/3$。两者均值都为 $2$、方差都为 $1$，但风险厌恶的对数效用给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a minimal counterexample, wealth X is $1$ or $3$ with equal probabilities; Y is $1/2$ or $8/3$ with probabilities $4/13$ and $9/13$. Both have mean $2$ and variance $1$, but the increasing, concave logarithmic utility gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E[\log X]=\tfrac12\log3\approx0.5493,$$
$$E[\log Y]=\tfrac4{13}\log(1/2)+\tfrac9{13}\log(8/3)\approx0.4658.$$

所以这位投资者严格偏好 X，而[[均值方差准则]]无法区分它们。取相同初始财富后，两者回报的前两个矩也相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This investor strictly prefers X while the mean–variance criterion ties them. With the same initial wealth, their returns also have identical first two moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值方差分析仍可在明确的模型中成立。例如终值财富为正态、效用为 $u(W)=-e^{-aW}$ 且 $a>0$ 时，$E[u(W)]=-\exp[-a\mu_W+a^2\sigma_W^2/2]$，最大化它等价于最大化 $\mu_W-a\sigma_W^2/2$。这是正态财富加指数效用的结论；不能把它直接推广到任何风险厌恶者或任何收益分布。若正态财富非退化，即 $\sigma_W^2>0$，其支持为整个实数轴，不能同时声称终值几乎处处严格为正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An explicit sufficient model is Gaussian terminal wealth with exponential utility $-e^{-aW}$: expected utility is $-\exp[-a\mu_W+a^2\sigma_W^2/2]$, giving an exact mean–variance certainty equivalent. This depends on both assumptions. Nondegenerate Gaussian wealth has support on the entire real line and cannot simultaneously be almost surely strictly positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
