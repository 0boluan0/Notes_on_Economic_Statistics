# Local and global optima in convex optimization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凸优化中的局部与全局最优*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **local maximizer** does at least as well as every allowed input sufficiently near it; a **global maximizer** does at least as well as every allowed input anywhere. These are different comparisons. If the allowed inputs form a [[凸集|convex set]] and the objective is [[凹凸性|concave]], a local maximizer automatically passes the global comparison. For minimization the objective must be convex. This card explains the local-to-global step; it does not assume a derivative exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**局部最大点**不劣于附近所有允许的输入，**全局最大点**不劣于全域所有允许的输入。这是两种比较。如果允许的输入组成[[凸集]]，目标函数又是[[凹凸性|凹函数]]，一个局部最大点就自动通过全局比较。最小化对应凸目标。本卡解释从局部到全局的这一步，不预设导数存在。

> [!summary] From local to global · 从局部到全局
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be convex and let $f:C\to\mathbb R$ be concave. Every local maximizer of $f$ over $C$ is global. Both the convex feasible set and the correctly oriented concavity assumption are essential.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 为凸集，$f:C\to\mathbb R$ 为凹函数。$f$ 在 $C$ 上的任何局部最大点都是全局最大点。结论依赖“可行集凸”和“最大化凹目标”同时成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word “local” is relative to the feasible set $C$. In the notation of [[局部极值与绝对极值|local and absolute extrema]], there is some radius $\varepsilon>0$ such that $f(y)\le f(x^*)$ whenever $y\in C$ and $\|y-x^*\|_2<\varepsilon$. The norm is ordinary Euclidean distance. Points outside $C$ are not competitors. Thus a boundary point or a point on a line segment in $\mathbb R^2$ can be a local maximum even though $C$ contains no full ball around it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“局部”要相对于可行集 $C$ 理解。按[[局部极值与绝对极值]]的记法，存在某个半径 $\varepsilon>0$，使所有满足 $y\in C$ 且 $\|y-x^*\|_2<\varepsilon$ 的输入都有 $f(y)\le f(x^*)$。这里范数表示通常的欧氏距离，$C$ 外的点不参与比较。因此边界点，甚至 $\mathbb R^2$ 中一条线段上的点，也可以是局部最大点，不要求 $C$ 在它周围包含一个完整的球。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a local maximum cannot be a false peak
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么局部最大不可能是“假峰顶”

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the segment argument below, the parameter $t$ is restricted to $0<t\le1$. It measures the fraction of the trip from $x^*$ to a proposed better point $y$. The two assumptions have separate jobs: convexity keeps that trip feasible; concavity makes every positive step on it an improvement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下段线段论证的参数限定为 $0<t\le1$；它表示从 $x^*$ 走向假设中更好点 $y$ 的路程比例。两个条件分别发挥作用：凸性保证这段路可行，凹性保证朝这个方向迈出的每一个正比例小步都改善目标值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $x^*$ were locally maximal but some $y\in C$ satisfied $f(y)>f(x^*)$. Convexity keeps $x_t=(1-t)x^*+ty$ in $C$, while concavity gives $f(x_t)\ge(1-t)f(x^*)+tf(y)>f(x^*)$ for every $t>0$. Taking $t$ arbitrarily small contradicts local maximality, so the local maximum must be global.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $x^*$ 是局部最大点，却存在 $y\in C$ 使 $f(y)>f(x^*)$。凸性保证线段 $x_t=(1-t)x^*+ty$ 仍在 $C$；凹性给出
$$
f(x_t)\ge (1-t)f(x^*)+tf(y)>f(x^*)\qquad (0<t\le1).
$$
取足够小的 $t$，$x_t$ 任意接近 $x^*$，与局部最大矛盾。因此局部最大必为全局最大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To make “sufficiently close” explicit, $y\ne x^*$ and $d=\|y-x^*\|_2>0$. Choose $t=\min\{1/2,\varepsilon/(2d)\}$. Then $0<t<1$ and $\|x_t-x^*\|_2=td<\varepsilon$. The displayed strict improvement therefore contradicts the precise local definition. No continuity, differentiability, compactness, or interior-point assumption was used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把“足够接近”写明：$y\ne x^*$，所以 $d=\|y-x^*\|_2>0$。取 $t=\min\{1/2,\varepsilon/(2d)\}$，就有 $0<t<1$ 且 $\|x_t-x^*\|_2=td<\varepsilon$。上面的严格改善于是与局部定义直接矛盾。证明没有额外使用连续、可微、紧致或内点条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strict concavity further rules out two distinct maximizers, but “at most one” must be separated from attainment. [[严格凹不保证解存在|The strict-concavity attainment boundary]] gives the counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格凹还能进一步排除两个不同最大点，但“至多一个”和“确实存在一个”必须分开；这个独立边界与取得性反例由 [[严格凹不保证解存在]] 承担。

> [!example] Boundary and corner maxima · 边界与尖点最大值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $C=[0,1]$, the affine function $f(x)=x$ is concave and has its maximum at the endpoint $1$, where its derivative is not zero. On $C=\mathbb R$, the concave function $g(x)=-|x|$ has a maximum at $0$, where it is not differentiable. Concavity of $g$ follows from $|(1-t)x+ty|\le(1-t)|x|+t|y|$. Both examples fit this theorem even though a zero-gradient test is unavailable or inappropriate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $C=[0,1]$ 上，仿射函数 $f(x)=x$ 是凹函数，最大点是导数不为零的端点 $1$。在 $C=\mathbb R$ 上，凹函数 $g(x)=-|x|$ 的最大点是不可导的 $0$；由 $|(1-t)x+ty|\le(1-t)|x|+t|y|$ 即得它的凹性。这两个例子都适用本定理，尽管零梯度检验不适用或无法使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Match the curvature to the objective
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最大化与最小化必须换对方向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximisation uses a concave objective; minimisation uses a convex objective. The statement “a local maximum of a convex function is global” has the curvature direction backwards. The feasible set must also be convex: even a strictly concave objective can have a nonglobal local optimum when the feasible set has disconnected pieces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最大化使用凹目标；最小化使用凸目标。把“凸函数局部最大是全局最大”当作通则是方向错误。可行域也必须是凸集：即使目标严格凹，若可行域由两个分离区域组成，局部最优仍可能不全局。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two explicit failures show why the conditions matter. For the convex function $x^2$ on $[-2,1]$, $1$ is a local maximum relative to that interval, but $f(-2)=4>f(1)=1$. For a nonconvex feasible set $C=[-2,-1]\cup[1,3]$, restrict the globally strictly concave function $f(x)=-(x-2)^2$ to $C$. The point $-1$ is locally maximal within the left component with value $-9$, but the feasible point $2$ has value $0$. The connecting segment passes through forbidden inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个明确反例说明条件的作用。凸函数 $x^2$ 在 $[-2,1]$ 上的 $1$ 是相对于区间的局部最大点，但 $f(-2)=4>f(1)=1$。另取非凸可行集 $C=[-2,-1]\cup[1,3]$，把全实线上的严格凹函数 $f(x)=-(x-2)^2$ 限制在 $C$ 上：$-1$ 在左分支内局部最大，值为 $-9$，但可行点 $2$ 的值为 $0$。两点之间的线段经过了不允许的输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The optional Hessian criterion in the next paragraph assumes $f$ is $C^2$ on an open convex domain, meaning its second partial derivatives exist and are continuous. It is one way to establish the global shape assumption, not a requirement of the local-to-global theorem. [[Hessian凹凸性判据|The Hessian concavity criterion]] and [[严格凹与负定Hessian|its strictness boundary]] give the precise versions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下一段可选的 Hessian 判据采用 $f$ 在开凸域上为 $C^2$ 的条件，即二阶偏导存在且连续。它是证明全域形状的一条路线，不是局部到全局定理本身的要求。具体版本见[[Hessian凹凸性判据]]与[[严格凹与负定Hessian]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In second-order terms, $\nabla^2f(x)\preceq0$ everywhere on an open convex domain establishes concavity, and an everywhere negative-definite Hessian is sufficient for strict concavity. A Hessian sign at one point gives only local information, as explained in [[Hessian 局部极大判据|the local-maximum Hessian criterion]], while semidefiniteness may leave flat directions, as in [[半定Hessian无结论|the semidefinite-Hessian boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二阶语言中，在开凸域上处处满足 $\nabla^2f(x)\preceq0$ 可用来证明 $f$ 凹；处处严格负定是严格凹的充分条件。但某一点的 Hessian 定号只给局部信息，见 [[Hessian 局部极大判据]]；半定还可能留下平坦方向，见 [[半定Hessian无结论]]。

> [!question]- Check your understanding · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does the globality proof require the feasible set to be convex as well as the objective to be concave?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 为什么凹性证明必须同时使用可行集的凸性？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The proof moves from a local point toward any allegedly better point along their connecting segment. Convexity is what keeps that segment feasible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 证明要把局部点与任意更优点之间的线段留在可行域内；若线段离开可行域，凹性无法制造任意接近的可行改进点。
