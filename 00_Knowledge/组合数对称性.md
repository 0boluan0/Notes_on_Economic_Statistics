# Binomial coefficient symmetry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*组合数对称性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing who is included also determines who is left out. If the available people are $A,B,C$ and we choose one person, each choice is paired with exactly one two-person group left out. We can recover the chosen person from that excluded group, so no information is lost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确定谁入选，就同时确定了谁未入选。候选人是 $A,B,C$，每次选一人，都恰好对应一个由另外两人组成的未入选集合；从这个集合也能恢复入选的人，因此没有丢失信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Chosen person | People left out |
|---|---|
| $\{A\}$ | $\{B,C\}$ |
| $\{B\}$ | $\{A,C\}$ |
| $\{C\}$ | $\{A,B\}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each one-person subset and each two-person subset occurs once in the table, so the two collections have the same size. The subsets in the two columns are different; the reversible pairing explains why their counts agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

表中每个单人集合和每个两人集合都恰好出现一次，所以选一人和选两人的数量相同。两列中的集合并不相同，数量相同来自可逆的对应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U$ be a fixed collection of $n$ distinct objects, and let $k$ be an integer with $0\le k\le n$. A [[组合数|binomial coefficient]] $\binom nk$ counts the unordered, nonrepeating selections of $k$ objects. For a selected subset $A$, its **complement in $U$**, written $U\setminus A$, consists of all objects in $U$ that were not selected. It has $n-k$ members.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定由 $n$ 个不同对象组成的全集 $U$，令 $k$ 为满足 $0\le k\le n$ 的整数。[[组合数]] $\binom nk$ 计算无序、无重复地选出 $k$ 个对象的数量。入选子集 $A$ 在 $U$ 中的**补集**记为 $U\setminus A$，由 $U$ 内所有未入选对象组成，共有 $n-k$ 个成员。

> [!abstract] Selected and excluded are reversible descriptions · 入选与未入选可以互相恢复
> $$\binom nk=\binom n{n-k}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The mapping $f(A)=U\setminus A$ pairs every $k$-element subset with one $(n-k)$-element subset. Taking the complement again returns $A$, so the mapping has an inverse. This is [[双射计数|counting by a bijection]], a one-to-one correspondence that covers both collections completely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 映射 $f(A)=U\setminus A$ 把每个 $k$ 元子集配到一个 $(n-k)$ 元子集。再次取补集就回到 $A$，因此映射可逆。这就是[[双射计数]]：两边所有对象都恰好一一配对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, choosing three committee members from ten people is equivalent to choosing the seven people left out. Thus $\binom{10}{3}=\binom{10}{7}=120$. We may count using the smaller of $k$ and $n-k$, which can shorten a calculation. The argument is about counts and needs no random sampling or probability assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，从十个人中选三人组成委员会，等价于选定哪七个人不参加，因此 $\binom{10}{3}=\binom{10}{7}=120$。计算时可以使用 $k$ 与 $n-k$ 中较小的一个，减少步骤。这个论证讨论的是数量，不需要随机抽样或概率假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factorial expression confirms the same relation: exchanging $k$ and $n-k$ only swaps the two denominator factors in $n!/[k!(n-k)!]$. The complement argument explains why this algebra reflects the underlying selections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阶乘表达式也验证了同一关系：在 $n!/[k!(n-k)!]$ 中交换 $k$ 与 $n-k$，只是调换分母两个因子。补集论证解释了这个代数对称性为什么对应实际的选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $k=0$, the empty subset is paired with all of $U$; both sides count one choice. If $n=0$, $U$ itself is empty and the map pairs the empty subset with itself, giving $\binom00=1$. With the convention $\binom nk=0$ for integer $k<0$ or $k>n$, the symmetry remains true outside the selection range as well: both sides are zero. Nonnegative integer $n$ is retained throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $k=0$ 时，空集对应整个 $U$，两边都只有一种选择；当 $n=0$ 时，$U$ 本身为空，空集与自己配对，得到 $\binom00=1$。如果约定整数 $k<0$ 或 $k>n$ 时 $\binom nk=0$，那么范围之外也保留对称性：两边同为零。这里始终要求 $n$ 是非负整数。

> [!warning] Transform the restrictions too · 限制也要随补集转换
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> From $A,B,C$, choosing one person **including $A$** has one result, $\{A\}$. Its complementary problem is to choose two people **excluding $A$**, which also has one result, $\{B,C\}$. Choosing two people while still requiring inclusion of $A$ gives two results instead. Complementation reverses membership; keeping the same verbal restriction can change the problem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $A,B,C$ 中选一人且**必须包含 $A$**，只有 $\{A\}$ 一种；对应的补集问题是选两人且**不能包含 $A$**，同样只有 $\{B,C\}$ 一种。如果仍要求“选两人且包含 $A$”，就有两种结果了。取补集会反转成员资格，不能原样照搬所有文字限制。
