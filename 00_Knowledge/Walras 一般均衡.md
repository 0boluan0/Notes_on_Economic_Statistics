# Walras 一般均衡把多个市场的价格与数量联立决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Walrasian general equilibrium jointly determines prices and quantities across markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子框架
> 一般均衡不把某个市场的需求和供给视为孤立对象，而是同时寻找一组价格与配置，使家庭预算选择、企业计划和各市场清算彼此相容。一个市场的价格改变会通过收入、投入成本和替代关系传到其他市场，因此价格通常是联立决定的。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> General equilibrium does not treat one market's demand and supply as isolated. It seeks prices and allocations that make household budget choices, firms' plans, and market clearing mutually consistent. A price change in one market travels through income, input costs, and substitution to other markets, so prices are generally jointly determined.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Walras 的历史贡献包括把交换、生产与资本形成的价格关系写成联立体系，并以 tâtonnement 描述需求超过供给时提价、供给超过需求时降价的试探。不同版次与子模型对失衡价格下是否成交的安排并不相同，不能一律改写成“一个现代拍卖人在任何交易前先算出全部均衡价格”。这个调价构造也不是对所有现实交易制度的描述，更没有自动证明实际动态必然收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Walras organised exchange, production, and capital-formation price relations as simultaneous systems and used tâtonnement to describe trial price increases under excess demand and decreases under excess supply. His editions and nested models differed over whether trade could occur at disequilibrium prices, so they should not all be rewritten as a modern auctioneer calculating every equilibrium price before any trade. The adjustment construction neither describes every real trading institution nor proves convergence of actual dynamics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“方程数等于未知数数目”也不是均衡存在、唯一或稳定的充分证明。后来 Arrow–Debreu 理论用连续性、凸性等明确条件建立存在结果；这些后来的定理扩展了 Walras 的对象，不能原封不动归为他已经证明的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counting as many equations as unknowns is not sufficient to prove existence, uniqueness, or stability. Later Arrow–Debreu theory established existence under explicit continuity, convexity, and related conditions. Those results develop the Walrasian object and should not be attributed wholesale to Walras as already proved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 能否只把咖啡价格改动、同时假定家庭收入和所有替代品价格完全不变，却仍称为完整一般均衡分析？
>
> **答案：** 不能；那是局部或条件分析。一般均衡必须把会实质反馈的预算、投入和其他市场一起求相容。

## 来源与核验

- [Walras, *Elements of Pure Economics*](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL32990431M/Elements_of_pure_economics)：核对多市场联立、清算对象与 tâtonnement 的原始框架。
- [Franco Donzelli, “Equilibrium and Tâtonnement in Walras's *Eléments*” (2007)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/hid/journl/v15y200734p85-138.html)：逐版核对 tâtonnement 在交换、生产与资本形成等子模型中的变化，避免把后来的统一化故事反投射给所有版次。
- [Arrow and Debreu, “Existence of an Equilibrium for a Competitive Economy” (1954)](https://doi.org/10.2307/1907353)：核对后来存在性定理确实另外陈述了消费、生产、凸性与连续性等条件；它不等于 Walras 的方程计数。
- [[竞争均衡预算与出清]] 与 [[第一福利定理]]：承接后来交换经济的现代均衡定义与福利性质，避免把后来的条件化定理反投射给早期 Walras。
