# 保留原债权选择且其他条件相同时自愿转股权不会降低持有人价值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Voluntary conversion cannot reduce holder value when keeping the original debt remains an option and all other conditions are unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给原有债务附加一项自愿[[可转换债券|转股选择]]，而不降低息票、改变优先级或加入其他限制，持有人至少能不转股。因此在相同信用和估值环境下，可转债价值不低于这份原债权的价值。这个比较不是说实际可转债总比市场上任意普通债更贵：真实发行条款往往同时改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add a voluntary [[可转换债券|conversion choice]] to existing debt without reducing coupons, changing priority, or introducing other restrictions. The holder can at least keep the debt. Under the same credit and valuation environment, value cannot fall below that of the original claim. This does not rank every actual convertible above every straight bond, because real issuance terms often change together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简到期例子只允许在 $T$ 选择，无违约、无息票、无赎回或其他条件，债权支付 $F$，也可换取价值 $mS_T$ 的 $m$ 股。该时点持有人选择较大者：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a minimal maturity-only example, assume no default, coupons, calls, or additional conditions. The claim pays $F$ at $T$ or can be exchanged for $m$ shares worth $mS_T$. The holder takes the larger value:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_T=\max(F,mS_T)=F+(mS_T-F)^+.$$

若 $F=100,m=2$，股价 40 时保留债权得 100，股价 60 时转股得价值 120 的股票；第二种状态不是 100 加 120。这里的非负转股增益来自可选择，不是股票一定上涨。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $F=100,m=2$, a share price of 40 leads to repayment of 100, while a price of 60 leads to shares worth 120, not 100 plus 120. The nonnegative incremental payoff comes from choice, not guaranteed stock appreciation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

提前转股会终止相应债务现金流，赎回可改变继续持债的机会，稀释和违约又会影响股票与债权的联合价值。因此一般可转债不能未经合同和模型检验，就按“普通债价格加一份独立股票欧式看涨期权”机械估值。债权底价本身也会随利率与信用变化，不是面值保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Early conversion terminates the corresponding debt cash flows; calls can change the opportunity to remain a creditor; dilution and default affect stock and debt jointly. A general convertible therefore cannot be mechanically valued as a straight bond plus an independent European stock call without checking the contract and model. The debt-value floor itself moves with rates and credit and is not a par guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
