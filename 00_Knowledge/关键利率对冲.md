# 关键利率对冲按各节点敞口联立求解对冲头寸

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A key-rate hedge solves jointly for holdings that offset exposures at each specified node.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当不同期限可以分别移动，抵销一个总 DV01 不足以抵销整条曲线。先对组合和所有对冲工具使用同一组 [[关键利率久期|节点冲击]]，统一插值、曲线重建、币种和估值假设。把每个因素的带符号美元／bp 敞口列成向量 $\mathbf d$；第 $j$ 个工具每单位多头在各因素上的敞口构成矩阵 $B$ 的第 $j$ 列。对冲持有量 $\mathbf h$ 应满足：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When maturities can move separately, cancelling one total DV01 does not cancel the whole curve. Use the same [[关键利率久期|node shocks]] for the portfolio and all hedge instruments, with aligned interpolation, curve rebuilding, currency, and valuation assumptions. Put signed dollar-per-bp exposures in vector $\mathbf d$. Column $j$ of $B$ contains every factor exposure of one long unit of hedge $j$. Hedge holdings must satisfy:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathbf d+B\mathbf h=\mathbf0.
$$

最小两节点例：组合敞口为 $(60,90)^T$；工具 A 每单位在两节点上的敞口为 $(20,5)^T$，工具 B 为 $(5,20)^T$，单位均为美元／bp。两个工具都不是只影响一个节点，因此同时求解：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a two-node example, the portfolio exposure is $(60,90)^T$. One unit of instrument A contributes $(20,5)^T$, and B contributes $(5,20)^T$, all in USD per bp. Neither instrument affects only one node, so solve simultaneously:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{pmatrix}20&5\\5&20\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}h_A\\h_B\end{pmatrix}
=-\begin{pmatrix}60\\90\end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\begin{pmatrix}h_A\\h_B\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-4\end{pmatrix}.
$$

读回检验：第一节点 $60-40-20=0$，第二节点 $90-10-80=0$。若只用 A 并按总和计算，原组合总敞口为 150、A 为 25，做空 6 单位虽使总和为零，却留下 $(-60,60)^T$。第一节点上移 1bp、第二节点下移 1bp 时，一阶损益为 $-(-60\times1+60\times(-1))=+120$ 美元，仍然对 [[曲线形变|非平行变化]] 敏感。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check each row: $60-40-20=0$ and $90-10-80=0$. Using A alone to match totals gives six short units, since total exposures are 150 and 25. The residual is nevertheless $(-60,60)^T$. A 1bp rise at the first node and 1bp fall at the second produces first-order P&L of $-(-60\times1+60\times(-1))=+120$ USD. The position still responds to [[曲线形变|nonparallel moves]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确一阶中性的可解条件是 $-\mathbf d$ 在 $B$ 的 [[列空间]] 中；方阵满秩时有唯一解。工具数量不少于节点数量并不保证可解，因为它们可能暴露方向重复。约束、整数单位或工具不足时，可以另定带权残余目标，但那是近似匹配，须报告剩余向量，不能只报一个接近零的总和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exact first-order neutrality is feasible when $-\mathbf d$ lies in the [[列空间|column space]] of $B$. A square full-rank matrix gives a unique solution. Having at least as many instruments as nodes is insufficient if their exposure directions are redundant. Constraints, integer units, or missing instruments can call for a weighted residual objective, but that is approximate matching: report the remaining vector, not only a near-zero total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的中性只覆盖所列节点的一阶变化，未消除节点外形状、交叉凸性、其他曲线、信用、波动率或模型误差。市场和支付状态变化后，应以完整重新估值及新的敏感度检查原头寸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neutrality covers only first-order moves in the listed nodes. It does not remove unrepresented curve shapes, cross-convexity, other curves, credit, volatility, or model error. After market or cash-flow-state changes, check the holdings with full repricing and updated sensitivities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
