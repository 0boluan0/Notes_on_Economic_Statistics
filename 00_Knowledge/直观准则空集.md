# 若一个离轨信号对所有发送者类型都被均衡支配，直观准则不对该信号后的信念增加限制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If an off-path signal is equilibrium dominated for every sender type, the Intuitive Criterion adds no restriction on beliefs after that signal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 空集不是“相信没人”，而是准则在此沉默
> 若 $\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)=\varnothing$，没有任何类型即使面对最有利的理性接收者回应也愿意发送 $s$。这个信号只能被解释为失误，而不能据偏离动机筛出更可能的类型；[[直观准则]]因此不新增 posterior 支持限制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)=\varnothing$, no type would send $s$ even under its most favorable rational receiver response. The signal can only be treated as an error, so deviation incentives do not identify a more plausible type and the Intuitive Criterion adds no posterior-support restriction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当保留集合非空时，直观准则要求

$$
p\bigl(\Theta_{IC}(s,\sigma)\mid s\bigr)=1.
$$

这个写法不能机械延伸到空集，因为任何概率分布都满足 $p(\varnothing)=0$，不可能对空集赋概率 1；同样，也不能对 $BR(\varnothing,s)$ 直接取 minimum 来执行正式通过检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the survivor set is nonempty, the criterion places posterior probability one on it. The formula cannot be extended mechanically to the empty set: every probability measure assigns zero to the empty set, and a minimum over $BR(\varnothing,s)$ is not the ordinary passing test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“不新增限制”也不等于该处什么都不用检查。原候选仍须满足采用的 PBE 条件：posterior 是合法分布，接收者反应在该 belief 下最优，而且相应续局没有制造有利偏离。只是这些义务来自 PBE，而不是直观准则在这个空集情形下额外筛出的类型支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No additional Intuitive-Criterion restriction does not mean that the information set is unchecked. The underlying PBE still requires a legal posterior, a receiver best response to that belief, and no profitable deviation. Those obligations come from PBE rather than from an extra type-support restriction in this empty-set case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 某个离轨信号对每个类型都被均衡支配。能否写“直观准则要求 posterior 对所有类型赋零概率”？
>
> **答案：** 不能。那不是合法概率分布；正确结论是直观准则在该信号后不增加 belief 限制，随后仍按 PBE 检查合法信念与最优反应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. Assigning zero probability to every type is not a probability distribution. The criterion adds no belief restriction there, while the underlying PBE requirements remain in force.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
