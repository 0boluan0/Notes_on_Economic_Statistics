# 导数的应用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Applications of derivatives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 从导数中读出函数的局部形状、最优选择和动态变化。
> **具体锚点：** 企业选择产量时，利润最大化常把“再生产一单位的收益”和“再生产一单位的成本”比较，即 $MR=MC$。
> **核心难点：** 一阶条件只生成候选点；约束、端点、二阶信息和模型范围共同决定答案。
> **为什么重要：** 它把微积分从求公式推进到决策、近似和解释。
> **继续：** 优化问题先读“优化”，动态问题转到“微分方程与增长率”，多变量约束见 [[多元优化：无约束、Lagrange、KKT 与包络|多元微分与约束优化]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Derivatives reveal local shape, candidate optima, approximation error, and dynamic rates of change.
> **Concrete anchor:** A firm's interior profit maximum often equates marginal revenue and marginal cost, $MR=MC$.
> **Central difficulty:** A first-order condition only generates candidates. Constraints, endpoints, second-order information, and the model's valid range determine the answer.
> **Why it matters:** This is where calculus becomes a language for decisions, approximation, and interpretation.
> **Continue with:** Use the optimisation workflow below, then move to [[多元优化：无约束、Lagrange、KKT 与包络|multivariable and constrained optimisation]] when several choices interact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 线性与二次近似
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Linear and quadratic approximation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $a$ 附近，$f(a+h)\approx f(a)+f'(a)h$；误差由曲率控制。加入二阶项得到 $f(a+h)\approx f(a)+f'(a)h+\frac12f''(a)h^2$。近似好不好不由公式外观决定，而由 $h$ 是否足够小、函数在邻域内是否光滑以及高阶导数是否可控决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Near $a$, $f(a+h)\approx f(a)+f'(a)h$, with error governed by curvature. Adding a second-order term gives $f(a+h)\approx f(a)+f'(a)h+\frac12f''(a)h^2$. Accuracy depends on a sufficiently small $h$, local smoothness, and control of higher derivatives, not on the formula's appearance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从导数读图像
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reading a graph from derivatives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$f'>0$ 表示局部递增，$f'<0$ 表示递减；$f''>0$ 表示斜率递增、图像向上弯。曲线描绘应按定义域、截距与渐近线、临界点、单调区间、凹凸性和端点行为依次进行。仅凭几个导数为零的点无法还原全局形状。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$f'>0$ means local increase and $f'<0$ local decrease; $f''>0$ means an increasing slope and upward curvature. A curve analysis should cover domain, intercepts and asymptotes, critical points, monotonic intervals, concavity, and endpoint behaviour. A few stationary points cannot determine the global shape.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 优化：把目标变成一阶条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Optimisation: turning an objective into a first-order condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写目标、选择变量和约束，再找可行域。内部可导最优点满足 $f'(x)=0$，但还要用二阶导数、导数符号变化或直接比较目标值验证；闭区间必须比较端点。若模型允许边界解，一阶条件可能永远不成立。经济学中的“边际收益等于边际成本”正是一阶条件的一个具体形式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the objective, choice variable, and constraints before finding the feasible set. An interior differentiable optimum satisfies $f'(x)=0$, but it must be verified by a second derivative, a derivative sign change, or direct comparison. Closed intervals require endpoint comparison. If boundary solutions are feasible, the first-order condition may never hold. The economic rule “marginal revenue equals marginal cost” is one concrete first-order condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 相关变化率与弹性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Related rates and elasticity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当多个量由同一时间驱动时，先写它们之间的恒等关系，再对时间求导。弹性 $\varepsilon=(dx/x)/(dy/y)$ 把单位变化转成百分比变化，适合比较量纲不同的响应，但应明确是点弹性还是弧弹性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When several quantities are driven by the same time variable, first write the identity linking them and then differentiate with respect to time. Elasticity $\varepsilon=(dx/x)/(dy/y)$ converts unit changes into percentage changes and allows dimensionless comparisons, but point and arc elasticity must be distinguished.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 微分方程与增长率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Differential equations and growth rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若变化率由当前状态决定，就出现微分方程，例如 $y'=ky$ 给出指数增长。解不仅要满足方程，还要满足初始条件。稳定性问的是受到小扰动后是否回到稳态，而不是只求出一个常数解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the current state determines its own rate of change, a differential equation arises; for example, $y'=ky$ produces exponential growth. A solution must satisfy both the equation and its initial condition. Stability asks whether small perturbations return to a steady state, not merely whether a constant solution can be found.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：利润最大化的完整检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: a complete profit-maximisation check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设产量 $q\ge0$，总收益 $R(q)=20q-q^2$，总成本 $C(q)=4q+20$。利润 $\pi(q)=16q-q^2-20$，一阶条件 $\pi'(q)=16-2q=0$ 给 $q=8$。由于 $\pi''(q)=-2<0$，且目标在 $q\ge0$ 上严格凹，该点是唯一全局最大值。若还有产能约束 $q\le6$，不能继续报 $q=8$，而应在新可行域内取边界解 $q=6$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let output satisfy $q\ge0$, with revenue $R(q)=20q-q^2$ and cost $C(q)=4q+20$. Profit is $\pi(q)=16q-q^2-20$, and $\pi'(q)=16-2q=0$ gives $q=8$. Since $\pi''(q)=-2<0$ and the objective is strictly concave on the feasible set, this is the unique global maximum. If a capacity constraint $q\le6$ is added, $q=8$ is infeasible and the solution moves to the boundary $q=6$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 $f'(x)=0$ 不能单独证明最大值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can $f'(x)=0$ not prove a maximum by itself?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它也可能对应最小值、平坦拐点，甚至只是众多候选之一；还要检查二阶信息、符号变化、端点和目标值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It may indicate a minimum, a flat inflection point, or merely one candidate among several. Check second-order information, sign changes, endpoints, and objective values.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个线性近似在哪些情况下会明显失真？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When is a linear approximation likely to fail badly?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 步长不小、邻域内曲率很大、函数不光滑或跨越了定义域/结构断点时。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When the step is not small, curvature is large, the function is nonsmooth, or the step crosses a domain or structural break.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 面对相关变化率题，第一步为什么不是立即求导？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is immediate differentiation not the first step in a related-rates problem?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 必须先建立各变量始终满足的关系；否则得到的导数没有共同约束，无法正确连接变化率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First establish the identity that all variables satisfy at every time. Without it, the derivatives share no constraint and cannot be related correctly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么严格凹性比二阶导数在一个点为负提供更强的最优性信息？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does strict concavity provide stronger optimality information than a negative second derivative at one point?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 一点处的二阶导数只支持局部分类；整个可行域上的严格凹性使任一驻点成为唯一全局最大值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A negative second derivative at one point is local evidence. Strict concavity over the feasible set makes any stationary point the unique global maximum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
