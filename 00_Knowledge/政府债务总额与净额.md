# 政府总债务统计债务工具负债而净债务另扣对应类别的金融资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gross government debt covers debt-instrument liabilities; net debt additionally deducts financial assets in corresponding instrument categories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总债务回答“在选定范围内，尚有多少债务工具负债”，净债务再考虑政府持有的相应金融资产。GFS的债务不只含[[政府公债]]，还可含应付账款、相关养老金负债等；股权和金融衍生品不因此都算债务。先按[[财政合并口径]]确定对范围外的负债，再谈总额或净额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gross debt measures debt-instrument liabilities within a stated perimeter; net debt also accounts for corresponding financial assets. GFS debt can include payables and relevant pension liabilities as well as [[政府公债|public borrowing]], but not every equity or derivative liability. Establish [[财政合并口径|consolidated coverage]] first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若合并总债务为600，持有现金和存款50、贷款债权30、股票70、建筑物200，且没有其他相应资产，按GFS对应债务工具类别扣除后的净债务为$600-50-30=520$。不是减去全部资产得到250。股票和建筑物仍影响资产负债表与财政能力，只是不在这个净债务指标的扣除集合中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose gross debt is 600, with cash and deposits 50, loan assets 30, equities 70 and buildings 200, and no other corresponding assets. Net debt is $600-50-30=520$, not 250 after deducting all assets. Equities and buildings still matter to the balance sheet, but not to this particular deduction set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净债务也不等于马上能偿还的净额。贷款30可能很难收回，现金50可能有用途限制；应另检查流动性、币种和期限。同时标明债务按名义值、市场值还是面值计量，不能把这些估值差异误作新借款或还本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net debt does not measure immediately available repayment capacity. Loans may be difficult to collect and cash may be restricted. Check liquidity, currency and maturity, and disclose whether debt uses nominal, market or face value. Valuation changes are not new borrowing or principal repayment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
