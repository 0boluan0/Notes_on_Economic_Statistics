# Riemann sum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*黎曼和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **Riemann sum** adds up approximate contributions from small pieces of an interval. In each piece, choose one input, read the function's value there, and multiply that value by the piece's width. For a graph, this makes a rectangle; for a velocity measured over time, it gives an approximate displacement. Adding the contributions gives one finite approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**黎曼和**把一个区间内各小段的近似贡献加起来：每段选一个输入，读取函数值，再乘以这段的宽度。对函数图像，这样得到一个矩形；对随时间变化的速度，这样得到一段近似位移。把各段贡献相加，就得到一次有限的近似。

> [!example] Two rectangles under a parabola · 抛物线下的两个矩形
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$. Split the interval at $1/2$, so each piece has width $1/2$. Sampling at the **right endpoints** gives heights $f(1/2)=1/4$ and $f(1)=1$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上取 $f(x)=x^2$。从 $1/2$ 处分成两段，每段宽 $1/2$。在各段的**右端点**采样，高度为 $f(1/2)=1/4$ 和 $f(1)=1$：
>
> $$S_{\mathrm{right}}=\frac14\cdot\frac12+1\cdot\frac12=\frac58.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Sampling at the **left endpoints** instead gives heights $f(0)=0$ and $f(1/2)=1/4$, so $S_{\mathrm{left}}=1/8$. Both are valid Riemann sums. They use the same pieces but different samples, and neither equals the exact integral $1/3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若改在**左端点**采样，高度就是 $f(0)=0$ 和 $f(1/2)=1/4$，所以 $S_{\mathrm{left}}=1/8$。两者都是合法的黎曼和：分段相同，采样点不同，而且都不等于精确定积分 $1/3$。

## Describe the pieces and the samples
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把分段和采样写清楚*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers, and let $f$ be a real-valued function defined throughout $[a,b]$. A **partition** $P$ is a finite ordered list of endpoints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 是有限实数，实值函数 $f$ 在整个 $[a,b]$ 上有定义。一个**分割** $P$ 是有限个按顺序排列的端点：

$$a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_n=b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ is a positive integer counting the pieces, not the endpoints: there are $n+1$ endpoints. Piece $i$ is $[x_{i-1},x_i]$, with positive width $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$. Choose one **sample point**, also called a **tag**, $\xi_i$ in this piece. It may be an endpoint or an interior point. The pieces need not have equal widths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的正整数 $n$ 数的是小段，不是端点：端点共有 $n+1$ 个。第 $i$ 段为 $[x_{i-1},x_i]$，其正宽度是 $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$。在这一段内选一个**采样点** $\xi_i$（英文也称 **tag**），它可以是端点，也可以是内部点。各段不必等宽。

> [!important] Height times width, then add · 高度乘宽度，再相加
>
> $$S(f,P,\xi)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The summation symbol adds the terms for $i=1,\ldots,n$. The notation records the function $f$, the partition $P$, and the list of tags $\xi=(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$. A negative height contributes a negative term: this is signed accumulation, not necessarily geometric area or total distance travelled.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 求和号把 $i=1,\ldots,n$ 的各项相加。记号分别记录函数 $f$、分割 $P$ 和采样点列表 $\xi=(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$。高度为负时，该项也为负；这里累积的是带符号的贡献，不一定是几何面积或路程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, split $[0,1]$ at $1/4$ and again use right endpoints for $x^2$. The widths are now $1/4$ and $3/4$, so the sum is $(1/4)^2(1/4)+1^2(3/4)=49/64$. Using the same weight $1/2$ for both heights would describe different pieces. A finite Riemann sum only needs the chosen function values to be finite; forming it does not prove that the function has an integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，从 $1/4$ 处分割 $[0,1]$，仍用右端点采样 $x^2$。这时段宽分别是 $1/4$ 和 $3/4$，因此和为 $(1/4)^2(1/4)+1^2(3/4)=49/64$。若仍把两个高度都乘以 $1/2$，描述的就是别的分段了。构造一个有限黎曼和只需所选函数值有限；能写出这个和，并没有证明函数可积。

## What must become small?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*需要趋于零的是什么？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **mesh** of $P$, written $\lVert P\rVert$, is its largest piece width:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分割 $P$ 的**网格大小**（mesh），记作 $\lVert P\rVert$，是最大的段宽：

$$\lVert P\rVert=\max_{1\le i\le n}(x_i-x_{i-1}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Making the mesh tend to zero ensures that **every** piece becomes small. Merely increasing the number of pieces does not: we could keep $[1/2,1]$ intact and subdivide only $[0,1/2]$. There would be more and more pieces, but the largest width would stay $1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

让网格大小趋于零，才能保证**每一段**都变小。仅增加段数不够：可以始终保留 $[1/2,1]$，只把 $[0,1/2]$ 越分越细。段数不断增加，最大段宽却仍为 $1/2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bounded function, the [[定积分|Riemann integral]] exists when all sufficiently fine partitions and all allowed tags give sums approaching one common finite number. Once integrability is known, any sequence of tagged partitions with mesh tending to zero gives that integral as its limit. [[连续函数可积|Continuity on a finite closed interval]] is a useful sufficient condition. The card on [[和式识别为积分|recognising a Riemann-sum limit]] uses this fact to evaluate limits of sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有界函数，若所有足够细的分割及其所有合法采样方式都使和趋向同一个有限数，[[定积分|黎曼积分]]就存在。已知可积后，任取一列网格大小趋于零的带采样点分割，所得和都趋向该积分。[[连续函数可积|有限闭区间上的连续性]]是一个常用充分条件。[[和式识别为积分]]会利用这一事实计算和式极限。

> [!warning] One successful sampling rule is not enough · 一种取点方法收敛还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,1]$, set $f(x)=1$ at rational numbers (ratios of integers) and $f(x)=0$ at irrational numbers. The equal-piece right endpoints $i/n$ are all rational, so every such sum is $1$. Yet every positive-length interval also contains irrational points: choosing an irrational tag in each piece makes every sum $0$, even as the mesh tends to zero. Thus the first sequence converges, but the function is not Riemann integrable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上，令函数在有理数（整数之比）处取 $1$，在无理数处取 $0$。等分后的右端点 $i/n$ 都是有理数，所以这种黎曼和始终等于 $1$。但每个正长度区间也含无理数：若每段都选无理采样点，和就始终为 $0$，即使网格大小趋于零也是如此。因此，第一种和式序列虽收敛，函数却不可黎曼积分。
