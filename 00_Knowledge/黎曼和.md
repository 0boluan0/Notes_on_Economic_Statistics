# 黎曼和把各小区间的函数采样值乘以区间宽度后相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Riemann sum adds sampled function values multiplied by subinterval widths*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $[a,b]$ 分成 $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_n=b$，在每段选一点 $\xi_i\in[x_{i-1},x_i]$。**黎曼和**是
$$S(f,P,\xi)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i,\qquad \Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}.$$
每项用一个矩形近似该段的累积量；负的函数值保留负号。若 $f$ 是速度，“速度 × 时间宽度”就是这一小段的近似位移。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partition the interval and choose one sample point in each piece. The Riemann sum is the sum of sample height times piece width, with negative heights retaining their signs. For velocity, each term approximates displacement over a short time interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在 $[0,1]$ 对 $f(x)=x^2$ 等分两段，取右端点得到 $S=(1/2)^2(1/2)+1^2(1/2)=5/8$。取左端点则为 $1/8$。两个数都是合法的黎曼和，却都不是这里的精确定积分 $1/3$。[[定积分]]进一步要求：最大区间宽度趋零时，无论怎样分段、采样，和都逼近同一个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two equal pieces under $x^2$ on the unit interval, right endpoints give $5/8$ and left endpoints give $1/8$. Both are Riemann sums, not the exact integral $1/3$. The [[定积分|definite integral]] requires a common limit as the largest piece width vanishes, independently of partitions and sample choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
