# 非正规就业是在法律上或实际中未获正式安排覆盖的有酬或营利性就业活动

按 ILO 2023 年第 21 届 ICLS 决议，非正规就业（informal employment）是为工资或利润生产商品、提供服务，但在法律上或实际中未被相关正式安排覆盖的活动。正式安排包括商业与税务制度、劳动法及社会保障等；判定须结合独立经营者、依赖型承包者、雇员或家庭帮工的具体工作关系，而不是一律查看有无书面合同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the ILO's 2023 resolution adopted by the 21st ICLS, informal employment comprises activities producing goods or services for pay or profit that lack coverage by formal arrangements in law or practice. Such arrangements include commercial and tax systems, labour legislation, and social protection. Classification depends on the work relationship—independent worker, dependent contractor, employee, or contributing family worker—not universally on a written contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以雇员为例，要看雇主是否实际承认该就业关系、劳动者是否实际获得相应保护。雇主是否为该岗位缴纳法定社会保险是核心识别特征，带薪年假和病假等也可帮助识别；具体组合按国家制度操作化。全民普惠保障或纸面上的法定权利本身，不能证明某个岗位已获有效覆盖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For employees, assess the employer's recognition of the relationship and effective access to protection. Employer contributions to job-dependent statutory social insurance are a defining feature; paid annual and sick leave can also inform classification, with operational criteria adapted to national institutions. Universal benefits or legal entitlements on paper alone do not establish effective coverage of a particular job.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[非正规部门]]回答经济单位属于什么部门；非正规就业回答岗位及其工作安排是否被覆盖。正规企业的员工、非正规企业的经营者或雇员、家庭雇用的家政工，都可能持有非正规岗位；“非正规就业人数”不能直接替换成“非正规部门就业人数”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[非正规部门|informal sector]] concerns economic units; informal employment concerns jobs and their arrangements. Informal jobs may be held by employees of formal enterprises, owners or employees of informal enterprises, or domestic employees engaged by households. Informal employment and employment in the informal sector are not interchangeable totals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非正规岗位仍是就业，不能计成[[失业率|失业]]或假设收入为零。同一人可同时有正规主业和非正规副业，统计时须核对主业、所有岗位还是人数口径；正规岗位中只有部分任务未获覆盖，也不自动把整个岗位改判为非正规。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An informal job is still employment, not [[失业率|unemployment]] or necessarily zero income. One person may have a formal main job and an informal secondary job, so distinguish main-job, all-job, and person-based measures. Partly uncovered tasks within a formal job do not automatically reclassify the entire job as informal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
