# Weighted least squares
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*加权最小二乘（WLS）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weighted least squares fits a regression by giving different importance to different squared residuals. A residual is an observed outcome minus its fitted value. [[普通最小二乘|Ordinary least squares]] gives every squared residual the same weight; WLS lets a less noisy measurement pull the fitted relationship more strongly when its greater precision is known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加权最小二乘用不同权重衡量各个残差的平方，再据此拟合回归。残差就是观测结果减去拟合值。[[普通最小二乘]]对每个残差平方赋予相同权重；如果知道某次测量更精确，WLS 可以让它对拟合关系产生更大的影响。

> [!example] Two instruments measuring one quantity · 两台仪器测量同一个量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Y_1=\mu+u_1$ and $Y_2=\mu+u_2$ measure the same unknown quantity $\mu$. The errors have mean zero, variances $1$ and $4$, and zero [[协方差|covariance]]. The inverse-variance weights are therefore $1$ and $1/4$, or equivalently $4$ and $1$. Minimising $4(Y_1-m)^2+(Y_2-m)^2$ over candidate means $m$ gives $\hat\mu=(4Y_1+Y_2)/5$. Readings $10$ and $14$ give $10.8$, rather than the unweighted average $12$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $Y_1=\mu+u_1$、$Y_2=\mu+u_2$ 测量同一个未知量 $\mu$。两个误差均值为零，方差分别为 $1$ 和 $4$，且[[协方差]]为零。逆方差权重为 $1$ 和 $1/4$，等价地也可用 $4$ 和 $1$。在候选均值 $m$ 中最小化 $4(Y_1-m)^2+(Y_2-m)^2$，得到 $\hat\mu=(4Y_1+Y_2)/5$。读数为 $10$、$14$ 时，结果是 $10.8$，而不是普通平均数 $12$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Across repeated measurements, this rule has variance $(16\times1+4)/25=4/5$, whereas the ordinary average has variance $(1+4)/4=5/4$. This is a comparison of [[方差|sampling variances]], not a guarantee that $10.8$ is closer to the unknown truth in this particular sample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 重复测量时，加权规则的方差为 $(16\times1+4)/25=4/5$，普通平均数的方差为 $(1+4)/4=5/4$。比较的是[[方差|抽样方差]]，并不保证在这一次样本中，$10.8$ 一定比 $12$ 更接近未知真值。

## From a weighted average to a regression
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从加权平均到回归*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the real-valued regression as $y=X\beta+u$. The vector $y$ contains $n$ outcomes, the $n\times k$ design matrix $X$ records $k$ regression terms per observation, $\beta$ contains their unknown coefficients, and $u$ contains the model errors. An intercept counts as a column of ones in $X$. Let $x_i^T$ be row $i$ of $X$; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Choose positive weights $w_i$ and put them on the diagonal of $W$. With [[线性无关|linearly independent]] columns of $X$, the unique WLS fit is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将实数回归写成 $y=X\beta+u$。向量 $y$ 收集 $n$ 个结果，$n\times k$ 设计矩阵 $X$ 为每个观测记录 $k$ 个回归项，$\beta$ 收集未知系数，$u$ 收集模型误差。截距对应 $X$ 中的一列 $1$。用 $x_i^T$ 表示 $X$ 的第 $i$ 行；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。选取正权重 $w_i$，放在对角矩阵 $W$ 上。若 $X$ 各列[[线性无关]]，唯一的 WLS 拟合为

$$
\hat\beta_W
=\arg\min_{b\in\mathbb R^k}\sum_{i=1}^n w_i(y_i-x_i^Tb)^2
=(X^TWX)^{-1}X^TWy.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $b$ is a candidate coefficient vector. Multiplying every weight by the same positive constant does not change the minimiser. Equivalently, multiply row $i$ of both $y$ and $X$ by $\sqrt{w_i}$ and run OLS. The intercept column must also be multiplied: it becomes $\sqrt{w_i}$, not a fresh column of ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $b$ 是待比较的系数向量。所有权重同时乘以同一个正常数，不改变最小化点。等价做法是将 $y$ 和 $X$ 的第 $i$ 行都乘以 $\sqrt{w_i}$，再做 OLS。截距列也必须一起变换：它变成 $\sqrt{w_i}$，不能另放一列全为 $1$ 的新截距。

## When inverse-variance weights are justified
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么时候逆方差权重有依据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the statistical comparison, fix $X$, or condition on its realised value. The [[条件期望|conditional-mean]] restriction $E(u\mid X)=0$ says that the errors average to zero at this design. Assume finite conditional second moments and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做统计比较时固定 $X$，或给定它的实现值。[[条件期望|条件均值]]限制 $E(u\mid X)=0$ 表示：在这个设计下，误差平均为零。再假设条件二阶矩有限，并且

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2H,\qquad
H=\operatorname{diag}(h_1,\ldots,h_n),\quad h_i>0,\quad 0<\sigma^2<\infty.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The known $h_i$ describe relative error variances; distinct errors have zero conditional covariance. This allows [[异方差|heteroskedasticity]], meaning unequal error variances. Set $W=H^{-1}$, so $w_i=1/h_i$. Dividing row $i$ by $\sqrt{h_i}$ makes every transformed error variance equal to $\sigma^2$ and leaves their cross-covariances zero. The [[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理|Gauss–Markov theorem]] then makes WLS the minimum-variance **linear unbiased** estimator, with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已知的 $h_i$ 描述各误差方差的相对大小；不同误差的条件协方差为零。这允许[[异方差]]，即误差方差不相等。取 $W=H^{-1}$，因此 $w_i=1/h_i$。将第 $i$ 行除以 $\sqrt{h_i}$ 后，每个误差的方差都变为 $\sigma^2$，交叉协方差仍为零。于是[[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理]]说明，WLS 是方差最小的**线性无偏**估计量，其协方差为

$$
E(\hat\beta_W\mid X)=\beta,\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\hat\beta_W\mid X)=\sigma^2(X^TH^{-1}X)^{-1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No Gaussian assumption or independence is needed for this finite-sample variance comparison; the stated second-moment conditions suffice. “Minimum” allows ties with OLS. The variance comparison also does not establish [[估计量一致性|consistency]] as the sample grows; that requires a sequence of designs and additional convergence conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个有限样本方差比较不需要正态性或独立性，上述二阶矩条件已经足够。“最小”允许与 OLS 打平。它也没有自动证明样本量增长时的[[估计量一致性|一致性]]；后者需要考虑设计矩阵序列及额外收敛条件。

> [!tip] Variance weights versus row scaling · 方差权重与行变换
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If error standard deviations have ratio $1:3$, their variances have ratio $1:9$, so WLS objective weights have ratio $9:1$. The row multipliers have ratio $3:1$. Weighting the squared residual and scaling the residual itself are different operations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若误差标准差之比为 $1:3$，方差之比就是 $1:9$，所以 WLS 目标函数中的权重之比为 $9:1$；行变换的乘数之比则为 $3:1$。给残差平方加权，与直接缩放残差，是两个不同操作。

## Weights also specify what is being fitted
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*权重也决定拟合的侧重点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
WLS is a fitting criterion even when weights are chosen for another purpose. Under the correct conditional mean $E(y\mid X)=X\beta$, any positive weights fixed given $X$ preserve conditional unbiasedness, but arbitrary weights do not receive the inverse-variance efficiency guarantee. If weights are estimated from the same outcomes, this fixed-weight argument no longer applies automatically; see [[可行GLS|feasible GLS]]. If errors are correlated, their marginal variances alone omit information needed by [[广义最小二乘|GLS]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使权重出于其他目的选择，WLS 仍是一个拟合准则。在正确条件均值 $E(y\mid X)=X\beta$ 下，任何给定 $X$ 后固定的正权重，都保留条件无偏性；但任意权重没有逆方差权重的效率保证。若权重由同一批结果估计，上述固定权重论证就不能自动沿用，见[[可行GLS]]。若误差之间相关，只使用各自方差还会漏掉[[广义最小二乘|GLS]]需要的相关信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a line is only an approximation to a curved conditional mean, weighting can change the [[条件均值与线性投影|best linear prediction target]]. For example, let $Z=-1,0,1$ with equal probabilities and $Y=Z^2$. The best unweighted line is $2/3+0Z$. Giving weights $1,4,1$ at these three points produces $1/3+0Z$: the middle point matters more. Reweighting can therefore change the target, although a change is not inevitable for every model and every weight choice. Precision weights, sampling weights, and frequency counts also carry different interpretations even when software uses the same input name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当直线只是弯曲条件均值的近似时，加权可能改变[[条件均值与线性投影|最佳线性预测的目标]]。例如 $Z$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，且 $Y=Z^2$；普通最佳直线为 $2/3+0Z$。若三处的权重改为 $1,4,1$，最佳直线就变成 $1/3+0Z$，因为中间点更受重视。因此重新加权可能改变目标，但并非任何模型、任何权重变化都会改变它。精度权重、抽样权重和频数还具有不同解释，即使软件使用相同的输入名称。
