# Pearson correlation coefficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*Pearson 相关系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Pearson correlation coefficient** measures linear co-movement after removing the variables' units. Its starting point is [[协方差|covariance]], the average product of deviations from the two means. Dividing by each variable's [[标准差|standard deviation]] measures those deviations on their own typical scale, making the result comparable across choices such as minutes and seconds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Pearson 相关系数**在消除变量单位后，衡量线性共同变化。它从[[协方差]]出发，也就是两个变量相对各自均值的偏差乘积的平均值；再除以各自的[[标准差]]，把偏差换成各自波动尺度的倍数，使分钟和秒这样的单位选择不再影响结果。

> [!abstract] Definition and conditions · 定义与条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If both [[方差|variances]] are finite and strictly positive, write $\sigma_X=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}$ and $\sigma_Y=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(Y)}$. The population Pearson correlation is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若两个[[方差]]都有限且严格为正，记 $\sigma_X=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}$、$\sigma_Y=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(Y)}$。总体 Pearson 相关系数定义为
>
> $$\rho_{XY}=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y},\qquad -1\leq\rho_{XY}\leq1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $\rho_{XY}$ names the correlation of $X$ and $Y$. The conditions $0<\sigma_X,\sigma_Y<\infty$ ensure that the division is meaningful. A constant variable has variance zero, so its correlation is **undefined**, not zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 记号 $\rho_{XY}$ 表示 $X$ 与 $Y$ 的相关系数。条件 $0<\sigma_X,\sigma_Y<\infty$ 保证这次除法有意义。常数变量的方差为零，因此其相关系数是**没有定义**，而不是零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, centre and rescale the variables as $Z_X=(X-E[X])/\sigma_X$ and $Z_Y=(Y-E[Y])/\sigma_Y$. Then $\rho_{XY}=E[Z_XZ_Y]$. Both standardised variables have mean zero and second moment one. The range follows because squared quantities cannot have negative averages: $E[(Z_X-Z_Y)^2]=2-2\rho_{XY}\ge0$ gives $\rho_{XY}\le1$, while $E[(Z_X+Z_Y)^2]=2+2\rho_{XY}\ge0$ gives $\rho_{XY}\ge-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地，先中心化并缩放：$Z_X=(X-E[X])/\sigma_X$、$Z_Y=(Y-E[Y])/\sigma_Y$，就有 $\rho_{XY}=E[Z_XZ_Y]$。两个标准化变量的均值都是零，二阶矩都是一。平方量的平均值不会为负：$E[(Z_X-Z_Y)^2]=2-2\rho_{XY}\ge0$ 给出 $\rho_{XY}\le1$，而 $E[(Z_X+Z_Y)^2]=2+2\rho_{XY}\ge0$ 给出 $\rho_{XY}\ge-1$，因此得到上述范围。

> [!example] A route label and waiting time · 线路标签与等待时间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ label a bus route and $Y$ be the wait in minutes. Assume the following probability table.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $X$ 标记公交线路，$Y$ 表示等待分钟数，概率表如下。
>
> | $X$ | $Y$ | Probability / 概率 |
> |---|---|---|
> | $0$ | $0$ | $1/8$ |
> | $0$ | $4$ | $1/8$ |
> | $1$ | $4$ | $3/8$ |
> | $1$ | $8$ | $3/8$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The table gives $E[X]=3/4$, $E[X^2]=3/4$, $E[Y]=5$, $E[Y^2]=32$, and $E[XY]=9/2$. Subtracting squared means or the product of means yields
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 表中可算得 $E[X]=3/4$、$E[X^2]=3/4$、$E[Y]=5$、$E[Y^2]=32$，以及 $E[XY]=9/2$。减去均值平方或均值乘积，得到
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac3{16},\qquad\operatorname{Var}(Y)=7,\qquad\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\frac34.$$
> $$\rho_{XY}=\frac{3/4}{\sqrt{3/16}\sqrt7}=\frac3{\sqrt{21}}\approx0.655.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is positive linear co-movement: route $1$ tends to have longer waits. It is not perfect correlation; at a fixed route, two waits remain possible. The number is not a probability or the fraction of waits predicted correctly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这是正的线性共同变化：线路 $1$ 倾向于等待更久。但它不是完全相关，因为固定线路后仍有两个可能的等待时间。这个数不是概率，也不是“预测正确的等待次数占比”。

## Interpreting the endpoints and changing units
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*端点与单位变化怎样理解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Correlation $1$ means that $Y=aX+b$ holds with probability one for constants $a>0$ and $b$; correlation $-1$ means the same with $a<0$. This is an exact affine relationship, not merely a vague “strong association”. For example, $Y=2X+3$ has correlation $1$ with $X$ whenever $X$ has finite positive variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相关系数为 $1$，表示存在常数 $a>0$ 和 $b$，使 $Y=aX+b$ 以概率 $1$ 成立；为 $-1$ 时，同样关系的斜率 $a<0$。这是精确的仿射关系，不只是笼统的“关联很强”。例如，只要 $X$ 的方差有限且为正，$Y=2X+3$ 与 $X$ 的相关系数就是 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding constants to either variable leaves correlation unchanged. Multiplying by positive constants also leaves it unchanged: covariance and the denominator scale by the same factor. If exactly one multiplier is negative, the sign reverses; if both are negative, it stays the same. Thus changing Celsius to Fahrenheit, $F=(9/5)C+32$, preserves correlation with another variable whenever the relevant correlations exist. Changing a variable to a constant makes correlation undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给任一变量加常数，不改变相关系数；乘以正常数也不改变，因为协方差和分母会按同样倍数缩放。如果恰好一个乘数为负，符号反转；两个都为负，则符号不变。因此，把摄氏温度换成华氏温度 $F=(9/5)C+32$，在相应相关系数存在时，不改变它与另一变量的相关系数。若把变量变成常数，相关系数就没有定义。

> [!warning] Zero is a limited conclusion · 零值的结论有边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the definition's conditions, $\rho_{XY}=0$ is equivalent to zero covariance. It can coexist with an exact nonlinear relationship; see [[零协方差不推独立|zero covariance does not imply independence]]. Pearson correlation concerns this linear summary; [[相关度量比较|other correlation measures]] answer different questions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在定义要求的条件下，$\rho_{XY}=0$ 等价于协方差为零；它仍可能与精确的非线性关系同时存在，见[[零协方差不推独立]]。Pearson 相关系数讨论的是这一线性摘要；[[相关度量比较|其他相关度量]]回答的问题不同。
