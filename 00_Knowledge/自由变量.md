# Free variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*自由变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **free variable** is an unknown that we can choose first when describing all solutions of a consistent linear system. Once the free variables are chosen, the equations determine the remaining, pivot variables. “Free” refers to an independently selectable coordinate of a solution, not to a variable that has no effect on the equations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**自由变量**是在描述一个相容线性系统的全部解时，可以先自行选值的未知量。自由变量选定之后，方程决定其余主元变量。“自由”指一个可独立选择的解坐标，不是说这个变量对方程没有影响。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A variable can appear and still be free
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the unknowns are $x_1,x_2,x_3$ and the only equation is $x_1+2x_3=4$. Its augmented matrix is already in [[行最简形|reduced row echelon form]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设未知量为 $x_1,x_2,x_3$，唯一的方程是 $x_1+2x_3=4$。对应增广矩阵已经是[[行最简形]]：

$$\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}1&0&2&4\end{array}\right].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[主元|pivot]] is in the coefficient column for $x_1$. Columns 2 and 3 have no pivots, so $x_2,x_3$ are free variables. Set $x_2=s$ and $x_3=t$; then the equation forces $x_1=4-2t$, and every solution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[主元]]位于 $x_1$ 的系数列。第2、3列没有主元，所以 $x_2,x_3$ 是自由变量。令 $x_2=s$、$x_3=t$，方程便要求 $x_1=4-2t$；全部解为

$$(x_1,x_2,x_3)=(4-2t,s,t),\qquad s,t\in\mathbb R.$$

> [!example] Changing a free coordinate changes other coordinates · 自由坐标会影响其他坐标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Choosing $(s,t)=(0,0)$ gives $(4,0,0)$; choosing $(s,t)=(5,1)$ gives $(2,5,1)$. Both satisfy the equation. The variable $x_2$ does not appear, whereas $x_3$ does appear and changes the required value of $x_1$. Both are free because either can be chosen without first fixing the other.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $(s,t)=(0,0)$ 得到 $(4,0,0)$；取 $(5,1)$ 得到 $(2,5,1)$，都满足方程。$x_2$ 没有出现在方程里，$x_3$ 则出现在方程里，并会改变 $x_1$ 所需的值。二者都自由，因为都可以在不先固定另一个的情况下选值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two parts to saying that this is the full solution set. Every pair $(s,t)$ produces a solution by substitution, and any solution must have $s=x_2$, $t=x_3$ and $x_1=4-2t$. Thus no solution is omitted, and each choice of free coordinates determines exactly one full solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“这是全部解”包含两点：代入后，每一对 $(s,t)$ 都确实给出解；反过来，任何解都只能取 $s=x_2$、$t=x_3$、$x_1=4-2t$。因此既没有遗漏解，也不会让同一组自由坐标对应多个完整解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recognize free coordinates after reduction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*化简之后再识别自由坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a consistent system $Ax=b$, let $A$ hold the coefficients, $x$ the unknowns and $b$ the right-hand sides. Use [[高斯消元|row elimination]] on the whole augmented matrix. A variable is free when its **coefficient column** contains no pivot in echelon form. It need not have a zero coefficient column, as $x_3$ above shows. The augmented column represents $b$ and is never a variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对相容系统 $Ax=b$，$A$ 存放系数、$x$ 存放未知量、$b$ 存放右端。在整个增广矩阵上作[[高斯消元]]。某个未知量对应的**系数列**在阶梯形中没有主元时，它就是自由变量。该列不必是零列，例中的 $x_3$ 就说明这一点。增广列表示右端 $b$，始终不是变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In reduced form, each nonzero equation has one pivot variable with coefficient $1$ and no other pivot variables. Move its free-variable terms to the right and the pivot variable is determined. In ordinary echelon form, back-substitute from the last nonzero row upward. Consistency ensures that all remaining zero-coefficient rows say $0=0$, so they impose no further restriction on the selected free coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在行最简形中，每条非零方程含有一个系数为 $1$ 的主元变量，不再含其他主元变量。把自由变量的项移到右边，主元变量就确定了。普通阶梯形则从最后一条非零行向上回代。相容性保证剩余零系数行都只是 $0=0$，因此不会再限制已选的自由坐标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If there are $n$ unknowns and $r$ coefficient pivots, there are $n-r$ free variables; the [[主元与自由变量|pivot and parameter count]] explains the general rule. The coordinates are chosen from the field of the system: real values for a real system, complex values for a complex system, or finitely many possibilities over a finite field. A positive number of free variables implies infinitely many solutions over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, but not over a finite field.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有 $n$ 个未知量、$r$ 个系数主元，就有 $n-r$ 个自由变量；[[主元与自由变量]]解释一般计数规律。坐标从系统所在的数域中选取：实系统取实数，复系统取复数，有限域中则只有有限种选择。存在自由变量在实数或复数域上意味着无穷多解，在有限域上并不如此。

## Check consistency before choosing parameters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add the equation $0=1$ to the opening example. The coefficient columns still leave $x_2,x_3$ without pivots, but **no values of any variables** can satisfy the new system. Nonpivot coefficient columns can still be identified structurally; there is no solution set to parameterize. In an echelon augmented matrix the contradiction appears as a pivot in the augmented column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若给开头例子加上 $0=1$，系数部分仍使 $x_2,x_3$ 没有主元，但**任何变量取值**都无法满足新系统。结构上仍可识别非主元系数列，却已经没有可供参数化的解集。阶梯形增广矩阵会以增广列主元显示这一矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the homogeneous system $Ax=0$, the zero vector always supplies a solution, so consistency is automatic. Setting one free variable to $1$ and the others to $0$ gives the special solutions used to [[RREF读取零空间基|construct a basis of the nullspace]]. The number of free variables is its [[零度|nullity]]. With no free variables, a consistent system has one solution; with no coefficient pivots, every unknown is free only if the right-hand side is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

齐次系统 $Ax=0$ 总有零向量解，因此自动相容。依次令一个自由变量取 $1$、其余取 $0$，得到用来[[RREF读取零空间基|构造零空间基]]的特殊解；自由变量个数就是[[零度]]。没有自由变量时，相容系统有唯一解；没有系数主元时，只有右端为零，全部未知量才都能自由选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Free” is relative to the chosen column order and parameterization. In $x_1+x_2=1$, the usual order makes $x_2$ free, yet one can equally choose $x_1$ first and write $x_2=1-x_1$. If columns are actually exchanged during elimination, [[列置换后的解向量|restore the original variable order]] when reporting a solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“自由”相对于所选列顺序与参数表达而言。在 $x_1+x_2=1$ 中，通常列序使 $x_2$ 成为自由变量，但也完全可以先选 $x_1$，写成 $x_2=1-x_1$。消元时若确实交换了列，报告解时必须[[列置换后的解向量|恢复原变量顺序]]。

> [!question]- A quick count · 检查参数个数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A consistent system has seven unknowns and four coefficient pivots. How many coordinates can be chosen independently? **Three.** Seven counts unknowns; the augmented column is excluded.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个相容系统有七个未知量、四个系数主元，可以独立选几个坐标？**三个。** 七数的是未知量，不包含增广列。
