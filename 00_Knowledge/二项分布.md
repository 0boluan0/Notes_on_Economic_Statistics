# Binomial distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*二项分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **binomial distribution** describes a count between zero and a fixed number $n$. Its standard model counts successes in $n$ trials that are [[相互独立|mutually independent]] and have the same success probability $p$. “Success” simply names the outcome being counted: it could mean a head on a coin, a defective item, or an answered question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**二项分布**描述一个从零到固定整数 $n$ 之间的计数。它的标准模型是在 $n$ 次[[相互独立]]、成功概率同为 $p$ 的试验中数成功次数。“成功”只是被计数结果的名称，可以是硬币正面、次品，也可以是答对题目，不一定代表好事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single success/failure record has a [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli distribution]]: it is $1$ for success and $0$ otherwise. The binomial variable records the total number of successes, so it can take more than two values. The theorem connecting the separate records to the total is explained in [[Bernoulli和的二项分布|sums of Bernoulli variables]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单次成功／失败的记录服从 [[Bernoulli分布]]：成功记 $1$，否则记 $0$。二项变量记录的是成功总次数，因此可以有不止两个取值。从各次记录推出总数分布的定理见[[Bernoulli和的二项分布]]。

> [!example] Count heads in three fair tosses · 三次公平抛币数正面
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Toss a fair coin independently three times and let $S$ count heads. Here $n=3$ and $p=1/2$. The eight ordered outcomes are equally likely. Their counts give this probability table:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 独立地抛三次公平硬币，让 $S$ 记录正面次数。这里 $n=3$、$p=1/2$。八种有顺序的结果等可能，按正面次数归组，得到下表：
>
> | Count $k$ · 次数 | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | $P(S=k)$ | $1/8$ | $3/8$ | $3/8$ | $1/8$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The counts themselves are not equally likely. Exactly two heads can occur in three orders—heads-heads-tails, heads-tails-heads, and tails-heads-heads—while three heads have only one order.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 次数本身并不等可能。恰好两个正面有“正正反、正反正、反正正”三种顺序，而三个正面只有一种顺序。

## The parameters specify a complete probability table
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*参数决定整张概率表*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $S\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p)$, read “$S$ has a binomial distribution with parameters $n$ and $p$”. Here $n$ is a fixed nonnegative integer, $p$ is a probability between $0$ and $1$, and $k$ is a candidate value of the count. The [[组合数|binomial coefficient]] $\binom nk$ counts ways to select the $k$ successful positions among $n$ positions. For $0<p<1$, the [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记作 $S\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p)$，读作“$S$ 服从参数为 $n,p$ 的二项分布”。其中 $n$ 是固定的非负整数，$p$ 是 $0$ 到 $1$ 之间的概率，$k$ 是要考察的成功次数。[[组合数]] $\binom nk$ 表示从 $n$ 个位置中选出 $k$ 个成功位置的方法数。在 $0<p<1$ 时，[[概率质量函数]]为

$$P(S=k)=\binom nk p^k(1-p)^{n-k},\qquad k=0,1,\ldots,n,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
with probability zero at all other values. The factors $p^k$ and $(1-p)^{n-k}$ describe $k$ successes and $n-k$ failures in one order; the coefficient counts the different orders. The separate Bernoulli-sum card derives why the trial assumptions permit this multiplication and addition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他取值的概率为零。$p^k$ 和 $(1-p)^{n-k}$ 对应一个固定顺序中的 $k$ 次成功与 $n-k$ 次失败，前面的组合数则数出所有这样的顺序。为什么试验条件允许这样相乘、相加，由 Bernoulli 和的卡片具体推导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\operatorname{Binomial}(10,0.3)$, ten is the number of trials, not the number of successes already observed. Each integer from $0$ to $10$ has positive probability, while a value such as $2.5$ cannot be a count. With only one trial, $\operatorname{Binomial}(1,p)$ is the Bernoulli distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\operatorname{Binomial}(10,0.3)$ 中，十是试验次数，不是已经观察到的成功次数。$0$ 到 $10$ 的每个整数都有正概率，而 $2.5$ 不能作为次数。只有一次试验时，$\operatorname{Binomial}(1,p)$ 就是 Bernoulli 分布。

> [!note] Certain outcomes at the boundaries · 边界参数对应确定的计数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $p=0$, then $S=0$ with probability one. If $p=1$, then $S=n$ with probability one. If $n=0$, there are no trials and $S=0$. These statements define the boundary cases directly, without needing to interpret a $0^0$ term in the PMF. Thus the positive-probability values can shrink to a single point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $p=0$，则 $S=0$ 的概率为一；若 $p=1$，则 $S=n$ 的概率为一。若 $n=0$，没有任何试验，因此 $S=0$。直接用这些陈述定义边界情形，就不用在 PMF 中处理 $0^0$。所以，真正具有正概率的取值有时会缩成一个点。

## Centre, spread, and choosing the model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*均值、散布与模型选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|mean]] $E[S]$ gives the average count under the distribution, while [[方差|variance]] measures its squared spread about that mean. For the binomial law,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望]] $E[S]$ 表示这个分布下的平均次数，[[方差]]衡量围绕均值的平方散布。对于二项分布，

$$E[S]=np,\qquad\operatorname{Var}(S)=np(1-p).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the three fair tosses, the mean is $1.5$ and the variance is $0.75$. A mean count of $1.5$ does not require any single experiment to produce a fractional count. At $p=0$ or $p=1$, variance is zero because the count is certain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对三次公平抛币，均值为 $1.5$，方差为 $0.75$。平均次数为 $1.5$，并不要求某次试验真的出现分数次结果。$p=0$ 或 $p=1$ 时，次数确定，所以方差为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The PMF defines the distribution. The independent common-$p$ trial model is a way to obtain that PMF. When analysing actual records, “there are $n$ binary observations” alone is generally insufficient: changing success probabilities or dependence can change the count distribution. Check those assumptions before assigning a binomial model; see the [[Bernoulli和的二项分布|Bernoulli-sum theorem and its boundaries]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PMF 定义了这个分布，相互独立且具有共同 $p$ 的试验模型则是一种得到它的方式。分析实际记录时，仅知道“有 $n$ 条二元观测”通常还不够：成功率变化或相互依赖，都可能改变总数的分布。使用二项模型前要检查这些条件，详见 [[Bernoulli和的二项分布|Bernoulli 和定理及其边界]]。
