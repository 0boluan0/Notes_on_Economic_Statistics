# 排放配额来自总量预算而减排信用来自相对基准的经核证减排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An emissions allowance comes from a capped budget; an emission-reduction credit comes from verified reductions against a baseline.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

排放配额与减排信用都可能被交易，但它们产生数量的依据不同。[[总量排放交易|配额]]把某个总预算分成有效排放授权；项目型减排信用则比较实际排放与无项目的基准，并按规则核证可计入的减排。不能因为两者都按吨计，就把“获发配额”与“证明新增减排”当成同一件事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allowances and credits may both trade, but their quantities arise differently. An [[总量排放交易|allowance]] divides a capped emissions budget into eligible authorisations. A project credit compares actual emissions with a no-project baseline and verifies eligible reductions under specified rules. Both being measured in tonnes does not make allowance allocation equivalent to proving additional reductions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对项目型信用，额外性要问：没有该项目或相关激励，所宣称的减排会不会照样发生？基准是需要论证的反事实，不一定等于历史排放、合法排放上限或企业自行选择的预测值。实际排放读数准确，也不能单独证明反事实正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For project credits, additionality asks whether the claimed reduction would have occurred without the project or incentive. The baseline is a counterfactual requiring justification, not automatically historical emissions, a legal maximum or a firm's preferred forecast. Accurate actual measurements alone cannot validate it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造例：项目用基准100、实际90申请10单位信用；但假设经过题目给定的有效核定，无项目排放本来就会是95，则新增减排只有5。若仍发10信用并允许另一排放源额外排10，总体会比真实5单位减排可抵消的数量多排5。问题出在基准，不在减法算错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a project claims ten credits from baseline emissions one hundred and actual emissions ninety. If a valid counterfactual specified in the example is ninety-five, additional reductions are only five. Issuing ten credits that authorise ten extra emissions elsewhere overstates the offset by five. The error is the baseline, not the subtraction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通总量配额的初始分配不逐项证明“这份配额对应某个新增减排项目”；它须在预算内合法发放、正确登记并按规则使用。若总量制度接受边界外信用，还需核对信用额外性、泄漏、持久性及是否重复使用，否则形式上的履约不保证原环境目标完整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary allowance allocation does not prove an additional project behind each allowance; it requires valid issuance within the budget, registration and use. If a capped system accepts outside credits, additionality, leakage, permanence and duplicate use must be checked so that formal compliance does not undermine the environmental target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里不指定某一现实项目的核证标准；是否有效必须读取所适用的方法和证据。配额与排放记录本身的质量检查见[[排放监测与履约]]，固定初始配额改变财富而不改边际激励的条件见[[初始配额中性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No particular project's verification standard is prescribed here; validity depends on its applicable method and evidence. See [[排放监测与履约|monitoring and compliance]] for records, and [[初始配额中性|initial-allocation neutrality]] for the conditions separating fixed endowments from marginal incentives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
