# 道德风险是行动难以充分订约且后果分担造成的行动激励问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Moral hazard is an action-incentive problem caused by imperfect contracting on actions and the sharing of their consequences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

道德风险指决策者的行动影响另一方的收益，但另一方无法无成本地充分观察、核验并据该行动执行契约；当契约让决策者只承担部分边际后果时，他选择的努力、预防或风险可能偏离对方希望的水平。这里讨论的是激励结构，不是对当事人品德的判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moral hazard arises when a decision-maker's action affects another party's payoff but cannot be fully and costlessly observed, verified, and made enforceable in a contract. If the decision-maker bears only part of the marginal consequences, effort, precaution, or risk choices may differ from what the other party wants. The term describes incentives, not personal morality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，火灾保险分担了失火损失，而保险人难以持续核验屋主是否认真维护防火设施，保障可能削弱预防激励。贷款中，相同借款人在取得资金后选择更冒险项目，也可能产生这类问题；这与[[逆向选择|原本不同风险类型的借款人选择是否申请]]不是同一机制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fire insurance shares fire losses, while an insurer may be unable to verify ongoing maintenance of fire precautions; coverage may therefore weaken prevention incentives. In lending, the same borrower may choose a riskier project after funding. This differs from [[逆向选择|pre-existing borrower types selecting whether to apply]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保障后的行为变化不能单独证明道德风险，更不能直接量出净福利损失。行为还可能因流动性改善、外部环境或真实需要变化而改变；应识别契约改变边际激励的部分。具体保险场景的类型、行动与共同冲击区分，复用[[逆向选择、道德风险与相关冲击]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A post-coverage behavioural change alone establishes neither moral hazard nor its net welfare cost. Liquidity, external conditions, or genuine needs may also change behaviour. Identify the component caused by altered marginal incentives. For the insurance-specific distinction among types, actions, and common shocks, see [[逆向选择、道德风险与相关冲击|three insurance-market mechanisms]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
