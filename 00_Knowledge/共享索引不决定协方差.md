# Shared indices and covariance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*共享索引不决定协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two events may both mention the same person, box or random outcome. That shared input tells us where to investigate dependence; it does not by itself tell us whether the events are dependent, or whether their indicator covariance is positive, zero or negative. Those questions depend on how the random inputs are generated and what each event asks about them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个事件可能都提到同一个人、箱子或随机结果。这个共同输入提示了应在哪里检查依赖关系，却不能单凭它判断事件是否相依，或指标协方差是正、零还是负。答案取决于随机输入怎样生成，以及两个事件各自问了什么。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For events $A,B$ with [[事件指示变量|indicators]] $I_A,I_B$, the relevant [[指示变量协方差|covariance calculation]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对事件 $A,B$ 及其[[事件指示变量|指示变量]] $I_A,I_B$，应检查的[[指示变量协方差|协方差]]为

$$\operatorname{Cov}(I_A,I_B)=P(A\cap B)-P(A)P(B).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term is the actual probability that both events happen. The second is the probability they would have under independence with the same marginals. A shared subscript supplies neither term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项是两个事件实际同时发生的概率，第二项是保持边际概率不变、若独立时的基准。共享一个下标，本身并没有算出这两项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A shared person with zero covariance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 共享一个人也可以协方差为零

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $B_1,B_2,B_3$ be the birthdays of three people, generated independently and uniformly over $d\ge2$ possible dates. Consider $A=$ “persons 1 and 2 match” and $C=$ “persons 1 and 3 match”. In symbols,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设三个人的生日为 $B_1,B_2,B_3$，它们独立且均匀地分布在 $d\ge2$ 个可能日期上。令 $A$ 表示“第 1、2 人同生日”，$C$ 表示“第 1、3 人同生日”，即

$$A=\{B_1=B_2\},\qquad C=\{B_1=B_3\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both questions use $B_1$. Once person 1's date is fixed, each other person has probability $1/d$ of matching it, and their birthdays remain independent. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个问题都使用 $B_1$。但给定第 1 人的日期以后，另外每人各有 $1/d$ 的概率与之相同，而且他们的生日仍然独立。因此

$$P(A)=P(C)=\frac1d,\qquad P(A\cap C)=\frac1{d^2}=P(A)P(C).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The events, and hence their indicators, are independent, with covariance zero. Uniformity matters: the probability of matching person 1 is the same whatever that person's date is. A shared input need not transmit useful information between the two yes-or-no answers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个事件及其指标独立，协方差为零。均匀性在这里有作用：无论第 1 人是哪天生日，别人匹配这个日期的概率都一样。共同输入未必会在两个是非答案之间传递有用信息。

> [!note]- Changing the birthday model changes the conclusion · 改变生日模型会改变结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep the three birthdays independent, but let date $t$ have probability $p_t$. Then $P(A)=P(C)=\sum_t p_t^2$ and $P(A\cap C)=\sum_t p_t^3$. For two dates with probabilities $3/4,1/4$, these are $5/8$ and $7/16$, so the covariance is $7/16-(5/8)^2=3/64>0$. The shared-person pattern is unchanged; the generating probabilities changed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 保持三人的生日独立，但令日期 $t$ 的概率为 $p_t$。此时 $P(A)=P(C)=\sum_t p_t^2$，$P(A\cap C)=\sum_t p_t^3$。若只有两个日期，概率为 $3/4,1/4$，两者分别是 $5/8$、$7/16$，协方差为 $7/16-(5/8)^2=3/64>0$。共享第 1 个人的结构没有变，改变的是生成概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Mechanisms can produce positive or negative covariance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 生成机制也可能产生正或负协方差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive example involving one die result, let $A$ be “six” and $B$ be “greater than four”. Every outcome in $A$ is also in $B$, written $A\subseteq B$. Such nested events satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正协方差可以出现在同一枚骰子的结果上：令 $A$ 为“六”，$B$ 为“大于四”。属于 $A$ 的结果也都属于 $B$，记为 $A\subseteq B$。对这类包含关系，

$$\operatorname{Cov}(I_A,I_B)=P(A)-P(A)P(B)=P(A)[1-P(B)]\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is strictly positive when $P(A)>0$ and $P(B)<1$. For a fair die it is $(1/6)(1-1/3)=1/9$. The reason is the nesting of the events, not the mere fact that the die is shared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $P(A)>0$ 且 $P(B)<1$ 时严格为正。公平骰子给出 $(1/6)(1-1/3)=1/9$。这里的原因是事件包含关系，而不只是“使用了同一个骰子”。

> [!example] Two draws from one finite pool · 从同一个有限总体抽两次
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A pool contains $N\ge2$ objects, of which $K$ are labelled successes, with $0\le K\le N$. Draw two objects uniformly without replacement. Let $X_1,X_2$ record whether the first and second draws are successes. Each draw has success probability $K/N$, but the probability of two successes is the fraction of ordered distinct-object pairs that are both successes:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 总体有 $N\ge2$ 个对象，其中 $K$ 个标为成功，$0\le K\le N$。均匀地连续抽取两个对象，不放回。用 $X_1,X_2$ 记录第一、第二抽是否成功。每抽成功概率都是 $K/N$，但两抽都成功的概率，是所有有序不同对象对中，两个都成功的比例：
>
> $$P(X_1=1)=P(X_2=1)=\frac KN,\qquad P(X_1=X_2=1)=\frac{K(K-1)}{N(N-1)}.$$
>
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)=\frac{K(K-1)}{N(N-1)}-\frac{K^2}{N^2}=-\frac{K(N-K)}{N^2(N-1)}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The covariance is negative when both types are present, $0<K<N$: taking a success removes one of the remaining successes. For $N=4,K=2$, it is $1/6-1/4=-1/12$. At $K=0$ or $K=N$, the records are constant and the covariance is zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两种对象都存在，即 $0<K<N$ 时，协方差为负：抽走一个成功对象，会减少剩余成功对象。取 $N=4,K=2$，结果是 $1/6-1/4=-1/12$。若 $K=0$ 或 $K=N$，记录为常数，协方差为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The practical check is to write the events and their generating model, then compare the actual intersection probability with the product. [[计数指标的索引策略|Indexing]] identifies the contributions being counted; it does not supply a dependence theorem. Even when all pairs are independent, [[两两独立不推出相互独立|the whole collection may still fail mutual independence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际使用时，应先写出事件和生成模型，再比较交概率与边际概率乘积。[[计数指标的索引策略|索引]]负责识别被计数的贡献，不能提供一个现成的独立性结论。即使每对都独立，[[两两独立不推出相互独立|整组仍可能不相互独立]]。
