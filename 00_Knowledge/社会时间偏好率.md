# 社会时间偏好率按跨期社会消费的边际价值确定贴现

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The social time preference rate discounts according to the marginal social value of consumption across dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

社会时间偏好率是以跨期社会消费权衡为依据的[[社会贴现率]]：比较未来与当前一单位实际消费各增加多少社会价值。它应区分对效用本身的时间权重与消费水平变化的边际效用效应；未来更富裕、消费边际效用递减时，即使没有纯粹的时间不耐心，未来额外一单位消费也可能权重较低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The social time preference rate is a [[社会贴现率|social discount rate]] based on intertemporal consumption trade-offs. It compares the marginal social value of future and current real consumption. Separate the time weight on utility from changes in marginal utility: richer future consumption with diminishing marginal utility can receive a lower marginal weight even without pure impatience.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用确定性两期、固定人口、代表性人均消费的明确评价模型说明：$U=u(c_0)+\beta u(c_1)$，$\beta>0$，$u'>0$。以当前消费为计价单位，未来一单位边际消费的价值和对应单期贴现率为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an explicit deterministic, two-period appraisal model with a fixed population and representative per-capita consumption: $U=u(c_0)+\beta u(c_1)$, with $\beta>0$ and $u'>0$. In current-consumption units, the marginal value of one future unit and the corresponding one-period rate are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$DF_{0,1}=\beta\frac{u'(c_1)}{u'(c_0)},\qquad r_{0,1}=DF_{0,1}^{-1}-1.$$

若 $u(c)=\ln c$、$c_0=100,c_1=102$、$\beta=1/1.01$，则 $DF=1/1.0302$，贴现率为3.02%。其中1%的纯时间贴现率与2%的消费增长共同起作用，不能把3.02%全解释成不重视未来。这个精确离散例不含风险，也不是通用政策参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With logarithmic utility, consumption rising from 100 to 102 and $\beta=1/1.01$, the discount factor is $1/1.0302$ and the rate is 3.02%. Both the 1% pure time discount rate and 2% consumption growth matter; the full rate cannot be interpreted as disregard for the future. This exact discrete example excludes risk and is not a universal policy parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际政策还可能单列风险成分。英国2026贴现补充说明用 $STPR=\rho+\mu g$，并明确其 $\rho=\delta+L$ 包含纯时间偏好与特定风险项；因此不能把该符号 $\rho$ 直接当成上述模型的纯时间参数。异质人群、风险与代际权重会增加额外判断，见[[社会贴现与代际评价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Policy frameworks may separately include risk. The UK's 2026 supplement uses $STPR=\rho+\mu g$ with $\rho=\delta+L$, combining pure time preference with a specified risk component. Its $\rho$ therefore cannot be identified directly with the simple model's pure time parameter. Heterogeneity, risk and intergenerational weights require further judgements; see [[社会贴现与代际评价|intergenerational appraisal]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
