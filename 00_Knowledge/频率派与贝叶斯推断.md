# 频率派以固定参数下的重复数据评估推断，贝叶斯方法用条件分布更新未知量的不确定性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Frequentist inference assesses procedures under repeated data; Bayesian inference updates uncertainty through conditioning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 频率派通常把参数视为固定未知，把数据看作随机，并考察程序在重复数据下的表现。贝叶斯方法为未知量指定[[先验分布]]，结合数据形成[[后验分布]]。两者都可以使用随机数据模型。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Frequentist assessment usually holds the unknown parameter fixed and varies the data. Bayesian inference places a prior on unknown quantities and conditions on the observed data. Both can model data as random.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

[[Bayes法则]]是概率恒等式，并不专属于一种学派。贝叶斯后验均值也是样本的函数，因此可以研究它的频率派偏差、方差和一致性。判断推断框架不能只看公式里是否出现期望或条件概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[Bayes法则|Bayes’ rule]] is a probability identity, not exclusive to one inferential school. A posterior mean is a sample function whose frequentist bias, variance, and consistency can be studied. Expectations or conditional probabilities alone do not identify the inferential framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
