# 无风险资产是在指定计价单位和持有期内收益事先确定的资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A risk-free asset has a return known in advance for a specified unit of account and holding period*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单期模型中，无风险资产的期末收益率在期初已确定：$R_f=r_f$ 几乎处处成立。这里的“无风险”指该持有期、该计价单位下的收益不随机，不是对所有日期、币种或购买力都没有风险的承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a one-period model, the terminal return on a risk-free asset is known at the start: $R_f=r_f$ almost surely. Risk-free means nonrandom over that holding period and in that unit of account, not safe at every date, in every currency, or in purchasing-power terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，若其他资产收益有有限二阶矩，无风险收益的方差及其与任何资产收益的协方差均为零。相关系数却因分母含零标准差而未定义，不能写成“与所有资产的相关系数为零”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its variance and covariance with any asset return are therefore zero when the other returns have finite second moments. Correlation is undefined because its denominator contains a zero standard deviation; it is not correct to assign zero correlation with every asset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(R_f)=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(R_f,R_i)=0.
$$

例如，期初投入 100 元、期末在所有模型状态下都收到 103 元，则这个单期名义收益为确定的 3%。若期末物价或兑换汇率不确定，实际购买力或外币收益仍可能变化。选择现实基准时复用 [[无风险利率口径]]：期限、币种、违约和再投资条件必须匹配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, an investment of 100 that pays 103 in every model state has a certain one-period nominal return of 3%. Uncertain terminal prices or exchange rates can still make its purchasing power or foreign-currency return random. For a practical benchmark, use [[无风险利率口径|risk-free rate conventions]] to check horizon, currency, default, and reinvestment conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“存在无风险资产”和“每位投资者可按相同利率无限借贷”是不同假设；后者还规定了可以怎样持有负的无风险头寸。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The existence of a risk-free asset and unrestricted borrowing and lending at one common rate are separate assumptions. The latter additionally specifies how a negative risk-free position can be financed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
