# 历史模拟把同一历史日的联合风险因子变动施加到当前组合，并从重估损失的经验分布估计风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Historical simulation applies each historical day's joint factor move to today's portfolio and estimates risk from the empirical revalued-loss distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 重放历史冲击，不是历史持仓
> 历史模拟固定今天的组合，把每个历史日的一整行联合风险因子变动施加到当前市场状态，再以全重估或经验证的近似得到情景损失。VaR 与 ES 从这些损失的经验分布读取。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Historical simulation fixes today's portfolio, applies each historical day's whole vector of joint factor moves to the current market state, and obtains scenario losses by full revaluation or a validated approximation. VaR and ES are then read from the empirical loss distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设当前因子为 $x_0$、组合价值为 $V(x_0)$，历史日 $i$ 的联合冲击为 $\Delta x_i$。按因子定义形成情景 $x_0^{(i)}$ 后，

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $x_0$ be the current factor vector, $V(x_0)$ the portfolio value, and $\Delta x_i$ the joint shock observed on historical day $i$. After mapping that shock into scenario $x_0^{(i)}$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L_i=-\left[V(x_0^{(i)})-V(x_0)\right].
$$

同一历史日必须作为一整行移动，才能保留样本内观察到的横截面依赖。逐因子从不同日期抽取冲击会制造未观察过的联合状态或抹掉共同压力。不同因子可采用对数、绝对或变换后变化，但单位与映射规则必须预先固定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each historical date must move as one row to preserve the cross-sectional dependence observed in the sample. Drawing each factor from a different date can invent joint states or erase common stress. Log, absolute, or transformed changes may be appropriate for different factors, but their units and mapping rules must be fixed in advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将 $n$ 个损失升序排列为 $L_{(1)}\le\cdots\le L_{(n)}$。采用经验左分位时，

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordering $n$ losses as $L_{(1)}\le\cdots\le L_{(n)}$, the empirical left quantile is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\widehat{\operatorname{VaR}}_\alpha=L_{(\lceil n\alpha\rceil)}.
$$

例如 $n=500,\alpha=.99$ 时，VaR 是升序第 495 个、即第 6 大损失。分位插值与 ES 对阈值质量的处理必须另行声明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n=500,\alpha=.99$, VaR is the 495th ascending or sixth-largest loss. Quantile interpolation and ES treatment of probability mass at the threshold must be reported separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[加权历史模拟]]、[[过滤历史模拟]] 与 [[历史模拟Bootstrap]] 分别改变日期权重、条件尺度和重抽机制；基本历史模拟本身不包含这些变体。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[加权历史模拟|Weighted historical simulation]], [[过滤历史模拟|filtered historical simulation]], and [[历史模拟Bootstrap|historical-simulation bootstrap]] respectively change date weights, conditional scaling, and resampling. Basic historical simulation does not itself include these variants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 历史模拟能否给股票列抽 2008 年某日、给汇率列抽 2020 年某日，再拼成一个“历史情景”？
>
> **答案：** 不能。基本历史模拟应保留同一日期的整行联合冲击；逐列拼接改变了依赖结构。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** Can historical simulation take the equity shock from one date, the FX shock from another, and combine them into a “historical scenario”?
>
> **Answer:** No. Basic historical simulation preserves each date's whole joint shock; column-wise date mixing changes dependence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 历史模拟只保留窗口中见过的冲击，不创造未观察到的尾部。
- 当前组合可重估，不等于历史制度代表未来。
- 全重估减少估值近似误差，但不能修复窗口、数据或映射错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Historical simulation cannot create shocks absent from its window.
- Revaluing today's portfolio does not make historical regimes representative of the future.
- Full revaluation reduces valuation approximation error but not window, data, or mapping error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
