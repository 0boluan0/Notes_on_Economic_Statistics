# Stationary weights of a birth–death chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*生灭链的平稳权重递推*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find how much equilibrium time a birth–death chain spends at each count, compare the traffic across one neighbouring pair. If the stationary mass at $n$ is $\pi_n$, upward traffic from $n$ to $n+1$ has rate $\pi_n\lambda_n$. Downward traffic across the same boundary has rate $\pi_{n+1}\mu_{n+1}$. Balancing these two quantities determines one weight from the preceding weight.

Here $\lambda_n$ and $\mu_n$ are the upward and downward rates **out of state $n$**, as defined in [[生灭链|a birth–death chain]]. A [[CTMC平稳分布|stationary distribution]] is a nonnegative probability row $\pi$ that remains unchanged under every elapsed-time transition $P(t)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要知道生灭链在平衡时把多少时间分配给各个数量，可以先看一对相邻状态之间的流量。状态 $n$ 的平稳质量为 $\pi_n$ 时，从 $n$ 到 $n+1$ 的向上流率是 $\pi_n\lambda_n$，跨同一边界向下的流率是 $\pi_{n+1}\mu_{n+1}$。让二者相等，就能由前一个权重算出后一个。

这里 $\lambda_n,\mu_n$ 是[[生灭链]]中**从状态 $n$ 出发**的向上、向下速率。[[CTMC平稳分布|平稳分布]]是非负且总和为一的行 $\pi$，在每个时长的转移 $P(t)$ 下保持不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Calculate three weights before normalizing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先算三个相对权重，再归一化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take states $0,1,2$ with $\lambda_0=\lambda_1=2$, $\mu_1=1$, $\mu_2=2$, and no outward jumps at either endpoint. The two adjacent equations are

$$2\pi_0=\pi_1,\qquad 2\pi_1=2\pi_2.$$

Thus the masses are in the ratio $1:2:2$. Their sum must be one, so

$$\pi=(1/5,\,2/5,\,2/5).$$

Across the first edge both flows are $2/5$ per unit time; across the second they are $4/5$. The flows need to match **across each edge**, not have one common value for every edge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取状态 $0,1,2$，令 $\lambda_0=\lambda_1=2$、$\mu_1=1$、$\mu_2=2$，两端均无向外跳转。两条相邻方程是 $2\pi_0=\pi_1$ 和 $2\pi_1=2\pi_2$，所以质量比例为 $1:2:2$。总和归一化后，得到 $\pi=(1/5,2/5,2/5)$。第一条边双向流率都是每单位时间 $2/5$，第二条边都是 $4/5$；要求的是**每条边的两个方向**匹配，不是所有边共用一个流率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why adjacent balance is enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么能够逐相邻状态平衡*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a formal solution of $\pi Q=0$, the state-$0$ equation is $\pi_0\lambda_0=\pi_1\mu_1$. At an interior state $n\ge1$, total incoming and outgoing flow gives

$$\pi_{n-1}\lambda_{n-1}+\pi_{n+1}\mu_{n+1}
=\pi_n(\lambda_n+\mu_n).$$

Rearranging shows that the net upward flow across edge $(n,n+1)$ equals the net upward flow across $(n-1,n)$. The first edge has net flow zero by the state-$0$ equation, so induction makes every edge's net flow zero:

$$\pi_n\lambda_n=\pi_{n+1}\mu_{n+1}.$$

These are [[CTMC详细平衡|detailed-balance equations]]: each pair balances separately. This reduction uses the line-shaped nearest-neighbour graph and its lower boundary. It is not a rule that every stationary CTMC must balance every pair; circulating flows on a cycle are possible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形式方程 $\pi Q=0$ 在状态 $0$ 给出 $\pi_0\lambda_0=\pi_1\mu_1$。在内部状态 $n\ge1$，总流入等于总流出，便得到上面的三项方程。移项后可见，边 $(n,n+1)$ 的净向上流等于边 $(n-1,n)$ 的净向上流。第一条边由状态 $0$ 的方程得知净流为零，于是递推得每条边净流都为零，即 $\pi_n\lambda_n=\pi_{n+1}\mu_{n+1}$。

这是[[CTMC详细平衡|详细平衡]]：每对状态分别匹配。这里的化简依靠相邻状态构成的线形图和下边界；它不是所有平稳 CTMC 都必须逐对平衡的规则，因为环上可以有循环净流。

> [!important] The product and its normalizing sum · 乘积与归一化和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $\{0,1,\ldots\}$ assume $0<\lambda_n<\infty$ and $0<\mu_{n+1}<\infty$ for every $n\ge0$. Define relative weights
>
> $$w_0=1,\qquad
> w_n=\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{\lambda_k}{\mu_{k+1}}\quad(n\ge1),\qquad
> Z=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}w_n.$$
>
> The product multiplies the ratios for the edges $0\leftrightarrow1$ through $n-1\leftrightarrow n$. Its denominator is $\mu_{k+1}$, the rate returning from the **next** state. Recursion gives $\pi_n=\pi_0w_n$; normalization therefore requires $Z<\infty$ and forces $\pi_0=1/Z$, so $\pi_n=w_n/Z$.
>
> For a finite irreducible chain on $\{0,\ldots,N\}$, stop the sum at $N$ and use positive rates on its internal edges. Then $Z$ is automatically finite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\{0,1,\ldots\}$ 上，假设每个 $n\ge0$ 的相邻双向速率均正且有限，即 $\lambda_n>0$、$\mu_{n+1}>0$。令 $w_0=1$，$w_n$ 为上面的速率比乘积，$Z$ 为全部权重之和。乘积依次经过 $0\leftrightarrow1$ 到 $n-1\leftrightarrow n$；分母是 $\mu_{k+1}$，也就是从**下一个状态**返回的速率。递推得到 $\pi_n=\pi_0w_n$，归一化因此要求 $Z<\infty$，并迫使 $\pi_0=1/Z$、$\pi_n=w_n/Z$。
>
> 对 $\{0,\ldots,N\}$ 上内部相邻双向速率均正的有限不可约链，把求和截到 $N$；这时 $Z$ 自动有限。

## When the weights describe the actual process
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*何时权重确实是过程的平稳概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the finite case, the [[有限CTMC平稳生成矩阵判据|finite generator criterion]] converts these normalized balance equations into $\pi P(t)=\pi$. It also follows that the stationary chain is [[CTMC详细平衡与可逆|reversible]].

On the infinite state space, additionally require that the minimal birth–death process is **non-explosive**, so it stays in its original state space at every finite time. Under the positive-rate assumptions above, the normalized weights then give its unique stationary distribution and the stationary process is reversible. For example, a uniform bound on $\lambda_n+\mu_n$ supplies a [[有界率非爆炸|sufficient non-explosion check]]. Without this process check, a summable formal solution of $\pi Q=0$ need not be stationary for the specified process: an [[最小CTMC|explosive minimal chain]] can lose mass into its cemetery state. The finite-state equivalence is not being applied to an infinite matrix without justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限状态时，[[有限CTMC平稳生成矩阵判据]]把这些归一化平衡方程转为 $\pi P(t)=\pi$，平稳链还具有[[CTMC详细平衡与可逆|可逆性]]。

无限状态时，还要求最小生灭过程**非爆炸**，即每个有限时刻仍在原状态空间内。在前述相邻速率均正的条件下，归一化权重才给出其唯一平稳分布，并使平稳过程可逆。例如对 $\lambda_n+\mu_n$ 给出统一上界，就能使用[[有界率非爆炸]]这一充分检查。没有过程条件时，可求和的形式解 $\pi Q=0$ 不一定是指定过程的平稳律：[[最小CTMC|爆炸的最小链]]会把质量丢入墓地状态。不能不加说明地把有限状态等价判据套到无限矩阵上。

> [!example] Equal edge rates can fail normalization · 相邻两率相等仍可能无法归一化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\lambda_n=\mu_{n+1}$ on every edge, then $w_n=1$. On infinitely many states, $Z=\infty$; no probability distribution can give every state the same positive mass. On a finite interval $\{0,\ldots,N\}$, the same recursion gives the uniform law $\pi_n=1/(N+1)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若每条边都满足 $\lambda_n=\mu_{n+1}$，则 $w_n=1$。无限多个状态时 $Z=\infty$，不可能给每个状态同样的正概率而总和为一。有限区间 $\{0,\ldots,N\}$ 上，同样的递推则得到均匀律 $\pi_n=1/(N+1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an internal rate is zero, first determine the [[跳链决定CTMC沟通类|communicating classes]] and their closed boundaries. Do not divide by a zero death rate or continue one positive-weight recursion through a one-way barrier. For example, an absorbing state supplies its own point-mass stationary law; multiple closed classes can supply different stationary mixtures. The positive-rate formula above is applied within the appropriate irreducible closed class, with its lower endpoint relabelled if necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若内部某个速率为零，先确定[[跳链决定CTMC沟通类|沟通类]]及其闭边界；不能除以零死亡率，也不能把同一条正权重递推跨过单向屏障。比如吸收态本身就提供点质量平稳律，多个闭类可以提供不同的平稳混合。前述正速率公式应在合适的不可约闭类内使用，必要时把它的下端点重新编号。
