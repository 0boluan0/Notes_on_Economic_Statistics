# 马尔萨斯陷阱是人口反馈在资源约束下吸收收入改善并使人均生活水平难以持续上升的机制

马尔萨斯陷阱（Malthusian trap）把两种关系连成一个反馈：生活条件改善使人口增加；在土地等资源受约束、每人可得产出随人口增加而下降时，人口增加又削弱先前的生活改善。这里的“陷阱”不是单指人多或收入低，而是收入改善难以持久的内生机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Malthusian trap joins two relationships into a feedback: improved living conditions increase population, while resource constraints cause output per person to fall as population expands. The additional population then erodes the initial improvement. The trap is an endogenous mechanism limiting persistent gains, not simply a large population or a low income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一块土地能养活更多人，不等于生活在其上的每个人会一直更富裕：还要追踪人口怎样响应，以及新增人口如何改变人均产出。把这两步写全，才能分析[[马尔萨斯技术冲击|一次技术改善的短期和长期结果]]；回落的参照点由[[马尔萨斯生存收入|人口净增长条件]]决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Land supporting more people does not imply permanently higher living standards for each person. Trace both the population response and its effect on output per person. These steps underpin the [[马尔萨斯技术冲击|short- and long-run effects of a technological improvement]]; the reference income is determined by the [[马尔萨斯生存收入|condition for zero net population growth]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是带条件的解释框架，不是“人口必然有害”的判断。若人口对收入的反应、生产技术或资源约束改变，同一反馈未必继续成立；仅观察到某国人口多且贫困，也不能证明这一机制正在起作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a conditional explanatory framework, not a judgement that population is inherently harmful. Changed population responses, technology or resource constraints may alter the feedback. A country being both populous and poor is not evidence by itself that this mechanism operates there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
