# 趋势、单位根与差分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trends, unit roots, and differencing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 区分可预测的确定性趋势与会永久累积冲击的随机趋势，从而避免伪回归和错误预测区间。
> **具体锚点：** 随机游走今天的一次冲击永久改变以后水平；围绕直线趋势波动的序列则会回到那条趋势。
> **核心难点：** ADF 的原假设是单位根，KPSS 的原假设是平稳；“未拒绝”不等于证明原假设为真。
> **为什么重要：** 去趋势、差分、协整和长期解释的选择都取决于这一判断。
> **继续：** 若多个 I(1) 序列可能共同移动，转到 [[协整与误差修正模型]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It distinguishes a predictable deterministic trend from a stochastic trend that permanently accumulates shocks, preventing spurious regressions and invalid forecast intervals.
> **Concrete anchor:** A shock to a random walk permanently shifts all later levels, whereas a trend-stationary series eventually returns to its deterministic trend line.
> **Central difficulty:** ADF has a unit-root null and KPSS has a stationarity null. Failure to reject never proves the null true.
> **Why it matters:** The choice among detrending, differencing, cointegration, and long-run level interpretation depends on this distinction.
> **Continue with:** If several $I(1)$ series may move together, see [[协整与误差修正模型|cointegration and error correction]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 确定性与随机趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deterministic and stochastic trends*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

趋势平稳过程 $y_t=a+bt+u_t$ 的冲击暂时，去除确定性趋势后 u 平稳。单位根过程如 $y_t=y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t$ 的冲击永久积累，方差随 t 增长；一阶差分后可能平稳。二者短样本图形可很相似，但长期预测和冲击解释完全不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the trend-stationary process $y_t=a+bt+u_t$, shocks are temporary and $u_t$ is stationary after removal of the deterministic trend. In a unit-root process such as $y_t=y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t$, shocks accumulate permanently and variance grows with $t$; first differences may be stationary. Short samples can look similar, but long-run forecasts and shock interpretations are fundamentally different.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对随机游走，$y_t=y_0+\sum_{j=1}^t\epsilon_j$，所以 $\operatorname{Var}(y_t)=t\sigma^2$。对趋势平稳序列，只要 $u_t$ 方差有限，偏离趋势的不确定性不会随时间无界增长。“远期预测区间是围绕一条线扩展”还是“围绕当前水平扩展”，正是两种模型的直观区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a random walk, $y_t=y_0+\sum_{j=1}^t\epsilon_j$, hence $\operatorname{Var}(y_t)=t\sigma^2$. For a trend-stationary series with finite-variance $u_t$, uncertainty around the trend does not grow without bound. The two models therefore produce forecast intervals that expand around a deterministic line versus around a current stochastic level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 差分与整合阶数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Differencing and order of integration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Delta y_t=y_t-y_{t-1}$。若差分 d 次后平稳，称 $I(d)$。过度差分会引入不必要的 MA 结构并放大噪声；不足差分保留非平稳性。季节差分处理固定季节频率的单位根，和普通差分不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\Delta y_t=y_t-y_{t-1}$. A series is $I(d)$ if it becomes stationary after $d$ differences. Over-differencing introduces unnecessary MA structure and amplifies noise, while under-differencing leaves nonstationarity. Seasonal differencing addresses a unit root at a fixed seasonal frequency and differs from ordinary differencing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个经典过度差分例子是对白噪声 $u_t$ 做差分：$\Delta u_t=u_t-u_{t-1}$ 是 MA(1)，且一阶自相关为 $-1/2$。原序列本已平稳，差分却人为创造了强负相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A classic over-differencing example applies a difference to white noise $u_t$: $\Delta u_t=u_t-u_{t-1}$ is MA(1) with lag-one autocorrelation $-1/2$. The original series was already stationary, but differencing created strong negative dependence artificially.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## DF 与 ADF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dickey–Fuller and augmented Dickey–Fuller tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以 $\Delta y_t=\alpha+\beta t+\gamma y_{t-1}+\sum_i\delta_i\Delta y_{t-i}+e_t$ 为例，单位根原假设是 $\gamma=0$。常数/趋势项和滞后阶数应与数据生成机制匹配；临界值不是普通 t 分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\Delta y_t=\alpha+\beta t+\gamma y_{t-1}+\sum_i\delta_i\Delta y_{t-i}+e_t$, the unit-root null is $\gamma=0$. Constant and trend terms, as well as lag order, must match the plausible data-generating mechanism; critical values are not those of an ordinary t distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

滞后差分项的作用是吸收检验回归的残差自相关，使关于 $\gamma$ 的参考分布可用。滞后太少会留下自相关，太多则损失功效。确定性项选错也会改变检验问题：含趋势的 ADF 问“是单位根还是趋势平稳”，不含趋势时并非同一零假设对立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lagged differences absorb residual autocorrelation in the test regression so that the reference distribution for $\gamma$ is usable. Too few lags leave dependence; too many reduce power. A wrong deterministic specification also changes the question: an ADF with trend distinguishes a unit root from trend stationarity, which is not the same alternative as in a regression without trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## KPSS 与联合判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*KPSS and joint interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

KPSS 以（水平或趋势）平稳为原假设。ADF 不拒绝且 KPSS 拒绝支持非平稳；ADF 拒绝且 KPSS 不拒绝支持平稳；其余组合提示样本信息不足、结构突变或设定问题。测试是证据组合而非机械裁决。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
KPSS takes level or trend stationarity as its null. ADF failure to reject combined with KPSS rejection supports nonstationarity; ADF rejection combined with KPSS failure to reject supports stationarity. Other combinations suggest weak information, structural breaks, or misspecification. Tests contribute evidence rather than mechanically deciding the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对四种结果的正确语气是“证据更支持……”而不是“证明……”。特别是 ADF 与 KPSS 都不拒绝时，可能是样本太短以致两个检验都缺乏功效，而不是序列同时平稳又有单位根。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The correct wording for all four result combinations is that evidence supports one description more strongly, not that it proves it. In particular, if neither ADF nor KPSS rejects, a short sample may leave both tests underpowered; the series is not simultaneously stationary and unit-root by logic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 伪回归与结构突变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Spurious regression and structural breaks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无关的趋势序列也可能给高 $R^2$ 和显著 t 值。结构断点会降低单位根检验功效，近单位根又在有限样本中难区分。应结合图形、领域知识、断点检验和不同样本窗。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unrelated trending series can generate a high $R^2$ and significant t statistics. Structural breaks reduce the power of unit-root tests, while near-unit-root processes are intrinsically difficult to distinguish in finite samples. Combine plots, domain knowledge, break tests, and alternative sample windows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 处理决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Treatment decisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测增长率可差分；关心水平长期关系时不要轻易丢弃水平信息，应检查协整或带趋势模型。任何处理都应与研究问题绑定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differencing may be appropriate when forecasting growth. When the long-run relationship among levels matters, do not discard level information mechanically; examine cointegration or a trend model. Every transformation must be tied to the research question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个稳妥流程是：先画水平、差分和季节差分；说明是否有经济上可预见的趋势/断点；在匹配的确定性项下组合 ADF、KPSS 及残差检查；最后将差分与去趋势的候选在真实预测目标上比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A defensible workflow is to plot levels, ordinary differences, and seasonal differences; state economically plausible trends or breaks; combine ADF, KPSS, and residual checks under matching deterministic terms; and finally compare differenced and detrended candidates on the actual forecasting target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 趋势平稳与单位根过程受到一次冲击后有何不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How do a trend-stationary process and a unit-root process differ after a one-time shock?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 趋势平稳过程最终回到确定性趋势；单位根过程的水平永久移动。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The trend-stationary process eventually returns to its deterministic trend; the unit-root process retains a permanent level shift.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### ADF 检验未拒绝单位根，能否说已经证明有单位根？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does failure to reject a unit root in an ADF test prove that a unit root exists?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。可能是检验功效不足、样本短、近单位根或设定错误；只能说证据不足以拒绝。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. Low power, a short sample, a near-unit root, or misspecification may explain the result; it says only that evidence is insufficient to reject.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么不能看到趋势就一律一阶差分？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why should every visibly trending series not be first-differenced automatically?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 趋势可能是确定性的，差分会改变问题并引入噪声；若关心长期水平关系，还可能丢掉协整信息。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The trend may be deterministic, in which case differencing changes the target and introduces noise. It can also discard cointegrating information when long-run level relations matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明为什么对白噪声差分是过度差分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why differencing white noise is over-differencing.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 白噪声本已平稳且没有可预测水平结构；差分不会消除需要处理的趋势，反而创造一阶负自相关和更大方差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> White noise is already stationary and has no predictable level structure. Differencing removes no needed trend; it creates lag-one negative autocorrelation and larger variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
