# The segment criterion for quasiconvexity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凸性线段判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[拟凸函数|quasiconvexity]] test asks whether moving between two inputs can produce a value **above both endpoint values**. It rules out such an upward overshoot on every segment. If the endpoints have values 2 and 5, the entire segment must stay at or below 5; values below 2 are allowed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[拟凸函数|拟凸性]]检验两个输入之间是否会出现**高于两端值**的函数值。它要求每条线段都不发生这种向上越界。若两端值为 2 和 5，整段都必须不超过 5，但允许低于 2。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The maximum-endpoint inequality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*较大端点值作为上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ on a [[凸集|convex set]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, with integer $n\ge1$. For $x,y\in U$ and $0\le t\le1$, the mixed input $z=tx+(1-t)y$ stays in the domain. The symbol $\max\{f(x),f(y)\}$ means the larger real endpoint value. Quasiconvexity is equivalent to the following inequality for every such pair and weight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f:U\to\mathbb R$ 的定义域 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 是[[凸集]]，$n\ge1$ 为整数。对 $x,y\in U$、$0\le t\le1$，混合输入 $z=tx+(1-t)y$ 留在定义域中。$\max\{f(x),f(y)\}$ 表示两个实数端点值中较大的一个。拟凸性等价于所有这样的点对与权重都满足下面的不等式。

在凸域上，
$$
f\text{ 拟凸}\iff f(tx+(1-t)y)\le\max\{f(x),f(y)\}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an all-pairs condition, not a test of just one midpoint. It requires no derivatives or continuity. Endpoint weights, equal inputs, empty domains and one-point domains cause no exceptions under the usual convex-set convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是对所有点对的要求，不是只检查一个中点。它不要求导数或连续性。按通常的凸集约定，端点权重、相同输入、空域与单点域都不构成例外。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From lower contour sets to segments and back
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*下轮廓集与线段之间的两个方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume first that $f$ is quasiconvex. Its [[下轮廓集|lower contour set]] $C_k^-=\{u\in U:f(u)\le k\}$ contains the inputs that do not exceed threshold $k$. Given $x,y$, choose $k=\max\{f(x),f(y)\}$. Both endpoints lie in $C_k^-$. This set is convex, so their mixture also lies in it: $f(tx+(1-t)y)\le k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先假设 $f$ 拟凸。[[下轮廓集]] $C_k^-=\{u\in U:f(u)\le k\}$ 包含所有不超过门槛 $k$ 的输入。给定 $x,y$，取 $k=\max\{f(x),f(y)\}$，两端都在 $C_k^-$ 中。该集合凸，所以混合点也在其中，即 $f(tx+(1-t)y)\le k$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the converse, assume the segment inequality. Fix any real $k$ and any $x,y\in C_k^-$. Both endpoint values are at most $k$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反方向，假设线段不等式成立。固定任意实数 $k$ 以及任意 $x,y\in C_k^-$。两端值都不超过 $k$，于是

$$f(tx+(1-t)y)\le\max\{f(x),f(y)\}\le k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every mixture stays in $C_k^-$, so this set is convex. An empty lower set is convex as well. This works for every threshold, giving quasiconvexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个混合点都留在 $C_k^-$ 中，所以该集合凸；空的下轮廓集也凸。由于任意门槛都成立，得到拟凸性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the [[拟凹性线段判据|quasiconcave segment criterion]] to the negative function and reverse the inequality. This bounds each segment by the larger endpoint value. A monotone cubic satisfies it without being convex on the whole real line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意两点与 $t\in[0,1]$ 成立。证明与[[拟凹性线段判据]]对偶：把函数换成 $-f$，再用 $\min\{-a,-b\}=-\max\{a,b\}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More explicitly, $f$ is quasiconvex exactly when $-f$ is quasiconcave, because their lower and upper sets agree after changing threshold sign. Multiplying $-f(z)\ge\min\{-f(x),-f(y)\}$ by $-1$ reverses the inequality and produces the maximum above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地，$f$ 拟凸恰好等价于 $-f$ 拟凹，因为把门槛取负后，前者的下轮廓集就是后者的上轮廓集。将 $-f(z)\ge\min\{-f(x),-f(y)\}$ 两边乘以 $-1$，不等号反向，右边便成为上面的最大值。

## Examples that separate the conditions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区分几个容易混淆的条件*

> [!example] An upward overshoot · 向上越界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=-x^2$ on $\mathbb R$, the endpoints $-1,1$ both give $-1$, while their midpoint gives 0. Since $0>\max\{-1,-1\}$, the function fails the criterion. It is concave, but not quasiconvex on this domain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实线上的 $f(x)=-x^2$，端点 $-1,1$ 的值都为 $-1$，中点值为 0。由于 $0>\max\{-1,-1\}$，它不符合判据。它是凹函数，却在这个定义域上不拟凸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^3$, [[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|monotonicity]] proves the criterion for every pair, not just one example. But [[凹凸性|convexity]] would compare with a weighted average, which is at most the larger endpoint value. At inputs $-2,0$, the cubic gives $f(-1)=-1$, whereas the midpoint chord value is $(-8+0)/2=-4$. The convex inequality $-1\le-4$ fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=x^3$，[[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|单调性]]证明任意点对都符合本判据，不只是某个例子。但[[凹凸性|凸性]]比较的是不超过较大端点值的加权平均。取输入 $-2,0$，三次函数在中点的值为 $f(-1)=-1$，弦的中点高度为 $(-8+0)/2=-4$，凸性要求的 $-1\le-4$ 并不成立。

$f(x)=x^3$ 在两输入之间的值介于端点值之间，所以符合这个判据，却不是整个实线上的凸函数。
