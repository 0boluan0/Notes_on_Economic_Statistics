# Revising a regression model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*回归模型的设定修复循环*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revising a regression model means using a specific failure to propose a change, then checking whether the changed model serves the original purpose better. A “specification” includes the variables and their mathematical form, the observations used, and assumptions about how outcomes vary. Changing any of these can change what the result means.

Begin with a question such as “Does demand bend upward as temperature rises?” A curve in a [[残差图边界|residual plot]] can motivate that question, but the plot does not prescribe a unique repair. The useful cycle is **state the problem → make a justified change → compare on the intended target → check again**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归设定修复，是从一个具体问题出发提出修改，再检查修改后的模型是否更好地服务于原来的用途。“设定”包括选哪些变量、用什么数学形式、纳入哪些观测，以及对结果变化方式作哪些假设。改变其中任何一项，都可能改变结果的含义。

例如，先问“温度升高时，需求是否向上弯曲？”[[残差图边界|残差图]]中的曲线可以促成这个问题，却不会自动指定唯一的修复。有效的循环是：**说清问题 → 有依据地修改 → 针对目标比较 → 重新检查**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A change with a visible purpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一次能说清目的的修改*

> [!example] Add one term, then compare / 加一项，再比较
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider five illustrative observations, with a numerical predictor $x=1,2,3,4,5$ and outcomes $y=13,18,25,34,45$. An intercept-and-slope fit gives $\hat y=3+8x$, leaving residuals $2,-1,-2,-1,2$. The systematic bend suggests trying a quadratic term while retaining the same outcome, sample, and existing terms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 五条示例观测的解释变量为 $x=1,2,3,4,5$，结果为 $y=13,18,25,34,45$。拟合截距和斜率后得到 $\hat y=3+8x$，残差为 $2,-1,-2,-1,2$。这个弯曲形状提示可以尝试加一个平方项，同时保留原结果变量、样本和已有项。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The expanded fit is $\hat y=10+2x+x^2$, with zero training residuals. That tells us what the added term achieved **on these five observations**. It does not establish perfect future prediction: adding a term expands the set of available fits, so the minimum training sum of squared residuals cannot increase.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 扩展后拟合为 $\hat y=10+2x+x^2$，训练残差全为零。这说明新增项**在这五条观测上**做了什么，却没有证明未来能完美预测：增加项扩大了可选拟合的范围，所以最小训练残差平方和本来就不会增大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two observations kept out of fitting have $(x,y)=(6,60),(7,70)$. The straight line predicts $51,59$; the quadratic predicts $58,73$. Their average squared prediction errors are respectively $(9^2+11^2)/2=101$ and $(2^2+(-3)^2)/2=6.5$. This constructed comparison favors the quadratic on those two observations, not in every future population. It also extrapolates beyond the training range, so it cannot certify the curve at much larger $x$.

If these results choose the model, the two observations are [[训练验证测试划分|validation data, not an independent final test]]. Record the decision, then evaluate the completed selection using data that did not guide it. For a causal question, better predictive fit alone still does not establish the required [[设定问题分层|identification assumptions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设另有两条未参与拟合的观测 $(x,y)=(6,60),(7,70)$。直线预测 $51,59$，二次曲线预测 $58,73$，平均平方预测误差分别为 $(9^2+11^2)/2=101$ 和 $(2^2+(-3)^2)/2=6.5$。这个构造的比较说明二次曲线在这两条观测上更好，不代表在任何未来总体都更好；这还是训练范围之外的外推，无法据此认证很大 $x$ 处的曲线。

如果依据这两个结果选模型，它们就属于[[训练验证测试划分|验证数据，而非独立的最终测试]]。记录选择，再用未指导这次选择的数据评价完成后的流程。若研究的是因果问题，更好的预测拟合仍不自动建立所需的[[设定问题分层|识别假设]]。

## 最小可追踪顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A minimal traceable sequence*

**Start with the data and the target.** Check coding, units, missingness, sample membership, and time order. A centimetre/metre error needs a data correction. A genuine extreme observation needs a reasoned [[异常观测处理原则|treatment of unusual observations]], not deletion merely to improve a plot. State the population, outcome scale, and intended comparison. For prediction, specify which inputs will actually be available.

**Turn the symptom into a candidate.** Examine the raw relationship and relevant residual plots. A [[偏残差图|partial-residual plot]] adds the fitted contribution of one predictor back to the residual to inspect its shape. An [[附加变量图|added-variable plot]] first removes the other predictors' linear contributions from both the outcome and the chosen predictor to inspect the remaining linear relationship. Name the change and its purpose: adding $x^2$ addresses curvature; changing a covariance estimate addresses uncertainty under specified conditions. These are different interventions on the analysis.

**Compare and revisit.** Explicit restrictions, [[LM设定检验|LM tests]], and [[RESET检验边界|RESET]] address selected statistical departures under their reference-distribution conditions. Predictive loss addresses a predictive goal. Recheck whether candidate terms have enough independent variation, whether particular observations drive the result, and whether mean shape, error scale, and time dependence remain adequately handled. A change that helps one aspect need not help all of them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**先核对数据与目标。** 检查编码、单位、缺失、样本归属和时间顺序。厘米与米混用需要纠正数据；真实的极端观测需要有依据的[[异常观测处理原则|异常观测处理]]，不能只为让图好看而删除。写清总体、结果尺度和想比较什么；用于预测时，还要说清当时实际能获得哪些输入。

**把症状转成候选。** 看原始关系和有关的残差图。[[偏残差图]]把某个变量的已拟合贡献加回残差，用来查看形状；[[附加变量图]]先从结果和所关注变量中分别扣除其他变量的线性解释部分，再查看剩余的线性关系。说明每项修改的目的：加入 $x^2$ 是处理曲率；在相应条件下更换协方差估计，是调整不确定性的计算。两者改变的是分析的不同部分。

**比较以后再次检查。** 明确的限制、[[LM设定检验]]和[[RESET检验边界|RESET]]，是在各自参考分布条件下检查选定偏离；预测损失服务于预测目标。随后重新检查候选项是否有足够独立变异、结果是否由个别观测驱动，以及均值形状、误差尺度和时间依赖是否处理妥当。一次修改改善了某一方面，不代表其他方面自动过关。

## Keep the comparison interpretable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让每次比较仍然可解释*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Save the starting specification and the reason for each revision. If the outcome transformation, observation exclusions, and controls all change at once, the before/after comparison cannot separate their contributions. Sequential versions or carefully chosen comparisons that remove one change at a time can clarify them; interactions between changes may still matter. Data errors should be corrected when discovered, with their consequences recorded.

For predictive comparisons, fit data-learned preprocessing only within the relevant training portion: [[验证管线隔离|pipeline isolation]] includes imputation and scaling. [[验证结构匹配|Match validation to the intended deployment]]—for example, predict later periods using earlier information, or hold out people when the target is new people. If the response scale changes, compare predictions on the scale and loss relevant to the original decision; transformed-scale errors are not automatically comparable to original-scale errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保存起点设定和每次修改的理由。如果一次同时变换结果、删除观测、更换控制变量，前后比较就无法分离各项修改的贡献。保留逐步版本，或有针对性地逐项撤去某项改动进行比较，可以帮助分辨；不同改动之间仍可能相互作用。发现数据错误时应纠正，并记录它对结果的影响。

比较预测能力时，从数据学习的预处理也只能在相应训练部分拟合；[[验证管线隔离]]包括插补与标准化。[[验证结构匹配|验证方式应贴合实际用途]]：预测未来要使用当时已经可得的信息，面向新个体则按个体留出。若变换了结果尺度，要在原决策关心的尺度和损失上比较；变换后的误差不能自动与原尺度误差比较。

> [!tip] A useful stopping point / 有依据的收尾
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Finish when the model is adequate for the stated purpose given the available evidence, and report material limitations and sensitivity to plausible specifications. “Every diagnostic failed to reject” is not a certificate of truth. Repeatedly revising from final-test feedback also turns that test into development data; see [[测试集自适应复用|adaptive test-set reuse]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当现有证据支持模型足以服务于明确用途时，可以收尾，同时报告重要限制和对合理设定的敏感性。“所有诊断都没拒绝”不是模型为真的证书。反复按照最终测试反馈修改，也会把测试变成开发数据，见[[测试集自适应复用]]。
