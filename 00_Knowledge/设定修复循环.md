# 回归设定修复应在数据、目标、候选机制与诊断之间迭代并保留单步可追踪性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression specification repair should iterate across data, targets, candidate mechanisms, and diagnostics while keeping each change traceable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子程序
> 模型设定不是一次性选出公式，而是“核对数据 → 固定目标与时间顺序 → 提出少量有机制的候选形式 → 估计 → 诊断和验证 → 回到候选”的循环。每次改变都应说明它修复哪个失败、改变了什么解释和尺度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Specification is not a one-shot formula choice. It cycles through checking data, fixing the target and temporal order, proposing a small set of mechanism-based forms, estimating, diagnosing and validating, and returning to the candidates. Each change should state the failure it targets and how interpretation or scale changes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小可追踪顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A minimal traceable sequence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 核对 coding、单位、缺失、样本构造和时间索引。
2. 写清总体、目标量、焦点比较、用途和时间顺序。
3. 画原始关系与有方向的残差图；需要区分控制后关系时，再看 partial-residual 或 added-variable 图，并提出少量可解释备择。
4. 用明确限制、LM/RESET、样本外损失或其他与目标匹配的证据比较。
5. 重新检查共线性、影响点、误差协方差和识别层。
6. 在实际决策尺度上报告效应或预测，并保留规格敏感性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Verify coding, units, missingness, sample construction, and the time index.
2. State the population, target, focal comparison, intended use, and temporal order.
3. Inspect raw relationships and directed residual plots; when the conditional relationship after controls matters, also use partial-residual or added-variable plots, then propose a small set of interpretable alternatives.
4. Compare them using explicit restrictions, LM/RESET, out-of-sample loss, or other goal-matched evidence.
5. Recheck collinearity, influence, error covariance, and identification.
6. Report effects or predictions on the decision scale and retain specification sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一次同时变换结果变量、删除观测并更换控制变量，就无法知道哪一步改变了结论。确需多项修改时，应保存逐步规格或消融比较。变换后还要在原尺度验证；修复均值曲率后还要重新检查方差、依赖与外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the outcome is transformed, observations deleted, and controls changed simultaneously, the source of any changed conclusion is unknowable. When several changes are necessary, retain sequential specifications or an ablation comparison. Validate transformations on the original scale, and recheck variance, dependence, and extrapolation after repairing mean curvature.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么“残差不好看，所以同时取 log、删点、换控制变量”不是可审计修复？
>
> **答案：** 因为多项变化混在一起，无法判断哪一步修复了什么，也无法区分目标尺度、样本和识别结构的改变。
