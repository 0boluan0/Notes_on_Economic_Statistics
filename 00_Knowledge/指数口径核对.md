# 使用指数前应核对完整名称样本权重收益币种和规则日期以确认比较对象一致
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Before using an index, check its full identity, constituents, weights, return convention, currency and rule date.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“大盘涨了多少”不是一个足够明确的比较。先找到指数提供商的完整名称、代码和方法文件，再确认样本范围、权重规则、价格或全收益口径、计价币种，以及用于该历史期间的规则版本。遇到“纳指”“道指”这类简称，先澄清具体系列，不能用家族名称代替某一条序列。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“How much did the market rise?” is underspecified. Identify the provider's full name, code and methodology, then check the universe, weights, price or total return, currency and rules applicable to the historical period. A family nickname does not identify a particular series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，同一组股票的[[市值加权指数]]与[[等权指数]]可能不同步，[[价格指数与全收益指数|价格和全收益]]也可能不同步；这些差异不是计算器故障。某日“沪深各有多少成分”“覆盖市值百分之几”是带日期的样本描述，不是指数永久定义。基值 100 或 1,000 只是尺度，同一指数的收益看相邻值之比，不看点数是否更大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same stocks can produce different [[市值加权指数|capitalization-weighted]] and [[等权指数|equal-weighted]] returns, or different [[价格指数与全收益指数|price and total returns]]. Constituent counts by venue and market coverage are dated descriptions, not permanent definitions. Base values of 100 or 1,000 set the scale; returns depend on ratios, not the number of index points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有核对完成后，才把基金或策略收益与这条指数比较；两边的日期、币种、收入与费用口径应明确对齐。[[指数基金]]的持仓和费用另有产品文件，不能由指数定义直接推知。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only then compare a fund or strategy with the benchmark, aligning dates, currencies, distributions and fee conventions. An [[指数基金|index fund]] also has its own holdings and product expenses, which the index definition cannot supply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
