# 当前不行权只比较本次决策而提前权利金衡量全部未来行权选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Current non-exercise is a local decision, whereas the early-exercise premium values all future exercise choices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[美式行权决策]] 中，若当前继续价值严格高于立即支付，当前应继续持有；这不表示以后每个状态都不应提前行权。等号时也可选择继续，但模型价值上与立即行权无差别。**提前行权权利金**则是同一市场模型、标的、执行价、到期日及支付约定下，美式价值 $A_t$ 相对欧式价值 $E_t$ 的差额。美式可选择只在到期行权，所以
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[美式行权决策|American exercise decision]], strictly higher continuation value than immediate payoff means one should continue now. It does not say that early exercise will be suboptimal in every future state. Continuing is also possible at equality, but is then equivalent in model value to immediate exercise. The early-exercise premium compares American value $A_t$ with European value $E_t$ under the same market model, underlying, strike, expiry and payoff convention. Because the American holder may exercise only at expiry if desired:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\Pi_t^{\mathrm{early}}=A_t-E_t\ge0.$$

因此可能同时有 $H_t<C_t=A_t$ 和 $A_t>E_t$：今天不行权，但未来某些状态下提前取得支付仍有价值。权利金为零需要更强的论据，例如 [[无股息看涨不提前行权]] 的全时点比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is possible that $H_t<C_t=A_t$ while $A_t>E_t$: exercise is unattractive today, but receiving the payoff early in some future states is valuable. A zero premium needs a stronger argument, such as the comparison at every exercise time in [[无股息看涨不提前行权|the non-dividend American-call result]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 可复算的两期看跌例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A reproducible two-period put example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取无股息股票 $S_0=100$、执行价 $K=100$，每期股价变动因子为 $u=1.2,d=0.8$，现金每期增长因子 $R=1.1$。模型在日期 $0,1,2$ 交易并允许行权，日期 $2$ 到期；这是有限日期的行权模型，对连续可行权合约只是离散化示例。无摩擦且可交易股票与现金，因 $d<R<u$，无套利风险中性概率由股票本身确定：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a non-dividend stock with $S_0=100$, strike $K=100$, up/down factors $u=1.2,d=0.8$, and per-period cash growth $R=1.1$. Trading and exercise occur on dates 0, 1 and 2, with expiry at 2. This is a finite-date exercise model, only a discretized illustration of a continuously exercisable contract. With frictionless stock and cash trading and $d<R<u$, the stock itself determines the no-arbitrage risk-neutral probability:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$p_Q=\frac{1.1-0.8}{1.2-0.8}=\frac34,\qquad
p_Q u+(1-p_Q)d=R.$$

股价和到期看跌支付如下；中间终点 $96$ 可由先涨后跌或先跌后涨到达。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stock prices and terminal put payoffs are shown below. The middle terminal price 96 is reached by either up–down or down–up.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
t&0&1&2\\\hline
S&100&120,\ 80&144,\ 96,\ 64\\
H_2&&&0,\ 4,\ 36
\end{array}
$$

日期 $1$ 的上、下节点分别比较：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the up and down nodes on date 1, compare:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
H_u&=0,& C_u&=\frac{\tfrac34\cdot0+\tfrac14\cdot4}{1.1}
=\frac{10}{11},& A_u&=\frac{10}{11};\\
H_d&=20,& C_d&=\frac{\tfrac34\cdot4+\tfrac14\cdot36}{1.1}
=\frac{120}{11},& A_d&=20.
\end{aligned}
$$

上节点等待，下节点提前行权。于是日期 $0$ 的立即支付为 $H_0=0$，美式模型的继续价值为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continue at the up node and exercise early at the down node. At date 0, immediate payoff is $H_0=0$, while continuation value in the American-style model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_0=\frac{\tfrac34\cdot\tfrac{10}{11}+\tfrac14\cdot20}{1.1}
=\frac{625}{121}\approx5.165289,
\qquad A_0=\max(0,C_0)=C_0.
$$

相同参数的欧式看跌只能在日期 $2$ 收取支付，两期终点概率为 $9/16,6/16,1/16$，故
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The corresponding European put pays only on date 2. Its terminal probabilities are $9/16,6/16,1/16$, so:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E_0=\frac{\tfrac9{16}\cdot0+\tfrac6{16}\cdot4+\tfrac1{16}\cdot36}{1.1^2}
=\frac{375}{121}\approx3.099174,
\qquad
\Pi_0^{\mathrm{early}}=\frac{250}{121}\approx2.066116>0.
$$

这给出所需区别：$0<C_0$，所以**当前不行权**；$A_0-E_0>0$，所以**提前行权权利仍有正价值**。正差额来自未来下节点的行权机会，而不是任意指定的上涨概率或真实世界预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This establishes the distinction: $0<C_0$, so do not exercise now, but $A_0-E_0>0$, so the early-exercise right has positive value. The premium comes from exercise at the future down node, not from an arbitrarily chosen up probability or a real-world forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Haugh–Kogan，[*Pricing American Options: A Duality Approach*，PDF 第 5–7 页，文内第 4–6 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/HaughKogan2001_rev2.pdf#page=5)：已重开完整市场、离散日期最优停止及继续价值定义。允许到期这一策略给出 $A\ge E$；本页 $100/120/80$ 两期树为独立反例，已用分数逐节点复算概率、现金贴现、行权选择和价差，不是论文原例或课程软件运行结果。
- Kohn–Allen，[*Derivative Securities*, Section 1，PDF 第 2 页](https://math.nyu.edu/~kohn/derivative.securities/2007/section1.pdf#page=2)：支持美式与欧式合约的行权日期区别。此页树例只在声明的离散日期范围内定价，不据此声称连续时间美式价格等于 $625/121$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Haugh–Kogan, PDF pp. 5–7 (printed pp. 4–6), was reopened for complete markets, discrete-date optimal stopping and continuation value. The admissible expiry-only strategy gives $A\ge E$. The independent 100/120/80 two-period counterexample was checked with exact fractions at every node, including probabilities, discounting, exercise choices and the premium; it is not a published numerical example or a course-software result.
- Kohn–Allen, Section 1, PDF p. 2, supports the exercise-date distinction between American and European contracts. This tree prices only the stated discrete exercise model, not the continuously exercisable contract at $625/121$.
