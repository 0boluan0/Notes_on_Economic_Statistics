# Nullity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **nullity** of a matrix counts how many independent input directions it sends to zero. It is a number, whereas the [[零空间|nullspace]] is the set of all such inputs. A nullity of two means that two independent coefficients are needed to describe every input that disappears; it does not mean that only two inputs disappear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵的**零度**数的是：有多少个彼此独立的输入方向会被它送到零。零度是一个数，[[零空间]]则是全部这类输入构成的集合。零度为二，表示描述所有被消去的输入需要两个独立系数，不是说只有两个输入被消去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an $m\times n$ matrix $A$ over a field $\mathbb F$, the precise definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $m\times n$ 矩阵 $A$，准确定义为

$$\operatorname{nullity}(A)=\dim N(A),\qquad
N(A)=\{x\in\mathbb F^n:Ax=0\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\dim$ means [[维数|dimension]]: the number of vectors in a [[基|basis]], an independent list that spans the space. Different bases can use different vectors, but always have the same length. Therefore nullity is well-defined and lies between $0$ and $n$, the number of input coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\dim$ 表示[[维数]]，即一组[[基]]中的向量个数；基是一组能张成整个空间的无关向量。不同基可以选不同向量，长度却相同，因此零度有确定值，且介于 $0$ 与输入坐标数 $n$ 之间。

> [!example] Two freely chosen coefficients · 两个可以自由选择的系数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Work over the real numbers and let
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实数域中取
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\2&4&6\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The second equation in $Ax=0$ is twice the first. Both reduce to $x_1+2x_2+3x_3=0$. Choose $x_2=s$ and $x_3=t$ freely; then $x_1=-2s-3t$. Thus
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $Ax=0$ 的第二个方程是第一个的两倍，约束只有 $x_1+2x_2+3x_3=0$。自由选择 $x_2=s$、$x_3=t$，便有 $x_1=-2s-3t$。所以
>
> $$x=s\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
> +t\begin{pmatrix}-3\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These two vectors span the nullspace because the formula describes every solution. They are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]: in a combination equal to zero, the second coordinate forces $s=0$, and the third forces $t=0$. They form a basis, so $\operatorname{nullity}(A)=2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个公式描述了每一个解，所以两向量张成零空间。它们还[[线性无关]]：若组合为零，第二坐标迫使 $s=0$，第三坐标迫使 $t=0$。因此它们构成一组基，$\operatorname{nullity}(A)=2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The independence check matters. Listing three nullspace vectors does not prove nullity three: the third might be the sum of the first two. Conversely, finding two independent nullspace vectors proves a lower bound of two; showing they span is what makes the count exact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无关性检查很重要。列出三个零空间向量，不能证明零度为三：第三个可能只是前两个之和。反过来，找到两个无关的零空间向量，只能证明零度至少为二；还要证明它们张成零空间，才能确定恰好为二。

## Reading the number from a solved system
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从解好的方程组读出这个数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After homogeneous equations are put in [[行最简形|reduced row echelon form]], each [[自由变量|free variable]] is a coordinate that can be chosen independently. The remaining variables, associated with [[主元|pivots]], are then determined by those choices. Set one free variable to one and the others to zero to obtain one special solution for each free variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把齐次方程组化为[[行最简形]]后，每个[[自由变量]]都可以独立选择，其余对应[[主元]]的变量则由这些选择确定。每次让一个自由变量为一、其余为零，就能为每个自由变量得到一个特殊解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every solution is the corresponding combination of these special solutions. Their free-coordinate entries form the standard unit columns, so a zero combination forces every coefficient to be zero. They are therefore a basis, and **nullity equals the number of free variables**. This is the reasoning behind the construction in [[RREF读取零空间基|reading a nullspace basis from RREF]]; it is not just a count of how many parameters somebody happened to write.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个解都是这些特殊解按自由变量取值形成的组合。特殊解在自由坐标位置形成标准单位列，所以组合若为零，各系数必须都为零。因此它们构成一组基，**零度等于自由变量个数**。这就是[[RREF读取零空间基]]背后的理由，不是随意数一份参数表达式里写了几个符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, with $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}$, solving gives $x=t(-1,-1,1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and makes a column. The one nonzero vector is a basis, so $\operatorname{nullity}(B)=1$. If the [[矩阵秩|rank]] is $r$, there are $r$ pivot variables among the $n$ inputs and hence $n-r$ free variables. The broader relationship is the [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}$ 的解为 $x=t(-1,-1,1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把一行变成一列。单个非零向量就是一组基，因此 $\operatorname{nullity}(B)=1$。若[[矩阵秩|秩]]为 $r$，则 $n$ 个输入中有 $r$ 个主元变量、$n-r$ 个自由变量。更一般的关系见[[秩零度定理]]。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nullity zero means $N(A)=\{0\}$: no nonzero input disappears, though the zero vector is still a solution. The zero space has an empty basis of length zero; the list containing the zero vector is not an independent basis. At the other extreme, a zero $m\times n$ matrix has nullity $n$, whatever the number of rows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零度为零，表示 $N(A)=\{0\}$：没有非零输入被消去，但零向量仍是解。零空间 $\{0\}$ 的基是长度为零的空列表；把零向量放在列表里，并不能得到无关的基。另一个极端是 $m\times n$ 零矩阵，它的零度为 $n$，与行数无关。

> [!warning] Count independent solutions, not zero entries · 数独立解的方向，不是数零元素
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The opening matrix has no zero entries, no zero rows and no zero columns, yet its nullity is two. The matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ has a zero row, yet its nullity is zero because $Ax=(x_1,x_2,0)$ vanishes only at the zero input.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 开头的矩阵没有零元素、零行或零列，零度却为二。矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ 有一行全零，零度却为零，因为 $Ax=(x_1,x_2,0)$ 只有在零输入时才为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, nullity zero alone does not make a rectangular matrix invertible as a map onto its entire output space. For a real matrix, the [[左零空间|left nullspace]] is $N(A^T)$ and generally has a different dimension from $N(A)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，零度为零本身不会让矩形矩阵成为到整个输出空间的可逆映射。对实矩阵，[[左零空间]]是 $N(A^T)$，其维数一般不同于 $N(A)$。
