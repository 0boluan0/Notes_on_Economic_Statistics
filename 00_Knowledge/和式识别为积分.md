# 把和式写成采样高度乘区间宽度才能识别其积分极限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Recognize an integral limit by exposing sampled heights and subinterval widths in the sum*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遇到随 $n$ 增大的求和式，先找 $\Delta x$，再找采样点，最后确认区间和可积性。对于连续 $f$，令 $\Delta x=(b-a)/n$、$x_i=a+i\Delta x$，就有
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^n f(x_i)\Delta x=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.$$
不能看到 $i/n$ 就忽略外面的系数；那一项决定累积的宽度或额外缩放。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the width first, then the sample locations, interval, and integrability condition. Continuous functions allow uniform right-endpoint sums to converge to the integral. A coefficient outside the sum may be the width or an additional scale factor and must be accounted for.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如
$$\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\left(2+\frac{3i}{n}\right)^2
=\frac13\sum_{i=1}^n\left(2+\frac{3i}{n}\right)^2\frac3n
\longrightarrow\frac13\int_2^5x^2\,dx=13.$$
也可以直接看作 $\int_0^1(2+3t)^2dt$；两种识别由[[定积分换元]]相连。反过来，$b>0$ 时用 $\Delta x=b/n$，从
$$\sum_{i=1}^n\left(\frac{ib}{n}\right)^2\frac bn
=\frac{b^3}{n^3}\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6\to\frac{b^3}3$$
就能从定义算出 $\int_0^bx^2dx$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the first example, the grid spans two to five with width $3/n$, leaving a factor of one third. The equivalent unit-interval formulation is connected by [[定积分换元|substitution]]. Conversely, the sum-of-squares formula evaluates the right-endpoint sums of $x^2$ directly and obtains its integral without first using an antiderivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
