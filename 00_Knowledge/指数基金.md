# 指数基金是以追踪指定指数表现为投资目标的基金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An index fund aims to track the performance of a specified index*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数基金以一个事先明确的指数作为追踪目标，按照相应规则组织[[投资组合]]，力求使基金表现接近该指数。指数是计算出来的基准，基金则是真实持有资产、承担费用并执行交易的投资载体；追踪目标不是回报必然相等的承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An index fund uses a specified index as its tracking target and constructs a [[投资组合|portfolio]] intended to approximate that index's performance. The index is a calculated benchmark; the fund is an investment vehicle that holds assets, incurs expenses, and executes trades. A tracking objective is not a promise of identical returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实现方式可以是完整复制、代表性抽样或在允许范围内使用衍生工具，未必逐项持有全部成分。指数基金既可采用普通开放式共同基金形式，也可采用[[ETF]]形式；反过来，ETF 也可主动管理。因此“指数基金”描述投资目标，不能代替“在哪交易、如何申赎”的结构分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Implementation may use full replication, representative sampling, or permitted derivatives; holding every component is not essential. An index fund can be an ordinary open-end mutual fund or an [[ETF]], while an ETF can also be actively managed. Index tracking describes an investment objective, not the trading and redemption structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如一个单期、不再平衡的两资产基准以 $60\%$ 配置 A、$40\%$ 配置 B；A 回报 $10\%$、B 回报 $-5\%$ 时，基准回报为 $0.6(10\%)+0.4(-5\%)=4\%$。真实基金的现金保留、抽样差异、费用及交易时点可使结果偏离；即使完全复制，也不能忽略费用后声称投资者必得 $4\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a one-period benchmark with no rebalancing, weights of 60% in A and 40% in B give a return of $0.6(10\%)+0.4(-5\%)=4\%$ when the assets return 10% and −5%. A real fund can differ because of cash balances, sampling, expenses, or trading timing. Even full replication does not justify promising investors 4% after ignoring fees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

被动跟踪不等于低风险：行业集中或少数成分占比很大的指数仍可高度集中，且市场共同风险不会因“指数”名称消失。风险判断须看指数规则和实际持仓，复用[[分散化与共同风险]]，不能把基金名称当作保本条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Passive tracking does not imply low risk. A sector index or an index dominated by a few constituents can remain concentrated, and common market risk does not disappear with the index label. Risk depends on index rules and actual holdings; [[分散化与共同风险|diversification and common risk]] apply rather than any principal guarantee implied by the name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
