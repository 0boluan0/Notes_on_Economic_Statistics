# 私人噪声本身不保证全局博弈唯一；唯一性与风险占优选择还取决于优势区域、单调结构和具体噪声条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Private noise alone does not guarantee a unique global-game equilibrium; uniqueness and risk-dominant selection also depend on dominance regions, monotone structure, and the specific noise conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全局博弈的著名用途，是用很小的私人信息噪声精炼完全信息协调博弈的多重均衡。但“玩家收到略有差异的信号”只描述了信息结构，不等于已经证明均衡唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Global games are well known for using small private-information noise to refine multiple equilibria of a complete-information coordination game. But saying that players receive slightly different signals specifies only the information structure; it does not prove uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 唯一性证明还需要什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What a Uniqueness Argument Still Needs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

典型论证还会用到：极端基本面上某个行动具有严格优势的 dominance regions；行动间战略互补与收益差的单调性；使最佳反应能按[[贝叶斯阈值策略|单一阈值]]排序的 single-crossing 结构；以及噪声相对于模型尺度足够小。缺少这些条件时，可能不存在阈值均衡，也可能存在多个阈值或非阈值均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A typical proof also uses dominance regions in which one action is strictly optimal at extreme fundamentals, strategic complementarity and monotonic payoff differences, a single-crossing structure that orders best responses by one [[贝叶斯阈值策略|cutoff]], and noise that is sufficiently small relative to the model. Without such conditions, a cutoff equilibrium may fail to exist or several cutoff or non-cutoff equilibria may remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 风险占优选择是特定结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk-Dominant Selection Is a Qualified Result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Carlsson–van Damme 的二维协调框架及其相应极限条件下，噪声趋小时可选择风险占优均衡。这不是所有带噪贝叶斯博弈的通用结论。应用时应写明基础完全信息博弈、信号分布、优势区域和取极限的方式，而不能只引用“global games 会选唯一均衡”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the Carlsson–van Damme two-player coordination framework and under its limiting conditions, vanishing noise selects the risk-dominant equilibrium. This is not a general result for every noisy Bayesian game. An application must state the underlying complete-information game, signal distribution, dominance regions, and limiting argument rather than merely assert that “global games select a unique equilibrium.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个直接反例方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Direct Route to Counterexamples*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地，MIT 讲义 §6.2.2 的正态 partnership 例子假设

$$
\theta=y+\tau\eta,
\qquad
x_i=\theta+\sigma\varepsilon_i,
\qquad
(\eta,\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2)\overset{iid}{\sim}N(0,1).
$$

令 $\alpha=\tau^2/(\sigma^2+\tau^2)$，并把 rank belief 对基本面期望的敏感度记为

$$
\lambda
=\frac{\sigma}{\tau^2\sqrt{\alpha+1}}.
$$

该例的 cutoff 方程为 $\Phi(\lambda(m-y))=m$，其中 $m=E[\theta\mid \hat x]$。讲义证明：$\lambda\le\sqrt{2\pi}$ 时，对所有先验均值 $y$ 都有唯一可理性化策略；$\lambda>\sqrt{2\pi}$ 时，$y$ 在 $1/2$ 附近会出现多重均衡。所以关键对象是由 $\sigma$ 与 $\tau$ 共同决定的敏感度 $\lambda$，不是一句没有模型尺度的“噪声大或小”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the normal partnership example in §6.2.2 of the MIT notes, $\theta=y+\tau\eta$ and $x_i=\theta+\sigma\varepsilon_i$, with $(\eta,\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2)$ i.i.d. standard normal. Writing $\alpha=\tau^2/(\sigma^2+\tau^2)$ and $\lambda=\sigma/(\tau^2\sqrt{\alpha+1})$, the cutoff equation is $\Phi(\lambda(m-y))=m$, where $m=E[\theta\mid\hat x]$. There is a unique rationalizable strategy for every prior mean $y$ when $\lambda\le\sqrt{2\pi}$, while multiple equilibria arise near $y=1/2$ when $\lambda>\sqrt{2\pi}$. The relevant object is therefore the sensitivity parameter jointly determined by $\sigma$ and $\tau$, not an unscaled statement that noise is simply “large” or “small.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
