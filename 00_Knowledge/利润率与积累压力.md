# 李嘉图的积累压力来自必需品生产困难对利润率和投资动力的挤压

Ricardo 所担心的是一条有条件的增长反馈：资本积累增加劳动需求和人口，更多人口需要更多生活资料；若新增食品只能靠更差的生产条件取得，维持劳动家庭的成本就会上升，从而压低一般利润率，削弱继续积累的动力。这里的约束落在**新增生活资料的生产条件**，并非资本一增加便必然使增长停止。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo's concern is a conditional growth feedback. Accumulation expands labour demand and population; more people need more necessities. If additional food requires worse production conditions, maintaining labouring households costs more, reducing the general profit rate and the incentive to accumulate. The constraint concerns **conditions for producing additional necessities**, not an automatic halt to growth whenever capital increases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条链需要逐步成立。短期粮食需求增加，可以先让粮价和农业利润暂时升高；若充足优地仍可投入，资本进入和供给增加能使价格回落。只有新增产量确实需要更多资源时，正常粮价才维持在更高水平，并经 [[李嘉图自然工资]] 与 [[工资利润的分配约束]] 进入分配过程。[[级差地租]] 上升是这种生产条件变化的另一结果，不是地主先提高租金便机械地压低全社会利润率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each link requires its own conditions. A short-run increase in food demand can temporarily raise grain prices and agricultural profits. If sufficient good land remains available, entry and expanding supply can reverse the price increase. A persistently higher normal price requires more costly additional production, transmitted through [[李嘉图自然工资|the natural wage]] and [[工资利润的分配约束|the distributive constraint]]. Higher [[级差地租|differential rent]] is another consequence, not an arbitrary rent increase mechanically lowering economy-wide returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润率又不同于利润总额。资本从 100 增至 200，而利润率从 20% 降至 19%，总利润仍从 20 增至 38。若资本后来从 1,000 增至 1,100，利润率从 7% 降至 6%，总利润才从 70 降至 66。这些是 Ricardo 原例缩小货币单位后的计算，说明增长中的收益“率”和收益“额”要分别追踪。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The profit rate also differs from total profit. Raising capital from 100 to 200 while the rate falls from 20% to 19% increases total profit from 20 to 38. Raising capital from 1,000 to 1,100 while the rate falls from 7% to 6% instead reduces the total from 70 to 66. These are rescaled versions of Ricardo's examples and show why rates and totals must be tracked separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

他也没有要求等到利润恰为零才停止积累。若收益已不足以补偿经营的麻烦和风险，继续把收入投入生产的动力就可能先消失。积累会如何变化，还取决于储蓄和投资行为，不能把某期利润下降直接改写成资本立刻减少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo does not require profit to reach exactly zero before accumulation stops. Returns can become insufficient to compensate for the trouble and risk of productive investment first. The path of accumulation also depends on saving and investment behaviour; a lower current profit is not equivalent to an immediate reduction in capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

农业改良、生产必需品的机械进步和便宜食品进口能够缓解这条压力，见 [[必需品降价与利润]]。原书把技术进步看作会反复打断下降倾向的力量，因此应把结论读成维持特定生产条件时的趋势，而不是对所有经济的不可逆命运判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Agricultural improvements, machinery that cheapens necessities and cheaper food imports can relieve the pressure; see [[必需品降价与利润|cheaper necessities and profits]]. Ricardo allows improvements repeatedly to interrupt the downward tendency. It is a conditional trend, not an irreversible fate for every economy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》VI.27–37](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=7#6.27)：支持市场调整与长期生产难度的区别、利润率下降倾向、技术缓解、积累动力和利润总额的数例。VI.31 明确讨论零利润前的风险与经营补偿；VI.34 支持本卡同比缩放的两个计算。
- 利润率依同一资本估值口径比较；本卡不另设现代投资方程，也不把原典趋势当作已经核验的当代经验规律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter VI supplies the adjustment mechanism, conditional tendency, technological qualification, investment incentives and profit-total examples. The calculations preserve a common capital-valuation basis; no modern investment equation or contemporary empirical law is attributed to the text.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
