# 反向估值是在固定模型和部分假设后求使估值等于市价的其余假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reverse valuation solves for the remaining assumptions that equate model value to price after fixing the model and other inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向估值把问题从“我算出多少价值”倒过来问：“要支持这个价格，模型需要什么经营结果？”它得到的是**给定条件下的价格隐含要求**，不是直接读出了每位投资者的真实信念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reverse valuation asks what operating outcome a model needs to support the observed price. It identifies a conditional price-implied requirement, not the actual beliefs of every investor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如用[[增长永续年金]]的简化模型，下一年可分配现金流 $C_1=60$、折现率 $r=10\%$，价格为 1,000，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a simplified [[增长永续年金|growing-perpetuity]] model with next-year distributable cash flow $C_1=60$, discount rate $r=10\%$, and price 1,000:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\frac{C_1}{r-g}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g=r-\frac{C_1}{P}=4\%,\qquad r>g.$$

若固定折现率改为 8%，同一价格隐含的增长率就变成 2%。所以一个价格不能同时识别增长、利润率、再投资和风险溢价；要把固定项写出来，再检验求得的假设是否与[[收入驱动预测|市场空间]]、[[再投资增长关系|资本需求]]相容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the discount rate at 8% instead, and the same price implies 2% growth. One price cannot identify growth, margins, reinvestment, and risk premia simultaneously. Disclose fixed inputs and test the implied result against [[收入驱动预测|market capacity]] and [[再投资增长关系|reinvestment needs]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Damodaran, Market-implied competitive advantage period](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/highgrowthperiod.htm)：核对固定其他回报条件、由市价反求优势期的思路；本卡以增长永续年金独立演算同一条件反求逻辑，并非完整企业估值模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Damodaran supports inferring an advantage period from price with other return assumptions fixed. The growing-perpetuity calculation independently illustrates that conditional inversion; it is not a complete company valuation model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
