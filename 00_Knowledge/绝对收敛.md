# Absolute convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*绝对收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When additions have different signs, a small running total may hide much larger amounts that cancel. **Absolute convergence** asks whether the total remains finite after replacing every term by its size. For real terms, this means turning negative terms positive before adding; the resulting total measures accumulated magnitude, rather than the balance after cancellation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当相加的项有正有负时，一个很小的累计结果可能掩盖了大量相互抵消的数。**绝对收敛**问的是：先把每一项都换成它的大小，再相加，最终总量是否仍有限。对实数项，这就是把负项变成正项；得到的是累计幅度，而非正负抵消后的余额。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two sequences of partial sums
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分别看两列部分和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that an [[无穷级数|infinite series]] is evaluated through its finite partial sums. Let $a_n$ be finite real or complex numbers, indexed by positive integers $n$. Compare
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无穷级数]]通过有限部分和来定义。设 $a_n$ 是有限的实数或复数，$n$ 为正整数。我们分别考察

$$
S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n,
\qquad
A_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}|a_n|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $N$ is the number of included terms. $S_N$ keeps the original signs or complex directions; $A_N$ adds only nonnegative magnitudes. For a complex number $a_n=u_n+i v_n$, where $u_n,v_n$ are real and $i^2=-1$, its magnitude is $|a_n|=\sqrt{u_n^2+v_n^2}$, the distance from zero in the complex plane. A complex limit means that both real and imaginary parts approach finite limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $N$ 是已经相加的项数。$S_N$ 保留原来的正负号或复数方向，$A_N$ 只累加非负大小。对复数 $a_n=u_n+i v_n$，$u_n,v_n$ 为实数，$i^2=-1$，其模为 $|a_n|=\sqrt{u_n^2+v_n^2}$，也就是复平面上到零的距离。复数极限指实部和虚部分别趋于有限极限。

> [!important] Definition / 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The series $\sum a_n$ **converges absolutely** when $A_N\to A$ for some finite real number $A$. Equivalently, $\sum|a_n|<\infty$. Take the magnitude of **each term**, not just the magnitude $|S_N|$ of a completed partial sum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果 $A_N\to A$，且 $A$ 是有限实数，就称 $\sum a_n$ **绝对收敛**，也写作 $\sum|a_n|<\infty$。这里取的是**每一项**的绝对值，而不是相加后再取 $|S_N|$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $A_{N+1}-A_N=|a_{N+1}|\ge0$, these magnitude totals never decrease. Absolute convergence says they approach a finite ceiling. It is stronger than ordinary convergence: [[绝对收敛推出收敛|absolute convergence implies convergence]] of $S_N$. This is a theorem about the definition, so one need not first calculate the original sum in order to prove absolute convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $A_{N+1}-A_N=|a_{N+1}|\ge0$，幅度累计值不会下降；绝对收敛要求它们逼近一个有限上限。这比普通收敛更强：[[绝对收敛推出收敛]]保证 $S_N$ 也有极限。这是由定义推出的定理，所以证明绝对收敛时，不必预先算出原级数的和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Same terms, different totals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一组项，两种不同的总量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start this example at $n=0$, so the first term is $1$. The [[几何级数|geometric-series formula]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子从 $n=0$ 开始，首项是 $1$。利用[[几何级数]]公式，得到

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(-\frac12\right)^n
=1-\frac12+\frac14-\cdots=\frac23,
\qquad
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left|\left(-\frac12\right)^n\right|
=1+\frac12+\frac14+\cdots=2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both are finite, so the original series is absolutely convergent. Its sum has magnitude $2/3$, while the sum of magnitudes is $2$. Thus absolute convergence does not say cancellation is absent or that these two totals agree. The finite triangle inequality $|S_N|\le A_N$, followed by a limit, instead gives the useful bound $|\sum a_n|\le\sum|a_n|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个和都有限，所以原级数绝对收敛。原和的绝对值是 $2/3$，各项绝对值之和却是 $2$。因此，绝对收敛并不表示没有发生抵消，也不表示两种总量相等。由有限和的三角不等式 $|S_N|\le A_N$ 再取极限，得到的是有用的界 $|\sum a_n|\le\sum|a_n|$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complex example is $a_n=(3+4i)/2^n$ for $n\ge1$. Since $|3+4i|=5$, its magnitude series is $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}5/2^n=5$, so it converges absolutely. Summing its real and imaginary geometric series gives the original sum $3+4i$. The same definition works without trying to label complex terms as positive or negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数例子可取 $a_n=(3+4i)/2^n$，$n\ge1$。因为 $|3+4i|=5$，幅度级数的和为 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}5/2^n=5$，所以绝对收敛；分别求实部和虚部的几何级数，原和为 $3+4i$。定义依然适用，无需把复数项分成正项和负项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How to use the definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*怎样用这个定义作判断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking magnitudes turns the problem into a nonnegative series, so tools such as [[级数直接比较判别|direct comparison]] become available. For example, $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}/n^2$ is absolutely convergent because its magnitude series is $\sum1/n^2$, which converges by the [[p级数判别|p-series test]] with $p=2$. No separate alternating-series argument is needed here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取模后，问题变成非负项级数，便可以使用[[级数直接比较判别]]等工具。例如，$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}/n^2$ 的绝对值级数是 $\sum1/n^2$，由 [[p级数判别]]中 $p=2>1$ 可知收敛，所以原级数绝对收敛。这里不需要再单独使用交错判别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the magnitude series diverges, the original series may still converge; that case is [[条件收敛|conditional convergence]]. It may instead diverge, as $\sum1/n$ does. For nonnegative terms, ordinary and absolute convergence coincide because $|a_n|=a_n$. Changing finitely many finite terms preserves the convergence type, although it may change the sum. The stronger protection against changing the order of infinitely many terms is a separate result: [[绝对收敛级数重排|every rearrangement using each term exactly once preserves an absolutely convergent sum]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果绝对值级数发散，原级数仍可能收敛，这时称为[[条件收敛]]；它也可能像 $\sum1/n$ 一样发散。对非负项，普通收敛与绝对收敛相同，因为 $|a_n|=a_n$。改变有限多个有限值项不会改变收敛类型，但可能改变和。至于改变无穷多项的排列顺序时仍能保住原和，则是另一个定理：[[绝对收敛级数重排|绝对收敛级数在每项恰好使用一次的任意重排后，和保持不变]]。
