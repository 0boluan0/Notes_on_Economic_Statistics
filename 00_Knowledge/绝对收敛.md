# 级数绝对收敛是指其各项绝对值组成的级数收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A series converges absolutely when the series of absolute values converges*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数项或复数项级数 $\sum a_n$，若 $\sum|a_n|$ 有有限和，就称原级数绝对收敛。判断时先取每一项的绝对值，再对这条非负项级数作收敛检验；这不是先把原级数求和再取绝对值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real or complex terms, absolute convergence means that $\sum|a_n|$ has a finite sum. Take the absolute value of each term before testing convergence, rather than taking the absolute value of a completed sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1/2)^n$ 绝对收敛，因为其绝对值级数是公比 $1/2$ 的 [[几何级数]]。原和为 $2/3$，绝对值级数的和为 $2$，两者并不相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1/2)^n$ converges absolutely because its absolute-value series is [[几何级数|geometric]] with ratio $1/2$. The two sums are $2/3$ and $2$, respectively; they need not agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么这样的检验能证明原级数有和，由 [[绝对收敛推出收敛]] 解释；如果原级数收敛而绝对值级数发散，则属于另一个概念 [[条件收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The implication for the original series is proved in [[绝对收敛推出收敛|absolute convergence implies convergence]]. A convergent series whose absolute-value series diverges is instead [[条件收敛|conditionally convergent]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
