# The determinant as a product of eigenvalues
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
行列式与特征值乘积

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagonal matrix $\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{bmatrix}$ doubles one coordinate and triples the other. It turns a unit square into a rectangle of area $6$. Its [[行列式|determinant]] is $6$, and its eigenvalues are $2$ and $3$: their product is also $6$. The same product rule works for every real or complex square matrix, including matrices that cannot be diagonalized. To use it correctly, repeated eigenvalues must be counted as repeated roots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对角矩阵 $\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{bmatrix}$ 把一个坐标加倍、另一个坐标变成三倍，单位正方形因此成为面积为 $6$ 的矩形。它的[[行列式]]为 $6$，特征值为 $2,3$，两者的乘积同样是 $6$。这个乘积规则适用于每个实或复方阵，包括不能对角化的矩阵；正确使用它的关键，是按多项式重根的次数重复计入特征值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[特征对|eigenvalue and eigenvector]] satisfy $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$. Over the real numbers this means that a direction is rescaled by $\lambda$, possibly reversing orientation or collapsing to zero. A real matrix can also have nonreal eigenvalues, so the full product is formed over the complex numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[特征对|特征值与特征向量]]满足 $Av=\lambda v$，其中 $v\ne0$。在实数范围内，这表示一个方向按 $\lambda$ 缩放，也可能反向或压到零。实矩阵还可能有非实特征值，因此完整乘积需要在复数范围内计根。

> [!important] Count every root / 按根的重数计入
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For an $n\times n$ real or complex matrix $A$, with $n\ge1$, list its complex eigenvalues as $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$, counting each with its algebraic multiplicity. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实或复 $n\times n$ 方阵 $A$，$n\ge1$，把复特征值按代数重数列成 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$，则
> $$\det A=\prod_{i=1}^n\lambda_i=\lambda_1\lambda_2\cdots\lambda_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic multiplicity]] counts how often a root occurs in the [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]], not how many independent eigenvectors it has. If that polynomial is $(t-2)^2(t+1)$, the list is $2,2,-1$, and the determinant is $-4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里的[[代数重数与几何重数|代数重数]]，数的是一个根在[[特征多项式]]中重复出现几次，而不是它有几个独立特征向量。若多项式为 $(t-2)^2(t+1)$，要列出 $2,2,-1$，行列式为 $-4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the constant term gives the product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 从常数项读出乘积

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the convention $\chi_A(t)=\det(tI-A)$, where $I$ is the identity matrix and $t$ is a scalar variable. This is a polynomial of degree $n$ with leading coefficient $1$. Over $\mathbb C$ it factors completely into its $n$ roots, with repetitions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
采用约定 $\chi_A(t)=\det(tI-A)$，其中 $I$ 是单位矩阵，$t$ 是标量变量。这个多项式的次数为 $n$、最高次项系数为 $1$；在 $\mathbb C$ 中，它能按全部 $n$ 个根（包括重根）分解为

$$
\chi_A(t)=\prod_{i=1}^n(t-\lambda_i).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluate both expressions at $t=0$. On the determinant side, [[整体缩放的行列式|scaling all $n$ rows by $-1$]] contributes a factor $(-1)^n$. On the factored side, each of the $n$ factors contributes one minus sign. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在两种表达式中都代入 $t=0$。行列式一侧，[[整体缩放的行列式|把全部 $n$ 行都乘以 $-1$]] 会产生因子 $(-1)^n$；分解式一侧，每个因子也各产生一个负号。因此

$$
\underbrace{\chi_A(0)}_{\text{constant term}}
=\det(-A)=(-1)^n\det A
=(-1)^n\prod_{i=1}^n\lambda_i.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cancel the common factor $(-1)^n$. Nothing in this argument asks for a basis of eigenvectors: it uses only the characteristic polynomial and its roots. The [[特征多项式底层域|underlying-field condition]] matters because a real polynomial need not factor completely over the reals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
约去共同因子 $(-1)^n$ 即得结论。整个论证只使用特征多项式及其根，没有要求存在特征向量基。[[特征多项式底层域|底层域的条件]]之所以重要，是因为实系数多项式未必能在实数范围内完全分解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## It also holds without diagonalizability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 不可对角化也成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
考虑

$$
J=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\chi_J(t)=(t-2)^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The eigenvalue $2$ occurs twice as a root. But $(J-2I)v=0$ reduces to $v_2=0$, so every eigenvector is a nonzero multiple of $(1,0)^T$. There is only one independent eigenvector, too few to diagonalize a two-dimensional matrix. Nevertheless,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
特征值 $2$ 作为根出现两次，但 $(J-2I)v=0$ 只给出 $v_2=0$，所有特征向量都是 $(1,0)^T$ 的非零倍数。只有一个独立特征向量，不够把二维矩阵对角化；然而

$$\det J=2\cdot2-1\cdot0=4=2\cdot2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two factors come from the two polynomial roots. Counting the single independent eigenvector would incorrectly leave only one factor of $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两个因子来自多项式的两个根；若错误地按唯一的独立特征向量计数，就会漏掉一个 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Complex eigenvalues of a real matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 实矩阵的复特征值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
矩阵

$$
R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\chi_R(t)=t^2+1
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
rotates the plane through $90^\circ$. No real line keeps its direction, and $t^2+1$ has no real root. Over $\mathbb C$, however, its roots are $i$ and $-i$, where $i^2=-1$. They give $i(-i)=1=\det R$. Using only real eigenvalues would omit both factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
把平面旋转 $90^\circ$。没有实直线保留原来的方向，$t^2+1$ 也没有实根；但在 $\mathbb C$ 中，它的根为 $i,-i$，其中 $i^2=-1$。两者相乘得到 $i(-i)=1=\det R$。只乘实特征值会把两个因子都漏掉。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, a real characteristic polynomial has nonreal roots in conjugate pairs, with matching multiplicities: conjugating its equation preserves its real coefficients. Each pair $a+bi,a-bi$ has product $a^2+b^2$, so the complete eigenvalue product is real, as the determinant of a real matrix must be.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一般地，实特征多项式的非实根成共轭对出现，重数也相同，因为对多项式方程取共轭不会改变实系数。每对 $a+bi,a-bi$ 的乘积为 $a^2+b^2$，所以完整乘积仍然是实数，符合实矩阵的行列式应为实数这一事实。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Consequences and scope
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 直接推论与边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product vanishes exactly when at least one eigenvalue is zero. By [[可逆性与非零行列式|the determinant criterion for invertibility]], a square matrix is therefore invertible exactly when zero is absent from its eigenvalues. For a [[三角矩阵|triangular matrix]], $tI-A$ is triangular too, so its characteristic polynomial is the product of $t-a_{ii}$ over the diagonal entries. Those entries are its eigenvalues, counted with repetition, and the theorem gives the familiar diagonal-entry product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
乘积为零，当且仅当至少有一个特征值为零。结合[[可逆性与非零行列式|可逆性的行列式判据]]，方阵可逆就等价于它没有零特征值。对于[[三角矩阵]]，$tI-A$ 仍是三角矩阵，因此特征多项式是各对角项 $t-a_{ii}$ 的乘积；对角元就是按重复次数计入的特征值，本定理便给出熟悉的对角元乘积。

> [!warning] Multiplicity and the scalar field / 重数与数域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep all $n$ algebraic factors, including complex roots and repetitions. Geometric multiplicity counts independent eigenvector directions and cannot replace this count. Over another scalar field, the same constant-term proof applies after passing, if necessary, to an extension field containing all characteristic roots; no diagonalization assumption is added.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要保留全部 $n$ 个代数因子，包括复根与重根。几何重数数的是独立特征向量方向，不能代替这种计数。对其他数域上的矩阵，必要时先扩到包含全部特征根的域，同一常数项证明仍然成立，也不需要额外假设可对角化。
