# 循环总成本应对实际迭代成本求和而不是机械相乘循环层数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sum actual iteration costs instead of mechanically multiplying loop depths*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析循环时，先确定每次迭代是否执行、执行多少次以及每次实际成本，再将这些成本求和；初始化、条件检查与更新也要计入选定的[[算法成本模型]]。顺序执行的代码块成本相加；嵌套循环按实际索引范围逐层求和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Analyze a loop by determining which iterations execute, how many occur, and the cost of each, then summing them. Include initialization, condition tests and updates in the chosen [[算法成本模型|cost model]]. Sequential blocks add their costs; nested loops require sums over the actual index ranges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若共有 $k(n)$ 轮，第 $i$ 轮主体成本为 $c_i(n)$，主体总成本是 $\sum_{i=1}^{k(n)}c_i(n)$。用逐项界推出总和界时，需要对所有这些轮次同时有效的统一常数与起始阈值，才能把“每轮 $O(g(n))$”合并为 $O(k(n)g(n))$；若每轮还有同样统一的 $\Omega(g(n))$ 下界，就得到 $\Theta(k(n)g(n))$。参见[[渐近界的统一常数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $k(n)$ iterations and body cost $c_i(n)$ at iteration $i$, the body costs $\sum_{i=1}^{k(n)}c_i(n)$. Deriving $O(k(n)g(n))$ by adding per-iteration $O(g(n))$ bounds requires constants and thresholds uniform across the iterations. A matching uniform lower bound gives $\Theta(k(n)g(n))$; see [[渐近界的统一常数|uniform asymptotic constants]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，内层每次做一个常数成本操作，下列两种执行次数不同：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if each inner iteration does one constant-cost operation, these two ranges produce different counts:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}1=nm,
\qquad
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\sum_{j=i}^{n-1}1
=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}(n-i)
=\frac{n(n+1)}2.
$$

第一种在 $m,n\ge1$ 时总时间为 $\Theta(nm)$；第二种为 $\Theta(n^2)$。三角形循环的内层并非每轮都做 $\Theta(n)$ 次，但求和仍给出平方阶。若内层固定只做 10 次，则总时间仅为 $\Theta(n)$。若有提前返回、切片或线性成员测试，还要把相应控制流程与成本加入求和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first has total time $\Theta(nm)$ for $m,n\ge1$; the second has $\Theta(n^2)$ time. The triangular inner loop does not take $\Theta(n)$ iterations on every outer iteration, yet its sum is quadratic. An inner loop of exactly 10 iterations gives only $\Theta(n)$ total time. Early returns, slices and linear membership tests must also enter the analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
