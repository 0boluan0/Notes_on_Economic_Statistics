# Efficiency of estimators
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*估计量的有效性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two methods can both estimate the right quantity on average, yet one may give much more variable answers when the sample is repeated. **Efficiency** compares that variability within a stated model and class of methods. Here the target is a single number, and the comparison is at the same fixed sample size. The [[方差|variance]] of an [[估计对象三分|estimator]] measures how widely its possible answers spread around their own average; it does not by itself measure distance from the desired target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种方法可能都在平均意义上估对目标，但换一份样本时，其中一种的答案波动更大。**有效性**在指定模型和候选方法类别内比较这种波动。这里讨论单个数值目标，并固定相同的样本量。[[估计对象三分|估计量]]的[[方差]]衡量可能答案围绕自身平均值的分散程度，本身不衡量离目标有多远。

> [!example] Use both observations or only the first? · 用两个观察，还是只用第一个？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Draw two observations independently and uniformly, with replacement, from the population values $0,2,4,6$. The population mean is $3$ and variance is $5$. Compare the rules
> $$T=\frac{X_1+X_2}{2},\qquad U=X_1.$$
> Both rules receive the same two-observation sample, but $U$ ignores its second value. Their expectations are both $3$. Independence makes the [[协方差|covariance]] zero, so
> $$\operatorname{Var}(T)=\frac{5+5}{4}=\frac52,
> \qquad \operatorname{Var}(U)=5.$$
> The average has half the variance of the first-observation rule. This is a comparison across possible samples. In the particular sample $(2,6)$, both answers, $T=4$ and $U=2$, are one unit from the target; in $(4,6)$, $T=5$ is two units away while $U=4$ is only one unit away.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从总体值 $0,2,4,6$ 中独立、均匀、有放回地抽两次；总体均值为 $3$，方差为 $5$。规则 $T$ 取两次观察的平均，规则 $U$ 只用第一次。两者拿到相同大小的样本，期望都为 $3$；独立性使[[协方差]]为零，因此平均数的方差为 $5/2$，只用第一次的方差为 $5$。方差减半是对可能样本整体的比较。在具体样本 $(2,6)$ 中，$T=4$、$U=2$ 都离目标一个单位；而 $(4,6)$ 中，$T=5$ 离目标两个单位，$U=4$ 反而只差一个单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same calculation works for any [[独立同分布|iid]] pair with common finite mean $\mu$ and finite variance $\sigma^2$: both rules are [[估计量无偏性|unbiased]] for $\mu$, and their variances are $\sigma^2/2$ and $\sigma^2$. Knowing the numerical mean in the small example lets us check the rules; neither rule uses that unknown mean as an input. A lower variance between these two candidates does not prove that $T$ is best among every possible estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一计算适用于任意具有共同有限均值 $\mu$、有限方差 $\sigma^2$ 的[[独立同分布|iid]]观察对：两种规则都[[估计量无偏性|无偏]]，方差分别为 $\sigma^2/2$ 和 $\sigma^2$。小例子事先给出总体均值，是为了核对规则；实际计算时，两种规则都不需要把未知均值当作输入。胜过这一个候选，并没有证明 $T$ 胜过所有可能的估计量。

## What must be fixed before saying “best”?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*说“最佳”之前，先固定比较范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the sampling model, the target, the sample size $n$, and the permitted estimators. In a model indexed by a parameter $\theta$, write the target as $g(\theta)$ and the candidate class as $\mathcal C_n$. The subscript $\theta$ on $E_\theta$ or $\operatorname{Var}_\theta$ says which possible population model supplies the probabilities. In the usual **unbiased, finite-variance comparison**, every candidate $S\in\mathcal C_n$ must satisfy $E_\theta[S]=g(\theta)$ and $\operatorname{Var}_\theta(S)<\infty$ for every allowed $\theta$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先说清抽样模型、目标、样本量 $n$，以及允许比较哪些估计量。用参数 $\theta$ 标记模型、$g(\theta)$ 表示目标、$\mathcal C_n$ 表示候选类别；$E_\theta$ 和 $\operatorname{Var}_\theta$ 的下标说明使用哪个可能总体的概率。在通常的**无偏且有限方差比较**中，每个候选 $S$ 都必须在每个允许的 $\theta$ 下满足 $E_\theta[S]=g(\theta)$，且方差有限。

> [!important] Minimum variance within the class · 类别内的最小方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An estimator $T\in\mathcal C_n$ is uniformly minimum variance within this class when
> $$\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T)\le \operatorname{Var}_\theta(S)
> \quad\text{for every }S\in\mathcal C_n\text{ and every allowed }\theta.$$
> “Uniformly” here means the same rule wins or ties at every parameter value. If the class contains all unbiased finite-variance estimators of the target, this is a **uniformly minimum-variance unbiased estimator (UMVUE)**. If the class is smaller, the claim is only about that smaller class. Finding a smaller variance at one parameter value is not enough for the displayed all-parameter claim.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若同一个 $T$ 对每个候选 $S$、每个允许参数值 $\theta$ 都满足上面的方差不等式，就在此类别中具有一致最小方差。“一致”在这里表示同一规则在所有参数值下都不差；不要与样本量增长时的[[估计量一致性|一致性]]混淆。若类别包括目标的全部无偏有限方差估计量，就称为**一致最小方差无偏估计量（UMVUE）**。类别更小时，结论也只覆盖该类别；只在一个参数值处更小，不足以证明全参数结论。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This use of “uniformly” concerns parameter values at fixed $n$; it is different from [[估计量一致性|consistency as sample size grows]]. Even a valid variance ordering does not guarantee a smaller realized error in every sample or a smaller probability of every possible tail event. Variance is the criterion being compared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“一致最小”在固定 $n$ 下比较不同参数值，不是随样本量增长的依概率收敛。有效的方差排序也不保证每份样本的实际误差更小，更不自动比较所有可能尾事件的概率；当前使用的准则是方差。

## Why a constant answer does not settle the problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么恒定答案不能解决估计问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule “always report $7$” has variance zero. For the population above, however, its bias is $7-3=4$ and its squared error is always $16$. It is excluded from an unbiased class for an unknown mean ranging over more than just $7$. A concentrated [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]] can be concentrated in the wrong place.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“永远回答 $7$”的方差为零，但对上面总体，其偏差为 $7-3=4$，平方误差恒为 $16$。当未知均值不只允许取 $7$ 时，它不属于对每个均值都无偏的类别。[[抽样分布]]可以很集中，却集中在错误的位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If biased methods are allowed, one useful criterion is [[均方误差|mean squared error (MSE)]], the expected squared distance from the target. With finite second moments, [[MSE偏差方差分解|MSE equals variance plus squared bias]]. A method can reduce variance enough to offset an introduced bias, so an unbiased-class efficiency result does not rank all biased alternatives. The units also matter: variance and MSE have squared target units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若允许有偏方法，可改用[[均方误差|均方误差（MSE）]]：估计值离目标的平方距离的期望。二阶矩有限时，[[MSE偏差方差分解|MSE 等于方差加偏差平方]]。降低方差的好处可能超过新偏差的代价，所以无偏类别内的有效性结论不能替所有有偏方法排序。方差和 MSE 的单位都是目标单位的平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two familiar results illustrate why the comparison class must be named. The **classical** [[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理|Gauss–Markov theorem]] calls OLS “best linear unbiased” under its design, mean and covariance assumptions: the competitor is linear in the observed response and unbiased under that model. The [[Cramér–Rao下界|Cramér–Rao inequality]] gives a variance lower bound under specific regularity conditions on the parametric model. The inequality alone does not construct an estimator attaining the bound; attainment for every parameter value must be checked separately. Neither phrase means “best method for every purpose.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个熟悉结果说明了为什么必须标明比较类别。**经典**[[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理|Gauss–Markov 定理]]在其设计、均值和协方差假设下称 OLS 为“最佳线性无偏”：候选对观察结果线性，并在该模型下无偏。[[Cramér–Rao下界]]则依赖参数模型的具体正则条件；不等式本身没有构造出达到下界的估计量，还须另行核对是否在所有参数值下达到。两者都不是“对所有目的都最佳”的意思。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card concerns finite-sample variance comparisons. “Asymptotic efficiency” instead compares appropriately scaled limiting distributions or variances and needs its own definition and assumptions. More simulated repetitions also do not make an unchanged estimator more efficient; they help us measure its existing behavior, as explained in [[样本量与模拟重复次数|sample size versus simulation repetitions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡讨论有限样本方差比较。“渐近有效性”改为比较适当缩放后的极限分布或方差，需要另外的定义与条件。增加模拟重复次数同样不会让不变的估计规则突然更有效；它帮助测清已有性质，见[[样本量与模拟重复次数]]。
