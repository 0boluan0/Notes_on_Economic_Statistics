# 麦考利久期是以现金流现值为权重的平均支付时间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Macaulay duration is the present-value-weighted average time to payment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

麦考利久期把每笔剩余现金流的支付时间，按该笔现值占全部现值的比例加权平均。它描述价值集中在多远的未来，而不是“累计收款首次收回买价”的回收期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Macaulay duration averages the remaining payment times using each payment's share of total present value. It describes when value is concentrated, not the first date on which cumulative receipts recover the purchase price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定现金流 $CF_k\ge0$，至少一笔为正；$t_k>0$ 是从估值日到付款日的日历年数。采用年名义到期收益率 $y$、每年复利 $m$ 次的模型，令 $a=1+y/m>0$，并以全部待收现金流的现值作为[[债券全价|全价]] $P$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold cash flows $CF_k\ge0$ fixed, with at least one positive payment. Let $t_k>0$ be calendar years from valuation to payment. Under nominal annual YTM $y$ compounded $m$ times a year, set $a=1+y/m>0$ and use the present value of all remaining payments as the [[债券全价|full price]] $P$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\sum_k CF_k a^{-mt_k},\qquad w_k=\frac{CF_k a^{-mt_k}}{P},\qquad D_M=\sum_k t_k w_k.$$

因为 $\sum_k w_k=1$，$D_M$ 以年计，落在正现金流的最早和最晚支付时间之间。零息债券只有一个支付时点，因此 $D_M=T$；若到期前还有正息票，则 $D_M<T$。用半年期数 $k$ 算出的加权平均必须除以 $2$，才能报告为年。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since the weights sum to one, $D_M$ is measured in years and lies between the earliest and latest positive payments. A zero-coupon bond has $D_M=T$; positive coupons before maturity give $D_M<T$. A weighted average calculated in half-year periods must be divided by two to report years.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如面值 $100$、每年息票 $8$、剩余 $3$ 年、年复利收益率 $5\%$ 的现金流为 $8,8,108$。由 $P=108.169744$ 得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For face value 100, annual coupons of 8, three years remaining and a 5% annually compounded yield, payments are $8,8,108$. With $P=108.169744$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$D_M=\frac{1(8/1.05)+2(8/1.05^2)+3(108/1.05^3)}{108.169744}\approx2.792046\text{ 年}.$$

权重随贴现口径变化。此处是单一 YTM 的现值权重，不把任意即期曲线贴现后的平均时间自动当成同一口径的 YTM 久期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weights depend on the discounting convention. These are single-YTM present-value weights; a timing average obtained from an arbitrary spot curve is not automatically the same YTM-based duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
