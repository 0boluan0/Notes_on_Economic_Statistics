# 内生增长理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Endogenous Growth Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把 Solow 模型中外生给定的技术增长拆开，解释研发、人力资本、知识外溢和创造性破坏如何使长期增长由经济内部决定。
> **具体锚点：** 一家企业研发新药要承担全部成本，但知识一旦被发现，多人可同时使用；专利给创新者租金，也可抬高应用价格并阻碍后续创新。
> **核心难点：** 知识的非竞争性使规模报酬与完全竞争出现张力；需要同时解释创新激励、市场势力和知识外溢。
> **为什么重要：** 在这些模型中，教育、研发、竞争、贸易与知识产权可影响长期增长，而不只是产出水平。
> **继续：** 先用 AK 模型理解“不递减的可积累要素”，再比较 Romer 的水平创新与 Aghion–Howitt 的质量替代；Solow 基准见 [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It opens the exogenous technology term in the Solow model and explains how R&D, human capital, knowledge spillovers, and creative destruction make long-run growth internally determined.
> **Concrete anchor:** A firm bears the full cost of discovering a drug, yet once discovered the knowledge can be used by many people at once. A patent creates rents for the innovator but can also raise access prices and obstruct later innovation.
> **Central difficulty:** Knowledge's nonrivalry creates tension between scale returns and perfect competition. Innovation incentives, market power, and knowledge spillovers must be analyzed together.
> **Why it matters:** In these models, education, R&D, competition, trade, and intellectual property can affect long-run growth rather than only the output level.
> **Continue:** Use the AK model to understand an accumulable factor without diminishing returns, then compare Romer's horizontal innovation with Aghion–Howitt quality replacement. For the Solow benchmark, see [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛|The Solow Growth Model, Steady State, and Convergence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节依据
> - Romer（1990）《[Endogenous Technological Change](https://doi.org/10.1086/261725)》：核验非竞争且部分可排他知识、研发投资与垄断竞争均衡。
> - Aghion 与 Howitt（1992）《[A Model of Growth Through Creative Destruction](https://doi.org/10.2307/2951599)》：核验质量改进、创新租金与旧技术被替代的机制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - Romer (1990), “[Endogenous Technological Change](https://doi.org/10.1086/261725),” verifies nonrival and partly excludable knowledge, R&D investment, and monopolistic-competition equilibrium.
> - Aghion and Howitt (1992), “[A Model of Growth Through Creative Destruction](https://doi.org/10.2307/2951599),” verifies quality improvement, innovation rents, and replacement of old technologies.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 内生增长的三个入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three entries into endogenous growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

AK 模型取消广义资本递减回报；Romer 型模型用非竞争知识、研发和规模效应；Schumpeterian 模型用创新替代与创造性破坏。政策可能影响长期增长，但市场力量、专利和资源错配也进入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The AK model removes diminishing returns to broad capital. Romer-type models use nonrival knowledge, R&D, and scale effects. Schumpeterian models use innovation replacement and creative destruction. Policy can affect long-run growth, but market power, patents, and resource misallocation also enter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个入口不应混成“技术很重要”一句话。AK 的关键是可积累要素整体不递减；Romer 的关键是新知识/品种的非竞争性与部分可排他性；Schumpeterian 的关键是新质量淘汰旧质量，同时创造总体增长和个体损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three entries should not be collapsed into “technology matters.” AK hinges on non-diminishing returns to an accumulable broad factor. Romer hinges on new knowledge or varieties being nonrival and partly excludable. Schumpeterian growth hinges on new quality displacing old quality, simultaneously creating aggregate growth and private losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## AK 的最小机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The minimal AK mechanism*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $Y=AK$、储蓄率为 $s$、折旧为 $\delta$，则

$$\dot K=sAK-\delta K,$$

因而

$$\frac{\dot K}{K}=sA-\delta.$$

只要 $sA>\delta$，资本与产出可按正率持续增长。这与 Solow 中资本越多边际产出越低的调整不同，但结论完全依赖“广义资本不递减”这个强假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y=AK$, saving be $s$, and depreciation be $\delta$. Then

$$\dot K=sAK-\delta K,$$

so

$$\frac{\dot K}{K}=sA-\delta.$$

Whenever $sA>\delta$, capital and output can grow persistently at a positive rate. This differs from Solow's declining marginal product of capital, but the conclusion depends entirely on the strong assumption that broad capital does not face diminishing returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 知识、研发与市场结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Knowledge, R&D, and market structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知识可被多个生产者同时使用，因此它是非竞争的；专利、秘密和互补资产又可使它部分可排他。固定研发成本若要被回收，创新者需要某种价格高于边际生产成本的租金；因而完全竞争与内生研发无法被无缝同时假定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowledge can be used by several producers at once and is therefore nonrival. Patents, secrecy, and complementary assets can make it partly excludable. Recovering a fixed R&D cost requires some rent with price above marginal production cost, so perfect competition and endogenous R&D cannot simply be assumed together without friction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Romer 型模型中，研发创造新设计或中间品种类；在创造性破坏模型中，新一代质量取代旧一代并消灭旧创新者租金。后者使竞争政策、进入、在位企业阻碍与劳动者转岗成为增长机制的一部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Romer-type models, R&D creates new designs or intermediate-good varieties. In creative-destruction models, a new quality replaces an old one and destroys the incumbent innovator's rent. Competition policy, entry, incumbent obstruction, and worker reallocation therefore become part of the growth mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 政策不是“补贴研发”一条结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Policy is not one conclusion to subsidize R&D*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知识溢出可使私人研发低于社会最优，但商业抢夺、重复研发和过快淘汰也可带来负外部性。因此要同时评估研发补贴、基础科学、专利长度/宽度、竞争与技能转换，不能从“知识外溢”直接跳到补贴越多越好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowledge spillovers can make private R&D lower than the social optimum, but business stealing, duplicated research, and excessively rapid obsolescence can create negative externalities. R&D subsidies, basic science, patent length and breadth, competition, and worker retraining must be evaluated together; “knowledge spills over” does not imply that larger subsidies are always better.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实证边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Empirical boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

储蓄、制度和增长相互决定，跨国回归不能简单识别模型参数。模型用于组织机制和反事实，不是国别增长率的完整预测器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Saving, institutions, and growth are jointly determined, so cross-country regressions do not simply identify model parameters. Models organize mechanisms and counterfactuals; they are not complete predictors of country growth rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把 AK 中的 $K$ 当成只有机器的狭义物质资本，却不解释为何么没有递减收益。
- 把知识非竞争误解为知识必然免费且不可排他。
- 从知识外溢直接推出任何研发补贴都提高福利，忽略项目选择、商业抢夺与财政机会成本。
- 用跨国研发与增长的相关性直接宣称已识别某一内生增长模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating $K$ in AK as only machines without explaining why it avoids diminishing returns.
- Mistaking nonrival knowledge for knowledge that is necessarily free and nonexcludable.
- Inferring that every R&D subsidy raises welfare while ignoring selection, business stealing, and fiscal opportunity cost.
- Using cross-country correlation between R&D and growth as direct identification of one endogenous-growth model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释知识的非竞争性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain knowledge nonrivalry in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 一个人使用一条公式或设计时，不会耗尽它并防止另一个人同时使用；但获取权仍可被专利或保密排除。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> One person's use of a formula or design does not use it up or prevent simultaneous use by another person, although access can still be restricted by patents or secrecy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### AK 为什么可以产生持续增长？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can the AK model generate sustained growth?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为广义资本的边际产出不随积累而递减到零；只要净投资率 $sA-\delta$ 为正，资本和产出就持续按比例增长。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The marginal product of broad capital does not diminish toward zero as it accumulates. Whenever net investment $sA-\delta$ is positive, capital and output keep growing proportionally.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Romer 型创新和创造性破坏的主要区别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the main distinction between Romer-type innovation and creative destruction?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Romer 型模型常通过新设计或品种增加知识广度；创造性破坏用新质量取代旧质量，因此进步同时消灭旧创新者的租金。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Romer-type models often expand the breadth of knowledge through new designs or varieties. Creative destruction replaces old quality with new quality, so progress simultaneously eliminates the old innovator's rent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么内生增长理论不意味着研发补贴越高越好？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does endogenous growth theory not imply that larger R&D subsidies are always better?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 研发有正知识外溢，也可有商业抢夺、重复投入和过快淘汰的负外部性；还需要考虑项目选择和公共资金机会成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> R&D has positive knowledge spillovers but can also create business stealing, duplicated effort, and excessively rapid obsolescence. Project selection and the opportunity cost of public funds also matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
