# Linear maps and matrix representations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性映射与矩阵表示*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **linear map** tells us which output vector comes from each input vector. A **matrix representation** records that rule as calculations on coordinates, after we choose how to describe the inputs and outputs. The map and its matrix answer different questions: what happens to the object, and how do the numbers describing it change?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**线性映射**规定每个输入向量被送到哪个输出向量。**矩阵表示**则是在约定输入、输出怎样记坐标之后，把这个规则记录成坐标之间的计算。二者分别回答：对象发生什么变化，以及描述它的数字怎样变化？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, projecting a plane vector onto the diagonal line through $(1,1)$ is a fixed geometric operation. In the usual horizontal–vertical coordinates it mixes two numbers; in coordinates along that line and its perpendicular it simply keeps the first coordinate and discards the second. We will calculate both descriptions below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，把平面向量投影到经过 $(1,1)$ 的对角线上，是一个确定的几何操作。使用通常的横纵坐标时，它混合两个数字；沿这条直线及其垂直方向记坐标时，它只需保留第一个坐标、去掉第二个坐标。下面把两种描述算出来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Giving the input and output their coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先说明两端怎样记坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V,W$ be finite-dimensional [[向量空间|vector spaces]] over the same scalar field $\mathbb F$ (for example, $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$). Vectors may be arrows, polynomials or other objects that can be added and scaled. A [[线性映射|linear map]] $T:V\to W$ preserves those two operations. Choose an ordered [[基|basis]] $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ for the input and an ordered basis $\mathcal C=(w_1,\ldots,w_m)$ for the output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V,W$ 是同一标量域 $\mathbb F$（例如实数域或复数域）上的有限维[[向量空间]]。向量可以是箭头、多项式，或者其他能够相加和乘标量的对象。[[线性映射]] $T:V\to W$ 保持这两种运算。为输入选有序[[基]] $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$，为输出选有序基 $\mathcal C=(w_1,\ldots,w_m)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Being a basis means that every input has exactly one expansion $x=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_nv_n$. Its coordinate column $[x]_{\mathcal B}=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)^T$ lists those coefficients in basis order. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], turning the displayed row into a column; $T(x)$ denotes the map. Likewise, $[T(x)]_{\mathcal C}$ is the list of coefficients needed to build the output from the $w_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基保证每个输入都有唯一展开 $x=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_nv_n$。坐标列 $[x]_{\mathcal B}=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)^T$ 按基的顺序列出这些系数；这里上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把所列的一行变成一列；$T(x)$ 则表示映射作用。同样，$[T(x)]_{\mathcal C}$ 是使用 $w_i$ 拼出输出所需的系数列。

> [!important] What the matrix acts on · 矩阵作用于什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The representing matrix $A=[T]_{\mathcal C\leftarrow\mathcal B}$ is the unique $m\times n$ matrix satisfying
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 表示矩阵 $A=[T]_{\mathcal C\leftarrow\mathcal B}$ 是满足下式的唯一 $m\times n$ 矩阵：
>
> $$[T(x)]_{\mathcal C}=A[x]_{\mathcal B}\qquad\text{for every }x\in V.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Read the arrow from right to left: input coordinates in $\mathcal B$ become output coordinates in $\mathcal C$. There are $n$ input coefficients and $m$ output coefficients. The matrix multiplies a coordinate column, which need not be the original object $x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 箭头从右向左读：$\mathcal B$ 下的输入坐标变成 $\mathcal C$ 下的输出坐标。输入有 $n$ 个系数，输出有 $m$ 个系数。矩阵乘的是坐标列，这个数列未必就是原来的对象 $x$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does such a matrix exist? A linear map is determined by its [[线性映射的基向量像|images of basis vectors]]. Write each $T(v_j)$ in the output basis, and put those coefficients in column $j$ of $A$. Linearity then gives the displayed equation for every linear combination. The [[表示矩阵的列|column construction]] explains the calculation and its uniqueness in detail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么这样的矩阵存在？线性映射由[[线性映射的基向量像|基向量的像]]决定。把每个 $T(v_j)$ 写成输出基的组合，将系数放进 $A$ 的第 $j$ 列；线性性便把上式推广到所有线性组合。[[表示矩阵的列]]详细解释这个构造及其唯一性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One projection, two matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个投影的两张矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the map $T(a,b)=((a+b)/2,(a+b)/2)$ on $\mathbb R^2$. It is the orthogonal projection onto the line $a=b$: the output lies on that line, while the removed vector is a multiple of $(1,-1)$, perpendicular to it. In the standard basis $\mathcal E=((1,0),(0,1))$ on both sides, its matrix is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^2$ 上取 $T(a,b)=((a+b)/2,(a+b)/2)$。它是到直线 $a=b$ 的正交投影：输出落在该直线上，减掉的向量则是与直线垂直的 $(1,-1)$ 的倍数。两端均采用标准基 $\mathcal E=((1,0),(0,1))$ 时，矩阵为

$$A=[T]_{\mathcal E\leftarrow\mathcal E}
=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use $\mathcal B=((1,1),(1,-1))$ on both sides. These vectors form a basis because $(a,b)=\frac{a+b}{2}(1,1)+\frac{a-b}{2}(1,-1)$ uniquely. The map fixes the first basis vector and sends the second to zero, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两端改用 $\mathcal B=((1,1),(1,-1))$。它们构成一组基，因为 $(a,b)=\frac{a+b}{2}(1,1)+\frac{a-b}{2}(1,-1)$ 且系数唯一。映射保留第一个基向量，把第二个送到零，因此

$$D=[T]_{\mathcal B\leftarrow\mathcal B}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

> [!example] Follow the same vector · 跟踪同一个向量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x=(3,1)$, the standard coordinates are $(3,1)^T$, and $A(3,1)^T=(2,2)^T$. In $\mathcal B$ coordinates, $x=2(1,1)+1(1,-1)$, so $[x]_{\mathcal B}=(2,1)^T$ and $D[x]_{\mathcal B}=(2,0)^T$. Decode this last column: $2(1,1)+0(1,-1)=(2,2)$. Both calculations give exactly the same output.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $x=(3,1)$，标准坐标为 $(3,1)^T$，而 $A(3,1)^T=(2,2)^T$。在 $\mathcal B$ 下，$x=2(1,1)+1(1,-1)$，所以 $[x]_{\mathcal B}=(2,1)^T$，$D[x]_{\mathcal B}=(2,0)^T$。把最后的坐标还原：$2(1,1)+0(1,-1)=(2,2)$。两种计算得到的是同一个输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, interpreting the same array $D$ in the standard basis gives the different map $(a,b)\mapsto(a,0)$, projection onto the horizontal axis. The numerical array alone does not identify an abstract map unless the spaces and bases are understood.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若在标准基下解释同一个数表 $D$，它表示的是另一映射 $(a,b)\mapsto(a,0)$，即投影到横轴。若未说明空间和基，单独一张数表就不能确定抽象映射的含义。

## What does, and does not, depend on the bases
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些东西随基改变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing bases leaves $T$ unchanged and **can** change its matrix; it need not always do so. The identity map $x\mapsto x$ has the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] in every basis when the same basis is used on both sides. With different input and output bases, however, even this map has a coordinate-conversion matrix. For the $\mathcal B,\mathcal E$ above,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换基保留 $T$，并且**可能**改变它的矩阵，不是每次必定改变。恒等映射 $x\mapsto x$ 在两端使用同一组基时，无论选哪组基，表示都为[[单位矩阵]]。但两端采用不同基时，连恒等映射也会表现为坐标转换矩阵。用上面的 $\mathcal B,\mathcal E$，有

$$[\mathrm{id}]_{\mathcal E\leftarrow\mathcal B}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This matrix changes the description, not the vector. For a map $V\to V$, comparing matrices in a single basis on each side leads to [[相似矩阵|similarity]]. For two successive maps, the two matrices can be multiplied directly only when they agree on the basis used for the intermediate space; see [[复合映射的表示矩阵|matrix representations of composition]]. These are consequences of keeping track of what each coordinate column means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个矩阵改变的是描述，不是向量。对 $V\to V$ 的映射，两端各用同一组基、再比较不同选基的结果，会得到[[相似矩阵|相似关系]]。连续施加两个映射时，只有两张矩阵对中间空间使用同一组基，才能直接相乘，见[[复合映射的表示矩阵]]。这些规则都来自对每个坐标列含义的追踪。
