# Connectedness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连通性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The set $(-1,0)\cup(0,1)$ has a left part and a right part, with the joining point missing. Within this set, each point has a small neighbourhood that stays on its own side. These two nonempty sides cover the whole set without overlapping. **Connectedness** rules out a split of this kind. The precise condition uses open sets, so it also works for spaces that cannot be drawn as a shape.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合 $(-1,0)\cup(0,1)$ 有左、右两部分，中间连接的位置缺失了。在这个集合内部，每个点都有一个留在自己一侧的小邻域。这两个非空部分不相交，却覆盖了整个集合。**连通性**排除的正是这种分割。准确条件使用开集，因此也适用于不能画成图形的空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What counts as a separation?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样的分割才表示不连通*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[拓扑|topological space]] $X$ comes with a specified family of [[开集|open sets]]. A **separation** of $X$ consists of two sets $U,V$ that are open in $X$, are both nonempty, and satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[拓扑|拓扑空间]] $X$ 已指定哪些集合是[[开集]]。$X$ 的一个**分离**，是两个在 $X$ 中都开、都非空的集合 $U,V$，并且满足

$$U\cap V=\varnothing,\qquad U\cup V=X.$$

> [!abstract] No separation · 不存在分离
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The space $X$ is **connected** if no such separation exists; otherwise it is disconnected. A subset $A\subseteq X$ is connected when it is connected as a space with the [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]]. Thus the pieces used to split $A$ must be open **in $A$**, not necessarily in the surrounding $X$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若不存在上述分离，就称 $X$ **连通**；否则称不连通。子集 $A\subseteq X$ 连通，是指给 $A$ 配上[[子空间拓扑]]后，它作为一个空间连通。因此，分割 $A$ 的两部分必须在 **$A$ 中**开，不要求它们在外部的 $X$ 中开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each requirement matters. Allowing an empty piece would let $X=X\cup\varnothing$ disqualify every space. Allowing overlap would not separate the points into two sides. Requiring the union to be all of $X$ prevents us from ignoring the points that might join the proposed pieces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一项要求都有作用。若允许一边为空，$X=X\cup\varnothing$ 就会把所有空间都排除；若允许重叠，就没有把点分成两侧；要求并集等于整个 $X$，则防止忽略那些可能把两边连接起来的点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a subset $A$, relative openness means $U=A\cap O$ for some ambient open set $O$. For example, take $A=[-2,-1]\cup[1,2]\subseteq\mathbb R$. Its two pieces are $A\cap(-\infty,0)$ and $A\cap(0,\infty)$, so both are open in $A$. They give a separation even though the closed intervals themselves are not open in $\mathbb R$. The earlier example $(-1,0)\cup(0,1)$ is separated by the same two ambient half-lines.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对子集 $A$，相对开意味着 $U=A\cap O$，其中 $O$ 是环境开集。例如 $A=[-2,-1]\cup[1,2]\subseteq\mathbb R$，两部分分别等于 $A\cap(-\infty,0)$ 和 $A\cap(0,\infty)$，所以都在 $A$ 中开。即使两个闭区间在 $\mathbb R$ 中不开，它们仍构成 $A$ 的分离。开头的 $(-1,0)\cup(0,1)$ 也由同样的两个环境半直线分开。

> [!example] A partition need not be a separation · 划成两份未必就是分离
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> We can write $[0,1]=[0,1/2)\cup[1/2,1]$. Both pieces are nonempty and disjoint, but the second is not open in $[0,1]$: every relative neighbourhood of $1/2$ contains points just below $1/2$. Thus this partition does not prove disconnection. In fact $[0,1]$ is connected; [[实线连通集是区间|the interval theorem]] supplies the proof.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 可以写出 $[0,1]=[0,1/2)\cup[1/2,1]$。两部分虽非空且不交，第二部分却不在 $[0,1]$ 中开：$1/2$ 的每个相对邻域都含有略小于 $1/2$ 的点。因此这不是分离。事实上 $[0,1]$ 连通，证明见[[实线连通集是区间|区间的连通性定理]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An equivalent test using open-and-closed sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用既开又闭的集合检验*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A set is [[闭集|closed]] when its complement in the current space is open. If $U,V$ separate $X$, then $V=X\setminus U$, so $U$ is both open and closed. Conversely, if an open-and-closed set $U$ is neither empty nor all of $X$, its complement is nonempty and open, giving a separation. Consequently, $X$ is connected exactly when its only open-and-closed subsets are $\varnothing$ and $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合[[闭集|闭]]，指它在当前空间中的补集开。若 $U,V$ 分离 $X$，则 $V=X\setminus U$，因此 $U$ 既开又闭。反过来，若一个既开又闭的 $U$ 既非空又不等于整个 $X$，它的补集也非空且开，就得到分离。因此，$X$ 连通当且仅当既开又闭的子集只有 $\varnothing$ 和 $X$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here we include the empty space as connected: it cannot be split into two nonempty pieces. A one-point space is connected for the same reason. The statement about open-and-closed subsets remains valid for the empty space, where $X$ and $\varnothing$ are the same set; it does not require two distinct subsets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里约定空空间连通，因为无法把它分成两个非空部分。单点空间同样连通。既开又闭子集的刻画对空空间仍成立，只是此时 $X$ 与 $\varnothing$ 是同一个集合，并不要求有两个不同的子集。

## Keep the topology and the question separate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区分所用拓扑与所问性质*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Connectedness is not the same as being open or closed in a larger space. Both $(0,1)$ and $[0,1]$ are connected subsets of the usual real line. It also depends on the topology: on $\{a,b\}$ the discrete topology permits the separation $\{a\},\{b\}$, whereas the topology with only $\varnothing$ and $\{a,b\}$ permits no separation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连通不等于在更大的空间中开或闭：通常实线中的 $(0,1)$ 与 $[0,1]$ 都连通。连通性还依赖拓扑：在 $\{a,b\}$ 上，离散拓扑允许用 $\{a\},\{b\}$ 分离；若开集只有 $\varnothing$ 和 $\{a,b\}$，则不存在分离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A related but stronger requirement is **path connectedness**: every pair $x,y$ can be joined by a [[连续性|continuous map]] $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ with $\gamma(0)=x$ and $\gamma(1)=y$. Path connectedness implies connectedness, using the connectedness of $[0,1]$ and [[连续像保持连通|preservation under continuous images]]. The converse fails in general, as recorded in the cited topology source. The definition here only excludes a separation; it does not promise a connecting path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相关但更强的要求是**道路连通性**：任意两点 $x,y$ 之间，都存在[[连续性|连续映射]] $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$，满足 $\gamma(0)=x$、$\gamma(1)=y$。由 $[0,1]$ 连通以及[[连续像保持连通]]，道路连通可以推出连通；反向在一般情形下不成立，下列拓扑来源明确记录了这一区别。本卡的定义只排除分离，并没有承诺存在连接道路。
