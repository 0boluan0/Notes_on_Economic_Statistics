# 期限转换与流动性转换分别描述合同到期错配与即时变现错配，两者相关但不等价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Maturity transformation concerns contractual payment dates, while liquidity transformation concerns obtaining cash now; they are related but not equivalent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 区分什么
> **期限转换**比较负债与资产的合同到期日：资金先到期、资产后回款时，中介需要续借。**流动性转换**比较客户索取权与底层资产现在能否迅速、低损失地换成现金。一个看时间表，一个看当下变现条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Maturity transformation compares contractual payment dates: funding matures before assets repay, so it must be rolled over. Liquidity transformation compares how quickly and cheaply customer claims and underlying assets can be turned into cash now. One concerns the payment calendar; the other concerns current saleability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

十年期政府债券可能距到期很远，却因市场深厚而容易出售；三个月后到期的定制贷款可能很快最终回款，今天却几乎找不到买家。因此“期限长”不能直接替换为“流动性差”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ten-year government bond can be far from maturity yet easy to sell in a deep market. A customised loan due in three months can repay soon but be almost impossible to sell today. Long maturity is therefore not a synonym for low market liquidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种转换常同时出现：短期可撤资负债支持长期、难出售资产时，续融资失败会迫使中介在最不利的时点卖出资产。但诊断时仍应分别测量到期缺口、撤资速度、市场深度、haircut 和后备融资，否则就不知道风险从哪一环开始。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The transformations often coexist. When short, runnable liabilities fund long, hard-to-sell assets, failed rollover can force sales at the worst time. Diagnosis must still measure maturity gaps, withdrawal speed, market depth, haircuts, and backup funding separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一只基金持有十年期、每日大量交易的政府债，另一只持有三个月后到期但不能转让的贷款。谁更难应付今日赎回？
>
> **答案：** 不能只看到期日。若其他条件相同，难以转让的三个月贷款可能带来更大的当日流动性困难。
