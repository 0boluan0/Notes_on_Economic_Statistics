# Mahalanobis pseudodistance with a singular scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*奇异尺度下的马氏伪距离*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a scale matrix has a direction with zero scale, its ordinary inverse does not exist. Replacing that inverse with a [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]] makes the quadratic expression computable, but it **ignores the zero-scale directions**. Two different points can then have zero separation. This is why the result is called a *pseudodistance*, rather than the full-space [[马氏距离|Mahalanobis distance]] with a positive-definite scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尺度矩阵有零尺度方向时，普通逆矩阵不存在。换成 [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]后，二次型虽然能够计算，却会**忽略零尺度方向**。两个不同的点可能得到零距离，所以结果叫作*伪距离*，不能直接沿用正定尺度下全空间[[马氏距离]]的解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\Sigma$ be a fixed real symmetric [[半正定矩阵|positive-semidefinite matrix]] of size $p\times p$, with $p\ge1$, and suppose it is singular. “Semidefinite” allows $v^T\Sigma v=0$ for nonzero $v$. For finite points $x,y\in\mathbb R^p$, define
$$d_+(x,y)=\sqrt{(x-y)^T\Sigma^+(x-y)}.$$
The superscript $+$ denotes the pseudoinverse, not addition. On a positive eigenvalue, it acts by taking the reciprocal; on a zero eigenvalue, it stays zero. This rule follows from the [[伪逆SVD公式|SVD formula for the pseudoinverse]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\Sigma$ 是固定的 $p\times p$ 实对称[[半正定矩阵]]，$p\ge1$，并且奇异。“半正定”允许非零 $v$ 满足 $v^T\Sigma v=0$。对有限坐标点 $x,y\in\mathbb R^p$，用上式定义 $d_+$。上标 $+$ 表示伪逆，不是加法：正特征值取倒数，零特征值保留为零；这是[[伪逆SVD公式]]在这里的应用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A two-dimensional counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个两维反例

> [!example] A vertical displacement disappears · 竖直位移消失了
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let
> $$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> \Sigma^+=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$
> For $x=(0,0)^T$ and $y=(0,5)^T$, the displacement is nonzero, but $d_+(x,y)=0$. More generally,
> $$d_+(x,y)=|x_1-y_1|.$$
> Only the first coordinate is measured. Any change in the second coordinate is invisible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两点 $(0,0)^T$ 与 $(0,5)^T$ 明明不同，伪距离却为零。一般情况下，这个伪距离只剩第一坐标差的绝对值；第二坐标怎样改变都不会被测到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A genuine [[度量空间|metric]] is nonnegative and symmetric, satisfies the triangle inequality, and gives zero **only** to identical points. Here the first three properties remain, but the last one fails. A *pseudometric* permits this failure while keeping the other properties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真正的[[度量空间|度量]]非负、对称，满足三角不等式，而且**只有同一点**之间的距离才为零。这里前三条仍成立，最后一条却失败了。*伪度量*允许这种失败，同时保留其他性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the pseudoinverse retains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*伪逆保留了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $\Sigma$ has rank $r$ with $1\le r<p$. Let $Q_r$ contain its $r$ perpendicular unit [[特征对|eigenvectors with positive eigenvalues]], and let $\Lambda_r=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r)$ contain those eigenvalues. Then
$$\Sigma=Q_r\Lambda_rQ_r^T,\qquad
\Sigma^+=Q_r\Lambda_r^{-1}Q_r^T,\qquad
d_+(x,y)=\|\Lambda_r^{-1/2}Q_r^T(x-y)\|_2.$$
This map first keeps the coordinates in the retained directions, then divides them by $\sqrt{\lambda_j}$. Its output has $r$ coordinates. Since $d_+$ is ordinary Euclidean distance between these outputs, nonnegativity, symmetry and the triangle inequality follow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\Sigma$ 的秩为 $r$，$1\le r<p$，取 $Q_r$ 的各列为正特征值对应的 $r$ 个两两垂直单位[[特征对|特征向量]]，$\Lambda_r=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r)$ 记录这些正特征值。上式中的变换先保留这些方向上的坐标，再分别除以 $\sqrt{\lambda_j}$，输出只有 $r$ 个坐标。$d_+$ 就是两个输出之间的欧氏距离，因此非负性、对称性与三角不等式仍成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It gives zero precisely when $x-y$ lies in the [[零空间|nullspace]] of $\Sigma$, meaning $\Sigma(x-y)=0$. If $r=0$, then $\Sigma=0$ and its pseudoinverse is also zero, so every pair has zero pseudodistance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

伪距离为零当且仅当 $x-y$ 位于 $\Sigma$ 的[[零空间]]，即 $\Sigma(x-y)=0$。若秩 $r=0$，$\Sigma$ 与伪逆均为零，每一对点之间的伪距离都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the retained subspace $\operatorname{range}(\Sigma)$, all points are combinations of the columns of $Q_r$. No nonzero displacement there lies in the nullspace, so $d_+$ becomes a genuine metric. The same holds on any fixed translate $a+\operatorname{range}(\Sigma)$. Another option is to identify points whenever their difference lies in the nullspace: each such class becomes one point, and the distance between classes is well-defined. In the two-dimensional example, this amounts to keeping the horizontal coordinate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

限制在保留的子空间 $\operatorname{range}(\Sigma)$ 时，各点都是 $Q_r$ 列向量的组合，其中没有非零位移同时落在零空间内，所以 $d_+$ 成为真正的度量。在任一固定平移 $a+\operatorname{range}(\Sigma)$ 上也一样。另一种处理是把差向量落在零空间内的点视为同一类，每类算一个点，便能定义类之间的距离。前面的二维例子中，这相当于只保留水平坐标。

> [!warning] Zero pseudodistance does not certify a plausible observation · 零伪距离不保证观测合理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the population is confined to a lower-dimensional affine subspace, a point outside it may differ only in a discarded direction and still receive zero pseudodistance. Check membership of that subspace separately. Replacing $\Sigma$ by $\Sigma+\varepsilon I$ with $\varepsilon>0$ instead creates a new positive-definite scale; it penalizes discarded directions and is a different construction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若总体只分布在低维仿射子空间里，落在空间外的点可能只沿被丢弃的方向偏移，却仍得到零伪距离，所以必须另外检查是否位于该子空间。改用 $\Sigma+\varepsilon I$、$\varepsilon>0$，则是在建立新的正定尺度；它会惩罚这些方向，与直接使用伪逆是不同做法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[样本协方差秩边界|rank bound for sample covariance]] explains why singularity is unavoidable with too few observations relative to the number of variables. A [[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑|singular Gaussian distribution]] provides a probabilistic example of a population confined to such an affine subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[样本协方差秩边界]]说明变量多、观测少时为何必然遇到奇异性；[[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑]]则给出总体局限在这种仿射子空间内的概率例子。
