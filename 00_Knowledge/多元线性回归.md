# 多元线性回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multivariate linear regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 用同一组解释变量同时解释多个连续响应，并利用响应之间的相关结构做联合推断。
> **具体锚点：** 营销投入可能同时影响销量、利润和留存率；分别回归会重复估计同一个设计矩阵，却无法直接检验联合效果。
> **核心难点：** “多元线性回归”这里指多个响应，不等同于一个响应加多个解释变量的 multiple regression。
> **为什么重要：** 它连接普通回归、MANOVA、重复测量和降维响应。
> **继续：** 单响应 OLS 见 [[OLS 线性回归]]；组均值的特殊情形见 [[多元均值推断与 MANOVA]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It uses one set of predictors to explain several continuous responses and uses correlation among those responses for joint inference.
> **Concrete anchor:** Marketing expenditure may affect sales, profit, and retention simultaneously. Separate regressions repeat the same design but cannot directly test a joint effect.
> **Central difficulty:** Here "multivariate regression" means several responses; it is not the same as multiple regression with one response and several predictors.
> **Why it matters:** It connects ordinary regression, MANOVA, repeated-measures models, and dimension-reduced responses.
> **Continue with:** See [[OLS 线性回归|OLS linear regression]] for one response and [[MANOVA 多元方差分析|MANOVA]] for categorical-design special cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 模型与维度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model and dimensions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写作 $Y=XB+E$：Y 为 $n\times q$ 响应矩阵，X 为 $n\times k$ 设计矩阵，B 为 $k\times q$ 系数矩阵。通常假设不同行的误差独立、同一行的 q 个响应具有协方差 $\Sigma$。先标维度可防止把响应协方差和系数协方差混淆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $Y=XB+E$, where $Y$ is an $n\times q$ response matrix, $X$ an $n\times k$ design matrix, and $B$ a $k\times q$ coefficient matrix. Errors from different rows are usually assumed independent, while the $q$ responses within one row have covariance $\Sigma$. Writing dimensions first prevents confusion between response covariance and coefficient covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常用矩阵正态表示是
$$
Y\sim MN_{n\times q}(XB,I_n,\Sigma),
$$
等价于 $\operatorname{vec}(Y)\sim N(\operatorname{vec}(XB),\Sigma\otimes I_n)$。这里 $I_n$ 表示观察行独立，$\Sigma$ 表示一个对象内多个响应的共变。若行之间也相关，例如重复测量或群组数据，就需要更一般的误差结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common matrix-normal formulation is
$$
Y\sim MN_{n\times q}(XB,I_n,\Sigma),
$$
equivalently $\operatorname{vec}(Y)\sim N(\operatorname{vec}(XB),\Sigma\otimes I_n)$. Here $I_n$ encodes independence across observational rows and $\Sigma$ encodes covariance among responses within one unit. Correlated rows, as in repeated measures or clustered data, require a more general error structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小二乘估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Least-squares estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 X 满列秩，$\hat B=(X^TX)^{-1}X^TY$。由于每个响应共享 X，系数点估计等同于分别做 q 个 OLS；多元结构主要体现在联合协方差估计和对 B 的联合检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $X$ has full column rank, $\hat B=(X^TX)^{-1}X^TY$. Because every response shares $X$, coefficient point estimates equal those from $q$ separate OLS regressions. The multivariate structure enters primarily through joint covariance estimation and joint tests on $B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对目标函数 $\operatorname{tr}[(Y-XB)^T(Y-XB)]$ 对 $B$ 求导，正规方程为 $X^TX\hat B=X^TY$。在所有响应共享同一 $X$ 且行间协方差为 $I_n$ 的设定下，未知的响应协方差 $\Sigma$ 不改变点估计。但它决定系数之间的联合不确定性，因此不能在推断时忽略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating $\operatorname{tr}[(Y-XB)^T(Y-XB)]$ with respect to $B$ gives the normal equations $X^TX\hat B=X^TY$. When all responses share $X$ and row covariance is $I_n$, the unknown response covariance $\Sigma$ does not alter point estimates. It does determine joint coefficient uncertainty and cannot be ignored for inference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平方和—交叉乘积分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sums-of-squares-and-cross-products decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差矩阵 $\hat E=Y-X\hat B$ 产生误差 SSP 矩阵 $E=\hat E^T\hat E$；特定线性假设产生 H 矩阵。Wilks、Pillai 等统计量比较 H 与 E 的广义特征值，检验解释变量是否对一组响应有联合影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual matrix $\hat E=Y-X\hat B$ produces the error SSP matrix $E=\hat E^T\hat E$, while a specified linear hypothesis produces an $H$ matrix. Wilks, Pillai, and related statistics compare generalised eigenvalues of $H$ and $E$ to test whether predictors have a joint effect on a set of responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为避免记号冲突，实务中常把模型误差写为 $U$ 或 $\mathcal E$，把误差 SSP 写为 $E_{SSP}$。无论记号如何，都要分清“$n\times q$ 的残差矩阵”与“$q\times q$ 的残差交叉乘积矩阵”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To avoid notation collisions, practice often denotes model errors by $U$ or $\mathcal E$ and the error SSP by $E_{SSP}$. Whatever notation is chosen, distinguish the $n\times q$ residual matrix from the $q\times q$ residual cross-product matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 线性假设与解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Linear hypotheses and interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般假设写成 $CBA=D$，可同时约束解释变量方向和响应组合。总体显著后应检查具体响应、方向和效应量。联合显著并不表示每个系数都显著，也不自动赋予因果解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A general hypothesis is written $CBA=D$, allowing simultaneous restrictions on predictor directions and response combinations. After global significance, examine the specific outcomes, directions, and effect sizes. Joint significance does not imply that every coefficient is significant and does not automatically justify a causal interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$C$ 可选择“营销投入”那一行系数，$A=(1,-1,0)^T$ 可检验该投入对销量与利润的标准化效果是否相同。若要检验它对三个响应是否全部无效，则用相应的系数行和 $A=I_q$。先写出 $C$、$A$、$D$ 才能说清检验到底问了什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $C$ can select the coefficient row for marketing expenditure, while $A=(1,-1,0)^T$ can test whether its standardised effects on sales and profit are equal. To test whether it has no effect on any of three responses, select that coefficient row and use $A=I_q$. Writing $C$, $A$, and $D$ first makes the tested question explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：点估计与联合检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: point estimation and a joint test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两个响应为销量 $Y_1$ 与利润 $Y_2$，解释变量是广告支出 $x$，分别 OLS 得到斜率 $\hat\beta_{x1}=2.0$、$\hat\beta_{x2}=0.3$。这两个数和两次单响应回归一样。但要检验 $H_0:(\beta_{x1},\beta_{x2})=(0,0)$，必须用两个估计的协方差；若销量和利润残差强正相关，两个估计不是两份独立证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the two responses be sales $Y_1$ and profit $Y_2$, with advertising expenditure $x$ as predictor. Separate OLS fits give slopes $\hat\beta_{x1}=2.0$ and $\hat\beta_{x2}=0.3$, exactly as in two single-response regressions. But testing $H_0:(\beta_{x1},\beta_{x2})=(0,0)$ requires the covariance of the two estimates. If sales and profit residuals are strongly positively correlated, the estimates are not two independent pieces of evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查行独立、异常观测、残差协方差稳定性和设计矩阵秩。响应很多而样本少时，协方差估计不稳；可考虑降维、正则化或明确聚焦少数对比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check row independence, unusual observations, stability of residual covariance, and design-matrix rank. With many responses and few observations, covariance estimation is unstable; consider dimension reduction, regularisation, or an explicit focus on a small number of contrasts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还应检查每个响应的线性、残差分布和高杠杆观测，以及“某个对象只在多维联合上异常”的情形。若响应量纲差异很大，响应组合的解释需要事先标准化或通过 $A$ 明确单位，不能让大数值响应无意中主导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also inspect linearity, residual distributions, and leverage for each response, as well as units that are unusual only in their joint response profile. If response scales differ greatly, interpretation of response combinations requires prior standardisation or an $A$ matrix that makes units explicit; numerically large responses should not dominate accidentally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 这里的 multivariate regression 与 multiple regression 有何区别？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How does multivariate regression here differ from multiple regression?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 前者有多个响应变量，后者通常只有一个响应但有多个解释变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The former has several response variables; the latter ordinarily has one response and several predictors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么点估计可逐响应做 OLS，仍需要多元模型？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a multivariate model still needed when point estimates can be obtained by response-wise OLS?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 响应相关性不改变共享设计下的 OLS 点估计，但会改变联合标准误、检验和同时解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Response correlation does not change OLS point estimates under a shared design, but it changes joint standard errors, tests, and simultaneous interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 联合检验显著能否说明每个响应都受解释变量影响？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does a significant joint test show that the predictor affects every response?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能；它只说明某个响应线性组合存在效果，需要后续对比定位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. It establishes an effect on some linear combination of responses; follow-up contrasts are needed to locate it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明 $CBA=D$ 中 $C$ 和 $A$ 各在选择什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words what $C$ and $A$ select in $CBA=D$.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $C$ 在解释变量/系数方向上做选择或对比，$A$ 在响应变量方向上做选择或组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> $C$ selects or contrasts predictor and coefficient directions, while $A$ selects or combines response directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
