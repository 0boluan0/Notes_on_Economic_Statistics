# 偿债能力的报表外核查把债务到期安排与可动用资源及潜在义务逐项对照
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solvency checks beyond ratios match debt maturities against accessible resources and potential obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先按期间列出合同到期付款和可能触发的提前偿付，再核对可动用现金、经营回款、可提款授信和可出售资产；最后加入担保、诉讼及其他承诺的情景影响。记录金额、时间、触发条件和证据，不把所有可能资源都当确定现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
List contractual payments and potential acceleration by period, then check available cash, operating collections, drawable credit lines, and saleable assets. Add scenarios for guarantees, litigation, and other commitments. Record amounts, timing, triggers, and evidence rather than treating every potential resource as certain cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

授信100但已用60、其余40仍需满足条件，不等于随时有100；土地账面价值200也不证明本月可按200出售。担保责任则要读[[资产担保与第三方保证]]和具体[[债务合约]]，不能把担保面值无条件当作今日付款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credit line of 100 with 60 drawn and conditions on the remaining 40 is not 100 immediately available. Land with a book value of 200 is not necessarily saleable for 200 this month. Read [[资产担保与第三方保证|collateral and third-party guarantees]] and the actual [[债务合约|debt contract]] rather than treating a guarantee's face value as a payment due today.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“报表外核查”指比率之外需要查询的证据，包括[[财务报表附注]]和合同，并不宣称相关义务一定未在资产负债表确认。已确认负债与另加的情景金额要避免重复计入。结论应说明哪些资金缺口依赖续贷、资产出售或豁免条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Beyond ratios” refers to evidence in [[财务报表附注|notes]], contracts, and other sources; it does not imply every obligation is unrecognised on the balance sheet. Avoid double-counting recognised liabilities and scenario additions. State which funding gaps depend on refinancing, asset sales, or waivers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
