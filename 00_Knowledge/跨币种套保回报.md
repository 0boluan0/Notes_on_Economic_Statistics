# 跨币种回报必须换到同一计价货币且远期套保把未来即期价替换成已锁定价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cross-currency returns must be converted into one numeraire, and a forward hedge replaces the future spot conversion with a locked price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子恒等式
> 固定 $S_t$ 与 $F_t$ 为每单位外币的本币价格。把一单位本币按 $S_t$ 换成外币、获得已知外币总回报 $R_f$ 后，未套保和完全远期套保的期末本币金额分别为
> $$R_f\frac{S_{t+1}}{S_t},\qquad R_f\frac{F_t}{S_t}.$$
> 这是给定交易与报价后的现金流算术；它本身不要求两条投资路径回报相等。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix $S_t$ and $F_t$ as domestic currency per unit of foreign currency. Starting with one unit of domestic currency, converting at $S_t$, and earning a known foreign gross return $R_f$ produces terminal domestic payoffs $R_fS_{t+1}/S_t$ when unhedged and $R_fF_t/S_t$ when fully hedged forward. These are payoff identities for specified trades, not a claim that domestic and foreign investment paths must earn the same return.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推导很直接：一单位本币先换得 $1/S_t$ 单位外币；外币本息成为 $R_f/S_t$。若不套保，期末按随机的 $S_{t+1}$ 换回；若在期初按 $F_t$ 卖出恰好相同的外币本息，则期末换回价格已锁定。远期只锁住已匹配名义本金和到期日的汇率腿，不消除外币资产违约、提前现金流、基差、流动性或对手方风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivation is mechanical: one domestic unit buys $1/S_t$ foreign units, which grow to $R_f/S_t$. Without a hedge, conversion occurs at the random future spot rate $S_{t+1}$; selling exactly those foreign proceeds forward at inception uses the locked rate $F_t$. A forward locks only the matched currency leg and maturity. It does not remove default, interim-cash-flow, basis, liquidity, or counterparty risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 共享例：4% 英镑与 5% 美元

取英镑为本币、美元为外币，$S_t$ 报 GBP/USD。英镑总回报 $R_d=1.04$，美元总回报 $R_f=1.05$。若
$$
\frac{F_t}{S_t}=\frac{1.04}{1.05}\approx0.99048,
$$
美元相对即期约有 $0.95\%$ 的一年远期贴水，套保美元路径的英镑终值为 $1.05(F_t/S_t)=1.04$。未套保终值则是 $1.05(S_{t+1}/S_t)$，可能高于或低于 $1.04$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take sterling as domestic and the dollar as foreign, with $S_t$ quoted in GBP per USD. Sterling earns $R_d=1.04$ and dollars earn $R_f=1.05$. If $F_t/S_t=1.04/1.05\approx0.99048$, the dollar is at an approximately 0.95% one-year forward discount and the hedged dollar route returns $1.05(F_t/S_t)=1.04$ in sterling. The unhedged terminal value is instead $1.05(S_{t+1}/S_t)$ and can lie above or below 1.04.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么写出 $R_fF_t/S_t$ 还没有证明 CIP？
>
> **答案：** 它只算出套保外币路径的期末本币金额。还必须说明本币路径与该路径具有可比风险、期限和可执行融资条件，无套利才要求两者相等。
