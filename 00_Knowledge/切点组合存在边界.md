# 零或负归一化分母不产生通常的有限正Sharpe切点组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A zero or negative normalization denominator does not produce the usual finite positive-Sharpe tangency portfolio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用 [[切点组合闭式解]] 的标准机会集：$\Sigma$ 正定、风险资产可无限卖空、净权重和为 1，并令 $a=\mu-r_f\mathbf1\ne0$。决定能否把最优正方向归一化成全额风险基金的是 $d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}a$，不只是“矩阵可逆”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the standard opportunity set of the [[切点组合闭式解|closed-form tangency result]]: positive-definite $\Sigma$, unrestricted short sales, net risky weights summing to one, and $a=\mu-r_f\mathbf1\ne0$. Whether the optimal positive direction can be normalized into a fully invested risky fund depends on $d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}a$, not merely on matrix invertibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $d=0$ 时，$\Sigma^{-1}a$ 是非零但净投资为零的风险头寸，不能除以 $d$ 变成权重和为 1 的基金。它仍可作为无风险资产之外的多空叠加；因此“通常的切点基金未定义”不等于“加入无风险资产后不存在有效配置”。在全额风险组合中，正 Sharpe 的 Cauchy–Schwarz 上界可以被逼近，却不能在有限权重上达到：取任意 $\mathbf1^\top w_0=1$，令 $w_k=w_0+k\Sigma^{-1}a$、$k\to\infty$，便得到仍满足预算且方向逐渐逼近该上界的序列。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $d=0$, $\Sigma^{-1}a$ is a nonzero risky position with zero net investment, so division by $d$ cannot turn it into a fund whose weights sum to one. It can still be used as a long–short overlay beside the risk-free asset. Thus an undefined conventional tangency fund does not imply that efficient allocations with a risk-free asset are absent. Fully invested risky portfolios can approach the positive Cauchy–Schwarz Sharpe bound but cannot attain it at finite weights: for any $\mathbf1^\top w_0=1$, the sequence $w_k=w_0+k\Sigma^{-1}a$, $k\to\infty$, remains fully invested and approaches the bound in direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $d<0$ 时，代数归一化 $w=\Sigma^{-1}a/d$ 虽然满足预算，但把方向翻转，达到的是负的 Sharpe 下界 $-\sqrt{a^\top\Sigma^{-1}a}$，不是最大正 Sharpe。若要形成正的有效风险暴露，可对这个负 Sharpe 基金取负头寸；这与“正持有同一个最优风险基金”的通常表述不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $d<0$, the algebraic normalization $w=\Sigma^{-1}a/d$ satisfies the budget but reverses the direction. It attains the negative Sharpe lower bound $-\sqrt{a^\top\Sigma^{-1}a}$, not a positive maximum. Positive efficient risky exposure can instead require a negative holding of that negative-Sharpe fund, unlike the usual prescription of holding the optimal risky fund positively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\mu=(4\%,8\%)^\top$、$\Sigma=0.01I_2$ 对照：$\mu_{GMV}=6\%$。若 $r_f=6\%$，则 $\Sigma^{-1}a=(-2,2)^\top$，净和为零。若 $r_f=8\%$，则闭式归一化变成 $(1,0)^\top$，Sharpe 为 $-0.4$；而可行组合 $(-1,2)^\top$ 的期望收益为 12%、波动为 $\sqrt{0.05}$，Sharpe 约为 $0.1789$，直接否定把前者称为最大值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $\mu=(4\%,8\%)^\top$ and $\Sigma=0.01I_2$, giving $\mu_{GMV}=6\%$. With $r_f=6\%$, $\Sigma^{-1}a=(-2,2)^\top$ has zero net sum. With $r_f=8\%$, normalization gives $(1,0)^\top$ with Sharpe $-0.4$. Yet the feasible portfolio $(-1,2)^\top$ has expected return 12%, volatility $\sqrt{0.05}$, and Sharpe approximately $0.1789$, directly disproving the supposed maximum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述非退化讨论排除了 $a=0$：此时所有全额风险组合的期望超额收益均为零，Sharpe 也都为零，不能唯一选出正 Sharpe 切点。加入卖空、杠杆或融资约束后，机会集改变，应重新求解，不能把这里的边界机械移用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nondegenerate discussion excludes $a=0$. In that case every fully invested risky portfolio has zero expected excess return and zero Sharpe, so there is no unique positive-Sharpe tangency choice. Short-sale, leverage, or financing restrictions change the opportunity set and require a new optimization rather than mechanical reuse of these boundaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Zivot, Portfolio Theory with Matrix Algebra, PDF pp. 34–38](https://faculty.washington.edu/ezivot/econ424/portfoliotheorymatrixslides.pdf#page=34) supports undefined normalization at zero, negative Sharpe with a negative denominator, and shorting that fund. Its algebraic tangency is not treated as an unconditional maximum. Equality signs, zero-net overlays, and both counterexamples are independently derived and checked from the matrix formula.
