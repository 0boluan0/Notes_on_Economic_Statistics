# Column space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*列空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **column space** of a matrix is the set of every output the matrix can produce. Think of its columns as available building blocks. Multiply them by any allowed scalar coefficients and add them; collect every vector that can result. The column space describes what is reachable, before asking which coefficients reach it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵的**列空间**是它能够产生的全部输出组成的集合。把各列看作现成的构件：分别乘上任意允许的标量系数，再相加，收集所有可能得到的向量。列空间先回答“哪些目标可达”，再进一步讨论用哪些系数到达它们。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A line inside a larger output space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real matrix below. Its second column is twice its first, so varying two coefficients still moves along only one line:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的实矩阵。第二列是第一列的两倍，因此即使调节两个系数，输出仍只能沿一条直线变化：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&0\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Ax=\begin{pmatrix}x_1+2x_2\\0\\x_1+2x_2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every real number $t$, choosing $(x_1,x_2)=(t,0)$ produces $(t,0,t)^T$. Conversely, every displayed output has that form. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], turning the listed row into a column. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个实数 $t$，选择 $(x_1,x_2)=(t,0)$ 都能产生 $(t,0,t)^T$；反过来，上式每个输出都具有这种形式。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把所列的一行变成一列。因此

$$C(A)=\{(t,0,t)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}
=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0,1)^T\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[张成|span]] means all linear combinations. The column space is a line through the origin in $\mathbb R^3$, not the whole three-dimensional output space. It is also much larger than the set of the two listed columns: it includes zero, negative multiples and every other real multiple of $(1,0,1)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 [[张成|span]] 表示全部线性组合。列空间是 $\mathbb R^3$ 中一条过原点的直线，不是整个三维输出空间；它也远不止列出的两根列向量，还包含零、负数倍以及 $(1,0,1)^T$ 的其他所有实数倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Span, image and a solvable target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*张成、像集与可解的目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=[a_1\ \cdots\ a_n]\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$, where $m,n$ are positive integers and $\mathbb F$ is the scalar field, usually $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Each column $a_j$ lies in $\mathbb F^m$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A=[a_1\ \cdots\ a_n]\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$，其中 $m,n$ 是正整数，$\mathbb F$ 为所选标量域，通常是 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$；每一列 $a_j$ 都属于 $\mathbb F^m$。定义

> [!abstract] All linear combinations of the columns · 各列的全部线性组合
> $$\boxed{C(A)=\operatorname{span}\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}
> =\{Ax:x\in\mathbb F^n\}.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The second equality is the [[Ax的列组合解释|column-combination identity]]. The map $x\mapsto Ax$ is [[线性映射|linear]], and its image is the set of values it takes. Thus the column space is also the image, or range, of this matrix map; its ambient space is $\mathbb F^m$, while the coefficient vector belongs to $\mathbb F^n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第二个等号来自 [[Ax的列组合解释|列组合恒等式]]。映射 $x\mapsto Ax$ 是[[线性映射]]，其像集就是它实际取到的值组成的集合。因此，列空间也叫这个矩阵映射的像或值域；环境空间为 $\mathbb F^m$，而系数向量属于 $\mathbb F^n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is a [[子空间|subspace]], not merely an arbitrary collection of outputs. It contains $0=A0$. If $u=Ax$ and $v=Ay$ are two outputs and $\alpha,\beta$ are scalars, then $\alpha u+\beta v=A(\alpha x+\beta y)$ is another output. Thus the set contains zero and is closed under linear combinations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它是一个[[子空间]]，不只是任意一堆输出：首先包含 $0=A0$；如果 $u=Ax$、$v=Ay$ 是两个输出，$\alpha,\beta$ 是标量，那么 $\alpha u+\beta v=A(\alpha x+\beta y)$ 又是一个输出。因此该集合包含零，并且对线性组合封闭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a specified target $b\in\mathbb F^m$, the system $Ax=b$ is **consistent**, meaning it has at least one solution, exactly when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对给定目标 $b\in\mathbb F^m$，方程组 $Ax=b$ **相容**，意思是至少存在一个解，当且仅当

$$Ax=b\text{ has a solution}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad b\in C(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the definition read in both directions: a solution supplies combination coefficients, and membership supplies such coefficients. In the opening example, $b=(3,0,3)^T$ is reachable with $x=(3,0)^T$ or $x=(1,1)^T$, while $(3,0,4)^T$ is unreachable. Existence and uniqueness are different questions; [[方程组解分类|solution classification]] uses the [[零空间|null space]] to describe the possible differences between solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是把定义沿两个方向读：有解就有组合系数，属于列空间就能找到这样的系数。开头例子中，$b=(3,0,3)^T$ 可由 $x=(3,0)^T$ 或 $x=(1,1)^T$ 得到，而 $(3,0,4)^T$ 不可达。存在与唯一是不同问题；[[方程组解分类]]借助[[零空间]]描述不同解之间可能的差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A basis does not need every original column
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一组基不必保留原矩阵的所有列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns always span $C(A)$, but they may repeat information. A [[基|basis]] must both span the space and be [[线性无关|linearly independent]], meaning no nontrivial coefficient combination is zero. From a finite column list, retain a column whenever it is not in the span of those already retained. Each addition preserves independence; every discarded column was already generated, so the final list still spans $C(A)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各列总能张成 $C(A)$，但其中可能有重复信息。一组[[基]]既要张成这个空间，又要[[线性无关]]，即只有全零系数才能组合出零。从有限列列表中依次选取尚未被已选列张成的列：每次加入都保持无关，而被丢下的列早已能由选中列生成，所以最终仍张成整个 $C(A)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, the single column $(1,0,1)^T$ is a basis; both original columns together are dependent. A basis need not even consist of original columns: $(3,0,3)^T$ alone is another basis of the same line. On the other hand, an independent selection is not automatically enough: for the $2\times2$ identity matrix, its first column alone is independent but does not span its column space $\mathbb R^2$. The number of vectors in a basis is the dimension, here called the [[矩阵秩|rank]] of $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在例子里，单独的 $(1,0,1)^T$ 就是一组基，两根原列一起则相关。基甚至不必由原列组成：单独的 $(3,0,3)^T$ 也是同一直线的一组基。另一方面，仅仅选到无关列还不够：对 $2\times2$ 单位矩阵，只取第一列虽无关，却不能张成其列空间 $\mathbb R^2$。基所含向量数就是维数，在这里称为 $A$ 的[[矩阵秩|秩]]。

## What row reduction preserves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Invertible row operations preserve the relations among the columns, but need not preserve their span as the same subset of $\mathbb F^m$. In the example, subtracting the first row from the third produces its [[行最简形|reduced row echelon form]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可逆行操作保留各列之间的线性关系，却不保证它们张成的空间仍是 $\mathbb F^m$ 中的同一个子集。在例子里，用第三行减第一行，得到其[[行最简形]]：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&0\\1&2\end{pmatrix}
\longrightarrow R=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now $C(R)$ is the line through $(1,0,0)^T$, whereas $C(A)$ is the line through $(1,0,1)^T$. Both have dimension one, but they are different lines. In general, if $R=EA$ with [[逆矩阵|invertible]] row-operation matrix $E$, then $C(R)=E(C(A))$. Also $Rz=0$ if and only if $Az=0$, so the same coefficient relations remain valid. This is why [[RREF读取列空间基|pivot positions found in the reduced matrix select columns from the original matrix]] when constructing a basis for $C(A)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在 $C(R)$ 是 $(1,0,0)^T$ 所在的直线，而 $C(A)$ 是 $(1,0,1)^T$ 所在的直线；维数同为一，直线却不同。一般地，若 $R=EA$，行操作矩阵 $E$ [[逆矩阵|可逆]]，则 $C(R)=E(C(A))$；同时 $Rz=0$ 当且仅当 $Az=0$，所以相同的系数关系仍然成立。这就是构造 $C(A)$ 的基时，为什么要[[RREF读取列空间基|依据化简后的主元位置，返回原矩阵选列]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the zero matrix, $C(A)=\{0\}$, not the empty set; its dimension is zero and the empty list is a basis. At the other extreme, $C(A)$ may equal all of $\mathbb F^m$. The number of rows specifies the ambient space, not necessarily the dimension of the reachable part. None of these definitions requires a square matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零矩阵的列空间为 $C(A)=\{0\}$，不是空集；其维数为零，空列表就是一组基。另一端，$C(A)$ 也可能等于整个 $\mathbb F^m$。行数指定环境空间，却不一定等于可达部分的维数。这些定义都不要求矩阵为方阵。
