# 超阈值法选取高于指定阈值的观测并建模其超额分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Peaks-over-threshold modelling selects observations above a specified threshold and models their excess distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

超阈值法（POT）先选择阈值 $u$，保留满足 $X>u$ 的观测，再把超额 $X-u$ 的条件分布作为建模对象。对于 CDF 为 $F$ 的随机变量，必须有 $P(X>u)=1-F(u)>0$，才能定义超额 CDF：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Peaks-over-threshold modelling chooses a threshold $u$, retains observations satisfying $X>u$, and models the conditional law of the excess $X-u$. For a variable with CDF $F$, the excess CDF requires $P(X>u)=1-F(u)>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F_u(y)=P(X-u\le y\mid X>u)
=\frac{F(u+y)-F(u)}{1-F(u)},\qquad y\ge0.
$$

例如损失数据为 $(2,7,4,9,1,6)$、阈值为 5，超阈值观测为 $(7,9,6)$，用于拟合的超额是 $(2,4,1)$。观测等于阈值不满足这里的严格超越规则。条件超额分布不等于总体损失分布；从它恢复无条件损失概率还需要阈值超越概率，见[[POT尾概率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For losses $(2,7,4,9,1,6)$ and threshold 5, the exceedances are $(7,9,6)$ and the excesses fitted are $(2,4,1)$. An observation equal to the threshold is not included under this strict-exceedance convention. The conditional excess law is not the whole loss distribution: recovering unconditional loss probabilities also needs the threshold-exceedance probability, as explained in [[POT尾概率|POT tail probabilities]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在足够高且近似合理的阈值上，常选[[广义Pareto分布|GPD]] 拟合 $F_u$；其渐近依据是[[超阈值极限定理]]。选择了阈值这一操作本身既不证明 GPD 近似成立，也不把有时间聚集的超额自动变成[[独立同分布]]样本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a sufficiently high threshold where the approximation is appropriate, a [[广义Pareto分布|GPD]] is commonly fitted to $F_u$, motivated by the [[超阈值极限定理|threshold-excess limit theorem]]. Selecting a threshold neither proves that approximation nor automatically makes clustered excesses [[独立同分布|i.i.d.]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Martin Haugh，[Extreme Value Theory，PDF 第 18–20、23 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/QRM/EVT_MasterSlides.pdf#page=20)：核对保留超阈值观测、转换为超额及条件 CDF。数值例按严格 $X>u$ 独立筛选与相减。
- McNeil–Frey，[1999-06-19 作者稿，PDF／文内第 7、9 页](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=7)：核对超额建模条件与原始金融数据的依赖边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Haugh's notes, PDF pp. 18–20 and 23, support selecting exceedances, subtracting the threshold, and the conditional CDF. The example is independently checked using strict $X>u$ and subtraction.
- The McNeil–Frey author draft, 19 June 1999, PDF and internal pp. 7 and 9, supports the excess-model conditions and the dependence caveat for raw financial data.
