# 无风险利率必须匹配现金流的币种期限以及名义实际口径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A risk-free rate must match the cash flow's currency, horizon, and nominal or real convention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子口径
> “无风险”总是相对于指定计价单位和持有期。若要让期末实际收益等于事先约定收益，工具既不能违约，也不能在目标期前产生必须按未知利率再投资的现金流。理论上最干净的基准是与目标日期匹配、以目标币种计价的无违约零息收益率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Risk-free” is always relative to a chosen unit of account and horizon. For realized terminal return to equal the promised return, the instrument must have neither default risk nor interim cash flows that must be reinvested at an unknown rate. The clean theoretical benchmark is therefore a default-free zero-coupon yield in the cash flow's currency and matched to its date.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个项目若用英镑名义现金流估值，就要配英镑名义利率；若改用实际现金流，则利率也必须是实际口径。长期现金流用短期票据利率，会把期限结构和再投资风险藏起来。政府债券也不自动无风险：若含主权违约溢价、币种错配或通胀口径不一致，票面收益率不能直接当模型里的 $R_f$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A project valued with nominal sterling cash flows needs a nominal sterling rate; real cash flows need a real rate. Using a short bill yield for a long-dated cash flow hides the term structure and reinvestment risk. A government bond is not automatically risk-free: sovereign default premia, currency mismatch, or inconsistent inflation conventions prevent its quoted yield from being used directly as the model's $R_f$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CAPM 回归中的周期性 $R_{f,t}$ 还要与收益观测频率一致；估值中的长期必要回报则要与现金流期限和所用市场风险溢价采用相容基准。把三个月票据利率、十年期市场溢价和另一币种现金流拼在一起，并不是“保守”，而是口径混合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a CAPM return regression, periodic $R_{f,t}$ must match the return interval. In valuation, the longer-horizon required return must use a risk-free benchmark consistent with cash-flow timing and with the equity-risk-premium convention. Combining a three-month bill rate, a long-horizon market premium, and cash flows in another currency is a mismatch, not conservatism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一家墨西哥公司的现金流全部用美元预测，是否应因为公司在墨西哥而机械使用墨西哥比索国债收益率？
>
> **答案：** 不应。利率首先匹配现金流的计价币种，而不是公司注册地；美元现金流要从美元无违约基准出发，再单独处理项目真正承担的风险。
