# 净值是同一时点按明确估值口径计算的资产总额减负债总额

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net worth is total assets minus total liabilities at a given date under a specified valuation basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净值（net worth）是资产负债表的净额，而不是一件资产：先说明主体、日期、资产和负债的覆盖范围以及估值口径，再计算差额。账面净值、市场估值净值和清算后的可回收净额不应混用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net worth is a balance-sheet residual, not an individual asset. Specify the entity, date, coverage of assets and liabilities, and valuation basis before subtracting. Book net worth, market-valued net worth, and recoverable value after liquidation are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$N=A-L.$$

资产为 150、负债为 100 时，净值为 50。若其中现金仅 5，其余是难以出售的设备，净值 50 并不意味着今天能支付 50。净值也不是可以直接拍卖的[[抵押品]]；抵押针对特定资产及其担保权利，是否可执行、是否已设其他优先权都要另查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assets of 150 and liabilities of 100 give net worth of 50. If only 5 is cash and the remainder consists of hard-to-sell equipment, net worth of 50 does not mean that 50 can be paid today. Nor is net worth itself auctionable [[抵押品|collateral]]: collateral concerns particular assets and security rights, whose enforceability and prior claims require separate examination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
