# 稳定期增长依靠再投资时终值现金流必须扣除与增长和新增回报匹配的投资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When stable growth depends on reinvestment, terminal cash flow must deduct investment consistent with growth and new-capital returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给[[估值终值]]设定增长率时，也要说明靠什么实现增长。若增长来自资本再投资，既提高长期增长率、又把全部经营利润当作可分配现金，就漏掉了增长需要的投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[估值终值|terminal growth assumption]] needs an economic source. When growth requires capital reinvestment, raising long-run growth while distributing all operating profit omits the required investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用[[再投资增长关系]]的时点：年末投入当年 NOPAT 的比例 $b$，新增资本从次年起产生回报 $R$；既有业务盈利不变，稳定期参数恒定。因此 $g=bR$，$b=g/R$。设第一笔稳定期税后经营利润为 $N_{n+1}$，对应现金为 $N_{n+1}(1-b)$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the timing in [[再投资增长关系|the reinvestment-growth relation]]: a fraction $b$ of annual NOPAT is reinvested at year-end, earning $R$ from the following year; existing earnings and stable-phase parameters remain unchanged. Thus $b=g/R$, and first stable-period cash is $N_{n+1}(1-b)$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$TV_n=\frac{N_{n+1}(1-g/R)}{w-g},\qquad R>0,\quad w>g.$$

在只用经营利润支持非负再投资的简单情形，还须 $0\le g/R\le1$。例如 $N_{n+1}=100$、新增回报 $R=10\%$、资本成本 $w=8\%$：增长 3% 要再投资 30，终值为 $70/0.05=1400$；增长 4% 要再投资 40，终值为 $60/0.04=1500$。固定第一笔 NOPAT 比较时，现金分子也变了，不能只把分母减小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With nonnegative reinvestment funded solely from operating profit, also require $0\le g/R\le1$. For $N_{n+1}=100$, $R=10\%$, and $w=8\%$, 3% growth requires reinvestment of 30 and gives terminal value 1400. Growth of 4% requires 40 and gives 1500. Holding first-period NOPAT fixed does not hold distributable cash fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进一步固定 $N_{n+1}$、$R$ 和 $w$，可直接求得 $\partial TV_n/\partial g=N_{n+1}(1-w/R)/(w-g)^2$。因此，当新增回报刚好等于资本成本时，$TV_n=N_{n+1}/w$，提高增长率的好处被额外投资抵消。若提高 $g$ 同时改变第一笔利润或风险，必须一起重算；上述偏导并没有控制那些变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding $N_{n+1}$, $R$, and $w$ fixed gives $\partial TV_n/\partial g=N_{n+1}(1-w/R)/(w-g)^2$. At $R=w$, value reduces to $N_{n+1}/w$: extra investment offsets growth. Changes to first-period profit or risk require joint recalculation and are not covered by this partial derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
