# Chinese remainder theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中国剩余定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Can one integer leave remainder two when divided by three and remainder three when divided by five? Eight does. So do twenty-three and minus seven: adding or subtracting fifteen preserves both remainders. The Chinese remainder theorem explains when several independently specified remainder conditions can always be combined, and what kind of uniqueness the combined answer has.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个整数能否除以三余二、除以五余三？八可以；二十三与负七也可以，因为加减十五都不会改变这两个余数。中国剩余定理说明：什么条件下可以任意指定多个余数，再把它们合并起来；以及合并后的答案究竟在哪种意义下唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A condition $x\equiv a_i\pmod{n_i}$ uses [[同余|congruence]]: the difference $x-a_i$ must be divisible by $n_i$. Here one unknown integer $x$ must meet every condition. The moduli are **pairwise coprime** when the [[最大公因数|gcd]] of the moduli at every two different positions in the list is one. This is a condition on every pair, not just on the gcd of the whole list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件 $x\equiv a_i\pmod{n_i}$ 使用[[同余]]关系，要求 $x-a_i$ 被 $n_i$ 整除。同一个未知整数 $x$ 必须同时满足全部条件。模数**两两互素**指每两个不同位置的模数之[[最大公因数]]都是一；要求逐对检查，不只是整个列表的共同 gcd 等于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The statement and the meaning of uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定理及唯一性的含义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a finite list of $k\ge2$ moduli, each an integer greater than one. The symbol $\prod$ means multiply all entries in this list, and $i$ identifies which condition is being read. The target integers $a_i$ may be zero, negative or outside the standard remainder range; replacing each by its remainder leaves the question unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用有限个模数，个数为整数 $k\ge2$，每个模数都是大于一的整数。符号 $\prod$ 表示把列表全部项相乘，下标 $i$ 标识正在读取哪一条条件。目标整数 $a_i$ 可以为零、负数，也可以超出标准余数范围；把它换成自己的余数不会改变问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n_1,\ldots,n_k>1$ be pairwise coprime integers, with $k\ge2$, and put $M=\prod_{i=1}^k n_i$. The **Chinese Remainder Theorem** states that for any integers $a_1,\ldots,a_k$, the following simultaneous congruences have solutions, and all solutions form exactly one residue class modulo $M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n_1,\ldots,n_k>1$ 为两两互素的整数，$k\ge2$，并令 $M=\prod_{i=1}^k n_i$。**中国剩余定理**说明：对任意整数 $a_1,\ldots,a_k$，下列联立同余有解，且所有解恰好组成一个模 $M$ 的同余类。

$$
x\equiv a_i\pmod{n_i},\qquad i=1,\ldots,k.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Unique” means unique modulo $M$, not a single integer solution. Equivalently, exactly one integer with $0\le x<M$ has the specified remainders. Thus standard representatives $x$ correspond bijectively to tuples $(\operatorname{rem}(x,n_1),\ldots,\operatorname{rem}(x,n_k))$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“唯一”指模 $M$ 唯一，不是只有一个整数解。等价地，$0\le x<M$ 中恰好一个整数对应这组余数；因此标准余数 $x$ 与余数组合 $(\operatorname{rem}(x,n_1),\ldots,\operatorname{rem}(x,n_k))$ 之间是一一对应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence says that every permitted tuple of conditions has at least one integer solution. Uniqueness says that any two solutions differ by a multiple of $M$. Neither statement should be inferred from the other: we will construct a solution first and then compare arbitrary solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在性说的是：每一组允许的条件至少有一个整数解。唯一性说的是：任意两个解都相差 $M$ 的整数倍。二者不能相互代替；下面先构造一个解，再比较任意两个解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Construct coefficients that select one remainder
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*构造一次只保留一个余数的系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The construction uses a [[模逆元|modular inverse]]: a multiplier whose product with the given integer is one modulo the chosen modulus. By the [[模逆元存在条件|existence criterion]], such a multiplier is available when the two integers are coprime. For each position $i$, we seek a coefficient that is one modulo $n_i$ and zero modulo every other modulus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造需要[[模逆元]]，即乘上待求逆整数后，在指定模数下余一的乘数。按[[模逆元存在条件]]，两个整数互素时就有这样的乘数。对每个位置 $i$，我们要找一个系数，使它模 $n_i$ 余一、模其余每个模数都余零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Construct a solution.** Let $M_i=M/n_i$. Pairwise coprimality gives $\gcd(M_i,n_i)=1$: by [[唯一素因数分解|unique prime factorisation]], no prime factor of $n_i$ occurs in the product of the other moduli. Hence an inverse $u_i\equiv M_i^{-1}\pmod{n_i}$ exists. Set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**构造解。** 令 $M_i=M/n_i$。两两互素保证 $\gcd(M_i,n_i)=1$：由 [[唯一素因数分解]]，$n_i$ 的任何素因子都不可能出现在其他模数的乘积中。因此可取 $u_i\equiv M_i^{-1}\pmod{n_i}$，并设

$$
x_0=\sum_{i=1}^k a_iM_i u_i.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Modulo a fixed $n_j$, the factor $M_j u_j$ is congruent to one, while every $M_i$ with $i\ne j$ is divisible by $n_j$. Therefore $x_0\equiv a_j\pmod{n_j}$, so the construction satisfies every congruence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对固定的 $n_j$ 取模时，$M_j u_j\equiv1$，而所有 $i\ne j$ 的 $M_i$ 都被 $n_j$ 整除。因此 $x_0\equiv a_j\pmod{n_j}$，构造同时满足每一式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $M_i$ is the product of all moduli except $n_i$. If a prime divided both $M_i$ and $n_i$, unique prime factorization would put that prime in some other modulus too, contradicting pairwise coprimality. Thus the inverse used above exists. Write $E_i=M_i u_i$. Its factor $u_i$ makes $E_i\equiv1\pmod{n_i}$, while the factors already present in $M_i$ make $E_i\equiv0\pmod{n_j}$ whenever $j\ne i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $M_i$ 是除 $n_i$ 以外所有模数的乘积。若某个素数同时整除 $M_i,n_i$，唯一素因数分解就会使这个素数也出现在某个其他模数中，与两两互素矛盾，所以上式所用逆元存在。记 $E_i=M_i u_i$；乘数 $u_i$ 让 $E_i\equiv1\pmod{n_i}$，而 $M_i$ 已含的因子保证对 $j\ne i$ 有 $E_i\equiv0\pmod{n_j}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The summation sign in $x_0=\sum_i a_iE_i$ adds these $k$ terms. When checking condition $j$, every term except $a_jE_j$ becomes zero, and that term becomes $a_j$. Since $j$ was arbitrary, the same integer satisfies all $k$ conditions. Negative choices of inverse representatives are harmless: only their congruence properties are used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x_0=\sum_i a_iE_i$ 中的求和符号表示把这 $k$ 项相加。检查第 $j$ 条条件时，除了 $a_jE_j$，其他项全部变为零；剩下的一项变为 $a_j$。由于 $j$ 任取，这个整数同时满足全部 $k$ 条条件。逆元代表可以选负数，因为这里只使用其同余性质。

> [!example] Two selectors · 两个余数选择系数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For moduli three and five, take $E_1=5\cdot2=10$ and $E_2=3\cdot2=6$. Ten is one modulo three and zero modulo five; six has the opposite pair of remainders. Thus targets two and three give $x_0=2\cdot10+3\cdot6=38$, whose standard remainder modulo fifteen is eight.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 模数为三与五时，可取 $E_1=5\cdot2=10$、$E_2=3\cdot2=6$。十模三余一、模五余零；六的两个余数恰好相反。因此目标二与三给出 $x_0=2\cdot10+3\cdot6=38$，其模十五标准余数是八。

## Why every solution is in the same product class
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么所有解属于同一个乘积类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Prove uniqueness.** If $x,y$ are both solutions, every $n_i$ divides $x-y$. Pairwise coprimality means their prime factors do not overlap, so their product $M$ divides $x-y$. Conversely, adding $tM$ to a solution preserves every remainder. Thus all integer solutions are exactly $x=x_0+tM$, with $t\in\mathbb Z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**证明唯一性。** 若 $x,y$ 都满足，则每个 $n_i$ 都整除 $x-y$。因为这些模数两两互素，它们的素因子互不重叠，故乘积 $M$ 整除 $x-y$。反过来，给任一解加上 $tM$ 不改变任何一个余数，所以全部整数解正是 $x=x_0+tM$，$t\in\mathbb Z$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To make the product-divisibility step explicit, put $z=x-y$. If $z=0$, every integer modulus divides it. Otherwise factor $|z|$ into primes. Each prime power occurring in any $n_i$ occurs in $|z|$ with at least that multiplicity, since $n_i\mid z$. Pairwise coprimality ensures that no prime is required by two different $n_i$, so multiplying the moduli does not demand additional copies of the same prime. Hence $M\mid |z|$, and also $M\mid z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把“乘积整除差”这一步展开：令 $z=x-y$。若 $z=0$，每个整数模数都整除它；否则对 $|z|$ 作素因数分解。因为 $n_i\mid z$，每个 $n_i$ 中出现的素数幂，在 $|z|$ 中至少以同样次数出现。两两互素保证不同 $n_i$ 不会要求同一个素数，因此把模数相乘不会额外累积同一素数的次数。于是 $M\mid |z|$，也就有 $M\mid z$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding a multiple of $M$ preserves all conditions because every $n_i$ divides $M$. [[带余除法|Division with remainder]] then chooses exactly one representative in $0,\ldots,M-1$. Thus a finite residue tuple determines one class, containing infinitely many integer solutions; it does not determine a unique integer until a representative range is specified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于每个 $n_i$ 都整除 $M$，加上 $M$ 的整数倍不会改变任何条件。再用[[带余除法]]，就能在 $0,\ldots,M-1$ 中选出恰好一个代表。因此有限余数组合确定一个类，该类含有无穷多个整数解；指定代表范围后，才得到唯一整数。

## Three moduli and the boundary of the theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*三个模数及定理边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose $x\equiv2\pmod3$, $x\equiv3\pmod5$ and $x\equiv2\pmod7$. Here $M=105$, $(M_1,M_2,M_3)=(35,21,15)$ and $(u_1,u_2,u_3)=(2,1,1)$ work. They give $x_0=2\cdot35\cdot2+3\cdot21+2\cdot15=233\equiv23\pmod{105}$. Dividing twenty-three by three, five and seven gives remainders two, three and two, respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x\equiv2\pmod3$、$x\equiv3\pmod5$、$x\equiv2\pmod7$。$M=105$，可取 $(M_1,M_2,M_3)=(35,21,15)$ 和 $(u_1,u_2,u_3)=(2,1,1)$，得到 $x_0=2\cdot35\cdot2+3\cdot21+2\cdot15=233\equiv23\pmod{105}$。核对 23 除以 3、5、7 的余数依次为 2、3、2。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This version requires pairwise coprime moduli. Without that condition, arbitrary remainder tuples need not be solvable: $x\equiv0\pmod4$ forces $x$ to be even, whereas $x\equiv1\pmod6$ forces it to be odd. Compatibility and uniqueness for noncoprime moduli belong to the generalised CRT and require separate analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡的模数必须两两互素。若不满足，不能保证任意余数组合都有解：例如 $x\equiv0\pmod4$ 强迫 $x$ 为偶数，而 $x\equiv1\pmod6$ 强迫它为奇数。非互素模数的兼容性和唯一性属于广义 CRT，需要另行判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Noncoprime moduli do not imply that every system fails. For instance, $x\equiv0\pmod4$ and $x\equiv0\pmod6$ both hold for zero and twelve. Those two solutions are different modulo the product twenty-four, so the claimed uniqueness modulo the product would also fail without its hypothesis. This example only marks the boundary; it is not a substitute for the general noncoprime theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模数不互素，也不意味着每组条件都无解。例如 $x\equiv0\pmod4$、$x\equiv0\pmod6$ 同时允许零与十二；它们模乘积二十四并不相同，所以缺少假设时，模乘积唯一性的结论也可能失效。这个例子只标明边界，并不代替一般非互素版本的定理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition does not require prime moduli: four and nine are composite but coprime. Conversely, the list six, ten, fifteen has overall gcd one but is not pairwise coprime. The theorem uses a finite product and a finite sum; it makes no claim for infinitely many moduli. A single modulus is just one congruence, while modulus one is outside the present convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件不要求各模数都是素数：四与九虽然都是合数，却互素。反过来，六、十、十五的整体 gcd 为一，却不两两互素。定理使用有限乘积与有限求和，没有对无穷多个模数作出断言。只有一个模数时退回单条同余，而模一不在当前约定中。
