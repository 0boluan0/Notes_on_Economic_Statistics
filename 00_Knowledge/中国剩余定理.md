# 两两互素模数下的任意余数组合唯一确定一个模其乘积的同余类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For pairwise coprime moduli, every tuple of remainders determines exactly one residue class modulo their product*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n_1,\ldots,n_k>1$ 为两两互素的整数，$k\ge2$，并令 $M=\prod_{i=1}^k n_i$。**中国剩余定理**说明：对任意整数 $a_1,\ldots,a_k$，下列联立同余有解，且所有解恰好组成一个模 $M$ 的同余类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n_1,\ldots,n_k>1$ be pairwise coprime integers, with $k\ge2$, and put $M=\prod_{i=1}^k n_i$. The **Chinese Remainder Theorem** states that for any integers $a_1,\ldots,a_k$, the following simultaneous congruences have solutions, and all solutions form exactly one residue class modulo $M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x\equiv a_i\pmod{n_i},\qquad i=1,\ldots,k.
$$

“唯一”指模 $M$ 唯一，不是只有一个整数解。等价地，$0\le x<M$ 中恰好一个整数对应这组余数；因此标准余数 $x$ 与余数组合 $(\operatorname{rem}(x,n_1),\ldots,\operatorname{rem}(x,n_k))$ 之间是一一对应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Unique” means unique modulo $M$, not a single integer solution. Equivalently, exactly one integer with $0\le x<M$ has the specified remainders. Thus standard representatives $x$ correspond bijectively to tuples $(\operatorname{rem}(x,n_1),\ldots,\operatorname{rem}(x,n_k))$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**构造解。** 令 $M_i=M/n_i$。两两互素保证 $\gcd(M_i,n_i)=1$：由 [[唯一素因数分解]]，$n_i$ 的任何素因子都不可能出现在其他模数的乘积中。因此可取 $u_i\equiv M_i^{-1}\pmod{n_i}$，并设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Construct a solution.** Let $M_i=M/n_i$. Pairwise coprimality gives $\gcd(M_i,n_i)=1$: by [[唯一素因数分解|unique prime factorisation]], no prime factor of $n_i$ occurs in the product of the other moduli. Hence an inverse $u_i\equiv M_i^{-1}\pmod{n_i}$ exists. Set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x_0=\sum_{i=1}^k a_iM_i u_i.
$$

对固定的 $n_j$ 取模时，$M_j u_j\equiv1$，而所有 $i\ne j$ 的 $M_i$ 都被 $n_j$ 整除。因此 $x_0\equiv a_j\pmod{n_j}$，构造同时满足每一式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Modulo a fixed $n_j$, the factor $M_j u_j$ is congruent to one, while every $M_i$ with $i\ne j$ is divisible by $n_j$. Therefore $x_0\equiv a_j\pmod{n_j}$, so the construction satisfies every congruence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**证明唯一性。** 若 $x,y$ 都满足，则每个 $n_i$ 都整除 $x-y$。因为这些模数两两互素，它们的素因子互不重叠，故乘积 $M$ 整除 $x-y$。反过来，给任一解加上 $tM$ 不改变任何一个余数，所以全部整数解正是 $x=x_0+tM$，$t\in\mathbb Z$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Prove uniqueness.** If $x,y$ are both solutions, every $n_i$ divides $x-y$. Pairwise coprimality means their prime factors do not overlap, so their product $M$ divides $x-y$. Conversely, adding $tM$ to a solution preserves every remainder. Thus all integer solutions are exactly $x=x_0+tM$, with $t\in\mathbb Z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x\equiv2\pmod3$、$x\equiv3\pmod5$、$x\equiv2\pmod7$。$M=105$，可取 $(M_1,M_2,M_3)=(35,21,15)$ 和 $(u_1,u_2,u_3)=(2,1,1)$，得到 $x_0=2\cdot35\cdot2+3\cdot21+2\cdot15=233\equiv23\pmod{105}$。核对 23 除以 3、5、7 的余数依次为 2、3、2。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose $x\equiv2\pmod3$, $x\equiv3\pmod5$ and $x\equiv2\pmod7$. Here $M=105$, $(M_1,M_2,M_3)=(35,21,15)$ and $(u_1,u_2,u_3)=(2,1,1)$ work. They give $x_0=2\cdot35\cdot2+3\cdot21+2\cdot15=233\equiv23\pmod{105}$. Dividing twenty-three by three, five and seven gives remainders two, three and two, respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡的模数必须两两互素。若不满足，不能保证任意余数组合都有解：例如 $x\equiv0\pmod4$ 强迫 $x$ 为偶数，而 $x\equiv1\pmod6$ 强迫它为奇数。非互素模数的兼容性和唯一性属于广义 CRT，需要另行判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This version requires pairwise coprime moduli. Without that condition, arbitrary remainder tuples need not be solvable: $x\equiv0\pmod4$ forces $x$ to be even, whereas $x\equiv1\pmod6$ forces it to be odd. Compatibility and uniqueness for noncoprime moduli belong to the generalised CRT and require separate analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
