# 重要性抽样从提议分布 q 抽样，并用似然比 p/q 把加权平均恢复为目标分布 p 下的期望
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Importance sampling draws from a proposal q and uses the likelihood ratio p over q to recover an expectation under the target distribution p*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 当目标量 $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$ 的重要贡献来自 $p$ 下很少出现的区域时，可以从更常访问该区域的提议分布 $q$ 抽样，再用权重 $p/q$ 修正分布改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When important contributions to $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$ come from a region rarely visited under $p$, sample from a proposal $q$ that visits it more often and correct the change of measure with weight $p/q$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在支持条件满足时，

$$
\mu
=\int h(x)p(x)\,dx
=E_q\!\left[h(X)\frac{p(X)}{q(X)}\right].
$$

令

$$
w(x)=\frac{p(x)}{q(x)},
\qquad X_i\overset{iid}{\sim}q,
$$

普通重要性抽样估计量为

$$
\widehat\mu_{IS}
=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N h(X_i)w(X_i).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary importance sampling averages $h(X_i)p(X_i)/q(X_i)$ for iid draws from $q$. The likelihood ratio is not an optional stabiliser; it is the change-of-measure correction that restores the target integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

proposal 的选择同时涉及正确性与效率：是否覆盖目标积分由 [[重要性抽样支持条件]] 判断；覆盖正确仍不保证有限方差，见 [[重要性抽样权重方差]]。只把 $q$ “推向稀有区域”而不检查这两项，可能比直接抽样更差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proposal choice has two separate obligations. Support coverage determines whether the target identity is retained, while the weighted second moment determines variance and efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通估计量与 [[自归一化重要性抽样]] 的加权比率不是同一个有限样本对象，不能在无偏性推导中互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordinary weighted mean and the self-normalised ratio are different finite-sample estimators and cannot be interchanged in an unbiasedness argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
