# Importance sampling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重要性抽样*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Importance sampling** estimates an average under one probability model by simulating from another model and correcting each output with a weight. It is useful when ordinary simulation rarely visits outcomes that matter to the average. The simulated observations keep the new sampling distribution; the correction is in the calculation, not a claim that the observations now come from the original distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**重要性抽样**用另一个概率模型生成样本，再给输出乘上权重，估计原模型下的平均值。当普通模拟很少碰到对平均值重要的结果时，这样做可能更有效。生成的观测仍来自新抽样分布；修正发生在计算中，并不是说观测本身已经服从原分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Make a rare outcome easier to observe
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让少见结果更容易被抽到*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a simulator has two outcomes: failure with probability $0.01$ and success with probability $0.99$. We want the failure probability. Record $h=1$ for failure and $h=0$ for success; this is an [[事件指示变量|indicator]], whose [[指示变量期望|expectation is the event probability]]. Direct [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo]] averages these zeros and ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设模拟器只有失败与成功两种结果，原概率分别是 $0.01$ 与 $0.99$。我们要估计失败概率：失败记 $h=1$，成功记 $h=0$。这个量叫[[事件指示变量]]，它的[[指示变量期望|期望就是事件概率]]。直接 [[Monte Carlo估计]]平均这些零与一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Instead, deliberately draw failure half the time. Call the original probabilities $p$ and the new sampling probabilities $q$; $q$ is the proposal distribution. Since failures are now sampled $50$ times as often, each failure contributes only $1/50=0.02$ to the weighted average. The weight is original probability divided by sampling probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在刻意让抽样中一半结果是失败。原概率记为 $p$，新抽样概率记为 $q$；$q$ 叫提议分布。失败被抽到的频率变成原来的 $50$ 倍，因此每次失败只向加权平均贡献 $1/50=0.02$。权重就是原概率除以抽样概率。

| Outcome / 结果 | Target $p$ / 原概率 | Proposal $q$ / 抽样概率 | Weight $p/q$ / 权重 | Output $h\,p/q$ / 加权输出 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Failure / 失败 | $0.01$ | $0.5$ | $0.02$ | $0.02$ |
| Success / 成功 | $0.99$ | $0.5$ | $1.98$ | $0$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under $q$, the weighted output therefore has mean $0.5(0.02)+0.5(0)=0.01$, the desired answer. Its variance is $0.0001$, whereas the direct zero-one output has variance $0.01(0.99)=0.0099$. At the same fixed number of independent draws, the estimator variance is reduced by a factor of $99$. This toy model has a known answer so we can check the mechanism; a large simulator would replace the easily computed zero-one output with its actual result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $q$ 下，加权输出的均值是 $0.5(0.02)+0.5(0)=0.01$，正好恢复所求概率。它的方差为 $0.0001$，直接抽样零一输出的方差则为 $0.01(0.99)=0.0099$。相同、预先固定的独立抽样次数下，估计量方差缩小到原来的 $1/99$。这个小模型故意采用已知答案，便于看清机制；大型模拟器则用真正的模拟结果替代这个简单输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the weighting restores the target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*权重为何恢复原来的目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $p$ and $q$ be normalised [[概率密度函数|densities]] on the same continuous space, and let $h(x)$ be the quantity recorded from outcome $x$. For discrete outcomes they are [[概率质量函数|probability mass functions]], and the integrals below become sums. The target is $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$, where the subscript tells us which distribution supplies the [[期望|expectation]]. Assume $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $p,q$ 是同一连续空间上已归一化的[[概率密度函数]]，$h(x)$ 是结果 $x$ 对应的记录值。离散情形中它们是[[概率质量函数]]，下面的积分改成求和即可。目标是 $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$，下标说明这个[[期望]]按哪个分布计算。假定 $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $q$ before sampling. It must cover the parts of the space that contribute to this integral; the exact [[重要性抽样支持条件|support condition]] is explained separately. Wherever $q(x)>0$, define $w(x)=p(x)/q(x)$. Then the density used to generate the point cancels in the expectation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在抽样前选定 $q$。它必须覆盖对这个积分有贡献的区域，准确的[[重要性抽样支持条件]]另有解释。在 $q(x)>0$ 处定义 $w(x)=p(x)/q(x)$，则计算期望时，生成点所用的密度会被约掉：

$$
E_q[h(X)w(X)]
=\int_{q>0}h(x)\frac{p(x)}{q(x)}q(x)\,dx
=\int h(x)p(x)\,dx=\mu.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed positive integer $N$, generate [[独立同分布|iid]] observations $X_1,\ldots,X_N$ from $q$ and average their corrected outputs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对预先固定的正整数 $N$，从 $q$ 生成[[独立同分布|iid]]观测 $X_1,\ldots,X_N$，再平均修正后的输出：

$$\widehat\mu_{IS}=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N Z_i,\qquad Z_i=h(X_i)\frac{p(X_i)}{q(X_i)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each $Z_i$ has mean $\mu$, so [[期望线性性|linearity]] makes the average [[估计量无偏性|unbiased]]. Independence justifies the usual variance-divided-by-$N$ calculation; the expectation identity alone does not need it. A scheme that changes $q$ adaptively or stops at a data-dependent time needs its own argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $Z_i$ 的均值都是 $\mu$，因此[[期望线性性]]保证平均值[[估计量无偏性|无偏]]。独立性支持通常的“方差除以 $N$”计算；单独这个期望恒等式不需要独立性。自适应改变 $q$ 或根据数据决定停止时间的方案，需要另作论证。

> [!warning] An ordinary mean is not a normalised weighted ratio · 普通均值不是归一化加权比率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The denominator here is $N$, not the observed sum of weights. Replacing it with $\sum_i w(X_i)$ creates [[自归一化重要性抽样|self-normalised importance sampling]], a different finite-sample estimator. Ordinary importance sampling needs the actual ratio $p/q$ to be computable. Unknown normalising constants are harmless only if they cancel in that ratio; otherwise a different construction is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里的分母是 $N$，不是观察到的权重之和。改成 $\sum_i w(X_i)$ 就变成[[自归一化重要性抽样]]，它是另一个有限样本估计量。普通重要性抽样需要能算出真实的 $p/q$；未知归一化常数只有在这个比率中相消时才无妨，否则需要另一个构造。

## Correctness and precision are separate checks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*目标正确与精度是两次检查*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First check support and the weight calculation. Then check the [[重要性抽样权重方差|second moment of the weighted output]], which controls variance and the usual standard error. Oversampling a visually interesting region does not automatically improve precision. Compare [[方差缩减比较口径|cost at the same target and precision]] as well: drawing from $q$ and evaluating its density may cost more.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查支持覆盖与权重计算，再检查[[重要性抽样权重方差|加权输出的二阶矩]]，它决定方差与通常的标准误。让某个看起来重要的区域更常出现，并不自动提高精度；还应按[[方差缩减比较口径|同一目标与精度比较成本]]，因为从 $q$ 抽样、计算密度比也可能更贵。
