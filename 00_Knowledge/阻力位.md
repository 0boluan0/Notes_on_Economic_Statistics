# 阻力位是技术分析中被用于判断上涨可能停顿或回落的价格区域

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Resistance is a price zone used in technical analysis to assess where an advance might pause or reverse downward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阻力位又称压力位，是观察者根据既有价格结构选出的参考区域，用于判断价格从下方向其接近时，上涨是否可能减缓、停止或转为回落。常见参照包括先前峰位和下降[[趋势线]]。画成“压力线”不意味着价格必在该精确点转向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Resistance is a reference zone selected from existing price structure to assess whether an advance approaching from below might slow, stop or reverse. Prior peaks and falling [[趋势线|trend lines]] are common references. A drawn line does not guarantee a reversal at its exact value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如先前高点集中在119至121，可在下一次上涨接近之前把它标为候选阻力。之后可能回落，也可能突破；突破后的候选角色见[[支阻转换]]。区域宽度和突破条件须事先规定，不能看完未来行情再无限移动边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prior highs around 119–121 may define a candidate resistance zone before the next approach. Price may retreat or break through; [[支阻转换|role reversal]] describes the candidate role after a break. Zone width and breach criteria must be predefined rather than repeatedly shifted after observing future prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阻力不是价格不可越过的上界；它与判断下跌可能停顿的[[支撑位]]承担相反方向的参考职责。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Resistance is not an upper bound that price cannot cross. It is the directional counterpart of [[支撑位|support]] to a decline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
