# Rank factorization and rank-one decomposition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*秩分解与秩一展开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix of rank $r$ can be built from $r$ rank-one pieces, and fewer pieces cannot suffice. To see why, separate two tasks: choose independent column directions, then record how each original column combines those directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

秩为 $r$ 的矩阵可以由 $r$ 个秩一矩阵相加得到，而且不能更少。理由可以分成两步：先挑出独立列方向，再记录原来的每一列怎样组合这些方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a matrix whose first and third columns are independent, while the second is twice the first:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看一个矩阵：第一、第三列相互独立，第二列是第一列的两倍。

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
F=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad A=CF.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns of $C$ are the chosen directions. The columns $(1,0)^T,(2,0)^T,(0,1)^T$ of $F$ tell us how much of each direction to use for the three columns of $A$. For example, $(2,0)^T$ means “twice the first direction, zero times the second.” The [[矩阵秩|rank]] is $2$: two independent columns suffice to generate every column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$C$ 的列是选定的方向。$F$ 的列 $(1,0)^T,(2,0)^T,(0,1)^T$ 记录构造 $A$ 各列时，每个方向分别取多少。例如 $(2,0)^T$ 表示“第一方向取两倍，第二方向取零倍”。这里[[矩阵秩|秩]]为 $2$，因为两个独立列就足以生成全部列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying one column of $C$ by the corresponding row of $F$ produces a whole matrix. Adding those two matrices gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $C$ 的一列乘以 $F$ 的对应行，会得到一个完整矩阵；把这两个矩阵相加，得到

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\end{pmatrix}
+\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the first and third columns are independent, so the rank is $2$. The displayed two-term decomposition reaches the minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此例第一、第三列无关，秩为 $2$；上面的两项展开恰好达到最少项数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ of positive rank $r$, choose a [[基|basis]] $c_1,\ldots,c_r$ of its [[列空间|column space]] from its columns, and place them in $C\in\mathbb R^{m\times r}$. Write every column of $A$ in this basis; its $r$ coefficients become the corresponding column of $F\in\mathbb R^{r\times n}$. This gives a **rank factorization** $A=CF$: the inner dimension is exactly $r$, $C$ has $r$ independent columns, and $F$ has $r$ independent rows. Indeed, the positions of the chosen original columns in $F$ form an identity matrix, so a zero combination of its rows forces every coefficient to be zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正秩为 $r$ 的 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，从原列中选出[[列空间]]的一组[[基]] $c_1,\ldots,c_r$，并排组成 $C\in\mathbb R^{m\times r}$。将原来的每列写成这组基的组合，其 $r$ 个系数组成 $F\in\mathbb R^{r\times n}$ 的对应列，于是得到**秩分解** $A=CF$：中间维数恰为 $r$，$C$ 有 $r$ 个独立列，$F$ 有 $r$ 个独立行。事实上，在所选原列的位置，$F$ 对应的列组成单位矩阵；因此其行的零组合只能让所有系数为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f_i^T$ denote row $i$ of $F$. The [[矩阵乘积外积展开|column-times-row expansion]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $F$ 第 $i$ 行为 $f_i^T$。按[[矩阵乘积外积展开|列乘行的展开]]，

$$
A=CF=\sum_{i=1}^{r}c_if_i^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each $c_i$ is nonzero. At the positions of the selected basis columns, $F$ contains the columns of $I_r$, so every $f_i$ is nonzero too. Hence every term is [[秩一矩阵外积刻画|rank one]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $c_i$ 都非零。在所选基列的位置，$F$ 的对应列恰好是 $I_r$ 的列，因此每个 $f_i$ 也非零，故每一项都[[秩一矩阵外积刻画|恰为秩一]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For minimality, suppose $A=\sum_{i=1}^{k}u_iv_i^T$. Every column of $A$ lies in the [[张成|span]] of $u_1,\ldots,u_k$, which has dimension at most $k$. Thus $r\le k$. An arbitrary sum may have redundant or cancelling terms; it is the **minimum** number of rank-one terms that equals rank. The zero matrix uses an empty sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再说明为什么不能更少。若 $A=\sum_{i=1}^{k}u_iv_i^T$，则 $A$ 每一列都落在 $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ 的[[张成]]中，其维数至多为 $k$，所以 $r\le k$。随意写出的和可能包含冗余或抵消；等于秩的是秩一项的**最少**数量。零矩阵对应空和。

> [!warning] An expansion need not be minimal · 展开式未必最短
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A **rank-one expansion** means a sum of rank-one matrices; it may use too many terms. A **rank factorization** of a rank-$r$ matrix uses factors of sizes $m\times r$ and $r\times n$, both of rank $r$. Its column-times-row expansion has exactly $r$ nonzero rank-one terms and is minimal. For instance, $E_{11}=2E_{11}+(-E_{11})$ is a valid two-term rank-one expansion of a rank-one matrix, but packaging those two terms into factors would use inner dimension $2$, so it would not be a rank factorization. Here $E_{11}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **秩一展开**只要求各项是秩一矩阵，项数可能过多。秩为 $r$ 的矩阵的**秩分解**使用 $m\times r$ 与 $r\times n$ 两个秩都为 $r$ 的因子；按列乘行展开后，恰有 $r$ 个非零秩一项，并达到最少。例如 $E_{11}=2E_{11}+(-E_{11})$ 是秩一矩阵的合法两项秩一展开，但若按这两项打包成矩阵乘积，中间维数会是 $2$，因而不是秩分解。这里 $E_{11}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$。
