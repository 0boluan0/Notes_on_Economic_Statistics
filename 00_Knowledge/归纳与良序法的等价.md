# Equivalence of induction and well ordering
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*归纳与良序原理的等价性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary induction seems to give us only the preceding case, whereas strong induction gives every earlier case. Why does the second method not prove more? Because we can package all earlier cases into **one statement** and apply ordinary induction to that statement. Well ordering expresses the same reasoning from the other direction: if the claim ever fails, examine its first failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
普通归纳看起来只给我们上一层，强归纳却给出全部较早情形，为什么后者并不能证明更多？因为可以把全部较早情形打包成**一条陈述**，再对这条陈述使用普通归纳。良序原理则从反方向表达同一推理：如果结论有失败的地方，就考察第一次失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the indices are the nonnegative integers $\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, with their usual arithmetic and order, and we use classical logic. A statement $P(n)$ may be true or false depending on $n$; this is a [[命题与谓词|predicate]]. The result compares proof principles on this domain, not arbitrary orders or arbitrary logical systems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里的下标是非负整数 $\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$，使用通常的运算、次序和经典逻辑。陈述 $P(n)$ 的真假随 $n$ 而定，称为[[命题与谓词|谓词]]。所比较的是这个论域上的证明原则，不是任意次序或任意逻辑系统。

> [!theorem] The three principles · 三个原则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For propositions over the nonnegative integers, ordinary induction, strong induction, and well ordering can be derived from one another, so strong induction has no additional logical proving power. They differ in presentation: ordinary induction exposes successor propagation, strong induction exposes dependence on all smaller objects, and well ordering exposes the least failure that would exist if the claim were false.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对非负整数上的命题，普通归纳、强归纳和良序法可以互相推出，因此强归纳并不具有额外的逻辑证明能力。差别在表达：普通归纳突出相邻传递，强归纳突出对全部较小对象的依赖，良序法突出“若失败就有最小失败”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The principles make different promises. [[普通数学归纳法|Ordinary induction]] uses $P(0)$ and the implication from $P(n)$ to $P(n+1)$ for every $n$. [[强数学归纳法|Strong induction]] uses $P(0)$ and an implication from all of $P(0),\ldots,P(n)$ to $P(n+1)$. The [[良序原理适用域|well-ordering principle for nonnegative integers]] says that every nonempty subset of $\mathbb N$ has a least member. We will show how to obtain each promise from the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
三个原则的承诺形式不同。[[普通数学归纳法]]使用 $P(0)$，以及对每个 $n$ 都成立的“由 $P(n)$ 推出 $P(n+1)$”；[[强数学归纳法]]使用 $P(0)$，以及“由 $P(0),\ldots,P(n)$ 全部成立推出 $P(n+1)$”；[[良序原理适用域|非负整数的良序原理]]则说，$\mathbb N$ 的每个非空子集都有最小元。下面说明这些承诺怎样相互推出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Package the earlier cases into one statement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把较早情形打包成一条陈述*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the hypotheses for a strong-induction proof are available: $P(0)$ is proved, and all cases through $n$ imply the next case for every $n\ge0$. Define a new predicate $Q$ by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
假设已经满足强归纳的前提：$P(0)$ 已证明，并且对每个 $n\ge0$，截至 $n$ 的全部情形共同推出下一层。定义新谓词 $Q$：

$$Q(n):\quad P(k)\text{ holds for every integer }k\text{ with }0\le k\le n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $Q(2)$ packages $P(0),P(1),P(2)$. It is one predicate about $2$, even though its meaning contains several claims. Each $Q(n)$ covers a finite initial segment; we have not assumed that every $P(k)$ already holds for all $k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，$Q(2)$ 打包了 $P(0),P(1),P(2)$。它是关于下标 $2$ 的一个谓词，含义却包含多项结论。每个 $Q(n)$ 只覆盖一个有限初始区间，并没有预先假设所有下标的 $P(k)$ 都成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use ordinary induction on $Q$. Its base $Q(0)$ is exactly $P(0)$. For the step, assume $Q(n)$. This supplies all the earlier cases required by the strong step, so it gives $P(n+1)$. Together with the cases already contained in $Q(n)$, we obtain $Q(n+1)$. Ordinary induction proves every $Q(n)$, and each $Q(n)$ includes $P(n)$. This derives strong induction from ordinary induction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
现在对 $Q$ 使用普通归纳。基例 $Q(0)$ 就是 $P(0)$。归纳步假设 $Q(n)$，它提供强归纳步所需的全部较早情形，因此得到 $P(n+1)$；再与 $Q(n)$ 中原有的情形合并，就得到 $Q(n+1)$。普通归纳证明所有 $Q(n)$，而每个 $Q(n)$ 都包含 $P(n)$，于是得到了强归纳。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, ordinary induction is a special case of strong induction: when all cases through $n$ are available, use only $P(n)$ to apply the ordinary step. A proof need not use every assumption it is allowed to use. Thus the two induction rules are equivalent; the strengthened predicate explains where the apparently extra information went.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
反过来，普通归纳是强归纳的特例：既然截至 $n$ 的全部情形都可以使用，就只取其中的 $P(n)$ 来应用普通归纳步。允许使用的假设不必全部用完。因此两种归纳规则等价；加强后的谓词解释了那些看似“额外”的信息去了哪里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Well ordering rules out a first failure
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*良序原理排除第一次失败*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Again assume the strong-induction hypotheses. Suppose, for a contradiction, that the claim fails somewhere, and collect its failing indices in
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
再次假设强归纳的前提已经成立。为反证，假设结论在某处失败，把失败下标收集成集合：

$$C=\{n\in\mathbb N:P(n)\text{ is false}\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By our supposition, $C$ is nonempty, so well ordering gives a least failing index $c$. The base case excludes $c=0$. Every index $k<c$ satisfies $P(k)$, since otherwise it would be a smaller member of $C$. Apply the strong step at $n=c-1$: all cases from $0$ through $c-1$ give $P(c)$. This contradicts $c\in C$. Therefore there are no failures, and strong induction follows from well ordering.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
按反设，$C$ 非空，所以良序原理给出最小失败下标 $c$。基例排除了 $c=0$。每个 $k<c$ 都满足 $P(k)$，否则它就是 $C$ 中更小的成员。现在在 $n=c-1$ 处应用强归纳步：从 $0$ 到 $c-1$ 的全部情形推出 $P(c)$，与 $c\in C$ 矛盾。因此不存在失败，由良序原理推出了强归纳。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a [[最小反例递降|least-counterexample argument]]. We did not have to produce a smaller failing object: here the smaller cases being true directly excludes the alleged least failure. The crucial facts are nonemptiness under the contradiction assumption, existence of a least element, and strict inequality for every earlier index used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这是一种[[最小反例递降|最小反例论证]]。这里无须构造更小的失败对象，而是利用全部较小情形成立，直接排除了假定的最小失败。关键是：反设下集合非空、最小元确实存在、所用较早下标都严格更小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Ordinary induction gives well ordering back
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*普通归纳也能推出良序原理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a nonempty subset $C$ of $\mathbb N$. Suppose it has no least member. We will use ordinary induction to prove a statement $D(n)$: none of $0,1,\ldots,n$ belongs to $C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
任取 $\mathbb N$ 的一个非空子集 $C$，假设它没有最小元。用普通归纳证明陈述 $D(n)$：$0,1,\ldots,n$ 都不属于 $C$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the base, $0$ cannot belong to $C$, because it would then be its least member. For the step, assume $D(n)$. If $n+1$ belonged to $C$, none of the smaller nonnegative integers could belong to $C$, by $D(n)$; hence $n+1$ would be its least member. This again contradicts the assumption. Therefore $n+1$ is absent too, proving $D(n+1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
基例中，$0$ 不可能属于 $C$，否则它就是最小元。归纳步假设 $D(n)$；若 $n+1\in C$，由 $D(n)$ 可知更小的非负整数全都不在 $C$ 中，所以 $n+1$ 就是最小元，再次违反反设。因此 $n+1$ 也不在 $C$ 中，得到了 $D(n+1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Induction now excludes every nonnegative integer from $C$, so $C$ is empty, contradicting its choice. Every nonempty $C$ must therefore have a least member. Notice that this proof never chooses a least counterexample: doing so here would assume the very principle being derived.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
归纳于是排除了 $C$ 中的每个非负整数，说明 $C$ 为空，与选定的非空条件矛盾。因此每个非空 $C$ 都有最小元。注意，这个方向的证明从未直接选取“最小反例”，否则就会预先使用正在推导的良序原理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We have proved both directions between ordinary and strong induction, well ordering implies strong induction, and ordinary induction implies well ordering. Chaining these implications connects every principle to every other one. Their equivalence does not mean that one particular proof must use all three.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
我们已经证明普通归纳与强归纳互相推出、良序原理推出强归纳、普通归纳推出良序原理。把这些蕴含连接起来，每个原则都能推出另两个。等价并不意味着某个具体证明必须把三种方法全部使用一遍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choosing a readable proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*怎样选择容易读懂的证明？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the step uses only the preceding case, ordinary induction is clearest. When the current object decomposes into arbitrary smaller sizes, strong induction is more natural. When a failing object would yield a smaller failing object, a least-counterexample proof is often shortest. The choice matches the problem's structure rather than selecting a “stronger axiom.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若递推只用上一层，普通归纳最清楚；若当前对象分解成任意较小尺寸，强归纳更自然；若能从一个失败对象制造更小失败对象，最小反例法通常最短。方法选择是在匹配问题结构，不是在选择“更强的公理”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A least-counterexample proof can also work by directly deriving the required property at the least alleged failure, as above. Choose the presentation that makes the dependency and the contradiction easiest to follow. If the original statement begins at an integer $n_0$ instead of $0$, replace the index by $n=n_0+m$ with $m\in\mathbb N$; the same equivalence then applies to that integer tail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
最小反例法也可以像上面那样，在假定的最小失败处直接推出要求的性质。应选择最容易看清依赖关系与矛盾所在的写法。若原命题从某整数 $n_0$ 而非 $0$ 开始，可令 $n=n_0+m$，其中 $m\in\mathbb N$，同一等价关系就适用于这段整数尾部。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The equivalence concerns proof principles for nonnegative-integer induction. It does not make the three presentations identical in readability, construction, or exposed information.
- Equal proving power does not waive the chosen method's obligations; its base cases, strict-smaller conditions, or well-ordering assumptions must still be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 这里的等价是针对非负整数归纳框架的证明原则；它不表示三种写法在可读性、构造方式或所暴露的信息上完全相同。
- 不能因“证明能力等价”就跳过所选方法的合法性条件；每一种仍需满足自己的基例、较小性或良序条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $P(n)$ defined by “$n\ge1$” satisfies every successor implication $P(n)\Rightarrow P(n+1)$ but fails at $0$. Switching from ordinary to strong induction does not repair the missing base. Nor does a nonempty set of real numbers necessarily have a least element: $(0,1)$ has none. The domain conditions do real work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，令 $P(n)$ 为“$n\ge1$”，则每个相邻蕴含 $P(n)\Rightarrow P(n+1)$ 都成立，但 $P(0)$ 为假。从普通归纳换成强归纳，也不能修复缺失的基例。非空实数集合也不一定有最小元，例如 $(0,1)$ 就没有。论域条件在推理中确实不可省。

> [!question]- Repackage the hypothesis · 重新打包假设
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A recurrence uses both $P(n-2)$ and $P(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)$. Does choosing strong induction mean ordinary induction cannot prove the claim? Suppose these dependencies apply for $n\ge2$ and $P(0),P(1)$ have been proved. **Answer:** No. The principles have equivalent proving power. Strong induction simply makes every smaller case directly available and therefore matches this dependency pattern more naturally. To see the ordinary-induction version, use $Q(m)$ meaning that every $P(k)$ with $0\le k\le m$ holds. For a target $n\ge2$, both required indices lie between $0$ and $n-1$, so $Q(n-1)$ supplies them. Strong induction writes that information directly; ordinary induction packages it in $Q$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个问题的递推会同时调用 $P(n-2)$ 和 $P(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)$。使用强归纳是否意味着普通归纳无法证明它？假设这组依赖用于 $n\ge2$，且 $P(0),P(1)$ 已证明。**答案：** 不意味着。两种原则证明能力等价；强归纳只是把所有较小情形直接放进假设，更贴合这个递推结构，写起来通常更自然。要写成普通归纳，令 $Q(m)$ 表示 $0\le k\le m$ 的所有 $P(k)$ 均成立。对目标 $n\ge2$，两个所需下标都位于 $0$ 到 $n-1$，因此 $Q(n-1)$ 提供它们；强归纳直接写出这些信息，普通归纳则把它们打包在 $Q$ 中。
