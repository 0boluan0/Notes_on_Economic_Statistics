# 普通归纳强归纳与良序法证明能力等价但呈现结构不同
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ordinary induction, strong induction, and well ordering have equivalent proving power but expose different structures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子关系
> 对非负整数上的命题，普通归纳、强归纳和良序法可以互相推出，因此强归纳并不具有额外的逻辑证明能力。差别在表达：普通归纳突出相邻传递，强归纳突出对全部较小对象的依赖，良序法突出“若失败就有最小失败”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For propositions over the nonnegative integers, ordinary induction, strong induction, and well ordering can be derived from one another, so strong induction has no additional logical proving power. They differ in presentation: ordinary induction exposes successor propagation, strong induction exposes dependence on all smaller objects, and well ordering exposes the least failure that would exist if the claim were false.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若递推只用上一层，普通归纳最清楚；若当前对象分解成任意较小尺寸，强归纳更自然；若能从一个失败对象制造更小失败对象，最小反例法通常最短。方法选择是在匹配问题结构，不是在选择“更强的公理”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the step uses only the preceding case, ordinary induction is clearest. When the current object decomposes into arbitrary smaller sizes, strong induction is more natural. When a failing object would yield a smaller failing object, a least-counterexample proof is often shortest. The choice matches the problem's structure rather than selecting a “stronger axiom.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 这里的等价是针对非负整数归纳框架的证明原则；它不表示三种写法在可读性、构造方式或所暴露的信息上完全相同。
- 不能因“证明能力等价”就跳过所选方法的合法性条件；每一种仍需满足自己的基例、较小性或良序条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** The equivalence concerns proof principles for nonnegative-integer induction. It does not make the three presentations identical in readability, construction, or exposed information.<br>
**2.** Equal proving power does not waive the chosen method's obligations; its base cases, strict-smaller conditions, or well-ordering assumptions must still be checked.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 一个问题的递推会同时调用 $P(n-2)$ 和 $P(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)$。使用强归纳是否意味着普通归纳无法证明它？
>
> **答案：** 不意味着。两种原则证明能力等价；强归纳只是把所有较小情形直接放进假设，更贴合这个递推结构，写起来通常更自然。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A recurrence uses both $P(n-2)$ and $P(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)$. Does choosing strong induction mean ordinary induction cannot prove the claim?
>
> **Answer:** No. The principles have equivalent proving power. Strong induction simply makes every smaller case directly available and therefore matches this dependency pattern more naturally.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
