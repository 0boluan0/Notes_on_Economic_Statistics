# 债券净价是不含应计利息的债券价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A bond's clean price excludes accrued interest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券净价（clean price）是把[[债券应计利息]]排除在外的价格。在普通名义债券的同一报价口径下，$P_{\rm clean}=P_{\rm dirty}-AI$。常见债券市场用净价报价，使读者能把报价与随付息日积累的利息分开看；实际屏幕是否采用净价仍须确认。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond's clean price excludes [[债券应计利息|accrued interest]]. Under one consistent quotation basis for an ordinary nominal bond, $P_{\rm clean}=P_{\rm dirty}-AI$. Many bond markets quote clean prices to separate price from interest accruing toward the coupon date; the convention of a particular display must still be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若报价以每 $100$ 面值计，净价 $98$ 并不表示整笔交易只付 $98$。例如持仓面值 $10{,}000$，每 $100$ 面值的净价 $98$、应计利息 $1$，不含费用的结算额为 $(98+1)\times10{,}000/100=9{,}900$。$98$ 是净价报价，$9{,}900$ 是按持仓规模换算后的全价金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quote of 98 per 100 face value does not mean the whole transaction costs 98. With face value 10,000, a clean quote of 98, and accrued interest of 1 per 100, the settlement amount before fees is $(98+1)\times10{,}000/100=9{,}900$. The 98 is the clean quote; 9,900 is the dirty-price amount scaled to the holding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净价去掉的是应计利息，不是违约、流动性或利率风险；也不能因为名字中有“净”就认为它已经扣除了手续费和税。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Clean” removes accrued interest, not default, liquidity, or interest-rate risk. Nor does it imply that fees and taxes have been deducted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [DMO Glossary，Clean price、Dirty price 与 Nominal amount/value](https://www.dmo.gov.uk/help/glossary/)：支持净价不含应计利息及名义金额口径；本页限普通名义债券，不套用指数债真实净价的特殊报价。
- [DMO FAQs，What is my gilt worth?](https://www.dmo.gov.uk/help/faqs/)：支持按“名义持仓乘全价／100”转换为持仓价值；$9{,}900$ 为自拟结算对照。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- DMO's glossary supports clean price excluding accrued interest and the nominal-amount basis. This page concerns ordinary nominal bonds, not special real-price quotations for index-linked bonds.
- DMO's FAQs support scaling a per-100 dirty quote by the nominal holding. The 9,900 settlement example is an original calculation.
