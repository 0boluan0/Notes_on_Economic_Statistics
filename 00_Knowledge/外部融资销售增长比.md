# 外部融资销售增长比是外部融资需求与新增销售额之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The external-financing-to-incremental-sales ratio divides required external financing by the increase in sales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个比率问的是：在**某个给定增长计划**下，每增加一元销售，对应多少外部融资需求。分母是新增销售额 $\Delta S$，不是预计总销售，也不是百分数形式的增长率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio asks how much external financing accompanies each additional unit of sales under a specified growth plan. Its denominator is incremental sales $\Delta S$, not total forecast sales or the percentage growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若可动用金融资产为零、经营资产收入比 $a$ 与经营负债收入比 $l$ 稳定，净利率 $m$、留存率 $b$ 固定，且 $S_1=S_0(1+g)$、$S_0>0$、$g\ne0$，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With no available financial assets, fixed operating-asset/liability ratios $a,l$, fixed margin $m$ and retention $b$, and $S_1=S_0(1+g)$ with $S_0>0$, $g\ne0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{EFN}{\Delta S}=a-l-mb\frac{1+g}{g}.$$

最后一项不是简单的 $mb$，因为留存利润来自**整年的预计销售 $S_1$**，而不是只来自销售增量。例如 $S_0=3000$、$S_1=4000$、$a-l=0.605$、$m=4.5\%$、$b=70\%$，融资需求为 $605-126=479$，比率为 $479/1000=47.9\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last term is not merely $mb$: retained income comes from all forecast sales $S_1$, not just the increment. With sales rising from 3,000 to 4,000, $a-l=0.605$, margin 4.5%, and retention 70%, the funding gap is $605-126=479$, or 47.9% of incremental sales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样经营比例下，若销售只增至 3,500，则融资需求为 $0.605\times500-0.0315\times3500=192.25$，比率是 38.45%。因此 47.9% 不能当成所有增长方案通用的固定融资比例，也不是严格意义上的边际融资需求；边际变化见[[融资需求敏感性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If sales instead rise to 3,500 under the same ratios, EFN is 192.25, or 38.45% of incremental sales. The earlier 47.9% is neither a universal financing fraction nor the marginal funding requirement; see [[融资需求敏感性|financing sensitivity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零增长时该比率未定义，但[[外部融资需求|资金金额公式]]仍可用。销售下降时分母为负，比率符号也可能掩盖实际资金剩余或缺口，此时优先报告金额。计算过程保留精度，避免先把 $500/3000$ 舍入为 16.7% 再代回产生偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At zero growth the ratio is undefined, but the [[外部融资需求|funding-level equation]] remains usable. Declining sales produce a negative denominator, which can obscure the meaning of the sign; report the funding amount first. Retain precision instead of rounding $500/3000$ to 16.7% before substitution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
