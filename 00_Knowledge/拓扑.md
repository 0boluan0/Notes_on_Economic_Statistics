# Topology
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拓扑*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A distance tells us how far apart two points are. A **topology** retains a different part of that information: which subsets count as open. It gives a consistent language for neighbourhoods and continuity without requiring numerical distances. To specify a topology, we must describe a family of subsets, rather than a single subset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

距离告诉我们两点相隔多远；**拓扑**保留的是另一层信息：哪些子集算作开集。它提供一致的邻域与连续性语言，却不要求数值距离。指定拓扑需要描述一族子集，而不是仅给出一个子集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the usual real line, $(0,1)$ is one [[开集|open set]], while the usual topology is the family of **all** sets that pass the local-ball test. Calling that whole family $\tau$ separates two levels: $x\in U$ says a point belongs to a set; $U\in\tau$ says the set is one of the chosen open sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在通常实数线上，$(0,1)$ 是一个[[开集]]，通常拓扑则收集**全部**通过局部开球检验的集合。把这整个族记为 $\tau$，就能区分两层关系：$x\in U$ 表示点属于集合；$U\in\tau$ 表示这个集合是所选开集之一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three rules for the family of open sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*开集族的三条规则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ be any set. Write $\mathcal P(X)$ for its power set, meaning the collection of all subsets of $X$. A proposed topology is a family $\tau\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$. For example, if $X=\{a,b\}$, then $\mathcal P(X)=\{\varnothing,\{a\},\{b\},X\}$. The symbol $\varnothing$ means the empty set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X$ 为任意集合，$\mathcal P(X)$ 是它的幂集，即全部子集构成的集合。候选拓扑是一族子集 $\tau\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$。例如 $X=\{a,b\}$ 时，$\mathcal P(X)=\{\varnothing,\{a\},\{b\},X\}$，其中 $\varnothing$ 表示空集。

> [!important] Topology axioms · 拓扑公理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The family $\tau$ must contain $\varnothing$ and $X$, contain the union of every subfamily of its members, and contain the intersection of every finite subfamily. With $I$ an arbitrary index set and $m\ge1$ a finite integer, the last two requirements read
> $$U_i\in\tau\ (i\in I)\ \Longrightarrow\ \bigcup_{i\in I}U_i\in\tau,$$
> $$U_1,\ldots,U_m\in\tau\ \Longrightarrow\ \bigcap_{j=1}^{m}U_j\in\tau.$$
> The pair $(X,\tau)$ is a topological space; the members of $\tau$ are its open sets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\tau$ 必须包含空集与全集；任取其中一族成员，它们的并仍在 $\tau$ 中；任取有限个成员，它们的交仍在 $\tau$ 中。$I$ 是任意指标集，$m\ge1$ 是有限整数。“并”保留至少属于一个成员的点，“交”保留属于每个成员的点。配对 $(X,\tau)$ 称为拓扑空间，$\tau$ 中的成员称为开集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Arbitrary” includes empty, finite, countably infinite and uncountable families. The union of an empty family is $\varnothing$; the intersection of an empty family of subsets of $X$ is taken to be $X$. Thus those empty-family cases agree with the first axiom. Nothing here asks that complements of open sets be open: open complements define [[闭集|closed sets]] instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“任意”包括空族、有限族、可数无穷族和不可数族。空族的并为 $\varnothing$；$X$ 的子集所构成的空族，其交约定为 $X$，与第一条一致。公理没有要求开集的补集仍开；补集为开的集合另称[[闭集]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why metric open sets satisfy these rules
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*度量开集为什么满足这些规则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$, let $\tau_d$ contain precisely the sets satisfying the [[开集|ball test]]. The empty set passes vacuously, and $X$ contains every ball. If $x$ belongs to a union of open sets, it belongs to at least one member; a ball already inside that member stays inside the union. This works however many sets are being united.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$ 中，令 $\tau_d$ 收集恰好通过[[开集|开球检验]]的集合。空集没有失败点，$X$ 包含每个球。若 $x$ 在一族开集的并中，它至少属于其中一个成员；已经留在该成员内的球，也留在并集内。无论取多少个集合的并都适用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an intersection of $m$ open sets, a point $x$ in the intersection has a positive permitted radius $r_j$ in each member. The minimum $r=\min\{r_1,\ldots,r_m\}$ is still positive, and $B_r(x)$ lies in every member. This proves that $\tau_d$ is a topology. It also explains why the finite intersection rule is stronger than a drawing intuition: one common positive radius must work for every member of the finite list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $m$ 个开集的交，交中的点 $x$ 在每个成员内都有正的可用半径 $r_j$。取 $r=\min\{r_1,\ldots,r_m\}>0$，球 $B_r(x)$ 就同时留在所有成员内。这证明 $\tau_d$ 是拓扑，也说明有限交的理由：要找出同一个正半径，同时适用于有限列表中的每一项。

> [!example] An infinite intersection can lose every positive radius · 无限交可能失去所有正半径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the usual real line, every $U_n=(-1/n,1/n)$ is open, but
> $$\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}U_n=\{0\}.$$
> Zero belongs to all the intervals. If $x\ne0$, choose an integer $n>1/|x|$; then $|x|>1/n$, so $x\notin U_n$. The intersection is a singleton and is not open. A shrinking list of positive radii need not have a positive lower bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 通常实数线上，每个 $U_n=(-1/n,1/n)$ 都开，但它们的无限交为 $\{0\}$。零属于全部区间；若 $x\ne0$，取整数 $n>1/|x|$ 就能把它排除。单点集不开。无限多个正半径不断缩小时，不一定还有共同的正下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A topology can exist without a metric
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拓扑可以不来自度量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $X=\{a,b\}$, consider $\tau=\{\varnothing,\{a\},X\}$. These three sets are nested, so every union and every nonempty finite intersection remains on the list; the empty cases also work. This is a topology. The set $\{a\}$ is open, but $\{b\}$ is not. Every open neighbourhood of $b$ contains $a$, while $a$ has the smaller open neighbourhood $\{a\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $X=\{a,b\}$ 上，取 $\tau=\{\varnothing,\{a\},X\}$。三个集合依次包含，因此任意并和非空有限交都仍在列表内，空族情形也成立。这是拓扑，其中 $\{a\}$ 开而 $\{b\}$ 不开。$b$ 的每个开邻域都含 $a$，但 $a$ 有更小的开邻域 $\{a\}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No metric on these two distinct points can induce this topology. If their distance were $c>0$, the ball of radius $c/2$ about $b$ would be $\{b\}$, making that singleton open. This contradicts the chosen family. Thus metric spaces supply topologies, but general topology is not merely another name for distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两个不同的点上，不存在能诱导该拓扑的度量。若两点距离为 $c>0$，以 $b$ 为中心、半径 $c/2$ 的球就是 $\{b\}$，使这个单点集成为开集，与所选开集族矛盾。因此度量能够产生拓扑，一般拓扑却不只是距离的另一个名称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even when a topology comes from a metric, it does not determine the numerical distances. On $\mathbb R$, the metrics $|x-y|$ and $2|x-y|$ give the same open sets: a ball of radius $r$ for the second rule is a ball of radius $r/2$ for the first. [[拓扑等价度量|Topologically equivalent metrics]] develops that distinction. Topology retains enough structure to define [[连续性|continuity]] through open-set preimages and to form [[子空间拓扑|relative topologies]] on subsets; it does not retain a particular scale of length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使拓扑来自度量，它也不决定数值距离。实数上的 $|x-y|$ 与 $2|x-y|$ 产生相同开集：第二种规则下半径 $r$ 的球，是第一种规则下半径 $r/2$ 的球，见[[拓扑等价度量]]。拓扑仍足以用开集原像定义[[连续性]]，并给子集构造[[子空间拓扑]]，但不保留特定长度尺度。
