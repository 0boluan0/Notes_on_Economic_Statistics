# 二分在精确算术中每轮把括区间宽度减半，因此完成 $n$ 次区间更新后的中点到区间内某个根的距离至多为初始宽度的 $2^{-(n+1)}$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In exact arithmetic, bisection halves the bracket width on every update, so the midpoint after $n$ completed updates is at most the initial width times $2^{-(n+1)}$ from a bracketed root*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子误差证书
> 在精确算术中，只要 [[二分求根]] 的连续性与异号括区间不变量成立，当前区间就至少含一个根。完成 $n$ 次对半更新后，区间宽度为 $L_n=L_0/2^n$；返回其中点时，位置误差至多为半个当前宽度，即 $L_0/2^{n+1}$。这是**位置误差界**，不是对残差 $|f(m_n)|$ 的界。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In exact arithmetic, provided that the continuity and sign-changing-bracket invariant of [[二分求根|bisection]] hold, the current interval contains at least one root. After $n$ completed halvings its width is $L_n=L_0/2^n$, and the returned midpoint is at most half that width, $L_0/2^{n+1}$, from a bracketed root. This is a **positional-error bound**, not a bound on the residual $|f(m_n)|$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先固定计数约定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fix the Iteration Convention First*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令初始括区间为 $[a_0,b_0]$，初始宽度为

$$
L_0=b_0-a_0>0.
$$

本文约定 $n$ 表示**已经完成的区间更新次数**：$n=0$ 时仍是初始区间；完成 $n$ 次更新后得到 $[a_n,b_n]$，并准备返回其中点

$$
m_n=\frac{a_n+b_n}{2}.
$$

若代码或教材把第一次取中点记作“第 1 轮”，公式的下标会整体平移；先声明计数约定，才能避免差一轮错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the initial bracket be $[a_0,b_0]$ with width $L_0=b_0-a_0>0$. Here $n$ counts **completed bracket updates**: $n=0$ still denotes the initial bracket; after $n$ updates the interval is $[a_n,b_n]$ and its midpoint is $m_n=(a_n+b_n)/2$. A text or program that calls the first midpoint evaluation “iteration 1” shifts the subscripts by one, so the counting convention must be stated before using the formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 宽度递推直接给出位置误差界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Width Recurrence Directly Certifies Positional Error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在精确算术中，每次更新恰好保留当前区间的一半，所以

$$
L_{n+1}=\frac{L_n}{2},
\qquad
L_n=b_n-a_n=\frac{L_0}{2^n}.
$$

括区间不变量保证 $[a_n,b_n]$ 内至少有一个根。取其中任意一个被括住的根 $r_n$，由于中点到区间内任一点的距离都不超过半个区间宽度，

$$
\boxed{
|m_n-r_n|
\le \frac{L_n}{2}
=\frac{L_0}{2^{n+1}}
}.
$$

若当前括区间内根唯一，可以把 $r_n$ 写成同一个固定根 $r$。若区间内有多个根，结论只需要“至少一个根仍被括住”，不声称算法事先指定了最终会逼近哪一个根。

浮点实现还要多一道停滞检查：当 $a_n$ 与 $b_n$ 已是相邻可表示数时，计算出的中点可能舍入成某个端点，于是机器中的区间不再严格减半。此时应按相邻浮点数、`xtol`/`rtol`、最大迭代或明确的停滞状态退出，不能继续套用精确算术递推制造不存在的新精度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In exact arithmetic, each update retains exactly one half of the current interval, so $L_{n+1}=L_n/2$ and hence $L_n=L_0/2^n$. The bracket invariant guarantees at least one root inside $[a_n,b_n]$. For any bracketed root $r_n$, the midpoint is no farther than half the interval width from any point in that interval, giving $|m_n-r_n|\le L_n/2=L_0/2^{n+1}$. If the current bracket contains a unique root, the same fixed symbol $r$ can be used throughout. With several roots, the statement certifies that a root remains enclosed; it does not predetermine which root the nested intervals will select. In floating-point arithmetic, adjacent representable endpoints may round their computed midpoint back to an endpoint. An implementation must then stop by an adjacent-float, tolerance, iteration-limit, or explicit stagnation condition instead of claiming another exact halving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 由目标位置误差反推轮数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solve Backwards from a Target Positional Error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要求返回中点与区间内某个根的距离不超过 $\varepsilon_x>0$，只需令

$$
\frac{L_0}{2^{n+1}}\le\varepsilon_x.
$$

因此最少的完整更新次数为

$$
\boxed{
n_{\min}
=
\max\!\left\{
0,
\left\lceil
\log_2\!\left(\frac{L_0}{2\varepsilon_x}\right)
\right\rceil
\right\}.
}
$$

外层的 $\max\{0,\cdot\}$ 处理“初始中点已经足够精确”的情形。若目标改成把整个括区间宽度压到 $\delta>0$，对应公式是

$$
n\ge
\max\!\left\{0,
\left\lceil\log_2\!\left(\frac{L_0}{\delta}\right)\right\rceil
\right\}.
$$

这两个式子分别控制“中点的位置误差”和“整个剩余区间宽度”，不要混用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To guarantee that the returned midpoint is within $\varepsilon_x>0$ of a bracketed root, require $L_0/2^{n+1}\le\varepsilon_x$. The minimum number of completed updates is therefore $n_{\min}=\max\{0,\lceil\log_2(L_0/(2\varepsilon_x))\rceil\}$. The outer maximum covers the case in which the initial midpoint is already accurate enough. If the goal is instead to reduce the full bracket width to $\delta>0$, use $n\ge\max\{0,\lceil\log_2(L_0/\delta)\rceil\}$. The first formula controls midpoint error; the second controls the entire remaining interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 例：从 $[1,2]$ 括住 $\sqrt2$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Example: Bracketing $\sqrt2$ on $[1,2]$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=x^2-2$，初始宽度 $L_0=1$。若要求中点位置误差不超过 $10^{-6}$，则

$$
n_{\min}
=
\left\lceil\log_2\frac{1}{2\times10^{-6}}\right\rceil
=19.
$$

完成 19 次更新后，

$$
|m_{19}-r|
\le\frac{1}{2^{20}}
\approx9.54\times10^{-7}.
$$

这是最坏情形保证；若某次中点恰好就是根，可以提前结束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2-2$ on $[1,2]$, $L_0=1$. Requiring midpoint error at most $10^{-6}$ gives $n_{\min}=\lceil\log_2(1/(2\times10^{-6}))\rceil=19$. After nineteen updates, $|m_{19}-r|\le2^{-20}\approx9.54\times10^{-7}$. This is a worst-case guarantee; an exact midpoint root permits earlier termination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 这不是残差界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*This Is Not a Residual Bound*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间宽度直接约束自变量中的距离，却没有单独约束 $|f(m_n)|$。在精确算术中，把方程缩放成

$$
g(x)=c f(x),\qquad c>0,
$$

不会改变根、端点符号关系或二分所选的半区间，却把同一点的残差放大为 $|g(m_n)|=c|f(m_n)|$。因此，不能从 $L_n$ 的公式推出一个与函数尺度无关的残差界；反过来也不能把小残差直接当作位置误差。两者的转换条件由 [[残差控制根误差]] 单独说明。

这个位置证书仍依赖 [[二分求根]] 的前提：函数在括区间上连续，端点符号信息可靠，而且每轮确实保留了含根的一半。若函数跳跃、求值产生 NaN，或浮点舍入误判符号，单纯“宽度变小”不能证明靠近零点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bracket width directly controls distance in the independent variable, but it does not by itself control $|f(m_n)|$. In exact arithmetic, rescaling the equation to $g(x)=cf(x)$ with $c>0$ leaves the roots, endpoint-sign relation, and bisection path unchanged, while multiplying every residual by $c$. Thus the width formula yields no scale-free residual bound, and a small residual cannot be read directly as positional error. [[残差控制根误差|Residual Control of Root Error]] states the additional conditions needed to convert between the two. The positional certificate still relies on bisection's premises: continuity on the bracket, trustworthy endpoint signs, and preservation of the root-containing half. A shrinking interval around a discontinuity, a NaN, or a misclassified floating-point sign is not a root certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 从宽度 $L_0=8$ 的有效括区间开始，完成 10 次更新后返回中点。最坏情形的位置误差是多少？这个数是否同时是残差上界？
>
> **答案：** $8/2^{11}=1/256\approx0.00390625$。它只界定中点到区间内某个根的距离；没有函数尺度或导数信息时，它不是 $|f(m_{10})|$ 的上界。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Starting from a valid bracket of width $8$, the midpoint after ten updates has worst-case positional error $8/2^{11}=1/256\approx0.00390625$. This bounds distance to a bracketed root only; without function-scale or derivative information it is not a bound on $|f(m_{10})|$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
