# 含截距且只有一个二元解释变量的 OLS 斜率等于两个样本组均值之差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*OLS with an intercept and one binary regressor has a slope equal to the sample mean difference*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 对 $Y_i=\alpha+\beta D_i+\varepsilon_i$，若 $D_i\in\{0,1\}$ 且两组都非空，OLS 满足 $\hat\alpha=\bar Y_0$、$\hat\beta=\bar Y_1-\bar Y_0$。这是样本代数性质，不需要因果识别假设。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With an intercept, a single binary regressor, and both groups observed, OLS fits the control mean as its intercept and the treated-minus-control sample mean as its slope. This is algebra, independent of causal identification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

把残差平方和按组写成
$$Q=\sum_{D_i=0}(Y_i-\alpha)^2+\sum_{D_i=1}(Y_i-\alpha-\beta)^2.$$
令 $m_0=\alpha,m_1=\alpha+\beta$，两组分别最小化自己的平方误差，各自解为样本均值；相减得到斜率。总体对应物与控制变量边界见[[指示变量系数]]，因果解释见[[随机分配识别]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parameterise the two fitted group means as $m_0=\alpha$ and $m_1=\alpha+\beta$. Each separate sum of squared errors is minimised at its sample mean, so their difference is the slope. See [[指示变量系数|indicator coefficients]] for the population and controlled-regression interpretations, and [[随机分配识别|random assignment]] for causality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
