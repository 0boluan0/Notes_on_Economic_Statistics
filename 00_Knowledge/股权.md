# 股权是公司所有权份额所对应的收益分配及公司治理权利

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equity is an ownership interest in a company carrying distribution and governance rights under its terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股权表示持有人拥有公司的一部分，而非公司欠其一笔必须按期偿还的贷款。权利组合可以包括获分配股利、剩余财产请求权以及表决等治理权利，具体取决于股份类别和章程；不应假设所有股权都有相同投票权或分配顺序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equity represents part ownership of a company, not a loan that the company must repay on schedule. The rights may include dividends when distributed, claims on remaining assets, and voting or other governance rights, depending on the share class and governing terms. Not all equity has identical voting rights or distribution priority.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通股的剩余请求权意味着：先满足债权人及其他优先请求权后，股东才分配剩余；没有剩余时可以一无所获。持有 10% 同类普通股，在同权且按比例分配的条件下可分得该类股份分配额的 10%，却不保证公司每期必须把利润分出 10% 的现金给他。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common equity is residual: creditors and other prior claims are satisfied before common shareholders receive what remains, possibly nothing. Holding 10% of an equal-rights common-share class gives 10% of distributions to that class when distributed proportionally; it does not require the company to pay that investor 10% of accounting profit in cash every period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
