# 扩展式博弈、逆向归纳与子博弈完美均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extensive-form Games, Backward Induction, and Subgame-perfect Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在有行动顺序、观察限制和路径外威胁的互动中，区分形式上的 Nash 均衡与真正可信的序贯行为。
> **具体锚点：** 垄断者威胁“进入者一进入就打价格战”，若进入后打价格战反而伤害自己，这个威胁不能阻止理性的进入。
> **核心难点：** 策略必须覆盖路径外信息集，子博弈不能切断信息集；SPE 要求每个子博弈中行为都构成 Nash 均衡。
> **为什么重要：** 承诺、声誉、信号、谈判和动态机制的结论都取决于可信性。
> **继续：** 先完全信息下做逆向归纳，再进入 belief 与 PBE；私人类型基础见 [[贝叶斯博弈]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** In interactions with sequential moves, limited observation, and off-path threats, distinguish a merely formal Nash equilibrium from genuinely credible sequential behavior.
> **Concrete anchor:** A monopolist threatens to start a price war if an entrant enters. If fighting would hurt the monopolist once entry occurs, the threat cannot deter a rational entrant.
> **Central difficulty:** Strategies must cover off-path information sets, a subgame cannot cut an information set, and SPE requires Nash behavior in every subgame.
> **Why it matters:** Conclusions about commitment, reputation, signaling, bargaining, and dynamic mechanisms all depend on credibility.
> **Continue with:** First use backward induction under complete information; then move to beliefs and PBE. For private types, see [[贝叶斯博弈|Bayesian Games]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 博弈树、信息集与策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Game Trees, Information Sets, and Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

节点表示历史，边表示行动，信息集把玩家行动时无法区分的节点放在一起。同一信息集上的可选行动必须相同。完美信息意味着每个信息集只有一个节点；完全信息则是收益和结构为共同知识，两者不是同义词。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nodes represent histories, edges represent actions, and an information set groups nodes that a player cannot distinguish when acting. The feasible actions must be identical at every node within an information set. Perfect information means that every information set is a singleton; complete information means that payoffs and the game structure are common knowledge. These are not synonyms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扩展式博弈中的行动是某个节点的一次选择，而策略是覆盖玩家每一个信息集的完整应对计划。即使某信息集在候选均衡路径上不会到达，也必须规定行动，因为判断偏离和可信性需要知道路径改变后会发生什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An action in an extensive-form game is a choice at one node, whereas a strategy is a complete contingent plan covering every information set of the player. Actions must be specified even at information sets that are not reached along the candidate equilibrium path, because evaluating deviations and credibility requires knowing what would happen after the path changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 逆向归纳与可信威胁
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Backward Induction and Credible Threats*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限完美信息博弈从最后决策节点向前解。进入者知道垄断者进入后的最佳反应，因此不会被事后不愿执行的惩罚吓住。逆向归纳给的是序贯理性，而非“后行动者总占优势”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite perfect-information game is solved from the final decision nodes backward. An entrant anticipates the incumbent's best response after entry and therefore is not deterred by a punishment the incumbent would not actually carry out. Backward induction imposes sequential rationality; it does not say that the player moving later always has an advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

操作时先在每个终端前节点选出行动者的最优行动，把该子树压缩为相应收益，再逐层向前。若有收益并列，应保留所有最优分支而不是任意删掉；若博弈无限期或信息不完美，简单逆向归纳可能不适用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operationally, choose the acting player's optimal action at each node immediately preceding a terminal node, replace that subtree with the resulting payoff, and continue backward. When payoffs tie, retain all optimal branches rather than deleting one arbitrarily. Simple backward induction may not apply to infinite-horizon games or games with imperfect information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 子博弈完美均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Subgame-perfect Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

SPE 要求策略在每个子博弈中都构成 Nash 均衡，排除依赖不可信路径外行动的 Nash 均衡。子博弈必须包含某单节点信息集及其全部后继，并且不能切断任何信息集；因此不完全信息博弈可能没有非平凡子博弈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An SPE requires the strategy profile to form a Nash equilibrium in every subgame, eliminating Nash equilibria sustained by non-credible off-path actions. A subgame must begin at a singleton information set, include all its successors, and never cut an information set. Consequently, a game with imperfect information may have no proper subgames.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限完美信息博弈中，逆向归纳产生 SPE；在一般扩展式博弈中，可用 one-shot deviation principle 检查每个信息集的单次偏离，但其适用需要有限或适当贴现、完美回忆等条件。不要把“找到一个 Nash 均衡”当作已经完成动态博弈求解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a finite perfect-information game, backward induction produces an SPE. In more general extensive-form games, the one-shot deviation principle can test deviations at each information set, subject to conditions such as finite horizon or appropriate discounting and perfect recall. Finding a Nash equilibrium alone does not complete the solution of a dynamic game.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：市场进入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Market Entry*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进入者先选进入或退出。退出给双方 $(0,2)$；进入后 incumbent 选容纳或打击，分别给 $(1,1)$ 与 $(-1,-1)$。从末端看 incumbent 会容纳，因为 $1>-1$，进入者因此选择进入，因为 $1>0$。策略“退出；进入后打击”可能在策略式中依靠路径外威胁出现，但不是 SPE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An entrant first chooses Enter or Stay Out. Staying out gives payoffs $(0,2)$. After entry, the incumbent chooses Accommodate or Fight, yielding $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$ respectively. Looking backward, the incumbent accommodates because $1>-1$; anticipating this, the entrant enters because $1>0$. The profile “stay out; fight after entry” may appear in the strategic form through an off-path threat, but it is not an SPE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见错误包括：只写均衡路径上的行动而没有完整策略；把任何后继节点都称为子博弈而切断信息集；从树根向后猜而没有从末端比较；以及用行动发生的时间先后来判断“完美信息”，却没有检查玩家实际观察到什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common errors include writing only the actions on the equilibrium path instead of complete strategies; calling every successor node a subgame even when an information set is cut; guessing forward from the root rather than comparing choices from the terminal nodes; and classifying information as perfect merely from the timing of actions without checking what players actually observe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 完全信息和完美信息有什么区别？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the difference between complete and perfect information?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 完全信息是博弈结构与收益已知；完美信息是行动时知道此前所有行动，即每个信息集为单节点。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Complete information means that the game structure and payoffs are known; perfect information means that all previous actions are observed when a player moves, so every information set is a singleton.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么一个 Nash 均衡可能不是子博弈完美均衡？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a Nash equilibrium fail to be subgame perfect?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Nash 只要求从初始节点看无人愿意偏离，可能依赖某个路径外子博弈中玩家事后不会执行的行动。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Nash equilibrium only rules out profitable deviations from the initial game and may rely on an action that a player would not carry out in an off-path subgame.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么策略必须写路径外行动？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must a strategy specify off-path actions?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 偏离会把博弈带到原路径之外；不规定这些行动，就无法比较偏离收益或判断威胁是否可信。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A deviation may move the game off the original path. Without specifying actions there, one cannot compare deviation payoffs or assess whether threats are credible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
