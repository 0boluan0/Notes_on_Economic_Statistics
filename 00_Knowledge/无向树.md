# Undirected trees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*无向树*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **undirected tree** is a network that is connected and contains no cycle. Think of three vertices joined in a line, $a-b-c$: every vertex can be reached from every other one, but there is no way to go around a circuit. Adding the edge from $c$ to $a$ creates a triangle and destroys the tree property. Adding an isolated vertex $d$ also destroys it, for a different reason: the resulting graph is disconnected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**无向树**是连成一体、又不含环的网络。把三个顶点连成一条线 $a-b-c$：任意顶点都能到达其他顶点，却没有绕成一圈的路线。再加上连接 $c$ 与 $a$ 的边，就出现三角形，不再是树。另加一个孤立顶点 $d$ 也会使它不再是树，但原因不同：整个图不连通了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Connected and acyclic
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*连通且无环*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the graphs are **finite and undirected**. Write $G=(V,E)$, where $V$ is the set of vertices and $E$ is the set of edges. An [[图类型的建模边界|undirected edge]] joins two endpoints without specifying a direction. The first examples are simple graphs: there are no self-loops or parallel edges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
本卡讨论**有限无向图**。记 $G=(V,E)$，其中 $V$ 是顶点集合，$E$ 是边集合。[[图类型的建模边界|无向边]]连接两个端点，不规定行进方向。开头的例子都是简单图：没有自环，也没有平行边。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[游走路径与环|path]] follows edges without repeating vertices. The graph is [[无向图连通分量|connected]] when every pair of vertices can be joined by a path. A **cycle** is a positive-length closed route with no repeated edge and no repeated vertex except that its first and last vertex coincide. **Acyclic** means that no such cycle exists anywhere in the graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[游走路径与环|路径]]沿边行进，不重复顶点。任意两个顶点之间都有路径时，图就是[[无向图连通分量|连通的]]。**环**是一条正长度的闭合路线：不重复边，除首尾是同一顶点外也不重复顶点。**无环**表示图中任何地方都不存在这样的环。

> [!definition] Tree / 树
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite undirected graph is a **tree** if it has at least one vertex, is connected, and is acyclic. Both structural conditions concern the whole graph: connectedness alone allows a triangle, while acyclicity alone allows several disconnected pieces.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 有限无向图至少有一个顶点，且**连通、无环**时，称为**树**。两个结构条件都针对全图：只有连通条件会允许三角形；只有无环条件会允许多个互不连通的部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition does not select a root or impose a branching shape. The line $a-b-c$ is a tree just as a star is. Choosing a root is additional structure. The [[Tree 与 DAG 的区别|distinction between trees and directed acyclic graphs]] matters because a directed acyclic graph excludes only cycles that follow its arrows: assigning arrows to a triangle can eliminate directed cycles while leaving the underlying undirected triangle. Such an orientation does not turn that underlying graph into a tree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
定义不要求指定根，也不要求画成分叉的形状。一条线 $a-b-c$ 和星形图都可以是树；选根属于额外结构。[[Tree 与 DAG 的区别|树与有向无环图的区别]]在于，后者只排除沿箭头方向闭合的环：给三角形的边安排箭头，可以消除有向环，但底层无向图仍是三角形。这样的定向并不会把底层图变成树。

## Smallest cases and the meaning of a cycle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*最小情形与环的约定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single vertex with no edges **is a tree**. Its length-zero path connects it to itself, and it has no cycle. Two isolated vertices are not a tree because they cannot be joined. The graph with no vertices is excluded by the explicit nonempty requirement, regardless of how a text phrases connectedness for the empty graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
只有一个顶点、没有边的图**是树**：长度为零的路径把它连到自身，图中又没有环。两个孤立顶点不是树，因为它们之间没有路径。没有顶点的空图由“至少有一个顶点”这一条件明确排除，不依赖其他教材对空图连通性的措辞。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simple undirected graph, a cycle needs at least three edges. Going from $a$ to $b$ and immediately back along the **same edge** repeats an edge, so it is only a closed walk, not a cycle. This is why even a one-edge tree is acyclic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
简单无向图中的环至少需要三条边。从 $a$ 到 $b$，再沿**同一条边**原路返回，会重复使用边，因此只是闭游走，不是环。这也解释了为什么只有一条边的树仍然无环。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the ambient model allows self-loops or distinguishable parallel edges, we count a self-loop as a cycle of length one and two parallel edges as a cycle of length two. The latter route uses one edge to go out and a different edge to return. Under this convention, a tree cannot contain either feature; the definition therefore agrees with the simple-graph definition rather than silently ignoring these short cycles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若所在模型允许自环或可区分的平行边，本卡把自环计作长度为一的环，把两条平行边计作长度为二的环。后一种路线去程和回程使用的是两条不同的边。在这一约定下，树不可能包含这两种结构；它与简单图中的定义一致，不会默默忽略短环。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the definition is understood, the [[有限树的等价刻画|equivalent characterizations of a finite tree]] explain why every pair of vertices has a unique path. That is a useful theorem for recognizing and using trees; it does not add another condition to this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
理解定义之后，可以通过[[有限树的等价刻画]]理解为什么任意两点间恰有一条路径。这是识别和使用树的重要定理，不是在本定义之外再增加一个条件。
