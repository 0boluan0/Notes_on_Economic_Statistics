# Total differential
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*全微分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **total differential** predicts how a function changes when several inputs move together. A [[偏导数|partial derivative]] varies just one input. Here we ask for one rule that combines all the input changes and remains accurate to first order, whichever way the input approaches the chosen point. This extends the one-variable [[微分|differential]] from a slope times a step to a linear map applied to a vector of steps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**全微分**预测几个输入同时改变时，函数会怎样变化。[[偏导数]]每次只改变一个输入；这里要找同一个规则，把所有输入增量组合起来，并且无论输入怎样趋近所选点，都能给出一阶准确的预测。这把一元[[微分]]中的“斜率乘步长”，推广成“线性映射作用于增量向量”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with $f(x,y)=xy$ at the base point $(2,3)$. Write the two input changes as $h_1,h_2$. Expanding the product gives the exact identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看 $f(x,y)=xy$，基点取 $(2,3)$，两个输入增量记为 $h_1,h_2$。展开乘积，得到精确恒等式

$$f(2+h_1,3+h_2)-f(2,3)=3h_1+2h_2+h_1h_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expression $3h_1+2h_2$ is linear in the input change: doubling both steps doubles this prediction, and predictions for two step vectors add. The product $h_1h_2$ is the error left over. The total differential is that linear rule; differentiability says that its remaining error becomes negligible compared with the length of the whole input step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$3h_1+2h_2$ 对输入增量是线性的：把两步都加倍，预测也加倍；两个增量向量的预测可以相加。乘积 $h_1h_2$ 是剩余误差。全微分就是这个线性规则；可微性要求余项相对于整个输入步长的长度变得可以忽略。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One approximation for all small input changes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个近似同时控制所有小扰动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $F:U\to\mathbb R^m$, where $U$ is an [[开集|open subset]] of $\mathbb R^n$, $a\in U$, and $m,n$ are positive integers. Thus $a$ has a small ball contained in the domain. The vector $a$ is the fixed base input, $h\in\mathbb R^n$ is its change, and $F(a+h)-F(a)\in\mathbb R^m$ is the output change. We use [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]], $\|h\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_i h_i^2}$, in each coordinate space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $F:U\to\mathbb R^m$，其中 $U$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 中的[[开集]]，$a\in U$，$m,n$ 都是正整数。因此 $a$ 周围有一个小球完全留在定义域内。向量 $a$ 是固定基点，$h\in\mathbb R^n$ 是输入增量，$F(a+h)-F(a)\in\mathbb R^m$ 是输出增量。在各坐标空间中都使用[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]，例如 $\|h\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_i h_i^2}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We call $F$ **differentiable at $a$** if there is a [[线性映射|linear map]] $L:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果存在[[线性映射]] $L:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$，使得下式成立，就称 $F$ **在 $a$ 可微**：

$$F(a+h)=F(a)+Lh+r(h),\qquad
\lim_{\substack{h\to0\\h\ne0}}\frac{\|r(h)\|_2}{\|h\|_2}=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $Lh$ means applying $L$ to $h$, and $r(h)$ is the remaining output error. The notation $r(h)=o(\|h\|_2)$ abbreviates the displayed limit. Explicitly, for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a $\delta>0$ such that $0<\|h\|_2<\delta$ implies $\|r(h)\|_2\le\varepsilon\|h\|_2$; choose $\delta$ small enough that $a+h\in U$. The same $\delta$ works for every such vector, including changes whose direction varies as their size shrinks. Checking only a few lines does not establish this condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Lh$ 表示让 $L$ 作用于 $h$，$r(h)$ 是剩余输出误差。记号 $r(h)=o(\|h\|_2)$ 就是所显示极限的简写。具体地，对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，都存在 $\delta>0$，使 $0<\|h\|_2<\delta$ 时有 $\|r(h)\|_2\le\varepsilon\|h\|_2$；同时把 $\delta$ 取得足够小以保证 $a+h\in U$。同一个 $\delta$ 对所有这样的向量都有效，包括大小缩小时方向也在改变的扰动。只检查几条直线不能证明这个条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This map is written $DF(a)$ or $dF_a$ and is called the derivative or total differential at $a$. The value $dF_a(h)$ is its predicted output change for the selected increment. The affine function $h\mapsto F(a)+DF(a)h$ approximates the **output level**, while the linear function $h\mapsto DF(a)h$ approximates the **output change**. On a domain with a boundary, this open-neighborhood definition applies to interior points; differentiability restricted to a boundary needs a separately specified convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个映射记为 $DF(a)$ 或 $dF_a$，称为 $a$ 处的导数或全微分。给定增量后，$dF_a(h)$ 是它预测的输出变化。仿射函数 $h\mapsto F(a)+DF(a)h$ 近似的是**输出值**，线性函数 $h\mapsto DF(a)h$ 近似的是**输出变化**。定义域有边界时，这里的开邻域定义用于内部点；限制在边界上的可微性需要另行指定约定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Scalar outputs, gradients, and differentials
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实值函数、梯度与全微分

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real-valued $f$, the linear map returns one number. Let $e_i$ be the vector with coordinate $i$ equal to $1$ and all other coordinates zero. Substituting $h=te_i$ into the differentiability formula and dividing by the nonzero scalar $t$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实值函数 $f$，线性映射输出一个数。令 $e_i$ 的第 $i$ 个坐标为 $1$、其余为零。把 $h=te_i$ 代入可微性公式，再除以非零标量 $t$，得到

$$\frac{f(a+te_i)-f(a)}t=Df(a)e_i+\frac{r(te_i)}t,
\qquad \left|\frac{r(te_i)}t\right|=\frac{|r(te_i)|}{\|te_i\|_2}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus each partial derivative exists and equals $Df(a)e_i$. Since $h=\sum_i h_i e_i$ and $Df(a)$ is linear, these partials determine its action. The **gradient** $\nabla f(a)$ is the column listing them. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], turning that column into a row:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，每个偏导数都存在，且等于 $Df(a)e_i$。又因为 $h=\sum_i h_i e_i$ 且 $Df(a)$ 线性，这些偏导便决定了它的作用。**梯度** $\nabla f(a)$ 是把它们排成的一列；上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把这一列变成一行：

$$\nabla f(a)=\begin{pmatrix}\partial_1f(a)\\ \vdots\\ \partial_nf(a)\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Df(a)h=\nabla f(a)^Th=\sum_{i=1}^n\partial_i f(a)h_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the customary notation $dx_i=h_i$, this is $df=\sum_i\partial_i f(a)\,dx_i$, with the base point understood. For vector outputs, apply the coordinate-line argument to every output component; their coefficients form the [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian matrix]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在通常的 $dx_i=h_i$ 记法中，这就是 $df=\sum_i\partial_i f(a)\,dx_i$，基点由上下文指定。对于向量输出，对每个输出分量使用同样的坐标线论证；这些系数组成[[Jacobian矩阵]]。

## The linear prediction and the actual finite change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不要把微分与有限增量混成同一个等式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the opening example, $df=3\,dx+2\,dy$ at $(2,3)$. Set $dx=0.01$ and $dy=-0.02$. The linear prediction is $3(0.01)+2(-0.02)=-0.01$. The actual change is $(2.01)(2.98)-6=-0.0102$, because the extra product is $-0.0002$. Both numbers are exact calculations of different quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的例子在 $(2,3)$ 处有 $df=3\,dx+2\,dy$。取 $dx=0.01$、$dy=-0.02$，线性预测为 $3(0.01)+2(-0.02)=-0.01$；真实增量为 $(2.01)(2.98)-6=-0.0102$，因为还有乘积项 $-0.0002$。这两个数分别是不同对象的精确计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remainder satisfies $|h_1h_2|\le(h_1^2+h_2^2)/2=\|h\|_2^2/2$, so its ratio to $\|h\|_2$ tends to zero. If both increments are multiplied by $t$, the linear part scales by $t$ and the remainder by $t^2$. More generally, differentiability alone supplies a small-step limit, not a numerical error guarantee for an arbitrarily chosen finite step. It also compares the error with input size, not with $|df|$: the linear prediction may be zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

余项满足 $|h_1h_2|\le(h_1^2+h_2^2)/2=\|h\|_2^2/2$，所以它除以 $\|h\|_2$ 后趋于零。若两个增量都乘 $t$，线性项乘 $t$，余项乘 $t^2$。一般而言，可微性本身给出的是小步极限，不能直接保证任意指定有限步长的数值误差。它比较的还是误差与输入长度，而不是误差与 $|df|$；线性预测可能恰好为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recognizing and verifying the linear part
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样识别线性部分与余项

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative, when it exists, is unique. If two linear maps $L_1,L_2$ both worked, subtraction and the triangle inequality would give $\|(L_1-L_2)h\|_2/\|h\|_2\to0$. For any fixed nonzero $v$, set $h=tv$. The quotient is the constant $\|(L_1-L_2)v\|_2/\|v\|_2$, so it must be zero. Thus the maps agree on every vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数一旦存在，就是唯一的。假如两个线性映射 $L_1,L_2$ 都符合定义，相减并使用三角不等式，就得到 $\|(L_1-L_2)h\|_2/\|h\|_2\to0$。固定任意非零 $v$，取 $h=tv$；这个商恒等于 $\|(L_1-L_2)v\|_2/\|v\|_2$，因而只能为零。于是两个映射对每个向量都相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiability also implies continuity at $a$. A fixed linear map between these finite-dimensional spaces satisfies $\|Lh\|_2\le C\|h\|_2$ for some finite constant $C$; this follows, for example, by bounding its finitely many matrix entries. Both $Lh$ and $r(h)$ therefore tend to zero. The output approaches $F(a)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可微还推出在 $a$ 连续。这些有限维空间之间的固定线性映射满足 $\|Lh\|_2\le C\|h\|_2$，其中 $C$ 是某个有限常数；例如可以用其有限个矩阵元素给出这个界。因此 $Lh$ 与 $r(h)$ 都趋于零，输出便趋于 $F(a)$。

> [!warning] A candidate is not yet a derivative · 候选不等于已经可微
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Computing all partial derivatives gives the only possible linear coefficients, but does not prove the remainder condition. [[偏导不可推可微|Pointwise partials alone are insufficient]], and even [[方向导数不可推可微|all directional derivatives can be insufficient]]. A useful sufficient condition is that the first partials exist in a neighborhood and are continuous at the base point; it is stronger than differentiability itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 算出所有偏导，只是找到了唯一可能的线性系数，还没有证明余项条件。[[偏导不可推可微|一点处的偏导全存在仍不够]]，甚至[[方向导数不可推可微|所有方向导数存在也可能不够]]。常用充分条件是一阶偏导在邻域存在、且在基点连续；它比可微本身更强。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As a short check, if $f(a+h)-f(a)=3h_1-2h_2+h_1h_2$, the differential is $h\mapsto3h_1-2h_2$. The same bound on $|h_1h_2|$ proves the required remainder limit. The multiplication term belongs to the error, even though it is part of the exact finite change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作一个简短检查：若 $f(a+h)-f(a)=3h_1-2h_2+h_1h_2$，全微分就是 $h\mapsto3h_1-2h_2$；上面对 $|h_1h_2|$ 的估计证明了余项极限。乘积项属于误差，尽管它当然属于真实有限增量。
