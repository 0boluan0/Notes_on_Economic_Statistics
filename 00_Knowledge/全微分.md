# 全微分是多元函数在一点对所有小扰动都有效的一阶线性近似
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The total differential is the first-order linear approximation that works for every small perturbation at a point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 可微的核心是一个统一的线性近似
> 设 $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$。若存在一个线性映射 $L:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$，使
> $$
> f(a+h)=f(a)+Lh+r(h),
> \qquad
> \lim_{h\to0}\frac{\|r(h)\|}{\|h\|}=0,
> $$
> 则称 $f$ 在 $a$ 可微，并把唯一的 $L$ 记为 $Df(a)$。关键不只是每条方向各有一个变化率，而是同一个 $L$ 对所有趋近方式都有效。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A function is differentiable at $a$ when a single linear map $L$ gives the displayed approximation and the remainder is negligible relative to the size of $h$. This unique map is denoted by $Df(a)$. The essential point is not a collection of unrelated directional rates, but one approximation that works for every way of approaching the point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实值函数、梯度与全微分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Real-valued functions, gradients, and the total differential*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ 可微，并把梯度记为列向量，则
$$
Df(a)h=\nabla f(a)^Th
=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(a)h_i.
$$
在形式微分记号中，可写成
$$
df=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}\,dx_i.
$$
这里的 $df$ 是精确的线性映射对扰动的作用，不是实际有限增量本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a differentiable real-valued function and a column-vector convention for the gradient, the displayed inner product is the derivative applied to the perturbation. The formal expression $df$ denotes this exact linear part. It is not identical to the finite change in the function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不要把微分与有限增量混成同一个等式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Do not turn the differential and a finite increment into the same equality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\Delta f=f(a+h)-f(a)$。可微性给出的安全写法是
$$
\Delta f=\nabla f(a)^Th+o(\|h\|).
$$
因此 $\nabla f(a)^Th$ 是一阶近似。写成“$df\approx\Delta f$”可以作为口头提示，但若不说明一个是线性部分、另一个是有限变化，就会掩盖余项和适用尺度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite increment is the sum of the displayed first-order term and a remainder that is small relative to $\|h\|$. Thus the gradient term approximates the increment. Informally writing that $df$ approximates $\Delta f$ is harmless only when the distinction between the linear part and the actual finite change remains explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x,y)=xy$ 在 $(2,3)$ 的微分为
$$
df=3\,dx+2\,dy.
$$
若 $h=(0.01,-0.02)^T$，线性项为 $-0.01$；实际增量还包含 $dx\,dy=-0.0002$。扰动缩小时，这个乘积项相对于 $\|h\|$ 更快趋于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x,y)=xy$ at $(2,3)$, the displayed differential gives the linear response. With the stated perturbation, its value is $-0.01$, while the exact increment also contains the product $dx\,dy=-0.0002$. That product vanishes faster than the perturbation size, which is precisely the role of the remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样识别线性部分与余项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to identify the linear part and the remainder*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义中的核心检查不是“能不能写出若干偏导”，而是能否找到一个线性映射 $L$，使剩余误差满足 $\|r(h)\|/\|h\|\to0$。偏导连续何时足以推出可微、偏导只在一点存在又为何不够，见 [[偏导不可推可微]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The defining test is not whether several partial derivatives can be written down, but whether one linear map $L$ leaves a remainder satisfying $\|r(h)\|/\|h\|\to0$. See [[偏导不可推可微]] for the continuous-partials sufficient condition and for why pointwise partial derivatives alone do not suffice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检：哪一项才是全微分？
> 若 $f(a+h)-f(a)=3h_1-2h_2+h_1h_2$，哪一部分是 $Df(a)h$，怎样验证剩余项符合定义？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f(a+h)-f(a)=3h_1-2h_2+h_1h_2$, which part is $Df(a)h$, and how does the remainder satisfy the definition?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 全微分是线性项 $Df(a)h=3h_1-2h_2$。余项为 $h_1h_2$，且 $|h_1h_2|\le \tfrac12\|h\|_2^2$，所以 $|h_1h_2|/\|h\|_2\to0$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The total differential is the linear term $3h_1-2h_2$. The remainder is $h_1h_2$, and $|h_1h_2|\le\tfrac12\|h\|_2^2$, so its ratio to $\|h\|_2$ tends to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
