# 资本预算与投资决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Capital Budgeting and Investment Decisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 判断一个项目增加的未来现金流是否补偿资金时间价值和风险，并在互斥项目间做一致选择。
> **具体锚点：** 新设备的成本不仅是购买价，还包括安装、营运资本和被占用厂房的机会成本；沉没研发费不因做项目而改变。
> **核心难点：** 使用增量、税后、项目现金流；融资成本通常进贴现率，不应又在项目现金流扣利息。
> **为什么重要：** NPV 直接衡量在给定资本成本下为所有者增加的价值。
> **继续：** 先建现金流时间线，再用 NPV；IRR、回收期和情景分析作为补充而非替代。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It asks whether a project's incremental future cash flows compensate for time value and risk and provides a consistent choice rule among mutually exclusive projects.
> **Concrete anchor:** The cost of new equipment includes not only purchase price but installation, working capital, and the opportunity cost of occupied premises; sunk research expenditure does not change with the current decision.
> **Central difficulty:** Use incremental, after-tax project cash flows. Financing cost normally belongs in the discount rate and should not also be deducted as interest from project cash flow.
> **Why it matters:** NPV directly measures value added to owners at the specified cost of capital.
> **Continue:** Build the cash-flow timeline first and then calculate NPV; use IRR, payback, and scenario analysis as supplements rather than substitutes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先问“接受项目相对拒绝项目改变了什么”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First ask what accepting the project changes relative to rejecting it*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

项目价值只来自决策能够改变的未来现金流。这个差分问题自动排除沉没成本，同时纳入机会成本、侵蚀、协同、税收和营运资本。若一笔现金流在接受与拒绝项目两种状态下相同，它不属于增量现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Project value comes only from future cash flows changed by the decision. This difference-in-states question automatically excludes sunk costs while including opportunity costs, cannibalization, synergies, taxes, and working capital. If a cash flow is identical whether the project is accepted or rejected, it is not incremental.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 增量现金流
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Incremental cash flows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只计“接受项目相对拒绝项目”变化的现金流：初始投资、机会成本、侵蚀/协同、税、折旧税盾、营运资本和终结回收。沉没成本不计，已拥有资源的机会成本要计。名义/实际和税前/税后口径要一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Include only cash flows that change when accepting rather than rejecting the project: initial investment, opportunity costs, cannibalization or synergies, taxes, depreciation tax shields, working capital, and terminal recovery. Exclude sunk costs but include the opportunity cost of resources already owned. Keep nominal versus real and pre-tax versus after-tax conventions consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个常见自由现金流构造是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One common construction of project free cash flow is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
FCF_t=EBIT_t(1-T)+Dep_t-CapEx_t-\Delta NWC_t.
$$

折旧本身不是现金流出，但它降低应税利润，形成 $Dep_t\times T$ 的税盾。营运资本在建立时是现金占用，在项目结束并真正收回时才形成流入。出售资产的终结现金流要按售价与税基之差计算税负或税收抵扣。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Depreciation is not itself a cash outflow, but it lowers taxable income and creates a tax shield of $Dep_t\times T$. Working capital absorbs cash when built and becomes an inflow only when genuinely recovered at project end. Terminal proceeds from selling an asset must include tax or tax relief based on the difference between sale price and tax basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## NPV
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Net present value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$NPV=\sum_t CF_t/(1+r)^t$。独立项目 NPV>0 接受；资本无限且项目可分时最大化 NPV。NPV 可加总，直接使用绝对价值，不受现金流符号多次变化导致的多重根困扰。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$NPV=\sum_t CF_t/(1+r)^t$. Accept an independent project when NPV is positive; with unlimited capital and divisible projects, maximize total NPV. NPV is additive, measures absolute value directly, and avoids the multiple-root problem caused by cash flows that change sign more than once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

NPV 规则依赖三个条件：现金流是增量且可归因的，贴现率与现金流风险和口径匹配，项目不会因未建模的选择权或资源冲突改变其他项目。NPV 不是“只要公式大于零就真实创造价值”；输入和边界仍需检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NPV rule depends on three conditions: cash flows are incremental and attributable; the discount rate matches their risk and convention; and the project does not alter other projects through unmodeled options or resource conflicts. NPV does not mean that any spreadsheet output above zero represents real value creation; its inputs and boundaries still require scrutiny.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算例：从经营增量到 NPV
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: from operating increments to NPV*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设备今天购买并安装共需 900，还要投入营运资本 100；三年内每年新增收入 600、现金成本 350，设备直线折旧三年、残值为零，税率 25%，第三年末收回营运资本。每年 $EBIT=600-350-300=-50$，税后经营利润为 $-37.5$，加回折旧后每年经营现金流为 262.5；第三年另收回 100。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equipment costs 900 to purchase and install today and requires 100 of working capital. For three years it adds annual revenue of 600 and cash cost of 350. It is depreciated straight-line over three years to zero salvage value, the tax rate is 25%, and working capital is recovered at the end of year three. Annual $EBIT=600-350-300=-50$, after-tax operating profit is $-37.5$, and adding back depreciation gives annual operating cash flow of 262.5; year three also recovers 100.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若资本成本为 8%，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an 8% cost of capital,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
NPV=-1{,}000+\frac{262.5}{1.08}+\frac{262.5}{1.08^2}+\frac{362.5}{1.08^3}\approx -244.4.
$$

即使每年“经营现金流为正”，项目仍未补偿初始投资与资本成本。这个算例也显示，看到会计亏损不能直接拒绝项目：折旧税盾使经营现金流大于税后利润，但最终仍要由 NPV 决策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even though annual operating cash flow is positive, the project does not cover its initial investment and cost of capital. The example also shows why an accounting loss is not by itself a reason to reject a project: the depreciation tax shield makes operating cash flow exceed after-tax profit, but NPV remains the decision criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## IRR、回收期与 MIRR
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*IRR, payback, and MIRR*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

IRR 使 NPV=0，便于表达回报率，但非常规现金流可有多个/无 IRR，互斥项目的规模和时序不同会与 NPV 冲突。回收期忽略期后现金流且普通版本不贴现。MIRR 明确融资/再投资率但仍不替代价值加总。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
IRR sets NPV to zero and conveniently expresses a rate of return, but non-conventional cash flows can have multiple IRRs or none, and scale or timing differences among mutually exclusive projects can make IRR conflict with NPV. Ordinary payback ignores both cash flows after the cutoff and discounting. MIRR specifies financing and reinvestment rates but still does not replace value additivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 互斥与资本约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mutual exclusivity and capital constraints*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

互斥项目优先选最高 NPV；寿命不同可用等额年金或一致替换链。在硬资本约束下需做组合优化，profitability index 只在特定可分项目条件下排序有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Among mutually exclusive projects, choose the highest NPV. For unequal lives, use equivalent annual value or a consistent replacement chain. Under hard capital rationing, optimize the project portfolio; the profitability index ranks correctly only under particular divisibility conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若项目不可分、预算为 100，一个成本 100、NPV 30 的项目与两个各成本 50、NPV 各 18 的项目相比，单看每个项目的现值指数可能有帮助，但真正问题是组合：后两个合计 NPV 36，应整体选择。存在人员、厂房或战略冲突时，还要把这些互斥约束显式写进组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If projects are indivisible and the budget is 100, compare one project costing 100 with NPV 30 against two projects each costing 50 with NPV 18. Individual profitability indices may help, but the true problem is the portfolio: the latter pair has total NPV 36 and should be chosen together. If staff, facilities, or strategic conflicts create mutual exclusivity, those constraints must also be modeled explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 风险、情景与敏感性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk, scenarios, and sensitivity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别销量、价格、成本、工期和残值等驱动；敏感性一次变一个，情景联合改变一致变量，模拟给分布。风险调整贴现率应匹配系统风险，项目特有风险可通过组合分散但仍影响财务困境和管理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify drivers such as volume, price, cost, schedule, and salvage value. Sensitivity analysis changes one input at a time, scenarios change a coherent set of inputs together, and simulation produces a distribution. A risk-adjusted discount rate should match systematic risk; project-specific risk may be diversifiable but can still affect financial distress and management.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实物期权入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry point to real options*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

延迟、扩张、收缩或放弃选择使项目价值超过静态 NPV；只有存在不确定性、管理灵活性和不可逆性时才有实物期权价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Options to delay, expand, contract, or abandon can make project value exceed static NPV; real-option value exists only when uncertainty, managerial flexibility, and some irreversibility are present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把已付咨询费计入项目现金流，却漏掉被项目占用土地的可售价值，正好把沉没成本与机会成本颠倒。
- 从收入直接扣折旧、资本开支和本金偿还，却又用 WACC 折现，可能混合会计费用、投资现金流与融资现金流。
- 在乐观情景提高销量却不增加存货、应收和产能，情景内部不一致。
- 因 IRR 较高选择规模很小的项目而放弃更高 NPV 的互斥项目，优化了比率而非价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Including a paid consulting fee while omitting the sale value of land occupied by the project reverses the treatment of sunk cost and opportunity cost.
- Deducting depreciation, capital expenditure, and principal repayment directly from revenue and then discounting with WACC can mix accounting expense, investment cash flow, and financing cash flow.
- Raising sales volume in an upside scenario without increasing inventory, receivables, or capacity makes the scenario internally inconsistent.
- Choosing a tiny project because it has a higher IRR while rejecting a mutually exclusive project with greater NPV optimizes a ratio rather than value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么沉没成本不进入项目增量现金流？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a sunk cost not enter incremental project cash flow?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 无论接受还是拒绝项目都已发生，决策不能改变它；机会成本则会因选择项目而放弃替代用途。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It has already occurred whether the project is accepted or rejected, so the decision cannot change it; an opportunity cost arises because accepting the project sacrifices an alternative use.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 互斥项目 NPV 与 IRR 冲突时选哪个？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When NPV and IRR conflict for mutually exclusive projects, which should be chosen?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 在一致资本成本和现金流估计下选择更高 NPV，因为它直接最大化增加的价值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With consistent costs of capital and cash-flow estimates, choose the higher NPV because it directly maximizes incremental value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 在现金流中扣利息后还能用 WACC 折现吗？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Can WACC still discount cash flows after interest has been deducted?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 通常不能，会把融资成本计算两次；项目自由现金流排除融资流，再由 WACC 体现资本成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Usually not, because that counts financing cost twice. Project free cash flow excludes financing flows, while WACC captures their cost in discounting.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么“项目有正利润”仍不足以接受？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why is positive project profit still insufficient for acceptance?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 会计利润没有完整反映投资时点、营运资本与资本机会成本；只有增量税后现金流折现后超过全部投入，项目才增加价值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Accounting profit does not fully capture investment timing, working capital, or the opportunity cost of capital. The project adds value only when discounted incremental after-tax cash flows exceed all required investment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
