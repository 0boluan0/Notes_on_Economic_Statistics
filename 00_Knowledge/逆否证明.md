# 逆否证明在否定结论更具体时改证非Q推出非P
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Contrapositive proof replaces the target by not-Q implies not-P when the negated conclusion is more concrete*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子程序
> 因为 $P\Rightarrow Q$ 与 $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$ 等价，可以改证逆否命题。它尤其适合 $Q$ 是“不可整除”“不属于”“没有某性质”等否定陈述，而 $\neg Q$ 能直接展开成可操作定义的情形。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Because $P\Rightarrow Q$ is equivalent to $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$, one may prove the contrapositive instead. This is especially useful when $Q$ is a negative property such as non-divisibility or non-membership and $\neg Q$ unfolds into a concrete definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要证“若 $n^2$ 为偶数，则 $n$ 为偶数”，直接从 $n^2=2k$ 提取 $n$ 的形式并不方便。逆否从“$n$ 不偶，即 $n=2k+1$”出发，直接算得 $n^2$ 仍为奇数，因此原命题成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove “if $n^2$ is even, then $n$ is even,” extracting the form of $n$ directly from $n^2=2k$ is awkward. The contrapositive begins with “$n$ is not even, so $n=2k+1$,” and directly shows that $n^2$ is odd, thereby proving the original implication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 逆否证明不是反证法：它直接证明另一个与原命题等价的 implication，不必推出矛盾。
- 必须正确交换并否定两端；$\neg P\Rightarrow\neg Q$ 不是逆否。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** Contraposition is not proof by contradiction. It directly proves an implication equivalent to the target and need not derive an inconsistency.<br>
**2.** The sides must be reversed and negated correctly; $\neg P\Rightarrow\neg Q$ is not the contrapositive.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 要证“若 $ab$ 为奇数，则 $a,b$ 都为奇数”，逆否命题应怎样写？
>
> **答案：** 若 $a$ 或 $b$ 至少一个不是奇数（对整数即为偶数），则 $ab$ 不是奇数（为偶数）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What is the contrapositive of “if $ab$ is odd, then both $a$ and $b$ are odd”?
>
> **Answer:** If at least one of $a$ and $b$ is not odd—hence is even for integers—then $ab$ is not odd, that is, it is even.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
