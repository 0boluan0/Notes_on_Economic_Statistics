# Proof by contraposition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逆否证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **proof by contraposition** proves an if–then claim by looking at what would have to be true if its conclusion failed. To show “if $n^2$ is even, then $n$ is even,” it may be easier to start with an odd integer $n$ and show that its square cannot be even. This changes the starting information while proving the same claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**逆否证明**从“结论不成立时会怎样”入手，证明一个“如果……那么……”的命题。例如要证明“若 $n^2$ 为偶数，则 $n$ 为偶数”，从奇整数 $n$ 出发，证明它的平方不可能为偶数，往往更容易。起点信息改变了，证明的仍是同一个命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reverse and negate both sides
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*交换两端，并同时否定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the original [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] as $P\Rightarrow Q$, where $P$ is the hypothesis and $Q$ the conclusion. The symbol $\neg$ means “not.” Its [[逆否与逆命题|contrapositive]] is

$$\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P.$$

Both implications fail in exactly the same situation: $P$ is true and $Q$ false. Therefore, in ordinary classical logic, they are equivalent. Proving the contrapositive proves the original implication; it does not prove the converse $Q\Rightarrow P$ merely by exchanging the sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
将原来的[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]]写作 $P\Rightarrow Q$，其中 $P$ 为假设，$Q$ 为结论；$\neg$ 表示“不”。它的[[逆否与逆命题|逆否命题]]是

$$\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P.$$

两个蕴含恰好都在 $P$ 真、$Q$ 假时失败，所以在通常的经典逻辑中二者等价。证明逆否命题就证明了原蕴含；这并不意味着只交换两端，就证明了逆命题 $Q\Rightarrow P$。

> [!important] A direct proof of an equivalent claim · 直接证明一个等价命题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Because $P\Rightarrow Q$ is equivalent to $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$, one may prove the contrapositive instead. State the contrapositive explicitly, assume $\neg Q$, and derive $\neg P$ using a [[直接证明|direct argument]]. The domain and any standing assumptions remain the same. This is especially useful when $Q$ is a negative property such as non-divisibility or non-membership and $\neg Q$ unfolds into a concrete definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因为 $P\Rightarrow Q$ 与 $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$ 等价，可以改证逆否命题。先明确写出逆否命题，再假设 $\neg Q$，用[[直接证明]]推出 $\neg P$。论域与其他原有假设保持不变。它尤其适合 $Q$ 是“不可整除”“不属于”“没有某性质”等否定陈述，而 $\neg Q$ 能直接展开成可操作定义的情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a method choice, not a requirement that $Q$ be written as a negative sentence. Use the direction that supplies workable information. Failing to find a convenient direct proof does not mean the original statement is false.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这只是选择方法的理由，不要求 $Q$ 必须写成否定句。应选择能给出可用信息的方向；暂时找不到方便的直接证明，也不意味着原命题为假。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## If a square is even, its integer root is even
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*整数的平方为偶数，则原整数为偶数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the domain be the integers $n\in\mathbb Z$. Here $P$ means “$n^2$ is even” and $Q$ means “$n$ is even.” An integer is even if it equals $2k$ and odd if it equals $2k+1$, for an integer $k$. [[带余除法|Division by two with remainder]] gives exactly one of these possibilities: the remainder is $0$ or $1$. Thus “not even” means “odd” **within this integer domain**, including negative integers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
论域取整数 $n\in\mathbb Z$。这里 $P$ 表示“$n^2$ 为偶数”，$Q$ 表示“$n$ 为偶数”。整数若等于某个整数 $k$ 的二倍 $2k$ 就是偶数，若等于 $2k+1$ 就是奇数。[[带余除法|除以二的带余除法]]恰好给出其中一种情形，因为余数只能是 $0$ 或 $1$。所以**在这个整数论域内**，“不是偶数”就表示“是奇数”，负整数也包括在内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove “if $n^2$ is even, then $n$ is even,” extracting the form of $n$ directly from $n^2=2k$ is awkward. The contrapositive begins with “$n$ is not even, so $n=2k+1$,” and directly shows that $n^2$ is odd, thereby proving the original implication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
要证“若 $n^2$ 为偶数，则 $n$ 为偶数”，直接从 $n^2=2k$ 提取 $n$ 的形式并不方便。逆否从“$n$ 不偶，即 $n=2k+1$”出发，直接算得 $n^2$ 仍为奇数，因此原命题成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The contrapositive is: if $n$ is odd, then $n^2$ is odd. Take any odd integer and write $n=2k+1$ with $k\in\mathbb Z$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
逆否命题为：若 $n$ 为奇数，则 $n^2$ 为奇数。任取奇整数，写成 $n=2k+1$，其中 $k\in\mathbb Z$。于是

$$n^2=(2k+1)^2=2(2k^2+2k)+1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $2k^2+2k$ is an integer, the square is odd. This proves $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$, hence the original implication. The proof did not assume that $n$ was even, nor attempt to infer the parity of $n$ merely by taking a square root of an equation for $n^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$2k^2+2k$ 为整数，因此平方是奇数。这证明了 $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$，从而证明原蕴含。证明既没有预先假设 $n$ 为偶数，也没有仅凭对 $n^2$ 的方程开平方，就宣称得到了 $n$ 的奇偶性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a claim about every $n$, the contrapositive must also be proved for every $n$ in the same domain. This is different from negating the whole universal claim: a single $n$ with $P(n)$ true and $Q(n)$ false would refute it. The [[量词否定规则|rules for negating quantifiers]] explain that different operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
原命题若讨论每个 $n$，逆否命题也必须对同一论域内每个 $n$ 成立。这不同于否定整个全称命题：只要找到一个 $P(n)$ 真而 $Q(n)$ 假的 $n$，就能反驳原命题。[[量词否定规则]]解释了后面这种不同的操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Negating “both” means “at least one fails”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*否定“两个都成立”，是“至少一个不成立”*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the target is: for integers $a,b$, if $ab$ is odd, then both $a$ and $b$ are odd. Negating the conclusion gives “$a$ is not odd **or** $b$ is not odd,” not “both are not odd.” Thus the contrapositive is: if at least one factor is even, their product is even.

If $a=2k$ with integer $k$, then $ab=2(kb)$ is even because $kb$ is an integer. If $b=2\ell$ instead, then $ab=2(a\ell)$ is even. These two cases cover “at least one”; their overlap, when both factors are even, causes no problem. This completes the contrapositive proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设目标为：对整数 $a,b$，若 $ab$ 为奇数，则 $a,b$ 都为奇数。否定结论得到“$a$ 不是奇数**或** $b$ 不是奇数”，不是“两个都不是奇数”。所以逆否命题为：若至少一个因子为偶数，则乘积为偶数。

若 $a=2k$，其中 $k$ 为整数，则 $ab=2(kb)$，且 $kb$ 为整数，所以乘积为偶数。若改为 $b=2\ell$，则 $ab=2(a\ell)$，也为偶数。两种情形覆盖了“至少一个”；两个因子都为偶数时的重叠没有问题。这便完成了逆否证明。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Contraposition is not proof by contradiction. It directly proves an implication equivalent to the target and need not derive an inconsistency.
- The sides must be reversed and negated correctly; $\neg P\Rightarrow\neg Q$ is not the contrapositive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 逆否证明不是反证法：它直接证明另一个与原命题等价的 implication，不必推出矛盾。
- 必须正确交换并否定两端；$\neg P\Rightarrow\neg Q$ 不是逆否。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, “divisible by $4$ implies even” is true. The incorrectly chosen claim “not divisible by $4$ implies not even” fails at $n=2$. Merely negating both sides without reversing them would therefore change the theorem. The correct contrapositive is “not even implies not divisible by $4$.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，“被 $4$ 整除则为偶数”是真的；错误选择的“不能被 $4$ 整除则不是偶数”在 $n=2$ 时失败。只否定两端而不交换顺序，会改变待证命题。正确的逆否是“不是偶数则不能被 $4$ 整除”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[反证法|proof by contradiction]], the starting assumption is the negation of the target statement, and the argument seeks an impossibility. For an implication, this means assuming $P$ and $\neg Q$ together. A contrapositive proof instead assumes $\neg Q$ and aims to establish $\neg P$. These describe different proof structures, although one method may be used inside another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[反证法]]先假设目标命题的否定，再寻找矛盾。对蕴含而言，这意味着同时假设 $P$ 与 $\neg Q$；逆否证明则假设 $\neg Q$，目标为建立 $\neg P$。两者描述不同的证明结构，但一种方法也可以嵌入另一种方法内部。

> [!question]- Check the connective · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What is the contrapositive of “if $ab$ is odd, then both $a$ and $b$ are odd”? **Answer:** If at least one of $a$ and $b$ is not odd—hence is even for integers—then $ab$ is not odd, that is, it is even. Requiring both factors to be even would miss the case $a=2,b=3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要证“若 $ab$ 为奇数，则 $a,b$ 都为奇数”，逆否命题应怎样写？**答案：** 若 $a$ 或 $b$ 至少一个不是奇数（对整数即为偶数），则 $ab$ 不是奇数（为偶数）。若要求两个因子都为偶数，就漏掉了 $a=2,b=3$ 的情形。
