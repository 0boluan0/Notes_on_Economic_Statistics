# Almost-sure absorption in a finite chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有限吸收链必然吸收*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Having some chance of reaching an endpoint is weaker than reaching it with probability one. In a **finite absorbing chain**, the route condition holds from every state, so a failed attempt cannot leave the system somewhere with no further opportunity. Finiteness lets us turn those opportunities into one common bound and prove eventual absorption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有机会到达终点，比以概率一到达终点弱得多。在**有限吸收链**中，每个状态都有吸收路线，所以一次尝试失败后，不会把系统留在再无机会的位置。有限性让这些机会具有共同的下界，由此证明最终吸收。

> [!example] Each wait can fail, but waiting forever has probability zero · 每次都可能失败，永远等待的概率却为零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take states $s,a$. From $s$, remain at $s$ with probability $3/4$ and move to $a$ with probability $1/4$; $a$ is [[吸收状态|absorbing]], so it is never left. Starting at $s$, avoiding absorption for $n$ steps requires $n$ consecutive waits. Hence
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取状态 $s,a$。从 $s$ 出发，以 $3/4$ 的概率继续等待，以 $1/4$ 的概率进入 $a$；$a$ 是进入后不再离开的[[吸收状态]]。从 $s$ 开始，前 $n$ 步没有吸收，需要连续等待 $n$ 次。因此
>
> $$\Pr_s(\tau_A>n)=(3/4)^n\longrightarrow0,\qquad A=\{a\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\tau_A$ is the first step at which the chain is in $A$, and $\Pr_s$ means probability starting at $s$. No fixed finite deadline guarantees success, but the event of never succeeding has probability zero. The general proof replaces a one-step attempt by a block of several steps.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $\tau_A$ 是第一次进入 $A$ 的时刻，$\Pr_s$ 表示从 $s$ 出发的概率。没有任何固定有限期限能够保证成功，但永远不成功的事件概率为零。一般证明只是把“一步尝试”换成“若干步组成的一段尝试”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The precise conclusion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先明确结论中的对象*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ be a [[吸收链|finite absorbing chain]] with a fixed [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous]] rule. Its nonempty absorbing-state set is $A$. Define the [[命中概率第一步方程|first hitting time]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ 是采用固定[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次]]规则的[[吸收链|有限吸收链]]，非空吸收状态集为 $A$。定义[[命中概率第一步方程|首次命中时刻]]

$$\tau_A=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\in A\},$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
with $\infty$ if there is no hit. Thus a start in $A$ gives $\tau_A=0$. For every starting state $i$, absorption occurs almost surely and the [[期望命中时间方程|mean waiting time]] is finite:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若从不命中，取 $\infty$；所以从 $A$ 内开始时，$\tau_A=0$。对每个起点 $i$，都有几乎必然吸收，而且[[期望命中时间方程|平均等待时间]]有限：

$$\Pr_i(\tau_A<\infty)=1,\qquad E_i\tau_A<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expectation $E_i$ also uses starting state $i$. The destination may depend on the path and initial state: the assertion is arrival at **some** member of $A$, not necessarily one prescribed member. All nonabsorbing states are [[常返与暂态|transient]]; absorption prevents further visits to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期望 $E_i$ 同样以 $i$ 为起点。最终去哪一个吸收态可以依赖路径和初态；结论是到达 $A$ 中的**某个**成员，不是必定到达某个预先指定的成员。所有非吸收状态都是[[常返与暂态|暂态]]，因为吸收之后再也无法访问它们。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn individual routes into a uniform chance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把各自的路线变成共同的机会*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every state is already absorbing, the result is immediate. Otherwise, for each nonabsorbing state $i$, choose one finite positive-probability route ending in $A$. Let $\ell_i\ge1$ be its length and $c_i>0$ the product of its transition probabilities. Following that route has probability $c_i$ and guarantees absorption within $\ell_i$ steps. Since there are finitely many starting states, we can choose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果全部状态都吸收，结论立即成立。否则，对每个非吸收状态 $i$，选一条最终进入 $A$ 的有限正概率路线。令 $\ell_i\ge1$ 为路线长度，$c_i>0$ 为沿途转移概率的乘积；沿该路线走的概率就是 $c_i$，并保证在 $\ell_i$ 步内吸收。因为起点只有有限多个，可以选出

$$m=\max_{i\notin A}\ell_i<\infty,\qquad
0<\delta<1\quad\text{with}\quad\delta\le\min_{i\notin A}c_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus every nonabsorbing starting state has probability at least $\delta$ of absorption within the next $m$ steps. A shorter route causes no problem: after reaching $A$, the chain stays there for the rest of the block. The constants need not be sharp; their positivity and uniformity are what matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

于是，从每个非吸收状态出发，接下来 $m$ 步内吸收的概率都至少为 $\delta$。路线较短也没有问题：提前到达 $A$ 后，剩下的步数都留在那里。常数不必最优，关键是严格为正，而且对所有起点共同有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a block ends without absorption, condition on the current state. The [[Markov性|Markov rule]] and time homogeneity give the same success bound for the next block. Repeated conditioning, not an assumption that the blocks are independent, yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一段结束时尚未吸收，就对当前状态条件化。[[Markov性]]和时间齐次性使下一段仍有同样的成功下界。重复条件化，而不是假设各段独立，得到

$$\Pr_i(\tau_A>km)\le(1-\delta)^k\qquad(k=0,1,2,\ldots).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The right side tends to zero. Since avoiding absorption forever implies avoiding it at every block endpoint, its probability is zero. This proves the probability-one conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右端趋于零；永远不吸收必然意味着在每个分段终点都未吸收，所以该事件的概率为零。这就证明了概率一结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same bound also controls the mean and visits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个界也控制平均时间与访问次数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonnegative integer-valued waiting time, including the possible value $\infty$, its [[期望命中时间方程|tail-sum identity]] is $E_i\tau_A=\sum_{n\ge0}\Pr_i(\tau_A>n)$. In each block of $m$ consecutive values of $n$, the tails are at most $(1-\delta)^k$. The [[几何级数|geometric series]] therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对取非负整数、并允许 $\infty$ 的等待时间，[[期望命中时间方程|尾概率求和恒等式]]为 $E_i\tau_A=\sum_{n\ge0}\Pr_i(\tau_A>n)$。每一段包含 $m$ 个连续的 $n$，这一段内的尾概率均不超过 $(1-\delta)^k$。由[[几何级数]]，得到

$$E_i\tau_A\le m\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(1-\delta)^k=\frac m\delta<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example we may take $m=1,\delta=1/4$; its exact mean is $1/(1/4)=4$ steps. In a larger chain $m/\delta$ is only an upper bound. Before absorption there are exactly $\tau_A$ observation times $0,\ldots,\tau_A-1$. The total visits to any particular nonabsorbing state are at most that count, so their expectation is finite, which gives [[常返与暂态|transience]]. This justifies calling the nonabsorbing part the transient part in the later [[吸收链标准形|block representation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开篇例子可以取 $m=1,\delta=1/4$，其精确均值为 $1/(1/4)=4$ 步。更大的链中，$m/\delta$ 通常只是上界。吸收之前恰有 $0,\ldots,\tau_A-1$ 这 $\tau_A$ 个观测时点；访问某个非吸收状态的总次数不超过这个数，因此其期望有限，便得到[[常返与暂态|暂态性]]。这也说明后续[[吸收链标准形|分块表示]]为什么能把非吸收部分称为暂态部分。

> [!warning] Why the scope cannot be dropped · 为什么不能删去适用范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If only some states can reach an absorbing state, a separate nonabsorbing closed class can trap the others. A chain consisting of one absorbing state and a disjoint two-state cycle is an example. It fails the definition of an absorbing chain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果只有部分状态能到吸收态，另一个非吸收闭类就可能留住其余状态。例如，一个吸收点加一个与它分离的二状态循环，并不满足吸收链的定义。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On the infinite state set $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, make 0 absorbing and move from each $i\ge1$ right with probability $3/4$, left with probability $1/4$. Every state has a positive route to 0, but the [[命中概率第一步方程|hitting probability]] is $(1/3)^i$, so from 1 there is probability $2/3$ of never being absorbed. The needed uniform route length and success bound need not exist. A fair walk instead hits 0 almost surely but has [[必达不推有限期望|infinite mean waiting]] from 1. Finiteness is doing real work in both conclusions above.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在无限状态集 $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 上，令 0 吸收；从每个 $i\ge1$，以 $3/4$ 的概率向右、以 $1/4$ 向左。每个状态都有到 0 的正概率路线，但[[命中概率第一步方程|命中概率]]为 $(1/3)^i$，所以从 1 出发有 $2/3$ 的概率永不吸收。证明所需的统一路线长度与成功下界未必存在。若改成公平游走，虽几乎必然命中 0，从 1 出发的[[必达不推有限期望|平均等待却无穷]]。有限性在上述两项结论中都不可忽略。

## Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Grinstead and Snell, Introduction to Probability, Theorem 11.3 and proof, PDF pp.425–426 / printed pp.417–418](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~prob/prob/prob.pdf#page=425): finite-state routes give a common block length and a geometric bound on nonabsorption. The finite-mean bound above follows directly by summing that bound; no matrix inverse is assumed.
- [Weber, Cambridge Markov Chains, §2.5, Example 3.5 and Theorem 4.2, PDF pp.12,16,18–19](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~rrw1/markov/M.pdf#page=16): closed-class obstruction, the biased half-line hitting probability $(q/p)^i$ when $p>q$, and nonnegative mean-time equations. The opening geometric example and visit-count comparison are checked directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Grinstead–Snell 定理11.3及证明（PDF425–426／印刷417–418页）：核对由有限状态路线得到共同分段长度和不吸收概率的几何界；本文再对该界求和得到均值上界，没有预先假设矩阵逆存在。
- Weber §2.5、例3.5及定理4.2（PDF12、16、18–19页）：核对闭类障碍、$p>q$ 时偏置半直线游走的命中概率 $(q/p)^i$ 和非负均值方程；开篇几何等待与访问次数比较直接核算。
