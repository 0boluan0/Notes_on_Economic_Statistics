# 利率、即期远期与期限结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interest Rates, Spot and Forward Rates, and the Term Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 说明不同期限的无风险现金流应分别按什么利率折现，以及收益率曲线的形状究竟包含哪些预期与风险补偿。
> **具体锚点：** 一年即期利率 3%、两年即期利率 4% 时，曲线隐含的一年后一年期远期利率约为 5.01%，但这不等于市场确信未来短率恰为 5.01%。
> **核心难点：** 即期率、远期率和到期收益率是不同对象；观察到的远期率同时可能含未来短率预期与期限风险溢价。
> **为什么重要：** 债券定价、固定收益交易、货币政策传导和利率风险管理都要求逐期限理解现金流。
> **继续：** 若贴现本身还不熟，先读 [[货币时间价值与贴现]]；定价见 [[债券定价、收益率与信用利差]]，敏感度见 [[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It determines which rate should discount a risk-free cash flow at each maturity and what expectations and risk compensation are embedded in the shape of the yield curve.
> **Concrete anchor:** If the one-year spot rate is 3% and the two-year spot rate is 4%, the curve implies a one-year forward rate beginning one year from now of about 5.01%, but that does not mean the market is certain the future short rate will be exactly 5.01%.
> **Central difficulty:** Spot rates, forward rates, and yield to maturity are different objects. An observed forward rate may contain both an expectation of future short rates and a term-risk premium.
> **Why it matters:** Bond pricing, fixed-income trading, monetary-policy transmission, and interest-rate risk management all require maturity-specific reasoning.
> **Continue:** If discounting itself is not yet secure, first read [[货币时间价值与贴现|Time Value of Money and Discounting]]. For pricing, see [[债券定价、收益率与信用利差|Bond Pricing, Yields, and Credit Spreads]]; for sensitivity, see [[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险|Bond Duration, Convexity, and Yield-Curve Risk]].

## 三个利率对象先分开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Separate the three rate objects first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **即期利率：** 今天锁定、从今天到某一到期日的一笔零息现金流回报，是逐期限折现的基本率。
- **远期利率：** 今天锁定、但借贷区间从未来某日开始的利率；它由相邻即期贴现因子的无套利关系导出。
- **到期收益率：** 使一只附息债券全部合同现金流现值等于当前价格的单一内部收益率；它把整条曲线压缩成一个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Spot rate:** The return locked in today on a zero-coupon cash flow from today to a specified maturity; it is the fundamental maturity-specific discount rate.
- **Forward rate:** A rate fixed today for a borrowing interval that begins on a future date; it follows from a no-arbitrage relation between adjacent spot discount factors.
- **Yield to maturity:** A single internal rate that equates an individual coupon bond's contractual cash flows to its current price; it compresses an entire curve into one number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 名义、实际与预期通胀
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Nominal rates, real rates, and expected inflation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确 Fisher 关系 $(1+i)=(1+r)(1+\pi^e)$，低利率时近似 $i\approx r+\pi^e$。事前实际利率使用预期通胀，事后实际回报使用实现通胀；二者服务于决策和结果评估的不同阶段。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact Fisher relation is $(1+i)=(1+r)(1+\pi^e)$; at low rates, $i\approx r+\pi^e$. The ex ante real rate uses expected inflation, whereas ex post real return uses realized inflation; they serve decision-making and outcome evaluation at different stages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

名义债券锁定货币单位，通胀保值债券更接近锁定购买力。两者收益率之差是盈亏平衡通胀的起点，但还可能包含通胀风险溢价、流动性差异和技术供需，不能直接当作纯粹的平均通胀预期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nominal bonds fix currency units, while inflation-linked bonds more closely fix purchasing power. Their yield difference is a starting point for breakeven inflation, but it can also contain an inflation-risk premium, liquidity differences, and technical supply–demand effects, so it is not a pure measure of expected average inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 债券价格与收益率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond prices and yields*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

息票债券价格是息票和本金现值，价格与要求收益率反向。YTM 是使现金流现值等于价格的单一利率，隐含持有至到期、无违约和再投资等解释条件。即期利率逐期限折现，远期利率由即期曲线隐含。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coupon bond's price is the present value of coupons and principal, and price moves inversely with required yield. YTM is the single rate that equates the present value of contractual cash flows to price; interpreting it as a realized return requires assumptions about holding to maturity, no default, and reinvestment. Spot rates discount each maturity separately, while forward rates are implied by the spot curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从即期率推出远期率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deriving a forward rate from spot rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在年复利口径下，投资两年期零息资产的累计回报必须等于先投一年、再按今天锁定的一年期远期率续投，否则存在无风险套利。因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under annual compounding, the cumulative return on a two-year zero-coupon investment must equal the return from investing for one year and then reinvesting for a second year at a forward rate fixed today; otherwise a riskless arbitrage exists. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(1+s_2)^2=(1+s_1)(1+f_{1,1}),
\qquad
f_{1,1}=\frac{(1+s_2)^2}{1+s_1}-1.
$$

代入 $s_1=3\%$、$s_2=4\%$，得到 $f_{1,1}=1.04^2/1.03-1\approx5.01\%$。这是今天价格蕴含的无套利远期率，不是未来短率的无误预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substituting $s_1=3\%$ and $s_2=4\%$ gives $f_{1,1}=1.04^2/1.03-1\approx5.01\%$. This is the no-arbitrage forward rate implied by today's prices, not an error-free forecast of the future short rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 利率风险结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The risk structure of interest rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同期限利率差来自违约预期、风险溢价、流动性、税收和条款。信用利差不是纯违约概率；回收率、流动性和风险厌恶也进入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Yield differences at the same maturity arise from expected default, risk premia, liquidity, taxes, and contractual terms. A credit spread is not a pure default probability; recovery, liquidity, and risk aversion also enter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 期限结构理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Theories of the term structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纯预期假说把长期率与未来短率预期连接；期限溢价/流动性偏好允许投资者要求持有长债补偿；市场分割强调不同期限供需。现实曲线通常混合预期与时变期限溢价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pure-expectations hypothesis links long rates to expected future short rates; term-premium or liquidity-preference theories allow investors to demand compensation for holding long bonds; market segmentation emphasizes supply and demand at different maturities. Actual curves normally combine expectations with time-varying term premia.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个方便的概念分解是“长期收益率 ≈ 未来短率平均预期 + 期限溢价”。它不是让你从一条曲线唯一反推两部分；需要模型、调查预期或其他价格信息才能识别。期限溢价还可能为负，例如长债在坏状态中具有对冲价值时。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful conceptual decomposition is “long yield ≈ average expected future short rates + term premium.” A single curve does not uniquely reveal the two components; identification requires a model, survey expectations, or other price information. The term premium can also be negative, for example when long bonds hedge adverse states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 收益率曲线解读
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interpreting the yield curve*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

倒挂可反映未来短率下降预期和期限溢价变化，不能单独作为确定性衰退预言。政策、供需和避险流会同时移动曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inversion can reflect expectations of lower future short rates as well as changes in term premia; by itself it is not a deterministic recession forecast. Policy, supply and demand, and safe-haven flows can all move the curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析曲线变化时至少区分平行移动、变陡/变平和曲率变化。只报告“十年期上涨”会漏掉短端是否变化；只看 10y–2y 利差也可能漏掉中段隆起和不同现金流的真实暴露。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At minimum, distinguish parallel shifts, steepening or flattening, and curvature changes. Reporting only that the ten-year yield rose ignores what happened at the short end; looking only at the 10y–2y spread can miss a hump in the middle and the actual exposures of different cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把一只附息债的 YTM 当作整条无风险即期曲线，会错误折现中间现金流。
- 把远期率直接称为市场预期，会忽略期限溢价。
- 比较不同债券收益率却不控制信用、税收、流动性和嵌入期权，会把风险结构误写成期限结构。
- 看到倒挂便断言衰退，混淆了预测相关性、因果机制与不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating a coupon bond's YTM as the full risk-free spot curve misprices its intermediate cash flows.
- Calling a forward rate a market expectation without qualification ignores the term premium.
- Comparing yields on different bonds without controlling for credit, taxes, liquidity, and embedded options mistakes risk structure for term structure.
- Declaring a recession certain whenever the curve inverts confuses predictive association, causal mechanism, and uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 YTM 不等于未来每期实际回报？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is YTM not the realized return in every future period?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它是把当前价格与合同现金流匹配的内部收益率，实际持有回报还受出售价格、违约和息票再投资利率影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It is the internal rate that matches the current price to contractual cash flows; realized holding return also depends on sale price, default, and the rate at which coupons are reinvested.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 名义利率和实际利率如何联系？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How are nominal and real interest rates related?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 通过预期通胀：精确式 $(1+i)=(1+r)(1+\pi^e)$，小数值下近似相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Through expected inflation: the exact relation is $(1+i)=(1+r)(1+\pi^e)$, with an additive approximation for small rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 收益率曲线倒挂能否单独证明衰退将发生？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Can an inverted yield curve alone prove that a recession will occur?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。它综合未来短率预期、期限溢价、供需和政策信息，只是风险信号而非确定因果结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. It combines expectations of future short rates, term premia, supply and demand, and policy information; it is a risk signal, not a deterministic causal conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么远期率与预期未来短率不是同一个概念？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why is a forward rate not the same concept as an expected future short rate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 远期率由今天可交易价格的无套利关系确定；持有长期风险需要的期限补偿也会进入价格，所以还需剥离期限溢价才能谈纯预期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A forward rate is fixed by no-arbitrage relations among today's tradable prices. Compensation for bearing long-horizon risk also enters those prices, so the term premium must be separated before interpreting a pure expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
