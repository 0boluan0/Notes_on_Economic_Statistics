# 单期单层债务模型中债权人取得资产与债额的较小值而股东取得非负剩余

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a one-period, single-debt-class model, creditors receive the lesser of assets and promised debt, while shareholders receive the nonnegative residual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设到期时可分配的资产为 $V_T\ge0$，唯一一层债务到期应付 $F\ge0$。假定债权先于普通股清偿、股东[[有限责任]]、无外部担保和破产处置成本，也没有其他优先请求权，则到期分配为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let distributable terminal assets be $V_T\ge0$ and the sole debt class promise $F\ge0$. With debt senior to common equity, [[有限责任|limited liability]], no outside guarantee or bankruptcy costs, and no other prior claims, terminal distributions are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$D_T=\min(V_T,F),\qquad E_T=\max(V_T-F,0),\qquad D_T+E_T=V_T.$$

若 $F=80$，资产为 100 时债权人得 80、股东得 20；资产只有 50 时债权人得 50、股东得 0。股东不是先取一份再把剩下的给银行，债权人也不能从本模型中不存在的资源收回缺口。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $F=80$, assets of 100 pay creditors 80 and shareholders 20; assets of 50 pay creditors 50 and shareholders zero. Shareholders do not take a share before creditors, and creditors cannot recover a shortfall from resources absent from the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是状态给定后的到期分配，不是今天的债券或股票价格。多层优先权、担保、转股安排或处置成本会改变分配式；进一步给资产过程和贴现规则后才能进入[[Merton违约模型|结构化违约定价]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are state-contingent terminal distributions, not today's bond or stock prices. Multiple priorities, guarantees, conversion rights, or resolution costs change the formulas. An asset process and pricing assumptions are additionally required for [[Merton违约模型|structural credit valuation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
