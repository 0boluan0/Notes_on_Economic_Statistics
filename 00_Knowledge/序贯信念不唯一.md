# Kreps–Wilson 一致性限制路径外信念的可达来源，但一般不保证离轨信念唯一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Kreps–Wilson consistency restricts the reachable origins of off-path beliefs but does not generally make those beliefs unique*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 一致性是共同可达性条件，不是唯一选择规则
> [[序贯均衡]]要求所有 beliefs 来自同一列完全混合策略的 Bayes 极限。这个条件排除互不相容的 belief 拼接，却仍可能有多列合格的 trembles 以不同相对速度趋近同一极限策略，从而留下不同的离轨 posterior。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Sequential equilibrium requires every belief to arise from one common sequence of completely mixed strategies. This rules out incompatible patchworks of beliefs, but several admissible tremble sequences may approach the same limiting strategy at different relative rates and yield different off-path posteriors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设某个路径外信息集含两个节点，而一列完全混合策略使它们的到达概率分别为 $\varepsilon_m$ 与 $\delta_m$。Bayes 法则给出

$$
\mu_m(x_1\mid I)
=\frac{\varepsilon_m}{\varepsilon_m+\delta_m}.
$$

即使 $\varepsilon_m\to0$、$\delta_m\to0$，这个比值仍取决于两者消失的相对速度。若 $\varepsilon_m/\delta_m\to c$，极限 belief 为 $c/(1+c)$；不同的可行 $c$ 可以生成不同的 consistent beliefs，而极限策略都把该信息集留在路径外。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a two-node off-path information set, Bayes' rule weights the nodes by $\varepsilon_m/(\varepsilon_m+\delta_m)$. Both reach probabilities may vanish while their ratio approaches different positive limits. Different feasible relative tremble rates can therefore generate different consistent beliefs under the same limiting strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不表示 belief 可以逐信息集任意挑选：每一份候选 belief system 仍须由一列**共同**的完全混合策略同时生成，并与序贯理性一起检验。结论只是说 consistency 缩小可行集合，却通常不会自动把集合缩成一个点；若要唯一预测，还需要收益结构、信息结构或更强选择原则提供额外约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Beliefs are not freely chosen information set by information set: each candidate belief system must still be generated jointly by one completely mixed sequence and must support sequential rationality. The point is only that consistency can shrink the feasible set without reducing it to a singleton.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 两列完全混合策略都趋近同一极限策略，却让两个路径外节点的到达概率比分别趋近 $1:1$ 与 $3:1$。一致性是否迫使两列得到同一 posterior？
>
> **答案：** 不会。相应极限 posterior 分别是 $1/2$ 与 $3/4$；只要每列在整棵树上都共同生成一套 beliefs，两者都可能 consistent。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. The limiting posteriors are $1/2$ and $3/4$. Either can be consistent if its sequence jointly generates the full belief system.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
