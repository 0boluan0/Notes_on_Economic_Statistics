# Expectation and nonlinear transformations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*期望与非线性变换的顺序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transforming an average and averaging transformed values are different operations. The first keeps only one summary number before applying the function; the second uses the function's value at every possible outcome. In general, a nonlinear function cannot be moved outside an [[期望|expectation]], the probability-weighted average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对平均值作变换，与对变换后的数值求平均，是两种不同运算。前者先把数据压缩成一个平均数，再应用函数；后者使用每个可能结果对应的函数值。一般不能把非线性函数直接移到[[期望]]，即概率加权平均的外面。

> [!example] Average sign versus average square · 符号的平均与平方的平均
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the [[随机变量|random variable]] $X$ be $-1$ or $1$, each with probability $1/2$, and let $g(x)=x^2$. The signs cancel when averaged, but the squares are both one:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设[[随机变量]] $X$ 以各半概率取 $-1$、$1$，令 $g(x)=x^2$。平均时正负号抵消了，平方后两个数却都是一：
>
> $$g(E[X])=g(0)=0,\qquad E[g(X)]=\tfrac12g(-1)+\tfrac12g(1)=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All numbers are finite; the difference comes from the order of operations, not from an undefined expectation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两边都是有限数；差异来自运算顺序，而不是某个期望没有定义。

## Compute the transformed values with their probabilities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先算变换后的数值，再按概率加权*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume that $X$ is real and integrable, that $g$ is a measurable real-valued function on the relevant inputs, and that $g(X)$ is integrable. Also require $g(E[X])$ to be defined. These conditions make the two finite quantities meaningful, but do not make them equal. [[LOTUS|LOTUS]] gives the correct computation: for discrete values $x_i$ with probabilities $p_i$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设实随机变量 $X$ 可积，$g$ 是在相关输入上有定义的可测实值函数，并且 $g(X)$ 也可积；还要保证 $g(E[X])$ 有定义。这些条件让两项有限数值都有意义，但不会使它们自动相等。[[LOTUS]]给出正确算法：若离散取值 $x_i$ 的概率为 $p_i$，

$$E[g(X)]=\sum_i p_i g(x_i),\qquad
g(E[X])=g\!\left(\sum_i p_i x_i\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The probabilities stay attached to the original outcomes; what changes inside the first average is the score $g(x_i)$. In a continuous model with density $f_X$, LOTUS replaces the sum by $\int g(x)f_X(x)\,dx$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重仍然是原来各结果的概率；第一个平均中被改变的是分数 $g(x_i)$。在密度为 $f_X$ 的连续模型中，LOTUS 把求和换成 $\int g(x)f_X(x)\,dx$。

## What guarantees equality, and what can happen by coincidence?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么保证相等，什么只是特定情形下相等？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An affine rule $g(x)=ax+b$, with fixed real $a,b$, always commutes with expectation for integrable $X$. This follows from [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定实数 $a,b$ 构成的仿射规则 $g(x)=ax+b$，对可积的 $X$ 总能与期望交换。这直接来自[[期望线性性]]：

$$E[aX+b]=aE[X]+b=g(E[X]).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonlinear function can still give equality for a particular distribution. For the same symmetric sign variable, take $g(x)=x^3$: both $E[X^3]$ and $(E[X])^3$ are zero. Therefore “nonlinear” warns that there is no general interchange rule; it does not say every nonlinear example must give different answers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非线性函数仍可能对某个特定分布给出相等结果。对同一个对称符号变量，取 $g(x)=x^3$，则 $E[X^3]$ 与 $(E[X])^3$ 都为零。因此“非线性”是在提醒没有一般的交换规则，并不是说每个非线性例子都必然不相等。

> [!important] The exact gap for a square · 平方函数的精确差距
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $E[X^2]<\infty$, the [[方差|variance]] identity gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$E[X^2]-(E[X])^2=\operatorname{Var}(X)\ge0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equality holds exactly when $X$ equals its mean almost surely. The nonnegative squared deviation can have zero expectation only when it vanishes with probability one. This square-specific equality condition does not extend to every nonlinear function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $E[X^2]<\infty$ 时，差值就是[[方差]]，恰好在 $X$ 几乎必然等于其均值时取零：非负的平方偏差只有以概率一为零，期望才会为零。这是平方函数的相等条件，不能推广成所有非线性函数的相等条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If [[凹凸性|convexity or concavity]] is known, [[期望的Jensen不等式|Jensen's inequality]] supplies a direction under its stated domain and integrability assumptions. Without such structure there is no universal direction: the sign example gives $E[X^2]>(E[X])^2$, while $g(x)=-x^2$ reverses it. Checking the [[矩存在性的使用边界|existence of the required moments]] comes before comparing either expression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若已知函数的[[凹凸性]]，[[期望的Jensen不等式|Jensen不等式]]会在其定义域与可积性条件下给出比较方向。没有这样的结构时，不存在统一方向：符号例子中 $E[X^2]>(E[X])^2$，而换成 $g(x)=-x^2$ 就反过来。在比较之前，仍须先检查[[矩存在性的使用边界|所需矩是否存在]]。
