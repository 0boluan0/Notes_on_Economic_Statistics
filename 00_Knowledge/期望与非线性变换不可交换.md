# 一般不能把函数的期望换成均值的函数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An expectation generally cannot be interchanged with a nonlinear function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使所有相关期望有限，一般也有 $E[g(X)]\ne g(E[X])$。仿射 $g(x)=ax+b$ 保证相等，但非线性在特定分布下仍可能偶然相等，因此不能把“只有线性才能相等”当作一般判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even with finite expectations, a nonlinear function usually cannot be moved outside expectation. Affine functions guarantee equality, while particular nonlinear functions and distributions may also give equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
取 $X$ 为 $\pm1$ 各一半、$g(x)=x^2$：$E[g(X)]=1$，而 $g(E[X])=0$。对于平方函数，二者之差正是 $\operatorname{Var}(X)$，所以有限二阶矩下只有 $X$ 几乎处处为常数时相等。计算 $E[g(X)]$ 应用 [[LOTUS]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a symmetric sign variable, mean square is one but squared mean is zero. Their difference is variance, so equality for the square function requires an almost surely constant variable when the second moment is finite. [[LOTUS]] gives the correct calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
