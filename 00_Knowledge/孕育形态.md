# 孕育形态是趋势后第二根较小K线实体包含在第一根长实体内的组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A harami is a formation after a trend in which a smaller second real body lies within the first long real body.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

孕育形态是明确走势之后的一组两根[[K线]]：第一根是沿先前趋势方向的较长实体，第二根是完全处在前一实体内部的小实体。上涨后先长阳、下降后先长阴分别构成偏看跌、偏看涨的背景；第二根可阴可阳，不以颜色相反作为必要条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A harami consists of two [[K线|candlesticks]] after an identifiable trend: a long first body in the trend's direction followed by a small body entirely inside it. A long up candle after an advance provides a bearish context; a long down candle after a decline provides a bullish context. The second candle may be up or down.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较的是实体而非上下影线；例如前实体为 $[100,106]$、后实体为 $[102,103]$，满足实体包含关系。若是后来的大实体反过来覆盖前实体，应查看[[吞没形态]]。第二实体收缩可作为该学派对原趋势迟疑的描述，不能单凭它断定反转；它也不能提前至第二根收盘前被确认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare real bodies rather than shadows: a second body $[102,103]$ lies inside a first body $[100,106]$. A larger later body containing the earlier body belongs to [[吞没形态|engulfing]]. The shrinking body is interpreted as hesitation within the charting school, not sufficient proof of reversal, and is not final before the second candle closes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
