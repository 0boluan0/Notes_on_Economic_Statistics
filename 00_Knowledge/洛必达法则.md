# L'Hôpital's rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*洛必达法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $x$ approaches $1$, both $x^2-1$ and $x-1$ approach zero. That alone does not determine their ratio. Factoring gives $(x^2-1)/(x-1)=x+1$ for $x\ne1$, so the limit is $2$. L'Hôpital's rule offers another route: compare the [[导数|derivatives]], here $2x$ and $1$. Under the theorem's assumptions, the limit of their ratio gives the same answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x$ 趋近 $1$，$x^2-1$ 与 $x-1$ 都趋于零，但仅凭这一点不能确定它们的比值。因式分解给出：当 $x\ne1$，$(x^2-1)/(x-1)=x+1$，所以极限是 $2$。洛必达法则提供另一条路径：比较两个函数的[[导数]]，这里分别是 $2x$ 和 $1$。满足定理假设时，导数比的极限会给出同一个答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation [[零比零未定式|$0/0$]] describes two separate limits, not an allowed division by zero. A [[函数极限|function limit]] tests nearby inputs, so the functions need not even have values at the target point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[零比零未定式|$0/0$]] 记录两个分别趋于零的极限，并不是允许用零作除数。[[函数极限]]检验的是附近输入，因此函数甚至可以没有目标点处的值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Fix one direction of approach
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先固定趋近方向，再检查假设*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use real-valued functions $f,g$ on an interval leading to the target. For $x\to a^+$ this is $(a,a+r)$ with some $r>0$; for $x\to a^-$ it is $(a-r,a)$. At infinity use a tail $(A,\infty)$ or $(-\infty,A)$. All limits in one application must use **the same direction**. Primes denote derivatives with respect to $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取定义在通向目标的区间上的实值函数 $f,g$。对 $x\to a^+$，区间可取某个 $(a,a+r)$，其中 $r>0$；对 $x\to a^-$，取 $(a-r,a)$。对无穷远输入，取尾部 $(A,\infty)$ 或 $(-\infty,A)$。同一次应用中的全部极限必须采用**同一趋近方向**。撇号表示对 $x$ 求导。

> [!important] A sufficient version of the rule · 一组充分条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On the chosen interval, assume $f$ and $g$ are differentiable everywhere and $g'(x)\ne0$ everywhere. Assume either $f(x)\to0$ and $g(x)\to0$, or $|f(x)|\to\infty$ and $|g(x)|\to\infty$. If
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在所选区间上，假设 $f,g$ 处处可导，且 $g'(x)$ 处处非零。再假设 $f(x),g(x)$ 都趋于零，或者 $|f(x)|,|g(x)|$ 都趋于无穷。若
>
> $$\lim\frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}=L,\qquad L\in\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty,-\infty\},$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> then the original quotient is defined sufficiently near the target along this interval and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 则在所选方向充分靠近目标时，原比值有定义，并且
>
> $$\lim\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=L.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite $L$ is an ordinary real limit; $+\infty$ and $-\infty$ specify signed divergence. They are not numerical values to substitute into a fraction. Neither differentiability at the excluded endpoint nor continuity of $f'$ and $g'$ is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 有限 $L$ 是通常的实数极限；$+\infty,-\infty$ 表示带方向的发散，不是可以代入分式的数。不要求在被排除的端点可导，也不要求 $f',g'$ 连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the $0/0$ case, nonvanishing $g'$ prevents $g$ from being zero elsewhere on a sufficiently small one-sided interval; the proof below explains why. In the infinite case, $|g(x)|\to\infty$ already makes $g(x)$ nonzero eventually. For a finite **two-sided** limit, apply the result on both sides and use the [[双侧极限判定|two-sided criterion]]. Signed infinite limits also require the same sign on both sides; see [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|infinite limits and limits at infinity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $0/0$ 情形中，$g'$ 不为零会阻止 $g$ 在足够小的单侧区间中再次取零，原因见下面的证明。在无穷情形中，$|g(x)|\to\infty$ 本身已保证 $g(x)$ 最终非零。有限**双侧**极限需在两侧分别应用，再用[[双侧极限判定]]；带符号的无穷极限同样要求两侧符号一致，见[[无穷极限与无穷远极限]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the zero-over-zero case works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中间点把变化率与原比值连接起来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $x\to a^+$ with finite $a$. Temporarily extend the functions by $f(a)=g(a)=0$. Their assumed zero limits make these extensions [[一点连续|continuous at $a$]]; original point values, if any, are irrelevant to the limit. Fix $x>a$ in the interval. If $g(x)=0$, [[罗尔定理|Rolle's theorem]] on $[a,x]$ would give $g'=0$ somewhere inside, a contradiction. Hence $g(x)\ne0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看有限 $a$ 处的 $x\to a^+$。暂时补上 $f(a)=g(a)=0$；已知的零极限使这两个延伸函数在 $a$ [[一点连续|连续]]，原来若有别的点值，也与该极限无关。固定区间中的 $x>a$。若 $g(x)=0$，对 $[a,x]$ 使用[[罗尔定理]]，就会在内部得到 $g'=0$，与假设矛盾。因此 $g(x)\ne0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[柯西中值定理|Cauchy mean value theorem]] compares two endpoint changes with derivatives at a common intermediate point. It supplies some $c_x$ with $a<c_x<x$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[柯西中值定理]]把两个端点变化量与同一个中间点的导数联系起来。因此存在 $a<c_x<x$，使

$$\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac{f(x)-f(a)}{g(x)-g(a)}=\frac{f'(c_x)}{g'(c_x)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $x\to a^+$, the point $c_x$ is forced toward $a$ because $0<c_x-a<x-a$. If the derivative ratio is within $\varepsilon$ of a finite $L$ whenever its input is within a chosen radius, then the displayed equality gives the same bound for the original ratio whenever $x$ is in that radius. If the derivative ratio eventually exceeds every positive threshold, the equality gives the same statement for $f/g$; negative divergence is handled in the same way. This proves all three possible kinds of $L$. The left-sided proof reverses the interval endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x\to a^+$，由于 $0<c_x-a<x-a$，中间点 $c_x$ 也被迫趋向 $a$。若导数比在输入足够靠近 $a$ 时与有限 $L$ 的距离小于 $\varepsilon$，上面的等式就使原比值在 $x$ 进入同一半径后满足同样误差界。若导数比最终超过任意正阈值，等式也把这个结论传给 $f/g$；负向发散同理。这证明了三种 $L$。左侧证明只需交换区间端点的顺序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x\to\pm\infty$ in the $0/0$ case, put $t=1/x$, so $t\to0$ from the corresponding side. Set $F(t)=f(1/t)$ and $G(t)=g(1/t)$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives $F'(t)=-f'(1/t)/t^2$ and $G'(t)=-g'(1/t)/t^2$. Their common nonzero factor cancels, reducing the question to the finite-endpoint proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $0/0$ 情形中，若 $x\to\pm\infty$，令 $t=1/x$，则 $t$ 从对应一侧趋于零。设 $F(t)=f(1/t)$、$G(t)=g(1/t)$。[[链式法则]]给出 $F'(t)=-f'(1/t)/t^2$、$G'(t)=-g'(1/t)/t^2$。导数比中的共同非零因子约去，问题便回到有限端点的证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The infinite case uses a fixed reference input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无穷情形要控制一个固定参照点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $|g(x)|\to\infty$, do not assign an infinite endpoint value and subtract it. Instead, fix an allowed input $y$ sufficiently far along the chosen approach. For $x$ closer to the target than $y$, the Cauchy theorem gives a point $c$ between $x$ and $y$. The denominator difference is nonzero, since otherwise Rolle's theorem would contradict $g'\ne0$. Rearranging its ratio identity gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $|g(x)|\to\infty$，不能把端点值补成无穷再作减法。应固定一个沿所选方向已经足够接近目标的合法输入 $y$。对比 $y$ 更靠近目标的 $x$，柯西中值定理给出位于 $x,y$ 之间的 $c$。分母差不为零，否则罗尔定理会与 $g'\ne0$ 矛盾。把比值等式整理为

$$\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=H_{x,y}\left(1-\frac{g(y)}{g(x)}\right)+\frac{f(y)}{g(x)},
\qquad H_{x,y}=\frac{f'(c)}{g'(c)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite $L$, first choose $y$ so that every derivative ratio between $y$ and the target is within $\varepsilon/3$ of $L$. Thus $|H_{x,y}|\le |L|+\varepsilon/3$. Keep $y$ fixed and move $x$ toward the target: $g(y)/g(x)$ and $f(y)/g(x)$ tend to zero. Their two contributions to the error can each be made less than $\varepsilon/3$, giving $|f(x)/g(x)-L|<\varepsilon$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限 $L$，先选 $y$，使它与目标之间每个导数比都与 $L$ 相差不足 $\varepsilon/3$，于是 $|H_{x,y}|\le |L|+\varepsilon/3$。随后保持 $y$ 不动，让 $x$ 继续靠近目标；$g(y)/g(x)$ 与 $f(y)/g(x)$ 都趋于零。这两项造成的误差各自都能小于 $\varepsilon/3$，从而得到 $|f(x)/g(x)-L|<\varepsilon$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the derivative ratio tends to $+\infty$, fix any $M>0$ and choose $y$ so all those ratios exceed $2(M+1)$. For sufficiently advanced $x$, the factor $1-g(y)/g(x)$ is larger than $1/2$ and $|f(y)/g(x)|<1$, so $f(x)/g(x)>M$. Apply this argument to $-f$ for $L=-\infty$. Notice that this proof actually needs only $|g(x)|\to\infty$, not divergence of $f$; the familiar $\infty/\infty$ case stated above is a sufficient special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若导数比趋于 $+\infty$，固定任意 $M>0$，选 $y$ 使这些导数比都大于 $2(M+1)$。当 $x$ 足够靠近目标，因子 $1-g(y)/g(x)>1/2$，且 $|f(y)/g(x)|<1$，因此 $f(x)/g(x)>M$。对 $-f$ 使用同一论证，就得到 $L=-\infty$。注意，这段证明实际上只需要 $|g(x)|\to\infty$，不要求 $f$ 发散；上面陈述的常见 $\infty/\infty$ 只是一个充分特例。

## Equal limits are not equal functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 它替换的是比值极限，不是函数本身

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2,g(x)=x$, the original quotient is $x$ and the derivative quotient is $2x$ when $x\ne0$. They differ at every such input, but both tend to zero as $x\to0$. The theorem transfers a **limit** under its hypotheses; it does not assert $f/g=f'/g'$ and does not differentiate the quotient by the quotient rule. The [[洛必达适用边界|applicability boundary]] explains what an unsuccessful derivative-ratio calculation can and cannot establish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f(x)=x^2,g(x)=x$，当 $x\ne0$，原比值是 $x$，导数比是 $2x$。它们在这些输入处都不同，却在 $x\to0$ 时同趋于零。定理是在假设成立时传递一个**极限**，并不声称 $f/g=f'/g'$，也不是用商法则对整个比值求导。[[洛必达适用边界]]进一步说明：导数比计算未成功时，究竟能、不能断言什么。

## An exponential example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume the [[指数函数求导|exponential derivative]] $(e^x)'=e^x$ has already been established. As $x\to0$, the numerator $e^x-1$ and denominator $x$ both tend to zero; both are differentiable near zero and the denominator derivative is $1$. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设[[指数函数求导]]中的 $(e^x)'=e^x$ 已经独立建立。$x\to0$ 时，分子 $e^x-1$ 与分母 $x$ 都趋于零；两者在零附近可导，分母导数恒为 $1$。因此

$$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1}{x}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x}{1}=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If that very limit is being used to define or prove the exponential derivative, this calculation cannot serve as its first proof: it would assume the result being established.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果恰好要用这个极限来定义或证明指数函数的导数，这个计算就不能充当首次证明，因为那会提前使用正在建立的结论。
