# 若函数比满足洛必达法则的假设且导数比趋于有限值或正负无穷，则原函数比具有相同极限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If a quotient satisfies L'Hôpital's hypotheses and the derivative quotient tends to a finite value or signed infinity, then the original quotient has the same limit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 设 $f,g$ 在趋近目标点的相应去心单侧邻域内可导，且 $g'(x)\ne0$。若
> $$
> f(x)\to0,\qquad g(x)\to0,
> $$
> 或 $|f(x)|,|g(x)|\to\infty$，并且
> $$
> \lim\frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}=L,
> $$
> 其中 $L\in\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty,-\infty\}$，则
> $$
> \lim\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=L.
> $$
> 同样的陈述可用于 $x\to\pm\infty$，只需把去心邻域换成相应的无穷远尾部。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f$ and $g$ are differentiable on the relevant punctured one-sided neighbourhood and $g'(x)\ne0$. If both functions tend to zero, or both magnitudes tend to infinity, and $f'(x)/g'(x)\to L$ for $L\in\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty,-\infty\}$, then $f(x)/g(x)\to L$. The same form applies at $\pm\infty$ on the corresponding tail.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它替换的是比值极限，不是函数本身
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*It replaces a quotient limit, not the functions themselves*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理允许我们在假设满足时，把一个难算的**比值极限**转成导数比的极限。结论是两个极限相同，而不是说 $f(x)/g(x)$ 与 $f'(x)/g'(x)$ 在附近逐点相等。具体的适用检查见 [[洛必达适用边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem converts one quotient limit into the limit of the derivative quotient. It asserts equality of the limits, not pointwise equality of the two quotients. See [[洛必达适用边界|the applicability boundary]] for the checks required before using it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x\to0$ 时，$(e^x-1)/x$ 呈 $0/0$ 型。分子、分母在 $0$ 的去心邻域可导，且分母导数恒为 $1$，所以
$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1}{x}
=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x}{1}
=1.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $x\to0$, $(e^x-1)/x$ has form $0/0$. The hypotheses hold, so its limit equals $\lim_{x\to0}e^x=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 洛必达法则在假设满足时，究竟把哪个对象换成了哪个对象？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under its hypotheses, what does L'Hôpital's rule replace by what?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 把 $\lim f/g$ 的计算转成 $\lim f'/g'$；它不主张两个比值函数逐点相等。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** It converts the calculation of $\lim f/g$ into that of $\lim f'/g'$; it does not claim pointwise equality of the quotient functions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
