# 农业剩余是在给定消费分配规则下农业总产出超过留农劳动者消费的部分

农业剩余在[[费景汉–拉尼斯模型]]中指农业总产出扣除留农劳动者消费之后，可用于支持非农人口的农业产品量。它衡量产品剩余，不是[[剩余劳动力|农业中有多少可无损移出的劳动]]，也不是扣除了全部投入机会成本的企业利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[费景汉–拉尼斯模型|Fei–Ranis model]], total agricultural surplus is agricultural output left after the consumption of workers remaining in agriculture, available to support the non-agricultural population. It measures goods, not [[剩余劳动力|labour that can leave without output loss]], and is not business profit net of all input opportunity costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以农业产品的相同物量单位计，设总产出为 $Q_A$，留农者消费为 $C_A$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measured in consistent units of agricultural goods, with output $Q_A$ and remaining agricultural workers' consumption $C_A$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
TAS=Q_A-C_A.
$$

基准第一、二阶段把每位留农劳动者的制度食品收入固定为 $b$，于是 $C_A=bL_A$。在这个特定模型里，总人口直接按农业与工业劳动配置，不另设儿童、退休者或失业组。若引入其他消费者，必须同时改消费账户，不能只增加就业人数分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In baseline Phases I and II, institutional food income per remaining agricultural worker is fixed at $b$, giving $C_A=bL_A$. This particular model allocates population directly between agricultural and industrial labour without separate children, retirees or unemployed groups. Adding other consumers requires changing the consumption accounts as well, not merely changing an employment denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如农业总产出800，留农劳动80人、每人消费8，农业剩余为 $800-80\times8=160$。若转移劳动后产出750，留农60人而每人消费仍为8，剩余反而增为270：**农业总产出下降并不自动意味着农业剩余下降**，因为留农者消费也变了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With output 800, 80 agricultural workers and consumption of 8 each, surplus is $800-80\times8=160$. If labour transfer reduces output to 750 while leaving 60 workers consuming 8 each, surplus rises to 270. Falling agricultural output does not by itself imply falling agricultural surplus, because rural consumption also changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

超过[[农业短缺点与商业化点|商业化点]]后，农业工资与消费规则发生变化，不能继续无条件使用初始 $bL_A$。产品能否实际运出、出售或转移给工业人口，还需要相应安排；1961年基准模型忽略转运成本并假定全部剩余能够释放，这不是定义本身的保证。需要判断工业人口平均可获得多少食品时，再除以工业劳动者数，得到[[平均农业剩余]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Beyond the [[农业短缺点与商业化点|commercialization point]], agricultural wages and consumption arrangements change, so initial $bL_A$ cannot be used unconditionally. Transporting, marketing or transferring the goods to industrial workers also requires suitable arrangements. The 1961 baseline neglects transfer costs and assumes that the entire surplus can be released; the definition itself does not guarantee either. Divide by industrial labour to obtain [[平均农业剩余|average agricultural surplus]] when asking about food available per industrial worker.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
