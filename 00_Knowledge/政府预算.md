# 政府预算是政府在特定期间将政策优先事项转化为收入、支出和融资安排并依制度取得授权的计划
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A government budget translates policy priorities into revenue, expenditure and financing plans for a specified period, subject to the applicable authorisation process.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府预算把“政府准备做什么”落实为一定期间的资金安排：预计取得多少[[财政收入]]、安排哪些支出、存在融资需求时如何筹资。预算草案是待审议的计划；经规定程序批准的预算才具有相应授权效力，具体效力与程序取决于该制度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A government budget connects policy intentions to expected [[财政收入|revenue]], planned expenditure and financing needs. A proposal is a plan awaiting consideration; an approved budget carries the authority granted by the relevant institutional process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

年度预算可以放在[[中期预算框架]]内编制，但多年预测本身不等于每一年的支出授权。税收也可能依据持续有效的税法征收，社会保障支出可能由常设法律规定；不能把预算理解为所有政府活动都只能由同一部年度拨款法产生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An annual budget may sit within a [[中期预算框架|medium-term budget framework]], but a multi-year forecast is not annual spending authority. Standing tax and benefit legislation may operate alongside annual appropriations; institutional coverage must be identified explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阅读预算时，至少辨认期间、机构范围、记账基础，以及“草案、获批安排、执行结果”中的哪一种。最后一种对应[[预算支出与决算支出]]；借款则应按[[财政收入与融资]]区分，而不能因为带来现金就列作财政收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the period, institutional coverage, accounting basis and whether figures are proposed, approved or realised. See [[预算支出与决算支出|budgeted expenditure and outturn]] for the last distinction, and [[财政收入与融资|revenue versus financing]] for borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [OECD, Recommendation on Budgetary Governance](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/319/body-text.en.html)，前言及原则 2、6、7：支持预算与政策、授权、整体财政呈现和执行的连接。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The preamble and Principles 2, 6 and 7 support the links between policy, authorisation, comprehensive presentation and execution.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [OECD, Quality Budget Institutions, Chapter 9](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/quality-budget-institutions_8e811202-en/full-report/budget-transparency_ed612352.html)，§§9.2.2、9.3.1：支持草案/批准预算之别及拨款法以外的财政活动；这里给出通用定义，不规定某国法律效力。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Sections 9.2.2 and 9.3.1 support the proposal/approved-budget distinction and activities outside annual appropriations. The definition does not prescribe any country's legal arrangements.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
