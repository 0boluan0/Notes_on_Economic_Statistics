# 无财富效应且可实现同一唯一有效配置时初始权利改变分配而不改变最终活动量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Initial entitlements change distribution rather than final activity when wealth effects are absent and the same unique efficient allocation is attainable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“协商能达到有效配置”和“权利给谁都得到相同污染量”是两个强度不同的判断。后者需要初始权利和转移不改变相关边际估值，并且同一个有效数量在各初始状态下都能通过可行、可执行的交易达到；唯一性也不能省略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficient bargaining and identical emissions under every entitlement are distinct claims. The latter requires entitlements and transfers not to change relevant marginal valuations, the same efficient quantity to remain attainable, and uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设工厂 $B(q)=100q-q^2/2$，渔民损害 $D(q)=q^2/2$，双方准线性且资金足以完成转移。共同目标 $B-D$ 的唯一最优为 $q=50$。若工厂有权排放，无协议时它选100，渔民付钱促其减到50；若渔民有权禁止排放，无协议时为0，工厂付钱换取排放到50。数量可相同，但无协议处境、谁付款和谁取得协议收益不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $B(q)=100q-q^2/2$ and $D(q)=q^2/2$, assume quasi-linear preferences and sufficient funds for transfers. Joint returns have the unique optimum $q=50$. A factory entitled to emit chooses 100 without agreement and can be paid to reduce it; fishers entitled to prohibit emissions can be paid to allow 50 from a zero-emission baseline. Equal quantities do not imply equal distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财富效应为什么重要？设某人的效用为 $\log m-e$，减少污染0.1的最高支付满足 $\log(m-T)-(e-0.1)=\log m-e$，所以 $T=m(1-e^{-0.1})$。可支配金额为100时愿付约9.52，为200时愿付约19.03。若权利变化改变财富，同一环境改善的估值就可能改变。这一算例展示不变性需要排除的渠道，不是一套已经求解的产权均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For utility $\log m-e$, willingness to pay for a 0.1 pollution reduction is $T=m(1-e^{-0.1})$: about 9.52 at wealth 100 and 19.03 at wealth 200. If an entitlement changes wealth, valuation can change. This illustrates a channel excluded by invariance, not a fully solved entitlement equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，即使有明确产权，仍要分别检查[[科斯定理|协商效率条件]]、财富与支付能力、可交易范围和分配后果。不能把“分配改变”当成一句无须进一步核查的附带说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check [[科斯定理|efficient bargaining]], wealth and payment capacity, tradability and distribution separately. Distributional changes may feed back into the quantity comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
