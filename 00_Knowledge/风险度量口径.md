# 风险度量口径是解释 VaR 与 ES 时必须共同声明的损失对象、时间、概率、组合与估计约定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A risk-measure convention is the set of loss-object, timing, probability, portfolio, and estimation choices that must accompany any VaR or ES figure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 风险度量口径是一组让 VaR 或 ES 数字具有确定含义的约定：损失正负号、估值时点、未来持有期、置信水平、报告货币、头寸范围、损益定义、历史数据窗口和估计方法。少一个关键字段，两个金额相同的风险数字也可能不是同一个对象。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A risk-measure convention fixes the sign of loss, valuation time, future horizon, confidence level, reporting currency, position scope, P&L definition, historical data window, and estimation method. Without these fields, equal-looking risk figures may describe different objects.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X_{t_0,H}$ 表示从估值时点 $t_0$ 到未来 $H$ 期的 P&L，以下采用损失为正：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_{t_0,H}$ denote P&L from valuation time $t_0$ over the next $H$ periods. The convention below treats loss as positive:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L_{t_0,H}=-X_{t_0,H}.
$$

一个可比较的风险报告至少应声明

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A comparable risk report should at least specify
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathcal C=
(t_0,H,\alpha,\mathcal P,c,\text{P\&L definition},\mathcal W,\mathcal M),
$$

其中 $\mathcal P$ 是头寸范围，$c$ 是报告货币，$\mathcal W$ 是历史估计窗口，$\mathcal M$ 是历史模拟、方差—协方差法或 Monte Carlo 等估计方法。$H$ 回答未来损失累计多久，$\mathcal W$ 回答估计时用了多少过去数据；两者是不同设计轴。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathcal P$ is the position scope, $c$ the reporting currency, $\mathcal W$ the estimation window, and $\mathcal M$ the estimation method. The horizon $H$ states how long future loss accumulates; $\mathcal W$ states how much past data enters estimation. They are separate design choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两家交易台都报告“99% VaR 为 £10m”，但一方使用一日 HPL，另一方使用十日 APL。金额与置信水平相同并不使它们可直接比较，因为持有期和损益对象不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, two desks may each report 99% VaR of £10m, while one uses one-day HPL and the other ten-day APL. Equal amounts and confidence levels do not make the figures comparable because the horizons and P&L objects differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[绝对与相对VaR]] 区分基准选择，[[FRTB回测损益]] 区分 APL、HPL 与 RTPL 的监管角色。前者回答相对什么计算损失，后者回答用哪一种损益做监管比较；两种约定不能互相替代。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[绝对与相对VaR|Absolute and relative VaR]] distinguish the benchmark choice, while [[FRTB回测损益|FRTB backtesting P&L]] separates the regulatory roles of APL, HPL, and RTPL. The former asks relative to what loss is measured; the latter asks which P&L is used in a regulatory comparison. The two choices are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “99% VaR = £10m”还缺哪些信息，才足以稳定解释？
>
> **答案：** 至少还缺估值时点、未来持有期、损失符号、币种、头寸范围、损益定义、数据窗口和估计方法。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** What information is still missing from “99% VaR = £10m” before it can be interpreted consistently?
>
> **Answer:** At minimum: valuation time, horizon, loss sign, currency, position scope, P&L definition, data window, and estimation method.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 固定口径只使数字可解释，不证明模型正确或校准充分。
- 监管参数是特定用途的约束，不是 VaR 或 ES 的普遍数学定义。
- actual、hypothetical 与 risk-theoretical P&L 不能合并成一个未说明的损益对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A fixed convention makes a number interpretable; it does not validate the model.
- Regulatory parameters are use-specific constraints, not universal mathematical definitions.
- Actual, hypothetical, and risk-theoretical P&L must not be merged into an unspecified P&L object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Basel Framework, MAR10](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/MAR/10.htm)：核对 VaR、ES、APL、HPL 与 RTPL 的监管术语。
- [Basel Framework, MAR32](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/MAR/32.htm)：核对持有期、置信水平和损益对象必须与回测用途匹配。
- [Basel Committee, *An internal model-based approach to market risk capital requirements*](https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbs17.pdf)：核对头寸、风险因子数据、持有期、历史观察期和估计方法都会改变 VaR 数字。
