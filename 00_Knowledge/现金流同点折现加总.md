# 多笔现金流应逐笔折现到同一时点后相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discount each cash flow to one common date before adding them*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子算法
> 对时点 $t=0,1,\ldots,T$ 的现金流 $CF_t$，若相应贴现因子为 $DF_t$，共同评价时点的价值是
> $$V_0=\sum_{t=0}^{T}CF_tDF_t.$$
> 在单一每期有效利率 $r$ 的简化情形，$DF_t=(1+r)^{-t}$。每笔先换到时点 0，再做带正负号的加总。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> For cash flows $CF_t$ at dates $t=0,1,\ldots,T$ with matching discount factors $DF_t$, value at the common date is
> $$V_0=\sum_{t=0}^{T}CF_tDF_t.$$
> With one effective rate $r$ per period, $DF_t=(1+r)^{-t}$. Convert each cash flow to time 0 first, then add it with its sign.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该加总原则也适用于状态加权的未来支付：先按正确概率与贴现因子计算每种日期/状态的贡献，再求和。若期限、风险或币种不同导致贴现因子不同，就不能未经换算，给整串现金流套用同一个因子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same additivity applies to state-weighted future payoffs: calculate each date/state contribution using its correct probability and discount factor, then sum. If maturity, risk, or currency requires different factors, one common factor cannot be imposed without conversion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么一年后的 $100$ 和三年后的 $100$，不能直接按平均期限两年折现成 $200/(1+r)^2$？设 $r>0$。
>
> **答案：** 正确现值是 $100/(1+r)+100/(1+r)^3$；它比 $200/(1+r)^2$ 多出 $100r^2/(1+r)^3$。可以先算出正确现值，再反求一个等效期限 $k$，但 $k$ 不是未经计算的平均到期日。问题在于随意合并期限，而不是数学上不存在等效指数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can 100 due in one year and 100 due in three years not simply be discounted at their average maturity as $200/(1+r)^2$? Assume $r>0$.
>
> **Answer:** The correct value is $100/(1+r)+100/(1+r)^3$, which exceeds $200/(1+r)^2$ by $100r^2/(1+r)^3$. An equivalent maturity $k$ can be recovered from the correctly computed value, but it is not an uncalculated average due date. The error is assigning a common maturity arbitrarily, not the mathematical impossibility of an equivalent exponent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
