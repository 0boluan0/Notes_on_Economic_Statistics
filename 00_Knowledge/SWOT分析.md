# SWOT分析是区分内部优势劣势与外部机会威胁的战略分析框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*SWOT analysis distinguishes internal strengths and weaknesses from external opportunities and threats.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

SWOT 把与研究对象及目标有关的因素分为四类：内部优势（Strengths）、内部劣势（Weaknesses）、外部机会（Opportunities）和外部威胁（Threats）。优势劣势评价企业已有资源与能力；机会威胁评价企业所处环境的变化，不能只按“好消息／坏消息”而不区分内外。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SWOT classifies factors relevant to a specified organization and objective as strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, or threats. Strengths and weaknesses concern internal resources and capabilities; opportunities and threats concern the external environment. Sorting only by good versus bad news misses the internal/external distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，研究一家虚构餐饮软件公司：可靠的客服团队可列为优势，单一客户占收入过高可列为弱点，餐馆数字化需求增加可列为机会，竞争者推出免费替代品可列为威胁。是否真正重要仍取决于证据和影响大小；同一环境变化也可能对不同业务同时带来机会和威胁。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fictional restaurant-software company, reliable support may be a strength, concentrated customer revenue a weakness, increasing demand for digitization an opportunity, and a free competing product a threat. Evidence and materiality still matter. The same environmental change may create both opportunities and threats for different activities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四格清单不是因果模型、估值公式或完整证据检验。写下“品牌强”仍需解释客户行为与可持续性，进一步见[[护城河证据]]；分类整齐不能补偿事实遗漏或未经验证的判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four boxes are not a causal model, valuation formula, or complete evidence test. “Strong brand” still needs an explanation of customer behavior and durability; see [[护城河证据|testing durable-advantage claims]]. Neat classification cannot repair missing facts or untested judgments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
