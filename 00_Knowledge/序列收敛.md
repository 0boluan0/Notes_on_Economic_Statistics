# Sequence convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*序列收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The list $1,1/2,1/3,1/4,\ldots$ gets as close to $0$ as we ask, and then stays that close. For example, every term from the eleventh onward is less than $0.1$ from $0$; every term from the hundred-and-first onward is less than $0.01$ away. **Convergence** makes this promise for every positive tolerance, however small. It concerns the entire sufficiently late part of the list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数列 $1,1/2,1/3,1/4,\ldots$ 可以按要求任意靠近 $0$，并从此一直保持这么近。例如，从第11项起，每项距 $0$ 都不到 $0.1$；从第101项起，每项距离都不到 $0.01$。**收敛**要求对每个正的容许误差都能作出这样的保证，无论误差多小。它控制的是足够靠后的全部项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sequence, a distance and a proposed limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*序列、距离与候选极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sequence $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ is an ordered infinite list: $x_n$ is the term at position $n$, and values may repeat. Work in a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$, where every term belongs to $X$ and $d$ measures distance. A proposed limit $x$ must also belong to $X$. The open ball $B_\varepsilon(x)=\{y\in X:d(y,x)<\varepsilon\}$ consists of points within a specified positive tolerance $\varepsilon$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

序列 $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ 是按顺序排列的无限列表：$x_n$ 是第 $n$ 项，取值可以重复。在[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$ 中讨论时，每一项都属于 $X$，距离由 $d$ 测量。候选极限 $x$ 也必须属于 $X$。开球 $B_\varepsilon(x)=\{y\in X:d(y,x)<\varepsilon\}$ 收集距离小于给定正容许误差 $\varepsilon$ 的点。

> [!important] Eventually every term · 最终每一项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> We write $x_n\to x$ and say that the sequence **converges to $x$** if
> $$\forall\varepsilon>0\quad\exists N\ge1\quad\forall n\ge N:\quad d(x_n,x)<\varepsilon,$$
> where $N$ and $n$ are integers. Equivalently, the real numbers $d(x_n,x)$ tend to $0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若对每个 $\varepsilon>0$ 都能找到整数 $N\ge1$，使每个整数 $n\ge N$ 都满足 $d(x_n,x)<\varepsilon$，就记作 $x_n\to x$，称序列**收敛到 $x$**。等价地，实数序列 $d(x_n,x)$ 趋于 $0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order of the choices matters. First someone prescribes $\varepsilon$; then we choose a cutoff $N$, which may depend on that tolerance. After this choice, the bound must hold for **every** later index. For $x_n=1/n$ in the usual real line, choosing any integer $N>1/\varepsilon$ works, since $n\ge N$ gives $1/n\le1/N<\varepsilon$. There is no requirement that one cutoff work for all positive tolerances at once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择的顺序很重要：先给定 $\varepsilon$，再选门槛 $N$；$N$ 可以随容许误差改变。选定后，条件必须对**每个**更靠后的索引成立。对通常实数距离下的 $x_n=1/n$，任选整数 $N>1/\varepsilon$ 即可，因为 $n\ge N$ 时有 $1/n\le1/N<\varepsilon$。不要求一个门槛同时适用于所有正容许误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the definition does and does not require
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定义要求什么，又不要求什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limit need not be an actual term: every $1/n$ is positive, although its limit is $0$. The terms need not move in one direction: $(-1)^n/n$ alternates between negative and positive values, but its distance from $0$ is still $1/n$. Convergence requires eventual closeness, not monotone movement or arrival at a final position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限不必等于某一项：每个 $1/n$ 都为正，极限却是 $0$。各项也不必朝同一方向移动：$(-1)^n/n$ 在正负两侧交替，但距 $0$ 仍为 $1/n$。收敛要求最终任意接近，不要求单调移动，也不要求抵达某个最后位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing any finite number of terms does not change convergence or its limit. If the changed positions are at most $K$, choose the new cutoff to be $\max\{N,K+1\}$. Beyond it, the old and new sequences agree and the same distance bound holds. Thus replacing the first two terms of $1/n$ by $1000$ and $-1000$ does not change the limit $0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改变有限多项，不会改变是否收敛及其极限。若改变的位置不超过 $K$，把新门槛取成 $\max\{N,K+1\}$ 即可；之后新旧序列相同，原距离界仍成立。因此，把 $1/n$ 的前两项改成 $1000$ 与 $-1000$，极限仍为 $0$。

> [!warning] Repeated visits are not enough · 反复进入还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sequence $(-1)^n$ returns to $1$ infinitely often, but also leaves it infinitely often. A ball of radius $1/2$ about $1$ misses every odd-indexed term, no matter how late the cutoff is. Returning arbitrarily late concerns a [[聚点不等于收敛|cluster point or a subsequence]]; convergence requires all sufficiently late terms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $(-1)^n$ 无穷多次回到 $1$，也无穷多次离开它。以 $1$ 为中心、半径 $1/2$ 的球漏掉每个奇数索引项，无论门槛多靠后。任意晚的时候还能回来，讨论的是[[聚点不等于收敛|聚点或子序列]]；整列收敛要求足够靠后的全部项。

## The ambient space and its neighbourhoods
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*环境空间与邻域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x_n=1/(n+1)$, every term lies in $X=(0,1)$. In the larger usual real line the limit is $0$, but $0\notin X$, so it cannot be a limit **in $X$**. There is no other limit in $X$: for any proposed $x>0$, eventually $x_n<x/2$, so $|x_n-x|>x/2$. The permitted ambient points are part of the convergence claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x_n=1/(n+1)$，每一项都在 $X=(0,1)$ 中。在更大的通常实数线中，极限为 $0$；但 $0\notin X$，因此它不能是在 **$X$ 中**的极限。$X$ 中也没有其他极限：对任意候选 $x>0$，最终 $x_n<x/2$，于是 $|x_n-x|>x/2$。允许哪些点作为环境成员，是收敛陈述的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distance matters as well. On the same set $\mathbb R$, use the discrete metric: different points have distance $1$. The sequence $1/n$ then fails to converge to $0$, because no term enters the radius-$1/2$ ball $\{0\}$. This changes the neighbourhood structure, not just the units used to report distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

距离规则也重要。在同一个集合 $\mathbb R$ 上改用离散度量，即不同点的距离都为 $1$，则 $1/n$ 不再收敛到 $0$，因为没有一项进入半径 $1/2$ 的球 $\{0\}$。这里改变的是邻域结构，不只是距离的计量单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, an [[开集|open neighbourhood]] of $x$ is an open set containing $x$. Convergence can be expressed as eventual membership in every such neighbourhood. In a metric space this is equivalent to the ball definition: balls are open, and every open neighbourhood contains a ball about $x$. This also defines convergence in a general [[拓扑|topological space]], where no distance may be given. Consequently, [[拓扑等价度量|metrics inducing the same open sets]] give the same convergent sequences and limits, although their numerical error sizes and rates can differ. [[序列极限唯一|Uniqueness of the limit]] is a separate theorem for metric spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，$x$ 的[[开集|开邻域]]是包含 $x$ 的开集。收敛可以表述为最终属于每个这样的邻域。在度量空间中，这与开球定义等价：球本身开，而每个开邻域都包含一个以 $x$ 为中心的球。一般[[拓扑|拓扑空间]]即使没有数值距离，也用这一邻域条件定义收敛。因此，[[拓扑等价度量|诱导相同开集的度量]]给出相同的收敛序列及极限，数值误差大小和收敛速率却可能不同。[[序列极限唯一]]则是度量空间中的另一个定理。
