# 净经营资产净利率是税后经营净利润与匹配净经营资产存量的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return on net operating assets is NOPAT divided by a matched net-operating-asset stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净经营资产净利率（RNOA）衡量经营净占用产生税后经营利润的能力：$RNOA=NOPAT/NOA^*$。分子用[[税后经营净利润]]，分母用与其业务范围一致的[[净经营资产]]；通常要求 $NOA^*>0$，并明确采用期初、期末还是平均存量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
RNOA measures after-tax operating profit relative to net operating investment: $RNOA=NOPAT/NOA^*$. Match [[税后经营净利润|NOPAT]] to the business scope of [[净经营资产|NOA]], normally require positive NOA, and state whether the stock is opening, ending, or average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA 本例使用期末 NOA，ABC 为 $206.72/1722\approx12.005\%$。插入同一期间的非零收入 $S$，可写成 $(NOPAT/S)(S/NOA^*)$：一部分是经营销售的税后利润率，一部分是净经营占用的周转。这有助于沿利润和资产两侧追问变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPA uses ending NOA, giving ABC approximately 12.005%. For nonzero period revenue, the ratio factors into $(NOPAT/S)(S/NOA^*)$: after-tax operating margin and net operating asset turnover. This directs analysis toward both profit and asset use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它与[[投入资本回报率]]属于同一经营利润对经营资本的分析思路；若资本范围和日期完全一致，数值也可相同。但本课程的期末 NOA 与增长模型的期初 IC 不能仅凭名称相近就互换。把这个经营回报接回股东回报，需要[[管理用ROE分解]]中的净融资成本和杠杆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
RNOA and [[投入资本回报率|ROIC]] share the idea of operating profit relative to operating capital and can coincide when scope and timing coincide. The course's ending NOA cannot silently replace a growth model's opening invested capital. [[管理用ROE分解|The operating-financing ROE decomposition]] connects operating return to equity return through financing cost and leverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
