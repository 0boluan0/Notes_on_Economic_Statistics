# 评估政策效应应区分固定行为的机械变化与行为调整带来的变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Policy evaluation distinguishes mechanical changes at fixed behaviour from changes caused by behavioural responses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

机械效应是在其他行为保持原样时，政策规则直接改变的数额；行为效应则来自家庭、企业或其他参与者对新规则的反应。第一步便于建立基准，但第二步可能改变总成本、受益人数或收入，不能把机械核算当成完整预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mechanical effect is the change produced by a policy rule while behaviour is held fixed. A behavioural effect comes from households, firms, or other participants responding to the new rule. The first step establishes a useful benchmark; the second can change total costs, participation, or revenues, so mechanical accounting is not a complete forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以自拟的简单工资税模型为例：税前时薪 $w$ 固定，税率为 $t$，应税劳动时数为 $L(t)$。若 $L$ 在考察的内点税率处可微，税收及其局部边际变化为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an original, simple wage-tax example. The pre-tax hourly wage $w$ is fixed, the tax rate is $t$, and taxable hours are $L(t)$. If $L$ is differentiable at the interior tax rate considered, revenue and its local marginal change are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
T(t)=twL(t),\qquad
\frac{dT}{dt}=\underbrace{wL(t)}_{\text{机械项}}+
\underbrace{twL'(t)}_{\text{行为项}}.
$$

第一项假装工时不变；第二项计入工时变化对税基的影响。只有知道[[劳动供给效应|工时反应]]，才能估计两项之和。若税率变动也影响税前工资、逃避税或收入形式，这个小模型就要扩展，不能把它漏掉的渠道解释为不存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term holds hours fixed; the second captures the hours response's effect on the tax base. Estimating their sum requires information about the [[劳动供给效应|hours response]]. If the tax change also affects pre-tax wages, evasion, or the form of income, the model must be extended rather than treating omitted channels as absent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际应用中，可以先报告“按原有行为计算”的结果，再明确加入哪些行为渠道及其证据。财政收入的变化也不等于社会净福利变化：税款跨主体的流动还要按[[转移支付核算]]处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an application, report the fixed-behaviour calculation first, then specify the response channels included and the evidence for them. A revenue change is not itself a change in net social welfare: the movement of tax payments across parties also requires [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
