# One-sided inverses of square matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方阵单侧逆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite square matrix, checking recovery on one side is enough. If multiplying a candidate on the left gives the identity matrix, it will also give the identity on the right; the same works with the sides reversed. The reason is that a square matrix has equally many input and output coordinates. This is a theorem about finite dimensions, not permission to swap arbitrary matrix factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限阶方阵，只核对一侧的恢复就足够：候选矩阵从左侧相乘得到单位矩阵，就自动从右侧相乘也得到单位矩阵；反过来也一样。关键在于方阵的输入、输出坐标数相同。这是一条有限维定理，不是允许随意交换矩阵因子。

> [!example] One check settles the other · 核对一侧就能确定另一侧
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad BA=I_2.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The calculation shows that $B$ undoes $A$ for every input. Because both matrices are $2\times2$, the theorem guarantees $AB=I_2$ too. Direct multiplication confirms it, but the theorem works for any finite size, without a second full product calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个计算说明，$B$ 对每个输入都能撤销 $A$。因为两个矩阵都是 $2\times2$，定理保证 $AB=I_2$ 也成立。直接相乘可以确认这一点，但定理适用于任意有限阶数，无须再完整计算一次乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[左逆|left inverse]] satisfies $BA=I_n$ and recovers every input after $A$ acts. A [[右逆|right inverse]] satisfies $AC=I_n$ and supplies an input for every requested output. An ordinary [[逆矩阵|inverse]] does both. Here $I_n$ denotes the identity, which changes no vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[左逆]]满足 $BA=I_n$，在 $A$ 作用后恢复每个输入；[[右逆]]满足 $AC=I_n$，为每个目标输出提供一个输入。普通[[逆矩阵]]兼具两者。这里 $I_n$ 是不改变任何向量的单位矩阵。

> [!abstract] The finite-square theorem · 有限阶方阵的定理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix, with $n$ a positive integer. If a same-size matrix $B$ satisfies $BA=I_n$, then $AB=I_n$ and $B=A^{-1}$. Likewise, $AC=I_n$ implies $CA=I_n$ and $C=A^{-1}$. Entries may be real or complex; the argument uses only linear algebra over their scalar field.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A$ 是 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n$ 为正整数。若同尺寸矩阵 $B$ 满足 $BA=I_n$，则 $AB=I_n$，且 $B=A^{-1}$。同样，$AC=I_n$ 推出 $CA=I_n$ 与 $C=A^{-1}$。元素可以是实数或复数；论证只使用相应标量域上的线性代数。

## 为什么另一侧自动成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $BA=I_n$. If $Ax=0$, then $x=BAx=B0=0$. Thus no nonzero input is lost: the [[零空间|null space]] consists only of zero. Equivalently, the $n$ columns of $A$ are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]—no nontrivial combination of them is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $BA=I_n$。如果 $Ax=0$，那么 $x=BAx=B0=0$。因此没有非零输入被丢失：[[零空间]]只有零向量。等价地，$A$ 的 $n$ 列[[线性无关]]，不存在系数不全为零却组合出零向量的情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These $n$ independent columns live in the $n$-dimensional output space, so they form a [[基|basis]] and span every possible output. In [[矩阵秩|rank]] language, $A$ has full column rank $n$ and, because it is square, full row rank $n$ as well. This is the point where equal finite dimensions matter. The [[右逆与满行秩|full-row-rank criterion]] supplies a right inverse $C$ with $AC=I_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这 $n$ 个独立的列处在 $n$ 维输出空间中，因此构成一组[[基]]，能够张成每个输出。用[[矩阵秩|秩]]来表达，$A$ 满列秩、秩为 $n$；因为是方阵，也就满行秩。相同的有限维数正是在这一步起作用。由[[右逆与满行秩]]，存在右逆 $C$ 满足 $AC=I_n$。

$$
B=BI_n=B(AC)=(BA)C=I_nC=C.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equality chain shows that the original left inverse is this right inverse, hence $AB=I_n$. Starting with $AC=I_n$ instead, every output equals $A(Cb)$, so $A$ has full row rank. Square dimensions make it full column rank; the [[左逆与满列秩|left-inverse criterion]] supplies a left inverse, and the same chain identifies it with $C$. In either case [[逆矩阵唯一性|uniqueness]] follows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等式链说明原来的左逆就是这个右逆，因此 $AB=I_n$。若从 $AC=I_n$ 出发，每个输出都能写成 $A(Cb)$，故 $A$ 满行秩；方阵使其也满列秩，再由[[左逆与满列秩]]得到左逆，同样的等式链迫使它等于 $C$。两种情况下，[[逆矩阵唯一性|逆的唯一性]]都成立。

## 条件边界

> [!warning] Different dimensions break the implication · 输入输出维数不同，结论会失效
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=(1,0)^T$ and $L=(1,0)$, we have $LA=I_1$, but $AL=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\ne I_2$. The left inverse recovers every one-coordinate input, while the two-coordinate output $(0,1)^T$ cannot be reached. Transposing this example gives a right inverse that is not a left inverse. The identity sizes differ because the matrix is rectangular.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=(1,0)^T$、$L=(1,0)$，则 $LA=I_1$，但 $AL=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\ne I_2$。左逆可以恢复每个一维输入，却无法让二维输出 $(0,1)^T$ 变得可达。把这个例子转置，就得到只有右逆而非左逆的情形。单位矩阵尺寸不同，正是因为原矩阵是矩形的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Infinite-dimensional linear operators are also outside this finite-matrix theorem. For finite matrices over any other scalar field, the same basis and identity arguments still work; no ordering, length or inner product was used. In a practical $4\times4$ check, $BA=I_4$ therefore establishes $AB=I_4$ exactly; numerical multiplication can still be used to check arithmetic errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无限维线性算子也不在这条有限矩阵定理的范围内。对其他标量域上的有限矩阵，同样的基与恒等式论证仍然成立，未使用大小次序、长度或内积。实际核对 $4\times4$ 矩阵时，$BA=I_4$ 因而已经严格推出 $AB=I_4$；数值相乘仍可用于排查算术错误。
