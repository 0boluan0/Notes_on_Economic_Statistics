# 方阵只要存在一侧逆就存在唯一双侧逆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A square matrix with a one-sided inverse has a unique two-sided inverse*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心定理
> 对有限维方阵 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，若存在 $B$ 使 $BA=I_n$，或存在 $C$ 使 $AC=I_n$，则 $A$ 可逆。相应的单侧逆必等于唯一的普通逆 $A^{-1}$，并自动在另一侧也成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a finite square matrix $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, either $BA=I_n$ or $AC=I_n$ implies that $A$ is invertible. The corresponding one-sided inverse equals the unique ordinary inverse $A^{-1}$ and automatically works on the other side.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么另一侧自动成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the identity on the other side follows automatically*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $BA=I_n$ 可知 $A$ 有左逆，所以 $A$ 满列秩。对方阵而言，满列秩就是满行秩，因此 $A$ 还存在某个右逆 $C$。于是
$$
B=B(AC)=(BA)C=C,
$$
同一个矩阵同时是左右逆。由 $AC=I_n$ 出发的证明完全对称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $BA=I_n$, then $A$ has a left inverse and therefore full column rank. For a square matrix, full column rank is also full row rank, so some right inverse $C$ exists. Hence $B=B(AC)=(BA)C=C$, and the same matrix is both a left and a right inverse. The proof starting from $AC=I_n$ is symmetric.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary of the theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“方阵”和“有限维”都是定理条件。矩形矩阵可能只有左逆或只有右逆，分别见 [[左逆]] 与 [[右逆]]；无限维线性算子也不属于这里的有限矩阵结论。普通逆为什么至多只有一个，则由 [[逆矩阵唯一性]] 单独说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both “square” and “finite-dimensional” are conditions of the theorem. A rectangular matrix may have only a [[左逆|left inverse]] or only a [[右逆|right inverse]], and infinite-dimensional operators lie outside this finite-matrix result. The separate theorem on [[逆矩阵唯一性|uniqueness of the ordinary inverse]] explains why a two-sided inverse is unique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 若 $A,B\in\mathbb R^{4\times4}$ 且 $BA=I_4$，还需要单独计算 $AB$ 才能判断它是否为 $I_4$ 吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A,B\in\mathbb R^{4\times4}$ and $BA=I_4$, must we separately compute $AB$ to decide whether it equals $I_4$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不需要。单侧逆已经推出 $A$ 满秩，因此 $B=A^{-1}$ 并有 $AB=I_4$；直接相乘仍可作为数值验算。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The one-sided inverse already implies that $A$ has full rank, so $B=A^{-1}$ and $AB=I_4$. Direct multiplication may still be used as a numerical check.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
