# 李嘉图等价是在固定政府购买路径和完整条件下税收融资时点不改变私人消费的中性命题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardian equivalence is neutrality of tax-financing timing for private consumption under a fixed spending path and a complete set of conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

李嘉图等价指：政府购买路径不变时，今天少征税、举债并在未来增税，若没有改变家庭完整的跨期可行集合，就不改变其最优消费路径。债券是私人资产，但家庭同时计入与之对应的未来税负；不能只看到当前减税而把它全部当成新增净财富。这不是“政府购买怎样变化都无效”的命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardian equivalence states that replacing current taxes with debt and future taxes leaves optimal consumption unchanged if government purchases and the household's complete intertemporal feasible set are unchanged. Households count both bond assets and corresponding future taxes rather than treating the tax cut as net new wealth. The proposition does not make changes in government purchases irrelevant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个充分条件基准是：税收为不改变边际激励的一次总付税；未来税负已知且由当前家庭或其有效联系的后代承担；家庭能按比较中同一利率自由借贷，没有约束变化；偏好、禀赋、初始资产和政府购买固定。基准也不引入违约、通胀再分配或债券额外流动性服务等渠道。条件的作用是保证预算集合与其他约束相同，而不只是某个税款现值碰巧相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sufficient benchmark uses lump-sum taxes, known future liabilities internalised by the household or an effectively linked dynasty, unrestricted borrowing and lending at the same comparison rate, and unchanged preferences, endowments, initial assets and public purchases. It also excludes channels such as default, inflation redistribution or extra liquidity services from bonds. The conditions preserve the complete feasible set, not merely one tax present value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用今天 $0$、明年 $1$ 的两期式说明。初始资产为零，毛利率 $R=1+r>0$，家庭收入为 $y_0,y_1$，一次总付税为 $T_0,T_1$。[[现金流同点折现加总|折到今天]]的约束是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For dates $0$ and $1$, let initial assets be zero, the gross rate be $R=1+r>0$, income be $y_0,y_1$, and lump-sum taxes be $T_0,T_1$. The constraint [[现金流同点折现加总|valued at date zero]] is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
c_0+\frac{c_1}{R}
=y_0-T_0+\frac{y_1-T_1}{R}.
$$

政府将当期税减少 $D$，并把下期税增加 $RD$，保持购买路径和偿付安排一致；政府一侧的限制见[[政府跨期预算约束]]。家庭现值资源变化为 $D-RD/R=0$，其余条件不变便得到同一消费选择；若最优解唯一，路径完全相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The government reduces current taxes by $D$ and raises next-period taxes by $RD$, consistently with the fixed purchase path and debt service; see the [[政府跨期预算约束|government intertemporal budget constraint]]. Household present-value resources change by $D-RD/R=0$. With all other conditions unchanged, optimal consumption choices are unchanged; a unique optimum gives the same path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $y_0=100,y_1=110,R=1.1$，政府只在今天购买 $10$。税款 $(10,0)$ 与 $(0,11)$ 均留下现值资源 $190$。若家庭最大化 $\ln c_0+\ln c_1$，一阶条件给 $c_1=1.1c_0$，两方案均有 $c_0=95,c_1=104.5$。当期私人储蓄 $s_0=y_0-T_0-c_0$ 却从 $-5$ 变为 $5$，增加 $10$，恰好抵消政府储蓄减少 $10$。这里允许借款；负储蓄不是计算错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $y_0=100,y_1=110,R=1.1$ and current public purchases be $10$, with none next year. Taxes $(10,0)$ and $(0,11)$ both leave resources of present value $190$. Maximising $\ln c_0+\ln c_1$ gives $c_1=1.1c_0$, hence $c_0=95,c_1=104.5$ in both cases. Current private saving $s_0=y_0-T_0-c_0$ rises from $-5$ to $5$, offsetting public dissaving of $10$. Borrowing is allowed, so negative saving is intentional.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个基准最适合用来定位具体破坏渠道：[[借贷约束与税收时点]]改变当前消费可行性；[[代际联系与债务中性]]检验未来纳税人是否进入当前家庭计划。发现某个基准条件不成立，只是否定这套充分条件的直接适用，不能不加模型就断定消费必然增加多少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the benchmark to identify precise channels: [[借贷约束与税收时点|borrowing constraints]] can change current feasibility, while [[代际联系与债务中性|intergenerational links]] determine whether future taxpayers enter the current plan. Failure of a sufficient condition does not by itself establish the sign or magnitude of a consumption response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
