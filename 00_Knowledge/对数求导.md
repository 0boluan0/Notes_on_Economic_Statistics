# 对数求导先对正值关系取对数以拆开乘积商和移动指数再求回原函数导数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Logarithmic differentiation first takes logarithms of a positive relation to separate products, quotients, and moving exponents, then recovers the original derivative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对已知在所考察区间为正的函数 $y=u(x)$，对数求导先写
> $$
> \ln y=\ln u(x),
> $$
> 利用对数律拆开乘积、商和幂，再求导得到 $y'/y$，最后乘回 $y$。它是组合已有规则的方法，不是一条取消定义域检查的新公式。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a function $y=u(x)$ known to be positive on the interval of interest, logarithmic differentiation writes $\ln y=\ln u(x)$, uses logarithm laws to separate products, quotients, and powers, differentiates to obtain $y'/y$, and finally multiplies back by $y$. It combines existing rules; it does not waive domain checks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

典型例子是 $y=x^x$，在 $x>0$ 上
$$
\ln y=x\ln x,\qquad
\frac{y'}y=\ln x+1,\qquad
y'=x^x(1+\ln x).
$$
直接套“幂法则”会漏掉指数也在变化；直接套“指数函数求导”又会漏掉底数在变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y=x^x$ on $x>0$, $\ln y=x\ln x$, so $y'/y=\ln x+1$ and $y'=x^x(1+\ln x)$. The ordinary power rule misses the moving exponent, while the fixed-base exponential rule misses the moving base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若函数只保证非零而不保证正，可在不跨零点的局部区间使用 $\ln|y|$；若底数为负且指数为一般实函数，表达式本身通常没有连续的实值定义，不能靠绝对值机械修复。最后应把 $y$ 换回原表达式并保留原定义域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the function is merely nonzero, $\ln|y|$ may be used on a local interval that does not cross a zero. A negative moving base with a general real exponent usually has no continuous real-valued definition and cannot be repaired mechanically by absolute values. Substitute the original expression back at the end and retain its domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $x^x$ 不能直接用 $d(x^r)/dx=rx^{r-1}$？
>
> **答案：** 幂法则把指数 $r$ 当常数；在 $x^x$ 中底数和指数都随 $x$ 变化。
