# Logarithmic differentiation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对数求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Logarithmic differentiation** makes certain derivatives easier by first rewriting a function with a logarithm. Products become sums, quotients become differences, and an exponent moves in front of a logarithm. After differentiating this simpler expression, we recover the derivative of the original function. It is especially useful when both the base and the exponent vary, as in $x^x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**对数求导**先用对数改写函数，让某些导数更容易计算：乘积变成和，商变成差，指数移到对数前面。对这个较简单的表达式求导之后，再恢复原函数的导数。当底数和指数都在变化时，例如 $x^x$，这个方法尤其有用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why differentiating a logarithm helps
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对数的导数为什么有用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a differentiable function $y=h(x)>0$ on the interval being considered. The [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] is then defined, and [[对数函数求导|its derivative together with the chain rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先考虑在所研究区间上可导且为正的函数 $y=h(x)>0$。此时[[自然对数]]有定义，由[[对数函数求导|对数导数和链式法则]]得到

$$\frac{d}{dx}\ln y=\frac{y'}y,
\qquad
y'=y\frac{d}{dx}\ln y.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $y$ depends on $x$, so the derivative on the left is not simply $1/y$: it includes the inner derivative $y'$. The ratio $y'/y$ measures change relative to the current value. Thus the method computes this relative rate first, then multiplies by the original value $y$. Finally substitute $h(x)$ back for $y$ and keep its original domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $y$ 依赖于 $x$，左侧的导数不能只写成 $1/y$，还要包含内层导数 $y'$。比值 $y'/y$ 衡量相对于当前函数值的变化率。因此，这个方法先求相对变化率，再乘上原来的函数值 $y$。最后把 $y$ 换回 $h(x)$，并保留原定义域。

> [!example] Both parts of x^x move · x^x 的底数和指数都在变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x>0$, set $y=x^x=\exp(x\ln x)$. This expression is positive and differentiable by [[指数函数求导|exponential differentiation]] and the [[链式法则|chain rule]]. Taking logarithms gives $\ln y=x\ln x$. The right-hand side is a product of two changing functions, so use the [[乘积法则|product rule]]:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x>0$ 上令 $y=x^x=\exp(x\ln x)$。由[[指数函数求导]]和[[链式法则]]，该表达式为正且可导。取对数后得到 $\ln y=x\ln x$；右侧的两个因子都在变化，因此使用[[乘积法则]]：
>
> $$\frac{y'}y=1\cdot\ln x+x\cdot\frac1x=\ln x+1,
> \qquad y'=x^x(1+\ln x).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $x=1$, the derivative is $1$; at $x=2$, it is $4(1+\ln2)\approx6.773$. The [[实数幂求导|ordinary power rule]] holds the exponent fixed, while the [[指数函数求导|fixed-base exponential rule]] holds the base fixed. Neither can be applied to $x^x$ as though its other part were constant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=1$ 处导数为 $1$；在 $x=2$ 处为 $4(1+\ln2)\approx6.773$。[[实数幂求导|普通幂法则]]把指数固定，[[指数函数求导|固定底数的指数求导法则]]把底数固定。对 $x^x$，不能把另一部分当常数直接套用其中任何一条。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, let $u$ and $v$ be differentiable and $u(x)>0$. Define $y=u(x)^{v(x)}=\exp(v(x)\ln u(x))$. Differentiating $\ln y=v\ln u$ accounts for both changing parts:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，设 $u$、$v$ 可导且 $u(x)>0$，定义 $y=u(x)^{v(x)}=\exp(v(x)\ln u(x))$。对 $\ln y=v\ln u$ 求导，就同时计算了两个变化来源：

$$y'=u^v\left(v'\ln u+v\frac{u'}u\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The term $v'\ln u$ comes from the moving exponent; $v\,u'/u$ comes from the moving base. If either is constant, its derivative vanishes and the familiar special rule remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$v'\ln u$ 来自指数的变化，$v\,u'/u$ 来自底数的变化。如果其中一个是常数，相应导数为零，就还原成已有的特殊法则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Nonzero functions can use an absolute logarithm
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*非零函数可以使用绝对值对数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A differentiable function may be negative on an interval. If it is nonzero there, use $\ln|y|$ instead: [[对数函数求导|the absolute-log derivative]] is still $(\ln|y|)'=y'/y$. Continuity keeps the sign fixed on an interval without zeros. For nonzero factors, $\ln|ab|=\ln|a|+\ln|b|$ and $\ln|a/b|=\ln|a|-\ln|b|$ let us work on either sign branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可导函数在某个区间上也可能为负。如果它在该区间不为零，可以改用 $\ln|y|$；[[对数函数求导|绝对值对数的导数]]仍为 $(\ln|y|)'=y'/y$。连续性保证函数在没有零点的区间上保持同一符号。对非零因子，$\ln|ab|=\ln|a|+\ln|b|$、$\ln|a/b|=\ln|a|-\ln|b|$，所以两个符号分支都可以处理。

> [!example] A product across different sign intervals · 分符号区间处理乘积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $y=(x-1)(x+2)$. Away from $x=1,-2$, taking the absolute logarithm gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $y=(x-1)(x+2)$。在 $x\ne1,-2$ 处，取绝对值对数得到
>
> $$\ln|y|=\ln|x-1|+\ln|x+2|,
> \qquad \frac{y'}y=\frac1{x-1}+\frac1{x+2}.$$
>
> $$y'=(x-1)(x+2)\left(\frac1{x-1}+\frac1{x+2}\right)=2x+1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This calculation works even on $-2<x<1$, where $y<0$. At the two zeros, the logarithmic step is unavailable. The original polynomial is nevertheless differentiable there: expanding it as $x^2+x-2$, or using the product rule directly, gives the same $2x+1$ at those points. Extending a simplified expression to an excluded point needs this separate justification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 即使在 $y<0$ 的区间 $-2<x<1$，这个计算也成立。在两个零点处，对数步骤不能使用；但原多项式仍可导。把它展开为 $x^2+x-2$，或直接使用乘积法则，能独立证明这两点的导数也为 $2x+1$。要把化简后的表达式用到原步骤排除的点，必须补上这样的理由。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute values do not define an arbitrary real power of a negative base. Replacing $u(x)^{v(x)}$ by $|u(x)|^{v(x)}$ changes the function. Unless a particular exponent and real branch have been specified, a negative base with a varying real exponent generally has no real-valued definition on a whole interval. Check that the original function exists before taking any logarithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绝对值不会自动定义负底数的任意实数幂。把 $u(x)^{v(x)}$ 换成 $|u(x)|^{v(x)}$，就改变了原函数。除非已经说明某种特殊指数和实值分支，否则负底数配上变化的实指数，一般无法在整个区间上定义为实值函数。取对数以前，先确定原函数确实有定义。
