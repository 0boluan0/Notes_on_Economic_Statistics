# Pseudorandom number generators
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*伪随机数生成器*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **pseudorandom number generator**, or **PRNG**, is an algorithm that produces a repeatable sequence of numbers designed to behave like random input for computation. In a [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo simulation]], it can supply the numbers from which we construct coin tosses, waiting times or entire simulated paths. The next number comes from a rule and stored memory, rather than a new physical toss each time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**伪随机数生成器**，简称 **PRNG**，用算法产生可重复的数字序列，使它在计算中表现得像随机输入。在 [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo 模拟]]里，这些数字可用来构造抛硬币、等待时间乃至整条模拟路径。每个新数字来自确定规则和保存的内部记忆，不是每次重新进行一次物理投掷。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Pseudo” describes the construction, not a verdict that the output is useless. A well-designed generator can be fast and statistically suitable for a simulation while also allowing us to replay a run. Those are separate claims: replay checks how the computation repeats; statistical suitability concerns how the generator interacts with the problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“伪”说明的是产生方式，不表示输出没有用途。设计良好的生成器可以运行很快、适合某项模拟，也能让我们重放一次运行。这几项性质要分开：复现关心计算能否重复，统计适用性关心生成器与具体问题怎样配合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A seed chooses a starting state
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*种子选定起始状态*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **state** is the generator’s current internal memory. A **seed** is an input used to initialise that memory; it need not be the whole state. For a state-based description, write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**状态**是生成器当前保存的内部记忆。**种子**是用于初始化这份记忆的输入，不一定等于完整状态。可以把状态式生成器写成

$$
S_0=I(s),
\qquad
S_{t+1}=T(S_t),
\qquad
U_t=G(S_t),
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $s$ is the seed, $I$ is the initialisation rule, $S_t$ is the state at step $t$, $T$ advances it, and $G$ converts a state into an output $U_t$. These letters name functions, not probabilities. Fix the complete initial state and the algorithm, then perform the same sequence of operations: the outputs are determined. Different seeds are not a general proof of different or independent streams. Replaying the same state is developed in [[伪随机复现|pseudorandom reproducibility]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $s$ 是种子，$I$ 是初始化规则，$S_t$ 是第 $t$ 步状态，$T$ 推进状态，$G$ 把状态变成输出 $U_t$。这些字母命名的是函数，不是概率。完整初始状态、算法及操作顺序固定后，输出就确定了；不同种子并不能普遍证明得到不同或独立的随机流。怎样重放状态，见[[伪随机复现]]。

> [!example] An eight-state machine · 八状态的小机器
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a hand-checkable example, use $S_0=1$ and $S_{t+1}=(5S_t+1)\bmod 8$, emitting $U_t=S_t/8$ for $t\ge1$. The notation “mod 8” means take the remainder after division by $8$. Thus the first update gives $6$; the next gives $31\bmod8=7$.
>
> The states after successive updates are $6,7,4,5,2,3,0,1$, and then $6$ again. The first outputs are $0.75,0.875,0.5$. Restarting at state $1$ repeats this exact sequence. This tiny machine illustrates state and replay; its short, structured cycle makes it unsuitable as a general simulation engine.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $S_0=1$，按 $S_{t+1}=(5S_t+1)\bmod8$ 更新，并在 $t\ge1$ 时输出 $U_t=S_t/8$。“mod 8”表示除以 $8$ 后取余数。第一次得到 $6$，下一次得到 $31\bmod8=7$。
>
> 更新后的状态依次是 $6,7,4,5,2,3,0,1$，再回到 $6$；前三个输出为 $0.75,0.875,0.5$。从状态 $1$ 重新开始，会逐项重现。这台小机器用来解释状态与重放；短且结构明显的循环不适合作为通用模拟引擎。

## Uniform-looking values are not an independence theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*数值分散得均匀，不等于证明独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mathematical input to many simulations is an [[独立同分布|iid]] sequence with a [[连续均匀分布|continuous uniform distribution]] on the unit interval: equal-length intervals have equal probability, and learning earlier draws does not alter later probabilities. A digital generator emits only finitely representable values and approximates that ideal. A histogram checks one aspect of the outputs; it does not check their entire joint behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

许多模拟在数学上使用单位区间上的[[连续均匀分布]]和[[独立同分布|iid]] 序列：等长区间有相同概率，知道先前结果也不改变后续结果的概率。数字生成器只能输出有限精度可表示的值，用来近似这一理想输入。直方图只检查输出的一部分特征，并没有检查全部联合行为。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The small machine makes the difference visible. Suppose its starting state is chosen uniformly from $0,\ldots,7$. Each subsequent state is still uniform over those eight values, because the update permutes them. Yet $P(S_1=6,S_2=7)=1/8$, while $P(S_1=6)P(S_2=7)=1/64$. Knowing $S_1$ fixes $S_2$, so the [[随机变量独立|independence product rule]] fails even though each position is individually uniform.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

小机器能直接展示这个区别。若起始状态在 $0,\ldots,7$ 中等可能选取，后面每个位置的状态仍在这八个值上均匀，因为更新把八个状态重新排列。但 $P(S_1=6,S_2=7)=1/8$，而边际概率乘积为 $1/64$。知道 $S_1$ 就确定了 $S_2$，所以即使每个位置单独均匀，也不满足[[随机变量独立|独立的乘积条件]]。

## Period and quality describe different properties
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*周期与质量描述不同性质*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A deterministic generator with a finite state space eventually revisits a state. From that revisit onward the same transitions repeat: this is a cycle, whose length is its state period. There may be an initial segment before the cycle, and different starting states can lead to different cycles. A large period avoids early cycling but does not by itself rule out patterns in short blocks. Mersenne Twister MT19937 is a well-known concrete design with period $2^{19937}-1$ for its intended nonzero-state cycle; that number is a property of this design, not of PRNGs in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确定性生成器的状态空间有限，最终会再次到达某个旧状态；从那里起，相同更新不断重复，构成循环，其长度是状态周期。进入循环前可能有一段过渡，不同起点也可能进入不同循环。周期长可以避免很早重复，却不能单独排除短片段中的模式。Mersenne Twister MT19937 是一个具体设计，其预期非零状态循环的周期为 $2^{19937}-1$；这是该设计的性质，不是所有 PRNG 的通性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In practice, use an identified, documented generator and the distribution-transformation routines appropriate to the model. A generator’s period, uniformity results and statistical tests support specific claims; none alone proves every model, transformation or program correct. Parallel work also requires a deliberate [[并行伪随机子流|stream allocation]]. Statistical quality for simulation does not establish [[伪随机与密码安全|cryptographic security]]. These are different responsibilities from the basic PRNG definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际使用时，应知道采用的是哪种有文档依据的生成器，并使用与模型对应的分布变换函数。周期、均匀性结论和统计测试各自支持特定主张，没有一项能单独证明所有模型、变换或程序正确。并行工作还需要明确的[[并行伪随机子流|随机流分配]]；适合模拟的统计质量也不证明[[伪随机与密码安全|密码安全性]]。这些职责与 PRNG 的基本定义分开。

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Owen 第3章第3.1–3.2节核对确定性重放、状态、有限状态循环、种子与流，以及生成器质量和应用正确性的区别；八状态例子是直接构造和核算。
- Python 官方文档核对状态/种子接口、有限精度均匀输出，以及 Mersenne Twister 的具体用途边界。
- Matsumoto–Nishimura 作者托管原稿第2、10页核对 MT19937 的具体周期和均匀性主张，保留1998年发表信息。
