# 伪随机数生成器以确定性的内部状态递推产生具有模拟统计性质的数列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A pseudorandom number generator produces a sequence with simulation-oriented statistical properties through a deterministic internal-state transition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 伪随机数生成器（PRNG）从有限内部状态出发，用确定性规则更新状态并输出数值；随机性描述的是序列面向模拟的统计表现，不是给定状态后的物理不可预测性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A pseudorandom number generator starts from finite internal state, updates it deterministically, and emits values. Randomness here describes statistical behaviour useful for simulation, not physical unpredictability once the state is fixed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可把一个 PRNG 抽象为

$$
S_0=I(s),
\qquad
S_{t+1}=T(S_t),
\qquad
U_t=G(S_t),
$$

其中 seed $s$ 经初始化映射 $I$ 形成状态，$T$ 更新状态，$G$ 输出数值。给定 $S_0$、算法和调用次数，后续序列已经确定；这正是 [[伪随机复现]] 可行的基础。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A seed is mapped into an initial state, a deterministic transition advances that state, and an output map emits values. Once the state, algorithm, and call count are fixed, the subsequent stream is determined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周期长、均匀性好或通过某组统计测试，只说明相应设计目标；它们不自动证明所有输出独立、分布变换正确或整个模拟模型正确。并行计算还需要明确的子流构造，见 [[并行伪随机子流]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A long period, equidistribution, or success on selected statistical tests supports those particular design claims. It does not by itself prove independence, correct distribution transforms, or a correct simulation model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“面向模拟的统计性质”不是密码学安全的同义词，具体用途边界由 [[伪随机与密码安全]] 单独承载。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simulation-oriented statistical quality is not synonymous with cryptographic security; that use boundary is kept in its own atom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
