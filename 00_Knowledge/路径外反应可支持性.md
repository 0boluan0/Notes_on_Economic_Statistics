# 路径外反应可支持性要求存在一份合法信念使接收者的离轨反应最优，并使所有发送者类型都不愿偏离到该信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Supportability of an off-path response requires a legal belief that makes the receiver's prescribed response optimal and leaves every sender type unwilling to deviate to that signal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 两个条件必须由同一离轨反应同时满足
> 对路径外信号选择 belief 的自由，只允许接收者在某份合法 posterior 下作最佳反应。该反应还会进入发送者的偏离收益，因此必须同时阻止每个类型偏离；若这两个条件没有共同可行解，候选策略就不能由该离轨续局支持。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Freedom to choose an off-path belief permits only a receiver best response to some legal posterior. That same response determines the sender's deviation payoff and must deter every type. A candidate is unsupported when the two requirements have no common feasible solution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 接收者反应的 belief 可实现性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Belief feasibility of the receiver's response*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限标准[[信号博弈]]中，设 $s$ 是候选策略下到达概率为零的信号，接收者在看到 $s$ 后采用混合行动 $\alpha(\cdot\mid s)\in\Delta(A)$。这项反应可由 weak-PBE belief 支持，首先要求存在

$$
p(\cdot\mid s)\in\Delta(\Theta)
$$

使

$$
\operatorname{supp}\alpha(\cdot\mid s)
\subseteq
\arg\max_{a\in A}
\sum_{\theta\in\Theta}p(\theta\mid s)u_2(\theta,s,a).
$$

也就是说，接收者以正概率采用的每个行动都必须在同一份 posterior 下实现最大期望收益。合法 belief 是类型集合上的概率分布；它不能为一个在所有 beliefs 下都严格劣势的行动提供支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a finite standard signaling game, let $s$ be an off-path signal and let the receiver use $\alpha(\cdot\mid s)$. The response is belief-feasible only if one posterior $p(\cdot\mid s)$ makes every action in the support of $\alpha$ maximize the receiver's expected payoff. No probability distribution over types can support an action that is strictly dominated for the receiver under every belief.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同一反应还要阻止发送者偏离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The same response must also deter sender deviations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $V_1(\theta)$ 是类型 $\theta$ 在候选路径上得到的均衡收益。若该类型改发 $s$，面对接收者反应 $\alpha$ 的收益为

$$
U_1(\theta,s;\alpha)
=
\sum_{a\in A}\alpha(a\mid s)u_1(\theta,s,a).
$$

可支持性还要求

$$
V_1(\theta)\ge U_1(\theta,s;\alpha)
\qquad\text{对每个 }\theta\in\Theta.
$$

因此，找到一份使接收者愿意执行 $\alpha$ 的 belief 只是第一步；如果某个发送者类型仍能从偏离获益，这份 belief 与反应不能支持原候选。反过来，一个严厉反应即使能阻止所有偏离，只要它不是任何合法 belief 下的接收者最佳反应，也不能进入 weak PBE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V_1(\theta)$ be type $\theta$'s payoff under the candidate. Deviating to $s$ yields $U_1(\theta,s;\alpha)$. Supportability requires $V_1(\theta)\ge U_1(\theta,s;\alpha)$ for every type. A belief that rationalizes the receiver's response is insufficient when some sender type still benefits from deviating; a deterrent response is likewise inadmissible when no legal belief makes it optimal for the receiver.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个可操作的存在性检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An operational existence test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个路径外信号 $s$，先求所有可由某份 belief 支持的接收者混合最佳反应，再与同时满足全部发送者类型不偏离不等式的反应集合取交集。交集非空时，任选其中一项反应并报告一份支持它的具体 belief；交集为空时，该候选不能靠调整路径外 belief 修复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each off-path signal, first derive the receiver responses that are best responses to at least one belief. Intersect this set with the responses that satisfy every sender type's no-deviation inequality. A nonempty intersection yields a supportable response together with a supporting belief; an empty intersection rejects the candidate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某个路径外行动能吓阻所有发送者类型，但接收者在任意类型 belief 下都严格偏好另一行动。它能否支持 weak PBE？
>
> **答案：** 不能。发送者不偏离只满足了一半条件；接收者的规定行动还必须是某份合法 belief 下的最佳反应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** An off-path action deters every sender type, but the receiver strictly prefers another action under every belief over types. Can it support a weak PBE?
>
> **Answer:** No. Deterrence satisfies only one requirement; the prescribed receiver action must also be optimal under some legal belief.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
