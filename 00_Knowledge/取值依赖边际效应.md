# 二次项和交互项使边际效应随变量取值变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Quadratic and interaction terms make marginal effects depend on variable values*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 当同一解释变量通过二次项或交互项多次进入线性参数模型时，它的边际效应是相关系数的组合，而不是某一个系数。对
> $$m(X,Z)=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2+\beta_3Z+\beta_4XZ,$$
> 有
> $$
> \frac{\partial m(X,Z)}{\partial X}=\beta_1+2\beta_2X+\beta_4Z.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When a regressor enters a linear-in-parameters model more than once through a quadratic or interaction term, its marginal effect is a combination of coefficients rather than a single coefficient. For $m(X,Z)=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2+\beta_3Z+\beta_4XZ$, $\partial m(X,Z)/\partial X=\beta_1+2\beta_2X+\beta_4Z$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 自然解释与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Natural interpretation and boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\beta_1$ 只是 $X=0,Z=0$ 处的 $X$ 斜率；$2\beta_2X$ 让斜率随 $X$ 自身变化，$\beta_4Z$ 让斜率随另一变量 $Z$ 变化。若 0 不在有意义或有数据支持的范围，主效应系数就不是实质上重要位置的效应；可对变量中心化，或直接报告若干有意义取值处的线性组合和置信区间。中心化会改变低阶系数所对应的参照点，但不会改变拟合值或同一实质对比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\beta_1$ is only the slope with respect to $X$ at $X=0,Z=0$. The term $2\beta_2X$ makes the slope vary with $X$ itself, while $\beta_4Z$ makes it vary with the other variable $Z$. If zero is not meaningful or supported by the data, the main-effect coefficient is not the effect at a substantively relevant point. Centre the variables or report coefficient combinations and confidence intervals at meaningful values. Centering changes the reference point represented by the lower-order coefficients but not fitted values or the same substantive contrast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对纯二次形式 $m(X)=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2$，若 $\beta_2\neq0$，斜率为零的驻点是
$$
X^*=-\frac{\beta_1}{2\beta_2}.
$$
当 $\beta_2<0$ 时它是局部最大值，当 $\beta_2>0$ 时是局部最小值。只有当 $X^*$ 落在实际观测或有可信数据支持的范围内，且二次设定本身经得起诊断时，才应把它解释为实质转折点；否则它只是样本支持外的外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the pure quadratic form $m(X)=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2$, if $\beta_2\neq0$, the stationary point is $X^*=-\beta_1/(2\beta_2)$. It is a local maximum when $\beta_2<0$ and a local minimum when $\beta_2>0$. Interpret it as a substantive turning point only if $X^*$ lies within the observed or credibly data-supported range and the quadratic specification itself survives diagnostic scrutiny; otherwise it is an extrapolation beyond the support of the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见的 polynomial hierarchy 会在放入 $X^2$ 时保留 $X$，从而使模型包含一般二次曲线而不是额外强加“在 0 处斜率为 0”的限制。把 $X$ 改写为 $X-c$ 可让低阶系数对应 $X=c$ 处的斜率，并可能缓解原始幂列造成的数值共线性；它只是等价重参数化，不增加样本中的独立信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The usual polynomial hierarchy retains $X$ when $X^2$ is included, allowing a general quadratic rather than imposing the extra restriction that the slope is zero at the origin. Rewriting the polynomial in $X-c$ makes the lower-order coefficient the slope at $X=c$ and may reduce numerical collinearity among raw-power columns. It is an equivalent reparameterisation and adds no independent information to the sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的导数只适用于把 $X$ 当作连续变量的情形。若 $X$ 是 0/1 或其他离散变量，应比较有限差分，例如 $m(1,Z)-m(0,Z)$，不能把对离散编码求导当作实际变化。交互中的另一个变量也若是离散的，最清楚的做法通常是写出各组的完整拟合式再作差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative above applies only when $X$ is treated as continuous. If $X$ is binary or otherwise discrete, use a finite difference such as $m(1,Z)-m(0,Z)$ rather than interpreting a derivative with respect to a discrete code as an actual change. When the interacting variable is also discrete, it is usually clearest to write the complete fitted equation for each group and then take the relevant difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交互系数 $\beta_4$ 是“$Z$ 每增加 1 单位，$X$ 的斜率改变多少”，不是 $X$ 的完整效应；二次项系数 $\beta_2$ 描述曲率，也不是任一点的斜率。即使这些项由 OLS 估计，边际效应也只是设定内的条件关联；因果解释仍需外生性和研究设计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interaction coefficient $\beta_4$ is the change in the slope of $X$ for a one-unit increase in $Z$, not the complete effect of $X$. The quadratic coefficient $\beta_2$ describes curvature rather than the slope at any particular point. Even when OLS estimates these terms, their marginal effects are conditional associations within the specification; causal interpretation still requires exogeneity and an appropriate research design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若工资方程含 `experience`、`experience²` 和 `female × experience`，则不同经验水平、不同组别的经验回报都不同。只报告 `experience` 系数会把“基准组在经验为 0 时的斜率”误当成所有人的固定回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a wage equation contains `experience`, `experience²`, and `female × experience`, the return to experience differs across experience levels and groups. Reporting only the `experience` coefficient mistakes the slope for the reference group at zero experience for a constant return applying to everyone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 在含 $XZ$ 的模型中，为什么不能把 $X$ 的系数直接称为“$X$ 的效应”？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a model containing $XZ$, why can the coefficient on $X$ not simply be called “the effect of $X$”?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 因为 $X$ 的斜率是 $\beta_X+\beta_{XZ}Z$；$\beta_X$ 只对应 $Z=0$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Because the slope of $X$ is $\beta_X+\beta_{XZ}Z$; $\beta_X$ applies only at $Z=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
