# The integral test for series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*级数的积分判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An infinite sum adds separate contributions; an integral accumulates along a continuous curve. When the contributions are heights sampled from a positive, steadily nonincreasing curve, unit-width rectangles let us compare these two totals. The **integral test** uses that comparison to decide whether the sum is finite, often without calculating the sum itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无穷求和累加一个个离散贡献，积分则沿连续曲线累积。当这些贡献来自一条正的、始终不增的曲线在整数处的高度时，可以用宽度为一的矩形比较两种总量。**积分判别**利用这个比较判断求和是否有限，通常不必求出级数的精确和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here an [[无穷级数|infinite series]] means the limit of its finite partial sums. An [[反常积分|improper integral]] similarly means the limit of finite integrals. The test relates those two limits; it does not replace either definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里，[[无穷级数]]指有限部分和的极限；[[反常积分]]同样通过有限截断积分的极限定义。这个判别联系的是两种极限，各自的定义仍然需要满足。

> [!example] Bounding a sum we cannot yet evaluate · 先判断是否有限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\sum_{n=1}^\infty1/n^2$, draw $f(x)=1/x^2$ on $x\ge1$. On $[n-1,n]$, the curve is at least as high as its right endpoint $f(n)$. Thus every rectangle of area $1/n^2$, for $n\ge2$, fits below the curve on that interval. Keeping the first term separately gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty1/n^2$，在 $x\ge1$ 上画出 $f(x)=1/x^2$。在 $[n-1,n]$ 上，曲线不会低于右端点的高度 $f(n)$。因此，对 $n\ge2$，面积为 $1/n^2$ 的矩形都能放在相应曲线下面。单独保留第一项，得到
>
> $$S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N\frac1{n^2}\le1+\int_1^N\frac{dx}{x^2}=2-\frac1N<2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The partial sums increase because all terms are positive, but never reach $2$. An increasing bounded sequence has a finite limit, so the series converges. This argument has not calculated its exact sum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 各项为正，所以部分和递增，却始终小于 $2$。递增且有上界的数列具有有限极限，因此级数收敛。这个论证没有求出精确的级数和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The conditions apply to an entire tail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*检查整个尾部上的条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a positive integer $N_0$. Suppose a real-valued function $f$ is continuous, positive and nonincreasing on $[N_0,\infty)$, and $a_n=f(n)$ for every integer $n\ge N_0$. “Nonincreasing” allows flat stretches: $x\le y$ implies $f(x)\ge f(y)$. Continuity guarantees that each finite interval has a [[连续函数可积|proper Riemann integral]]. Under these conditions,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择正整数 $N_0$。设实值函数 $f$ 在 $[N_0,\infty)$ 上连续、为正且单调不增，并且每个整数 $n\ge N_0$ 都满足 $a_n=f(n)$。“单调不增”允许水平的一段：$x\le y$ 时有 $f(x)\ge f(y)$。连续性保证每个有限区间上[[连续函数可积|正常黎曼积分存在]]。这些条件成立时，

$$\sum_{n=N_0}^\infty a_n\text{ converges}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\int_{N_0}^\infty f(x)\,dx\text{ converges}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both limits must be finite for convergence. Any earlier terms must be defined finite numbers; adding finitely many of them changes only a finite constant. Thus the conditions need hold only from $N_0$ onward, not necessarily from the first term. When $f$ is differentiable in the tail's interior, $f'\le0$ throughout it, together with the assumed endpoint continuity, gives nonincreasing behaviour by the [[导数单调性判据|derivative criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收敛要求两种极限都是有限数。更早的各项必须是已定义的有限数；加入有限多个这样的项，只是增加一个有限常数。因此，条件从 $N_0$ 开始成立就足够，不必从第一项开始。若函数在尾区间内部可导、处处满足 $f'\le0$，配合已有的端点连续条件，[[导数单调性判据]]就保证函数单调不增。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why rectangles give both directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩形为什么能给出两个方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $[n,n+1]$, the curve is no higher than $f(n)$, so $\int_n^{n+1}f\le f(n)$. On $[n-1,n]$, it is no lower than $f(n)$, so $f(n)\le\int_{n-1}^{n}f$. Adding these inequalities over finitely many intervals, with the first term kept separately for the upper bound, yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[n,n+1]$ 上，曲线不高于 $f(n)$，所以 $\int_n^{n+1}f\le f(n)$；在 $[n-1,n]$ 上，曲线不低于 $f(n)$，所以 $f(n)\le\int_{n-1}^{n}f$。对有限多个区间相加，并在上界处单独留下第一项，得到

$$\int_{N_0}^{N+1}f(x)\,dx
\le\sum_{n=N_0}^{N}f(n)
\le f(N_0)+\int_{N_0}^{N}f(x)\,dx,\qquad N\ge N_0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the integral converges, the right side bounds all partial sums. They are nondecreasing, so the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property]] gives their finite limit: each sum lies below its supremum and eventually within any prescribed distance of it. If the integral diverges, its nonnegative accumulation tends to $+\infty$; the left inequality forces the partial sums to do so too. These two alternatives prove the equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若积分收敛，右边为所有部分和提供统一的上界。部分和单调不减，因此[[实数的确界性质]]给出有限极限：每个部分和都不超过上确界，并最终任意接近它。若积分发散，非负累积就趋于 $+\infty$；左侧不等式迫使部分和也如此。两种情况证明了敛散等价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same finite inequalities remain valid for a nonnegative nonincreasing $f$ that is merely Riemann integrable on every finite tail interval. The continuous positive statement is a convenient version, not a claim that strict positivity or strict decrease is necessary. Monotonicity between the sample points still matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于非负、单调不增，并在每个有限尾区间上黎曼可积的 $f$，同样的有限不等式仍然成立。连续正函数的表述只是方便使用的版本，并不是说必须严格为正或严格递减。整数采样点之间的单调性仍然重要。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose a useful function, then evaluate its tail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先选择函数，再计算它的尾积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\sum_{n=2}^\infty1/[n(\ln n)^2]$, take $f(x)=1/[x(\ln x)^2]$ on $[2,\infty)$. Here $\ln$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. The function is positive and continuous, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\sum_{n=2}^\infty1/[n(\ln n)^2]$，取 $[2,\infty)$ 上的 $f(x)=1/[x(\ln x)^2]$。这里 $\ln$ 是[[自然对数]]。函数为正且连续，并且

$$f'(x)=-\frac{\ln x+2}{x^2(\ln x)^3}<0\quad(x\ge2),\qquad
\int_2^R f(x)\,dx=\frac1{\ln2}-\frac1{\ln R}\longrightarrow\frac1{\ln2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The antiderivative is $-1/\ln x$, whose derivative is $f$. All hypotheses hold, so the series converges. Starting at $1$ would introduce an undefined term because $\ln1=0$; “ignore a finite prefix” never authorises keeping an undefined term in the original series. For reciprocal powers, the same route leads to the [[p级数判别|p-series test]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原函数是 $-1/\ln x$，求导即得 $f$。全部前提成立，因此级数收敛。若从 $1$ 开始，会因 $\ln1=0$ 出现未定义项；“忽略有限前项”不允许把未定义项留在原级数里。对倒数幂，同样的路线给出 [[p级数判别]]。

> [!warning] Convergence is not equality of totals · 同敛散不表示数值相等
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[几何级数|geometric series]] $\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^{-n}$ sums to $1/(e-1)$, whereas $\int_1^\infty e^{-x}\,dx=1/e$. Rectangles and curved regions have different areas. For quantitative bounds after a finite sum, use [[积分判别余项界|integral remainder bounds]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[几何级数]] $\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^{-n}$ 的和是 $1/(e-1)$，而 $\int_1^\infty e^{-x}\,dx=1/e$。矩形面积与曲线下面积不同。需要有限求和后的定量控制时，使用[[积分判别余项界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, convergence of an arbitrary continuous function's integral does not guarantee convergence of its integer samples. The [[尾积分收敛不推函数趋零|narrow-spike example]] has finite integral but height one at every integer $n\ge2$, so its sampled series diverges. The curve is not nonincreasing. Checking only $a_n\to0$ would also be insufficient, as the [[调和数对数增长|harmonic sums]] show.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，任意连续函数的积分收敛，并不能保证其整数采样值组成的级数收敛。[[尾积分收敛不推函数趋零|窄尖峰反例]]的积分有限，却在每个整数 $n\ge2$ 处高度都为一，所以采样级数发散；这条曲线并不单调不增。只检查 $a_n\to0$ 也不够，[[调和数对数增长|调和部分和]]就是反例。
