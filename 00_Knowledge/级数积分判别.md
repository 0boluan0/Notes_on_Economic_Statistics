# 最终由连续正且单调不增函数取值得到的级数与相应尾积分同敛散
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A series eventually sampled from a continuous, positive, nonincreasing function converges exactly when its tail integral converges*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设存在整数 $N_0$，使 $f$ 在 $[N_0,\infty)$ 上连续、为正、单调不增，且 $a_n=f(n)$ 对所有 $n\ge N_0$ 成立。则 $\sum_{n=N_0}^\infty a_n$ 与 [[反常积分]] $\int_{N_0}^\infty f(x)\,dx$ 同敛散。条件只须在尾部成立；原级数若还有有限个已定义的前项，也具有相同敛散性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $f$ is continuous, positive, and nonincreasing on $[N_0,\infty)$, and $a_n=f(n)$ for every $n\ge N_0$. Then the series and the [[反常积分|improper integral]] of $f$ on that tail converge or diverge together. A finite prefix of defined terms does not affect the conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单调性让每个宽度为 1 的矩形夹住相邻曲线面积。对整数 $N\ge N_0$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monotonicity places each unit-width rectangle between neighbouring areas under the curve. For every integer $N\ge N_0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\int_{N_0}^{N+1}f(x)\,dx
\le\sum_{n=N_0}^{N}f(n)
\le f(N_0)+\int_{N_0}^{N}f(x)\,dx.
$$

因此积分有界时部分和也有界，积分趋于无穷时部分和也无界。这里得到的是敛散等价，不是“级数和等于积分值”；例如 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^{-n}=1/(e-1)$，但 $\int_1^\infty e^{-x}\,dx=1/e$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bounds transfer boundedness or divergence to infinity between the integrals and partial sums. They establish equivalent convergence, not equal values: $\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^{-n}=1/(e-1)$, whereas $\int_1^\infty e^{-x}\,dx=1/e$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用前应检查整个尾区间上的单调性，不能只看到 $a_n\to0$ 就跳过条件。调和级数的具体矩形比较与增长速度见 [[调和数对数增长]]；截断后的定量误差另见 [[积分判别余项界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check monotonicity on the full tail interval; $a_n\to0$ alone does not justify the test. See [[调和数对数增长|logarithmic growth of harmonic numbers]] for the harmonic example and [[积分判别余项界|integral remainder bounds]] for truncation error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
