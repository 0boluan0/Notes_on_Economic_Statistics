# 因果注意力掩码把每个输入位置的可读 key 限制为自身及左侧位置
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A causal attention mask restricts each input position to keys at that position or to its left*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**因果注意力掩码**（causal attention mask）是限制序列内注意力读取方向的规则：第 $i$ 个输入位置的 query 只能读取第 $j\le i$ 个输入位置的 key/value。这里的“因果”指自回归序列的信息可见性约束，位置按从左到右的顺序编号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **causal attention mask** restricts the direction of attention within a sequence: the query at input position $i$ may read only keys and values at input positions $j\le i$. Here, “causal” refers to the visibility constraint used in autoregressive sequence processing, with positions numbered from left to right.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[缩放点积注意力]] 中，令未掩码分数矩阵为 $S\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$。常用的加法掩码把不允许的连接设为 $-\infty$，再沿 key 轴做 [[Softmax]]：

$$
M_{ij}=\begin{cases}0,&j\le i,\\-\infty,&j>i,\end{cases}
\qquad
A=\operatorname{softmax}_{\mathrm{key}}(S+M).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[缩放点积注意力|scaled dot-product attention]], let $S\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ be the unmasked score matrix. The additive mask assigns $-\infty$ to forbidden connections before [[Softmax|softmax]] is applied along the key axis, as in the displayed equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个输入位置的掩码是

$$
M=\begin{bmatrix}
0&-\infty&-\infty\\
0&0&-\infty\\
0&0&0
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

第一行只能读第一个输入，第二行可以读前两个输入，第三行可以读全部三个输入。对角线保留；右侧权重在 [[注意力掩码归一化|允许位置上的归一化]] 后为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For three input positions, the displayed mask lets the first row read only the first input, the second row read the first two, and the third row read all three. The diagonal remains visible; rightward weights become zero after [[注意力掩码归一化|normalization over the allowed positions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读取自身是否泄漏目标，要看输入与标签的对齐。例如输入位置 $i$ 放 $x_i$、该位置的 logit 预测 $x_{i+1}$ 时，读取 $x_i$ 是合法历史。[[Transformer自回归约束|输入—标签错位与因果掩码共同实现 next-token 约束]]。掩码也不决定哪些位置计训练损失；那是 [[SFT损失掩码|loss mask 的职责]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether reading the current input leaks the target depends on input–label alignment. If input position $i$ holds $x_i$ and its logit predicts $x_{i+1}$, then $x_i$ is valid history. [[Transformer自回归约束|Input–label shifting and causal masking jointly enforce next-token prediction]]. This mask also does not select which positions contribute training loss; that is the role of [[SFT损失掩码|a loss mask]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Vaswani et al. (2017), *Attention Is All You Need*](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7#S3.SS2.SSS3)，§3.2.3：支持 decoder 读取当前位置及此前位置，以及在 softmax 输入端设置 $-\infty$ 的实现。
- [Raffel et al. (2020), *Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer*](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.10683v4#S3.SS2.SSS1)，§3.2.1、Fig. 3：支持 $j>i$ 连接不可见的索引约定。三位置矩阵为按该规则直接构造的例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Vaswani et al., §3.2.3, specifies visibility up to and including the current decoder position and masking with $-\infty$ before softmax.
- Raffel et al., §3.2.1 and Figure 3, supports the convention that connections with $j>i$ are forbidden. The three-position matrix is constructed directly from that rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
