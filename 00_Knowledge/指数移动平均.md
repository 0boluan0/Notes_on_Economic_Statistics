# 指数移动平均是用固定权重递推更新且历史权重按几何比例衰减的平均
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An exponential moving average updates recursively with a fixed weight and geometrically declining historical weights.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数移动平均（exponential moving average，EMA）是将当期观测与上期平均值按固定权重结合的递推平均。展开递推后，较久观测的权重按几何比例衰减；其数值还取决于初始化方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exponential moving average (EMA) recursively combines the current observation with the previous average using a fixed weight. Expanding the recursion yields geometrically declining historical weights. The numerical result also depends on initialization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡以收盘价 $C_t$ 为输入，采用技术分析常见的 $N$ 期约定：$N$ 为正整数，平滑系数为 $\alpha_N=2/(N+1)$；取得首 $N$ 个有效收盘价后，以它们的 [[简单移动平均]] 初始化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For closing prices $C_t$, this card uses the common $N$-period technical-analysis convention: $N$ is a positive integer and $\alpha_N=2/(N+1)$. Initialize with the [[简单移动平均|simple average]] of the first $N$ valid closes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E_{N,N}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}C_i,\qquad
E_{N,t}=\alpha_N C_t+(1-\alpha_N)E_{N,t-1},\quad t>N.
$$

这里的 $N$ 控制平滑系数，不表示只保留最近 $N$ 期：早于该窗口的历史通过上一期 EMA 继续影响今天。首 $N$ 期不足时不输出，缺失价格不填零；同一串价格若改用首价作种子，早期结果会不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $N$ determines the smoothing coefficient; it does not truncate history to the latest $N$ periods. Older observations remain influential through the preceding EMA. No value is produced before the initial window is complete, and missing prices are not replaced with zero. Using the first price as the seed instead changes early outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若仅种子相差 $\delta$，之后输入与 $\alpha$ 相同，经过 $k$ 次更新后的差为 $(1-\alpha)^k\delta$。这与 [[SES权重机制|指数平滑的几何权重机制]] 相同，但 EMA 在此只是价格滤波，未附带 SES 的平坦多步预测或无趋势、无季节假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If only the seed differs by $\delta$, identical subsequent inputs and $\alpha$ leave a difference of $(1-\alpha)^k\delta$ after $k$ updates. This shares the [[SES权重机制|geometric-weight mechanism of exponential smoothing]], but a price EMA does not impose SES's flat multi-step forecasts or its absence-of-trend-and-seasonality assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $N=3$，收盘价依次为 $10,12,11,15,14$。$\alpha=1/2$，第三期种子为 $11$，第四期 EMA 为 $13$，第五期为 $13.5$。第五期三期 SMA 则为 $(11+15+14)/3=13.3333$；两种平均的权重机制不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $N=3$ and closes of $10,12,11,15,14$, $\alpha=1/2$. The period-three seed is $11$, followed by EMA values of $13$ and $13.5$. The period-five three-period SMA is $13.3333$, reflecting its different weighting rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
