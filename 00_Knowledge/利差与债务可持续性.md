# 利率低于增长会减轻既有债务率压力但不能单独保证债务可持续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest below growth eases the inherited debt-ratio burden but does not alone guarantee sustainability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同口径的[[债务率动态]]中，正债务率乘上$(i-n)/(1+n)$。名义有效利率低于名义GDP增长时，这一项为负；用一致平减指数转成实际量后，就是常说的$r<g$。它只说明利差项在拉低债务率，没有替初级赤字、金融资产交易、汇率重估和到期融资作判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive debt, the [[债务率动态|debt-dynamics]] term $(i-n)b/(1+n)$ is negative when nominal effective interest is below nominal GDP growth. With a consistent deflator, this is the familiar $r<g$. It says nothing by itself about primary deficits, asset transactions, revaluation or refinancing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如债务率60%、$i=2\%$、$n=4\%$、初级赤字为GDP的3%，且无其他调节：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With debt 60% of GDP, interest 2%, growth 4%, a primary deficit of 3% and no other adjustments:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta b=\frac{0.02-0.04}{1.04}\times0.60+0.03
\approx0.01846.
$$

债务率仍增加约1.85个百分点。这个例子证明低利差不足以保证债务率下降；是否可持续，还要看后续财政反应、融资条件和冲击。即便一组固定参数给出收敛的债务率，也不能省略[[公债再融资风险|到期资金]]与[[债务可持续性|实际可行性]]检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The debt ratio still rises by about 1.85 percentage points. This establishes that a favourable differential does not guarantee a falling ratio, not that this path is necessarily unsustainable. Even convergence under fixed parameters does not replace [[公债再融资风险|funding]] and [[债务可持续性|feasibility]] checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，$r>g$也不自动意味着必然违约：足够且可行的初级盈余可以抵销利差压力。真正需要检查的是整个路径，而不是把某一年利差的符号当成政策许可证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, $r>g$ does not imply inevitable default: a sufficient feasible primary surplus can offset the differential. Assess the whole path rather than treating one year's sign as a policy licence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
