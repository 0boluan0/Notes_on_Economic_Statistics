# 人类发展指数以健康、教育和生活水平的标准化维度指数概括平均发展成就

人类发展指数（Human Development Index，HDI）是把健康、教育和生活水平三个维度的标准化指数取几何平均，概括一国平均发展成就的综合指标。生活水平维度使用按[[购买力平价]]换算的人均[[国民总收入]]（GNI），不是人均 GDP；HDI 本身也不是贫困人口比例或完整的福利评价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Human Development Index (HDI) summarizes a country's average development achievements as the geometric mean of normalized health, education and standard-of-living indices. Its living-standard dimension uses [[购买力平价|purchasing-power-parity]] GNI per capita, based on [[国民总收入|gross national income]], rather than GDP per capita. The HDI is neither a poverty headcount nor a complete welfare assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

健康指标是出生时预期寿命 $LE$。教育指标包括 25 岁及以上人口的平均受教育年限 $MYS$，以及入学年龄儿童在当前各年龄入学率持续的假设下可期望接受的教育年限 $EYS$；后者不是对每个儿童实际完成学业的保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Health is measured by life expectancy at birth, $LE$. Education uses mean years of schooling among people aged 25 and older, $MYS$, and expected years of schooling for a child of school-entry age, $EYS$, conditional on prevailing age-specific enrolment patterns continuing. The latter is not a guarantee of each child's eventual attainment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按 UNDP《2025 人类发展报告》技术说明，目标界限分别为：$LE$ 的 20–85 年、$EYS$ 的 0–18 年、$MYS$ 的 0–15 年、人均 GNI 的 100–75,000 个 2021 PPP 美元。下式的输入采用这些界限内的数值；计算时寿命超过 85 年、预期教育超过 18 年、人均 GNI 超过 75,000 的展示值须先按规定封顶。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the UNDP 2025 technical specification, the goalposts are 20–85 years for $LE$, 0–18 for $EYS$, 0–15 for $MYS$, and 100–75,000 in 2021 PPP dollars for GNI per capita. The formulas below use inputs within these bounds. Displayed observations above 85 years of life expectancy, 18 expected years of schooling or 75,000 GNI per capita must first be capped as prescribed for calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
I_H=\frac{LE-20}{85-20},\qquad
I_E=\frac12\left(\frac{EYS}{18}+\frac{MYS}{15}\right),
$$

$$
I_Y=\frac{\ln Y-\ln100}{\ln75000-\ln100},\qquad
HDI=(I_H I_E I_Y)^{1/3}.
$$

教育内部取两个标准化指标的算术平均；收入先取对数，以体现收入增加对该维度指数的边际贡献递减；最后三个维度取几何平均。不能把四个原始数字直接相加，也不能把收入取对数理解为“收入不重要”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Education averages its two normalized indicators arithmetically. Income is logarithmically transformed, giving additional income a diminishing marginal contribution to its dimension index. The three dimensions are then combined geometrically. Adding the four raw observations is invalid, and taking logs does not make income irrelevant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造例：$LE=72$、$EYS=14.4$、$MYS=12$，令 $Y=100\times750^{0.8}\approx19,955$。四项标准化后均为 $0.8$，故 $I_H=I_E=I_Y=0.8$，$HDI=0.8$。若三个维度指数改为 $1,0.4,1$，算术平均仍为 $0.8$，HDI 却为 $0.4^{1/3}\approx0.737$：几何平均使维度间的不均衡降低总指数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an original example, let $LE=72$, $EYS=14.4$, $MYS=12$ and $Y=100\times750^{0.8}\approx19,955$. All four normalized inputs equal $0.8$, so each dimension index and the HDI equal $0.8$. If the dimension indices instead are $1,0.4,1$, their arithmetic mean remains $0.8$, but the HDI becomes $0.4^{1/3}\approx0.737$: geometric aggregation lowers the result when dimensions are unbalanced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相同的三个维度平均值仍可能掩盖人群内部差异。等维度权重、目标界限和聚合方法是指数的明确设计，不是未经选择的自然事实；比较时要报告版本、指标参考年及 PPP 基期，不能把报告出版年当成每个观测的年份。识别哪些人遭遇多重剥夺，要转向[[多维贫困]]，而非从 HDI 倒推。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same three average dimension values may conceal differences among people. Equal dimension weights, goalposts and aggregation are explicit design choices. Comparisons should state the methodological edition, reference years and PPP base year; publication year is not necessarily observation year. Identifying who experiences overlapping deprivation requires [[多维贫困|multidimensional poverty analysis]], not inference from the HDI alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
