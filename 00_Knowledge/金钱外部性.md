# 金钱外部性是他人的活动经由市场价格变化影响自身利益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A pecuniary externality is an effect of another person's activity on one's interests through changes in market prices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金钱外部性指别人的活动通过产品价格、工资、租金等市场价格影响自己的收入或支出。比如新买家增加铜的需求，使铜价上升；原有用铜企业成本提高，铜的卖方则得到更高价格。这与污染直接破坏渔业生产条件是不同渠道。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pecuniary externality operates through market prices such as product prices, wages or rents. New copper demand can raise buyers' costs while benefiting sellers. This differs from pollution directly damaging fishing conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在完整竞争市场且没有其他扭曲的基准中，价格反映稀缺程度并协调选择，**不能仅凭某个买方多付钱或竞争者利润下降，再宣布一个外部性效率损失**。这不表示受损者的分配问题不重要；效率与分配仍应[[政府干预的效率与分配理由|分别说明]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the complete competitive-market benchmark without other distortions, prices communicate scarcity and coordinate choices. A buyer paying more or a competitor losing profit does not alone establish an externality-related efficiency loss. [[政府干预的效率与分配理由|Distributional concerns remain a separate question]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用时先问影响是否只经过价格，再问所用的竞争和完整市场条件是否成立。存在其他约束或失灵时，必须重新分析价格变化如何影响可行选择，不能把基准结论扩展成“价格引起的后果永远无关”。项目评价还要避免把交易收入与已经估值的最终收益重复相加，参见[[财务与社会评价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check whether the effect operates only through prices and whether the competitive, complete-market conditions hold. Other constraints or failures require renewed analysis; the benchmark does not make all price-mediated consequences irrelevant. In appraisal, distinguish [[财务与社会评价|financial receipts from social gains]] to avoid double-counting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
