# 收益率不变时规则债券的付息后价格逐期靠近面值而全价在付息前含将付息票
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*At a constant yield, regular post-coupon prices move toward par, while pre-payment dirty prices include the approaching coupon*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对无违约、无改变现金流的期权、固定等额息票 $C\ge0$、按面值 $F>0$ 偿还的债券，若每期收益率 $i>-1$ 保持不变，就可以比较各付息日刚支付息票后的价格。剩余完整期数从 $N\ge2$ 变为 $N-1\ge1$ 时，两种状态都尚未偿还本金，这些价格与面值的距离缩小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a default-free bond with no option altering its cash flows, equal fixed coupons $C\ge0$, and redemption at par $F>0$, hold the period yield $i>-1$ constant. Compare prices immediately after coupon payments. As complete remaining periods decrease from $N\ge2$ to $N-1\ge1$, principal remains outstanding in both states and these prices move closer to face value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This follows because
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_N-F=(C-iF)\sum_{k=1}^{N}(1+i)^{-k},$$

每减少一期，就从正的求和因子中去掉一项。因此溢价逐期缩小，折价逐期缩小，平价仍为平价。该推理也允许负收益率；例如 $F=100,C=5,i=-1\%$ 时，剩余两期价格约 $112.1824$，剩余一期约 $106.0606$，仍逐步接近 $100$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Removing one remaining period removes one positive term from the factor. Premiums and discounts therefore shrink, while par remains par. Negative yields are allowed: with $F=100,C=5,i=-1\%$, prices with two and one periods remaining are about 112.1824 and 106.0606, respectively, both moving toward 100.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“靠近面值”必须区分支付边界。在仍有权收取末次息票、采用规则应计利息且无另设除息交易限制的理论模型中，临到期前，剩余支付是 $F+C$，所以全价趋于 $F+C$；应计利息趋于 $C$，净价才趋于 $F$。本息全部支付后，债券不再保有现金流，其价值是 $0$，不是 $F$。实际市场若有提前除息日，应先判断结算后买方到底取得哪些支付权利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The payment boundary matters. In a theoretical model retaining entitlement to the final coupon, with regular accrual and no separate ex-coupon trading restriction, the remaining payment just before maturity is $F+C$. Dirty price approaches $F+C$; accrued interest approaches $C$, so clean price approaches $F$. After all principal and interest have been paid, no cash flows remain and value is zero, not $F$. If a market has an earlier ex-coupon date, first check which payments the buyer acquires at settlement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正收益率下，两次付息日之间全价随时间积累，到付息时扣除所付息票，形成锯齿；不能把整条全价轨迹画成单调拉回面值的直线。即使采用连续付息模型，恒定连续贴现率 $\delta\ne0$、每年现金息流 $K$ 时，$P(\tau)=K(1-e^{-\delta\tau})/\delta+Fe^{-\delta\tau}$ 通常也是曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a positive constant yield, dirty price accumulates between coupons and drops by the paid coupon at payment, producing a sawtooth. The full dirty-price path is not a straight monotonic pull to par. Even with continuous coupons, a constant continuous discount rate $\delta\ne0$ and annual cash-flow intensity $K$ give $P(\tau)=K(1-e^{-\delta\tau})/\delta+Fe^{-\delta\tau}$, generally a curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
