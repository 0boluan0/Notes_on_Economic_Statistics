# 经验分布反函数约定把样本 p 分位数取为第 ceil(np) 个顺序统计量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The empirical inverse-CDF convention takes the sample p-quantile at rank ceil(np)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 对 $0<p\le1$，经验分布反函数的一种约定为 $\hat q_p=X_{(\lceil np\rceil)}$。先把数据排序，再算 $np$，向上取整得到秩，最后取该位置的观察值。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $0<p\le1$, the empirical inverse-CDF convention chooses the order statistic at rank $\lceil np\rceil$. Sort, calculate $np$, round upward, and read the observation at that rank.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

排序样本 $4,7,9,11,12,15,19,22$ 中，$\hat q_{0.25}=X_{(2)}=7$，$\hat q_{0.5}=X_{(4)}=11$，$\hat q_{0.75}=X_{(6)}=15$。偶数样本的中位数还常约定为中间两值平均；分位数算法不唯一，比较时必须说明约定。见[[顺序统计量]]与[[四分位距]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the eight sorted observations shown, the quartiles are 7 and 15 and the median is 11 under this convention. Another common even-sample median averages the two central observations. Quantile algorithms differ, so specify the convention. See [[顺序统计量|order statistics]] and [[四分位距|the interquartile range]].
