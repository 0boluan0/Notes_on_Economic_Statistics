# 在人口规模、均值和排序不变时基尼对等额渐进转移的反应取决于双方排名距离

在等权有限人口、非负收入和正均值条件下，从较富者向较穷者转移同一金额，如果不改变任何人的收入排序，[[基尼系数]]的降幅取决于双方的排名距离，而不是只看双方原有的收入金额。固定排名距离的等额转移可有相同影响；跨越更多排名的转移则使基尼下降更多。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an equally weighted finite population with nonnegative incomes and a positive mean, an equal-sized transfer from a richer to a poorer person reduces the [[基尼系数|Gini coefficient]] according to the distance between their ranks, provided no income ordering changes. The effect is not determined simply by their initial income levels. Equal transfers over the same rank distance have equal effects; transfers across more ranks reduce the Gini further.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将收入从低到高排序，$i<j$。从第 $j$ 人向第 $i$ 人转移 $\delta>0$，其他收入不变、转移不耗损资源，并要求转移后原排序仍成立。由成对差公式可得排序表达式及变化量：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order incomes from lowest to highest, with $i<j$. Transfer $\delta>0$ from person $j$ to person $i$, leaving other incomes unchanged and losing no resources. Require the original ordering to remain valid after the transfer. The pairwise formula gives the following ranked expression and change:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
G=\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n}(2k-n-1)y_{(k)}}{n^2\mu},
\qquad
\Delta G=\frac{[(2i-n-1)-(2j-n-1)]\delta}{n^2\mu}
=-\frac{2\delta(j-i)}{n^2\mu}.
$$

以 $10,20,30,40$ 为起点，$n=4$、$\mu=25$、$G=0.25$。第 2 人向第 1 人转移 1 后为 $11,19,30,40$，$G=0.245$；第 4 人向第 1 人转移 1 后为 $11,20,30,39$，$G=0.235$。金额和均值相同，排名距离分别为 1 与 3，降幅也相差三倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start from $10,20,30,40$, so $n=4$, $\mu=25$ and $G=0.25$. A transfer of 1 from rank 2 to rank 1 gives $11,19,30,40$ and $G=0.245$. A transfer of 1 from rank 4 to rank 1 gives $11,20,30,39$ and $G=0.235$. Transfer size and mean are identical, but rank distances of 1 and 3 produce reductions in the same ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若转移改变排序，须重新排序计算，不能沿用旧 $i,j$。也不能把“对中间收入最敏感”当作无条件定理：同样的收入差距在不同区间可能夹着不同数量的人。该命题讨论一次转移的反应；[[相同基尼与分布形状]]讨论的是总指标遗漏分布细节，两者不是同一边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a transfer changes ranks, reorder and recalculate instead of retaining the old $i,j$. Nor is “most sensitive to middle incomes” an unconditional theorem: the same monetary gap may contain different numbers of people in different parts of a distribution. This proposition concerns the response to a transfer; [[相同基尼与分布形状|equal Ginis with different distribution shapes]] concerns information lost through aggregation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
