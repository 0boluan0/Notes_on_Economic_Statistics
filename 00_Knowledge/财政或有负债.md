# 财政或有负债是特定未来事件发生后才触发政府支付义务的安排或预期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contingent fiscal liabilities are arrangements or expectations under which specified future events trigger government payment obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次性债务担保是直观例子：企业正常还款时，政府不必代偿；企业违约并符合担保条件时，政府才承担相应支付。显性或有负债来自合同或法律安排；隐性风险来自可能救助、灾害应对等预期，并不自动等于当前已确认的法定义务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a one-off debt guarantee, government pays only if the debtor defaults and the guarantee conditions are met. Explicit contingencies arise from legal or contractual arrangements; implicit risks include expected support or disaster responses, not automatically recognised current legal obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设政府为范围外企业的一笔本金100提供担保。这不意味着当期[[政府债务总额与净额|总债务]]立刻增加100，也不意味着预期财政损失就是100。实际风险取决于触发概率、担保覆盖、追偿和多个风险是否一起兑现；最大担保额、预期损失和已支付金额应分开报告。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Guaranteeing principal 100 for an entity outside the reporting perimeter does not automatically add 100 to current [[政府债务总额与净额|gross debt]] or imply an expected loss of 100. Triggers, coverage, recovery and correlated losses matter. Report exposure, expected loss and realised payments separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也不能把所有担保都归为表外或有负债：GFS对标准化担保计划的预计调用准备，以及某些衍生工具，有已确认负债的处理。支付金额不确定本身，也不足以让一项已成立义务变成或有义务。若救助预期改变事前借贷激励，可继续看[[软预算约束]]；风险兑现后再做[[债务存量流量调节|账户衔接]]，避免重复计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every guarantee is off-balance-sheet: GFS recognises liabilities for standardised guarantee provisions and certain derivatives. Uncertain amounts alone do not make an existing obligation contingent. Expected support can create [[软预算约束|soft budget constraints]]; realised costs need [[债务存量流量调节|account reconciliation]] without double-counting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
