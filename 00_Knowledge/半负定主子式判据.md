# 实对称矩阵半负定当且仅当所有主子式奇数阶非正而偶数阶非负

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric matrix is negative semidefinite exactly when every principal minor has the required alternating weak sign*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 实对称，则
$$
A\preceq0\iff(-1)^{|I|}\det A_{I,I}\ge0
\quad\text{对每个非空下标集 }I.
$$
对 $-A$ 使用[[半正定主子式判别]]，并利用 $\det(-A_{I,I})=(-1)^{|I|}\det A_{I,I}$ 即得。必须检查全部主子式，不能只留[[顺序主子式]]。

二维 $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$ 的要求是 $a\le0,c\le0,ac-b^2\ge0$。允许零，也包括[[负定矩阵|负定]]情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the [[半正定主子式判别|positive-semidefinite principal-minor criterion]] to the negative matrix. Odd-order minors must be nonpositive and even-order minors nonnegative. Every index set is required, not merely the leading sequence. The two-dimensional conditions explicitly include both diagonal entries and the determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
