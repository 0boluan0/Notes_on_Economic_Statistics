# Principal-minor test for negative semidefiniteness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*半负定的主子式判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[半负定矩阵|negative-semidefinite matrix]] gives no positive quadratic value in any direction. Its determinant test allows zero, but it must examine **every principal minor**, not just the nested upper-left sequence. Odd-order minors must be nonpositive; even-order minors must be nonnegative. The order of the selected block determines which sign is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[半负定矩阵]]在任何方向上都不产生正的二次型值。对应的行列式判据允许零，但要检查**每一个主子式**，不能只看左上角那一串嵌套方块。奇数阶主子式必须非正，偶数阶必须非负；选择的方块有几阶，就决定检查哪一种符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $A=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$. Its [[主子式|principal minors]] are obtained by retaining the same row and column indices. The three choices give $-1$, $-1$, and $(-1)(-1)-1=0$. The two first-order values are nonpositive and the second-order value is nonnegative, so the test passes. For a real column $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], direct calculation confirms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $A=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$。[[主子式]]通过同时保留相同行、列下标得到；三个选择分别给出 $-1$、$-1$ 和 $(-1)(-1)-1=0$。两个一阶值非正，二阶值非负，所以通过判据。取实列向量 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，直接计算也得到

$$x^TAx=-(x_1-x_2)^2\le0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nonzero vector $(1,1)^T$ gives zero. Thus this matrix is semidefinite but not definite. Zero values are permitted, not compulsory: a [[负定矩阵|negative-definite matrix]] also satisfies the semidefinite criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非零向量 $(1,1)^T$ 给出零值，因此该矩阵半负定但非负定。这里是允许零，不是要求一定有零；[[负定矩阵]]也满足半负定判据。

## All nonempty index sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*检查全部非空下标集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix with a finite integer $n\ge1$. Assume it is [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric]], $A=A^T$, or [[Hermitian 矩阵|complex Hermitian]], $A=A^*$. The star denotes conjugate transpose; in the complex case use $z^*Az\le0$ for every complex column $z$. Hermitian symmetry makes these values real.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为 $n\times n$ 矩阵，有限整数 $n\ge1$。假设它[[实对称矩阵|实对称]]，即 $A=A^T$，或为复 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，即 $A=A^*$。星号表示共轭转置；复数情形要求每个复列向量 $z$ 都满足 $z^*Az\le0$，Hermitian 性使这些值为实数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonempty subset $I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$, let $A_{I,I}$ retain exactly those rows and columns, in increasing order. Its [[行列式|determinant]] is a principal minor of order $|I|$, where $|I|$ counts the indices. Every selected block is again symmetric or Hermitian, so its determinant is real. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非空子集 $I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$，记 $A_{I,I}$ 为按递增次序保留这些行与列的子矩阵。它的[[行列式]]是 $|I|$ 阶主子式，其中 $|I|$ 表示下标个数。每个选出的块仍实对称或 Hermitian，所以行列式为实数。判据为

> [!important] Weak signs on the full collection · 整组主子式的非严格符号
> $$A\preceq0\iff(-1)^{|I|}\det A_{I,I}\ge0
> \quad\text{for every nonempty }I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The notation $A\preceq0$ means negative semidefinite. For odd $|I|$, the condition is $\det A_{I,I}\le0$; for even $|I|$, it is $\det A_{I,I}\ge0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $A\preceq0$ 表示半负定。$|I|$ 为奇数时，要求 $\det A_{I,I}\le0$；为偶数时，要求 $\det A_{I,I}\ge0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are $2^n-1$ nonempty index sets, including nonconsecutive ones. For $n=3$, check the three diagonal entries, the three two-by-two minors on $\{1,2\}$, $\{1,3\}$ and $\{2,3\}$, and the full determinant. Their required signs are, respectively, nonpositive, nonnegative and nonpositive. Equal numerical values from different selections still count as different tests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空下标集共 $2^n-1$ 个，也包括不连续的选择。$n=3$ 时，要检查三个对角元、下标 $\{1,2\}$、$\{1,3\}$、$\{2,3\}$ 上的三个二阶主子式，以及整个行列式；这三组依次要求非正、非负、非正。不同选择即使数值相等，仍属于不同检查项。

## Reduce the proof to the positive-semidefinite theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把证明化为半正定定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $B=-A$. The identity $z^*Bz=-z^*Az$ gives $A\preceq0\iff B\succeq0$, where $B\succeq0$ means [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]]. The [[半正定主子式判别|positive-semidefinite principal-minor theorem]] says that $B$ is semidefinite exactly when all its principal minors are nonnegative. For every nonempty $I$, selecting the block and negating commute:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $B=-A$。由 $z^*Bz=-z^*Az$，得到 $A\preceq0\iff B\succeq0$，其中 $B\succeq0$ 表示[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]。[[半正定主子式判别]]说明，$B$ 半正定恰好等价于它的全部主子式非负。对每个非空 $I$，先选方块再取负，与先取负再选方块相同：

$$B_{I,I}=-A_{I,I},\qquad
\det B_{I,I}=\det(-A_{I,I})=(-1)^{|I|}\det A_{I,I}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last equality uses [[整体缩放的行列式|whole-matrix determinant scaling]]: all $|I|$ rows contribute one factor $-1$. Thus the nonnegative principal-minor conditions for $B$ are exactly the displayed alternating weak conditions for $A$. Both directions follow, with ordinary transpose replacing the star in the real case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一步使用[[整体缩放的行列式]]：$|I|$ 行各贡献一个因子 $-1$。因此，$B$ 的全部主子式非负，恰好转化为 $A$ 按阶数交替的非严格符号，两个方向同时成立。实数情形只需把星号换成普通转置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive theorem's full proof explains why the collection must be complete. Adding $\varepsilon>0$ to each diagonal entry makes every leading determinant a sum of nonnegative principal-minor terms plus the strictly positive term $\varepsilon^k$. Sylvester's strict theorem then applies, and letting $\varepsilon$ tend to zero recovers semidefiniteness. This argument works for Hermitian matrices as well as real symmetric ones; the exact expansion is in the linked theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正性定理的完整证明解释了为何必须检查整组主子式：给每个对角元加上 $\varepsilon>0$ 后，每个顺序行列式都成为若干非负主子式项与严格正项 $\varepsilon^k$ 的和，于是可以使用严格的 Sylvester 定理，再让 $\varepsilon$ 趋零得到半定性。这个论证同时适用于 Hermitian 与实对称矩阵，精确展开式见所链接的定理。

## The two-dimensional test, including zero entries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*二维条件，包括零对角元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the real symmetric matrix $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$, listing the three principal minors gives exactly
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$，列出三个主子式便得到完整条件

$$a\le0,\qquad c\le0,\qquad ac-b^2\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither diagonal condition may be dropped. If $a=0$, the determinant condition reads $-b^2\ge0$, forcing $b=0$; the remaining condition is $c\le0$. This handles the boundary without dividing by $a$. For a complex Hermitian matrix $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\\overline b&c\end{pmatrix}$, $a,c$ are real, $\overline b$ is the conjugate of $b$, and the corresponding conditions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个对角条件都不能省略。若 $a=0$，行列式条件变为 $-b^2\ge0$，迫使 $b=0$；剩下的条件就是 $c\le0$。这样不必除以 $a$，也能处理零边界。对复 Hermitian 矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\\overline b&c\end{pmatrix}$，其中 $a,c$ 为实数，$\overline b$ 是 $b$ 的共轭，相应条件为

$$a\le0,\qquad c\le0,\qquad ac-|b|^2\ge0,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $|b|^2=b\overline b$ is the squared magnitude. For example, with $i^2=-1$, $H=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-i\\i&-1\end{pmatrix}$ has principal minors $-1,-1,0$ and satisfies $z^*Hz=-|z_1+iz_2|^2\le0$. This also shows directly why conjugation matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $|b|^2=b\overline b$ 为模平方。例如，令 $i^2=-1$，则 $H=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-i\\i&-1\end{pmatrix}$ 的主子式为 $-1,-1,0$，且 $z^*Hz=-|z_1+iz_2|^2\le0$，直接说明了取共轭的作用。

## Why leading minors alone miss a positive direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*只检查顺序主子式为什么会漏掉正方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $D=\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$ has diagonal entries $0,1$ and zeros elsewhere. Its two [[顺序主子式|leading principal minors]] are $L_1=L_2=0$, so $(-1)^kL_k\ge0$ for both orders. But its omitted first-order principal minor is $d_{22}=1>0$. The input $(0,1)^T$ gives quadratic value $1$, ruling out negative semidefiniteness. Allowing zero destroys the shortcut to only the leading sequence; the [[负定顺序主子式判据|strict negative-definite criterion]] has a different scope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[对角矩阵]] $D=\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$ 的对角元为 $0,1$，其余元素为零。两个[[顺序主子式]]均为零，因此两阶都满足 $(-1)^kL_k\ge0$。但漏掉的一阶主子式为 $d_{22}=1>0$，输入 $(0,1)^T$ 给出二次型值 $1$，否定半负定。一旦允许零，便不能继续只检查顺序一串；[[负定顺序主子式判据]]的严格版本使用不同范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symmetry assumption is essential too: $\begin{pmatrix}-1&4\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ has all principal minors $-1,-1,1$, with the required signs, but gives value $2$ at $(1,1)^T$. Finally, for $n=1$ the test is $a_{11}\le0$; the zero matrix passes at every size, and every negative-definite matrix passes. The word semidefinite does not require a zero determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对称性前提也不可省略：$\begin{pmatrix}-1&4\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ 的全部主子式为 $-1,-1,1$，符号符合要求，但在 $(1,1)^T$ 上给出值 $2$。最后，一维时只需 $a_{11}\le0$；零矩阵在任何维数都通过，每个负定矩阵也都通过。“半定”不要求行列式一定为零。
