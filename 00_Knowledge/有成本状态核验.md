# 有成本状态核验是外部人必须付出资源才能查明私人观察结果的信息结构

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Costly state verification is an information structure in which outsiders must expend resources to establish a privately observed outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有成本状态核验（costly state verification）描述这样一类模型：项目结果已经实现，内部人知道真实结果，外部人若想确认它就必须支付审计等核验成本。合同需要同时约定状态如何核实以及各状态怎样支付，不能只写一个依赖真实利润的公式就假定利润已为双方所知。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Costly state verification describes models where an outcome has been realized and is known to an insider, but an outsider must incur an audit or verification cost to establish it. A contract must address both verification and payments; writing a formula based on true profit does not make that profit common knowledge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如经营者知道收入是 50 还是 100，出资人必须花费 5 才能准确核实。核验成本是消耗掉的资源，不是双方之间纯粹转移的支付。Townsend 的基准模型还假定一旦核验就准确揭示结果；存在审计误差时需另建信息结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the operator knows whether revenue is 50 or 100, while the investor must spend 5 to verify it exactly. Verification consumes resources rather than merely transferring money between the parties. Townsend's baseline also assumes that verification reveals the outcome without error; audit errors require a different information structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它核实的是实现后的状态，不等于监督经营者此前是否努力。实际贷款可以同时需要两者；这里的模型并未把所有监督问题消除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The object verified is a realized state, not necessarily the operator's earlier effort. Real lending may require both outcome verification and action monitoring; the model does not remove every monitoring problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
