# The first-order criterion for concavity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凹性一阶判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tangent line or plane is usually a prediction near one input. For a differentiable concave function, it does more: it gives an upper bound for the function at every input in its convex domain. Conversely, if every tangent plane gives that global upper bound, the function is concave. This criterion translates a comparison between chords into a comparison using derivatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切线或切平面通常只预测某个输入附近的函数值。对可微凹函数，它还提供更强的信息：在整个凸定义域内，函数都不高于这个切平面。反过来，如果每一个切平面都给出这样的全域上界，函数就是凹的。这个判据把弦的比较转成了导数的比较。

> [!example] One tangent bounds the whole parabola · 一条切线界住整条抛物线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(u)=-u^2$, take the base input $u=1$. The value is $-1$ and the derivative is $-2$, so the tangent value at another input $v$ is $T_1(v)=-1-2(v-1)=1-2v$. Its excess over the true value is $T_1(v)-f(v)=(v-1)^2\ge0$. At $v=3$, the tangent gives $-5$ while the function gives $-9$. The upper bound is valid even away from the base input.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(u)=-u^2$，在基点 $u=1$ 处，函数值为 $-1$、导数为 $-2$，所以另一个输入 $v$ 处的切线值为 $T_1(v)=-1-2(v-1)=1-2v$。它比真实函数值高 $(v-1)^2\ge0$。例如 $v=3$ 时，切线值为 $-5$，真实值为 $-9$；即使离开基点，上界仍成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the tangent expression means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*切平面表达式在说什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[凸集|convex domain]] contains the segment between any two of its inputs. A [[凹凸性|concave function]] has a value at each mixed input at least as large as the same weighted average of the endpoint values. Those are comparisons of real outputs; the inputs may be vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[凸集|凸定义域]]包含任意两输入之间的线段。[[凹凸性|凹函数]]在混合输入处的值，不低于端点输出的同权重平均。比较的是实数输出，输入则可以是向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U$ be a nonempty [[开集|open]] convex subset of $\mathbb R^n$, with $n\ge1$, and let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable at every point. Here differentiability means a [[全微分|total differential]], not merely the existence of separate partial derivatives. At a base input $x$, the linear map $Df(x)$ predicts the change caused by a displacement $h$. Therefore

$$T_x(y)=f(x)+Df(x)(y-x)$$

is the tangent affine function, meaning a constant plus a linear expression in $y$. The scalar $Df(x)(y-x)$ is the predicted output change from $x$ toward $y$. In one dimension it is $f'(x)(y-x)$; in several dimensions it is the gradient's dot product with $y-x$, as explained in [[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|the gradient representation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 中非空的开凸集，$n\ge1$，且 $f:U\to\mathbb R$ 在每一点可微。这里的可微是存在[[全微分]]，不只是各偏导数分别存在。在基点 $x$，线性映射 $Df(x)$ 预测增量 $h$ 带来的函数变化。因此 $T_x(y)$ 是切平面的仿射表达式，即常数加上关于 $y$ 的线性式；其中标量 $Df(x)(y-x)$ 预测从 $x$ 朝 $y$ 移动时的输出变化。一维时它是 $f'(x)(y-x)$，多维时则是梯度与 $y-x$ 的点积，见[[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|梯度表示]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A global bound at every base point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每个基点都要给出全域上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under these assumptions, concavity is equivalent to $f(y)\le T_x(y)$ for every pair $x,y\in U$. Both quantifiers matter: every base point and every comparison input. The forward proof takes a derivative along a segment; the converse combines two tangent inequalities at the mixed point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U$ 开且凸，$f:U\to\mathbb R$ 可微，则
$$
f\text{ 凹}\iff f(y)\le f(x)+Df(x)(y-x)\quad\forall x,y\in U.
$$
凹性作用于 $x+t(y-x)$，减去 $f(x)$ 后除以 $t>0$ 并令 $t\downarrow0$，便得上界。反向在 $z=tx+(1-t)y$ 对两端使用上界，按权重相加时，$t(x-z)+(1-t)(y-z)=0$，剩下凹性的弦不等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Concavity along a segment yields the tangent upper bound by taking a directional limit. Conversely, applying the bound at a mixture to both endpoints cancels the weighted derivative terms and gives the chord inequality. This characterizes [[凹凸性|curvature]] globally on the domain, not merely a local approximation at one point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是[[凹凸性]]的全域判据，不只是某一个点的局部近似；取负号得到凸函数的切平面下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Derive the tangent bound from concavity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从凹性推出切平面上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix any $x,y\in U$. If they coincide, the required inequality is equality. Otherwise put $h=y-x$. For $0<t\le1$, convexity of $U$ keeps $x+th$ in the domain, and concavity gives

$$f(x+th)\ge(1-t)f(x)+t f(y).$$

Subtract $f(x)$ and divide by the positive scalar $t$:

$$\frac{f(x+th)-f(x)}{t}\ge f(y)-f(x).$$

Differentiability at $x$ says that the left side tends to $Df(x)h$ as $t\downarrow0$. Hence $f(y)-f(x)\le Df(x)(y-x)$. The same reasoning works for every pair, proving the global upper bound. We divided by a real parameter, not by a vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取 $x,y\in U$。若两点重合，所需不等式就是等式；否则令 $h=y-x$。当 $0<t\le1$ 时，定义域的凸性保证 $x+th$ 仍在域内，凹性给出上面的第一式。减去 $f(x)$，再除以正标量 $t$，得到差商不等式。由 $x$ 处可微，$t\downarrow0$ 时左边趋于 $Df(x)h$，于是 $f(y)-f(x)\le Df(x)(y-x)$。这对任意两点都成立，便得全域上界；整个过程除的是实数参数，不是向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recover concavity from all tangent bounds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从所有切平面上界恢复凹性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now assume the tangent upper bound holds for every pair. Choose any $x,y\in U$ and $0<t<1$, and set $z=tx+(1-t)y$. Apply the bound with the same base point $z$ to each endpoint:

$$f(x)\le f(z)+Df(z)(x-z),\qquad
f(y)\le f(z)+Df(z)(y-z).$$

Multiply the first inequality by $t$, the second by $1-t$, and add. Since the differential is linear,

$$tDf(z)(x-z)+(1-t)Df(z)(y-z)
=Df(z)\bigl(tx+(1-t)y-z\bigr)=0.$$

What remains is $tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)\le f(z)$, the defining concavity inequality. At $t=0$ or $t=1$ it is equality, so all weights in $[0,1]$ are covered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在假定切平面上界对任意两点都成立。任取 $x,y\in U$、$0<t<1$，令 $z=tx+(1-t)y$。以同一个混合点 $z$ 为基点，分别对端点 $x,y$ 使用上界。第一式乘 $t$，第二式乘 $1-t$，相加后利用全微分的线性性；由于两增量的加权和恰为零，导数项完全抵消。剩下 $tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)\le f(z)$，正是凹性定义。$t=0$ 或 $t=1$ 时直接取等，所以覆盖了 $[0,1]$ 中所有权重。

## Keep the hypotheses and quantifiers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保留条件与量词*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Concavity places the graph above its chords and below its tangent planes; these compare different affine functions, so the directions are consistent. The theorem needs no second derivative or continuity of the derivative. Concavity itself also applies to nondifferentiable functions, such as $-|u|$; at its corner the displayed derivative-based test is unavailable. Negating $f$ reverses all inequalities and gives the corresponding tangent lower bound for convex functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

凹函数的图像在弦上方、切平面下方；比较对象不同，因此两个方向并不矛盾。本定理不需要二阶导数，也不要求导数连续。凹性本身还适用于不可微函数，例如 $-|u|$；但在尖角处，不能使用这里含导数的判据。把 $f$ 取负会使不等号反向，得到凸函数的切平面下界。

> [!warning] One base point is not the criterion · 一个基点还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $g(u)=-(u^2-1)^2$ on $\mathbb R$. At $u=1$, both $g(1)$ and $g'(1)$ are zero, so this tangent is the global upper bound zero. Yet $g(-1)=g(1)=0$ and $g(0)=-1$: the midpoint lies below the endpoint average. The function is not concave. The theorem requires the upper-bound property at every base point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $g(u)=-(u^2-1)^2$，定义域为 $\mathbb R$。在 $u=1$ 处，函数值与导数都是零，因此这条切线确实是全域上界零。但 $g(-1)=g(1)=0$、$g(0)=-1$，中点低于端点平均，函数并不凹。判据要求每个基点的切平面都具有上界性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stated open-domain hypothesis supplies ordinary total derivatives at all base points; convexity supplies the connecting segments. An included boundary or a lower-dimensional feasible set needs a specified extension or relative derivative formulation, not an automatic application of this exact statement. Once concavity is established, [[凹函数驻点全局最优|the stationary-point global-optimality result]] is a separate application of the tangent bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的开域条件保证每个基点都有通常意义的全微分，凸性则保证连接线段可用。若包含边界，或可行集处在较低维子空间中，就要说明延拓或相对导数的约定，不能直接照搬本陈述。建立凹性以后，[[凹函数驻点全局最优]]是对切平面上界的另一个应用。
