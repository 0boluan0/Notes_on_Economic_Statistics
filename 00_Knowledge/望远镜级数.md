# Telescoping series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*望远镜级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **telescoping series** becomes easier to understand when we expand a finite number of its terms: many contributions cancel with later contributions, leaving only a few terms at the beginning and end. The name describes this cancellation, like a telescope collapsing into a shorter tube. It does not by itself promise that the infinite series converges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**望远镜级数**的特点是：先展开有限多项，就会发现许多贡献与后面的贡献相消，只留下开头和末尾少数几项，像望远镜的镜筒收拢一样。这个名称描述的是消项结构，本身并不保证无穷级数收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the positive terms $1/[n(n+1)]$, where $n=1,2,\ldots$. Combining two fractions over a common denominator gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看正数项 $1/[n(n+1)]$，其中 $n=1,2,\ldots$。把两个分数通分，就得到

$$\frac1n-\frac1{n+1}
=\frac{(n+1)-n}{n(n+1)}=\frac1{n(n+1)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus each term is a difference between two nearby entries of the list $1,1/2,1/3,\ldots$. The first three terms add up as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以每项都是列表 $1,1/2,1/3,\ldots$ 中相邻两项之差。前三项相加时，

$$S_3=\left(1-\frac12\right)
+\left(\frac12-\frac13\right)
+\left(\frac13-\frac14\right)=1-\frac14=\frac34.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The negative $1/2$ cancels the next positive $1/2$, and the same happens to $1/3$. The final negative $1/4$ has no partner inside this finite sum, so it must remain. As in the [[无穷级数|definition of an infinite series]], $S_N$ means the sum of the first $N$ original terms, and convergence is decided by the limit of these finite totals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负的 $1/2$ 与下一项中的正 $1/2$ 抵消，$1/3$ 也一样。最后的负 $1/4$ 在这个有限和里没有配对项，所以必须保留。按[[无穷级数]]的定义，$S_N$ 表示前 $N$ 个原始项的部分和；是否收敛，要看这些有限总量的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the last term before taking a limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先保留末项，再取极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any positive integer $N$, the same cancellation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意正整数 $N$，同样消项得到

$$S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac1{n(n+1)}
=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left(\frac1n-\frac1{n+1}\right)
=1-\frac1{N+1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now $1/(N+1)\to0$, so $S_N\to1$ and the infinite sum is $1$. The remaining term also measures the error: $1-S_N=1/(N+1)$. For example, $S_{99}=0.99$ has error exactly $0.01$, whereas every $N\ge100$ gives error strictly below $0.01$. Cancellation supplied a finite formula; the limit of its remaining term supplied convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在才令 $N$ 增大：由于 $1/(N+1)\to0$，有 $S_N\to1$，所以无穷和为 $1$。保留下来的末项还给出了误差：$1-S_N=1/(N+1)$。例如 $S_{99}=0.99$，误差恰为 $0.01$；每个 $N\ge100$ 的误差才严格小于 $0.01$。消项给出有限公式，剩余项的极限才证明收敛。

> [!warning] Cancel inside a finite sum · 在有限和内消项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Do not replace this argument by subtracting $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/(n+1)$ from $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/n$. Both harmonic tails diverge, as the [[级数通项判别|harmonic counterexample]] explains. Their separate infinite values cannot be subtracted as $\infty-\infty$. Subtraction through the same finite cutoff $N$ is ordinary arithmetic and is valid.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不能把论证换成两条无穷级数 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/n$ 与 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/(n+1)$ 相减。[[级数通项判别|调和反例]]解释了它们为何发散，两个分别发散的“值”不能作 $\infty-\infty$ 运算。先在相同有限截断点 $N$ 处相减，则只是合法的有限次运算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The adjacent-difference pattern
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相邻差的一般形式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $b_1,b_2,\ldots$ be finite real numbers. Use the differences $a_n=b_n-b_{n+1}$ as the terms to be added. The $b_n$ are the auxiliary entries that cancel; the $a_n$ are the actual terms of the series. For a fixed positive starting index $m$ and an integer $N\ge m$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $b_1,b_2,\ldots$ 都是有限实数，用差 $a_n=b_n-b_{n+1}$ 作为相加的项。$b_n$ 是参与抵消的辅助数，$a_n$ 才是级数的通项。固定正整数起始索引 $m$，对整数 $N\ge m$，

$$\sum_{n=m}^{N}(b_n-b_{n+1})
=(b_m-b_{m+1})+\cdots+(b_N-b_{N+1})
=b_m-b_{N+1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This identity holds even for $N=m$, when there is only one difference. It contains $N-m+1$ original terms, so $N$ is the last index rather than always the number of terms. With $m=1$, it becomes $S_N=b_1-b_{N+1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个恒等式在 $N=m$、只有一个差时也成立。原始项数为 $N-m+1$，所以 $N$ 是最后一个索引，不总是项数。取 $m=1$，就得到 $S_N=b_1-b_{N+1}$。

> [!important] Read the remaining boundary value · 检查剩余的边界值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For this adjacent-difference form, the series converges exactly when $b_{N+1}$ has a finite limit $B$. Its sum is then $b_m-B$. Conversely, if the partial sums tend to a finite $S$, the same identity gives $b_{N+1}\to b_m-S$. This is a statement about [[序列收敛|convergence of a sequence]], with the starting index $m$ held fixed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对这种相邻差形式，级数收敛当且仅当 $b_{N+1}$ 有有限极限 $B$，此时和为 $b_m-B$。反过来，若部分和趋于有限数 $S$，同一恒等式就给出 $b_{N+1}\to b_m-S$。这里用的是[[序列收敛]]，起始索引 $m$ 始终固定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remaining limit need not be zero. If $b_n=2+1/n$, then $B=2$ and $b_1-B=3-2=1$: the differences are exactly the opening example. If instead $b_n=n$, every difference equals $-1$ and $S_N=-N$, so cancellation still occurs but the series diverges. With $b_n=(-1)^{n-1}$, $S_N=1-(-1)^N$ alternates between $2$ and $0$ and also diverges. There is no general requirement that telescoping terms be positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

剩余项的极限不一定为零。若 $b_n=2+1/n$，则 $B=2$，$b_1-B=3-2=1$，它的差正好就是开头的例子。若 $b_n=n$，每个差都为 $-1$，$S_N=-N$；虽然仍能消项，级数却发散。若 $b_n=(-1)^{n-1}$，则 $S_N=1-(-1)^N$ 在 $2$ 与 $0$ 之间交替，也发散。望远镜结构并不要求通项为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The starting index changes the answer too. For the original $b_n=1/n$, summing from $n=3$ leaves $1/3-1/(N+1)$, so the infinite tail sums to $1/3$. Keeping the old first boundary value $1$ would count terms that are no longer present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

起始索引也会改变结果。对原来的 $b_n=1/n$，若从 $n=3$ 开始，剩下的是 $1/3-1/(N+1)$，所以这段无穷尾和为 $1/3$。仍保留原来的首端值 $1$，就会把已经没有的项算进去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## More than one term may survive at each end
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两端可能各留下不止一项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shift can skip an index. For instance,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消项时也可能隔开一个索引，例如

$$\frac1{n(n+2)}=\frac12\left(\frac1n-\frac1{n+2}\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $N\ge2$, the negative list starts at $1/3$, so $1$ and $1/2$ have no negative partners at the start. At the far end, $-1/(N+1)$ and $-1/(N+2)$ have no positive partners. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $N\ge2$，带负号的列表从 $1/3$ 才开始，因此开头的 $1$ 和 $1/2$ 没有负号项与之配对；末端的 $-1/(N+1)$ 和 $-1/(N+2)$ 也没有正号项配对。所以

$$\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac1{n(n+2)}
=\frac12\left(1+\frac12-\frac1{N+1}-\frac1{N+2}\right)
\longrightarrow\frac34.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed formula also checks directly at $N=1$. The important feature is the full expression left at the two ends, not a rule that exactly one term must remain at each end. In another telescoping pattern, inspect the limit of that combined remaining expression; do not delete it just because the upper index will tend to infinity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个公式在 $N=1$ 时也能直接核对。关键在于两端留下的完整表达式，而不是规定每端恰好只剩一项。遇到其他消项形式，应检查整个剩余表达式的极限，不能因为上标将趋于无穷，就把末端项直接删掉。
