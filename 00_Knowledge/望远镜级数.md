# 望远镜级数是在每个有限部分和中大量中间项相消的无穷级数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A telescoping series is an infinite series whose finite partial sums contain extensive cancellation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

望远镜级数的特征是：把前 $N$ 项展开后，中间项相消，只留下少量首尾项。最常见的形式是 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(b_n-b_{n+1})$，因为其部分和恰为 $S_N=b_1-b_{N+1}$；若 $b_{N+1}\to B\in\mathbb R$，和就是 $b_1-B$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a telescoping series, expansion of the first $N$ terms leaves only a few boundary terms. In the standard form $\sum(b_n-b_{n+1})$, the partial sum is $b_1-b_{N+1}$, giving sum $b_1-B$ when $b_{N+1}\to B$ is finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $1/[n(n+1)]=1/n-1/(n+1)$，于是
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac1{n(n+1)}=1-\frac1{N+1}\longrightarrow1.
$$
这里先在有限和里消项，保留末项，再取极限。若直接把它拆成两条各自发散的调和级数相减，就失去了合法的极限运算依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $1/[n(n+1)]=1/n-1/(n+1)$, the finite sum is $1-1/(N+1)$ and tends to one. Cancellation takes place before the limit, with the final term retained. Subtracting two separately divergent harmonic series would not justify the calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
