# Heteroskedasticity-robust covariance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*异方差稳健协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some observations are noisier than others. In an income regression, for example, the spread of incomes around the fitted relation may differ across groups. This is [[异方差|heteroskedasticity]]. A heteroskedasticity-robust covariance estimate, often called **HC** or **White covariance**, keeps the OLS fit and changes how its uncertainty is estimated. Large squared residuals contribute more estimated noise, while the design determines how that noise affects each coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些观测比另一些更嘈杂。例如收入回归中，不同群体的收入可能在拟合关系周围有不同的分散程度，这就是[[异方差]]。异方差稳健协方差估计常简称 **HC** 或 **White 协方差**：保留 OLS 拟合，改变对其不确定性的估计。较大的残差平方提供较大的噪声贡献，而设计决定这些噪声如何影响各个系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From error variance to coefficient uncertainty
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从误差方差到系数的不确定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $y_i=x_i^T\beta+u_i$ for $n$ observations and $k$ coefficients. The column vector $x_i$ contains row $i$'s predictors, including a $1$ if an intercept is fitted; stacking $x_i^T$ gives the design matrix $X$. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. For the basic conditional-mean argument, assume independent observations, $E(u\mid X)=0$, finite conditional error variances, and a design with [[线性无关|linearly independent columns]] and $n>k$. Let $A=(X^TX)^{-1}$ and $\sigma_i^2=\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X)$. The [[协方差失效下的OLS|general OLS covariance]] simplifies to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n$ 个观测、$k$ 个系数，写作 $y_i=x_i^T\beta+u_i$。列向量 $x_i$ 包含第 $i$ 行解释变量；含截距时也含一个 $1$，把 $x_i^T$ 叠起来就是设计矩阵 $X$。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]。基础条件均值论证假定观测独立、$E(u\mid X)=0$、条件误差方差有限，设计各列[[线性无关]]且 $n>k$。令 $A=(X^TX)^{-1}$、$\sigma_i^2=\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X)$，[[协方差失效下的OLS|一般 OLS 协方差]]简化为

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\hat\beta\mid X)
=A\left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_ix_i^T\sigma_i^2\right)A.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[回归残差|residual]] $\hat u_i=y_i-x_i^T\hat\beta$ is what remains after fitting. We cannot observe the error $u_i$ or its variance directly. **HC0** inserts each squared residual in the middle sum:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[回归残差]] $\hat u_i=y_i-x_i^T\hat\beta$ 是拟合后剩下的部分。真正的误差 $u_i$ 及其方差都不能直接观察。**HC0** 在中间求和中代入每行的残差平方：

$$
\widehat V_{\mathrm{HC0}}
=A\left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_ix_i^T\hat u_i^2\right)A.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $j$th estimated [[标准误含义|standard error]] is $\sqrt{(\widehat V_{\mathrm{HC0}})_{jj}}$, not the diagonal entry itself. The outer product $x_ix_i^T$ distributes row $i$'s contribution across coefficient variances and covariances. A single squared residual is not a consistent estimate of that row's variance merely because other rows are added. The large-sample argument concerns the combined average of these contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 $j$ 个估计[[标准误含义|标准误]]为 $\sqrt{(\widehat V_{\mathrm{HC0}})_{jj}}$，而非对角元本身。外积 $x_ix_i^T$ 把第 $i$ 行的贡献分配到系数的各个方差和协方差。增加其他行，并不会自动让这一行的一个残差平方成为其方差的一致估计；大样本论证针对的是这些贡献汇总后的平均。

> [!example] Four observations, the same fitted slope · 四个观测，同一条拟合斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Regress $y=(0,2,0,6)^T$ on an intercept and $x=(0,0,1,1)^T$. The two group means are $1$ and $3$, so the fitted intercept is $1$ and slope is $2$. Residuals are $(-1,1,-3,3)^T$ and their squared sum is $20$.
>
> Classical estimation uses $s^2=20/(4-2)=10$ and gives slope variance $10$. HC0 instead adds the squared-residual contribution from each group mean: $(1+1)/2^2+(9+9)/2^2=5$. Its slope standard error is $\sqrt5$ rather than $\sqrt{10}$. The fitted slope is still $2$. This tiny dataset illustrates the calculations; it does not establish reliable large-sample calibration, and “robust” need not mean “larger”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用截距及 $x=(0,0,1,1)^T$ 回归 $y=(0,2,0,6)^T$。两组均值是 $1$ 和 $3$，拟合截距为 $1$、斜率为 $2$。残差是 $(-1,1,-3,3)^T$，平方和为 $20$。
>
> 经典估计用 $s^2=20/(4-2)=10$，得到斜率方差 $10$。HC0 则汇总两个组均值的残差平方贡献：$(1+1)/2^2+(9+9)/2^2=5$，斜率标准误为 $\sqrt5$ 而非 $\sqrt{10}$，但拟合斜率仍是 $2$。小数据仅用于展示算法，不证明大样本校准可靠；“稳健”也不意味着数值更大。

## Why HC versions differ
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*HC 的几个版本为何不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fitting shrinks residuals. The [[Hat杠杆值|leverage]] $h_{ii}=x_i^TAx_i$ measures how strongly an observation influences its own fitted value. Under the correctly specified homoskedastic model, $E(\hat u_i^2\mid X)=(1-h_{ii})\sigma^2$; this explains a source of HC0's finite-sample downward bias. Common variants change the quantity replacing $\sigma_i^2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟合会收缩残差。[[Hat杠杆值|杠杆值]] $h_{ii}=x_i^TAx_i$ 衡量一个观测对自身拟合值的影响。在正确设定的同方差模型下，$E(\hat u_i^2\mid X)=(1-h_{ii})\sigma^2$，这解释了 HC0 有限样本向下偏差的一种来源。常见版本改变代替 $\sigma_i^2$ 的量：

| Version | Contribution used in the middle sum · 中间求和使用的量 |
|---|---|
| HC0 | $\hat u_i^2$ |
| HC1 | $\dfrac{n}{n-k}\hat u_i^2$ |
| HC2 | $\dfrac{\hat u_i^2}{1-h_{ii}}$ |
| HC3 | $\dfrac{\hat u_i^2}{(1-h_{ii})^2}$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
HC1 rescales globally; HC2 and HC3 adjust by observation, with HC3 making a larger adjustment when $0<h_{ii}<1$. They require positive denominators: $n>k$ for HC1 and $h_{ii}<1$ for HC2/HC3. Full rank and $n>k$ alone do not exclude an individual leverage of one. In the example every leverage is $1/2$, giving slope variances $5,10,10,20$ for HC0–HC3. This equality of HC1 and HC2 is particular to the example. No version promises exact finite-sample inference under arbitrary heteroskedasticity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

HC1 整体缩放，HC2、HC3 按观测调整；$0<h_{ii}<1$ 时 HC3 的放大更大。分母必须为正：HC1 需 $n>k$，HC2/HC3 需 $h_{ii}<1$。满秩且 $n>k$ 并不排除某个观测的杠杆值恰好为一。例中每个杠杆值为 $1/2$，HC0–HC3 的斜率方差依次是 $5,10,10,20$；HC1 与 HC2 相等是这个例子的特点。没有一个版本能对任意异方差保证精确有限样本推断。

## What makes the large-sample argument work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*大样本依据与使用边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One sufficient framework uses [[独立同分布|iid]] observations $(x_i,y_i)$, a fixed number of coefficients, [[矩存在性的使用边界|finite fourth moments]] of the response and predictors (finite expected fourth powers), and a [[正定矩阵|positive-definite]] $Q=E(x_ix_i^T)$, so every nonzero direction has a positive expected squared predictor combination. For the population linear-projection coefficient, write $u_i=y_i-x_i^T\beta$ and $S=E(x_ix_i^Tu_i^2)$. Then the usual OLS central limit theorem has limiting covariance $Q^{-1}SQ^{-1}$, and HC0 consistently estimates this covariance at the matching scale: $n\widehat V_{\mathrm{HC0}}\xrightarrow{p}Q^{-1}SQ^{-1}$. The arrow denotes [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]]: the chance of an appreciable error in the scaled estimate tends to zero. The factor $n$ matters because coefficient variance is typically of order $1/n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一套充分框架是：$(x_i,y_i)$ [[独立同分布]]，系数个数固定，响应与解释变量有[[矩存在性的使用边界|有限四阶矩]]（四次幂的期望有限），且 $Q=E(x_ix_i^T)$ 为[[正定矩阵]]，即每个非零方向的解释变量组合都有正的平方期望。对总体线性投影系数，令 $u_i=y_i-x_i^T\beta$、$S=E(x_ix_i^Tu_i^2)$。常规 OLS 中心极限定理的极限协方差为 $Q^{-1}SQ^{-1}$，HC0 在匹配尺度下一致估计它：$n\widehat V_{\mathrm{HC0}}\xrightarrow{p}Q^{-1}SQ^{-1}$。箭头表示[[依概率收敛]]：缩放后的估计出现明显误差的概率趋于零。这个 $n$ 很关键，因为系数方差通常是 $1/n$ 阶。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a normal approximation to a specified scalar contrast, its limiting variance must also be positive. This is a sufficient theorem, not a claim that fourth moments are always necessary or that any independent dataset is adequate. Small samples, dominant observations, many fitted coefficients or nearly uninformative designs need further analysis. The [[条件均值与线性投影|projection target]] can remain meaningful when the exact conditional mean is nonlinear; HC does not turn that projection into a correct conditional-mean or causal model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对指定标量组合使用正态近似，还要求该方向的极限方差为正。这是充分定理，不是说四阶矩永远必要，或任何独立数据都已经足够。样本小、单点主导、拟合系数很多或设计几乎不能提供信息时，需要另作论证。精确条件均值为非线性时，[[条件均值与线性投影|线性投影目标]]仍可能有意义；HC 不会把该投影变成正确的条件均值或因果模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The independence framework also leaves out serial and within-group covariance. Use [[标准误口径匹配|the actual sampling and dependence structure]] to assess [[HAC协方差|HAC]] or [[聚类标准误边界|cluster inference]], including their own conditions. Pre-specifying HC for plausible heteroskedasticity does not require first obtaining a significant [[White异方差检验|White test]]. If a robust option leaves coefficients unchanged, check the covariance matrix and the ensuing intervals/tests, and report the precise HC variant and reference law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的独立观测框架也省去了跨期与组内协方差，应按[[标准误口径匹配|实际抽样与依赖结构]]判断[[HAC协方差|HAC]]或[[聚类标准误边界|聚类推断]]及其各自条件。设计允许未知异方差时，可以预先指定 HC，不必先做出显著的[[White异方差检验]]。开启稳健选项后系数没变，应核对协方差矩阵以及随之变化的区间和检验，并报告具体 HC 版本与参考分布。

- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/07_异方差.md#4.3. 保留 OLS，只修正推断|Local course, §4.3]] and [MIT14.32 recitation, PDF pp.3–4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-32-econometrics-spring-2007/c68f54a5cac9d51f45f5916c123a223f_rec_4_20.pdf#page=3): retain the course distinction between changing the fit and changing its reported uncertainty. The matrix formula and HC versions are checked against Hansen and the implementation documentation.
- [White (1980)](https://doi.org/10.2307/1912934): historical original reference and further reading; the accessible sources above support the derivation used here.
