# 训练后量化从已有模型生成低精度表示而不进行量化感知的端到端训练

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Post-training quantization produces a low-precision representation of an existing model without quantization-aware end-to-end training*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

训练后量化（post-training quantization，PTQ）以已有模型为起点，选择量化参数、进行必要的转换并生成低精度表示，而不通过[[量化感知训练|量化感知的端到端训练]]来适应量化。具体方法可以不需要数据，也可以使用少量校准数据，甚至进行局部重构或参数变换；PTQ 并不只是一条数值类型转换指令。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Post-training quantization (PTQ) starts from an existing model, selects quantization parameters, applies any required transformations, and produces a low-precision representation without [[量化感知训练|quantization-aware end-to-end training]]. A method may be data-free or use a small calibration set, local reconstruction, or parameter transformations. PTQ is therefore more than a numerical type cast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 校准具体决定什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What calibration determines*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个简单静态方案使用代表性输入运行模型，收集中间激活的范围，再为这些激活确定 [[数值量化|scale 和 zero-point]]。这些输入展示部署时可能出现的数值；校准是否需要任务标签取决于方法，简单范围统计不需要标签。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simple static scheme runs representative inputs through the model, collects activation ranges, and selects [[数值量化|scales and zero-points]]. The inputs expose numerical values likely to occur at deployment. Whether calibration needs task labels depends on the method; simple range statistics do not require them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，校准中观察到某组非负激活范围为 $[0,1.5]$。若采用 2-bit 无符号码、$z=0$ 的 min-max 方案，则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose calibration observes a nonnegative activation group ranging from $0$ to $1.5$. A two-bit unsigned min-max scheme with $z=0$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s=\frac{1.5-0}{2^2-1}=0.5,
\qquad \text{levels}=\{0,0.5,1,1.5\}.
$$

部署时若出现 $x=2$，编码先得到 $2/0.5=4$，再截到最大码 3，反量化为 $1.5$。所以校准得到范围，不等于证明以后所有输入都在范围内；失配后会出现[[量化舍入与截断|截断误差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If deployment produces $x=2$, encoding first gives $2/0.5=4$, which clips to the maximum code three and dequantizes to $1.5$. A calibrated range does not prove that all future inputs lie inside it. A mismatch can introduce [[量化舍入与截断|clipping error]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## PTQ 与静态或动态是两种划分

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*PTQ and static/dynamic quantization are different classifications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

静态量化提前固定某些量化参数；动态量化在运行时根据当前数值重新估计相应参数。例如当前组最大值为 $2$ 时，动态方案可以改用 $s=2/3$，容纳 $2$，同时改变该组其他数值的等级间距。PTQ/QAT 则说明模型是否在训练中适应了量化；它们不直接决定部署参数是否动态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Static quantization fixes certain parameters beforehand; dynamic quantization estimates the relevant parameters from current runtime values. If a group's current maximum is $2$, a dynamic scheme could use $s=2/3$, accommodating $2$ while changing the level spacing for other values. PTQ/QAT instead describes whether training adapts the model to quantization. It does not by itself determine whether deployment parameters are dynamic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PTQ 获得的低精度模型仍需检查目标任务质量与[[量化加速条件|实际推理收益]]；“无需端到端重训”是制作流程属性，不是质量或速度保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A PTQ model still requires task-quality checks and verification of [[量化加速条件|actual inference gains]]. Avoiding end-to-end retraining is a workflow property, not a quality or speed guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Nagel et al. (2021), *A White Paper on Neural Network Quantization*](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.08295v1)，§3–3.1、§3.4：支持从预训练网络出发、无需端到端训练、数据可无或少量校准，以及范围选择和局部输出误差优化的作用。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Sections 3–3.1 and 3.4 describe conversion of pretrained networks without end-to-end training, optional calibration data, quantization-range selection, and local output-error optimization.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Xiao et al. (2022), *SmoothQuant*](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.10438v1)，§2 区分离线参数与运行时参数，§3 展示 PTQ 中的模型参数变换。范围为 $[0,1.5]$ 的例子是构造计算，未声称代表某个模型的激活分布。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Section 2 distinguishes offline and runtime parameter estimation; §3 demonstrates model-parameter transformations within PTQ. The $[0,1.5]$ range example is constructed and does not describe a measured model distribution.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
