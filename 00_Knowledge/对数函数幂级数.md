# The logarithm power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对数函数幂级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The logarithm series computes $\ln(1+x)$ by adding powers of the change $x$ from $1$. For example, $\ln(1.1)$ uses $x=0.1$, and its first three terms give $0.1-0.1^2/2+0.1^3/3\approx0.0953333$. To know whether this is a good approximation, we need both a valid interval and a bound on the terms left out. The [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] itself is defined whenever $1+x>0$; this particular expansion has a smaller range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对数级数把相对于 $1$ 的变化量 $x$ 的各次幂相加，用来计算 $\ln(1+x)$。例如，求 $\ln(1.1)$ 时取 $x=0.1$，前三项得到 $0.1-0.1^2/2+0.1^3/3\approx0.0953333$。要知道这个近似是否可靠，既要确定有效区间，也要估计遗漏部分。[[自然对数]]本身只要求 $1+x>0$，但这一份展开的适用范围更小。

> [!important]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For real $x$,
>
> $$\ln(1+x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}x^n}{n}
> =x-\frac{x^2}{2}+\frac{x^3}{3}-\cdots,
> \qquad -1<x\le1.$$
>
> Here $n=1,2,\ldots$ is the summation index. The [[幂级数收敛半径|radius of convergence]] about zero is $1$. The series converges absolutely for $|x|<1$ and conditionally at $x=1$; it diverges at $x=-1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $n=1,2,\ldots$ 是求和索引，展开中心为零，[[幂级数收敛半径]]为 $1$。$|x|<1$ 时绝对收敛；$x=1$ 时条件收敛；$x=-1$ 时发散。上式在实数范围内的完整适用区间是 $(-1,1]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Integrate a geometric series inside its radius
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先在半径内部积分几何级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[几何级数|geometric series]] with ratio $-t$ gives $1/(1+t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-t)^n$ for $|t|<1$. Fix $|x|<1$. The entire finite segment between $0$ and $x$ stays inside that radius, so [[幂级数逐项积分|termwise integration]] is valid. The [[自然对数积分表示|integral representation of the logarithm]], after shifting the integration variable by one, gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以 $-t$ 为公比的[[几何级数]]在 $|t|<1$ 时给出 $1/(1+t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-t)^n$。固定 $|x|<1$，$0$ 与 $x$ 之间的整个有限线段都位于半径内部，因此可以[[幂级数逐项积分]]。将[[自然对数积分表示]]中的积分变量平移一，得到

$$\ln(1+x)=\int_0^x\frac{dt}{1+t}
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relabelling $n+1$ as the index gives the displayed logarithm series. The lower limit $0$ fixes the value $\ln1=0$, so there is no undetermined additive constant. For negative $x$, the integral is oriented from $0$ to $x$; reversing its limits changes its sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $n+1$ 重新作为索引，就得到前面的对数级数。下限取 $0$，固定了 $\ln1=0$，所以没有待定的积分常数。$x<0$ 时，积分方向从 $0$ 指向 $x$；交换上下限会改变符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute convergence inside follows directly from $|x|^n/n\le |x|^n$ and geometric comparison. At $x=1$, the result is the alternating harmonic series: the [[交错级数判别|alternating test]] gives convergence, while taking absolute values leaves the divergent harmonic series. This is [[条件收敛|conditional convergence]]. At $x=-1$, every term is $-1/n$, so the partial sums tend to $-\infty$. For $|x|>1$, the magnitude ratio of consecutive terms tends to $|x|>1$, so the terms do not tend to zero. These facts establish the exact radius and endpoint behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内部的绝对收敛直接来自 $|x|^n/n\le |x|^n$ 与几何比较。在 $x=1$ 处得到交错调和级数，[[交错级数判别]]保证收敛，但取绝对值后变成发散的调和级数，因此属于[[条件收敛]]。在 $x=-1$ 处，每项都是 $-1/n$，部分和趋向 $-\infty$。若 $|x|>1$，相邻项幅度之比趋于 $|x|>1$，所以通项不趋零。这些事实确定了精确半径及端点行为。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Identify the endpoint sum using a finite remainder
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用有限余项识别端点的和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convergence at $x=1$ does not by itself identify the sum as $\ln2$; [[幂级数端点判别|endpoint analysis]] separates these questions. Let $N\ge1$ be an integer and $S_N(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}(-1)^{n+1}x^n/n$. The finite geometric identity, valid for $t\ne-1$, is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x=1$ 收敛，还不单独说明它的和就是 $\ln2$；[[幂级数端点判别]]需要区分这两个问题。取整数 $N\ge1$，记 $S_N(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}(-1)^{n+1}x^n/n$。对 $t\ne-1$，有限几何恒等式为

$$\frac1{1+t}=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}(-t)^n+\frac{(-1)^Nt^N}{1+t}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integrate this finite identity from $0$ to any fixed $x>-1$. The denominator stays positive along the whole segment, and no infinite sum is interchanged with an integral:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $0$ 积到任意固定的 $x>-1$，整段分母都为正，而且这里只积分有限恒等式，没有交换无穷求和与积分：

$$R_N(x):=\ln(1+x)-S_N(x)
=(-1)^N\int_0^x\frac{t^N}{1+t}\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=1$, the integrand has $0\le t^N/(1+t)\le t^N$. Consequently $|R_N(1)|\le1/(N+1)\to0$. This proves the endpoint equality with $\ln2$, including its value rather than just convergence. The finite identity itself also holds for $x>1$, but its remainder need not tend to zero there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x=1$ 处，有 $0\le t^N/(1+t)\le t^N$，因此 $|R_N(1)|\le1/(N+1)\to0$。这就证明了端点的和等于 $\ln2$，不仅仅是收敛。有限恒等式本身在 $x>1$ 也成立，但那里的余项不必趋零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose a truncation with a certified error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*选择有误差保证的截断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same remainder gives useful bounds on both sides of zero. On $0\le x\le1$, use $1+t\ge1$. On $-1<x<0$, put $r=|x|<1$; throughout the segment, $1+t\ge1-r$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个余项也能给出零点两侧的可用界。$0\le x\le1$ 时使用 $1+t\ge1$；$-1<x<0$ 时令 $r=|x|<1$，整段都有 $1+t\ge1-r$。于是

$$|R_N(x)|\le
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x^{N+1}}{N+1},&0\le x\le1,\\[6pt]
\dfrac{r^{N+1}}{(N+1)(1-r)},&-1<x<0,\ r=|x|.
\end{cases}$$

> [!example]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x=0.1$ and $N=3$, the error is at most $0.1^4/4=0.000025$. The first omitted term is negative, so the integral remainder shows
>
> $$0.0953083\ldots\ \le\ \ln(1.1)\ \le\ 0.0953333\ldots.$$
>
> The positive-input bound also follows from the [[交错级数误差界|alternating-series remainder bound]]. For negative $x$, the terms all have the same negative sign; use the integral bound rather than treating them as alternating.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $x=0.1,N=3$，误差至多为 $0.1^4/4=0.000025$。第一项遗漏项为负，积分余项给出上面的夹逼区间。正输入的界也能由[[交错级数误差界]]得到；负输入时各项都为负，不能当成交错级数，应使用积分余项界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The real function $\ln(1+x)$ exists for every $x>-1$, including $x=2$. The expansion about zero does not: at $x=2$ its terms have magnitudes $2^n/n$, which fail to approach zero. A change of expansion centre or another identity may help compute such values; a larger domain for the function never enlarges this series automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实函数 $\ln(1+x)$ 在每个 $x>-1$ 都有定义，包括 $x=2$；但这个零点展开在那里无效，因为通项幅度 $2^n/n$ 不趋零。改用别的展开中心或恒等式可能有助于计算，但函数定义域更大，不会自动扩大这一份级数的有效范围。
