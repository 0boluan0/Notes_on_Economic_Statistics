# ln(1+x)的Maclaurin级数在负一到一之间成立并在右端点条件收敛到ln2
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Maclaurin series for ln(1+x) holds between minus one and one and converges conditionally to ln 2 at the right endpoint*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $-1<x\le1$，
$$
\ln(1+x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}x^n}{n}
=x-\frac{x^2}{2}+\frac{x^3}{3}-\cdots.
$$
收敛半径为 $1$；$|x|<1$ 时绝对收敛，$x=1$ 时条件收敛，$x=-1$ 时为负调和级数而发散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed representation is valid for $-1<x\le1$. Its radius is one: convergence is absolute in the interior and conditional at $x=1$; at $x=-1$ it becomes the divergent negative harmonic series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $|x|<1$，将几何级数 $1/(1+t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-t)^n$ 从 $0$ 到 $x$ 逐项积分，即得到上式。由 $0$ 作下限自动确定积分常数。右端点不能仅靠内部逐项积分公式代入；可先对有限几何和积分，得到
$$
\ln2-\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}n
=(-1)^N\int_0^1\frac{t^N}{1+t}\,dt,
\qquad
\left|\ln2-\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}n\right|
\le\frac1{N+1}\to0.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interior equality follows by integrating the geometric series from zero to $x$. For the right endpoint, integrate the finite geometric identity instead: the displayed remainder integral tends to zero and proves that the endpoint sum is specifically $\ln2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数 $\ln(1+x)$ 在所有 $x>-1$ 都有定义，但这一个以 $0$ 为中心的展开在 $x>1$ 发散；函数的定义域与某个展开的有效范围不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function exists for every $x>-1$, but this expansion about zero diverges for $x>1$. A function's domain and a particular series' validity range are different objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
