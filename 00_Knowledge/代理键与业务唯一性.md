# 代理键简化引用但不能替代业务唯一约束

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A surrogate key simplifies references but does not replace business uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子原则
> 代理键是为数据库身份人工生成、通常没有业务含义的键。它能让引用稳定而简短，却不会自动禁止同一业务事实被重复录入。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A surrogate key is an artificial database identity, usually without business meaning. It can make references short and stable, but it does not automatically prevent the same business fact from being recorded twice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设课程表使用 `course_id` 作代理主键。若业务上 `(department, course_number, effective_term)` 才决定一门课程版本，那么只约束 `course_id` 仍允许下面两行同时存在：

```text
course_id = 41, department = ST, course_number = 443, effective_term = 2026
course_id = 87, department = ST, course_number = 443, effective_term = 2026
```

两行的代理键不同，但业务上可能是重复。正确设计通常保留代理主键，同时对真正不能重复的业务属性组合加唯一约束。若业务键中的列允许 `NULL`，还要明确空值是否也应视为相同；在 PostgreSQL 中，普通 `UNIQUE` 默认把多个 `NULL` 视为互不相同，需要时应配合 `NOT NULL` 或 `NULLS NOT DISTINCT`。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a course table uses `course_id` as its surrogate primary key, while `(department, course_number, effective_term)` identifies a course version in the domain. Constraining only `course_id` permits two rows with different generated IDs but the same business identity. A sound design often keeps the surrogate primary key and separately enforces uniqueness on the business key. If a business-key column is nullable, its null semantics must also be explicit: ordinary PostgreSQL `UNIQUE` treats nulls as distinct, so the invariant may require `NOT NULL` or `NULLS NOT DISTINCT`.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自然键也并非永远更好：业务标识可能很长、会变化，或其“永不重复”只是暂时假设。选择哪一个作主键是设计判断；无论选择什么，都要把业务唯一性明确写成约束，而不是寄希望于应用代码记得检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A natural key is not automatically superior: it may be long, mutable, or based on a uniqueness assumption that later fails. Choosing the primary key is a design decision. In either case, business uniqueness should be declared explicitly rather than left to application code to remember.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 每行都有不同的自增 `id`，为什么表里仍可能有业务重复？
>
> **答案：** 自增值只保证数据库身份不同；若业务上决定同一事实的属性组合没有唯一约束，同一事实可以获得两个不同 `id`。
