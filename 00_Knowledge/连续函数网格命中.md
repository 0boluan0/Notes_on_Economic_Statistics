# Continuity and eventual grid hits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续函数的网格命中*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a function is continuous at a zero, nearby inputs also produce small function values. That small neighbourhood is what a sufficiently fine grid can reach, even when no grid point equals the zero exactly. This gives an existence guarantee for [[固定步长求根|fixed-step root search]]: for any fixed positive [[求根残差|residual tolerance]], enough refinement eventually supplies an acceptable candidate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果函数在一个零点处连续，那么输入靠近它时，函数值也会变小。足够细的网格能进入这个小邻域，即使没有任何网格点恰好等于零点。这给 [[固定步长求根]] 一个存在性保证：固定任意正的 [[求根残差|残差容差]] 后，足够加密最终会提供合格候选。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$, with finite real endpoints $a\le b$. Use the exact grid $G_h=\{a+jh:j=0,1,\ldots,\lfloor(b-a)/h\rfloor\}$, where $h>0$, and accept an input when $|f(x)|\le\tau$ for a fixed $\tau>0$. The floor symbol means rounding down to an integer. The grid starts at $a$ and does not step past $b$; $b$ need not itself be a grid point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$，其中端点是有限实数且 $a\le b$。使用精确网格 $G_h=\{a+jh:j=0,1,\ldots,\lfloor(b-a)/h\rfloor\}$，其中 $h>0$；固定 $\tau>0$，以 $|f(x)|\le\tau$ 验收输入。下取整符号表示向下取整数。网格从 $a$ 出发，不越过 $b$，但 $b$ 本身未必是网格点。

> [!important] The guarantee is local to a root · 保证只需根处连续
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Assume there is a root $r\in[a,b]$ and that $f$ is continuous at $r$ relative to $[a,b]$. Then there is a number $\delta>0$ such that **every** step $0<h<\delta$ produces an accepted grid point. Continuity at other points and a nonzero derivative at the root are not required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设闭区间内有根 $r$，而且 $f$ 相对于 $[a,b]$ 在 $r$ 连续。那么存在某个 $\delta>0$，使**每个**满足 $0<h<\delta$ 的步长都能产生合格网格点；不要求其他点连续，也不要求根处导数非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a nearby grid point must be accepted
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么邻近网格点一定合格

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is a useful eventual guarantee. Suppose $r\in[a,b]$ is a root and $f$ is [[一点连续|continuous at $r$]] relative to this interval. For a fixed $\tau>0$, continuity supplies a radius $\delta>0$ such that $|x-r|<\delta$ implies $|f(x)|<\tau$ for $x\in[a,b]$. Choose $h<\delta$ and look at the grid point immediately to the left of $r$, allowing equality:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有一个有用的最终保证。设 $r\in[a,b]$ 是根，而且 $f$ 相对于这个区间在 $r$ 处 [[一点连续|连续]]。固定 $\tau>0$ 后，连续性给出某个 $\delta>0$，使区间内满足 $|x-r|<\delta$ 的点都有 $|f(x)|<\tau$。取 $h<\delta$，再看根左侧紧邻的网格点，允许两者重合：

$$
q=a+h\left\lfloor\frac{r-a}{h}\right\rfloor\in G_h,
\qquad 0\le r-q<h<\delta.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This $q$ is accepted. The argument also covers a root at $b$, even if $b$ itself is not sampled, and a root at $a$ is already on every grid. Thus sufficiently fine exact grids will hit under this continuity hypothesis. Merely knowing continuity does not provide a numerical value of $\delta$ or a practical step count; a rate bound can provide that missing information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个 $q$ 就会通过验收。即使 $b$ 本身没有被采样，论证也涵盖位于 $b$ 的根；位于 $a$ 的根则从一开始就在每个网格里。因此在这一连续性条件下，足够细的精确网格确实会命中。不过只知道连续，并不会直接给出 $\delta$ 的数值或实用的扫描次数；变化率界可以补上这项信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The point $q$ exists in the grid because $r\le b$ makes its integer index no larger than $\lfloor(b-a)/h\rfloor$. The definition of the floor gives the distance inequality. If $q=r$, the residual is already zero. Otherwise continuity controls the small remaining distance. The proof uses the root to identify a successful candidate mathematically; the scan itself does not need to know the root in advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $r\le b$，$q$ 的整数下标不超过 $\lfloor(b-a)/h\rfloor$，所以它确实在网格中；下取整的定义给出距离不等式。若 $q=r$，残差已经为零；否则由连续性控制剩余的小距离。证明借助根来指出一个必定成功的候选，并不要求搜索程序预先知道根的位置。

> [!example] A radius becomes a grid step · 把输入半径变成网格步长
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In exact decimal arithmetic, take $f(x)=x-0.55$ on $[0,1]$ and $\tau=0.02$. With $h=0.1$, the nearest grid points $0.5$ and $0.6$ both have residual $0.05$, so the scan misses. Here the explicit formula gives $|f(x)|=|x-0.55|$, so we can choose $\delta=0.02$. Now set $h=0.019<\delta$. The left grid point is $q=28(0.019)=0.532$, and its residual is $0.018<0.02$: the finer grid enters the acceptance region. We obtained this numerical radius from the linear formula, not from the bare word “continuous”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在精确十进制算术中，令 $f(x)=x-0.55$、区间为 $[0,1]$、$\tau=0.02$。取 $h=0.1$ 时，最近的网格点 $0.5$ 和 $0.6$ 的残差都是 $0.05$，所以未命中。这里具体公式给出 $|f(x)|=|x-0.55|$，因此可取 $\delta=0.02$。再令 $h=0.019<\delta$，根左侧的网格点为 $q=28(0.019)=0.532$，残差为 $0.018<0.02$，较细网格进入了接受区域。这个数值半径来自线性公式，而不只是“连续”两个字。

## An eventual guarantee does not choose a numerical step
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最终保证不等于已知数值步长

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order of choices matters: first fix a positive tolerance, then obtain a suitable input radius, then make the step smaller than that radius. A particular coarse grid may still [[固定步长漏根|miss the whole acceptance region]]. The theorem does not say that one halving is enough, or that any one smaller step preserves a previous hit. It says that all sufficiently small steps work for this fixed function, root, interval, and tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择的顺序很重要：先固定正容差，再得到合适的输入半径，最后让步长小于这个半径。某个较粗网格仍可能 [[固定步长漏根|完全错过接受区域]]。定理并没有说只减半一次就够，也没有说任意一次较小步长都保留先前的命中；它说的是，对固定的函数、根、区间和容差，所有足够小的步长都会成功。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A known bound on how fast the function varies can make the choice explicit; [[导数界控制网格步长|a derivative bound gives one sufficient step rule]]. This theorem is about exact grid points and mathematical function values. Turning it into a successful program also requires a complete, terminating scan and reliable residual evaluations; the [[固定步长求根|method card]] explains those implementation conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若知道函数变化速度的界，就能把步长选择具体化；[[导数界控制网格步长]]给出一种充分规则。本定理讨论精确网格点和数学函数值。要把它变成成功运行的程序，还需要完整且终止的扫描，以及可靠的残差求值；这些实现条件见 [[固定步长求根]]。
