# 央行管理利率是中央银行直接规定其账户或操作适用的利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An administered rate is a rate the central bank directly sets for its accounts or operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

管理利率是央行给合格参与者的可用交易条件，而非市场成交后算出来的统计值。准备金付息利率、某项央行借款便利的利率、常备隔夜逆回购报价，都可以属于这一类；每种利率适用的对象、资产和方向不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An administered rate is an available transaction term for eligible participants, not a statistic calculated from market trades. Examples include reserve remuneration, a central-bank lending facility rate, and a standing reverse-repo offer. Their counterparties, assets, and payment directions differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一家合格银行可在央行把边际余额存一夜、获得年化4%的收益。若信用、流动性和其他成本相同，它没有理由只为3.5%把同一笔钱贷给别人；反过来，一项可用的央行融资便利也会影响银行愿意向市场支付多少。这些外部选项通过报价与交易竞争影响[[联邦基金利率|市场利率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an eligible bank can earn 4% annualised overnight at the central bank. With identical credit, liquidity, and other costs, lending the same funds for 3.5% is unattractive. An accessible central-bank borrowing facility similarly affects what a bank will pay in the market. Outside options influence [[联邦基金利率|market rates]] through competition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在美国的充足准备金实施框架中，IORB 是准备金余额付息率，ON RRP 报价为合格货币市场参与者提供另一种隔夜投资选择。不能从这两个例子推出“每个人都能拿到同样收益”：准入和中介成本应另看[[管理利率的市场边界]]。管理利率的数值也不必等于目标区间的边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the U.S. ample-reserves framework, IORB remunerates reserve balances and the ON RRP offer provides another overnight option to eligible money-market participants. Access and intermediation costs require the separate [[管理利率的市场边界|market-boundary analysis]]. An administered rate need not coincide with a target-range endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
