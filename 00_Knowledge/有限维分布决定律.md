# Kolmogorov extension theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*Kolmogorov 扩张定理：从有限观测拼出过程律*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Kolmogorov extension theorem** answers a construction question. Suppose we have specified the joint behaviour of every finite selection of times. Can all these descriptions belong to one stochastic process? For real-valued observations, the answer is yes when the descriptions agree wherever they overlap. The theorem also says that they determine a unique probability law on a precisely specified space of paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Kolmogorov 扩张定理**回答一个构造问题：假如已经规定了每个有限时点组的联合行为，能否找到同一个随机过程，让这些描述全部成立？对于实值观测，只要它们在重叠处保持一致，就可以。定理还说，它们会在一个明确指定的路径可测空间上确定唯一的概率律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[有限维分布|finite-dimensional distribution]] describes several observations together, such as $(X_1,X_2,X_3)$. Removing the third observation must leave exactly the law we separately prescribed for $(X_1,X_2)$. This operation is [[边际分布|taking a marginal]]: in a probability table, add over all possible values of the discarded coordinate. “Consistent” means that every such check succeeds, for every finite selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个[[有限维分布]]同时描述若干观测，例如 $(X_1,X_2,X_3)$。删去第三个观测后，必须恰好得到我们另行为 $(X_1,X_2)$ 指定的分布。这个操作就是[[边际分布|取边际]]：在概率表中，把被删坐标的所有可能取值加起来。“一致”要求每个有限时点组的每次这种检查都通过。

> [!example] Can the small descriptions coexist? · 小描述能否同时成立？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the proposed pair $(X_1,X_2)$ is $(0,0)$ or $(1,1)$, each with probability $1/2$. Its first coordinate is fair: $P(X_1=1)=1/2$. A separate proposal $P(X_1=1)=3/4$ contradicts it. No process can satisfy both. By contrast, assigning probability $2^{-n}$ to every binary pattern at each set of $n$ distinct times is consistent: summing over one bit adds two equal terms and gives $2^{-(n-1)}$. This whole family specifies independent fair bits.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假定拟定的配对 $(X_1,X_2)$ 以各 $1/2$ 的概率取 $(0,0)$ 或 $(1,1)$，它的第一个坐标就满足 $P(X_1=1)=1/2$。如果又单独规定 $P(X_1=1)=3/4$，两份描述矛盾，任何过程都不可能同时满足。相反，对每组 $n$ 个互异时点，让每种二进制模式的概率都为 $2^{-n}$，这一整族是相容的：删去一位时加起两项，得到 $2^{-(n-1)}$。它规定的是独立的公平比特。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The space on which the law is unique
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*唯一性究竟针对什么空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $T$ for the index set. A point $x\in\mathbb R^T$ assigns one real value $x_t$ to every $t\in T$; it is a possible complete path. A [[概率空间|probability model]] also needs an event family. Start with questions involving finitely many coordinates, such as $x_1>0$ and $x_3\le2$ together. These are **cylinder events**: the other coordinates are unrestricted. Close this collection under complements and countable unions to obtain the **product [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]]**, written $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)^{\otimes T}$. Here $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$ is the Borel event family generated by open subsets of the real line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $T$ 为指标集。$\mathbb R^T$ 中的一个点 $x$，给每个 $t\in T$ 指定一个实数 $x_t$，因此是一条可能的完整路径。[[概率空间|概率模型]]还需要事件族。先考虑只查看有限个坐标的问题，例如同时要求 $x_1>0$、$x_3\le2$。这叫**柱事件**，其余坐标不受限制。把它们补充到对补集与可数并封闭，得到**乘积[[σ-代数]]** $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)^{\otimes T}$；其中 $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$ 是由实数开集生成的 Borel 事件族。

> [!abstract] Real-valued version · 实值版本
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every finite subset $J\subset T$, let $\mu_J$ be a probability law on $\mathbb R^J$ with its Borel sigma-algebra. Require, for every $K\subset J$ and every Borel set $A\subset\mathbb R^K$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个有限子集 $J\subset T$，给定 $\mathbb R^J$ 的 Borel σ-代数上的概率律 $\mu_J$。要求任意 $K\subset J$ 以及任意 Borel 集 $A\subset\mathbb R^K$ 都满足：
>
> $$\mu_J\!\left(\{x\in\mathbb R^J:(x_t)_{t\in K}\in A\}\right)=\mu_K(A).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Then there exists exactly one probability measure $P$ on $(\mathbb R^T,\mathcal B(\mathbb R)^{\otimes T})$ with these finite-coordinate laws. On this space, define $X_t(x)=x_t$: observing a coordinate gives the desired process.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 此时，$(\mathbb R^T,\mathcal B(\mathbb R)^{\otimes T})$ 上存在唯一的概率测度 $P$，其有限坐标分布恰好是这些 $\mu_J$。在该空间上定义 $X_t(x)=x_t$，即读取路径的第 $t$ 个坐标，就得到了所需过程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The consistency equation is also written $(\pi_{J\to K})_\#\mu_J=\mu_K$: $\pi_{J\to K}$ drops the unwanted coordinates, and the subscript $\#$ means the law after applying that map. If we index laws by ordered tuples of distinct times instead of sets, consistency must include both deleting coordinates and reordering them. This card states the real-valued theorem; arbitrary measurable state spaces need additional hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一致性等式也写成 $(\pi_{J\to K})_\#\mu_J=\mu_K$：$\pi_{J\to K}$ 删去不需要的坐标，下标 $\#$ 表示经过该映射之后的分布。如果用互异时点的有序元组、而不是集合来编号分布，就必须同时要求删去坐标和重排坐标的一致性。本卡陈述实值版本；不能无条件推广到任意可测状态空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the theorem contributes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定理额外完成了哪一步*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consistency lets us assign a probability to each cylinder without contradictory answers. Extending these assignments to a countably additive probability measure on the whole generated sigma-algebra is the substantial existence step. It is not just a matter of multiplying probabilities: multiplication would introduce an independence assumption absent from the theorem. Uniqueness says that two probability measures agreeing on all finite-coordinate events agree on that generated sigma-algebra.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一致性让我们能够给各个柱事件指定概率，而不会得到相互矛盾的答案。把这些指定扩张成整个生成 σ-代数上满足可数可加性的概率测度，才是存在性结论中实质的一步。这不是简单地把概率相乘；相乘会额外引入定理并未要求的独立性。唯一性则说，两个概率测度若在全部有限坐标事件上相同，就在这个生成的 σ-代数上相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an already constructed real-valued process, its complete finite-dimensional family therefore determines its **law** on the canonical product space. It does not determine the underlying experimental outcome $\omega$ or make two realisations identical. One-time marginals alone are [[边际分布不定时序依赖|insufficient even to determine temporal dependence]]. For uncountable time, the product-space conclusion also does not automatically give [[有限维分布不定路径正则性|continuous paths for a particular version]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对已经构造好的实值过程，全部有限维分布因此确定其在典范乘积空间上的**律**；它不确定底层实验结果 $\omega$，也不让两次实际实现相同。只有单时点边际，甚至[[边际分布不定时序依赖|不足以确定时序依赖]]。当时间不可数时，这个乘积空间结论也不会自动给[[有限维分布不定路径正则性|某个具体版本赋予连续路径]]。
