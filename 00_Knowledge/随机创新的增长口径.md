# 随机质量跳跃的平均对数增长与期望产出增长使用不同公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Random quality jumps imply different formulas for average log growth and growth of expected output.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|研发劳动恒定的 Aghion–Howitt 基本模型]] 中，每次创新使生产率和产出乘以 $\gamma>1$，但成功时间随机。设研发效率为 $\lambda>0$、稳态研发劳动为 $\bar n>0$，到达强度 $z=\lambda\bar n$，日历时间为 $\tau$，累计创新数为 $J(\tau)$。由 [[研发的泊松到达]]，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|stationary Aghion–Howitt allocation]], each discovery multiplies productivity and output by $\gamma>1$, but arrival times are random. Let research productivity be $\lambda>0$, research labour $\bar n>0$, arrival intensity $z=\lambda\bar n$, calendar time $\tau$, and cumulative discoveries $J(\tau)$. The [[研发的泊松到达|research arrival mechanism]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
J(\tau)\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(z\tau),\qquad
Y(\tau)=Y_0\gamma^{J(\tau)},\qquad
\ln\frac{Y(\tau)}{Y_0}=J(\tau)\ln\gamma.
$$

最后一项是每次跳幅固定为 $\ln\gamma$ 的 [[复合泊松过程|复合泊松和]]。使用 [[泊松过程矩|计数的均值与方差]]，任意长度为 $h$ 的区间都有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The log change is a [[复合泊松过程|compound Poisson sum]] with constant jump size $\ln\gamma$. Using the [[泊松过程矩|mean and variance of Poisson counts]], an interval of length $h$ has
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[\Delta\ln Y]=zh\ln\gamma,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\Delta\ln Y)=zh(\ln\gamma)^2.
$$

因此原文的平均增长率（AGR）是每单位时间的**期望对数增量** $g_{\log}=z\ln\gamma$。相邻单位区间的计数独立同分布且均值有限，由 [[强大数定律]]，长期实现的单位时间对数增长也几乎必然趋于此值；任意有限一年仍可能没有创新。非整数时点可把累计计数夹在相邻整数时点的计数之间，得到同一个长期极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper's average growth rate is the expected log increment per unit time, $g_{\log}=z\ln\gamma$. Counts over successive unit intervals are iid with finite mean, so the [[强大数定律|strong law]] makes the realised long-run log growth rate converge almost surely to this value. A particular finite year can still contain no innovation. Counts at noninteger times are bounded by those at the adjacent integers, giving the same long-run limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若先对产出水平取期望，再问它怎样增长，泊松概率相加给出另一式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking expectations of output levels first instead gives, by summing Poisson probabilities,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{E[Y(\tau)]}{Y_0}
=\sum_{k=0}^\infty\gamma^k e^{-z\tau}\frac{(z\tau)^k}{k!}
=e^{z\tau(\gamma-1)},
\qquad g_{E[Y]}=z(\gamma-1).
$$

**$E[\ln Y]$ 与 $\ln E[Y]$ 不能交换。** 例如 $z=0.028,\gamma=1.5$，平均对数增长约为 $0.011353$，期望产出水平的指数增长率为 $0.014$。后者意味着一年期望产出比为 $e^{0.014}$，并不是说每年实现的产出一定增加 $1.4\%$；在这一个单位期间没有创新的概率仍约为 $97.24\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expectation and logarithms cannot be interchanged. With $z=0.028$ and $\gamma=1.5$, average log growth is about $0.011353$, while expected output has exponential growth rate $0.014$. The latter gives a one-period expected output ratio of $e^{0.014}$, not a guaranteed realised increase of $1.4\%$. The probability of no innovation during that period remains about $97.24\%$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[私人与社会研发|期望消费现值]] 使用产出水平的期望，所以出现 $\gamma-1$。另一类带连续产品线的 Schumpeterian 模型，可借大量产品线聚合使总量确定性增长；其 $g=z(\gamma-1)$ 需要该聚合设定，不能套回本卡的单一总量随机跳跃过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[私人与社会研发|Expected consumption welfare]] uses expected output levels, hence $\gamma-1$. Other Schumpeterian models aggregate a continuum of product lines and can have deterministic aggregate growth $g=z(\gamma-1)$. That aggregation assumption differs from the single aggregate jump process here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
