# The same four subspaces do not determine a matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*四个子空间相同仍可有不同的线性作用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two matrices can have identical [[四个基本子空间|four fundamental subspaces]] yet send the same input to different outputs. The spaces describe which outputs are reachable, which inputs disappear, and the corresponding row and output-constraint spaces. They do not specify every input–output pair. In particular, equality of the four spaces does not even force the matrices to be scalar multiples of one another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个矩阵的[[四个基本子空间|四个基本子空间]]可以完全相同，却把同一个输入送到不同输出。这些空间描述哪些输出可达、哪些输入被消去，以及相应的行空间和输出约束空间，并没有指定每一组输入与输出的配对。特别地，四空间相同甚至不能保证两个矩阵互为标量倍数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with real matrices $A,B\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ of the same size, where $m,n$ are positive integers. The [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$ consists of all outputs $Ax$ in $\mathbb R^m$, and the [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)$ consists of inputs $x\in\mathbb R^n$ satisfying $Ax=0$. The [[行空间|row space]] is $C(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, with row coordinates written as columns; the [[左零空间|left nullspace]] is $N(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb R^m$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Having the same four spaces means each corresponding pair is equal **as a set in these fixed coordinates**, not merely that its dimensions agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

讨论同尺寸的实矩阵 $A,B\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，其中 $m,n$ 为正整数。[[列空间]] $C(A)$ 收集 $\mathbb R^m$ 中的全部输出 $Ax$；[[零空间]] $N(A)$ 收集满足 $Ax=0$ 的输入 $x\in\mathbb R^n$。把行的坐标竖写成列后，[[行空间]]记为 $C(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb R^n$；[[左零空间]]为 $N(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb R^m$，上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。所谓四空间相同，指每一对对应空间**在这些固定坐标下是同一个集合**，不只是维数相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A concrete pair with the same four spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*四个空间完全相同的一对矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the diagonal matrix $A$ below and let $B=I_2$, the two-dimensional [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取下面的对角矩阵 $A$，并令 $B=I_2$ 为二维[[单位矩阵]]：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns $(2,0)^T,(0,3)^T$ of $A$ generate every $(u,v)^T$: use coefficients $u/2,v/3$. Its rows generate the whole plane for the same reason. The equation $Ax=0$ is $2x_1=0$, $3x_2=0$, so only the zero input satisfies it. Also $A^T=A$, so its left nullspace is zero as well. For $B$, its columns and rows are the standard coordinate directions, and $Bx=x$. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A$ 的两列 $(2,0)^T,(0,3)^T$ 能生成任意 $(u,v)^T$：组合系数取 $u/2,v/3$ 即可。同理，它的各行也生成整个平面。方程 $Ax=0$ 就是 $2x_1=0$、$3x_2=0$，只有零输入满足；又因 $A^T=A$，左零空间同样只有零。$B$ 的行列都是标准坐标方向，而且 $Bx=x$。于是

$$\begin{aligned}
C(A)&=C(B)=\mathbb R^2,&C(A^T)&=C(B^T)=\mathbb R^2,\\
N(A)&=N(B)=\{0\},&N(A^T)&=N(B^T)=\{0\}.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both matrices are invertible, but their actions differ:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两矩阵都可逆，作用却不同：

$$A\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}2x_1\\3x_2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Geometrically, $B$ leaves every vector unchanged; $A$ stretches the first coordinate by $2$ and the second by $3$. The input $(1,0)^T$, for instance, has different outputs. Both maps reach the whole plane and send only zero to zero, so the four spaces do not record these different stretching factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几何上，$B$ 保持每个向量不变；$A$ 将第一坐标放大两倍、第二坐标放大三倍。例如输入 $(1,0)^T$，两者的输出就不同。它们都能到达整个平面，而且只有零被送到零，所以四空间没有记录这两种不同的伸缩因子。

> [!example] Not even one common scale factor · 连一个共同倍数也不存在
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A=kB$, the top-left entry would require $k=2$, while the bottom-right entry would require $k=3$. No real scalar satisfies both. This checks the stronger failure: the same four spaces imply neither equality nor scalar proportionality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $A=kB$，左上角要求 $k=2$，右下角却要求 $k=3$，没有实数同时满足。因此这里不只是矩阵不相等，连互为标量倍数也不成立。

## The direction of the implication matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*充分条件不能倒用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any real matrix $A$ and any nonzero real scalar $k$, the matrices $A$ and $kA$ do have the same four spaces. Scaling each column by $k$ leaves its [[张成|span]] unchanged: the scaled columns are combinations of the originals, and division by $k$ recovers each original column. The same argument applies to rows. For the two nullspaces,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意实矩阵 $A$ 与非零实数 $k$，$A$ 和 $kA$ 确实具有相同四空间。把每列乘 $k$ 不改变[[张成]]：缩放后的列是原列的组合，除以 $k$ 又能恢复每一原列；行也同理。对两个零空间，则有

$$(kA)x=0\iff Ax=0,\qquad (kA)^Ty=0\iff A^Ty=0,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
since $k$ can be cancelled. This includes $A$ and $-A$. The converse fails by the example above. The condition $k\ne0$ matters: if $A\ne0$, multiplying it by zero makes both column and row spaces $\{0\}$ and makes both nullspaces their entire ambient spaces. For $A=0$, the matrices already coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为可以消去非零的 $k$。这包含 $A$ 与 $-A$ 的情形，但上面的反例否定了逆命题。条件 $k\ne0$ 不可省：若 $A\ne0$，乘零会使行空间与列空间都变成 $\{0\}$，两个零空间则充满各自环境空间；若 $A=0$，两矩阵本来就相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing the complete solution set of $Ax=b$ for **every fixed, unchanged right-hand side** $b$ is stronger. For the example, the target $b=(2,3)^T$ has solution $(1,1)^T$ under $A$ but $(2,3)^T$ under $B$. Equal column spaces tell us that both systems have a solution; they do not match up the solutions. The separate result [[全部右端的解集确定矩阵|all right-hand-side solution sets determine the matrix]] explains why matching the full solution sets for every same $b$ does determine the map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道**每个固定且保持不变的右端** $b$ 对应的完整解集，是更强的信息。上例中，对目标 $b=(2,3)^T$，$A$ 对应的解是 $(1,1)^T$，$B$ 对应的解却是 $(2,3)^T$。列空间相同只告诉我们两系统都有解，没有把具体解配成相同。[[全部右端的解集确定矩阵]]另行说明：若对每个相同的 $b$，完整解集都一致，才获得足以确定映射的信息。
