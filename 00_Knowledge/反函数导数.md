# Derivative of an inverse function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*反函数导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverse function answers the original function's question backwards. If $f$ maps an input $x$ to an output $y$, its inverse maps that output back to $x$. The **inverse derivative** measures how much recovered input changes per unit of output. When the forward [[导数|derivative]] is nonzero and an appropriate local inverse exists, the two rates are reciprocals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反函数把原来的输入输出关系倒过来。如果 $f$ 把输入 $x$ 映到输出 $y$，反函数就把这个输出还原为 $x$。**反函数导数**衡量输出改变一个单位时，还原出的输入改变多少。在原函数[[导数]]非零、并且存在合适的局部反函数时，这两个变化率互为倒数。

> [!example] Return from 9 to 3 · 从 9 返回 3
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Restrict $f(x)=x^2$ to $x>0$. Its inverse is $q(y)=\sqrt y$, defined for $y>0$. At $x=3$, the [[整数幂求导|power rule]] gives $f'(3)=6$: a small input change produces about six times as much output change. Therefore the inverse rate at the corresponding output $y=9$ is $q'(9)=1/6$. The corresponding points matter: it is $f'(3)$, not $f'(9)$, that we invert.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $f(x)=x^2$ 限制在 $x>0$，反函数就是定义在 $y>0$ 上的 $q(y)=\sqrt y$。由[[整数幂求导]]，$f'(3)=6$：输入的小变化会使输出改变约六倍。因此，在对应输出 $y=9$ 处，反向变化率是 $q'(9)=1/6$。一定要对应同一对输入输出：取倒数的是 $f'(3)$，不是 $f'(9)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A local branch and its assumptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*局部分支与适用条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $f^{-1}$ denotes an inverse function, not the reciprocal $1/f$. To have a single recovered input, different inputs in the chosen interval must have different outputs. A function may fail this globally but satisfy it on a smaller interval; this restriction is a **local branch**. For $x^2$, the positive and negative half-lines give different branches, $\sqrt y$ and $-\sqrt y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $f^{-1}$ 表示反函数，不是函数值的倒数 $1/f$。要让还原出的输入唯一，所选区间内不同输入必须产生不同输出。整个定义域上做不到，也可能在较小区间上做到；这种限制称为选择一个**局部分支**。对 $x^2$，正半轴和负半轴分别给出 $\sqrt y$、$-\sqrt y$ 两个反函数分支。

> [!abstract] A sufficient local theorem · 一组足够的局部条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f$ is continuously differentiable on an open interval around $x_0$, meaning that $f'$ exists there and is continuous, and suppose $f'(x_0)\ne0$. Then $f$ has an inverse $q$ on suitably small intervals around $x_0$ and $y_0=f(x_0)$. This inverse is differentiable, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $f$ 在 $x_0$ 周围的某个开区间上连续可微，也就是 $f'$ 在那里存在且连续，并且 $f'(x_0)\ne0$。那么，缩小到 $x_0$ 和 $y_0=f(x_0)$ 周围的合适区间后，$f$ 有反函数 $q$，且这个反函数可导，满足
>
> $$q'(y_0)=\frac{1}{f'(x_0)}=\frac{1}{f'(q(y_0))}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are sufficient conditions for obtaining both a local inverse and its derivative; continuous differentiability is not claimed to be necessary. The input to $q'$ is an original **output** $y_0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这是同时保证局部反函数存在且可导的一组充分条件，并不是说连续可微是必要条件。$q'$ 的输入 $y_0$ 是原函数的一个**输出**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why do these assumptions help? Continuity of $f'$ keeps its sign unchanged in a sufficiently small interval. The [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]], which expresses a secant slope as a derivative at an intermediate point, then makes $f$ strictly increasing or strictly decreasing there. It therefore has a continuous inverse on the corresponding output interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些条件如何起作用？$f'$ 连续且在 $x_0$ 非零，使它在足够小的区间内保持同一符号。[[拉格朗日中值定理]]把割线斜率写成某个中间点的导数，因此 $f$ 在该区间严格递增或严格递减。这样，它就在对应的输出区间上有连续的反函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the reciprocal is a derivative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么这个倒数确实是导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y\ne y_0$ near $y_0$, set $x=q(y)$, so $y=f(x)$. Injectivity gives $x\ne x_0$, and continuity of $q$ gives $x\to x_0$ when $y\to y_0$. Now compare the inverse difference quotient with the original one:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对接近 $y_0$ 且不等于 $y_0$ 的 $y$，令 $x=q(y)$，于是 $y=f(x)$。一一对应保证 $x\ne x_0$；反函数连续则保证 $y\to y_0$ 时 $x\to x_0$。比较反函数与原函数的差商：

$$\frac{q(y)-q(y_0)}{y-y_0}
=\frac{x-x_0}{f(x)-f(x_0)}
=\frac{1}{\dfrac{f(x)-f(x_0)}{x-x_0}}
\longrightarrow\frac{1}{f'(x_0)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limiting denominator is nonzero, so its reciprocal has a finite limit. This proves inverse differentiability rather than assuming it. Once that is known, the identity $f(q(y))=y$ and the [[链式法则|chain rule]] give the shorter calculation $f'(q(y))q'(y)=1$. Geometrically, reflection in the line $y=x$ exchanges horizontal and vertical changes, so corresponding nonzero tangent slopes are reciprocals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母的极限非零，所以倒数有有限极限。这一步是在证明反函数可导，而不是事先假定它可导。证明以后，再对恒等式 $f(q(y))=y$ 使用[[链式法则]]，就得到更简短的 $f'(q(y))q'(y)=1$。几何上，关于直线 $y=x$ 的反射交换横纵增量，因此对应的非零切线斜率互为倒数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the positive square branch, $f'(x)=2x$, so $q'(y)=1/(2\sqrt y)$ for $y>0$. On the negative branch the inverse is $-\sqrt y$, and its derivative is $-1/(2\sqrt y)$ instead. Choosing the branch also chooses which original slope belongs in the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对平方函数的正分支，$f'(x)=2x$，所以在 $y>0$ 上有 $q'(y)=1/(2\sqrt y)$。负分支的反函数是 $-\sqrt y$，导数则是 $-1/(2\sqrt y)$。选择分支，也就决定了分母应该使用原函数的哪一个斜率。

> [!warning] Invertible does not always mean a finite inverse derivative · 可逆不等于反函数有有限导数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $f(x)=x^3$ is one-to-one on all real numbers, but $f'(0)=0$. Its inverse $q(y)=\sqrt[3]y$ has difference quotient $q(h)/h=1/|h|^{2/3}$ at zero, which tends to $+\infty$. Thus its inverse exists, but it has no finite derivative there. A zero forward slope does not justify dividing by zero or reporting an ordinary derivative of infinity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $f(x)=x^3$ 在所有实数上都是一一对应的，但 $f'(0)=0$。反函数 $q(y)=\sqrt[3]y$ 在零点的差商为 $q(h)/h=1/|h|^{2/3}$，趋于 $+\infty$。所以反函数虽然存在，在那里却没有有限导数。原斜率为零不允许我们除以零，也不能把无穷大写成普通导数值。
