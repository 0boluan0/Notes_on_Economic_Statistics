# 局部可逆且原函数导数非零时反函数导数等于原导数在对应点的倒数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a locally invertible function with nonzero derivative, the inverse derivative is the reciprocal of the original derivative at the corresponding point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 设 $f$ 在 $x_0$ 附近连续可微且 $f'(x_0)\ne0$。则 $f$ 在该点附近有局部反函数；若 $y_0=f(x_0)$，
> $$
> (f^{-1})'(y_0)=\frac{1}{f'(x_0)}
> =\frac{1}{f'(f^{-1}(y_0))}.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f$ is continuously differentiable near $x_0$ and $f'(x_0)\ne0$. Then $f$ has a local inverse near that point. For $y_0=f(x_0)$, $(f^{-1})'(y_0)=1/f'(x_0)=1/f'(f^{-1}(y_0))$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

恒等式 $f(f^{-1}(y))=y$ 经 [[链式法则]] 求导得到
$$
f'(f^{-1}(y))(f^{-1})'(y)=1,
$$
于是得到倒数公式。几何上，函数与反函数的图像关于 $y=x$ 对称，对应切线交换横纵增量，所以非零斜率互为倒数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiate the identity $f(f^{-1}(y))=y$ to obtain $f'(f^{-1}(y))(f^{-1})'(y)=1$. Geometrically, reflection across $y=x$ swaps horizontal and vertical increments, so corresponding nonzero slopes are reciprocals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如把 $f(x)=x^2$ 限制在 $x>0$，其反函数为 $f^{-1}(y)=\sqrt y$。因此
$$
\frac{d}{dy}\sqrt y=\frac{1}{2\sqrt y},\qquad y>0.
$$
限制分支不可省略：未限制的 $x^2$ 在实数上不是一一对应；而 $f'(x_0)=0$ 时倒数公式也没有给出有限导数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Restrict $f(x)=x^2$ to $x>0$, where its inverse is $\sqrt y$. The formula gives $d\sqrt y/dy=1/(2\sqrt y)$ for $y>0$. The branch restriction matters because $x^2$ is not one-to-one on all real numbers, and a zero original derivative cannot yield a finite reciprocal through this formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
