# 识别证券先确定谁在什么条件下向谁支付以及持有人拥有何种权利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identify a security by who pays whom, under which conditions, and what rights its holder possesses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面对一个产品名称，先把它还原为一张简短的权利清单：发行人或对手是谁；我持有股权、债权、基金份额还是合约权利；哪些日期有支付；金额由什么决定；资源不足时轮到谁；谁有赎回、回售、转换或行权的选择；途中还需要投入什么现金。条款没有交代的部分先留作未知，不用产品类别替它补答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Translate a product name into a short rights map: issuer or counterparty; equity, debt, fund units, or contractual rights; payment dates and amounts; priority when resources are insufficient; exercise, call, put, or conversion choices; and interim cash requirements. Leave unspecified terms unknown rather than filling them in from the category label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两张证券都标注“年率 5%”：一张是不可递延的债券息票，另一张是按条款支付的优先股股利。相同百分比并不意味着同样的付款义务或[[受偿顺序]]。还须分别识别息票率是否重置、计息本金是否调整；固定息票率本身不足以确定未来金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two securities may both quote 5%: a nondeferrable bond coupon and a contractual preferred dividend. Equal percentages do not imply identical payment obligations or [[受偿顺序|priority]]. Separately identify rate resets and principal adjustments; a fixed coupon rate alone does not determine future payment amounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完成清单后，再去选择模型：已给定无违约现金流才能直接进入[[固定现金流债券定价]]；存在选择权时，要把不同状态下的现金流分支写清。[[股票]]、[[债券]]、[[证券投资基金]]和[[衍生工具]]是查条款的入口，不是由低到高排成一条风险刻度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only then choose a model: [[固定现金流债券定价|discount fixed bond cash flows]] when those default-free flows are actually specified; represent state-dependent branches when options exist. [[股票|Stocks]], [[债券|bonds]], [[证券投资基金|funds]], and [[衍生工具|derivatives]] are entry points to contractual analysis, not positions on one universal risk scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
