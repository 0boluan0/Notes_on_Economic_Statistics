# 泰勒规则是把名义政策利率与通胀偏离和产出缺口联系起来的反馈规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Taylor rule is a feedback rule relating the nominal policy rate to inflation deviations and the output gap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

泰勒规则按经济状态调整短期名义政策利率，而不是永远固定同一个利率。一个常见表示是以均衡实际利率和通胀为基准，再对通胀偏离目标、实际产出偏离潜能作系统反应；它是分析参照，不是所有央行必须执行的法律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule adjusts a short-term nominal policy rate to economic conditions rather than fixing the rate forever. A common version adds responses to inflation and output gaps to a benchmark real rate plus inflation. It is an analytical benchmark, not a universal legal requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
i_t=r_t^*+\pi_t+\alpha(\pi_t-\pi^*)+\beta x_t,
\qquad x_t=100\frac{Y_t-Y_t^*}{Y_t^*}.
$$

这里利率和通胀以百分数记录，$x_t$ 是百分比[[产出缺口]]；$r_t^*$ 是所采用的均衡实际利率估计，$\pi^*$ 是通胀目标。原始 1993 年例式取 $r^*=2$、$\pi^*=2$、$\alpha=\beta=0.5$，通胀用过去四个季度的变化，产出基准用趋势产出。这些取值和基准不是跨时期不变常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rates and inflation are in percent; $x_t$ is the percentage [[产出缺口|output gap]]. The benchmark real rate is an estimate. Taylor's 1993 illustration used 2 for both the real-rate benchmark and inflation target, coefficients of 0.5, four-quarter inflation and trend output. Those choices are not timeless constants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教学计算：沿用上述参数，若通胀为 3%、产出低于基准 2%，则 $i=2+3+0.5(3-2)+0.5(-2)=4.5\%$。若产出缺口不变而通胀再高 1 个百分点，名义利率建议值增加 1.5 个百分点；总反应系数是 $1+\alpha$，不是只有 $\alpha$。相关原则单独见[[泰勒原则]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With inflation at 3 percent and output 2 percent below its benchmark, the prescription is 4.5 percent. Holding the output gap fixed, one additional percentage point of inflation raises it by 1.5 percentage points. The total response coefficient is $1+\alpha$, not just $\alpha$; see the [[泰勒原则|Taylor principle]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际应用须固定价格指数、频率、[[潜在产出]]估计与[[经济数据的实时口径|当时可得的数据版本]]。模型遗漏、金融条件和工具约束可能要求进一步判断；不能从偏离这条简式便直接判定政策失误，也不能用它决定资产购买规模。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Applications must specify the price index, frequency, estimated [[潜在产出|potential output]] and [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data vintage]]. Omitted mechanisms, financial conditions and instrument constraints require judgment. A deviation is not by itself a policy error, and this equation does not prescribe asset purchases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Taylor（1993），Discretion versus Policy Rules in Practice，pp. 195–199、202](https://web.stanford.edu/~johntayl/Onlinepaperscombinedbyyear/1993/Discretion_versus_Policy_Rules_in_Practice.pdf#page=8)：反馈规则、原始例式及变量单位；原文中的利率记作 $r$，本卡改记 $i$ 以免与实际利率混淆。
- [Federal Reserve，Principles for the Conduct of Monetary Policy，“The Taylor rule”](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/principles-for-the-conduct-of-monetary-policy.htm)：一般表示、总通胀反应及实际应用边界。4.5% 为教学构造并直接复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taylor supplies the original specification; the Federal Reserve explains its interpretation and limits. The numerical application is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
