# 线性最小二乘系数解唯一当且仅当矩阵满列秩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The coefficient solution to linear least squares is unique exactly when the matrix has full column rank*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心判别
> 对 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，线性最小二乘的系数解对每个 $b\in\mathbb R^m$ 都唯一，当且仅当
> $$
> N(A)=\{0\},
> $$
> 也就是 $A$ 满列秩。无论这个条件是否成立，最佳拟合向量 $A\hat x$ 始终唯一。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, the coefficient solution to linear least squares is unique for every $b\in\mathbb R^m$ exactly when $N(A)=\{0\}$, equivalently when $A$ has full column rank. Whether or not this condition holds, the fitted vector $A\hat x$ is always unique.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么秩决定系数而不决定拟合值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why rank controls coefficients but not the fitted vector*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 满列秩，则 $A^TA$ 正定可逆，正规方程只有一个解。若 $A$ 秩亏，取任意非零 $z\in N(A)$；只要 $\hat x$ 是一个最小二乘解，
$$
A(\hat x+z)=A\hat x,
$$
所以 $\hat x+z$ 产生同一目标值，也是另一个解。系数不唯一来自零空间自由度，而不是列空间中的最近点不唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ has full column rank, then $A^TA$ is positive definite and invertible, so the normal equations have one solution. If $A$ is rank deficient, choose nonzero $z\in N(A)$. Whenever $\hat x$ is a least-squares solution, $A(\hat x+z)=A\hat x$, so $\hat x+z$ produces the same objective value and is another solution. Coefficient non-uniqueness comes from null-space freedom, not from non-uniqueness of the nearest point in the column space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\end{bmatrix}$、$b=2$。所有满足 $x_1+x_2=2$ 的向量都给出零残差，例如 $(2,0)^T$、$(1,1)^T$ 和 $(0,2)^T$。拟合值都等于 $2$，但系数向量不同；若还要求二范数最小，则需要再加一个选择原则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=[1\ 1]$ and $b=2$. Every vector satisfying $x_1+x_2=2$ has zero residual, including $(2,0)^T$, $(1,1)^T$, and $(0,2)^T$. The fitted value is always $2$, but the coefficient vector differs. Selecting the smallest 2-norm solution requires an additional principle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么“最佳拟合值唯一”不能推出“最小二乘系数唯一”？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does uniqueness of the fitted vector not imply uniqueness of the least-squares coefficients?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 若 $z\in N(A)$，则 $A(\hat x+z)=A\hat x$；不同系数可以映到同一个唯一拟合值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** If $z\in N(A)$, then $A(\hat x+z)=A\hat x$, so distinct coefficient vectors can map to the same unique fitted vector.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
