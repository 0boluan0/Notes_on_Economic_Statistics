# Mean independence without independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值独立不推出独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two groups can have the same average and still behave very differently. [[均值独立|Mean independence of $Y$ from $X$]] requires only $E[Y\mid X]=E[Y]$. [[随机变量独立|Independence]] would require learning $X$ to leave every probability about $Y$ unchanged. A group with no variation and a group with symmetric positive and negative outcomes give a concrete counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两组可以有相同平均值，却表现得很不一样。[[均值独立|$Y$ 对 $X$ 均值独立]]只要求 $E[Y\mid X]=E[Y]$；[[随机变量独立|独立性]]则要求知道 $X$ 后，关于 $Y$ 的各项概率都不变。让一组完全没有波动，另一组在正负两端对称波动，就能得到反例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build the complete joint model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看完整的联合模型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $X=0$ or $1$ with equal probability. When $X=0$, set $Y=0$. When $X=1$, choose $Y=-1$ or $1$ with equal probability. Each interior entry below is a **joint probability** $P(X=x,Y=y)$; row and column totals are the corresponding marginal probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先等概率选择 $X=0$ 或 $1$。当 $X=0$ 时，令 $Y=0$；当 $X=1$ 时，等概率选择 $Y=-1$ 或 $1$。表中内部格子是**联合概率** $P(X=x,Y=y)$，行和、列和是相应的边际概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| $X\backslash Y$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ | Row total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $0$ | $0$ | $1/2$ | $0$ | $1/2$ |
| $1$ | $1/4$ | $0$ | $1/4$ | $1/2$ |
| Column total | $1/4$ | $1/2$ | $1/4$ | $1$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To read the row $X=1$ as a [[条件概率|conditional distribution]], divide its entries by the row total $1/2$. Thus the two nonzero conditional probabilities are $(1/4)/(1/2)=1/2$. An equivalent construction is $X\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)$, an independent symmetric sign $U\in\{-1,1\}$, and $Y=XU$; the [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli variable]] switches the sign on or off.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把 $X=1$ 这一行读成[[条件概率|条件分布]]，需要除以行和 $1/2$。所以两个非零条件概率均为 $(1/4)/(1/2)=1/2$。等价构造是：$X\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)$，$U$ 为独立于 $X$ 的等概率正负号，令 $Y=XU$；这里 [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli 变量]]决定是否开启这个正负号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The mean stays fixed, but a probability changes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值不变，概率却变了*

$$
E[Y\mid X=0]=0,\qquad E[Y\mid X=1]=(-1)\frac12+1\frac12=0,\qquad E[Y]=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two positive-probability groups cover the whole model, so $E[Y\mid X]=0=E[Y]$ almost surely. All variables are bounded, so every moment used here exists. This establishes mean independence in the direction **$Y$ from $X$**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两个正概率组覆盖整个模型，因此 $E[Y\mid X]=0=E[Y]$ 几乎处处成立。变量均有界，所以这里使用的矩全部存在。这证明的是 **$Y$ 对 $X$** 的均值独立。

> [!warning] One changed event disproves independence · 一个事件就能否定独立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> From the column total, $P(Y=0)=1/2$. But $P(Y=0\mid X=0)=1$: learning $X=0$ makes a zero outcome certain. Equivalently, $P(X=0,Y=0)=1/2$ differs from $P(X=0)P(Y=0)=1/4$. Therefore $X$ and $Y$ are dependent despite the constant conditional mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从列和可知 $P(Y=0)=1/2$，但 $P(Y=0\mid X=0)=1$：知道 $X=0$ 后，零结果变成必然。也可以比较 $P(X=0,Y=0)=1/2$ 与 $P(X=0)P(Y=0)=1/4$，两者不相等。因此条件均值虽然恒定，$X,Y$ 仍然相依。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[条件方差|conditional variance]] makes the missing information visible. It is $0$ when $X=0$, and $1$ when $X=1$. The common centre is zero, but the spread changes. Equal averages have not made the full conditional distributions equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[条件方差]]进一步显示被均值遗漏的信息：$X=0$ 时为 $0$，$X=1$ 时为 $1$。中心同为零，波动却不同；平均值相等并没有让整个条件分布相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reverse the direction and read the columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*反过来沿着列看*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same table also shows why mean independence is directional. Before observing $Y$, $E[X]=1/2$. If $Y=0$, the only possible row is $X=0$, so $E[X\mid Y=0]=0$. If $Y=-1$ or $1$, the only possible row is $X=1$, so $E[X\mid Y=-1]=E[X\mid Y=1]=1$. These conditional means are not $1/2$. Thus $X$ is **not** mean independent of $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一张表也说明均值独立有方向。尚未观察 $Y$ 时，$E[X]=1/2$。若 $Y=0$，只可能处于 $X=0$ 这一行，所以 $E[X\mid Y=0]=0$；若 $Y=-1$ 或 $1$，只可能处于 $X=1$，所以两个条件均值均为 $1$。它们都不等于 $1/2$，因此 $X$ **不**对 $Y$ 均值独立。
