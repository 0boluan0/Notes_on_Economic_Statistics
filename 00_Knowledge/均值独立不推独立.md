# 均值独立允许条件方差改变，因此不能推出独立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mean independence allows changing conditional variance and need not imply independence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)$，独立的 $U$ 等概率取 $-1,1$，设 $Y=XU$。则 $E[Y\mid X=0]=E[Y\mid X=1]=0=E[Y]$，但
$$P(Y=0\mid X=0)=1\ne\frac12=P(Y=0).$$
两组条件方差分别为 0 和 1，所以相同均值并未保住相同分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An independent symmetric sign multiplied by a Bernoulli variable gives constant zero conditional means but conditional variances zero and one. The probability of a zero outcome changes after conditioning, disproving independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
反向的均值独立也不成立：$E[X]=1/2$，而 $E[X\mid Y=0]=0$、$E[X\mid Y=\pm1]=1$。这个例子同时展示弱于独立和方向性两个后果，后者源于同一联合分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reverse mean-independence direction also fails: conditioning on a zero product forces X=0, while a nonzero product forces X=1. The same joint model therefore exhibits both the weaker restriction and its directionality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
