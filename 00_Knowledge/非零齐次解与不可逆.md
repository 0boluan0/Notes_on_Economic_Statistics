# A square matrix with a nonzero homogeneous solution is singular
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*方阵存在非零齐次解，则不可逆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a square real matrix $A$, finding **one $z\ne0$ with $Az=0$** proves that $A$ has no [[逆矩阵|inverse]]. If an inverse existed, multiplying by it would imply
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实方阵 $A$，找到**一个满足 $Az=0$ 的非零向量 $z$**，就足以证明 $A$ 没有[[逆矩阵]]。假如逆矩阵存在，左乘它便得到

$$
z=A^{-1}Az=A^{-1}0=0,
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
contradicting $z\ne0$. Equivalently, $A$ sends the distinct inputs $0$ and $z$ to the same output. More generally, $A(x+tz)=Ax$ for every real $t$, so the lost direction cannot be recovered uniquely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与 $z\ne0$ 矛盾。等价地说，$A$ 把不同的输入 $0$ 和 $z$ 送到了同一个输出。更一般地，对任意实数 $t$，都有 $A(x+tz)=Ax$，所以丢失的方向无法唯一恢复。

$$
C=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&-1\\-1&1&0\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
C\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This cyclic difference matrix is singular. In contrast, $I0=0$ says nothing against the invertibility of $I$: **the zero vector is always in the [[零空间|nullspace]]**. The word “nonzero” is essential. For a rectangular matrix the same contradiction rules out a **left inverse**, while ordinary two-sided invertibility is a square-matrix question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个循环差分矩阵不可逆。相反，$I0=0$ 完全不妨碍 $I$ 可逆：**[[零空间]]永远包含零向量**。“非零”是不能省略的条件。若矩阵是矩形的，同样的反证会排除**左逆**；普通双侧可逆性则是方阵问题。
