# 下降三法是跌势中长阴线内部短暂反弹整理后再由阴线下破的组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Falling three methods is a downtrend formation with a pause inside an initial long down candle's range followed by a renewed downward breakout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下降三法是已有跌势中的三阶段[[K线]]组合：先有长阴线，再有数根较小、通常向上反弹的K线，最后由阴线恢复下跌并创出该段新低。中间小K线保持在第一根阴线的高低区间内；经典五根版本为一根长阴、三根小线、最后一根阴线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Falling three methods is a three-stage [[K线|candlestick]] formation in a downtrend: a long down candle, several small candles usually rebounding, and a final down candle resuming the decline to a new low for the move. The intermediate candles stay within the first candle's high-low range. The classical five-candle version has one long down candle, three small candles and a final down candle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它对应[[上升三法]]的下跌方向，休整阶段的根数不必机械固定为三。若采用最后一根收盘创新低的口径，完整信号必须等到那次收盘；仅有长阴线之后的小阳线反弹还不够。持续形态是学派分类，不保证跌势延续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is the downward counterpart of [[上升三法|rising three methods]], and the pause need not contain exactly three candles. Under a new-low closing convention, the signal becomes complete only at that final close; small up candles after the initial down candle are insufficient. Continuation is a charting classification rather than a guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
