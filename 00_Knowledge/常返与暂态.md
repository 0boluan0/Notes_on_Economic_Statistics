# Recurrence and transience
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*常返与暂态*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A state is **recurrent** if, after starting there, a Markov chain returns to it with probability one. It is **transient** if there is a positive chance of never returning. This distinction concerns the entire future, not how often the state appeared in a short observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从一个状态出发，Markov 链若以概率一在将来返回它，该状态就**常返**；若有正概率永远不返回，它就**暂态**。这里讨论的是全部未来，不是一次短观察里出现了几次。

> [!example] Many visits can still be transient · 访问很多次也可能暂态
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let state $0$ stay at $0$ with probability $0.99$ and move to state $1$ with probability $0.01$. State $1$ is [[吸收状态|absorbing]]: once entered, it is never left. Starting at $0$, the first positive return occurs at step one if the chain stays; after moving to $1$, return is impossible. Thus the return probability is $0.99<1$, so $0$ is transient. State $1$ returns to itself at the next step with certainty and is recurrent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 让状态 $0$ 以 $0.99$ 的概率留在 $0$，以 $0.01$ 的概率去 $1$；状态 $1$ 是进入后不再离开的[[吸收状态]]。从 $0$ 出发，若第一步留下，就已在正时刻回返；若去了 $1$，便再也回不来。所以回返概率是 $0.99<1$，$0$ 仍为暂态。状态 $1$ 则必定下一步回到自身，是常返状态。

## The first positive return
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先定义第一次正时刻回返*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous]] [[离散时间Markov链|Markov chain]] on a finite or countable state set. Write $\Pr_i$ and $E_i$ for probability and expectation when the chain starts at state $i$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑有限或可数状态集上的[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次]][[离散时间Markov链]]。用 $\Pr_i$ 与 $E_i$ 表示从状态 $i$ 出发时的概率和期望，定义

$$\tau_i^+=\inf\{n\ge1:X_n=i\},\qquad f_i=\Pr_i(\tau_i^+<\infty).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\tau_i^+$ selects the earliest positive integer return time; it is $\infty$ if no return occurs. The superscript $+$ excludes time zero. A self-loop counts as a return at step one: the chain need not leave before returning. This is the positive-return convention in [[n步转移非首达|first passage versus occupancy]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\tau_i^+$ 选出最早的正整数回返时刻；若从不返回，就取 $\infty$。上标 $+$ 排除了时刻零。自环算作一步回返，不要求先离开再回来。这就是[[n步转移非首达]]里区分的正时刻回返约定。

> [!abstract] The probability threshold is exact · 概率门槛是精确的
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> State $i$ is recurrent when $f_i=1$, and transient when $f_i<1$. Recurrence does not give a deterministic deadline for return or a finite mean return time. The latter distinction is [[正常返与零常返|positive versus null recurrence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $f_i=1$ 时，状态 $i$ 常返；$f_i<1$ 时暂态。常返没有给出一个确定的返回期限，也没有保证平均回返时间有限；后一问题由[[正常返与零常返]]区分。

## Why the number of visits gives an equivalent test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么访问总数给出等价判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V_i$ count all visits to $i$, including the starting visit at time zero. Each time the chain returns, it is again at $i$ under the same transition rule. Its chance of another eventual return is again $f_i$. This restart fact follows by applying the Markov property at each possible finite return time and summing over those times; it is not a claim that the states at successive dates are independent. Consequently, for integers $k\ge1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $V_i$ 统计访问 $i$ 的总次数，包括时刻零的初始访问。每次回返后，链又处在 $i$，使用同一转移规则，所以下一次最终再返回的概率仍为 $f_i$。这一重启事实可对每个可能的有限回返时刻应用 Markov 性，再对这些时刻求和得到；它没有声称相邻日期的状态独立。因此，对整数 $k\ge1$，

$$\Pr_i(V_i\ge k+1)=f_i^k,\qquad \Pr_i(V_i\ge1)=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f_i=1$, the probability of at least any finite number of visits is one, so there are infinitely many visits almost surely. If $f_i<1$, $f_i^k\to0$, so only finitely many visits occur almost surely. Yet there need not be a fixed upper bound on their number. In the opening example, at least 100 visits have probability $0.99^{99}>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f_i=1$，至少访问任意有限次的概率都是一，因此几乎必然访问无穷多次。若 $f_i<1$，则 $f_i^k\to0$，几乎必然只访问有限次；但访问次数未必有确定上限。开头例子里，至少访问 100 次的概率为 $0.99^{99}>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $\mathbf1_{\{X_n=i\}}$ for the [[事件指示变量|indicator]], equal to one at a visit and zero otherwise. Counting visits and taking nonnegative expectations gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\mathbf1_{\{X_n=i\}}$ 表示[[事件指示变量]]：访问时为一，否则为零。把次数相加并取非负期望，得到

$$V_i=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathbf1_{\{X_n=i\}},\qquad
E_iV_i=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_{ii}^{(n)}
=\begin{cases}(1-f_i)^{-1},&f_i<1,\\\infty,&f_i=1.\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $p_{ii}^{(n)}$ is the [[DTMC转移复合|occupancy probability]] after $n$ steps. The interchange of expectation and the increasing partial sums is justified by [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence]]. Alternatively, count how many positive integers are at most $V_i$ to obtain $E_iV_i=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Pr_i(V_i\ge k+1)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}f_i^k$, with the first term equal to one. The [[几何级数|geometric series]] gives the displayed value. Thus divergence of $\sum_n p_{ii}^{(n)}$ is equivalent to recurrence, not to a large value of one particular return probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $p_{ii}^{(n)}$ 是 $n$ 步后的[[DTMC转移复合|占据概率]]。期望与递增部分和的交换由[[单调收敛定理]]保证。也可以把 $V_i$ 写成“有多少个正整数不超过它”，得到 $E_iV_i=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Pr_i(V_i\ge k+1)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}f_i^k$，首项为一，再用[[几何级数]]求和。因此，$\sum_n p_{ii}^{(n)}$ 发散等价于常返，而不是某个单独回返概率很大就足够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening transient state, $p_{00}^{(n)}=0.99^n$ and $E_0V_0=100$. A large finite expected number of visits is entirely compatible with transience. [[马氏链沟通类|Communicating states]] share recurrence or transience; the proof compares return-probability sums by joining positive-probability routes. In a finite chain the [[有限链类分解|closed-class decomposition]] gives a useful structural test. For countably infinite chains, mutual accessibility alone does not guarantee recurrence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的暂态状态满足 $p_{00}^{(n)}=0.99^n$，所以 $E_0V_0=100$。很大的有限平均访问次数完全可以与暂态并存。[[马氏链沟通类|互通状态]]具有相同的常返或暂态类型；证明通过拼接正概率路线，比较回返概率级数。有限链中可使用[[有限链类分解|闭类分解]]作结构判断；可数无限链中，仅仅互达并不能保证常返。
