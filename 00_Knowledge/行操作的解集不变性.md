# Solution preservation under row operations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*行操作的解集不变性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row operations help solve a system by replacing its equations with easier ones. **Preserving the solution set** means that exactly the same assignments to the unknowns satisfy the old and new systems. The individual equations can change; what remains unchanged is the set of assignments that satisfy all of them together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行操作通过把方程换成更容易处理的方程，帮助求解。**保持解集**是指：满足原方程组与新方程组的未知量取值完全相同。单条方程可以改变，保持不变的是同时满足全部方程的那些取值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Replace an equation without losing it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*换一条方程，但不丢掉信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real unknowns $x,y$, start with $x+y=3$ and $2x+y=4$. Subtract twice the first equation from the second, including the right-hand side:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数未知量 $x,y$，从 $x+y=3$、$2x+y=4$ 出发。用第二条方程减去第一条的两倍，右端也要同步相减：

$$
\begin{cases}x+y=3,\\2x+y=4\end{cases}
\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}x+y=3,\\-y=-2.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new second equation gives $y=2$, and the first gives $x=1$. Why has no solution been lost or added? Every old solution satisfies the new difference equation. Conversely, adding twice the unchanged first equation to the new second equation restores $2x+y=4$. The operation is reversible because the first equation remains available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新第二条给出 $y=2$，代入第一条得 $x=1$。为什么没有漏解或添解？每个原解都满足相减后的方程；反过来，把未变的第一条的两倍加到新第二条，就恢复了 $2x+y=4$。第一条仍然保留，所以操作可以撤销。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The augmented matrix stores coefficients and right-hand sides together; its divider marks where the equals signs belong. In this example the same operation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增广矩阵把系数和右端一起记录，竖线标出等号原来所在的位置。本例的同一个操作写成

$$
\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1&1&3\\2&1&4\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1}
\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1&1&3\\0&-1&-2\end{array}\right].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $R_i$ denotes row $i$, and the arrow means “replace that row”. [[初等矩阵|Elementary row operations]] allow swapping rows, scaling a row by a nonzero number, or adding a multiple of a different row to it. Each operation has an inverse of the same kind.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $R_i$ 表示第 $i$ 行，箭头表示“替换这一行”。[[初等矩阵|初等行操作]]允许换行、整行乘非零数、把另一行的倍数加到本行。每一步都有同类的逆操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the whole solution set is unchanged
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么整个解集保持不变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write a system as $Ax=b$: the $m\times n$ matrix $A$ stores coefficients of $m$ equations in $n$ unknowns, the column $x$ stores the unknowns, and the column $b$ stores the $m$ given right-hand sides. Positive integers $m,n$ need not be equal. All entries are in the same field, such as $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. A finite sequence of legal row operations is left multiplication by an invertible $m\times m$ matrix $E$, a product of elementary matrices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把方程组写成 $Ax=b$：$m\times n$ 矩阵 $A$ 记录 $m$ 条方程中 $n$ 个未知量的系数，列向量 $x$ 记录未知量，列向量 $b$ 记录 $m$ 个给定右端。正整数 $m,n$ 不必相同，所有元素取同一域，例如实数域或复数域。有限串合法行操作等于左乘可逆的 $m\times m$ 矩阵 $E$；它是初等矩阵的乘积。

> [!important] The same operations on both sides · 两侧使用同样的操作
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every candidate column $x$,
>
> $$
> Ax=b\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(EA)x=Eb.
> $$
>
> Thus $[A\mid b]\mapsto[EA\mid Eb]$ preserves the entire solution set, including when it is empty or has many elements.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每一个候选列向量 $x$，都有上述双向等价。因此 $[A\mid b]\mapsto[EA\mid Eb]$ 保持整个解集；即使原系统无解或有多个解，也同样成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The forward implication follows by multiplying $Ax=b$ on the left by $E$ and using [[矩阵乘法|associativity of matrix multiplication]]: $E(Ax)=(EA)x$. For the reverse implication, multiply $(EA)x=Eb$ by the [[逆矩阵|inverse]] $E^{-1}$ to recover $Ax=b$. This proves equivalence for each possible $x$, not merely that one previously found solution still works. No inverse of $A$ is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正向只需对 $Ax=b$ 两侧左乘 $E$，再用[[矩阵乘法|矩阵乘法结合律]]得到 $E(Ax)=(EA)x$。反向对 $(EA)x=Eb$ 两侧左乘[[逆矩阵|逆矩阵]] $E^{-1}$，就恢复 $Ax=b$。这是对每一种可能的 $x$ 都成立的等价，不只是证明某个已找到的解仍有效；完全不要求 $A$ 可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The right-hand side must travel with the rows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 必须同步变换右端

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the opening example, changing only the coefficients would give $x+y=3$ and $-y=4$. That different system has $(x,y)=(7,-4)$, not $(1,2)$. [[行等价|Row equivalence]] of coefficient matrices alone therefore does not in general preserve solutions for an unchanged nonzero $b$. When $b=0$, however, $Eb=0$, so reversible row operations preserve the [[零空间|null space]], the set of inputs sent to zero. Their effects on the other spaces are described in [[行操作与四个子空间|row operations and the four subspaces]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在开头的例子里，若只改系数、不改右端，就会得到 $x+y=3$、$-y=4$，其解是 $(x,y)=(7,-4)$，而非 $(1,2)$。因此，系数矩阵[[行等价]]本身不能保证在非零右端不变时解集仍不变。若 $b=0$，则 $Eb=0$，可逆行操作会保持[[零空间]]，也就是被送到零的全部输入。其他空间怎样变化，见[[行操作与四个子空间]]。

> [!warning] Losing a row can add solutions · 抹掉方程可能添解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start with $x=1$, $y=2$. Multiplying the second equation by zero leaves $x=1$, $0=0$, which allows every value of $y$. More generally, a singular left factor $E$ still gives $Ax=b\Rightarrow EAx=Eb$, but the reverse implication can fail. Some special systems may happen to keep the same solutions; singular multiplication gives no general guarantee.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $x=1$、$y=2$ 出发，把第二条乘零，变成 $x=1$、$0=0$，此时任意 $y$ 都可以。一般地，奇异左因子 $E$ 仍保证 $Ax=b\Rightarrow EAx=Eb$，但反向可能不成立。某些特殊系统可能恰好仍保持解集；奇异乘法不能提供一般保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This theorem concerns row operations with the unknown coordinates held fixed. Swapping coefficient columns instead changes which variable a column represents; any such change must be tracked and undone when reporting the solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本定理在未知量坐标固定时讨论行操作。交换系数列则改变各列代表的变量；若这样做，报告解时必须记录并恢复对应关系。
