# 到期收益率是使单只债券承诺现金流现值等于价格的单一内部收益率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Yield to maturity is the single internal rate that equates one bond's promised cash flows to its price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子度量
> 给定结算日的全价 $P$（dirty price，即净价加应计利息）与结算日之后的承诺现金流 $CF_k$，到期收益率 $y$ 是满足
> $$P=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{CF_k}{(1+y/m)^{m t_k}}$$
> 的内部收益率；$m$、$t_k$、日计数和应计利息必须遵守该债券的结算与报价惯例。市场屏幕常报净价，但求解 YTM 时要回到全价。它把一只债券的整串现金流压缩成一个数，不是一条逐期限即期曲线。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Given settlement dirty price $P$—clean price plus accrued interest—and promised post-settlement payments $CF_k$, yield to maturity $y$ is the internal rate satisfying the displayed equation. The frequency $m$, payment times $t_k$, day count, and accrued interest must follow the bond's settlement and quotation conventions. Market displays often show clean price, but solving for YTM requires dirty price. YTM compresses one bond's payment stream into a single number; it is not a maturity-by-maturity spot curve.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零息债只有一笔到期现金流；只有在发行人或信用担保、币种、优先级、结算日、复利和其他现金流口径都一致时，它的 YTM 才与该工具对应的 [[即期利率]] 重合。附息债则不同：各期现金流原则上应按各自贴现因子估值，而 YTM 用同一个 $y$ 同时折现，因此会受息票率与现金流分布影响。两只期限相同但息票不同的债券，即使面对同一条贴现曲线，也可能报出不同 YTM。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero-coupon bond has one maturity payment. Its YTM coincides with the instrument's corresponding [[即期利率|spot rate]] only when issuer or credit guarantee, currency, seniority, settlement, compounding, and other cash-flow conventions match. A coupon bond differs: its payments should in principle be valued with their respective discount factors, whereas YTM applies one $y$ to all of them. YTM therefore depends on coupon rate and payment distribution. Two bonds with the same maturity but different coupons can have different YTMs even when priced from the same discount curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

YTM 还是“承诺现金流收益率”，不是无条件的实际持有回报。把它解释为持有至到期的已实现复合回报，至少还需要发行人按约付款，并处理息票再投资；提前出售时，未来售价也进入回报。信用债的高 YTM 因而不能直接读成高预期回报，违约概率、回收、流动性和风险补偿都可能进入价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
YTM is a yield on promised payments, not an unconditional realised holding return. Interpreting it as a realised compound return to maturity also requires contractual payment and an account of coupon reinvestment; an early sale brings the future sale price into the return. A credit bond's high YTM therefore cannot simply be read as a high expected return: default probability, recovery, liquidity, and risk compensation may all enter its price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通债券通常有一条经济上相关的 YTM；现金流符号多次变化时，内部收益率可能多根或无根，边界见 [[IRR 的根与失效边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary bond normally has one economically relevant YTM. With repeated changes in cash-flow signs, an internal rate may have multiple roots or no root; see [[IRR 的根与失效边界|IRR roots and failure boundaries]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两只同到期日但息票率不同的国债，为什么不能只因期限相同就假定 YTM 必然相等？
>
> **答案：** 两只债券把权重放在不同日期的现金流上；当即期曲线不平坦时，用一个数压缩各自现金流会得到不同的 YTM。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does a shared maturity date not imply identical YTMs for two government bonds with different coupon rates?
>
> **Answer:** The bonds put different weights on payments at different dates. With a non-flat spot curve, compressing their respective cash flows into one rate can produce different YTMs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Bank of England, *Estimating the Term Structure of Interest Rates*, §2.1 n.5](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/1994/estimating-the-term-structure-of-interest-rates)：核对债券估值方程中的价格是净价加应计利息，而不是屏幕净价本身。
- [Bank of England, Yield curve terminology and concepts, footnotes 2–3](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves/terminology-and-concepts)：核对 YTM 是用一个利率折现全部息票与本金，及其相对 spot rate 的分析局限。
- [Federal Reserve, The U.S. Treasury Yield Curve, §§2.1–2.2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2006/200628/index.html)：核对零息、附息和 par yield 的现金流差异。
- [[固定现金流债券定价]]、[[债券全价]]、[[到期收益率与实现收益]]：复用逐笔定价、含息报价及承诺 IRR 与实现复合收益的区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Bank of England's *Estimating the Term Structure of Interest Rates*, Section 2.1, note 5, supports using clean price plus accrued interest in the valuation equation rather than the displayed clean quote alone.
- Bank of England's terminology page, current footnotes 2–3, supports single-rate discounting of coupons and principal and its analytical limitations relative to spot rates.
- The Federal Reserve's *The U.S. Treasury Yield Curve*, Sections 2.1–2.2, supports the differences between zero-coupon, coupon-bearing, and par-yield cash-flow structures.
- [[固定现金流债券定价|Fixed-cash-flow bond valuation]], [[债券全价|dirty price]], and [[到期收益率与实现收益|YTM versus realised return]] supply per-payment valuation, accrued-interest-inclusive pricing, and the distinction between promised IRR and realised compound return.
