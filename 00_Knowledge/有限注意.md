# 有限注意是同一时点可用于选择和处理信息的认知资源受到限制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limited attention means cognitive resources for selecting and processing information are constrained at a given time*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限注意意味着：信息虽然已经可以取得，人也未必同时注意到、读完并理解所有内容。限制在处理能力与注意分配，而不一定在信息是否公开；所以“公开了”与“已被充分处理”要分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Available information need not all be noticed, read, and understood simultaneously. The constraint concerns processing capacity and allocation, not necessarily disclosure. Availability and processing are distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个直觉例子：你原本在读甲公司的盈利公告，同一时刻又出现许多公司的公告。即使甲的数字没有变化，你分给它的注意力也可能减少。投资者分心假说据此预测：其他条件相当时，即时反应较弱，而随后同方向的公告后漂移较强。Hirshleifer、Lim 与 Teoh 用同期其他公司公告数研究了这组联合预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine reading one firm's earnings while many other announcements arrive. Attention may fall even if its figures do not change. The distraction hypothesis predicts weaker immediate response but stronger subsequent same-direction drift, other things equal. Hirshleifer, Lim, and Teoh study these joint predictions using competing announcements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是可能机制，不是“消息越多股价一定涨得越少”的规则。其他公告可能传递真实行业信息，公告时点也可能经过选择；检验要控制这些替代解释。注意力少也不自动导致市场误价：其他交易者可能补位。[[有效市场三种形式]]界定公开信息范围，[[行为解释证据链]]检验从认知限制到价格的具体通道。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a possible mechanism, not an unconditional price rule. Competing news may convey fundamentals, and announcement timing may be selected. Other traders may offset inattention. Use [[有效市场三种形式|the information-set distinction]] and [[行为解释证据链|mechanism-specific evidence]] to test the pricing channel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
