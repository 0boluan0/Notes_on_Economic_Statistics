# 三重顶是上涨后三次近同高试探并跌破其间支撑的形态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A triple top forms after an advance when three tests of a similar high are followed by a break below the intervening support.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三重顶是在先前上涨之后，价格三次试探近似同一高位、峰间发生回落，并在第三峰之后跌破其间 [[支撑位]]的反转形态。三个近同高峰是候选轮廓，向下突破是这里采用的完成条件；它比 [[双重顶]]多一次高位试探，不以中峰显著高于两侧为定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A triple top follows an advance and contains three tests of a similar high, separated by pullbacks, followed by a break below the intervening [[支撑位|support]]. Three peaks give a candidate outline; breakdown completes this convention. It adds one test to a [[双重顶|double top]] and does not require a distinctly higher middle peak.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

峰高容差、峰谷识别及支撑的取法须先固定。两处谷位不同高时，应先声明使用哪条边界或支撑区域，再检验价格是否突破；不能事后换到最容易给出成功信号的谷位。第三峰出现并不保证跌破，而真正下单与实际退出受 [[交易信号时序]]约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify peak tolerance, turning-point identification and the support construction in advance. If the intervening troughs differ, choose the boundary or support region before testing the break; do not retrospectively select whichever level produces a successful signal. A third peak does not ensure breakdown, and orders and exits remain subject to [[交易信号时序|execution timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，选定三峰均为 100、两谷均为 94，并冻结收盘严格下破规则，则第三峰后收盘 93 才越过 94。若第三峰后价格继续上穿 100，本次“将要下跌”的叙事不成立，不能把那个未来高点从图中删掉以保留三重顶故事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With three selected peaks at 100, two troughs at 94 and a strict closing-break rule, a later close at 93 crosses support. If price instead rises above 100 after the third peak, an imminent-decline story is unsupported; the later high cannot be erased to preserve the story.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
