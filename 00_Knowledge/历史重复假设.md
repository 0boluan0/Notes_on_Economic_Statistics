# 历史重复假设是相似市场行为模式可能在不同时间再次出现的技术分析前提
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The history-repetition assumption holds that similar patterns of market behaviour may recur at different times.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史重复假设认为，交易者行为及其价格表现可能出现相似模式，因此研究过去的图形可能帮助形成关于以后行情的假说。这里的“重复”是模式相似，不是未来价格会逐点复制某张历史图。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The history-repetition assumption proposes that trader behaviour and resulting price patterns may recur, so historical charts can help generate hypotheses about later markets. Repetition means pattern similarity, not a point-for-point replay of an earlier price path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用这项假设时，应在看后续走势之前固定哪些特征算相似、容许多大误差、观察多长窗口。否则每次都在事后调整画法，就无法区分同一规则再次出现和研究者重新解释了一张图。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before seeing subsequent prices, specify the features defining similarity, their tolerances, and the observation window. If the interpretation is altered afterward every time, recurrence of one rule cannot be distinguished from a fresh interpretation of each chart.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如两段行情都“先涨、回落、再涨”，这三个词可以涵盖大量不同路径。只有把转折点识别、幅度和确认时点具体化，才能统计该模式出现之后发生了什么；统计结果又需要未参与规则选择的资料，见 [[训练验证测试划分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Rise, pull back, rise again” describes many different paths. Turning-point detection, magnitudes, and confirmation times must be specified before subsequent outcomes can be counted consistently. Evaluation then needs data not used to select the rule; see [[训练验证测试划分|training, validation, and test roles]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
