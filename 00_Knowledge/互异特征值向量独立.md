# Linear independence for distinct eigenvalues
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*互异特征值对应的特征向量为什么独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix may stretch different directions by different factors. If those factors are all different, the corresponding eigenvectors cannot contain a redundant direction: none can be assembled from the others. This lets us establish independence from the eigenvalues, without row-reducing a matrix of eigenvectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个矩阵可以把不同方向乘上不同倍数。如果这些倍数两两不同，对应的特征向量就不会包含多余方向：任何一个都不能由其余向量拼出来。因此，不必把特征向量排成矩阵再行化简，也能从特征值判断它们独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[特征对|eigenpair]] satisfies $Av=\lambda v$, with $v\ne0$: applying the matrix $A$ multiplies that vector by the scalar $\lambda$. [[线性无关|Linear independence]] means that a [[张成|linear combination]] equals zero only when all its scalar coefficients are zero. Each coefficient says how much of one vector is used in the combination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个[[特征对]]满足 $Av=\lambda v$，其中 $v\ne0$：矩阵 $A$ 作用后，该向量被乘上标量 $\lambda$。[[线性无关]]表示：只有各个标量系数全为零，[[张成|线性组合]]才等于零；每个系数说明组合中使用了相应向量的多少倍。

> [!example] Independent does not mean perpendicular · 独立不等于垂直
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over the real numbers, take
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实数范围取
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad v_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad v_2=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$
> $$Av_1=2v_1,\qquad Av_2=3v_2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The eigenvalues $2$ and $3$ differ. For a direct check, $cv_1+dv_2=(c+d,d)^T=0$ forces $d=0$, then $c=0$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], turning the coordinate row into a column. The vectors are independent even though their [[向量点积|dot product]] is $1$, so they are not perpendicular.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 特征值2和3不同。直接检查，$cv_1+dv_2=(c+d,d)^T=0$ 先迫使 $d=0$，再得 $c=0$。这里 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把横排坐标写成列向量。这两个向量独立，但[[向量点积|点积]]为1，所以并不垂直。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The result applies to the entire list
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*结论保证整组向量独立*

> [!important] Distinct eigenvalues supply independent vectors · 互异特征值给出独立向量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix a scalar field $\mathbb F$, such as $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over that field, with $n\ge1$. If $Av_i=\lambda_i v_i$ for $i=1,\ldots,k$, where $k$ is a positive integer, every $v_i$ is nonzero, and the scalars $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_k$ are pairwise distinct, then the whole list $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ is linearly independent over $\mathbb F$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定标量域 $\mathbb F$，例如 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。设 $A$ 为该域上的 $n\times n$ 方阵，$n\ge1$。若对 $i=1,\ldots,k$ 有 $Av_i=\lambda_i v_i$，其中 $k$ 为正整数，每个 $v_i$ 非零，且标量 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_k$ 两两不同，那么整组 $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ 在 $\mathbb F$ 上线性无关。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Pairwise distinct” refers to the eigenvalues. The conclusion is independence of the entire vector list, which is stronger than merely checking that no two vectors are multiples. In an $n$-dimensional space, an independent list has at most $n$ members, so a matrix cannot have more than $n$ distinct eigenvalues in that field.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“两两不同”说的是特征值；结论是整组向量独立，比“任意两个都不成比例”更强。在 $n$ 维空间中，独立向量最多有 $n$ 个，因此方阵在该域内也不可能有超过 $n$ 个互异特征值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Applying the matrix separates the coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*施加矩阵后，怎样把系数分开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof removes one vector at a time. For one vector, $c_1v_1=0$ implies $c_1=0$ because $v_1\ne0$. Now assume the result has been proved for every list of $k-1$ eigenvectors with distinct eigenvalues. To handle $k$ vectors, suppose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明每次消去一个向量。只有一个向量时，因为 $v_1\ne0$，$c_1v_1=0$ 必须有 $c_1=0$。现在假设结论对任意 $k-1$ 个对应互异特征值的向量都成立。为了处理 $k$ 个，先假设

$$c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Applying $A$ multiplies each term by its own eigenvalue. Multiplying the original equation by $\lambda_k$ instead multiplies every term by the same number. Subtract the second equation below from the first:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

施加 $A$，每一项会乘上自己的特征值；把原等式乘上 $\lambda_k$，则每一项都乘同一个数。用下面第一式减第二式：

$$\begin{aligned}
c_1\lambda_1v_1+\cdots+c_k\lambda_kv_k&=0,\\
c_1\lambda_kv_1+\cdots+c_k\lambda_kv_k&=0.
\end{aligned}$$

$$c_1(\lambda_1-\lambda_k)v_1+\cdots+c_{k-1}(\lambda_{k-1}-\lambda_k)v_{k-1}=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $v_k$ term has disappeared. The remaining $k-1$ vectors have distinct eigenvalues, so the induction assumption forces each coefficient $c_i(\lambda_i-\lambda_k)$ to be zero. Every difference $\lambda_i-\lambda_k$ is nonzero; dividing by it gives $c_i=0$ for $i<k$. Substitution into the original equation leaves $c_kv_k=0$, hence $c_k=0$ as well. This identifies exactly where distinctness is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$v_k$ 那一项消失了。剩余 $k-1$ 个向量对应互异特征值，所以归纳假设迫使每个系数 $c_i(\lambda_i-\lambda_k)$ 为零。差 $\lambda_i-\lambda_k$ 都非零，除以它便得 $i<k$ 时 $c_i=0$。代回原式只剩 $c_kv_k=0$，故 $c_k=0$。这也说明了证明究竟在哪一步需要特征值互异。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What this lets us conclude
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样使用这个结论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an $n\times n$ matrix has $n$ distinct eigenvalues in the chosen field, select one eigenvector for each. The resulting $n$ independent vectors form a [[基|basis]]: every vector has a unique expansion in them. Using them as columns of a matrix gives a [[对角化|diagonalization]]. Distinct eigenvalues are sufficient, but not necessary: the real matrix $2I_2$ has two independent eigenvectors $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T$ with the same eigenvalue $2$. Here $I_2$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. The complete test is the [[可对角化判据|diagonalizability criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $n\times n$ 方阵在选定域内有 $n$ 个互异特征值，每个选一个特征向量，就得到 $n$ 个独立向量。它们构成一组[[基]]，每个向量都能由它们唯一展开；把它们作为矩阵的列，就能进行[[对角化]]。互异特征值是充分条件，却不是必要条件：实矩阵 $2I_2$ 的 $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T$ 都对应特征值2，仍然独立；这里 $I_2$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。完整判断见[[可对角化判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem also explains why bases from different [[特征空间|eigenspaces]] can be combined without creating a dependence. Group a proposed zero combination by eigenvalue, obtaining $w_1+\cdots+w_s=0$ with $w_i\in E_{\lambda_i}$. Discard any zero $w_i$; the remaining terms are eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues, so none can remain. Thus every group sum is zero; independence within each chosen basis then forces all the original coefficients to be zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个定理也说明，不同[[特征空间]]各自选出的基合在一起，不会新产生依赖关系。把一个等于零的组合按特征值分组，写成 $w_1+\cdots+w_s=0$，其中 $w_i\in E_{\lambda_i}$。先去掉等于零的 $w_i$；剩下的都是互异特征值的特征向量，由定理可知不可能还有非零项。因此各组和都为零，再用各组基自身的独立性，就得到原来所有系数都为零。

> [!note] Keep the hypotheses in view · 留意前提
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A zero eigenvalue is allowed; a zero eigenvector is not. All vectors must belong to the same matrix and scalar field. Repeated eigenvalues leave independence undecided: vectors within one eigenspace may be independent or may be multiples. No orthogonality conclusion follows for a general matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 允许零特征值，不允许零特征向量。所有向量必须属于同一个矩阵、同一个标量域。特征值重复时，这个定理不替你判断：同一特征空间里的向量既可能独立，也可能成比例。对一般矩阵，结论不包含正交性。

> [!question]- Four distinct values · 四个互异值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A $4\times4$ matrix has four distinct eigenvalues in the working field. Must we additionally calculate the rank of four corresponding eigenvectors? No: the theorem guarantees independence, so those four columns already form a basis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个 $4\times4$ 方阵在当前域内有四个互异特征值，还需另外计算相应四个特征向量的秩吗？不需要；定理已经保证它们独立，四列自动构成一组基。
