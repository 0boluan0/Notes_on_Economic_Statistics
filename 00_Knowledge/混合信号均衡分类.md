# 在混合信号策略下，相同的类型条件分布是合并、互不相交的类型支持是分离，其余情形是半分离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under mixed signaling strategies, identical type-conditional distributions are pooling, pairwise disjoint type supports are separating, and all remaining cases are semi-separating*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发送者类型为 $\theta\in\Theta$，其行为策略 $\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)\in\Delta(S)$ 给出该类型的信号分布。下列判定规则把混合信号策略分成三个互斥且穷尽的类别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the sender's type be $\theta\in\Theta$, with behavior strategy $\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)\in\Delta(S)$. The following rule partitions mixed signaling strategies into three mutually exclusive and exhaustive classes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三类策略结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Three Strategy Structures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **合并（pooling）：** 所有类型使用同一个完整信号分布；
- **分离（separating）：** 任意两个不同类型使用的信号支持互不相交；
- **半分离（semi-separating）：** 既不满足合并条件，也不满足分离条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Pooling:** every type uses the same complete distribution over signals;
- **Separating:** the signal supports of any two distinct types are disjoint;
- **Semi-separating:** the strategy is neither pooling nor separating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形式上，合并要求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Formally, pooling requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)=\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta')
\qquad\text{for all }\theta,\theta'\in\Theta.
$$

分离要求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separating requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)
\cap
\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta')
=\varnothing
\qquad\text{whenever }\theta\ne\theta'.
$$

半分离则同时包含“至少一对类型的完整分布不同”和“至少一对类型共享一个正概率信号”。见证这两个条件的类型对可以不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Semi-separation therefore requires both that at least one pair of types use different complete distributions and that at least one pair share a signal played with positive probability. The two conditions need not be witnessed by the same pair of types.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 纯策略是退化分布的特例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pure Strategies Are the Degenerate Special Case*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若每个类型都以概率 1 发送某个信号，合并就退化为“所有类型发送同一信号”，分离就退化为“不同类型发送不同信号”。接收者是否在后续行动之间混合，不改变对发送者信号结构的分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When every type sends one signal with probability one, pooling reduces to all types sending the same signal, while separating reduces to distinct types sending distinct signals. Whether the receiver mixes over subsequent actions does not change the classification of the sender's signaling strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 术语边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Terminological Boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

文献对 mixed pooling、partial pooling、hybrid 与 semi-separating 的用词并不完全统一。这里的口径是一种清晰的混合扩展：它保留纯策略三分法，并确保每份发送者策略恰好落入一个类别。分类只描述信号分布的结构；成为均衡还须满足 posterior、一致性、最佳反应与偏离约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Terminology such as mixed pooling, partial pooling, hybrid, and semi-separating is not completely uniform across the literature. The rule above is a precise mixed-strategy extension that preserves the pure-strategy trichotomy and assigns every sender strategy to exactly one class. It classifies the structure of signal distributions only; equilibrium additionally requires valid posteriors, consistency, best responses, and deviation constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
