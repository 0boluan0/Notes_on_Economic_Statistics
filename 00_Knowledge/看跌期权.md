# 看跌期权赋予持有人按执行价卖出标的的权利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A put gives its holder the right to sell the underlying at the strike price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通看跌期权（put）的买方可以按 $K$ 卖出约定标的。到期时若 $S_T<K$，按高于市价的 $K$ 卖出有价值；若 $S_T\ge K$，可以放弃。按一单位标的、无履约障碍写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A plain put lets its holder sell at $K$. At expiry this is valuable when $S_T<K$, while the holder can decline when $S_T\ge K$. Per underlying unit, with performance available,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$X_T=\max(K-S_T,0).$$

取 $K=100$，市价为 80、100、120 时，到期支付依次是 20、0、0。若期初支付权利金 6，忽略利息和费用，则对应净损益为 14、−6、−6，盈亏平衡价为 94。对于价格非负的股票，最大到期支付为 100，而不是无限大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $K=100$, terminal prices of 80, 100, and 120 yield payoffs of 20, 0, and 0. An initial premium of 6 gives profits of 14, −6, and −6 before interest and fees; breakeven is 94. For a nonnegative stock price, the maximum terminal payoff is 100, not infinity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

买入看跌与卖出股票的义务不同：前者持有选择权，后者已经建立股票空头。若用实物交割的看跌行权，必须交付股票；原本没有股票时，可能形成新的空头或需要买入交付。若已有一股股票并配上一份同期限看跌，到期组合支付为 $S_T+\max(K-S_T,0)=\max(S_T,K)$，这是一个有价格下限的[[套期保值|保护组合]]，但成本还包括期权费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Buying a put is not the same obligation as short-selling stock. Physical exercise requires delivery and can create a short position or require buying shares for delivery. One share plus a matching put pays $\max(S_T,K)$ at expiry, creating a [[套期保值|protected position]]; its cost still includes the premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
