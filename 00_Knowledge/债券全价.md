# 普通名义债券全价是净价与应计利息之和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An ordinary nominal bond's dirty price equals clean price plus accrued interest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券全价（dirty price、full price）是在同一单位下包括应计利息的价格。对普通名义债券，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond's dirty price, or full price, includes accrued interest on the same unit basis. For an ordinary nominal bond,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_{\rm dirty}=P_{\rm clean}+AI.$$

它对应买卖交割时债券本身的含息金额；手续费、税及其他交易费用另按规则计入实际资金收付。净价和应计利息若都按每 $100$ 面值报价，全价也按每 $100$ 面值计，不能混入整笔持仓金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is the bond's accrued-interest-inclusive settlement price. Fees, taxes, and other transaction costs enter actual cash settlement separately. If clean price and accrued interest are quoted per 100 face value, dirty price is also per 100; do not mix those quotes with whole-position amounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如净价 $98$、应计利息 $1$，全价为 $99$。若结算后买方能收取的所有未来现金流已列好，求[[到期收益率]]时等式左边应放这 $99$，不是 $98$；应计利息也不能再作为额外未来现金流加一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a clean price of 98 and accrued interest of 1, dirty price is 99. Once all future payments accruing to the buyer after settlement are listed, the [[到期收益率|YTM]] equation uses 99 on the left, not 98. Accrued interest must not also be added again as an extra future payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数化债券等可能另有指数比率、真实报价和名义结算的换算。先确认单位与产品规则，再使用净价加应计关系；“全价”不代表任意屏幕上标出的 all-in 指标都只有这两个部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Index-linked bonds may require index ratios and conversion between real quotations and nominal settlement. Check units and product rules before applying the clean-plus-accrued relationship. “Dirty price” does not imply that every displayed “all-in” measure has only these two components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- [DMO Glossary，Dirty price](https://www.dmo.gov.uk/help/glossary/)：支持普通国债的净价加应计关系，并明确部分指数债的额外换算。
- [DMO 第 4 版收益率公式，印刷第 4 页／PDF 第 5 页及注 2](https://www.dmo.gov.uk/media/334d05fo/yldeqns_v4.pdf#page=5)：支持债券未来支付的价格—收益率式采用每 $100$ 名义本金的全价，而非净价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- DMO's glossary supports clean price plus accrued interest and the additional conversion for certain index-linked gilts.
- DMO's fourth-edition formulae, printed p. 4 / PDF p. 5 and note 2, support use of dirty price per 100 nominal in the price-yield equation.
