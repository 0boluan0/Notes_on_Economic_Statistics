# 专业劳动者在产品完成并售出以前需要有人预先提供生活资料、材料和工具

当劳动者专门生产一种商品时，不能等到商品卖出才开始吃饭、取得原料和使用工具。Smith 因此指出：[[分工]] 要持续进行，就需要事先准备一批资源，支持从开始生产到完成销售的这段时间。这些资源可以由劳动者本人持有，也可以由其他人提供。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A worker specialising in one commodity cannot wait until it is sold before eating, obtaining materials or using tools. Smith therefore argues that sustained [[分工|division of labour]] requires resources available in advance to cover the interval between starting production and completing the sale. The worker or another person may provide them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以织工为例：他要先拿到纱线、使用织机，并在织布期间生活；布织好以后，还可能要等买者付款。只有这段期间所需的物品已经由某处供给，他才不必不断停下织布，转去生产自己的全部生活必需品。Smith 的重点是这个生产与收入之间的时间差，不是要求每个劳动者先攒出一笔个人现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A weaver needs yarn, access to a loom and subsistence while weaving; even after the cloth is finished, the sale may take time. Advance provision allows the weaver to continue rather than repeatedly stop to produce every necessity personally. Smith's point concerns the time gap between production and its proceeds, not a requirement that every worker first accumulate personal cash savings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 stock 包括可供使用的生活资料、材料和工具。货币可以帮助取得这些物品，但持有货币与实际拥有所需资源并不是同一句话。分工更细、同样人数能处理更多材料时，所需的原料和工具也可能增加；提供预付资源的人则有动机改善工作安排和机器，使这些资源产生更多产品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relevant stock includes provisions, materials and tools available for use. Money can help obtain them, but holding money is not identical to having the required resources. Finer division of labour may allow the same workforce to process more materials and consequently require more inputs and equipment. Those advancing resources also have an incentive to improve organisation and machinery so that their stock produces more output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这说明扩大专业生产需要什么支持。新增支持从哪里来，是 [[Smith积累机制]] 讨论的问题；制成的产品是否有足够买者，则是 [[分工的市场限制]]。有订单但缺乏生产期间的资源，和有资源却卖不出产品，是不同的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This condition identifies what supports expanded specialised production. [[Smith积累机制|Smith's account of capital accumulation]] asks where additional provision comes from; [[分工的市场限制|the market constraint]] asks whether enough buyers exist. Having orders but lacking resources during production differs from having resources but being unable to sell the output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Smith，[《国富论》第二篇导论](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=15)，第2–4段（Econlib 的 II.I.2–4）：织工在完成并售出前需要生活资料、材料和工具；资源可以由本人或他人持有；存量增加支持更细分工、更多材料及机器改进。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paragraphs 2–4 of Book II's introduction supply the weaver example, provision by the worker or someone else, and the connection between a larger stock, finer division and equipment.
