# 人口红利是年龄结构变化为提高人均经济表现创造的有条件机会

人口红利指人口年龄结构变化创造的经济增长潜力，典型窗口是劳动年龄人口相对增加、儿童抚养份额下降。这里的“红利”首先是机会，不能把年龄比例变化直接当作已经获得的收入增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A demographic dividend is the potential economic gain created by changes in population age structure, typically as the working-age share rises and child dependency falls. The dividend is initially an opportunity; an age-share change is not itself realized income growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把机会转成收益，人们需要健康、教育、能发挥技能的工作及相应的生产条件。年龄窗口也不是永久的：同一代人继续变老后，年龄结构可能转为另一种供养关系。[[人口转型]]说明窗口从何而来，[[年龄结构与人均产出]]检验机会是否转成实际产出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Realization requires health, education, jobs that use people’s skills and supporting productive conditions. The age window is not permanent: as the same cohort ages, support relationships change. The [[人口转型|demographic transition]] explains the window’s origin; [[年龄结构与人均产出|age structure and output per capita]] tests whether it translates into production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，看到[[年龄抚养比]]下降时，可以判断人口结构的某项条件变得有利；还不能从中读出 GDP 增长了多少，更不能把同期全部增长都归因于人口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A falling [[年龄抚养比|age dependency ratio]] can identify a favourable demographic condition. It cannot reveal how much GDP has grown or attribute all contemporaneous growth to demography.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
