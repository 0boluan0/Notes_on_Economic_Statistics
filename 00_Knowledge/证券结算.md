# 证券结算是通过转移证券及所需资金履行交易义务的过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securities settlement discharges transaction obligations by transferring securities and, where required, funds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券结算（settlement，此处也称交收）是依约转移证券以及适用时的资金，使交易义务得到履行的过程。证券可以通过簿记转移，不必搬运纸质凭证。最终交收是其中法律上不可撤销、无条件完成转移或清偿的时点，须由适用法律与系统规则确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securities settlement transfers securities and, where applicable, funds to discharge transaction obligations. Book-entry transfers need no movement of paper certificates. Final settlement is the legally defined point at which a transfer or discharge is irrevocable and unconditional under applicable law and system rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成交确定交易条件，[[证券清算]]核对待履行义务，都不等于已完成最终交收。账户显示成交记录或临时记账，也不能单独证明该转移具有最终性。无付款证券转移同样可以结算；买卖交易若有证券与现金两项对应义务，则还要检查两者是否通过[[货银对付]]联结。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Execution establishes trade terms and [[证券清算|clearing]] reconciles obligations; neither is final settlement. A trade record or provisional account entry does not by itself establish finality. Securities transfers can settle free of payment; where a sale exchanges securities for cash, check whether [[货银对付|delivery versus payment]] links the obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，周一成交约定买入 100 股，每股 20 元，在约定交收日尚未完成收券或付款时，不能把“成交 2,000 元”记成“两条交收腿均已最终完成”。交收日如何计算依产品、市场、营业日和有效规则；$T+k$ 的交收周期也不是“买入后必须等 $k$ 天才能再次卖出”的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a Monday purchase of 100 shares at 20, a trade value of 2,000 does not mean both settlement legs are final while securities or payment remain outstanding. Settlement-date calculation depends on the product, market, business-day calendar, and effective rules. A $T+k$ settlement cycle does not itself define a prohibition on resale for $k$ days.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最终性交收也需要届时可用的证券与资金。日终净额小不保证日内备付需求小，复用[[支付最终性与日内流动性|最终性与日内资金需求的区别]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Final settlement still requires available securities and funds at the relevant time. Small end-of-day net obligations need not mean small intraday funding needs; see [[支付最终性与日内流动性|finality versus intraday liquidity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- [CPSS–IOSCO, PFMI，Principle 8，印刷第 64–66 页／PDF 第 70–72 页](https://www.bis.org/pages/principles-financial-market-infrastructures/d101a.pdf#page=70)：已实际重开，支持最终性的法律时点、临时状态与最终完成的区别，以及失败交收仍需管理；Annex H 印刷第 174、176、178–179 页支持簿记、证券结算系统、value date 等术语。再交易资格与交收日期是不同问题的辨析，不从该原则推导某市场当前交易限制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- PFMI Principle 8, printed pp. 64–66 / PDF pp. 70–72, was reopened for legal finality and settlement-failure boundaries. Annex H, printed pp. 174, 176, and 178–179, supports book-entry, settlement-system, and value-date terminology. The distinction between resale eligibility and settlement timing does not infer current trading restrictions for any particular market.
