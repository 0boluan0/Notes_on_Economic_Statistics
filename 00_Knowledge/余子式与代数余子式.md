# Minors and cofactors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*余子式与代数余子式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **minor of an entry** measures what remains after you cross out that entry's row and column: take the [[行列式|determinant]] of the smaller square matrix. Its **cofactor** is that number multiplied by a position-dependent factor, either $+1$ or $-1$. These numbers are the ingredients of [[余子式展开|cofactor expansion]], which computes a determinant using smaller ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个元素的**余子式**这样得到：划掉这个元素所在的整行与整列，再对剩下的小方阵取[[行列式]]。它的**代数余子式**还要乘一个由位置决定的因子 $+1$ 或 $-1$。这些数是[[余子式展开]]的组成部分，使我们能用低阶行列式计算高阶行列式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Cross out a row and a column
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先删去一行一列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work over the real numbers in this example. In the matrix below, focus on the entry $2$ in row $1$, column $2$. Delete row $1$ and column $2$, keeping the remaining rows and columns in their original order:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的例子使用实数。关注矩阵中第 $1$ 行、第 $2$ 列的元素 $2$；删去第 $1$ 行与第 $2$ 列，其余行列保持原次序：

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\0&4&5\\1&0&6\end{pmatrix},
\qquad A_{\widehat1,\widehat2}=\begin{pmatrix}0&5\\1&6\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The hat marks the deleted index. The remaining matrix is a $2\times2$ object; its determinant is the scalar $M_{12}=0\cdot6-5\cdot1=-5$. Since $1+2$ is odd, its cofactor is $C_{12}=-M_{12}=5$. The entry $2$, the remaining matrix, the minor $-5$, and the cofactor $5$ are four different objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

帽号表示删去的下标。剩下的是一张 $2\times2$ 矩阵；它的行列式才是标量 $M_{12}=0\cdot6-5\cdot1=-5$。因为 $1+2$ 为奇数，代数余子式为 $C_{12}=-M_{12}=5$。原元素 $2$、剩余矩阵、余子式 $-5$、代数余子式 $5$ 是四个不同对象。

> [!important] Matrix, minor, cofactor · 子矩阵、余子式、代数余子式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over one field $\mathbb F$, such as the real or complex numbers, with integer $n\ge1$. Here $a_{ij}$ is the entry in row $i$, column $j$, and $1\le i,j\le n$. Let $A_{\widehat i,\widehat j}$ be the $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ matrix obtained by deleting that row and column without reordering the others. Define
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A=(a_{ij})$ 为同一域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 矩阵，例如实矩阵或复矩阵，其中 $n\ge1$ 为整数。$a_{ij}$ 表示第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的元素，且 $1\le i,j\le n$。令 $A_{\widehat i,\widehat j}$ 表示删去这行这列、其余行列不重排后得到的 $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ 矩阵。定义
>
> $$M_{ij}=\det A_{\widehat i,\widehat j},\qquad
> C_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}M_{ij}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $M_{ij}$ is the **minor of $a_{ij}$** and $C_{ij}$ is its **cofactor**, also called its algebraic cofactor. Both are scalars in the same field as the entries.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $M_{ij}$ 是 $a_{ij}$ 的**余子式**，$C_{ij}$ 是其**代数余子式**；两者都是与原元素同一域中的标量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The checkerboard gives a multiplier
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*棋盘格给出的是乘数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factors $(-1)^{i+j}$ alternate as follows; a plus means multiply by $+1$, and a minus means multiply by $-1$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因子 $(-1)^{i+j}$ 按下面的棋盘格交替；加号表示乘 $+1$，减号表示乘 $-1$：

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
+&-&+&\cdots\\
-&+&-&\cdots\\
+&-&+&\cdots\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does **not** predict whether the resulting real cofactor is positive or negative. The opening example has an odd-position cofactor $C_{12}=5>0$ because its minor is negative. Conversely, in $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-2\end{pmatrix}$ the even-position cofactor $C_{11}=-2<0$. Zero minors give zero cofactors. For complex entries there is no general positive/negative ordering, but multiplication by $\pm1$ still makes sense. In a field with $1+1=0$, the two multipliers coincide; the same algebraic definition remains valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它**不能**预言最终的实数代数余子式是正是负。开头例子中，奇数位置的 $C_{12}=5>0$，原因是余子式本身为负。反过来，在 $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-2\end{pmatrix}$ 中，偶数位置的 $C_{11}=-2<0$。余子式为零时，代数余子式也为零。复数通常不能比较正负，但乘以 $\pm1$ 仍有意义；在满足 $1+1=0$ 的域中，这两个乘数相同，同一个代数定义依然成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cofactor does not depend on the deleted entries. In the opening matrix, $a_{21}=0$, yet deleting row $2$ and column $1$ gives $M_{21}=2\cdot6-3\cdot0=12$, hence $C_{21}=-12$. A zero matrix entry can therefore have a nonzero cofactor; its contribution $a_{21}C_{21}$ to an expansion is nevertheless zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代数余子式不依赖被删去的那些元素。开头矩阵中 $a_{21}=0$，但删去第 $2$ 行和第 $1$ 列得到 $M_{21}=2\cdot6-3\cdot0=12$，所以 $C_{21}=-12$。原元素为零，代数余子式仍可能非零；不过它在展开中的贡献 $a_{21}C_{21}$ 仍为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word *minor* also has a broader use: the determinant of any square submatrix selected by equal numbers of rows and columns. Here we focus on the entry minor obtained by deleting exactly one row and one column. A [[主子式|principal minor]] retains the same index set for rows and columns. For $n\ge2$, deleting row $i$ and column $i$ gives such a selection; deleting different indices does not. Some texts instead use “minor” for the deleted matrix itself. In this note $A_{\widehat i,\widehat j}$ always names the matrix and $M_{ij}$ its determinant. The [[伴随矩阵|adjugate]] uses the transpose of the cofactor matrix, so that distinction also prevents an extra or missing determinant operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

minor 也有更广的用法，指选取相同数量的行与列得到任意方形子矩阵后，再取它的行列式。本卡聚焦于恰好删去一行一列得到的元素余子式。[[主子式]]要求保留的行、列下标集相同：当 $n\ge2$ 时，删去第 $i$ 行和第 $i$ 列满足这一要求，删去不同下标则不满足。有些教材也把删剩的矩阵本身称作 minor；本卡始终用 $A_{\widehat i,\widehat j}$ 指矩阵，用 $M_{ij}$ 指它的行列式。[[伴随矩阵]]使用代数余子式矩阵的转置，分清这些对象也能避免多取或少取一次行列式。

## Order one and the empty determinant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 一阶矩阵与空行列式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=[a]$, deleting the only row and column leaves the $0\times0$ empty matrix. The standard convention is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A=[a]$ 为一阶矩阵，删去唯一的行和列后就得到 $0\times0$ 空矩阵。标准约定为

$$\det(\varnothing)=1,\qquad M_{11}=C_{11}=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Leibniz 行列式公式|permutation formula]], there is one permutation of the empty index set and its product has no factors; that empty product is $1$. This is not a claim that the original entry $a$ equals one. It makes the recursive expansion work even when $a=0$: $\det[a]=aC_{11}=a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[Leibniz 行列式公式|排列公式]]中，空下标集有唯一一个排列，对应乘积没有任何因子；空乘积约定为 $1$。这不是说原元素 $a$ 等于一。它使递归展开在 $a=0$ 时也成立：$\det[a]=aC_{11}=a$。

> [!question]- Check the multiplier · 检查乘数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $M_{23}=5$ over the real numbers, what is $C_{23}$? Since $2+3$ is odd, $C_{23}=-5$. If instead $M_{23}=-5$, the same multiplier gives $C_{23}=5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实数情形，若 $M_{23}=5$，则 $2+3$ 为奇数，故 $C_{23}=-5$。若改成 $M_{23}=-5$，同一个乘数就给出 $C_{23}=5$。
