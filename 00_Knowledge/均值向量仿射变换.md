# Mean under an affine transformation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值向量的仿射变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the same fixed linear combinations and shifts to every possible vector. The new mean is obtained by applying that very same rule to the old mean. This lets us change units, subtract a reference level, or form several new measurements without recomputing their whole joint distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个可能向量都使用同一组固定线性组合和偏移量，新均值就等于把同一规则作用于旧均值。因此，改变单位、减去基准值或组合出新的测量时，不必重新计算整个联合分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the [[随机向量|random vector]] $X=(H,W)^T$ records height in centimetres and weight in kilograms, with [[均值向量|mean vector]] $\mu=(170,65)^T$. Form three outputs: height in metres, weight unchanged, and height in centimetres above a $170$ cm reference. Using [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设[[随机向量]] $X=(H,W)^T$ 记录身高（厘米）和体重（千克），[[均值向量]]为 $\mu=(170,65)^T$。构造三个输出：以米为单位的身高、保持不变的体重，以及相对 $170$ 厘米基准的身高差。用[[矩阵乘法]]写成

$$Y=AX+b,\qquad A=\begin{bmatrix}0.01&0\\0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad b=\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\-170\end{bmatrix},\qquad Y=\begin{bmatrix}H/100\\W\\H-170\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two input coordinates and three output coordinates, so $A$ is $3\times2$. Each row states a fixed calculation; it is not an observed person or a random sample. The output mean is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入有两个坐标，输出有三个坐标，所以 $A$ 为 $3\times2$。每一行规定一条固定计算规则，不代表一名观察到的人，也不是一条随机样本。输出均值为

$$E[Y]=A\mu+b=\begin{bmatrix}1.70\\65\\0\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three coordinates have units metres, kilograms and centimetres respectively. In the equally likely two-profile model $(160,50)^T$ or $(180,80)^T$, the new vectors are $(1.60,50,-10)^T$ and $(1.80,80,10)^T$; their average confirms the result. Keeping only the first two outputs gives the original unit conversion $\operatorname{diag}(0.01,1)X$, with mean $(1.70,65)^T$. Here $\operatorname{diag}(0.01,1)$ is the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with diagonal entries $0.01,1$ and zero off-diagonal entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个坐标的单位依次为米、千克和厘米。若原模型以各半概率取 $(160,50)^T$、$(180,80)^T$，新向量就是 $(1.60,50,-10)^T$、$(1.80,80,10)^T$，平均后正好得到上式。只保留前两个输出，就恢复单位变换 $\operatorname{diag}(0.01,1)X$，均值为 $(1.70,65)^T$。这里 $\operatorname{diag}(0.01,1)$ 是对角元为 $0.01,1$、其余元素为零的[[对角矩阵]]。

> [!important] Fixed affine transformation · 固定仿射变换
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X\in\mathbb R^p$ have integrable coordinates, and let $\mu=E[X]$. For a fixed real matrix $A\in\mathbb R^{q\times p}$ and a fixed vector $b\in\mathbb R^q$, with positive integers $p,q$, the transformed vector $Y=AX+b$ is integrable coordinatewise and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X\in\mathbb R^p$ 各坐标可积，$\mu=E[X]$。取固定实矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{q\times p}$ 和固定向量 $b\in\mathbb R^q$，其中 $p,q$ 为正整数，则变换后的 $Y=AX+b$ 也逐坐标可积，而且
>
> $$\boxed{E[AX+b]=A\,E[X]+b=A\mu+b.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Affine” means a linear rule $AX$ followed by a fixed shift $b$. The matrix may be rectangular or rank-deficient; it need not be invertible. No independence among the coordinates and no Gaussian assumption are required. Only finite first moments are used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“仿射”表示线性规则 $AX$ 再加一个固定偏移 $b$。矩阵可以是矩形，也可以秩亏，不要求可逆。不需要各坐标独立，也不需要 Gaussian 假设；这里只使用有限一阶矩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the same rule moves the mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么直接移动同一个均值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For output coordinate $k$, the coefficient $A_{kj}$ tells us how much of input coordinate $X_j$ is used. The constant $b_k$ is then added:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对第 $k$ 个输出，系数 $A_{kj}$ 决定第 $j$ 个输入坐标 $X_j$ 以多少权重参与组合，最后再加常数 $b_k$：

$$(AX+b)_k=\sum_{j=1}^{p}A_{kj}X_j+b_k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First check that this output has a finite absolute mean. The triangle inequality gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认这个输出的绝对值期望有限。三角不等式给出

$$E\big|(AX+b)_k\big|\le\sum_{j=1}^{p}|A_{kj}|\,E|X_j|+|b_k|<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] can be applied to this finite sum of integrable terms:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在可以对这些可积项组成的有限和使用[[期望线性性]]：

$$E[(AX+b)_k]=\sum_{j=1}^{p}A_{kj}E[X_j]+b_k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stacking these $q$ scalar equalities gives the vector formula. Linearity concerns sums and fixed coefficients; it does not need independence. For example, the result remains valid when two input coordinates are identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把这 $q$ 条标量等式重新排成向量，就得到定理。线性性处理的是求和与固定系数，不需要独立；例如，两个输入坐标完全相同，也不妨碍结论。

> [!warning] The coefficients must be fixed · 系数必须固定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A random coefficient cannot generally be pulled through expectation. For a fair sign $Z=\pm1$, set the scalar coefficient $A=Z$ and input $X=Z$. Then $E[AX]=E[Z^2]=1$, while $E[A]E[X]=0$. Thus even replacing a random coefficient by its mean does not recover the displayed rule without additional assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 随机系数一般不能直接移出期望。令公平符号 $Z=\pm1$，取标量系数 $A=Z$、输入 $X=Z$，则 $E[AX]=E[Z^2]=1$，但 $E[A]E[X]=0$。所以，额外条件缺失时，即使把随机系数换成其均值，也不能恢复上面的规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This rule transforms a centre; it does not by itself determine a distribution, a tail probability or a measure of spread. With finite second moments, [[协方差仿射变换|the separate covariance transformation]] describes the resulting covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本规则变换的是中心，本身并不能确定分布、尾部概率或波动程度。在有限二阶矩下，新的协方差由另一条[[协方差仿射变换]]公式描述。

> [!question] Compute the transformed mean · 计算新均值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $E[X]=(2,-1)^T$, $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&3\\0&-2\end{bmatrix}$ and $b=(4,1)^T$, what is $E[AX+b]$? The first coordinate is $2+3(-1)+4=3$ and the second is $-2(-1)+1=3$, so the answer is $(3,3)^T$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $E[X]=(2,-1)^T$，$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&3\\0&-2\end{bmatrix}$、$b=(4,1)^T$，第一项为 $2+3(-1)+4=3$，第二项为 $-2(-1)+1=3$，故 $E[AX+b]=(3,3)^T$。
