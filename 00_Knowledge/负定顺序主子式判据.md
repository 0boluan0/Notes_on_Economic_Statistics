# 实对称矩阵负定当且仅当顺序主子式奇数阶负而偶数阶正

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric matrix is negative definite exactly when its leading minors alternate negative and positive by order*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 实对称，$L_k=\det A_{1:k,1:k}$。则
$$
A\prec0\iff(-1)^kL_k>0\quad(k=1,\ldots,n).
$$
由[[负定矩阵|负定]]等价于 $-A$ 正定，对 $-A$ 使用[[Sylvester 正定判据]]即可：其第 $k$ 阶子矩阵整体乘了 $-1$，行列式乘 $(-1)^k$。

二维例 $A=\begin{pmatrix}-3&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}$ 有 $L_1=-3,L_2=6-1=5$，故负定。只检验最后一个行列式的正负并不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negating the matrix converts [[负定矩阵|negative definiteness]] into positive definiteness. Its order-k leading minor gains the factor $(-1)^k$, so [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester's criterion]] yields the alternating strict signs. The example satisfies both required tests; checking only the final determinant is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
