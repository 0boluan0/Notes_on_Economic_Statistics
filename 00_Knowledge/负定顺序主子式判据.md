# Leading-minor test for negative definiteness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*负定的顺序主子式判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[负定矩阵|negative-definite matrix]] gives a strictly negative quadratic value in every nonzero direction. This theorem lets us test that requirement using one determinant at each matrix size. The signs alternate: negative at odd orders and positive at even orders. The alternation comes from negating **every entry of the matrix**, not from an arbitrary rule to memorize.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[负定矩阵]]在每个非零方向上都给出严格负的二次型值。本定理把这个要求变成每一阶检查一个行列式：奇数阶为负，偶数阶为正。这种交替来自把**整个矩阵取负**，并不是一条无缘由的记忆规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, consider the real symmetric matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，考虑实对称矩阵

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}-3&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its first upper-left determinant is $L_1=-3$; its full determinant is $L_2=(-3)(-2)-1=5$. These are exactly the required two signs. To see what the conclusion means, take a real column $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$, where $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Directly completing the square gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它的一阶左上角行列式是 $L_1=-3$，整个行列式为 $L_2=(-3)(-2)-1=5$，恰好满足两阶的符号要求。为了看清结论，取实列向量 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。直接配方得到

$$x^TAx=-3x_1^2+2x_1x_2-2x_2^2
=-3\left(x_1-\frac{x_2}{3}\right)^2-\frac53x_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both terms are nonpositive. They can both vanish only when $x_2=0$ and then $x_1=0$, so every nonzero input gives a strictly negative value. The determinant test reaches the same conclusion without first finding this completed-square expression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两项都非正，同时为零只能先有 $x_2=0$，再有 $x_1=0$。所以每个非零输入都给出严格负值。行列式判据无需先找到这个配方式，也能得到同一结论。

## Which determinants and which matrices?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*检查哪些行列式，适用于哪些矩阵？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer. Assume $A$ is either an $n\times n$ [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric matrix]], $A=A^T$, or a [[Hermitian 矩阵|complex Hermitian matrix]], $A=A^*$. The star denotes conjugate transpose. In the complex case negative definiteness tests the real scalar $z^*Az<0$ for every nonzero complex column $z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数。假设 $A$ 为 $n\times n$ 的[[实对称矩阵]]，即 $A=A^T$；或为复 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，即 $A=A^*$。星号表示共轭转置。复数情形的负定要求每个非零复列向量 $z$ 都使实数 $z^*Az<0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its [[顺序主子式|leading principal minor]] of order $k$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它的 $k$ 阶[[顺序主子式]]为

$$L_k=\det A_{1:k,1:k},\qquad k=1,\ldots,n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $1:k$ means retain the first $k$ rows and the first $k$ columns, in the current order. The [[行列式|determinant]] is a number, not the selected block itself. Each such block is symmetric or Hermitian, so its determinant is real and its sign can be compared with zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $1:k$ 表示按当前次序保留前 $k$ 行和前 $k$ 列。[[行列式]]是一个数，不是所选方块本身。每个这样的块仍然实对称或 Hermitian，因此行列式为实数，能够比较它与零的大小。

> [!important] All orders, alternating strict signs · 全部阶数，严格交替
> $$A\prec0\iff(-1)^kL_k>0\quad(k=1,\ldots,n).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $A\prec0$ means negative definite. Thus $L_1<0$, $L_2>0$, $L_3<0$, and so on through order $n$. No tested determinant may be zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $A\prec0$ 表示负定。因此 $L_1<0$、$L_2>0$、$L_3<0$，依此检查到第 $n$ 阶；被检查的行列式不能为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n=1$, this is just $a_{11}<0$. In the real two-dimensional case $A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$, it is $a<0$ and $ac-b^2>0$. These already force $c<0$: the second inequality gives $ac>b^2\ge0$, and division by the negative number $a$ reverses the inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$n=1$ 时只需 $a_{11}<0$。对实二阶矩阵 $A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$，条件为 $a<0$ 且 $ac-b^2>0$。这已经迫使 $c<0$，因为第二条说明 $ac>b^2\ge0$，再除以负数 $a$ 时不等号反向。

## Why negation produces exactly these signs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么取负号恰好得到这些符号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $B=-A$. Negation preserves symmetry or Hermitian symmetry and reverses every quadratic value:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $B=-A$。取负保持实对称或 Hermitian 性，并使每个二次型值反号：

$$z^*Bz=-z^*Az,\qquad
A\prec0\iff B\succ0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $B\succ0$ means [[正定矩阵|positive definite]]; for real vectors replace $*$ by $T$. By [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester's criterion]], $B$ is positive definite exactly when all of its leading principal minors are positive. For each order $k$, its leading block is $-A_{1:k,1:k}$. [[整体缩放的行列式|Scaling a whole matrix]] by $-1$ scales each of its $k$ rows, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$B\succ0$ 表示[[正定矩阵|正定]]；实向量情形把 $*$ 换成 $T$。由[[Sylvester 正定判据]]，$B$ 正定恰好等价于它的全部顺序主子式为正。第 $k$ 阶的左上块是 $-A_{1:k,1:k}$；[[整体缩放的行列式|整个矩阵乘以 $-1$]]时，它的 $k$ 行各贡献一个负因子，所以

$$\det B_{1:k,1:k}=\det(-A_{1:k,1:k})=(-1)^kL_k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, demanding positivity for every leading minor of $B$ is precisely demanding $(-1)^kL_k>0$ for every $k$. Each step is an equivalence, proving both necessity and sufficiency. The exponent is the order of the block currently being tested, not always the full dimension $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，要求 $B$ 的每个顺序主子式为正，恰好就是要求每一阶都满足 $(-1)^kL_k>0$。每一步都是等价关系，所以必要性与充分性同时得到证明。指数取的是当前方块的阶数，不是每次都用整个矩阵的维数 $n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complex example $H=\begin{pmatrix}-2&i\\-i&-2\end{pmatrix}$, where $i^2=-1$, is Hermitian. Its leading minors are $-2$ and $4-i(-i)=3$, so it is negative definite. Complex off-diagonal entries are allowed; the determinants and Hermitian quadratic values used for the sign test remain real.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数例子 $H=\begin{pmatrix}-2&i\\-i&-2\end{pmatrix}$ 中 $i^2=-1$，矩阵为 Hermitian。其顺序主子式为 $-2$ 与 $4-i(-i)=3$，所以负定。允许非对角元是复数；判别符号所用的行列式与 Hermitian 二次型值仍为实数。

## Why shorter or weakened checks fail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么不能少检查或直接放宽不等号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Checking only the full determinant is insufficient. The [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_2$ has determinant $1$, the correct sign for order two, but its first leading minor is $1>0$, and every nonzero quadratic value is positive. Conversely, a negative-definite three-by-three matrix has a negative full determinant: the final sign depends on dimension. It is the entire alternating sequence that matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只看整个行列式不够。[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$ 的行列式为 $1$，符合二阶的符号，但一阶顺序主子式为 $1>0$，且所有非零二次型值均正。反过来，三阶负定矩阵的整个行列式必须为负：最后的符号随维数变化，真正要检查的是完整的交替序列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Symmetry cannot be omitted either. The nonsymmetric matrix $C=\begin{pmatrix}-1&4\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ has leading minors $-1,1$, but for $x=(1,1)^T$ it gives $x^TCx=-1+4-1=2$. The criterion does not apply to this matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对称性同样不能省略。非对称矩阵 $C=\begin{pmatrix}-1&4\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ 的顺序主子式为 $-1,1$，但取 $x=(1,1)^T$，有 $x^TCx=-1+4-1=2$；本判据不能用于这个矩阵。

> [!warning] Semidefiniteness changes the collection · 半定时检查范围也改变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replacing $>0$ by $\ge0$ in the leading-minor formula does not establish [[半负定矩阵|negative semidefiniteness]]. For $\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$, the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with entries $0,1$, both leading minors vanish, yet the second coordinate gives a positive quadratic value. The correct [[半负定主子式判据|semidefinite test]] uses **all principal minors**, with weak signs by order.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把顺序主子式公式的 $>0$ 换成 $\ge0$，不能证明[[半负定矩阵|半负定]]。[[对角矩阵]] $\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$ 的对角元为 $0,1$，两个顺序主子式都为零，第二坐标方向却给出正二次型值。正确的[[半负定主子式判据]]需要检查**全部主子式**，并按阶数使用非严格符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are exact sign conditions. A determinant reported as nearly zero by floating-point computation needs an error assessment before its strict sign can be certified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些都是精确的符号条件。浮点计算得到一个接近零的行列式时，需要先评估误差，才能确认严格符号。
