# L¹ boundedness and uniform integrability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

L¹有界不推出一致可积

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A uniform bound on $E|X_n|$ limits each variable’s total average absolute size. It does not say where that average comes from: a fixed amount can be concentrated in values that become larger and rarer. [[一致可积|Uniform integrability]] asks for the stronger statement that one sufficiently large cutoff removes only a small weighted contribution from **every** member.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统一控制$E|X_n|$，只限制每个变量的总平均绝对大小，没有说明这份平均由哪些数值贡献。数值越来越大、出现越来越罕见的尖峰，仍可能一直贡献相同的平均值。[[一致可积]]要求更强：同一个足够大的阈值，能让**每个**成员在阈值之外的加权贡献都很小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calling a family **$L^1$-bounded** means $\sup_nE|X_n|<\infty$. It is a boundedness property; [[L1收敛|$L^1$ convergence]] instead compares each $X_n$ with a specified limit through $E|X_n-X|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

称一个族**$L^1$有界**，就是$\sup_nE|X_n|<\infty$。这是有界性；[[L1收敛|$L^1$收敛]]则通过$E|X_n-X|$比较每一项与指定极限，两者不是同一个条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A spike that moves beyond every cutoff
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Draw one $U$ uniformly from $(0,1)$: an interval of length $a$ starting at zero has probability $a$. Define all the variables from this same draw, using an [[事件指示变量|indicator]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从$(0,1)$均匀抽取一个$U$：从零开始、长度为$a$的区间概率为$a$。用这同一次抽取和[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]定义整个序列：

$$X_n=n\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}.
$$

> [!example] Increasing height, decreasing frequency · 越高越罕见
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $n=10$, the value 10 appears with probability 1/10. At $n=1000$, the value 1000 appears with probability 1/1000. In both cases, and for every $n$, $E|X_n|=n(1/n)=1$. Thus the family is $L^1$-bounded.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $n=10$时，以1/10概率取10；$n=1000$时，以1/1000概率取1000。每一种情形都满足$E|X_n|=n(1/n)=1$，所以整个族$L^1$有界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now choose any cutoff $K\ge0$. We may choose an integer $n>K$. All the nonzero values of this $X_n$ lie beyond the cutoff, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在任意选一个阈值$K\ge0$，总能再找到整数$n>K$。此时$X_n$的全部非零值都落在阈值之外，因此

$$E[|X_n|\mathbf1_{\{|X_n|>K\}}]=n\cdot\frac1n=1,\qquad
\sup_nE[|X_n|\mathbf1_{\{|X_n|>K\}}]=1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The supremum cannot exceed one because the entire first absolute moment is one. Hence it is exactly one for every cutoff and never tends to zero. This is the failure of uniform integrability. The family does not need independence; all members here use the same $U$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上确界也不会超过1，因为总一阶绝对矩就是1。因此每个阈值对应的上确界都恰好为1，永远不会趋零。一致可积正是在这里失败；这个反例不需要独立性，所有成员使用同一个$U$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each outcome $U=u>0$, once $n>1/u$, the variable is zero forever. So $X_n\to0$ [[几乎必然收敛|almost surely]], yet $E|X_n-0|=1$. This also shows why a pathwise limit alone cannot justify passing expectations to the limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定任何结果$U=u>0$，当$n>1/u$后变量便永远为零，所以$X_n\to0$[[几乎必然收敛|几乎必然]]；但$E|X_n-0|=1$不变。这同时说明仅有路径极限不足以交换期望与极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why uniform integrability does imply the bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么一致可积能推出有界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a common cutoff $K$ with every tail expectation below one. Split the average into values at most $K$ and those above it:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一致可积，可选共同阈值$K$，使所有尾部期望小于1。把平均拆成阈值内与阈值外：

$$E|X_i|=E[|X_i|\mathbf1_{\{|X_i|\le K\}}]+E[|X_i|\mathbf1_{\{|X_i|>K\}}]\le K+1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same bound works for every $i$. The valid implication is therefore UI $\Rightarrow L^1$-bounded; the spike example disproves its converse. A [[Lp有界推出一致可积|uniform bound on a higher absolute moment]] supplies the missing tail control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个界对每个$i$都成立，所以有效方向是“一致可积$\Rightarrow L^1$有界”；尖峰例子推翻反向。[[Lp有界推出一致可积|统一高阶绝对矩界]]则能提供缺失的尾部控制。
