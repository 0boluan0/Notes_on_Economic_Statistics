# 非正规身份本身不足以识别企业的经营约束与增长潜力

[[非正规部门|非正规身份]]是统计分类，不是经营约束的诊断。同属这一类别的企业仍可在资本、经营能力、市场机会和增长潜力上有显著差异；小规模、低资本或未登记本身，都不足以识别企业的主要约束或它能否扩张。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[非正规部门|Informal status]] is a statistical classification, not a diagnosis of business constraints. Firms within it can differ substantially in capital, managerial capabilities, market opportunities, and growth potential. Small size, low capital, or lack of registration alone cannot identify the binding constraint or capacity to expand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Grimm、Knorringa 与 Lay 用 2001–2002 年西非七座城市的企业调查作了一个具体区分：有些低资本企业的可观察经营者特征接近表现较好的企业，却尚未达到同样经营表现，作者称其为“受约束的瞪羚”。这说明“现在很小”与“没有增长潜力”不是同一判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using enterprise surveys from seven West African cities in 2001–2002, Grimm, Knorringa, and Lay identify low-capital firms whose observable owner characteristics resemble those of better-performing firms despite lower realized performance. They call these firms “constrained gazelles.” Present smallness and lack of growth potential are different judgements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一项基于调查特征与经营表现的分类研究，不是随机发放信贷后的效果证明。其“表现较好者、受约束者、生存型经营者”划分依赖样本和阈值；既不能当成所有国家都适用的固定三类，也不能仅凭被分入某组就断言贷款会使该企业增长。研究所用的旧调查定义也不能直接替代 2023 年 ILO 标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a survey-based classification, not experimental evidence that credit causes growth. Its top-performer, constrained-gazelle, and survivalist groups depend on the sample and thresholds. They are not a universal three-type taxonomy, and group membership does not establish that a loan will generate growth. The historical survey definition also differs from a direct application of the 2023 ILO standard.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际诊断应把企业现状与待验证的约束分开：资本少时，现有经营能力、市场需求与准入障碍分别如何？这些线索用于提出可检验假说，再进入[[正规化政策评价]]，不能让“非正规”标签直接决定统一的登记或信贷处方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate observed conditions from hypothesized constraints: when capital is scarce, what do managerial capabilities, market demand, and entry barriers each look like? Such evidence generates testable hypotheses for [[正规化政策评价|formalization-policy evaluation]], not a uniform registration or credit prescription based on the informal label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Grimm, Knorringa & Lay（2012），Constrained Gazelles，ISS Working Paper 537](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/31826/wp537.pdf)：印刷／PDF 第 7–8 页的调查范围、历史定义及分类方法；第 27 页的阈值敏感性与政策效果仍须具体评价的限制。支持异质性与现有规模不能单独判潜力，不支持普遍政策因果结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The primary study supports heterogeneity, the distinction between realized size and potential, and limits of its classification. The diagnostic questions synthesize those limits; they are not estimated treatment effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
