# 自然对数等于倒数函数从一到正输入的有向积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The natural logarithm is the oriented integral of the reciprocal function from one to a positive input*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个 $x>0$，
$$\ln x=\int_1^x\frac{dt}{t}.$$
[[自然对数]]已经是指数函数的反函数；这里给出**同一个函数**的累积表示。$x>1$ 时积分为正，$0<x<1$ 时因上下限反向而为负，$x=1$ 时为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an accumulation representation of the same [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] already defined as the exponential inverse. Orientation makes it positive above one, negative between zero and one, and zero at one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令右边为 $L(x)$。[[积分求导定理]]给 $L'(x)=1/x$，[[对数函数求导]]给 $(\ln x)'=1/x$；两者在连通区间 $(0,\infty)$ 上只差常数，而 $L(1)=\ln1=0$，故完全相同。这里不能把“导数相同”单独当作相等，还需要这个共同初值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The accumulation and the already-defined logarithm have identical derivatives on the connected positive half-line. They differ by a constant, and their shared value at one makes that constant zero. Matching derivatives alone would not establish equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个表示也解释乘法为何变成加法。对 $a,b>0$，分段并令 $t=au$：
$$L(ab)=\int_1^a\frac{dt}{t}+\int_a^{ab}\frac{dt}{t}
=L(a)+\int_1^b\frac{du}{u}=L(a)+L(b).$$
有向积分使这一步同样适用于 $a$ 或 $b$ 小于一的情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Splitting at the first factor and scaling the second integral shows why multiplication becomes addition. Oriented bounds keep the derivation valid when either positive factor is below one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
