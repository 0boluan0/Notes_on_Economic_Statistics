# 闭区间连续且内部可导的函数若端点值相等则至少有一个内部点导数为零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A function continuous on a closed interval and differentiable inside with equal endpoint values has an interior point where its derivative is zero*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 设 $a<b$，$f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 连续、在 $(a,b)$ 可导，且 $f(a)=f(b)$。则至少存在一个 $c\in(a,b)$ 使 $f'(c)=0$。定理保证存在，不保证该点唯一，也不提供寻找它的算法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a<b$. If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, differentiable on $(a,b)$, and $f(a)=f(b)$, then some $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies $f'(c)=0$. The theorem guarantees existence, not uniqueness or a direct algorithm.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 [[极值定理]]，$f$ 在闭区间取得最大、最小。若两值相同，函数恒定，任意内点都可；若不同，因为端点函数值相等，至少一个非平凡极值在内部取得。[[无约束一阶条件]] 的一元情形随后给出零导数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extreme value theorem supplies attained maxima and minima. If they are equal, the function is constant and every interior point works. Otherwise, equal endpoint values force at least one nontrivial extremum into the interior, where the first-order necessary condition gives a zero derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个假设各有角色。去掉闭区间连续性，函数可能跳过水平切线；去掉内部可导性，$|x|$ 在 $[-1,1]$ 端点值相等却只有尖点；去掉端点相等，只剩一般的 [[拉格朗日中值定理]]，目标斜率不再是零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each hypothesis has a role. Without continuity the graph may jump past a horizontal tangent; without interior differentiability, $|x|$ on $[-1,1]$ has equal endpoints but only a corner; without equal endpoint values, the relevant target is the secant slope in the mean value theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 函数在 $[a,b]$ 上严格单调时，为什么不能同时满足 Rolle 定理的端点条件？
>
> **答案：** 严格单调会使 $a<b$ 时 $f(a)\ne f(b)$；除非区间退化，而定理要求 $a<b$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why can a strictly monotone function on $[a,b]$ not also satisfy Rolle's equal-endpoint condition?
>
> **Answer:** Strict monotonicity gives $f(a)\ne f(b)$ when $a<b$. A degenerate interval would avoid that comparison, but the theorem requires $a<b$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
