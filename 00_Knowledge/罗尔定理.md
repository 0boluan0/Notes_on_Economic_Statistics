# Rolle's theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*罗尔定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine a graph that starts and finishes at the same height. If it is continuous throughout the interval and has a finite tangent slope at every interior point, then somewhere inside it has a horizontal tangent. **Rolle's theorem** makes this statement precise. The zero slope is a statement about one moment of change, not a claim that the function stays constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象一条图像从某个高度出发，最后回到同一高度。如果它在整个区间连续，而且每个内点都有有限的切线斜率，那么内部至少有一处切线水平。**罗尔定理**把这件事说清楚。零斜率描述的是某一刻的变化，不是说整个函数保持不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $f(x)=x(2-x)$ on $[0,2]$ starts at $0$, rises to $1$, and returns to $0$. Its [[导数|derivative]], the instantaneous rate of change, is $f'(x)=2-2x$. At $x=1$ this rate is zero: the graph has stopped rising before it begins falling. Rolle's theorem guarantees such a point even when solving the derivative equation explicitly is difficult.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$[0,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=x(2-x)$ 从 $0$ 上升到 $1$，再回到 $0$。它的[[导数]]，也就是瞬时变化率，为 $f'(x)=2-2x$。在 $x=1$ 处，变化率为零：图像停止上升，然后开始下降。即使很难显式求解导数方程，罗尔定理也能保证这类点存在。

> [!important] Equal heights force a zero slope · 端点等高保证内部零斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers, and let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be a real-valued function. Assume that $f$ is continuous on the whole closed interval $[a,b]$, differentiable at every point of the open interval $(a,b)$, and $f(a)=f(b)$. Then there is at least one $c$ with
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $a<b$ 是有限实数，$f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ 是实值函数。假设 $f$ 在整个闭区间 $[a,b]$ 连续，在开区间 $(a,b)$ 的每一点可导，而且 $f(a)=f(b)$。则至少存在一个 $c$ 满足
>
> $$a<c<b,\qquad f'(c)=0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $a,b$ are the endpoints and $c$ is an interior input. The conclusion asserts existence; it does not assert uniqueness or determine $c$ from the endpoint values alone.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $a,b$ 是端点，$c$ 是内部的一个输入。结论保证存在，不保证唯一，也不单靠两个端点值确定 $c$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[一点连续|Continuity at a point]] says that nearby allowed inputs give outputs close to the value there. On $[a,b]$, this includes right-side continuity at $a$ and left-side continuity at $b$. Derivatives are required only at interior points. The derivative function $f'$ need not itself be continuous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[一点连续]]表示：附近的合法输入给出的输出，接近该点的函数值。在 $[a,b]$ 上，这也包括 $a$ 处从右侧连续、$b$ 处从左侧连续。可导性只要求在内部成立，并不要求导数函数 $f'$ 本身连续。

## Why an interior extreme value exists
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么能找到内部的极值点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[极值定理|extreme value theorem]] says that a continuous real function on a nonempty finite closed interval attains a largest and a smallest value. This interval is nonempty because $a<b$. Write $K=f(a)=f(b)$ for the common endpoint height.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[极值定理]]保证：实值连续函数在非空的有限闭区间上取得最大值与最小值。这里 $a<b$，所以区间非空。记 $K=f(a)=f(b)$，即两个端点共同的高度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is constant, every interior point has derivative zero, and we are done. Otherwise, some value is above $K$ or below $K$. In the first case the attained maximum is above $K$, so neither endpoint can be a maximizing point; a maximum occurs inside. In the second case the attained minimum is below $K$ and likewise occurs inside. Thus a nonconstant function has an interior extreme point $c$ to which we can apply the following argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $f$ 是常函数，每个内点的导数都是零，证明已经完成。否则，至少有一个函数值高于或低于 $K$。若有值高于 $K$，取得的最大值也高于 $K$，所以两个端点都不能是最大值点，最大值必在内部取得。若有值低于 $K$，同理最小值在内部取得。因此，非恒定情形也能找到一个内部极值点 $c$，再使用下面的论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a local maximum, small changes $h$ satisfy $f(c+h)-f(c)\le0$. Dividing by $h>0$ gives a nonpositive difference quotient; dividing by $h<0$ gives a nonnegative one. Since $f$ is differentiable at $c$, both sides approach the same finite number $f'(c)$. That number must be both nonpositive and nonnegative, hence zero. A minimum reverses the two inequalities and gives the same conclusion. This is the one-variable [[无约束一阶条件|first-order necessary condition for an interior extremum]], also called Fermat's lemma.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在局部最大值点，足够小的增量 $h$ 满足 $f(c+h)-f(c)\le0$。除以 $h>0$，差商非正；除以 $h<0$，差商非负。由于 $f$ 在 $c$ 可导，两侧差商趋于同一个有限数 $f'(c)$。这个数既非正又非负，只能为零。局部最小值点把两个不等号反过来，结论相同。这就是[[无约束一阶条件]]的一元内点情形，也叫 Fermat 引理。

## What the assumptions prevent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这些条件排除了什么*

> [!warning] A jump or a corner can remove every zero slope · 跳跃或尖角可以消灭全部零斜率点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,1]$, set $f(x)=x$ for $0\le x<1$ but set $f(1)=0$. The endpoint values agree, and every interior derivative equals $1$. There is no zero derivative: continuity fails at the right endpoint. On $[-1,1]$, the function $f(x)=|x|$ is continuous with equal endpoint values, but its derivatives are $-1$ for $x<0$ and $1$ for $x>0$, while the derivative at $0$ does not exist. The corner blocks the conclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上，对 $0\le x<1$ 令 $f(x)=x$，却令 $f(1)=0$。端点值相等，每个内点的导数都为 $1$，没有零导数点；缺失的是右端点连续性。在 $[-1,1]$ 上，$f(x)=|x|$ 连续且端点值相等，但 $x<0$ 时导数为 $-1$，$x>0$ 时为 $1$，在 $0$ 处导数不存在；这个尖角使结论失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These examples show that removing a hypothesis loses the general guarantee. They do not say that every function failing a hypothesis must lack a zero derivative. Equal endpoint values also matter: $f(x)=x$ on $[0,1]$ satisfies the continuity and differentiability requirements but has derivative $1$ everywhere inside. For unequal endpoints, the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] targets the average slope instead of zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些例子说明：删去条件，会失去对所有这类函数的保证；并不是说凡不满足条件的函数都没有零导数点。端点相等也有作用：$[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x$ 连续且内部可导，但内部导数始终为 $1$。端点不等时，[[拉格朗日中值定理]]寻找的是等于平均斜率的导数，而不一定是零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A constant function shows why the point need not be unique: every $c\in(a,b)$ works. Conversely, a strictly increasing or strictly decreasing function cannot satisfy equal endpoint values when $a<b$. That follows directly from strict monotonicity; it does not require its derivative to be strictly positive or strictly negative everywhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常函数说明了为什么结论不保证唯一：每个 $c\in(a,b)$ 都符合要求。反过来，在 $a<b$ 时，严格递增或严格递减的函数不可能具有相等的端点值。这直接来自严格单调的含义，并不要求其导数处处严格为正或严格为负。
