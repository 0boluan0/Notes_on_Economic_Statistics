# The gradient formula for directional derivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方向导数的梯度公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[方向导数|directional derivative]] measures the rate of change when several inputs move together along a fixed straight line. For a differentiable function, the coordinate rates can be combined: multiply each [[偏导数|partial derivative]] by the corresponding component of the movement vector, then add. The gradient formula explains why this calculation works.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[方向导数]]测量的是多个输入沿一条固定直线一起变化时的变化率。对于可微函数，可以把坐标变化率组合起来：每个[[偏导数]]乘以运动向量的对应分量，再求和。梯度公式说明这种算法为什么成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let $f(x,y)=x^2y$ and start at $a=(1,2)$. The coordinate rates there are $f_x(a)=4$ and $f_y(a)=1$. Moving along $(1+t,2-t)$ increases $x$ at rate $1$ and decreases $y$ at rate $1$, so the formula predicts the rate $4(1)+1(-1)=3$. This is a rate at $t=0$, rather than an exact formula for every finite change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，令 $f(x,y)=x^2y$，从 $a=(1,2)$ 出发。此处坐标变化率为 $f_x(a)=4$、$f_y(a)=1$。沿 $(1+t,2-t)$ 运动时，$x$ 以速率 $1$ 增加，$y$ 以速率 $1$ 减少，因此公式预言变化率为 $4(1)+1(-1)=3$。这是 $t=0$ 处的变化率，并不是对任意有限变化都精确成立的增量公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$, where $U$ is an [[开集|open subset]] of $\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge1$, and $a\in U$. An open domain allows sufficiently small positive and negative steps along every fixed vector $v\in\mathbb R^n$. Assume $f$ is [[全微分|differentiable at $a$]]: a single linear approximation describes all sufficiently small input displacements. Collect its partial derivatives into the column vector called the **gradient**,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:U\to\mathbb R$，其中 $U$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 的[[开集]]，$n\ge1$，$a\in U$。开放的定义域允许沿每个固定向量 $v\in\mathbb R^n$ 迈出足够小的正、负步子。假设 $f$ 在 $a$ 处[[全微分|可微]]，即同一个线性近似能描述所有足够小的输入位移。把它的偏导数排成列向量，称为**梯度**：

$$
\nabla f(a)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(a)\\
\vdots\\
\dfrac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}(a)
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

> [!important] One linear approximation gives every directional rate · 同一个线性近似给出所有方向变化率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these assumptions, for every fixed $v$ the finite, two-sided directional derivative exists and satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在这些假设下，对每个固定的 $v$，有限的双侧方向导数都存在，并满足
>
> $$
> D_vf(a)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}{t}
> =Df(a)v=\nabla f(a)^Tv
> =\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(a)v_i.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $Df(a)$ is the total differential. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so $\nabla f(a)^Tv$ is the [[向量点积|dot product]]: multiply matching coordinates and add. The vector $v$ need not have length $1$. At $v=0$ we use the natural constant-path extension, which gives zero and selects no geometric direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $Df(a)$ 是全微分，上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]；因此 $\nabla f(a)^Tv$ 是[[向量点积|点积]]，即对应坐标相乘再相加。$v$ 不必具有长度 $1$。当 $v=0$ 时，使用自然的恒定路径延拓，结果为零，但没有选择几何方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the formula holds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么公式成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiability supplies a [[线性映射|linear map]] $L=Df(a)$ and a remainder $r(h)$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可微性给出一个[[线性映射]] $L=Df(a)$ 和余项 $r(h)$，使

$$
f(a+h)=f(a)+Lh+r(h),\qquad
\lim_{h\to0,\ h\ne0}\frac{|r(h)|}{\|h\|_2}=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] $\|h\|_2$ measures the input displacement. The limit says the error becomes negligible relative to that displacement, regardless of how $h$ approaches zero. Fix $v\ne0$ and put $h=tv$. For $t\ne0$, linearity gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[欧氏范数]] $\|h\|_2$ 衡量输入位移的大小。极限说明：不论 $h$ 怎样趋于零，误差相对于位移都可以忽略。固定 $v\ne0$，令 $h=tv$。对 $t\ne0$，由线性性得到

$$
\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}{t}=Lv+\frac{r(tv)}{t},
\qquad
\left|\frac{r(tv)}{t}\right|
=\frac{|r(tv)|}{\|tv\|_2}\,\|v\|_2\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $D_vf(a)=Lv$. If $v=0$, the difference quotient is zero for every $t\ne0$, and $L0=0$, so the same identity holds without dividing by a zero norm. Finally, let $e_i$ be the coordinate vector whose $i$th entry is $1$ and whose other entries are zero. The direction $e_i$ gives the partial derivative, hence $Le_i=\partial f/\partial x_i(a)$. Since $v=\sum_i v_i e_i$, linearity gives $Lv=\sum_i v_i Le_i=\nabla f(a)^Tv$. This also explains why the matrix of the scalar-valued differential is a row, $Df(a)=\nabla f(a)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $D_vf(a)=Lv$。若 $v=0$，对每个 $t\ne0$，差商都是零，且 $L0=0$；无需除以零范数，同一个等式仍成立。最后，令 $e_i$ 为第 $i$ 个分量是 $1$、其余分量是零的坐标向量。方向 $e_i$ 给出偏导数，所以 $Le_i=\partial f/\partial x_i(a)$。由 $v=\sum_i v_i e_i$ 和线性性，得到 $Lv=\sum_i v_i Le_i=\nabla f(a)^Tv$。这也说明实值函数全微分的矩阵是行向量，即 $Df(a)=\nabla f(a)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the calculation along the line
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to $f(x,y)=x^2y$, $a=(1,2)$ and $v=(1,-1)^T$. Its polynomial expansion supplies a linear approximation, so differentiability holds. The gradient is $(4,1)^T$, giving $D_vf(a)=3$. Direct substitution verifies the rate and displays the higher-order error:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到 $f(x,y)=x^2y$、$a=(1,2)$、$v=(1,-1)^T$。多项式展开提供了线性近似，所以可微性成立。梯度为 $(4,1)^T$，得到 $D_vf(a)=3$。直接代入既核对了变化率，也显示出高阶误差：

$$
f(1+t,2-t)=(1+t)^2(2-t)=2+3t-t^3,
\qquad
\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}t=3-t^2\longrightarrow3.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact change is $3t-t^3$, while $3t$ is its first-order part. The derivative is the limiting coefficient $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确增量为 $3t-t^3$，其中 $3t$ 是一阶部分；导数是极限中的系数 $3$。

## Check the hypothesis before using the shortcut
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiability at the single point $a$ is sufficient; continuous partial derivatives throughout a neighborhood are not required by this theorem. Merely having partial derivatives, or even derivatives along every line, does not establish that hypothesis. The rates can fail to come from one linear map, as explained in [[方向导数不可推可微|directional derivatives without differentiability]]. This is a sufficient theorem, not a claim that every use of the displayed equality proves differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单个点 $a$ 处可微就足够；本定理不要求整个邻域的偏导数连续。但仅有各偏导数，甚至每条直线上的方向导数，都没有建立这个前提。这些变化率可能并不来自同一个线性映射，见[[方向导数不可推可微]]。这是一个充分性定理，不能反过来声称写出所示等式就证明了可微性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity concerns change per unit of the line parameter $t$. Changing the length of $v$ changes that rate. To compare geometric directions per unit distance, first fix a length convention and use [[方向导数尺度|direction-vector normalization]]. The [[多元链式法则|multivariable chain rule]] extends the same idea from a straight line to a differentiable input path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个等式描述的是直线参数 $t$ 每单位变化带来的变化率；改变 $v$ 的长度，也会改变这个数值。若要按每单位距离比较几何方向，应先规定长度口径，并使用[[方向导数尺度|方向向量归一化]]。[[多元链式法则]]把同一个思路从直线路径扩展到可微的输入路径。

> [!question]- Apply the formula · 应用公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f$ is differentiable at $a$, with $\nabla f(a)=(2,-1)^T$. Along $v=(3,4)^T$, the rate is $2(3)-1(4)=2$. The justification is the common linear approximation supplied by differentiability; the vector has not been normalized.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $f$ 在 $a$ 处可微，且 $\nabla f(a)=(2,-1)^T$，沿 $v=(3,4)^T$ 的变化率就是 $2(3)-1(4)=2$。依据是可微性提供的共同线性近似；这里并未把向量归一化。
