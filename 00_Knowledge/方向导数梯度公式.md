# 实值函数在一点可微时，沿向量 v 的方向导数等于梯度与 v 的内积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a differentiable real-valued function, the directional derivative along v equals the inner product of the gradient with v*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 建立在可微性上的恒等式
> 若 $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ 在 $a$ 可微，则对任意向量 $v$，
> $$
> D_vf(a)=Df(a)v=\nabla f(a)^Tv.
> $$
> 左边按 [[方向导数]] 的定义沿直线取极限，右边让同一个 [[全微分]] 作用于 $v$。这个公式是可微性的结论，不是方向导数的定义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable at $a$, then $D_vf(a)=Df(a)v=\nabla f(a)^Tv$ for every vector $v$. The left-hand side is defined by a limit along a line, whereas the right-hand side applies the same [[全微分|total differential]] to $v$. The identity follows from differentiability; it is not the definition of a [[方向导数|directional derivative]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么公式成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the formula holds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可微性给出
$$
f(a+h)=f(a)+Df(a)h+r(h),
\qquad
\frac{r(h)}{\|h\|}\to0.
$$
令 $h=tv$。对 $t\ne0$，
$$
\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}{t}
=Df(a)v+\frac{r(tv)}{t}.
$$
余项满足
$$
\left|\frac{r(tv)}{t}\right|
=\frac{|r(tv)|}{\|tv\|}\,\|v\|\to0,
$$
所以取极限得到 $D_vf(a)=Df(a)v$。采用列梯度约定时，实值函数的导数矩阵是行向量 $Df(a)=\nabla f(a)^T$，从而得到梯度内积公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiability gives the displayed linear approximation. Substituting $h=tv$ leaves $Df(a)v$ plus a remainder. The remainder divided by $t$ vanishes because it is already negligible relative to $\|tv\|$. Thus $D_vf(a)=Df(a)v$. Under the column-gradient convention, the derivative of a scalar-valued function is the row vector $Df(a)=\nabla f(a)^T$, which yields the inner-product formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x,y)=x^2y$，在 $a=(1,2)$ 有
$$
\nabla f(a)=\begin{pmatrix}4\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$
沿 $v=(1,-1)^T$ 的方向导数为
$$
D_vf(a)=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}=3.
$$
这与把直线 $(1+t,2-t)$ 代入 $f$ 后在 $t=0$ 求一元导数的结果相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x,y)=x^2y$ at $a=(1,2)$, the gradient is $(4,1)^T$. Taking its inner product with $v=(1,-1)^T$ gives $D_vf(a)=3$, which agrees with differentiating the line restriction $f(1+t,2-t)$ at $t=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary of use*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若只知道每条直线上的方向导数存在，不能无条件套用梯度内积公式：这些方向导数未必来自同一个线性映射，见 [[方向导数不可推可微]]。方向向量的长度为何还会改变数值、比较几何方向时为何必须统一长度，则由 [[方向导数尺度]] 说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The existence of directional derivatives along individual lines does not by itself justify the gradient formula: those rates may fail to arise from one linear map, as shown in [[方向导数不可推可微|the directional-derivative boundary]]. The separate issue of how vector length changes the reported rate, and why geometric directions require a common normalization, is handled by [[方向导数尺度|directional-derivative scaling]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知 $f$ 在 $a$ 可微、$\nabla f(a)=(2,-1)^T$，沿 $v=(3,4)^T$ 的方向导数是多少？为什么这里可以直接使用梯度内积？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f$ is differentiable at $a$, with $\nabla f(a)=(2,-1)^T$. What is the directional derivative along $v=(3,4)^T$, and why may the gradient formula be used here?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** $D_vf(a)=2\cdot3-1\cdot4=2$。可微性保证所有方向的一阶变化都由同一个全微分给出，所以梯度内积公式适用。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** $D_vf(a)=2\cdot3-1\cdot4=2$. Differentiability guarantees that one total differential generates the first-order change in every direction, so the gradient formula applies.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
