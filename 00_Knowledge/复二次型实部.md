# The real part of a complex quadratic expression
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复二次表达式的实部*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complex matrix can produce a complex number when placed between a conjugate-transposed vector and the vector itself. If we need only the real part of that number, a particular Hermitian matrix contains exactly the information needed. For a first example, take the one-entry matrix $A=[3+2i]$ and column $z=[2]$, where $i^2=-1$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把复矩阵放在一个向量的共轭转置与该向量之间，结果可能是复数。如果只需要这个数的实部，一个特定的 Hermitian 矩阵就保存了全部所需信息。先看单元素矩阵 $A=[3+2i]$ 与列向量 $z=[2]$，其中 $i^2=-1$：

$$z^*Az=12+8i,\qquad \operatorname{Re}(z^*Az)=12.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a scalar $s=a+bi$ with real $a,b$, its conjugate is $\overline s=a-bi$ and its real part is $\operatorname{Re}s=a$. The star $*$ denotes [[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|conjugate transpose]]: exchange rows and columns, then conjugate every entry. Here $A^*=[3-2i]$. Averaging $A$ with $A^*$ gives $H=[3]$, and $z^*Hz=12$, exactly the real part we wanted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数 $a,b$ 组成的标量 $s=a+bi$，共轭为 $\overline s=a-bi$，实部为 $\operatorname{Re}s=a$。星号 $*$ 表示[[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|共轭转置]]：交换行列，再对每个元素取共轭。这里 $A^*=[3-2i]$；把 $A$ 与 $A^*$ 平均，得到 $H=[3]$，而 $z^*Hz=12$，恰好是所需实部。

> [!important] The Hermitian part recovers the real part · Hermitian 部分恢复实部
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer and let $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$ be any square matrix. Define its **Hermitian part** by
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$ 是任意复方阵。定义其 **Hermitian 部分**
>
> $$H=\frac{A+A^*}{2}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Then $H^*=H$, so $H$ is [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]], and every column $z\in\mathbb C^n$, including zero, satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 则 $H^*=H$，所以 $H$ 为 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，且每个列向量 $z\in\mathbb C^n$，包括零向量，都满足
>
> $$\boxed{\operatorname{Re}(z^*Az)=z^*Hz.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The starting matrix $A$ need not be Hermitian or invertible. The statement concerns the conjugate-transpose expression $z^*Az$; the ordinary-transpose expression $z^TAz$ is a different [[二次型|complex quadratic-form convention]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原矩阵 $A$ 不需要 Hermitian，也不需要可逆。结论针对共轭转置表达式 $z^*Az$；使用普通转置的 $z^TAz$ 属于另一种[[二次型|复二次型约定]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Average a scalar with its conjugate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把标量与其共轭取平均*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $s=z^*Az$. The matrix sizes are $(1\times n)(n\times n)(n\times1)$, so $s$ is one scalar. Conjugate transpose reverses the order of a product and satisfies $(z^*)^*=z$. For a scalar it is just conjugation. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $s=z^*Az$。矩阵尺寸为 $(1\times n)(n\times n)(n\times1)$，所以 $s$ 是一个标量。乘积取共轭转置时反转因子顺序，并有 $(z^*)^*=z$；对标量而言，这个操作就是取共轭。因此

$$\overline s=(z^*Az)^*=z^*A^*z,$$
$$\operatorname{Re}s=\frac{s+\overline s}{2}
=\frac{z^*Az+z^*A^*z}{2}
=z^*\left(\frac{A+A^*}{2}\right)z=z^*Hz.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also, $H^*=(A^*+A)/2=H$. This proves both the identity and the Hermitian property directly, without a spectral decomposition or a positivity assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另外，$H^*=(A^*+A)/2=H$。这同时直接证明恒等式与 Hermitian 性质，不需要谱分解，也不需要正性条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What happens to the imaginary contribution?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*虚部的贡献去了哪里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complementary part is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

剩下的部分是

$$K=\frac{A-A^*}{2},\qquad A=H+K,\qquad K^*=-K.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition $K^*=-K$ is called skew-Hermitian. For $r=z^*Kz$, taking conjugates gives $\overline r=z^*K^*z=-r$. If $r=a+bi$, this equality forces $a=0$, so $r$ is purely imaginary, possibly zero. Meanwhile $z^*Hz$ is real. Thus $H$ supplies the real contribution and $K$ supplies the purely imaginary contribution. “Taking the real part” discards the latter; it does not say that the latter was zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件 $K^*=-K$ 称为斜 Hermitian。对 $r=z^*Kz$ 取共轭，得到 $\overline r=z^*K^*z=-r$。若 $r=a+bi$，此式迫使 $a=0$，所以 $r$ 为纯虚数，也可能为零；另一方面，$z^*Hz$ 为实数。因此 $H$ 提供实部贡献，$K$ 提供纯虚部贡献。“取实部”丢弃了后者，并不是说后者原本为零。

> [!warning] The entire value need not be preserved · 整个值不一定保持
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=[i]$ and $z=[1]$, we have $H=0$, but $z^*Az=i$ and $z^*Hz=0$. Replacing $z^*Az$ itself by $z^*Hz$ would be wrong. If $A^*=-A$, the real part vanishes for every $z$, while imaginary values may remain. The separate [[Hermitian二次型实值判据|real-valuedness criterion]] states when the entire value is real for every complex input.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=[i]$、$z=[1]$，则 $H=0$，但 $z^*Az=i$、$z^*Hz=0$。把整个 $z^*Az$ 替换成 $z^*Hz$ 就错了。若 $A^*=-A$，实部对所有 $z$ 都为零，但仍可能有虚部。对所有复输入都取完整实值的条件，另见 [[Hermitian二次型实值判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Averaging also exchanges positions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*平均时还要交换位置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In dimension greater than one, the Hermitian part is generally not the matrix obtained by taking each entry's real part. If $a_{jk}$ denotes row $j$, column $k$ of $A$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维度大于一时，Hermitian 部分一般不等于“逐元素取实部”所得的矩阵。若 $a_{jk}$ 表示 $A$ 第 $j$ 行、第 $k$ 列的元素，则

$$h_{jk}=\frac{a_{jk}+\overline{a_{kj}}}{2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Notice the reversed indices in the second term. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

注意第二项的行列下标交换了。例如，

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1+2i\\3&4-i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
H=\begin{pmatrix}2&2+i\\2-i&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
z=\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}.
$$
$$Az=\begin{pmatrix}i\\4+4i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Hz=\begin{pmatrix}1+2i\\2+3i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
z^*Az=4-3i,\qquad z^*Hz=4.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The off-diagonal entries of $H$ can still be nonreal; they are conjugate partners. If we instead take entrywise real parts, we get $E=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$ and $z^*Ez=6-2i$, whose real part is $6$, not $4$. The exchange of positions in $A^*$ is essential.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$H$ 的非对角元仍可能不是实数，但它们互为共轭。如果改为逐元素取实部，得到 $E=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$，于是 $z^*Ez=6-2i$，实部为 $6$，不是 $4$。所以 $A^*$ 中交换行列位置这一步不可省略。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When both $A$ and the input are real, $A^*=A^T$ and the entire scalar is real. The identity reduces to [[二次型对称化|real symmetrization]], $x^TAx=x^T((A+A^T)/2)x$. For complex inputs, keep the conjugations and the real-part operator stated in the theorem. Reality also does not imply positivity: the Hermitian part itself can give positive, zero, or negative quadratic values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $A$ 与输入都为实数时，$A^*=A^T$，整个标量本来就是实数，恒等式便退化为[[二次型对称化]]：$x^TAx=x^T((A+A^T)/2)x$。复输入下则要保留定理中的共轭与取实部操作。取实值也不等于取正值：Hermitian 部分本身可以给出正、零或负的二次型值。
