# 复矩阵二次表达式的实部只由其 Hermitian 部分决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The real part of a complex quadratic expression depends only on the Hermitian part of its matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心恒等式
> 对 $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$，令
> $$
> H=\frac{A+A^*}{2}.
> $$
> 则对每个 $x\in\mathbb C^n$，
> $$
> \operatorname{Re}(x^*Ax)=x^*Hx.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $H=(A+A^*)/2$, every complex vector satisfies $\operatorname{Re}(x^*Ax)=x^*Hx$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $A$ 写成
$$
A=H+K,
\qquad
K=\frac{A-A^*}{2},
$$
其中 $H^*=H$、$K^*=-K$。标量 $x^*Hx$ 为实数，而 $x^*Kx$ 等于自身共轭的相反数，所以是纯虚数。实部因此只留下 $H$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Decompose $A=H+K$ with $H^*=H$ and $K^*=-K$. The scalar $x^*Hx$ is real, while $x^*Kx$ is purely imaginary because its conjugate equals its negative. Taking the real part therefore removes the skew-Hermitian contribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能把这条式子误写成 $x^*Ax=x^*Hx$。取一维例子 $A=[i]$、$x=[1]$，则 $H=0$，但 $x^*Ax=i$。至于 $x^*Ax$ 何时对每个复向量都为实数，完整刻画见 [[Hermitian二次型实值判据]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The full expressions need not be equal: for $A=[i]$ and $x=[1]$, the Hermitian part is zero but $x^*Ax=i$. [[Hermitian二次型实值判据|The Hermitian real-valuedness criterion]] characterises exactly when $x^*Ax$ is real for every complex vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $A^*=-A$，为什么 $\operatorname{Re}(x^*Ax)=0$ 对所有 $x$ 成立？
>
> **答案：** 此时 Hermitian 部分 $(A+A^*)/2$ 为零；等价地，$x^*Ax$ 总是纯虚数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A^*=-A$, why does $\operatorname{Re}(x^*Ax)=0$ hold for every $x$?
>
> **Answer:** The Hermitian part $(A+A^*)/2$ is zero; equivalently, $x^*Ax$ is always purely imaginary.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
