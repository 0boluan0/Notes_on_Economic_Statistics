# Adjugate matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*伴随矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **adjugate** is a new square matrix built from the [[余子式与代数余子式|cofactors]] of a square matrix. First compute one cofactor for each entry; then turn the rows of that cofactor matrix into columns. This last [[矩阵转置|transpose]] matters: the adjugate is generally different from the cofactor matrix before transposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**伴随矩阵**由一个方阵的[[余子式与代数余子式|代数余子式]]组成：先为每个元素计算代数余子式，再把所得矩阵的行变成列。最后这一步[[矩阵转置|转置]]很重要；伴随矩阵一般不同于尚未转置的代数余子式矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, start with $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$. Deleting one row and one column leaves a single entry. The checkerboard multipliers give $C_{11}=4$, $C_{12}=-3$, $C_{21}=-2$, and $C_{22}=1$. Put these values in their original positions, then transpose:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，取 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$。删去一行一列后只剩一个元素，再乘棋盘格指定的乘数，得到 $C_{11}=4$、$C_{12}=-3$、$C_{21}=-2$、$C_{22}=1$。先把它们放在各自原来的位置，再转置：

$$
C(A)=\begin{pmatrix}4&-3\\-2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{adj}(A)=C(A)^T
=\begin{pmatrix}4&-2\\-3&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cofactor $C_{12}=-3$ moves from row $1$, column $2$, to row $2$, column $1$. The notation $C(A)$ names the whole cofactor matrix; $C_{ij}$ names one scalar entry. Neither denotes an extra determinant operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代数余子式 $C_{12}=-3$ 从第 $1$ 行、第 $2$ 列移到第 $2$ 行、第 $1$ 列。$C(A)$ 表示整张代数余子式矩阵，$C_{ij}$ 表示其中一个标量元素；这两个记号都不要求再取一次行列式。

> [!important] The indices exchange places · 两个指标交换位置
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=(a_{ij})\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, where $n\ge1$ is an integer and $\mathbb F$ is a field, such as the real or complex numbers. Delete row $i$ and column $j$ to obtain $A_{\widehat i,\widehat j}$. With $\det$ denoting the [[行列式|determinant]], define
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A=(a_{ij})\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，其中整数 $n\ge1$，$\mathbb F$ 是一个域，例如实数域或复数域。删去第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列得到 $A_{\widehat i,\widehat j}$；用 $\det$ 表示[[行列式]]，则
>
> $$C_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}\det A_{\widehat i,\widehat j},\qquad
> \operatorname{adj}(A)=C(A)^T,\qquad
> (\operatorname{adj}(A))_{ij}=C_{ji}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus the entry at $(i,j)$ in the adjugate deletes row $j$ and column $i$ of the original matrix. The dimensions remain $n\times n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此，伴随矩阵的 $(i,j)$ 元素要从原矩阵删去第 $j$ 行、第 $i$ 列来计算；所得矩阵仍为 $n\times n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general $2\times2$ matrix, the same construction gives a useful small formula:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般的 $2\times2$ 矩阵，同样的步骤给出常用小公式：

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C(A)=\begin{pmatrix}d&-c\\-b&a\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{adj}(A)=\begin{pmatrix}d&-b\\-c&a\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This construction explains the pattern of swapping the diagonal entries and negating the off-diagonal entries in dimension two. In larger dimensions, each cofactor is itself a determinant, so this simple entry-swapping recipe no longer suffices. The reason for placing cofactors in transposed positions is developed in the [[伴随矩阵恒等式|adjugate identity]]: multiplying by the original matrix pairs entries with the appropriate cofactor expansions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解释了二阶情形“交换两个对角元、对两个非对角元取负”的规律。更高阶时，每个代数余子式本身需要计算一个行列式，不能只照搬这种元素交换规则。为什么要把代数余子式放到转置位置，由[[伴随矩阵恒等式]]进一步说明：与原矩阵相乘时，这些位置恰好形成相应的余子式展开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No division occurs when constructing the adjugate. It therefore exists even when the matrix is **singular**, meaning it has no [[逆矩阵|inverse]]. For instance,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造伴随矩阵没有除法，因此即使原矩阵是**奇异矩阵**，也就是没有[[逆矩阵]]，伴随矩阵仍然存在。例如，

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\det B=0,\qquad
\operatorname{adj}(B)=\begin{pmatrix}4&-2\\-2&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the adjugate is nonzero, but $B$ is not invertible. Only when the determinant is nonzero does the separate [[伴随矩阵求逆|adjugate inverse formula]] allow division by it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里伴随矩阵不是零矩阵，$B$ 却不可逆。只有行列式非零时，另一条[[伴随矩阵求逆|伴随求逆公式]]才允许除以行列式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a $1\times1$ matrix $A=[a]$, deleting its only row and column leaves the empty $0\times0$ matrix. Its determinant is defined as $1$, so $C_{11}=1$ and $\operatorname{adj}([a])=[1]$. This includes $a=0$: the construction gives $[1]$ without dividing by $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $1\times1$ 矩阵 $A=[a]$，删去唯一的一行一列，剩下空的 $0\times0$ 矩阵。其行列式约定为 $1$，所以 $C_{11}=1$，$\operatorname{adj}([a])=[1]$。$a=0$ 时也成立：构造直接给出 $[1]$，并不需要除以 $a$。

> [!warning] Adjugate and Hermitian adjoint · 代数伴随与共轭转置
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Classical adjoint** is another name for the adjugate. For a complex matrix acting with the [[内积|standard inner product]], however, its **adjoint** is the conjugate transpose $A^*=\overline A^{\,T}$: transpose and conjugate every entry. The adjugate uses cofactors and ordinary transpose, with no conjugation. For example, with $i^2=-1$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **Classical adjoint** 是伴随矩阵的另一名称；但对采用[[内积|标准内积]]的复矩阵作用，**adjoint** 表示共轭转置 $A^*=\overline A^{\,T}$，即转置后逐个取复共轭。伴随矩阵使用代数余子式和普通转置，不取共轭。例如，其中 $i^2=-1$，
>
> $$D=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> \operatorname{adj}(D)=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> D^*=\begin{pmatrix}-i&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> More generally, the matrix of an inner-product adjoint is the conjugate transpose when the input and output bases are orthonormal. Arbitrary inner products or nonorthonormal coordinates need that distinction. [[矩阵转置|Ordinary and conjugate transpose]] supplies the notation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 更一般地，在输入与输出采用标准正交基时，内积伴随算子的表示矩阵由共轭转置公式给出；一般内积或非标准正交坐标不能直接套用该公式。相关记号可参看[[矩阵转置|普通转置与共轭转置]]。
