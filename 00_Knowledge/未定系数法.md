# 未定系数法按强迫项的函数族猜测一个特解并由代入确定系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The method of undetermined coefficients guesses a particular solution from the forcing family and determines its coefficients by substitution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 对线性常系数非齐次方程，若强迫项属于在时间平移下封闭的简单函数族，可以猜一个同族特解，代回方程后按同类项求未知系数。常见对应是：常数配常数，多项式配同次数完整多项式，$q_d(t)\lambda^t$ 配同形的多项式乘指数，正弦输入配同频率的正弦与余弦组合。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a linear constant-coefficient equation, a forcing family preserved by time shifts suggests a trial solution from the same family. Substitution and coefficient matching then determine one particular solution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

步骤是：先把常数项也并入总强迫 $g_t$；识别 $g_t$ 的最小封闭函数族；写出含全部必要项的试探式；检查它是否与齐次解重叠；最后代入并比较系数。多项式输入必须保留从最高次到常数的所有项，因为平移 $(t-1)^d$ 会产生更低次幂。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combine all forcing terms, identify the smallest shift-closed family, include every required basis term, check for overlap with the homogeneous solution, and then match coefficients. A polynomial trial must include lower powers because shifting a polynomial generates them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个方法不是任意输入的万能算法。若 $g_t$ 是一条不规则实测路径、系数随时间变化，或方程非线性，简单的有限维试探族通常不封闭；此时应改用[[递推迭代]]、[[滞后多项式求逆]]、状态空间法或数值求解。即使试探成功，它也只给一个特解，仍需加齐次解并施加初值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The method is not universal. Irregular observed inputs, time-varying coefficients, and nonlinear recurrences generally do not remain in a small trial family. Iteration, operator inversion, state-space methods, or numerical solution may then be more appropriate. A successful trial still supplies only one particular solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 常数强迫
> 对 $y_t=0.5y_{t-1}+3$，试 $y_t^p=C$，代入得 $C=0.5C+3$，所以 $C=6$。完整解还要加 $A(0.5)^t$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A constant trial for $y_t=0.5y_{t-1}+3$ gives $C=6$; the full solution still includes the homogeneous term $A(0.5)^t$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 强迫项为 $3t^2$ 时，为什么不能只试 $Ct^2$？
>
> **答案：** 因为滞后会把 $(t-1)^2$ 展开成二次、一次和常数项；试探式通常要写成 $C_2t^2+C_1t+C_0$。
