# Studentized residuals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*学生化残差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A residual tells us how far an observed outcome lies from its fitted value. **Studentizing** asks a further question: how large is that gap compared with an estimated scale appropriate to this observation? A gap of two units need not be equally unusual at every predictor position, because least squares fits some positions more closely than others.

Two versions differ in where they estimate the common error scale. **Internal studentization** uses the complete sample. **External studentization** estimates that scale after leaving the observation out. Both are different from the unscaled prediction error obtained by deleting a case. Keeping these quantities separate is essential when reading software output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差告诉我们，观测结果距离拟合值有多远。**学生化**再问一步：相对于适合这条观测的估计尺度，这个缺口有多大？同样是两个单位的偏差，在不同解释变量位置未必同样特殊，因为最小二乘会对某些位置拟合得更紧。

两种做法的区别，是用什么数据估计共同的误差尺度。**内部学生化**使用全部样本；**外部学生化**删去这条观测后再估计尺度。两者又都不同于未经缩放的删点预测误差。读软件输出时，必须分清这几个量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why each observation needs its own scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么每条观测需要自己的尺度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use unweighted [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares (OLS)]] on a real $n\times p$ design matrix $X$: $n$ is the number of observations and $p$ counts all fitted coefficients, including an intercept if present. Assume [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]], so the coefficients are unique, and $n>p$. For observation $i$, the [[回归残差|residual]] is $e_i=y_i-\hat y_i$. The [[Hat杠杆值|hat value]] $h_{ii}$ measures how much changing its own outcome moves its own fitted value, with the design fixed.

To interpret a residual variance, now assume a correct linear mean $y=X\beta+u$, $E[u\mid X]=0$, and $\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$, with $0<\sigma^2<\infty$. Here $u$ is the unobserved error vector and $I_n$ is the identity matrix. The [[条件期望|conditional mean]] $E[u\mid X]$ averages errors at the given design; the [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] records their variances and pairwise covariances. The displayed covariance assumption means equal variances and zero pairwise error covariances. It does not yet require normal errors. With $H=X(X'X)^{-1}X'$ and $e=(I_n-H)u$, [[满列秩投影公式|projection algebra]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用不加权的[[普通最小二乘|OLS]] 拟合实数 $n\times p$ 设计矩阵 $X$：$n$ 是观测数，$p$ 统计全部拟合系数，若含截距也计入。假设设计[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]、系数唯一，且 $n>p$。第 $i$ 条观测的[[回归残差|残差]]为 $e_i=y_i-\hat y_i$；[[Hat杠杆值|Hat 值]] $h_{ii}$ 表示保持设计不变时，该点自己的结果会多大程度推动自己的拟合值。

要解释残差方差，再假设均值模型正确：$y=X\beta+u$，$E[u\mid X]=0$，$\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$，其中 $0<\sigma^2<\infty$。$u$ 是不可直接观测的误差向量，$I_n$ 是单位矩阵。[[条件期望|条件均值]] $E[u\mid X]$ 表示给定设计后的误差平均；[[协方差矩阵]]记录各误差的方差与两两协方差。上述条件表示方差相等、两两协方差为零，此处尚不要求正态。令 $H=X(X'X)^{-1}X'$，则 $e=(I_n-H)u$，由[[满列秩投影公式|投影代数]]得到

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(e\mid X)=\sigma^2(I_n-H),\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(e_i\mid X)=\sigma^2(1-h_{ii}).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The prime means transpose. The second formula follows from the diagonal of the first; symmetry and $(I_n-H)^2=I_n-H$ explain why only one projection remains. Greater leverage leaves a smaller residual variance under this model. It does **not** mean the observation's underlying error variance is smaller: that is still $\sigma^2$.

Estimate the error variance by $s^2=\sum_j e_j^2/(n-p)$, the residual sum of squares divided by the remaining degrees of freedom. Its square root $s$ is an error-scale estimate. When $s>0$ and $h_{ii}<1$, the internally studentized residual is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撇号表示转置；第二式是第一式的第 $i$ 个对角元。由于对称性和 $(I_n-H)^2=I_n-H$，方差式中只剩一次投影。在这个模型下，杠杆越大，留下的残差方差越小；这**不表示**该观测背后真正的误差方差更小，后者仍是 $\sigma^2$。

用 $s^2=\sum_j e_j^2/(n-p)$ 估计误差方差，即残差平方和除以剩余自由度；$s$ 是它的平方根，估计误差尺度。当 $s>0$、$h_{ii}<1$ 时，内部学生化残差为

$$r_i=\frac{e_i}{s\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its denominator estimates the residual's own [[标准差|standard deviation]], rather than using $s$ alone. The result has no measurement unit. Names vary: R calls this default `rstandard`, while some textbooks call it an internally studentized residual. Check the formula, not only the label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母估计的是这条残差自身的[[标准差]]，不是只除以 $s$，因此结果没有测量单位。名称因软件而异：R 的默认 `rstandard` 对应这里的量，有些教材称它为内部学生化残差。应核对公式，不能只看标签。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Delete the case, then estimate the scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*删去该点，再估计尺度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Delete row $i$, keep the same predictor terms, and refit using the remaining $n-1$ observations. Write $\hat y_{i(i)}$ for this new fit's prediction at the omitted point's original predictor values. The unstandardized **deleted residual**, also called a PRESS prediction error, is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

删去第 $i$ 行，保留同样的解释变量项，用其余 $n-1$ 条观测重新拟合。把新拟合在被删点原来解释变量位置上的预测记为 $\hat y_{i(i)}$。未经标准化的**删除残差**，也叫 PRESS 预测误差，为

$$d_i=y_i-\hat y_{i(i)}=\frac{e_i}{1-h_{ii}}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This deletion identity is sample algebra, requiring the original and deleted designs to have full column rank; for full-rank $X$, the deletion condition is equivalent to $h_{ii}<1$. It needs no distributional assumption. The value $d_i$ still has the outcome's units.

For an external scale estimate, require the additional residual degree of freedom $n-p-1>0$. Let $e_{j(i)}$ be the residual for a retained observation $j$ after refitting, and define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个删点恒等式属于样本代数，要求原设计和删点后设计均满列秩；对满列秩 $X$，删点条件等价于 $h_{ii}<1$，无需分布假设。$d_i$ 仍有结果变量的单位。

要估计外部尺度，还需删点后剩余自由度 $n-p-1>0$。用 $e_{j(i)}$ 表示重新拟合后保留观测 $j$ 的残差，并定义

$$
s_{(i)}^2=\frac{\sum_{j\ne i}e_{j(i)}^2}{n-p-1}
=\frac{(n-p)s^2-e_i^2/(1-h_{ii})}{n-p-1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $s_{(i)}>0$, the externally studentized residual is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $s_{(i)}>0$ 时，外部学生化残差为

$$
t_i=\frac{e_i}{s_{(i)}\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}}
=\frac{d_i}{s_{(i)}/\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second expression explains the scaling: under the covariance model above, $d_i$ has variance $\sigma^2/(1-h_{ii})$, so its estimated standard deviation is $s_{(i)}/\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}$. Dividing $d_i$ by $s_{(i)}\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}$ would be the wrong normalization. R's `rstudent` uses the external scale, while `rstandard(type="predictive")` returns the unscaled PRESS residual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二种写法解释了缩放：在上述协方差模型下，$d_i$ 的方差为 $\sigma^2/(1-h_{ii})$，因此其估计标准差是 $s_{(i)}/\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}$。若把 $d_i$ 除以 $s_{(i)}\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}$，便用了错误的标准化。R 的 `rstudent` 使用外部尺度，而 `rstandard(type="predictive")` 返回未缩放的 PRESS 残差。

> [!example] One gap, three different numbers / 同一个缺口，三个不同的数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit only an intercept to outcomes $(0,1,2,7)$. The fit is their mean $2.5$, so the last residual is $e_4=4.5$. Here $p=1$, $h_{44}=1/4$, and $s^2=29/3$. Thus $r_4=9/\sqrt{29}\approx1.67$.
>
> Delete the last outcome. The retained mean is $1$, the remaining squared residuals sum to $2$, and $s_{(4)}^2=2/(3-1)=1$. The deleted prediction error is $d_4=7-1=6$, whereas $t_4=3\sqrt3\approx5.20$. The internal scale was enlarged by the same observation whose departure we were measuring.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对结果 $(0,1,2,7)$ 只拟合截距，拟合值是均值 $2.5$，最后一条残差为 $e_4=4.5$。此时 $p=1$、$h_{44}=1/4$、$s^2=29/3$，所以 $r_4=9/\sqrt{29}\approx1.67$。
>
> 删去最后一条，剩余均值为 $1$，残差平方和为 $2$，因此 $s_{(4)}^2=2/(3-1)=1$。删除预测误差是 $d_4=7-1=6$，外部学生化残差却是 $t_4=3\sqrt3\approx5.20$。内部尺度曾被我们正在检查的同一观测抬高。

## Why external scaling only reduces masking
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么外部学生化只是减轻自我掩蔽

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An unusual outcome can pull the fit toward itself and inflate the scale estimated from the complete sample. External scaling removes that case from the scale estimate, helping expose this form of self-masking. It cannot guarantee detection: several unusual observations may support one another, so deleting only one leaves the others in the fit. Nor does a large $|t_i|$ automatically imply large [[删点影响定义|influence on a chosen coefficient or prediction]]. It measures a scaled outcome departure; influence asks how a specified fitted result changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特殊结果既可能把拟合拉向自身，也可能抬高全样本估计的尺度。外部学生化在估计尺度时排除该点，有助于揭示这种自我掩蔽，但不能保证发现所有问题：多个特殊观测可能相互支撑，删去一个后，其余观测仍留在拟合中。大的 $|t_i|$ 也不自动表示对某个系数或预测有很大[[删点影响定义|影响]]；它衡量标准化后的结果偏离，影响则问指定拟合结果改变多少。

## Conditions for a reference distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed full-rank design and a correct mean, independent [[正态分布|normal]] $N(0,\sigma^2)$ errors with $\sigma^2>0$ give each **prespecified** external residual the exact [[Student t分布|Student t law]] with $n-p-1$ degrees of freedom, under the rank and degree conditions above. The deleted prediction error is normal and independent of the deleted fit's residual variance estimate; the latter is a scaled [[卡方分布|chi-square variable]]. This produces the normal-over-independent-scale ratio. Internal studentization reuses the observation in its scale and does not give that same t law. Neither the word “studentized” nor division by an estimated scale guarantees variance one.

The largest residual selected after inspecting all rows does not have the single prespecified row's reference law. Treat numerical cutoffs as [[影响诊断阈值|screening rules with distribution and multiple-comparison conditions]], then investigate the data and model.

At $h_{ii}=1$, the ordinary residual is forced to zero and these divisions fail; deleting that row loses full rank. Zero residual degrees of freedom or a zero estimated scale also prevent the corresponding finite ratio. Under heteroskedasticity or correlated errors, the ordinary deletion identities remain valid for unweighted OLS when both designs retain full rank, but the displayed variance interpretation and t calibration do not follow. Weighted fits and generalized linear models require their own matched definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定且满列秩的设计、正确均值、独立[[正态分布|正态]] $N(0,\sigma^2)$ 误差且 $\sigma^2>0$，再加上述秩和自由度条件，会使每个**事先指定**观测的外部学生化残差精确服从自由度 $n-p-1$ 的 [[Student t分布|Student t 分布]]。删除预测误差为正态，且与删点拟合的残差方差估计独立；后者是缩放的[[卡方分布|卡方变量]]，从而形成“正态除以独立尺度”的比值。内部学生化把该点重复用于尺度估计，并不服从同一个 t 律。“学生化”这个名称或除以估计尺度，都不保证方差为一。

检查所有行后挑出的最大残差，不服从单个事先指定观测的参考律。数值阈值应作为带有分布、多重比较条件的[[影响诊断阈值|筛查规则]]，随后调查数据和模型。

$h_{ii}=1$ 时，普通残差被强制为零，上述除法失效，删去该行也会失去满列秩。剩余自由度为零或估计尺度为零，同样不能得到相应的有限比值。异方差、误差相关时，当完整与删点设计均满列秩时，不加权 OLS 的普通删点恒等式仍成立，但上述方差解释和 t 校准不能直接沿用；加权拟合、广义线性模型需要各自匹配的定义。
