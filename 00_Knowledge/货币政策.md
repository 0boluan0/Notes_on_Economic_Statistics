# 货币政策是中央银行运用货币金融工具影响金融条件以实现其政策目标的行动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monetary policy uses central-bank monetary and financial instruments to influence financial conditions in pursuit of policy objectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币政策由中央银行或相应货币当局实施，目标和工具依制度而定。操作利率、准备金安排、资产买卖和沟通等可影响市场利率、信贷、资产价格与预期；政策目标、具体工具和传导结果是不同层次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monetary policy is implemented by a central bank or monetary authority under a jurisdiction-specific mandate. Operating rates, reserve arrangements, asset transactions, and communication can affect rates, credit, asset prices, and expectations. Objectives, instruments, and outcomes are distinct levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如一次政策利率下调可能降低部分浮动借款成本，但存量固定利率合同不一定立即重定价，贷款参考报价也不等于央行直接设定的操作利率。宽松意图不保证贷款需求、银行风险承担或所有股价立即上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A policy-rate cut may lower some floating borrowing costs, while existing fixed-rate contracts need not reprice immediately. A quoted lending reference rate is not identical to the central bank's operating rate. Easing intent does not guarantee more loan demand, bank risk-taking, or immediate gains in every stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行信贷并非把准备金按固定倍数机械放大；对记账和约束，复用[[货币乘数的因果边界]]。在证券研究中进一步区分政策行动和[[意外信息定价|相对原预期的消息]]，再分析[[政策传导的估值渠道]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bank lending is not a mechanical fixed multiple of reserves; reuse [[货币乘数的因果边界|the causal limits of the money multiplier]]. For securities, distinguish an action from [[意外信息定价|news relative to expectations]], then trace [[政策传导的估值渠道|valuation channels]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
