# Uniform continuity on compact domains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续函数在紧致集上一致连续*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, the estimate $|x^2-y^2|\le2|x-y|$ gives one input tolerance that works everywhere in the interval. The compact-domain theorem guarantees such a tolerance for every continuous function on $[0,1]$, even when no simple estimate is available. **Compactness of the domain turns point-by-point continuity into uniform continuity.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，估计 $|x^2-y^2|\le2|x-y|$ 给出了适用于整个区间的输入门槛。紧致定义域定理保证：$[0,1]$ 上的每个连续函数，即使没有方便的估计式，也存在某个共同门槛。**定义域的紧致性能够把逐点连续提升为一致连续。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall the distinction: [[连续性|continuity]] permits the input tolerance to depend on the point, while [[一致连续|uniform continuity]] requires one tolerance for every pair of inputs once the output accuracy is specified. A [[紧致性|compact set]] is one for which every open cover has a finite subcover. In metric spaces, compactness also has an equivalent sequence test used below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先回忆区别：[[连续性]]允许输入门槛依赖所在点；[[一致连续]]要求给定输出精度后，一个门槛同时适用于每一对输入。[[紧致性|紧致集]]指每个开覆盖都能抽出有限子覆盖。在度量空间中，紧致性还有一个等价的序列判据，下面的证明会用到它。

> [!important] The compact-domain theorem · 紧致定义域定理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $(K,d_K)$ be a compact [[度量空间|metric space]], let $(Y,d_Y)$ be any metric space, and let $f:K\to Y$ be continuous. Then $f$ is uniformly continuous:
> $$\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\ \forall x,y\in K,\quad
> d_K(x,y)<\delta\ \Longrightarrow\
> d_Y(f(x),f(y))<\varepsilon.$$
> The metric $d_K$ measures input distance, and $d_Y$ measures output distance. If $K$ is a compact subset of a larger metric space, use its inherited distance and require continuity relative to $K$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $(K,d_K)$ 是紧致[[度量空间]]，$(Y,d_Y)$ 是任意度量空间，映射 $f:K\to Y$ 连续，则 $f$ 一致连续，即上式成立。其中 $d_K$ 测量输入距离，$d_Y$ 测量输出距离。若 $K$ 是较大度量空间中的紧致子集，使用其继承的距离，并按 $K$ 内的输入检查连续性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real-valued function on $[a,b]$ with finite real endpoints $a<b$, [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|Heine–Borel]] supplies compactness, so continuity on that interval suffices. At $a$ and $b$, only approaches from inside the interval are relevant. The theorem needs no derivative. Empty and one-point domains also satisfy the conclusion, and the codomain $Y$ need not be compact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有限实端点 $a<b$ 的区间 $[a,b]$ 上的实值函数，[[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|Heine–Borel 定理]]保证区间紧致，因此在该区间上连续就足够。端点 $a,b$ 只检查从区间内趋近的输入；定理不要求导数存在。空定义域与单点定义域也满足结论，陪域 $Y$ 则不必紧致。

## Why bad pairs cannot keep escaping
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么不断出现的坏点对无法逃开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the conclusion fails. Negating uniform continuity gives **one fixed output gap** $\varepsilon_0>0$ that no input threshold can control. For each positive integer $n$, choose a pair $x_n,y_n\in K$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设结论不成立。否定一致连续，意味着存在**一个固定的输出差距** $\varepsilon_0>0$，无论输入门槛多小都控制不了。于是对每个正整数 $n$，都能选出 $x_n,y_n\in K$，使

$$
d_K(x_n,y_n)<\frac1n,\qquad
d_Y(f(x_n),f(y_n))\ge\varepsilon_0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By [[紧致性的序列判别|the sequence criterion for metric compactness]], the sequence $(x_n)$ has a subsequence $(x_{n_j})$ converging to a point $c$ still in $K$. Here $n_1<n_2<\cdots$ are the retained indices. The paired points also converge to $c$, because the triangle inequality gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据[[紧致性的序列判别|度量紧致性的序列判据]]，$(x_n)$ 有一个收敛子列 $(x_{n_j})$，且极限 $c$ 仍属于 $K$；$n_1<n_2<\cdots$ 是保留的索引。配对的另一列也趋于 $c$，因为三角不等式给出

$$
d_K(y_{n_j},c)
\le d_K(y_{n_j},x_{n_j})+d_K(x_{n_j},c)
<\frac1{n_j}+d_K(x_{n_j},c)\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity at $c$ now makes both output sequences converge to $f(c)$; this is [[连续性的等价判别|the sequence test for continuity]]. Applying the output-space triangle inequality,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $c$ 处连续，两列输出都趋于 $f(c)$，这里使用[[连续性的等价判别|连续性的序列判据]]。再在输出空间使用三角不等式，

$$
d_Y(f(x_{n_j}),f(y_{n_j}))
\le d_Y(f(x_{n_j}),f(c))+d_Y(f(c),f(y_{n_j}))
\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This contradicts the lower bound $\varepsilon_0$ for every selected pair. Therefore a common input tolerance exists. The decisive step was finding a limit **inside the domain**, where continuity could be applied. Simply collecting infinitely many positive local tolerances would not suffice: they might approach zero and have no common positive lower bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与每个所选点对的输出距离都至少为 $\varepsilon_0$ 矛盾，所以共同输入门槛存在。关键是找到了一个**仍在定义域内**的极限，因而可以使用连续性；仅仅收集无穷多个正的局部门槛并不够：它们可能趋于零，因而没有共同的正下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which hypotheses do the work?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪些条件不能随意省略*

> [!example] A missing endpoint can matter · 缺失的端点可能很重要
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $f(x)=1/x$ is continuous on the bounded interval $(0,1)$, but that domain is not compact. For positive integers $n$, put $x_n=1/(n+1)$ and $y_n=1/(n+2)$. Then
> $$|x_n-y_n|=\frac1{(n+1)(n+2)}\longrightarrow0,\qquad
> |f(x_n)-f(y_n)|=1.$$
> Uniform continuity fails, for example at output tolerance $1/2$. Both input sequences approach the missing point $0$, so continuity at a point of the domain cannot finish the preceding proof.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $f(x)=1/x$ 在有界区间 $(0,1)$ 上连续，但该定义域不紧致。对正整数 $n$，取 $x_n=1/(n+1)$、$y_n=1/(n+2)$，输入距离趋于零，输出距离却恒为 $1$，因此输出精度 $1/2$ 无法用共同门槛保证。两列输入都趋于缺失的点 $0$，无法像前面的证明那样，在定义域内某点处使用连续性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity is also essential. On the compact interval $[0,1]$, let $h(x)=0$ for $x<1/2$ and $h(x)=1$ for $x\ge1/2$. Points $1/2-1/n$ and $1/2$, for $n\ge3$, approach each other while their outputs differ by $1$. Compactness alone does not repair the jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性同样重要。在紧致区间 $[0,1]$ 上，令 $x<1/2$ 时 $h(x)=0$，$x\ge1/2$ 时 $h(x)=1$。当 $n\ge3$ 时，$1/2-1/n$ 与 $1/2$ 越来越接近，输出却始终相差 $1$。仅有紧致性不能消除跳跃。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compactness is a sufficient condition here, not a necessary condition for every uniformly continuous map: $f(x)=x$ on $\mathbb R$ is uniformly continuous although $\mathbb R$ is not compact. In a general metric space, “closed and bounded” cannot simply replace “compact”; [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|that equivalence has a restricted scope]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

紧致性在这里提供充分条件，并不是每个一致连续映射都必须具有的定义域条件：$\mathbb R$ 上的 $f(x)=x$ 一致连续，而 $\mathbb R$ 不紧致。在一般度量空间中，也不能直接把“紧致”换成“闭且有界”；[[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|这种等价有特定适用范围]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What a common tolerance lets us control
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*共同门槛能用来控制什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $[a,b]$, choose the common $\delta$ for a desired $\varepsilon$. In any partition whose largest subinterval length is less than $\delta$, every two points in the same subinterval have output distance below $\varepsilon$. The estimate works in all subintervals simultaneously. This is the useful step behind [[连续函数可积|Riemann integrability of continuous functions]]: sufficiently short pieces have uniformly small output variation. The theorem guarantees that a suitable $\delta$ exists; it does not by itself give a numerical formula for it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[a,b]$ 上，为指定的 $\varepsilon$ 选好共同 $\delta$。只要一个分割中最大的子区间长度小于 $\delta$，同一子区间内任意两点的输出距离就小于 $\varepsilon$；所有子区间同时受控。这是[[连续函数可积|连续函数黎曼可积]]证明中的有用步骤：足够短的各段都具有统一小的输出变化。定理保证合适的 $\delta$ 存在，但本身不提供它的数值公式。

## 来源与核验

- [Lebl, §3.4, Example 3.4.3 and Theorem 3.4.4, PDF pp.138–139](https://www.jirka.org/ra/realanal.pdf#page=138): the closed-interval theorem, a sequential contradiction proof, and failure for $1/x$ on $(0,1)$. - [Lebl, §5.2, Lemma 5.2.7, PDF p.195](https://www.jirka.org/ra/realanal.pdf#page=195): using uniform continuity to control upper and lower sums in the continuous-function integrability proof.
