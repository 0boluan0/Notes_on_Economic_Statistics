# 压力测试与逆向压力测试
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stress Testing and Reverse Stress Testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 评估严重但合理、可能不在统计样本中的多因子冲击怎样穿过头寸、流动性、融资和业务约束；逆向测试则从失败条件反推情景。
> **具体锚点：** “利率升 200bp”不是完整银行压力：还要同时说明曲线形状、信用利差、存款流失、存款 beta、抵押品折扣和管理反应。
> **核心难点：** 情景不是概率分位；真正困难是保持宏观叙事、风险因子路径、行为反应和资产负债表反馈的一致性。
> **为什么重要：** 历史分布无法充分覆盖结构断点、市场关闭、相关破裂和集中挤兑，压力测试是统计风险度量的必要补充。
> **继续：** 先定义业务失败或风险承受阈值，再构建因果一致的因子路径并全重估；日常分位风险见 [[VaR、ES 与回测]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It evaluates how severe but plausible multi-factor shocks—possibly absent from the statistical sample—pass through positions, liquidity, funding, and business constraints. Reverse stress testing starts from failure conditions and works backward to scenarios.
> **Concrete anchor:** “Rates rise 200 basis points” is not a complete bank stress. Curve shape, credit spreads, deposit outflow, deposit beta, collateral haircuts, and management response must also be specified.
> **Central difficulty:** A scenario is not a probability quantile. The real challenge is internal consistency among the macro narrative, risk-factor path, behavioral response, and balance-sheet feedback.
> **Why it matters:** Historical distributions cannot fully cover structural breaks, market closure, correlation breakdown, and concentrated runs, making stress testing an essential complement to statistical risk measures.
> **Continue:** Define business failure or risk-appetite thresholds first, then build a causally coherent factor path and revalue fully. For routine quantile risk, see [[VaR、ES 与回测|VaR, Expected Shortfall, and Backtesting]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 压力与逆向压力测试
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stress and reverse stress testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

压力测试给严重但合理的多因子情景并全重估；逆向压力从业务失败条件反推能导致它的情景。两者补充统计分位模型对结构断点、流动性和相关破裂的盲点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stress testing specifies severe but plausible multi-factor scenarios and fully revalues positions; reverse stress testing starts from a business-failure condition and works backward to scenarios that could cause it. Both supplement statistical quantile models where structural breaks, liquidity, and correlation breakdown are blind spots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通压力问“若情景发生会亏多少”，逆向压力问“什么组合的冲击会让资本、流动性、保证金或业务连续性越过临界点”。后者不是寻找宇宙中最极端情景，而是发现此前没有注意到却足以导致失败的可行路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary stress testing asks how much would be lost if a scenario occurred; reverse stress testing asks which combination of shocks would push capital, liquidity, margin, or business continuity beyond a critical point. It does not seek the most extreme imaginable event but plausible paths sufficient to cause failure that were previously overlooked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 情景分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Scenario analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史、假设和反向情景应同时改变相关因子并全重估。情景不是概率分位，除非另有概率模型；其价值在于揭示非线性、相关破裂和管理脆弱点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical, hypothetical, and reverse scenarios should move related factors jointly and fully revalue positions. A scenario is not a probability quantile unless a separate probability model is supplied; its value lies in revealing nonlinearity, correlation breakdown, and managerial vulnerabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史情景重放过去共同变化，但当前头寸、市场结构和政策制度可能不同；假设情景能针对当前脆弱性，却容易变成任意冲击；逆向情景聚焦失败路径，却需要治理层明确什么叫“不可接受”。三类应互补而不是互相替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical scenarios replay past joint movements, but current positions, market structure, and policy regimes may differ. Hypothetical scenarios can target current vulnerabilities but risk becoming arbitrary. Reverse scenarios focus on failure paths but require governance to define unacceptable outcomes. The three types complement rather than replace one another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一套从叙事到损失的流程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A workflow from narrative to loss*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 明确目标：资本、流动性、利润、保证金还是业务连续性。
2. 写出冲击来源与时间路径，而不是只列互不相干的因子数值。
3. 把宏观变量映射到完整市场因子、违约和客户行为。
4. 对非线性头寸全重估，并加入交易容量、抵押品与融资反馈。
5. 允许有约束的管理行动，避免既假设管理层完全静止又假设其无成本完美对冲。
6. 将结果映射到风险承受能力、应急方案和可执行决策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Define the objective: capital, liquidity, earnings, margin, or business continuity.
2. State the source and time path of the shock rather than listing unrelated factor values.
3. Map macro variables to complete market factors, defaults, and customer behavior.
4. Fully revalue nonlinear positions and add trading-capacity, collateral, and funding feedback.
5. Allow constrained management actions, avoiding both a completely passive management assumption and costless perfect hedging.
6. Map results to risk appetite, contingency plans, and executable decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数值锚点：保证金反馈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical anchor: margin feedback*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基金持有市值 100、以 20 自有资金和 80 短期融资支持。资产跌 10% 后价值 90，债务仍为 80，自有资金降到 10，杠杆从 5 倍升到 9 倍。若融资方要求最大 5 倍，基金需把资产降到 50、债务降到 40，即在价格已跌后再卖出 40；压力损失不能只算最初 10。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fund holds assets worth 100 financed by 20 of equity and 80 of short-term debt. After a 10% asset decline, value is 90 while debt remains 80, so equity falls to 10 and leverage rises from 5 to 9. If lenders require maximum leverage of 5, the fund must reduce assets to 50 and debt to 40, selling 40 after prices have already fallen. Stress loss cannot stop at the initial 10.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 每个因子都取独立极端值，组合在经济上不可能；或所有因子机械用历史相关，错过相关破裂。
- 只重估市场价值，不加入保证金、融资到期、存款流失和火售。
- 把压力结果附上任意发生概率，伪装成精确分位。
- 报告巨大损失却不连接风险承受阈值和应急行动，测试无法改变决策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Assigning independent extremes to every factor creates an economically impossible combination, while mechanically preserving all historical correlations misses correlation breakdown.
- Revaluing market positions without margin, maturing funding, deposit outflow, and fire sales.
- Attaching an arbitrary probability to a stress result and presenting it as a precise quantile.
- Reporting a large loss without connecting it to risk-appetite thresholds and contingency actions, leaving the test unable to change decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 压力情景与 99% VaR 有什么根本区别？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the fundamental difference between a stress scenario and 99% VaR?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> VaR 是给定模型与分布下的概率分位；压力情景是有因果叙事的严重状态，除非另建概率模型，否则没有固定置信水平。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> VaR is a probability quantile under a specified model and distribution. A stress scenario is a severe state with a causal narrative and has no fixed confidence level unless a separate probability model is built.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：逆向压力测试为什么从失败条件开始？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does reverse stress testing start from a failure condition?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 先定义不可接受结果能迫使分析寻找真正绑定的资本、流动性或业务约束，发现传统情景未覆盖的冲击组合，而不是随意挑几个大数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Defining an unacceptable outcome first forces the analysis to identify binding capital, liquidity, or business constraints and to find shock combinations omitted by conventional scenarios instead of choosing arbitrary large numbers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么压力测试应包含管理行动但不能假设完美对冲？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why should stress testing include management action without assuming perfect hedging?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 真实机构会行动，但行动受时间、市场容量、抵押品、授权和成本约束；忽略行动高估损失，无约束完美行动又低估压力。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Real institutions respond, but responses are constrained by time, market capacity, collateral, authority, and cost. Ignoring action overstates loss, while unconstrained perfect action understates stress.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
