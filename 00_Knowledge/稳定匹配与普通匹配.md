# Graph matching and preference stability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*普通匹配与偏好稳定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An assignment can give everyone a different partner and still contain a pair who would both rather be together. Ordinary [[匹配定义|graph matching]] checks which pairs are allowed and whether selected pairs reuse an endpoint. **Preference stability** asks whether two people not paired together would both prefer each other to their assigned partners. These checks use different information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份分配可以让每个人都有不同的伙伴，却仍有两个人更愿意彼此配对。普通[[匹配定义|图匹配]]检查哪些配对被允许，以及选中的配对是否重复使用端点。**偏好稳定性**进一步检查：当前没有彼此配对的两个人，是否都更喜欢对方而不是自己的已分配伙伴。两种检查所用的信息不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the standard one-to-one model used here: two finite groups $L$ and $R$ have the same size; every cross-group pair is acceptable; and each person ranks every member of the opposite group in a strict order, without ties. A candidate outcome is a [[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|perfect matching]], so everyone has exactly one partner. A **[[阻挡对|blocking pair]]** is an unselected pair whose members both strictly prefer each other to their current partners. A **[[稳定匹配|stable matching]]** is a candidate with no blocking pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡采用标准一对一模型：两个有限群体 $L,R$ 等大，所有跨组配对都可以接受，每个人对另一组全部成员给出无并列的严格排序。候选结果是一份[[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|完美匹配]]，每人恰有一个伙伴。**[[阻挡对]]**是一对当前未彼此配对、却都严格更喜欢对方而非现任伙伴的人。**[[稳定匹配]]**就是没有阻挡对的候选结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Completeness concerns whom a person ranks, and strictness concerns the absence of ties; neither requires different people to agree. The preference of $a$ for $y$ says nothing by itself about whether $y$ prefers $a$. Both comparisons are required to block an outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整性规定每个人给谁排序，严格性规定没有并列，都不要求不同人的排序一致。$a$ 更喜欢 $y$，本身并不能说明 $y$ 也更喜欢 $a$。阻挡一个结果，需要双方的比较同时成立。

> [!example] Perfect, maximum, and still unstable · 完美、最大，却不稳定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $L=\{a,b\}$ and $R=\{x,y\}$. Preferences are $a:y\succ x$, $b:x\succ y$, $x:b\succ a$, and $y:a\succ b$; here $\succ$ means “prefers to.” The matching $M=\{ax,by\}$ covers all four people and has the maximum possible two edges. Yet $a$ prefers $y$ to $x$, and $y$ prefers $a$ to $b$. Thus $(a,y)$ is a blocking pair, so this perfect maximum matching is unstable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $L=\{a,b\}$、$R=\{x,y\}$。偏好为 $a:y\succ x$、$b:x\succ y$、$x:b\succ a$、$y:a\succ b$，其中 $\succ$ 表示“更喜欢”。匹配 $M=\{ax,by\}$ 覆盖四人，并达到最多的两条边。但 $a$ 喜欢 $y$ 胜过 $x$，$y$ 也喜欢 $a$ 胜过 $b$，所以 $(a,y)$ 构成阻挡对。这份完美且最大的匹配并不稳定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same preferences, $M'=\{ay,bx\}$ gives everyone their first choice, so neither unselected pair can block it. It is stable. Getting everyone's first choice is sufficient in this particular example, but it is not the definition of stability: a person may prefer somebody else while that other person refuses the change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一偏好下，$M'=\{ay,bx\}$ 让每个人都得到第一选择，任一未选配对都不能阻挡它，因此稳定。在这个例子里，全员第一选择足以保证稳定，但它不是稳定性的定义：一个人可能更喜欢别人，而对方不愿意更换伙伴。

## What the graph can and cannot tell us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*允许边图能告诉我们什么？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[二分图配对关系|allowed-edge graph]] records a yes/no relation. In the complete model above it contains all four possible pairs, regardless of the preference rankings. Both displayed perfect matchings use allowed edges without repeating endpoints. The graph therefore establishes their feasibility, while the rankings distinguish their stability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二分图配对关系|允许边图]]只记录“允许／不允许”。在上面的完整模型里，它包含全部四个可能配对，不论偏好怎样排列。两份展示的完美匹配都只使用允许边，而且端点不重复。因此，图确认它们可行，偏好排序才区分它们是否稳定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[Hall 定理|Hall's theorem]] asks whether an allowed-edge graph has a matching covering a designated side. It does not inspect preferences or certify that a particular matching has no blocking pair. In this example Hall's condition holds, but one of the perfect matchings is unstable. Changing the rankings while keeping the same allowed edges may change which matchings are stable without changing the Hall condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Hall 定理]]判断允许边图中是否存在覆盖指定一侧的匹配，不读取偏好，也不证明某一份匹配没有阻挡对。本例满足 Hall 条件，却有一份不稳定的完美匹配。保持允许边不变，只改变排序，可能改变稳定结果，而 Hall 条件完全不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the finite equal-size complete model, a stable outcome is perfect by the stated candidate requirement, and hence is also [[极大与最大匹配|maximum in cardinality]]. The converse fails, as the example shows. This implication uses the model; it should not be exported unchanged to incomplete-list variants that permit unmatched people.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在本卡有限、等大、完整的模型中，稳定结果按候选要求就是完美的，因此也具有[[极大与最大匹配|最大基数]]。反向不成立，前面的例子已说明。这一蕴含依赖模型，不能原样套到允许有人未匹配的不完整列表版本。

## Stability and total cost can disagree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*稳定性和总成本可能给出不同答案*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same example and supply a separate cost: $c(ax)=c(by)=0$, while $c(ay)=c(bx)=10$. There are only two perfect matchings. The diagonal one has total cost zero and is the minimum-cost perfect matching, but it is unstable. The crossed one is stable and has cost twenty. Feasibility, lowest cost, and stability are distinct success criteria even when every candidate has the same number of pairs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍用上例，另给独立的成本数据：$c(ax)=c(by)=0$，$c(ay)=c(bx)=10$。这个实例只有两份完美匹配。对角配对总成本为零，是最低成本完美匹配，却不稳定；交叉配对稳定，总成本为二十。即使每个候选的配对数相同，可行、成本最低与稳定仍是不同的成功标准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The costs were additional input, not numbers inferred from the rankings. An ordinal ranking tells us which partner one person prefers; it does not specify how much better that partner is, or how to add that preference to another person's. Even when a common numerical objective is supplied, optimizing its sum is a different requirement from excluding blocking pairs. See [[Hall 条件与带权最优|feasibility versus weighted optimization]] when choosing that objective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些成本是额外输入，不是从排序自动推出来的数字。序数偏好只告诉我们某人更喜欢谁，不说明好多少，也不说明如何与另一个人的偏好相加。即使另外给出了统一数值目标，优化总和仍不同于排除阻挡对。选择数值目标时，可参看[[Hall 条件与带权最优|可行性与带权优化的区别]]。

> [!warning] Declare a variant before changing the test · 改变模型时先声明规则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With unacceptable partners, unequal side sizes, ties or multiple places per receiver, first specify which outcomes are feasible and what counts as an improving deviation. An unmatched person needs a comparison with remaining unmatched; ties require a convention for stability; multiple places require a capacity-aware blocking rule. The simple two-current-partners test above belongs to the complete strict one-to-one model. Capacity feasibility alone does not supply these preference rules.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若存在不可接受的伙伴、两侧人数不等、并列偏好，或接收方有多个名额，先规定哪些结果可行，以及怎样才算双方愿意的改配。未匹配者需要与“继续未匹配”比较；并列需要指定稳定性口径；多个名额需要考虑容量的阻挡规则。上面的两位现任伙伴比较属于完整严格一对一模型，容量可行性本身不会给出这些偏好规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus an application may first remove ineligible pairs, then require designated coverage, and then add a cost or stability target. State all three explicitly. The shared word “matching” does not determine which question has been solved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，应用问题可以先排除不合资格配对，再规定覆盖对象，然后增加成本或稳定性目标。三者都应明确写出。仅凭都叫“匹配”，无法知道究竟解决了哪一个问题。
