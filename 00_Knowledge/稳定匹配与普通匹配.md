# 稳定匹配用偏好与阻挡对而普通匹配只用允许边

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stable matching uses preferences and blocking pairs, whereas ordinary graph matching uses only allowed edges*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区别
> 普通 bipartite matching 只要求选中的允许边不共享端点。在本课程使用的标准 stable-matching 模型中，两侧等大，每位参与者都对另一侧给出完整且严格的偏好排序；结果既必须是可行的 perfect matching，还必须不存在一对当前未彼此配对、却都更愿意选择彼此而非各自现任配偶的 blocking pair。偏好稳定性是在 matching 可行性之上新增的成功标准，而不是替代它。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ordinary bipartite matching asks only for allowed edges with no shared endpoints. In the standard stable-matching model used in this course, the two sides have equal size and every participant gives a complete strict ranking of the opposite side. The outcome must be a feasible perfect matching and must also have no pair that is not matched to each other but whose members both prefer one another to their assigned partners. Preference stability adds a success criterion to matching feasibility; it does not replace feasibility.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

perfect 或 maximum 并不推出 stable。设当前 matching 是 $(a,x),(b,y)$；即使它覆盖所有人，只要 $a$ 更喜欢 $y$ 而不是 $x$，同时 $y$ 更喜欢 $a$ 而不是 $b$，当前未彼此配对的 $(a,y)$ 就会阻挡这个结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perfect or maximum does not imply stable. Suppose the current matching is $(a,x),(b,y)$. Even though it covers everyone, if $a$ prefers $y$ to $x$ and $y$ prefers $a$ to $b$, the pair $(a,y)$, whose members are not matched to each other, blocks the outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，stable 也不等于总权重最大。ordinal preferences 只给出各自的排序，不自动提供可以跨人相加的共同数值尺度；即使人为给出权重，消除 blocking pairs 与优化权重总和仍是两个目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, stable does not mean maximum total weight. Ordinal preferences rank partners for each participant but do not automatically provide a common cardinal scale that can be added across people. Even if numerical weights are supplied, eliminating blocking pairs and optimising total weight remain distinct objectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，一个真实分配问题可能需要先用 allowed edges 排除不合资格组合，再选择究竟要求覆盖、成本最优还是偏好稳定。不能因为两类问题都叫 matching，就把 Hall condition 当成稳定性判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A real assignment problem may first use allowed edges to exclude ineligible pairs and then decide whether its target is coverage, minimum cost, or preference stability. The shared word “matching” does not turn Hall's condition into a stability criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 一个 matching 覆盖两侧全部顶点，为什么仍可能不稳定？
>
> **答案：** 覆盖只说明没有人未匹配；若一对非当前伴侣双方都更喜欢彼此，仍存在 blocking pair。
