# 货币政策操作目标是央行通过日常工具直接引导的市场变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An operating target is the market variable a central bank steers through its day-to-day instruments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

操作目标回答“今天在市场上要把哪个量或价格引导到哪里”，例如某种隔夜利率或其目标区间。它把最终政策意图转成可以实施和监测的近期任务；价格稳定、就业等最终目标则需要经过家庭、企业和市场的反应才能实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An operating target specifies which market price or quantity to steer and towards what level—for example, an overnight rate or target range. It translates policy intent into an implementable, observable near-term task. Ultimate objectives such as price stability and employment require subsequent responses by households, firms, and markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以一个假设决定为例：央行希望缓和通胀压力，将隔夜利率目标区间上调，然后改变[[央行管理利率]]并安排市场操作。这里“降低通胀”是最终目的，“隔夜利率目标区间”是操作目标，“付息与交易”是工具，银行贷款报价和长期收益率是传导中的价格。工具、被引导的变量、最后的结果不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a central bank seeks to ease inflation pressure, raises an overnight target range, and changes [[央行管理利率|administered rates]] and market operations. Lower inflation is the objective, the overnight range is the operating target, remuneration and transactions are instruments, and loan quotes and long yields are transmission prices. These roles are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

名称本身不固定角色。某央行直接规定的“政策利率”可以是工具价格；它希望市场成交靠近的另一利率才是市场操作目标。先查制度中的具体对象，再从[[货币政策]]进入传导，不能把美国的[[联邦基金利率]]当成所有央行的共同目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labels alone do not determine roles. A directly set policy rate may be an instrument price, while a different traded rate is the market target. Identify the framework before tracing [[货币政策|monetary policy]]; the [[联邦基金利率|federal funds rate]] is not every central bank's target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
