# 资产负债率是同一报表口径下总负债占总资产的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The liabilities-to-assets ratio is total liabilities divided by total assets under the same reporting basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以 $L$ 表示总负债、$A>0$ 表示总资产，资产负债率为 $L/A$，通常写成百分数。CPA例 $1040/2000=52\%$。负债包括经营应付款和有息借款等，不只是银行贷款；供应商也是债权人。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For total liabilities $L$ and positive total assets $A$, the ratio is $L/A$, usually expressed as a percentage. CPA's example is $1040/2000=52\%$. Liabilities include operating payables as well as interest-bearing borrowing; suppliers are creditors too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它描述账面融资结构，不直接给出违约概率、借款余量或清算损失。账面资产不是保证能实现的售价，且债权受偿还取决于担保与[[受偿顺序]]。因此50%不是普遍安全线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio describes book financing structure, not default probability, remaining borrowing capacity, or liquidation losses. Book values are not guaranteed sale proceeds, and recoveries depend on collateral and [[受偿顺序|claim priority]]. Fifty percent is not a universal safety threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若权益为负，比例可超过100%，应说明账面资本缺口而非强行截到100%；跨公司先统一合并范围和“总负债”口径。与[[产权比率]]、[[权益乘数]]的联系来自[[会计恒等式]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative equity can produce a ratio above 100%, indicating a book capital deficit rather than a value to cap at 100%. Align consolidation and liability definitions before comparison. Its links to [[产权比率|liabilities-to-equity]] and the [[权益乘数|equity multiplier]] follow from the [[会计恒等式|accounting identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
