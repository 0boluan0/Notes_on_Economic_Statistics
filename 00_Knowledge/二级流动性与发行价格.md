# 在其他风险和现金流相同的条件下预期转售成本降低可以提高投资者愿付的发行价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Holding cash flows and other risks constant, lower expected resale costs can raise the issue price investors are willing to pay.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资者在[[一级市场]]出资时，可能预期自己会在到期前退出。如果未来只能打很大折扣才能卖掉，他今天就会少付钱，或要求更高收益补偿。改善[[二级市场]]的[[市场流动性]]，因此可以通过降低预期退出代价影响发行融资，而不需要把每笔二级交易的钱直接交给公司。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An investor subscribing in the [[一级市场|primary market]] may need to exit before maturity. A costly future exit can reduce today's willingness to pay or increase the required return. Better [[市场流动性|market liquidity]] in the [[二级市场|secondary market]] can therefore affect issuance without transferring secondary-trade proceeds directly to the issuer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个只含预期现金流的教学例子看方向：假定一年后的转售收入在扣费前均为 110，折现率固定为 10%。预期出售费用为 5 时，愿付现值为 $105/1.1\approx95.45$；费用降为 1 时，为 $109/1.1\approx99.09$。这里固定了其他风险，不是在断言现实中的交易量增加必然降低资本成本；流动性改善也可能伴随杠杆、信息或风险结构变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified cash-flow example, expected resale proceeds before costs are 110 in one year and the discount rate is fixed at 10%. Expected selling costs of 5 imply a present value of $105/1.1\approx95.45$; costs of 1 imply $109/1.1\approx99.09$. Other risks are held fixed. Higher trading volume alone does not establish a lower real-world cost of capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
