# 公共选择与财政联邦主义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public Choice and Fiscal Federalism*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释政治参与者的激励怎样塑造公共决策，并判断哪些公共职能适合中央协调、哪些适合地方分权。
> **具体锚点：** 地方办学更能响应本地偏好，却会让税基贫富差异转成教育资源差距，因此常需中央最低标准和均等化转移。
> **核心难点：** 中位选民结论依赖单维和单峰偏好；分权优势又取决于本地信息、跨区外部性、规模经济和财政责任是否匹配。
> **为什么重要：** 预算和公共服务不是全知规划者的直接选择，政治激励与政府层级会改变同一政策工具的实际结果。
> **继续：** 先识别决策者、议程与问责，再按受益范围、信息位置和外部性划分政府职能。
> 政府并不是一个自动最大化社会福利的单一行动者。本卡分析选民、政治家、官僚和利益集团怎样形成政策，以及公共职能为什么在中央与地方之间分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Explain how political actors' incentives shape public decisions and determine which functions call for central coordination and which benefit from decentralization.
> **Concrete anchor:** Local school provision responds to local preferences but can translate tax-base inequality into unequal education resources, motivating national minimum standards and equalization transfers.
> **Central difficulty:** The median-voter result requires one-dimensional, single-peaked preferences, while the benefits of decentralization depend on local information, spillovers, economies of scale, and aligned fiscal responsibility.
> **Why it matters:** Budgets and public services are not direct choices of an omniscient planner; political incentives and government levels change the results of the same policy instrument.
> **Continue with:** Identify decision-makers, agenda control, and accountability first, then assign functions according to benefit reach, information location, and spillovers.
> Government is not a unitary actor that automatically maximizes social welfare. This card analyzes how voters, politicians, bureaucrats, and interest groups produce policy and why public functions are allocated between central and local governments.

## 公共选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public Choice*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多数投票在单峰偏好下一维议题上可能由中位选民决定；多维偏好可出现循环。官僚、利益集团和政治代理人有自身激励，预算结果不是一个全知社会规划者的直接选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under majority voting on a one-dimensional issue with single-peaked preferences, the median voter may determine the outcome; multidimensional preferences can generate cycles. Bureaucrats, interest groups, and political agents have their own incentives, so the budget is not the direct choice of an omniscient social planner.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中位选民结论依赖政策能排在一条轴上、每人偏好单峰、议程和参与规则稳定。放松条件后，提案顺序、投票参与、政党捆绑和信息不对称会影响结果；“多数支持”也不自动说明收益超过成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The median-voter result requires policy to lie on one dimension, preferences to be single-peaked, and agenda and participation rules to be stable. Once these conditions are relaxed, proposal order, turnout, party bundling, and asymmetric information affect outcomes. Majority support does not automatically mean that benefits exceed costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 财政联邦主义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fiscal Federalism*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

地方公共品偏好不同支持分权，跨地区外部性、再分配和规模经济支持中央协调。辖区流动既可改善匹配，也可能引发税基竞争和分层。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heterogeneous preferences for local public goods support decentralization, while cross-border externalities, redistribution, and economies of scale support central coordination. Mobility across jurisdictions can improve matching but may also create tax-base competition and stratification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

职能分配可按受益范围、信息位置、规模经济和公平目标判断：街道维护常适合地方，跨区域污染需更高层协调，全国再分配通常依赖中央税基。支出责任与收入权不匹配会产生纵向财政缺口，需要转移支付，但软预算约束又可能削弱地方纪律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Functions can be allocated according to benefit reach, location of information, economies of scale, and equity objectives. Street maintenance often suits local government, cross-border pollution requires higher-level coordination, and nationwide redistribution usually needs a central tax base. A mismatch between expenditure responsibilities and revenue authority creates a vertical fiscal gap and a need for transfers, but soft budget constraints can weaken local discipline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：学校财政
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: School Finance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

地方管理学校可利用家长偏好和本地信息，却会让富裕地区凭更大税基获得更多资源。中央政府可用均等化转移和最低标准缓解差距，但过细条件可能压缩地方创新。合理设计要区分最低公平保障、地方边际选择和跨区学生流动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Local school management can use parental preferences and local information, but wealthier areas may obtain more resources from larger tax bases. Central equalization transfers and minimum standards can reduce disparities, while overly detailed conditions may suppress local innovation. Sound design separates minimum equity guarantees, local marginal choice, and student mobility across districts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把观察到的政策直接解释成“社会偏好”会忽略议程与组织力量；把分权等同于效率会忽略跨区外部性和税基差异；把中央统一等同于公平又会忽略信息损失和执行差异。应比较具体制度下谁拥有信息、权力、收入和问责。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interpreting observed policy directly as “social preference” ignores agenda control and organized power. Equating decentralization with efficiency ignores spillovers and tax-base inequality. Equating central uniformity with equity ignores information loss and implementation variation. Compare who holds information, authority, revenue, and accountability under each concrete institution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimal Self-check*

### 中位选民结论为什么不能无条件用于所有政策？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can the median-voter result not be applied unconditionally to every policy?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它依赖单维政策、单峰偏好和多数规则；多维或非单峰偏好可产生循环和议程权力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It depends on a one-dimensional policy space, single-peaked preferences, and majority rule. Multidimensional or non-single-peaked preferences can generate cycles and agenda power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 哪些条件支持把职能交给地方政府？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which conditions support assigning a function to local government?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 受益范围局部、偏好差异大、本地信息重要且跨区外部性和规模经济较小时，分权更有优势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Decentralization is more attractive when benefits are local, preferences differ substantially, local information matters, and cross-border spillovers and economies of scale are limited.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 转移支付为什么可能产生软预算约束？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can intergovernmental transfers create a soft budget constraint?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 地方若预期超支或债务最终由上级救助，边际支出的真实成本不会完全由本地承担，财政纪律会减弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> If local governments expect higher authorities to rescue overspending or debt, they do not bear the full marginal cost of expenditure and fiscal discipline weakens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
