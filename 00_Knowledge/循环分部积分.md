# Cyclic integration by parts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*循环分部积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes a calculation brings back the very integral we started with. That can be useful: if the returned copy has a different coefficient, we can move it to the other side and solve for the unknown integral. **Cyclic integration by parts** uses this idea after a finite number of valid [[分部积分|integration-by-parts]] steps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时算着算着，最初那个积分又出现了。这也可能是好事：如果返回的积分带着不同的系数，就能移到等式另一边，解出未知积分。**循环分部积分**是在有限次合法的[[分部积分]]之后使用这个思路。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pattern is different from repeatedly lowering a polynomial's degree. An exponential keeps its form under differentiation, while sine and cosine turn into each other with signs. Their product can therefore return to the starting shape. The needed [[指数函数求导|exponential]] and [[正弦余弦求导|sine/cosine derivative rules]] also let us check the answer at the end.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与不断降低多项式次数的模式不同：指数函数求导后保持形状，正弦与余弦则带着符号互相转换，因此它们的乘积可能回到起点。所需的[[指数函数求导]]和[[正弦余弦求导]]也能用于最后核对答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the same unknown through both steps
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两步始终使用同一个未知对象*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We want an [[原函数|antiderivative]] of $e^x\cos x$. To make all intermediate equalities exact, first fix the lower limit at zero and define two functions by [[定积分|definite integrals]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标是求 $e^x\cos x$ 的[[原函数]]。为了让中间每个等号都精确成立，先固定下限为零，用[[定积分]]定义两个函数：

$$I(x)=\int_0^x e^t\cos t\,dt,\qquad J(x)=\int_0^x e^t\sin t\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $t$ is the variable being accumulated; $x$ is the upper endpoint. For negative $x$, the integral uses the usual reversed-limit sign. Both integrands are continuous, so the [[积分求导定理|accumulation derivative theorem]] gives $I'(x)=e^x\cos x$. This choice also fixes $I(0)=J(0)=0$, avoiding an unexplained choice of constants between steps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $t$ 是累积时使用的变量，$x$ 是上限；$x<0$ 时按通常的反向上下限符号理解。两个被积函数都连续，因此[[积分求导定理]]给出 $I'(x)=e^x\cos x$。这个选择还固定了 $I(0)=J(0)=0$，避免在两步之间不加说明地更换常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the first integral, choose $u=\cos t$ and $dv=e^t dt$. Then $du=-\sin t\,dt$ and $v=e^t$. Integration by parts gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一步取 $u=\cos t$、$dv=e^t dt$，则 $du=-\sin t\,dt$、$v=e^t$。分部积分给出

$$I(x)=[e^t\cos t]_0^x+J(x)=e^x\cos x-1+J(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the remaining integral, choose $u=\sin t$ and $dv=e^t dt$. Now $du=\cos t\,dt$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对余下积分取 $u=\sin t$、$dv=e^t dt$，此时 $du=\cos t\,dt$，所以

$$J(x)=[e^t\sin t]_0^x-I(x)=e^x\sin x-I(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitute this second equation into the first. The returned term is $-I$, not $+I$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把第二条代入第一条。返回的项是 $-I$，而不是 $+I$：

$$I(x)=e^x(\cos x+\sin x)-1-I(x),$$
$$2I(x)=e^x(\sin x+\cos x)-1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $I(x)=\tfrac12e^x(\sin x+\cos x)-\tfrac12$. Dropping the fixed constant and allowing any additive constant gives the original indefinite answer:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

于是 $I(x)=\tfrac12e^x(\sin x+\cos x)-\tfrac12$。把这个固定常数归入任意加法常数，就得到原来的不定积分答案：

> [!example] The returned integral makes an equation · 返回的积分构成可解等式
> $$\int e^x\cos x\,dx=\frac12e^x(\sin x+\cos x)+C.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Differentiating the product gives $\tfrac12e^x[(\sin x+\cos x)+(\cos x-\sin x)]=e^x\cos x$. The sine terms cancel and the two cosine terms explain the factor $1/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对乘积求导得到 $\tfrac12e^x[(\sin x+\cos x)+(\cos x-\sin x)]=e^x\cos x$。正弦项抵消，两个余弦项说明了系数 $1/2$ 的来源。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In conventional indefinite-integral working, the same two steps are written $I=e^x\cos x+J$ and $J=e^x\sin x-I$, suppressing compatible additive constants until the final $+C$. The fixed-lower-limit calculation explains that shorthand without treating the family of all antiderivatives as a single uniquely specified function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通常的不定积分演算把同样两步写成 $I=e^x\cos x+J$、$J=e^x\sin x-I$，暂时省略相容的加法常数，最终统一补 $+C$。固定下限的计算解释了这种简写，不会把全部原函数的族误当成唯一指定的一个函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A return is useful only if its coefficient lets us solve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*关键是回归系数能否让我们解出未知量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the same fixed integral function $I(x)$ returns after finitely many valid steps in the exact identity $I(x)=G(x)+cI(x)$, where $G$ is known and $c$ is a known real constant. Rearranging gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设经过有限次合法步骤，同一个固定积分函数 $I(x)$ 返回，并形成精确恒等式 $I(x)=G(x)+cI(x)$，其中 $G$ 已知，$c$ 是已知实常数。移项得到

$$(1-c)I(x)=G(x),\qquad I(x)=\frac{G(x)}{1-c}\quad(c\ne1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For indefinite integration the resulting antiderivative family is $G(x)/(1-c)+C$, with any fixed basepoint constants absorbed into $C$. If $c=1$, the unknown cancels and only $0=G(x)$ remains. A valid cycle of this kind has merely recovered an identity; it has not determined $I$. If the returned coefficient is a function $c(x)$, division is valid only where $1-c(x)\ne0$; points where $1-c(x)=0$ require separate analysis. The constant-coefficient rule above does not cover those points automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于不定积分，得到的原函数族是 $G(x)/(1-c)+C$，固定基点常数归入 $C$。若 $c=1$，未知量抵消，只剩 $0=G(x)$。这样的合法循环只是恢复一个恒等式，没有确定 $I$。若回归系数是函数 $c(x)$，只能在 $1-c(x)\ne0$ 处相除；满足 $1-c(x)=0$ 的点须另行分析。上面的常系数规则不会自动覆盖那些点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A concrete unproductive loop starts with $\int xe^x dx$, takes $u=x$ and $dv=e^x dx$, then applies integration by parts to the remaining $\int e^x dx$ using $u=e^x$, $dv=dx$. That second choice reverses the first manipulation. Substitution returns $I=I$, providing no value. In contrast, the cosine example returns $c=-1$, so $1-c=2$ and the equation is informative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个没有推进的具体循环是：从 $\int xe^x dx$ 出发，取 $u=x$、$dv=e^x dx$；随后对剩下的 $\int e^x dx$ 再取 $u=e^x$、$dv=dx$。第二步撤销了第一步的操作，代回只得到 $I=I$，没有算出值。相比之下，余弦例子的回归系数为 $c=-1$，所以 $1-c=2$，等式确实能提供答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every step must also be executable. Putting the unknown original antiderivative into the choice of $v$ does not create a method. A useful cycle follows from known antiderivatives, keeps the signs and boundary terms, and yields a nonzero coefficient difference. Check the final primitive by differentiation; for a definite answer, evaluate that primitive at both endpoints or carry the boundary terms throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一步还必须能实际执行。把尚未知的原函数塞进 $v$ 的选择，不会凭空产生方法。有效循环必须从已知原函数出发，保留符号和边界项，并产生非零的系数差。最后求导检查原函数；若要定积分答案，再计算两端值之差，或从头到尾保留边界项。
