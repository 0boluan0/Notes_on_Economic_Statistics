# Strong mathematical induction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*强数学归纳法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes the next case depends on a much smaller case, not just the one immediately before it. To factor $12$, for example, it is useful to know how to factor $3$ and $4$; knowing something about $11$ alone does not directly supply those factors. Strong induction lets the proof use every earlier case in a specified integer range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有时下一种情形依赖的是远一些的较小情形，不只是紧邻的上一种。例如分解 $12$ 时，知道怎样分解 $3$ 与 $4$ 很有用；只知道关于 $11$ 的结论，并不能直接提供这两个因子。强归纳允许证明使用指定整数范围内已经位于当前层之前的全部情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What may be assumed in the step?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*归纳步里可以使用哪些假设？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $P(n)$ be a [[命题与谓词|predicate]] on integers $n\ge n_0$, with fixed integer starting point $n_0$. As in [[普通数学归纳法|ordinary induction]], first prove the initial case. The change is the hypothesis available in the step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
固定整数起点 $n_0$，令 $P(n)$ 是整数 $n\ge n_0$ 上的[[命题与谓词|谓词]]。与[[普通数学归纳法]]一样，先证明起始情形；改变的是归纳步可以使用的假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a strong-induction proof of $P(n)$, the step for $n+1$ may assume all earlier statements $P(n_0),P(n_0+1),\ldots,P(n)$ and then derive $P(n+1)$. This suits arguments that decompose the current object into one or more smaller objects of varying sizes. The hypothesis is broader, but the smallest cases must still connect to every recurrence path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
强归纳证明 $P(n)$ 时，在第 $n+1$ 层可假设 $P(n_0),P(n_0+1),\ldots,P(n)$ 全部成立，再推出 $P(n+1)$。它适合把当前对象分解成一个或多个不固定的更小对象；可用假设更宽，但仍必须核对最小对象和递推是否接得上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule therefore asks for $P(n_0)$ and, for every integer $n\ge n_0$, an argument that all cases from $n_0$ through $n$ together imply $P(n+1)$. One need not use every available case. The argument must, however, justify that every case actually used lies in that earlier range. The word “strong” refers to the fuller hypothesis, not extra logical power; the [[归纳与良序法的等价|equivalence of the induction principles]] is a separate result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
因此，规则要求证明 $P(n_0)$，并对每个整数 $n\ge n_0$，证明从 $n_0$ 到 $n$ 的全部情形合起来能够推出 $P(n+1)$。不一定要用完所有可用假设，但实际引用的每个下标都必须落在这个较早范围内。“强”指归纳假设包含更多情形，并非额外的逻辑证明能力；[[归纳与良序法的等价|归纳原则的等价性]]是另一个结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mechanism still advances one conclusion at a time. To prove $P(n_0+1)$, only $P(n_0)$ is earlier; after obtaining it, the available interval extends through $n_0+1$. The next step extends that interval again. Any fixed target index is reached after finitely many such extensions. One never assumes an unbounded collection of future cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这个机制仍然每次推进一个新结论。证明 $P(n_0+1)$ 时，较早情形只有 $P(n_0)$；得到新结论后，可用范围扩展到 $n_0+1$，下一步再扩展一次。任意固定的目标下标都能经过有限次这样的扩展到达，不需要假设尚未到达的无限多种未来情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why smaller factors are available
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么可以调用较小因子的结论？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[素数|prime]] is an integer greater than one whose only positive divisors are one and itself; a larger-than-one integer that is not prime is composite. We will prove **existence** of a prime-factor product for every integer $m\ge2$. This argument does not prove uniqueness of that product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[素数]]是大于一且正因子只有一和自身的整数；大于一而不是素数的整数叫合数。下面证明每个整数 $m\ge2$ **存在**素数乘积表示；这个论证不证明表示的唯一性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove that every integer greater than one is a product of primes, split the case $n+1$: if it is prime, the claim is immediate; if it is composite, write it as a product of two integers between $2$ and $n$ and apply the strong induction hypothesis to both factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
证明每个大于 $1$ 的整数都是素数乘积时，对 $n+1$ 分类：若它是素数，结论立即成立；若为合数，则写成两个都在 $2$ 到 $n$ 之间的整数之积，并对这两个因子使用强归纳假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here are the details. Take $P(m)$ to mean “$m$ is a finite product of primes.” The first case is $m=2$: the one-factor product $2$ works. Now fix $n\ge2$ and assume $P(2),\ldots,P(n)$. If $n+1$ is prime, its one-factor product works again. Otherwise it has a positive divisor $d$ with $1<d<n+1$, so $n+1=de$ for an integer $e$. Both $d$ and $e$ are at least $2$, and each is at most $n$. Thus both $P(d)$ and $P(e)$ are available. Concatenating their two finite prime lists gives a prime product equal to $de=n+1$. This completes the step in both cases and proves the claim for all integers at least $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
把 $P(m)$ 定为“$m$ 是有限个素数的乘积”。起始情形 $m=2$ 可用只有一个因子的乘积 $2$。现在固定 $n\ge2$，假设 $P(2),\ldots,P(n)$ 成立。若 $n+1$ 是素数，同样使用一个因子的乘积。否则，它有正因子 $d$ 满足 $1<d<n+1$，于是 $n+1=de$，其中 $e$ 为整数。$d,e$ 都至少为 $2$，且都至多为 $n$，因此可以调用 $P(d)$ 与 $P(e)$。把两个有限素数列表接在一起，就得到乘积为 $de=n+1$ 的素数列表。两种情形都完成归纳步，故结论对所有至少为 $2$ 的整数成立。

> [!example] Which earlier cases? · 看清引用哪几层
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $12=3\cdot4$, the argument uses $P(3)$ and $P(4)$; the latter may give $4=2\cdot2$. Together these give $12=3\cdot2\cdot2$. Neither factor is $12$, so the current conclusion was not used to prove itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在 $12=3\cdot4$ 中，论证使用 $P(3)$ 与 $P(4)$；后者可以给出 $4=2\cdot2$，合起来便有 $12=3\cdot2\cdot2$。两个因子都不是 $12$，因此没有用当前结论证明它自己。

## Starting a recurrence that jumps back
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*回退多步的递推怎样启动？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose coins have values $3$ and $5$, with unlimited copies of each. We claim every integer amount at least $8$ can be formed. The general construction for an amount $m\ge11$ is: form $m-3$, then add one $3$-coin. The inequality $m-3\ge8$ puts that smaller amount inside the intended domain. This construction does not handle $8,9,10$, so prove these separately:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
假设硬币面额为 $3$ 和 $5$，每种都有足够多枚。要证明每个至少为 $8$ 的整数金额都能凑出。对 $m\ge11$，一般构造是先凑出 $m-3$，再添一枚 $3$ 元硬币；不等式 $m-3\ge8$ 保证较小金额仍在要证的论域内。这个构造不能处理 $8,9,10$，因此先分别证明：

$$8=3+5,\qquad 9=3+3+3,\qquad 10=5+5.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After these starts, assume every amount from $8$ to $m-1$ is representable. For $m\ge11$, this includes $m-3$, giving the construction above. Thus the initial cases and the general step cover the whole domain. Equivalently, one can use $8$ as the formal base case and handle $9$ and $10$ as special cases inside the inductive step. Either presentation must actually establish all three starting amounts; the label “strong induction” does not make them automatic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有了这些起始情形后，假设从 $8$ 到 $m-1$ 的金额都能凑出。对 $m\ge11$，其中就包含 $m-3$，所以前述构造可用。起始情形与一般递推共同覆盖整个论域。也可以形式上只把 $8$ 写作基例，再在归纳步中单独处理 $9$ 和 $10$；无论怎样组织，都必须确实证明这三个起始金额，不能靠“强归纳”这个名称自动获得。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Access to all smaller cases does not permit using the current claim $P(n+1)$. Every subobject must be strictly smaller and remain inside the induction domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“可用全部较小情形”不等于可以使用当前结论 $P(n+1)$；分解得到的对象必须严格更小且仍在归纳论域中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a recurrence can jump back by several indices, enough starting cases must be established, or the strong-induction statement must genuinely cover the whole initial interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若递推每次可能回退多步，必须证明足够多的起始情形，或采用一个确实涵盖最小区间的强归纳表述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The required starts depend on the actual recurrence. The prime-factor proof needs the initial case $2$ because its prime branch already handles subsequent small primes; it does not need a list of unrelated extra bases. Conversely, the coin construction with a jump of three needs the three starts shown above. Extra hypotheses do not repair an undefined subproblem, an omitted small case or a circular appeal to the current case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
需要哪些起始情形，要看实际递推。素数乘积证明以 $2$ 启动，之后的小素数已经由素数分支处理，不需要再罗列无关的基例。相反，上面回退三步的凑硬币构造确实需要展示三个起始金额。更宽的归纳假设不能修复未定义的子问题、漏掉的小情形，或对当前结论的循环引用。

> [!question]- Check the subproblem sizes · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In proving $P(n)$, suppose the object splits into subobjects of sizes $n-2$ and $2$. Why is strong induction more natural than a hypothesis containing only $P(n-1)$?
>
> **Answer:** Strong induction directly supplies the property for every strictly smaller index, including $P(n-2)$ and, when needed, $P(2)$. The proof must still ensure that those indices lie in the initialized domain. For example, if that domain starts at $2$, this particular split can use both hypotheses only when $n\ge4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在证明 $P(n)$ 时，你把 $n$ 分成大小为 $n-2$ 与 $2$ 的两个子对象。普通归纳假设只给出 $P(n-1)$，为什么强归纳更自然？
>
> **答案：** 因为强归纳允许直接使用所有严格更小下标的性质，包括 $P(n-2)$ 与需要时的 $P(2)$；但仍要先保证这些下标位于已启动的论域中。例如论域从 $2$ 开始时，这种具体分拆须在 $n\ge4$ 时才能同时使用两项假设。
