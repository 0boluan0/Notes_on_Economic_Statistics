# 正定协方差与仅预算约束下全局最小方差权重为逆协方差乘全一向量后的归一化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With positive-definite covariance and only a budget constraint, GMV weights are the normalized inverse-covariance product with the vector of ones.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若收益协方差矩阵 $\Sigma\succ0$，允许任意实数权重且只要求 $\mathbf1^Tw=1$，则[[全局最小方差组合]]唯一存在，且
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\Sigma\succ0$, weights may be any real numbers, and the only constraint is $\mathbf1^Tw=1$, the [[全局最小方差组合|GMV portfolio]] exists uniquely and satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A=\mathbf1^T\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf1>0,\qquad
w_G=\frac{\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf1}{A},\qquad
\sigma_G^2=\frac1A,\qquad
\mu_G=\frac{\mathbf1^T\Sigma^{-1}\mu}{A}.
$$

正定性保证普通逆矩阵存在，并由[[正定逆仍正定]]保证分母严格为正。这里没有非负权重约束；“只需要协方差”说的是最优权重，而不是它的期望收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive definiteness guarantees the ordinary inverse, and [[正定逆仍正定|positive definiteness of that inverse]] makes the denominator strictly positive. No nonnegative-weight constraint is imposed. Dependence on covariance alone refers to the weights, not to their expected return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么是唯一全局解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it is the unique global solution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述 $w_G$ 满足预算，且 $\Sigma w_G=\mathbf1/A$。任意其他可行权重可写为 $w_G+h$，其中 $\mathbf1^Th=0$。因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stated $w_G$ meets the budget and satisfies $\Sigma w_G=\mathbf1/A$. Any other feasible weight vector is $w_G+h$ with $\mathbf1^Th=0$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(w_G+h)^T\Sigma(w_G+h)
=\frac1A+\frac{2\mathbf1^Th}{A}+h^T\Sigma h
=\frac1A+h^T\Sigma h.
$$

当 $h\ne0$，最后一项严格为正，故不存在方差相同或更低的另一个可行权重。这同时证明最优性和唯一性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $h\ne0$, the final term is strictly positive. No other feasible allocation has equal or lower variance, proving both optimality and uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$ 的行列式为 $0.003375>0$，且首个顺序主子式为正，故正定。计算得 $A=800/27$，$w_G=(3/4,1/4)^T$，$\sigma_G^2=27/800=0.03375$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example matrix has determinant $0.003375>0$ and a positive first leading principal minor, so it is positive definite. Calculation gives $A=800/27$, $w_G=(3/4,1/4)^T$, and $\sigma_G^2=27/800=0.03375$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\Sigma$ [[协方差奇异性|奇异]]，这个普通逆公式不可用，但最小方差问题仍可能有解；若再要求 $w_i\ge0$，则须检查上述权重是否满足约束，否则转向[[禁止卖空的最小方差优化]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\Sigma$ is [[协方差奇异性|singular]], this ordinary-inverse formula is unavailable even though the optimization may still have solutions. If $w_i\ge0$ is additionally required, verify feasibility of these weights or use [[禁止卖空的最小方差优化|constrained optimization]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
