# 庇古税校准边际行为而税收总额不必等于污染总损害或实际赔偿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Pigouvian tax targets marginal behaviour; its revenue need not equal total pollution damage or compensation actually received.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[庇古税]]问“下一单位活动应增加多少私人代价”；损害总额问“已经造成多少损失”；赔偿问“谁实际向受害者支付多少”。这三个问题不能用同一个金额回答，也不能因企业交了税就认定受害者已经获赔。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[庇古税|Pigouvian tax]] asks how much private cost the next unit should add. Total damage measures the loss caused, while compensation records payments actually reaching victims. These are distinct questions; payment of a tax does not establish compensation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令最优剩余排放为 $e^*$，单位税率 $t^*=D'(e^*)$；若 $D(0)=0$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For efficient remaining emissions $e^*$ and unit rate $t^*=D'(e^*)$, with $D(0)=0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\underbrace{R=t^*e^*}_{\text{税收 / revenue}},\qquad
\underbrace{D(e^*)=\int_0^{e^*}D'(u)\,du}_{\text{总损害 / total damage}}.$$

单位税率是最后一小单位的损害，总损害是全部排放单位造成损害的累计。若 $D(e)=e^2/2$ 且 $e^*=50$，则 $t^*=50$、税收 2500、总损害 1250。只有在特殊函数和基准下两者才可能相等；例如恒定单位损害且 $D(0)=0$ 时，税收和损害数额相同，但钱仍未必支付给受害者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rate reflects marginal damage at the chosen point, whereas total damage cumulates harm across all units. With $D(e)=e^2/2$ and $e^*=50$, the rate is fifty, revenue 2,500 and damage 1,250. Equality is possible in special cases, such as constant unit damage with zero initial damage, but equal amounts still do not establish that victims receive the money.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税收进入政府账户后，可以有不同用途。是否补偿到受损者、不同群体最终负担多少、收入如何使用，都要另外判断。[[政府干预的效率与分配理由|边际激励得到纠正不自动证明分配更公平]]；赔偿合同若改变边际决策，也可能实现[[外部性内部化]]，因此“赔偿”和“激励”并非互斥类别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue can be used in different ways. Compensation targeting, final burdens and revenue use require separate analysis. [[政府干预的效率与分配理由|Correcting incentives does not itself establish a fairer distribution]]. Conversely, a compensation contract can achieve [[外部性内部化|internalisation]] if it changes marginal decisions; compensation and incentives are not mutually exclusive categories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做社会评价时还要应用[[转移支付核算]]：同一评价边界内、相同货币权重下，企业交税与政府收税是两端转移；污染损害和真实减排成本则是另外的福利变化。若目标已经写成私人净收益减损害，不能再把税款全额扣一遍当作额外资源损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]] in social appraisal: with a common boundary and equal monetary weights, the firm's tax payment and government receipt are two ends of a transfer. Pollution damage and real abatement costs are separate welfare changes. An objective already measuring net private benefit minus damage must not subtract tax revenue again as a resource loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
