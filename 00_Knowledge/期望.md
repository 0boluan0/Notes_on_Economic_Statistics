# Expected value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **expected value**, or **mean**, is a probability-weighted average of a [[随机变量|random variable]]'s possible values. More likely values contribute more to this average. It describes the centre of the distribution, rather than promising what one observation will be.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**期望**，也叫**均值**，是把[[随机变量]]的可能取值按概率加权后得到的平均数。一个值出现的概率越大，它对这个平均数的贡献就越大。期望描述整张分布的中心，并不是保证下一次会出现哪个结果。

> [!example] The probabilities are the weights · 用概率作权重
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $X$ is $2$ with probability $0.75$ and $6$ with probability $0.25$. Write $E[X]$ for its expected value. Multiplying each value by its probability and adding gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $X$ 以 $0.75$ 的概率取 $2$，以 $0.25$ 的概率取 $6$。用 $E[X]$ 表示它的期望，把每个值乘以对应概率后相加：
>
> $$E[X]=2\times0.75+6\times0.25=3.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The ordinary average of the two listed values is $4$, but that gives them equal weight. The probability-weighted answer is $3$, closer to the more likely value $2$. The weights already sum to one, so no additional division is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把列出的两个数直接平均会得到 $4$，但那相当于给二者相同权重。按概率加权得到的 $3$ 更靠近较常出现的 $2$。这里权重已经相加为一，所以不用再除以一个总权重。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the same idea as a [[加权平均|weighted average]]. If each possible value marks a position on a ruler and its probability is the mass placed there, the expectation is the balance point. In the example, $3$ is neither a possible value nor the most likely value. A fair six-sided die provides another familiar case: its mean is $(1+2+3+4+5+6)/6=3.5$, although no single roll is $3.5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与[[加权平均]]的思路相同：把取值当成尺上的位置，把概率当成放在该位置的质量，期望就是平衡点。在上例中，$3$ 既不是可能取值，也不是最可能取值。公平六面骰子也是一样：均值为 $(1+2+3+4+5+6)/6=3.5$，但任何一次投掷都不会掷出 $3.5$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Computing the weighted centre
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*怎样计算这个加权中心*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a discrete variable, its [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]] gives $p_X(x)=P(X=x)$, the probability attached to the value $x$. Let $S$ be the finite or countable set carrying all its probability. The expectation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对离散变量，[[概率质量函数]] $p_X(x)=P(X=x)$ 给出数值 $x$ 对应的概率。令 $S$ 是承载全部概率的有限或可数取值集合，则期望为

$$E[X]=\sum_{x\in S}x\,p_X(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum includes one value-times-probability contribution for each $x$. If $X$ instead has a [[概率密度函数|density]] $f_X$, probability is spread over intervals. A small width $dx$ receives probability weight represented by $f_X(x)\,dx$, and integration adds the weighted values:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和中，每个 $x$ 都贡献一项“取值乘概率”。如果 $X$ 有[[概率密度函数|密度]] $f_X$，概率就分布在区间上；小段宽度 $dx$ 对应的概率权重由 $f_X(x)\,dx$ 表示，通过积分把这些加权取值累积起来：

$$E[X]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x f_X(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the uniform waiting-time example on $[0,0.1]$ seconds, $f_X(x)=10$ on that interval and zero elsewhere. The [[定积分|definite integral]] adds these contributions over the interval: its mean is $\int_0^{0.1}10x\,dx=0.05$ seconds. The density height supplies the weighting; integrating $x$ alone would use the wrong weights. Expectation has the same units as the variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[0,0.1]$ 秒上的均匀等待时间例子中，区间内 $f_X(x)=10$，区间外为零。[[定积分]]把整个区间上的这些贡献累积起来，得到均值 $\int_0^{0.1}10x\,dx=0.05$ 秒。密度提供了权重；只对 $x$ 积分会用错权重。期望与原变量具有相同单位。

## When the answer is a finite number
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*什么时候期望是有限数值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formulas above give a finite real expectation when $X$ is **integrable**. This means that the average magnitude of $X$ is finite. The absolute value $|X|$ keeps the size of a value and removes its sign, so the condition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的公式要给出有限实数期望，需要 $X$ **可积**，也就是 $X$ 的数值大小平均起来有限。绝对值 $|X|$ 保留大小而去掉正负号，因此条件写作

$$E[|X|]<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a PMF this asks whether $\sum_x |x|p_X(x)$ is finite; with a density it asks whether $\int |x|f_X(x)\,dx$ is finite. The condition is automatic for a variable with finitely many finite values, such as a die. For unbounded variables, rare but extremely large values can make it fail. The detailed check is explained in [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment existence conditions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有 PMF 时，要检查 $\sum_x |x|p_X(x)$ 是否有限；有密度时，要检查 $\int |x|f_X(x)\,dx$ 是否有限。像骰子这样只取有限个有限数值的变量，自动满足条件；无界变量则可能因为极少出现但非常大的数值而不满足。具体检查见[[矩存在性的使用边界]]。

> [!warning] Infinite contributions cannot cancel by declaration · 无穷贡献不能靠一句“抵消”处理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Separate the positive contributions from the magnitudes of the negative contributions. If both totals are infinite, subtracting them would be $\infty-\infty$, so the expectation is undefined. Symmetry alone does not repair this. A nonnegative variable may instead have the extended expectation $+\infty$; that is a defined extended value, but not a finite mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先把正值的贡献与负值贡献的大小分开。如果两部分都累积成无穷，相减就是没有定义的 $\infty-\infty$；不能仅凭对称就宣布均值为零。非负变量则可能具有扩展意义下的期望 $+\infty$，这是有定义的扩展值，但不是有限均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expectation is defined from the distribution without requiring repeated experiments. Under appropriate sampling assumptions, a [[强大数定律|law of large numbers]] connects it to a long-run sample average. That connection is a theorem with conditions, rather than the definition of expectation itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期望直接由分布定义，不需要先重复做试验。在适当的抽样条件下，[[强大数定律]]会把它与长期样本平均联系起来；这种联系是带条件的定理，而不是期望的定义本身。

> [!note]- One definition covers all distributions · 统一表示所有分布的写法
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On a [[概率空间|probability space]], $\Omega$ denotes the original outcomes and $P$ their probability measure. If $F_X$ is the [[累积分布函数|CDF]] of $X$, the general definition can be written in either form below. The first averages the recorded value over original outcomes; the second averages numerical values under their distribution. The notation $dF_X(x)$ denotes distributional probability weight and works even when no density exists.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在[[概率空间]]中，$\Omega$ 表示原始结果，$P$ 是分配概率的测度。若 $F_X$ 是 $X$ 的[[累积分布函数|CDF]]，一般定义可写成下面两种形式：前者按原始结果平均记录值，后者按数值的分布进行平均。$dF_X(x)$ 表示分布提供的概率权重，即使没有密度也能使用。
>
> $$E[X]=\int_\Omega X\,dP=\int_{\mathbb R}x\,dF_X(x).$$
