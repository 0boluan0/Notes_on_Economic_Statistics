# Embedded jump chains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*嵌入跳链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[CTMC定义|continuous-time Markov chain]] records both the states visited and the time spent in each state. Its **embedded jump chain** keeps only the state sequence at actual changes. It answers “where does the process go next?” while discarding “how long does it wait?” The discrete index counts jumps, not minutes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CTMC定义|连续时间 Markov 链]]同时记录访问的状态与每个状态中的停留时长。**嵌入跳链**只留下每次真正改变状态时的状态序列：它回答“下一步去哪里”，丢掉“要等多久”。离散下标计数的是跳跃次数，不是分钟数。

> [!example] Keep the order, remove the clock / 保留顺序，去掉时钟
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Imagine a path starting in $A$, changing to $B$ at time $0.2$, returning to $A$ at $0.7$, and entering an absorbing state $C$ at $1.1$. The jump-chain record is $A,B,A,C$. The waits $0.2,0.5,0.4$ have disappeared; the two visits to $A$ remain distinct visits. Repeated observations of $A$ before time $0.2$ would not create extra jumps.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设一条路径从 $A$ 出发，时刻 $0.2$ 跳到 $B$，$0.7$ 回到 $A$，$1.1$ 进入吸收态 $C$。跳链记录为 $A,B,A,C$。停留时长 $0.2,0.5,0.4$ 被删去了，而两次访问 $A$ 仍是两次不同访问。在 $0.2$ 之前重复观察到 $A$，不产生额外跳跃。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Index the actual changes

Use a standard right-continuous, time-homogeneous pure-jump chain $X_t$ on a finite or countable state space, with finite exit rate at each state. Set $T_0=0$. While $T_n$ is finite, define the next genuine jump time and the state recorded there by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*按真正变化的次数编号*

考虑有限或可数状态空间上的标准右连续、时间齐次纯跳链 $X_t$，每个状态的出口率有限。令 $T_0=0$；当 $T_n$ 有限时，下一次真实跳时和该时刻记录的状态定义为

$$
T_{n+1}=\inf\{t>T_n:X_t\ne X_{T_n}\},
\qquad J_n=X_{T_n}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ counts actual jumps, with the initial state recorded at index $0$. If the set defining $T_{n+1}$ is empty, $T_{n+1}=\infty$ and there is no next genuine jump. At a finite jump time, right continuity means $J_n$ is the state just entered. The [[CTMC强Markov性|strong Markov property]] restarts the chain at these random entry times, so the distribution of $J_{n+1}$ given the previous recorded states depends only on $J_n$. Thus $(J_n)$ is a [[离散时间Markov链|discrete-time Markov chain]] up to termination, with the absorption convention below if a full infinite sequence is desired.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ 计数真实跳跃，初始状态记在下标 $0$。若定义 $T_{n+1}$ 的集合为空，则 $T_{n+1}=\infty$，没有下一次真实跳跃。在有限跳时，右连续性使 $J_n$ 表示刚进入的状态。[[CTMC强Markov性|强 Markov 性]]允许在这些随机进入时刻重新开始，因此给定此前记录的状态后，$J_{n+1}$ 的分布只依赖 $J_n$。所以 $(J_n)$ 在终止前是一条[[离散时间Markov链|离散时间 Markov 链]]；若需要完整的无限序列，可采用下文的吸收约定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Convert rates into destination probabilities

For the row-convention [[生成矩阵约束|generator]] $Q=(q_{ij})$, $q_{ij}\ge0$ is the direct-jump rate from $i$ to a different state $j$. The [[出口率|total exit rate]] is $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}=-q_{ii}$. If $0<q_i<\infty$, the jump-chain transition matrix $R=(r_{ij})$ has row
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把速率变成去向概率*

在行约定的[[生成矩阵约束|生成矩阵]] $Q=(q_{ij})$ 中，$q_{ij}\ge0$ 是从 $i$ 直接跳到另一个状态 $j$ 的速率。[[出口率|总出口率]]为 $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}=-q_{ii}$。当 $0<q_i<\infty$ 时，跳链转移矩阵 $R=(r_{ij})$ 的这一行为

$$
r_{ij}=\Pr(J_{n+1}=j\mid J_n=i)=\frac{q_{ij}}{q_i}\quad(j\ne i),
\qquad r_{ii}=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditional probability is considered on visits to $i$ of positive probability. Dividing by the sum of all outgoing rates makes the row sum $1$; the entries of $R$ are probabilities without time units. The diagonal is zero because the next actual change must leave $i$. With rates $2$ and $3$ toward two destinations, those destinations have probabilities $2/5$ and $3/5$. The [[CTMC指数停留时间|mean holding time]] is separately $1/5$ time unit. The joint wait-and-destination law is explained in [[生成矩阵分解|generator decomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个条件概率用于以正概率访问 $i$ 的情形。除以所有离开速率之和，使这一行的和变成 $1$；$R$ 的元素是没有时间单位的概率。对角元为零，因为下一次真正变化必须离开 $i$。若两条离开方向的速率为 $2$ 和 $3$，相应去向概率就是 $2/5$ 和 $3/5$；[[CTMC指数停留时间|平均停留时间]]另为 $1/5$ 个时间单位。等待与去向的联合规律见[[生成矩阵分解]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the sequence stops

If $q_i=0$, state $i$ is absorbing. There is no next genuine destination, and $q_{ij}/q_i$ is undefined. A convenient matrix convention sets $r_{ii}=1$ and $r_{ij}=0$ for $j\ne i$, repeating the final state forever in the completed discrete chain. Those repetitions are bookkeeping; they do not correspond to infinitely many physical jumps.

For a countable state space, genuine jump times can approach a finite limit, called [[CTMC爆炸|explosion]]. The sequence of states still describes each pre-explosion jump, but does not by itself prescribe what the continuous-time process does after that limit. Finite state spaces with finite rates do not have this problem. Merely knowing that each individual rate is finite is not enough in an infinite state space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*序列何时停止*

若 $q_i=0$，状态 $i$ 吸收，没有下一次真实去向，$q_{ij}/q_i$ 也没有定义。为方便矩阵运算，可以约定 $r_{ii}=1$、$r_{ij}=0$（$j\ne i$），在补全的离散链中永远重复最终状态。这些重复只是记账，不代表物理上发生无限次跳跃。

在可数状态空间中，真实跳时可能趋近有限极限，称为[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸]]。状态序列仍描述每次爆炸前跳跃，但它本身不能规定连续时间过程在这个极限之后怎样演化。有限状态且速率有限时没有这个问题；无限状态空间中，仅逐个速率有限还不够。

> [!warning] A clock event is not always a jump / 时钟事件不一定是真实跳跃
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[CTMC均匀化|Uniformization]] uses a common faster clock and permits an event to leave the state unchanged. For example, a candidate clock of rate $10$ with actual exit rate $5$ can retain the current state with probability $1/2$ at each event. Its discrete chain includes these virtual self-loops. The embedded **genuine-jump** chain removes them. Observing the CTMC at fixed time intervals produces yet another sequence, which can miss several changes between observations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[CTMC均匀化|均匀化]]使用统一的较快时钟，允许一次事件发生后状态仍不变。例如候选时钟速率为 $10$、真实出口率为 $5$ 时，每次候选事件可以用 $1/2$ 的概率保留原状态。对应离散链包含这些虚自环，而嵌入的**真实跳链**去掉了它们。在固定时间间隔观察 CTMC 得到的又是另一条序列，可能漏掉两次观察之间的多次变化。
