# 博弈的表示、策略与占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Representing Games, Strategies, and Dominance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把“我的结果取决于别人怎么做”的互动写成可分析的玩家、行动、信息、策略和收益。
> **具体锚点：** 囚徒困境中双方各自选择占优行动，却得到对双方都更差的结果，说明个体理性不保证集体最优。
> **核心难点：** 行动是某节点的一次选择，策略是对所有可能信息情形的完整计划；扩展式和策略式不能混为一张图。
> **为什么重要：** 均衡、承诺、信号、拍卖和机制设计都依赖先把博弈表示正确。
> **继续：** 静态互动进入 [[纳什均衡与混合策略|纳什均衡、混合策略与寡头竞争]]；有先后和信息集时进入 [[扩展式博弈、逆向归纳与子博弈完美均衡|扩展式博弈、子博弈精炼与序贯信念]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Represent interactions in which my outcome depends on what others do in terms of players, actions, information, strategies, and payoffs.
> **Concrete anchor:** In the Prisoner's Dilemma, both players choose a dominant action and yet obtain an outcome that is worse for both, showing that individual rationality does not ensure collective optimality.
> **Central difficulty:** An action is one choice at a node, while a strategy is a complete contingent plan for every possible information situation; strategic form and extensive form must not be treated as the same diagram.
> **Why it matters:** Equilibrium, commitment, signaling, auctions, and mechanism design all depend on representing the game correctly first.
> **Continue with:** For static interaction, go to [[纳什均衡与混合策略|Nash Equilibrium and Mixed Strategies]]; for sequencing and information sets, go to [[扩展式博弈、逆向归纳与子博弈完美均衡|Extensive-form Games, Backward Induction, and Subgame-perfect Equilibrium]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 博弈的五个基本问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Five Basic Questions for Representing a Game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析前先问：谁是玩家、何时能行动、每次可选什么、行动时知道什么、终局收益是什么。遗漏信息结构会改变整个博弈；同样的收益表配上不同观察顺序，均衡可能完全不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before analyzing a game, ask who the players are, when each can act, what actions are available, what each player knows when acting, and what the terminal payoffs are. Omitting the information structure changes the game itself; the same payoffs paired with a different observation order can produce entirely different equilibria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写模型时应把自然或随机事件也明确放入时序，并说明概率是否为共同知识。收益必须对应终局而非只写货币金额：风险、声誉、未来机会或非货币偏好若影响选择，也属于效用的一部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When writing the model, place chance events explicitly in the timeline and state whether their probabilities are common knowledge. Payoffs must correspond to terminal outcomes rather than merely monetary amounts: risk, reputation, future opportunities, and non-monetary preferences belong in utility whenever they affect choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 策略式与扩展式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strategic Form and Extensive Form*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

策略式用玩家集合、每人的策略集合和收益函数描述同时或不关心时序的互动。扩展式显式表示节点、行动顺序、信息集和终局收益。把扩展式转成策略式时，每名玩家的纯策略必须对其所有信息集都指定行动，包括均衡路径外不会到达的位置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strategic form describes an interaction through a set of players, each player's strategy set, and payoff functions when moves are simultaneous or timing is irrelevant. Extensive form explicitly represents nodes, the order of actions, information sets, and terminal payoffs. When converting an extensive-form game into strategic form, each player's pure strategy must prescribe an action at every information set, including those never reached on the equilibrium path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若玩家在两个彼此独立的信息集上各有两个行动，他就有 $2\times2=4$ 个纯策略，而不是两个。判断策略数量时对同一玩家各信息集的行动数相乘，再在收益矩阵中比较完整计划；这是动态博弈转成策略式时最常见的漏项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a player has two actions at each of two separate information sets, that player has $2\times2=4$ pure strategies, not two. Count strategies by multiplying the number of actions across that player's information sets and compare complete plans in the payoff matrix. Missing these contingent choices is the most common error when converting a dynamic game into strategic form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 严格与弱占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict and Weak Dominance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一策略对对手每种策略都给更高收益，它严格占优；若从不更差且至少一处更好，它弱占优。严格劣势策略可安全迭代删除且次序不影响保留下来的 Nash 均衡集合；弱劣势删除可能依赖次序并误删均衡，必须谨慎。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strategy is strictly dominant if it yields a higher payoff against every strategy of the opponents; it is weakly dominant if it is never worse and is better somewhere. Strictly dominated strategies can be iteratively eliminated safely, and the order does not affect the surviving Nash equilibria. Eliminating weakly dominated strategies can depend on the order and can remove equilibria, so it requires care.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较占优时必须使用同一个玩家的收益，并在对手每一种可能策略下逐列或逐行比较。一个策略只在“多数情形”更好不构成占优；某策略不是占优策略，也不表示它不可能成为最佳反应或均衡策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To test dominance, compare the same player's payoffs against every possible strategy of the opponents, column by column or row by row. Being better in most cases is not dominance. Conversely, a strategy that is not dominant may still be a best response or part of an equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 理性、共同知识与预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rationality, Common Knowledge, and Prediction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“玩家理性”只说其在信念下优化，不自动说明信念正确。迭代删除常隐含更高阶的理性共同知识。博弈模型的结论应说明依赖的是占优、最佳反应、信念一致还是时序理性，而不是笼统说“理性人会这样做”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player rationality says only that a player optimizes given beliefs; it does not make those beliefs correct. Iterated elimination often relies on higher-order common knowledge of rationality. A game-theoretic conclusion should state whether it rests on dominance, best responses, belief consistency, or sequential rationality, rather than vaguely asserting that “a rational person would do this.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 效率与均衡不是一回事
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Efficiency and Equilibrium Are Different Questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Pareto 效率比较可行结果是否还能让某人更好且无人更差；均衡问单方偏离是否有利。一个结果可以是均衡却低效，也可以高效却不是均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pareto efficiency asks whether another feasible outcome could make someone better off without making anyone worse off; equilibrium asks whether a unilateral deviation is profitable. An outcome can be an inefficient equilibrium or an efficient non-equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：囚徒困境
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: The Prisoner's Dilemma*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设合作—合作收益为 $(3,3)$，背叛—合作为 $(5,0)$，合作—背叛为 $(0,5)$，背叛—背叛为 $(1,1)$。无论对手做什么，背叛都严格优于合作，因此 $(背叛,背叛)$ 是占优策略均衡；但 $(合作,合作)$ 让双方都更好。模型没有说玩家愚蠢，而是说明个人偏离激励阻止了可取的集体结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let mutual cooperation yield $(3,3)$, defection against cooperation yield $(5,0)$, cooperation against defection yield $(0,5)$, and mutual defection yield $(1,1)$. Defection strictly dominates cooperation regardless of the opponent's action, so $(Defect,Defect)$ is the dominant-strategy equilibrium. Yet $(Cooperate,Cooperate)$ makes both players better off. The model does not say that players are foolish; it shows how unilateral incentives block a collectively preferable outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若收益矩阵与博弈树给出不同结论，先检查是否漏写了路径外行动或信息集；若迭代删除的结果依赖顺序，检查是否误删弱劣势策略；若把一个高总收益结果称为“均衡”，必须重新逐个玩家验证单边偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the payoff matrix and the game tree appear to give different conclusions, check whether off-path actions or information sets were omitted. If iterated elimination depends on the order, verify that weak rather than strict dominance was used. If a high-total-payoff outcome is labeled an equilibrium, test unilateral deviations player by player again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么扩展式博弈中的策略必须包含未到达节点的行动？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must a strategy in an extensive-form game include actions at unreached nodes?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 策略是完整应对计划；均衡和精炼要比较偏离后会发生什么，因此必须定义离开当前路径后的行动。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A strategy is a complete contingent plan. Equilibrium and refinement compare what happens after deviations, so actions away from the current path must be defined.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 弱劣势策略为什么不能像严格劣势那样随意删除？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can weakly dominated strategies not be eliminated as freely as strictly dominated strategies?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 弱比较含有收益相等情形，删除某策略会改变其他玩家的最佳反应集合，结果可能依赖删除顺序并丢失 Nash 均衡。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Weak comparisons include payoff ties. Removing one strategy can change other players' best-response sets, so the result may depend on deletion order and may discard a Nash equilibrium.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 囚徒困境说明了均衡与效率的什么区别？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does the Prisoner's Dilemma show about the difference between equilibrium and efficiency?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 双方的占优策略构成 Nash 均衡，但存在让双方都更好的合作结果；单方激励稳定不等于集体福利最大。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The dominant strategies form a Nash equilibrium, yet a cooperative outcome makes both players better off. Stability against unilateral deviation does not maximize collective welfare.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
