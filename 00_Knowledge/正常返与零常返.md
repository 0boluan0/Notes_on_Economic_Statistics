# Positive and null recurrence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正常返与零常返*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing that a state will be revisited with probability one leaves another question: **how large is the average waiting time?** Positive recurrence means that this average is finite. Null recurrence means that return is still almost sure, but rare, very long excursions make the average infinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道某状态会以概率一再次被访问以后，还要问：**平均等多久？** 正常返表示这个平均值有限；零常返仍然几乎必定返回，但少见而极长的远游使平均值无穷。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a finite or countable [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous Markov chain]]. Starting at $i$, let $\tau_i^+=\inf\{n\ge1:X_n=i\}$ be the first positive return time, set to infinity on no return. The notation $E_i$ means expectation for that starting state. First check [[常返与暂态|recurrence]], namely $\Pr_i(\tau_i^+<\infty)=1$. Among recurrent states,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑有限或可数状态的[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次 Markov 链]]。从 $i$ 出发，令 $\tau_i^+=\inf\{n\ge1:X_n=i\}$ 为首次正回返时间，不回返时取无穷；$E_i$ 表示从该状态出发的期望。首先检查[[常返与暂态|常返]]，即 $\Pr_i(\tau_i^+<\infty)=1$。在常返状态中，

> [!abstract] Classify the mean after the return probability · 先判断回返概率，再判断均值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write $m_i=E_i\tau_i^+$. The state is **positive recurrent** if $m_i<\infty$, and **null recurrent** if $m_i=\infty$. Since $\tau_i^+\ge1$, a finite $m_i$ is at least one. “Null” does not mean a zero waiting time or zero chance of return.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 记 $m_i=E_i\tau_i^+$。$m_i<\infty$ 称为**正常返**，$m_i=\infty$ 称为**零常返**。由于 $\tau_i^+\ge1$，有限的 $m_i$ 至少为一。“零”并不是等待时间为零，也不是回返概率为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A transient state also has $E_i\tau_i^+=\infty$ under this extended-time convention, because there is positive probability of never returning. It is not null recurrent. Conversely, a finite mean return time already forces return with probability one. The three possibilities are transient, null recurrent and positive recurrent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按这个扩展时间约定，暂态状态也有 $E_i\tau_i^+=\infty$，因为它有正概率永远不回来；但它不是零常返。反过来，平均回返时间有限已经强制回返概率为一。三种类型分别是暂态、零常返和正常返。

> [!example] Returning every second step · 每两步返回一次
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the chain $0\to1\to0\to\cdots$, the first positive return to either starting state is exactly two steps. Thus $m_0=m_1=2$ and both states are positive recurrent, despite having [[状态周期|period]] two. Finite mean return does not require aperiodicity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $0\to1\to0\to\cdots$ 这条链，从任一状态出发，首次正回返都恰好需要两步。因此 $m_0=m_1=2$，两状态都正常返，虽然[[状态周期|周期]]为二。有限平均回返不要求非周期。

## A walk that returns almost surely but has infinite mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*必然回来，平均却无穷的随机游走*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On all integers $\mathbb Z$, move one unit left or right at each step, independently with probabilities $1/2,1/2$. To understand a return to $0$, first start at $1$. Stop temporarily when the walk reaches either $0$ or a positive integer $N>1$. The finite-interval [[命中概率第一步方程|hitting equations]] give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在全部整数 $\mathbb Z$ 上，每一步独立地以各 $1/2$ 的概率向左或向右一格。为了理解返回 $0$，先从 $1$ 出发，暂时在首次到达 $0$ 或正整数 $N>1$ 时停止。有限区间的[[命中概率第一步方程]]给出

$$\Pr_1(\text{hit }0\text{ before }N)=1-\frac1N.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a start $k\in\{0,\ldots,N\}$, the solution is $h(k)=1-k/N$: it has boundary values $h(0)=1,h(N)=0$ and satisfies $h(k)=\tfrac12h(k-1)+\tfrac12h(k+1)$. Hitting $0$ before $N$ is one way to ever hit $0$, so the latter probability is at least $1-1/N$ for every $N$. Letting $N$ grow proves that it equals one. The same argument applies from $-1$ by reflection, hence starting at $0$ and taking its first step leads to an almost-sure positive return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $k\in\{0,\ldots,N\}$ 出发，解为 $h(k)=1-k/N$：边界值是 $h(0)=1,h(N)=0$，内部满足 $h(k)=\tfrac12h(k-1)+\tfrac12h(k+1)$。先于 $N$ 到达 $0$ 是最终到达 $0$ 的一种方式，因此最终命中概率对每个 $N$ 都至少为 $1-1/N$。让 $N$ 增大就证明该概率为一。从 $-1$ 出发可作镜像论证，所以从 $0$ 踏出第一步后，也几乎必然在正时刻回来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now let $T_{\{0,N\}}$ be the time to reach either boundary. The [[期望命中时间方程|mean-time equations]] have solution $e(k)=k(N-k)$: its boundary values are zero and $e(k)=1+\tfrac12e(k-1)+\tfrac12e(k+1)$. Therefore $E_1T_{\{0,N\}}=N-1$. These finite-interval means are finite because, in blocks of $N$ steps, following only left steps gives a fixed positive chance to exit. On each path $T_{\{0,N\}}$ is no greater than the time $H_0$ to hit $0$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再令 $T_{\{0,N\}}$ 为到达任一边界的时间。[[期望命中时间方程]]的解为 $e(k)=k(N-k)$：边界为零，内部满足 $e(k)=1+\tfrac12e(k-1)+\tfrac12e(k+1)$，所以 $E_1T_{\{0,N\}}=N-1$。有限区间的这些均值确实有限，因为每一段 $N$ 步里，连续向左走给出了一个固定正概率的退出机会。逐条路径看，到任一边界的时间不大于命中 $0$ 的时间 $H_0$，于是

$$E_1H_0\ge N-1\quad\text{for every }N>1,\qquad E_1H_0=\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The return from $0$ consists of its first step and such an excursion from $1$ or $-1$. Its mean is infinite, although the return probability is one. This proves that the symmetric integer walk is null recurrent. The example is precisely the distinction between [[必达不推有限期望|almost-sure arrival and finite mean waiting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $0$ 返回，要先走一步，再经历从 $1$ 或 $-1$ 开始的这类远游。虽然回返概率为一，平均时间却无穷，因此整数线上对称随机游走是零常返。这正是[[必达不推有限期望|几乎必达到与有限平均等待]]之间的区别。

## Why finite chains have no null recurrent states
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么有限链不会出现零常返状态*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every recurrent state of a finite chain lies in a [[有限链类分解|finite closed communicating class]]. Within that class, choose a positive-probability route to the chosen state from every state, including a positive-length return route from the chosen state itself. Finiteness gives a common maximum route length $L$ and a positive lower bound $\varepsilon$ on the chance of return within such a block. Thus the probability of waiting through $k$ blocks is at most $(1-\varepsilon)^k$, which has a finite sum. The mean return time is finite. In particular, all states of a finite [[不可约链|irreducible chain]] are positive recurrent; a finite chain may still have transient states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限链中的每个常返状态，都位于一个[[有限链类分解|有限闭沟通类]]内。从类内每个状态选择一条正概率路线到目标状态，并为目标状态自身选择一条正长度的回返路线；有限性让这些路线有共同的最大长度 $L$，并让每段内回返机会有正的共同下界 $\varepsilon$。于是，等过 $k$ 段仍未返回的概率不超过 $(1-\varepsilon)^k$，其和有限，平均回返时间也有限。特别地，有限[[不可约链]]的所有状态都正常返；有限链仍然可以包含暂态状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite or countable homogeneous chains, states in one communicating class share the same type: transient, positive recurrent or null recurrent. This is a theorem about return behaviour, not merely the graph definition of communication. The quantitative connection between finite mean return and stationary probability is developed in [[Kac回返公式|Kac’s return formula]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限或可数齐次链，同一沟通类内的状态类型一致：全为暂态、全为正常返，或全为零常返。这是关于回返行为的定理，不是仅靠互通的图定义就成立的解释。有限平均回返与平稳概率之间的定量关系，见[[Kac回返公式]]。
