# Main effects and reference values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*交互模型中的主效应与参照值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coefficient beside a single variable can change meaning when a product term is present. In an interaction model, that coefficient describes a comparison at a specified reference setting of the other variable. Before interpreting its magnitude or p-value, name that setting. Here “main effect” means the coefficient of a lower-order term under the stated coding, not every software procedure called a main-effect test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有乘积项时，单独一个变量旁边的系数不再代表处处相同的变化：它描述另一变量处于指定参照状态时的比较。读大小或 p 值之前，要先说清这个状态。本卡的“主效应”指给定编码下低阶项的系数，并不涵盖软件中所有被称为主效应检验的程序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the reference before reading the coefficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先读参照，再读系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[类别连续交互|indicator–continuous model]], let $X$ be experience and let $D=0,1$ distinguish a reference and comparison group. Write a conditional-mean model, with integrable outcome $Y$, as
$$m(x,d)=\beta_0+\beta_Xx+\beta_Dd+\beta_{XD}xd.$$
A **simple effect** is a change or difference at a fixed value of the other predictor. Thus $\beta_X$ is the slope in group $D=0$, while the slope in group $D=1$ is $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$. The coefficient $\beta_D$ is the group gap at $X=0$; at $X=x$ that gap is $\Delta(x)=\beta_D+x\beta_{XD}$. If the expression is only a fitted approximation, these are fitted rather than automatically true mean contrasts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[类别连续交互]]模型中，令 $X$ 为经验，$D=0,1$ 区分基准组与比较组；对可积结果 $Y$，写出上式条件均值模型。**简单效应**就是固定另一解释变量取值时的变化或差异。因此 $\beta_X$ 是 $D=0$ 组斜率，$D=1$ 组斜率是 $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$；$\beta_D$ 是 $X=0$ 时的组差，$X=x$ 时则是 $\Delta(x)=\beta_D+x\beta_{XD}$。如果式子只是拟合近似，这些是拟合对比，并不自动等于真实均值对比。

> [!example] Zero at the reference need not mean zero elsewhere · 参照点为零，其他位置未必为零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $m(x,d)=10+2x+0d+0.5xd$, the groups have the same mean $10$ at $x=0$. At $x=10$, their means are $30$ and $35$, so the gap is $5$. The coefficient on $D$ is exactly zero, yet the group difference is not zero everywhere. Similarly, a zero $\beta_X$ would give a flat reference-group line while the comparison-group line could still have slope $\beta_{XD}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $m(x,d)=10+2x+0d+0.5xd$，在 $x=0$ 时两组均值都是 $10$；在 $x=10$ 时分别为 $30$、$35$，差为 $5$。$D$ 系数恰好为零，仍不表示所有经验水平都没有组差。同理，$\beta_X=0$ 只会让基准组的线水平，比较组仍可有斜率 $\beta_{XD}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A statistically nonsignificant estimate is weaker still: under the chosen valid test, it means that the data have not rejected its particular [[零假设|null hypothesis]], not that the parameter has been proved zero. Testing $\beta_D=0$ concerns $x=0$ only; testing $\beta_D+10\beta_{XD}=0$ concerns ten years. The second test needs [[线性组合推断|the combination’s uncertainty]], not two separate coefficient p-values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计值“统计不显著”的含义还要更弱：在所采用的有效检验下，数据没有拒绝相应[[零假设]]，并不是证明参数为零。检验 $\beta_D=0$ 只针对 $x=0$，检验 $\beta_D+10\beta_{XD}=0$ 才针对十年；后者需要[[线性组合推断|组合本身的不确定性]]，不能读取两个单项 p 值代替。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Move the reference point without changing the lines
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*移动参照点，保持两条线不变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a fixed meaningful value $c$ and define the centred variable $X_c=X-c$. Then $X=X_c+c$. Substitution into the **complete** model gives
$$m= (\beta_0+c\beta_X)+\beta_XX_c
 +(\beta_D+c\beta_{XD})D+\beta_{XD}X_cD.$$
The new intercept is the reference-group mean at $X=c$. The new coefficient on $D$ is the group gap at $X=c$. Both slopes and the interaction coefficient are unchanged. The intercept, $X_c$, $D$ and $X_cD$ all remain present; [[交互层级原则|the hierarchy principle]] explains why deleting one first can break this equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选定固定且有意义的 $c$，定义中心化变量 $X_c=X-c$，即 $X=X_c+c$。代入**完整**模型得到上式：新截距是 $X=c$ 时的基准组均值，新 $D$ 系数是 $X=c$ 时的组差；两组斜率与交互系数不变。这里仍保留截距、$X_c$、$D$ 和 $X_cD$；[[交互层级原则]]说明先删掉某项为什么可能破坏这种等价。

> [!example] Ten years becomes zero on the new scale · 新刻度的零点是十年
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the fitted wage rule $12+0.80X-1.50D-0.25XD$, set $X_c=X-10$. The same rule becomes
> $$20+0.80X_c-4D-0.25X_cD.$$
> At $X_c=0$, predicted wages are still $20$ and $16$. The $D$ coefficient changes from $-1.50$ to $-4$ because it now describes a different experience level. It does not mean that the fitted evidence changed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把工资拟合规则 $12+0.80X-1.50D-0.25XD$ 的经验改为 $X_c=X-10$，同一规则就变成上式。在 $X_c=0$ 时，预测仍为 $20$、$16$；$D$ 系数从 $-1.50$ 变成 $-4$，是因为它现在描述不同的经验点，并不表示拟合证据改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the same observations, weights and unconstrained least-squares criterion, the old and new design columns span the same [[列空间|column space]], so the fitted values and residuals are identical. A full-rank design gives uniquely corresponding coefficient estimates; in a rank-deficient design, individual coefficients may remain nonunique. A fixed substantive contrast and its coherently transformed covariance give the same inference. Testing the newly named $D$ coefficient, however, asks a different question from testing the old one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在观察、权重与无约束最小二乘准则相同的情况下，新旧设计列张成相同[[列空间]]，所以拟合值与残差相同。设计满秩时，系数估计唯一对应；秩亏时单个系数仍可能不唯一。同一个实质对比与一致转换的协方差会给出相同推断，但检验新命名的 $D$ 系数，问的已经不是检验旧系数时的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $c$ for interpretation and support, rather than to obtain an attractive p-value. If one group is observed only at $0$–$5$ years and the other at $15$–$20$, setting $c=10$ creates no ten-year observations. [[中心化共线性边界|Centring]] is an algebraic re-expression, not additional information or a cure for missing [[交互稀疏支持|common support]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择 $c$ 应考虑解释与数据支持，而不是寻找好看的 p 值。如果一组只观察到 $0$–$5$ 年，另一组只观察到 $15$–$20$ 年，令 $c=10$ 不会产生十年处的观察。[[中心化共线性边界|中心化]]是代数改写，不是新增信息，也不能补足缺失的[[交互稀疏支持|共同支持]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Changing a group reference changes a simple comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*更换类别基准会更换简单比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[类别类别交互|two-indicator model]], let the four mean values be $m(0,0)=2$, $m(1,0)=5$, $m(0,1)=4$ and $m(1,1)=10$. With the second indicator’s reference at $0$, the coefficient for changing the first from $0$ to $1$ is $5-2=3$. Put the second indicator’s reference at $1$ instead: the same-direction coefficient becomes $10-4=6$. The four means stay fixed; the simple comparison has moved to another layer. [[基准组重编码|Reference-group recoding]] explains the underlying change of coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[类别类别交互|两个指示变量的模型]]中，设四格均值为 $m(0,0)=2$、$m(1,0)=5$、$m(0,1)=4$、$m(1,1)=10$。以第二变量的 $0$ 层为参照时，第一变量从 $0$ 到 $1$ 的系数是 $5-2=3$；把第二变量的参照改为 $1$，同方向系数变为 $10-4=6$。四格均值不变，简单比较移到了另一层。[[基准组重编码]]解释这种坐标改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An average over the two layers is another target: equal weighting gives $(3+6)/2=4.5$, while other target weights can give another average. A software ANOVA main-effect test may concern such a specified average or another contrast, rather than a single treatment-coded coefficient. Always state the coding, reference values and tested contrast. “Effect” here is a statistical comparison; a [[条件均值因果边界|causal interpretation]] needs its own argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跨两层取平均又是另一个目标：等权平均为 $(3+6)/2=4.5$，其他目标权重可能给出不同平均。软件的 ANOVA 主效应检验可能针对这种指定平均或其他对比，而不是一个基准编码系数。应始终说明编码、参照值与检验对比。这里的“效应”是统计比较，[[条件均值因果边界|因果解释]]需要另有依据。

- The centring identities and numerical examples are checked by substitution. [[列空间|Column space]] and [[基准组重编码|recoding]] explain fitted-value invariance; [[线性组合推断|linear-combination inference]] supplies the covariance transformation.

- Stata 官方 FAQ 核对不同基准层对应不同简单对比，以及单系数检验与 ANOVA 主效应检验的区别。
- Penn State 第 8.6–8.7 节核对依组变化的斜率与指定另一解释变量取值的重要性；MIT 原 PDF 第 19–28 页核对两个指示变量的基准解释。
- 中心化恒等式与数值例子均代入核算；列空间、换基准与线性组合推断分别支撑拟合值不变及协方差转换。
