# 劳动力成为资本主义生产的商品以劳动者的双重自由为条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Labour-power becomes a commodity for capitalist production under the condition of the worker's double freedom*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Marx 用**双重自由**说明[[Marx劳动力|劳动力]]成为资本主义生产的商品所需的社会条件：劳动者是自身能力的所有者，能够自由地按期限出售其使用；同时，劳动者与独立生产所需的生产资料和生活条件分离，必须依靠出售劳动力维持生活。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marx uses **double freedom** to describe the social conditions under which [[Marx劳动力|labour-power]] becomes a commodity for capitalist production. Workers own their capacity and can freely sell its use for a limited period; at the same time, separation from the means of production and subsistence needed for independent production makes them dependent on selling it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一层自由要求限期出售：劳动者暂时把能力交给买者使用，持续保有对自身能力的所有权；若把自己一次性永久卖掉，原文所分析的自由劳动力商品关系就改变了。第二层自由则涉及生产条件：独立制鞋者要有皮革、工具，以及做鞋和等待售出期间的生活资料，才能出售自己生产的鞋。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first freedom requires a sale for a limited period: the worker temporarily places the capacity at the buyer's disposal while retaining ownership of it. A permanent sale of the person would change the free-labour commodity relation analysed in the text. The second freedom concerns production conditions. An independent shoemaker needs leather, tools and subsistence while making and selling shoes in order to sell that product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，市场上的法律平等与生产条件的不对称可以同时存在：双方以商品所有者身份订约，而一方拥有用于预付的[[Marx资本|货币资本]]，另一方依赖出卖劳动能力。Marx 把这种关系视为历史发展的结果；“双重自由”是对资本主义生产关系的结构说明，不是以个人是否有少量财产为依据的就业分类规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Legal equality in the market can therefore coexist with unequal production conditions. Both parties contract as commodity owners, while one holds [[Marx资本|money capital]] to advance and the other depends on selling working capacity. Marx treats this relation as historically produced. Double freedom explains the structure of capitalist production relations; it is not an employment-classification rule based on whether an individual owns a few assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
