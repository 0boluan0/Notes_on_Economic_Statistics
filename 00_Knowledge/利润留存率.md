# 利润留存率是当期净利润中未作为相应股利分配的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The earnings retention ratio is the share of current net income not distributed as corresponding dividends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润留存率 $b$ 衡量企业本期赚到的净利润有多大比例留在企业。设同一主体、同一权益范围的本期净利润为 $NI$，按同一期间政策分配的股利为 $Div$，在 $NI\ne0$ 时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The retention ratio $b$ measures the share of current net income kept in the business. Let $NI$ and $Div$ refer to the same entity, equity scope, and period's distribution policy. For $NI\ne0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$b=\frac{NI-Div}{NI}=1-\frac{Div}{NI}=1-d.$$

其中 $d$ 为股利支付率。净利润 100、按本期利润分配股利 30，留存利润是 70，留存率是 70%。**70 是本期增加额，不是历年留存收益余额的增长率**；如果期初留存收益是 500，本期增加 70 对应余额增长 14%，不是 70%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $d$ is the payout ratio. Net income of 100 and dividends of 30 leave retained income of 70, a 70% retention ratio. The 70 is a current addition, not the growth rate of accumulated retained earnings. Starting retained earnings of 500 would increase by 14%, not 70%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $b$ 解释成零至一之间的“利润分配份额”，需要净利润为正、股利不超过相应利润。亏损期、特别股利或动用以前年度积累分红，可能得到负值或失去直观比例含义；零利润时比率未定义。预测还应说明股利是按当期利润计提还是在下一期支付，实际现金时点由[[现金预算]]承接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interpreting $b$ as a share between zero and one requires positive profit and dividends no larger than that profit. Losses, special dividends, or distributions from prior accumulations can produce negative or unintuitive ratios; zero profit makes the ratio undefined. Specify whether dividends relate to current earnings or are paid later, and put payment timing in the [[现金预算|cash budget]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本比率不是[[再投资增长关系|经营再投资额占 NOPAT 的比例]]。留存利润可能用于应收、存货、设备或还债；若要知道钱实际在哪里，见[[留存收益不是现金]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not the [[再投资增长关系|operating reinvestment-to-NOPAT ratio]]. Retained income may support receivables, inventory, equipment, or debt repayment; see [[留存收益不是现金|why retained earnings are not cash]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
