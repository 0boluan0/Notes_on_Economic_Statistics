# 债务可持续性要求可信的财政与融资路径能够持续履约而不依赖不可行的调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Debt sustainability requires a credible fiscal and financing path that services obligations without infeasible adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可持续性不是一个单独的“安全债务率”。它问：在合理基准及冲击下，政府能否用可实现的初级余额、增长和融资安排履行债务，同时避免不可承受的经济与政治代价？因此，既要看长期偿付能力，也要看债务到期时有没有资金续接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sustainability is not one universal safe debt ratio. It asks whether feasible primary balances, growth and financing can service debt under a realistic baseline and shocks without intolerable economic or political costs. Long-run repayment capacity and near-term funding both matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断时先用[[债务率动态]]说明预测为何上升或下降，再检查[[债务稳定初级余额|需要的盈余]]是否可信。随后按到期表检查[[公债再融资风险]]，并加入汇率、利率、增长和[[财政或有负债]]情景。情景参数之间有关联，不能一边假定大幅财政调整、一边无解释地保持增长和融资成本完全不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Explain the projected path through [[债务率动态|debt dynamics]], then test the feasibility of the [[债务稳定初级余额|required surplus]]. Check [[公债再融资风险|rollover needs]] and scenarios for currency, interest, growth and [[财政或有负债|contingencies]]. Explain joint feedbacks rather than combining large adjustment with unchanged growth and funding costs by assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，基准预测靠连续十年维持GDP的5%初级盈余才稳定债务，不能仅因电子表格曲线平了就验收；还需说明收入、支出、执行和政治支持从哪里来。反过来，一次冲击使债务率上升也不自动证明不可持续：已有现金缓冲、后续调整和可用融资可能改变结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A spreadsheet requiring primary surpluses of 5% of GDP for ten years is not validated merely by a flat debt curve: specify the revenue, expenditure, implementation and political basis. Nor does one shock-induced rise prove unsustainability; buffers, subsequent adjustment and financing can change the assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [IMF, DSA Guidance 2013](https://www.imf.org/external/np/pp/eng/2013/050913.pdf)，p.4 §2–3：支持现实初级余额、融资结构、再融资风险和冲击路径的联合判断；此处提炼分析问题，不沿用该年程序或数值阈值。5%十年为假设例，不是经验安全线。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The cited conceptual discussion supports joint assessment of fiscal feasibility, financing and shocks. No historical procedural threshold is imposed; the surplus path is illustrative.
