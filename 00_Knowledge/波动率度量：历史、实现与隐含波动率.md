# 波动率度量：历史、实现与隐含波动率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Volatility Measurement: Historical, Realized, and Implied Volatility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 区分用过去收益估计的波动、用高频路径汇总的实现波动、从期权价格反推的风险中性隐含波动，以及对未来真实波动的条件预测。
> **具体锚点：** 同一天同一资产可以有 20% 历史年化波动、25% 期权隐含波动和一个 GARCH 下一日预测；三者回答的问题不同，不是彼此谁“算错了”。
> **核心难点：** 年化、收益频率、均值处理、微观结构噪声、跳跃和风险溢价都会改变数字；隐含波动不是未来实现波动的无偏预测。
> **为什么重要：** 期权定价、VaR、保证金、仓位规模和波动率交易都依赖波动输入，混用口径会直接制造虚假风险与收益。
> **继续：** 时变预测模型见 [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH]]；多资产共同变化见 [[相关性、Copula 与尾部依赖]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It distinguishes volatility estimated from past returns, realized volatility aggregated from a high-frequency path, risk-neutral implied volatility backed out from option prices, and conditional forecasts of future real-world volatility.
> **Concrete anchor:** On the same day for the same asset, annualized historical volatility can be 20%, option-implied volatility 25%, and a GARCH one-day forecast another number. They answer different questions rather than proving that one is wrong.
> **Central difficulty:** Annualization, return frequency, mean treatment, microstructure noise, jumps, and risk premium all change the number. Implied volatility is not an unbiased forecast of future realized volatility.
> **Why it matters:** Option pricing, VaR, margin, position sizing, and volatility trading all depend on volatility inputs, and mixing conventions creates false risk and return directly.
> **Continue:** For time-varying forecast models, see [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH|Conditional Heteroskedasticity: ARCH and GARCH]]. For multi-asset co-movement, see [[相关性、Copula 与尾部依赖|Correlation, Copulas, and Tail Dependence]].

## 先问数字从哪里来、给哪个测度用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First ask where the number comes from and under which measure it is used*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史和实现波动是观察真实世界收益路径后的统计量；条件波动是给定过去信息对下一期真实世界方差的预测；隐含波动是在选定期权定价模型下使模型价格等于市场价格的参数，处于风险中性定价环境。四者可相互比较，但不能无条件替换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical and realized volatility are statistics computed from observed real-world return paths. Conditional volatility forecasts next-period real-world variance given past information. Implied volatility is the parameter that makes a chosen option-pricing model match a market price under risk-neutral valuation. The four can be compared but not substituted unconditionally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 波动率估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Volatility estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史波动常由收益平方平均年化，滚动窗口给等权。隐含波动由期权价格反推出风险中性定价参数，不等于未来实现波动的无偏预测。高频实现波动需处理微观结构噪声。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical volatility is often estimated by averaging squared returns and annualizing, with a rolling window assigning equal weights. Implied volatility is backed out from option prices as a risk-neutral pricing parameter and is not an unbiased forecast of future realized volatility. High-frequency realized volatility must address market-microstructure noise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若日对数收益标准差为 1%，在独立同分布近似下按 252 个交易日年化为 $1\%\sqrt{252}\approx15.87\%$。若收益有自相关、波动聚集或长期期限，平方根年化只是约定近似，必须用适合期限的动态模型检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If daily log-return standard deviation is 1%, then under an IID approximation annualization over 252 trading days gives $1\%\sqrt{252}\approx15.87\%$. With return autocorrelation, volatility clustering, or long horizons, square-root annualization is only an approximation and must be tested with horizon-appropriate dynamics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实现方差常近似为日内高频收益平方和，采样太稀会丢信息，太密则买卖价跳动、异步成交和价格离散产生噪声。实际应用会比较采样频率、使用 realized kernels 或其他稳健方法，而不是默认最高频最好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Realized variance is often approximated by summing squared intraday returns. Sampling too sparsely loses information, while sampling too frequently introduces bid–ask bounce, asynchronous trading, and price discreteness. Applications compare frequencies or use realized kernels and other robust methods rather than assuming the highest frequency is best.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## EWMA 与 GARCH
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*EWMA and GARCH*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

EWMA 递推 $\sigma_t^2=\lambda\sigma_{t-1}^2+(1-\lambda)r_{t-1}^2$，权重指数衰减且无固定长期均值。GARCH 加常数和更一般持久性。模型选择看标准化残差、预测和结构变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EWMA follows $\sigma_t^2=\lambda\sigma_{t-1}^2+(1-\lambda)r_{t-1}^2$, with exponentially decaying weights and no fixed long-run mean. GARCH adds a constant and more general persistence. Model choice should use standardized residuals, forecast performance, and structural change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\lambda=0.94$、昨日方差为 $0.0001$、昨日收益为 2%，新方差为 $0.94\times0.0001+0.06\times0.02^2=0.000118$，新日波动约 1.086%。大收益提高预测波动，影响随后按 $\lambda$ 逐步衰减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\lambda=0.94$, yesterday's variance was $0.0001$, and yesterday's return was 2%, the new variance is $0.94\times0.0001+0.06\times0.02^2=0.000118$, giving daily volatility about 1.086%. A large return raises forecast volatility, and its effect then decays at rate $\lambda$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里保留 EWMA 与 GARCH 的风险管理定位；模型条件、平稳性与估计细节集中在 [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH]]，避免在两处维护不同推导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This file retains the risk-management role of EWMA and GARCH. Model conditions, stationarity, and estimation detail live in [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH|Conditional Heteroskedasticity: ARCH and GARCH]] to avoid maintaining conflicting derivations in two places.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 隐含波动率曲面
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The implied-volatility surface*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同执行价和期限的期权通常有不同隐含波动，形成 skew/smile 与期限结构；一个“该股票隐含波动 25%”若没有执行价、期限、期权类型和模型便不完整。曲面变化还应区分 sticky-strike、sticky-delta 等参数化与无套利约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Options at different strikes and maturities usually have different implied volatilities, forming a skew or smile and a term structure. “This stock's implied volatility is 25%” is incomplete without strike, maturity, option type, and model. Surface changes also require parameterization choices such as sticky strike or sticky delta and no-arbitrage constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 预测验证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Forecast validation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

波动预测应使用未来实现方差或与业务目标一致的代理，在滚动/扩展窗口做时序外样本比较。除 MSE/QLIKE 等损失，还要检查 VaR exceptions、区间覆盖和经济决策表现；看样本内拟合不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volatility forecasts should be evaluated against future realized variance or a proxy aligned with the business objective in rolling or expanding time-series out-of-sample tests. Beyond losses such as MSE or QLIKE, inspect VaR exceptions, interval coverage, and decision performance; in-sample fit is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 将日波动直接乘 252 而不是在方差可加近似下乘 $\sqrt{252}$。
- 把隐含波动称为市场对未来实现波动的纯预期，忽略风险溢价和模型。
- 高频采样越密越信任，未处理微观结构噪声。
- 在全样本选 EWMA/GARCH 参数，再用同一时期声称预测优越。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Multiplying daily volatility by 252 rather than by $\sqrt{252}$ under the variance-additivity approximation.
- Calling implied volatility a pure market expectation of future realized volatility while ignoring risk premium and model.
- Trusting denser high-frequency sampling automatically without treating microstructure noise.
- Selecting EWMA or GARCH parameters on the full sample and claiming forecast superiority on the same period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：历史波动、实现波动与隐含波动为什么可以同时不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why can historical, realized, and implied volatility all differ at the same time?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 历史波动总结选定过去窗口，实现波动总结某个已发生高频路径，隐含波动从期权价格和风险中性模型反推；信息集、时期和测度不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Historical volatility summarizes a chosen past window, realized volatility summarizes a realized high-frequency path, and implied volatility is inferred from option prices under a risk-neutral model. Their information sets, periods, and measures differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### EWMA 中更小的 $\lambda$ 会怎样？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does a smaller $\lambda$ do in EWMA?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 给最新平方收益更高权重，响应冲击更快但预测更噪，旧冲击衰减也更快。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It gives the newest squared return more weight, responds faster but more noisily, and makes old shocks decay more quickly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么最高频数据不一定给出最好实现波动？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why need the highest-frequency data not produce the best realized-volatility estimate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 极高频价格含买卖价跳动、离散报价和异步交易噪声，平方和会把这些非有效价格变化误作经济波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Ultra-high-frequency prices contain bid–ask bounce, discrete quotes, and asynchronous-trading noise, so squared returns misclassify non-efficient-price movement as economic volatility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
