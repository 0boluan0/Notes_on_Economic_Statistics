# 公共项目评价范围界定纳入核算的人群和影响持续的时期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The scope of project appraisal defines whose impacts count and over what time horizon.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“社会净收益”总是相对于一个明确范围说的：纳入本市居民、全国居民还是全球受影响者？只看到施工结束，还是看到运营、维护与退役？评价范围决定哪些得失进入账户，是算数之前必须公开的约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Social net benefit is relative to an explicit scope: local, national or global stakeholders, and construction alone or operation, maintenance and decommissioning as well. This scope determines what enters the account and must be stated before aggregation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设甲市修路，让本市减少 30 的拥堵损失，却将价值 20 的噪声损害转移给邻市。只看甲市会得到 30；将两市纳入同一等权货币账户，才得到 10。若政策目标确实只针对甲市，也应另列邻市影响，不能把核算之外误说成没有影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A constructed road scheme removes congestion damage worth 30 locally but shifts noise damage worth 20 to a neighbouring city. A local account gives 30; a common equally weighted account gives ten. An explicitly local mandate does not make external impacts disappear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间范围也会改变比较：一种设施便宜但五年后要更换，另一种初建较贵却能用十年，不能只比较同一年的付款。应覆盖相关服务期间，并交代期末仍可使用的资产价值与退役负担。寿命不同本身不指定替换方案，见 [[不同寿命项目的替换假设]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inexpensive asset requiring replacement after five years cannot be compared with a ten-year asset using initial payments alone. Cover the relevant service period and terminal asset values or liabilities. Different lives do not themselves justify a replacement assumption; see [[不同寿命项目的替换假设|replacement assumptions for unequal project lives]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

范围确定后，还要说明如何加总不同人的金额。边界内的等额付款和收款在等权货币账户中可按 [[转移支付核算]] 抵消；若收款人不在范围内，或采用分配权重，就应按实际评价规则计算。**纳入谁**与**给予多大权重**是相连但不同的选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After setting the scope, specify aggregation. Internal payments may cancel under [[转移支付核算|equal-weight transfer accounting]], but not unchanged when recipients are excluded or weights differ. Inclusion and weighting are related, distinct decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
