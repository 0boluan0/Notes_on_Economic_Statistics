# 贷款被证券化并离开发起人资产负债表不等于中介功能和风险转换已经消失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A securitised loan leaving the originator's balance sheet does not mean that intermediation and risk transformation have disappeared*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> [[证券化]]可以把贷款从发起人移到载体，再把资产池现金流分配给证券投资者。但筛选、服务、分档、融资、做市、担保和流动性承诺仍由一组机构承担。原来集中在一家银行的中介功能，可能只是被拆到更长的链条上。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Securitisation can move loans from an originator to a vehicle and distribute pool cash flows to security holders. Screening, servicing, tranching, funding, market making, guarantees, and liquidity promises are still performed by institutions. Functions once concentrated in one bank may simply be distributed across a longer chain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条简化链条可以是：贷款机构发起房贷并卖给 SPV，SPV 发行抵押支持证券，交易商用短期回购持有库存，基金又以可赎回份额向投资者融资。最终家庭没有直接筛选房贷借款人；信息生产、信用风险承担和流动性承诺被分布在多个节点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simplified chain may run from mortgage origination and sale to an SPV, through mortgage-backed securities, repo-funded dealer inventory, and a fund issuing redeemable shares. The household still does not screen mortgage borrowers directly; information production, credit-risk bearing, and liquidity promises are distributed across nodes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 风险去了哪里必须另查

收到出售现金、会计终止确认、信用风险实质转移和监管资本减免是四项不同判断。发起人可能保留服务权、第一损失档、担保、回购义务、流动性便利或隐性支持；链条的其他节点也可能用短期负债融资长期资产。所以“贷款已卖出”只是风险追踪的起点，不是终点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Receiving cash, obtaining accounting derecognition, transferring credit risk in substance, and receiving regulatory-capital relief are four different judgments. Servicing rights, first-loss tranches, guarantees, repurchase obligations, liquidity facilities, or implicit support can retain exposure at the originator, while other nodes may short-fund long assets. “The loan was sold” begins rather than ends the tracing exercise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 银行已把房贷卖给证券化载体。为什么不能直接写“银行已退出，风险由市场承担”？
>
> **答案：** 还要查银行是否保留档次、担保、服务或流动性承诺，以及证券由谁持有、怎样融资。风险可能转移、回流，或在链条中变成新的期限和流动性错配。

## 来源与核验

- [Federal Reserve Board, *Financial Stability Monitoring*](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2013/201321/index.html)：核对证券化信用、ABCP、回购和货币市场基金等多节点组成的信用中介链。
- [Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, *Traditional Versus Shadow Banking*](https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/page-one-economics/2012/02/01/traditional-versus-shadow-banking)：核对传统银行把多项转换置于一张表，而非银行链条由多个专门机构完成相似功能。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The Federal Reserve monitoring framework was checked for multi-node securitised-credit chains involving ABCP, repo, and money market funds.
- The St. Louis Fed note was checked for similar transformations occurring under one roof in traditional banking and across specialised institutions in nonbank chains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
