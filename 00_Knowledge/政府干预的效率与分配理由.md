# 政府干预可以针对效率缺口或分配目标而两类理由都需要具体方案评价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government intervention may address inefficiency or distributive objectives, and either rationale requires evaluating a concrete policy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效率理由问“是否存在尚未实现的互利机会或可减少的资源损失”；分配理由问“现有收入、消费或风险分担是否符合采用的社会目标”。两者可以同时存在，也可以分别存在。一个[[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto 有效]]的配置仍可能不符合社会选择的分配标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An efficiency rationale asks whether mutually beneficial opportunities remain unrealised or resource losses can be reduced. A distributive rationale asks whether income, consumption, or risk sharing meets the adopted social objective. The two can coexist or arise separately: a [[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto-efficient]] allocation may still fail a chosen distributive criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，解释干预理由时，应分别写出[[市场失灵|效率缺口的机制]]与[[社会福利函数|分配评价的依据]]。例如，对污染损害定价主要针对私人和社会成本不一致；给低收入者更多资源，则还包含如何评价不同人的处境这一选择。第二个理由不必先假装成第一个理由。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State the [[市场失灵|mechanism behind the efficiency shortfall]] separately from the [[社会福利函数|basis of the distributive evaluation]]. Pricing pollution damage addresses a gap between private and social costs; directing more resources to low-income people also involves a choice about how people's circumstances are valued. The latter rationale need not be recast as the former.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

提出理由之后，仍要比较税收补贴、规制、公共提供或公共融资等具体工具的行为后果和实施成本。效率与分配可能需要权衡，也可能一起改善，不能预先规定它们必然冲突。[[第二福利定理|一次总额转移的理论分离]]与[[福利定理边界|现实信息和激励约束]]必须分开理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After identifying a rationale, compare the behavioural consequences and implementation costs of concrete instruments such as taxes, subsidies, regulation, public provision, or public financing. Efficiency and distribution may involve a trade-off or improve together. Distinguish the [[第二福利定理|theoretical separation using lump-sum transfers]] from [[福利定理边界|real information and incentive constraints]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
