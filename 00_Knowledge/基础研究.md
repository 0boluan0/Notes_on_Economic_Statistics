# 基础研究以获得现象与事实背后的新基础知识为主要目的而不预设特定应用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic research seeks new foundational knowledge about phenomena and facts without a particular application in view.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基础研究是为了获得关于现象和可观察事实之基础原理的新知识而进行的理论或实验研究。区分标准是**研究当时的主要目标**，不是研究者属于大学还是企业，也不是成果后来有没有商业用途。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Basic research is theoretical or experimental inquiry seeking new knowledge about the foundations of phenomena and observable facts. Classification follows the main aim when the research is undertaken, not the researcher's employer or whether a commercial use eventually emerges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，研究一种材料怎样吸收电磁辐射，目标是理解其机制，可以是基础研究；为设计某种探测器而寻找最合适的材料，则有了特定的应用目标。这是用目的区分研究类型，不是说两种活动在现实中绝不交叉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investigating how a material absorbs electromagnetic radiation to understand the mechanism can be basic research. Seeking a suitable material for a particular detector introduces an applied objective. Activities can overlap even though their aims differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“没有特定应用”也不等于完全没有方向。面向能源等宽广领域、尚未确定具体用途的研究，仍可能属于定向基础研究。至于成果是否容易被他人重复使用、能否收费排他，是另一个[[物品的竞争性与排他性|消费与产权问题]]；不能仅凭“基础”二字判定应由谁出资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A broad orientation, such as energy, is compatible with basic research when no particular use is specified. Reuse and enforceable exclusion are separate [[物品的竞争性与排他性|questions about consumption and property rights]]; the label alone does not determine who should fund the work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
