# 异方差与自相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当回归误差的波动大小随观察变化，或不同观察的误差彼此相关时，重新获得可信推断并在适当条件下提高效率。
> **具体锚点：** 高收入家庭消费误差更分散是异方差；同一地区连续年份受持久未观测冲击是自相关/组内相关。
> **核心难点：** 在外生性仍成立时 OLS 系数可一致，但经典标准误错误；若误差相关还伴随滞后因变量或内生性，系数本身也可能偏。
> **为什么重要：** “换稳健标准误”修的是推断，不是模型中的所有问题。
> **继续：** 先画残差并识别依赖层级，再选择 robust、cluster、HAC、WLS 或 GLS。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When regression errors have observation-specific variances or are correlated across observations, these methods recover credible inference and, under suitable conditions, improve efficiency.
> **Concrete anchor:** Greater dispersion in consumption errors among high-income households is heteroskedasticity. Persistent unobserved shocks affecting the same region across years create autocorrelation or within-group dependence.
> **Central difficulty:** If exogeneity still holds, OLS coefficients may remain consistent while classical standard errors fail. If correlated errors accompany a lagged dependent variable or endogeneity, the coefficients themselves may also be biased.
> **Why it matters:** Replacing standard errors with a robust version repairs inference, not every problem in the model.
> **Continue with:** Plot the residuals and identify the level of dependence before choosing robust, clustered, HAC, WLS, or GLS methods.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两类问题及后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The two problems and their consequences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差是 $Var(u_i\mid X)$ 随 i/X 变化；自相关是 $Cov(u_t,u_s\mid X)\ne0$。若条件均值零，OLS 点估计通常仍无偏/一致，但不再有效，经典方差公式失效。依赖结构也会改变有效样本量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity means that $Var(u_i\mid X)$ varies across observations or with $X$; autocorrelation means that $Cov(u_t,u_s\mid X)\ne0$. Under a zero conditional mean, OLS point estimates are usually still unbiased or consistent, but they are no longer efficient and the classical variance formula fails. Dependence also changes the effective sample size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 异方差诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnosing heteroskedasticity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差—拟合值图可见漏斗和非线性。Breusch–Pagan 用误差方差与指定变量关系，White 更一般但参数多。检验拒绝不说明异方差的经济来源；大样本中轻微偏离也会显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A residual-versus-fitted plot can reveal funnel shapes and nonlinear patterns. Breusch–Pagan tests a specified relationship between error variance and covariates; White's test is more general but uses many parameters. Rejection does not identify the economic source of heteroskedasticity, and even mild departures can be statistically significant in large samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 自相关诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnosing autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

画残差时序和 ACF，使用 Durbin–Watson（局限于特定一阶设定）、Breusch–Godfrey 或 Ljung–Box 等。含滞后因变量、季节性或结构突变时要选适当检验，不能只看 DW 接近 2。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Plot residuals over time and inspect their ACF; possible tests include Durbin–Watson, which is limited to specific first-order settings, Breusch–Godfrey, and Ljung–Box. With lagged dependent variables, seasonality, or structural breaks, choose a test suited to the setting rather than relying on whether DW is close to 2.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 稳健标准误
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Robust standard errors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

HC 标准误允许横截面异方差；cluster-robust 允许簇内任意相关、簇间独立；HAC/Newey–West 允许一定时间相关和异方差。少量聚类需小样本修正或替代推断。误差结构选择应由抽样/处理分配决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
HC standard errors allow cross-sectional heteroskedasticity. Cluster-robust standard errors allow arbitrary within-cluster correlation while assuming independence across clusters. HAC or Newey–West allows specified temporal dependence and heteroskedasticity. With few clusters, use small-sample corrections or alternative inference. The sampling and treatment-assignment structure should determine the error model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## WLS 与 GLS
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*WLS and GLS*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若方差函数已知到比例，WLS 按逆方差加权可提高效率；GLS 用误差协方差变换模型。可行 GLS 先估协方差。协方差模型错设时可能比 OLS 更差，因此常同时报告稳健推断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the variance function is known up to scale, inverse-variance weighting in WLS can improve efficiency. GLS transforms the model using the error covariance matrix; feasible GLS first estimates that covariance structure. A misspecified covariance model can perform worse than OLS, so robust inference is often reported alongside it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不能被修复的部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What these corrections cannot repair*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

稳健标准误不解决遗漏变量、反向因果、测量误差或错误函数形式。若序列相关来自遗漏动态，先重设均值方程而非只改标准误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Robust standard errors do not solve omitted variables, reverse causality, measurement error, or an incorrect functional form. If serial correlation reflects omitted dynamics, respecify the conditional-mean equation before merely changing the standard errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 异方差下 OLS 系数和经典标准误分别怎样？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under heteroskedasticity, what happens to OLS coefficients and classical standard errors?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 外生性等条件仍成立时系数可无偏/一致，但经典同方差标准误通常错误且 OLS 不再有效。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If exogeneity and the other coefficient conditions still hold, OLS can remain unbiased or consistent. Classical homoskedastic standard errors are generally wrong, however, and OLS is no longer efficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### cluster 的层级应怎样选择？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How should the clustering level be chosen?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 选择误差可能共同相关或处理被共同分配的层级；不是为了得到更小 p 值而尝试多个层级。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Cluster at the level where errors may share dependence or treatment is jointly assigned. Do not try several levels merely to obtain a smaller p-value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 为什么 Newey–West 不能修复遗漏动态？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can Newey–West not repair omitted dynamics?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它只调整估计量方差；若均值方程漏掉滞后结构，系数含义和预测结构仍错误。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It adjusts only the estimated variance. If the conditional-mean equation omits lagged structure, the coefficient interpretation and forecasting structure remain wrong.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
