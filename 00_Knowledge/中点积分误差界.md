# The midpoint error bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中点积分误差界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[中点法|midpoint rule]] gives an approximate integral from finitely many function values. An **error bound** adds a different piece of information: a number that the possible discrepancy cannot exceed. Its input is not just the sample values, but a bound on how rapidly the slope can change between them, measured by the [[高阶导数|second derivative]] $f''$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[中点法]]用有限个函数值给出积分近似，**误差界**再补充另一种信息：实际偏差不可能超过的数。它需要的不只是采样值，还包括采样点之间斜率变化速度的界；这个变化由[[高阶导数|二阶导数]] $f''$ 描述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, two midpoint rectangles give $5/16$ for $\int_0^1x^2\,dx$, whose true value is $1/3$. Here the discrepancy is exactly $1/48$. The theorem below gives this upper bound using $f''=2$, without needing to know the true integral first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两个中点矩形把 $\int_0^1x^2\,dx$ 近似为 $5/16$，而真值为 $1/3$，偏差恰为 $1/48$。下面的定理只用 $f''=2$ 就能给出这一界，无须先知道真积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The quantities that must be controlled
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*需要控制哪些量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers and assume the real-valued function $f$ belongs to $C^2([a,b])$. Here $C^2$ means that $f$ is continuous on the closed interval, has first and second derivatives inside, and these derivatives extend continuously to both endpoints. This is a convenient sufficient smoothness assumption. Choose a finite constant $K_2\ge0$ with $|f''(x)|\le K_2$ everywhere on the interval. Any valid upper bound works; it need not be the smallest one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 为有限实数，假设实值函数 $f\in C^2([a,b])$。这里 $C^2$ 表示 $f$ 在闭区间连续，内部有一阶、二阶导数，且这两个导数都能连续延伸到端点。这是一组方便的充分光滑性条件。选取有限常数 $K_2\ge0$，使区间内处处满足 $|f''(x)|\le K_2$。任何有效上界都可以，不必恰好取最小的界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $I=\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$ be the true integral. Divide the interval into a positive integer $N$ of equal pieces of width $h=(b-a)/N$, and let $M_N$ be the midpoint approximation. The signed error is $E_N=I-M_N$; the absolute error is $|E_N|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $I=\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$ 为真积分。把区间等分为正整数 $N$ 段，段宽为 $h=(b-a)/N$，中点近似记作 $M_N$。带符号误差为 $E_N=I-M_N$，绝对误差为 $|E_N|$。

> [!important] A whole-interval guarantee · 覆盖整个区间的保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these assumptions,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述条件下，
>
> $$\boxed{|I-M_N|\le\frac{K_2(b-a)h^2}{24}
> =\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{24N^2}.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interval length and curvature bound both matter. Keeping them fixed and doubling $N$ divides this guaranteed upper bound by four. It does not assert that every particular function's actual error is divided by four; cancellation or exactness can make the actual error much smaller.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间长度与二阶导数界都重要。保持二者不变，把 $N$ 加倍，会使这个保证上界变成四分之一。但这不表示每个具体函数的实际误差都变成四分之一；抵消或恰好精确可能使实际误差小得多。

## Why symmetric sampling leaves a second-order global error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对称取样为何留下二阶误差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider one piece $[u,v]$, with width $h=v-u$, centre $m=(u+v)/2$, and half-width $\ell=h/2$. On the left half, use [[分部积分|integration by parts]] twice, first with an antiderivative $x-u$ of $1$ and then with $(x-u)^2/2$ of $x-u$. On the right half use $x-v$ and $(x-v)^2/2$. The two exact identities are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考察一段 $[u,v]$，宽度为 $h=v-u$，中心为 $m=(u+v)/2$，半宽为 $\ell=h/2$。左半段做两次[[分部积分]]：先把 $1$ 的原函数选为 $x-u$，再把 $x-u$ 的原函数选为 $(x-u)^2/2$。右半段相应使用 $x-v$ 和 $(x-v)^2/2$。得到两个精确恒等式：

$$\begin{aligned}
\int_u^m f(x)\,dx
&=\ell f(m)-\frac{\ell^2}{2}f'(m)
 +\frac12\int_u^m(x-u)^2f''(x)\,dx,\\
\int_m^v f(x)\,dx
&=\ell f(m)+\frac{\ell^2}{2}f'(m)
 +\frac12\int_m^v(x-v)^2f''(x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding cancels the two slope terms and leaves $2\ell f(m)=hf(m)$ as the midpoint rectangle. Thus the one-piece error is a weighted integral of the second derivative:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两式相加时，两个斜率项相消，剩下的 $2\ell f(m)=hf(m)$ 正是中点矩形。于是单段误差成为二阶导数的加权积分：

$$\int_u^v f(x)\,dx-hf(m)
=\frac12\int_u^m(x-u)^2f''(x)\,dx
 +\frac12\int_m^v(x-v)^2f''(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weights $(x-u)^2/2$ and $(x-v)^2/2$ are nonnegative. Applying the [[积分绝对值界|integral triangle inequality]] and $|f''|\le K_2$ therefore bounds the absolute local error by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重 $(x-u)^2/2$ 与 $(x-v)^2/2$ 都非负。用[[积分绝对值界]]及 $|f''|\le K_2$，单段绝对误差至多为

$$\frac{K_2}{2}\left(\frac{(h/2)^3}{3}+\frac{(h/2)^3}{3}\right)
=\frac{K_2h^3}{24}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]], the global signed error is the sum of the $N$ local errors. Its absolute value is at most the sum of their absolute values, giving $NK_2h^3/24=K_2(b-a)h^2/24$. This is why a cubic local bound becomes a squared-step global bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[定积分区间可加性]]，整体带符号误差是 $N$ 个局部误差之和。它的绝对值至多为各局部绝对值之和，所以得到 $NK_2h^3/24=K_2(b-a)h^2/24$。这说明单段的三次步长界为何变成整体的二次步长界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same exact identity gives the direction under these smoothness assumptions. If $f''\ge0$ everywhere, every weighted contribution is nonnegative, so $M_N\le I$; if $f''\le0$, then $M_N\ge I$. These are weak inequalities: an affine function has $f''=0$ and is exact. An increasing function alone does not determine the direction, because an increasing graph may bend either way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这组光滑性条件下，同一个精确恒等式还给出方向：若处处 $f''\ge0$，每项加权贡献都非负，所以 $M_N\le I$；若处处 $f''\le0$，则 $M_N\ge I$。这里允许等号：一次或常函数的 $f''=0$，恰好精确。单有函数递增不足以确定方向，因为递增图像仍可能向两种方向弯曲。

## Use the bound to choose the number of samples
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用误差界选择采样数量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, use $K_2=2$. With $N=2$, the bound is $2/(24\cdot4)=1/48$. Directly, $I-M_2=1/3-5/16=1/48$, so the constant is attained in this example. This is not merely an order-of-magnitude estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，取 $K_2=2$。$N=2$ 时，界为 $2/(24\cdot4)=1/48$。直接算得 $I-M_2=1/3-5/16=1/48$，此例恰好达到界，因此它不只是一个数量级估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a desired absolute tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, choose any positive integer satisfying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若希望绝对误差不超过 $\varepsilon>0$，可以选取任何满足下式的正整数：

$$N\ge\sqrt{\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{24\varepsilon}}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Round upward when necessary and keep $N\ge1$, even when $K_2=0$. For $f(x)=1/x$ on $[1,2]$, $f''(x)=2/x^3\le2$, so $K_2=2$ works. To guarantee error at most $10^{-3}$, the threshold is $\sqrt{1/(12\cdot10^{-3})}\approx9.129$; $N=10$ gives the certified bound $1/1200<10^{-3}$. This is a sufficient sample count, not a claim that nine samples must fail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要时向上取整，而且即使 $K_2=0$ 也要保持 $N\ge1$。对 $[1,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=1/x$，有 $f''(x)=2/x^3\le2$，因此可取 $K_2=2$。若要求误差不超过 $10^{-3}$，门槛为 $\sqrt{1/(12\cdot10^{-3})}\approx9.129$；取 $N=10$ 得到保证界 $1/1200<10^{-3}$。这是足够的采样数量，不是在断言九次采样必然失败。

> [!warning] Do not infer smoothness from the samples · 不能从采样点推断整段光滑
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=|x|$ on $[-1,1]$, the second derivative is zero away from the corner but the stated $C^2$ assumption fails at zero. Taking $K_2=0$ after ignoring that corner is invalid: with $N=1$, $M_1=0$ while the integral is $1$. Continuity still makes midpoint sums converge, but this theorem has not supplied a global zero error bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=|x|$，拐角以外二阶导数为零，但零点不满足所述 $C^2$ 条件。不能忽略拐角就取 $K_2=0$：当 $N=1$，$M_1=0$，真积分却是 $1$。连续性仍保证中点和收敛，但本定理并没有给出整体零误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the same valid $K_2$, interval and $N$, the [[梯形积分误差界|trapezoidal guarantee]] has constant $1/12$, twice this midpoint bound. That compares upper bounds, not a universal ratio of actual errors. The displayed guarantee also assumes exact function values and arithmetic; inaccurate measurements and rounding introduce separate errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在相同有效 $K_2$、区间与 $N$ 下，[[梯形积分误差界]]的常数为 $1/12$，保证值是中点界的两倍。这比较的是上界，不是实际误差的通用比例。所列保证还默认函数值与算术精确；测量不准和舍入会引入另外的误差。
