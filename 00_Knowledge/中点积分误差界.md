# 二阶导数绝对值有界时复合中点法的绝对误差不超过区间长度乘步长平方乘该界的二十四分之一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With a bound on the second derivative, composite midpoint error is at most one twenty-fourth of that bound times interval length times squared step size*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$、$f\in C^2([a,b])$，并在整个区间上有 $|f''(x)|\le K_2$。令 $I=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$，用 $N\ge1$ 个等宽小区间、$h=(b-a)/N$ 计算 [[中点法]] 的 $M_N$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$, $f\in C^2([a,b])$, and $|f''(x)|\le K_2$ throughout the interval. Set $I=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$, and compute the [[中点法|composite midpoint value]] $M_N$ using $N\ge1$ equal subintervals with $h=(b-a)/N$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\boxed{|I-M_N|\le\frac{K_2(b-a)h^2}{24}
=\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{24N^2}.}
$$

这里 $C^2$ 是直到二阶导数连续的充分条件，$K_2$ 必须控制整段而非仅取样点。对同一 $K_2$ 和 $N$，本公式的上界是 [[梯形积分误差界]] 的一半；这是两个保证值的比较，并不保证每个具体函数的实际误差恰好相差两倍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $C^2$ assumption requires continuous derivatives through order two, and $K_2$ must bound the entire interval. With the same $K_2$ and $N$, this bound is half [[梯形积分误差界|the trapezoidal bound]]. That compares guarantees, without asserting an exact ratio between the actual errors for every function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对称取样为何留下二阶误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why symmetric sampling leaves a second-order global error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在宽度为 $h$ 的一段 $[u,v]$ 上令 $m=(u+v)/2$。左右半段各做两次分部积分，中点处的一阶导数项相消，得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a subinterval $[u,v]$ of width $h$, let $m=(u+v)/2$. Integrating by parts twice on each half makes the first-derivative terms at the midpoint cancel, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\int_u^v f(x)\,dx-hf(m)
=\frac12\int_u^m(x-u)^2f''(x)\,dx
+\frac12\int_m^v(x-v)^2f''(x)\,dx.
$$

因此每段绝对误差至多 $\tfrac{K_2}{2}\{(h/2)^3/3+(h/2)^3/3\}=K_2h^3/24$；累加 $N$ 段给出 $NK_2h^3/24$。两个核都非负，所以 $f''\ge0$ 时中点法低估，$f''\le0$ 时高估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The absolute local error is at most $\tfrac{K_2}{2}\{(h/2)^3/3+(h/2)^3/3\}=K_2h^3/24$. Summing $N$ such bounds gives $NK_2h^3/24$. Both kernels are nonnegative, so the midpoint rule underestimates when $f''\ge0$ and overestimates when $f''\le0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x)=x^2$、$[0,1]$、$N=2$，取 $K_2=2$，误差界为 $1/48$。直接算 $M_2=\tfrac12[(\tfrac14)^2+(\tfrac34)^2]=5/16$，而 $I-M_2=1/3-5/16=1/48$，恰好达到界。要保证误差不超过 $\varepsilon>0$，可取任意正整数 $N\ge\sqrt{K_2(b-a)^3/(24\varepsilon)}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ with $N=2$, take $K_2=2$ to obtain a bound of $1/48$. Directly, $M_2=\tfrac12[(\tfrac14)^2+(\tfrac34)^2]=5/16$ and $I-M_2=1/3-5/16=1/48$, attaining the bound. For tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, choose any positive integer $N\ge\sqrt{K_2(b-a)^3/(24\varepsilon)}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对 $f(x)=|x|$ 在 $[-1,1]$ 上，能因取了很多中点就直接使用这个 $K_2$ 公式吗？
>
> **答案：** 不能；$x=0$ 处不满足所述二阶光滑性条件。中点和仍可因连续性而收敛，但这个误差保证尚未建立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=|x|$ on $[-1,1]$, does using many midpoints justify this $K_2$ formula? **Answer:** No. The required second-order smoothness fails at $0$. Continuity still gives convergence of midpoint sums, but does not establish this error guarantee.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
