# Categorical–continuous interaction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*类别与连续变量的交互*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose hourly wages rise with years of experience. A model that gives two groups the same slope says that one extra year is associated with the same wage increase in both groups. A **categorical–continuous interaction** lets that increase depend on the group. With two groups, it represents two lines whose intercepts and slopes can both differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设小时工资随工作经验变化。如果给两个组规定同一斜率，就表示多一年经验对应的工资变化在两组相同。**类别与连续变量的交互**允许这种变化因组而异。对于两个组，它用两条线表示关系，并允许截距与斜率都不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build the product, then substitute the group
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先构造乘积，再代入组别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ be hourly wage and $X$ years of experience. The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $D$ is $0$ for the reference group and $1$ for the comparison group. Assume $E|Y|<\infty$, so the [[条件期望|conditional mean]] $m(x,d)=E[Y\mid X=x,D=d]$ describes the average wage at a given experience and group. The following is a proposed form for that mean:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Y$ 表示小时工资，$X$ 表示经验年数；[[事件指示变量]] $D$ 对基准组取 $0$、比较组取 $1$。假设 $E|Y|<\infty$，使[[条件期望|条件均值]] $m(x,d)=E[Y\mid X=x,D=d]$ 描述给定经验与组别的平均工资。我们提出如下均值形式：

$$m(x,d)=\beta_0+\beta_Xx+\beta_Dd+\beta_{XD}xd.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four $\beta$ symbols are fixed model coefficients. The new predictor $XD$ is an ordinary product: it equals $0$ in the reference group and equals $X$ in the comparison group. Thus $\beta_{XD}XD$ changes only the comparison group’s slope. The separate term $\beta_DD$ allows an intercept difference. In a [[指示变量系数|model without this interaction]], the two slopes would be forced to agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四个 $\beta$ 是固定的模型系数。新增解释变量 $XD$ 就是普通乘积：基准组中为 $0$，比较组中等于 $X$。因此 $\beta_{XD}XD$ 只调整比较组斜率，另一个单独的 $\beta_DD$ 则允许截距不同。[[指示变量系数|没有这项交互的模型]]会强制两组斜率相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Group | Mean line | Change per extra unit of $X$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference, $d=0$ | $m(x,0)=\beta_0+\beta_Xx$ | $\beta_X$ |
| Comparison, $d=1$ | $m(x,1)=(\beta_0+\beta_D)+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})x$ | $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 组别 | 均值直线 | $X$ 多一单位时的变化 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 基准组 $d=0$ | $m(x,0)=\beta_0+\beta_Xx$ | $\beta_X$ |
| 比较组 $d=1$ | $m(x,1)=(\beta_0+\beta_D)+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})x$ | $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtraction at the **same** $x$ gives the group gap $\Delta(x)=m(x,1)-m(x,0)=\beta_D+\beta_{XD}x$. Hence $\beta_{XD}$ is the difference between slopes, not the comparison group’s entire slope. Equivalently, it is how much the group gap changes per extra unit of $x$. The coefficient $\beta_D$ is only the gap at $x=0$; [[交互主效应基准|reference values]] explain why this matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在**同一个** $x$ 处相减，组差为 $\Delta(x)=m(x,1)-m(x,0)=\beta_D+\beta_{XD}x$。因此 $\beta_{XD}$ 是两组斜率之差，不是比较组的完整斜率；也可理解为 $x$ 多一单位时，组差改变多少。$\beta_D$ 只给出 $x=0$ 时的组差，这正是[[交互主效应基准|参照值]]重要的原因。

> [!example] Wages at ten years · 十年经验处的工资
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the fitted rule
> $$\widehat m(x,d)=12+0.80x-1.50d-0.25xd.$$
> A hat marks a value estimated from data. The reference line is $12+0.80x$; the comparison line is $10.50+0.55x$. At ten years the fitted wages are $20$ and $16$, so the comparison-minus-reference gap is $-4$. At twenty years they are $28$ and $21.50$, giving $-6.50$. The interaction coefficient $-0.25$ says the gap becomes $0.25$ more negative per year; both groups’ fitted wages still increase with experience.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如拟合规则为上式，帽子表示由数据估计的量。基准组直线是 $12+0.80x$，比较组是 $10.50+0.55x$。十年经验时，两组拟合工资为 $20$、$16$，比较组减基准组是 $-4$；二十年时为 $28$、$21.50$，差为 $-6.50$。交互系数 $-0.25$ 表示组差每年再减 $0.25$，并不表示比较组工资随经验下降：两组斜率仍都为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The mean model and the data must be distinguished
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值设定与数据支持是两件事*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Writing the expression as $E[Y\mid X,D]$ assumes it is the true conditional-mean form on the relevant support. If it is only a fitted [[条件均值与线性投影|linear approximation]], the same substitutions describe fitted lines, but do not prove that the true means are straight. For continuous $X$, conditional means are functions defined almost everywhere; the notation does not divide by $P(X=x)=0$. Evaluating the proposed line at an unsupported point is a model extrapolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把式子写成 $E[Y\mid X,D]$，是在相关支持范围内假设真实条件均值具有这个形式。如果它只是拟合出来的[[条件均值与线性投影|线性近似]]，代入规则仍能解释拟合线，却不能证明真实均值是直线。连续 $X$ 的条件均值是几乎处处定义的函数，记号不意味着除以 $P(X=x)=0$；在没有数据支持的位置评价直线，属于模型外推。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To estimate four unrestricted line coefficients uniquely by ordinary least squares using only the columns $1,X,D,XD$, each group must be observed at at least two distinct $X$ values. This is the [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full-column-rank]] requirement in this particular design. With additional controls, the entire design must still be checked. Having estimable lines does not guarantee a supported same-$x$ comparison: groups observed only at $0$–$5$ and $15$–$20$ years can each supply a slope, while their gap at ten years relies on extrapolation. See [[交互稀疏支持|interaction support]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果普通最小二乘只用 $1,X,D,XD$ 四列，要唯一估计四个不受限制的直线系数，每组都至少要有两个不同的 $X$ 取值；这是该设计的[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]条件。加入其他控制后，还要检查整个设计。能估计两条线，不等于同一 $x$ 处存在实际比较：一组只观察到 $0$–$5$ 年，另一组只观察到 $15$–$20$ 年，各组仍可提供斜率，但十年处的组差依赖外推，见[[交互稀疏支持]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Ask for the contrast that answers the question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把问题写成完整对比*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parallel lines mean $\beta_{XD}=0$. Identical lines mean the joint restriction $\beta_D=\beta_{XD}=0$. Equality only at a chosen $x_0$ means $\beta_D+x_0\beta_{XD}=0$. These are different [[零假设|null hypotheses]]. A test of one interaction coefficient answers the slope question; it does not answer all three questions at once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平行线对应 $\beta_{XD}=0$；整条线相同对应联合限制 $\beta_D=\beta_{XD}=0$；只在选定 $x_0$ 处相同对应 $\beta_D+x_0\beta_{XD}=0$。这是不同的[[零假设]]。单独检验交互系数回答的是斜率问题，不能同时回答三个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed ten-year comparison, estimate $\widehat\Delta(10)=\widehat\beta_D+10\widehat\beta_{XD}$. Its estimated variance is
$$\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\widehat\Delta(10))=\widehat V_{DD}+100\widehat V_{XD,XD}+20\widehat V_{D,XD},$$
where $\widehat V$ is the complete estimated [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] of the coefficient estimates, using a method appropriate for the sampling scheme. The diagonal terms are variances and the cross term is their covariance. [[线性组合推断|Linear-combination inference]] explains why adding separate standard errors is incorrect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定比较十年经验时，估计的是 $\widehat\Delta(10)=\widehat\beta_D+10\widehat\beta_{XD}$，估计方差如上。$\widehat V$ 是按合适抽样假设得到的完整系数估计量[[协方差矩阵]]；对角项是方差，交叉项是协方差。[[线性组合推断]]解释了为什么不能把两行标准误直接相加。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These slopes and gaps initially describe association within the mean model. Calling a group gap a treatment effect requires a justified [[条件均值因果边界|causal identification argument]]; adding an interaction does not provide it. With more than two categories, [[多类别指示编码|reference-group indicators]] and their products with $X$ extend the same construction to one line per group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些斜率与组差首先描述均值模型中的关联。把组差称为处理效应，需要得到论证的[[条件均值因果边界|因果识别依据]]，新增一个交互项不会提供该依据。类别超过两个时，使用[[多类别指示编码|基准组编码]]及各指示变量与 $X$ 的乘积，可把同一构造推广为每组一条线。
