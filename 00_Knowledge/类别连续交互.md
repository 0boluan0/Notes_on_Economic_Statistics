# 在线性模型中，指示变量与连续变量的交互允许比较组相对基准组同时改变截距和斜率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a linear model, an indicator-by-continuous interaction allows the comparison group to differ from the reference group in both intercept and slope*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 两条组别回归线
> 对 $D\in\{0,1\}$，写
> $$
> E[Y\mid X,D]
> =\beta_0+\beta_XX+\beta_DD+\beta_{XD}XD.
> $$
> 把 $D=0$ 与 $D=1$ 分别代入，便得到基准组与比较组各自的截距和斜率；交互项的作用是允许两条线不再平行。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $D\in\{0,1\}$, the model $E[Y\mid X,D]=\beta_0+\beta_XX+\beta_DD+\beta_{XD}XD$ yields one line for the reference group and another for the comparison group. The interaction allows the lines to have different slopes rather than forcing them to remain parallel.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 对象 | 条件均值 | $X$ 的斜率 |
|---|---|---|
| $D=0$ 基准组 | $\beta_0+\beta_XX$ | $\beta_X$ |
| $D=1$ 比较组 | $(\beta_0+\beta_D)+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})X$ | $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$ |
| 给定 $X=x$ 的组差 | $\beta_D+\beta_{XD}x$ | — |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Object | Conditional mean | Slope in $X$ |
|---|---|---|
| Reference group, $D=0$ | $\beta_0+\beta_XX$ | $\beta_X$ |
| Comparison group, $D=1$ | $(\beta_0+\beta_D)+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})X$ | $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$ |
| Group difference at $X=x$ | $\beta_D+\beta_{XD}x$ | — |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\beta_{XD}$ 是两组斜率之差，不是任一组的完整斜率；$\beta_D$ 是 $X=0$ 时的组差，不是所有 $X$ 上的固定差异。若问题是“10 年经验处的工资组差”，目标量就是 $\beta_D+10\beta_{XD}$，其区间必须通过[[线性组合推断]]计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\beta_{XD}$ is the difference between slopes, not either group's complete slope; $\beta_D$ is the group difference at $X=0$, not a constant difference for every $X$. If the question concerns the wage gap at ten years of experience, the target is $\beta_D+10\beta_{XD}$ and its interval must be obtained through [[线性组合推断|inference for a coefficient combination]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\widehat{wage}=12+0.80X-1.50D-0.25XD$。基准组斜率是 $0.80$，比较组斜率是 $0.55$；在 $X=10$ 时组差是 $-1.50-0.25\times10=-4.00$，而不是孤立读取 $-1.50$ 或 $-0.25$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\widehat{wage}=12+0.80X-1.50D-0.25XD$, the reference slope is $0.80$ and the comparison slope is $0.55$. At $X=10$, the group difference is $-4.00$, not either isolated coefficient $-1.50$ or $-0.25$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在上述一般模型中，检验“两组整条回归线相同”需要什么限制？
>
> **答案：** 联合检验 $H_0:\beta_D=\beta_{XD}=0$；只检验交互系数为零仅回答斜率是否相同。
