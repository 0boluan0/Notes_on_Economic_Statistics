# 重农主义的“不生产阶级”是其核算中不产生净产品但仍参与生产与交换的非农业阶级
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The physiocratic “sterile class” is the non-agricultural class that participates in production and exchange without generating net product in physiocratic accounts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> Quesnay 把手工业者、商人和其他非农业劳动者列入 **classe stérile（“不生产”阶级）**。按重农主义的核算，这一阶级的收入用于补回原料、生活资料和经营预付，不像农业那样留下可作为土地收入的 [[重农主义净产品]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Quesnay placed artisans, merchants, and other non-agricultural workers in the **classe stérile**. In physiocratic accounts, this class uses its receipts to restore materials, subsistence, and operating advances; unlike agriculture, it leaves no [[重农主义净产品|net product]] available as land revenue.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它在《经济表》中做什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What it does in the Tableau*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Quesnay 经济表]] 中，这一阶级从生产阶级购买食物和原料，把部分原料加工成制成品，再卖给生产阶级和所有者阶级。它的支出和收入是年度循环的必要部分；分类线只在于，这些交易最终是否在补回预付之外留下重农主义净产品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Quesnay 经济表|Tableau]], this class buys food and raw materials from the productive class, transforms some materials into manufactures, and sells those goods to productive and proprietor classes. Its receipts and expenditure are necessary parts of the annual circuit. The classification asks only whether these exchanges leave physiocratic net product after advances have been restored.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一名工匠购入羊毛和食物，制作并售出呢料。若按 Quesnay 的设定，售货收入只恢复原料、生活资料和经营预付，没有多出一笔可交给土地所有者的净产品，这名工匠就属于“不生产”阶级。这个名称的误读与现代增加值口径的区别，由 [[不生产阶级不等于无产出]] 单独承担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an artisan buys wool and food, makes cloth, and sells it. If, under Quesnay's assumptions, the receipts merely restore materials, subsistence, and operating advances without leaving a net product payable to proprietors, the artisan belongs to the sterile class. The separate boundary note explains why this label does not mean [[不生产阶级不等于无产出|no output or zero modern value added]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> Quesnay 用哪一条标准把工匠与商人划入“不生产”阶级？
>
> **答案：** 看该活动在补回原料、生活资料和经营预付后，是否留下重农主义净产品；不是看它是否生产有用物品。
