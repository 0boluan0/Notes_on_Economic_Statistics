# Positive semidefiniteness of covariance matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*协方差矩阵的半正定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A proposed table of variances and covariances must be consistent with every way of combining the measurements. If we add two measurements, subtract them, or give them other fixed weights, the resulting random number cannot have negative variance. **Positive semidefiniteness** is the matrix form of this requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一张方差、协方差表，必须与各种组合测量值的方式相容。不论把两个测量值相加、相减，还是赋予其他固定权重，结果都是一个随机数，它的方差不能为负。**半正定性**就是把这个要求写成矩阵语言。

> [!example] Positive diagonal entries are not enough · 对角为正仍可能不合法
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose someone proposes $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{bmatrix}$ as the [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] of two real variables $X_1,X_2$. Its diagonal would give each variable variance $1$, and its off-diagonal entries would give covariance $2$. The [[和的方差协方差项|variance formula for a difference]] would then require
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设有人把 $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{bmatrix}$ 当作实变量 $X_1,X_2$ 的[[协方差矩阵]]。对角意味着各自方差为 $1$，非对角意味着协方差为 $2$。那么[[和的方差协方差项|差值的方差公式]]会要求
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}(X_1-X_2)=1+1-2\times2=-2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A [[方差|variance]] averages squared deviations and cannot be negative. Thus no such random vector exists. Looking only at individual variances checked the coordinate directions but missed the combination $X_1-X_2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[方差]]平均的是平方偏移，不可能为负，因此不存在这样的随机向量。只看各自方差，相当于只检查坐标轴方向，却漏掉了 $X_1-X_2$ 这一组合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Checking every fixed linear combination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检查每一个固定的线性组合*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ be a real [[随机向量|random vector]], where $p\ge1$ and every $E[X_j^2]$ is finite. Write $\mu=E[X]$ for its mean vector and $\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$ for its covariance matrix. Choose any fixed real weight vector $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_p)^T$. The scalar $a^TX=\sum_j a_jX_j$ is the weighted measurement; $a$ need not have length one, positive entries, or entries summing to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ 是实[[随机向量]]，$p\ge1$，且每个 $E[X_j^2]$ 都有限。记均值向量为 $\mu=E[X]$、协方差矩阵为 $\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$。任取固定实权重向量 $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_p)^T$，标量 $a^TX=\sum_j a_jX_j$ 就是加权后的测量值。这里不要求 $a$ 长度为一、各项为正，或各项和为一。

> [!important] Every covariance quadratic form is a variance · 每个协方差二次型都是方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[线性投影方差|linear-combination variance identity]] gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$\boxed{a^T\Sigma a=\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)\ge0\quad\text{for every }a\in\mathbb R^p.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Also $\Sigma=\Sigma^T$, because swapping covariance arguments changes nothing. A symmetric real matrix with a nonnegative [[二次型|quadratic form]] in every direction is called [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]], written $\Sigma\succeq0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[线性投影方差|线性组合的方差恒等式]]给出上式。另外，交换协方差的两个变量不会改变结果，所以 $\Sigma=\Sigma^T$。在每个方向的[[二次型]]都非负的实对称矩阵，就叫作[[半正定矩阵]]，记为 $\Sigma\succeq0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is the reason behind the identity. The centered weighted measurement is $a^T(X-\mu)$. Its square is the scalar $a^T(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^Ta$. The weights are fixed, so finite [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

恒等式背后的原因是：加权测量值中心化后为 $a^T(X-\mu)$，平方就是标量 $a^T(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^Ta$。权重固定，所以有限[[期望线性性]]给出

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)
&=E[(a^T(X-\mu))^2]\\
&=a^T E[(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^T]a
=a^T\Sigma a.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second-moment assumptions make these expectations finite. The proof uses no independence or normality. In the four-outcome example from the covariance definition, $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二阶矩假设保证这些期望有限。证明没有使用独立或正态假设。在协方差定义卡的四结果例子里，$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}$，所以

$$a^T\Sigma a=a_1^2+2a_1a_2+2a_2^2=(a_1+a_2)^2+a_2^2\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The difference weights $(1,-1)^T$ give variance $1$; the sum weights $(1,1)^T$ give variance $5$. These are valid combinations of the same covariance entries, unlike the impossible table in the opening example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差值权重 $(1,-1)^T$ 给出方差 $1$，求和权重 $(1,1)^T$ 给出方差 $5$。同一组协方差元素在这些组合下都相容，而开头那张表会产生不可能的负方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Zero directions are allowed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*允许存在零方差方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Semidefinite” allows equality for a nonzero $a$. For instance, if $X=(Z,2Z)^T$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$, then $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$. The weights $a=(2,-1)^T$ give $a^TX=0$ and therefore $a^T\Sigma a=0$. Both individual variances are positive. [[协方差奇异性|The singularity criterion]] explains this exact relation and why a covariance matrix need not be invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“半正定”允许某个非零 $a$ 使结果为零。例如 $X=(Z,2Z)^T$ 且 $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$ 时，$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$。权重 $a=(2,-1)^T$ 使 $a^TX=0$，所以 $a^T\Sigma a=0$，但两个单独坐标的方差都为正。[[协方差奇异性]]解释了这种精确关系，以及为什么协方差矩阵不一定可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $q$ is a unit [[特征对|eigenvector]] with $\Sigma q=\lambda q$ and $q^Tq=1$, its projection variance is $q^T\Sigma q=\lambda$. Thus all covariance eigenvalues are nonnegative; an eigenvalue is a variance along its unit direction, not an arbitrary signed matrix entry. This supplies the variance interpretation used in [[主成分目标|principal component analysis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $q$ 是单位[[特征对|特征向量]]，满足 $\Sigma q=\lambda q$ 且 $q^Tq=1$，那么投影方差为 $q^T\Sigma q=\lambda$。因此协方差特征值都非负；特征值是在相应单位方向上的方差，并不是任意一个可以为负的矩阵元素。这也是[[主成分目标|主成分分析]]使用的方差解释。

> [!note]- Complex-valued measurements · 复数测量值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $Z\in\mathbb C^p$ with $E|Z_j|^2<\infty$, define $\mu=E[Z]$ and $\Sigma=E[(Z-\mu)(Z-\mu)^*]$, where $*$ means conjugate transpose. Then $\Sigma=\Sigma^*$ is [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]]. For any fixed $a\in\mathbb C^p$, the same multiplication gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$a^*\Sigma a=E\bigl[|a^*(Z-\mu)|^2\bigr]\ge0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The conjugate makes this an absolute square, hence a real nonnegative quantity. Replacing it by an ordinary complex square would lose that guarantee.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $Z\in\mathbb C^p$，若 $E|Z_j|^2<\infty$，定义 $\mu=E[Z]$、$\Sigma=E[(Z-\mu)(Z-\mu)^*]$，其中 $*$ 表示共轭转置。此时 $\Sigma=\Sigma^*$ 是 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]；任取固定 $a\in\mathbb C^p$，相同的乘法给出上式。共轭使乘积成为绝对值平方，保证结果实且非负；普通复数平方没有这个保证。
